# Check: Egypt | Overseas-recruitment-agency register and compliance desk (old score 5.0/10)

1. Duty: **confirmed** (from secondary sources; the gazette text of Decision 272/2025 is still unread). Law 14/2025 was published in Official Gazette issue 18 on 3 May 2025 and took effect 1 Sep 2025. Art. 41 sets the licensing conditions, including a EGP 1m guarantee. Art. 43 bans charging the worker anything except "١٪ من أجر السنة الأولى كمصروفات إدارية". This settles the 1% vs 2% question: the 2% figure comes from the old law and drafts. Art. 48 requires contracts and requests for work abroad to be sent to the ministry, which has 15 days to object (Wikisource article summaries). Decision 272/2025 summaries add: four registers kept separately for domestic and overseas placements, pages numbered with serial numbers and stamped by the General Administration, and immediate notification of each electronic entry (Egyin, 5 Oct 2026); notarised employer contracts, a EGP 1m deposit topped up within 15 days, 1-year licence, renewal 2 months ahead for EGP 5,000, late fee EGP 2,000/month (Mondaq/Sadany, 1 Jun 2026). The monthly hiring statement appears only in Mondaq and is still unverified. https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/قانون_العمل_14_لسنة_2025_-_مصر ; https://www.egyin.com/article/151629 ; https://www.mondaq.com/contracts-and-commercial-law/1794584/licensing-of-operating-companies-in-egypt-%7C-decision-272-of-2025
2. Trigger: **confirmed**. Youm7 (10 Sep 2026) reports Minister Hassan Radad granting overseas-placement firms "مهلة لمدة 6 أشهر لتوفيق أوضاعها" under Decision 272/2025. Banker.news (9 Sep 2026) reports the formal instrument as a Prime Minister's decision extending the deadline by "ستة أشهر إضافية". This matches Law 14/2025 Art. 6: one year to comply (to about 1 Sep 2026), extendable by PM decision. The new end date of about 1 Mar 2027 is inferred, since neither article gives a calendar date. https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/10/وزير-العمل-يمنح-شركات-إلحاق-عمالة-بالخارج-مهلة-6-أشهر/7541644 ; https://banker.news/151756
3. Competition: **confirmed, with one risk to watch**. Arabic and English searches found no Egyptian product built for licensed placement agencies. The results were Gulf/Musaned recruitment-office tools, generic ATS products (Zimyo, Recruitera) and recruitment firms themselves. The ministry has no agency-facing register or contract-submission portal yet. It is, however, building one: in Dec 2025 it was developing a public database of licensed firms (banker.news), in Oct 2025 it announced a contract-verification platform, and in Aug 2026 the minister said a "منصة العمل الرقمية" was "nearing launch" for organising employment at home and abroad. Whether that platform will take over agency registers is unknown, and this is the kill condition the original report named. https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/2/وزير-العمل-لدينا-منظومة-متكاملة-لحماية-ورعاية-العمالة-المصرية/7500867 ; https://www.banker.news/119128
4. Market count: **confirmed as reported, but ambiguous and possibly overstated**. Youm7 (7 Oct 2023) gives 1,482. That equals 251 new plus 1,231 renewed licences, so it may count licensing actions rather than live firms. Other ministry figures are 920 and 845 "valid" firms (older, undated). No 2026 total was found. https://www.youm7.com/story/2023/10/7/وزارة-العمل-1482-شركة-مرخصة-لإلحاق-العمالة-المصرية-للعمل-بالخارج/6327857 ; https://almalnews.com/925120/
5. Overlap: **confirmed no overlap**. inputs/countries/egypt.md mentions Labour Law 14/2025 only for payroll/HR, an idea killed by ZenHR and others. It does not cover recruitment agencies. /home/user/mini-ChatGpt/rerun/inputs/countries/egypt.md

Suggested score: 5.0/10. The duty, grace period and lack of competitors all hold, and the 1% fee cap is now confirmed in the law. The upside is still capped by a small, unclear base of active firms and the ministry's upcoming digital platform, which the original score already discounted.
