# Check: Kosovo | Foreign-worker permit tracker (old score 4/10)

Checked 2026-10-08.

1. Trigger: **partly confirmed.** The MIA notice dated 16 Jan 2026 says "Zbatimi i plotë fillon nga data 15 mars 2026". But this is full enforcement of the 2013 law after two postponements, and it is phased in gradually. It is aimed at entry, address registration and Kosovo Serbs without Kosovo documents (EUSR transcript of 14 Mar 2026), not at new employer duties. The employer's permit obligations have existed since 2013/2018. https://mpb.rks-gov.net/f/57/11006/INFORMACION-P%C3%8BR-SHTETASIT-E-HUAJ-Q%C3%8B-HYJN%C3%8B-N%C3%8B-REPUBLIK%C3%8BN-E-KOSOV%C3%8BS ; https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/kosovo/transcript-eusr-dialogue-s%C3%B8rensen%E2%80%99s-press-remarks_en
2. Duty: **confirmed.** Law 06/L-036 (Gazette No. 6, 3 May 2018) amends Law 04/L-219 (Gazette No. 35, 5 Sep 2013) and is still in force: no replacement law adopted was found. Art. 47(1) reads "Kërkesa për vazhdimin e lejes së qëndrimit të përkohshëm duhet të paraqitet brenda tridhjetë (30) ditëve para skadimit ... në DSHAM". Art. 132(1) reads "Nga Inspektorati i Punës me gjobë ... dhjetëmijë (10.000) deri në njëzetëmijë (20.000) euro për çdo të huaj do të dënohet punëdhënësi - një person juridik" (EUR 3,000-5,000 for a natural person). Art. 128(2) sets EUR 5,000-7,000 for a legal-person employer. Temporary work stay permits run 6-12 months, so renewals recur. https://www.kosovopolice.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Ligj-Nr.06-L-036-pe%CC%88r-ndryshimin-dhe-plotesimin-e-Ligjit-Nr.-04L-219-pe%CC%88-te%CC%88-Huaijt-20.04.2018.pdf
