1. Duty: PARTLY CONFIRMED, legal basis misattributed (score-relevant). Law I-996 (consolidated version in force from 2024-11-01) Art. 10(1) and 14 require the business to keep accounting registers, a document register and turnover ledgers ("apyvartos žiniaraščiai"), make documents in two copies, do a stocktake "ne rečiau kaip kartą per metus" (Art. 14(8)), and file statistical reports "pagal Tauriųjų metalų ir brangakmenių apskaitos tvarkos apraše nustatyta tvarka" (Art. 10(6)).
