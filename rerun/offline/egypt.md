# Egypt: quiet regulated industries (offline screen, as of 2026-10-08)

**Bottom line.** No strong opportunity found. Egyptian regulators mostly build the portal themselves (medical waste, industrial environmental register, marriage officiants, food-establishment registration, narcotics tracking), or the sector is already served by accounting/CRM vendors (gold shops, real estate brokers). Three modest ideas survive, none above 5/10. The best is a back-office and register tool for licensed **overseas recruitment agencies** (5.0). Two weaker near-misses follow: **NGO bookkeeping/annual-filing pack** (3.5) and **real estate broker compliance kit** (3.5).

Items in the earlier general report (EPTTS serialization, ACI air freight, ETA monitor for accountants) are not repeated here.

Confidence note: most Egyptian legal detail below comes from news summaries and law-firm blogs. The only legal text I opened directly was Law 149/2019 (manshurat.org). Gazette issue numbers were not found for Decision 272/2025 or Decision 578/2025.

## Opportunities

None of the three is strong. They are listed best first.

### Opportunity: Register and compliance desk for licensed overseas recruitment agencies

**Industry:**
Private employment agencies placing Egyptians abroad (and at home), licensed by the Ministry of Labour (شركات إلحاق العمالة / وكالات التشغيل الخاصة).

**Buyer:**
Owner or manager of a licensed agency, usually a small family or partner-owned LLC. Activity is concentrated: a minister's statement of Dec 2024 reportedly said about 150 of 1,120 firms were active (unverified, from press summary).

**Trigger / Why now:**
- Labour Law 14/2025, in force 1 Sep 2025 (per the earlier general report; not re-read here).
- Ministerial Decision 272/2025 on operating inside and outside Egypt (issuer and content per [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/contracts-and-commercial-law/1794584/licensing-of-operating-companies-in-egypt-%7C-decision-272-of-2025), dated 1 Jun 2026).
- In Sept 2026 the Labour Minister gave agencies 6 months to bring their status into line ([Youm7, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/10/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D9%8A%D9%85%D9%86%D8%AD-%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A5%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AC-%D9%85%D9%87%D9%84%D8%A9-6-%D8%A3%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%B1/7541644)), so the window ends around March 2027.
- The ministry keeps suspending firms (11 suspended Dec 2025, per [Youm7](https://www.youm7.com/story/2025/12/2/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%B1-%D8%A5%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%81-11-%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AC-%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7-%D8%A3%D8%AD%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86/7219329)).

**Current workflow:**
1. Agency keeps four registers: job seekers, employer requests, workers contracted (and amounts collected), and the follow-up register (سجل التعقيب) for workers whose paperwork was finished. Domestic and foreign registers are kept apart.
2. Paper registers must be numbered and stamped by the General Administration, with no erasures. Electronic registers must notify the Administration of each entry immediately.
3. Contracts with employers are notarised and a copy goes to the ministry.
4. Agency may charge the worker at most 1% of first-year pay, only after placement.
5. Licence renewal: request at least 2 months before expiry, with a statement of activities, EGP 5,000 fee; late renewal adds EGP 2,000 per month up to EGP 10,000; more than a year late voids the licence.

**Pain:**
- Violations (charging workers, no written contract) can mean licence revocation or suspension ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/contracts-and-commercial-law/1794584/licensing-of-operating-companies-in-egypt-%7C-decision-272-of-2025)).
- Security deposit EGP 1m can be debited for fines.
- Pain from the paperwork itself is unverified: I found no complaint about register-keeping.

**Existing solutions:**
- Generic recruitment-office software built for Gulf domestic-worker agencies: Daftra ([recruitment-office page](https://www.daftra.com/%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AC-%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D9%85%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85/)), Qoyod (Saudi, tied to the Musaned platform).
- Generic applicant-tracking tools (Zimyo, Heygurus); Odoo; custom builds on Mostaql.
- Law firms selling licensing as a service (for example Sadany Khalifa's Decision 272 explainer).
- The ministry's own portal for job seeker registration and the agency licence lookup. Whether it offers agencies a register or API is unverified.

**The gap:**
No product found that implements Decision 272's registers (four registers, domestic/foreign split, serial numbering, entry-by-entry notification), guards the 1% fee cap, tracks notarised contracts and sends renewal/insurance reminders.

**Possible product:**
A web app for agency owners. It keeps the four registers in the Decision's format, prints stamped-ready paper copies, flags fee-cap breaches, stores contracts, and runs a licence/insurance/renewal calendar and a half-year activity statement for renewal.

**MVP:**
Register entry forms, PDF print of numbered pages, contract archive, fee-cap check, renewal calendar. No ministry integration.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
EGP 1,500-4,000 per agency per month (estimate). With perhaps 300-500 reachable agencies, a ceiling of roughly EGP 5-20m a year (estimate).

**How to find first customers:**
- The ministry's licensed-agency lookup (public query service).
- Overseas-labour divisions of the Cairo and Giza chambers of commerce (Giza division head is Ahmed Saad, Cairo division head Mounir El-Masry, per [Youm7, May 2026](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/5/6/%D8%B4%D9%8F%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AC-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%B4-%D9%85%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%87%D8%A7/7404236)).
- Ministry or chamber briefings during the grace period.

**Risks:**
- Small, shrinking active base (a trade-body headline reports a 50% fall in overseas hiring demand; I did not open it).
- Duty text is only known through secondary summaries; I could not open the Decision itself.
- Ministry may mandate its own electronic register.
- Gulf-side platforms hold the real placement workflow.

**Kill condition:**
Kill if the General Administration publishes a free electronic register for agencies, or if five agencies interviewed say a consultant or their accountant already handles registers and renewals for under EGP 1,000 a month.

**Score:** 5.0/10

**Willingness to pay:** uncertain. These agencies earn grey fees and may prefer a done-for-you licensing consultant over software.

**Founder access:** a non-local solo founder could build it but would struggle to sell without Arabic fluency and a chamber introduction.

**Offline evidence:**
Paper registers stamped by the General Administration; notarised contracts; renewal by written request ([Egyin summary of the ministry booklet](https://www.egyin.com/article/151629/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%AE%D8%B5-%D9%84%D9%87%D8%A7-%D8%A8%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%BA%D9%8A%D9%84)).

**Offline channel:**
Cairo and Giza chamber of commerce divisions for overseas labour placement; outreach from the ministry's public licensed-agency lookup.

**Market count:**
1,482 licensed (Oct 2023, ministry via Youm7); Giza division alone 420-500 firms (estimate); active firms far fewer (unverified).

### Opportunity: Bookkeeping and annual-filing pack for associations (near-miss)

**Industry:**
Non-governmental associations and foundations under Law 149/2019 (الجمعيات والمؤسسات الأهلية).

**Buyer:**
Treasurer or hired accountant of a small association; secondarily accounting offices that keep books for several associations.

**Trigger / Why now:**
Weak. The law dates from 2019, the executive regulation from Decision 104/2021. 2025-26 changes are state-side: a ministry e-system for services and periodic reports, and a grants e-system launched 29 Sep 2025. Annual NGO funding of EGP 12.7bn was reported for 2026 ([Cairo24](https://www.cairo24.com/2502331)).

**Current workflow:**
1. Record income and expenses in regular books kept at the association's headquarters.
2. Prepare an annual budget on the state fiscal year and a closing account.
3. If income or expenses exceed EGP 100,000, a registered auditor reviews the closing account; the auditor sends the report to the administrative authority.
4. Publish the closing account at least 7 days before the general assembly.
5. The authority may object within 15 working days; board decisions are notified to the authority within 15 working days.

**Pain:**
Misuse of funds carries fines of EGP 50,000-500,000 (Art. 95) and possible one-year suspension by the minister (Art. 45). No penalty is set for bad bookkeeping as such. Pain from bookkeeping itself is unverified.

**Existing solutions:**
- Gulf NGO software: Qoyod, Golden Hat "Jam3iyati" (Bahrain), Nzamk, Lotus Pro, charityerp.
- Odoo NGO modules.
- General Egyptian accounting packages (Daftra, Dexef) used by accountants.
- The ministry's own e-system for service requests and periodic reports (scope unverified).

**The gap:**
No Egyptian product found that outputs the Law 149 closing account, auditor package, 7-day publication notice and 15-working-day decision notifications with a deadline calendar. This is a template-and-reminder layer, not a new ledger.

**Possible product:**
Small cloud ledger for associations with Law 149 report templates and a deadline calendar, sold mainly to accountants running many associations.

**MVP:**
Income/expense ledger by source, closing-account template, assembly and objection deadline reminders, auditor export.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
EGP 300-800 per association per month (estimate); accountant bundle EGP 50-100 per association.

**How to find first customers:**
Directorates of Social Solidarity trainings and regional federations of associations (unverified channels). No public register download was confirmed.

**Risks:**
Low willingness to pay; generic accounting software already usable; ministry system may absorb reporting; political sensitivity of the sector.

**Kill condition:**
Kill if the ministry's e-system already generates the closing account and deadline alerts, or if accountants say they use plain Excel and refuse to pay.

**Score:** 3.5/10

**Willingness to pay:** weak. Buyers would mostly pay an accountant for a done-for-you service.

**Founder access:** poor for a non-local founder (Arabic, sensitive regulator, no known public contact list).

**Offline evidence:**
Books kept at headquarters; auditor reports go to the authority; a secondary summary says registers are stamped by the authority (unverified).

**Offline channel:**
Social Solidarity directorates and regional federations (unverified); the Fund supporting association projects funded 11,373 associations (as reported), which is a possible list source.

**Market count:**
About 36,000 on the national NGO map, Sept 2026 (estimate, article not pinned down); 52,500 registered Dec 2021 ([Shorouk](https://www.shorouknews.com/news/view.aspx?cdate=02122021&id=22a0f161-0d91-4edd-a656-110a9ba49639)).

Art. 36: auditor sends report to authority. Art. 42: decisions notified within 15 working days. Penalty for bookkeeping failures: none stated; misuse of funds Art. 95.

### Opportunity: Compliance kit for registered real estate brokers (near-miss)

**Industry:**
Real estate brokerage (السمسرة العقارية), newly forced into a registry at GOEIC (the General Organization for Export and Import Control, under the Ministry of Investment).

**Buyer:**
Registered brokerage company (individuals cannot register alone; commercial register, tax card and premises needed).

**Trigger / Why now:**
Ministry of Investment Decision 578/2025 (published 17 Jan 2026) sets broker types and classes by deal value; the regularisation deadline passed on 25 Jul 2026 ([Agrinewz](https://www.agrinewz.com/Section-209/%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%88%D9%83-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF/%D9%85%D9%87%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%B3%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AD%D8%AA%D9%89-25-%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AB%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%86%D9%87%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%81%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A3%D9%88%D8%B6%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D8%B3%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%B9%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D8%B5%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A8%D8%B3-%D9%88%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AC%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%87-1441662)). Inspection campaigns are running.

**Current workflow:**
1. Register at GOEIC after a training course; weekly list of registered brokers is published.
2. Use a written brokerage contract; commission is agreed freely.
3. Pay commissions above EGP 10,000 by bank or electronic channel, not cash.
4. Record sales, purchases and commissions in writing for inspection ([Cairo24](https://www.cairo24.com/2468869)).

**Pain:**
Practising unregistered: up to 2 years in prison and EGP 50,000-1,000,000 (Art. 16 as reported). For registered brokers the recurring burden is record-keeping, not a filing; evidence of complaints not found.

**Existing solutions:**
Real-estate CRMs sold in Egypt: Engaz, Solid Egypt, myCRM, Aqarify, Bmby; portals such as Property Finder (which signed a protocol with GOEIC to help license brokers, [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/property-finder-egypt-signs-cooperation-protocol-with-the-general-organization-for-export-and-import-control-nx38vlah)).

**The gap:**
No CRM advertises GOEIC-format commission records, contract templates, registration-number fields and AML flags. That is a feature set, not a product.

**Possible product:**
Contract-template and commission-ledger add-on with proof-of-non-cash-payment storage.

**MVP:**
Broker contract templates (sale, purchase, dual, rental), commission ledger, payment evidence upload, inspection export.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
EGP 500-1,500 per brokerage per month (estimate).

**How to find first customers:**
GOEIC weekly published list of registered brokers (outreach from a public register).

**Risks:**
CRM vendors add compliance features quickly; broker count may stay small because registration needs a company and capital; legal basis cited inconsistently (Decision 578/2025 vs Law 21/2022 vs Law 120/1982).

**Kill condition:**
Kill if GOEIC publishes a mandatory electronic contract/commission system, or if two of the CRM vendors announce GOEIC fields.

**Score:** 3.5/10

**Willingness to pay:** moderate for software, since brokers already pay for CRMs and portals.

**Founder access:** moderate. The sector is Arabic-language and well covered by local vendors.

**Offline evidence:**
Trade was informal and cash-based; written contracts and non-cash commissions are new duties.

**Offline channel:**
GOEIC's weekly public list of registered brokers.

**Market count:**
Not found. About 1,000 brokers trained by April 2026 ([Agrinewz](https://www.agrinewz.com/Section-209/%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%88%D9%83-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF/%D9%85%D9%87%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%B3%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AD%D8%AA%D9%89-25-%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AB%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%86%D9%87%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%81%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A3%D9%88%D8%B6%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D8%B3%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%B9%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D8%B5%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A8%D8%B3-%D9%88%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AC%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%87-1441662)), which is not a registration count.
