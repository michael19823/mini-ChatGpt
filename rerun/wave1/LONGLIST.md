# Wave 1 long list (less strict view)

Every idea the wave checked, graded rather than pass/fail. An idea is removed only when a check named the product, rule or fact that kills it, or scored it below 3.

- **Strong**: both Opus checks scored it 4 or more.
- **Likely**: the first Opus check scored it 4 or more; the adversarial second check found nothing that kills it but was unsure or scored lower.
- **Possible**: checked, not refuted, scored 3: the duty is real but something is weaker (market size, willingness to pay, partial competition).
- **Not checked**: scored 3 or less in discovery and left over by the per-country check caps.
