# BiH AML/CFT law for small obliged entities, turned into product requirements

Status: draft, research in progress (2026-10-09). Primary source for the law: the official Službeni glasnik BiH 13/2024 issue (19 Feb 2024), PDF hosted by the judiciary portal ([Sl. glasnik BiH 13/24, PDF](https://portalfo1.pravosudje.ba/vstvfo-api/vijest/download/127392)). Article numbers below are from the Bosnian-language version in that issue. Short name below: "the Law" or "ZSPNFTA".

Abbreviations: FOO = Financial Intelligence Department of SIPA (the FIU). FUZIP = FBiH Administration for Inspection Affairs. RUIP = RS Republic Administration for Inspection Affairs. CDD = client identification and monitoring ("identifikacija i praćenje"). STR = suspicious transaction report. CTR = cash transaction report. PEP = politically exposed person. AMLS = FOO's reporting software.

## Who is obliged

Source for this section: Art. 5 of the Law ([Sl. glasnik BiH 13/24](https://portalfo1.pravosudje.ba/vstvfo-api/vijest/download/127392); also [Paragraf free text, Art. 1-35](https://www.paragraf.ba/propisi/bih/zakon-o-sprecavanju-pranja-novca-i-finansiranja-teroristickih-aktivnosti.html)).

Other obliged entities (banks, MCOs, leasing, e-money, exchange offices, insurers, brokers, fund managers, factoring, VASPs, gambling, post) are out of scope for a small-firm product (Art. 5(1)(a)-(n), (r)).

Size rules that matter for small firms:
- No general exemption for micro firms. Policies must be "proportionate to the nature and size" of the obliged entity (Art. 9(4), Art. 56(2)).
- Firms with 4 or fewer employees: if no authorised person is appointed, the legal representative (or the person running the business) is deemed the authorised person (Art. 48(4)).
- Firms with 4 or fewer employees that are not financial institutions are exempt from the yearly independent internal and external audit of the AML system (Art. 55(5)). They still need internal control (Art. 55(1)).
- Sector exemption from a documented risk assessment is possible only if the FOO and the supervisor say so in guidelines (Art. 10(4)). I found no such guideline (unverified).

Cash rule for everyone (not only obliged entities): a seller of goods, real estate or services may not accept cash above 30,000 KM, including linked payments; it must be paid to the seller's transaction account (Art. 41(1)-(3)). Real estate sale contracts of 30,000 KM or more must be paid by bank transfer (Art. 41(5)). Vehicle, vessel and aircraft dealers may not accept cash above 30,000 KM (Art. 41(7)). FUZIP, RUIP and Brčko enforce Art. 41 against non-obliged sellers (Art. 93(2)).

## Reporting to FOO (channels and formats)

**What is reported, by small DNFBPs:**

| Report | Trigger | Deadline | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Suspicious transaction report (STR, "prijava sumnjive transakcije") | Any attempted or completed suspicious transaction, suspicious funds, or suspicious client or person, regardless of amount. Includes a client refused because CDD could not be done (consider STR). | Immediately and before the transaction is executed, stating the expected execution period. Hold the transaction until reported. If that is impossible: no later than the next working day after execution, with reasons. FOO answers within 24 hours (48 hours at weekends) and may suspend up to 5 working days. | Law Art. 42, 14(4), 66 ([Law](https://portalfo1.pravosudje.ba/vstvfo-api/vijest/download/127392)) |
| Cash transaction report (CTR) | Cash transaction of 30,000 KM or more; linked cash transactions totalling 30,000 KM or more | Immediately, at the latest 3 days after the transaction | Law Art. 43(1)(a)-(b), (2) |
| High-risk-country transaction report | Any transaction of 30,000 KM or more with a natural or legal person located or seated in a country on the Art. 86 lists | Same, 3 days | Law Art. 43(1)(c)-(d) |
| Notary / lawyer reports (not bookkeepers) | Real estate sale contracts of 30,000 KM or more, loan contracts of 30,000 KM or more, documents transferring management/disposal of assets; Rulebook adds cash gifts of 30,000 KM or more and any transfer of company shares | 8 days from certification | Law Art. 44; Rulebook Art. 34 ([Rulebook](https://sipa.gov.ba/assets/files/foo-docs/Pravilnik%20BH.pdf)) |
| Authorised-person registration | Appointment or change of authorised person, deputy and responsible senior manager | 8 days | Law Art. 48(5); Rulebook Art. 42 |
| Own indicator list | Each new list and each amendment | 30 days from drafting, to FOO and the supervisor | Law Art. 57(3) |
| Reply to FOO request | Written request for data | Without delay, at most 8 working days | Law Art. 64(3) |

Note for bookkeepers: a bookkeeper rarely "executes" a client's transaction. In practice their STRs will mostly be about clients and funds seen in the books, and the CTR duty applies only where the firm itself receives or pays cash for a client. Whether FOO expects bookkeepers to report cash transactions they only see in clients' books is not settled in the sources I read (unverified; ask FOO).

**Channel:**
- The default channel is the FOO's software, AMLS ("Softver za sprečavanje pranja novca i finansiranja terorističkih aktivnosti") (Law Art. 46(1), 47). AMLS allows secure electronic reporting and two-way exchange (Art. 47(1)) ([Law](https://portalfo1.pravosudje.ba/vstvfo-api/vijest/download/127392)).
- Fallback, only "in exceptional cases" when the entity has no technical access to AMLS: post, courier, e-mail, phone or fax, sent immediately. If sent by phone, e-mail or fax, it must be confirmed through AMLS or by post or courier no later than the next working day (Art. 46(2)-(3)). FOO staff then type paper reports into AMLS (Art. 47(2)).
- Access starts with AMLS registration: the entity sends the FOO, by post or electronically, the decision appointing the authorised person and deputy with names, phones, e-mails, the firm's name, address and JIB, plus (non-financial firms) a current court-register extract or registration decision and the statistical classification notice (Rulebook Art. 42) ([Rulebook](https://sipa.gov.ba/assets/files/foo-docs/Pravilnik%20BH.pdf)).
- FOO's 2025 report says most reports arrive electronically through AMLS ([FOO report 2025](https://sipa.gov.ba/assets/files/foo-docs/FOOIZVJESTAJ2025BOS.DOC)).
- The FOO lists the "Instruction on filling in forms and electronic data entry for reporting cash transactions by obliged entities" (Sl. glasnik BiH 41/15) as still part of its legal framework in 2026 (same source). I could not read it; field layout is (unverified).
- **It is not goAML.** BiH uses its own AMLS. The EU funded a new AMLS; procurement and go-live are planned for mid-2027 (same source). Any integration built now may need to change in 2027.
- The Law requires a by-law setting the exact content and layout of electronic transaction data (Art. 46(4)-(5)). The 2026 Rulebook covers bank statement exports in Excel (Rulebook Art. 35) but I did not find a DNFBP report form in it (unverified).

**Report content.** The STR must carry the Art. 61(1) data (Art. 42(2)). That means: client name, seat and ID number; data on the person acting for a legal person; natural person's name, address, date and place of birth, JMBG and ID issuer; reasons for the relationship and the client's activity; date and time; amount and currency; purpose and recipient; method; sender for foreign remittances; origin of funds; **reasons for suspicion**; and every 25% owner's name, address, date and place of birth (Art. 61(1)(a)-(m)) ([Law](https://portalfo1.pravosudje.ba/vstvfo-api/vijest/download/127392)).

**Volumes for context.** In 2025 FOO received 1,263 STRs (660 from banks, 601 from other obliged entities) and 585,789 cash and other threshold reports (41,255 from non-banks). Notaries were the largest non-bank reporters ([FOO report 2025](https://sipa.gov.ba/assets/files/foo-docs/FOOIZVJESTAJ2025BOS.DOC)). The FOO must acknowledge each STR and give feedback (Law Art. 73).

**Tipping off.** Nothing about an STR, FOO request, order or analysis may be disclosed to the client or third parties (Law Art. 88(1)). Good-faith reporters are not liable for damage (Art. 90(2)).
