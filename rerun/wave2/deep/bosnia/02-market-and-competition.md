# BiH AML kit: market size, buyers and competition (deep dive 02)

Date: 9 Oct 2026. Builds on [the B1 report](../reports/bosnia-and-herzegovina-b1.md). Scope: market size, buyers, competition, channels and regional expansion. Law, product design and go-to-market detail are covered by the other three agents.

## Summary

- **The free baseline is much stronger than B1 assumed.** In RS, the accountants' association (Savez računovođa i revizora RS, "SRRRS") gives away a 62-page Word pack of model AML documents for accounting firms, accounting sole traders and audit firms. It covers almost every document on the inspector's list ([SRRRS AML page](https://srrrs.org/propisi/propisi-iz-oblasti-sprecavanja-pranja-novca-i-finansiranja-teroristickih-aktivnosti/); [the DOCX](https://srrrs.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Modeli_dokumentacije_prema_Zakonu_SPN_FTA.docx)). In FBiH, SRR FBiH published a 40-page risk framework on 8 Oct 2026, with an indicator list and risk-country lists, but no fill-in forms ([SRR FBiH news](https://www.srr-fbih.org/objavljen-okvir-za-pristup-zasnovan-na-riziku-za-sprecavanje-pranja-novca-i-finansiranja-936)). **A paid "document kit" for accountants is no longer a product.** What is still unserved is the ongoing work: the per-client register, CDD and PEP/sanctions checks with a log, reminders, the training log and an inspection pack.
- **The buyer count is lower than B1's 3,000.** Using the professional registers, I count:
  - about **850 FBiH accounting firms** that have at least one active certified accountant in public practice;
  - an estimated **450-800 in RS**;
  - a few dozen in Brčko.
  Together that is about **1,300-1,700 accounting providers**, with a ceiling of about 2,500 if many bookkeepers are missing from the registers. Add 86 FBiH audit firms (plus an estimated 30-40 in RS) and about 250-350 real estate agencies. My working base is **about 2,000 obliged small entities** (range 1,700-2,900), before car dealers.
- **Buyers are micro firms.** Of 847 FBiH firm names, 695 have only one licensed certified accountant (my count of the [SRR FBiH register](https://app.srr-fbih.org/iframe/licence_cr_new)). In RS, bureaus run by technicians with secondary-school qualifications feared closure under the new accounting law ([Paragraf, 30 Oct 2025](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/30102025/30102025-vijest2.html)).
- **What buyers already pay** (all prices are per year unless stated):
  - Paragraf Lex legal database: 754.65 KM with VAT ([Paragraf](https://www.paragraf.ba/pretplata.php));
  - a one-day AML seminar: about 175-199 KM per person;
  - a 2-hour SRRRS webinar: 150 KM ([SRRRS](https://srrrs.org/pdv-tretman-prekogranicnih-transakcija-i-kontrola-obveznika-11007/));
  - Pantheon Web Light accounting software: from EUR 15.33 a month ([Datalab BH](https://www.datalab.ba/pantheon/accounting/)).
  A software line of 250-450 KM a year is plausible. A document pack priced above zero is not.
- **Competition:**
  - No BiH software product was found in this pass (45 searches in four languages, plus direct checks of association, regulator and vendor sites).
  - A-count (Croatia) is still the only close analogue, at EUR 0, 281 or 540 a year. It is in Croatian only and makes no BiH claim ([a-count.hr](https://a-count.hr)).
  - Datalab (Pantheon) has a BiH office, an accounting-office edition and a paid add-on ("Granule") model. It is the most likely local entrant, or a partner ([Datalab BH](https://www.datalab.ba/cjenovnik-dodatnih-usluga/)).
- **Revised year-3 revenue:** about **60,000-170,000 KM a year** (base case about 90,000 KM). B1 put the base case at 135,000 KM. The most promising route is a partnership with SRRRS and an approved FBiH CPD provider: "the official model pack, filled in and kept current".
- **Regional:**
  - Serbia had 5,476 registered accounting providers in 2023 ([biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/vodjenje-poslovnih-knjiga-poveravati-samo-firmama-sa-dozvolom/)).
  - North Macedonia has 2,040 registered accounting providers today (my count of the [ISOS registers](https://www.isos.mk/registar-na-drushtva-3/)).
  - Montenegro's FIU is building electronic questionnaires that automatically generate risk analyses for non-financial obliged entities ([Council of Europe, via search snippet](https://www.coe.int/sr/web/podgorica/-/montenegro-strengthens-anti-money-laundering-supervision-by-upgrading-financial-intelligence-unit-it-system)).
  - Serbia is the best second market.

## Buyer profile and pain

**Size.**
- Most accounting providers are one-person or two-person offices. In FBiH, 695 of 847 firms with a CR in public practice have only one licensed CR, and only 17 have five or more (my count of the [register](https://app.srr-fbih.org/iframe/licence_cr_new)).
- The average section M enterprise has about 3.7 staff (26,172 / 7,140) and about 350,000 KM turnover ([BHAS](https://bhas.gov.ba/data/Publikacije/Bilteni/2025/NUM_00_2024_TB_1_HR.pdf); my arithmetic, and it includes non-accounting firms).
- In RS, many "knjigovodstveni biroi" are run by certified accounting technicians with secondary-school education. They feared the new accounting law would close them ([Paragraf, 30 Oct 2025](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/30102025/30102025-vijest2.html)).
- The number of clients per office was not found (unverified). A one-CR office likely keeps books for 20-80 clients (my estimate).

**Small-firm relief that shapes the product.** Where an obliged firm has four or fewer staff, the legal representative is deemed the authorised person. The firm then only sends that person's data to the FIU (FOO) instead of a formal appointment decision. The SRRRS pack includes a separate letter for this case (Art. 48(4) of the law, as explained in the [SRRRS model pack](https://srrrs.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Modeli_dokumentacije_prema_Zakonu_SPN_FTA.docx)). Most buyers fall in this group.

**Software they use.**
- Datalab BH sells PANTHEON with a dedicated accounting-office edition ("Poslovni program za računovodstvene servise"). It has partner and add-on programmes ([Datalab BH](https://www.datalab.ba/pantheon/accounting/)) and a long cooperation with the Sarajevo economics faculty ([EFSA press release](https://www.efsa.unsa.ba/ef/sites/default/files/saopstenje_za_medije_-_efsa_i_datalab_-_mart_2024.pdf)).
- I could not identify the other local bookkeeping software vendors or their market shares (unverified). This is a gap for the go-to-market agent.

**How they comply today.**
- *RS:* the free SRRRS model pack (dated March 2026, posted June 2026) is the obvious default. It cites Rulebook Sl. glasnik BiH 8/26 and the RS Inspectorate guidelines of 24 Sep 2025 ([SRRRS](https://srrrs.org/propisi/propisi-iz-oblasti-sprecavanja-pranja-novca-i-finansiranja-teroristickih-aktivnosti/)).
- *FBiH:* the 2019 SRR FBiH guidelines, the nine-part 2023 booklet set and the new 8 Oct 2026 framework ([SRR FBiH](https://www.srr-fbih.org/obveze-kvalificiranih-osoba-vezano-za-spn-i-fta)); FUZIP's questionnaires and guides ([FUZIP](https://fuzip.gov.ba/)); seminars. Copying the RS pack is easy, because the law is state-level. Its references to the RS Inspectorate would need editing.
- CPD seminars already include AML. Finconsult's 2026 "Plan 3" seminar (7 CPD points) has a module on the role of accountants and auditors in AML ([Finconsult plan](https://finconsult.ba/godisnji-plan-seminara/)).

**Pain (documented).**
- RS, Jan 2026: randomly chosen bookkeepers got 10 days to send internal acts plus a 13-item questionnaire. People called the rules formalistic and disproportionate. A consultant said some questions cannot be answered and that "the application is absurd" for one-person firms. An accountant said the work collides with year-end closing and VAT returns due on the 10th, and that judging what is suspicious "should be SIPA's job" ([Paragraf, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/29012026/29012026-vijest1.html)).
- FBiH: FUZIP's special programme runs from 28 Sep to the end of 2026. Fines are 20,000-80,000 KM for a missing risk analysis, CDD gaps or irregular records. Every obliged entity that has not done so must send its own risk assessment to the authority ([Paragraf, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/01102026/01102026-vijest1.html)).
- Competing burdens in 2026:
  - the new RS accounting law, with a register alignment deadline of 30 Jun 2026 ([SRRRS](https://srrrs.org/pregled-dokumentacije-za-uskladjivanje-sa-zakonom-10435/));
  - a new chart of accounts ("novi kontni okvir") in FBiH ([Finconsult panel agenda](https://finconsult.ba/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Program-i-agenda-Panel-diskusija-Racunovodstvena-profesija-u-BIH-stanje-i-perspektive-i-Promocija-knjige-Profesionalno-financijsko-racunovodstvo.pdf)).
- I found no public Facebook or LinkedIn group discussion (searched; unverified). No results of the RS January action have been published.

**Discussion.**
- Nobody in BiH sells software for this job. I found no Serbian, Montenegrin or Slovenian product for small obliged entities either; Croatia has A-count.
- The documents, however, are now free and good, at least in RS. The paid opportunity has moved from "write my documents" to "run my AML file". That means one client register with CDD, UBO and PEP fields, a logged sanctions/PEP check, client risk scores, yearly review dates, a training log, the seven registers the RS pack lists, and a printable answer to the FUZIP or RS questionnaire.
- A-count proves buyers in a richer market pay EUR 281-540 for exactly that.
- Three groups have no free model pack: real estate agencies, car dealers and other dealers. The SRRRS pack covers accounting and audit firms only. This is a small but clean gap.

## Channels

- **SRRRS (Banja Luka).**
  - Profile: mandatory membership; 9 regional societies plus a Brčko association; Board chair Prof. Dragan Mikerević ([SRRRS](https://srrrs.org/o-nama/drustva/); [Finconsult agenda](https://finconsult.ba/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Program-i-agenda-Panel-diskusija-Racunovodstvena-profesija-u-BIH-stanje-i-perspektive-i-Promocija-knjige-Profesionalno-financijsko-racunovodstvo.pdf)).
  - Reach: it runs paid webinars with the magazine Finrar through its "eRačunovođa" platform, and a yearly international congress in September ([SRRRS webinar](https://srrrs.org/pdv-tretman-prekogranicnih-transakcija-i-kontrola-obveznika-11007/); [30th congress](https://srrrs.org/30-medjunarodni-kongres-racunovodstvene-i-revizorske-profesije-republike-srpske-23-25-09-2026-10491/)).
  - Already gives members tools: calculators, templates and an audit software pack ([SRRRS tools](https://srrrs.org/alati/softverski-paket-za-revizore/)).
  - The pitch: "a digital, auto-filled version of your model pack, plus the client register". This is the strongest single channel.
- **SRR FBiH (Mostar, Sarajevo office).**
  - Profile: about 3,900 active CRs; Board chair Augustin Mišić.
  - CPD: 40 hours a year. Only the Savez or approved providers may deliver organised CPD ([SRR FBiH KPR](https://www.srr-fbih.org/kontinuirani-profesionalni-razvoj)).
  - **Can our training count as CPD?** Only through a BiH-registered legal entity that answers the Savez's yearly public call, gets each programme approved and pays a fee for using the Savez name.   - The fast route is to partner with one of the 10 approved providers, for example Finconsult (Tuzla), which already teaches AML.

## Implications for positioning and pricing

1. **Do not sell the documents. Give them away.** Generate the firm's documents free, using the same structure as the SRRRS pack, in Latin and Cyrillic and in FBiH, RS and Brčko variants. Charge for the running file: the client register with CDD, UBO and PEP fields, logged sanctions and PEP checks, client risk scores, review and expiry reminders, the training log, the seven registers and a one-click "FUZIP/RS questionnaire" pack.
2. **Partner with the people who wrote the free pack.** Offer SRRRS a co-branded digital version. In FBiH, partner with an approved CPD provider such as Finconsult or Revicon, so the onboarding webinar earns CPD hours.
3. **Price below A-count and near what buyers already pay for small tools:**
   - free for up to 5 clients;
   - **about 25 KM a month (300 KM a year)** for up to 30 clients;
   - **about 50 KM a month (600 KM a year)** for unlimited clients and 3 users.
   Both paid prices sit below one Paragraf subscription (755 KM). B1's prices hold for the paid tiers, but there should be no paid "kit".
4. **Real estate agencies and car dealers are a secondary niche.** They have no free model pack. Sell them a simple flat plan, about 200-300 KM a year (my estimate). Bookkeepers who serve these clients are a natural reseller channel.
5. **Revised revenue (year 3, my estimates, unverified):**
   - low: 1,700 x 5% x 300 KM = about **25,000 KM**;
   - base: 2,000 x 10% x 450 KM = about **90,000 KM**;
   - good: 2,500 x 15% x 450 KM = about **170,000 KM**.
   Serbia could roughly double or treble the base with the same engine and a Serbian legal layer.
6. **Watch Datalab and Paragraf.** Each has the distribution to add a module or templates within months. An integration or white-label deal with one of them may be worth more than direct sales.
