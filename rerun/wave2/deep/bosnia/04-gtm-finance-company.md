# BiH AML kit: go-to-market, business model, company setup and financials (agent 04)

Date: 9 October 2026. Money is in KM (convertible marks); 1 EUR = 1.95583 KM (fixed peg). "Net" means before 17% VAT (PDV). Anything marked "my estimate" is a planning assumption, not a sourced fact.

## Summary

- **Price it like a cheap second tool, not like legal advice.** BiH bookkeepers pay 360-960 KM a year net for their core accounting software ([Softkom](https://softkom.ba/cjenovnik/); [Billans](https://billans.ba/); [PK Office](https://www.poreznikalkulator.ba/)) and 645 KM net for Paragraf Lex ([Paragraf](https://www.paragraf.ba/pretplata.php)). Plans: **Solo 290 KM, Office 590 KM, Consultant 1,190 KM a year, all net**. That is 35-50% below Croatia's A-count ([a-count.hr](https://a-count.hr)). A free self-check brings in leads. Add-ons are an inspection-pack review (150 KM) and a done-for-you setup (450 KM) delivered by partner consultants.
- **Sell yearly in advance, by bank transfer against a pro-forma invoice.** That is how BiH firms buy software today. Cards (Monri) come later.
- **Incorporate a small BiH d.o.o. in month 1.** RS needs 1 KM of capital; FBiH needs 1,000 KM ([Advokat Prnjavorac](https://advokat-prnjavorac.com/lawoffice/company-formation-Sarajevo.html)). Do not sell from an EU company. Small BiH buyers would face unclear reverse-charge VAT and possible withholding-tax forms on a 290 KM purchase ([Unija](https://unija.com/bs/primljene-usluge-od-pravnih-lica-iz-inostranstva/)). The d.o.o. can stay below the 100,000 KM VAT threshold ([Paragraf](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/18122024/18122024-vijest2.html)) for about two years.
- **Timing.** FUZIP's AML inspection programme runs now to end-2026 and is framed as part of the FATF action plan ([FUZIP](https://fuzip.gov.ba/)). Use it for pre-sales and concierge pilots. Accountants are busy from December to March: annual statements are due on 28 February ([Unija](https://unija.com/bs/zavrsni-obracuni/)). The main selling windows are mid-April to June and September to November. Grey-list pressure should last to about mid-2028 (my estimate).
- **Channels in priority order:**
  1. accountants' associations (SRRiF-FBiH, the RS Savez, and the new RS association "Glas računovođa i revizora", founded March 2026 with 121 people at its founding assembly ([Paragraf](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/18032026/18032026-vijest4.html)));
  2. Paragraf as seminar partner and possible reseller;
  3. BiH bookkeeping-software vendors as referral partners (PK Office, Billans, Softkom);
  4. direct outreach from public registers;
  5. referrals.
  Year-1 marketing budget: **about 24,000 KM** (EUR 12,300), plus partner commissions.
- **Base case** (10% of about 3,000 buyers by month 36): 290 paying customers and **about 123,000 KM ARR** (EUR 63,000) at month 36. It is profitable before founder pay from month 14 on a trailing 12-month basis. Peak cash need is **about 19,000 KM** (EUR 9,500) if the founder takes no pay, or about 41,000 KM if the founder pays themself 3,000-5,000 KM a month from year 2.
  - **Low case:** about 103 customers and 34,000 KM ARR. It never pays back in 36 months.
  - **High case:** about 543 customers and 255,000 KM ARR.
- **This is a good small business, not a venture.** Year-3 profit before founder pay is about 55,000 KM (EUR 28,000) in the base case. Real upside needs Serbia (about 5,500 accounting providers; [biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/vodjenje-poslovnih-knjiga-poveravati-samo-firmama-sa-dozvolom/)) and then Montenegro.
- **Exit.** The likely buyers are Paragraf, a regional bookkeeping-software vendor, or A-count. Small SaaS firms sell for about 2-4x revenue or 3-6x profit ([BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/state-of-saas-valuations-2026); [beancount.io](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/11/bootstrapped-saas-valuation-multiples-2026-acquire-com-indie-founders-guide)). On the base case that is about 250,000-500,000 KM.
- **Kill criteria:**
  - fewer than 3 pre-orders from 30 conversations by day 30;
  - fewer than 25 paying customers by end of April 2027 (month 6);
  - fewer than 60 by October 2027;
  - a first-year renewal rate below 50%.

Packaging rules:
- **Yearly prepaid is the default.** BiH firms pay suppliers by bank transfer against a pro-forma invoice (predračun), and Paragraf sells this way ([Paragraf](https://www.paragraf.ba/pretplata.php)). Chasing twelve bank transfers a year for 24 KM each is not worth it.
- **Never sell the document set alone.** It sits inside the subscription. The client register, reminders, training log and yearly update are what drive renewal.
- **Discounts:**
  - 15% for members of partner associations: SRRiF-FBiH, the member societies of the RS Savez, and Glas računovođa i revizora.
  - 20% in the first year for the first 100 "founding" customers who sign by 31 March 2027.
  - 149 KM for the first year for 15-30 pilot customers, in return for feedback and a testimonial.
- **Price increase:** about 10% in year 3, timed to replace the VAT step (see below).

### VAT (PDV) points

- The VAT registration threshold rose from 50,000 to 100,000 KM of yearly turnover in early December 2023. 2,365 businesses asked to leave the VAT system afterwards ([Paragraf, 18 Dec 2024](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/18122024/18122024-vijest2.html)). Many sole bookkeepers are therefore not VAT-registered and cannot deduct VAT. For them, VAT is a real cost.
- A new d.o.o. stays under 100,000 KM in year 1 and most of year 2 in the base case, so it can invoice without VAT. That is a 17% price edge over VAT-registered rivals such as Paragraf.
- The base case passes 100,000 KM in year 3. Publish prices as "net, VAT added where applicable" from day one. Then registering for VAT does not look like a price rise.

## Go-to-market (channels, sales motion)

### Timing: inspections, FATF, and the accountants' calendar

- **Now (Oct-Dec 2026).** FUZIP's special AML programme started on 28 September 2026 and runs to end-2026. It covers bookkeeping, accounting and tax firms, auditors and real estate intermediaries ([Paragraf, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/01102026/01102026-vijest1.html)). On 25 September FUZIP said it is carrying out measures from the FATF action plan. On 7-8 October it published questionnaires for bookkeepers and real estate intermediaries ([FUZIP](https://fuzip.gov.ba/)). Demand is highest now, but the product will not be ready for most of it. Use this window for pre-sales, a waitlist and "concierge" pilots, where a lawyer finishes the template documents by hand.
- **RS.** The RS Inspectorate ran random document requests in January-February 2026 ([Paragraf, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/29012026/29012026-vijest1.html)). A repeat in early 2027 is plausible but not announced (unverified).
- **FATF.** BiH went on the grey list in June 2026. The action plan names consistent DNFBP supervision and notary supervision ([FATF, June 2026](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/content/fatf-gafi/en/publications/High-risk-and-other-monitored-jurisdictions/increased-monitoring-june-2026.html)). FATF plenaries are usually held in February, June and October. Expect inspection drives and news before each, because BiH must show progress (my inference). Exit typically takes about two years, so pressure should last to about mid-2028 (my estimate).
- **Accountants' calendar.**
  - Annual financial statements are due on 28 February in both entities ([Unija](https://unija.com/bs/zavrsni-obracuni/)).
  - VAT returns are due monthly by the 10th ([Paragraf, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/29012026/29012026-vijest1.html)).
  - Annual tax returns follow in March (unverified).
  - So December to March is a poor time to ask for attention. **The selling windows are mid-April to June and September to November.** July-August is slow.

- **Free FUZIP self-check.** 15 questions mapped to the FUZIP bookkeeper questionnaire and the RS 13-item questionnaire. Output: a red/amber/green score and a list of missing documents.

### Sales motion

- **Self-serve first.**
  - Free self-check, then a 14-day trial with no card (cards are less common), then a pro-forma invoice, then the account is activated when payment arrives.
  - Promise: "inspection-ready documents in one hour".
- **A light human touch for bigger plans.**
  - A weekly 30-minute live group demo.
  - A phone or Viber call for Office and Consultant plans and for anyone who asks.
  - BiH buyers trust a named local person (my estimate). The part-time local hire does these calls in the local language.
- **Onboarding.**
  - The profile wizard produces documents the same day.
  - The firm signs the documents, uploads the signed decision on the authorised person, and imports its client list from Excel.
  - An optional 20-minute onboarding call.
- **Customer success.**
  - Monthly email: "what changed in the rules".
  - Prompts for expiring client IDs.
  - A yearly staff-training webinar that fills the training log.
  - A yearly risk-assessment review wizard.
- **Renewal drivers.**
  - The yearly risk-assessment update and the yearly training duty.
  - The client register: records must be kept for years (10 years per a consultancy, unverified), and leaving means exporting them.
  - Rule changes handled for the customer.
  - An inspection pack always ready.
  - Send the renewal invoice 45 days ahead, with a "your compliance score this year" summary.

## Company setup and payments

### Recommendation

Set up a BiH d.o.o. in month 1 and use it to invoice in KM. The founder can own it directly or through an EU holding company and can live abroad. Do not sell to BiH micro firms from an EU company, for the reasons below. Budget about 2,500-4,000 KM for setup, including the lawyer (my estimate).

### Option A: BiH d.o.o. (recommended)

| Point | Republika Srpska (RS) | Federation of BiH (FBiH) |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum share capital | 1 KM | 1,000 KM (at least 100 KM per founder; at least half paid in before filing) |
| Official fees | Through the APIF one-stop shop. A 2020 city guide lists a 35 KM APIF fee, about 132 KM in total plus 40 KM to the indirect tax authority, and notary fees on top ([Zvornik guide](https://gradzvornik.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Vodic-za-registraciju-privrednog-drustva-ENG.pdf)). 2026 amounts (unverified) | About EUR 500-700 for court, notary, gazette, tax and statistics, plus EUR 150-300 for stamp, translations, bank and courier |
| Time | About 3-5 days once documents are ready. What a non-resident founder needs:
- A special power of attorney, signed before a notary at home and apostilled, plus a court-interpreter translation.
- A foreigner ID number (ID broj za stranca) if a non-resident is director.
- No BiH residence is needed to own or direct the company ([Advokat Prnjavorac](https://advokat-prnjavorac.com/lawoffice/company-formation-Sarajevo.html)).
- Whether a director who lives abroad needs a BiH work permit is (unverified). Ask the lawyer.

Which entity:
- **RS (Banja Luka)** is cheaper (1 KM capital) and usually faster. It can sell anywhere in BiH, because VAT is state-level.
- **FBiH (Sarajevo or Mostar)** is nearer the biggest buyer pool and FUZIP's programme. A Sarajevo address may read better to FBiH buyers.
- Choose the entity where the first local hire or partner lives. With no such person, choose RS.

Running duties and costs:
- An external bookkeeper (about 150-250 KM a month, my estimate).
- Yearly financial statements by 28 February ([Unija](https://unija.com/bs/zavrsni-obracuni/)) and a corporate tax return.
- Payroll filings once there is staff.
- VAT registration once turnover passes 100,000 KM.

### Option B: sell from an EU company (not recommended for BiH)

- **EU VAT.** A B2B service sold to a non-EU business is normally outside EU VAT (general EU rule).
- **BiH VAT.** A VAT-registered BiH buyer must self-assess BiH VAT on services from abroad (reverse charge) ([Unija](https://unija.com/bs/primljene-usluge-od-pravnih-lica-iz-inostranstva/)). It is unclear how a non-VAT-registered buyer pays, or whether a foreign seller must appoint a BiH tax representative (unverified). Many target buyers are under the 100,000 KM threshold, so this would be the normal case.
- **Withholding tax.** FBiH withholding tax covers payments to non-residents for IP rights and for management, technical and education services. Unija reads the 2025 amendment to the FBiH rulebook (Sl. novine FBiH 75/25) to mean that services delivered electronically from abroad are performed outside BiH, so no withholding applies ([Unija](https://unija.com/bs/primljene-usluge-od-pravnih-lica-iz-inostranstva/)). An inspector could still treat a software subscription as a licence fee. The usual rate is 10% (11.11% grossed up), and the buyer must file forms by the 10th of the following month ([Unija](https://unija.com/bs/porez-po-odbitku-fbih/)). RS rules were not checked (unverified).
- **Payment friction.** The buyer would pay an EUR invoice abroad. Card payment through an EU gateway would work, but small BiH firms prefer bank transfer.
- **Bottom line.** On a 290 KM purchase, any tax doubt stops the sale. A local d.o.o. removes it. If registration is late, a local accounting office can invoice the first pilots as reseller.

### Payments

- **Default: bank transfer.** Send a pro-forma invoice, then the final invoice after payment ([Paragraf model](https://www.paragraf.ba/pretplata.php)).
- **Cards, later: Monri.** Monri is a payment gateway for merchants in Croatia, BiH and the EU. It supports cards, saved-card tokens for recurring billing, Apple Pay and Google Pay ([Monri plugin page](https://ar.wordpress.org/plugins/?p=189098)). Its BiH fees were not found. Card fees for BiH merchants are "often 1.5-3.5% per transaction" plus monthly fees, and some estimates put the total at 2-12%. Since a 2025 court ruling, banks also owe VAT on card fees ([Paragraf, 23 Oct 2025](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/23102025/23102025-vijest1.html)). That is another reason to prefer bank transfer.
  - Whether WSPay or CorvusPay serve BiH merchants is (unverified).
  - Stripe and PayPal were not confirmed for BiH-based merchants (unverified).
- **Fiscalisation.**
  - Under the RS Law on Fiscalisation, a card payment counts as a "cash" payment that needs a fiscal receipt. A bank transfer between firms is "non-cash" ([RS law, Paragraf](https://www.paragraf.ba/propisi/republika-srpska/zakon-o-fiskalizaciji.html)). Whether a B2B bank-transfer sale needs a fiscal receipt is not settled in my sources (unverified).
  - FBiH is moving to a new fiscalisation law: the proposal went to parliament in November 2025, with government amendments in January 2026 ([Paragraf, 21 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/21012026/21012026-vijest1.html)). Its status is (unverified).
  - **Rule: start with bank transfer only. The company's bookkeeper confirms fiscal duties before cards are switched on.**

## Contracts and insurance

**Documents to have before the first paid customer:**
1. General terms (Opšti uslovi korištenja), with the pro-forma invoice acting as the order form.
2. A data processing agreement (ugovor o obradi ličnih podataka). BiH's new Law on Personal Data Protection (Sl. glasnik BiH 12/2025) follows the GDPR and applies from 5 October 2025 ([Paragraf, 24 Sep 2025](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/24092025/24092025-vijest2.html)). The customer is the controller and we are the processor. The product stores ID copies, beneficial-owner and PEP data, so security matters.
3. A privacy policy and a list of sub-processors (EU hosting).
4. A partner/reseller agreement and a consultant agreement for done-for-you work, which also splits liability.
5. A lawyer engagement letter covering the template review and the yearly updates.

**Key clauses:**
- "A tool, not legal advice." The customer stays the obliged entity.
- The templates reflect the law as of a stated date and were reviewed by a named lawyer.
- Updates follow within an agreed number of days after a rule change.
- Liability is capped at the fees paid in the last 12 months, and fines are excluded.
- **Inspection promise (marketing, capped):** if an inspector finds a defect in a generated document caused by our error, we fix it within 5 working days and refund that year's fee.
- Data is kept as long as the AML law requires (10 years per a consultancy, unverified), and there is a full export on exit.
- BiH law and a court in Banja Luka or Sarajevo.

**Data location:** host in the EU (Germany or Finland). Whether a transfer to the EU needs any filing under the new law is (unverified). Ask the lawyer.

**Insurance:**
- Professional liability (osiguranje od profesionalne odgovornosti), plus cyber cover if available.
- No BiH price was found. Budget 1,000-2,000 KM a year for a 100,000-200,000 KM limit (my estimate, unverified).
- Serbia's draft accounting law would make liability insurance compulsory for accounting providers ([Paragraf RS, 4 Jun 2026](https://www.paragraf.rs/dnevne-vesti/050626/050626-vest12.html)). This is a sign that insurers in the region price this kind of cover.

**Trademark:**
- File the brand with the Institute for Intellectual Property of BiH (Mostar) under the Law on Trademarks (Sl. glasnik BiH 53/10) ([IPR BiH rulebook](https://ipr.gov.ba/upload/documents/dokumenti_podstranice/pravna-regulativa/bosanski/Zakoni_i_drugi_propisi_BiH_iz_oblasti_intelektualnog_vlasni%C5%A1tva/pravilnik_zig_bos.pdf)).
- Classes 9 and 42 (software), and 41 (training).
- Fees were not found. Budget about 1,000 KM including an attorney (unverified).
- Extend to Serbia and Montenegro before entering them.

**Lawyer costs:**
- The FBiH lawyers' tariff moved to a point value of 5.60 KM in 2025 ([Paragraf, 13 Jun 2025](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/13062025/13062025-vijest5.html)). A written fee agreement can override it.
- Negotiate a fixed fee: about 6,000 KM for the templates plus terms and DPA, then about 300 KM a month on retainer for rule changes (my estimate).

Unit economics, base case (my estimate):
- First-year price about 350-425 KM against CAC about 260-370 KM, so payback is about 9-12 months.
- With 75%, then 85% renewal and about 90% gross margin, a customer stays about 4 years and is worth about 1,400-1,500 KM. LTV/CAC is about 4-5.
- The ceiling is the small buyer pool, not the unit economics.

What the numbers mean:
- **Cash need is small.** About 20,000 KM (EUR 10,000) covers the base case if the founder takes no pay. Plan 40,000 KM (EUR 20,000) to allow for founder pay or slow sales.
- **Founder income is modest in BiH alone.** The base case pays the founder about 3,000-5,000 KM a month only from year 3. Regional expansion is the route to a bigger income.
- **The low case is a kill signal.** It is visible by month 6 (about 17 customers against a target of 50).

## Exit and partnerships

**Likely acquirers:**
- **Paragraf** (Paragraf Lex: legal database, seminars and daily news in BiH, Serbia and Montenegro). It sells to the same buyers and lacks a tool layer. This is the most natural buyer and also the most likely copier.
- **BiH bookkeeping-software vendors:** Porezni kalkulator / PK Office, Billans (with partner BTS Computers), Softkom, AB Soft, Infosistem BN ([infosistembn.com](https://www.infosistembn.com/)). An AML module raises their price per customer and makes customers stay.
- **Regional accounting publishers:** RRiF and TEB in Croatia, and Serbian legal-database publishers. They are buyers if the product reaches Serbia.
- **A-count (FEDRA d.o.o., Zadar):** a merger, a licence, or a sale of the BiH/Serbia base to them ([a-count.hr](https://a-count.hr)).
- **KYC or identity vendors** in the region. They are less likely buyers, because the deal size is small.

**Value:**
- Small SaaS businesses sell for about 2-4x yearly revenue or about 3-6x profit (SDE) ([BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/state-of-saas-valuations-2026); [beancount.io](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/11/bootstrapped-saas-valuation-multiples-2026-acquire-com-indie-founders-guide)).
- Base case at month 36: about 250,000-500,000 KM (EUR 125,000-250,000).
- High case: about 500,000-1,000,000 KM.
- A strategic buyer that fears a competitor may pay more. Grey-list exit (about 2028) will lower the multiple, so sell before or soon after it (my estimate).

**Partnerships to sign in year 1:**
