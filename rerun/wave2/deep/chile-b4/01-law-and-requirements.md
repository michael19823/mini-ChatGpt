## Working notes (raw facts already verified; to be folded into the sections)

- Law art. 3: "los corredores de propiedades y las empresas dedicadas a la gestión inmobiliaria; los notarios; los conservadores" are obliged to report suspicious operations and must name an official to deal with the UAF.
- Law art. 5: keep special records at least 5 years; report cash operations above USD 10,000 (dollar observado of the day).
- Law art. 6-7: tipping-off ban; prison plus 100-400 UTM fine.
- Law art. 19-20: minor (C62 breaches) up to 800 UF; less serious (art. 5: ROE and records) up to 3,000 UF; serious (art. 2 b, 3: ROS, info requests) up to 5,000 UF; caution always; up to 3x for repeats within 12 months. Art. 21: directors and legal reps can be fined too. Art. 22 bis: 3 years (minor) / 5 years limitation. Art. 24: claim at Court of Appeals of the sanctioned party's domicile.
- Law art. 38: report acts by UN-listed persons. Art. 40: must be in UAF register; UAF may publish name and RUT.
- C62 b.3: natural persons and micro/small firms (Ley 20.416) may name the natural person, a partner, administrator or executive as compliance officer. CONFIRMED.
- C62 d.3: nil ROE ("reporte negativo") required. CONFIRMED. Annex 1: brokers, real-estate firms, notaries, conservadores = semestral ROE, due in the first 10 business days of July and January.
- C62 Annex 2: notaries and conservadores CDD threshold for occasional transactions = 1,000 UF; others USD 3,000.
- Oficio 543: Clave Única from 19 Oct 2026; separate machine access for automated ROE/ROS senders (request via SIAC with system type, report types, IP range, tech contact); MiUAF replaces the portal gradually from 2027.
- UAF Supervision results 2025 / Plan 2026 (Apr 2026) https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Resultados_DFC_2025_y_Plan_2026_VF_xsmFFOU.pdf : 252 supervision actions Mar 2025-Feb 2026 (notaries 38, gestión inmobiliaria 27, corredores 17, conservadores 1). Committee outcomes: 3 sanction, 50 follow-up, 63 "representación", 18 closed. Top gaps in high-risk sectors incl. brokers/real estate/notaries: PEP CDD 33; annual training 29; ROE >USD10k 23; client file permanent 22; UN list monitoring 22; client file occasional 18; manual distribution+update 16; BO info for legal-person clients 14; having a manual 14; keeping UAF registration data updated 12. 8 verification standards: KYC, ongoing monitoring, ROE, ROS, compliance officer, training, manual, records. Types: integral, coverage, risk-focused, contingent, follow-up, technical report supervision (sample check of ROE and ROE negativo timeliness). Modes: in situ, online, hybrid.
