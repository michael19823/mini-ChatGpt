# Chile UAF AML/CFT law for property brokers, real-estate firms and notaries, turned into product requirements

Status: complete as of 2026-10-10 (open questions listed at the end). The law below comes from primary texts downloaded from uaf.cl on 10 Oct 2026.

## Summary

- **One law and one circular set almost every duty.** Ley 19.913 lists the obliged sectors (art. 3) and the fines (art. 19-20). UAF Circular 62 has applied since 1 June 2025. It merged and repealed 24 earlier circulars, including Circular 49 of 2012 (C62 CUARTO, SEXTO). There are no regional rules. The UAF is the only AML supervisor for these four sectors.
- **Who is obliged.** Law art. 3 names "los corredores de propiedades y las empresas dedicadas a la gestión inmobiliaria; los notarios; los conservadores". Natural persons are included. There is no size or turnover exemption.
  - At 31 Dec 2025 the UAF register held 2,134 real-estate firms, 1,413 brokers, 497 notaries and 99 conservadores ([Stats 2025](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Estadistico_2025.pdf), p. 7).
  - The only size relief: a natural person or a micro or small firm (Ley 20.416) may name the owner, a partner, an administrator or an executive as compliance officer (C62 b.3).
- **What must exist on paper.**
  - UAF registration from the first day of activity. Every change must be reported within 10 business days (C62 a.1, a.4, b.6).
  - A compliance officer (Law art. 3; C62 B).
  - A prevention manual with 7 required parts, including a risk policy. It must be approved by the top body, updated at least every 2 years and given to every worker, with proof (C62 J).
  - A client file for every client, updated at least once a year. It must hold 7 data items, BO declarations for legal-person clients, a PEP check and UN-list screening evidence (C62 F, G, H, c.9).
  - Four permanent registers for this segment: cash operations, suspicious operations (including discarded cases), DDC, and PEP operations. Keep them at least 5 years after the relationship ends (C62 E; Law art. 5).
  - A register of analysed cases, with open and close dates, the reason, a description, the conclusion and why a ROS was sent or not (C62 c.8).
  - A yearly training record for all staff, including the OdC (C62 L).
- **Reporting.**
  - The **ROS** has no threshold. It must be sent "as soon as possible", and at once for a UN-list match (C62 c.4, c.11).
  - The **ROE** covers cash above USD 10,000. All four sectors file it **every six months**, in the first 10 business days of July and January. **A nil report ("ROE negativo") is compulsory** (C62 d.1, d.3; Annex 1). In 2026 the windows were 2-15 Jan and 1-14 July. The next window is 4-15 Jan 2027 ([UAF ROE calendar 2026](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Calendario_ROE_2026_fJZ3WvN.pdf)).
  - Both reports go only through the UAF "Portal de Entidades Reportantes". The portal moves to Clave Única login on 19 Oct 2026, and a new platform, MiUAF, starts to replace it gradually in 2027 (Of. 543).
- **Key thresholds.**
  - DDC for one-off deals: USD 3,000 for brokers and real-estate firms, but 1,000 UF for notaries and conservadores (C62 f.4.2; Annex 2).
  - BO: 10% of capital or votes, or effective control (C62 g.2).
  - A BO declaration delayed more than 40 business days is a red flag for a possible ROS (C62 g.3.8).
  - PEP status lasts at least 1 year after leaving office (C62 h.1).
- **Penalties.**
  - Breach of a circular is a "minor" offence: a written reprimand and up to 800 UF.
  - Breach of the ROE or record-keeping duty (art. 5) is "less serious": up to 3,000 UF.
  - Failure to file a ROS, or to answer a UAF request, is "serious": up to 5,000 UF.
  - Repeat offences within 12 months can be fined up to 3 times as much. Directors and legal representatives can be fined too (Law art. 19-21).
  - Real fines are small. In 2025, 17 notaries paid UF 660 in total, 4 real-estate firms UF 105, and 1 broker UF 40 ([Stats 2025](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Estadistico_2025.pdf), p. 21).
- **Enforcement has moved from fines to remediation.**
  - Of 252 supervision actions from March 2025 to February 2026, notaries (38), real-estate firms (27) and brokers (17) were 3 of the top 5 sectors.
  - The UAF committee chose a sanction procedure in only 3 cases. It chose follow-up in 50, a formal observation letter ("representación") in 63, and closure in 18 ([DFC 2025](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Resultados_DFC_2025_y_Plan_2026_VF_xsmFFOU.pdf), p. 5-6).
  - The UAF opened **no** new sanction procedures in 2025, because of the switch to Circular 62 (Stats 2025, p. 20).
  - The most common gaps were PEP checks, annual training, ROE, client files, UN-list screening, manual distribution and updating, BO data, having a manual at all, and keeping UAF registration data current (DFC 2025, p. 9). **This list is a ready-made product scope.**
- **Changes coming.**
  - Clave Única login from 19 Oct 2026. A machine-to-machine channel for automated ROE and ROS senders (Of. 543).
  - MiUAF from 2027.
  - Chile's new data protection law, Ley 21.719, in force on 1 Dec 2026.
  - A bill to raise UAF fines (Boletín 15.975-25) is stuck in a joint committee of both chambers ("comisión mixta") since Aug 2026.
  - A broker licensing bill (Boletín 18.241-03) has sat in the Senate Economy Committee since May 2026 with no report.
  - The notary reform, Ley 21.772, took effect in April 2026 and will bring new notaries into office.
- **Product.** 68 testable requirements follow, each traced to its legal source. The core is a guided register, not a document pack:
  - a registration and deadline tracker;
  - a manual and risk-policy generator;
  - a client file with BO, PEP and UN screening;
  - an unusual-case log;
  - an ROE helper, including the semi-annual nil report for every entity and every SPV;
  - a training record;
  - an inspection pack.
  The product must not file the ROS for the client: only the OdC may file it (UAF FAQ). It must also respect the tipping-off ban, which covers service providers (Law art. 6).

**Thresholds and exemptions.**
- **No threshold for being obliged.** Every broker, real-estate firm, notary and conservador is obliged, whatever its size or deal volume. Law art. 3 covers "las personas naturales y las personas jurídicas" in these sectors.
- **Registration starts with the activity.** An entity must apply to the UAF "desde el comienzo del ejercicio de su actividad" (C62 a.1). It stays obliged until it proves it has stopped, for example with an SII certificate of closure ("término de giro"). A notarised sworn statement is not enough ([UAF FAQ](https://www.uaf.cl/es-cl/preguntas-frecuentes); C62 a.5-a.6).
  - **Product consequence:** a finished one-project SPV must still file a nil ROE every six months until it is formally closed.
- **Size relief is limited to the OdC.** Natural persons, and firms that are micro (sales up to 2,400 UF a year) or small (up to 25,000 UF) under Ley 20.416, may name the owner, a partner, an administrator or an executive as OdC (C62 b.3; thresholds from [SII](https://www.sii.cl/preguntas_frecuentes/factura_electronica/001_003_6503.htm)). All other duties apply in full.
- **No rental-only carve-out.** C62 does not separate sales from rentals. Rental deals at or above USD 3,000, and ongoing relationships, trigger DDC like any other client (C62 f.4). Whether a rental-management mandate counts as a "permanent relationship" is not stated (unverified; see open questions).
- **The electronic-transfer register does not apply.** It covers only entities that provide transfer services (C62 e.1, I).
- **The coverage gap is large.** Industry sources put active brokers at 5,000 to more than 20,000, against about 1,400-1,460 registered ([Emol, Aug 2025](https://www.emol.com/noticias/Economia/2025/08/27/1176126/corredores-de-propiedades.html)). Unregistered active brokers are in breach of C62 a.1 and Law art. 3 and 40. The UAF does not appear to pursue them actively (inferred; no enforcement data found).

**Who in the entity does what.**
- **OdC.** Deals with the UAF and coordinates the prevention policies. Must hold a senior post with access to all operations and documents (C62 b.1-b.2). Must not have a conviction for money laundering, terrorist financing or a predicate offence (C62 b.4). Must not be a former UAF employee within 6 months of leaving (C62 b.5). **Only the OdC may send a ROS** ([UAF FAQ](https://www.uaf.cl/es-cl/preguntas-frecuentes)). The ROE guide lets the OdC "or the enabled web user" log in to send the ROE.
- **Legal representative.** Registers the OdC in the portal (UAF FAQ). Any change of legal representative must be reported within 10 business days (C62 b.6). Can also be fined personally (Law art. 21).
- **Top governing body.** Approves the manual (C62 j.3). Senior management approval is needed to start or keep a PEP or high-risk relationship (C62 f.10.4, h.4.1).

## Duty-by-duty table

Penalty classes (Law art. 19-20):
- **Minor**: breach of UAF instructions (art. 19 a). Written reprimand plus a fine of up to 800 UF. The UAF must prove the offender knew the instruction (art. 20.1).
- **Less serious**: breach of art. 5, the cash-reporting and record duty (art. 19 b). Written reprimand plus up to 3,000 UF.
- **Serious**: breach of art. 2 b (answering UAF requests) or art. 3 (reporting suspicious operations and naming an official) (art. 19 c). Written reprimand plus up to 5,000 UF.
- **For all classes**: up to 3 times the fine for a repeat offence within 12 months, and directors and legal representatives can be fined too (art. 20-21).

The sanctions policy footnotes confirm this mapping: ROE and records are "less serious", while ROS and information requests are "serious" ([Sanctions policy](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/politica_sancionatoria.pdf), p. 15).

Notes on the table:
- **Electronic means are allowed for every duty** (C62 SEGUNDO), and registers may be electronic (C62 e.1). Software can lawfully hold the whole programme.
- **A cash payment can trigger three duties at once.** A deal paid largely in cash is a red flag in the Red Flags Guide (15.4, 15.23). It also triggers DDC (item 7) and the ROE (item 18).
- **The 30 real-estate red flags are a ready library.** Section 15 of the Red Flags Guide has 30 flags for "brokers or real-estate managers, notaries and conservadores". Examples: cash purchases (15.4, 15.23), buyers fronting for someone else (15.6), foreign companies buying (15.24), quick resales at rising prices (15.22, 15.26), and sale or rent proceeds sent to high-risk countries or unrelated third parties (15.29) ([Red Flags Guide](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/GuiaSe%C3%B1alesAlerta2023.pdf), p. 44-45).

### Data fields fixed by UAF forms

**BO form fields** ([BO form](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Declaraci%C3%B3nBFJun2025_giwIbRd.pdf)):
- Declaration type: new client / one-off transaction / data update / update without changes.
- Declaring entity: RUT or ID no., company name, address, city, place of incorporation, tax address, phone, legal representative's ID and name, company type (anónima, colectiva, en comandita, limitada, EIRL, other), and senior management (ID, post, name).
- Section 2.1: BOs at 10% or more. Section 2.2: persons with effective control below 10%. For each: ID, full name, address, city, country, tax domiciles, and % share.
- Section 3: confirmation of no change since declaration no. X of date Y, for clients with no change within a year.
- Section 4: the declarant's nationality status (Chilean / resident foreigner / non-resident foreigner), ID, names, place of origin, and relation to the entity.
- Sworn signature, place and date.
- The help sheet shows how to compute indirect shares by multiplying along the chain (for example 90% × 50% = 45%) ([AyudaBF](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/AyudaBF.pdf)).

**PEP form** ([PEP form](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/DeclaracionPEP.pdf)): the client's name, ID or passport no. and nationality. A declaration that the client is or is not a spouse, civil partner or relative up to second degree of, or in a joint-action pact with, a holder of any of the 17 listed roles, current or former. Signature, place and date.

**ROE Simplificado (brokers)**, as shown in the portal screenshot (ROE guide p. 7):
- Period: semester and year.
- Office where the deal took place: head office or branch, street type, name and number, unit, region, comuna, phone, postal code, e-mail.
- Person carrying the cash ("conductor"): first name, paternal and maternal surnames, ID type and number, nationality, type of representation.
- Principal ("mandante"): the same identity fields.
- Each transaction: cash-flow direction, date, document number, operation or product, declared activity, currency, amount.

**ROS form**, 4 steps (ROS guide p. 4-19):
- **Step 1, the operation.** Type (ML, TF or both). Link to a previous ROS (yes or no, with reference). One-off deal (date) or a set of operations (from-to dates and count). Amount in CLP, digits only. Products used. Facts in time order: who, what, when, where, 250-1,000 characters. What was suspicious: 250-500 characters. What or who triggered the alert (from a list, or "other" with details). Analysis period.
- **Step 2, each reported or linked person.**
  - Role: reported, or linked (with the type of link). Natural or legal person.
  - Name: up to 100 characters. Paternal and maternal surnames: up to 40 characters each.
  - ID: national RUN/RUT (the check digit is validated) or foreign ID.
  - Natural persons: nationality and country of residence. Legal persons: country of incorporation and country of operation.
  - Address: known or unknown (unknown is allowed only for linked persons). Street type, street name (up to 50 characters, upper case, no symbols), number (up to 10 characters, or S/N), extra details, region, comuna, postal code.
  - Mobile and e-mail: required, or tick "no information".
  - Economic activities (multi-select) and extra activity details (up to 4,000 characters).
- **Step 3, attachments.** JPG, PDF, TIF, DOC, XLS, ZIP, RAR, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, PPT or PNG, up to 50 MB each, each with a description of up to 40 characters.
- **Step 4, review.** Then send. The UAF assigns the ROS number and issues a sending certificate with a verification code. A ROS cannot be corrected, and annulment is exceptional ([ROS FAQ](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/2025_Preguntas_frecuentes_ROS_S6jEvei.pdf)).

## Supervisors and enforcement evidence

**Supervisor.** The UAF is the only AML supervisor for brokers, real-estate firms, notaries and conservadores. Its Supervision and Compliance Division ("DFC") runs the inspections, and its Legal Division runs the sanction procedures (Sanctions policy p. 6). No sector regulator shares the role for these sectors. The UAF's joint inspections in 2025 were with the CMF, the casinos regulator and the social security regulator ([DFC 2025](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Resultados_DFC_2025_y_Plan_2026_VF_xsmFFOU.pdf), p. 5).

**How inspections work.**
- **Modes**: in situ, online (telematic) or hybrid.
- **Types**:
  - integral (full review);
  - coverage (essentials for priority sectors: policies, OdC, basic DDC, general monitoring, training);
  - risk-focused (for example BO, PEP, high-risk clients, suspicious operations);
  - contingent (triggered by news or other authorities);
  - follow-up;
  - "technical report supervision", a sample check of ROE and nil-ROE timeliness.
- **Eight verification standards**: client knowledge, ongoing monitoring, ROE, ROS, compliance officer, training, manual, records (DFC 2025, p. 11-13; [Supervision policy](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Res._Exenta_N101_Pol%C3%ADtica_de_Supervisi%C3%B3n_Basada_en_Riesgos_UAF.pdf), section 4.2).
- **Report and outcomes**: each inspection ends with a compliance verification report ("Informe de Verificación de Cumplimiento Normativo", IVCN). A Supervision and Sanction Committee then picks one of four outcomes: sanction, follow-up, formal observation ("representación") or closure (DFC 2025, p. 5).
- **Low-impact gaps** go to a compliance plan with deadlines: an observation letter, an improvement plan, a mitigation report or a follow-up visit. Breaching the plan sends the case to sanctions (Sanctions policy p. 12-13).

## Regional differences

- **None in the law.** Ley 19.913 and C62 apply nationwide. Chile is a unitary state, and no region issues its own AML rules. The UAF supervises "anywhere in the national territory" (Law art. 2).
- **Supervision is concentrated in Santiago.**
  - In 2025, 75% of private-sector supervisions (129 of 172) were in the Metropolitan Region. Next came Coquimbo (8), Valparaíso (7) and Tarapacá (6) (Stats 2025, p. 19).
  - In the DFC count (252 actions), 69% (175) were in the Metropolitan Region. Next came Tarapacá with 16 (the Iquique free zone), Coquimbo 11, Los Lagos 10, Valparaíso 9 and Magallanes 7 (DFC 2025, p. 7).
  - A regional broker is somewhat less likely to be inspected (inferred).
- **Courts.** Sanction claims go to the Court of Appeals of the sanctioned party's domicile (Law art. 24). So case law can differ slightly by region (inferred).
- **Notaries and conservadores are territorial offices.** Their appointment and oversight are being reformed by Ley 21.772 (see Upcoming changes). AML supervision stays with the UAF.
- **Forms use region and comuna pick-lists** (ROE and ROS guides). The software needs the official list of Chile's 16 regions and 346 comunas (counts from my own knowledge, unverified).
- **Language:** Spanish only. All UAF forms and guides are in Spanish. The UAF English translation of the Law is non-binding ([UAF English text](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Law_19913_upMay2026_1.pdf), disclaimer).

## PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS

Each requirement is testable. "Basis" gives the legal source. "Law" = Ley 19.913. "C62" = Circular 62 (point). "Ann." = C62 annex. "Of.543" = Oficio Circular 543. "DFC" = the UAF 2025 supervision results. "L21719" = the data protection law. Requirements marked **[v1]** belong in the 3-week MVP. The others are for the sellable 6-8 week version or later.

**A. Entity profile, scope and set-up**

1. **[v1]** The system must store the entity's UAF category (corredor de propiedades, empresa de gestión inmobiliaria, notario, conservador), its legal form (natural person or company), its RUT and its company size (micro, small, medium or large, by annual sales in UF). Test: choosing "notario" sets the one-off DDC threshold to 1,000 UF; choosing "corredor" sets it to USD 3,000. Basis: Law art. 3; C62 f.4.2; Ann. 2.
2. **[v1]** The system must apply every duty to every entity regardless of size. Size may change only the OdC eligibility rule. Test: a natural-person broker with no staff still gets the manual, client-file, ROE, screening and training tasks. Basis: Law art. 3; C62 b.3.
3. **[v1]** One account must be able to hold several obliged entities, such as a developer group with many SPVs, with separate registers, manuals, OdCs and deadlines for each. It must also show a combined deadline view. Test: 10 SPVs show 10 separate nil-ROE tasks for the January window. Basis: C62 a.6, d.3; the portal's "Cambiar Entidad" module (ROE guide p. 7).
4. **[v1]** The system must have an entity status of "activity ended, closure pending". It must keep all duties active until an SII término de giro (or equivalent) has been sent to the UAF and the UAF has confirmed de-registration. Test: a closed project without a closure certificate still gets nil-ROE tasks. Basis: C62 a.5-a.6; UAF FAQ.
5. All content, forms and outputs must be in Chilean Spanish. Legal citations must use C62's own point labels (for example "literal g.3.8"). Test: no screen, generated document or export contains untranslated English. Basis: all UAF forms and guides are in Spanish.

**B. UAF registration and compliance officer**

6. **[v1]** The system must store the registration data the UAF holds for the entity, for the OdC and for the legal representative. At least: full name, RUN, address, phone and e-mail of each. Basis: C62 a.2, b.6.
7. **[v1]** Any edit to a stored registration field, the OdC or the legal representative must open a task "report to the UAF" due in **10 business days**, counted with Chilean public holidays. The task must store the date sent, the SIAC request number and a PDF of the proof. Test: a change entered on a Friday before a public holiday shows the correct due date. Basis: C62 a.4, b.6; Law art. 26.
8. **[v1]** The system must generate an OdC appointment document with an eligibility checklist:
   - a senior post with access to all operations (or the micro/small-firm option);
   - no conviction for money laundering, terrorist financing or an art. 27 predicate offence;
   - not a UAF employee in the last 6 months.

   The document must be signed by the legal representative. Test: an OdC cannot be saved as "eligible" with an unanswered item. Basis: C62 b.1-b.5.
9. The system must track the yearly expiry of the UAF portal password and remind the OdC 15 days before. Basis: UAF FAQ (the password is valid one year).
10. The system must store the UAF registration certificate (folio, verification code, issue date) and warn when it is older than 60 days. Basis: UAF FAQ (60-day validity).

**C. Manual and risk policy**

11. **[v1]** The manual generator must produce one document containing all 7 required parts of C62 j.3: internal organisation; DDC, BO and PEP policies; the risk policy; suspicious-operation detection and confidential reporting; the UN-list review procedure; the risk-country review procedure; and immediate reporting of UN matches. It must also include the ROS confidentiality procedure (c.5) and the BO request procedure (g.3.3). Test: a coverage checklist fails if any of the 9 parts is empty. Basis: C62 j.3, c.5, g.3.3.
12. **[v1]** The manual must have sector variants (broker, real-estate developer or manager, notary, conservador). Each must use that sector's thresholds, ROE form and red flags. Basis: Ann. 1-2; Red Flags Guide section 15.
13. The risk policy must rate the entity's exposure as high, medium or low. It must name threats, vulnerabilities and impact for clients, geography, products and services, and channels. It must cite the UAF National Risk Assessments as its base, and record the reasoning for each simplified or enhanced measure. Basis: C62 j.3.3, f.8-f.12.
14. **[v1]** The system must record approval of the manual by the top governing body (name, role, date) and block "in force" status without it. Basis: C62 j.3.
15. **[v1]** The system must schedule a manual review no later than 2 years after the last approval, and show "overdue" the day after. Test: a manual approved on 1 Jun 2025 shows overdue on 2 Jun 2027. Basis: C62 j.2.
16. The vendor must be able to flag every customer manual as "update needed" when the law or a UAF circular changes. The manual must show the date of the change. Basis: C62 j.2 ("lo antes posible").
17. **[v1]** The system must record delivery of each manual version to each worker (date, method, worker acknowledgement). It must show workers who have not received the current version. Basis: C62 j.2; DFC gap 7.

**D. Client file (DDC)**

18. **[v1]** The client form must hold the 7 C62 f.3 items for natural persons and legal persons. Items 1-7 must be required before a client can be marked "verified". Basis: C62 f.3.
19. **[v1]** For every deal the system must ask for the amount, currency and date. It must convert to USD (or UF for notaries and conservadores) at the official rate of that day. It must trigger DDC for one-off clients at or above the threshold, counting linked deals together. Test: two USD 1,800 deals for the same client in one week trigger DDC for a broker. Basis: C62 f.4.2; Ann. 2.
20. **[v1]** The user must be able to mark a client as a "permanent relationship". This triggers DDC before or at its start, whatever the amount. Basis: C62 f.4.1.
21. **[v1]** A "suspicion" switch must trigger full DDC regardless of thresholds or exemptions. Basis: C62 f.4.3.
22. Each verification step must be logged with the source (ID seen, SII or registry check, other), date, user and attached evidence. Basis: C62 f.5.
23. **[v1]** If a client refuses data, or data is flagged as false, the system must open an unusual-case record (see section H) and prompt a ROS assessment. Basis: C62 f.6.
24. **[v1]** Each client file must have a next-review date no later than 12 months after the last update. "Relevant change" events must bring the date forward. Overdue files must appear on the dashboard. Basis: C62 f.2; DFC gaps 4 and 6.
25. The system must let the user record the client's declared purpose and expected activity, and compare later deals against it. A mismatch must create an unusual-case record. Basis: C62 f.7.
26. **[v1]** Each client must carry a risk rating (low, medium, high) set by rules from the risk policy. High risk must require the enhanced measures of C62 f.10: purpose, source of funds, source of wealth, senior approval, closer monitoring and more frequent updates, each with a field and evidence. Basis: C62 f.9-f.10.
27. Simplified measures may be applied only to clients rated low risk under the policy. The measure used must be recorded from the 7 options in C62 f.12. Basis: C62 f.11-f.12.

**E. Beneficial owner**

28. **[v1]** For every legal-person or legal-arrangement client, the system must produce the UAF BO form with all its fields. Users may add fields but not remove UAF fields. Basis: C62 g.3.1; BO form.
29. **[v1]** The BO form must be fillable by the client online through a secure link, or printed for wet signature. It must store the signed form, the date and the signer's identity. Basis: C62 g.3.1 (in person or electronically). The legal weight of a simple electronic signature is unverified; see open questions.
30. **[v1]** The BO module must compute indirect ownership by multiplying shares along each chain. It must flag every natural person at 10% or more, and record effective control below 10%. Test: the three worked cases in the UAF help sheet give the same result. Basis: C62 g.2; [AyudaBF](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/AyudaBF.pdf).
31. **[v1]** On sending a BO request, the system must start a 40-business-day clock. On day 41 without a form, it must open an unusual-case record with "unjustified delay" and offer "assess ROS" and "decline or stop the relationship". Basis: C62 g.3.8.
32. Each BO declaration must have a verification record: the sources checked, the result and the user. A "verified" status must be impossible without one. Basis: C62 g.3.4.
33. For foreign legal-person clients, the system must require the identity and address of the most senior manager abroad and of the legal representatives in Chile. Basis: C62 g.3.7.
34. The yearly BO update must offer the form's "update without changes" option, referring to the earlier declaration's number and date. Basis: BO form, section 3; C62 f.2.

**F. PEP**

35. **[v1]** The system must ask, for every client and every BO, whether the person is a PEP (the 17 roles of C62 h.3, current or within at least 1 year of leaving office) and whether they are linked to one (spouse, civil partner, relative up to second degree, joint-action pact). It must produce the UAF PEP form for signature. Basis: C62 h.1-h.4; PEP form.
36. The entity must be able to extend the 1-year PEP period and add PEP categories in its risk policy, and the system must apply the longer period. Basis: C62 h.1.
37. **[v1]** A PEP client or PEP BO must require recorded senior-management approval before the relationship starts or continues, plus source-of-wealth and source-of-funds fields with evidence. Test: a PEP client cannot be "active" without approval. Basis: C62 h.4.1-h.4.2, g.3.6; DFC gap 1.
38. **[v1]** The system must keep a PEP register: the list of PEP clients, their operations with backup, and the h.4 information. It must be exportable on its own. Basis: C62 e.1 (PEP register).
39. A client who becomes a PEP during the relationship must trigger the approval workflow again. Basis: C62 h.4.1.

**G. UN sanctions and risk countries**

40. **[v1]** The system must screen every client, prospect, BO and representative against the UN Security Council consolidated list at onboarding. It must re-screen the whole client base automatically every time the list changes, and at least weekly. Each check must be logged with time, list version and result. Basis: C62 c.9-c.10; Law art. 38; DFC gap 5. (The weekly floor is my proposal; the circular says "periodic and systematic".)
41. **[v1]** Screening evidence must be kept at least 3 years and cannot be deleted before then. Basis: C62 c.9.
42. **[v1]** A confirmed UN match must create an "immediate ROS" task with no analysis step, marked urgent, and must alert the OdC at once. A possible match must go to the OdC for review, and the decision must be recorded. Basis: C62 c.11; Law art. 38.
43. The system must hold the FATF "increased monitoring" and "call for action" lists and the SII preferential tax regime list, each with an effective date. A client or deal linked to a listed country must be rated high risk (enhanced DDC), and FATF "call for action" countries must be flagged for countermeasures. The vendor must update the lists within 5 business days of each FATF plenary or SII change. Basis: C62 k.1-k.3. (The 5-day update target is my proposal.)

**H. Unusual operations and ROS**

44. **[v1]** The system must ship the UAF Red Flags Guide flags for this sector (section 15, flags 15.1-15.30) and the general flags, and let the entity add its own. Each deal can be tagged with flags. Tagging any flag creates an unusual-case record. Basis: C62 c.6; Red Flags Guide p. 44-45.
45. **[v1]** Each unusual case must record: open date, trigger, a description of the operations, the people involved, the analysis phases, steps taken, sources consulted, conclusion (ROS or discard), reasons, and close date. Closure must be impossible without a conclusion and reasons. Basis: C62 c.7-c.8.
46. **[v1]** Closed cases, both ROS and discarded, must stay in the suspicious-operations register for at least 5 years. Basis: C62 c.8, e.1, e.2.
47. **[v1]** For a ROS decision, the system must prepare a draft matching the UAF ROS form:
    - the step 1 and step 2 fields;
    - character counters (facts 250-1,000; reason 250-500; activity details up to 4,000; name up to 100; surnames up to 40);
    - RUT check-digit validation;
    - region and comuna pick-lists;
    - an attachment list that blocks non-accepted file types and files over 50 MB.

    Basis: ROS guide p. 4-15.
48. **[v1]** The system must not send the ROS itself. It must show the draft to the OdC for copying into the portal. It must then store the UAF sending certificate (ROS number, verification code, date). Only the OdC role may mark a ROS "sent". Basis: UAF FAQ (only the OdC sends a ROS); ROS guide p. 19.
49. The system must show elapsed time from case opening to ROS sending, and warn when a case marked "ROS" has not been sent within 2 business days (my proposed internal target; the circular says "as soon as possible"). Basis: C62 c.4.
50. **[v1]** ROS cases and their documents must be visible only to the OdC and users the OdC names. Every view must be logged. No client-facing screen, e-mail or export may reveal that a case or a ROS exists. Test: a client portal user sees nothing about cases. Basis: Law art. 6-7; C62 c.5.

**I. Cash operations (ROE)**

51. **[v1]** Every receipt or payment must record the means of payment. Only notes and coins count as cash. Cheques, transfers and vale vistas do not. Cash deposits into the entity's own bank accounts must be recordable. Basis: C62 d.2; ROE guide p. 3.
52. **[v1]** Cash amounts must be converted at the Banco Central "dólar observado" of the operation day. Each cash operation above USD 10,000 must be added to the cash register and the next ROE. Test: CLP 9,600,000 at 950 CLP/USD (USD 10,105) is included; the same amount at 970 (USD 9,897) is not. Basis: Law art. 5; C62 d.1.
53. **[v1]** For brokers, the system must produce the ROE Simplificado data (period, office address, conductor, principal and transaction fields) in the portal's field order. For real-estate firms, notaries and conservadores, it must fill the UAF 2-sheet Excel template once its layout is confirmed (see open questions). Basis: ROE guide p. 4, 7.
54. **[v1]** For each entity and each half-year, the system must create a ROE task (with data, or nil). It must appear 10 days before the window opens, which is the first 10 business days of July and January. The task must stay open until the user records the UAF status "Aprobado". Test: an entity with no cash in Jan-Jun 2027 gets a "send nil ROE" task due on the 10th business day of July 2027. Basis: C62 d.3-d.5; Ann. 1; UAF ROE calendar.
55. The ROE task must accept the UAF status (Ingresado, Recibido, Aprobado, Aprobado Fuera de Plazo, Rechazado) and the ROE certificate (folio, code, date). A "Rechazado" status must reopen the task, and "Aprobado Fuera de Plazo" must be flagged as a breach. Basis: C62 d.5; ROE guide p. 9-11.
56. A correction workflow must exist with a deadline of 10 business days after the filing deadline. When swapping a data ROE for a nil one, or the reverse, it must attach a PDF of the supporting documents. Basis: C62 d.6-d.7; ROE guide p. 10.
57. **[v1]** The cash register must keep, for every reported operation, all supporting documents linked to the approved ROE, for at least 5 years. Basis: Law art. 5; C62 e.1-e.2.

**J. Registers, retention and inspection pack**

58. **[v1]** The system must keep the four registers that apply to this segment (cash, suspicious, DDC, PEP) as separate, filterable lists, each exportable to Excel and PDF. Basis: C62 e.1.
59. **[v1]** Retention must run from the end of the relationship or the last one-off deal. Records must not be deletable before 5 years from that date (3 years for screening logs). Deletion after the period must be a deliberate admin action, logged. Basis: C62 e.2, c.9; Law art. 5.
60. **[v1]** An "inspection pack" export must produce, in one ZIP:
    - the current and previous manual with approvals and delivery records;
    - the OdC appointment;
    - the registration data;
    - the four registers;
    - the training register;
    - screening logs;
    - the case register;
    - ROE statuses and certificates;
    - a cover index mapped to the UAF's 8 verification standards.

    Test: the pack builds for any chosen date range in under 2 minutes for 2,000 clients. Basis: Law art. 2 f; C62 TERCERO, e.2; DFC standards.
61. The system must log every UAF information request (date received, deadline, what was asked, reply date, attachments). It must warn 2 business days before the deadline. Basis: Law art. 2 b, 19 c.
62. The system must hold an inspection record: type and mode, findings, any "representación" or improvement plan, each action with an owner and due date, and proof of completion. Basis: Sanctions policy p. 12-13; DFC 2025 (follow-up supervisions).

**K. Training**

63. **[v1]** The training register must store, for each session, the method (in person, online, e-learning), date, content, and each attendee's name, role and form of participation. Test: an attendance record cannot be saved without method and date. Basis: C62 l.2.
64. **[v1]** Each training plan must include the entity's own current manual and the UAF Red Flags Guide as minimum content. The system must let users attach certificates from UAF e-learning courses as extra evidence. Basis: C62 l.2; Stats 2025 p. 23.
65. **[v1]** The system must list every worker, including the OdC, and show who has no training in the last 12 months. Test: a worker trained on 10 Oct 2025 shows as overdue on 11 Oct 2026. Basis: C62 l.1; DFC gap 2. (Reading "once a year" as "within 12 months" is my interpretation.)

**L. Security, confidentiality and data protection**

66. **[v1]** The vendor must bind its staff and sub-processors in writing to the art. 6 confidentiality duty. Vendor support staff must not be able to see case or ROS data without a logged, time-limited grant from the OdC. Basis: Law art. 6 (covers "persons who provide services in any capacity"), art. 7.
67. Before 1 Dec 2026, the system must support the duties of Ley 21.719:
    - a data processing agreement with each customer;
    - standard contractual clauses for any transfer outside Chile;
    - encryption at rest and in transit;
    - role-based access;
    - full audit logs;
    - a breach-notification procedure;
    - export of a person's data on request.

    AML records must be shielded from deletion requests during the retention period. Basis: L21719 (in force 1 Dec 2026), standard contractual clauses approved Dec 2025; C62 e.2. Exact L21719 article numbers are unverified.
68. Every record (client file, BO, PEP, screening, case, training, ROE) must have an append-only audit trail showing who created or changed it and when. Basis: C62 c.7, f.5, g.3.4 (documented and recorded checks); Law art. 7 (penalty for altering or hiding documents).

## Open questions

1. **Who is "the client" of a broker?** Seller, buyer, landlord, tenant, or all of them? Does a rental-management mandate count as a "permanent relationship" that needs DDC whatever the amount and a yearly update? C62 does not say. This shapes the data model. Ask the UAF through SIAC, or a Chilean AML lawyer.
2. **What counts as an "empresa dedicada a la gestión inmobiliaria"?** Does every one-project SPV that sells units qualify? The register suggests yes (2,134-2,244 entries). No UAF definition was found.
3. **Simple electronic signature.** Is a simple electronic signature (Ley 19.799) enough for the BO sworn declaration and the PEP declaration, or is an advanced signature or wet ink needed? (unverified)
4. **Non-bank ROE Excel template.** What is the exact layout of the 2-sheet Excel ROE template? It is downloadable only inside the portal.
5. **Recommended DDC forms.** What do the "Formularios Recomendados DDC" in the portal contain? (unverified)
6. **Machine access for vendors.** Can a third-party SaaS use the Of. 543 machine access to file ROE (and ROS) for many entities? What will MiUAF offer (APIs, delegated users)?
7. **UAF e-learning as training.** Does the UAF accept its own e-learning certificates as annual training, given that the minimum content includes the entity's own manual?
8. **Sector guidance.** Has the UAF issued a "Enfoque Sectorial" (sector guidance) for real estate or notaries under C62 f.8? None was found on the circulars page.
9. **PEP data.** Is there any official or free PEP data source for Chile, for example asset declarations on InfoProbidad, or must the product rely on declarations plus commercial lists? (unverified)
10. **Fine increases.** What fine increases does Bill 15.975-25 contain in its latest version, and will the comisión mixta settle it in 2026?
11. **SII BO register.** Was an SII beneficial-owner register enacted? If so, can obliged entities query it?
12. **Notary reform.** Does Ley 21.772 or its regulations change any AML-related duty for notaries, or only appointment and digital rules? Is the reported retirement at 75 confirmed?
13. **Data law details.** Does Ley 21.719 require a foreign controller or processor to name a representative in Chile? What are the exact articles on transfers and breach notification? (unverified)
14. **Unregistered brokers.** Does the UAF pursue unregistered brokers at all? No enforcement against non-registration was found.
15. **Inspection checklist.** The UAF's internal "planilla de revisión" is not public. A transparency request (Ley 20.285) could obtain it and make the inspection pack match it exactly.
