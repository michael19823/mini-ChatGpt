# Chile UAF kit: market size, buyers and competition (deep dive 02)

Date: 10 Oct 2026. Builds on [the B4 report](../reports/chile-b4.md). Scope: market size, buyers, competition, channels and regional expansion. Law, product design and go-to-market detail are covered by the other agents.

Status: complete (10 Oct 2026). Exchange rates used: UF 1 = CLP 41,136 and USD 1 = CLP 982 ([mindicador.cl](https://mindicador.cl/api)), so UF 1 is about USD 42.

## Summary

- **Hard buyer count.** I counted the UAF register of 30 June 2026 ([UAF xlsx](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Sujetos_Obligados_inscritos_en_la_UAF_al_30.06.2026.xlsx)): **1,459 property brokers** (664 of them sole agents), **2,244 real-estate firms** (about 1,000-1,500 developer groups by my name grouping), **483 notaries** and **92 conservadores**. That is 4,278 entities and about 3,000-3,500 buying decisions. The group grew by a net 298 entities in a year (3,980 in June 2025). Another 1,453 small obliged firms (car dealers, exchange houses, auction houses, customs agents) could use the same tool.
- **Enforcement is shifting, not fading.** Final sanctions on brokers fell to almost zero after 2020, but sanctions lag inspections by two to three years. UAF supervision of the four target sectors nearly doubled, from 43 actions in 2024 to 83 in 2025. **Notaries were the most supervised sector of all in 2025 (38, about 8% of notaries)** and drew 26 sanctions in 2024-2025 ([UAF 2026 plan](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Resultados_DFC_2025_y_Plan_2026_VF_xsmFFOU.pdf); [UAF sanctions](https://www.uaf.cl/es-cl/publicaciones-uaf/sanciones-ejecutoriadas)). For 2026 the UAF adds cheap "coverage" reviews and sample checks of nil cash-operation reports (ROE Negativo), which brokers, developers and notaries must file every six months.
- **Fines are small, the gaps are basic.** Fines in this group in 2024-2025 were UF 15-60 (USD 630-2,500) plus a public reprimand. The UAF's own top-ten gap list is PEP checks, annual training, ROE, client files, UN list screening, the manual and registration updates. The one broker sanctioned in 2025 had none of the five basics.
- **Correction to B4: there are now direct rivals.** **C-ONLINE** sells a UAF module aimed at "notarios, corredores" for **UF 2.5 a month + VAT plus a setup fee** ([C-ONLINE](https://uaf.conline.cl/)). **Lexizum** is launching AML screening at UF 3-65 a month ([Lexizum](https://www.lexizum.com/)). **Regcheq** (600+ clients) covers almost the full duty list and is integrated with the developer CRMs PlanOK, Moby Suite and SCI ([Regcheq partners](https://regcheq.com/es-cl/partners)). **Gesintel** and **Neitcom** sell screening to notaries. The UAF runs a **free e-learning campus** for registered entities ([UAF campus](https://capacitacion.uaf.cl/campus/)).
- **The opening that remains.** Nobody shows a self-serve, publicly priced kit for the one-to-ten-person office. Nobody shows the deadline calendar, the Circular 62 ROS analysis register, the five registers and an inspection folder. C-ONLINE's UF 30 a year is too much for a sole broker, who earns about UF 60 from one side of a typical sale.
- **Willingness to pay.** Anchors: broker association fee UF 2.5 a year, broker courses CLP 156,000-289,000, a UC AML course CLP 410,000, C-ONLINE UF 30 a year. I propose UF 5-10 a year for brokers and small developers and about UF 24 a year for notaries. That gives **about UF 3,760 (USD 158,000) a year by year 3** at modest uptake, with notaries worth as much as brokers.
- **Channels.** The public register (names and RUTs, new entrants every six months), the sanctions list, the nil-ROE windows and the Clave Única switch on 19 October 2026; broker associations ACOP, COPROCH and ANACOPRO and their courses; the notaries' directory and digital-notary networks; and compliance boutiques. Developer CRMs are already tied to Regcheq.
- **Regional.** Peru is the best second market (same buyer types, only general-purpose rivals so far). Paraguay fits the deadline features. Argentina (AMLify) and Uruguay (Cumplo360, HADA) have local incumbents. Mexico is large but crowded.
- **Verdict for this area.** The market is real but small and quiet. It is winnable at a low, public price with notaries as the paying anchor and brokers as volume. The main threats are C-ONLINE or Regcheq moving down-market on price.

### Enforcement by segment (my count of the UAF sanctions register)

The UAF lists every final sanction ("sanciones ejecutoriadas") with the sector and the cause ([UAF, Sanciones ejecutoriadas](https://www.uaf.cl/es-cl/publicaciones-uaf/sanciones-ejecutoriadas)). I counted 1,546 final sanctions from November 2011 to 25 September 2025 (appeal rows removed; sector names normalised).

| Year of resolution | Brokers | Real-estate firms | Notaries | All sectors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2012 | 75 | 22 | 12 | 260 |
| 2018 | 24 | 26 | 3 | 176 |
| 2019 | 19 | 32 | 3 | 176 |
| 2020 | 14 | 17 | 0 | 79 |
| 2021 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 18 |
| 2022 | 1 | 10 | 0 | 20 |
| 2023 | 1 | 7 | 0 | 65 |
| 2024 | 0 | 5 | 10 | 57 |
| 2025 (to 25 Sep) | 1 | 1 | 16 | 45 |
| **Total 2011-2025** | **245** | **167** | **50** | **1,546** |

What this shows:
- **Brokers have almost dropped out of enforcement.** They had 245 sanctions in total, but only 4 since 2021.
- **Real-estate firms get a handful a year.** Most older cases were for late or missing cash-operation reports (ROE).
- **Notaries are now the main target in this group.** 26 notaries were sanctioned in 2024-2025, all after on-site inspections ("Fiscalización"). With about 490 notaries, that is roughly 5% of all notaries sanctioned in two years.
- **Overall sanction volume is a third of the 2018-2019 level** (45-65 a year against 176).

**Typical fines are small.** I read the resolutions for all five 2023-2025 broker and real-estate cases ([UAF sanction PDFs](https://www.uaf.cl/media/archivos_sanciones/087-2023.pdf)):

| [056-2024](https://www.uaf.cl/media/archivos_sanciones/056-2024.pdf) | E Molina Morel Inmobiliaria Ltda. | real estate | reprimand + UF 40 (2 Jun 2025) |

| [028-2024](https://www.uaf.cl/media/archivos_sanciones/028-2024.pdf) | Notary Nancy de la Fuente Hernández | notary | reprimand + UF 40 (5 Sep 2025) |

The broker and real-estate cases I checked for class (087-2023, 088-2023, 090-2023) were "leve" (minor), where the legal ceiling is UF 800. Two notary cases I read were fined UF 30 ([102-2023](https://www.uaf.cl/media/archivos_sanciones/102-2023.pdf)) and UF 60 ([007-2024](https://www.uaf.cl/media/archivos_sanciones/007-2024.pdf)). So final fines in this group run from **UF 15 to UF 60** (about USD 630 to 2,500 at UF 1 = CLP 41,136 and USD 1 = CLP 982 on 9-10 Oct 2026, [mindicador.cl](https://mindicador.cl/api)). Every fine also comes with a written reprimand, a public listing by name on the UAF site, and a record that counts towards a repeat offence.

**Sanctions lag inspections by two to three years.** The broker Corretajes Sierpe y Sierpe was inspected on 24 November 2022 and sanctioned on 25 September 2025 ([087-2023](https://www.uaf.cl/media/archivos_sanciones/087-2023.pdf)). The notary in 028-2024 was inspected on 28 March 2023 and sanctioned on 5 September 2025 ([028-2024](https://www.uaf.cl/media/archivos_sanciones/028-2024.pdf)). So the low sanction counts for 2021-2025 reflect the low inspection counts of 2020-2023, not today's activity.

### Supervision is rising fast in this group

The UAF now publishes a yearly supervision report with counts by sector ([UAF, results 2024 and plan 2025](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Resultados_2024_y_Enfasis_2025.pdf); [UAF, results 2025 and plan 2026, April 2026](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Resultados_DFC_2025_y_Plan_2026_VF_xsmFFOU.pdf)). I read the sector charts.

| Sector | Supervision actions 2024 | Supervision actions 2025 (Mar 2025-Feb 2026) | Registered entities 2026 | Share supervised in 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Notaries | 12 | **38** (top sector) | 483 | about 8% |
| Real-estate firms | 17 | 27 | 2,244 | about 1.2% |
| Property brokers | 13 | 17 | 1,459 | about 1.2% |
| Conservadores | 1 | 1 | 92 | about 1% |
| All sectors | 163 | 252 (incl. 134 follow-ups) | 9,784 | about 2.6% |

- Supervision of the four target sectors nearly doubled, from 43 to 83 actions. Given the two-to-three-year lag, more broker and real-estate sanctions are likely in 2026-2028 (my inference).
- 68% of 2024 supervisions were done online, not on site ([UAF 2024 report](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Resultados_2024_y_Enfasis_2025.pdf)). That makes it cheap for the UAF to reach small firms anywhere.
- For 2026 the UAF adds two cheap, wide tools ([UAF 2026 plan](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Resultados_DFC_2025_y_Plan_2026_VF_xsmFFOU.pdf)):
  - **"Supervisiones de cobertura"**: short reviews of groups of entities, checking basics such as written policies, the named compliance officer, basic KYC, monitoring and training.
  - **"Supervisión técnica de reportes"**: sample checks that each entity files its ROE and its nil ROE ("ROE Negativo") on time.
- Brokers, real-estate firms, notaries and conservadores must all file ROE or nil ROE **every six months**. The 2026 windows are 1-14 July 2026 and 4-15 January 2027 ([UAF ROE calendar 2026](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Calendario_ROE_2026_fJZ3WvN.pdf); [UAF calendar page](https://www.uaf.cl/es-cl/sujetos-obligados/sector-privado/calendario-del-reporte-roe)). This settles an open question in the B4 report: a nil report is required.

## Buyer profile and pain

**Who they are.**
- **Brokers.** 664 of the 1,459 registered brokers are natural persons, that is sole agents (my count of the [UAF register](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Sujetos_Obligados_inscritos_en_la_UAF_al_30.06.2026.xlsx), RUT below 50 million). Most of the 795 companies are small Ltda or SpA firms. Big brands are few: I found 9 "Fuenzalida" entities, 2 Assetplan, 1 Century21 office, and no entity named RE/MAX (my name search; RE/MAX offices may register under other names, unverified). A broker earns about 2% of the price from each side of a sale, a month's rent on a lease, and 10% of rent for management ([Anacopro](https://anacopro.cl/producto/curso-de-corredor-de-propiedades/)).
- **Real-estate firms.** Only 4 of 2,244 are natural persons. Most are project companies (SPVs). By name stem I get about 1,500 groups, and the true number is probably lower because one group may use several names: 1,226 stems appear once, and 47 stems have 5 or more entities, which together hold 422 entities (my grouping of the [UAF register](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Sujetos_Obligados_inscritos_en_la_UAF_al_30.06.2026.xlsx); name grouping is rough). The register grew by 242 real-estate entities in a year, as new SPVs register.
- **Notaries and conservadores.** All are natural persons (the office holder). The notary reform, Ley 21.772, has been in force since 2 April 2026. It ends life tenure with an age limit of 75, fills posts by public contest through Alta Dirección Pública, and lets the government cap notary fees ([Meganoticias](https://www.meganoticias.cl/nacional/520237-cambios-por-ley-de-notarias-23-04-2026.html); [SERNAC](https://www.sernac.cl/portal/604/w3-article-88514.html)). Turnover is already visible: 43 notaries joined and 56 left the UAF register in 12 months (my comparison of the 2025 and 2026 registers). Each new notary must set up a UAF system from scratch.

**How they comply today (evidence from UAF findings).** The UAF's own inspection results are the best evidence of current practice:
- **2025 inspections, high-risk sectors** (notaries, car dealers, exchange houses, brokers, real-estate firms). The ten most common gaps, by number of finished inspection reports ([UAF 2026 plan, p. 9](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Resultados_DFC_2025_y_Plan_2026_VF_xsmFFOU.pdf)):

| Gap found | Entities |
|---|---|
| KYC: PEP clients | 33 |
| Annual AML training | 29 |
| ROE: recording and sending cash operations above USD 10,000 | 23 |
| KYC: client file for permanent clients | 22 |
| KYC: monitoring UN resolutions (list screening) | about 22 (label partly cut off in the chart) |
| KYC: client file for occasional clients | 18 |
| Manual: distribution to staff and updating | 16 |
| KYC: beneficial-owner data for company clients | 14 |
| Having a prevention manual at all | 14 |
| Registers: keeping UAF registration data up to date | 12 |

- **A typical broker case.** Corretajes Sierpe y Sierpe had none of the basics: no client files, no PEP procedure, no UN list checks, no staff training and no manual. It sent no defence ([UAF 087-2023](https://www.uaf.cl/media/archivos_sanciones/087-2023.pdf)).
- **A typical small developer.** Inmobiliaria e Inversiones MMC S.A. had one employee and no manual. After the inspection it "contracted the Gesintel system" for list searches. The UAF cut the fine to UF 15 because of the low risk and the fixes ([UAF 090-2023](https://www.uaf.cl/media/archivos_sanciones/090-2023.pdf)).
- **A typical notary case.** No ROE, no client file for operations above UF 1,000, no PEP checks, no UN screening, no manual with the minimum content, and no update of UAF registration data ([UAF 102-2023](https://www.uaf.cl/media/archivos_sanciones/102-2023.pdf); [UAF 007-2024](https://www.uaf.cl/media/archivos_sanciones/007-2024.pdf)).

**Software they already use.**
- Brokers use listing portals and broker CRMs. The ones I checked (Tokko Broker, Kiteprop) show no AML features ([Tokko Broker](https://www.tokkobroker.com/es-ar/); [Kiteprop](https://www.kiteprop.com/ar)). Chile-specific broker CRMs were not checked one by one (unverified).
- Developers use sales CRMs such as PlanOK, Moby Suite and SCI. These link to Regcheq rather than build their own AML module ([Regcheq partners](https://regcheq.com/es-cl/partners); [Moby Suite integrations](https://www.mobysuite.com/cl/integraciones?integration=regcheq)).
- Notaries are a named target of the screening services Gesintel and Neitcom, so some of them probably use one already (usage share unverified) ([gesintel.cl](https://www.gesintel.cl/); [Neitcom](https://neitcom-compliance.cl/soluciones/ley-19913/)).

**Their pain, in order.**
1. **Not knowing what "good" looks like.** The gaps above are basics, not edge cases. Small firms lack a manual, a client file and a training record.
2. **Deadlines.** Nil ROE twice a year, a 10-business-day limit for registration changes, a manual review every two years, and yearly training. Missing ROE has always been a top cause of fines ([Diario Estrategia](https://www.diarioestrategia.cl/texto-diario/mostrar/1401968/uaf-usuarios-zonas-francas-corredores-propiedades-notarios-empresas-gestion-inmobiliarias-explican-55-multas-infracciones-normativa-antivalado)), and the UAF is now sampling it ([UAF 2026 plan](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Resultados_DFC_2025_y_Plan_2026_VF_xsmFFOU.pdf)).
3. **Evidence at inspection.** The UAF asks the compliance officer to show records on the spot. Missing evidence becomes a charge ([UAF 087-2023](https://www.uaf.cl/media/archivos_sanciones/087-2023.pdf)).
4. **Beneficial owners.** Circular 62 extends BF checks to real-estate intermediation and cuts the threshold for one-off deals from USD 15,000 to USD 3,000 ([Cuatrecasas](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/es/latam/gobierno-corporativo-compliance/art/unidad-analisis-financiero-dicta-circular-62-sujetos-obligados)).

**Public complaints.** My Spanish searches found no forum threads, press stories or association statements in which brokers complain about UAF work (unverified; Facebook and WhatsApp groups are not searchable). This is a quiet market. The sale will have to be led by education and deadlines, not by an existing outcry.

**What this means.**
- **A solo broker will not pay C-ONLINE's UF 30 a year.** That is more than ten times the Anacopro membership fee and about half a typical commission on one sale. A broker who sells a UF 3,000 home earns about UF 60 from one side ([Anacopro](https://anacopro.cl/producto/curso-de-corredor-de-propiedades/)). A price of UF 5 to 8 a year (about USD 210-335) is about a tenth of that commission and below one fine. That looks payable (my estimate, unverified until pilots).
- **Notaries can pay more.** They are the most inspected group (about 8% a year in 2025) and the most sanctioned in 2024-2025. They also handle high volumes of client operations. UF 1.5 to 3 a month matches C-ONLINE and Lexizum Starter (my estimate). The fee caps under Ley 21.772 may squeeze notary budgets (unverified effect).
- **Developers pay per group, not per SPV.** A group with 5 to 30 SPVs needs one subscription that covers all of them. Large developers already buy Regcheq through their CRM. The opening is the 1,226 one-off developers and small groups (my grouping).
- **The fine is not the main lever.** At about 1.2% inspection odds a year and UF 15-60 fines, the expected yearly fine for a broker is under UF 1. The levers are fear of the public listing, the coming wave of cheap "coverage" and nil-ROE checks, banks asking for proof of compliance (unverified), and simply saving time.

**What the table says.**
- **The market is not empty any more.** The B4 report found "no broker-specific tool". In fact C-ONLINE sells a UAF module to notaries and brokers at UF 2.5 a month, and Lexizum is launching with public prices. Regcheq covers almost the whole duty list for larger firms and is wired into developer CRMs.
- **Nobody yet owns the solo broker and the small developer.** C-ONLINE's UF 30 a year plus a setup fee is about 5 times the UF 6 a year the B4 report assumed. Regcheq, Gesintel and Neitcom sell by demo and quote.
- **The gaps are the "paperwork" duties.** No product page I read shows a deadline calendar (10-day registration changes, two-year manual review, yearly training, ROE periods), a ROS analysis register with the Circular 62 fields, or an inspection-ready export of the five registers. Only C-ONLINE mentions a manual. These are exactly the items the UAF cites in fines (see "Buyer profile and pain").
- **Screening is becoming a commodity.** Four vendors sell PEP and UN list checks. A new product should include screening, but it should not compete on screening depth.


## Channels

**1. The register itself (direct outreach).**
- The UAF publishes every reporting entity by name, sector and RUT every six months ([UAF register page](https://www.uaf.cl/es-cl/sujetos-obligados/sector-privado/inscritos-en-la-uaf); [June 2026 xlsx](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Sujetos_Obligados_inscritos_en_la_UAF_al_30.06.2026.xlsx)). The file has no email or phone. Contacts must be found from websites, the broker associations' member lists and the notaries' directory (enrichment cost unverified).
- Each new edition shows newcomers: 87 new brokers, 242 new real-estate entities and 43 new notaries in the last year (my comparison). New entrants are the best leads, because they must build a system from zero.
- The sanctions register names every sanctioned entity ([UAF sanctions](https://www.uaf.cl/es-cl/publicaciones-uaf/sanciones-ejecutoriadas)). These are warm leads for a "fix it before the follow-up visit" offer. The UAF ran 134 follow-up supervisions in 2025 ([UAF 2026 plan](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Resultados_DFC_2025_y_Plan_2026_VF_xsmFFOU.pdf)).

**2. Calendar moments.**
- The UAF portal moves to Clave Única login on 19 October 2026 ([Prieto](https://www.prieto.cl/en/uaf-implementa-autenticacion-mediante-clave-unica-en-el-portal-de-entidades-reportantes-2/)).
- The nil-ROE windows are 4-15 January 2027 and July each year ([UAF ROE calendar](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Calendario_ROE_2026_fJZ3WvN.pdf)).
- The UAF campus intake windows (the last one closed 8 June 2026) ([UAF campus](https://capacitacion.uaf.cl/campus/)).

**3. Broker associations and training schools.**
- **ACOP** calls itself the largest broker association in Chile, with more than 80 years of history. It has a public member list, regional chapters and a training centre. Its broker course runs from 19 October to 16 December 2026 ([ACOP](https://www.acop.cl/); [ACOP members](https://www.acop.cl/listadoSocios.aspx)). The B4 report put membership at about 300 firms ([Infonegocios](https://infonegocios.info/default/chile-ojo-si-arrendas-este-verano-1-de-cada-4-corredores-de-propiedades-esta-registrado)).
- **COPROCH** (Asociación Gremial de Corredores de Propiedades de Chile) has a member search, an ethics committee and a training page ([COPROCH](https://www.coproch.cl/)).
- **ANACOPRO** has members from Arica to Punta Arenas. It charges UF 6 to join and UF 2.5 a year, and sells broker courses online ([Anacopro](https://anacopro.cl/como-ser-socio-anacopro/); [Anacopro courses](https://anacopro.cl/catalogo-cursos/)).
- **Colegio Inmobiliario** sells a course "to become a broker" ([link from Anacopro's site](https://colegioinmobiliario.cl/producto/curso-especial-para-ser-corredor-de-propiedades/)).
- New brokers must take a course from a training body under standard NCh2728 to join ANACOPRO ([Anacopro](https://anacopro.cl/como-ser-socio-anacopro/)). An AML module sold inside these courses is a natural add-on (my suggestion).

**4. Notaries.**
- The notaries' association site (notariosyconservadores.cl) has a directory of all notaries and conservadores and a document-check service ([Notarios y Conservadores](https://notariosyconservadores.cl/)).
- Digital notary platforms work with networks of notaries: Despapeliza (Legaliza.io) signed an agreement with Fundación Red Notarial ([Descubre](https://www.descubre.vc/noticia/despapeliza-y-fundaci-n-red-notarial-impulsan-la-digitalizaci-n-notarial-en-chile-2025-09-03)), and Legora connects signers to a network of partner notaries ([ITSitio](https://www.itsitio.com/ch/soluciones/chile-transformacion-digitalnotarial/)). These are partnership targets for a notary KYC add-on (my suggestion).
- New notaries appointed by public contest under Ley 21.772 are a clear entry point ([Meganoticias](https://www.meganoticias.cl/nacional/520237-cambios-por-ley-de-notarias-23-04-2026.html)).

**5. Developers.**
- PlanOK, Moby Suite and SCI are already Regcheq partners ([Regcheq partners](https://regcheq.com/es-cl/partners)). Partnering with them would mean competing with Regcheq inside their marketplaces.
- The CChC and the ADI are the developers' trade bodies (channel value unverified).

**6. Law firms, compliance boutiques and accountants.**
- Regcheq's partner list shows the model: law firms that write AML manuals (Carey, Guerrero Olivos, Aninat, HD Group Compliance, Compliance Metrics) and a Big Four firm (Deloitte) refer clients to software ([Regcheq partners](https://regcheq.com/es-cl/partners)). Smaller boutiques could resell a cheap kit to their small clients.
- Accountants who keep the books of small brokers are a likely reseller channel (unverified).

**7. Events and content.**
- Regcheq runs a "Compliance Leaders" podcast and an evening event on 15 October 2026 ([Regcheq](https://regcheq.com/es-cl)). Law-firm alerts on Circular 62 rank well in search ([Cuatrecasas](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/es/latam/gobierno-corporativo-compliance/art/unidad-analisis-financiero-dicta-circular-62-sujetos-obligados); [AZ](https://www.az.cl/claves-para-entender-el-impacto-regulatorio-de-la-circular-n62-de-la-uaf/)). Plain-Spanish content aimed at brokers and notaries is still thin. My searches found none written for brokers.

## Implications for positioning and pricing

**Position: "UAF in a box" for the one-to-ten-person office, not another screening engine.**
- Lead with what nobody shows: a deadline calendar (nil ROE twice a year, 10-day registration changes, two-year manual review, yearly training), the Circular 62 ROS analysis register, the five registers, and a one-click "inspection folder". These match the UAF's own top-ten gap list ([UAF 2026 plan](https://www.uaf.cl/media/documentos/Informe_Resultados_DFC_2025_y_Plan_2026_VF_xsmFFOU.pdf)).
- Include list screening (UN lists are free) and PEP checks, but treat them as hygiene. Four vendors already sell screening.
- Offer a manual generator and staff training on the entity's own manual. Link to the free UAF campus rather than fight it.
- Self-serve sign-up, card payment and a public price. Every serious rival is quote-only except C-ONLINE and Lexizum.

**Price (my proposal, to test in pilots).** UF 1 = about USD 42.

| Plan | Who | Price | Compared with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo | sole broker or conservador, 1 user | UF 0.5 a month or UF 5 a year | C-ONLINE UF 2.5 a month + setup |
| Office | broker firm or small developer, up to 5 users, 1-3 entities | UF 1 a month or UF 10 a year | Lexizum Starter UF 3 a month |
| Notary | notary office, up to 15 users, higher screening volume | UF 2 a month | C-ONLINE UF 2.5 a month; Gesintel quote-only |
| Group | developer group with many SPVs | UF 1.5 a month + UF 0.2 per extra entity | Regcheq quote-only |
| Partner | accountant or compliance boutique | 30-40% reseller margin, or UF 3 a month for 10 client entities | none |

**Rough size check (year 3).** 8% of brokers (117 x UF 6) + 6% of about 1,500 developer groups (90 x UF 15; fewer groups would mean fewer buyers but larger ones) + 10% of notaries (48 x UF 24) + 5% of conservadores (5 x UF 24) + 3% of 1,453 adjacent small entities (44 x UF 10) = about 702 + 1,350 + 1,152 + 120 + 440 = **about UF 3,760 a year, roughly USD 158,000** (counts from the UAF register; shares and prices are my assumptions). This matches the B4 report's estimate. The notary segment is worth as much as the broker segment with a third of the customers.

**Risks for positioning.**
- C-ONLINE could cut its price or drop the setup fee. Regcheq could launch a self-serve tier.
- Lexizum's launch adds a priced screening rival (launch status unverified).
- Brokers outside the register (5,000 to 20,000+) are not buyers until the licensing bill passes ([Emol](https://www.emol.com/noticias/Economia/2025/08/27/1176126/corredores-de-propiedades.html)).
