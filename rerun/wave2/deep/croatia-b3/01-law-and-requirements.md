# Croatia: employer duties for third-country workers, turned into product requirements

Status: complete as of 2026-10-10 (open points are listed under Open questions). Written in plain English. Every legal claim cites the article and a URL. "(unverified)" marks what I could not confirm in a primary source.

**Main sources and short names**

- **ZoS** = Zakon o strancima (Aliens Act), NN 133/20, 114/22, 151/22, 40/25, 55/26. I read Chapter VI (work, Art. 88-149), Chapter X (residence registration, Art. 176-180), Chapters XIII-XIV (inspection, Art. 231-242.a), Chapter XVII (fines, Art. 247-254) and the transitional rules, in the consolidated text on [zakon.hr](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima). - **PB** = Pravilnik o boravku državljana trećih zemalja u RH (the residence rulebook), NN 20/22, 155/22, 43/26. I read the amendment [NN 43/26](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2026_04_43_511.html) in full. It was published 22 Apr 2026 and has been in force since 30 Apr 2026 (Art. 40).

The amendment was published 29 Jun 2026 and has been in force since 7 Jul 2026 ([TEB](https://teb.hr/novosti/2026/zakon-o-izmjenama-i-dopunama-zakona-o-suzbijanju-neprijavljenoga-rada-nar-nov-br-6926/)).

Abbreviations: TCN = third-country national, meaning anyone who is not a citizen of an EEA state or Switzerland (ZoS Art. 3(3)). PU = police administration (policijska uprava). PP = police station. HZZ = Croatian Employment Service. LMT = labour-market test (test tržišta rada). DIRH = State Inspectorate (Državni inspektorat). HZMO = pension insurance institute. Permit = residence-and-work permit (dozvola za boravak i rad), which is the EU "single permit".

## Summary

- **The duty is real, detailed and recently tightened.** The Aliens Act sets more than 25 separate employer duties for TCN workers. They cover the hiring checks, the application file, short notice deadlines, renewals, housing, the police-area limit, changes of employer, job or user, the board-member permit, and (from 2027) a language exam. Three texts changed in 2026: the residence rulebook (in force 30 Apr), the Aliens Act (4 Jun) and the Undeclared Work Act (7 Jul). The 2024 worker-records rulebook still governs the employer's register.
- **Corrections to the lead data (all checked in the law text):**
  - The notice to police when a contract ends is due in **5 working days**, not 15 (ZoS Art. 95(1)). The 15-day rule and the 660-920 EUR fine seen in Varaždin cases are from the old text. The fine is now **1,500-3,000 EUR** for a company plus **500-1,000 EUR** for the responsible person (Art. 251(1)-(2)).
  - The **A1.1 Croatian exam** applies from **4 Jun 2027**. It covers workers who have been in Croatia for at least 1 year on an HZZ-opinion permit or an earthquake-reconstruction permit. The employer pays for it, and the proof goes into the worker register (ZoS Art. 92.a; NN 55/26 Art. 70).
  - The **board-member rule is confirmed.** If a company's only director(s) are TCNs, one of them must apply for a key-personnel permit within **30 days** of entry or change in the court register (ZoS Art. 111(5)). Until that is done, the company cannot get permits for workers (Art. 93(1)(17)).
  - The **5,000-15,000 EUR per worker** fines are correct for unpermitted work or work outside the permit's job or employer (Art. 247(2)). Illegal stay costs **10,000-20,000 EUR per worker** (Art. 247(1)).
  - **Form 18a is the health and vaccination certificate**, not the housing statement. The housing statement is **Form 17a** (PB Art. 8(2), 50).
  - The **HZZ turnover test** is account inflows of at least **100,000 EUR** in the last 12 months for a legal person, or **40,000 EUR** for a natural person (ZoS Art. 99(1)(7)). The gov.hr page still shows an older rule (10,000 EUR a month), so the state's own guidance is out of date ([gov.hr](https://www.gov.hr/hr/rad-stranaca/1214)).
- **Penalties stack up fast:**
  - 30-day closure of the premises, unless the employer pays **3,981.68 EUR per worker** within 3 days (ZoS Art. 240).
  - TCN work against the Aliens Act also counts as **undeclared work** (ZSNR Art. 3(3), restated by NN 69/26 from 7 Jul 2026). That costs **2,650 / 6,630 / 8,000 EUR per worker** (first, second, and third or later finding within 3 years; the 8,000 EUR step is new in NN 69/26). On top of that comes back pay at the median gross wage for each month worked (ZSNR Art. 7, 10).
  - An employer that does not pay the amount, or that is caught a third time, goes on the ministry's **public blacklist**. A closure order under ZoS Art. 240 that is not bought out does the same. Listing lasts up to 1 year, or ends early on payment. A listed employer cannot get new permits and can lose existing ones (ZSNR Art. 12-12.a; ZoS Art. 93(1)(8), 94(1)(9), 99(1)(9)).
  - **A missing or wrong worker register** is a "most serious" labour offence: **8,090-13,270 EUR** (ZR Art. 229(1)(1)). A final fine for such an offence also blocks the HZZ positive opinion (ZoS Art. 99(1)(6)).
- **Enforcement is active.** In 2025 labour inspectors ran 7,903 inspections. They found 961 TCNs working illegally and closed 263 employers. 156 of those paid 1,059,126.88 EUR to lift the closure, and 581 employers were on the blacklist on 19 Dec 2025 (DIRH 2025, pp. 26-28). That was done with only 97 labour-relations inspectors (65% of posts filled).
- **Filing channels are fragmented and the state tracks nothing for the employer:**
  - The LMT and HZZ-route permits go through the HZZ "Burza rada" web portal.
  - Extensions, seasonal and Art. 110 permits go by **e-mail**: a hand-filled, scanned Form 2a sent to one of 42 PU/PP addresses, as PDF, max 20 MB (MUP guide).
  - Decisions arrive in the employer's **e-Građani** inbox (ZoS Art. 91(13), 96(2)).
  - Notices (end of contract, housing change, posting) have **no form or channel set in the law** (unverified).
  - No public API or status tracker was found. HZZ's page offers no status tracking ([HZZ](https://hzz.hr/usluge/radne-dozvole-za-zaposljavanje-stranaca-i-test-trzista-rada/)).
  - So the product is an **employer-side register, rules engine, deadline engine and document pack**. It is not a filing integration.
- **Rule churn is the moat and the risk.** By about 4 Dec 2026 the minister must align the residence rulebook, the language-exam rulebook, the visa rulebook and the HZZ records rulebook (NN 55/26 Art. 69). The JEER real-time work-records rulebook was due by about 7 Oct 2026 (NN 69/26 Art. 15). The housing grace period ends about **30 Apr 2027** (PB Art. 39), and the language exam starts on **4 Jun 2027**.
- **Product:** 68 testable requirements follow, each traced to an article. The core is:
  - a TCN worker register that meets PER Art. 3-9 and exports an inspection file;
  - a permit-type rules engine (HZZ route, seasonal, Art. 110, key personnel, no-permit categories);
  - a deadline engine for the 3-, 5-, 8-, 15-, 30- and 90-day clocks;
  - a pre-filing eligibility check for the HZZ opinion (Art. 99, 100.a);
  - a police-area and job/employer placement guard;
  - a housing calculator for PB Art. 47-49 with Form 17a;
  - generators for the zadužnica, the worker notices and the notices to police;
  - an agency multi-client view.

**No size threshold and no micro-firm exemption.** Size and profile change only the conditions for getting an HZZ-route permit:

- At least one Croatian, EEA or Swiss worker employed continuously, full-time and open-ended in the last 12 months. For a seasonal "obrt", one such worker in the previous season is enough (ZoS Art. 99(1)(4), (2)). A natural-person employer counts as an employee (Art. 99(3)).
- Account inflows of at least 100,000 EUR (legal person) or 40,000 EUR (natural person) in the 12 months before the month of filing (Art. 99(1)(7), (4)-(5)).
- No account block for more than 30 days in a row in the last 6 months (Art. 99(1)(8)).
- Croatian, EEA or Swiss full-time staff of at least **20%** of the TCN staff, or **10%** for shortage occupations. Positive opinions already issued count towards the TCN total (Art. 99(11)-(12), (14)).
- **Cap on applications:** employers with up to 50 Croatian, EEA or Swiss staff may file at most **50** HZZ-route applications "in total". Those with 51-250 may file at most **250** (Art. 100.a). How "in total" and the end of the cap ("until the conditions of Art. 99(11)-(12) apply") are counted is unclear (unverified).

**Lighter regimes:**

- **Seasonal work of up to 90 days** in agriculture, forestry, hospitality or tourism needs no LMT and no HZZ opinion (Art. 97(7), 104(2)). It also has no zadužnica (Art. 95(7)), no language exam (Art. 92.a(8)), and no unemployment or change-of-employer rules (Art. 103.a(15), 103.d(21)). The housing duty still applies (Art. 104(5)(3), (9)-(10)).
- **Renewal** with the same employer, worker and occupation needs no LMT (Art. 97(7), 98(1)).
- **Shortage occupations** on the HZZ Governing Board list need no LMT (Art. 101), but the worker may work only in the PUs where the occupation is in shortage (Art. 88(10)-(11)).
- **Art. 110 categories** (32 of them) need no LMT and no HZZ opinion. They include key personnel, majority owners (51%), blue card, crew on vessels, and the earthquake and energy-renovation works.

**Language-exam exemptions** (from 4 Jun 2027): seasonal workers; people who finished primary, secondary or higher education in Croatia; and speakers of a "South Slavic subgroup" language (ZoS Art. 92.a(7)-(8)). That probably exempts the two largest origin groups: Bosnia and Herzegovina (17,330 permits in H1 2026) and Serbia (15,863). It does not exempt Nepal, the Philippines or India ([MUP statistics, June 2026](https://mup.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/2026/7/Mjesecne%20statistike%20LIPANJ%202026.pdf)). Whether Macedonian and Bulgarian speakers count is (unverified).

## Regional differences

The law is national. There are no county or city versions of these duties. Geography still matters in five ways:

1. **Police-area limit.** An LMT permit is valid only in the PU(s) where the test was run, and a shortage-occupation permit only in PUs where the job is in shortage (ZoS Art. 88(10)-(17)). There are **20 PUs**: Zagreb, Istria, Split-Dalmatia, Primorje-Gorski Kotar, Zadar, Šibenik-Knin, Dubrovnik-Neretva, Krapina-Zagorje, Varaždin, Koprivnica-Križevci, Sisak-Moslavina, Bjelovar-Bilogora, Virovitica-Podravina, Požega-Slavonia, Vukovar-Srijem, Osijek-Baranja, Brod-Posavina, Karlovac, Međimurje and Lika-Senj (MUP guide e-mail list). Agencies and builders moving crews between counties are most exposed.
2. **Shortage list.** HZZ's Governing Board publishes the list of occupations exempt from the LMT as a DOCX on the HZZ site. The current file is dated 2023, uploaded October 2024, with a separate "exceptions" file from 25 Sep 2024 ([HZZ](https://hzz.hr/usluge/radne-dozvole-za-zaposljavanje-stranaca-i-test-trzista-rada/)). The law requires it to be by area (Art. 88(10), 101), but whether the file lists PUs is (unverified).
3. **Earthquake and energy-renovation permits** (Art. 110(32)) are valid only for works in Zagreb city and the Krapina-Zagorje, Zagreb, Sisak-Moslavina and Karlovac counties, or for EU-co-financed energy renovation projects (ZoS Art. 88(13)-(14), 110(32)).
4. **Competent office.** Filing and notices go to the PU or PP of the worker's (intended) residence (ZoS Art. 90(1), 91(1), 92(1)). Coastal PUs have their own seasonal sub-offices (Poreč, Rovinj, Umag, Makarska, Hvar, Brač, Krk, Mali Lošinj and others) (MUP guide). Processing times vary by office (Novi list reports; unverified per office).
5. **Housing.** Co-owner consent depends on the building (4+ units). Camp housing applies only in tourism zones (PB Art. 47(1), (3)). Daily cross-border commuters from a neighbouring country need no Croatian address (ZoS Art. 178(6)).

Volume by PU (H1 2026): Zagreb 21,092; Istria 15,739; Split-Dalmatia 12,271; Primorje-Gorski Kotar 9,799; Dubrovnik-Neretva 8,058 ([MUP June 2026](https://mup.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/2026/7/Mjesecne%20statistike%20LIPANJ%202026.pdf)).

## PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS

Each requirement is testable. "Basis" gives the legal source, using the short names above (ZoS, PB, PER, ZR, ZSNR, MUP guide). Dates and amounts must come from a parameter table (R5), not code.

**A. Set-up, scope and rule engine**

1. **Employer profile.** Store: legal form (company, obrt or other natural person, family farm, association); OIB; seat and its PU; registered activities with a flag for "actively carried out"; and the count of Croatian, EEA and Swiss staff who are full-time and open-ended. For agencies, also store the Ministry of Labour register number. Test: the agency module cannot be switched on without a register number. Basis: ZoS Art. 3(10), (32), 99(1)(2), (4); ZR Art. 44(3), 52(4).
2. **Worker classification wizard.** Citizenship plus status puts each worker in exactly one regime:
   - EEA or Swiss (out of scope);
   - no-permit TCN (Art. 89(1), 16 categories);
   - HZZ-route permit (Art. 97);
   - seasonal (up to 90 days, up to 9 months, or multi-season up to 3 years);
   - Art. 110 point 1-32;
   - work registration certificate (30 or 90 days).

   Test: a TCN with permanent residence gets only the 8-day notice task and no permit tasks. Basis: ZoS Art. 3(3), 88(1), 89, 97, 104, 110, 139-141.
3. **Duty matrix by regime.** The regime switches duties on and off as the law says. For example, seasonal up to 90 days means no LMT, no HZZ opinion, no zadužnica, no language exam, no unemployment clock and no change-of-employer flow, but the housing duty stays. Test: changing a worker from Art. 97 to seasonal-90 removes the zadužnica and exam tasks and keeps the housing tasks. Basis: ZoS Art. 92.a(8), 95(7), 97(7), 103.a(15), 103.d(21), 104(1)-(2), (5), (9).
4. **Rules versioned by date.** Every deadline, amount and threshold is stored with its article, its source URL and effective-from/to dates. The engine picks the rule in force on the event date. Permits issued under the old law keep their validity, and procedures already started finish under the old law. Test: a contract end before the change uses the old notice rule; one on 1 Jun 2026 or later uses 5 working days. Basis: NN 55/26 Art. 67-68, 70; ZoS Art. 95(1).
5. **Parameter table, editable without a code release.** Holds: the previous year's average gross wage (zadužnica, key-personnel pay, Art. 113); the median gross wage (back pay); 100,000 / 40,000 EUR inflows; 26,544.56 EUR capital; 3,981.68 EUR closure buy-out; 2,650 / 6,630 / 8,000 EUR undeclared-work amounts; and all fine ranges. Basis: ZoS Art. 95(2), 99(1)(7), 113, 240(3), 247-251; ZSNR Art. 7, 10.
6. **Working-day calendar.** Croatian public holidays and weekends are used for "working days" deadlines. Calendar days are used everywhere else. Test: 5 working days from an event on Friday 18 Dec 2026 ends on Monday 28 Dec 2026, skipping 25-26 Dec and the weekends. Basis: ZoS Art. 95(1) ("pet radnih dana"); other articles in plain days.

**B. Eligibility checks before filing**

7. **HZZ-opinion pre-check.** A checklist of every Art. 99(1) condition. Each condition has a pass/fail value, an evidence field and an evidence date:
   - active registered activity linked to the occupation;
   - tax-debt certificate and all tax reports filed;
   - 1+ Croatian/EEA/Swiss worker continuously for 12 months (previous season for a seasonal obrt);
   - no criminal conviction (labour, social security, general safety);
   - no final fine for the "most serious" labour offences, or for social-security or safety-at-work offences;
   - inflows of at least 100,000 EUR (legal person) or 40,000 EUR (natural person) over the 12 months counted from the month before filing;
   - no account block over 30 days in a row in the last 6 months;
   - not on the undeclared-work list.

   Test: a natural person with 39,999 EUR of inflows fails. Basis: ZoS Art. 99(1)-(5), (11)-(12).
8. **Ratio calculator.** Shows how many more TCNs the employer can take: Croatian/EEA/Swiss full-time staff must be at least 20% of TCN staff (10% for shortage occupations), counting opinions already issued. Test: 10 qualifying staff and 45 TCNs give 5 more at 20%, or 55 more at 10%. Basis: ZoS Art. 98(6), 99(11)-(14).
9. **Application cap counter.** Counts HZZ-route applications against the 50 or 250 cap and warns at 80%. The counting method is a setting, because the law's wording is unclear. Basis: ZoS Art. 100.a.
10. **LMT tracker.** Records the request date, occupation and PU(s). Shows HZZ's 15-day answer date and the 90-day filing window from the day the result is received. Warns against a repeat request within 90 days for an occupation where HZZ found available workers. Test: day 91 after the result shows "LMT expired; new test needed". Basis: ZoS Art. 98(5)-(8).
11. **Blacklist status.** Stores whether the employer (or an agency's user) is on the ministry's undeclared-work list, with the date checked and a monthly re-check task. If listed, it shows "permits may be refused or cancelled" and the removal routes: pay the amount (removed within 3 days unless it is the third finding), or automatic removal 1 year after the decision. Basis: ZSNR Art. 12-12.a (NN 69/26); ZoS Art. 93(1)(8), 94(1)(9), 99(1)(9).
12. **Offence log.** Records final misdemeanour and criminal sanctions with their category. It flags those that block permits: "most serious" labour offences under ZR Art. 229, social-security or safety offences, two housing fines, and unpaid fines. Basis: ZoS Art. 93(1)(4), (6), (13), 99(1)(5)-(6); ZR Art. 229.

**C. Application file builder**

13. **Document checklist by regime and by first application or extension.** Includes:
    - Form 2a (or 4a for a blue card), marked "must be signed by hand";
    - passport copy, with a validity check;
    - employment contract signed by both parties, with a start date that depends on the permit;
    - proof of no criminal conviction (first application only);
    - qualification or experience proof matching the LMT, with a translation into Croatian, a Latin-script Slavic language or English;
    - recognition decision for regulated professions;
    - housing proof and Form 17a where the employer houses the worker;
    - Form 18a (max 90 days old) for a visa-regime first application.

    For extensions: 6 payslips; proof of health insurance; a health exam (max 90 days old) for visa-regime workers in Croatia under 3 years; the A1.1 certificate from 4 Jun 2027.

    Test: an extension for a Nepali worker on an Art. 97 permit filed after 4 Jun 2027, with more than 1 year of stay, cannot be marked "ready" without the A1.1 certificate. Basis: ZoS Art. 92.a(6), 97(3)-(6), 104(5), 111(1)-(2); PB Art. 8(2)-(4), 46, 46.a, 50; MUP guide items 2-4; [HZZ page](https://hzz.hr/usluge/radne-dozvole-za-zaposljavanje-stranaca-i-test-trzista-rada/).
14. **Visa-regime flag** taken from the worker's citizenship (EU visa list). It drives Form 18a, the health exam on extension and the zadužnica. Test: a Serbian citizen (visa-free) gets no Form 18a task; a Nepali citizen does. Basis: PB Art. 8(2)-(3); ZoS Art. 95(2); [Regulation (EU) 2018/1806](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2018/1806/oj).
15. **Contract and pay checks.** Warn if pay is below the reference wage the user enters for the occupation or collective agreement. Warn if a rent deduction from pay is in the contract, or if rent is above 30% of net pay. Basis: ZoS Art. 97(8)-(9), 98(3), 103.f(3)(1); PB Art. 49(1).
16. **E-mail filing pack.** Builds one PDF bundle of at most 20 MB and picks the right PU/PP e-mail address from the worker's intended residence, using a versioned table of the 42 addresses. Test: a 25 MB bundle triggers a compress-or-split step. Basis: MUP guide items 1, 5, 6; ZoS Art. 90(1).
17. **Filing log and decision clocks.** Stores the send date, channel, address and acknowledgement. Shows the 5-working-day window in which police may ask for missing items. Shows the decision deadline for each route:
    - HZZ route: 90 days, plus 30 in exceptional cases;
    - extension: before expiry;
    - seasonal extension: 3 days;
    - change of employer: 45 days;
    - HZZ opinion on a change of user, job or employer, or on additional work: 5 days.

    Test: an Art. 97 filing on 1 Oct 2026 shows "decision due by 30 Dec 2026". Basis: ZoS Art. 91(8)-(9), 92(2), 100(5), 103.c(4), 103.d(5), (11), 103.e(4), 106(6); MUP guide item 9.
18. **Certificate capture.** On upload of the police certificate (received in e-Građani), the user records: employer or agency, user, occupation, workplace, validity, LMT PUs, shortage PUs, legal basis and (for multi-season permits) work periods. These fields then drive R35-R39. Basis: ZoS Art. 91(3)-(5), 96(2), 104(14).

**D. Deadline and notice engine**

19. **Outcome notice to the worker.** Due 3 days after a decision or HZZ opinion. Generates a written notice in Croatian plus English or the worker's language, and stores the signed receipt. Basis: ZoS Art. 88(19); fine Art. 251(4)(1).
20. **Zadužnica.** For a visa-regime worker on an Art. 97 permit, receipt of the certificate creates a task due in 5 days. The amount is the parameter "average gross wage, previous year". The product tracks two triggers: the worker does not start on the contract date, or the employer dismisses within 3 months for a reason other than misconduct or summary dismissal. Either trigger creates an 8-day payment task. Test: a business-reason dismissal at month 2 creates the payment task; a misconduct dismissal does not. Basis: ZoS Art. 95(2)-(3), (7), 93(1)(15), 94(1)(13).
21. **Start-of-work gate.** A worker cannot be marked "may start" until: (a) the certificate has been delivered to the employer, or the biometric-card request has been filed; and (b) the pension and health insurance registration date is on or before the first day of work. A seasonal-90 worker may start from the date the certificate is delivered. Test: the first day of work set before the insurance registration date is rejected. Basis: ZoS Art. 91(6)-(7); ZSNR Art. 3(2)(1).
22. **Copies to the worker.** Tasks: give a copy of the contract before work starts, and a copy of the insurance registration within 8 days after the registration deadline. Basis: ZR Art. 14(5).
23. **Extension clock.** Due date = expiry minus 30 days (seasonal: minus 15). Alerts at 60, 45 and 30 days (configurable), then escalate. Once filed before expiry, the worker shows as "may stay and work until the decision" and "not undeclared work". Test: a permit expiring on 30 Nov 2026 shows a deadline of 31 Oct 2026. Basis: ZoS Art. 92(1), (5)-(6), 106(5); ZSNR Art. 3(4); fines ZoS Art. 251(4)(2), (9).
24. **End-of-contract and lost-condition notice.** These events create a notice due in **5 working days**: termination, end of a fixed term, the worker leaving, death, the employer losing an Art. 99 condition, the end of an assignment, and the loss of housing. The product generates the notice to the PU of the worker's residence and stores proof of sending. Test: a termination on Friday 2 Oct 2026 shows "due Friday 9 Oct 2026" (counting from the next working day; that counting rule is unverified). Basis: ZoS Art. 95(1); fine Art. 251(1)(1).
25. **Agency user changes.** A new assignment contract creates a request due in 3 days. The end of an assignment starts a 30-day countdown, with the warning "permit will be cancelled". A user in a different occupation is blocked until a change of occupation is approved. Basis: ZoS Art. 100(4), (10), (12), 94(1)(12); fine Art. 251(4)(3).
26. **Change of occupation.** Requires an LMT record, then a request within 3 days of the new contract. The worker's job field in the register cannot change until the positive HZZ opinion date is entered. Basis: ZoS Art. 103.c; fine Art. 251(4)(4).
27. **Hiring a worker who holds another employer's permit.** The wizard checks:
    - 6 months worked, if the worker entered on that permit (or a recorded exception);
    - no extension pending, and not in the last 30 days of validity;
    - the worker's remaining allowed unemployment (3 or 6 months, plus 3 if exploited).

    It then creates the 3-day notice. The start date cannot be before the positive HZZ opinion. Test: a worker with 5 months' service and no exception is blocked. Basis: ZoS Art. 103.a(1)-(2), (11)-(12), 103.b, 103.d(1)-(2), (4)-(6), (17)-(18); fine Art. 251(4)(5).
28. **Additional work for a second employer.** Allowed only if the first job is full-time (40 h). The request is due in 3 days. If the first job ends, a stop task is created. Basis: ZoS Art. 103.e; fine Art. 251(4)(6).
29. **Housing change notice.** Due in 15 days (HZZ route) or 8 days (seasonal), with a pre-filled Form 17a. Basis: ZoS Art. 103.f(2), 104(9); PB Art. 50; fine Art. 251(4)(7)-(8).
30. **Moving or posting a worker.** Sending a worker to work away from their registered residence creates an address-registration task (15 days). Posting to an EEA state or Switzerland creates a notice task (8 days). Basis: ZoS Art. 178(1), (3), (5); fines Art. 251(4)(15)-(16).
31. **No-permit TCN hire.** Creates an 8-day notice to the PU or PP of the worker's residence. Basis: ZoS Art. 89(4).
32. **Short jobs.** For work registration certificate categories, the request must be filed before day 1. Days used are counted against the 30- or 90-day yearly limit. Basis: ZoS Art. 139-143.
33. **TCN director module.** The company profile lists directors and their citizenship. If all directors are TCNs, entering or changing one creates a 30-day key-personnel application task. It checks the Art. 113 conditions: capital over 26,544.56 EUR; 3 Croatian full-time open-ended staff paid at least the average gross wage; director's gross pay at least 1.5x the average. While the task is open, all HZZ-route files show "blocked". Basis: ZoS Art. 93(1)(17), 111(5)-(8), 113(1)-(2), (4).
34. **Language exam (from 4 Jun 2027).** For Art. 97 and Art. 110(32) workers, the product computes the 1-year date. It applies the exemptions: seasonal; schooled in Croatia; South Slavic language (a setting, until the rulebook defines it). It creates a "book exam" task before the extension window, records the provider and cost as an employer expense, and files the certificate in the worker register. Test: a Filipino worker on an Art. 97 permit since 1 Mar 2026, whose extension is due by 1 Aug 2027, gets an exam task. Basis: ZoS Art. 92.a; NN 55/26 Art. 70.
35. **Worker-side reminders.** Optional messages to the worker in their language: biometric card (8 or 30 days), address change (15 days), unemployment report to HZZ (5 days), returning the card after seasonal work (8 days). Basis: ZoS Art. 103.a(4), 104(19), 169(1), 178(1).

**E. Placement guard (police area, job, employer)**

36. **Site-to-PU mapping.** Every work site has an address that maps to one of the 20 PUs, using a versioned municipality-to-PU table. Basis: ZoS Art. 91(5); MUP guide list.
37. **Placement block.** Assigning a worker to a site in a PU not on their certificate, to a different occupation, or to a different employer or user is blocked. An override needs a reason and is logged. Test: a worker with an LMT for PU Zagreb assigned to a site in Split is blocked. Basis: ZoS Art. 88(3)-(4), (6)-(7), (10)-(18); fines Art. 247(2), (6); cancellation Art. 94(1)(4)-(5).
38. **LMT planning.** When an employer plans sites in several PUs, the product lists the PUs to name in the LMT, or in a later extra-PU LMT. Basis: ZoS Art. 98(8).
39. **Special permits.** Earthquake and energy-renovation workers can be placed only in the listed PUs and project types. Multi-season seasonal workers can be scheduled only inside their certificate's work periods. A shift of up to 30 days needs a new certificate. Basis: ZoS Art. 88(13)-(14), 104(12)-(17), 110(32).

**F. Housing**

40. **Housing unit record.** Stores: type (flat in a multi-unit building; building with 1-2 units; tourist building; camp; employer building with a use permit; temporary site building); whether the address is in the spatial-units register; floor area; number of units in the building; toilets; heating (or "summer only"); and occupants with sex and family links. Basis: PB Art. 47-48; ZoS Art. 179(3).
41. **Capacity calculator.** Allowed occupants = 1 + floor((area - 14) / 6). Cap at 8 for a flat, and at 10 per 150 m² for a 1-2 unit building. Camps are capped at 3% of beds, seasonal only, until 30 Apr 2027. Test: 56 m² gives 8, 50 m² gives 7, and a 120 m² flat still gives 8. Basis: PB Art. 47(1), 48(4)-(6), 39(3).
42. **Consent and layout flags.** Co-owner consent is required if the building has 4+ units and a flat has more than 4 unrelated adults. Separate sleeping rooms are required for men and women unless they are family. One toilet per flat, or per 150 m². Basis: PB Art. 47(3), 48(3), (7)-(8), 77(2).
43. **Rent and lease.** Rent must be at most 30% of net pay and never deducted automatically. The product generates a lease, or a statement of terms if housing is free, and a pre-filled Form 17a. Basis: ZoS Art. 103.f(3); PB Art. 49-50.
44. **Grace period.** Workers registered at the same address before 30 Apr 2026 are marked "housing rules from 30 Apr 2027", with an alert 60 days before. Basis: PB Art. 39(1)-(2).
45. **Seasonal notice.** Housing seasonal workers in a tourist building generates a notice to the local government and the PU (number of workers, address, duration) before they move in. Basis: PB Art. 47(2).
46. **Housing fine counter.** Shows a warning after one housing fine, because a second fine bars future permits. Basis: ZoS Art. 93(1)(13), 251(1)(2)-(3).

**G. Worker register, inspection pack and retention**

47. **Statutory register.** All 13 PER Art. 3(1) fields for every worker. Field 7 holds the permit or certificate details: type, number, dates, employer, occupation and PUs. From 4 Jun 2027 it also holds the A1.1 proof. Test: the register export has 13 non-empty fields for a complete worker. Basis: ZR Art. 5; PER Art. 3(1); ZoS Art. 92.a(4).
48. **Written overview** per worker, printable on the employer's own template. Basis: PER Art. 4.
49. **Agency workers** appear in the user's register, without fields 11 and 13. Basis: PER Art. 2(3), 3(3).
50. **Source documents** are linked to each field, using the PER Art. 8 document types (contract, insurance forms, qualifications, wage records, assignment contract, referral note). Basis: PER Art. 3(4), 8.
51. **Retention engine:**
    - written overview: to the end of the year employment ends;
    - contracts, agreements, insurance forms and qualifications: 6 years after the end of that year;
    - pension documents: 40 years;
    - health-insurance documents: 6 years from the year they were made;
    - other documents: 6 years.

    A legal-hold flag stops deletion during a dispute. At the end, a deletion workflow runs with a log. Basis: PER Art. 9; GDPR Art. 5(1)(e).
52. **Authority access log.** Every export for an authority records the date, purpose and the person or body. Basis: PER Art. 5(5)-(6).
53. **Change log.** Each change records the date the worker reported it (the worker must report within 8 days). Basis: PER Art. 5(2).
54. **Inspection pack.** One click produces a PDF or ZIP per worker and per site: the register entry, certificate, contract, insurance registration, recent payslips, time records, housing proof and Form 17a, and every notice with its proof of sending. Basis: ZR Art. 5(3); ZoS Art. 242; PER Art. 4-5.
55. **Immutable event dates.** Contract-end, notice and filing dates cannot be overwritten. Edits keep the history with user and time. Basis: ZoS Art. 95(1) (the deadline runs from the event date); Varaždin decisions above.
56. **Time records.** Store or import monthly time records. If the employer's activity is on the JEER list (a setting), show the real-time JEER duty. Basis: PER Art. 13-18; ZSNR Art. 14, 21.

**H. Agencies and accountants (multi-client)**

57. **Multi-client accounts** with strict separation of each client's data and roles per client. Basis: ZoS Art. 100; [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) Art. 28, 32.
58. **Per-user checks for agencies.** Each user company gets its own Art. 99 check, blacklist status and Art. 93 check. Assignment contracts and referral notes are stored per assignment. Basis: ZoS Art. 100(2)-(3), 103.d(16); PER Art. 8(2).

**I. Ongoing employer health**

59. **Monthly employer check.** Tasks to confirm tax-debt status, account-block days, on-time wages and contributions, blacklist status and active registered activity. A "no" answer flags that the permits are at risk and offers the 5-working-day notice. Basis: ZoS Art. 94(1)(3), (6)-(7), 95(1), 102.
60. **Fine exposure dashboard.** Each overdue or open task shows its fine range (legal person, responsible person, natural person), multiplied per worker where the law says "per worker". Basis: ZoS Art. 240, 247, 251; ZR Art. 229; ZSNR Art. 7.

**J. Language, legal content and data protection**

61. **Language.** Croatian UI and documents for employers. Worker-facing notices and leaflets in Croatian, English and the main origin languages (Nepali, Hindi, Filipino and others; a setting). Basis: ZoS Art. 88(19); PB Art. 51(3) (police give seasonal workers information in Croatian and English).
62. **Legal content process.** Every rule shows its citation and "last checked" date. A public changelog lists rule updates. Target: rules updated within 10 working days of publication in Narodne novine. Basis: NN 55/26 Art. 69 and NN 69/26 Art. 15 (rulebooks due through Dec 2026).
63. **Special data.** Health certificates (Form 18a, health exams) and criminal-record certificates are stored encrypted, visible only to named roles, and used only for the permit file. Basis: GDPR Art. 9(2)(b), 10, 32; PER Art. 5(3).
64. **Processor terms.** A data-processing agreement is signed with every customer. Hosting is in the EU, with a list of sub-processors. Basis: GDPR Art. 28, 44.
65. **No filing claims.** The product prepares files and reminders. The user submits them to HZZ, MUP and e-Građani. The UI and terms say it is not legal advice. Basis: no public filing API found (Filing channels section).
66. **Exports.** CSV/XLSX import and export of the worker register, so data can come in from spreadsheets and payroll. Basis: PER Art. 2(2) (paper or electronic).
67. **Shortage and PU tables.** A versioned copy of HZZ's shortage-occupation list with its source date, and the PU and municipality tables. An alert fires when HZZ publishes a new file. Basis: ZoS Art. 101(3)-(4).
68. **Audit trail of acknowledgements.** For each worker notice (R19, R22) and each notice to police (R24-R31), store the generated document, the sending method and the proof. Test: the inspection pack lists every notice with its proof. Basis: ZoS Art. 88(19), 95(1), 103.f(2), 178(5); ZR Art. 14(5).

## Open questions

1. **Notice channel and form.** The law sets no form or channel for the Art. 95(1) end-of-contract notice, the Art. 103.f(2) housing notice, the Art. 178(5) posting notice or the Art. 89(4) no-permit notice. Do PUs accept these at the `radna.dozvola.*@mup.hr` addresses? Ask 2-3 PUs (Zagreb, Istria, Split).
2. **5 working days: from when, and how counted.** Article 95 shows amendments by NN 40/25 and NN 55/26. A search summary of a 2025 court notice (no longer online) applied "5 working days from the day after termination" to a contract that ended on 28 Apr 2025. That suggests the rule dates from the March 2025 amendment (unverified). Check the NN 40/25 text.
3. **Application cap (Art. 100.a).** Is "ukupno 50/250 zahtjeva" a lifetime, yearly or concurrent count? When does it stop applying?
4. **Shortage list by PU.** Does HZZ's current list name PUs, as Art. 88(10) implies? Is the 2023 file still current?
5. **Language exam.** The exam rulebook (method, approved providers and cost) is due by about 4 Dec 2026. Which languages count as the "South Slavic subgroup" (Macedonian? Bulgarian?)?
6. **Residence rulebook alignment** (due about 4 Dec 2026). Will it add new forms for notices or change Form 17a and Form 18a? No draft was found on e-Savjetovanja.
7. **JEER scope.** Which activities will the rulebook due about 7 Oct 2026 add? Construction is named in the bill's rationale ([PZE 307](https://www.sabor.hr/sites/default/files/uploads/sabor/2026-05-28/144702/PZE_307.pdf), p. 3).
8. **Fees.** Current permit and card fees. The 74.32 EUR + 31.85 EUR figures come from the [EU Immigration Portal](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/employed-worker-croatia_en) and were not checked against the fee tariff (unverified).
9. **Fines for no-permit notice and TCN directors.** No specific fine was found for missing the Art. 89(4) notice or the Art. 111(5) 30-day filing. Which article would a court apply?
10. **Average gross wage used by MUP in 2026** for the zadužnica (the 2025 annual figure). The Jan-Aug 2025 average was 1,993 EUR (search summary of DZS).
11. **Address-change deadline for seasonal workers.** 3 days (PB Art. 51 information sheet) or 15 days (ZoS Art. 178(1))?
12. **Zadužnica form.** Must it be solemnised by a notary? When is it returned?
13. **Status tracking.** Will HZZ or MUP add status tracking or an API? It is not on the HZZ page today.
14. **Misdemeanour record.** How long does a final "most serious" labour fine block the HZZ opinion (Art. 99(1)(6))? The law sets no period.
