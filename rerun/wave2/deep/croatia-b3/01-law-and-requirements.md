### Working notes (verified from primary text; will be folded into sections)

- ZoS Art. 95(1): employer must notify police within **5 working days** when the contract ends or another permit condition lapses. Fine Art. 251(1): legal person 1,500-3,000 EUR; responsible person 500-1,000 EUR (251(2)); natural-person employer 700-1,500 EUR (251(3)).
- ZoS Art. 95(2)-(3): visa-regime worker on an HZZ-opinion permit (Art. 97): employer must lodge a promissory note (zadužnica) for one average monthly gross wage within 5 days of receiving the permit certificate; pay that amount within 8 days if the worker does not start on time, or if the employer dismisses within 3 months (except for misconduct). Not for seasonal workers. Non-delivery of zadužnica = ground to cancel permit (Art. 94(1)(13)); non-payment = ground to refuse future permits (Art. 93(1)(15)).
- ZoS Art. 92(1): extension request at least 30 days before expiry, same employer/occupation. Fine Art. 251(4)(2): 500-1,500 EUR (legal person), 50-400 EUR responsible person, 100-700 EUR natural person (251(6)).
- ZoS Art. 106(5): seasonal extension at least 15 days before expiry. Fine 251(4)(9).
- ZoS Art. 92.a (NN 55/26): Croatian language and Latin script exam (A1.1) after 1 year on an Art. 97 or Art. 110(32) permit; employer pays; proof is part of the statutory worker register; must be attached to extension. Paras (1), (4), (6) in force 4 Jun 2027 (NN 55/26 Art. 70). Exempt: seasonal workers; those schooled in Croatia; speakers of a South Slavic language.
- ZoS Art. 88(10)-(17): worker on a labour-market-test permit may work only in the police administration (PU) where the test was done; shortage occupation only in PUs where it is a shortage occupation. Art. 91(5): the permit certificate lists these PUs. Art. 98(8): employer can ask HZZ to test more PUs (before or after the permit).
- ZoS Art. 88(19): employer must tell the worker in writing the outcome of the permit application / HZZ opinion within 3 days. Fine 251(4)(1).
- ZoS Art. 88(8)-(9): before hiring, employer must inspect the worker's valid residence authorisation; employer not liable if the document was forged unless it knew.
- ZoS Art. 88(18): a permit holder may work in all of the same employer's business premises, subject to the PU limits.
- ZoS Art. 88(21): employer rules apply to subcontractors.
- ZoS Art. 89(4): workers who need no permit (family reunification, permanent residence etc.) - employer must notify police within 8 days of start of work. (No specific fine found in Art. 251; unverified.)
- ZoS Art. 91(6): worker may start on the certificate only if registered for health and pension insurance.
- ZoS Art. 91(8)-(9): decision within 90 days (incl. LMT), +30 days in exceptional cases.
- ZoS Art. 91(12): permit max 3 years (contract length); seasonal 90 days / 9 months / 3-year multi-season.
- ZoS Art. 91(13), 96(2): certificates and calls to employer delivered electronically to the employer's e-Građani inbox.
- ZoS Art. 93(1): refusal grounds incl. (3) employer not actively running registered business linked to the occupation; (8) on undeclared-work list; (13) fined twice for housing; (15) did not pay Art. 95(2) amount; (16) no language exam proof; (17) company whose only directors are third-country nationals without a key-personnel permit.
- ZoS Art. 94(1): cancellation grounds incl. (5) work outside permitted employer or PU; (6) wages/contributions not paid (checked in Tax Administration / Central Register of Insured Persons); (12) agency did not conclude new assignment contract within 30 days; (13) no zadužnica.
- ZoS Art. 97(3): attachments: employment contract; proof worker meets LMT requirements; proof of adequate housing if employer provides/brokers housing. 97(6): recognition of foreign qualification for regulated professions. 97(9): pay not below Croatian workers in same occupation.
- ZoS Art. 98: LMT requested from HZZ before the application (except seasonal <=90 days in agriculture, forestry, hospitality and tourism, renewals with same employer and occupation, shortage list, Art. 110 cases). HZZ answers in 15 days; employer then has 90 days to apply. HZZ refuses LMT if employer not actively running registered activity, or if in last 90 days HZZ found available workers.
- ZoS Art. 99(1): HZZ positive opinion conditions: active registered activity; no tax debt and all tax reports filed; at least one same Croatian/EEA worker full-time open-ended in last 12 months (seasonal craft: in previous season); no convictions; account inflows in last 12 months >= 100,000 EUR (legal person) or 40,000 EUR (natural person); account not blocked >30 days continuously in last 6 months; not on undeclared-work list. 99(11): Croatian/EEA full-time staff >= 20% of third-country staff (10% for shortage occupations).
- ZoS Art. 100.a: employers with up to 50 Croatian/EEA staff: max 50 Art. 97 applications in total; 51-250: max 250. Applies "until the conditions for Art. 99(11)-(12) apply" (unclear; unverified).
- ZoS Art. 100(4): temp agency: new user in same occupation - request to police within 3 days of the assignment contract; HZZ opinion within 5 days. 100(10): permit cancelled if no new assignment contract within 30 days of end of previous one.
- ZoS Art. 103.a: allowed unemployment 3 months (permit <2 years) or 6 months (>2 years); worker must report to HZZ within 5 days.
- ZoS Art. 103.b-103.d: change of employer allowed after 6 months of work for workers who came on that permit (earlier if employer abused); new employer runs LMT and notifies police within 3 days of the new contract; decision in 45 days; not allowed during extension or in last 30 days of validity.
- ZoS Art. 103.c: change of occupation with the same employer: LMT + request within 3 days of the new contract.
- ZoS Art. 103.e: additional work for a second employer (full-time 40h at first): second employer runs LMT and files within 3 days.
- ZoS Art. 103.f: adequate housing where employer provides or brokers it; notify police of any change in adequacy within 15 days; rent not excessive, not automatically deducted; written lease. Fine for inadequate housing 251(1)(2); late notice 251(4)(7).
- ZoS Art. 104(9): seasonal: change of housing notified within 8 days.
- ZoS Art. 111(5) (NN 55/26): company whose only director(s) are third-country nationals: that person (or at least one of them) must apply for a key-personnel permit within 30 days of court-register entry or change. 111(7)-(8): court register sends OIBs to MUP; MUP notifies inspection if no application. Art. 113(1): key-personnel permit needs capital > 26,544.56 EUR, 3 Croatian full-time open-ended staff paid at least average gross wage, director's gross pay >= 1.5x average gross wage. Art. 113(4): a registered representative cannot get an Art. 97 permit for the same company.
- ZoS Art. 178(3): if the employer sends the worker to work away from the registered residence, worker or employer must register the new address (15 days, 178(1)). 178(5): posting to another EEA state or Switzerland - notify police within 8 days. 178(7): address registration refused if more people already registered than the floor area allows.
- ZoS Art. 239: labour inspectors (Državni inspektorat) inspect work of third-country nationals and employer-provided housing; police inspect residence registration. Art. 240: 30-day closure of the premises, lifted by paying 3,981.68 EUR per worker within 3 days.
- ZoS Art. 247(1)-(8): illegal stay 10,000-20,000 EUR per worker (legal person); no permit / wrong job / wrong employer 5,000-15,000 EUR per worker; responsible person 2,000-6,000 EUR; natural-person employer 1,500-3,000 EUR per worker; same for user companies of temp agencies (247(6)).
- PB (NN 43/26) Art. 46, 46.a: housing proof = lease certified by notary or Tax Administration with rent and floor area, or employer-owner statement with floor area; must state number of people already living there. For first-entry permits only Form 17a at filing; full proof at residence registration.
- PB Art. 47-49: housing types (flat; small building 1-2 units; tourist building; camps max 3% of beds for seasonal staff, transitional to 30 Apr 2027; employer building with use permit for worker housing; temporary site buildings). 14 m² for first person + 6 m² per additional; max 8 per flat; max 10 per 150 m² in small building; separate sleeping by sex; co-owner consent if >4 unrelated adults in a flat in a building of 4+ units; rent max 30% of net pay, not auto-deducted; written lease or statement of terms (also when free).
- PB Art. 50: Form 17a = signed statement that housing meets Arts. 47-49 (no notarisation), with the application and on any change of adequate housing.
- PB Art. 8(2)-(4): visa-regime worker: Form 18a health and vaccination certificate (<=90 days old) with first application; health exam proof (<=90 days old) for extension if in Croatia < 3 years.
- PB Art. 39 (transitional): housing rules do not apply for one year (to about 30 Apr 2027) to workers who stay at the same registered address.
- PB Art. 51: seasonal worker info sheet: report housing change in 8 days; address change in 3 days (differs from ZoS Art. 178(1) 15 days - conflict to check).
