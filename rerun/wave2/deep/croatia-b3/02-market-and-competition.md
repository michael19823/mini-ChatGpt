## Buyer segments

Working notes (being turned into the table):
- 94,113 residence-and-work permits in H1 2026 (37,230 new, 36,601 renewals, 20,282 seasonal) ([MUP June 2026](https://mup.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/2026/7/Mjesecne%20statistike%20LIPANJ%202026.pdf)).
- Temporary-work agency register (my count, 10 Oct 2026): 715 entries; 699 with no adverse status, 8 bankrupt, 5 dormant, 3 in liquidation. 500 d.o.o., 186 j.d.o.o., 29 sole traders (obrt). Zagreb (all districts) about 320, Rijeka 40, Split 26, Varaždin 18, Osijek 17, Zadar 13 ([MRMS register "Agencije"](http://apzp.mrms.hr/Home/Agencije)).
- Ministry agency-work report 2025: 960 agencies in the app during 2025, 168 deleted during the year, 620 filed data, 334 of those assigned no worker at all. 18,527 agency workers, 25,717 assignments, 8,043 foreigners (43%), 98% of them third-country nationals ([Izvješće o agencijskom radu za 2025.](http://apzp.mrms.hr/IzvjescaDocs/2025.pdf), scanned PDF, pp. 3-4, 15).
- 2024 report: 815 agencies, 699 filed, 516 assigned nobody; 19,559 agency workers; 8,114 foreign (41%), 7,960 third-country ([2024 report](http://apzp.mrms.hr/IzvjescaDocs/2024.pdf)).
- Valid permits on the day (Feb 2025): 113,504 third-country workers with a valid permit and registered address ([Government presentation, Feb 2025, slide "Dozvole za boravak i rad u 2025."](https://vlada.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/Vijesti/2025/Veljaca/6_veljace/Izmjene_i_dopune_Zakona_o_strancima.pdf)). Same deck: 2024 permits by sector: construction 75,071; hospitality and tourism 56,228; industry 28,486; transport 16,149; trade 7,925. Ratio rule: at most 6 foreign workers per domestic full-time worker after a labour-market test, 12 for shortage jobs; firms with up to 50 staff may file up to 50 requests.
- Employer anchor: in Jan-Apr 2022, "over 7,000 companies" asked HZZ for permission to hire nearly 50,000 foreigners, about 7 per firm ([Croatia Week / Hina, 13 May 2022](https://www.croatiaweek.com/over-7000-companies-in-croatia-apply-for-permission-to-employ-50000-foreign-nationals/)).
- Employer anchor 2: 3,910 employers got an HZZ opinion in one early period of the 2021 model, 1,304 (35.6%) negative ([Novi list, undated, about 2021](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=532240)) (date unverified).
- HZZ reportedly gives negative opinions for workers from all Asian countries except the Philippines since July 2025 (search summary of [moj-posao.net](https://moj-posao.net/EN/Faq/Details/83324/Do-kraja-godine-cemo-vjerojatno-srusiti-rekord-po-broju-stranih-radnika/2) and Novi list) (unverified).
- CORRECTION to the B3 report: "41% of foreign workers work through agencies" is wrong. 41% is the share of foreigners among agency workers. Only about 8,000 third-country nationals were on agency contracts, i.e. under 10% of valid permits.

## Competitor table and discussion

Working notes:
- Moja Firma price page (fetched 10 Oct 2026): Free (5 staff), Pro 29.99 EUR/month (25 staff), Business 59.99 EUR/month (100 staff). Features: time records, holidays, leave, team calendar, employee document archive, "HR pregled i upozorenja", travel orders, invoices, AI assistant, even a firearms-trade register. No word on foreign workers or permits. "Online card payment is currently not available"; paid plans are sold by quote ([mojafirma.hr/cijene](https://mojafirma.hr/cijene)).
- PickJobs (pick.jobs): job board and app; announced agency profiles. No permit tracking. Sponsored article, 21 Nov 2023 ([index.hr](https://www.index.hr/clanak.aspx?id=2511093)).
- Searches in Croatian and English for permit-tracking software (5 searches) found only consultancies and agencies: Manpower, Dynamic Staffing Services, AtoZ Serwis Plus ([dss-hr.com](https://www.dss-hr.com/blog/?p=2929); [atozserwisplus.com](https://www.atozserwisplus.com/work-permit/croatia)).
