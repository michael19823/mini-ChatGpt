# Croatia B3: market size, buyers and competition (deep dive 02)

Date: 10 Oct 2026. Builds on [the B3 report](../reports/croatia-b3.md). Scope: buyer counts, buyer profile, willingness to pay, competitors, channels and regional expansion. Law, product, go-to-market and payments are in the other section files.

## Summary

- **The worker base is solid, but it is shrinking a little.**
  - 105,231 third-country nationals held a valid residence-and-work permit on 20 Apr 2026. This figure comes from the MUP state secretary ([Informator, 21 Apr 2026](https://informator.hr/vijesti/9467)).
  - MUP issued 106,395 permits from January to July 2026: 41,716 new, 41,388 renewals and 23,291 seasonal ([MUP, July 2026](https://mup.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/statistika/2026/8/Mjese%C4%8Dne%20statisike%20SRPANJ%202026.pdf)).
  - Yearly volume peaked at 206,529 in 2024. It fell to 170,723 in 2025 ([Government deck, Feb 2025](https://vlada.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/Vijesti/2025/Veljaca/6_veljace/Izmjene_i_dopune_Zakona_o_strancima.pdf); [Novi list, 17 Jan 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1610671)).
  - Renewals are now as many as new hires. That suits a tool that runs deadlines.
- **The agency segment is much smaller than the B3 report assumed. Two corrections:**
  - The B3 report said 41% of foreign workers work through agencies. That is wrong. 41-43% is the share of foreigners *among agency workers*. Only about 7,900 third-country nationals were on temporary-agency contracts in 2025 ([Ministry agency-work report 2025, p. 15](http://apzp.mrms.hr/IzvjescaDocs/2025.pdf)).
  - The register lists 715 temporary-work agencies today (my count of the [MRMS register](http://apzp.mrms.hr/Home/Agencije)). However, only 286 of the 620 agencies that reported for 2025 assigned any worker at all ([2025 report, pp. 3-4](http://apzp.mrms.hr/IzvjescaDocs/2025.pdf)).
  - Insiders say only about 50 of some 800 recruitment agencies for foreign workers are really active ([Novi list, 2 Sep 2025](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1523976)).
- **Direct employers are the main market.** No source publishes how many employers hold permits.
  - Over 7,000 firms asked HZZ for about 50,000 foreign workers in just January to April 2022 ([Croatia Week / Hina, 13 May 2022](https://www.croatiaweek.com/over-7000-companies-in-croatia-apply-for-permission-to-employ-50000-foreign-nationals/)).
  - My estimate is **10,000-20,000 employers** with at least one third-country worker, of which **about 3,000-6,000 have 10 or more** (unverified).
  - New rules filter out the smallest firms. To get an HZZ opinion, a firm needs at least 100,000 EUR inflow in 12 months and domestic staff equal to 10-20% of its foreign staff ([Novi list / Hina, 5 Feb 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1622670); [RRiF](https://www.rrif.hr/izmjene_zakona_o_strancima_stupaju_na_snagu_4_lipn-2521-vijest/)).
- **No Croatian software does this job.** I checked in 18 searches in Croatian, English and Slovene, plus the vendor sites.
  - Moja Firma is a Croatian HR app at 0, 29.99 and 59.99 EUR a month. It has a document archive and "HR warnings", but no permit features. It cannot take card payments online ([mojafirma.hr/cijene](https://mojafirma.hr/cijene)). It already uses a 2026 foreign-worker guide to market its general HR tool ([Moja Firma guide, 14 Feb 2026](https://mojafirma.hr/clanci/zaposljavanje-stranaca-radne-dozvole)). It is the most likely fast follower.
  - The closest real product is HRappka in Poland. It has a legalisation module with expiry reminders, but only for Polish law ([hrappka.pl](https://hrappka.pl/en/?p=61870)).
- **Willingness to pay is real but untested.**
  - Firms already pay about 106 EUR in state fees per permit ([EU Immigration Portal](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/employed-worker-croatia_en)) and 125 EUR for a half-day workshop ([Informator](https://informator.hr/seminari/novosti-u-zaposljavanju-i-radu-drzavljana-trecih-zemalja-primjeri-i-iskustva-iz-prakse)).
  - Fines for a late notice or a missed renewal run from 500 to 3,000 EUR. Work outside the permit costs 5,000-15,000 EUR per worker ([zakon.hr](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima), as cited in the B3 report).
  - Agency and lawyer fees are not published (5 searches).
- **Pain is documented.**
  - HOK says small employers "have neither the time nor the capacity" for the paperwork ([Virovitica.net, 1 Sep 2025](https://aem.hr/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2.-Tri-lica-hrvatskog-trzista-rada.pdf)).
  - An agency director says the state application "constantly crashes" ([Novi list, 2 Sep 2025](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1523976)).
  - Police announced more intensive site checks in April 2026 ([Novi list / Hina, 16 Apr 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1666962)).
- **Regional:**
  - Slovenia is the best second market. About 147,000 foreigners work there, about 15% of the employed, and I found no local tool (search summary of [RTV SLO](https://www.rtvslo.si/gospodarstvo/lani-v-dolocenih-mesecih-celo-okoli-milijon-delovno-aktivnih-stopnja-brezposelnosti-rekordno-nizko/732688); unverified).
  - Serbia has about 65,000-69,000 employed foreigners ([N1](https://n1info.rs/biznis/koliko-je-stranaca-zaposleno-u-srbiji/)). Montenegro has about 24,000 permit holders ([N1](https://n1info.rs/region/u-crnoj-gori-ubuduce-boravak-samo-za-strance-sa-parama/)).
  - Both have different laws and are later options.

**How the numbers fit together.**
- About 105,000 workers spread over 10,000-20,000 employers gives 5-10 workers per employer on average.
- The agencies hold only about 8,000 of these workers (under 8%). The rest are hired directly.
- Most permits are in four coastal police districts and Zagreb County. The coast is seasonal. Zagreb County is mostly renewals (construction and industry).

**Market direction.**
- Volume is falling slowly. The first nine months of 2025 had 136,194 permits, against 158,837 a year earlier ([Novi list / Hina, 5 Feb 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1622670)). January to July 2026 is about 7% below the same period of 2025: 106,395 against 115,023 (my arithmetic; the 2025 figure is from a search summary of the [MUP July 2025 release](https://mup.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/statistika/2025/8/Mjese%C4%8Dne%20statisike%20SPRANJ%202025.pdf)).
- Sources say HZZ has given negative opinions for workers from Asian countries other than the Philippines since July 2025 (search summary of Novi list and moj-posao.net; unverified).
- Renewals now equal new hires. The renewal base holds up even when new hiring slows.
- Seasonal permits will be valid for three years for the same worker and employer instead of one ([Novi list / Hina, 5 Feb 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1622670); [Informator](https://informator.hr/vijesti/9467)). There will be fewer seasonal renewals, but the records must be kept longer.

## Buyer profile and pain

**Who they are.**
- Mostly small and medium firms in tourism, construction, industry, transport and trade (see the sector split above).
- Since June 2026, an employer needs all of the following to get an HZZ opinion ([Novi list / Hina, 5 Feb 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1622670); [RRiF](https://www.rrif.hr/izmjene_zakona_o_strancima_stupaju_na_snagu_4_lipn-2521-vijest/)):
  - at least 100,000 EUR inflow in 12 months (40,000 EUR for a natural person);
  - domestic staff equal to 10-20% of its foreign staff;
  - no account blocked for more than 30 days;
  - no place on the blacklist.
  So the buyer is a working business, not a one-person shell.
- Typical sizes (my reading, unverified):
  - a family hotel or camp with 5-30 seasonal workers;
  - a builder with 10-80 workers in one or two police districts;
  - a manufacturer or logistics firm with 20-200 workers;
  - an agency with 20-300 workers placed at many client firms.
- Agencies are small companies. 500 of the 715 registered are ordinary d.o.o. and 186 are j.d.o.o. They cluster in Zagreb ([MRMS register](http://apzp.mrms.hr/Home/Agencije), my count). The average active agency placed about 65 workers in 2025: 18,527 ÷ 286 (my arithmetic, [2025 report](http://apzp.mrms.hr/IzvjescaDocs/2025.pdf)).

**How they comply today.**
- Filing goes through the HZZ online application (labour-market test and opinion) and the police. Seasonal permits up to 90 days use the "Burza rada" app ([N1](https://n1info.hr/english/news/employers-can-apply-online-for-stay-and-work-permits-for-seasonal-workers)). The February 2026 bill moves the notice of a terminated contract to the employer, filed electronically through e-Građani ([Novi list / Hina, 5 Feb 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1622670)). The state therefore gives free filing channels but no tracking.
- Agencies and lawyers do much of the filing for small firms. Their commissions vary by the worker's skills and the working conditions, and agencies also pay each other commissions ([Novi list, 2 Sep 2025](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1523976)).
- Deadlines appear to be kept in spreadsheets or in the payroll and HR file (unverified; I found no product and no survey). The statutory worker register must hold the permit or the work-registration certificate as a field ([Pravilnik, NN 73/17](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2017_07_73_1773.html); now [NN 55/24](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2024_05_55_969.html)).
- Firms learn the rules from:
  - HGK county seminars with police and HZZ speakers ([HGK Varaždin, 7 Oct 2026](https://hgk.hr/documents/program-zakon-o-strancima7-10-266a859819dbcbf.pdf); [HGK Varaždin, 14 Apr 2025](https://www.hgk.hr/documents/program-novi-zakon-o-strancima-14-04-202567eb8643279d9.pdf));
  - paid workshops ([Informator](https://informator.hr/seminari/novosti-u-zaposljavanju-i-radu-drzavljana-trecih-zemalja-primjeri-i-iskustva-iz-prakse));
  - the HOK webinar "Obrtnički aktualac – Zakon o strancima" ([Virovitica.net](https://aem.hr/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2.-Tri-lica-hrvatskog-trzista-rada.pdf)).

**Software they already use.**
- Payroll or ERP from their accountant, often an outsourced bureau (unverified).
- A time-records app. Time records are mandatory, and apps like Moja Firma and DigRa ERV target small firms ([mojafirma.hr](https://mojafirma.hr/cijene); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6751578393)).
- None of these track permits as far as I found.

**Pain (documented).**
- *Paperwork is too much for small firms.* HOK says small employers "have neither the time nor the capacity to cope with all the bureaucratic demands". It also says legal deadlines "exist only on paper" and police decisions sometimes come when the season is almost over ([Virovitica.net, 1 Sep 2025](https://aem.hr/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2.-Tri-lica-hrvatskog-trzista-rada.pdf)). HOK asks for digital filing with status tracking ([Informator, 25 Aug 2025](https://informator.hr/vijesti/8627)).
- *The state application is unreliable.* An agency director: "The application linked to the new law constantly crashes, is full of errors and bugs". It also "locks" a worker so the workplace cannot be changed. Requests that took 7 days now take months ([Novi list, 2 Sep 2025](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1523976)).
- *Short notices are missed.*
  - Until the December 2025 change, agencies often deregistered workers two or three days after a permit expired and paid a fine of about 60 EUR ([Novi list, 17 Jan 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1610671)).
  - Varaždin's misdemeanour court decided a cluster of 2025 cases (PP-1387, 1388, 1390, 1412, 1414, 1415 and 1417/2025) on late notice of contract end. Each case turned on the date the contract ended ([zakon.hr case law](https://www.zakon.hr/c/sudska-praksa/654468/pp-1412-2025-4---opcinski-sud-u-varazdinu); [PP-1387/2025](https://www.zakon.hr/c/sudska-praksa/677882/pp-1387-2025-2---opcinski-sud-u-varazdinu)). A dated event log is exactly what decides these cases.
- *Enforcement is rising.*
  - Police announced continued "intensive checks" of permits and employers. One Koprivnica site check found work without a valid permit, work outside the permitted job, a permit not returned after expiry and an unregistered posting ([Novi list / Hina, 16 Apr 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1666962)).
  - Inspectors found 961 third-country nationals working illegally in 2025. They banned 263 employers for 30 days (DIRH 2025, as cited in the [B3 report](../reports/croatia-b3.md)).
- *The rules change often.* The law was amended in March 2025 and June 2026, and a new residence rulebook took effect in April 2026, with housing rules of 14 m² for the first worker and 6 m² for each extra worker, and rent of at most 30% of net pay ([Informator, 21 Apr 2026](https://informator.hr/vijesti/9467)). More rulebooks are due by about December 2026 (B3 report).
- *Foreign workers cost more than domestic ones.* HOK's president says housing, onboarding and administration make a foreign worker more expensive than a domestic one ([Virovitica.net](https://aem.hr/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2.-Tri-lica-hrvatskog-trzista-rada.pdf)). Admin time saved is therefore a real cost saved.
- *Forums and social groups:* not checked (out of search budget; unverified).

**Reading.**
- A firm with 10 foreign workers already pays about 1,100 EUR a year in state fees, assuming about one permit per worker per year (my arithmetic). It also pays agency commissions and staff time.
- One missed renewal costs at least 500 EUR. One worker found outside the permitted police district or job costs 5,000 EUR or more.
- A tool at 2-3 EUR per worker per month costs 240-360 EUR a year for 10 workers, which is less than one fine.
- Moja Firma sets the reference price for a small-firm HR tool: 30-60 EUR a month. A permit tool priced inside that band will feel normal.
- Nobody has tested this price with a buyer (unverified).

## Competitor table and discussion

"Duty list" covers these items:
1. a register of third-country workers with permit data;
2. expiry and renewal dates (seasonal: file 15 days before expiry);
3. notice of contract end;
4. a check that each worker stays in the permitted police district, job and employer;
5. housing rules;
6. the zadužnica within 5 days;
7. employer eligibility (inflow, domestic share, blacklist);
8. the board-member permit within 30 days;
9. the A1.1 language exam from June 2027;
10. posting notices;
11. an inspection-ready file.

**Discussion.**
- Nobody sells a tool that runs the Croatian duty list. My searches found consultancies, agencies and generic HR apps only. The same holds in Slovenia (1 search).
- The state covers filing for free but not the deadlines. That is the room for software: a dated register, a deadline engine, rule checks before filing (eligibility, housing, police district) and an inspection pack.
- The threat is a cheap feature, not a rival product. Moja Firma could add a "permit expiry" field and reminders in weeks. It would struggle to keep police-district, housing and eligibility rules current. It also cannot take card payments, which slows self-serve sales.
- Agencies are both buyers and resellers. There are fewer active ones than assumed: about 286 temporary-work agencies with assignments, plus perhaps 50-100 serious recruiters.

## Implications for positioning and pricing

1. **Sell to direct employers first, with agencies as a premium tier.**
   - Direct employers hold over 90% of the workers.
   - Agencies are fewer than assumed: about 286 temporary-work agencies with assignments and perhaps 50-100 serious recruiters. They have the sharpest multi-client pain, however (police-district lock, many workers).
2. **Position as "the deadline and inspection file for your foreign workers", not as HR software.**
   - Don't fight Moja Firma on time records.
   - Offer import and export (CSV) so the tool sits beside payroll and time apps.
   - Do the Croatian rules generic apps will not keep current: police district and job lock, housing size and rent rules, eligibility pre-check (100,000 EUR inflow, domestic share, blacklist), seasonal 15-day renewal, zadužnica within 5 days, board-member 30 days, A1.1 exam from June 2027, and a dated log for notices filed via e-Građani.
3. **Make renewals the core feature.** Renewals were 41,388 of 106,395 permits in 2026 so far ([MUP](https://mup.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/statistika/2026/8/Mjese%C4%8Dne%20statisike%20SRPANJ%202026.pdf)). They recur, and they are where missed dates and fines happen.
4. **Pricing (my proposal, untested):**
   - Free up to 3 workers (beats Moja Firma's free tier on the one thing it lacks).
   - Employer plan: **2.50 EUR per active foreign worker per month, minimum 19 EUR**. 10 workers cost 25 EUR, 40 workers 100 EUR. This sits inside the 30-60 EUR band buyers know from HR apps and below one 500 EUR fine.
   - Agency plan: **149 EUR per month up to 100 workers, then 1.50 EUR per worker**, with unlimited client employers and a per-client export.
   - Yearly prepay with 2 months free, paid by card. This matters because Moja Firma has no online card payment.
5. **Size check (my arithmetic, unverified):**
   - Theoretical ceiling: 105,000 workers × 2.50 EUR × 12 = about 3.2 million EUR a year if every worker were tracked.
   - Base case in year 3:
     - 4% of workers tracked, about 4,200, through about 300 employers: about 125,000 EUR;
     - plus 25 agencies at an average of 180 EUR a month: about 54,000 EUR;
     - total **about 180,000 EUR a year**.
   - Low case: about 60,000 EUR. High case: about 350,000 EUR.
   - This is close to the B3 base case, but the weight shifts from agencies to employers.
6. **Speed matters.** The window is the next 6-12 months, while rulebooks settle and before a generic HR app adds a permit field. Launch before the 2027 season hiring (January-March 2027). Use the A1.1 exam start (June 2027) as a second hook.
7. **Expansion path:** Croatia, then Slovenia (same currency, payments and sectors), then Serbia.
