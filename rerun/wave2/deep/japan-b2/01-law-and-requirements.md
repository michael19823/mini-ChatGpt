# Japan: 動物取扱業 ledger, records and annual report law, turned into product requirements

Status: IN PROGRESS (2026-10-10). Primary texts read in full from e-Gov XML on 2026-10-10. Remaining sections are being filled.

Short names used below:
- "Act" = 動物の愛護及び管理に関する法律 (Act No. 105 of 1973), current text on e-Gov, last amended by Act No. 30 of 5 June 2026 ([e-Gov Act](https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/348AC1000000105)).
- "Order" = 動物の愛護及び管理に関する法律施行令 (Cabinet Order No. 107 of 1975) ([e-Gov Order](https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/350CO0000000107)).
- "Rules" = 動物の愛護及び管理に関する法律施行規則 (MOE Ordinance No. 1 of 2006) ([e-Gov Rules](https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/418M60001000001)).
- "Standards" = 第一種動物取扱業者及び第二種動物取扱業者が取り扱う動物の管理の方法等の基準を定める省令 (MOE Ordinance No. 7 of 2021, the 飼養管理基準省令, "基準省令") ([e-Gov Standards](https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/503M60001000007)).
- MOE = Ministry of the Environment (環境省). "Prefecture" below means the prefectural governor or, in the 20 designated cities, the mayor (Act Art. 10(1)).

## Legal core found so far (working notes, will be folded into the sections)

- Ledger: Act Art. 21-5(1); Rules Art. 10-2 lists 13 fields; per animal for dogs and cats, per breed (品種等) for others (Rules 10-2(2)); keep 5 years from the date of entry (Rules 10-2(3)); electronic storage allowed (Rules 10-2(4)); try to file trade slips and post-mortem certificates (Rules 10-2(5)).
- Report: Act Art. 21-5(2); Rules Art. 10-3. Period 1 April to 31 March; due within 60 days after the period ends (so by 30 May); form 様式第十一の二; monthly totals for new animals and for sold/handed-over/dead; first period for a new registrant runs from registration date to 31 March.
- Who: 動物販売業者等 = registered sellers, renters, exhibitors and 譲受飼養業 (Act 21-5(1); Order Art. 2). Second-type (non-profit) handlers who rehome dogs/cats must keep the ledger but not report (Act 24-4(2); Rules 10-10).
- Penalties: 過料 up to ¥200,000 for no report / false report and for no ledger / no entry / false entry / not kept (Act Art. 49). Sign not displayed: 過料 up to ¥100,000 (Art. 50). Refusing a report request or inspection: fine up to ¥300,000 (Art. 47(3)). Breaking an order or suspension, or operating unregistered: up to ¥1,000,000 (Art. 46). Cancellation or up to 6 months' suspension for any breach of the Act or its ordinances (Art. 19(1)(6)).
