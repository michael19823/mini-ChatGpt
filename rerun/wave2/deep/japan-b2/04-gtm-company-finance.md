Notes so far (to be folded in):
- NTA pamphlet 国境を越えた役務の提供に係る消費税の課税関係について (Jul 2024, revised Jun 2026): cloud software use = 電気通信利用役務の提供. Self-serve web signup that cannot in practice block consumers = 消費者向け even if the site says "for businesses". So the foreign seller (not the buyer) is the JCT taxpayer, subject to the small-business exemption. Buyer input credit needs an 適格請求書 from the seller. The 80/70/50/30% transitional relief does NOT apply. The 少額特例 (purchases under ¥10,000 incl. tax, small businesses, to 30 Sep 2029) DOES apply. https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/0024003-087_01.pdf
- Platform taxation from 1 Apr 2025 for app-store sales by foreign businesses.
