Notes so far (to be folded in):
- NTA pamphlet 国境を越えた役務の提供に係る消費税の課税関係について (Jul 2024, revised Jun 2026): cloud software use = 電気通信利用役務の提供. Self-serve web signup that cannot in practice block consumers = 消費者向け even if the site says "for businesses". So the foreign seller (not the buyer) is the JCT taxpayer, subject to the small-business exemption. Buyer input credit needs an 適格請求書 from the seller. The 80/70/50/30% transitional relief does NOT apply. The 少額特例 (purchases under ¥10,000 incl. tax, small businesses, to 30 Sep 2029) DOES apply. https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/0024003-087_01.pdf
- Platform taxation from 1 Apr 2025 for app-store sales by foreign businesses.
- Withholding: SaaS = service fee, not royalty, so no 20.42% withholding (ayusawa-partners column, updated 5 Sep 2026) https://ayusawa-partners.jp/column/it-kenkyukaihatsu-zeigaku
- Foreign company registration: Company Act 817/818, continuous transactions need a Japan representative (one resident) and registration; 過料 up to ¥1M (976) or registration-tax equivalent (979). MOJ page https://www.moj.go.jp/MINJI/minji07_00275.html . MOJ+MIC asked 48 foreign IT firms to register in 2022 (bengo4 https://www.bengo4.com/c_23/n_14775/ ; Arab News https://www.arabnews.jp/en/business/article_78947).
- GK setup: Kaizen quote (2026) ¥400,000 service + ¥60,000 government + ¥10,000 misc = from ¥558,000 incl. ¥88,000 capital-receiving agent; tax filings ¥200,000; bank account support ¥420,000 (40% refund if refused); virtual office ¥15,500/month + ¥20,000 deposit; 4-5 weeks; recommends capital ¥5M+ for bank accounts; banks may refuse virtual-office companies. https://kaizencpa.com/download/jp/Japan%20Goudou%20Kaisha%20Registration%20Procedures%20and%20Fees(JP).PDF
- Official fees: KK 登録免許税 ¥150,000 min, 定款認証 ¥30,000-50,000 (¥15,000 conditional), paper stamp ¥40,000; GK ¥60,000 min, no notarisation; totals KK ~¥196,000 electronic, GK ~¥75,000+ electronic (sogyotecho) https://sogyotecho.jp/company_fee/
- 均等割 Tokyo 23 wards, capital ≤¥10M, ≤50 staff: ¥70,000/yr even with losses (freee kb, updated 23 Jun 2026) https://www.freee.co.jp/kb/kb-launch/kaisyasetsuritsu-costs/ ; freee 登記おまかせ ¥50,000 vs market ~¥100,000.
- Stripe: JCB acceptable on accounts in AU, CA, HK, JP, NZ, SG, CH, UK, US, EEA (not Iceland) https://docs.stripe.com/payments/cards/supported-card-brands ; Konbini only Japan-based accounts https://support.stripe.com/questions/enabling-konbini-payments-for-japan-based-stripe-accounts ; Ireland pricing: EEA 1.5%+€0.25, UK 2.5%+€0.25, international 3.15%+€0.25, +2% FX, Billing 0.7%, Tax 0.5%, Invoicing 0.4% https://stripe.com/ie/pricing
- Paddle: Japan 10% Consumption Tax B2B & B2C (help page, updated 1 Aug 2025) https://paddle.com/help/sell/tax/which-countries-does-paddle-charge-sales-tax-or-vat-for/
- Stripe Managed Payments (MoR): sellers in US, CA, EU countries, CH, GB, NO, LI, AU, HK, JP, SG; customers 195+ countries (Japan not restricted); SaaS eligible https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments/eligibility ; fee 3.5% on top (third-party: dodopayments)
