- CONFIRMED from primary text (old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2500037500): Order 268 of 27 Nov 2025, registered MoJ 28 Nov 2025 No. 37500; adds "item 8" to the qualification requirements (Order 473, reg. No. 30721) with rows 73-82; in force 1 Jan 2027 (para 4). Rows 79 and 81 and annexes 3, 5, 6 restated by Order No. 128-НҚ of 15 May 2026 (in force 12 Jul 2026). Approved by MoH, MoD, MCI, MES, MNE, MDDAI, MVD.
- Row 73: working curricula per typical plans (Order 557 of 20 Dec 2012) and typical programme (Order 499 of 12 Aug 2016). Evidence: copies of long-term plans per age group, cyclograms, individual child development card, approved by the head.
- Row 74: teachers with pedagogical higher/TVET education or retraining (no work-experience requirement); >=75% main-place-of-work teachers; >=20% teachers with categories moderator/expert/researcher/master (except preschools with fewer than two groups); head and teacher education/experience per Order 338 (13 Jul 2009, typical qualification characteristics). Evidence: Annex 1 staffing form. Staff teachers >=75% of all teachers.
- Row 75: teaching-methodical complexes per Order 216 (22 May 2020); teaching and play material per Order 70 (22 Jan 2016, equipment norms). Evidence: Annex 2 (library fund form).
- Row 76: medical room/point and medical licence (sub-type pre-doctor PHC for children) per MoH ҚР ДСМ-25 (15 Mar 2022); Annex 3; copy of contract with health organisation. Exempt: preschools with up to 3 groups inclusive.
- Row 77: catering unit with sanitary-epidemiological conclusion per ҚР ДСМ-59 (9 Jul 2021, preschool sanitary rules) and ҚР ДСМ-16 (17 Feb 2022, catering). Annex 4.
- Row 78: own premises or economic management/operational/trust management, or lease >=5 years; group rooms meeting ҚР ДСМ-76 (5 Aug 2021) and fire rules Order 55 (MES, 21 Feb 2022). Evidence: Annex 5; copies of title/lease; sanitary-epidemiological conclusion per building; copy of fire inspection/preventive control act per Entrepreneurial Code. New organisations: commissioning act, order appointing fire-safety persons, fire instructions, evacuation plan, minimum primary extinguishing means list, acceptance act of fire automatics. If violations remedied (Ent. Code Art. 152), control body reply allowed.
- Row 79 (as of 128-НҚ): equipment and furniture per Order 70; groups and group size per Order 385 (31 Aug 2022, model rules); third-level domain in edu.kz; equipped clothes lockers; beds for daytime sleep (except part-day mini-centres incl. pre-school groups); toilets and washbasins per ҚР ДСМ-76; anti-terror equipment per Order 117 (30 Mar 2022). Evidence: Annex 5, Annex 6. Lockers and beds counted by planned intake (new) or current enrolment (existing).
- Row 80: teacher in-service training at least once in 3 years, >=36 hours (Law on Education Art. 37(4); Law on Status of Teacher Art. 18(1)); heads: training in profile and education management at least once in 3 years. Evidence: Annex 8 (training for last 5 years).
- Row 81: data in NOBD per admin data forms (Order 570, 27 Dec 2012) and an education management information system with current databases matching NOBD. Evidence: Annex 6.
- Row 82: conditions for children with special educational needs per Order 92 (29 Apr 2025, psychological-pedagogical support service rules). Evidence: Annex 5.
- Annex 1 fields: No; full name; year/place of birth; education (diploma specialty, qualification, institution, year; retraining); main place of work (address, post, experience); practical work experience in profile; criminal record (absence/presence); category, date, order number; medical exam (personal medical book); master's degree; PhD; scientific degree; academic title; honours; recognition of foreign education; subject taught.
- Annex 3 note: medical licence status checked via e-license.kz. Annex 5 note: property rights data not submitted if available from the Unified State Real Estate Cadastre IS.
- Annex 8 fields: full name; topic; place and period; organisation; hours and experience; form of completion; signed by head.

- CONFIRMED Joint order MoE 165-НҚ (12 Jun 2026)/MNE 73 (15 Jun 2026), reg. 38978 on 17 Jun 2026, amends joint order 708/832 (Dec 2015) "risk criteria and checklists in child-rights protection" (Committee for the Protection of Children's Rights). Preschool orgs = HIGH risk by objective criteria. Subjective: NOBD data and prior control results; one gross violation => score 100 => preventive control with visit. Preschool checklist (Annex 3), 9 items: (1) education services contract with a parent [minor]; (2) no more than 3 SEN children per age group [significant]; (3) no unlawful expulsions [significant]; (4) 4-week seasonal forward menu [gross]; (5) daily menu matches forward menu [significant]; (6) premises, equipment, furniture [significant]; (7) anti-terror: alert system, CCTV, panic button (mobile/fixed), access control/intercom, fencing, vehicle speed-reduction means [gross]; (8) anti-terror protection passport agreed with local police head [gross]; (9) special conditions for children with disabilities (if any) [gross]. Subjective criterion: unfulfilled recommendations from preventive control without visit = 100 points.

- CONFIRMED Public service rules: Order No. 483 of 30 Nov 2022 (reg. 30833), new edition by Order No. 248 of 31 Oct 2025 (reg. 37314, old.adilet V2500037314), in force 1 Jan 2027. Licensor for preschool = territorial departments of the Committee for Quality Assurance in Education (MoE). Channel: egov.kz / elicense.kz, e-signature (ЭЦП) required. Completeness check 2 working days (incomplete/expired docs => reasoned refusal to proceed). Departments: 22 working days to check documents against qualification requirements AND conduct permit control with a site visit; expert opinion to commission (composition by Department director order); commission decides in 2 working days; e-licence in 2 working days; head signs in 1. Pre-refusal hearing: notice >=3 working days before end of term; objection within 2 working days. Total term: issue not later than 30 working days; re-issue 3 working days; reorganisation 30. Fee: 10 MRP on issue (Tax Code art. 554 cited - old code numbering), 1 MRP on re-issue; no fee for annex to licence. Documents for preschool: e-request signed with ЭЦП; fee proof; info forms per annexes 1,2,3,4,5,6,8 (admin forms 1-КК, 2-КК, 3-КК, 4-КК, 5-КК, 6-КК, 8-КК; one-off; submitted with application; electronic); e-copies of documents per Order 473. Registration data and medical licence pulled by the licensor from state IS. Re-issue triggers: change of IP name/registration/address; reorganisation; change of legal entity name/location; change of object address without moving. Refusal grounds: non-compliance with qualification requirements, unpaid fee, negative response of approving state body, no consent to personal-data access. Helpline 8 (7172) 74-24-30; 1414.
