## Buyer profile and pain

Working notes:

- finratings.kz, 12 Feb 2025: in 2023, 7,497 inspections of preschools, violations in 90.7%, 4,027 officials fined 1.12 bn tenge in total (avg about 278,000 tenge, my division), up to 1 m tenge in some cases; owners take loans to pay fines; 80% of rural private kindergartens rent. https://finratings.kz/news/533-v-kazakhstane-milliardnye-shtrafy-dushat-chastnye-detsady/
- Pain from state attendance-verification tools: Kyzylorda officials required private kindergartens to photograph children daily; Shymkent collected fingerprints; Almaty piloted QR entry (informburo). https://informburo.kz/novosti/v-kyzylorde-cinovniki-obyazali-castnye-detskie-sady-ezednevno-fotografirovat-detei ; https://informburo.kz/novosti/v-detskih-sadah-shymkenta-nachali-sobirat-otpechatki-detey-84651.html
- Batyrbekova (informburo, undated): about a third of kindergartens operate illegally. https://informburo.kz/stati/vybiraya-chastnyy-detskiy-sad-ne-nadeytes-chto-gosudarstvo-ego-kontroliruet.html
- Ministry presentation Oct 2025: 11,800+ preschool organisations, 1 m+ children; vouchers in 19 cities and 7 districts, ~300,000 children. https://www.gov.kz/uploads/2025/10/14/c3c68141af1d43359c78c1b3dbc529af_original.1183085.pdf
- Associations (search summary of informburo/forbes): Bilim Invest (president Kulyanda Batyrbekova) unites 538+ kindergartens; Republican Association of Private Education Organisations (Roza Sadykova); Kazakhstan Continuing Education Association (Leila Kulenova); Atameken. https://forbes.kz/news/newsid_282340 ; https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-v-kazaxstane-poprosili-edinyx-pravil-licenzirovaniia

## Competitor table and discussion

Working notes:
- Search for named kindergarten licensing consultants (RU, KZ) found only Ybcase (general education licence) and Dogovor24 (templates). Kaspi Obyavleniya listing check: no kindergarten licence offer.
- ZERO app (App Store): circles schedule, attendance, contracts, payments; no planning or licence features. https://apps.apple.com/tj/app/zero/id6504515977
- BALAM: "first and only app for kindergartens and parents in Kazakhstan" (Astana Hub article). https://astanahub.com/en/article/god-detei-v-rk-kakie-startapy-dlia-detei-sozdany-na-baze-astana-hub
- Ministry automated the individual child development card (bizmedia, Aug 2024). https://bizmedia.kz/2024-08-26-v-kazahstane-poyavilis-individualnye-karty-razvitiya-rebenka

Not in 2024 law per 01 file. https://informburo.kz/novosti/v-kazaxstane-predlagayut-avtomaticeski-licenzirovat-detsady-uspesno-prosedsie-attestaciyu
