# Kazakhstan kindergarten licensing kit: market size, buyers and competition (deep dive 02)

Date: 10 Oct 2026. Builds on [the A1 report](../reports/kazakhstan-a1.md). Scope: market size, buyers, competition, channels and regional expansion. The law, product design and go-to-market detail are covered in the other section files. Legal facts here (fines, licence rows, MRP) follow [01-law-and-requirements.md](01-law-and-requirements.md).

Exchange rate used for rough US$ figures: 500 tenge per US$. Informburo's header showed 467-487 tenge per US$ on the pages I opened, so US$ figures here are slightly understated ([informburo](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-v-kazaxstane-poprosili-edinyx-pravil-licenzirovaniia)).

## Summary

- **The buyer base is firm: about 6,200-6,500 private preschool organisations.**
  - Official statistics give 11,681 preschool organisations in 2024: 5,476 state and 6,205 private. Together they serve 986,422 children, 53% of them in private ones. My source is a January 2026 Damu fund analysis that cites the National Statistics Bureau and the Taldau centre ([Damu PDF](https://damu.kz/poleznaya-informatsiya/damu_analytics/analitika/Анализ%20сектора%20частного%20образования%20в%20РК.pdf), p. 2-3, 7).
  - The Ministry's April 2026 figure is 11,909 organisations, of which 6,526 are private ([Tengri](https://tengrinews.kz/tengri-institutions/novyie-pravila-chto-jdt-chastnyie-detsadyi-v-kazahstane-594367/amp/)).
  - The number of private organisations grew by about 400 a year, from 4,570 in 2020 to 6,205 in 2024 (my arithmetic from the Damu PDF, p. 7). Each new kindergarten will need a licence before it opens.
- **Demand is concentrated in the south and in the two big cities.** Five areas hold 4,011 of the 6,205 private organisations, or 65% (my sum from the Damu PDF, p. 8): Turkestan region (1,119), Almaty region (994), Almaty city (826), Shymkent (554) and Astana (518). The north is almost all state-run: Pavlodar has 16 private organisations and North Kazakhstan 19.
- **Mini-centres are a weak segment.** There were 2,964 mini-centres in 2024 (Damu PDF, p. 7). The national split by ownership was not found. In Kostanay region only 5 of 335 mini-centres were private in 2023 ([Kostanay education department plan for 2024](https://www.gov.kz/uploads/2024/1/20/f091ca262b59cbd7a7d3867019dd45b7_original.294208.docx)). Most mini-centres appear to be state units, often attached to schools (unverified nationally).
- **The buyer is a small business living on state money.** The average private organisation has about 84 children (my arithmetic: 518,984 / 6,205). In Kostanay in 2023, the state order paid on average 52,954 tenge per child a month, plus a parent fee of 20,880 tenge. The 64 private kindergartens on the state order there received 4.4 billion tenge, which is about 69 million tenge (US$137,000) each a year (my division, [Kostanay plan](https://www.gov.kz/uploads/2024/1/20/f091ca262b59cbd7a7d3867019dd45b7_original.294208.docx)). Losing the licence means losing that money, so the stakes are high. Margins are thin, though, and owners have said so loudly since 2020 ([inbusiness.kz, 2020](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/news/chastnye-detsady-v-kazahstane-na-grani-razoreniya)).
- **Nobody sells this job as a product.** I ran 40 searches in Russian, Kazakh, English and Uzbek and opened 21 pages. I found no software and no named service that prepares a kindergarten for the licence or for inspections.
  - The closest players do other jobs. Umai CRM is a CRM for children's centres at US$49-299 a month, with 961 centres registered ([Umai](https://www.umaicrm.com/)). Dogovor24 sells legal templates at 60,000-1,360,000 tenge a year ([Dogovor24 subscription](https://dogovor24.kz/subscription)). Ybcase is a general licensing consultancy with no published price ([Ybcase](https://ybcase.com/fintech/polucit-licenziu-na-obrazovatelnuu-deatelnost-v-kazahstane)). The state systems (eLicense, NOBD, the ED24/e-orda/Indigo attendance tools) do not check readiness.
  - The real substitutes are free: the regional quality departments' briefings, the associations' advice, and the owner's own methodist.
- **Willingness to pay is real but must be priced against cheap anchors.**
  - Owners already pay for legal templates (Dogovor24's cheapest yearly package is 60,000-75,000 tenge), for CRM software (Umai from US$41 a month billed yearly), for teacher training (one paid course cost 57,000 tenge, [informburo](https://informburo.kz/novosti/57-tysiac-tenge-za-povysenie-kvalifikacii-o-narusenii-prav-pedagogov-zaiavili-v-minprosvete)) and for fines (278,000 tenge per fined official on average in 2023, my division from [finratings.kz](https://finratings.kz/news/533-v-kazakhstane-milliardnye-shtrafy-dushat-chastnye-detsady/)).
  - I suggest 40,000-60,000 tenge (US$80-120) for a one-off readiness check and file. After that, 5,000-10,000 tenge (US$10-20) a month per site for inspection readiness and staff records. That is about 0.1-0.2% of a typical private kindergarten's income (my estimate).
- **Channels are concentrated and reachable.** Five owner associations and Atameken run the licensing dialogue. They are Leila Kulenova's Kazakhstan Continuing Education Association; Dinara Kudaikulova's "Adilet"; Kulyanda Batyrbekova's "Bilim Invest", which claimed more than 538 kindergartens in 2022; and Roza Sadykova's Republican Association of Private Education Organisations ([forbes.kz](https://forbes.kz/news/newsid_282340); [informburo, 1 Oct 2026](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-v-kazaxstane-poprosili-edinyx-pravil-licenzirovaniia)). The Damu fund has backed about 1,660 kindergartens with loans or guarantees (my sum from the Damu PDF, p. 19). Those are formal, bank-using owners, and a natural first list.
- **Regional expansion is weak.** Uzbekistan has 38,058 preschool organisations, but 28,729 are small family units, and its preschool licensing rules are unconfirmed ([talimxabarlari.uz](https://talimxabarlari.uz/?p=53948)). Kyrgyzstan has only about 2,011 ([economist.kg](https://economist.kg/society/2026/06/05/birth-rate-kindergartens-kyrgyzstan/)). Better second steps are inside Kazakhstan: 828 private schools and 326 private colleges face the same licensing and inspection regime (Damu PDF, p. 2).
- **Revenue size (my estimate).** If 5-10% of private kindergartens subscribe by year 3, at 72,000-120,000 tenge a year, plus one-off files for new and phased-in kindergartens, revenue would be about US$75,000-205,000 a year, with a midpoint near US$120,000 (see Implications). That is close to the A1 base case. A good small business, not a big one.

Notes on the table:

- **The BNS and Ministry counts differ by about 300** (6,205 against 6,526). That is two years of growth plus different counting dates. I use 6,500 as the 2026 base.
- **Sole traders count too.** From 2027 the licensing article of the Law on Education also covers sole traders who run preschool programmes ([Law 148-VIII](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z2400000148), per the 01 file). So small home-based operators that register are also buyers.
- **A shadow segment exists but will not buy.** In an undated article, Kulyanda Batyrbekova estimated that about a third of kindergartens worked illegally ([informburo](https://informburo.kz/stati/vybiraya-chastnyy-detskiy-sad-ne-nadeytes-chto-gosudarstvo-ego-kontroliruet.html)). These are not in the statistics and are not a target.
- **Old open data is useless for counting.** The data.egov.kz "kindergartens" dataset was last updated on 25 Oct 2016 and needs an API key ([data.egov.kz](https://data.egov.kz/datasets/view?index=kindergartens)). No downloadable current register of preschool notifications or licences was found. A regional example: East Kazakhstan's quality department counts 381 preschool organisations subject to licensing ([gov.kz control-vko](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/control-vko/press/news/details/1269193?lang=kk)).
- **State kindergartens are a possible B2G deal later.** There are about 2,500 state kindergartens (5,476 state organisations minus about 2,960 mini-centres, if nearly all mini-centres are state; my estimate). They also need the licence, but akimat education departments buy for them through public procurement. That needs a local company and is out of scope for a first product (unverified that departments would buy).

## Buyer profile and pain

**Who the buyer is.**
- **A single-site small business.** The average private organisation has about 84 children (my arithmetic, Damu PDF p. 7). In Kostanay, 63 private kindergartens held state-order places for 8,057 children, about 128 each ([Kostanay plan](https://www.gov.kz/uploads/2024/1/20/f091ca262b59cbd7a7d3867019dd45b7_original.294208.docx)). The sector averages about 8.7 teachers per organisation (my arithmetic: 101,493 teachers / 11,681 organisations).
- **The owner is often a former teacher or kindergarten head, and usually a woman.** Kulenova described the sector as "tens of thousands of entrepreneurs and teachers, 90% of whom are women" ([informburo, Jul 2022](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-grozyat-zabastovkoi-iz-za-vvedeniya-licenzirovaniya)). One kindergarten app founder was a former kindergarten head ([newtimes.kz](https://newtimes.kz/obshchestvo/157867-cifrovizaciya-v-obrazovanii-kazahstanka-pridumala-mobilnoe-prilozhenie-dlya-detsadov)).
- **Income is mostly state money.** In Kostanay in 2023, the state paid 52,954 tenge per child a month and parents paid 20,880 ([Kostanay plan](https://www.gov.kz/uploads/2024/1/20/f091ca262b59cbd7a7d3867019dd45b7_original.294208.docx)). At 84 children that is about 6.2 million tenge (US$12,400) a month (my estimate). Fully private fees are much higher in big cities: Damu puts the average at 125,000 tenge a month, and 150,000-200,000 in Almaty and Astana (Damu PDF p. 9, citing Numbeo).
- **Premises are often rented or converted houses.** In 2020, 70% of private kindergartens in the capital rented their space ([inbusiness.kz, 2020](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/news/chastnye-detsady-v-kazahstane-na-grani-razoreniya)). A 2025 report says 80% of rural private kindergartens rent ([finratings.kz](https://finratings.kz/news/533-v-kazakhstane-milliardnye-shtrafy-dushat-chastnye-detsady/)). Some run in rented cottages ([forbes.kz](https://forbes.kz/news/newsid_282340)) or in private homes on housing plots ([informburo, 1 Oct 2026](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-v-kazaxstane-poprosili-edinyx-pravil-licenzirovaniia)).
- **Many borrow.** About 1,660 kindergartens have had Damu-backed loans since 2010 (Damu PDF p. 19, my sum). Owners have also taken loans to pay fines ([finratings.kz](https://finratings.kz/news/533-v-kazakhstane-milliardnye-shtrafy-dushat-chastnye-detsady/)).

**How they comply today.**
- **They already keep most documents for the state order.** In 2022 Kulenova said licensing had in effect been replaced by the state-order competition. She added: "We just didn't get the licence itself; we always submitted all the other documents and passed checks by the authorised bodies" ([informburo, Jul 2022](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-grozyat-zabastovkoi-iz-za-vvedeniya-licenzirovaniya)). The Ministry says 96% of private organisations meet the state-order placement rules ([Tengri](https://tengrinews.kz/tengri-institutions/novyie-pravila-chto-jdt-chastnyie-detsadyi-v-kazahstane-594367/amp/)). So the job is mostly gap-finding and assembling the file in the licence format. It is not starting from zero.
- **The new gaps are specific.** Compared with what they already have, the licence adds (per the 01 file and [Order 268](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2500037500)):
  - a lease of at least 5 years;
  - at least 75% of teachers on main-job contracts;
  - at least 20% of teachers with a category;
  - 36 hours of training every 3 years, with certificates;
  - an edu.kz domain;
  - NOBD data that matches reality;
  - anti-terror equipment and the anti-terror passport;
  - the annex data forms (1-КК to 8-КК) filed with the application.
- **Paperwork is done by the head and a methodist.** Methodist pay in Kazakhstan ranges from about 100,000 to 500,000 tenge a month in CVs and job ads (hh.kz, via search summary: [hh.kz resumes](https://hh.kz/resumes/metodist-v-detskij-sad)). Small kindergartens often combine the head and methodist roles (search summary of Kaspi and hh.kz listings, unverified).
- **They lean on free official help.** Regional quality departments brief heads in public and advise them to start preparing the document set now ([MTRK, 24 Jul 2026](https://mtrk.kz/ru/2026/07/24/licenzirovanie-detskikh-sadov-startu/)). Atameken and the associations collect questions and lobby ([informburo, 1 Oct 2026](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-v-kazaxstane-poprosili-edinyx-pravil-licenzirovaniia)).

**Software they already use.**
- **State-imposed attendance and enrolment tools.** Parents had to install ED24 so that kindergartens could get state funding. Many could not finish registration ([zakon.kz, Jan 2024](https://www.zakon.kz/stati/6420659-verifitsiruy-menya-ili-zachem-kazakhstanskim-roditelyam-navyazyvayut-novoe-prilozhenie-dlya-detsadov.html)). The ed24.kz domain now opens an "EORDA" portal of 82 children's education services. It links to Indigo enrolment, and its footer names «КДС-ФРАНЧАЙЗИНГ» ([ed24.kz](https://ed24.kz/)).
- **NOBD**, the national education database, must be kept current. It is also a licence row (01 file).
- **The Ministry's automated individual child development card** ([bizmedia, Aug 2024](https://bizmedia.kz/2024-08-26-v-kazahstane-poyavilis-individualnye-karty-razvitiya-rebenka)).
- **Some commercial tools**: Umai CRM, BALAM and ZERO (see the competitor table). Accounting software is likely 1C (unverified).

**Pain, with evidence.**
- **Unclear and inconsistent rules.** On 1 Oct 2026, owners asked for one joint explanation covering licensing, land, construction, sanitary and fire rules. They want clarity on "which set of documents is enough", separate rules for existing kindergartens, and a transition period. Atameken's Azamat Beisenbenov said one region accepts an existing kindergarten's documents while another demands a change of land or building use ([informburo](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-v-kazaxstane-poprosili-edinyx-pravil-licenzirovaniia)). In May 2024, Astana owners were given one month to fix land-use violations, which they called impossible (same source).
- **Fear of closure.** A lawyer estimated that more than half of private kindergartens are in premises that break land or fire rules. Changing a building's designated use costs 20-40 million tenge in documents alone ([inbusiness.kz, May 2026](https://inbusiness.kz/index.php/ru/news/kazahstan-riskuet-ostatsya-bez-poloviny-chastnyh-detsadov)). The Ministry disputes the forecast of 50% closures ([Tengri](https://tengrinews.kz/tengri-institutions/novyie-pravila-chto-jdt-chastnyie-detsadyi-v-kazahstane-594367/amp/)).
- **Heavy inspection history.** In 2023 there were 7,497 inspections of preschools. Violations were found in 90.7% of them, and 4,027 officials were fined 1.12 billion tenge in total ([finratings.kz](https://finratings.kz/news/533-v-kazakhstane-milliardnye-shtrafy-dushat-chastnye-detsady/)). Since 1 Feb 2026, budget-funded organisations, including private ones on the state order, can be inspected without notice (01 file; [informburo](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vnezapnye-proverki-skol-i-detskix-sadov-smogut-provodit-v-kazaxstane)).
- **Digital control fatigue.** Kyzylorda officials made private kindergartens photograph children every day ([informburo](https://informburo.kz/novosti/v-kyzylorde-cinovniki-obyazali-castnye-detskie-sady-ezednevno-fotografirovat-detei)). Shymkent kindergartens collected children's fingerprints ([informburo](https://informburo.kz/novosti/v-detskih-sadah-shymkenta-nachali-sobirat-otpechatki-detey-84651.html)). Almaty piloted QR-code entry in state kindergartens ([informburo](https://informburo.kz/novosti/bez-smartfona-ne-vhodit-v-chetyryoh-rajonah-almaty-testiruyut-dostup-v-detsady-po-qr-kodu)). Owners may resist one more app unless it clearly saves work.
- **Thin margins.** In 2020, owners said they were "on the verge of closing" when state payments stopped ([inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/news/chastnye-detsady-v-kazahstane-na-grani-razoreniya)). Kostanay owners asked for higher per-child funding ([inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/uvelichit-podushevoe-finansirovanie-v-chastnyh-detsadah-prosba-kostanajskih-predprinimatelej)).
- **A history of organised resistance.** In 2022, the Kazakhstan Continuing Education Association threatened a strike over licensing ([informburo, Jul 2022](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-grozyat-zabastovkoi-iz-za-vvedeniya-licenzirovaniya)). Other associations supported licensing ([forbes.kz](https://forbes.kz/news/newsid_282340)). Today the industry says it is not against licensing, but wants "a single list of clear and achievable requirements" ([informburo, 1 Oct 2026](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-v-kazaxstane-poprosili-edinyx-pravil-licenzirovaniia)). That sentence is close to a product brief.
- **Staff qualification gaps.** In Kostanay, 1,204 of 3,451 preschool teachers (35%) had a category in 2023 ([Kostanay plan](https://www.gov.kz/uploads/2024/1/20/f091ca262b59cbd7a7d3867019dd45b7_original.294208.docx)). That is above the 20% licence threshold for the region as a whole. But the figure mixes state and private, and small private kindergartens may fall below it (unverified). Owners also asked for the rules on proving teacher qualifications to be clarified ([informburo, 1 Oct 2026](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-v-kazaxstane-poprosili-edinyx-pravil-licenzirovaniia)).

**What I could not find.** No owner forum threads, Telegram channels or WhatsApp groups turned up in search. They almost certainly exist, but are closed or not indexed (unverified). Owner interviews are still needed.

**Reading.**
- **Value is high, budgets are thin.** A tool that protects about 69 million tenge a year of state money is easy to justify. But owners compare prices with free help and with a Dogovor24 package at 60,000-75,000 tenge a year.
- **Ceilings.** Umai's US$41-49 a month (about 20,000-25,000 tenge) is the ceiling for "software I use every day" in this sector. A compliance tool used a few times a month should sit well below that.
- **Proposed price points** (my estimates):
  - a readiness check and licence file at 40,000-60,000 tenge one-off;
  - a subscription at 5,000-10,000 tenge a month (60,000-120,000 a year) per site;
  - a chain or consultant plan at 25,000-40,000 tenge a month for up to 10 sites.
- **One-off revenue pool.** If every private kindergarten bought the one-off file at 50,000 tenge, the pool would be about 326 million tenge (US$650,000) (my arithmetic: 6,526 × 50,000). A realistic 10-20% share gives US$65,000-130,000, spread over 2027-2029. To that add about 400 new private kindergartens a year.

**Discussion.**
- **No incumbent does the job.** No product or named service combines the licence gap check, the annex data forms, the staff thresholds and the inspection checklists. The A1 finding holds after a wider search.
- **The free baseline is official explanation, not a tool.** Regional departments and associations explain the rules. The 1 Oct 2026 meeting shows owners still lack "a single list of clear and achievable requirements" ([informburo](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-v-kazaxstane-poprosili-edinyx-pravil-licenzirovaniia)). A tool that turns rows 73-82 and the two checklists into one plain list per site fills exactly that gap.
- **The threat is a free list from a trusted body.** If the Ministry or an association publishes a good free checklist, a paid one-off "pack" loses value. The defensible part is the ongoing work, which a static list cannot do:
  - staff thresholds recomputed as people join and leave;
  - training and category expiry alerts;
  - evidence stored per checklist item;
  - inspection follow-up with deadlines.
- **The most dangerous fast follower is the e-orda/Indigo vendor.** It is already inside every state-order kindergarten. A licensing module bundled free by akimats would be hard to beat on price (unverified intent). The defence is to partner with associations early and to stay focused on the private owner, who is the one fined.
- **Umai CRM is the obvious partner.** It sells to children's centres and kindergartens in four countries and lacks compliance features. A referral or integration deal is plausible (unverified interest).

## Implications for positioning and pricing

- **Position: "Licence-ready and inspection-ready, in one list."** Echo the owners' own demand for "a single list of clear and achievable requirements" ([informburo](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-v-kazaxstane-poprosili-edinyx-pravil-licenzirovaniia)). Present it as preparation, not a guarantee of a licence.
- **Lead with what is new.** Most owners already hold state-order documents. The pitch is "find your gaps against the new licence rows and fill in the annex forms". Do not pitch "build your file from scratch".
- **Make the recurring part the core.** The licence has no time limit (01 file), so the one-off file is a door-opener. Value that lasts is:
  - staff thresholds (75% main-job, 20% category) kept current;
  - training and category expiry alerts;
  - the 29-item and 9-item checklists as self-audits with stored evidence;
  - inspection follow-up with deadlines.
  Unannounced checks since Feb 2026 make this credible.
- **Pricing (my proposal):**
  - **Free:** a 15-minute self-check of rows 73-82 with a red/amber/green result, offered through associations and quality departments. It builds trust and blunts a free association checklist.
  - **Licence file:** 40,000-60,000 tenge one-off per site. Below Dogovor24's cheapest yearly pack, and about the size of the state fee (43,250 tenge).
  - **Inspection-ready subscription:** 5,000-10,000 tenge a month per site, or 50,000-100,000 a year paid upfront. Well below Umai's 20,000-25,000 a month CRM price.
  - **Association or chain deal:** 20-30% discount for members. A free partner seat for consultants who bring clients.
- **Target first:** private kindergartens with a state order in Almaty, Almaty region, Shymkent, Turkestan and Astana (4,011 organisations), plus Damu borrowers (about 1,660). Interface in Russian first, with Kazakh close behind; Turkestan and Shymkent will need Kazakh.
- **Market size reality check (my estimates, year 3):**
  - Subscriptions: 5-10% of 6,500 private organisations is 325-650 sites. At 72,000-120,000 tenge a year, that is 23-78 million tenge.
  - One-off files: 300-500 a year (phased licensing of existing kindergartens plus about 400 new private ones a year) at 50,000 tenge, or 15-25 million tenge.
  - Total: about 38-103 million tenge, or US$75,000-205,000. The midpoint, about US$120,000, matches the A1 base case.
- **Payments caution.** Kazakh owners may prefer Kaspi or bank transfer against a local invoice over a foreign card checkout (unverified). Test this in pilots; the 04 and 05 files cover it.
