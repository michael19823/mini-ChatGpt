## Working notes (raw)

- Inputs from 01: licence has no time limit; no transitional clause; phasing in practice over ~3 years; fee 10 MRP = 43,250 tenge (2026), 46,930 in 2027 if MRP 4,693; personal data must be stored in KZ (Art. 12(2)); unannounced checks since 1 Feb 2026 for budget-funded children's orgs.
- Inputs from 02: 6,205 (BNS 2024) / 6,526 (MoE Apr 2026) private preschools; 65% in Turkestan, Almaty region, Almaty city, Shymkent, Astana; ~400 net new private a year; anchors Dogovor24 60-75k tenge/yr, Umai US$41-49/mo, licence help on Kaspi from 15-50k tenge; avg private org ~84 children; Kostanay state-order ~69m tenge/yr per private kindergarten.
