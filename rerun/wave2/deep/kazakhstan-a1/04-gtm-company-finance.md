# Kazakhstan A1: go-to-market, payments, company setup and financials (deep dive 04)

Status: complete as of 10 Oct 2026. Builds on [the A1 report](../reports/kazakhstan-a1.md), [01-law-and-requirements.md](01-law-and-requirements.md) and [02-market-and-competition.md](02-market-and-competition.md). "My estimate" marks numbers I derived; "(unverified)" marks facts I could not confirm. "Per search summary" means I saw the fact in a search result's summary of that page, not on the page itself. Money is in tenge; US$ at about 500 tenge per US$.

## Summary

- **Price it like the state fee, then sell the upkeep.** A free 15-minute self-check brings in leads. The **Licence File costs 49,000 tenge** (US$99), about the 43,250-tenge state licence fee and below Dogovor24's cheapest yearly template pack (60,000-75,000 tenge). It includes 3 months of **Inspection-Ready at 7,900 tenge a month or 79,000 a year**, far below a children's-centre CRM at about 20,000 tenge a month ([Umai](https://www.umaicrm.com/)). A **Network plan at 29,000 tenge a month** serves chains and consultants. No price promises a licence.
- **Sell from the foreign company through Paddle at launch, to the owner as a person.** Paddle supports Kazakh buyers and collects Kazakhstan's 16% VAT on consumer sales ([Paddle](https://paddle.com/help/sell/tax/which-countries-does-paddle-charge-sales-tax-or-vat-for/)). We keep about 80% of the sticker price.
- **B2B sales from abroad are awkward.** A Kazakh company that pays a foreign seller may have to withhold 15-20% income tax, or 10% under most treaties for royalties ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/withholding-taxes); [Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/ru/blog/withholding-tax-in-kazakhstan-2026-services-royalties)). It also gets no local e-invoice, and a VAT-registered buyer owes 16% reverse-charge VAT. A bank wire abroad costs a business at least 20,000 tenge ([finratings.kz](https://finratings.kz/news/15412-halyk-bank-vvedet-novuiu-komissiiu-za-perevody-v-rossiiu-i-belarus/)). Use a local reseller for invoice buyers until there is a local company.
- **Open a local TOO (LLP) around month 8-10**, when 60 sites pay or invoice requests pile up.
  - Costs: the state fee for a small TOO is zero. A lawyer charges from 400,000 tenge for a TOO with foreign founders and 300,000 for the parent company's BIN ([mybuh.kz](https://mybuh.kz/yuridicheskie-uslugi/)). All in, about **0.8-1.0 million tenge (US$1,600-2,000) via a lawyer, or 0.5-0.8 million if done in person**, plus 432,500 tenge of capital.
  - Practicalities: the director's IIN probably needs a visit in person (sources differ). Running costs are about 0.8-1.0 million tenge a year, mostly the accountant.
  - Payoff: the TOO adds about 5-6 million tenge a year of profit, because local Kaspi and invoice sales avoid Paddle's VAT and fee. Astana Hub status could make its corporate tax and VAT zero ([Forbes.kz](https://forbes.kz/blogs/nyuans-dlya-relokantov-56a065)).
- **Timing.** Build now, launch on **1 December 2026**, and catch the start of licensing on 1 January 2027. After that, follow the regional phased schedules (2027-2029), the September school-year start and the August staff changes.
- **Channels, in order:** owner associations and Atameken; partners (consultants, accountants, training providers, Umai CRM); Russian and Kazakh search pages; WhatsApp outreach to 4,011 private preschools in the five biggest areas; paid social; Damu. Year-1 marketing budget: **6.0 million tenge (US$12,000)**.
- **Base case (36 months):**
  - Volume: 945 Licence Files and **347 subscribers at month 36**.
  - Billings: **22.9 / 37.6 / 45.5 million tenge** in years 1-3 (US$46,000 / 75,000 / 91,000).
  - Profit before founder pay: -9.0 / +9.1 / +13.2 million tenge.
  - Break-even on a trailing 12 months comes in month 17 (March 2028). **Peak cash need is about 10 million tenge (US$21,000)**; plan 15-20 million.
  - Low case: about 92 subscribers and losses throughout. High case: 920 subscribers and 51 million tenge profit in year 3.
- **Verdict on the numbers: a small, real business that Kazakhstan alone does not make big.** In the base case the product pays for itself but not for a founder's salary within 36 months. Upside needs the same engine for 828 private schools and 326 private colleges (same regulator), then possibly Uzbekistan.
- **Exit:** a sale or licence to the ED24/e-orda vendor, Bilim Group (now part-owned by Freedom Holding), Umai or Dogovor24. On the base case, expect about 50-100 million tenge (US$100,000-200,000).
- **Kill criteria:**
  - fewer than 8 of 15 owners willing to pay 40,000 tenge or more by 15 Nov 2026;
  - fewer than 40 paid Files by 31 Jan 2027;
  - fewer than 100 Files or under 25% conversion to subscription by 30 Apr 2027;
  - fewer than 80 subscribers or churn above 4% a month by 31 Oct 2027.

### VAT treatment

- **Paddle phase (months 1-8):** prices are VAT-inclusive for private buyers. Paddle pays Kazakhstan's 16% VAT out of the price ([Paddle tax list](https://paddle.com/help/sell/tax/which-countries-does-paddle-charge-sales-tax-or-vat-for/)). We keep about 80% of the sticker price after VAT and fees.
- **Local TOO phase (month 9 on):** the TOO is not a VAT payer while turnover stays below 10,000 MRP a year (43.25 million tenge in 2026; about 46.9 million in 2027). The same sticker price is then shown "without VAT", and we keep about 98.5% after Kaspi fees. The base case stays under the threshold for the TOO's local billing through year 3 (about 32 million tenge in year 3). The high case crosses it in year 2, so it needs Astana Hub status (VAT-exempt priority activity) or a 16% price rise.
- **Do not show different prices to different payers.** Keep one sticker price. The VAT difference is our cost, not the buyer's.

### Sales motion

- **Low-touch self-serve for single kindergartens.** Free self-check → result by e-mail and WhatsApp → Licence File by card (Paddle) or, from month 9, by Kaspi invoice → 15-minute onboarding call on WhatsApp → "Inspection-ready score" report in month 3 → subscription.
- **Assisted sale for chains, consultants and associations.** A demo call, a pilot of 1 site, then a signed contract and a local invoice.
- **One local person does the talking.** A Russian- and Kazakh-speaking customer-success and sales person, part-time from month 1 and full-time from month 4. The founder runs product, content and partnerships.
- **Trust rules.** Never ask for the owner's e-signature (ЭЦП) keys. Owners file on eGov themselves. KZ-CERT has warned about Instagram and OLX accounts offering kindergarten services that collect people's data and ЭЦП keys ([Tengri](https://tengrinews.kz/kazakhstan_news/obieiavleniia-o-detskix-sadax-nazvali-opasnymi-375426/)). Say "we never ask for your ЭЦП" on every page.
- **Position as preparation, not a guarantee.** Echo the owners' own demand for "a single list of clear and achievable requirements" ([informburo](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-v-kazaxstane-poprosili-edinyx-pravil-licenzirovaniia)).

## Payments and tax friction

Units: MRP (monthly calculation index) is 4,325 tenge in 2026; the draft 2027 budget sets 4,693 tenge ([bes.media, Sep 2026](https://bes.media/news/mrp-4-693-tenge-minimalnaya-zarplata-85-tysyach-chto-zalozhili-v-byudzhet-kazahstana-na-2027-god/)). US$ figures use about 500 tenge per US$, as in the 02 file. That is conservative: the rate shown in informburo's page header in October 2026 was between about 452 and 487 tenge per US$ on different fetches ([informburo](https://informburo.kz/cards/registraciya-too-v-rk-posagovoe-rukovodstvo); 02 file).

### The short version

- **Sell to the owner as a person, by card, through Paddle, at launch.** Paddle supports buyers in Kazakhstan, prices them in US$ with tax included, and is registered to collect Kazakhstan's 16% VAT on sales to consumers ([Paddle supported countries](https://developer.paddle.com/concepts/sell/supported-countries-locales); [Paddle tax list](https://paddle.com/help/sell/tax/which-countries-does-paddle-charge-sales-tax-or-vat-for/)). A private person who pays does not have to withhold tax (see below). This is the cleanest route from a foreign company.
- **Selling to the kindergarten's company from abroad causes friction.** If the owner pays from a TOO (LLP) or IP (sole trader) account, the buyer may have to withhold 15-20% income tax at source. If it is VAT-registered, it also owes 16% "VAT for a non-resident"; most kindergartens are not (my reading). It cannot get a local e-invoice (ESF). On a 49,000-tenge purchase this can add 11-41% in tax plus paperwork (my arithmetic below).
- **Bank wires abroad do not suit small tickets.** Halyk Bank charges businesses 0.35% with a minimum of 20,000 tenge per foreign-currency transfer ([finratings.kz, Jul 2026](https://finratings.kz/news/15412-halyk-bank-vvedet-novuiu-komissiiu-za-perevody-v-rossiiu-i-belarus/)). That is 40% of a 49,000-tenge sale.
- **Local habits favour Kaspi and invoices.** Kaspi Pay charges merchants 0.95% for a Kaspi QR payment, 1.75% for a Kaspi Gold card and 2.3% for other banks' cards ([Kaspi Pay tariffs](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/kz/kaspi_pay/conditions/q1445)). Kaspi's partner guide covers connection for sole traders (IP) and TOOs ([Kaspi guide](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/app/connection), per search summary), so it needs a local entity. Card and QR takings need a fiscal receipt; Kaspi's own cash-register function does this automatically ([finratings.kz](https://finratings.kz/news/9037-kaspi-pay-ne-vsegda-zameniaet-onlain-kassu-chto-dolzhny-znat-ip/amp), per search summary).
- **So open a local TOO once demand is proven** (about month 8-10, see Company setup). It lets you invoice in tenge with an ESF, take Kaspi and bank transfers, and sell to the 96% of private kindergartens that run on state-order money and keep formal books. Until then, use Paddle for owners who pay by card, and a local reseller partner for buyers who insist on an invoice.

### Can Kazakh cards pay a foreign seller online?

- Kazakhstan is not on Paddle's list of blocked countries (Russia and Belarus are) ([Paddle](https://developer.paddle.com/concepts/sell/supported-countries-locales)).
- Stripe can charge in tenge: KZT is on its list of presentment currencies ([Stripe currencies](https://docs.stripe.com/currencies)). Kazakhstan is not a Stripe seller country, so this only helps a seller based abroad.
- Kaspi says purchases abroad are charged in the shop's currency and debited in tenge at Kaspi's rate, with the rate shown before you confirm, and "always without commission" when made through the Kaspi app ([Kaspi guide, updated 5 Mar 2025](https://guide.kaspi.kz/client/ru/gold/shopping/alipay/q16423)). That page is about paying abroad through the app. Whether Kaspi Gold cards pass 3-D Secure smoothly on a European card checkout is (unverified). Test it with the first 10 pilot buyers.
- Kaspi limits transfers abroad to about US$2,500 per operation and US$10,000 a month, per one report ([digitalbusiness.kz](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-06-27/kaspi-kz-snizil-komissiyu-za-perevodi-v-drugie-banki/)). That is far above our ticket size.

### Seller side: must a foreign seller register for Kazakh VAT?

- **B2C (selling to people): yes, unless a merchant of record does it.** A foreign company that sells electronic services to individuals is a VAT payer in Kazakhstan if, for example, the buyer lives in Kazakhstan, pays through a Kazakh bank, or uses a Kazakh phone code. It goes through a simplified ("conditional") registration with the State Revenue Committee ([KGD guidance](https://www.kgd.gov.kz/sites/default/files/pages/nalogooblozhenie_inostrannyh.docx), per search summary). The rate from 1 Jan 2026 is 16% ([KPMG, Aug 2025](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/kz/pdf/2025/08/Beyond-the-Horizon-rus.pdf), per search summary; [Paddle](https://paddle.com/help/sell/tax/which-countries-does-paddle-charge-sales-tax-or-vat-for/)).
- **With Paddle, Paddle is the seller of record and carries that duty.** We sell to Paddle; Paddle sells to the buyer.
- **B2B: no registration.** A VAT-registered business buyer accounts for the VAT itself (next section).
- **Local TOO:** registration is compulsory only above 10,000 MRP of turnover a year, or 43.25 million tenge in 2026 and about 46.9 million in 2027 (my arithmetic; threshold per [KPMG](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/kz/pdf/2025/08/Beyond-the-Horizon-rus.pdf), per search summary). The base case stays under it (about 32 million tenge of local billing in year 3); the high case crosses it in year 2. Astana Hub participants are exempt from VAT on their priority IT activity ([Forbes.kz](https://forbes.kz/blogs/nyuans-dlya-relokantov-56a065), per search summary).

### Buyer side: what a Kazakh business pays on top when it buys from abroad

**1. VAT for a non-resident (reverse charge), 16%: only for VAT-registered buyers.**
- Under the 2026 Tax Code, services bought from a non-resident are taxable turnover only of a buyer that is a VAT payer (Art. 449(1)). Software use, licences, maintenance and updates have their place of supply where the buyer does business (Art. 459(2)(4)). The buyer pays by the 25th of the second month after the period (Art. 506) and reports it on form 300 ([mybuh.kz, 22 Jan 2026](https://mybuh.kz/useful/nds-za-nerezidenta-kak-nachislyat-i-kogda-oplachivat.html)).
- Firms on a special tax regime, such as the simplified declaration, cannot register for VAT (Art. 99(3)), so they owe nothing here (same source). One secondary source says the opposite ([Statsnet VAT calculator](https://statsnet.co/calculators/kz/vat)); mybuh cites the articles, so I follow it.
- Electronic services are excluded from the buyer's turnover if the foreign company already put Kazakh VAT into its price as a separate line (Art. 454(3)(6), same source).
- The tax office added an "ESF for a non-resident" function to its e-invoice system for this under the 2026 Code ([KGD press release](https://kgd.gov.kz/sites/default/files/108574/scan_documents/press-reliz_na_rus.docx), per search summary).
- Most private kindergartens are probably not VAT payers, because preschool education is largely VAT-exempt and many use special regimes (my reading; unverified). Large chains may be. For a VAT-registered buyer the VAT can be credited, so it is a cash-flow and paperwork cost, not a real one (Art. 480(7)(2), same source).

**2. Corporate income tax withheld at source (КПН у источника выплаты).**
- The 2026 Tax Code (No. 214-VIII, Division 15) lists services that are Kazakh-source income wherever they are performed. Art. 679(1)(3) names management, consulting, engineering, marketing, advertising, design, audit, financial, legal and **information-processing** services ([Uppersetup summary](https://uppersetup.com/ru/blog/withholding-tax-in-kazakhstan-2026-services-royalties)). PwC's list (reviewed 23 Jul 2026) names management, financial, consulting, engineering, marketing, auditing and legal ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/withholding-taxes)).
- Rates: 20% for services, 15% for royalties ([Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/ru/blog/withholding-tax-in-kazakhstan-2026-services-royalties); [PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/withholding-taxes)). Updates of software versions now count as royalties ([Moore Kazakhstan, Aug 2025](https://kazakhstan.moore-global.com/international-taxation-in-kazakhstan-from-2026/)). How a SaaS subscription is classed (royalty, information processing or a service performed abroad that is not taxed) is **(unverified)**. Ask a local tax adviser before selling B2B from abroad.
- Who must withhold: legal entities and sole traders (IP) are tax agents (Art. 683). Private individuals mostly are not ([Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/ru/blog/withholding-tax-in-kazakhstan-2026-services-royalties)).
- Treaties cut the royalty rate to 10% for most likely seller countries: Cyprus, Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands, the UK, the USA, the UAE, Georgia, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Czechia, Bulgaria and Singapore. Estonia's rate stays at 15% ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/withholding-taxes)). To use a treaty, the seller must give the buyer an official certificate of tax residence in time (Art. 702, 705-706). If the buyer pays the tax from its own pocket instead of withholding, the treaty does not apply (Art. 698(3)) ([Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/ru/blog/withholding-tax-in-kazakhstan-2026-services-royalties)).

**3. What it adds up to for one sale (my arithmetic).** A TOO that pays a foreign company 49,000 tenge and handles taxes correctly:
- **Best case** (royalty, treaty rate 10%, residence certificate supplied in time, buyer not VAT-registered): it withholds 4,900 tenge from the payment, or pays about 5,440 on top if the price is net. About 11% extra, plus a withholding return.
- **Worst case** (information-processing service at 20%, not withheld, buyer VAT-registered): 49,000 × 0.20/0.80 = 12,250 tenge of withholding paid from its own pocket, plus 7,840 tenge of VAT (creditable later). About 41% up front, 25% for good.
- On top: a withholding return, and a currency-control file at the bank if paid by wire.
- Small buyers paying by card often skip withholding, but then the owner, not we, carries the risk. That is a poor thing to sell to a client who is buying "compliance".

**4. When the owner pays personally.** A private person is not a tax agent for these services, and Paddle collects the VAT. There is no extra tax. The kindergarten cannot book the cost as an expense without a local invoice (my reading). At 49,000-79,000 tenge a year that is a small price for most owners.

### Bank transfers

- **From Kazakhstan to a foreign account:** Halyk Bank's base tariff for businesses is 0.35% of the amount, minimum 20,000 tenge, maximum 120,000 tenge ([finratings.kz](https://finratings.kz/news/15412-halyk-bank-vvedet-novuiu-komissiiu-za-perevody-v-rossiiu-i-belarus/)). Fine for a chain paying 300,000 tenge a year; useless for a 49,000-tenge sale.
- **Currency control:** operations from US$50,000 are reported to the National Bank and contracts above US$500,000 need registration ([Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/ru/article/llp-too-in-kazakhstan-for-foreigners-2026-registration-visa)). Our tickets are far below.
- **Within Kazakhstan (once a TOO exists):** interbank transfers in tenge cost 1%, minimum 450 and maximum 3,000 tenge at Halyk ([finratings.kz](https://finratings.kz/news/10468-halyk-bank-peresmotrel-usloviia-dlia-biznesa-s-2026-goda/)). Most owners will simply pay a Kaspi invoice or QR.

### Recommended set-up

1. **Months 0-8:** Paddle checkout in US$ (US$99 one-off, US$16 a month or US$159 a year). Show the tenge equivalent on the website. Market the purchase to the owner personally. Turn on Paddle's invoice option for the few businesses that want it (unverified that Paddle offers this for Kazakhstan).
2. **For buyers who need a tenge invoice and ESF before the TOO exists:** sell through one or two local resellers (a licensing consultant or an accounting firm that serves kindergartens). The reseller buys seats from us at 30% off and invoices locally. Only one cross-border flow then remains, and the reseller deals with its tax.
3. **From month 8-10:** a local TOO invoices in tenge, takes Kaspi Pay and bank transfers, and issues ESFs. Paddle stays for owners who prefer a card.
4. **Data:** wherever the company sits, store personal data on servers in Kazakhstan ([Personal Data Law Art. 12(2)](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z1300000094), per the 01 file). Kazakh hosting can be bought from abroad: Serverspace bills in EUR for VMs in Almaty (per the 03 file's notes; unverified). This rule does not by itself force a local company.

### Form: TOO (LLP) owned by the founder's foreign company

- **Who can own it.** Founders can be residents or non-residents, individuals or companies, with 100% foreign ownership allowed ([Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/ru/article/llp-too-in-kazakhstan-for-foreigners-2026-registration-visa); [moneypanda](https://moneypanda.kz/kz/news/kak-otkryt-too-v-kazakhstane-v-2024-godu-dva-sposoba-323), per search summary).
- **Minimum capital.** Sources disagree. One says 100 tenge for a small business, with 100 MRP (432,500 tenge) as the practical amount banks like to see ([Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/ru/article/llp-too-in-kazakhstan-for-foreigners-2026-registration-visa)). Another says the minimum for a small business is 100 MRP ([vitvet, Apr 2026](https://vitvet.com/articles/strany/kazahstan/kazakhstan-kompaniya-registraciya-vedenie-deyatelnosti/)). Plan 432,500 tenge either way (it stays in the company as working cash).
- **Official registration fee.** Zero for a small business TOO. 6.5 MRP applies to medium and large ones ([dogovor24](https://dogovor24.kz/questions/registracija_biznesa-20/20), per search summary; unverified against the Tax Code). Informburo's guide says the fee applies online only to large enterprises ([informburo, Mar 2024](https://informburo.kz/cards/registraciya-too-v-rk-posagovoe-rukovodstvo)). Registration takes 1 working day on egov.kz once you have the IIN/BIN and an e-signature ([Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/ru/article/llp-too-in-kazakhstan-for-foreigners-2026-registration-visa)).
- **The catch is the paperwork for foreigners.**
  - A foreign parent company first needs a Kazakh BIN as a non-resident taxpayer. A local firm charges 300,000 tenge for this ([mybuh.kz](https://mybuh.kz/yuridicheskie-uslugi/)).
  - The director needs an IIN and an e-signature. Since 2024 an individual's IIN can no longer be obtained by power of attorney; it needs personal presence at a public service centre (ЦОН) in Kazakhstan ([Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/ru/article/llp-too-in-kazakhstan-for-foreigners-2026-registration-visa)). A search summary of a dogovor24 answer says a notarised power of attorney can still be used ([dogovor24](https://dogovor24.kz/questions/kakie-neobhodimo-dokumenty-dlya-registracii-too-esli-odnim-iz-uchastnikov-yavlyaetsya-inostrannyi-grazhdanin-6451.html), unverified). Treat a 2-3 day trip as likely.
  - If the sole participant is a foreign company, the owner does not need a C5 business visa, but a foreign director living there needs a C3 visa ([Uppersetup](https://uppersetup.com/ru/article/llp-too-in-kazakhstan-for-foreigners-2026-registration-visa)). The simple way round is to appoint the local operations manager as director (my suggestion; needs a trusted person).
  - Opening a bank account takes 2-4 weeks. One foreign-owned Almaty TOO was refused by three banks because it had no local counterparties; the fourth opened the account within three weeks after it showed draft agreements with Kazakh partners ([vitvet, Apr 2026](https://vitvet.com/articles/strany/kazahstan/kazakhstan-kompaniya-registraciya-vedenie-deyatelnosti/)). Pilot contracts and reseller agreements will help.

## Contracts and liability

- **Online contract.** Kazakh law treats an offer to anyone that contains all essential terms as a public offer (Civil Code Art. 395). Paying for the service is accepted as acceptance (Art. 396(3)), and local offer templates rely on this ([finance.kz](https://finance.kz/dictionary/aktsept-7), per search summary; [dogovor24](https://dogovor24.kz/questions/chto-takoe-publichnaya-oferta-19471.html), per search summary). Publish the offer in Russian and Kazakh, with an English version for the foreign entity's records.
- **Two contract sets.**
  - *Card sales via Paddle:* Paddle's buyer terms plus our end-user licence terms (Paddle requires the seller's terms for the product).
  - *Local TOO:* a public offer for small buyers, and a signed contract with an act of work done and an ESF for chains, associations and resellers.
- **Data processing.** The kindergarten is the owner of the personal data of children, parents and staff. We are a third party that processes it on its behalf. The contract must say data are stored in Kazakhstan, list the data, state the purposes and security measures, and say how a breach is reported (Personal Data Law Art. 12(2), 16, 25, per the 01 file). Do not store the anti-terror passport, which is "for official use" (01 file).
- **Liability.**
  - Say plainly that the product is preparation and evidence management, not a guarantee of a licence or of passing an inspection. The decision is the licensing department's, and land, premises and lease problems cannot be fixed by software.
  - Cap liability at the fees paid in the last 12 months. An advance agreement that excludes or limits liability for an intentional breach is void (Civil Code Art. 359(3)). In an adhesion contract with a consumer, a cap is void where the law already sets the amount of liability (Art. 358) ([prg.kz](https://prg.kz/m/amp/document/3206061/12/4010000), per search summary; the source showed an older edition, so confirm the current text with the local lawyer). Owners who buy through Paddle as private persons may count as consumers.
  - Keep a dated legal-basis note on each checklist item, so a wrong item can be traced and fixed fast.
  - Add a regional-practice disclaimer: different regional departments may ask for different documents (informburo, 1 Oct 2026, in the 01 file).
- **Governing law and courts.** For Paddle sales, Paddle's terms apply. For the TOO, Kazakh law and Kazakh courts (they are where the buyer is and are cheaper for both sides).
- **Insurance.** Professional-liability insurance for software firms is not common in Kazakhstan (unverified). Instead, keep the cap, the disclaimers and a legal reviewer on retainer.
- **Content partner contract.** The local lawyer or methodist who reviews the legal content signs a contract that assigns copyright in templates to us. They also accept a defined share of responsibility for errors in their review, capped at their fee.

**Unit economics, base case (my estimate):**
- A subscriber pays about 7,400 tenge a month, keeps about 95% after fees, and stays about 40 months at 2.5% churn. That is about 280,000 tenge of lifetime net revenue, plus the 49,000-tenge File.
- CAC is about 32,000-54,000 tenge per new paying customer, or about 85,000 tenge per new subscriber in year 1. LTV/CAC is about 3-4.
- Gross margin after hosting and payment costs is above 85%. The cost base is people and content, not servers.

**What the numbers mean:**
- **Cash need is small.** About 10 million tenge (US$21,000) covers the base case with no founder pay. Plan **15-20 million tenge (US$30,000-40,000)** to allow for founder trips, slower sales or a delay in the TOO.
- **Kazakhstan alone pays for itself but not for a founder.** Base-case year-3 profit is about 13 million tenge (US$26,000) before founder pay. With modest founder pay the base case does not break even within 36 months.
- **Volume of Licence Files is the main lever.** A 30% shortfall wipes out most of the profit. Track it monthly from December 2026.
- **The local TOO is worth it.** It adds about 5-6 million tenge a year of profit in years 2-3 against about 1.7 million in set-up and running costs, because it removes the 16% VAT and Paddle's fee from most sales.
- **The low case is visible early.** It has 34 Files by January 2027 and 81 by April 2027, against 80 and 190 in the base case.

## Exit and partnerships

- **Most likely outcome: a profitable small business, or a sale of the product to a local player.** On the base case the year-3 business has about 33 million tenge of subscription ARR plus about 14 million of File sales.
- **Likely buyers or partners:**
  - **The ED24 / e-orda / Indigo vendor** (the footer names «КДС-ФРАНЧАЙЗИНГ», 02 file, [ed24.kz](https://ed24.kz/)). It already reaches every state-order kindergarten, so our module fills a gap it has. It is also the main competitive threat. Approach it in year 2 for a licence or integration deal.
  - **Bilim Group.** Freedom Holding took a stake in Bilim Group in August 2026 and plans to invest in learning content and AI tools ([fbroker.kz](https://fbroker.kz/en/news/51344-freedom-holding-corp-priobrel-doliu-v-bilim-group-ru-2); [kapital.kz](https://kapital.kz/business/151343/timur-turlov-priobrel-dolyu-v-bilim-group.html)). A funded edtech consolidator is a natural buyer.
  - **Umai CRM** (children's centres in four countries, [Umai](https://www.umaicrm.com/)) and **Dogovor24** (legal templates, 491,234 claimed clients; 02 file). Both could bundle the product.
  - **Licensing consultants** such as Ybcase, as white-label buyers of the Network plan.
- **Price.** Small SaaS businesses typically sell for about 2-4 times revenue or 3-6 times profit, per the sources used in the Bosnia study ([BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/state-of-saas-valuations-2026); [beancount.io](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/11/bootstrapped-saas-valuation-multiples-2026-acquire-com-indie-founders-guide); not re-checked here). On the base case at month 36, 2-4 times about 45 million tenge of revenue is 90-180 million tenge, and 3-6 times about 13 million of profit is 40-80 million. A small, local, regulation-driven product will sit at the low end: plan on about 50-100 million tenge (US$100,000-200,000) (my estimate).
- **Market context.** Edtech deal numbers in Kazakhstan fell 35% in 2024 and investment halved ([digitalbusiness.kz](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2025-06-24/v-kazahstane-opredelili-luchshie-obrazovatelnie-kompanii-reyting/), per search summary). Do not plan on venture money.
- **Partnership options before any exit:** a revenue share with an association that white-labels the tool for its members; or a licence of the rules engine to the ED24 vendor.

## Milestones and kill criteria

**Milestones (base case):**
- **1 Nov 2026:** MVP built; 15 interviews done; lawyer and methodist contracted.
- **1 Dec 2026:** paid launch; content signed off; security test passed; Paddle live.
- **10 Jan 2027 (day 90):** 50 paid Licence Files; 3 partners signed.
- **30 Apr 2027 (month 6):** about 190 Files and 46 subscribers; first month-3 conversion rate measured.
- **Jun-Jul 2027 (month 8-9):** TOO registered and invoicing; Kaspi Pay live; Astana Hub application filed.
- **31 Oct 2027 (month 12):** about 355 Files and 135 subscribers; private-school module in build.
- **Oct 2028 (month 24):** cumulative cash positive; second local hire; private schools live.
- **Oct 2029 (month 36):** about 350 subscribers; ARR about 33 million tenge; decide on Uzbekistan or a sale.

**Kill criteria (stop, pivot or shrink to a side project if any is true):**
1. **By 15 Nov 2026:** fewer than 8 of 15 interviewed owners say they would pay 40,000 tenge or more for the File, **or** no association agrees to a co-branded webinar.
2. **By 31 Jan 2027:** fewer than 40 paid Files (low case: 34).
3. **By 30 Apr 2027:** fewer than 100 paid Files, **or** fewer than 25% of File buyers convert to a paid subscription after the free 3 months.
4. **By 31 Oct 2027:** fewer than 80 paying subscribers, **or** monthly churn above 4%.
5. **At any time:** the government announces automatic licences for state-order kindergartens **and** a free official self-check tool. Then keep only the inspection-readiness subscription, or stop.
