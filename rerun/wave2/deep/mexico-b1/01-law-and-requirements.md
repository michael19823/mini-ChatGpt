# Mexico private-security register law, turned into product requirements

Status: IN PROGRESS (draft 1, 2026-10-10). Federal law and Reglamento read in full from the Chamber of Deputies PDFs. Filing channel, enforcement, states and upcoming changes still being researched.

Short names used below:
- **LFSP** = Ley Federal de Seguridad Privada, DOF 6 Jul 2006, last reform DOF 17 Oct 2011 ([LFSP PDF, Cámara de Diputados](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFSP.pdf)).
- **RLFSP** = Reglamento de la LFSP, DOF 18 Oct 2011, no reforms ([RLFSP PDF](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/regley/Reg_LFSP.pdf)).
- **DGSP** = Dirección General de Seguridad Privada (the federal regulator, inside the security ministry, now SSPC).
- **Registro Nacional** = Registro Nacional de Empresas, Personal y Equipo de Seguridad Privada (LFSP art. 8-13).
- **RNPSP** = Registro Nacional del Personal de Seguridad Pública (the police personnel register; private operational staff must be inscribed there too, LFSP art. 28-III). The person's key there is the CUIP.
- **UMA** = Unidad de Medida y Actualización. Fines written in "salario mínimo" are now read in UMA.

## Who is obliged (draft)

- Any "prestador de servicios" (individual or company) that provides private security in **two or more states** needs a DGSP authorisation (LFSP art. 1, 15, 16; RLFSP art. 3). Single-state firms are under state law only (LFSP art. 1, second paragraph).
- Seven modalities (LFSP art. 15): I persons; II property; III transport of goods or valuables; IV alarms and electronic monitoring; V information security; VI prevention and responsibility systems (background checks, polygraph); VII linked activities (vehicle armouring; CCTV, GPS, access control, electric fences, ballistic vests per RLFSP art. 5-IV-b).
- Individuals working alone (escorts, guards not in a company) carry the same duties as company staff (LFSP art. 2-IV, 34).
- No size threshold or exemption by headcount. Authorisation lasts **one year** and must be revalidated (LFSP art. 17, 19).
