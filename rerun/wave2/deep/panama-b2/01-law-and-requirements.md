- Ley 23/2015 consolidated text (CNBC/MEF): art. 12 enlace (UAF + supervisor liaison; until named, the legal rep or the practising professional is the liaison); art. 23 (as replaced by Ley 254/2021 art. 6) points to Ley 124/2020 art. 40 for the SONF list; art. 26 risk assessment (document, keep updated, provide to supervisor); art. 26A risk-based CDD, update high-risk more often; art. 26B financial and transactional profile; art. 27 natural-person CDD (SONF limited to numerals 2, 3, 4); art. 28 legal-person CDD (SONF limited to numerals 1, 2, 3, 8); art. 29 (Ley 70/2019) high-risk update at least yearly, retention 5 years from end of relationship; art. 34 PEP EDD, 2 years after leaving office; art. 35 may use registered "empresas de cumplimiento", SONF stays responsible; art. 36 refuse/stop if client blocks CDD, may file ROS; art. 38-39 nature of business, ongoing monitoring (manual sets review periodicity); art. 40 risk-based controls, tech tools for list screening and segmentation; art. 41 special exam of unusual ops, document findings in writing, EDD for FATF high-risk countries; art. 42 know-your-employee; art. 45 independent evaluation (SONF: as regulated by supervisor); art. 47 continuous training; art. 49 preventive freezing on UN lists, notify UAF immediately; art. 53 cash/quasi-cash reports >= B/.10,000 ("que apliquen"); art. 54 ROS immediately, complementary ROS; art. 56 tipping-off ban; art. 60 fines B/.5,000 to B/.5,000,000; art. 61 SSNF may order Registro Público to suspend corporate rights of entities whose resident agent fails; art. 62 progressive fines. Source: https://cnbc.mef.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/ley-23-de-2015.pdf
- Ley 124/2020 art. 40 (quoted inside Ley 23 note): SONF list incl. (11) lawyers and CPAs only when doing activities a-k for a client, incl. (k) resident agent services; (12) notaries. Art. 44 = registration duty (Comunicado 04-2022). Res. S-010-2021 of 2 Dec 2021, in force 7 Dec 2021, GO 29429 of 6 Dec 2021 = SSNF en línea registration app.
- Res. S-022-2025 (21 Mar 2025, GO 30248 of 1 Apr 2025): numbered PRIMERO-DÉCIMO; on request via email registered in SSNF en línea to the SONF and/or "Persona de Enlace"; 15 business days from confirmation of sending/receipt in SONF server; update on request or when facts change; one questionnaire per regulated activity; published on SSNF website; false data -> criminal complaint; repealed I-REG-003-2017 and I-REG-005-2017 (extra-situ form); in force on publication.
- SSNF Guía Matriz de Riesgo del SONF (2022 v1.0): evaluation/update periods max 1 year, keep 5 years; statistical calibration and validation of client matrix (Pareto / normal distribution); documentation reviewed at least yearly, all versions kept 5 years; independent evaluation at least every 2 years by external auditors, certified professionals or compliance firms, kept at least 2 years; at least 2 risk levels (3 is standard; justify more); written compliance commitment in the manual with evidence staff read it; annual training plan (employees, dates, content, hours, evaluation method); firm matrix per Acuerdo JD-02-2022 art. 3; four indicator groups; weights per family sum to 100%; mitigants per JD-02-2022 art. 4; inherent -> treatment -> residual; client matrix uses same criteria; transactionality per JD-02-2022 art. 14.
- SSNF Guía de debida diligencia (JD-02-2022, 2022 v1.0): basic CDD fields for natural persons (a-k) and legal persons (a-i); verification documents; BO threshold 25% shares/votes, else control by other means, else senior managing official; date BO status acquired; SDD refresh at least every 3 years; EDD refresh at least yearly; partner approval for high-risk in law firms; EDD mandatory for certain activities (real estate with funds handling; asset administration; nominee arrangements); nominee mitigants: yearly refresh, written agreement, sworn statement per entity incl. 8 hours training; annual sworn statement to SSNF per JD-02-2022 art. 11(b); training providers; ROS triggers; financial and transactional profile; cash >= USD 10,000 special attention; crypto = high risk; FATF non-cooperative = high risk. References Res. S-002-2021 of 2 Jul 2021 (unusual ops / ROS guide), Res. JD-014-16 of 6 Dec 2016 (compliance firm registration), DE 35/2022 arts. 2, 3, 9, 18, 22, 23, 25.
- Acuerdo JD-02-2022 (28 Jul 2022; GO 29601-B, 17 Aug 2022; in force on publication; replaces JD-01-2020). PDF: https://ssnf.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/ssnfWEB/documentos/Acuerdo-No.-JD-02-2022.pdf . Art. 1 scope (activities a-k); art. 3 own documented risk assessment, factors per I-REG-001-017 + NRA + sector reports + SSNF info + press; review at least once a year or on relevant event/weakness; SSNF may request it and order manual changes; art. 4 mitigants by size: (a) RBA methodology and risk matrix for firm, clients and BOs, (b) CDD, (c) prevention manual, (d) tech tools, (e) continuous training, (f) designated compliance responsible; plus periodic independent evaluations, available on request; art. 5 basic CDD fields (natural 10 fields; third party/BO 6 fields incl. date BO status acquired; legal person 9 fields incl. BO/officer data a-g); art. 6 EDD triggers a-g (cash > B/.10,000 single or summed per day/week; nominee director/shareholder services; PEP/close associate/family; high-risk jurisdictions; foreign entities with bearer share registers; NRA activities; high risk per own assessment); refuse/stop if client blocks EDD; EDD refresh per manual and at least once a year; art. 7 SDD (10 categories) refresh at least every 3 years, verification may follow; art. 8 verification documents natural (6) and legal (9) incl. 25% shareholder register; art. 9 SSNF access: CDD evidence; client list segmented quantitatively/qualitatively incl. terminated clients and reason; contracts or sworn statement; risk methodology and results; art. 10 nominee mitigants a-d incl. 8 hours training before appointment, yearly training after; art. 11 annual sworn statement to SSNF on nominee services over prior 12 months (designees name, ID, occupation, nationality, domicile; requesting person; entities served; resignations), first 15 calendar days of July each year (first one 30 Sep 2022); resident-agent lawyers must feed same data into RUBF on request; art. 12 keep nominee files 5 years from dissolution or end of service; art. 13 nature of business, source of funds/wealth, resident agent DD on the entity and its purpose; verify declared activity when high risk, mismatch, or art. 41 special exam; art. 14 financial and transactional profile; art. 15 ongoing monitoring (periodic review, compliance culture, tech tools with alerts); partners of sociedades civiles are ultimately responsible; art. 16 ROS; art. 18 sanctions per Ley 23/Ley 124 and sanction procedure; art. 19 JD-01-2022 = sanction procedure.

- Res. S-037-2026 (7 Jul 2026; GO 30586-A of 10 Aug 2026): art. 1 the Guide's content is the "estándar mínimo obligatorio" every SONF must put in its prevention manual; art. 2 applies to all SONF of Ley 124 art. 40; art. 3 in force 3 months after GO publication (so about 10 Nov 2026); art. 4 repeals I-REG-001-017 of 3 Apr 2017. Comunicado SSNF-DR-002-2026 (13 Aug 2026) says it "entra en vigor" from publication (conflict with art. 3; treat 10 Nov 2026 as the safe date). Guide content: approval page (name, version, approval date, approver name/role/signature, effective date); approver by type (board by minutes; law firm partners or managing partner; sole professional himself); change log (version, date, description, section, approver); update triggers a-e; warns copied/downloaded manuals fail RBA; firm matrix vs client risk evaluation are two instruments; client matrix 4 factors (client, product/service, channel, geography), three levels (Nivel 1 simple 1-3 points per factor, 4-6 low, 7-9 medium, 10-12 high; Nivel 2 weighted, weights 100% with justification; Nivel 3 SSNF 2022 client-matrix guide); qualitative override with documented justification; client matrix must be congruent with firm matrix and firm matrix revised if client mix drifts; lists: restrictive (UN) vs control (OFAC, EU, FATF, Basel, TI CPI, Canada OSFI, UK, PEP lists); verify client, BO, 25%+ holders, officers, signatories; record result and responsible person; RTE cash report: monthly within first 10 business days if regular; semiannual declaration (30 Jun, 31 Dec) within first 10 business days after semester if occasional; one-time "Declaración Jurada Definitiva" if none (DE 35 art. 24), filed per UAF manuals; UN list received via UAF en Línea, screen active and inactive clients, BOs, suppliers, document date, list code, reviewer, result; freeze and notify UAF via UAF en Línea; no-match also documented and communicated as UAF sets; monitoring frequency by risk; red-flag list specific to activity; special exam with max resolution times; ROS immediately with supporting docs; KYE policy (sole practitioners declare N/A); training policy with evidence; nominee services section; checklist of ~40 items (pages 66-69).

Fields: natural/legal, ID/RUC, address, declared activity, sector (art. 40), SSNF registration number, declarant, period from-to, tick box.

- Res. S-033-2026 (19 Jun 2026; GO 30557-B of 30 Jun 2026; in force on publication): UN lists + preventive freezing for all SONF. Art. 4 lists: UN (restrictive, binding: 1267, 1988, 1373, 1718, 1737, 2161, 2170, 2178, 2199 and successors) vs control/reference lists (national: CNBC Res. 02-018 of 27 Mar 2018 list of Venezuelan PEPs; FATF high-risk; Basel AML Index; TI CPI; EU consolidated; Canada OSFI; UK consolidated; OFAC SDN and consolidated). Art. 5 UAF emails the SONF's registered address on each UN list update; SONF must check its database and answer UAF; keeping enlace emails current with UAF is the SONF's duty (no excuse if notice not received).
