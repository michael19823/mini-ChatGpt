## Buyer profile and pain

Working notes (being filled):

- SSNF lawyers' self-assessment questionnaire guide: an online form of at least 131 questions. It asks for the activities done, UAF code, SSNF registration number and CUR, number of employees at end-2024, the total client count, client counts by 16 client types (final vs intermediary, national vs foreign, PEP, multiple nationalities), and whether represented companies have beneficial owners in a long list of high-risk countries ([SSNF guide, lawyers questionnaire](https://ssnf.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/ssnfWEB/GUIAS/Gu%C3%ADa%20para%20responder%20el%20Cuestionario%20%20de%20Autoevaluaci%C3%B3n%20%E2%80%93%20Sector%20Profesionales%20-%20Abogados.pdf); viewed pages 3-6 and 30-31). An accountants' version exists too.
- SSNF Practical Guide for the Prevention Manual (79 pages, 2026): written for both firms and independent professionals; the manual must be updated at least when rules change, new services start, structure changes, an SSNF supervision finds gaps, or the risk profile changes; ends with a completeness checklist ([SSNF Guía Práctica Manual](https://ssnf.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/ssnfWEB/GUIAS/Gu%C3%ADa%20Pr%C3%A1ctica%20para%20el%20Manual%20de%20Prevenci%C3%B3n%20de%20BC-FT-FPADM.pdf)).

## Willingness to pay

Working notes (being filled):

- SSNF register of executed sanctions (file named Aug 2025 but listing items to 17 Jul 2026): 38 sanctions in publication, 19 of them in the professional sector. My count of the professional amounts: 14 at B/.5,000, then B/.9,120, 9,243, 9,600, 12,000 and 12,500; one without amount. Six professional fines (Nov 2025 to Jun 2026) were B/.5,000 each for not sending all information the SSNF requested (art. 41(1) Acuerdo JD-01-2022). Three were B/.5,000 for not registering in the RUBF, one for not registering in SSNF en Línea. Free-zone fines run B/.5,000-59,500; casinos up to B/.43,200 ([SSNF sanctions register](https://ssnf.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/ssnfWEB/Sanciones/Sanciones_Agosto_2025.pdf)).
- Resident-agent fees per company: US$300-450 a year (Relo Firm pages conflict) ([Relo Firm](https://www.relofirm.com/?p=13)); bundled annual compliance US$1,099 ([Expanship](https://www.expanship.com/pa/compliance/pricing)).
- COX per-client costs: about US$4 for screening plus risk classification of a new client ([COX services](https://agilecheck.app/es/kyc/services)).
- USMA 120-hour SONF diploma (May 2026), price not published, taught by Daisy Chacón (registered compliance firm) ([USMA](https://usma.ac.pa/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/INFO.WEB-Prevencion-de-lavado-de-dinero.pdf)).

## Competitor table and discussion

Working notes (being filled):

- **COX by AgileCheck (agilecheck.app) is the closest direct rival.** Self-serve, pay-per-credit (US$4.99 per credit, no monthly minimum, 30-day free trial with up to 5 test clients, no card). It has a dedicated "Cumplimiento SSNF en Panamá" page, lawyer and accountant pages, and lists Panama among 7 countries. Prices: list search US$0.99; individual or legal-entity DD with risk classification US$2.99; digital onboarding US$0.99; adverse media US$0.99; risk re-evaluation US$0.99; bulk list search US$0.09 per subject; deep investigations US$19.96-59.88; AI assistant free. Modules include a configurable client risk matrix, immutable audit log, PDF/CSV exports, a training tracker and a whistleblower channel. The SSNF page also summarises recent SSNF rules: JD-02-2022 own risk assessment reviewed yearly and an annual sworn statement for nominal services in the first 15 days of July; Res. S-033-2026 (UN lists binding, from 30 Jun 2026); Res. S-037-2026 (Practical Guide for the Prevention Manual mandatory for all SONF, from 10 Nov 2026) ([COX SSNF Panamá](https://agilecheck.app/es/ssnf-panama); [COX services and prices](https://agilecheck.app/es/kyc/services); [COX lawyers](https://agilecheck.app/es/lawyers); [COX training tracker](https://agilecheck.app/es/compliance-training-tracker)).
- MyLegalDesk (Panama City, beta in 2026, access on request, no prices): practice management for Panamanian lawyers with client due diligence by client type, missing-document flags, stored list-screening results, a 5-factor client risk score (activity, volume, channel, source of funds, geography), frozen methodology per evaluation, append-only audit log, and a resident-agent company list with fees, renewals and periodic due-diligence reviews. No mention of SSNF, UAF, RUBF or training ([MyLegalDesk](https://mylegaldesk.app/); [Stanford CodeX TechIndex](https://techindex.law.stanford.edu/companies/mylegaldesk)).
- PEP Check Panamá (sof-IA): credit-based PEP/sanctions/adverse-news lookup, "ten sources", PDF certificate per query, history; 30 free credits on approval; prices not public (plans storefront hidden in the app code) ([pepcheckpanama.com](https://pepcheckpanama.com/); [sof-IA](https://sof-ia.net/)).
- AgileCheck: claims 400+ clients across Latin America in financial and DNFBP sectors; AML, Defense, News modules; demo-led sales ([AgileCheck](https://agilecheck.odoo.com/)).
- InteliCumplo (APC Intelidat with FICO/Tonbeller), launched 18 Oct 2019 as full-lifecycle KYC tool for Ley 23 ([Panamá América, 18 Oct 2019](https://www.panamaamerica.com.pa/economia/nueva-herramienta-intelicumplo-servira-las-empresas-para-prevenir-el-blanqueo-de-capitales)). Current status unverified.
- 40 SSNF-authorised compliance firms (June 2026), incl. KPMG, TP Panamá, OMC Group, Auditaxes, ILH Compliance ([SSNF list](https://ssnf.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/ssnfWEB/empresas-cumplimiento/cumplimientoJunio2026.pdf)).
- Regulator view: SSNF national risk assessment cites "little use of technology tools (e.g. risk matrices, automatic list-screening engines)" as a cause of the lawyer sector's medium-high risk; firms meet 76% of prevention mechanisms vs 42% for individual lawyers ([SSNF ENR update, Nov 2025](https://cnbc.mef.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Informe-de-Evaluacion-Nacional-de-Riesgo-ALA-SSNF-7.1.25.pdf)).
- Alpicio SIP (Alpicio Holding, Panama, "2018-2026"): integrated AML system for financial and non-financial obliged subjects under Ley 23; KYC files with basic/extended/enhanced DD, risk matrix engine (196 countries), transaction monitoring, alert workflow to ROS, list screening via Alpicio List; targets banks, trust companies and law firms; demo only, no prices ([Alpicio SIP](https://www.alpicio.com/sip.html)).
- Herca Compliance (Grupo Herca Internacional, SSNF compliance firm): resells PULPO third-party risk platform and Refinitiv World-Check One; no prices ([Herca](https://hercacompliance.com/software-de-cumplimiento/)).
- Inspektor (Risk Consulting Global Group, Colombia; Panama page): list screening (UN, PEP, restrictive lists), unlimited users, bulk queries, web service; "buy your licence"; no Panama price found ([Inspektor Panamá](https://www.riskglobalconsulting.com/es-pa/inspektor/)).
- Toritool Compliance (SSNF compliance firm 020-2023, ex-supervisors): consultancy plus own "digital tool" to manage documents, generate reports, build the manual; claims 50 sectors covered; attends ASSOCUPA congresses; no prices ([Toritool](https://toritool.com/)).
- Price check of 29 compliance-firm websites (from the SSNF list emails): none publishes a price (my scan, Oct 2026).
