## Working notes (to be folded into sections)

### Res 196/2020 (full text read on Base Legal)
Source: [Base Legal, Res SEPRELAD 196/2020](https://baselegal.com.py/docs/78668570-6ae3-11eb-990a-525400c761ca). Signed Asunción 4 Sep 2020; Base Legal shows publication 4 Dec 2020. Art. 2 of the resolution repeals only Art. 2 of Res 85/15.
- Art. 1 scope: vehicle = new or used land unit, 2+ wheels, own engine 600 cc+; incl. motorcycles, cars, trucks, machinery, trailers (non-exhaustive).
- Art. 2 integral system (compliance + risk mgmt), covers delegated processes.
- Art. 3 self-assessment every 2 years; methodology check every 4 years; documented, available to SEPRELAD. Art. 4 factors: clients, products/services, channels, geography. Art. 5 new products/tech: report before launch. Art. 6 new geographic zones: report before.
- Art. 7 Máxima Autoridad approves policies, Manual, Code, OC appointment, resources, ROS sending.
- Art. 8 OC senior, direct access, autonomous, no conflicts; owner can be OC in empresa unipersonal; branch encargados allowed.
- Art. 9 OC functions (15 items).
- Art. 10 appointment notice 5 business days; changes 5 bd; SEPRELAD may object 10 bd (silence = accepted); removal 5 bd; interim 48 h; temporary absence max 6 months; vacancy max 60 calendar days.
- Art. 11 Manual (Annex I), staff acknowledgment. Art. 12 Code of ethics, infractions + sanctions, acknowledgment, collective code for associations.
- Art. 13 annual internal control evaluation (OC may do it); report to SEPRELAD within 90 days after FY close; content Annex II.
- Art. 14 external audit by SEPRELAD-registered auditor; annual; report within 180 days after FY close. (Amended by Res 328/2026 - details pending.)
- Art. 15 annual training programme; records 5 years. Art. 16 minimum topics.
- Art. 17-30 CDD: Art. 19 stages, verification deferrable up to 60 days; Art. 20 records 5 years from last op/end; Art. 21 general regime fields; Art. 22 simplified (single payment <= 15 minimum wages; instalments <= 20 MW/yr; trade-in excluded; no suspicion); Art. 24 enhanced; Art. 26 refuse/terminate; Art. 27 profile; Art. 28 rating; Art. 29 monitoring rules approved by MA, analysis max 30 calendar days from alert; Art. 30 third-party reliance + sworn statement.
- Art. 31 register of ALL operations regardless of amount; Art. 32 retention 5 years incl. analyses and reasons for not reporting.
- Art. 33 ROS: unusual-op analysis up to 90 calendar days; ROS within 24 h of classifying as suspicious; SEPRELAD may return, 15 bd to fix. Art. 34 ROS content. Art. 35 via ROS Web; answer SEPRELAD requests in 4 business days. Art. 36 confidentiality / tipping-off. Art. 37 negative report: no ROS in 3 consecutive months -> report within 10 business days after the quarter. Art. 38 freeze immediately for UN-listed persons.
- Annexes I (manual), II (internal control report, 12 items), III (9 red flags), IV (lists: UN, FATF, OFAC, EU, others), V (risk criteria).

### Annual form (Formulario Anual de Información del SO), SIRO
Source: [SEPRELAD instructivo, sector automotores (PDF, created 27 Jun 2022)](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/userfiles/files/resoluciones/06-22-instructivo-formulario-anual-automotores.pdf). Earlier Res 243/2020 version: Excel by e-mail in first 20 calendar days of March ([Base Legal Res 243/2020](https://baselegal.com.py/docs/c8583c6f-671b-11eb-86f7-525400c761ca), seen in search snippet).
Fields: Ventas (clients national/foreign/PEP/PF/PJ, number of vehicles sold; cobros: financial/cooperative, crypto, other goods; cash by currency + PYG equivalent at BCP rate); Compras (same for suppliers); Importaciones (units received, total value PYG, foreign suppliers, units imported, number of payment orders; payments; cash); Zona geográfica (Zone 1-3, branches, clients per zone of residence); countries of import; armoured vehicles Y/N; price range in USD (3 options); observations; sworn declaration; ticket de cumplimiento.

### SEPRELAD Memoria 2025 (PDF read locally)
Source: [Memoria 2025](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Memoria-Anual-de-Gestion-Ano-2025.pdf).
- RO for automotores: basis "Capítulo I Artículo 7° numeral 3 de la Res 085/2015 y Artículo 31° del Reglamento ... Res 196/2020"; RO received since 2015; 453 SOs reported RO in SIRO in 2025; ops 2025: venta 82,591, importación 51,698, compra 21,730 = 156,019 (H1 96,678, H2 59,341). RO are "de forma mensual, trimestral o semestral" depending on sector.
- ROS: 23 automotive SOs filed ROS in 2024, 19 in 2025.
- In situ inspections 2025: 76 total, 35 automotor (table says 38); selection by sector risk matrices.
- Annual reports regularisation: 32% automotor after warnings (H2 2024 - Q1 2025).
- "sanción pecuniaria significativa a un sujeto obligado del sector automotor" in 2025.
- Res 31/2019 (sanction procedure), Art. 3 grounds for preliminary investigation: 4 SOs in 2025, 1 automotive.
- Registry: Res 218/11; 1,569 requests via SIRO; 1,119 completed (229 vehicle); 450 cancelled.
- Deregistration: Res 126/23 replaced by Res 460/25 (SIRO module "Baja de Sujetos Obligados"); 12 vehicle SOs deregistered 2025.
- Formulario Anual via SIRO, "vencimiento es el 31 de mayo de cada año"; 331 vehicle SOs filed in 2025. New annual form and automotive matrix "será implementado para el periodo 2026".
- External auditor register: Res 218/2020; 71 admitted-recommended in 2025.
- DGAFE/DGSR meeting 2 Oct 2025 on adding new fields to the automotive RO.
- SIRO risk-matrix module Phase 1 = automotive: prefill from RO; mitigants to "declare": Manual PLA/FT, self-assessment, training.
- SIRO sanctions module added (apercibimientos, multas, suspensiones).
- ENR (national risk assessment) update in progress (preliminary conclusions 2025).

### SEPRELAD Memoria 2024 (PDF read locally)
Source: [Memoria 2024](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/2024.pdf).
- 2,043 SOs monitored via SIRO; 1,692 warned (apercibimiento) under Res 410/2024 and 681/2024: 1,238 real estate, 454 vehicle.
- Obligations checked: RN (Art. 37 Res 196), RO (Art. 31 Res 196), annual form (Res 246/2022, Res 165/2022), external audit (Art. 14) and internal control (Art. 13).
- Registry: 2,042 requests via SIRO; 1,566 done; 532 cancelled. Registration flow: e-form -> supervision check -> canon payment validation -> OC gets SIRO user. 2024 form added OC nationality, office address, sworn statement.
- Canon: set yearly by resolution; 2024 = Res 30 of 25 Jan 2024; Res 226 of 9 May 2024 amnesty to 30 Jun 2024. Registration fees: Res 007/2018, 235/2020, 29/2023.
- Res 507/2024 opened a sumario administrativo against one automotive SO.
- Other SIRO resolutions: 165/2022, 246/2022, 326/2022 (RN via SIRO), 202/2023, 146/2023 + 16/2024 (ROS/RN via SIRO for other SOs).
- Supervision manual: Res 239/2020.
