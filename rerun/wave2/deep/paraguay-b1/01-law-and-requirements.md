## Working notes (to be folded into sections)

### Res 196/2020 (full text read on Base Legal)
Source: [Base Legal, Res SEPRELAD 196/2020](https://baselegal.com.py/docs/78668570-6ae3-11eb-990a-525400c761ca). Signed Asunción 4 Sep 2020; Base Legal shows publication 4 Dec 2020. Art. 2 of the resolution repeals only Art. 2 of Res 85/15.
- Art. 1 scope: vehicle = new or used land unit, 2+ wheels, own engine 600 cc+; incl. motorcycles, cars, trucks, machinery, trailers (non-exhaustive).
- Art. 2 integral system (compliance + risk mgmt), covers delegated processes.
- Art. 3 self-assessment every 2 years; methodology check every 4 years; documented, available to SEPRELAD. Art. 4 factors: clients, products/services, channels, geography. Art. 5 new products/tech: report before launch. Art. 6 new geographic zones: report before.
- Art. 7 Máxima Autoridad approves policies, Manual, Code, OC appointment, resources, ROS sending.
- Art. 8 OC senior, direct access, autonomous, no conflicts; owner can be OC in empresa unipersonal; branch encargados allowed.
- Art. 9 OC functions (15 items).
- Art. 10 appointment notice 5 business days; changes 5 bd; SEPRELAD may object 10 bd (silence = accepted); removal 5 bd; interim 48 h; temporary absence max 6 months; vacancy max 60 calendar days.
- Art. 11 Manual (Annex I), staff acknowledgment. Art. 12 Code of ethics, infractions + sanctions, acknowledgment, collective code for associations.
- Art. 13 annual internal control evaluation (OC may do it); report to SEPRELAD within 90 days after FY close; content Annex II.
- Art. 14 external audit by SEPRELAD-registered auditor; annual; report within 180 days after FY close. (Amended by Res 328/2026 - details pending.)
- Art. 15 annual training programme; records 5 years. Art. 16 minimum topics.
- Art. 17-30 CDD: Art. 19 stages, verification deferrable up to 60 days; Art. 20 records 5 years from last op/end; Art. 21 general regime fields; Art. 22 simplified (single payment <= 15 minimum wages; instalments <= 20 MW/yr; trade-in excluded; no suspicion); Art. 24 enhanced; Art. 26 refuse/terminate; Art. 27 profile; Art. 28 rating; Art. 29 monitoring rules approved by MA, analysis max 30 calendar days from alert; Art. 30 third-party reliance + sworn statement.
- Art. 31 register of ALL operations regardless of amount; Art. 32 retention 5 years incl. analyses and reasons for not reporting.
- Art. 33 ROS: unusual-op analysis up to 90 calendar days; ROS within 24 h of classifying as suspicious; SEPRELAD may return, 15 bd to fix. Art. 34 ROS content. Art. 35 via ROS Web; answer SEPRELAD requests in 4 business days. Art. 36 confidentiality / tipping-off. Art. 37 negative report: no ROS in 3 consecutive months -> report within 10 business days after the quarter. Art. 38 freeze immediately for UN-listed persons.
- Annexes I (manual), II (internal control report, 12 items), III (9 red flags), IV (lists: UN, FATF, OFAC, EU, others), V (risk criteria).

### Res 85/2015 (Base Legal) - still cited by SEPRELAD as RO basis
Source: [Base Legal Res 85/2015](https://baselegal.com.py/docs/cd1c84e2-67c0-11eb-86f7-525400c761ca/doc). Date 25 Mar 2015. Reglamento Art. 7: (1) ROS via ROS_WEB within 60 days (superseded by Res 196 Art. 33: 24 h); (2) negative report quarterly, first 5 days of Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct (superseded by Res 196 Art. 37: 10 business days after quarter); (3) RO: ALL import, purchase, sale and consignment operations, semiannual, first 5 days of January and July; originally Excel by e-mail per Annex A. Annex A Planilla 1 (sales/purchases): date; client name; CI/RUC; nationality; occupation; address; phone; cash paid; financed amount; number of instalments; brand, model, year; chassis no.; seller name; seller CI/RUC; SEPRELAD registration no. Planilla 2 (imports): customs declaration no.; official date; customs office; importer RUC + name; seller; customs broker; description; net/gross weight; FOB USD; country of origin/provenance; used >10 yrs. Annex B: sworn declaration. Art. 4: old USD 10,000 cash threshold (removed by Res 196). Res 85/15 Art. 10: Customs must require SEPRELAD registration certificate.
Memoria 2025 still cites "Capítulo I Art. 7 numeral 3 Res 085/2015 and Art. 31 Res 196/2020" as RO basis; RO stats reported by semester. Base Legal text of Res 196 says it repeals only "Art. 2" of Res 85/15.
Sworn declaration form still on SEPRELAD site: [declaracion-jurada-so-automotores.pdf](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/userfiles/files/biblioteca/declaracion-jurada-so-automotores.pdf): firm data, client data, operation type, payment (cash/cheque/other), currency, contado/cuotas, total amount, vehicle type/brand/model/year/chassis/colour, source of funds tick boxes (savings, sale of property/vehicle, dividends, salary, pension, inheritance, other), signatures.

### Ley 1015/97 consolidated (SEPRELAD PDF, created 12 Jun 2025)
Source: [Ley 1015/1997 actualizada](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/transparencia/transparencia/ley10151997actualizada.pdf). Amended by Ley 3783/2009, Ley 6497/2019, Ley 6960/2022.
- Art. 13 (Ley 6960/2022) obliged sectors list; vehicles not named, fall under "ñ) actividades y profesiones no financieras" / "u) actos de comercio en general que impliquen transferencias de dinero o valores"; list "no es taxativa"; designated by Res 85/15.
- Art. 14 CDD at start and continuously; SEPRELAD may set simplified/general/enhanced. Art. 15 measures: identify + verify with reliable independent sources; identify BO + reasonable verification; purpose; periodic monitoring; if CDD impossible: don't start, don't execute, terminate, ROS.
- Art. 16 risk-based procedures and systems; BO transparency.
- Art. 17 register ALL operations, national and international, with amounts, currencies, elements allowing reconstruction; include documents, correspondence, CDD records, analyses whether or not reported.
- Art. 18 keep 5 years from operation or end of relationship.
- Art. 19 ROS on any operation done or attempted, regardless of amount; ROS content + OC + SO data confidential. Art. 20 no tipping-off. Art. 21 internal control procedures; notify staff of duty. Art. 22 duty to provide info to SEPRELAD; secrecy not opposable.
- Art. 24 sanctions. Natural persons: warning note; public reprimand; fine up to 500 monthly minimum wages; fine 1-10% of operation; removal with 3-10 yr ban; (f-h oddly listed). Legal persons: warning note; public reprimand; fine up to 5,000 monthly minimum wages; fine up to 50% of operation; suspension/closure up to 1 year; revocation. Officers personally liable when involved.
- Art. 25 (Ley 6960/2022) grading: intent, past conduct, gains, self-correction, gravity, recidivism (register in Art. 28(8)).
- Art. 28(9) SEPRELAD regulates, supervises and sanctions SOs without natural supervisor; 28(10) fees.
- Art. 35 list of administrative faults (a)-(m): no written policies; no risk system; CDD failures; not keeping records; inadequate operation register; no compliance officer; obstructing inspection; late/no ROS; tipping-off; not cooperating; not doing external audit; any other breach.
- Art. 36 5-year limitation. Art. 37 appeals: reconsideration 10 business days; contencioso 18 business days. Art. 38 fines enforceable; where no natural supervisor all fine goes to SEPRELAD.
