# Paraguay AML/CFT law for vehicle importers, dealers and used-car lots, turned into product requirements

Status: complete as of 2026-10-10 (open questions at the end). Main legal text: SEPRELAD Resolution 196/2020, read in full on Base Legal ([Res 196/2020, Base Legal](https://baselegal.com.py/docs/78668570-6ae3-11eb-990a-525400c761ca); official scan: [SEPRELAD PDF](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/resoluciones/resoluciones/res-seprelad-n-196-20-automotores.pdf)). The parent law is Law 1015/97 in SEPRELAD's consolidated text of June 2025 ([Ley 1015/97 actualizada](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/transparencia/transparencia/ley10151997actualizada.pdf)). Article numbers below come from those two texts unless stated.

Abbreviations: SO = obliged subject ("sujeto obligado"). OC = compliance officer ("oficial de cumplimiento"). SIRO = SEPRELAD's online reporting system ("Sistema Integrado de Reporte de Operaciones", formerly ROS_WEB). ROS = suspicious operation report. RN = negative report ("reporte negativo"). RO = objective operations report ("reporte de operaciones"). FA = annual form ("formulario anual"). CDD = customer due diligence ("debida diligencia del cliente", DDC). PEP = politically exposed person. MW = monthly minimum wage. FY = fiscal year. "Máxima Autoridad" = the firm's top authority (owner, board or manager).

## Summary

- **The rule is Res 196/2020, still in force.** SEPRELAD signed it on 4 Sep 2020; Base Legal shows publication on 4 Dec 2020. It applies to anyone who commercially imports, buys, sells or consigns motor vehicles ([Res 196](https://baselegal.com.py/docs/78668570-6ae3-11eb-990a-525400c761ca)). It repealed only Art. 2 of the old Res 85/2015 (same source). SEPRELAD still cites Res 85/2015 Art. 7(3) together with Res 196 Art. 31 as the basis of the operations report ([Memoria 2025](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Memoria-Anual-de-Gestion-Ano-2025.pdf)).
- **One amendment is unread.** Res 328/2026 amends Art. 14 (external audit) of the annex to Res 196/20 and Res 201/20. I saw it only in a search snippet of the leyes.com.py index; its text is (unverified) ([leyes.com.py index](https://www.leyes.com.py/buscar/?c=28), HTTP 403 when fetched).
- **No size exemption.** Every importer, dealer, lot or consignor is obliged, whatever its size. The only reliefs are that the owner may be the OC in a one-person firm (Art. 8) and a lighter "simplified" CDD for small sales (Art. 22).
  - Simplified CDD covers a single payment of up to 15 MW. It also covers instalment sales of up to 20 MW of payments a year.
  - With the MW at Gs 3,044,000 from 1 Jul 2026, those limits are Gs 45.66 million and Gs 60.88 million. That is about USD 7,500 and USD 10,000 ([Bloomberg Línea](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/como-queda-el-salario-minimo-en-paraguay-tras-el-reajuste-del-5-anunciado-por-pena/); [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/america/america-latina/2026/06/18/el-presidente-de-paraguay-reajusto-un-5-el-salario-minimo-supero-a-la-inflacion-y-alcanzara-los-usd-500/)).
- **Recurring filings, all through SIRO:**
  - **Operations report (RO).** Every import, purchase, sale and consignment, with no amount threshold. It is filed each quarter, between the 11th and 20th of the month after the quarter ([Circular 02/2025 via Vouga](https://www.vouga.com.py/en/la-seprelad-recuerda-plazos-y-obligaciones-de-reporte-a-los-sujetos-obligados/); Res 196 Art. 31).
  - **Negative report (RN).** Due within 10 business days after any quarter with no ROS (Art. 37).
  - **Annual form (FA).** Due by 31 May ([Memoria 2025](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Memoria-Anual-de-Gestion-Ano-2025.pdf)).
  - **Internal control report.** Due within 90 days of FY end, i.e. 31 March ([SEPRELAD, 31 Mar 2026](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=3836)).
  - **External audit report.** It must come from a SEPRELAD-registered auditor and is due within 180 days of FY end, i.e. 30 June (Art. 14; [Circular 02/2025](https://www.vouga.com.py/en/la-seprelad-recuerda-plazos-y-obligaciones-de-reporte-a-los-sujetos-obligados/)).
  - **Annual SIRO fee ("canon").** Gs 321,000 in 2025, due 31 May. **Gs 331,000 in 2026** for the "Automotores" row, due **30 June 2026**, 2% surcharge per month late ([Res 48/2025](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/userfiles/files/resoluciones/resolucion-n48-2025-canon-anual-2025.pdf); [Res 56/2026 scan](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/resoluciones/resoluciones/RESOLUCION%20N%C2%B0%2056.pdf); [SEPRELAD notice](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=3710)).
  - **Annual confirmation of the firm's SIRO data.** Introduced by Res 435/2026 of 20 Aug 2026 ([Vouga](https://www.vouga.com.py/en/seprelad-implementa-una-nueva-funcionalidad-para-la-actualizacion-y-confirmacion-de-datos/)). SIRO enforces it with a blocking pop-up form. It reached financial sectors on 15 Jun 2026 and real estate on 5 Oct 2026; vehicles were not yet named on 10 Oct 2026 ([SEPRELAD, 15 Jun 2026](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=4042); [SEPRELAD, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=4412)).
  - **Event-driven filings.** A ROS is due within 24 hours of classing an operation as suspicious (Art. 33). OC changes must be notified within 5 business days (Art. 10).
- **What must exist on paper.** It is never filed but is always inspected:
  - a manual (Annex I) and a code of ethics, both with staff acknowledgements (Art. 11-12);
  - the OC appointment (Art. 8-10);
  - a risk self-assessment every 2 years, with the method checked every 4 years (Art. 3);
  - an annual training plan and records (Art. 15-16);
  - client files with fixed data fields (Art. 21-22);
  - client risk ratings (Art. 28, Annex V);
  - monitoring rules approved by management, with alerts analysed within 30 days (Art. 29);
  - a register of all operations (Art. 31);
  - a register of unusual operations that were not reported (Art. 9);
  - list-screening evidence against UN, FATF, OFAC and EU lists (Annex IV).
  - Everything is kept for 5 years (Art. 20, 32; Law 1015 Art. 18).
- **SIRO takes bulk files.** The SIRO sign-up form asks whether the firm will send ROs "por archivo JSON" in a format SEPRELAD supplies ([SIRO registration guide](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2-Inscripcion.pdf)). For real estate, SEPRELAD switches the firm to JSON on request by note, after which one-by-one entry is closed ([SEPRELAD, 22 Aug 2025](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=3156); the request goes by note to mesaentrada@seprelad.gov.py with firm name, RUC, OC name and institutional e-mail). The vehicle-sector JSON schema was not found (unverified). SIRO has no public API that I found, so the product drafts the filing and a person submits it.
- **Outsourcing is expressly allowed.** Circular UIF-SEPRELAD/SE 02/2025 (14 Nov 2025) says outside management or advisory services may prepare:
  - manuals, codes of ethics, self-assessments and risk matrices;
  - internal and external audit reports;
  - watch-list screening and monitoring;
  - and may prepare *and submit* the reports SEPRELAD requires.
  - Source: [Vouga](https://www.vouga.com.py/en/la-seprelad-recuerda-plazos-y-obligaciones-de-reporte-a-los-sujetos-obligados/). I saw this in a law-firm summary, not the circular itself (unverified).
- **Enforcement is real and rising.**
  - 2024: 454 vehicle firms received warning notes for missed objective duties (RN, RO, FA, internal control, audit) ([Memoria 2024](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/2024.pdf)). A formal sanction case ("sumario") was opened against one vehicle firm by Res 507/2024 (same source).
  - 2025: about 35 on-site inspections of vehicle firms, chosen by risk matrix. One vehicle firm received a "significant" money fine ([Memoria 2025](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Memoria-Anual-de-Gestion-Ano-2025.pdf)).
  - The fine scale goes up to 5,000 MW for firms (about Gs 15.2 billion) and 500 MW for individuals. Fines can also be a share of the operation: up to 50% for firms and 1-10% for individuals (Law 1015 Art. 24).
- **No regional variation in the law.** Paraguay has one national regime and one supervisor (SEPRELAD). Geography enters only as a risk factor. The FA groups departments into 3 zones, and Zone 1 holds Asunción-Central and the border departments ([FA instructions](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/userfiles/files/resoluciones/06-22-instructivo-formulario-anual-automotores.pdf)).
- **Changes coming:**
  - a new FA and vehicle risk matrix "for the 2026 period";
  - a SIRO risk-matrix module, starting with vehicles, that pre-fills data from ROs and asks firms to *declare* their manual, self-assessment and training;
  - new RO fields under study;
  - Res 435/2026 annual data confirmation;
  - the Res 328/2026 audit change;
  - a new national risk assessment.
  - Sources: [Memoria 2025](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Memoria-Anual-de-Gestion-Ano-2025.pdf) and the sources above.
  - Separately, the new data-protection law 7593/2025 applies from about late 2027 ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/paraguay-adopta-su-ley-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales/)).
- **Product.** 118 testable requirements follow, each traced to an article. The core is:
  - a sales/KYC log that produces the quarterly RO (web-entry checklist or JSON) and the FA totals;
  - a deadline calendar;
  - generators for the manual, code, OC appointment, risk self-assessment and Annex II internal control report;
  - PEP and list screening with SIRO UN-list change alerts;
  - an alert-to-ROS workflow with legal timers and strict confidentiality;
  - an inspection and audit evidence pack.

## Who is obliged

**Legal chain.**
- Law 1015/97 Art. 13 (text of Law 6960/2022) lists obliged sectors. Vehicle traders are not named one by one. They fall under "ñ) las actividades y profesiones no financieras" and "u) las que realicen actos de comercio en general, que impliquen transferencias de dinero o valores". The list "no es taxativa" ([Ley 1015/97 actualizada](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/transparencia/transparencia/ley10151997actualizada.pdf)).
- SEPRELAD designated the sector as obliged by Res 85/2015 of 25 Mar 2015 ([Res 85/2015, Base Legal](https://baselegal.com.py/docs/cd1c84e2-67c0-11eb-86f7-525400c761ca/doc)).
- Res 196/2020 now sets its duties ([Res 196](https://baselegal.com.py/docs/78668570-6ae3-11eb-990a-525400c761ca)).
- SEPRELAD supervises and sanctions SOs with no other ("natural") supervisor (Law 1015 Art. 28(9)).

**Who exactly** (Res 196 title and Art. 1; [Ferrere summary](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-personas-fisicas-o-juridicas-involucrad/)):

| Who | Condition | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Natural or legal persons who import motor vehicles | Commercial activity | Includes new-car distributors and used-car importers. The FA has a separate imports section ([FA instructions](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/userfiles/files/resoluciones/06-22-instructivo-formulario-anual-automotores.pdf)). |
| Natural or legal persons who buy and sell motor vehicles | Commercial activity | Used-car lots ("playas de autos"), dealers ("concesionarias"). |
| Natural or legal persons who take vehicles on consignment for sale | Commercial activity | Consignment lots. |
| "Vehículo automotor" | New or used land unit with 2 or more wheels, self-propelled, engine 600 cc or more. Includes motorcycles, cars, trucks, machinery, and also trailers without their own engine. The list is not exhaustive. | Res 196 Art. 1. Small motorcycles under 600 cc are outside the definition (my reading). |

**Thresholds and exemptions.**
- **No turnover, staff or amount threshold for being obliged.** Res 196 has no de minimis rule. The old USD 10,000 cash threshold of Res 85/2015 Art. 4 is gone ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-personas-fisicas-o-juridicas-involucrad/)).
- **One-person firms.** The owner may act as OC (Art. 8).
- **Simplified CDD only, never exemption.** It covers a single payment of up to 15 MW, or instalments of up to 20 MW a year. A trade-in vehicle owned by the client does not count toward the total. It never applies when there is suspicion (Art. 22).
- **Private persons selling their own car once** are not covered, because the rule targets commercial activity (my reading of Art. 1 and Res 85/2015; unverified).
- **Delegation does not move the duty.** The system must cover processes delegated to intermediaries (Art. 2). Reliance on third parties for identification leaves the SO responsible (Art. 30).

**Registration.**
- Every SO must be on SEPRELAD's "Registro Administrativo de Sujetos Obligados sin supervisión natural" (Res 218/11). Since 2022 it registers online in SIRO under Res 188/2022 ([Memoria 2025](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Memoria-Anual-de-Gestion-Ano-2025.pdf); [Res 188/2022 index entry](https://www.portafoliocorporativo.com.py/legislaciones/seprelad-resolucion-n-188-6476)).
- Old Res 85/2015 certificates lapsed on 1 Sep 2022 for firms that did not re-register by 31 Aug 2022 ([SEPRELAD notice, search snippet; page now 404](https://seprelad.gov.py/aviso-importante-para-el-sector-automotores-n696)).
- A firm that stops trading must request removal ("baja") through the SIRO module of Res 460/2025 ([Memoria 2025](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Memoria-Anual-de-Gestion-Ano-2025.pdf)).
- Banks, finance companies, exchange houses, insurers and cooperatives were told to check the SEPRELAD registration certificate during their own CDD (Res 85/2015 resolution Art. 4, [Base Legal](https://baselegal.com.py/docs/cd1c84e2-67c0-11eb-86f7-525400c761ca/doc)). GAFILAT found that unregistered firms cannot access financial institutions (GAFILAT MER 2022 para. 445, as cited in the lead report: [MER](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf)).

**How many.**
- 2025 activity: 453 vehicle SOs filed ROs, covering 82,591 sales, 51,698 imports and 21,730 purchases. 331 filed the FA. 229 vehicle registrations were completed. 12 vehicle SOs were removed from the register ([Memoria 2025](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Memoria-Anual-de-Gestion-Ano-2025.pdf)).
- 2024: 454 vehicle firms were warned and 357 filed the FA ([Memoria 2024](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/2024.pdf)).

Penalty key (L1015 Art. 24). The fault list is in L1015 Art. 35(a)-(m):
- **Scale F (firm).** Warning note; public reprimand; fine up to 5,000 MW (Gs 15.22 billion at the July 2026 MW); fine up to 50% of the operation; suspension, closure or disqualification up to 1 year; revocation.
- **Scale P (person: owner, OC, manager).** Warning note; public reprimand; fine up to 500 MW (Gs 1.522 billion); fine of 1-10% of the operation; removal with a 3-10 year ban.
- Officers of a sanctioned firm get scale P when involved (Art. 24, last para.). Grading considers intent, past conduct, gains, self-correction, gravity and recidivism (Art. 25).
- In practice so far, objective-duty failures draw warning notes first (Mem24).

## Supervisors and enforcement evidence

**Supervisor.**
- SEPRELAD is both the FIU and the supervisor of SOs with no natural supervisor (L1015 Art. 27-28). Its Dirección General de Supervisión y Regulaciones (DGSR) supervises and inspects.
- Supervision follows the risk-based Supervision Manual, Res 239/2020 ([Mem24](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/2024.pdf)).
- Sanction cases follow Res 31/2019: preliminary investigation, then "sumario administrativo" ([Mem25](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Memoria-Anual-de-Gestion-Ano-2025.pdf)).
- Fines collected go wholly to SEPRELAD where there is no natural supervisor (L1015 Art. 38).
- Appeals: reconsideration within 10 business days, then the administrative court within 18 business days (Art. 37). Sanction cases expire after 5 years (Art. 36).

**How SEPRELAD checks.**
1. **Desk monitoring in SIRO.** In 2024 SEPRELAD checked 2,043 SOs through SIRO modules and warned 1,692 of them: 1,238 real-estate and 454 vehicle firms. The warnings were under Res 410/2024 and 681/2024, for missing RN (Art. 37), RO (Art. 31), FA (Res 246/2022), internal control (Art. 13) and external audit (Art. 14) ([Mem24](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/2024.pdf)). SEPRELAD also wrote to vehicle firms in Aug 2024 that controls were focused on them ([SEPRELAD](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=1044)).
2. **Risk matrix.** The FA and RO data feed a sector risk matrix that picks inspection targets ([Mem25](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Memoria-Anual-de-Gestion-Ano-2025.pdf)).
3. **On-site inspections.** 2025 had 76 inspections, 35 of them in the vehicle sector; a second table in the same report shows 38. Selection used exposure, size, activity type and compliance history (Mem25). Earlier counts were 6, 0, 4, 20, 10 and 0 for 2016-2021 (GAFILAT MER 2022 Table 63, via the lead report: [MER](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf)).
4. **Random inspections since 2021 (Res 36/2021).** They asked for the manual, the OC appointment and training records "y otros" ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/inspecciones-aleatorias-de-seprelad-bajo-resolucion-36-21/)).
5. **Feedback after inspections.** 51 vehicle-sector people attended remedial-action feedback sessions (Mem25). Online feedback sessions on the 2025 in-situ and extra-situ results ran 1-3 Dec 2025 ([SEPRELAD](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=3566)).

**Fine amounts at the July 2026 MW (Gs 3,044,000).**
- 500 MW = Gs 1.522 billion (about USD 250,000).
- 5,000 MW = Gs 15.22 billion (about USD 2.5 million).
- The USD values use Infobae's "MW about USD 500" ([Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/america/america-latina/2026/06/18/el-presidente-de-paraguay-reajusto-un-5-el-salario-minimo-supero-a-la-inflacion-y-alcanzara-los-usd-500/)).
- The size of the 2025 vehicle-sector fine was not published (unverified).

**Criminal exposure.**
- Criminal Code Art. 196 punishes money laundering. Art. 196(5) also punishes anyone who, "por negligencia grave", does not know the illicit origin of an object: up to 2 years in prison or a fine ([SEPRELAD FAQ quoting CP Art. 196](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?page_id=1810)).
- For a dealer who takes large cash, documented CDD is the defence against a gross-negligence claim (my inference).

**Bank access.**
- An unregistered firm cannot access financial institutions, and banks must check registration (GAFILAT MER para. 445 via the lead report; Res 85/2015 resolution Art. 4, [Base Legal](https://baselegal.com.py/docs/cd1c84e2-67c0-11eb-86f7-525400c761ca/doc)).

## Regional differences

- **None in the law.** Paraguay is unitary. Law 1015/97, Res 196/2020 and SEPRELAD apply the same way in all 17 departments and Asunción. I found no departmental or municipal AML rule (search, 2026-10-10).
- **Geography is a mandatory risk factor.** Annex V asks the firm to weigh country and locality of birth or residence, FATF high-risk countries, its own country list, and "differences between domestic zones" ([Res 196](https://baselegal.com.py/docs/78668570-6ae3-11eb-990a-525400c761ca)).
- **The FA uses three zones** ([FA instructions](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/userfiles/files/resoluciones/06-22-instructivo-formulario-anual-automotores.pdf)):
  - Zone 1: Central-Asunción, Concepción, San Pedro, Amambay, Canindeyú, Alto Paraná, Itapúa, Región Occidental (Chaco).
  - Zone 2: Caaguazú, Guairá, Cordillera, Caazapá.
  - Zone 3: Paraguarí, Ñeembucú, Misiones.
  - Zone 1 contains the capital area and the main border departments with Brazil and Argentina. That is where most imports and cash would be expected (my inference).
- **Reach outside Asunción.** In 2024 SEPRELAD ran sector training in Encarnación, Filadelfia, Loma Plata, Guairá, Caaguazú and Katueté/Concepción ([SEPRELAD posts via REST search](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?cat=36); titles listed 2024-07 to 2024-11). Buyers are spread across the country, so the product must work fully online.
- **Language.** All forms and rules are in Spanish. SEPRELAD material also carries Guaraní text ([Informe 2022](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/userfiles/files/biblioteca/informe2022.pdf)), but no Guaraní filing duty was found.

**A. Set-up and scope**

1. **Firm profile.** Store: person type (física or jurídica); RUC; name; activities from import, purchase, sale and consignment (multi-select); head office and each branch with department and city; SEPRELAD registration number and constancia date; the SIRO "Envío JSON" choice; staff count; fiscal-year end (default 31 Dec). Test: set-up cannot finish with no activity ticked, and every deadline in group K moves when the FY end changes. Basis: R196 Art. 1; SIRO-G.
2. **No size switch.** No duty may be switched off for size or turnover. The only size option is "one-person firm", which allows the owner to be the OC. Test: a one-person firm still gets every task in groups B to L. Basis: R196 Art. 8 (owner as OC); R196 has no de minimis rule.
3. **Vehicle scope.** Each vehicle record holds a type from the Art. 1 list (motorcycle, tricycle, quad, car, pickup, truck, bus, tractor, agricultural, construction, industrial or road machinery, trailer, other) and engine size in cc. A self-propelled unit under 600 cc gets the flag "outside the Art. 1 definition, confirm". A trailer is in scope with no engine. Test: a 125 cc motorbike shows the flag; a trailer does not. Basis: R196 Art. 1.
4. **Zone mapping.** Every branch and every client address maps to one of the three FA zones. Test: a client in Caaguazú lands in Zone 2; one in Misiones in Zone 3. Basis: FA-I.
5. **Registration pack.** For a new firm, produce a data sheet that mirrors the SIRO registration form: firm data, banks used, branches, services, years in sector, staff, annual income, assets, liabilities, equity (Gs), shareholders, directors, titular and alternate OC. Add the list of PDFs to upload and the fee and receipt steps. Test: every field of the SIRO form appears once in the pack. Basis: L1015 Art. 28(9); Res 188/2022; SIRO-G; [SEPRELAD steps](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=3140).
6. **Registration status.** Track status (requested, fee paid, constancia issued, deregistered). Show a red banner on every page while no constancia exists, because banks check it. Test: a firm without a constancia date sees the banner. Basis: R85 resolution Art. 4; GAFILAT MER 2022 para. 445.
7. **Deregistration.** When the user marks "activity ended", produce the SIRO "Baja" checklist and keep all records read-only for 5 years. Test: after "activity ended", new sales cannot be entered but all records stay exportable. Basis: Res 460/2025 (Mem25); R196 Art. 32.

**B. Governance and the compliance officer**

8. **Top-authority approvals.** Store who the Máxima Autoridad is (owner, manager or board). Every item that needs its approval must record approver name, role, date and a signed file: policies, manual, code, OC appointment and removal, monitoring rules, training plan, and the decision to send a ROS. A document cannot be "in force" without that record. Test: publishing the manual without approval is refused. Basis: R196 Art. 7, 10, 11, 12, 15, 29(4).
9. **OC eligibility check.** The appointment wizard asks: senior rank, direct access to the Máxima Autoridad, autonomy, no conflicting role. It warns if the owner is named OC in a firm that is not one-person. For an interim OC it skips only the rank question. Test: an interim with rank "staff" passes; a titular with rank "staff" is flagged. Basis: R196 Art. 8, 10.
10. **OC notice data.** The OC record holds: names; ID type and number; nationality; office address; phone; e-mail; home address; sketch-map file; utility-bill file; CV file. The SIRO notice draft stays "incomplete" until all are present. Test: removing the CV marks the draft incomplete. Basis: R196 Art. 10.
11. **OC change timers.** Any change to the OC record, including phone, e-mail or home address, starts a 5-business-day timer to notify SEPRELAD. A removal needs a Máxima Autoridad decision with reasons, then a 5-business-day timer. An interim appointment starts a 48-hour timer. Test: a phone change on Friday 2 Oct 2026 is due Friday 9 Oct 2026 (no holiday in between). Basis: R196 Art. 10.
12. **Objection window.** After the OC notice, show "SEPRELAD may object until" = 10 business days counted from the day after receipt; then mark "accepted by silence". Basis: R196 Art. 10.
13. **Absence and vacancy limits.** Track absences of the titular OC: warn at 5 months, flag a breach at 6 consecutive months. Track vacancy: warn at day 45, flag at day 60 (calendar days from the day after the OC left). Test: for an OC who leaves on 1 Mar, the vacancy hits its 60-day limit on 30 Apr and shows a breach from 1 May. Basis: R196 Art. 10.
14. **Standing interim.** Allow a pre-named interim OC who takes over automatically when an absence is logged. Basis: R196 Art. 10, footnote.
15. **OC duties map.** Show the 15 OC functions as a checklist. Link each to the module that proves it (e.g. function 7 to the screening log, function 9 to the unusual-not-reported register, function 11 to the annual report). Test: each function shows linked evidence or "no evidence". Basis: R196 Art. 9.
16. **OC annual report to management.** Generate a yearly OC report from system data, signed off by the Máxima Autoridad. It must describe any significant change compared with the internal control evaluation. Basis: R196 Art. 9(11); Annex II, last paragraph.
17. **Reserved codes.** Store any reserved code SEPRELAD assigns to the firm or OC. Use the code, not names, on ROS drafts and other OC messages. Basis: R196 Art. 10, 36.

**C. Risk self-assessment**

18. **Four factors.** The self-assessment covers clients, products and services, distribution channels and geography. Each factor has written analysis, inherent risk, controls and residual risk. Test: the PDF has one section per factor with text, not only scores. Basis: R196 Art. 3-4; L1015 Art. 16.
19. **Pre-filled facts.** The draft pulls facts from the system: cash share of sales, share of PEP and non-resident clients, sales by zone, imports by country, volumes. Test: changing one sale from transfer to cash changes the cash share in a new draft. Basis: R196 Art. 3-4, Annex V.
20. **ENR reference.** The assessment cites the national risk assessment (ENR) version in force and its vehicle-sector findings. Basis: R196 Art. 16, 28.
21. **Review cycle.** Next assessment due = last approval + 2 years. Next method check due = last check + 4 years. Warnings at 60 and 30 days. Test: approval on 1 Jan 2026 shows next due 1 Jan 2028. Basis: R196 Art. 3.
22. **Versions.** Every version is kept with its approval record and becomes read-only once approved. Basis: R196 Art. 3 (documented, available to SEPRELAD), 32.
23. **Pre-launch risk report.** Before a new product, new technology (e.g. online sales, crypto payments), a relevant change, or a branch in a new zone, the user must complete a risk report. A branch in a department not used before stays "pending risk report" until approved. Test: adding an Alto Paraná branch to a Central-only firm leaves it pending. Basis: R196 Art. 5-6.
24. **SIRO declaration sheet.** Produce a one-page sheet listing the manual, the self-assessment and the training with dates and approvals, to back what the planned SIRO risk-matrix module will ask firms to declare. Basis: Mem25 (module in development; its fields are unverified).

**D. Manual and code of ethics**

25. **Manual generator.** The manual contains every item of the five Annex I blocks. A coverage checklist fails if any item is missing. Test: removing the "internal sanctions" part shows a gap. Basis: R196 Art. 11, Annex I.
26. **Firm-specific content.** The manual includes the firm's own facts: roles and names, monitoring rules in force, who classes alerts as unusual, CDD thresholds, retention rules. Basis: R196 Art. 11; Art. 29(7) and footnote 14.
27. **Red flags.** The manual lists the nine Annex III red flags plus the firm's own. Basis: R196 Annex III; Art. 9(8).
28. **Update triggers.** When a legal source used by the manual changes in the content library (e.g. Res 328/2026, a new FA), the manual is marked "review needed" and the OC gets a task. Basis: R196 Art. 9(3).
29. **Code of ethics.** The code states that breaches are infractions with proportionate sanctions and includes the confidentiality duty. Basis: R196 Art. 12.
30. **Shared code.** A firm may adopt an association's common code by upload, with its own approval record. Basis: R196 Art. 12.
31. **Staff acknowledgements.** Each employee confirms reading the manual and the code, per version, by e-signature or signed scan. A list shows who is pending. Test: a new manual version resets everyone to "pending". Basis: R196 Art. 11-12; Annex I block 5.

**E. Training**

32. **Annual plan.** A yearly training plan proposed by the OC and approved by the Máxima Autoridad. Basis: R196 Art. 15.
33. **Minimum topics.** The plan fails validation if it lacks the Art. 16 topics: ML/TF offences, the ENR, the rules, typologies, red flags and reporting procedures. Basis: R196 Art. 16.
34. **Training register.** Per session: date, topic, trainer, duration, attendees, materials and test result. Kept 5 years. Basis: R196 Art. 15.
35. **Non-attendance.** Flag staff who missed training and record the make-up session or internal sanction. Basis: R196 Art. 15.
36. **Built-in course.** Ship a short Spanish course for sellers on Res 196, red flags and the client form, with a quiz, so a small lot can deliver its plan. Basis: R196 Art. 15-16 (product choice).
37. **Rule-change notices.** A change in the content library creates a "tell staff" task and logs who read the notice. Basis: R196 Annex II item 10.

**F. Client due diligence**

38. **Party roles.** Each operation links parties by role: buyer, seller (when the firm buys), consignor, importer or foreign supplier, representative or attorney, beneficial owner, third-party payer. Basis: R196 Art. 21, 31; Annex III flag 7.
39. **Regime choice.** The system proposes the CDD regime per operation. Simplified only if all hold: single payment of 15 MW or less, or instalment payments of 20 MW or less a year; low risk; no suspicion; not in a mandatory-enhanced group. Enhanced for non-residents, trusts, non-profits, PEPs and high-risk clients. General otherwise. Test: a single cash payment of Gs 50 million on 1 Aug 2026 goes to general (limit Gs 45.66 million). Basis: R196 Art. 22, 24.
40. **Trade-in rule.** For the simplified-regime test, exclude the value of the client's own vehicle handed in as a down payment, or money shown to come from selling a vehicle the client owned. Store the proof (e.g. title). Test: a Gs 60 million price with a Gs 20 million trade-in counts as Gs 40 million. Basis: R196 Art. 22, footnote 12.
41. **What counts as payments.** In the instalment test, "payments" = instalments, deliveries, "refuerzos" or equivalents in a 12-month period. Basis: R196 Art. 22, footnote 12.
42. **Minimum-wage table.** The MW is a dated table, not a constant. Each operation uses the MW in force on its date. Test: an operation on 30 Jun 2026 uses Gs 2,899,048; one on 1 Jul 2026 uses Gs 3,044,000. Basis: R196 Art. 22; Decreto 6225/26 ([ABC](https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2026/06/26/reajuste-del-salario-minimo-estos-son-los-pagos-por-hora-dia-y-jornada-nocturna/)).
43. **General regime, natural person.** Names; ID type and number; nationality; address; phone and e-mail; RUC or SET non-taxpayer certificate; source-of-funds declaration; proof that income fits the operations. The file cannot be "complete" without them. Basis: R196 Art. 21.
44. **General regime, legal person.** Name; RUC; deed and amendments (or the last amendment); address; phone and e-mail; copy of powers of representation; beneficial owners and legal representatives; source-of-funds declaration; proof that income fits. Basis: R196 Art. 21.
45. **Simplified regime fields.** Natural person: names, ID, activity, phone or e-mail, address. Legal person: name, RUC certificate, agents and legal representatives with names and ID, activity, phone or e-mail, main office address. Verification may never fall below this. Basis: R196 Art. 22, 25.
46. **Document capture.** Capture ID and documents by phone camera or upload. Store date, time, user and verification method (original seen, public source checked). Basis: R196 Art. 19-20, 25, 28.
47. **Deferred verification.** "Verify later" is allowed only if the manual's risk rule allows it. It sets a deadline of at most 60 days and blocks new operations with that client after the deadline. Test: a client opened on 1 Mar with deferred verification cannot buy on 1 May. Basis: R196 Art. 19.
48. **Source-of-funds declaration.** Generate a per-sale sworn declaration in Spanish with: firm data; salesperson; client data; operation type; payment means (cash, cheque, transfer, other); currency; cash or instalments; total amount; vehicle type, brand, model, year, chassis, colour; source tick-boxes (savings, sale of property or vehicle, dividends, salary, pension, inheritance, other); signatures. Capture by e-signature or signed scan. Basis: R196 Art. 21(7); R85 Annex B form (whether that exact form is still required is unverified).
49. **Income fit.** For general and enhanced clients, require income evidence (e.g. salary slip, tax return, bank statement) and record the reviewer's conclusion. Basis: R196 Art. 21(8)-(9).
50. **Beneficial owner.** For legal persons, capture each natural person with 10% or more of shares, more than 25% of votes, who manages or uses the assets, can appoint or remove management, or controls through the statutes. Store the basis of control. If none is found, record why. Test: a company with a 12% shareholder cannot be completed without that person. Basis: L1015 Art. 15, 16; Law 6446/2019 definition ([SEPRELAD FAQ](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?page_id=1810)).
51. **Attorneys and third-party buyers.** When a buyer acts by power of attorney or for someone else, require the power, identify the principal and record the source of funds. Otherwise raise Annex III flag 7. Basis: R196 Art. 21; Annex III(7).
52. **Refusal.** If mandatory CDD data is missing at closing, block "complete sale", offer "refuse or end relationship", and open an unusual-operation review. Test: closing a general-regime sale without a source of funds is blocked. Basis: R196 Art. 26; L1015 Art. 15.
53. **Third-party reliance.** When an intermediary (e.g. a finance company or broker) identified the client, require its sworn statement and copies of the documents. The firm stays responsible. Basis: R196 Art. 30.
54. **Periodic update.** Each client file has a next-review date set by risk (configurable, e.g. high 12 months, medium 24, low 36). A relevant change also triggers an update. Basis: R196 Art. 20, 27-28.

**G. PEPs and sanctions lists**

55. **PEP declaration.** At onboarding the client sees a short PEP explanation and signs a PEP sworn declaration (yes or no, office, relationship). Basis: R50 Art. 6.
56. **PEP scope.** The PEP flag covers the domestic offices listed in R50 Art. 4, foreign PEPs, officials of international organisations, relatives to the 2nd degree by blood or marriage, and entities where a PEP holds 10% or more. A former PEP stays PEP for 2 years after leaving office. Test: a client who left office 18 months ago is still PEP. Basis: R50 Art. 1-4, 7.
57. **PEP controls.** A PEP client cannot proceed without senior management approval, source of wealth and source of funds. The client goes on enhanced monitoring. Basis: R50 Art. 7; R196 Art. 24.
58. **PEP data source.** Support PEP list import (CSV) and an optional third-party PEP database. Store the source and date of each check. Basis: R50; R196 Annex V(1)(f).
59. **Sanctions screening.** Screen every client, beneficial owner, representative, supplier and counterparty, at onboarding and before each operation, against: the UN Security Council consolidated list; FATF high-risk and monitored jurisdictions; OFAC; the EU terrorist list; and any list SEPRELAD names. Store list, list date, match score and decision. Basis: R196 Art. 9(7), Annex IV.
60. **Re-screening on change.** On each UN list update, re-screen the whole client base within 24 hours and show new hits. The product polls the UN list daily, and the OC logs each SIRO list notice. Test: adding a test name to a mock list makes the matching client appear as a hit on the next run. Basis: Annex IV ("toda la base de clientes"); [SEPRELAD, 9 Jan 2026](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=3639); L6419.
61. **Confirmed match.** On a confirmed UN-list match: block the operation; prompt the firm to freeze funds or assets "immediately and without delay"; open a ROS draft; show the SEPRELAD contact steps. Basis: R196 Art. 38; L6419 (article numbers unverified).
62. **Country lists.** Keep FATF lists and the firm's own country list as dated tables. Use them in the geography factor and to flag clients linked to listed countries. Basis: R196 Annex IV(2), Annex V(3).

**H. Client risk rating and profile**

63. **Scoring model.** Every client gets a rating from a documented model with at least the Annex V criteria: person type; legal form; occupation or activity; whether the client is itself an SO; CDD regime; PEP; product type; channel; currency; cash or financial system; country and locality of birth, nationality or incorporation; country and locality of residence or operation; FATF and own lists; differences between domestic zones; real or expected volume. Test: the rating PDF shows the value of each criterion. Basis: R196 Art. 28, Annex V.
64. **ENR and self-assessment inputs.** The model weights reference the ENR findings and the firm's self-assessment. Basis: R196 Art. 28.
65. **Profile.** Each client has a profile: purpose, expected volume and frequency, activity, income. Basis: R196 Art. 27.
66. **Rating history.** Every rating change is stored with date, reason and user. Basis: R196 Art. 28 ("debidamente registrada").
67. **Rating drives regime.** A "high" rating forces the enhanced regime and senior approval. Basis: R196 Art. 24.

**I. Monitoring, unusual and suspicious operations**

68. **Default rules.** Ship rules that turn the nine Annex III red flags into measurable tests, with editable thresholds. Examples: flag 2, one client buys N or more vehicles within 60 days; flag 4, price below X% of a reference value; flag 5, an instalment plan paid off early in cash; flag 6, cash advances above a threshold within N days; flag 8, a PEP refuses the form; flag 9, an advance paid and then cancelled with a refund by transfer. Basis: R196 Art. 29(1)-(2), Annex III.
69. **Rule approval.** Rules and thresholds take effect only after Máxima Autoridad approval. Changes are versioned. Only authorised roles can see them. Basis: R196 Art. 29(4).
70. **Rule method.** Generate a document explaining how each rule and threshold was set. Basis: R196 Art. 29(4).
71. **Staff alerts.** Any user can raise an alert from a sale screen. It goes only to the OC. Basis: R196 Art. 29(3); Annex I block 4 (internal reporting channels).
72. **Alert register.** Each alert stores: operation ID; date, time and source of the alert; steps taken; final reasoned decision with date and time; attached files. Basis: R196 Art. 29(6).
73. **30-day timer.** Each alert must be classed "unusual" or "not unusual" within 30 calendar days. Warnings at day 20 and day 27; a breach is logged. Test: an alert raised on 1 Mar is overdue on 1 Apr. Basis: R196 Art. 29(8); Art. 33, footnote 15.
74. **90-day timer.** An unusual operation must be closed as "suspicious" or "not suspicious, with reasons" within 90 calendar days. Basis: R196 Art. 33.
75. **Unusual-not-reported register.** Every unusual operation closed as not suspicious enters a register with its reasons, kept 5 years. Test: the register export has a non-empty reason for each case. Basis: R196 Art. 9(9), 32.
76. **ROS draft.** Classing an operation as suspicious creates a ROS draft with the Art. 34 content: identities of all parties with their CDD data; third parties; list and description of operations (dates, amounts, currencies, accounts used and linked when paid through banks, services, place); irregularities; other information; attachments. It warns if the narrative only discusses amounts. Basis: R196 Art. 34.
77. **24-hour timer.** Classing as suspicious starts a 24-hour countdown with alerts to the OC and the Máxima Autoridad. The ROS record stores the decision time, approval, submission time and SIRO receipt. Basis: R196 Art. 7, 33, 35.
78. **Returned ROS.** Log a ROS returned by SEPRELAD and start a 15-business-day timer. Show "counts as not filed" if it lapses. Basis: R196 Art. 33.
79. **No identity in the ROS.** The ROS draft must not contain the firm's or the OC's names. It uses reserved codes. Test: a text check of the draft finds no firm name, RUC or OC name. Basis: R196 Art. 36.
80. **Tipping-off guard.** ROS and alert decisions are visible only to the OC, the Máxima Autoridad and named assistants. Sellers see no ROS status, and the client file shows no flag that reveals a report. Test: a seller account cannot reach any ROS or alert-decision field by screen, search or export. Basis: R196 Art. 33, 36; L1015 Art. 19-20.
81. **File update after an alert.** When a client justifies an unusual operation, the client file and profile must be updated before the alert can close. Basis: R196 Art. 29(9).

**J. Operations register and the quarterly RO**

82. **Complete register.** Every import, purchase, sale and consignment is recorded, whatever the amount. There is no option to leave a client out. Deleting an operation needs a reason and leaves an audit trail. Basis: R196 Art. 31; L1015 Art. 17.
83. **Sale and purchase fields.** At least the R85 Annex A fields: date; client name; CI or RUC; nationality; occupation; address; phone; cash paid; amount financed; number of instalments; brand, model, year; chassis number; seller name and CI or RUC; the firm's SEPRELAD registration number. Also: payment form (cash, card, transfer, compensation or trade-in), currency, modality (cash price or credit), PEP yes or no, colour. Basis: R85 Annex A; R196 Art. 31; the real-estate SIRO spec as a pattern (vehicle fields unverified).
84. **Import fields.** Customs declaration number and date; customs office; importer RUC and name; foreign seller; customs broker; description; net and gross weight; FOB value in USD; country of origin and provenance; units; payment orders and channels. Basis: R85 Annex A (imports sheet); FA-I imports section.
85. **Consignment fields.** Consignor identity with full CDD, consignment contract date, agreed price, and a link to the later sale. Basis: R196 Art. 1, 31 (field list is my design).
86. **Bulk import.** Import operations from a CSV or Excel template and check each row: ID format, required fields, chassis present and not duplicated in open stock. Test: a row without a chassis is rejected with a message; the others load. Basis: R196 Art. 20 (IT records), 31.
87. **Quarterly RO package.** Per quarter produce (a) a JSON file in SEPRELAD's schema if the firm chose JSON, or (b) a web-entry checklist in SIRO field order. The filing window is the 11th to the 20th of January, April, July and October. Test: the Q3 2026 package is ready from 1 Oct and due 20 Oct 2026. Basis: R196 Art. 31; Res 216/2021; C02; SIRO-G (JSON option). The vehicle JSON schema is unverified.
88. **Schema versions.** The RO field map and JSON schema carry versions and effective dates, because SEPRELAD is studying new RO fields. Basis: Mem25.
89. **Reconciliation.** Before filing, reconcile RO counts with the sales ledger and with the FA totals. A difference blocks "ready to file". Basis: R196 Art. 31; Annex II item 5.
90. **Filing evidence.** Per RO, store the SIRO submission date, period, record count and any receipt or screenshot. Basis: Mem24 (SEPRELAD checks RO filing from SIRO data).

**K. Periodic filings and the deadline calendar**

91. **One calendar.** It shows: RO (11th-20th of Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct); RN (10 business days after each quarter with no ROS); internal control report (90 days after FY end); external audit report (180 days after FY end); FA (31 May); canon (date set in each year's resolution, 30 Jun in 2026); SIRO data confirmation (yearly, and 5 business days after a change); self-assessment (2 years); method check (4 years); training plan (yearly); OC notices (5 business days or 48 h). Each item sends e-mail and WhatsApp reminders at 14, 7 and 1 day. Basis: R196 Art. 3, 10, 13, 14, 15, 31, 37; C02; R56; R435; Mem25.
92. **Date rule.** When the computed date and SEPRELAD's published date differ, show the earlier one as the target and both as notes. Example for FY 2026: 90 days after 31 Dec is 31 Mar 2027, while C02 gives 30 March; 180 days is 29 Jun 2027, while C02 gives 30 June. A per-year override with a source link handles extensions (e.g. Res 158/2026 moved 31 Mar 2026 to 8 Apr 2026). Basis: R196 Art. 13-14; C02; [SEPRELAD, 31 Mar 2026](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=3836).
93. **RN logic.** The RN task appears only for a quarter with no ROS filed. It is due 10 business days after the quarter ends. Test: a quarter with a ROS filed in February has no RN task. Basis: R196 Art. 37; Res 326/2022 (Mem24).
94. **FA builder.** Compute every FA figure for the calendar year from the register: sales clients by national, foreign, PEP, natural and legal person; vehicles sold; collections through banks or cooperatives, crypto and other goods; cash by currency with the Gs equivalent at the BCP rate; the same for purchases and suppliers; imports (units received, total value in Gs, foreign suppliers, units in transit, landed and received, payment orders, payment channels, cash); Zones 1-3 (head office, branches, clients by zone of residence); import countries; armoured vehicles yes or no; price range in USD. Test: FA totals equal the sum of the year's RO exports. Basis: FA-I; Res 246/2022.
95. **Exchange rates.** Store BCP reference rates by date for converting cash in foreign currency. Basis: FA-I.
96. **FA versions.** The FA field map is versioned. The new FA "for the 2026 period" must load as content, without a code change. Basis: Mem25.
97. **Internal control report.** Generate the Annex II report from system data: CDD actions; risk-assessment results; compliance with manual and code, breaches and sanctions; approvals; check of the operations register; new red flags; monthly counts and amounts of unusual operations and ROS; manual changes; training (content, number trained, repeat trainees); training review and rule-change notices; ended third-party CDD contracts; other points. The OC signs it, then the firm files it in SIRO. Basis: R196 Art. 13, Annex II; Res 202/2023.
98. **Monthly statistics.** Keep monthly counts and amounts of unusual operations and ROS for Annex II item 7. Basis: R196 Annex II.
99. **Auditor workspace.** An auditor role with read-only access to policies, manual, assessments, training, monitoring rules, registers and earlier findings. It includes a client-sample tool (random sample by risk tier, files exported as PDF or ZIP). ROS content is shown only if the OC allows it. Basis: R196 Art. 14(a)-(g).
100. **Auditor registration check.** Store the auditor's SEPRELAD registration and the date it was checked on the [SIRO public lookup](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/siro/consultaExterna/consultaExternaSoAe.xhtml). Warn if missing. Basis: R196 Art. 14; Res 218/2020.
101. **Findings tracker.** Audit and inspection findings become tasks with owner, due date and evidence. Open items carry into next year's audit as "follow-up of earlier findings". Basis: R196 Art. 14(g); Mem25 (remedial-action sessions).
102. **Canon task.** Show the yearly canon with the amount from the content library (Gs 331,000 in 2026), the BNF account, and the steps to download the liquidation in SIRO "Cuentas". After the user marks "paid", remind them to upload the receipt within 24 hours. Estimate the 2% monthly surcharge if late. Basis: R56; [SEPRELAD, 6 Feb 2026](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=3710).
103. **Data confirmation.** A yearly task to confirm SIRO firm and user data. An automatic task within 5 business days of any change to firm name, address, phone, mobile, e-mail or user data in the product. Basis: R435; [SEPRELAD, 15 Jun 2026](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=4042).
104. **SEPRELAD requests.** Log each information request, including ITC requests in SIRO, with a 4-business-day timer and the reply attached. Basis: R196 Art. 35; L1015 Art. 22.

**L. Records, security and privacy**

105. **Retention.** Keep operation records, CDD files, analyses, reasons for not reporting and training records for at least 5 years: from the operation, or from the end of the relationship for client data. Block deletion before then. Test: deleting a client whose last sale was 3 years ago is refused. Basis: R196 Art. 15, 20, 32; L1015 Art. 18.
106. **Fast retrieval.** Any client file, operation or register exports to PDF in under one minute, for an inspector or a SEPRELAD request. Basis: R196 Art. 20 (easy retrieval), 35.
107. **Audit trail.** Log every create, edit, delete, export and view on client, alert and ROS data, with user and time, in a tamper-evident store. Basis: R196 Art. 20, 32; L1015 Art. 17 (operations must be rebuildable).
108. **Roles.** At least: owner or Máxima Autoridad, OC, interim OC, seller, back office, auditor (read-only), and consultant with several client firms. Basis: R196 Art. 29(4), 33, 36.
109. **Security.** Encryption in transit and at rest, two-factor login, daily backups with a tested restore, and a stated hosting region. Pass an external security test before sale. Basis: R196 Art. 35 (OC must protect confidentiality); L7593 security duties (from about late 2027).
110. **Privacy.** A Spanish privacy notice for clients, a processor agreement with each firm, and a way to handle data-subject requests that respects AML retention. Basis: L7593; R196 Art. 32.

**M. Inspection readiness and multi-client use**

111. **Inspection pack.** One click produces a ZIP with: constancia; OC appointment and SIRO notice; manual, code and approvals; staff acknowledgements; self-assessment and method; training plan and records; monitoring rules and approval; alert and unusual-not-reported registers (ROS content left out unless the OC opts in); filing history (RO, RN, FA, internal control, audit, canon receipts); sample client files. Basis: Res 36/2021 random inspections asked for the manual, OC appointment and training "y otros" ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/inspecciones-aleatorias-de-seprelad-bajo-resolucion-36-21/)); Res 239/2020 supervision manual (Mem24).
112. **Gap score.** A red, amber or green status for each duty in the duty table above. Basis: R196 as a whole.
113. **Multi-client view.** An auditor or consultant sees all client firms' deadlines and gaps in one list. Data stays isolated per firm. Basis: C02 (outsourcing allowed, unverified); Mem25 (auditor register).
114. **Outsourcing record.** If an outside adviser prepares or submits filings, record the engagement, its scope, and that the firm stays responsible. Basis: C02 (unverified); R196 Art. 2, 30.

**N. Content and change management**

115. **Legal content library.** All deadlines, thresholds, MW values, fee amounts, list sources, form field maps and templates sit in a dated content library, not in code. Each item cites its legal source and effective date. Test: switching the RN deadline from "10 business days after the quarter" to "days 1-10 of the next month" (the pattern in the newer Res 111/2026 for another sector) needs no deploy. Basis: [Res 111/2026 notice](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=3850); Res 328/2026; Mem25.
116. **Business-day calendar.** Paraguayan public holidays are stored per year and used for every business-day timer. Basis: R196 Art. 10, 33, 35, 37 (business-day deadlines).
117. **Language.** All screens and documents are in Spanish (Paraguay). Basis: all rules and SIRO forms are in Spanish.
118. **Human decisions only.** Every rating, alert closure and ROS decision is recorded as made by a named user of the firm. The product never files in SIRO or closes an alert on its own. Basis: R196 Art. 7, 9(10), 30 (responsibility stays with the firm).

## Open questions

1. **Vehicle RO fields and JSON schema.** Res 216/2021 authorised SIRO for the vehicle RO ([SEPRELAD, 23 Aug 2024](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/?p=1044)), but I could not find its text, the current SIRO field list, or the vehicle JSON schema. The 2021 SIRO vehicle manual link in the press is dead ([La Nación, 12 Feb 2021](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2021/02/12/seprelad-instruye-en-reporte-de-operaciones-al-sector-automotriz/) links to a removed Google Drive file). Ask SEPRELAD (DGAFE, via E-Porandu or mesaentrada@seprelad.gov.py) for the schema, and whether vehicle firms can switch to JSON by note as real-estate firms can. A pilot customer's SIRO screens would also answer it. (unverified)
2. **RO frequency for vehicles.** Quarterly (11th-20th) rests on Circular 02/2025 "other SOs" and SEPRELAD's Sep 2026 training title "Reporte Trimestral". SEPRELAD's 2025 report still gives vehicle RO statistics by half-year, and Res 85/2015 had a half-yearly RO. Confirm in SIRO. (unverified)
3. **Res 328/2026.** It amends Art. 14 (external audit) for vehicles and real estate. Its text, date and effect (deadline, content, any small-firm relief) are unknown. leyes.com.py blocks access (HTTP 403) and the SEPRELAD site search found nothing. (unverified)
4. **Circular 02/2025 itself.** I saw only Vouga's summary. The primary text should confirm the outsourcing clause (outside firms may prepare and submit reports) and the exact dates (30 March vs 31 March; 30 June vs 29 June). (unverified)
5. **Source-of-funds form.** Whether the Res 85/2015 Annex B sworn declaration is still the required format, or any form with the Art. 21(7) content is enough. (unverified)
6. **Upload formats.** File type and any template for the internal control report and external audit report in SIRO (Res 202/2023). (unverified)
7. **New FA and risk-matrix module.** Field list and start date of the new FA "for the 2026 period" (likely due 31 May 2027) and of the SIRO risk-matrix module for vehicles ([Memoria 2025](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Memoria-Anual-de-Gestion-Ano-2025.pdf)). (unverified)
8. **SIRO data-confirmation pop-up and 2FA for vehicles.** Finance (15 Jun 2026) and real estate (5 Oct 2026) got the forced update; vehicles not yet. Whether two-factor login applies to vehicles. (unverified)
9. **Freezing procedure.** Article numbers of Law 6419/2019 and the channel SEPRELAD wants for a freeze report (Res 196 Art. 38 footnote 19 says SEPRELAD will set the medium). (unverified)
10. **"Commercial" test.** Whether a private person who sells a few cars a year is an SO. Res 196 targets commercial activity; no numeric test was found. (unverified)
11. **Minimum wage basis.** Res 196 says "salarios mínimos". I assume the monthly minimum wage for unspecified activities (Gs 3,044,000 from 1 Jul 2026). (unverified)
12. **Exchange-rate date for the FA.** The FA converts foreign-currency cash at the BCP rate, but which date's rate (operation date or year-end) is not stated in the instructions I read. (unverified)
13. **Enforcement detail.** Amount of the 2025 "significant" fine on a vehicle firm; whether SEPRELAD publishes sanction decisions. (unverified)
14. **ENR version.** Approval date of the new national risk assessment, which training and risk assessments must cite. (unverified)
15. **Data hosting.** Cross-border transfer rules of Law 7593/2025 for hosting client data outside Paraguay (e.g. EU or US cloud). Its regulation is pending. (unverified)
