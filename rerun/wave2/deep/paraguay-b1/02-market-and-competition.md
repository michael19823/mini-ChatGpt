**Reading the numbers.**
- About **1,700** vehicle firms are on the register. About **845** pay the yearly fee. About **450-620** file their routine reports. About **160-330** also do the annual form or the external audit. Only about **20** ever file a suspicious-operation report.
- **The core buyer pool is the ~845 fee-paying firms.** They are inside the system, so they face the deadlines and the warnings. The ~850 registered firms that do not pay are either closed or deep non-compliers (inference).
- **The best-qualified prospects are the 300-500 firms that miss some duties.** For example, 453 file operation reports, but only 331 file the annual form and 160 send the audit. They try to comply and fail on the harder items (inference from the gap).
- **62% of registrations are individuals**, not companies. These are owner-run lots. Most of them sit in Central, Alto Paraná (Ciudad del Este, the main import hub) and Asunción.
- New entrants run at about 230 a year. That is a steady flow of firms that must set up a manual, an officer and the SIRO account from scratch.
