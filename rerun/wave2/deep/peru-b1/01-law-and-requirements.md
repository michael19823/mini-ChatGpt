# Peru: AML (SPLAFT) law for casinos, slot rooms and online betting, turned into product requirements

Status: IN PROGRESS (2026-10-10). Primary source read in full: the official El Peruano separata of Res. SBS 01015-2026 (8 Apr 2026, 28 pages), hosted by LP Derecho ([PDF](https://img.lpderecho.pe/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Resolucion-SBS-01015-2026-LPDerecho.pdf)). Also read: Res. SBS 03622-2025 for online operators (El Peruano, 14 Oct 2025) ([PDF](https://img.lpderecho.pe/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Resolucion-SBS-03622-2025-LPDerecho.pdf)).

Short names used below:
- "Norma" = the land-based AML rule approved by Art. Primero of Res. SBS 01015-2026 (articles 1-29).
- "Online norma" = the AML rule for remote games and remote sports betting, approved by Res. SBS 03622-2025.
- "RIS" = the sanctions regulation for MINCETUR-supervised gaming firms, Res. SBS 5389-2013, as amended by Res. SBS 01015-2026 (Arts. Quinto and Sexto).
- UIT = Peru's tax unit, S/ 5,500 in 2026 ([El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/285208-mef-establece-en-s-5-500-la-unidad-impositiva-tributaria-para-2026)).
- OC = oficial de cumplimiento (compliance officer). RO = registro de operaciones. ROS = suspicious transaction report. IAOC = annual compliance-officer report. IAI = annual internal-audit report. DGJCMT = MINCETUR's gaming directorate.

## Key facts found so far (draft notes, to be turned into sections)

- Res. SBS 01015-2026 entered into force on 9 Apr 2026, the day after publication, with **no adaptation period**. Res. SBS 1695-2016 lost effect the same day (Art. Octavo).
- Online norma: in force 15 Oct 2025 (Art. Noveno), 120 days to implement (Art. Cuarto), so about 12 Feb 2026.
- Risk assessment (Norma Art. 4.1) is required only if the firm (1) runs a casino room, (2) runs 500 or more slot machines in total, or (3) runs slot rooms in Tacna, Puno, Ucayali, Loreto, Tumbes or Madre de Dios. Update at least every 3 years (Art. 4.4).
- OC can serve only one obliged firm at a time, unless a corporate OC for a group (Norma Art. 19.2; Online norma Art. 5.2). OC needs a direct labour or contractual link (Art. 20.1(h)).
- GM can be OC part-time only if MEPECO, 10 or fewer workers, not in a group, and only gaming activity (Art. 19.4).
- RO: every chip/ticket cash-out at the cage of US$ 2,500 or more, and every promo prize winner at any amount (Art. 14.2-14.3). Logged on the day, chronological, in IT systems, with backup (14.4). Sent to UIF by the OC in the structure and frequency the SBS sets by resolution; template only on Portal PLAFT (14.6).
- IAOC: 13 minimum items; approved within 30 days of year end; sent by 15 Feb to DGJCMT and to UIF via Portal PLAFT (Art. 27). IAI annexed, same date (Art. 18.3-18.4).
- Fines (RIS Annex 1, as amended): leves 1-3 UIT; graves on manual/code 4 UIT; muy graves (ROS, UN lists, freezing) 8 UIT.
