## Payments and tax friction

### Draft findings so far

- Stripe: Peru is not a Stripe account country (Latin America only Brazil and Mexico on [stripe.com/global](https://stripe.com/global)). A foreign Stripe account can charge in PEN; PEN is a supported presentment currency, but not for American Express ([Stripe currencies](https://docs.stripe.com/currencies)). US fees: 2.9% + 30c, +1.5% international card, +1% if conversion; Billing 0.7%; Tax 0.5% ([Stripe pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing)).
- Paddle: Peru buyers supported, PEN listed, prices tax-inclusive ([Paddle countries](https://developer.paddle.com/concepts/sell/supported-countries-locales.md)). Paddle charges Peru 18% VAT on B2C only ([Paddle tax](https://paddle.com/help/sell/tax/which-countries-does-paddle-charge-sales-tax-or-vat-for/), updated 1 Aug 2025). Paddle bans gambling products ("betting-related products", "games of chance") but does not name software sold to casinos ([Paddle prohibited list](https://paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/what-am-i-not-allowed-to-sell-on-paddle), updated 13 Apr 2026).
- Withholding: SaaS fees paid to a non-resident are Peruvian-source "digital services" (LIR art. 9(i); Reglamento art. 4-A(b)), withheld at 30% ([SUNAT Informe 055-2021](https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/oficios/2021/informe-oficios/i055-2021-7T0000.pdf); [UP Forseti](https://revistas.up.edu.pe/index.php/forseti/article/download/2831/1863/7336), search summary). Treaties with Chile, Canada, Mexico, Korea and Portugal: business profits, no Peru tax without a PE (Informe 055-2021). Switzerland and Brazil treaties treat digital services as royalties (same).
- IGV: B2B buyer self-assesses 18% IGV on "utilización de servicios" (Form 1662; RS 000047-2026/SUNAT) ([LP Derecho](https://lpderecho.pe/tratamiento-tributario-en-el-impuesto-a-la-renta-e-igv-de-los-servicios-con-sujetos-no-domiciliados/); [SUNAT RS 047-2026](https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/superin/2026/000047-2026.pdf)). Gaming income is outside IGV ([Focus Gaming News](https://focusgn.com/latinoamerica/el-juego-pagara-mas-impuestos-en-peru), search summary), so IGV is a cost to these buyers either way.
