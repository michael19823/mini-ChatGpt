# Argentina A6: AML compliance pack for small jewellers and precious-metal dealers (UIF Res. 55/2024)

## Summary

**Verdict: no-go. Score: 2/10.**

The duty is real, detailed and in force. UIF Resolution 55/2024 makes every jeweller and precious-metal dealer in the chain keep client files, run a risk self-assessment, appoint a compliance officer, train staff and file monthly and annual reports ([Res. 55/2024](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-397648/texto)). The killer is market size. The FATF/GAFILAT 2024 mutual evaluation counts only **360 registered dealers in precious metals and stones** in all of Argentina (March 2024), and calls the sector small and low-risk ([FATF MER Argentina 2024, Table 1.2 and para. 117](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)). The UIF ran only 16 AML inspections of this sector from 2019 to March 2024 ([same, Table 6.4](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)). Even at full capture and a Mexican-style price, revenue would stay well below what a bootstrapped product needs. The idea only makes sense as one vertical module inside a wider tool for Argentina's non-financial obliged subjects (notaries, real estate agents, accountants, trustees), which number more than 30,000.

## Duty

**Legal basis.**
- Ley 25.246, art. 20 inc. 16, lists as obliged subjects those who trade luxury goods or export, import, make or process jewellery or goods with precious metals or stones ([Res. 55/2024 recitals and art. 1](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-397648/texto)).
- UIF Res. 55/2024 (signed 25/03/2024, Boletín Oficial 26/03/2024) sets the minimum AML/CFT/CPF rules for this group. It replaced Res. 28/2011 and art. 5 of Res. 70/2011 for these subjects (art. 38) ([Boletín Oficial aviso 305168](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/305168/1); [Marval](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/novedades-de-la-uif-sobre-sujetos-dedicados-a-la-compraventa-de-arte-joyas-y-bienes-suntuarios-16794)).
- Note: the "2025 Boletín Oficial notice" flagged as unchecked in the lead is the same aviso 305168. When fetched, it returns Res. 55/2024 itself ([Boletín Oficial](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/305168/1)). It is not a separate 2025 amendment.
- Res. 54/2024 covers art, antiques, philately and numismatics, not jewellery ([Marval](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/novedades-de-la-uif-sobre-sujetos-dedicados-a-la-compraventa-de-arte-joyas-y-bienes-suntuarios-16794)).

**Scope.** The whole chain is covered, from mining to retail and secondary markets. Costume jewellery and semi-precious stones are excluded (art. 2 c, o) ([Res. 55/2024](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-397648/texto)). There is no separate simplified regime for small firms. Natural persons may do most duties themselves ([same](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-397648/texto)).

**Thresholds (all in SMVM, the minimum wage).** The SMVM is AR$391,200 for October 2026 ([Cronista](https://www.cronista.com/economia-politica/salario-minimo-vital-y-movil-de-cuanto-es-en-argentina-en-octubre-2026/); [Chequeado](https://chequeado.com/nota/el-salario-minimo-sera-de-383-800-en-septiembre-de-2026-y-acumula-una-caida-real-del-397-desde-noviembre-de-2023)).
- Someone becomes a "client" at 6 SMVM or more, about AR$2.35M (art. 2 d) ([Res. 55/2024](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-397648/texto)).
- Operations above 20 SMVM, about AR$7.8M, go in the monthly report (art. 36 a) ([same](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-397648/texto)).
- An external independent review is required when operations exceed 180 SMVM, about AR$70M, in a calendar year (art. 18 a) ([same](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-397648/texto)).
- Peso figures are my own arithmetic on the October 2026 SMVM.

**What must be done, and when** ([Res. 55/2024](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-397648/texto)):

| Duty | Article | Frequency / deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Risk self-assessment report plus methodology | art. 5 | Every 2 years, filed by 30 April; first one by 30/04/2026, covering 2024-2025 |
| Risk tolerance declaration | art. 6 | Same cycle as the self-assessment |
| Written AML manual, signed off by staff | art. 9 | Reviewed every year |
| Compliance officer, main and alternate, registered with UIF | arts. 10-12 | Changes reported within 24 h; removal within 15 days |
| Client ID, beneficial owner, risk rating (high/medium/low) | arts. 20-29 | File updates every 1/3/5 years by risk level |
| RePET terrorist-list screening | art. 8 | Before onboarding and ongoing |
| Staff training, with records kept | art. 17 | Every year |
| Record keeping | art. 16 | At least 10 years |
| External independent review (above 180 SMVM per year) | art. 18 a | Every 2 years; first report by 31/10/2026 |
| Internal audit (legal persons not under external review) | art. 18 b | Ongoing |
| Unusual-operations register and suspicious reports (ROS) | arts. 34-35 | ROS within 24 h of deciding something is suspicious, max 90 days after the operation |
| Monthly systematic report (RSM) | art. 36 a | Days 1-15 of each month |
| Annual systematic report (RSA) | art. 36 b | 2 Jan - 15 Mar; first one in 2025 |

- Months with nothing to report still need a "sin movimientos" RSM ([UIF reporting FAQ](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/172339)).
- Registration is fully online in SRO+ under UIF Res. 37/2026. Registered subjects must update their data within 5 working days. If they fail to register or keep data current, SRO+ blocks them automatically and they cannot file RSM or ROS ([Colegio de Escribanos summary of Res. 37/2026](https://www.colegio-escribanos.org.ar/noticias/2026_04_14_UIF-Res-37-26-Informe.pdf)).

**Penalties.** Res. 55/2024 points to Chapter IV of Ley 25.246 (art. 37) ([Res. 55/2024](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-397648/texto)). After the 2024-2025 reforms (Ley 27.739, Decreto 274/2025), art. 24 provides ([ST Abogados](https://stabogados.com.ar/penal/leyes-penales-especiales/ley-25246/arts-24-26/)):
- a warning, or a warning with publication in the Boletín Oficial;
- for ROS failures, a fine of 1 to 10 times the value of the operation;
- for any other breach, 15 to 2,500 "módulos" per infraction;
- disqualification as compliance officer for up to 5 years.

One módulo is AR$54,140 under Res. UIF 95/2025 ([Res. 95/2025](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-414295/texto)). That puts an ordinary breach between about AR$0.8M and AR$135M. Whether the módulo was updated again in 2026 is unverified.

**Enforcement in practice. It is weak.**
- From 2019 to March 2024 the UIF made 3 on-site and 13 remote AML inspections of dealers in precious metals and stones ([FATF MER 2024, Table 6.4](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)).
- Before the 2024 reform, typical fines were AR$20,000-100,000 (about US$25-125). The FATF called them "not dissuasive" ([same, para. 577](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)).
- In 2024, across all sectors, the UIF made 235 supervisions, opened 68 sanction proceedings and imposed 25 fines ([UIF Resumen Ejecutivo Gestión 2024](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/2016/09/uif_resumen_ejecutivo_gestion_2024_-_v03.pdf)).
- An earlier check found that in 2014 the UIF imposed fines of AR$221.7M but collected about AR$250k ([Página/12](https://www.pagina12.com.ar/diario/economia/2-242795-2014-03-27.html)).
- I found no published sanction against a jeweller or gold buyer under Res. 55/2024 (unverified either way).

**Recent or upcoming changes.**
- Accountants' bodies asked UIF to extend the 30/04/2026 self-assessment deadline ([CPCECABA](https://consejo.org.ar/noticias/2026/reiteramos-prorroga-para-la-presentacion-del-informe-de-autoevaluacion-de-riesgos-ante-la-uif); [FACPCE note](https://www.facpce.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Nota-FACPCE-a-UIF-31-03-26.pdf)).
- The UIF moved the accountants' external review to March 2027 ([Blog del Contador](https://siap.blogdelcontador.com.ar/novedades/uif-prorrogo-marzo-2027-informe-revision-externa-contadores/)).
- I found no equivalent extension or threshold change for jewellers (unverified). The 31/10/2026 external-review deadline for jewellers may still stand.

## Buyers

- **Registered count: 360.** That is the number of dealers in precious metals and stones registered with the UIF as of March 2024 ([FATF MER 2024, Tables 1.2 and 6.1](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)). From 2019 to 2024 the UIF received 125 applications in this category and rejected 77 ([same, Table 6.1](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)). So registrations grow by only a few dozen a year.
- **Risk view.** The National Risk Assessment rated the sector low. The FATF said these dealers "have a good understanding of their risks", called the sector "relatively small", and gave it the lowest weight among sectors ([same, paras. 117, 121 and IO.4 summary](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)). The sector files "very few to no" suspicious reports ([same, para. 478](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)).
- **Unregistered universe.** There are clearly many more jewellers, workshops and "compro oro" shops than 360. One gold-buying house reported 300 operations a day in 2026 as households sold heirlooms ([Última Hora / AFP](https://www.ultimahora.com/argentinos-liquidan-joyas-de-la-abuela-en-la-odisea-de-llegar-a-fin-de-mes)). I found no official count of jewellery shops (INDEC or ARCA activity codes not checked) (unverified). Firms that have not registered are unlikely to buy compliance software until enforcement reaches them.
- **Segments.** The registered base likely includes larger retailers, manufacturers, importers, refiners and bullion dealers (unverified; the FATF report gives no split). Coin and bar dealing by exchange houses falls under Ley 18.924 and BCRA rules, not Res. 55/2024 ([Res. 174/2023 context](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-389541/texto)) (partly unverified).
- **How they comply today.** They file RSM through the free UIF tools (SRO+, RSM Masivo app) ([UIF RSM page](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/uif/rsm)). Other work goes to accountants, AML consultants and law firms ([ST Abogados compliance service](https://stabogados.com.ar/civil/empresas/compliance-sujetos-obligados/); [Marval](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/novedades-de-la-uif-sobre-sujetos-dedicados-a-la-compraventa-de-arte-joyas-y-bienes-suntuarios-16794)). Spreadsheets and Word manuals are likely (unverified).

**For comparison**, other UIF-supervised non-financial sectors are much bigger ([FATF MER 2024, Table 1.2](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)):

| Sector | Registered (Mar 2024) |
|---|---|
| Notaries | 11,325 |
| Real estate agents | 10,365 |
| Trust service providers | 6,024 |
| Accountants | 4,712 |
| Payment cards and systems | 312 |
| Precious metals and stones | 360 |
| Casinos | 86 |

## Competition

- **Free state tools.**
  - The UIF's SRO+ portal handles registration, RSM, RSA and ROS filing ([Res. 37/2026 summary](https://www.colegio-escribanos.org.ar/noticias/2026_04_14_UIF-Res-37-26-Informe.pdf)).
  - The free RSM Masivo desktop app (installer 7.2) does bulk filing, corrections and cancellations ([UIF RSM page](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/uif/rsm)).
  - Neither tool does client files, risk scoring, the self-assessment, the manual or training records.
- **Free templates.** FACPCE published a free guide and a model risk matrix for the self-assessment report (ITAER) ([FACPCE ITAER guide](https://www.facpce.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/GUIA-PARA-ELABORAR-EL-INFORME-TECNICO-DE-AUTOEVALUACION-DE-RIESGOS-ITAER-002.pdf); [Contadores en Red](https://contadoresenred.com/uif-guia-para-la-elaboracion-del-informe-tecnico-de-autoevaluacion-de-riesgos-vto-30-4-2026/)). It is written for accountants under Res. 42/2024, but the structure carries over.
- **Consultants and law firms.** Firms such as Marval and ST Abogados advise obliged subjects ([Marval](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/novedades-de-la-uif-sobre-sujetos-dedicados-a-la-compraventa-de-arte-joyas-y-bienes-suntuarios-16794); [ST Abogados](https://stabogados.com.ar/civil/empresas/compliance-sujetos-obligados/)). Professional councils run annual mandatory AML training ([CPCE Salta](https://www.consejosalta.org.ar/2026/08/capacitacion-anual-obligatoria-en-materia-de-prevencion-de-la-ft-para-sujetos-obligados-y-sus-colaboradores-3/)). I found no prices.
- **Software.**
  - Across about ten Spanish and English searches (this pass and earlier ones), I found no Argentine AML product aimed at jewellers.
  - Generic ID-verification and screening APIs sell into Argentina, for example Didit ([Didit](https://didit.me/es/blog/verificacion-de-identidad-cumplimiento-kyc-y-aml-en-argentina/)).
  - The closest analogue is Mexico's KYC Systems, a jeweller-specific AML tool from MXN 5,000 a month plus VAT, built for Mexican law (LFPIORPI) ([KYC Systems](https://kyc-systems.com/actividades-vulnerables/software-antilavado-metales-joyas.html)). It does not serve Argentina.
  - The Argentine market may hold local AML vendors I did not find (unverified).
- **External reviewers.** Reviews must be done by people on the UIF's register of independent external reviewers (Res. 67/2017, amended by Res. 132/2024), who need a university degree and AML credentials ([Blog del Contador](https://blogdelcontador.com.ar/news-44446-resolucion-uif-132-2024-registro-de-revisores-externos-independientes); [Abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/index.php/modificaciones-al-registro-y-requisitos-del-revisor-externo-independiente/35499)). A software newcomer cannot sell this part without a registered professional.

## Willingness to pay

- **Fines.** The new fine floor of 15 módulos is about AR$0.8M per breach. The ceiling of 2,500 módulos is about AR$135M ([ST Abogados](https://stabogados.com.ar/penal/leyes-penales-especiales/ley-25246/arts-24-26/); [Res. 95/2025](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-414295/texto)). On paper that is a real incentive.
- **Chance of a fine.** The odds of being inspected are very low: 16 inspections in about 5 years across 360 firms ([FATF MER 2024](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)). The expected cost of not complying is therefore small.
- **Price benchmark.** A Mexican jeweller tool charges from MXN 5,000 a month ([KYC Systems](https://kyc-systems.com/actividades-vulnerables/software-antilavado-metales-joyas.html)). Argentine prices are unverified. A plausible Argentine price is AR$30,000-80,000 a month, or about AR$250k-600k for a one-off self-assessment and manual pack (my estimate, unverified).
- **Revenue ceiling.** Capturing all 360 firms at about US$30 a month would give roughly US$130k a year (my arithmetic; the exchange rate is unverified). Realistic capture of 10-20% gives US$13-26k a year.
- **Retail conditions.** Retail trade fell 5.2% between Q3 2023 and Q3 2025 ([El Economista / UBA](https://eleconomista.com.ar/economia/informe-uba-industria-argentina-pierde-160-empleos-dia-retrocede-niveles-1985-n93152/amp)). Small jewellers are squeezed.

## Channels

- **UIF itself.** The UIF disseminates guidance to obliged subjects through SRO messaging ([FATF MER 2024](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)). It does not list vendors.
- **The public registry.** The UIF's public "consulta de sujetos obligados registrados" lookup ([UIF reporting FAQ](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/172339)) could help identify the registered firms (unverified whether it can be searched by category).
- **Trade bodies.** I did not find an active national jewellers' chamber online (unverified). Local trade groups exist, and 3 November is the Día del Joyero y Relojero ([La Voz de San Justo](https://www.lavozdesanjusto.com.ar/un-legado-de-oro-el-oficio-de-ser-joyero-en-tiempos-de-crisis)).
- **Accountants.** The most practical route is through accountants: the professional councils (CPCE) and FACPCE already run AML training and publish guides ([FACPCE](https://www.facpce.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/GUIA-PARA-ELABORAR-EL-INFORME-TECNICO-DE-AUTOEVALUACION-DE-RIESGOS-ITAER-002.pdf); [CPCE Salta](https://www.consejosalta.org.ar/2026/08/capacitacion-anual-obligatoria-en-materia-de-prevencion-de-la-ft-para-sujetos-obligados-y-sus-colaboradores-3/)). Accountants who serve several obliged clients would buy a multi-client tool.
- **Registered external reviewers.** They are a natural referral partner.

## Risks

- **Market too small (killer).** Only 360 registered firms exist, in a sector the FATF and the national assessment rate low-risk ([FATF MER 2024](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)).
- **Weak enforcement.** There are few inspections and, historically, tiny fines ([same, para. 577](https://www.mpf.gob.ar/procelac-lavado/files/2020/04/Argentina-Mutual-Evaluation-Report-2024.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)). Unregistered informal gold buyers will not buy.
- **Deadline slippage and threshold changes.** Accountants won delays in 2026 ([Blog del Contador](https://siap.blogdelcontador.com.ar/novedades/uif-prorrogo-marzo-2027-informe-revision-externa-contadores/)). The same may happen for jewellers (unverified).
- **State tools.** SRO+ is growing: it now handles all registration and documents ([Res. 37/2026 summary](https://www.colegio-escribanos.org.ar/noticias/2026_04_14_UIF-Res-37-26-Informe.pdf)). Free professional templates already cover the self-assessment.
- **Liability and licensing.** The external review needs a registered reviewer. A badly done self-assessment exposes the client to fines of up to 2,500 módulos.
- **Economy.** Inflation and peso pricing make subscriptions hard to price.

## First product

The case for a jeweller-only product is weak. If it is built at all, build a **multi-sector UIF compliance kit**, with jewellers as one template. Notaries, real estate agents, trustees and accountants follow the same pattern: client file, risk matrix, self-assessment, manual, training log, RSM.

First 30 days:
1. Build a client-file (legajo) form with the 6-SMVM trigger and an automatic SMVM table, plus high/medium/low risk scoring per art. 25 of Res. 55/2024.
2. Add a monthly RSM helper: flag operations above 20 SMVM, export in the format RSM Masivo accepts (schema to verify), and remind the user to file "sin movimientos".
3. Build a self-assessment (ITAER) and risk-tolerance generator, reusing the FACPCE matrix structure, plus a manual template with annual sign-off.
4. Add a training log, a 10-year record archive and RePET screening (manual upload of the list).
5. Add a calendar of deadlines: RSM days 1-15, RSA 2 Jan-15 Mar, self-assessment 30 April every two years, external review.
6. Sell first to 5-10 accountants who serve obliged clients, not to jewellers directly.
