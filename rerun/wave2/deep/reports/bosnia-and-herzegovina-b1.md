# AML/CFT compliance kit for small obliged entities in Bosnia and Herzegovina (bosnia-and-herzegovina-b1)

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 6/10.**

The duty is real, current and being enforced right now. Bookkeepers, accountants, auditors and real estate intermediaries must have a written ML/TF risk assessment, internal acts, an authorised person, client ID files and records under the state AML law (Sl. glasnik BiH 13/2024). Inspectors in both entities are checking them now, with fines of 5,000-200,000 KM ([Paragraf, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/01102026/01102026-vijest1.html); [Paragraf, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/29012026/29012026-vijest1.html)). FATF added BiH to its grey list in June 2026, and its action plan names DNFBP supervision and notaries ([FATF June 2026 list, copy](https://fiu.rks-gov.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/FATF-Jurisdictions-under-Increased-Monitoring-19-June-2026.pdf)), so pressure will stay high for 2-3 years. I found no local software and no free state tool that produces the documents. The state offers only questionnaires and guides. Against it: the market is a few thousand very price-sensitive micro firms, much of the need is a one-off document set, and accountants or consultants can copy templates cheaply. A low-priced "kit plus yearly update" product could work as a side business. It is unlikely to grow large.

## Duty

- **Legal basis.** Law on Prevention of Money Laundering and Financing of Terrorist Activities, Sl. glasnik BiH 13/2024. It is a state-level law and applies in FBiH, RS and Brcko ([Paragraf law text](https://www.paragraf.ba/propisi/bih/zakon-o-sprecavanju-pranja-novca-i-finansiranja-teroristickih-aktivnosti.html)).
- **Who is obliged.** Art. 5(1)(o) covers auditors, accountants, tax advisers, notaries and lawyers. Art. 5(2)(c) covers real estate intermediaries, and for rentals only where monthly rent is 20,000 KM or more (same source; [Paragraf, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/29012026/29012026-vijest1.html)). Art. 48 specifically binds providers of accounting and tax advisory services ([Unija](https://unija.com/bs/uloga-racunovodje-u-sprjecavanju-pranja-novca/)).
- **What must be done.** The duties are ([Paragraf law text](https://www.paragraf.ba/propisi/bih/zakon-o-sprecavanju-pranja-novca-i-finansiranja-teroristickih-aktivnosti.html)):
  - a documented ML/TF risk assessment covering client, country, product/service and channel risk factors;
  - internal policies and procedures, proportionate to size (Art. 9(4)). I found no exemption for micro firms;
  - an authorised person and a deputy (Art. 9(2)(h));
  - internal control and audit (Art. 9(2)(j));
  - staff training;
  - client identification and monitoring, including for transactions of 30,000 KM or more, single or linked (Art. 12(1)(b), Art. 4(c));
  - record keeping and data protection (Art. 9(2)(l));
  - suspicious transaction reports to the FIU, the Financial Intelligence Department of SIPA (FOO) (Art. 42).
- **Record retention.** At least 10 years after the relationship or transaction ends, according to a consultancy summary ([Unija](https://unija.com/bs/uloga-racunovodje-u-sprjecavanju-pranja-novca/)). The lead file said 5 years. I could not read the article in the law itself, so the 10 years is (unverified).
- **Risk assessment goes to the regulator.** FUZIP says every obliged entity that has not yet done so must send its own risk assessment to the FOO ([Paragraf, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/01102026/01102026-vijest1.html)). The yearly update cycle comes from the earlier check of Art. 10 (lead file). I did not re-confirm it.
- **Rulebook.** The Council of Ministers adopted the implementing rulebook at its 98th session on 30 Dec 2025. It covers risk assessment, client identification and monitoring, and how data goes to the FOO ([Paragraf, 5 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/05012026/05012026-vijest4.html)). It applies from 11 Feb 2026, and obliged entities had 90 days, to 12 May 2026, to align ([BDK Advokati search snippet](https://bdkadvokati.com/?p=19887); the fetched page did not show it, so this is partly unverified). FUZIP confirms publication in early Feb 2026 and the 90-day deadline ([Paragraf, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/01102026/01102026-vijest1.html)). I did not find whether the rulebook sets fixed forms or an electronic filing portal (unverified).
- **Reporting channel.** The FOO records reports in its AMLS database ([NameScan](https://namescan.io/coverages/bosniaandherzegovina)). I could not confirm how a small obliged entity submits them (portal, e-mail or paper) (unverified).
- **Penalties.** Fines run from 5,000 to 200,000 KM. A missing risk analysis, CDD failures or irregular records cost 20,000-80,000 KM ([Paragraf, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/01102026/01102026-vijest1.html)). Responsible persons face up to 20,000 KM ([Paragraf, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/29012026/29012026-vijest1.html)).
- **Enforcement evidence.**
  - **FBiH.** The Federal Market Inspectorate (FUZIP) began a special AML programme on 28 Sep 2026. It runs to end-2026 and covers bookkeeping, accounting and tax firms, auditors, gambling operators, TCSPs, dealers and real estate intermediaries. Inspectors check the risk assessment, client ID, records, internal procedures and STR handling ([Paragraf, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/01102026/01102026-vijest1.html)). On 7-8 Oct 2026 FUZIP published AML questionnaires for bookkeeping/accounting providers, real estate intermediaries and gambling organisers ([FUZIP home](https://fuzip.gov.ba/); [questionnaire for bookkeepers](https://fuzip.gov.ba/obaveze-u-oblasti-sprecavanja-pranja-novca-upitnik-za-pruzaoce-knjigovodstvenih-i-racunovodstvenih-usluga/)).
  - **RS.** In Jan 2026 the RS Inspectorate gave randomly chosen auditors, bookkeepers, accountants and tax advisers 10 days (to 9 Feb 2026) to submit their internal acts, a 13-item questionnaire and a checklist. Those who did not respond faced extraordinary inspection. A consultant called the burden on sole traders "absurd" ([Paragraf, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/29012026/29012026-vijest1.html)). The RS Tax Administration reported 400 inspections leading to 32 reports ([Paragraf, 12 Nov 2025](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/12112025/12112025-vijest3.html)).
  - I found no published count of fines issued to DNFBPs (unverified).
- **International pressure.** FATF listed BiH under increased monitoring on 19 June 2026. Its action plan includes better supervision of notaries and consistent AML/CFT supervision of DNFBPs ([FATF list, copy](https://fiu.rks-gov.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/FATF-Jurisdictions-under-Increased-Monitoring-19-June-2026.pdf); [VinciWorks](https://vinciworks.com/blog/is-bosnia-and-herzegovina-heading-back-to-the-fatf-grey-list/)). MONEYVAL rated real estate high-risk and flagged weak risk understanding ([Paragraf, 12 Nov 2025](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/12112025/12112025-vijest3.html)). Grey-list exit usually takes about two years, so pressure should last into 2028 (my estimate).
- **Political complication.** In 2025 the RS Assembly passed a law banning the work of "extra-constitutional" state bodies, including SIPA. This left banks and notaries unsure whether to keep reporting to SIPA, and most unofficially kept following the state law ([Paragraf, 19 Mar 2025](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/19032025/19032025-vijest3.html); [Paragraf, 26 Mar 2025](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/26032025/26032025-vijest3.html)). The RS Inspectorate's 2026 checks show RS still enforces the state law for DNFBPs.

## Buyers

- **Real estate.** FBiH has 135 registered real estate agencies and 177 certified agents (Federal Ministry of Trade register, via [prostor.ba](https://prostor.ba/en/clanak/37), undated). FBiH statistics list 1,659 legal entities in section L (real estate activities) at Dec 2022, but most are landlords and developers, not brokers ([FZS, Dec 2022](https://fzs.ba/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/23.2.pdf)). I found no RS or Brcko agency count (unverified).
- **Accounting and bookkeeping.** FBiH section M (professional, scientific, technical) had 4,535 legal entities and 4,068 natural-person businesses at Dec 2022 ([FZS](https://fzs.ba/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/23.2.pdf)). This includes lawyers, engineers, architects and others, and I could not split out division 69.20. A 2021 Foreign Trade Chamber analysis, cited in a search snippet, gave 631 registered companies in the sector in 2020, down from 771 in 2018. The source and scope are unclear (unverified). RS planned a Ministry of Finance register of bookkeeping entrepreneurs (unverified whether adopted).
- **Notaries.** Numbers are set per seat by the governments. At roughly one per 20,000 inhabitants that is about 150-170 nationwide (my estimate; benchmark from the [Brcko notary law](https://advokat-prnjavorac.com:8443/zakoni/Zakon_o_notarima_Brcko_distrikta.pdf)). Notaries already apply the law and have chamber support ([Paragraf, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/29012026/29012026-vijest1.html)), so they are a weak first target.
- **Lawyers.** They are covered only when they take part in specified transactions (lead file). There are likely low thousands of them, but the obliged subset is small (unverified).
- **Total addressable.** My estimate is about 2,500-5,000 obliged small entities across BiH, mostly bookkeeping firms and sole bookkeepers, plus about 300-500 real estate agencies and some auditors, dealers and TCSPs (unverified).
- **How they comply today.** Most firms have no AML file or a copied template. In RS, the Jan 2026 checks found sole traders unprepared and complaining ([Paragraf, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/29012026/29012026-vijest1.html)). Others attend seminars and draft documents in-house, or use their accountant or a consultant. No survey data exists (unverified).

## Competition

- **Free state material.**
  - FUZIP publishes AML questionnaires per sector (Oct 2026) and checklists ([FUZIP](https://fuzip.gov.ba/); [kontrolne liste](https://fuzip.gov.ba/kontrolne-liste/)).
  - It has also published a guide for real estate dealers ([FUZIP guide, Mar 2026](https://fuzip.gov.ba/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Vodic-za-trgovce-nekretninama_ZA-WEB.pdf)) and risk-assessment guidelines since April 2026 ([Paragraf, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/01102026/01102026-vijest1.html)).
  - There is a national risk assessment summary ([FBiH Government](https://fbihvlada.gov.ba/uploads/documents/sazetak-procjene-rizika-od-pranja-novca-i-finansiranja-terorizma-u-bih_1644320551.pdf)).
  - None of this generates a firm's own risk assessment, internal acts or client files. **No free state tool does the job.** The questionnaires are useful product input, because they show what inspectors ask.
- **Seminars.** Paragraf Lex runs AML seminars in Sarajevo and Banja Luka, including one for accountants and auditors. The 2025 fee was 150 KM for subscribers and 170 KM otherwise, plus 17% VAT ([Paragraf seminar](https://www.paragraf.ba/savjetovanje-strane/za-racunovodje-revizore-sprecavanje-pranja-novca-efikasna-primjena-zakona.html)). The Oct 2026 run is scheduled ([Paragraf](https://www.paragraf.ba/savjetovanje-strane/sprecavanje-pranja-novca-za-obveznike-sta-treba-znati-i-kako-prepoznati.html)). Paragraf is also the obvious party to add templates to its legal database (risk).
- **Consultancies.**
  - Unija ETL Accounting (Sarajevo, Mostar) writes on accountants' AML duties. No AML package or price is listed ([Unija](https://unija.com/bs/uloga-racunovodje-u-sprjecavanju-pranja-novca/)).
  - Forvis Mazars sells AML support, including client risk assessment and risk matrices, in Serbia ([Forvis Mazars RS](https://www.forvismazars.com/rs/sr/nase-usluge/savetovanje/aml-podrska-ili-spnft-podrska)). It is not shown for BiH and its prices are not public.
  - Local consultants such as Dev Con (Mišel Pavlica) comment publicly ([Paragraf, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/29012026/29012026-vijest1.html)).
- **Regional software.**
  - A-count (Croatia) offers an AML ("SPNFT") workflow for accounting offices, from the first client enquiry to the finished risk assessment, with a 30-day free trial ([LinkedIn post](https://hr.linkedin.com/in/danijela-dellavia)). It is built for Croatian law and I found no BiH version or price (unverified). This is the closest regional analogue and a possible entrant.
  - Croatian template articles exist, e.g. the RRiF model internal act ([RRiF](https://www.rrif.hr/clanak-12858/)).
- **International.** Credas, Thirdfort, FigsFlow and SmartSearch are UK-focused and have no BiH forms ([FigsFlow list](https://figsflow.com/uk/top-6-aml-software-for-accountants-in-2025/)). Asseco SEE's AML system is built for banks ([Asseco](https://see.asseco.com/news-events/news/3630/?L=-5776)).
- **Bottom line.** There is no BiH-specific SMB AML tool. The real competitors are copied Word templates, cheap consultants and doing nothing.

## Willingness to pay

- A one-day seminar costs about 175-200 KM with VAT per person ([Paragraf](https://www.paragraf.ba/savjetovanje-strane/za-racunovodje-revizore-sprecavanje-pranja-novca-efikasna-primjena-zakona.html)). That is a clear signal of what a sole bookkeeper will spend on AML in a year.
- The cost of failing is far higher: 20,000-80,000 KM for a missing risk analysis or poor CDD ([Paragraf, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/01102026/01102026-vijest1.html)). Even a minimum fine is 25-30 times a seminar fee.
- Staff time: a new deadline lands on top of year-end closing and monthly VAT returns due on the 10th, and accountants say the workload is real ([Paragraf, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/29012026/29012026-vijest1.html)).
- Plausible pricing (my estimate, unverified):
  - 150-300 KM for a one-off starter kit (risk assessment, internal acts, appointment decisions, training record);
  - 15-30 KM a month, or about 200-300 KM a year, for the client register, CDD forms, yearly update and reminders;
  - firm plans for bookkeeping offices at about 50 KM a month.
- Buyers resent the duty ([Paragraf, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/29012026/29012026-vijest1.html)). They will pay to make the problem go away cheaply, not for a sophisticated tool.

## Channels

- **Accountant associations.** Savez računovođa i revizora RS (11 member societies) runs a yearly symposium ([Komora RS programme](https://komorars.ba/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/17-Simpozijum-PROGRAM-–-kopija-3.pdf)). The FBiH certified-accountant body issues certificates under the 2021 FBiH accounting law ([FBiH law](https://www.paragraf.ba/propisi/fbih/zakon-o-racunovodstvu-i-reviziji-u-federaciji-bosne-i-hercegovine.html)). Both run CPD events. Offer webinars that count for CPD points (unverified whether allowed).
- **Seminar organisers.** Partner with Paragraf Lex: sell the kit to its seminar attendees or bundle it with the seminar ([Paragraf](https://www.paragraf.ba/savjetovanje-strane/sprecavanje-pranja-novca-za-obveznike-sta-treba-znati-i-kako-prepoznati.html)).
- **Regulator outputs.** FUZIP publishes questionnaires and lists of covered sectors ([FUZIP](https://fuzip.gov.ba/)). Map each product screen to the questionnaire items and market it as "answer the FUZIP questionnaire in 1 hour".
- **Real estate.** Reach brokers through the FBiH Chamber of Commerce real estate group ([KFBiH](https://www.kfbih.com/en/?p=29519)) and the Ministry of Trade register of 135 agencies ([prostor.ba](https://prostor.ba/en/clanak/37)).
- **Direct.** Public business registers and Google Maps lists of "knjigovodstveni servis" allow cold outreach. Bookkeepers can also resell the kit to their own clients, such as car dealers and small agencies.

## Risks

- **Market small and cheap.** Probably a few thousand buyers paying a few hundred KM each. A realistic ceiling is perhaps 100-300k KM a year (my estimate). This suits a side business, not a venture.
- **One-off need.** After the documents exist, renewal depends on the yearly risk-assessment update and on client-file use. Many will buy once or copy a colleague's set.
- **Copycats.** Paragraf, accounting software vendors or consultants could add a template pack quickly. A-count (Croatia) could localise.
- **Regulator tool.** FUZIP or the FOO could publish model internal acts or a risk-assessment template, which is common in the region. Today they publish only guides and questionnaires.
- **Political fragmentation.** The RS–SIPA conflict could produce RS-specific rules or a separate RS FIU ([Paragraf, 19 Mar 2025](https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/19032025/19032025-vijest3.html)). Supervisors differ by entity, so templates need FBiH, RS and Brcko variants.
- **Repeal unlikely.** The grey listing makes weakening the law very unlikely in 2026-2028. Pressure may fade after delisting.
- **Liability.** A buyer fined despite using the kit may blame the vendor. Disclaimers and "proportionate to size" wording are needed. Don't sell legal advice without a lawyer partner.
- **Language.** Bosnian, Croatian and Serbian (Latin and Cyrillic) variants are needed. They are mutually understandable but expected in the official forms.

## First product

**Version 1: "AML paket za knjigovođe i agencije"**, a web form wizard that produces signed-ready documents.

1. Firm profile questionnaire: entity (FBiH, RS or Brcko), activity, staff, client types, cash exposure, foreign clients.
2. Auto-generated risk assessment, scored on client, geography, service and channel factors, in the format FUZIP and the FOO expect.
3. Internal acts: AML policy and procedures, decision appointing the authorised person and deputy, training plan and attendance record, internal control checklist.
4. Client file: CDD and beneficial-owner form per client, a simple risk score, PEP/sanctions self-check prompts, and a 30,000 KM linked-transaction flag.
5. STR helper: indicator checklist and a draft report text for the FOO. Do not submit it automatically.
6. Records register with retention dates (10 years, unverified) and a yearly review reminder.
7. "FUZIP questionnaire" mode that pre-fills answers from the stored data.

**First 30 days:**
- Week 1: get the rulebook text and the FUZIP questionnaires for bookkeepers and real estate. Map every item. Have a local AML lawyer review the templates.
- Week 2: build the templates (DOCX/PDF) and the wizard. A static form plus document generation is enough.
- Week 3: pilot with 10 bookkeeping offices and 5 agencies, at a 99 KM launch price.
- Week 4: run a webinar with an association or Paragraf. Publish a landing page titled "FUZIP kontrole do kraja 2026".

Add the client register subscription only after paid kit sales prove demand.

## Open questions

- The exact record-retention article (5 or 10 years) and the penalty article numbers in Law 13/2024.
- Whether the 2026 rulebook prescribes forms or an electronic FOO portal for small entities.
- Counts of bookkeeping firms and sole bookkeepers in FBiH, RS and Brcko, and of RS and Brcko real estate agencies.
- Fines actually issued in the FUZIP autumn 2026 programme and the RS 2026 checks.
- A-count's price and whether it plans a BiH version. Whether BiH accounting software vendors plan AML modules.
- Whether certified-accountant bodies give CPD credit for AML training.
