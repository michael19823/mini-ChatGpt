# Chile B4: UAF anti-money-laundering kit for property brokers, real-estate firms and notaries

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 4/10.**

The duty is real, current and has been widened. Ley 19.913 and UAF Circular 62 (in force since 1 June 2025) make property brokers, real-estate management firms and notaries register with the UAF and appoint an internal compliance officer. They must also keep a prevention manual updated every two years, train all staff every year, screen clients against UN lists, collect beneficial-owner declarations, and keep five permanent registers. The UAF register at 30 June 2025 lists 8,970 reporting entities. Of these, 1,383 are property brokers, 2,009 are real-estate management firms, 496 are notaries and 93 are conservadores. That is about 4,000 target entities, many of them one-person brokers or single-project SPVs. Fines are real but small: in 2018, brokers paid about 34 UF (about USD 1,400) on average per sanction. Regcheq and Gesintel already sell AML SaaS in Chile, and law firms sell manuals. A cheap, real-estate-specific kit could find a niche. But the market is small, the fine pressure is weak, and most active brokers are not even registered, so this looks like a modest side business rather than a strong standalone one.

## Duty

**Legal basis.**
- Ley 19.913, art. 3, lists the obliged sectors. They include property brokers (corredores de propiedades), real-estate management firms and notaries ([vLex](https://vlex.cl/vid/crea-registro-nacional-corredores-914511237); [Carey](https://www.carey.cl/api/archivo/uaf-dicta-nuevo-marco-normativo-para-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo?lang=es)).
- UAF Circular 62 was issued on 19 March 2025 and has been in force since 1 June 2025. It merges and replaces more than 20 earlier circulars, including Circular 49 of 2012 ([Guerrero Olivos alert](https://guerrero.cl/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DOC_Circular-N°62-UAF.pdf); [AZ](https://www.az.cl/claves-para-entender-el-impacto-regulatorio-de-la-circular-n62-de-la-uaf/)).

**What must be done (Circular 62).**
- **Registration.** Register with the UAF from the start of the activity. Report changes to registered data within 10 business days, up from 5 ([Prieto](https://www.prieto.cl/en/nueva-circular-n62-instrucciones-generales-para-sujetos-obligados-segun-la-ley-19-9133-2/); [AZ](https://www.az.cl/claves-para-entender-el-impacto-regulatorio-de-la-circular-n62-de-la-uaf/)).
- **Compliance officer (OdC).** Every entity must name one. AZ says an external person may not hold the role ([AZ](https://www.az.cl/claves-para-entender-el-impacto-regulatorio-de-la-circular-n62-de-la-uaf/)). The earlier check said micro and small firms can name the owner. I could not confirm this in the circular text (unverified). Either way, the role cannot be outsourced. Consultants can only support it.
- **Manual.** A written prevention manual, updated at least every two years and handed to every worker, with a record of delivery ([Prieto](https://www.prieto.cl/en/nueva-circular-n62-instrucciones-generales-para-sujetos-obligados-segun-la-ley-19-9133-2/)).
- **Training.** All staff once a year. The record must show the method, date, and each attendee's name and participation. The minimum content is the entity's own manual and the UAF Red Flags Guide ([Prieto](https://www.prieto.cl/en/nueva-circular-n62-instrucciones-generales-para-sujetos-obligados-segun-la-ley-19-9133-2/)).
- **UN list screening.** Screen clients and prospective clients against UN Security Council lists periodically and systematically. Keep the search evidence for at least three years. A match must be reported at once through a ROS ([AZ](https://www.az.cl/claves-para-entender-el-impacto-regulatorio-de-la-circular-n62-de-la-uaf/); [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/uaf-dicta-nuevo-marco-normativo-para-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo/36509)).
- **Beneficial owner.** This is new for real estate. Clients that are legal entities must sign a declaration on the UAF's base form. The beneficial owner is anyone with at least 10% of capital or votes, or with effective control. The entity must check the declaration reasonably and record that check. If a client delays the declaration more than 40 business days, that is a red flag that may trigger a ROS ([Guerrero Olivos](https://guerrero.cl/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DOC_Circular-N°62-UAF.pdf); [AZ](https://www.az.cl/claves-para-entender-el-impacto-regulatorio-de-la-circular-n62-de-la-uaf/)).
- **PEPs.** Check whether a client or beneficial owner is a politically exposed person. If so: senior management approval, source of wealth and funds, and ongoing due diligence ([Prieto](https://www.prieto.cl/en/nueva-circular-n62-instrucciones-generales-para-sujetos-obligados-segun-la-ley-19-9133-2/)).
- **ROS.** Report suspicious operations as soon as possible, with supporting documents. There is no threshold ([Prieto](https://www.prieto.cl/en/nueva-circular-n62-instrucciones-generales-para-sujetos-obligados-segun-la-ley-19-9133-2/)). The analysis must be documented: its phases, the steps taken and the sources consulted ([Carey](https://www.carey.cl/api/archivo/uaf-dicta-nuevo-marco-normativo-para-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo?lang=es)).
- **ROE.** Report cash operations above USD 10,000 (about 450 UF), including cash deposits into the entity's own accounts. Depending on the sector, this is due monthly, quarterly or every six months ([AZ](https://www.az.cl/claves-para-entender-el-impacto-regulatorio-de-la-circular-n62-de-la-uaf/); [Prieto](https://www.prieto.cl/en/nueva-circular-n62-instrucciones-generales-para-sujetos-obligados-segun-la-ley-19-9133-2/)). I did not confirm the exact frequency for brokers, or whether a "nil" ROE is required (unverified).
- **Records.** Keep five permanent registers: cash operations, suspicious operations, due diligence/KYC, PEP operations, and electronic transfers. Keep them at least 5 years after the relationship ends ([Guerrero Olivos](https://guerrero.cl/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DOC_Circular-N°62-UAF.pdf); [AZ](https://www.az.cl/claves-para-entender-el-impacto-regulatorio-de-la-circular-n62-de-la-uaf/)).
- **Proportionality.** In consultation, the UAF dropped a proposed mandatory Prevention Committee, for a more proportional approach by size ([Guerrero Olivos](https://guerrero.cl/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DOC_Circular-N°62-UAF.pdf)).

**Penalties (Ley 19.913 art. 20).** Fines go up to 800 UF for minor breaches, 3,000 UF for less serious ones and 5,000 UF for serious ones. Repeat breaches within 12 months can be fined up to three times as much. Every sanction comes with a written reprimand ([iura.cl art. 20](https://iura.cl/19913/20)). The limitation period is 3 years for minor and 5 years for serious breaches ([Carey on Ley 21.314](https://www.carey.cl/api/archivo/efectos-en-materia-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-de-la-ley-n-21-314-que-regula-a-los-agentes-del-mercado-y-asesores-previsionales?lang=es)).

**Enforcement in practice.**
- In 2018 the UAF closed 199 sanction cases with UF 6,250 in fines. Brokers: 23 sanctioned, 775 UF in total. Notaries: 18, 760 UF. Real-estate firms: 26, 705 UF. Most fines came from on-site inspections (107 fines) and late or missing ROE (86 fines) ([Diario Estrategia](https://www.diarioestrategia.cl/texto-diario/mostrar/1401968/uaf-usuarios-zonas-francas-corredores-propiedades-notarios-empresas-gestion-inmobiliarias-explican-55-multas-infracciones-normativa-antivalado)). So the average was about 27 to 42 UF per sanction.
- In 2023 the UAF ran 125 supervisions. Notaries (31) and real-estate firms (27) were the two most inspected sectors. It opened 118 sanction cases, up 127%, of which real-estate firms were 9.3% and notaries 6.8% ([DF, April 2024](https://www.df.cl/mercados/banca-fintech/bancos-y-administradoras-generales-de-fondos-concentraron-mas-de-la)).
- Courts keep upholding small UAF fines: 15 UF ([Diario Constitucional 2025](https://www.diarioconstitucional.cl/2025/08/06/corte-suprema-respalda-sancion-de-la-uaf-por-incumplimientos-en-deber-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos/)), 40 UF for a remittance firm ([2026](https://www.diarioconstitucional.cl/2026/03/18/corte-de-santiago-confirma-multa-de-40-uf-a-empresa-de-remesas-por-incumplimientos-en-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos/)), and 50 UF for a casino ([2025](https://www.diarioconstitucional.cl/2025/05/18/se-confirma-multa-de-50-uf-a-casino-por-incumplir-obligaciones-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos/)).
- Earlier, real-estate firms were among the most sanctioned sectors in 2021, mostly for a missing manual or missing training ([Prieto](https://www.prieto.cl/en/sanciones-de-la-uaf-2021-empresas-del-sector-inmobiliario-y-administradoras-de-fondos-de-inversion-fueron-las-mas-sancionadas-2/)).
- I found no official 2024 or 2025 sanction totals (unverified).

**Changes in progress.**
- The UAF reporting portal moves to Clave Única login on 19 October 2026 (reported as "Circular N°543", 25 August 2026). Automated ROE/ROS filers get separate access ([Prieto](https://www.prieto.cl/en/uaf-implementa-autenticacion-mediante-clave-unica-en-el-portal-de-entidades-reportantes-2/)).
- A government bill creating an economic intelligence subsystem would raise maximum fines, up to 45,000 UF for serious breaches. Its status is unverified ([Hacienda](https://www.hacienda.cl/subsecretaria/noticias/proyecto-que-crea-subsistema-de-inteligencia-economica-aumenta-montos-de-las)).
- Broker regulation bill Boletín 18.241-03. ECIJA describes a mandatory national broker register, AML training as a licence condition, a mandatory AML policy, KYC and beneficial-owner checks, 10-year record keeping, fines of 10 to 1,000 UF, and 12 to 24 months to adapt ([ECIJA, July 2026](https://www.ecija.com/actualidad-insights/proyecto-de-ley-boletin-n-18241-03/)). ECIJA names the supervisor as the "SVS", a body replaced by the CMF in 2017, so its summary may be unreliable. A Senate motion to license brokers was filed in May 2026 by senators Calisto, Espinoza, Sanhueza, Walker and Sepúlveda ([Diario Constitucional](https://www.diarioconstitucional.cl/2026/05/19/iniciativa-legal-busca-regular-el-corretaje-de-propiedades-y-exige-licencia-para-ejercer-la-actividad/)). Its committee stage is unverified. An earlier broker register bill (Boletín 10391) passed the Chamber in 2018 and stalled ([Cámara](https://www.camara.cl/cms/?p=14584)).

## Buyers

**Official count.** The UAF register of reporting entities at 30 June 2025, as republished by CIPER, has 8,970 rows ([CIPER/UAF list](https://www.ciperchile.cl/wp-content/uploads/Buscador_sujetos_obligados_inscritos_en_la_UAF_al_30.06.2025.xlsx-Entidades-Supervisadas.pdf)). My count by sector from that file:

| Sector | Registered entities (30/06/2025) |
|---|---|
| Free-zone users | 2,702 |
| Real-estate management firms (gestión inmobiliaria) | 2,009 |
| Property brokers (corredores de propiedades) | 1,383 |
| Notaries | 496 |
| Exchange houses | 329 |
| Auction houses | 282 |
| Vehicle dealers (new/used) | 278 |
| Customs agents | 273 |
| Conservadores (property registrars) | 93 |

The counts come from my own row tally and may be off slightly where names wrap.

- **Core target.** Brokers, real-estate firms, notaries and conservadores total about 3,980 entities. The register grew from 5,640 entities in 2015, when there were 1,037 brokers and 478 real-estate firms ([DIPRES](https://www.dipres.gob.cl/597/articles-60655_doc_pdf.pdf)). Real-estate registrations grew fourfold.
- **Size.** About half of the registered brokers are companies (Ltda, SpA, SA). The rest appear to be natural persons, i.e. sole agents (my tally from the same file). Many real-estate firms are one-project SPVs ("Inmobiliaria X SpA") of the same developer group. So the number of buying decisions is lower than 2,009.
- **Coverage gap.** Industry sources put active brokers at 5,000 to 7,000 (ACOP), "more than 20,000" (Sotheby's Realty), and more than 30,000 SII registrations in 2020 ([Emol, Aug 2025](https://www.emol.com/noticias/Economia/2025/08/27/1176126/corredores-de-propiedades.html)). Only 1,383 brokers are registered with the UAF. Most active brokers are therefore outside the system.
- **How they comply today.** Mostly in-house and by hand: Word manuals, Excel logs, manual searches on UN lists, and filing on the free UAF portal. Larger developers and notaries use law firms or AML SaaS (Gesintel, Regcheq). This is inferred from vendor sector lists and law-firm marketing (partly unverified). Missing manuals and missing training are the classic findings ([Prieto 2021](https://www.prieto.cl/en/sanciones-de-la-uaf-2021-empresas-del-sector-inmobiliario-y-administradoras-de-fondos-de-inversion-fueron-las-mas-sancionadas-2/)), which suggests many small firms do the minimum or less.

## Competition

- **UAF Portal de Entidades Reportantes (free).** It handles registration and ROS/ROE filing, with Clave Única login from October 2026 ([Prieto](https://www.prieto.cl/en/uaf-implementa-autenticacion-mediante-clave-unica-en-el-portal-de-entidades-reportantes-2/)). It does not keep the KYC file, the screening log, the training register or the manual. The UAF also publishes free guidance: a Red Flags Guide and a beneficial-owner base form ([Prieto](https://www.prieto.cl/en/nueva-circular-n62-instrucciones-generales-para-sujetos-obligados-segun-la-ley-19-9133-2/); [Guerrero Olivos](https://guerrero.cl/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DOC_Circular-N°62-UAF.pdf)). I found no free UAF e-learning course for Chile (unverified). Other regional FIUs (Colombia, Ecuador, Panama) have one, so the UAF could add one later.
- **Regcheq (Chile, Las Condes).** A RegTech platform covering Ley 19.913: KYC, monitoring against national and international watch lists, and ROS. It is described as "low cost" and claims 500+ clients in Latin America. No public price and no free trial ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10047938/Regcheq/); [Ensun](https://ensun.io/company/regcheq-64a56ca95c5c13bc92c74a90); [ComparaSoftware](https://www.comparasoftware.com/regcheq)). This is the strongest threat for SMEs. Whether it has a real-estate package is unverified.
- **Gesintel AMLupdate (Chile).** Due diligence, continuous monitoring, UBOfinder (beneficial owners, 1.5 million Chilean firms covered), PEP lists, onboarding and whistleblower channel. It targets notaries, banks, casinos, car dealers, municipalities and "sujetos obligados" (free zones). It does not list brokers or real-estate firms. Claims 350+ clients. No public price ([gesintel.cl](https://www.gesintel.cl/)).
- **Avos Tech AMLcheck.** KYC, ongoing monitoring and alerts. Not specific to real estate ([search summary of Avos Tech release, 2025](https://www.mediabanco.com/?p=340006), unverified detail).
- **Law firms and consultants.** Prieto, Carey, AZ, Guerrero Olivos, ECIJA and CMS all publish Circular 62 alerts and sell manuals, training and reviews ([AZ](https://www.az.cl/claves-para-entender-el-impacto-regulatorio-de-la-circular-n62-de-la-uaf/); [Carey](https://www.carey.cl/api/archivo/uaf-dicta-nuevo-marco-normativo-para-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo?lang=es); [CMS](https://cms.law/es/chl/publication/alerta-compliance2)). They show no prices. Smaller compliance boutiques probably sell manual-plus-training packs (unverified).
- **Broker CRMs and portals.** I found no Chilean broker CRM with a UAF module ([search summary](https://www.df.cl/economia-y-politica/detectan-cientos-de-empresas-y-personas-incumpliendo-norma-anti-lavado), no product found). This is unverified for CRMs such as Wasi or Kiteprop, which I could not check.
- **Templates.** I found no published model manual for Chilean brokers. Large firms publish their own: Salfacorp has a full AML model PDF ([Salfacorp](https://salfacorp.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/1-Modelo-de-Prevencion-de-Lavado-y-Blanqueo-de-Activos.pdf)), and small firms can copy such documents for free.

## Willingness to pay

- **Cost of fines.** Typical real-estate fines are small: averages of 27 to 42 UF per sanction in 2018 ([Diario Estrategia](https://www.diarioestrategia.cl/texto-diario/mostrar/1401968/uaf-usuarios-zonas-francas-corredores-propiedades-notarios-empresas-gestion-inmobiliarias-explican-55-multas-infracciones-normativa-antivalado)), and 15 to 50 UF in court cases from 2025 and 2026 ([Diario Constitucional](https://www.diarioconstitucional.cl/2026/03/18/corte-de-santiago-confirma-multa-de-40-uf-a-empresa-de-remesas-por-incumplimientos-en-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos/)). 1 UF is about CLP 39,000 to 40,000, or about USD 41 (unverified rate). So a typical fine is about USD 600 to 2,000. The legal ceiling is much higher: 800 to 5,000 UF, tripled for repeats ([iura.cl](https://iura.cl/19913/20)). The real cost is also the reprimand, the time spent on the inspection, and the reputational risk with banks.
- **Inspection odds.** The UAF ran about 125 supervisions in 2023 across all sectors, 27 of them at real-estate firms and 31 at notaries ([DF](https://www.df.cl/mercados/banca-fintech/bancos-y-administradoras-generales-de-fondos-concentraron-mas-de-la)). That is roughly a 1% chance per year for a real-estate firm and about 6% for a notary.
- **What buyers pay today.** I found no public prices from consultants or SaaS vendors (unverified). Enterprise AML SaaS in Chile is quote-based.
- **Plausible price.** About 0.5 to 1 UF per month (CLP 20,000 to 40,000) for a solo or small broker. About 1.5 to 3 UF per month for a developer group with several SPVs, or for a notary. A one-off pack (manual plus annual training plus certificate) might fetch 3 to 6 UF. All of these prices are my estimates (unverified).
- **Revenue ceiling.** With about 4,000 target entities and fewer real buying decisions, 10% uptake at an average of 12 UF/year is about 4,800 UF, or roughly USD 200,000 a year. That is fine for a one-person business but not more. The upside comes from the broker licensing bill, if it brings thousands of unregistered brokers into the system.

## Channels

- **ACOP** (Cámara Nacional de Servicios Inmobiliarios), led by Soledad Gaete. It is the main broker trade body, with about 300 member firms, regional chapters (Maule, GRECOP) and a "professionalisation" agenda ([Infonegocios](https://infonegocios.info/default/chile-ojo-si-arrendas-este-verano-1-de-cada-4-corredores-de-propiedades-esta-registrado); [Revista Economía](https://www.revistaeconomia.com/acop-suma-nuevos-actores-regionales-a-su-agenda-de-profesionalizacion-del-corretaje-inmobiliario/)). It runs annual broker events ([vLex](https://vlex.cl/vid/acop-camara-desarrollo-ix-547492158)). This is the best partner: AML training fits its push for licensing.
- **COPROCH** (Asociación Gremial de Corredores de Propiedades de Chile) and **Anacopro** (Asociación Nacional de Corredores de Propiedades) ([PJUD filing](https://www.pjud.cl/prensa-y-comunicaciones/docs/download/63672); [Emol](https://www.emol.com/noticias/Economia/2025/08/27/1176126/corredores-de-propiedades.html)).
- **Notaries.** The Asociación de Notarios, Conservadores y Archiveros is a likely channel (unverified). Gesintel already sells to this segment.
- **Developers.** The ADI (Asociación de Desarrolladores Inmobiliarios) and the CChC are likely channels for real-estate SPVs (unverified).
- **Direct outreach.** The UAF register itself, republished by CIPER, gives names and RUTs of all 3,980 target entities ([CIPER/UAF list](https://www.ciperchile.cl/wp-content/uploads/Buscador_sujetos_obligados_inscritos_en_la_UAF_al_30.06.2025.xlsx-Entidades-Supervisadas.pdf)). That allows direct outreach, and a check of the 10-day update duty.
- **Accountants and law firms.** Use them as resellers. Small brokers often rely on their accountant for SII matters (unverified).
- **Content.** Timely content on Circular 62, the Clave Única switch (19 October 2026) and the broker bill.

## Risks

- **Small market, weak pressure.** About 4,000 entities, small typical fines and about 1% yearly inspection odds for real-estate firms. Many owners will choose to do nothing.
- **Regcheq or Gesintel move down-market.** Both already cover screening, beneficial owners and ROS. A real-estate starter plan from either would squeeze a newcomer.
- **UAF free tools.** The UAF already gives the beneficial-owner form, the Red Flags Guide and the filing portal for free. A free e-learning course, as in Colombia, Ecuador and Panama, would remove the training revenue.
- **Officer cannot be outsourced.** The compliance officer must be internal ([AZ](https://www.az.cl/claves-para-entender-el-impacto-regulatorio-de-la-circular-n62-de-la-uaf/)). An "outsourced officer" service is therefore not possible. The product must be a tool and a guide, not a substitute.
- **Bill uncertainty.** Broker licensing has stalled before (Boletín 10391 in 2018). The 2026 bills may stall too.
- **Liability.** If a client is fined for a weak manual sold by the vendor, it may blame the vendor. This needs disclaimers and review by a Chilean lawyer.
- **Data.** Storing client IDs and beneficial-owner data brings Chile's new personal data law into play. Ley 21.719 is due in force in December 2026 (unverified date).
- **Barriers.** The work is in Spanish only and needs Chilean legal knowledge. There is no licensing barrier for selling the software itself.

## First product

**Version 1: "Kit UAF Inmobiliario" (web app plus document pack), for brokers, small developers and notaries.**

1. **Onboarding check.** Is the entity registered with the UAF? Are the OdC and legal representative up to date? A 10-business-day reminder after any change.
2. **Manual generator.** A Circular 62 manual tailored to brokers, developers or notaries, built from a questionnaire. It includes a two-year review reminder and a log of delivery to each worker.
3. **Client file (KYC).** Client identity. The UAF beneficial-owner form sent by link for e-signature, with a 40-business-day alert. A PEP question. UN Security Council list screening with timestamped evidence stored for 3+ years.
4. **Registers.** The five permanent registers, with CSV export for inspections. A ROE helper that flags cash payments above USD 10,000 and tracks the period due.
5. **ROS analysis log.** A guided record of the phases, steps and sources consulted, then a hand-off to the UAF portal. Do not file on the user's behalf in version 1.
6. **Annual training.** A 45-minute video and quiz covering the Red Flags Guide, with an attendance record and certificates.

**First 30 days.**
- Days 1 to 7: get the official Circular 62 text, the UAF beneficial-owner form and the Red Flags Guide. Have a Chilean AML lawyer review the outline.
- Days 8 to 20: build the manual generator, the client file with UN list screening (the consolidated UN XML is public) and the training record.
- Days 21 to 30: interview 10 brokers from the UAF register and pitch ACOP. Run a paid pilot with 3 to 5 firms at about 0.5 UF per month.
