# Croatia B3: compliance kit for employers of third-country workers

## Re-assessment (owner's criteria)

**Verdict: maybe. New score: 6/10. Old score: 5/10.**

**The case.** Employers of third-country workers must keep permits, notices, housing and renewals in order, and the state tools only take applications. Those tools are slow and buggy, with no status tracking ([Novi list, 2 Sep 2025](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1523976); [Informator, 25 Aug 2025](https://informator.hr/vijesti/8627)). I still found no Croatian product that tracks foreign-worker permits and deadlines. The best first buyers are the 752 active employment agencies, which run many workers and many client employers at once ([Novi list, 17 Jan 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1610671)). A simple register, deadline engine and pre-filing checklist could reach about 200,000 EUR a year by year 3. That is a fair small business, but willingness to pay is not yet proven.

### Room for improvement over the portal or current practice

- **The portal itself is painful.** An agency director said the HZZ/MUP application "constantly crashes, is full of errors and bugs". It also locks a worker's record so the workplace cannot be changed. Requests that took 7 days took 2-3 months ([Novi list, 2 Sep 2025](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1523976)).
- **Long waits and no status view.** The crafts chamber (HOK) says employers wait more than four months for a permit. It asked for a digital platform to file and track status ([Informator, 25 Aug 2025](https://informator.hr/vijesti/8627)). Status checks were only "being examined" by HZZ ([Novi list](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=875299)). Software cannot fix state delays. It can keep an employer's own case list, statuses and chase dates in one place.
- **More data to prepare before each filing.** Since 30 April 2026, a permit request needs housing proof: an owner's statement on new form 18a with rent and floor area. Visa-regime workers also need health and vaccination proof, and extensions need a health exam certificate ([Informator, 11 May 2026](https://informator.hr/vijesti/bitne-izmjene-pravilnika-o-boravku-drzavljana-trecih-zemalja)). Flats in buildings with 4+ units need co-owner consent if more than four unrelated adults live there (same). Occupancy caps apply: at most 8 workers in a 56 m² flat, 10 per 150 m² house (ministry presentation as reported in a search summary; (unverified)). A pre-filing checklist and housing check fits this well.
- **Short deadlines the portal does not track.** Notice to police after dismissal: 5 days according to an agency director ([Novi list, 2 Sep 2025](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1523976)). The exact legal deadline is still (unverified). Address changes: 8 days (from a 2025 report; (unverified)). Posting abroad: 8 days ([zakon.hr, Art. 178](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima)).
- **Enforcement got stricter.** An agency owner said that until a December 2025 change, employers often deregistered workers 2-3 days after expiry and paid a fine of about 60 EUR ([Novi list, 17 Jan 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1610671)). Current fines for the same lapses are 500-3,000 EUR ([zakon.hr, Art. 251](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima)). Missing a date now costs much more.
- **Multi-client work.** In 2024, 41% of foreign workers worked through temporary-work agencies ([Novi list, 17 Jan 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1610671)). Agencies juggle many workers, client sites and police areas. The new police-area limit makes this harder ([RRiF](https://www.rrif.hr/izmjene_zakona_o_strancima_stupaju_na_snagu_4_lipn-2521-vijest/)).
- **Inspection readiness.** The permit is a required field in the statutory worker register ([Pravilnik, NN 55/24](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2024_05_55_969.html)). Inspectors found 961 illegally working third-country nationals in 2025 and closed 263 employers ([DIRH 2025 report](https://dirh.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/dokumenti/GODISNJI%20IZVJESTAJ%20O%20RADU%20DIRH%20ZA%202025.%20godinu.pdf)).
- **Demand for help is visible.** HGK Varaždin ran a seminar on 7 Oct 2026, "Strani radnici i nove obveze poslodavaca u 2026", with police, HZZ and visa speakers ([HGK programme](https://hgk.hr/documents/program-zakon-o-strancima7-10-266a859819dbcbf.pdf)). Informator sells a 125 EUR workshop on the topic ([informator.hr](https://informator.hr/seminari/novosti-u-zaposljavanju-i-radu-drzavljana-trecih-zemalja-primjeri-i-iskustva-iz-prakse)).

### Competitor reality check

- **Moja Firma** (Croatian HR app): free up to 5 staff, 29.99 EUR/month up to 25, 59.99 EUR/month up to 100. Features are time records, leave, travel orders, a document archive and "HR overview and warnings". The price page says nothing about foreign workers, permits or expiry reminders ([mojafirma.hr/cijene](https://mojafirma.hr/cijene)). It does publish a foreign-worker guide ([mojafirma.hr](https://mojafirma.hr/clanci/zaposljavanje-stranaca-radne-dozvole)). It is cheap and close, so it is the likeliest fast follower. It does not do the job today.
- **DigRa ERV** and similar Croatian working-time apps cover records, leave and shifts. No permit tracking was found ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6751578393)). Some digital record tools can track ID-card or medical-exam expiry, but none was confirmed for residence permits ([Unija](https://unija.com/hr/kadrovska-evidencija-kljucna-za-uspjesno-poslovanje/)).
- **Payroll suites** (Pantheon, Synesis): one targeted search found no foreign-worker module (unverified; not checked vendor by vendor).
- **Foreign tools**: HRappka handles foreign-worker legalisation under Polish law only ([hrappka.pl](https://hrappka.pl/en/?p=61870)). An Odoo expiry add-on costs about 249 USD but has no Croatian rules ([ecosire](https://ecosire.com/apps/odoo/hr-employee-documents-expiry)).
- **EOR firms** charge about 199-599 USD per worker per month. They handle third-country permits only through partners, case by case ([employsome](https://employsome.com/employer-of-record/best-croatia-eor/); [People Managing People](https://peoplemanagingpeople.com/tools/best-employer-of-record-services-croatia/)). This is far too expensive for seasonal hotel or building staff.
- **Agencies and lawyers** do the filing as a service. Their fees are not published ([Novi list](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1523976) says commissions vary). They are more likely buyers than rivals.
- **Conclusion:** no local product does the whole job, and the near ones are partial. This is an opening, not a killer.

### Price per customer

- **Anchors.** HR app: 29.99-59.99 EUR/month ([mojafirma.hr/cijene](https://mojafirma.hr/cijene)). A 2.5-hour workshop: 125 EUR ([informator.hr](https://informator.hr/seminari/novosti-u-zaposljavanju-i-radu-drzavljana-trecih-zemalja-primjeri-i-iskustva-iz-prakse)). One missed notice: 500-3,000 EUR fine. Lifting a 30-day shutdown: 3,981.68 EUR per worker ([DIRH](https://dirh.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/dokumenti/GODISNJI%20IZVJESTAJ%20O%20RADU%20DIRH%20ZA%202025.%20godinu.pdf)). State fees: 74.32 EUR per permit plus 31.85 EUR card ([EU Immigration Portal](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/employed-worker-croatia_en)).
- **Direct employer:** 2-3 EUR per foreign worker per month, or tiers of about 25-79 EUR/month. Typical small employer: about 40 EUR/month (estimate).
- **Agency or accountant (multi-client):** 99-299 EUR/month by worker count. Typical: about 150 EUR/month (estimate). One avoided fine pays for a year.
- These prices are my estimates. No buyer has confirmed them (unverified).

### Revenue estimate (year 3)

- **Agencies:** 752 active agencies ([Novi list, 17 Jan 2026](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1610671)) x 8% share = 60 customers x 150 EUR x 12 = **108,000 EUR**.
- **Direct employers:** 10,000 employers (low end of my 10,000-25,000 estimate, (unverified)) x 2% = 200 customers x 40 EUR x 12 = **96,000 EUR**.
- **Total base case: about 204,000 EUR a year.**
- Low case: 30 agencies x 100 x 12 = 36,000 + 100 employers x 30 x 12 = 36,000 = **72,000 EUR**.
- High case: 100 agencies x 200 x 12 = 240,000 + 400 employers x 50 x 12 = 240,000 = **480,000 EUR**.
- Note: a Sep 2025 article said about 800 agencies registered but only about 50 active ([Novi list](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1523976)). The Jan 2026 ministry figure (752 active) is used here. If the real active base is nearer 50-100 serious agencies, the agency line falls to about 20,000-40,000 EUR.

### Ease of implementation and sale

- **Build: easy to medium.** Version 1 is a worker register, a deadline engine, a pre-filing checklist (form 18a, health proof, zadužnica, turnover) and an inspection export. Excel import gets data in fast. No state API is needed.
- **Onboarding: easy.** Agencies already keep worker lists in spreadsheets (unverified). Import plus reminders shows value in a day.
- **Sale: medium.** Agencies are a named, countable list. HGK county seminars, Informator, RRiF and TEB reach HR staff and accountants ([HGK programme](https://hgk.hr/documents/program-zakon-o-strancima7-10-266a859819dbcbf.pdf)). Small hotels and builders are seasonal and price-sensitive.
- **Upkeep: the hard part.** Rules changed in March 2025, April 2026 and June 2026, and more rulebooks are due by about December 2026 ([zakon.hr](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima)). The product needs a lawyer on call.

### Remaining risks

- Willingness to pay is not tested. Agencies may stay on spreadsheets.
- Moja Firma or a payroll vendor could add a permit-expiry field cheaply.
- Permits fell 17% in 2025 ([moj-posao.net](https://moj-posao.net/EN/Faq/Details/83934/Lani-je-izdano-vise-od-170-tisuca-dozvola-stranim-radnicima-najvise-u-graditeljstvu/2)). Stricter rules may push out small employers.
- The real number of active agencies and employers is unclear (see above).
- A wrong deadline in the tool could cause a fine. Terms must exclude legal advice.
- The state may add status tracking to the HZZ app, which would remove one selling point (but not deadline or housing tracking).

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 5/10.**

The duty is real, it changes often, and it is enforced. Croatia issued 94,113 residence-and-work permits in the first half of 2026 alone, 36,601 of them renewals ([MUP, June 2026 statistics](https://mup.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/2026/7/Mjesecne%20statistike%20LIPANJ%202026.pdf)). Employers face fines of 5,000-15,000 EUR per worker for unpermitted work, and 500-3,000 EUR for missed notices and renewals ([Zakon o strancima, consolidated, Arts. 247 and 251](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima)). Labour inspectors found 961 third-country nationals working against the law in 2025 and shut down 263 employers for 30 days ([DIRH annual report 2025, pp. 26-27](https://dirh.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/dokumenti/GODISNJI%20IZVJESTAJ%20O%20RADU%20DIRH%20ZA%202025.%20godinu.pdf)). I found no Croatian software that tracks permits, deadlines and the 2026 rule changes. The weak points are thin product depth (mostly reminders and checklists), unknown willingness to pay among small hotels and builders, agencies and lawyers already doing the hard part, and a state e-records system (JEER) that is growing towards permit checks.

## Duty

**Legal basis.** Zakon o strancima (NN 133/20, 114/22, 151/22, 40/25, 55/26). The last amendment was published 27 May 2026 and is in force from 4 June 2026 ([zakon.hr](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima); [RRiF](https://www.rrif.hr/izmjene_zakona_o_strancima_stupaju_na_snagu_4_lipn-2521-vijest/)). The amending act is NN 55/26 ([Narodne novine](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2026_05_55_692.html); page was down when fetched, so the article texts below come from the zakon.hr consolidated version).

**What changed on 4 June 2026** ([RRiF](https://www.rrif.hr/izmjene_zakona_o_strancima_stupaju_na_snagu_4_lipn-2521-vijest/)):
- An employer that does not actively run a registered business tied to the job cannot get a permit.
- A worker hired after a labour-market test may work only in the police administration area where the test was done. For shortage jobs, only in areas where the job is on the HZZ shortage list.
- Changing employer or occupation is made easier. The "after 6 months" detail came from the first check and is (unverified) here.
- New conditions for the HZZ positive opinion.
- The Croatian language exam (A1.1 claim unverified) is postponed to 4 June 2027. The employer pays for it ([RRiF](https://www.rrif.hr/izmjene_zakona_o_strancima_stupaju_na_snagu_4_lipn-2521-vijest/); [tportal](https://www.tportal.hr/vijesti/clanak/velike-promjene-za-strane-radnike-bez-znanja-jezika-nema-ostanka-sindikati-nezadovoljni-odredbom-foto-20260424)). The transitional article says Art. 92.a applies one year after entry into force ([zakon.hr, NN 55/26 Art. 70](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima)).
- Board members who are third-country nationals must hold a key-personnel work permit. The application is due within 30 days of entry or change in the court register ([Crowe Croatia](https://www.crowe.com/hr/news/sto-nam-donose-nove-izmjene-zakona-o-strancima), secondary source; not checked against the gazette text, so (unverified)).
- Permits issued under the old law stay valid until expiry. Pending cases finish under the old law ([zakon.hr, NN 55/26 Arts. 67-68](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima)).
- More rules are coming. The government must issue a decree within 3 months and rulebooks (residence, language exam, visas, HZZ records) within 6 months, so roughly by December 2026 ([zakon.hr, NN 55/26 Art. 69](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima)).

**Other live employer duties:**
- Notify police when the contract ends or a permit condition lapses (Art. 95(1)). The first check found a 15-day deadline; one agency director quoted 5 days ([Novi list](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1523976)). Exact current deadline (unverified).
- Notify police within 8 days when a worker is posted to another EEA state or Switzerland (Art. 178(5)) ([zakon.hr](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima)).
- File extension requests on time; report changes of employer, occupation or accommodation; request approval for additional work (Arts. 92, 100, 103.c-103.f) ([zakon.hr, Art. 251(4)](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima)).
- Provide adequate housing. The amended residence rulebook (NN 43/26, in force 30 April 2026) sets about 14 m² for one worker, at most 8 workers per flat, and rent at most 30% of net pay ([Novi list](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=1669564); [informator.hr](https://informator.hr/vijesti/bitne-izmjene-pravilnika-o-boravku-drzavljana-trecih-zemalja)). Exact figures differ between sources (partly unverified).
- Lodge a promissory note (zadužnica) of 1,821 EUR per worker within 5 days for visa-country workers hired on an HZZ opinion ([mojafirma.hr](https://mojafirma.hr/clanci/zaposljavanje-stranaca-radne-dozvole)).
- Prove turnover to get an HZZ opinion. One source says 10,000 EUR a month over 6 months ([informator.hr](https://informator.hr/vijesti/ostvareni-promet-po-racunu-novi-uvjet-za-izdavanje-radnih-dozvola)); another says 100,000 EUR inflow over 12 months ([TEB](https://www.teb.hr/novosti/2025/dozvole-za-boravak-i-rad-temeljem-misljenja-hzz-a/)). Current figure (unverified).
- Record each worker's permit in the statutory register of workers ([Pravilnik, NN 55/24](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2024_05_55_969.html)). Paper or electronic is allowed. Not keeping the register can cost 8,090-13,270 EUR for a legal person ([Unija](https://unija.com/hr/kadrovska-evidencija-kljucna-za-uspjesno-poslovanje/)).

**Penalties (current consolidated text)** ([zakon.hr, Arts. 247, 251](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima)):

| Offence | Legal person | Responsible person | Natural-person employer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Worker staying illegally (247(1)) | 10,000-20,000 EUR per worker | 3,000-6,000 EUR | 2,000-6,000 EUR per worker |
| No permit, or work outside the permitted job or employer (247(2)) | 5,000-15,000 EUR per worker | 2,000-6,000 EUR | 1,500-3,000 EUR per worker |
| Late notice of contract end or lost conditions; bad housing (251(1)) | 1,500-3,000 EUR | 500-1,000 EUR | 700-1,500 EUR |
| 16 lesser failures: no extension request, no notice of employer/job/housing change, posting (251(4)) | 500-1,500 EUR | 50-400 EUR | 100-700 EUR |

A 2025 Varaždin court case applied a 660-920 EUR fine for late notice ([zakon.hr case law](https://www.zakon.hr/c/sudska-praksa/654468/pp-1412-2025-4---opcinski-sud-u-varazdinu)). That matches the older fine band; the current band for that offence is 1,500-3,000 EUR.

**Enforcement is real.** In 2025 labour inspectors ran 7,903 labour-relations inspections. They found 961 third-country nationals working without a required permit or registration ([DIRH 2025 report, p. 26](https://dirh.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/dokumenti/GODISNJI%20IZVJESTAJ%20O%20RADU%20DIRH%20ZA%202025.%20godinu.pdf)). They banned 263 employers from operating for 30 days. 156 of them paid 3,981.68 EUR per worker to lift the ban, 1,059,126.88 EUR in total (same, p. 27). 581 employers were on the Ministry's undeclared-work "blacklist" on 19 December 2025. Since March 2025 a listing can block new permits and cancel existing ones (same, p. 28). Police and inspectors checked housing of 62 Indian and Nepali workers in Čazma in August 2026 ([tportal](https://www.tportal.hr/vijesti/clanak/zbog-jezivog-otkrica-u-cazmi-oglasio-se-i-bozinovic-jednostavno-nedopustivo-20260804)). Capacity is thin: only 97 labour-relations inspectors at end 2025 (DIRH report, p. 26).

- **Geography.** Zagreb county police area 21,092; Istria 15,739; Split-Dalmatia 12,271; Primorje-Gorski kotar 9,799; Dubrovnik 8,058 ([MUP](https://mup.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/2026/7/Mjesecne%20statistike%20LIPANJ%202026.pdf)). The new police-area limit makes this map matter to employers.
- **Origin.** Bosnia and Herzegovina 17,330, Serbia 15,863, Philippines 14,201, Nepal 12,874 ([MUP](https://mup.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/2026/7/Mjesecne%20statistike%20LIPANJ%202026.pdf)).
- **Number of employers.** Not published. The government notes that permit counts do not equal workers or employers ([moj-posao.net](https://moj-posao.net/EN/Faq/Details/83934/Lani-je-izdano-vise-od-170-tisuca-dozvola-stranim-radnicima-najvise-u-graditeljstvu/2)). My estimate is 10,000-25,000 employers, based on roughly 115,000 valid permits and 5-10 foreign workers per employer (unverified).
- **How they comply today.** Employment agencies, lawyers and accountants handle the filing; the employer stays liable even when an agency does the work ([search summary of 2024 ruling](https://www.rrif.hr/ustupanje_stranih_radnika_-2667-sudska_praksa/), unverified). Filing goes through the HZZ online application or to the police. Expiry and notice tracking appears to be done in spreadsheets or HR files (unverified).

## Competition

- **State tools.** The HZZ online application handles permit requests and the HZZ opinion. The "Burza rada" app handles seasonal permits up to 90 days ([N1](https://n1info.hr/english/news/employers-can-apply-online-for-stay-and-work-permits-for-seasonal-workers)). A status-check feature was only "being examined" ([Novi list](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=875299)). These tools file applications. They do not track expiry dates, notice deadlines or housing rules for the employer. **No free state tool covers ongoing compliance tracking** (as far as found).
- **State e-records (JEER).** The Ministry of Labour and APIS IT are upgrading the unified electronic work-records system to real time. It links to the MUP permit system and the pension institute. It starts with platform work (taxi, delivery) and should expand to construction ([tportal](https://www.tportal.hr/biznis/clanak/hrvatska-uvodi-elektronicku-evidenciju-rada-u-stvarnom-vremenu-20260608); [informator.hr](https://informator.hr/vijesti/predstavljen-projekt-elektronicke-evidencije-radnog-vremena-i-edukativne-kampanje)). It serves inspectors, not employers, but it raises the risk of being caught.
- **Croatian HR tools.** Moja Firma (Solution Press d.o.o.) does time records, leave and travel orders: free up to 5 staff, 29.99 EUR/month up to 25, 59.99 EUR/month up to 100 ([mojafirma.hr/cijene](https://mojafirma.hr/cijene)). It publishes a detailed foreign-worker guide but has no permit-tracking feature ([mojafirma.hr](https://mojafirma.hr/clanci/zaposljavanje-stranaca-radne-dozvole)). It is the most likely fast follower. Payroll and HR suites (Pantheon, Synesis, others) were not checked one by one (unverified).
- **Foreign software.** HRappka (Poland) tracks foreign-worker legalisation ([hrappka.pl](https://hrappka.pl/en/?p=55001)). SAP SuccessFactors has a visa module. Odoo has a document-expiry add-on ([ecosire](https://ecosire.com/apps/odoo/hr-employee-documents-expiry)). Factorial sells general HR for construction ([Factorial](https://factorialhr.com/sr-RS/hr-softver-za-gradevinsku-industriju)). None is localised to Croatian law (as found).
- **Services.** Employment agencies, law firms, accountants; EOR firms (Deel, Playroll) from about 599 USD per worker per month ([People Managing People](https://peoplemanagingpeople.com/tools/best-employer-of-record-services-croatia/); [Playroll](https://playroll.com/work-permit-visas/croatia)).
- **Content and training.** Informator, RRiF and TEB sell seminars, webinars and guides on this exact topic ([informator.hr](https://informator.hr/seminari/novosti-u-zaposljavanju-i-radu-drzavljana-trecih-zemalja-primjeri-i-iskustva-iz-prakse); [RRiF](https://www.rrif.hr/novosti_u_zaposljavanju_drzavljana_trecih_zemalja-2517-vijest/); [TEB](https://www.teb.hr/seminari-i-webinari/izmjene-i-dopune-zakona-o-strancima-2/)).

## Willingness to pay

- **Official fees** are small: 74.32 EUR per permit decision plus 31.85 EUR for the card ([EU Immigration Portal](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/employed-worker-croatia_en)).
- **Training.** A 2.5-hour online workshop on these rules costs 125 EUR incl. VAT for non-subscribers ([informator.hr](https://informator.hr/seminari/novosti-u-zaposljavanju-i-radu-drzavljana-trecih-zemalja-primjeri-i-iskustva-iz-prakse)). That is a signal that firms pay to keep up.
- **Agency and lawyer fees** per permit were not found in four searches (unverified).
- **Cost of failure.** One missed renewal notice costs 500-1,500 EUR; a late end-of-contract notice 1,500-3,000 EUR; a worker outside the permitted job or area 5,000-15,000 EUR ([zakon.hr](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima)). A 30-day shutdown is avoided only by paying 3,981.68 EUR per worker ([DIRH](https://dirh.gov.hr/UserDocsImages/dokumenti/GODISNJI%20IZVJESTAJ%20O%20RADU%20DIRH%20ZA%202025.%20godinu.pdf)). Being blacklisted can cancel all permits (same).
- **Plausible price.** 2-4 EUR per foreign worker per month, or 29-99 EUR per month per employer by tier, in line with Moja Firma's pricing. Agencies could pay 150-400 EUR per month for multi-client use (estimate, unverified).

## Channels

- **HGK county chambers.** HGK Varaždin ran "Strani radnici i nove obveze poslodavaca u 2026" on 7 October 2026 with MUP police and HZZ speakers ([HGK programme](https://hgk.hr/documents/program-zakon-o-strancima7-10-266a859819dbcbf.pdf)). Earlier HGK seminars ran in Zadar and Zagreb ([HGK Zadar](https://hgk.hr/documents/programseminar-zaposljavanje-stranih-radnika-zadar67ed23a64419a.pdf); [HGK 2025](https://www.hgk.hr/documents/program-novi-zakon-o-strancima-14-04-202567eb8643279d9.pdf)). Sponsoring or speaking at these is a direct route.
- **Professional publishers.** Informator, RRiF and TEB reach accountants and HR staff. Partnering or advertising with them fits.
- **Employers' associations.** HUP calls this one of the most demanding regulatory areas ([Croatia Week](https://www.croatiaweek.com/croatia-foreign-worker-permit-renewals-surpass-new-hires-2026/)). HUP published member experiences of the law ([HUP](https://hup.hr/EasyEdit/UserFiles/anita-dzapo/2022/prva-godina-novog-zakona-o-strancima-iskustva-clanova-hup-a.pdf)).
- **Agencies and accountants as resellers.** They already handle the filing and would gain a portfolio dashboard.
- **Content SEO.** Croatian searches like "zakon o strancima izmjene 2026" pull publisher and law-firm articles; a clear checklist page could rank (unverified).

## Risks

- **Thin product.** The core is dates, reminders and checklists. Generic HR tools can add a permit-expiry field quickly. Moja Firma already writes the guide.
- **Services own the relationship.** Agencies and lawyers do the filing and may see a tool as a threat, or just keep using spreadsheets.
- **State tools.** JEER links real-time work records to MUP permits ([tportal](https://www.tportal.hr/biznis/clanak/hrvatska-uvodi-elektronicku-evidenciju-rada-u-stvarnom-vremenu-20260608)). HZZ may add status tracking. Neither alerts employers today (as found).
- **Rule churn.** The law changed in March 2025, April 2026 (rulebook) and June 2026. More rulebooks are due by about December 2026 ([zakon.hr](https://www.zakon.hr/z/142/Zakon-o-strancima)). Churn creates demand but needs a legal editor.
- **Shrinking volume.** Permits fell 17% in 2025 ([moj-posao.net](https://moj-posao.net/EN/Faq/Details/83934/Lani-je-izdano-vise-od-170-tisuca-dozvola-stranim-radnicima-najvise-u-graditeljstvu/2)). Stricter rules may push out the smallest employers.
- **Market size.** Perhaps 10,000-25,000 employers (unverified). At 50 EUR/month, 500 paying customers is 300,000 EUR/year; reachable but not large.
- **Liability.** Wrong deadline or wrong rule leads to fines. Terms must exclude legal advice. No licence appears needed for software (unverified).
- **Language.** Croatian only for employers; worker-facing parts may need Nepali, English or other languages.

## First product

**Version 1: "Strani radnici" tracker for small employers and agencies.**
- Worker register with permit type, issue and expiry date, police area, employer, occupation, housing address, zadužnica status.
- Deadline engine: renewal reminders (60/30/15 days), end-of-contract notice, posting notice (8 days), housing-change notice, board-member 30-day filing, language exam from June 2027.
- Rule checks: flag a worker placed outside the permitted police area or job; flag housing over 8 people or under the size rule.
- Inspection-ready export: the permit field for the statutory worker register, and a one-page file per worker.
- Agency view: many client employers in one account.

**First 30 days.**
1. Week 1: get the NN 55/26 gazette text and the NN 43/26 rulebook; confirm every deadline with one lawyer.
2. Week 2: build the register, CSV import from Excel, and e-mail/SMS reminders.
3. Week 3: add the police-area and housing checks and a PDF export.
4. Week 4: pilot with 5-10 hotels or builders in Istria and Split, and 2 agencies. Offer a free tier up to 5 workers. Pitch HGK county chambers and Informator for a webinar slot.
