# Czechia A1: Wine-grower record books and declarations desk

## Summary

**Verdict: no-go. Score: 2/10.**

The duty is real and backed by large fines. Every wine producer must keep evidence books in a set format (Act 321/2004 Sb. § 30, decree 88/2017 Sb. § 13). Producers must also file harvest, production and stock declarations and Wine Fund levy data. The earlier checks found no Czech software for this. That finding is wrong. At least four Czech products already do the job, and they are cheap: Agrowin Sklepmistr (including a "Vinařský sešit" module for small winemakers), Vitipad (3,800 Kč a year), VineOS (free tier, Pro 2,490 Kč a year) and Vinservis eVin (1,200 Kč a month). A state-subsidised course at the Valtice wine academy already trains winemakers on two of them. The paying market is small: the Wine Fund counts 1,342 levy-paying wineries, and 1,061 of these make under 20,000 litres a year. A newcomer would be the fifth vendor in a market worth only a few million Kč a year.

## Duty

- **Legal basis.** Anyone who holds or trades a wine product for business must keep evidence books under EU rules, the Act and its implementing decree (Act 321/2004 Sb., § 30(1)). The books are closed each year as of 31 July (§ 30(5)). Source: https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2004-321 (consolidated version 15, in force from 1 July 2025).
- **Implementing decree.** Decree 88/2017 Sb. § 13 sets the forms: Annex 12 (yield and sugar content), 13 and 14 (grape purchases and purchase slips), 15 (sales of grapes or must), 16 (processing and output), 17 and 18 (sugar, concentrated must, acidification and deacidification), 19 and 20 (spirits from marc and lees), 21 (monthly stocks), 22 (stock and treatment per vessel), 25 (bulk wine sales) and 26 (sparkling wine). Source: https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2017-88 (version in force from 1 August 2024). The decree was amended by 143/2023 Sb. and 305/2023 Sb. (https://www.epravo.cz/_dataPublic/sbirky/2023/sb0143-2023.pdf ; https://www.dauc.cz/predpisy/1416/88-2017-sb).
- **EU rules.** The decree now refers to Delegated Reg. (EU) 2018/273 and Implementing Reg. (EU) 2018/274 for the scope and timing of entries. These replaced Reg. 436/2009, as the earlier checks suspected (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2017-88).
- **Electronic books are allowed.** Each electronic entry must be irreversible and must name the person who made it. A correction must leave the original entry visible. In electronic books, the responsible person's name replaces the signature (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2017-88). This settles the open question from the lead file.
- **Small producers.** A producer making no more than 500 hl a year can use simpler per-vessel records (Annex 22) or a single combined form (Annex 23 or 24) (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2017-88). The exact scope of this relief is (unverified) beyond the summary of the decree text.
- **Entry deadlines.** For bulk wine, a receipt into stock must be entered by the first working day after it happens. Monthly opening and closing stock must be entered by the third working day (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2017-88). The Supreme Administrative Court upheld a fine for late entries and tied the deadline to the moment the producer takes possession (https://sbirka.nssoud.cz/cz/vinohradnictvi-a-vinarstvi-vedeni-evidence-lhuta-pro-zapis-do-evidencnich-knih.p2882.html).
- **Declarations.** Harvest declaration as of 31 December, due 15 January. Growers who process their whole crop themselves are exempt (§ 29(1)-(3)). Production declaration due 15 January (§ 29(4)). Stock declaration on a ministry template (§ 29(5)) (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2004-321). The 2025 MZe calendar listed stocks as of 31 July, due by 10 September (https://mze.gov.cz/public/portal/mze/-a54336---l4RokNv5/kalendar-pro-vinohradniky-a-vinare-2025). I found no 2026 calendar (unverified for 2026).
- **Wine Fund levy.** 0.50 Kč per litre of wine first placed on the market. Producers of under 1,000 litres a year are exempt. Producers under 20,000 litres pay once, by 25 January. Larger ones pay quarterly. Levy data goes to the Fund on its template by the due date (§ 35) (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2004-321). It can be sent through Portál farmáře or a data box (https://portal.gov.cz/sluzby-vs/S22740).
- **Filing channels are free.** Declarations go through the eAgri / Portál farmáře Registr vinic or to ÚKZÚZ (https://portal.gov.cz/sluzby-vs/S19411 ; https://mze.gov.cz/public/portal/mze/-q335635---s2lriK1I/manual-pro-podavani-el-hlaseni-vinaru).
- **Penalties.** Not keeping evidence books, or not showing them on request, is an offence under § 39(6), with a fine of up to 5,000,000 Kč (§ 39(7)(c)). Failing to file the production, stock or harvest declaration also carries up to 5,000,000 Kč (§ 39(2)(y), (2)(z), (3)(i)). ÚKZÚZ and SZPI share supervision (§ 40(2)) (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2004-321).
- **Enforcement.** Court files show SZPI fines for poor records. In one case grapes were recorded for only some suppliers, so batches could not be traced (https://vyhledavac.nssoud.cz/DokumentOriginal/Index/556055). Other cases show fines from 25,000 Kč up to 250,000 Kč, which was reduced on appeal to 220,000 Kč, plus a combined fine of 60,000 Kč (https://www.nssoud.cz/files/SOUDNI_VYKON/2016/0266_2As__1600055_20170223074647_prevedeno.pdf ; https://vyhledavac.nssoud.cz/DokumentOriginal/Index/548851). Which fine belongs to which case is (unverified). SZPI's total fines across all food sectors reached 189 million Kč in 2024 (https://www.pravniprostor.cz/clanky/spravni-pravo/szpi-v-akci-nejcastejsi-prestupky-jak-se-jim-vyhnout). I found no wine-only count of inspections or fines (unverified).
- **Recent changes.** I found no 2025 or 2026 amendment that changes the record-keeping duty. The Act's consolidated text is dated 1 July 2025, but I could not see what that amendment changed (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2004-321) (unverified). New EU wine labelling rules (e-labels) are a side driver; VineOS already sells e-label QR codes (https://vineos.cz/).

## Buyers

- **Wine Fund levy payers (2025 fact sheet).** 1,061 small wineries (up to 20,000 l, average levy 2,736 Kč a year, which implies about 5,500 l). 237 medium wineries (20,000 to 200,000 l, average levy 26,603 Kč). 44 large wineries (over 200,000 l). That is 1,342 in total (https://vinarskyfond.cz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Prinos-VF_2026.pdf).
- **Registered producers.** 2,236 registered wine producers and 596 bulk-wine sellers, from an MZe information response (around 2021) (https://mze.gov.cz/public/portal/mze/-q412235---kNHyrkQe/poskytnute-informace-na-zadosti-o-2). The roughly 900 producers above the levy count probably make under 1,000 l a year. That inference is (unverified).
- **Growers.** About 13,700 growers with planted vineyards at the end of 2022, most of them hobby-sized (https://cdn0.erstegroup.com/content/dam/cz/csas/www_csas_cz/Dokumenty-korporat/Dokumenty/Vinařský_trh_v_Česku_10_2023.pdf). Most do not need cellar books.
- **Reachable list.** The Wine Fund's newsletter reaches over 1,600 subjects (https://vinarskyfond.cz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Prinos-VF_2026.pdf).
- **How they comply today.** Many small winemakers likely keep paper books. The others use Czech software (see Competition). A state-funded course teaches the software, which shows a push to go digital (https://www.vinarska-akademie.cz/kurz-digitalni-evidence.html). Software adoption rates are (unverified).

## Competition

A free state tool covers only filing. The state does not keep the cellar books. But the Czech private market already has several products that do:

| Product | What it does | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Agrowin Sklepmistr** (WIN and CLOUD) | Full cellar records. Generates evidence books for SZPI inspections, sends production and harvest declarations, imports from Portál farmáře. "Vinařský sešit" module is a simple paper replacement for small winemakers. https://www.agrowin.cz/ | Not published (unverified) |
| **Vitipad** (Vialtek s.r.o., Brno) | Batches, vessels, grape intake, purchase slips, printable evidence book, harvest/production/stock declarations, LPIS link. https://vitipad.cz/funkce | 380 Kč a month or 3,800 Kč a year, flat, no limits on users or volume. https://vitipad.cz/cenik |
| **VineOS** | Vineyard and cellar, offline-first, automatic PDF wine book, ÚKZÚZ reports (stocks, production, Wine Fund) as PDF, XLSX or XML, EU e-label, AI assistant. https://vineos.cz/ | Free up to 3 batches. Pro 2,490 Kč a year (99 EUR). https://vineos.cz/ |
| **Vinservis eVin 6 and eVin Blok** | Electronic records of grapes and wine production, costing, Excel export. https://www.vinservis.cz/ | 1,200 Kč a month excl. VAT hosted. From 2,700 Kč a month on the customer's server plus from 40,000 Kč setup. https://www.vinservis.cz/Content/CenikEvin.aspx |
| **"Vinařská evidence"** (download on Slunečnice.cz) | Harvest to bottling, batches, per-tank stock. Version from 2020. https://www.slunecnice.cz/sw/vinarska-evidence/ | Seen only in a search snippet (unverified) |
| **Foreign tools** | vintrace, InnoVint, Vinsight, Vintners Advantage. US/EU features, no Czech annex forms. https://www.capterra.com/winery-software/s/free/ | Higher; not Czech-specific |

- The Valtice wine academy course "Digitální evidence ve vinohradnictví a vinařství" teaches SKLEPMISTR and VITIPAD. It costs 9,500 Kč, or 1,710 Kč with the MPSV "Jsem v kurzu" subsidy. State officials take part (https://www.vinarska-akademie.cz/kurz-digitalni-evidence.html). This gives the two incumbents a quasi-official channel.
- No free Excel template was found (https://www.epravo.cz/top/zakony/sbirka-zakonu/sb/2017/88). The search results here are thin, so this is (unverified).
- I did not confirm the FULLCOM FIS vineyard extension that the challenge check mentioned (https://dspace.vsb.cz/items/6a0ef420-2346-4af6-bcbd-b2a716fedc2e) (unverified).

## Willingness to pay

- Market prices are low: 2,490 to 3,800 Kč a year for full small-winery tools (VineOS, Vitipad), and 14,400 Kč a year for hosted eVin (https://vineos.cz/ ; https://vitipad.cz/cenik ; https://www.vinservis.cz/Content/CenikEvin.aspx).
- A typical small winery pays only 2,736 Kč a year in Wine Fund levy (https://vinarskyfond.cz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Prinos-VF_2026.pdf). This is a rough guide to its scale and budget.
- The maximum fine is 5,000,000 Kč, and real fines run from tens to hundreds of thousands of Kč (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2004-321 ; https://www.nssoud.cz/files/SOUDNI_VYKON/2016/0266_2As__1600055_20170223074647_prevedeno.pdf). So fear of fines does drive buying, but buyers can already get a tool for under 4,000 Kč.
- A rough ceiling: if 1,342 wineries paid 3,000 Kč a year, the whole market would be about 4 million Kč (about 160,000 EUR) a year. That is shared among four or more vendors already. This is my estimate, not a sourced figure.

## Channels

- **Vinařský fond.** Newsletter to over 1,600 subjects. It funds training (2,000 Kč per participant to course organisers) and supports wine associations (https://vinarskyfond.cz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Prinos-VF_2026.pdf).
- **Vinařská akademie Valtice.** Subsidised digital-records course, currently built around the incumbents (https://www.vinarska-akademie.cz/kurz-digitalni-evidence.html).
- **Svaz vinařů ČR** (Velké Bílovice), the largest sector association. Its member count was not found (https://rejstrik-firem.kurzy.cz/48847488) (unverified). Also the Aliance rodinných vinařství (https://www.podnikatel.cz/rejstrik/aliance-rodinnych-vinarstvi-cr-z-s-22681167/) and village wine clubs.
- **ÚKZÚZ and MZe.** The annual "Kalendář pro vinohradníky a vinaře" and the filing manuals (https://mze.gov.cz/public/portal/mze/-a54336---l4RokNv5/kalendar-pro-vinohradniky-a-vinare-2025).
- Agricultural accountants and the Mendel University wine faculty in Lednice are possible routes (unverified).

## Risks

- **Killer: an existing, cheap, local, crowded market.** Four or more Czech vendors already print the evidence books and generate the declarations, one has a free tier, and the two leaders have a state-subsidised training channel (sources above).
- **Market too small.** 1,342 levy payers, mostly under 20,000 litres (https://vinarskyfond.cz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Prinos-VF_2026.pdf).
- **State tool.** Filing is already free through Portál farmáře. If the state extended this to electronic books, all private tools would lose value. I found no sign of such a plan (unverified).
- **Liability.** Software errors in the books could lead to fines of up to 5,000,000 Kč (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2004-321). Electronic books must meet the irreversibility and authorship rules (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2017-88).
- **Rule repeal.** Low risk. The duty comes from EU Regulations 2018/273 and 2018/274 (https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2017-88).
- **Language.** Czech only; the competitors are native.

## First product

I would not build this. If someone still wanted to try, the only gap I can see is a done-for-you service rather than software:

- A "season close and declarations" service for the roughly 1,000 to 2,000 producers who still keep paper books. Each autumn or January the service would enter their paper books into a compliant tool, check them against the Annex forms, and file the harvest, production, stock and Wine Fund returns.
- First 30 days: interview 15 to 20 small South Moravian winemakers about whether they keep paper books and what they would pay. Partner with an existing tool (Vitipad or VineOS) instead of building one. Test a fixed fee of 2,000 to 5,000 Kč for the January filing season.
- A new software product would need a clear edge over VineOS (free tier, offline, AI, e-labels). I did not find one.
