# Indonesia A1: Refill drinking-water depot (DAMIU) SLHS and lab-test compliance desk

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 4/10.**

Indonesia has about 84,558 registered refill drinking-water depots (DAMIU). Only 4.01% of them hold the hygiene-sanitation certificate (SLHS) they need ([JawaPos, 29 Jul 2026](https://www.jawapos.com/ekonomi/2607290097/70-persen-depot-air-isi-ulang-tak-higienis-kemendag-soroti-rendahnya-kepatuhan-pelaku-usaha)). The duties are real and enforced in places. Depots need an SLHS, a lab test at least every 6 months, and records that an inspector checks: microfilter changes, UV lamp dates, raw-water receipts, staff health checks and training certificates. They must also report water results to the health office at least twice a year ([inspection form IKL DAM](https://mbg.bogorkab.go.id/web/docref/Form_IKL_DAM.pdf); [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/izin-usaha-depot-air-minum-lt57f5cb1631360)). I found no software that does this job. But the buyers are family micro-businesses. A microbiology test costs about Rp40,000. Some districts issue the SLHS for free. Enforcement is local and happens in bursts. So the value of a pure software tool is low. The most workable shape is a done-for-you service (sample pickup, lab booking, SLHS file, logbook) in one metro area, sold through Asdamindo or labs. A better-funded adjacent market is the free-meal kitchens (SPPG), which face hard SLHS deadlines.

## Duty

**Legal basis**
- **Kepmenperindag 651/MPP/Kep/10/2004** on technical requirements for depots. Art. 6(2) requires a product test at a government-designated or accredited lab "at least once every 6 months". Art. 10(2) sanctions run from verbal warning to written warning, temporary suspension and licence revocation. Art. 2 requires a raw-water supply guarantee letter and a water-test report ([Hukumonline, updated 29 Sep 2025](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/izin-usaha-depot-air-minum-lt57f5cb1631360)). Jakarta still cited this decree when it closed depots in October 2026 ([industry.co.id](https://www.industry.co.id/read/156986/satpol-pp-dki-tutup-18-depot-air-minum-yang-tak-berizin-dan-bermasalah)). I found no new Kemendag regulation replacing it (unverified).
- **PP 28/2025** (risk-based licensing). KBLI 11052 (refill drinking water) is medium-high risk. It needs an NIB plus a standard certificate, and the SLHS is listed as a PB UMKU (supporting licence) ([Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/izin-usaha-depot-air-minum-lt57f5cb1631360); [OSS KBLI page](https://oss.go.id/en/kbli/detail/f7488909-1dc3-4798-ae4a-1c73e8600fe4)).
- **Permenperin 9/2021**, annex pp. 538–539, lists the SLHS from the district health office (Dinkes) as a special requirement ([Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/izin-usaha-depot-air-minum-lt57f5cb1631360)).
- **Permenkes 2/2023** on environmental health. It is in force from 12 Jan 2023 and repeals Permenkes 492/2010 and 736/2010 on drinking-water quality and oversight ([BPK register](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/245563); [pasal.id text](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permenkes-no-2-tahun-2023)). It requires internal monitoring by the operator and external monitoring by Dinkes "periodically or at any time". Tests must be at an accredited or government-designated lab (art. 19(2)). Producers had 2 years to comply (art. 50) ([pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permenkes-no-2-tahun-2023)). The BPK page notes it was partly revoked by Permenkes 3/2026 on disease control ([BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/245563)). The exact test interval in its annex is not in the scraped text. Secondary sources disagree: monthly microbiology and 6-monthly chemistry ([halalmui.org](https://halalmui.org/post-1/)), or every 3 months ([Poltekkes Bandung thesis](https://repo.poltekkesbandung.ac.id/id/eprint/15499/4/Abstrak.pdf)). **Interval under Permenkes 2/2023: unverified.**
- **Permenkes 14/2021 as amended by Permenkes 17/2024** sets health-sector business standards under risk-based licensing ([BBKK Makassar SOP](https://bbkkmakassar.kemkes.go.id/assets/files/1._SOP_Penerbitan_Dokumen_Kesehatan_Lingkungan.pdf)). A Kemenkes 2024 training curriculum cites Permenkes 14/2021 as the basis for depot oversight (via search snippet of [siakpel.kemkes.go.id](https://siakpel.kemkes.go.id/upload/akreditasi_kurikulum/kurikulum-1-33363532-3336-4937-b731-333333343936.pdf); page returned 403 to me).
- **Regional rules** also exist. Example: Kota Bekasi Perda 2/2024 on depots (reported in search; [BPK download, unverified that this link is the Perda](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/354546/2024pd32232.pdf)).

**What must be done or kept** (Kemendag restated six duties on 28 Jul 2026 ([BCA Sekuritas](https://bcasekuritas.co.id/latest-news/news/kemendag-tegaskan-enam-kewajiban-pelaku-usaha-depot-air-minum))):
1. NIB and standard certificate.
2. SLHS.
3. Food-grade gallons.
4. Check, rinse, wash and sanitise customer gallons.
5. Written raw-water supply guarantee from a licensed supplier.
6. Periodic water test at an accredited or government-designated lab.

**What the inspector scores.** The environmental health inspection form for depots (IKL DAM) has 165 non-compliance points. Score = 100 − (points/165 × 100) ([Form IKL DAM](https://mbg.bogorkab.go.id/web/docref/Form_IKL_DAM.pdf)). The form's page numbers (201–206) suggest it comes from a national regulation's annex, likely Permenkes 17/2024 (unverified). Items that need records:
- Microfilter within its service life, "proven by documents/records", and a microfilter replacement log if no backwash.
- Steriliser (UV/ozone/RO) installation date and rated life.
- Written receipt or certificate for raw-water purchase.
- Staff health check at least once a year, with a health certificate.
- Hygiene-sanitation training certificate for owner and operators.
- Testing all parameters and E. coli "at the frequency set by the rules".
- **Reporting water results to Dinkes at least twice a year.**

**SLHS process.** Apply to the district Dinkes. A sanitarian inspects, water is sampled and tested, and then the certificate is issued. It takes "a few weeks" ([izin.co.id](https://izin.co.id/blog/sertifikat-laik-sehat-depot-air-minum/)). The lab result must be no older than 1 month and from an accredited lab ([Medcom, Asdamindo](https://www.medcom.id/ekonomi/bisnis/1bVrY67b-asdamindo-dorong-depot-air-minum-taat-regulasi)). Files include ID, floor plan, training certificates and staff health letters ([izin.co.id service page](https://izin.co.id/jasa-sertifikat-laik-sehat.php)). Validity is 3 years per [Warta Ekonomi / Jiva Svastha](https://wartaekonomi.co.id/read577010/air-jernih-belum-tentu-aman-jiva-svastha-ajak-warga-cek-ulang-depot-isi-ulang) and 2 years per [izin.co.id](https://izin.co.id/blog/sertifikat-laik-sehat-depot-air-minum/). **Validity: unverified.** Wonosobo Dinkes issues the SLHS for free ([Dinkes Wonosobo](https://dinkes.wonosobokab.go.id/media/upload/20220610103244_830.pdf)). Some districts route SLHS through OSS, e.g. Lumajang ([Lumajang SOP](https://dinkesp2kb.lumajangkab.go.id/uploads/pengumuman/2_SOP_SLHS_Kesling_.pdf)).

**Enforcement**
- Satpol PP DKI closed depots on 10–11 Dec 2025 for no permit and E. coli ([industry.co.id](https://www.industry.co.id/read/146840/satpol-pp-dki-jakarta-tutup-damiu-tanpa-izin)).
- Satpol PP DKI closed 18 more depots on 1, 2 and 5 Oct 2026 after Labkesda found coliform. It said inspections will continue ([industry.co.id](https://www.industry.co.id/read/156986/satpol-pp-dki-tutup-18-depot-air-minum-yang-tak-berizin-dan-bermasalah)).
- 2025 lab tests found contamination in 84% of depots tested in Bandung (61/72), 73% in Sumedang (11/15) and 78% in South Jakarta (21/27) ([JawaPos](https://www.jawapos.com/ekonomi/2607290097/70-persen-depot-air-isi-ulang-tak-higienis-kemendag-soroti-rendahnya-kepatuhan-pelaku-usaha)).
- Natuna Dinkes monitors depots through Puskesmas, with no closures reported ([Antara](https://megapolitan.antaranews.com/berita/513354/pemkab-nantuna-awasi-depot-air-minum-lewat-puskesmas)).
- Kemendag's July 2026 push is education with the Jiva Svastha foundation, not new sanctions ([BCA Sekuritas](https://bcasekuritas.co.id/latest-news/news/kemendag-tegaskan-enam-kewajiban-pelaku-usaha-depot-air-minum)).
- I found no money fines for depots. Sanctions are warnings, closure and licence revocation.

## Buyers

- **84,558** registered depots as of 25 Dec 2025. Only **4.01%** (about 3,390) hold SLHS ([JawaPos](https://www.jawapos.com/ekonomi/2607290097/70-persen-depot-air-isi-ulang-tak-higienis-kemendag-soroti-rendahnya-kepatuhan-pelaku-usaha); [Kontan](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/upaya-mendorong-kepatuhan-pelaku-usaha-depot-air-isi-ulang)).
- Earlier figures: 78,378 depots and 1,755 SLHS in Q1 2024 ([RRI](https://rri.co.id/berita-lain/788682/asdamindo-31-78-persen-penduduk-indonesia-minum-air-isi-ulang)). Kemenkes counted 77,436 depots, of which 60,875 (78.6%) had been inspected and 1,822 (2.35%) held SLHS (via search snippet of [Kemenkes curriculum](https://siakpel.kemkes.go.id/upload/akreditasi_kurikulum/kurikulum-1-33363532-3336-4937-b731-333333343936.pdf)).
- East Java: only 374 depots had applied for SLHS by Nov 2024 ([Jatim Times](https://jatimtimes.com/baca/325049/20241113/120900/privacy)).
- Asdamindo says 31.78% of Indonesians drink refill water ([RRI](https://rri.co.id/berita-lain/788682/asdamindo-31-78-persen-penduduk-indonesia-minum-air-isi-ulang)).
- **Segment.** Mostly family micro-businesses. Investment under the old rules was capped at Rp200 million excluding land and building ([Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/izin-usaha-depot-air-minum-lt57f5cb1631360)). There are also franchise chains, e.g. a Rp350 million, 10-year franchise package listed on Majoo ([Majoo](https://majoo.id/layanan/franchise/detail/voss-water-indonesia)).
- **How they comply today.** Most do not comply. Those that do use Dinkes or Puskesmas sanitarians, Labkesda labs, and some paid permit agents ([izin.co.id](https://izin.co.id/jasa-sertifikat-laik-sehat.php)). Records are kept in paper notebooks ([IT Telkom Purwokerto thesis](https://repository.ittelkom-pwt.ac.id/10573/4/BAB%20I.pdf)).
- **Adjacent buyer with stronger pressure: SPPG free-meal kitchens.** 25,925 were operating and 13,576 (52.37%) held SLHS on 15 Apr 2026 (search snippet; [Antara Jogja](https://jogja.antaranews.com/berita/793933/kepala-bgn-sebut-20-persen-sppg-untuk-mbg-sudah-memiliki-slhs)). BGN set a 10 Aug 2026 deadline with permanent closure as the sanction ([DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1821484/semua-sppg-harus-segera-urus-slhs-paling-lambat-senin-besok)). A Kemenkes circular of 6 Oct 2025 makes Dinkes issue SLHS within 14 days of complete files ([Kemenkes](https://kemkes.go.id/id/kemenkes-terbitkan-surat-edaran-percepatan-penerbitan-slhs)). These kitchens are state-funded and face real closure risk.

## Competition

- **Free state path.** Dinkes and Puskesmas sanitarians inspect. Labkesda labs test. OSS takes SLHS applications in some districts ([Lumajang SOP](https://dinkesp2kb.lumajangkab.go.id/uploads/pengumuman/2_SOP_SLHS_Kesling_.pdf); [Tulungagung via beritajatim, for SPPG](https://beritajatim.com/?p=1442593)). None of these tracks the depot's recurring tests, logs or renewals for the owner. I found no national Kemenkes app for depot owners. A Depok inspection system ("Dsimfoniku") is a local tool for inspectors, not owners ([Unigal journal](https://jurnal.unigal.ac.id/abdimasgaluh/article/view/17612)). **No free state tool covers the owner's recurring job.**
- **Permit agents.** izin.co.id sells SLHS processing for depots. Its price is not published ("special price", ask on WhatsApp) ([izin.co.id](https://izin.co.id/jasa-sertifikat-laik-sehat.php)). This is a one-off service with no ongoing tracking.
- **Labs.** Labkesda and private accredited labs. Some send staff to take samples ([challenge check source, Bukittinggi Labkes](https://labkes.bukittinggikota.go.id/storage/faq/DOcC8IQyPMuaVFUITIUTTNDrt8ZbQehunzBhNC9u.pdf)).
- **Training.** Asdamindo runs hygiene courses with Kemenkes and Kemenperin ([industry.co.id](https://www.industry.co.id/read/134849/gandeng-kemenkes-kemenperin-gelar-kursus-standar-sanitasi-depot-air-minum); [industry.co.id Bali](https://www.industry.co.id/read/135528/pelaku-usaha-depot-air-minum-di-bali-antusias-ikuti-pelatihan-manajemen-higiene-dan-sanitasi)).
- **Software.** Only academic depot ordering, POS and IoT prototypes ([UNP](https://ejournal.unp.ac.id/index.php/voteknika/article/download/118934/107504); [Atma Luhur](https://repository.atmaluhur.ac.id/handle/123456789/4870)). No commercial compliance app found. General POS vendors (e.g. Majoo) serve depots but not this job (unverified for specific features).

## Willingness to pay

- **Lab costs are low.** Bukittinggi Labkes charges Rp40,000 for microbiology, Rp580,000 for a drinking-water chemistry-physics package and Rp615,000 with residual chlorine ([Bukittinggi Labkes](https://labkes.bukittinggikota.go.id/storage/faq/DOcC8IQyPMuaVFUITIUTTNDrt8ZbQehunzBhNC9u.pdf)). Old Cirebon rates were Rp20,000 per parameter ([Perda Cirebon 4/2008](https://pasal.id/peraturan/perda/perda-kota-cirebon-4-2008)). Samarinda prices per parameter, e.g. TDS Rp26,500 ([Labkesda Samarinda](https://labkesda.samarindakota.go.id/laman/jenis-pemeriksaan-dan-tarif-laboratorium)).
- **SLHS fee** can be zero (Wonosobo) ([Dinkes Wonosobo](https://dinkes.wonosobokab.go.id/media/upload/20220610103244_830.pdf)).
- **Annual hard cost** of compliance is roughly Rp0.7–1.5 million per depot (2–12 micro tests plus 2 chemistry tests). This is my estimate from the rates above (unverified).
- **Cost of not complying** is closure, not a fine. Closure is rare outside Jakarta's sweeps. That weakens the urge to pay.
- The 4% compliance rate shows most owners do not pay even the cheap state route today. The real barrier is the cost of sanitation upgrades (building, UV, food-grade fittings) ([challenge check](https://insight.kontan.co.id/news/isu-higienitas-ganjal-bisnis-depot-air-minum-isi-ulang)).
- **Plausible prices.** An app at Rp25,000–50,000 per month (unverified tolerance). A done-for-you SLHS package at Rp750,000–2,000,000 one-off, plus an annual "test and log" plan at Rp100,000–150,000 per month including lab costs (unverified). Lab revenue share is likely needed to make margins work.

## Channels

- **Asdamindo** (Asosiasi Depot Air Minum Isi Ulang Indonesia). Chair Erik Garnadi. Runs seminars and courses with Kemenkes and Kemenperin ([industry.co.id](https://www.industry.co.id/read/135528/pelaku-usaha-depot-air-minum-di-bali-antusias-ikuti-pelatihan-manajemen-higiene-dan-sanitasi); [Medcom](https://www.medcom.id/ekonomi/bisnis/1bVrY67b-asdamindo-dorong-depot-air-minum-taat-regulasi)). Membership size: unverified.
- **Kemendag Ditjen PKTN** and the **Jiva Svastha Nusantara Foundation** run public education on depot duties ([JawaPos](https://www.jawapos.com/ekonomi/2607290097/70-persen-depot-air-isi-ulang-tak-higienis-kemendag-soroti-rendahnya-kepatuhan-pelaku-usaha)).
- **Labkesda and private accredited labs.** Every compliant depot must use one. A lab partner gets more samples.
- **Dinkes and Puskesmas sanitarians.** They already visit depots and could recommend a logbook (B2G angle).
- **Equipment suppliers** (UV lamps, filters, RO units) and franchise brands. They touch every depot at setup and filter replacement (unverified as a channel).
- **Satpol PP sweep areas** (Jakarta) create urgent demand in a short window.

## Risks

- **Low willingness to pay.** Micro owners ignore even free or cheap compliance today (4% SLHS). This is the main risk.
- **Weak, local enforcement.** Closures cluster in Jakarta. Kemendag's 2026 action is education only ([BCA Sekuritas](https://bcasekuritas.co.id/latest-news/news/kemendag-tegaskan-enam-kewajiban-pelaku-usaha-depot-air-minum)).
- **Rule uncertainty.** Test interval differs across 651/2004 (6 months) and secondary readings of Permenkes 2/2023 (monthly or quarterly). SLHS validity is 2 or 3 years. Rules vary by region (Bekasi Perda).
- **Physical job.** The core work is a sample, a lab test and an inspection. Software only reminds and stores. A service model is operations-heavy and local.
- **State tooling.** OSS is absorbing SLHS applications ([Lumajang](https://dinkesp2kb.lumajangkab.go.id/uploads/pengumuman/2_SOP_SLHS_Kesling_.pdf)). Kemenkes could add tracking (unverified, no plan found).
- **Liability.** A service that takes samples or advises could be blamed after a poisoning case.
- **Language.** Must be fully in Bahasa Indonesia, WhatsApp-first.

## First product

**Version 1: "Buku Depot" WhatsApp-first compliance logbook plus a test-booking service in one city (e.g. Jakarta or Bandung).**

It should:
1. Hold the depot profile: NIB, SLHS number and expiry, raw-water supplier letter.
2. Keep the logs the IKL form checks: microfilter changes, UV/RO install date and rated life, raw-water receipts (photo), staff health certificates and training certificates.
3. Send WhatsApp reminders for micro tests, 6-monthly chemistry tests, the twice-yearly Dinkes report and SLHS renewal.
4. Book a test with a partner lab and arrange sample pickup. Store the PDF result.
5. Print a self-check against the 165-point IKL form and a ready file for the SLHS application.

**First 30 days**
- Week 1: Read the Permenkes 2/2023 annex and Permenkes 17/2024 annex to fix test intervals and SLHS validity. Call 3 Dinkes offices.
- Week 1–2: Sign one accredited lab in one city for pickup and a referral fee.
- Week 2: Build the logbook as a WhatsApp bot plus simple web page. Encode the IKL checklist.
- Week 3: Visit 30 depots in a recent sweep area. Offer a free self-check and a paid SLHS package.
- Week 4: Pitch Asdamindo for a member price. Test an SPPG version (kitchens need the same SLHS, lab and IKL steps, with stronger deadlines).
