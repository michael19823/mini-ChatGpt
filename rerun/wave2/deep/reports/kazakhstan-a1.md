# Kazakhstan A1: Licensing-readiness file for private kindergartens and mini-centres

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 5/10.**

The duty is real. From 1 January 2027, every preschool organisation in Kazakhstan, state or private, needs a licence for preschool education ([zakon.kz, 10 Jun 2026](https://www.zakon.kz/sovety-yurista/6520878-kakie-detskie-sady-zakroyut-s-novogo-goda-gotov-sadik-letom.html); [MTRK, 24 Jul 2026](https://mtrk.kz/ru/2026/07/24/licenzirovanie-detskikh-sadov-startu/)). About 6,526 of the 11,909 preschools are private, and 96% of those depend on the state order ([Tengri, Apr 2026](https://tengrinews.kz/tengri-institutions/novyie-pravila-chto-jdt-chastnyie-detsadyi-v-kazahstane-594367/amp/)). A kindergarten without a licence loses that state money, so the stakes are high. I found no software and no named "turnkey" provider aimed at kindergarten licensing. But four things hold the score down. The licence is mostly a one-off job. The hardest requirements are physical: premises, land use and a lease of at least 5 years, and software cannot fix those. Budgets are thin. And as of 1 October 2026 the Atameken chamber is still lobbying for a transition period and softer rules ([24.kz, 1 Oct 2026](https://24.kz/kz/zha-aly-tar/kogam/795811-kazakstanda-zekemensik-balabaksalardy-licenzialauga-zana-talaptar-kojylady)). The best version is a cheap self-check plus document pack, sold through Russian- and Kazakh-language owner channels. It would then turn into a recurring tool for inspection-readiness and staff records.

## Duty

- **Legal basis.**
  - A law of 30 December 2024 amends the Law on Permits and Notifications and Article 57 of the Law on Education. It makes preschool education a licensed activity from 1 January 2027, under a non-transferable class 1 licence ([zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/sovety-yurista/6520878-kakie-detskie-sady-zakroyut-s-novogo-goda-gotov-sadik-letom.html)).
  - A government resolution added preschool education to the list of licensed education sub-types, effective 1 January 2027 ([bes.media](https://bes.media/news/detskie-sadi-budut-rabotat-po-litsenzii-s-2027-goda-postanovlenie-prinyato-268796/)).
  - The qualification requirements are in Order No. 268 of 27 November 2025, which amends the requirements order No. 473 of 24 November 2022 ([zakon.kz, 20 Apr 2026](https://www.zakon.kz/stati/6515172-zachem-vvodyat-litsenzirovanie-detsadov-v-kazakhstane-i-kak-poluchit-razreshenie-na-rabotu.html); [zakon.uchet.kz, V2500037500](https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/V2500037500)). I could not open the order text myself. Both fetches failed. The detailed list below comes from the earlier check and from news reports.
  - The licence service rules are in Order No. 483 of 30 November 2022 ([inform.kz](https://www.inform.kz/ru/kak-poluchit-litsenziyu-na-zanyatie-obrazovatelnoy-deyatelnostyu-v-kazahstane-31ee6e), per search summary).
- **What must be shown.** These are the ministry's listed requirements:
  - Working curricula that match the state preschool standard.
  - Qualified teachers who take regular training.
  - Teaching materials.
  - A medical licence and an equipped medical room.
  - Play materials, plus adequate equipment and furniture.
  - A sanitary-epidemiological conclusion for the building.
  - Conditions for children with special educational needs.

  Sources: [zakon.kz, 20 Apr 2026](https://www.zakon.kz/stati/6515172-zachem-vvodyat-litsenzirovanie-detsadov-v-kazakhstane-i-kak-poluchit-razreshenie-na-rabotu.html).
  - The North Kazakhstan quality department adds more to the list: long-term plans, individual child development cards, cyclograms (activity schedules), emergency preparedness and sanitary inspection acts ([MTRK](https://mtrk.kz/ru/2026/07/24/licenzirovanie-detskikh-sadov-startu/)).
  - Premises must be owned or held under trust or operational management, or leased for at least 5 years ([inbusiness.kz, 8 May 2026](https://inbusiness.kz/index.php/ru/news/kazahstan-riskuet-ostatsya-bez-poloviny-chastnyh-detsadov)).
  - The earlier check read the order itself. It also found these requirements: at least 75% permanent staff, at least 20% of teachers with categories, anti-terror protection, an edu.kz domain, NOBD data entry, and 36 hours of training at least every 3 years. A medical room is not needed for up to 3 groups. I did not re-confirm these details (unverified here).
- **Process.**
  - Applications go online through eGov.kz or elicense.kz ([zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/stati/6515172-zachem-vvodyat-litsenzirovanie-detsadov-v-kazakhstane-i-kak-poluchit-razreshenie-na-rabotu.html)).
  - Territorial departments of the Education Ministry's quality committee issue the licence. Review takes up to 30 working days ([same](https://www.zakon.kz/stati/6515172-zachem-vvodyat-litsenzirovanie-detsadov-v-kazakhstane-i-kak-poluchit-razreshenie-na-rabotu.html)).
  - Review has two stages: a commission checks the documents, then staff visit the site and take photos and video ([MTRK](https://mtrk.kz/ru/2026/07/24/licenzirovanie-detskikh-sadov-startu/)).
  - The state fee is 10 MRP, about 43,200 tenge ([ybcase](https://ybcase.com/fintech/polucit-licenziu-na-obrazovatelnuu-deatelnost-v-kazahstane); [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/index.php/ru/news/kazahstan-riskuet-ostatsya-bez-poloviny-chastnyh-detsadov)).
  - A separate licence annex is issued for each site address ([search summary of prg.kz licence rules](https://prg.kz/m/amp/document/30118747/12/590000)) (unverified).
- **Timing: firm start, phased roll-out.**
  - One lawyer says there is no transition period. He also says nobody can apply before 1 January 2027, because notification rules apply until then, so up to about 12,000 kindergartens may apply at once ([zakon.kz, 10 Jun 2026](https://www.zakon.kz/sovety-yurista/6520878-kakie-detskie-sady-zakroyut-s-novogo-goda-gotov-sadik-letom.html)).
  - The ministry instead says the roll-out will be phased, based on each organisation's readiness ([Tengri](https://tengrinews.kz/tengri-institutions/novyie-pravila-chto-jdt-chastnyie-detsadyi-v-kazahstane-594367/amp/); [zakon.kz, 20 Apr 2026](https://www.zakon.kz/stati/6515172-zachem-vvodyat-litsenzirovanie-detsadov-v-kazakhstane-i-kak-poluchit-razreshenie-na-rabotu.html)).
  - North Kazakhstan region plans a 3-year schedule. Of its 429 institutions, more than 200 will be checked in 2027 ([MTRK](https://mtrk.kz/ru/2026/07/24/licenzirovanie-detskikh-sadov-startu/)).
  - Earlier plans spoke of a schedule for existing kindergartens running to 2030 ([inbusiness.kz](https://www.inbusiness.kz/ru/news/kakie-peremeny-gotovit-licenzirovanie-detsadov-roditelyam-i-biznesu), per search summary).
  - Taken together, demand will probably spread over 2027-2029, not hit in a single January spike. How existing kindergartens operate legally while they wait for their turn is not clear (unverified).
- **Licence term.** Not confirmed. Children's camps get 5-year licences ([azattyq-ruhy](https://rus.azattyq-ruhy.kz/news/108831-litsenziia-na-piat-let-i-edinye-trebovaniia-kak-izmenitsia-detskii-otdykh-v-kazakhstane)). Education licences in Kazakhstan have usually been open-ended, but I did not confirm this for kindergartens (unverified). If the licence is open-ended, the job is a one-off apart from re-issue, new sites and later inspections.
- **Penalties and consequences.**
  - Without a licence, a kindergarten cannot operate and gets no state order ([zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/sovety-yurista/6520878-kakie-detskie-sady-zakroyut-s-novogo-goda-gotov-sadik-letom.html)).
  - A suspended licence bars the holder from the state-order competition and from taking new children ([search summary of state-control rules](https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/V2600038978)) (unverified).
  - Schools offer a precedent. In 2024, 58 schools operated without a licence. Courts fined them under Article 463 of the Administrative Code, and directors paid over 92,000 tenge ([kapital.kz](https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/131843/v-kazakhstane-oshtrafovali-58-shkol-za-ot-sut-stviye-litsenzii.html); [zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/sobytiia/6457328-58-shkol-v-kazakhstane-rabotali-bez-litsenzii.html)).
  - A specific fine for kindergartens is unverified.
- **Enforcement climate.**
  - Since 1 February 2026, a control procedure allows unannounced inspections of organisations that provide children with food, accommodation, education or recreation ([Tengri](https://tengrinews.kz/tengri-institutions/novyie-pravila-chto-jdt-chastnyie-detsadyi-v-kazahstane-594367/amp/)).
  - Law No. 236-VIII of 4 December 2025 brought in risk-based planned inspections and unannounced inspections for schools and kindergartens ([inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/v-kazahstane-shkoly-i-detsady-budut-proveryat-po-novomu), per search summary) (unverified).
  - One report says the surprise inspections apply to budget-funded organisations ([azattyq-ruhy](https://rus.azattyq-ruhy.kz/news/99400-v-kazakhstane-s-fevralia-2026-goda-vvodiatsia-vnezapnye-proverki-detskikh-uchrezhdenii/amp), per search summary) (unverified). Most private kindergartens take state-order money, so they may still be covered (unverified).
  - A joint order includes a separate inspection checklist for preschool education ([zakon.uchet.kz V2600038978](https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/V2600038978), per search summary; revised 12 Jun 2026).
  - Past enforcement was heavy. In 2023, 7,497 inspections found violations in 90.7% of cases, and 4,027 officials were fined a total of 1.12 billion tenge ([finratings.kz](https://finratings.kz/news/533-v-kazakhstane-milliardnye-shtrafy-dushat-chastnye-detsady/), per search summary). Whether all of these were preschool inspections is unverified.
- **Pending change.** On 1 October 2026, Atameken and the Kazakhstan Continuing Education Association asked for several changes ([24.kz](https://24.kz/kz/zha-aly-tar/kogam/795811-kazakstanda-zekemensik-balabaksalardy-licenzialauga-zana-talaptar-kojylady)):
  - one joint explanation from all agencies covering licensing, land, construction, sanitary and fire rules;
  - separate rules for existing kindergartens;
  - a transition period;
  - no suspension of work or loss of state order over unfinished paperwork.

  No decision was found.

## Buyers

- **Kindergartens.** There are 11,909 preschool organisations, of which 6,526 are private ([Tengri](https://tengrinews.kz/tengri-institutions/novyie-pravila-chto-jdt-chastnyie-detsadyi-v-kazahstane-594367/amp/)). Almaty alone has 1,098 kindergartens, 896 of them private ([bes.media via search summary](https://bes.media/news/v-almaty-uskoryat-stroitelstvo-i-obnovlenie-detskih-sadov/)). Kindergartens employ about 99,330 staff ([zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/sovety-yurista/6520878-kakie-detskie-sady-zakroyut-s-novogo-goda-gotov-sadik-letom.html)).
- **Mini-centres.** The National Statistics Bureau counted 3,070 mini-centres in 2023, down from 3,950 in 2019 ([kapital.kz](https://kapital.kz/economic/134140/na-skol-ko-uvelichilos-kolichestvo-doshkol-nykh-organizatsiy-v-rk-za-pyat-let.html), per search summary). Pavlodar education departments say mini-centres and pre-school classes need the "preschool education and training" sub-type licence ([gov.kz Pavlodar](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/control-pavlodar/press/news/details/1178560?lang=kk), per search summary). Many mini-centres sit inside state schools (unverified).
- **Core paying segment.** About 6,500 private kindergartens. Most are small single-site firms, often in converted houses on residential plots ([24.kz](https://24.kz/kz/zha-aly-tar/kogam/795811-kazakstanda-zekemensik-balabaksalardy-licenzialauga-zana-talaptar-kojylady)). State kindergartens (about 5,400) will be prepared by akimat education departments and are a weak buyer (unverified).
- **How they comply today.** Owners or heads do the paperwork themselves. Some get help from general business-licensing consultants such as Ybcase ([ybcase](https://ybcase.com/fintech/polucit-licenziu-na-obrazovatelnuu-deatelnost-v-kazahstane)). Most already meet state-order placement rules, since 96% hold the state order ([Tengri](https://tengrinews.kz/tengri-institutions/novyie-pravila-chto-jdt-chastnyie-detsadyi-v-kazahstane-594367/amp/)). The regional quality departments advise heads to start preparing the full document set now ([MTRK](https://mtrk.kz/ru/2026/07/24/licenzirovanie-detskikh-sadov-startu/)).
- **Pain points.**
  - A lawyer estimates that more than half of private kindergartens are in premises that break land or fire rules ([inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/index.php/ru/news/kazahstan-riskuet-ostatsya-bez-poloviny-chastnyh-detsadov)). The ministry disputes the forecast of 50% closures ([Tengri](https://tengrinews.kz/tengri-institutions/novyie-pravila-chto-jdt-chastnyie-detsadyi-v-kazahstane-594367/amp/)).
  - Changing a building's designated use costs 20-40 million tenge in documents alone ([inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/index.php/ru/news/kazahstan-riskuet-ostatsya-bez-poloviny-chastnyh-detsadov)).
  - Kindergartens lack teaching kits from the ministry's list ([pkzsk.info](https://pkzsk.info/detskie-sady-severo-kazahstanskoj-oblasti-nachnut-liczenzirovat-s-2027-goda/), per search summary).

## Competition

- **Free state tools.** elicense.kz and eGov handle filing and fee payment ([zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/stati/6515172-zachem-vvodyat-litsenzirovanie-detsadov-v-kazakhstane-i-kak-poluchit-razreshenie-na-rabotu.html)). NOBD holds staff and child data, and ED24 and Indigo handle vouchers and attendance ([zakon.kz](https://www.zakon.kz/stati/6420659-verifitsiruy-menya-ili-zachem-kazakhstanskim-roditelyam-navyazyvayut-novoe-prilozhenie-dlya-detsadov.html)). None of these checks readiness or builds the evidence file. They are filing channels and registers, not a substitute.
- **Kindergarten software.**
  - BALAM, a parent app covering health, menu and schedule ([newtimes.kz](https://newtimes.kz/obshchestvo/157867-cifrovizaciya-v-obrazovanii-kazahstanka-pridumala-mobilnoe-prilozhenie-dlya-detsadov)).
  - A student-built platform from Atyrau for preschool planning, still in development ([kazpravda](https://kazpravda.kz/n/atyrauskie-shkolniki-razrabotali-cifrovuyu-platformu-dlya-detsadov/)).
  - ZERO, a generic school-management app ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/tj/app/zero/id6504515977)).
  - 1C:General Education Institution (per the earlier check).

  None of these is known to do licensing readiness. Prices were not found.
- **Consultants.** Ybcase offers end-to-end help with education licences: document pack, audit of premises, staff and programmes, and a compliance check. Preschool gets only a passing mention, and no price is published ([ybcase](https://ybcase.com/fintech/polucit-licenziu-na-obrazovatelnuu-deatelnost-v-kazahstane)). Dogovor24 answers education-licence questions and sells templates ([dogovor24](https://dogovor24.kz/questions/too-hochet-poluchit-licenziyu-ili-razreshenie-dlya-uchebnogo-centra-kak-mozhno-eto-sdelat-9205.html)). No kindergarten-specific "turnkey licence" provider was found in four Russian- and Kazakh-language searches.
- **Bottom line.** There is a clear gap for a kindergarten-specific checklist and document pack. The likely fast followers are general licensing consultants, methodology centres that already sell plans and cyclograms to kindergartens (unverified), and the regional quality departments' own free explainer sessions.

## Willingness to pay

- **What is at stake.** Losing the state order. The per-child payment has been cited at about 27,000 tenge a month ([search summary, undated source](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/news/takaya-situaciya-slozhilas-lish-v-odnom-regione-kazahstana)) (unverified for 2026). For a 100-child kindergarten, that is roughly 2.7 million tenge (about US$5,600) a month (unverified estimate).
- **Direct costs.** The state fee is about 43,200 tenge ([ybcase](https://ybcase.com/fintech/polucit-licenziu-na-obrazovatelnuu-deatelnost-v-kazahstane)). A land-use change costs 20-40 million tenge ([inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/index.php/ru/news/kazahstan-riskuet-ostatsya-bez-poloviny-chastnyh-detsadov)). School directors running without a licence were fined over 92,000 tenge ([kapital.kz](https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/131843/v-kazakhstane-oshtrafovali-58-shkol-za-ot-sut-stviye-litsenzii.html)).
- **Plausible prices** (my estimates; no market price found):
  - A self-check plus document-pack tool at 20,000-50,000 tenge one-off (about US$40-100).
  - A done-for-you file review at 150,000-300,000 tenge (unverified).
  - An after-licence subscription for inspection-readiness and staff-training records at 5,000-10,000 tenge a month (unverified).
- **Against that.** Budgets are thin. Owners have asked for higher per-child funding ([inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/uvelichit-podushevoe-finansirovanie-v-chastnyh-detsadah-prosba-kostanajskih-predprinimatelej)) and complain about the fines ([finratings.kz](https://finratings.kz/news/533-v-kazakhstane-milliardnye-shtrafy-dushat-chastnye-detsady/)). The rough total for one-off packs is 6,500 × about 40,000 tenge, or about 260 million tenge (about US$540,000). That is fine for a small business but not large.

## Channels

- **Atameken (National Chamber of Entrepreneurs).** It is actively running the private kindergarten licensing dialogue (director Azamat Beisenbenov) ([24.kz](https://24.kz/kz/zha-aly-tar/kogam/795811-kazakstanda-zekemensik-balabaksalardy-licenzialauga-zana-talaptar-kojylady)). Its regional chambers run business-support seminars (unverified).
- **Kazakhstan Continuing Education Association** (Leila Kulenova) and other associations of private preschools ([informburo](https://informburo.kz/novosti/vladelcy-castnyx-detsadov-v-kazaxstane-poprosili-edinyx-pravil-licenzirovaniia); [forbes.kz](https://forbes.kz/news/newsid_282340)).
- **Regional quality departments** (for example North Kazakhstan) brief kindergarten heads in public ([MTRK](https://mtrk.kz/ru/2026/07/24/licenzirovanie-detskikh-sadov-startu/)). Akimat meetings, such as Almaty's, gather owners ([bes.media](https://bes.media/news/v-almaty-uskoryat-stroitelstvo-i-obnovlenie-detskih-sadov/)).
- **Owner WhatsApp/Telegram groups, Instagram and methodology-centre mailing lists** (unverified). These are the likely low-cost route.
- **Partners.** Accountants who serve kindergartens, and general licensing consultants as resellers (unverified).

## Risks

- **Delay or softening.** Atameken wants a transition period and separate rules for existing kindergartens ([24.kz, 1 Oct 2026](https://24.kz/kz/zha-aly-tar/kogam/795811-kazakstanda-zekemensik-balabaksalardy-licenzialauga-zana-talaptar-kojylady)). A phased roll-out over 3 years spreads demand thin ([MTRK](https://mtrk.kz/ru/2026/07/24/licenzirovanie-detskikh-sadov-startu/)).
- **One-off job.** The licence term for kindergartens is unconfirmed. If the licence is open-ended, recurring revenue must come from inspections and staff records.
- **The hard part is not paperwork.** Premises, land use and lease length decide most outcomes ([inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/index.php/ru/news/kazahstan-riskuet-ostatsya-bez-poloviny-chastnyh-detsadov)). A tool can flag these problems but cannot fix them.
- **Regional inconsistency.** Different regions may accept different documents ([24.kz](https://24.kz/kz/zha-aly-tar/kogam/795811-kazakstanda-zekemensik-balabaksalardy-licenzialauga-zana-talaptar-kojylady)). Content needs regional notes.
- **State tooling.** The ministry could publish an official self-check or an elicense form wizard (unverified). The regional departments already run free briefings.
- **Liability.** A pack that "passes" a kindergarten that is then refused damages trust. Position it as preparation, not a guarantee.
- **Language.** Both Russian and Kazakh are needed. A local partner is needed for sales.
- **Timing.** Today is 9 October 2026. That leaves about 12 weeks before applications open, but the phased schedule extends the window into 2028-2029.

## First product

- **Version 1 (web, Russian and Kazakh).**
  - A readiness questionnaire mapped to each point of Order 473 as amended by Order 268. It covers staff share and categories, training hours, premises title and lease length, sanitary conclusion, medical licence or contract, anti-terror items, special-needs conditions, curricula and development cards, and edu.kz and NOBD.
  - A red/amber/green report that puts land and premises risks first.
  - Auto-filled annex forms and a document checklist for upload to elicense.kz.
  - A teacher register with training-hour and category expiry alerts.
- **First 30 days.**
  1. Get the current text of Order 473 as amended by Order 268, including the annex forms, and the preschool inspection checklist (V2600038978).
  2. Interview 10 private kindergarten owners through Atameken or Instagram.
  3. Build the questionnaire and PDF report, plus Word or Excel templates for the annexes.
  4. Pilot with 20 kindergartens in one city (Almaty: 896 private).
  5. Sign one consultant partner for paid file reviews.
  6. Add an inspection-readiness module from the checklist as the recurring product.

## Open questions

- Licence term for kindergartens: open-ended or fixed?
- The official phased schedule by region, and the legal status of existing kindergartens while they wait for their turn.
- Whether Atameken's request for a transition period gets a response before 1 January 2027.
- Fines for preschools under the Administrative Code (Article 463 or others).
- Whether the 1 February 2026 surprise inspections cover private kindergartens that hold the state order.
- The 2025-2026 count of mini-centres and how many are private.
- Prices charged by consultants or methodology centres for licensing preparation.
