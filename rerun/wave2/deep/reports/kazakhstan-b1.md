# Kazakhstan B1: compliance kit for private security firms (quarterly MVD report, weapons register, 5-day notices)

## Re-assessment (owner's criteria)

**Verdict: maybe. New score: 5/10. Old score: 3/10.**

**The case.** About 4,200 private security firms operate in Kazakhstan, with roughly 97,000 guards. That is about 22 guards per firm, so these are real payroll businesses, not micro-firms (https://24.kz/ru/news/social/702739-skolko-chastnykh-okhrannykh-predpriyatij-dejstvuet-v-kazakhstane; https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/144505/trebovaniya-k-ohrannym-organizaciyam-v-kazahstane-usilyat.html). Since 2026 they owe a quarterly 13-block report to the MVD by email, 5-working-day notices, and weapons duties (https://mybuh.kz/news/okhrannye-agentstva-budut-sdavat-otchety-v-mvd/). There is no state portal and no local product: firms do it by hand. The report alone is too thin to sell. But a cheap register-plus-deadlines tool for guards, weapons, posts and branches, which produces the MVD report as a by-product, has no real competitor. The weak points are the lack of a fine for the report itself and a hard, fragmented sale.

### Room for improvement over the portal or current practice

- **There is no portal to improve on.** The report goes as a plain email to the police security-control unit, with no template format stated (https://mybuh.kz/news/okhrannye-agentstva-budut-sdavat-otchety-v-mvd/). Firms must keep the source data themselves.
- **Record-keeping the state does not do for the firm.** Ten of the 13 blocks are registers a firm must keep all year: service weapons by type, special equipment, weapons held but not used, protected schools and preschools, terrorism-vulnerable sites, employee crimes, and staff dismissed for negative reasons (same source). Today this most likely lives in Excel (unverified).
- **Deadlines that are easy to miss.** These are the report by the 25th, a 5-working-day notice of suspension, resumption or work outside the registered place, a 10-working-day weapons hand-in on suspension, and control firing of rifled weapons, where a missed deadline can suspend the permit (https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/144505/trebovaniya-k-ohrannym-organizaciyam-v-kazahstane-usilyat.html).
- **Multi-branch work.** The report covers each branch and representative office (https://mybuh.kz/news/okhrannye-agentstva-budut-sdavat-otchety-v-mvd/). Many firms run several branches (https://statsnet.co/companies/kz/59706887).
- **Inspection readiness.** Joint order MVD No. 389 / MNE No. 67 of June 2026 rewrote the risk criteria and checklists for this sector (https://zakon.uchet.kz/kaz/docs/V2600038906). A tool that mirrors the checklist has a clear selling point. The 5-day notice as a risk criterion comes from the earlier check and was not re-read (unverified).
- **Pain evidence is weak.** I found no complaints, guides or paid filing help for this report. The duty is only months old. The real pain signal is the regulator's: weapons of about 800 suspended firms are unaccounted for, which is why the rules were tightened (https://24.kz/ru/news/social/702739-skolko-chastnykh-okhrannykh-predpriyatij-dejstvuet-v-kazakhstane).

### Competitor reality check

- **State system.** No portal exists. "E-kuzet" still cannot be confirmed: a direct search found nothing (https://www.inform.kz/ru/v-kazahstane-ne-uchteno-oruzhie-800-ohrannih-kompaniy-deputat-e894d2 was seen only in an earlier search summary; unverified).
- **1C.** No industry configuration for Kazakh security firms turned up. The nearest 1C product is for occupational safety, not guards or weapons (https://www.1cbit.kz/1csoft/ohrana-truda-predpriyatiay/).
- **Bitrix24 apps (Russian).** "CRM Okhrannoe predpriyatie" by ITConstruct covers equipment sales, service pipelines and complaints. It has no guard register, weapons, licences or police reports. It has 106 installs and requires a paid Bitrix24 Market Plus subscription (https://www.bitrix24.ru/apps/app/itconstruct.crm_okhrannoe_predpriyatie/). Another ready CRM targets alarm installers (https://www.bitrix24.ru/apps/app/152747.gotovaya_crm_dlya_okhrannykh_organizatsii/). Neither does the job.
- **Anolla.** It does shift scheduling, with a free plan and paid features at unpublished prices. It has no weapons register, licence tracking or official reporting, and no Kazakh language (https://anolla.com/ru/programmnoe-obespecenie-dlia-oxrannyx-uslug).
- **Alarm-monitoring software** such as "Fregat Pult" handles alarm consoles, not compliance (https://apps.apple.com/ru/app/%D1%84%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B3%D0%B0%D1%82-%D0%BF%D1%83%D0%BB%D1%8C%D1%82-%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%B7%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8F/id1571259285).
- **International guard tools** (GuardsPro, QR-Patrol, TrackTik) are English-only and not localised, as listed in the Competition section below.
- **Bottom line:** the incumbent is Excel plus email. That is an opening, not a killer.

### Price per customer

- **What firms pay today.** Outsourced accounting in Almaty averages 200,000 to 250,000 tenge a month (https://digitalbusiness.kz/2025-11-20/kazhdomu-ip-v-kazahstane-ponadobitsya-buhgalter-rasskazivaem-skolko-eto-budet-stoit/). A first Art. 470 fine for a small firm is 40 MRP, about 173,000 tenge. A repeat costs 80 MRP or more and can carry a ban on activity (https://kodeksy-kz.com/ka/ob_administrativnyh_pravonarusheniyah/470.htm; MRP 4,325: https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/143213/mrp-mzp-i-drugie-raschetnye-pokazateli-na-2026-god.html). A suspended weapon permit stops armed contracts (https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/144505/trebovaniya-k-ohrannym-organizaciyam-v-kazahstane-usilyat.html).
- **Report-only tool:** 2,000 to 3,000 tenge a month at most (unverified estimate). Not worth building on its own.
- **Register plus deadlines plus report:** 10,000 to 15,000 tenge a month per firm, plus about 3,000 tenge per extra branch (unverified estimate). A year at 15,000 tenge is 180,000 tenge, about one first fine, and under 10% of one month of outsourced accounting.
- **With shift schedules and timesheets for guards:** 20,000 to 30,000 tenge a month (unverified estimate). This is in line with international guard tools at about USD 10 to 40 a month (https://www.guardspro.com/trackforce-alternative; https://www.capterra.com/p/144873/ProxiGuard-Patrol-Management/).
- **Per accountant or consultant:** there is no sign that accountants or consultants file this report for firms (unverified). So multi-client pricing is not a main lever.

### Revenue estimate (year 3)

- **Buyers:** about 3,500 active firms. That is 4,200 operating per a deputy, less some attrition (https://24.kz/ru/news/social/702739-skolko-chastnykh-okhrannykh-predpriyatij-dejstvuet-v-kazakhstane). This excludes alarm installers and training centres, which are not counted.
- **Base case:** 3,500 x 6% share x 150,000 tenge a year (12,500 tenge a month) = 31.5 million tenge, about USD 63,000 at roughly 500 tenge per USD (exchange rate unverified).
- **Good case** (shift module included): 3,500 x 8% x 300,000 tenge a year (25,000 tenge a month) = 84 million tenge, about USD 168,000.
- **Low case:** 3,500 x 3% x 120,000 tenge = 12.6 million tenge, about USD 25,000.
- A small but real business for one or two people in the base case. It is not a large company.

### Ease of implementation and sale

- **Build: easy.** It needs a Russian and Kazakh web app with registers, reminders and an Excel export of the 13 blocks. There is no state API to integrate, because filing is by email.
- **Onboarding: medium.** Each firm must load its guards, weapons and sites once. That is about 20 to 100 records for a typical firm (unverified estimate).
- **Sale: medium to hard.** The buyers are fragmented, conservative directors, often ex-police. There is no accountant channel. Possible routes are the Association of Security Organisations (https://kazpravda.kz/n/sila-v-edinstve-xd/; activity unverified), direct outreach via registers by OKED code (https://statsnet.co/companies/kz/59706887), and accounting media such as mybuh.kz, which covers this duty.
- **Product-led selling is plausible.** A free quarterly-report generator could serve as the lead magnet for the paid register.

### Remaining risks

- **No fine for the report itself.** Art. 470 lists other breaches but not the report or the notices (https://kodeksy-kz.com/ka/ob_administrativnyh_pravonarusheniyah/470.htm). The pitch must rest on inspections, weapons permits and saved staff time.
- **The rule is not pinned down.** The MVD order number and the official template were still not found. A Kazakh-language search turned up Order No. 83 (the Committee's regulation) and the June 2026 risk-criteria order, but no report form (https://zakon.uchet.kz/kaz/docs/G26C0000083; https://zakon.uchet.kz/kaz/docs/V2600038906).
- **A state system could appear.** If "E-kuzet" or an MVD portal takes over the report (unverified), the export feature loses value. The registers and deadlines would still be needed.
- **Data sensitivity.** Weapons and school-site data may make firms wary of the cloud. Offering local export and data hosted in Kazakhstan would help.
- **Weak pain evidence.** No complaints or consultant market for this report were found. Pilot demand must be tested before building much.

### Fixes to the first pass

- The weapons hand-in deadline in the final law is 10 working days (https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/144505/trebovaniya-k-ohrannym-organizaciyam-v-kazahstane-usilyat.html). The 2024 draft said 15 (https://mybuh.kz/news/izmeneniya-dlya-okhrannykh-organizatsiy). The 2024 draft also moved inspections of security firms to a new Art. 20-1 of the security law (same source; final text unverified).
- The 4,200 operating and 800 suspended figures come from the 24.kz article itself, dated 2 April 2025. The article does not mention E-kuzet.
- The cited training-centre decree (P1100000751) and earlier named-centre decrees appear to have been repealed (search summary of https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/P1100000751; unverified). Treat the current training-centre rules and counts as unknown.

## Summary

**Verdict: no-go. Score: 3/10.**

The duty is real. Law No. 263-VIII of 12 February 2026 requires private security firms, alarm installers and guard training centres to report to the police. It also requires a notice within 5 working days of a suspension, a resumption or work outside the registered place. The reporting form is reported as a quarterly email with 13 data blocks, due by the 25th. The market is also real: about 4,463 registered firms at 1 January 2026, of which perhaps 3,500 are active. But the thing a tool would automate is one email template a quarter. I found no fine for failing to send it: the security article of the Administrative Code (Art. 470) does not mention reports. No local product exists. That is more likely because firms don't feel a need than because a gap is waiting. The trigger alone won't make firms pay. A broader back-office product for guard companies might, but that is a different and harder business.

## Duty

**Legal basis**
- Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. 263-VIII of 12.02.2026 amends several laws, including the Law "On security activity" No. 85-II of 19.10.2000. Its subjects are security activity, housing relations and law-enforcement service (https://zakon.uchet.kz/kaz/docs/Z2600000263, title seen in search results only; https://bes.media/amp/tokaev-podpisal-zakon-ob-uzhestochenii-pravil-dlya-ohrannih-organizatsiy-v-kazahstane/). The Senate approved it on 29.01.2026 (https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/144505/trebovaniya-k-ohrannym-organizaciyam-v-kazahstane-usilyat.html).
- The amendments take effect in April 2026 (https://mybuh.kz/news/k-okhrannym-agentstvam-izmenili-trebovaniya/). The exact date is unverified.
- A Prime Minister's order of 13.04.2026 (No. 43-ө) sets out how the law will be implemented, according to a search summary of https://zakon.uchet.kz/kaz/docs/Z2600000263 (unverified). Its list of follow-up acts was not found.
- The law itself does not fix the report form or frequency. The "authorised body" sets them (https://mybuh.kz/news/k-okhrannym-agentstvam-izmenili-trebovaniya/).
- The MVD order that sets the quarterly form was **not found**. Searches on zakon.uchet.kz, adilet.zan.kz and prg.kz turned up no number or registration (unverified). Two fetches of zakon.uchet.kz timed out, and adilet.zan.kz returned only a page header.

**What must be filed or done**
- *Quarterly report.* It goes by email to the security-activity control unit of the MVD Police Department. It is due by the 25th of the month after each quarter. Three kinds of entity must file: private security organisations (including branches and representative offices), firms that install and service security alarms, and specialised training centres. The 13 blocks cover service weapons and special equipment by type, protected schools and preschools, cooperation with the police on public order, crimes by employees, dismissals for negative reasons, and protected objects vulnerable to terrorism (https://mybuh.kz/news/okhrannye-agentstva-budut-sdavat-otchety-v-mvd/, 19.05.2026). The article gives no order number and no effective date.
- *5-working-day notices* of a suspension, resumption or work outside the registered place. They can go on paper or electronically (https://mybuh.kz/news/k-okhrannym-agentstvam-izmenili-trebovaniya/; https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/144505/trebovaniya-k-ohrannym-organizaciyam-v-kazahstane-usilyat.html).
- *Weapons.* A firm that suspends work must hand its weapons and ammunition to the police within 10 working days. Owners of rifled weapons must present them for control firing, and a missed deadline can lead to suspension of the weapon permit (https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/144505/trebovaniya-k-ohrannym-organizaciyam-v-kazahstane-usilyat.html). The lead file says control firing is every 5 years. I did not re-confirm the interval (unverified).
- *Staff.* Heads of branches and other managers must now meet the same requirements as guards (https://mybuh.kz/news/k-okhrannym-agentstvam-izmenili-trebovaniya/).
- *Licence subtypes.* The security licence is to be split into several subtypes (search summary of https://bes.media/news/tokaev-podpisal-zakon-ob-uzhestochenii-pravil-dlya-ohrannih-organizatsiy-v-kazahstane/). Details are unverified.

**Regulator and inspections**
- MVD Order No. 83 of 09.02.2026 made the Committee for Control of Security Activity the MVD agency in charge. It runs state control of security firms and of the anti-terror protection of vulnerable objects, and it coordinates the regional control units (OKOD/GKOD) (https://bizmedia.kz/2026-02-23-chem-budet-zanimatsya-komitet-po-kontrolyu-za-ohrannoj-deyatelnostyu-mvd-rk/; https://www.zakon.kz/pravo/6508849-chem-budet-zanimatsya-komitet-po-kontrolyu-za-okhrannoy-deyatelnostyu.html).
- Joint order MVD No. 389 (01.06.2026) / Ministry of National Economy No. 67 (03.06.2026) changed the risk criteria and checklists used for inspections (https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/V2600038906). The earlier challenge check says the 5-day notice is now a risk criterion. I could not re-open the text: it timed out.
- MVD Order No. 373 of 25.05.2026 amends the rules for state services in security activity, effective 12.07.2026 (search summary of https://zakon.uchet.kz/rus/docs/V2000020224).

**Penalties**
- Administrative Code Art. 470, "Violation of the legislation on security activity", was checked current to 06.10.2026. It fines officials and small businesses 40 MRP, medium businesses 80 MRP and large businesses 100 MRP. The violations it lists are: hardening of state-protected sites, foreign ownership, hiring unqualified guards, guards without ID or uniform, licence restrictions, and training breaches. A repeat offence within a year costs 80, 130 or 150 MRP, with or without a ban on activity (https://kodeksy-kz.com/ka/ob_administrativnyh_pravonarusheniyah/470.htm).
- **Art. 470 says nothing about failing to file the quarterly report or the 5-day notice** (same source). Some general article on failing to give information might apply (unverified). I found none.
- MRP in 2026 is 4,325 tenge (https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/143213/mrp-mzp-i-drugie-raschetnye-pokazateli-na-2026-god.html; https://tengrinews.kz/kazakhstan_news/utverjden-novyiy-mrp-na-2026-god-v-kazahstane-586608/). So a first Art. 470 fine for a small firm is about 173,000 tenge (about USD 360). A repeat fine is about 346,000 tenge.

**Enforcement evidence**
- In 2021, an East Kazakhstan police operation ("Service Weapon") produced 6 protocols for weapon-storage breaches. One private firm was fined 29,170 tenge after a guard left a pistol under a desk (https://www.caravan.kz/?p=719374).
- A deputy said about 800 suspended firms had weapons whose whereabouts were unknown (https://24.kz/ru/news/social/702739-skolko-chastnykh-okhrannykh-predpriyatij-dejstvuet-v-kazakhstane, 02.04.2025). That is what drove the new weapons rules.
- I found no 2026 news of fines for missed reports.

## Buyers

- **4,463** private security organisations were registered with the police at 1 January 2026, employing over 97,000 guards (https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/144505/trebovaniya-k-ohrannym-organizaciyam-v-kazahstane-usilyat.html).
- Another figure is **5,129** organisations and 97,116 qualified guards with the right to carry weapons (https://www.inbusiness.kz/kz/news/elimizdegi-97-116-kuzet-kyzmetkerine-karu-alyp-zhuru-kuky-berilgen; https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/prezident-kazahstana-podpisal-popravki-po-voprosam-ohrannoj-deyatelnosti). The date of this figure is unclear.
- About **4,200** operate and 800 of them are suspended, according to a deputy in April 2025 (https://24.kz/ru/news/social/702739-skolko-chastnykh-okhrannykh-predpriyatij-dejstvuet-v-kazakhstane).
- In one city, only 283 of 581 registered firms were working, and 66 held service-weapon permits (https://kapital.kz/gosudarstvo/16328/v-stolitse-iz-581-okhrannoy-organizatsii-rabotayet-lish-283.html, undated). This suggests only a minority hold weapons.
- Alarm installers and guard training centres also file the report (https://mybuh.kz/news/okhrannye-agentstva-budut-sdavat-otchety-v-mvd/). I found no count for them (unverified).
- **Realistic market:** about 3,000 to 3,700 active firms, mostly small LLPs ("TOO"). Most firms have many branches registered separately, for example "Nurkuzet" and "Alim-Kuzet" (https://statsnet.co/companies/kz/59706887; https://statsnet.co/companies/kz/59700608).
- **How they comply today:** this is unverified. The report is an email, so firms most likely fill a Word or Excel template in-house, done by the director, a deputy or a "weapons officer". Payroll and tax run in 1C through an accountant.

## Willingness to pay

- **The trigger is weak.** It means 4 reports a year and the odd notice. That is perhaps 2 to 4 hours of staff time a quarter (unverified estimate).
- **No fine for missing the report was found** (https://kodeksy-kz.com/ka/ob_administrativnyh_pravonarusheniyah/470.htm). So the "avoid a fine" pitch does not work for the core feature.
- **Fines that do exist are small:** about 173,000 to 432,500 tenge for a first Art. 470 breach, depending on size (MRP 4,325). The real threat is a licence ban on repeat offences.
- **Plausible price:** 5,000 to 15,000 tenge per month (about USD 10 to 30) per firm for a full back-office tool. That is in line with the international guard tools above. A tool that only does the report would struggle to earn even 2,000 to 3,000 tenge a month (unverified estimate).
- **Upside:** if 5% of 3,500 active firms paid 10,000 tenge a month, that is about 21 million tenge (about USD 44,000) a year. It is a modest side business, not a company.

- **Regional police control units (OKOD/GKOD)** hold seminars for firms after law changes (unverified). They will not endorse a vendor.

## Risks

- **No penalty, low pull.** This is the main risk and it is fatal for a report-only product. The report is a template and nothing punishes skipping the tool.
- **State system.** "E-kuzet" (unverified) or any MVD portal would replace the email report, and the regulator could move reporting online at any time. Kazakhstan has a habit of digitising state reporting (eGov, e-licensing).
- **The rule is not pinned down.** The MVD order number, form and effective date are unconfirmed, so the 13 blocks could change.
- **Small, shrinking market.** About 800 firms are suspended, and the rules are getting stricter. The new weapons and licence-subtype rules may push out small firms.
- **Data sensitivity.** Weapons registers and protected school sites are sensitive. Firms may refuse to put them in a cloud tool, and the police may dislike it.
- **Language.** The product needs Russian and Kazakh. That is manageable.
- **Liability** for a wrong report is low, because no fine was found.

## First product

Only worth building as a wedge into a wider guard-company back office, not as a report tool.

What a first version should do:
1. Keep a register of branches, posts or objects, guards (training certificate, periodic check, ID), and weapons and special equipment (serial number, permit, holder, control-firing date).
2. Generate the quarterly 13-block report in the MVD layout from that register (Excel/Word), ready to email.
3. Remind the firm of deadlines: report by the 25th, 5-day notices, 10-day weapon surrender on suspension, control firing, guard certificate expiry.
4. Provide a notice template for suspension, resumption and work outside the registered place.

First 30 days:
- Get the actual MVD order and the template from 3 to 5 firms or a training centre.
- Build a Russian-language web app with the register, the report export and email reminders.
- Pilot it free with 10 firms through one training centre or the association.
- Measure whether anyone would pay 5,000 tenge a month. If not, stop.
