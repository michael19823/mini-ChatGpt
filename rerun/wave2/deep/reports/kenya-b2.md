# Kenya B2: AML/goAML onboarding and CDD-record pack for small estate agencies and law firms

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 4/10.**

The duty is real and in force. Since the 2023 and 2025 amendments to POCAMLA, estate agents and advocates doing property, client-money, company or trust work are reporting institutions. They must register with the Financial Reporting Centre (FRC) on goAML, do CDD on every client, screen for sanctions and PEPs, keep records for 7 years, appoint an MLRO, keep a written risk assessment and policy, and file STRs and cash reports (https://lsk.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/AML_CFT_CPF_FAQs_Law_Firms.pdf ; https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/companies/property-agents-face-daily-fines-for-hiding-home-buyers-5239650).

I found no Kenya-specific CDD-and-record tool for small firms. The gaps in the case are about demand and supervision. Few firms have registered: 112 of 1,504 targeted agents by October 2025. The FRC made only 44 DNFBP inspections against a target of 487 in FY2024/25. The 2025 FRC risk profiling covered only 442 entities across four DNFBP sectors (https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/budget-cuts-hit-kenya-s-fight-against-dirty-cash-5331662 ; https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/real-estate-law-firms-top-kenya-s-money-laundering-risk-list-5540984).

The Law Society of Kenya (LSK) is building an AML module in its own ERP for advocates to file STRs, SARs and cash reports. That takes the filing step away from any third-party tool for law firms (LSK FAQ Q23, link above). A CDD, risk-assessment and inspection-readiness pack can still sell. It works best as a cheap add-on sold through compliance consultants or LSK and estate-agent networks, not as a standalone SaaS.

## Duty

**Legal basis**
- Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2009 (POCAMLA), amended in 2023 and by the AML/CFT Laws (Amendment) Act 2025, which was signed on 17 June 2025 (https://issafrica.org/enactafrica/enact-observer/can-anti-money-laundering-amendments-get-kenya-off-fatf-s-grey-list ; https://www.pwc.com/ke/en/assets/pdf/highlights-of-aml-cft-act-2023.pdf).
- POCAMLA Regulations 2023 (POCAMLR) (https://lsk.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/AML_CFT_CPF_FAQs_Law_Firms.pdf).
- LSK Legal Sector Guidelines on AML/CFT/CPF, approved by the LSK Council on 13 October 2025 under s.4A of the LSK Act (https://lsk.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/The-Legal-Sector-Guidelines-on-AML-CFT-CPF.pdf).

**Who is in scope**
- Real estate agents involved in selling, mortgaging, charging, letting or managing immovable property (FRC notice as reported by https://k24.digital/411/govt-issues-a-mandatory-directive-to-all-real-estate-agencies/amp). Because letting and managing are included, property managers are in scope, not only sales agents.
- Advocates who manage client money or assets, buy or sell property for clients, form or manage companies or trusts, act as nominees, or provide trust services. This is under s.48 POCAMLA. The LSK says "most general practice firms will fall within scope" and that the duties apply "to all advocates in private practice regardless of firm size", sole practitioners included (LSK FAQ Q1 and Q5, https://lsk.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/AML_CFT_CPF_FAQs_Law_Firms.pdf).

**What must be done**

| Duty | Source |
|---|---|
| Register with the FRC on goAML (s.47A). Failure is an offence under s.47A(5). The estate agent deadline was 14 Nov 2025. | https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/companies/property-agents-face-daily-fines-for-hiding-home-buyers-5239650 |
| CDD on every client, and ongoing (s.45; Regs 14-19). Individuals: name, ID/passport copy, KRA PIN, address, source of funds, purpose. Companies: certificate, CR12, directors, and UBOs above 10%. | LSK FAQ Q6-Q8, Q10 |
| EDD for PEPs and their family and associates, high-risk countries, and unusual deals (Reg 20). SDD is allowed for low-risk clients (Reg 21). | LSK FAQ Q11-Q14 |
| Sanctions screening against the UN list and Kenyan designations before onboarding and before each transaction. Freeze assets and report on a match. | LSK FAQ Q32-Q33 |
| Written firm-wide risk assessment (Reg 7), updated on material change. The LSK and FRC may ask to see it on inspection. | LSK FAQ Q16-Q17 |
| Written AML/CFT/CPF policy (Reg 28(a)), MLRO appointment (Reg 12), and documented staff training at least once a year. | LSK FAQ Q29-Q31 |
| STR when suspicion forms (s.44(2)). The 2023 amendment set "within two days after suspicion arose", where the old rule was 7 days from the transaction. | https://www.pwc.com/ke/en/assets/pdf/highlights-of-aml-cft-act-2023.pdf (search snippet; statute text unverified) ; LSK FAQ Q19 |
| Cash transactions of USD 15,000 or more reported to the FRC. Business Daily says agents report deals over USD 15,000 "every Friday" on an FRC template with the buyer's name, ID, address, occupation and source of income, plus beneficial owners of corporate cash buyers. | https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/companies/property-agents-face-daily-fines-for-hiding-home-buyers-5239650 ; https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/companies/bank-accounts-tip-offs-trigger-seizure-of-illicit-wealth-5539122 (search snippet) |
| Keep CDD, transaction and internal reports for at least 7 years (s.46). Electronic records are fine if they are tamper-evident. | LSK FAQ Q27-Q28 |
| Refuse or end the business if CDD cannot be completed. Do not tip off the client. | LSK FAQ Q15, Q24 |

**The STR deadline conflict.** Earlier checks found 2 days, 7 working days and 3 days. PwC's summary of the 2023 Act gives two days from suspicion (s.44(2)). The 7-working-days figure appears in secondary real-estate material (https://cifar.eu/news/anti-money-laundering-rules-reporting-for-the-kenyan-real-estate-sector), and FNJ describes the duty as "as soon as reasonably practicable" (https://fnjassociates.co.ke/suspicious-transaction-reporting-in-kenya/). I take 2 days as the current rule, but I could not read the enacted text (unverified).

**Penalties**
- Up to KSh 25m for institutions and KSh 5m for individuals, plus KSh 10,000 a day for up to 180 days of continuing failure (https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/companies/property-agents-face-daily-fines-for-hiding-home-buyers-5239650).
- Since 2023 the FRC can impose administrative sanctions itself (https://streamlinefeed.co.ke/news/how-the-frc-is-driving-kenyas-financial-intelligence-anti-money-laundering-agenda).
- The LSK can impose separate disciplinary sanctions under s.4A(2)(c) of the LSK Act (LSK FAQ Q35).
- Estate agents also face Estate Agents Act penalties for practising without EARB registration: up to KSh 20,000 or 2 years in jail (https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/regulator-proposes-sh1m-fine-for-bogus-real-estate-agents-2237680).

**Enforcement: real, but thin**
- The LSK says it has been doing onsite and offsite AML inspections of law firms, usually with the FRC, and that inspection is mandatory (LSK FAQ Q36).
- The FRC's 2025 risk assessment rated 153 real estate agencies and 74 law firms medium or high risk. The FRC said this triggers a 2026 on-site inspection cycle, and its Director-General promised "active, ground-level supervision of the legal profession" (Business Daily, 29 July 2026, https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/real-estate-law-firms-top-kenya-s-money-laundering-risk-list-5540984).
- Against that, in FY2024/25 the FRC made 44 DNFBP and non-bank inspections against a target of 487, and registered 455 entities against a target of 990. Its budget fell from KSh 1.70bn in 2022/23 to KSh 570m (https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/budget-cuts-hit-kenya-s-fight-against-dirty-cash-5331662).
- A parliamentary committee proposed an extra KSh 388m for 2026/27 to close FATF gaps (https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/agency-gets-sh388m-more-to-rescue-kenya-from-dirty-money-list-5475740).
- I found no published FRC fine against a named estate agent or law firm. Visible fines are CBK penalties on banks (https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/companies/cbk-fines-slapped-on-banks-forex-bureaus-5623470).
- In bank cases the DPP has approved charges against the CEOs of three banks under s.44(2) (https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/national/article/2001554622/ceos-of-three-top-banks-to-be-charged-in-sh363m-money-laundering-probe).

**Driver.** Kenya was grey-listed by FATF in February 2024, partly for weak supervision of law firms and real estate. It was still pushing to exit in mid-2026, which keeps pressure on DNFBP supervision (https://theafricanmirror.africa/special-features/special-features-analysis/kenyas-greylisting-for-weak-action-on-money-laundering-and-terrorism-financing-what-that-means-and-what-must-happen-next/ ; https://www.dawan.africa/news/kenya-intensifies-anti-financial-crime-drive-as-agencies-push-for-fatf-grey-list-exit).

**Coming changes**
- The LSK is building an AML module in its ERP for STR, SAR and CTR filing. It is not live yet, so advocates still file through FRC channels (LSK FAQ Q23).
- The LSK issued a Law Firm Statistical Questionnaire (v1, June 2026), returned as a PDF by email to mlro@lsk.or.ke (https://lsk.or.ke/aml-cft-cpf-compliance-hub/).

## Buyers

**Estate agents**
- The FRC targeted 1,504 agents for registration. Only 112 had registered by 21 October 2025 (https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/companies/property-agents-face-daily-fines-for-hiding-home-buyers-5239650).
- Earlier, 411 registered agents filed only 2 STRs in the five years to 2021 (https://nation.africa/kenya/business/how-real-estate-put-kenya-on-dirty-cash-grey-list--4545410).
- The EARB gazetted its 2026 list of registered agents and agency firms in Kenya Gazette Vol. CXXVIII No. 56 of 27 March 2026. I could not count the entries (https://estateagentsboard.or.ke/official-publication-of-the-2026-kenya-gazette-registered-estate-agents-and-estate-agents-companies/).
- An earlier check found an undated EARB figure of about 3,000 registered agents and claims of more than 40,000 informal agents (unverified).

**Law firms**
- Kenya has about 17,000 advocates in active practice out of about 26,000 admitted (The Star, Nov 2025, https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2025-11-23-is-kenya-over-producing-law-graduates). Other sources give 22,000+ practising (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_Society_of_Kenya).
- I found no count of law firms (unverified). If 17,000 practising advocates work in firms averaging 3-5 advocates, that implies roughly 3,000-6,000 firms and sole practices (my estimate, unverified). Per the LSK, most of them are in scope.

**Risk-profiled entities.** The FRC's 2025 profiling covered 442 entities: 193 real estate agencies (153 medium/high risk), 86 law firms (74 medium/high), 113 dealers in precious metals and stones, and 30 trust and company service providers (https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/real-estate-law-firms-top-kenya-s-money-laundering-risk-list-5540984). This is the group most likely to face a 2026 inspection.

**Realistic paying pool.** The likely buyers are a few hundred registered, inspected firms: about 300 profiled agencies and law firms, plus the registered share of 1,504 agents. A wider long tail of law firms is legally in scope but barely supervised.

**How they comply today**
- Mostly by hand: Word and Excel KYC forms, ID copies on file, and the free LSK template policy (https://lsk.or.ke/aml-cft-cpf-compliance-hub/).
- Some use consultants such as FNJ & Associates, which offers risk assessment, policy drafting, training and audit readiness (https://fnjassociates.co.ke/?p=2469).
- STR filing is almost nil. Banks file most STRs (https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/financial%20reporting%20centre/89085/era-of-wash-wash-alarm-over-increased-money-laundering-as-suspicious-transactions-surge).

## Willingness to pay

- **Fines are large on paper.** They run up to KSh 25m for institutions and KSh 5m for individuals, plus KSh 10,000 a day (https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/companies/property-agents-face-daily-fines-for-hiding-home-buyers-5239650). I found no actual fine on an agent or law firm, so firms see the risk as low.
- **Consultant prices are not published.** A 2-day AML training seat costs GBP 2,100, about KSh 350,000 (https://shasat.co.uk/training-courses/aml-compliance-workshop-nairobi-gid-24011/). A Kenyan consultant risk assessment plus policy probably costs KSh 50,000-300,000 one-off (unverified estimate).
- **Kenyan SaaS benchmarks are low.** Property management tools run from free to about USD 77 a month (https://www.softwaresuggest.com/property-management-software/kenya ; https://slashdot.org/software/p/iOSoft-PMS/).
- **The LSK says CDD cost falls on the firm,** but firms may pass it to clients as disbursements (LSK FAQ Q9). A per-file fee of KSh 300-1,000, recharged to the client, may be an easier sell than a subscription (unverified).
- **Plausible price.** About KSh 2,500-7,500 a month (USD 20-60) per small firm, plus per-check ID or screening fees at cost plus margin. Or KSh 30,000-60,000 a year as an "inspection-ready" bundle. At 200 paying firms on KSh 4,000 a month, that is about KSh 9.6m (USD 75k) a year. That is a small business, not a big one.

## Channels

- **LSK:** its Compliance Hub and webinar schedule (https://lsk.or.ke/aml-cft-cpf-compliance-hub/), CPD events, and its AML/CFT directorate (https://www.myjobmag.co.ke/job/aml-cft-compliance-officer-compliance-ethics-directorate-law-society-of-kenya-1). Branch chapters, e.g. the Kiambu chapter with 277 advocates (https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/?p=175850).
- **EARB:** the gazette list of registered agents is a free lead list (https://estateagentsboard.or.ke/official-publication-of-the-2026-kenya-gazette-registered-estate-agents-and-estate-agents-companies/). EARB public notices on verifying agents (https://thekenyatimes.com/business/estate-agent-board-issues-fresh-notice-to-all-property-buyers-and-tenants/).
- **Industry bodies:** the Kenya Property Developers Association and the Institution of Surveyors of Kenya (no AML programme found, unverified).
- **Consultants and accountants:** AML consultants such as FNJ and CAMS-certified individuals can resell the tool as their delivery platform. ICPAK publishes MLRO material for accountants (https://www.icpak.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Job-Title-Money-Laundering-Reporting-Officer-MLRO85.pdf).
- **Partners:** property management SaaS vendors (Kodi with Co-op Bank, iOSoft) as an add-on, and banks that must check property deposits.
- **Timing hooks:** the FRC's 2026 on-site inspection cycle for the 153 and 74 medium/high-risk firms, and LSK inspections.

## Risks

1. **The regulator builds the tool.** The LSK is building an ERP AML module for STR/SAR/CTR filing (LSK FAQ Q23). If it grows to cover CDD records, the law-firm half of this idea goes away. goAML is the free FRC filing channel.
2. **Weak enforcement means weak demand.** The FRC made 44 inspections against a target of 487, on a shrinking budget (https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/budget-cuts-hit-kenya-s-fight-against-dirty-cash-5331662). Only about 7% of targeted agents had registered. No public fines on DNFBPs were found. If Kenya exits the grey list, pressure may ease.
3. **Small market.** The core paying pool is in the hundreds, not thousands.
4. **Generic KYC vendors move down-market.** Smile ID, Youverify and Sumsub already hold the Kenyan ID-check rails. A newcomer will resell them, so margins on checks are thin.
5. **Liability and data protection.** Holding ID copies and PEP results brings duties under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 and registration with the ODPC (unverified for this use). A missed sanctions match could be blamed on the tool.
6. **Law-firm trust.** Advocates resisted AML coverage, and a 2024 court settlement made the LSK the regulator (https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/lawyers-sign-pact-with-state-to-report-clients-dirty-cash--4338000). Firms may distrust third-party storage of client data.
7. **Language is not a barrier.** All rules and practice are in English, and the Swahili search found nothing distinct.
8. **No public goAML API.** Any filing help is limited to preparing the report (unverified for Kenya).

## First product

**V1: an "inspection-ready AML file" for a firm of 1-20 staff. Web and mobile, English.**

1. Client onboarding forms for individuals and companies that match POCAMLR Regs 15-16: ID/passport, KRA PIN, address, source of funds, purpose, and UBOs above 10% from the CR12. Each produces a timestamped PDF record kept for 7 years.
2. Sanctions and PEP check against the UN consolidated list and Kenyan TFS notices, which the LSK publishes in 2026 as Notices No. 1-15. Store an audit log of each check. Optional paid IPRS/KRA check through Smile ID or Youverify.
3. Client risk score (low, medium or high) that triggers SDD or EDD and records senior sign-off for PEPs.
4. Firm-wide risk assessment wizard (Reg 7) and a policy generator built on the LSK template, with yearly review reminders.
5. MLRO register: internal suspicion reports, the decision to file or not, and the reason. A 2-day STR countdown, and a pre-filled summary to paste into goAML.
6. A USD 15,000 cash-deal flag with a weekly cash-report checklist for agents.
7. Training log with a short annual quiz.
8. One-click "inspection pack" export.

**First 30 days**
- Week 1: read the POCAMLR 2023 text and the LSK Guidelines, and confirm the STR deadline and CTR cadence with the FRC helpline. Interview 10 estate agents from the EARB gazette list and 10 small law firms.
- Week 2: build the onboarding forms, PDF records and UN-list screening.
- Week 3: build the risk assessment wizard, MLRO register and inspection export.
- Week 4: pilot free with 5 firms and 2 consultants, and test a KSh 3,000-5,000 monthly price. Approach the LSK AML directorate about listing on the Compliance Hub, and ask what the ERP module will cover.
