# Malaysia B2: Co-operative compliance desk (accounts, audit and AGM pack for small koperasi)

## Summary

**Verdict: no-go. Score: 3/10.**

The duty is real. Every koperasi must keep books, have them audited each year by an auditor approved by the Malaysia Co-operative Societies Commission (Suruhanjaya Koperasi Malaysia, SKM), and present the audited accounts at its annual general meeting. Non-compliance is widespread: 7,779 koperasi do not keep accounts and 2,304 are unaudited (https://thesun.my/news/malaysia-news/over-7000-cooperatives-fail-to-maintain-accounts-skm-offers-guidance-support-ramanan-gg13401135/).

**What kills the idea:** in April 2026 SKM itself opened a public tender to build "Sistem Perakaunan Konsep Mudah Koperasi (EASY COOP 2.0)", a simplified accounting system for koperasi (tender S005/2026, closed 21 May 2026) (https://www.skm.gov.my/images/01-utama/sebutharga/pelawaan/2026/1.IKLANSEBUTHARGAS0052026.pdf). That is the core of this product, built by the regulator, and most likely free.

The market also makes paying customers unlikely:
- About 86% of koperasi are "micro", with turnover of RM200,000 or less (https://ir.uitm.edu.my/140611/1/140611.pdf).
- Many are dormant, with no income.
- The audit still needs a human SKM-approved auditor.
- SKM and ANGKASA already pay accountants through small grants.
- Act 502 is due to be replaced by a new Co-operative Act, so the rules may change soon.

## Duty

**Legal basis.** Co-operative Societies Act 1993 (Act 502).
- **Section 58, bookkeeping.** A model by-law text says that under section 58 the board must keep proper, up-to-date accounting records. Entries must be made "tidak lewat daripada tiga puluh hari" (no later than 30 days) after each transaction. Trading accounts, the profit and loss account and the balance sheet must be ready "tidak lewat daripada dua bulan" (within two months) after year-end so they can be audited (https://anyflip.com/xpxqb/iakj/basic). This is a by-law template, not the Act text, so the exact statutory wording is (unverified).
- **Sections 60/61, audit.** Accounts must be audited every financial year by an auditor approved by SKM under section 61. Guideline GP26 of 1 September 2024 sets the approval rules (https://skm.gov.my/images/01-utama/perundangan/garis-panduan/gp26-01-09-2024.pdf). An approved auditor must hold a Malaysian Institute of Accountants practising licence, Ministry of Finance audit approval under the Companies Act, and Audit Oversight Board registration. There is an alternative route through the Institute of Cooperative & Management Auditors (ICMA) (https://mapt.kpf.my/DocMapt44/Agenda11.pdf ; https://skm.gov.my/images/perkhidmatan/1--Panduan-Memohon-Kelulusan-Juruaudit---2412024.pdf).
- **AGM.** The same by-law template says audited accounts must be presented at the general meeting no later than six months after year-end (https://anyflip.com/xpxqb/iakj/basic). For calendar-year koperasi that means 30 June. The rule that audited accounts reach SKM at least 30 days before the AGM is (unverified).
- **Submission portal.** I found no evidence of an online annual-return or financial-statement portal. SKM's online services cover registration (e-Daftar) and auditor approval (https://www.skm.gov.my/en/frequently-asked-question ; https://skm.gov.my/images/perkhidmatan/1--Panduan-Memohon-Kelulusan-Juruaudit---2412024.pdf). A filing portal is (unverified).

**Penalties and enforcement.**
- **Fines.** I found no published fine or compound amount for late or missing audited accounts. The Cooperatives (Compounding of Offences) Regulations 2010 are cited in the earlier check, but the amounts are (unverified).
- **Loss of support.** A koperasi that fails the audit requirement can lose eligibility for SKM facilities (https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2218963). SKM funding schemes require up-to-date audited statements, sometimes for three years (https://www.skm.gov.my/images/06-Pusat-Sumber/pengumuman/2022/dasar-peraturan-tmpskm/bukudasartmpskm.pdf ; https://www.skm.gov.my/en/frequently-asked-question).
- **Cancellation.** Koperasi with no business, no AGM and no audit for more than three years face cancellation. In Negeri Sembilan, 340 of 768 koperasi are inactive (https://www.bharian.com.my/amp/berita/kes/2026/04/1536622/bhplus). SKM cancelled 61 rural koperasi in Sarawak in 2023-2025 (https://angkasa.coop/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/SKM-Kenal-Pasti-Faktor-Kegagalan-Koperasi-di-Pedalaman-Sarawak-–-Mohamad-Alamin.pdf).
- **Funding freeze.** In February 2026 the ministry warned of a funding freeze or legal action for poor performers (https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/malaysia/2026/02/11/funding-only-for-performing-cooperatives-poor-performers-face-freeze-or-legal-action-ministry-warns/208860). The page returned 403, so the details are (unverified).

**Upcoming change.** A new Co-operative Act will replace Act 502 entirely. As of August 2025 the plan was gazettement in June 2026 and entry into force in December 2026 (https://www.utusan.com.my/ekonomi/2025/08/ruu-koperasi-baharu-dijangka-berkuatkuasa-akhir-2026/). In December 2025 the acting minister called it "a completely new act" in its final stage (https://berita.rtm.gov.my/?p=171117 ; https://www.bharian.com.my/amp/berita/nasional/2025/12/1482310/bhplus). I found no confirmation that it was tabled or passed, and its accounting and audit rules are (unverified). An earlier reform proposal talked of "self-regulation" (https://pardocs.sinarproject.org/documents/2019-march-april-parliamentary-session/written-replies-soalan-bertulis/p14p2m1-soalan-bukanlisan-225.pdf).

## Buyers

- **Total.** 16,468 registered koperasi in 2025, up 1.1% on 2024, with about 7.33 million members. Selangor has 2,217, Sabah 2,047 and Perak 1,755. Source: a Kosmo/Bernama report seen in search results (https://www.kosmo.com.my/?p=631574 or https://www.bernama.com/radio/news.php?id=2575395; which article holds these figures is unverified). The 2024 total was 15,809 (https://www.kosmo.com.my/2024/07/29/kerajaan-bantu-koperasi-tidak-aktif/).
- **Active versus dormant.** In June 2020, of 14,668 koperasi, about 9,300 were active, 2,000 inactive and 3,000 dormant (https://www.kosmo.com.my/?p=86823). I found no national split for 2025.
- **Size.** At 31 December 2020, SKM statistics put 12,550 of 14,629 koperasi (85.8%) in the micro cluster (turnover of RM200,000 or less), 1,358 in the small cluster and 479 in the medium cluster. The rest were large (https://ir.uitm.edu.my/140611/1/140611.pdf).
- **Non-compliance.** 7,779 koperasi fail to maintain accounts and 2,304 are unaudited, per the Deputy Minister in Dewan Rakyat in December 2024 (https://thesun.my/news/malaysia-news/over-7000-cooperatives-fail-to-maintain-accounts-skm-offers-guidance-support-ramanan-gg13401135/).
- **How they comply today.**
  - Volunteer board members (Anggota Lembaga Koperasi, ALK) keep the books, often weakly. SKM cites ALK governance weakness and lack of systematic management (https://angkasa.coop/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/SKM-Kenal-Pasti-Faktor-Kegagalan-Koperasi-di-Pedalaman-Sarawak-–-Mohamad-Alamin.pdf).
  - Licensed accountants prepare the accounts and approved auditors audit them. ANGKASA says inactive koperasi have no income yet must still pay a licensed accountant (https://www.kosmo.com.my/?p=483353).
  - Some use SKM's free Excel tool p-SKM or MOCCIS SPBK (see Competition).

## Competition

- **SKM EASY COOP 2.0 (regulator-built, in procurement).** SKM is buying analysis, design, development, testing, integration, migration, training and commissioning of a "Sistem Perakaunan Konsep Mudah Koperasi (EASY COOP 2.0)" (https://www.skm.gov.my/images/01-utama/sebutharga/pelawaan/2026/1.IKLANSEBUTHARGAS0052026.pdf).
  - The words "migration" and "2.0" imply that an Easy Coop 1.0 already exists. "Easy Coop" appears as a menu item on SKM sites (https://www.skm.gov.my/en/frequently-asked-question).
  - Price to koperasi: likely free (unverified). Go-live date: (unverified).
  - This is the direct killer.
- **p-SKM ("Perakaunan Sistem Konsep Mudah").** A free SKM accounting tool built on Microsoft Excel, project completed 2015 (https://itu.int/net4/wsis/stocktaking/Prizes/2024/Details/14493811550000000). It is the probable predecessor of Easy Coop (unverified).
- **MOCCIS SPBK.** Web-based koperasi accounting, member and financing system. Developed in 2004, it says SKM reviewed it for compliance with koperasi financial reporting rules. No price found (https://pustaka2.upsi.edu.my/index/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Flyer-SPBK.pdf).
- **ANGKASA (the national apex body).**
  - Its recovery unit helps koperasi prepare financial statements, which a licensed accountant then certifies (https://www.kosmo.com.my/?p=483353).
  - Its recovery workshops cover settling overdue accounts and holding AGMs.
  - It paid RM1,000 AGM aid to 30 koperasi and RM1,000 accounting-service aid to 30 inactive koperasi (https://angkasa.coop/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/SKM-Kenal-Pasti-Faktor-Kegagalan-Koperasi-di-Pedalaman-Sarawak-–-Mohamad-Alamin.pdf).
- **SKM and the Malaysia Co-operative Institute (Institut Koperasi Malaysia, IKMa).** Free guidance, training and help preparing accounts, plus an audit and accounting grant of RM3,000 (Peninsular) or RM3,500 (Sabah/Sarawak) (https://thesun.my/news/malaysia-news/over-7000-cooperatives-fail-to-maintain-accounts-skm-offers-guidance-support-ramanan-gg13401135/).
- **Approved audit and accounting firms.** They do bookkeeping and audit as a bundle. No published fee scales found (unverified).
- **Generic Malaysian cloud accounting (e.g. Bukku).** Not koperasi-specific. Prices not checked (unverified) (https://form.jotform.com/212354183144449).
- **AGM-pack or compliance-calendar SaaS for koperasi.** None found.

## Willingness to pay

- I found no published price for koperasi bookkeeping, audit or software. The size of the SKM grant (RM3,000-3,500) suggests that a small koperasi's yearly accounting plus audit costs roughly this much (unverified inference).
- ANGKASA sees even RM1,000 accounting aid as meaningful help for inactive koperasi (https://angkasa.coop/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/SKM-Kenal-Pasti-Faktor-Kegagalan-Koperasi-di-Pedalaman-Sarawak-–-Mohamad-Alamin.pdf). It also calls the accountant's fee a burden because these koperasi have no income (https://www.kosmo.com.my/?p=483353).
- I found no direct fines to weigh against. The real cost of non-compliance is losing SKM funding or being cancelled, which matters little to a koperasi that is already dormant.
- Conclusion: the 85% micro segment has very low willingness to pay. The roughly 2,000 small, medium and large koperasi can pay, but they already use accountants or systems like SPBK (unverified).

## Channels

- **SKM state offices.** They run guidance and recovery work, and list approved auditors (https://www.bharian.com.my/amp/berita/kes/2026/04/1536622/bhplus).
- **ANGKASA.** Recovery workshops and an AGM season with thousands of member koperasi (https://angkasa.coop/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Bengkel-Pemantapan-Jati-Diri-dan-Pemulihan-Kop.-2026-250226.pdf).
- **IKMa.** Training and capacity programmes for board members (https://angkasa.coop/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/SKM-Kenal-Pasti-Faktor-Kegagalan-Koperasi-di-Pedalaman-Sarawak-–-Mohamad-Alamin.pdf).
- **ICMA and SKM-approved audit firms.** These are the most realistic resellers. A tool that cuts the auditor's prep time could be sold to auditors rather than to koperasi (unverified).
- **KOPENAS and other secondary koperasi and federations** (https://kopenas.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/BUKU-UUK.pdf).

## Risks

1. **The regulator builds its own tool (confirmed).** EASY COOP 2.0 is in procurement for SKM (https://www.skm.gov.my/images/01-utama/sebutharga/pelawaan/2026/1.IKLANSEBUTHARGAS0052026.pdf).
2. **The law changes.** A completely new Co-operative Act is pending, targeted for late 2026 (https://www.utusan.com.my/ekonomi/2025/08/ruu-koperasi-baharu-dijangka-berkuatkuasa-akhir-2026/). The formats and duties for small koperasi could change, or be simplified.
3. **Buyers have no money.** About 86% are micro (https://ir.uitm.edu.my/140611/1/140611.pdf), and many are dormant with no income (https://www.kosmo.com.my/?p=483353).
4. **Subsidised competition.** SKM and ANGKASA grants pay accountants directly, and IKMa and SKM guidance is free.
5. **Licensing.** The audit itself must be done by an SKM-approved, MIA-licensed auditor (https://skm.gov.my/images/01-utama/perundangan/garis-panduan/gp26-01-09-2024.pdf). A software newcomer cannot sell the step that is actually mandatory.
6. **Language.** The work is mostly in Bahasa Malaysia, with a strong rural, Sabah and Sarawak skew.

## First product

Only worth doing as a narrow wedge, and only after checking what EASY COOP 2.0 will cover. If pursued:
- **v1:** a free-to-start web checklist and AGM pack generator in Malay, built around the by-law timeline:
  - 30-day entry reminders;
  - accounts ready by year-end + 2 months;
  - auditor appointment;
  - AGM within 6 months.
  - It would produce the notice, agenda, minutes template, ALK report and resolution templates (dividend, auditor reappointment).
- **Sales target:** sell to approved audit firms and ANGKASA as a white-label "client readiness" tool, not to dormant koperasi.
- **First 30 days:**
  - Interview 5 approved auditors and 2 SKM state offices.
  - Get the EASY COOP 2.0 scope from the tender documents (RM10 through Tenderwizard) or from SKM.
  - Build the AGM-pack generator plus an export of a trial balance into the SKM financial statement format.
  - Stop if EASY COOP covers AGM documents or SKM will not let third parties link to it.
