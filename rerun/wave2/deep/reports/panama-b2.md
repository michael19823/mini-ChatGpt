# Panama B2: SONF compliance kit for small law firms, accountants and other non-financial obliged subjects

## Re-assessment (owner's criteria)

**Verdict: maybe. New score: 6/10. Old score: 5/10.**

**The case.** About 7,900 lawyers, law firms and accountants are registered SONF, and the duties they carry mostly live outside the free state portals ([SSNF dashboard, Dec 2025](https://monitoreo.antai.gob.pa/api/submissions/491290/files/107632/download)). The portals take filings. They do not keep the client file, the risk matrices, the yearly review, the training log or the 5-year history the SSNF guide demands ([SSNF Guía de Matriz de Riesgo del SONF](https://ssnf.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Guía-de-Matriz-de-Riesgo-del-SONF.pdf)). The SSNF's own FAQ shows real portal pain: three separate registrations, an Excel bulk upload that rejects a whole batch over one bad field, and pages that time out and wipe typed data ([SSNF RUBF FAQ v3.0](https://cdn.corprensa.com/la-prensa/uploads/2023/04/17/Preguntas%20frecuentes%20sobre%20el%20registro.pdf)). No local product found does the whole job; the ones found are screening tools with, at most, a client risk matrix. A cheap Spanish "SONF programme in a box", sold to small offices and through compliance firms, could reach roughly US$110k-310k a year by year 3. That is a fair bootstrapped business, but not a big one, and the buyers are price-sensitive lawyers.

**Room for improvement over the portal or current practice**

- **Three portals, no single view.** A SONF must register in UAF en Línea, SSNF en Línea and, as a resident agent, the RUBF reached through Panamá Digital ([SSNF RUBF FAQ v3.0](https://cdn.corprensa.com/la-prensa/uploads/2023/04/17/Preguntas%20frecuentes%20sobre%20el%20registro.pdf)). Nothing ties the three together or tracks their deadlines.
- **Bulk upload is fragile.** The SSNF says the RUBF Excel template will not load if any field has an error. It tells users to clean all data first and load 50 to 100 records at a time ([same FAQ, Q31](https://cdn.corprensa.com/la-prensa/uploads/2023/04/17/Preguntas%20frecuentes%20sobre%20el%20registro.pdf)). Data preparation and validation before upload is a clear software job.
- **The portal loses work.** If the user is slow, the page refreshes and erases the data entered, so users must save as they go (Q32). Users also hit "error 403" and a "your connection is not private" warning they are told to click through (Q33-34) ([same FAQ](https://cdn.corprensa.com/la-prensa/uploads/2023/04/17/Preguntas%20frecuentes%20sobre%20el%20registro.pdf)).
- **The portal asks users to keep side records.** For listed companies, the RUBF could not yet record them properly, and the SSNF asked resident agents to "keep a file" of those entities until it adds the feature ([same FAQ, Q19](https://cdn.corprensa.com/la-prensa/uploads/2023/04/17/Preguntas%20frecuentes%20sobre%20el%20registro.pdf)).
- **Tight deadlines on request.** A resident agent has 5 business days to complete RUBF registration once invited, and 30, 60 or 90 days to load all its companies, depending on how many it serves ([same FAQ, Q6](https://cdn.corprensa.com/la-prensa/uploads/2023/04/17/Preguntas%20frecuentes%20sobre%20el%20registro.pdf)). The self-assessment questionnaire must come back within 15 business days of the SSNF email ([Res. S-022-2025, art. 4](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/blogs.dir/2/2026/06/728/resolucion-n0-s-022-2025-de-21-de-marzo-de-2025-uso-de-cuestionarios-de-autoevaluacion-para-medir-los-riesgos-asociados-al-blanqueo-de-capitales-63-66.pdf)). Answers ready from a client book save real time.
- **Record-keeping the portals never do.** Firm and client risk matrices, a yearly review of policies and matrices with every version kept 5 years, an independent review every 2 years, and an annual training plan with attendees, dates, hours and tests ([SSNF Guía de Matriz de Riesgo del SONF](https://ssnf.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Guía-de-Matriz-de-Riesgo-del-SONF.pdf)).
- **Supervision volume is high.** The SSNF ran about 9,000 extra-situ supervisions and 600 on-site visits in 2020-2024 ([La Estrella, 24 Mar 2025](https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/registro-unico-de-beneficiario-final-alcanza-una-carga-del-91-JB11321967)). In 2025 alone, 2,576 subjects got questionnaires ([SSNF dashboard, Dec 2025](https://monitoreo.antai.gob.pa/api/submissions/491290/files/107632/download)). So inspection readiness is a live need, not a remote one.
- **Time is the main complaint.** The president of the Colegio Nacional de Abogados said lawyers drop resident-agent work because the time to meet all the requirements is not worth the fee, and old client profiles are hard to document ([La Estrella, 30 Aug 2022](http://www.laestrella.com.pa/articulo/LE476625)). A vendor blog describes today's practice as scattered Excel files, loose screenshots and processes held in email ([sof-IA](https://sof-ia.net/blog/sujetos-obligados-no-financieros-ssnf-panama)).

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 5/10.**

The duty is real, recurring and enforced. Panama's Superintendencia de Sujetos No Financieros (SSNF) had 12,802 registered non-financial obliged subjects (SONF) at 31 Dec 2025, and 7,915 of them (62%) are in the professional sector of lawyers, law firms and accountants ([SSNF dashboard, Dec 2025](https://monitoreo.antai.gob.pa/api/submissions/491290/files/107632/download)). The SSNF has imposed 117 sanctions, 78 of them on law firms or individual lawyers. In 2025 it sent self-assessment questionnaires to 2,576 subjects and started 135 on-site inspections (same source). The free state portal ("SSNF en línea") already handles the filings themselves: registration, the self-assessment questionnaire and the extra-situ forms. A product therefore cannot sell "filing". It has to sell the internal programme that the filings and inspections test. That programme is a client risk matrix, an annual review, an annual training plan, and evidence ready for a 15-business-day questionnaire deadline. At least 35 SSNF-registered compliance firms already sell that programme as a service ([Morgan & Morgan / APEC, 2023](https://morimor.com/es/category/alvaro-tomas-es)). Small lawyers are price-sensitive; some have dropped regulated work rather than pay the compliance cost ([La Estrella, 30 Aug 2022](http://www.laestrella.com.pa/articulo/LE476625)). The case for a cheap Spanish-language SaaS is decent but not strong. The FATF fifth-round evaluation of Panama in 2027 is a near-term push ([La Estrella, 24 Oct 2025](https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/panama-inicia-su-preparacion-de-cara-a-la-quinta-evaluacion-del-gafi-en-2027-KI16968561)).

## Duty

**Legal basis**

- **Ley 23 of 27 Apr 2015** is the core AML/CFT/CPF law. It covers lawyers, accountants and notaries only when they do listed activities for a client: buying or selling property, managing money or accounts, or creating or managing legal entities ([sof-IA summary](https://sof-ia.net/blog/sujetos-obligados-no-financieros-ssnf-panama); [Ley 23 consolidated text, MEF/CNBC](https://cnbc.mef.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/ley-23-de-2015.pdf)). Article 12 requires a compliance officer, and a written prevention manual is also required (sof-IA, above). Article 26, as replaced by Ley 254 of 2021, requires the subject to identify, assess and understand its risks by client, product and channel ([TP Panama / search summary](https://www.tppanama.com/la-debida-diligencia-no-es-un-tramite/)) (article wording unverified against the Gaceta).
- **Ley 124 of 7 Jan 2020** created the SSNF as an autonomous supervisor. Article 14(j) lets the Superintendent issue binding guides. Article 43, as amended by Ley 254 of 12 Nov 2021, lets the SSNF demand data on operations, clients, products and manuals ([Res. S-022-2025, Gaceta 30248](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/blogs.dir/2/2026/06/728/resolucion-n0-s-022-2025-de-21-de-marzo-de-2025-uso-de-cuestionarios-de-autoevaluacion-para-medir-los-riesgos-asociados-al-blanqueo-de-capitales-63-66.pdf)).
- **Decreto Ejecutivo 35 of 6 Sep 2022**, article 6, requires a risk-based approach from supervisors (Res. S-022-2025, above).
- **Acuerdo JD-02-2022 of 28 Jul 2022** sets the duties of lawyers and accountants. Its article 3 requires a firm-level risk assessment that covers all clients, products, channels, and the amounts and frequency of client operations ([SSNF Guía de Matriz de Riesgo del SONF](https://ssnf.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Guía-de-Matriz-de-Riesgo-del-SONF.pdf); [La Estrella, 2022](http://www.laestrella.com.pa/articulo/LE476625)).

**What must be done, and how often**

- **Register** in SSNF en línea and keep the record updated. The SSNF has sanctioned 6 subjects for not registering and 1 for not updating, under Ley 124 ([SSNF dashboard, Dec 2025](https://monitoreo.antai.gob.pa/api/submissions/491290/files/107632/download)). The earlier check reported a $5,000 fine for resident agents who do not register ([SSNF Comunicado 004-2022](https://ssnf.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Comunicado-004-2022.pdf)) (not re-read here).
- **Self-assessment questionnaire.** Res. S-022-2025 of 21 Mar 2025 (Gaceta 30248, 1 Apr 2025) replaced the old extra-situ evaluation form. It is **not periodic**. The SSNF sends a request by email, and the subject must return the completed questionnaire within **15 business days** (article 4). The subject must update it when the SSNF asks, or when facts change its earlier result (article 5). Each sector gets its own questionnaires, one per regulated activity, and they are published on the SSNF website (article 6). False data is reported to prosecutors (article 7). Non-compliance is sanctioned (article 8) ([Res. S-022-2025](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/blogs.dir/2/2026/06/728/resolucion-n0-s-022-2025-de-21-de-marzo-de-2025-uso-de-cuestionarios-de-autoevaluacion-para-medir-los-riesgos-asociados-al-blanqueo-de-capitales-63-66.pdf)).
- **Firm risk matrix and client risk matrix.** Evaluation and update periods "may not exceed one year". Evaluations must be kept for 5 years. The client matrix needs documented statistical calibration and validation. Policies, procedures, methodologies and matrices must be reviewed **at least once a year**, with every version kept for 5 years. An **independent review at least every 2 years** is required, by external auditors, certified professionals or compliance firms, and must be kept for 2 years. An **annual training plan** must record attendees, dates, content, hours and the evaluation method ([SSNF Guía de Matriz de Riesgo del SONF](https://ssnf.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Guía-de-Matriz-de-Riesgo-del-SONF.pdf)). The SSNF also publishes a client-matrix guide ([Guía de Matriz de Riesgo de cliente](https://ssnf.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Guía-de-Matriz-de-Riesgo-de-cliente.pdf)).
- **Customer due diligence and PEPs.** Clients must be identified and verified, and screened against sanctions and PEP lists ([sof-IA](https://sof-ia.net/blog/sujetos-obligados-no-financieros-ssnf-panama)). Res. S-008-2026 (March 2026) tightened PEP identification and enhanced due diligence ([LexLatin, 16 Apr 2026](https://lexlatin.com/entrevistas/panama-sistema-financiero-quinta-ronda-gafi-desafios); [La Estrella](https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/panama-amplia-vigilancia-a-personas-expuestas-politicamente-y-sus-familiares-por-hasta-dos-anos-FK20913107)).
- **Prevention manual.** A secondary source says the SSNF due-diligence guide requires updating the manual at least every six months (sof-IA, above) (unverified against the guide). A Resolution S-037-2026 of 7 Jul 2026 with a guide for AML manuals was referenced in a search listing (unverified).
- **Suspicious transaction reports (ROS) to the UAF.** These must be sent "immediately" once a suspicion is confirmed, under Ley 70 of 2019 ([UAF guide via Superbancos](https://www.superbancos.gob.pa/documentos/leyes_y_regulaciones/circulares/2019/guia_operaciones_sospechosas.pdf)) (detail from the search summary). Volumes from lawyers are small: 34 ROS in the first seven months of 2021 ([Panamá América](https://panamaamerica.com.pa/economia/aumenta-un-15-el-registro-de-operaciones-sospechosas-en-la-uaf-en-el-2021-1192926)). The secure-platform "ADSO code" in the lead could not be confirmed (unverified).
- **Beneficial owner register (RUBF).** Resident agents must upload beneficial owners under Ley 129 of 2020. The SSNF has sanctioned 8 subjects for not registering in the RUBF ([SSNF dashboard](https://monitoreo.antai.gob.pa/api/submissions/491290/files/107632/download)). The RUBF itself is free ([IMF FSAP technical note](https://www.superbancos.gob.pa/documentos/evaluaciones_fmi/TN-AML-FT-SPN-Panama-FSAP.pdf)).

**Penalties and enforcement**

- **Fines.** Reported ranges are $1,000 to $30,000 for minor breaches, $30,000 to $300,000 for serious ones and $300,000 to $1 million for very serious ones ([Panamá América, undated](https://panamaamerica.com.pa/sociedad/multas-de-hasta-un-millon-por-ocultar-o-negar-informacion-1168504)) (date unverified). The earlier check cited $5,000 to $5 million ([Chambers](https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/view/203792615/600)). Missing the old extra-situ form carried the $5,000 minimum fine under Ley 23 ([TVN](https://www.tvn-2.com/nacionales/intendencia-mef-sancionara-obligados-financieros_1_1419074.html)).
- **Sanctions.** The SSNF reports 117 sanctions to 31 Dec 2025 (100 money fines, 17 disciplinary). 45 hit law firms and 33 hit individual lawyers, together 66%. 102 were for breaches of Ley 23 ([SSNF dashboard, Dec 2025](https://monitoreo.antai.gob.pa/api/submissions/491290/files/107632/download)). The count was 109 at 31 Jul 2025 ([SSNF dashboard, Jul 2025](https://monitoreo.antai.gob.pa/api/submissions/416663/files/94741/download)), so about 8 new sanctions came in five months.
- **Inspections and questionnaires in 2025.** 135 on-site and beneficial-owner inspections were started, 104 of them (77%) in the professional sector. 209 extra-situ supervisions were started, 86 professional. 2,576 subjects received self-assessment questionnaires and were risk-categorised, 1,612 of them professional (Dec 2025 dashboard).
- **Court test.** The Supreme Court upheld a $350,000 SSNF fine on CNO (Odebrecht) for know-your-employee failures ([La Prensa](https://www.prensa.com/judiciales/la-corte-suprema-mantiene-sancion-contra-odebrecht-por-incumplir-normas-antilavado/)).

**Changes ahead.** The FATF fifth-round evaluation of Panama is due in 2027, and it will focus on measurable results, including sanctions and statistics ([La Estrella, 24 Oct 2025](https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/panama-inicia-su-preparacion-de-cara-a-la-quinta-evaluacion-del-gafi-en-2027-KI16968561)). Named gaps include beneficial-owner transparency and supervision of complex structures ([LexLatin, 16 Apr 2026](https://lexlatin.com/entrevistas/panama-sistema-financiero-quinta-ronda-gafi-desafios)). No repeal or delay was found. The direction is tighter rules.

**Plainly:** the free state tool covers registration and every filing to the SSNF. It does not keep the firm's own client file, client risk matrix, annual review log or training records. That gap is the opening. Local screening tools (PEP Check, InteliCumplo, AgileCheck) and 35+ compliance firms are already working in that gap.

## Willingness to pay

- **Prices:** none were found for compliance firms, InteliCumplo, PEP Check or AgileCheck (unverified). A plausible price is US$30-80 a month per small office for software, or US$300-1,200 a year (estimate, unverified). Outsourced compliance from a registered firm probably costs several hundred dollars a month (unverified).
- **Cost of a fine:**
  - The minimum fine is reported at $1,000 to $5,000 ([Panamá América](https://panamaamerica.com.pa/sociedad/multas-de-hasta-un-millon-por-ocultar-o-negar-informacion-1168504); [TVN](https://www.tvn-2.com/nacionales/intendencia-mef-sancionara-obligados-financieros_1_1419074.html)).
  - The independent review required every 2 years ([SSNF guide](https://ssnf.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Guía-de-Matriz-de-Riesgo-del-SONF.pdf)) is a recurring cash cost that firms already pay.
- **Odds of a sanction:**
  - About 1,600 professional-sector subjects got a questionnaire in 2025, and about 104 got an on-site inspection ([SSNF dashboard](https://monitoreo.antai.gob.pa/api/submissions/491290/files/107632/download)).
  - Sanctions remain rare: 117 in about 9 years.
  - So a typical small office faces a real chance of a request it must answer in 15 business days, but a low chance of a fine. That supports a low price point, not a premium one.
- **Price sensitivity:** lawyers already say compliance time is not worth the resident-agent fee ([La Estrella, 2022](http://www.laestrella.com.pa/articulo/LE476625)).

## Channels

- **Colegio Nacional de Abogados (CNA):** its president has spoken publicly on SSNF burdens ([La Estrella](http://www.laestrella.com.pa/articulo/LE476625)). It is a route for CLE-style training (contact unverified).
- **Colegio de Contadores Públicos Autorizados de Panamá:** a route to accountants (not checked).
- **APEC, the association of SSNF-registered compliance firms:** these firms are better treated as white-label partners or resellers than as rivals ([Morgan & Morgan](https://morimor.com/es/la-salida-de-la-lista-de-gafi-y-las-empresas-de-cumplimiento)).
- **APEDE:** has run training for non-financial obliged subjects ([La Estrella](https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/200114/apede-capacitara-sujetos-obligados-financieros)).
- **ASOCUPA (compliance officers' association):** mentioned in the search results (unverified).
- **SSNF training events:** the SSNF ran 644 trainings in 2020-2024 ([La Estrella](https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/registro-unico-de-beneficiario-final-alcanza-una-carga-del-91-JB11321967)). Being present at these, or being cited in SSNF guidance, would help (unverified as feasible).
- **Real estate:** ACOBIR could be a second segment (not checked).
- **Content and SEO:** a free 15-day questionnaire checklist and a matrix template in Spanish. sof-IA already uses this approach.

## Risks

- **The state tool expands.** The SSNF already runs the portal, the questionnaires and the RUBF. If it adds a client-matrix module, the core product loses value. The UAF is also modernising its reporting platforms ([La Estrella, 2025](https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/panama-inicia-su-preparacion-de-cara-a-la-quinta-evaluacion-del-gafi-en-2027-KI16968561)). No such SSNF plan was found.
- **No fixed annual deadline for the questionnaire.** It comes on request only, so it is a weaker trigger than a calendar filing. The annual matrix review and training plan are the true recurring hooks, but they are softer duties.
- **Small market.** About 7,900 professional subjects. Many are sole practitioners with few regulated clients. Registrations are slowing (about 846 in 2025).
- **Local competition.** 35+ registered compliance firms, plus at least three screening tools.
- **Licensing.** Ley 23 defines "empresas de cumplimiento" as firms registered with the SSNF that support due diligence for obliged subjects ([search summary of Ley 23](https://cnbc.mef.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/ley-23-de-2015.pdf)). A service offer would likely need registration. Pure software probably would not (unverified).
- **Liability.** A wrong risk rating could feature in an SSNF sanction. Terms of use and an "officer decides" design are needed.
- **Language and trust.** Panamanian legal Spanish is required, and local references matter to lawyers.

## First product

**V1 ("Cumplimiento SONF"), a Spanish web app for a 1-20 person law or accounting office:**

1. A client file with the due-diligence fields from the SSNF guide, document upload, and a 5-year retention log.
2. A client risk matrix pre-loaded with the SSNF methodology: three levels, and factors for client, product, channel and geography. It needs a simple calibration report, using the Pareto distribution that the guide mentions.
3. A firm risk matrix built from the client book, with an annual review reminder and a version history.
4. PEP and sanctions screening through a third-party API, either a cheap generic one or a partnership with PEP Check or AgileCheck.
5. A "Questionnaire mode": when an SSNF request arrives, the app starts a 15-business-day countdown and pre-fills answers from the client book. The user then types the answers into SSNF en línea.
6. An annual training plan and attendance log, plus a template prevention manual.
7. An export pack for an inspection or for the 2-year independent review.

**First 30 days:**

- **Week 1:** get the current sector questionnaires and instructions from the SSNF website. Read JD-02-2022 and Res. S-008-2026. Interview 10 small lawyers and 3 APEC firms.
- **Week 2:** build the client file and client matrix.
- **Week 3:** build the questionnaire countdown and pre-fill, plus the exports.
- **Week 4:** run a pilot with 5 offices and one compliance firm as a reseller. Test a price of US$39-59 a month.
