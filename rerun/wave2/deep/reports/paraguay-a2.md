# Paraguay A2: register, beneficial-owner and transparency pack for non-profits

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 4/10.**

The duties are real, they recur, and they have grown since 2024. Associations, foundations and similar bodies must update the beneficial-owner register by 30 June every year under Ley 6446/2019. If they receive or manage funds for public policy, Ley 7363/2024 and Decreto 4806/2025 add annual forms A-E (including a balance sheet) and a fund-use publication on their own website every six months. But every filing goes through free state portals (SIARA, run by the Ministry of Economy and Finance, MEF). The best-defined buyers (NGOs that get public or donor money) are probably only hundreds to low thousands of organisations (unverified). The thousands of volunteer bodies (comisiones vecinales, cooperadoras) are mostly outside Ley 7363 or have no money. The law also faces two pending constitutional challenges. The best angle is a cheap "transparency page + compliance calendar" tool, sold mainly through accountants. That makes a small side business, not a strong standalone one.

## Duty

**1. Beneficial-owner and legal-entity register (Ley 6446/2019).**
- Who: sociedades, asociaciones, fundaciones, juntas de saneamiento, juntas or comisiones vecinales, sindicatos, employee associations, cooperadoras escolares, comisiones agrícolas and other Civil Code legal persons. Legal basis: Ley 6446/2019, Decreto 3241/2020 art. 2, and Resolución 8/2022, which ended an earlier exclusion (Res AT 1/20) (https://www.ultimahora.com/obligados-registracion-direccion-general-personas-y-estructuras-juridicas-y-beneficiarios-finales-n3034380).
- Deadlines: new entities file within 45 business days; changes within 15 business days; an annual update by 30 June, even if nothing changed (same source). Ferrere also cites 30 June as the annual deadline under Ley 6446/19 and Decreto 3241/2020 (https://www.ferrere.com/en/news/recordatorio-clave-sobre-obligaciones-societarias-anuales/).
- Records: keep supporting documents for 5 years. The beneficial-owner threshold is 10% ownership or other control (https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/se-promulga-nueva-ley-que-requiere-la-comunicacion-y-el-registro-de-beneficiarios-finales-y-otras-informaciones-entre-otras-nuev/; https://www.openownership.org/en/map/country/paraguay/). For an association, "owner" usually means the board and controllers (unverified).
- Penalties: fines of 50-500 jornales mínimos under art. 9 (https://www.ultimahora.com/obligados-registracion-direccion-general-personas-y-estructuras-juridicas-y-beneficiarios-finales-n3034380). Ferrere put this at about Gs. 4.2M-42.2M (USD 650-6,500) at earlier wage levels (https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/se-promulga-nueva-ley-que-requiere-la-comunicacion-y-el-registro-de-beneficiarios-finales-y-otras-informaciones-entre-otras-nuev/). The minimum wage from 1 July 2026 is reported as Gs. 3,044,000 (https://cazvid.com/es/blog/cuanto-gana-un-contador-en-paraguay, a commercial source, so unverified). That puts the range at about Gs. 5.9M-58.5M (my calculation, using a 26-day jornal). Decreto 5871/2021 adds RUC blocking and a ban on dealing with financial institutions (https://impuestospy.com/impuestos/decreto-n-5-871-2021/).
- Practical lever: SEPRELAD Res 202/20 makes banks and other obliged subjects ask for the beneficial-owner registration certificate during KYC (https://www.ferrere.com/en/news/exigencia-de-que-sujetos-obligados-de-la-ley-1-015-97-requieran-la-constancia-de-inscripcion-en-el-registro-de-beneficiarios-fin/). So any association with a bank account has a real reason to stay current.
- Portal: from March 2026, SIARA (MEF) is the only channel for filings with this register. It needs qualified electronic signatures and electronic identity profiles for legal representatives (BKM Berkemeyer guide, April 2026: https://www.legal500.com/guides/wp-json/wp/v2/comparative_guide/136640). The guide talks about S.A. and S.R.L. companies. Whether associations face the same signature rule is unverified.

**2. Non-profit control and transparency (Ley 7363/2024 and Decreto 4806/2025).**
- Status: promulgated 15 November 2024 (https://www.ultimahora.com/pena-promulga-ley-garrote-contra-las-ong; https://www.lanacion.com.py/politica/2024/11/15/pena-promulgo-ley-de-control-y-transparencia-de-ong/). Regulated by Decreto 4806/2025 of 23 October 2025 (https://impuestospy.com/impuestos/decreto-n-4806-2025/). SIARA went live on 1 December 2025 (https://prensamercosur.org/2025/12/01/mitic-pone-en-produccion-el-sistema-siara-para-la-gestion-de-organizaciones-sin-fines-de-lucro/).
- Scope (Decreto arts. 5-6): bodies that receive or manage funds of any origin and apply them to participation in public policy. Excluded: churches and religious confessions, political parties, and international bodies. Amateur sports bodies, sindicatos, campesino and student groups, comisiones vecinales and juntas de saneamiento are covered only if they receive or manage public funds for public policy (https://impuestospy.com/impuestos/decreto-n-4806-2025/).
- Registration (art. 10): existing bodies within 90 days and new ones within 30 days, counted from MITIC's notice that the system is live (same source). That implies roughly early March 2026 (my inference, unverified). In a letter of 31 March 2026, the Episcopal Conference asked for at least 12 more months (https://adn.celam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Paraguay-051-2026-Mtro-de-Economia-y-Finanzas.pdf, cited via search snippets; the file now returns HTTP 410). Whether an extension was granted is unverified.
- Annual report (art. 12): by 30 June, file to the DGPEJBF Form A (activities, programmes, beneficiaries), B (funding sources and their use), C (professionals and staff), D (linked persons and structures) and E (balance sheet). The data is a sworn statement filed electronically (art. 8) (https://impuestospy.com/impuestos/decreto-n-4806-2025/).
- Semiannual publication (art. 14): publish fund-use data on the organisation's own website, in digital and open-data formats, with history kept. Deadlines are the last day of July (first half) and the last day of January (second half). In justified cases it can be published on the authority's portal instead (same source).
- Other: join SIFEN e-invoicing within 90 days (art. 11). Answer information requests within 15 business days (art. 15). State bodies must check registration before signing agreements (art. 16) (same source).
- Sanctions: the decree sets no amounts. It sends procedure to Chapter IV of Ley 7363, and final sanctions are published on the authority's portal (arts. 17-18) (same source). The Senate version of July 2024 had a warning, then fines of 100-1,000 monthly minimum wages, a one-year suspension and deregistration (https://www.ultimahora.com/aplanadora-cartista-impone-ley-de-oeneges-pese-al-pedido-de-iglesia). The Executive's changes were adopted in the lower house (https://www.ultimahora.com/pena-promulga-ley-garrote-contra-las-ong). The final amounts are unverified.

**3. SEPRELAD (AML).** OSFL are obliged subjects under Ley 1015/97 as amended by Ley 3783/09, art. 2. They must register and stay "active". Res 490/2022 sets a risk-based AML rule for OSFL (https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2024/10/05/la-ong-presidida-por-esposa-de-leite-aparece-como-inactiva-ante-seprelad/). OSFL send suspicious-operation and negative reports through SIRO, and SEPRELAD has run random inspections since 2021 (https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/seprelad-amplia-la-implementacion-del-sistema-de-reporte-de-operaciones-sospechosas-para-varias-categorias-de-sujetos-obligado-1/; https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/inspecciones-aleatorias-de-seprelad-bajo-resolucion-36-21/). A CEJ director cited an annual SEPRELAD system fee of Gs. 400,000 (https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2024/07/10/la-ley-mordaza-a-las-ong-afectara-a-10000-comisiones-vecinales-dicen/). The reporting frequency of negative reports is unverified.

**Enforcement evidence.** The register's legal teeth (RUC blocking, bank KYC) are documented. I found no published fines on non-profits under Ley 6446 or Ley 7363 (unverified). SEPRELAD publishes an active/inactive status list that the press uses (https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2024/10/05/la-ong-presidida-por-esposa-de-leite-aparece-como-inactiva-ante-seprelad/). TEDIC counts more than 13 mandatory registries for NGOs (https://www.tedic.org/en/?p=27007).

**Pending changes.** Two unconstitutionality actions are pending. The first was filed 16 December 2025 by CODEHUPY for 33 organisations (https://eusee.hivos.org/alert/csos-file-action-of-unconstitutionality-against-anti-ngo-law/). The second was filed in July 2026, with about 50 organisations seeking suspension (https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2026/07/08/organizaciones-de-la-sociedad-civil-presentan-segunda-accion-contra-la-ley-anti-ong/). A June 2026 Senate bill would exempt comisiones vecinales from Ley 7363's demands; its study was postponed (https://www.ultimahora.com/cuestionan-que-cartismo-busque-flexibilizar-ley-garrote-para-comisiones-vecinales; https://www.ultimahora.com/postergan-ley-para-comisiones-vecinales).

## Buyers

- No official count of non-profits in SIARA, in the beneficial-owner register or at SEPRELAD was found (searched; unverified).
- A CEJ director said the bill would hit "more than 10,000 comisiones vecinales", plus school parent committees (https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2024/07/10/la-ley-mordaza-a-las-ong-afectara-a-10000-comisiones-vecinales-dicen/). Under the decree, most of these are covered only if they handle public funds.
- Funded segment: the 2025 budget sends over Gs. 56,000 million to NGOs (https://www.abc.com.py/economia/2025/01/16/presupuesto-destinara-a-las-ong-mas-de-g-56000-millones/). Congress lists of transfer recipients exist (https://bicameral.congreso.gov.py/index.php/download_file/25396/403). My estimate is a few hundred to about 2,000 professional NGOs and foundations with budgets and staff (unverified).
- The Episcopal Conference stresses a "broad and heterogeneous universe" of entities facing the SIARA burden (https://adn.celam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Paraguay-051-2026-Mtro-de-Economia-y-Finanzas.pdf, via search snippet). Church-linked schools and charities registered as separate associations may still be in scope (unverified).
- How they comply today: larger NGOs use their accountant or a law firm (Ferrere, Berkemeyer publish client alerts). Small bodies fill the portal themselves or do not comply (unverified; no survey found).

## Competition

- **Free state tools:** SIARA (siara.mef.gov.py) handles registration, the beneficial-owner filings and forms A-E (https://prensamercosur.org/2025/12/01/mitic-pone-en-produccion-el-sistema-siara-para-la-gestion-de-organizaciones-sin-fines-de-lucro/; https://www.legal500.com/guides/wp-json/wp/v2/comparative_guide/136640). It is free, so the filing itself is not a product. Art. 14 lets organisations ask to publish on the authority's portal "in justified cases" (https://impuestospy.com/impuestos/decreto-n-4806-2025/). This is a partial free substitute for the website duty.
- **Law and accounting firms:** Ferrere, Berkemeyer and similar firms advise on these duties (https://www.ferrere.com/en/news/recordatorio-clave-sobre-obligaciones-societarias-anuales/; https://www.legal500.com/guides/wp-json/wp/v2/comparative_guide/136640). No public prices were found.
- **Software:** a Spanish search found only Spanish-market NGO tools (GONG, Gestión Tercer Sector, about 24 products compared) with no Paraguay localisation (https://www.softwaredoit.es/software-ong-fundaciones/software-ong-fundaciones.html). No Paraguayan SaaS for forms A-E or the semiannual transparency page was found.
- **Free guidance:** the ICNL checklist (May 2025) covers general, tax, labour, municipal, customs, DNCP, AML and Ley 7363 duties (https://www.icnl.org/post/news/paraguay-non-profit-checklist). NGO networks such as CODEHUPY may give members free help (unverified).

## Willingness to pay

- Fines are large for a small NGO: about Gs. 5.9M-58.5M under Ley 6446 (see Duty). The Senate draft of Ley 7363 had fines of 100-1,000 minimum wages, but the final amounts are unverified (https://www.ultimahora.com/aplanadora-cartista-impone-ley-de-oeneges-pese-al-pedido-de-iglesia). The real threat is probably RUC blocking and losing bank access or state agreements (https://impuestospy.com/impuestos/decreto-n-5-871-2021/; https://impuestospy.com/impuestos/decreto-n-4806-2025/).
- Benchmarks: organisations already pay SEPRELAD's system about Gs. 400,000 a year (https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2024/07/10/la-ley-mordaza-a-las-ong-afectara-a-10000-comisiones-vecinales-dicen/). An employed accountant earns about Gs. 3.6M-10.9M a month (https://cazvid.com/es/blog/cuanto-gana-un-contador-en-paraguay, unverified). I found no published fee tables for accountants serving non-profits.
- Plausible price: Gs. 150,000-350,000 a month (about USD 20-45) per organisation for a hosted transparency page, calendar and form-prep templates. Accountants could pay a per-client rate (unverified; no market test). Volunteer comisiones and cooperadoras will pay close to nothing.
- Against paying: the NGO sector opposes the law politically and is filing lawsuits against it (https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2026/07/08/organizaciones-de-la-sociedad-civil-presentan-segunda-accion-contra-la-ley-anti-ong/). Some may spend on lawyers, not tools, or wait for the courts.

## Channels

- Accountants and law firms that already serve NGOs, as resellers. Ferrere and Berkemeyer show the topic is in demand (https://www.ferrere.com/en/news/recordatorio-clave-sobre-obligaciones-societarias-anuales/). The Colegio de Contadores is a possible partner (unverified).
- NGO networks: CODEHUPY (33-50 organisations in the lawsuits), Semillas para la Democracia, CIRD (https://eusee.hivos.org/alert/csos-file-action-of-unconstitutionality-against-anti-ngo-law/; https://www.ultimahora.com/pena-promulga-ley-garrote-contra-las-ong). Hostility to the law may make them prefer a "comply with minimum effort" tool.
- ICNL and donor programmes that fund compliance capacity (https://www.icnl.org/post/news/paraguay-non-profit-checklist).
- Public-fund recipients: Congress transfer lists name the funded NGOs, which gives a ready lead list (https://bicameral.congreso.gov.py/index.php/download_file/25396/403).
- Episcopal Conference: churches are excluded, but it lobbies for church-linked ESFL (https://adn.celam.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Paraguay-051-2026-Mtro-de-Economia-y-Finanzas.pdf, via snippet).
- Municipalities, for comisiones vecinales that receive transfers (unverified reach).

## Risks

- **Free state portal:** SIARA does the filing. The MEF could add a hosted transparency page, which Art. 14 already allows in justified cases (https://impuestospy.com/impuestos/decreto-n-4806-2025/).
- **Repeal or suspension:** two constitutional actions are pending, with requests to suspend the law (https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2026/07/08/organizaciones-de-la-sociedad-civil-presentan-segunda-accion-contra-la-ley-anti-ong/). The comisiones vecinales carve-out bill is pending (https://www.ultimahora.com/cuestionan-que-cartismo-busque-flexibilizar-ley-garrote-para-comisiones-vecinales).
- **Small, poor market:** the paying segment is probably hundreds to low thousands (unverified), with tight budgets.
- **Weak enforcement signal:** no published non-profit fines were found (unverified).
- **Liability:** forms are sworn statements (art. 8). A tool must not file on the user's behalf without care.
- **Electronic signature:** SIARA needs qualified e-signatures and identity profiles (https://www.legal500.com/guides/wp-json/wp/v2/comparative_guide/136640). Users must do this themselves, which limits automation.
- **Politics:** the law is labelled the "ley garrote" or "anti-ONG" law. Selling compliance can look like siding with it.

## First product

V1, "Transparencia OSFL":
1. A compliance calendar per organisation: the 30 June beneficial-owner update and forms A-E, end-of-July and end-of-January publications, the 15-day change notices, SEPRELAD status, and SIFEN onboarding. Reminders go by WhatsApp and email.
2. A hosted, Art. 14-compliant transparency page (subdomain or embeddable), with fund-use tables in HTML plus CSV/JSON open data, and the semester history kept.
3. Spreadsheet templates and a checklist that map the organisation's books to forms A-E, so the data can be pasted into SIARA.
4. A multi-client dashboard for accountants.

First 30 days:
- Days 1-7: get the real SIARA form fields from 3-5 NGOs or accountants who filed in June 2026. Confirm the final Ley 7363 sanction articles and any extension.
- Days 8-20: build the transparency-page generator (upload CSV, publish, archive) and the calendar.
- Days 21-30: pilot with 2 accounting firms and 5 funded NGOs taken from the Congress transfer list. Test a price of about Gs. 200,000 a month.
