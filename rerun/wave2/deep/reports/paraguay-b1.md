# Paraguay B1: AML compliance kit for vehicle importers, dealers and used-car lots (SEPRELAD Res 196/2020)

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 5/10.**

Under SEPRELAD Resolution 196 of 4 September 2020, every business that imports, buys, sells or consigns motor vehicles must run a full anti-money-laundering (AML/CFT) system. That means a manual, a code of ethics, a compliance officer, customer checks (KYC), suspicious-operation reports (ROS) or quarterly negative reports, an annual self-evaluation, an annual external audit and a risk assessment every two years ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-personas-fisicas-o-juridicas-involucrad/)).

SEPRELAD had 1,642 vehicle-sector businesses on its register, the second-largest non-financial group after real estate. Enforcement is real but not monetary: 704 warning notes and 329 temporary 60-day registry suspensions, and no fines on non-financial firms up to 2021 ([GAFILAT Mutual Evaluation 2022](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf), Tables 55 and 65). An unregistered obliged firm cannot use banks (same report, para. 445). That gives buyers a concrete reason to stay compliant.

I found no software built for this sector. Today's offers are law-firm advice, compliance-officer courses, a PEP database and SEPRELAD's free SIRO portal, which only takes filings. The case is held back by a small, partly informal market with no price evidence. Most of the value may come from extending the same kit to the other SEPRELAD-supervised sectors, which follow the same template: real estate (1,658 registered) and jewellers (162).

## Duty

- **Legal basis.** Law 1015/97, as amended by Law 3783/09, makes vehicle traders obliged subjects (Ley 3783/09 text: [familiar.com.py](https://www.familiar.com.py/documents/Ley_3783-09.pdf)). SEPRELAD Res 196 of 4 Sept 2020 is the sector rule, and it repealed Res 85/15 ([Ferrere, 28 Sep 2020](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-personas-fisicas-o-juridicas-involucrad/)). GAFILAT cites it as "Res. 196/2020", Art. 5 being the new-product risk assessment ([GAFILAT MER 2022](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf), CT184). I could not open the official text on seprelad.gov.py, so article numbers other than Art. 5 are (unverified).
- **Scope.** Firms and individuals whose main or commercial activity is importing, buying, selling or consigning motor vehicles. This covers distributors and used-car lots, and obligations remain even when tasks are delegated to intermediaries ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-personas-fisicas-o-juridicas-involucrad/)).
- **What must be kept or filed** (all from [Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-personas-fisicas-o-juridicas-involucrad/) unless noted):
  - An AML/CFT manual and a code of ethics. A trade association such as CADAM may adopt a collective code.
  - A compliance officer who is senior and independent. In a one-owner business the owner may hold the role.
  - A risk assessment at least every 2 years, with the methodology reviewed every 4 years, plus a risk assessment before launching new products or entering new areas.
  - KYC with risk profiles and monitoring. A simplified route applies to single payments up to 15 minimum wages (about USD 4,700 in 2020) and to instalment sales up to 20 minimum wages a year. The old USD 10,000 threshold is gone.
  - ROS filed with SEPRELAD, and a negative report whenever no ROS was filed in 3 months, so about 4 a year.
  - An annual internal evaluation report sent to SEPRELAD within 90 days of fiscal year end.
  - An external audit report sent within 180 days of fiscal year end. The auditor must be on SEPRELAD's register of specialised AML external auditors ([Ferrere, Jul 2024](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/la-serpelad-crea-un-modulo-de-consultas-para-verificar-el-catastro-de-sujetos-obligados-registrados-en-el-siro/)). For the vehicle sector, the first audits were due for fiscal 2021 ([GAFILAT MER](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf), para. 443).
  - An annual staff training programme ([Amaral via InfoNegocios, May 2026](https://infonegocios.com.py/default/capacitacion-en-prevencion-de-lavado-obligacion-legal-para-algunos-ventaja-estrategica-para-todos)).
  - Risk "information capture" forms. SEPRELAD approved forms for the vehicle sector in Res 243/2020, and it uses them to set how often and how hard it supervises each firm ([GAFILAT MER](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf), criterion 28). For real estate these forms are filed every six months (para. 441). The same six-month cycle for vehicles is likely but (unverified).
  - Registration with SEPRELAD. Under Res 375/2016, registration is done online and supporting documents follow within 10 days ([Ferrere](https://www.ferrere.com/en/news/paraguay-nuevo-procedimiento-de-registro-para-los-sujetos-no-regulados-ante-la-secretaria-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-dinero/)). Firms that are not registered cannot access financial institutions, and banks must check registration ([GAFILAT MER](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf), para. 445).
- **Filing channel.** In Feb-Mar 2023 the vehicle sector was moved onto SEPRELAD's SIRO system for ROS and negative reports ([Ferrere, Apr 2023](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/seprelad-amplia-la-implementacion-del-sistema-de-reporte-de-operaciones-sospechosas-para-varias-categorias-de-sujetos-obligados/)). In July 2024 SEPRELAD added a public lookup of registered obliged firms and auditors ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/la-serpelad-crea-un-modulo-de-consultas-para-verificar-el-catastro-de-sujetos-obligados-registrados-en-el-siro/)). SEPRELAD's homepage announced SIRO training in October 2026 ([seprelad.gov.py](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/)).
- **Penalties.** The sanction scale under Law 1015/97 as summarised in 2021:
  - Firms: a warning, a fine of up to 5,000 minimum wages (about USD 1.6m), a fine of up to 50% of the operation, licence suspension or closure for up to a year, or revocation.
  - Individuals: a fine of up to 500 minimum wages, or removal from office with a 3-10 year ban.
  - Source: [Ferrere, Oct 2021](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/inspecciones-aleatorias-de-seprelad-bajo-resolucion-36-21/). The scale may have changed under later amendments (unverified).
- **Real enforcement.**
  - For vehicle firms, 2015-2021 brought 704 warning notes and 329 temporary 60-day registry suspensions ([GAFILAT MER](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf), Table 65). GAFILAT found no monetary sanctions on non-financial obliged firms (para. 597) and urged Paraguay to apply them (para. 601).
  - On-site inspections of the vehicle sector numbered 6, 0, 4, 20, 10 and 0 in 2016-2021 (Table 63). There were 38 observations in 2021, all still pending (Table 66).
  - Since 2021, SEPRELAD has run random inspections of obliged firms, vehicle sellers included, asking for the manual, the compliance officer appointment and training records ([Ferrere, Res 36/21](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/inspecciones-aleatorias-de-seprelad-bajo-resolucion-36-21/)).
  - I found no fine against a dealer for 2022-2026 (unverified either way).
- **Recent changes.** I found no repeal or replacement of Res 196/2020. The searches turned up no 2024-2026 resolution for the vehicle sector. A new SEPRELAD head took office ([Última Hora](https://www.ultimahora.com/alcaraz-asumio-en-seprelad-y-pondero-fortalecimiento)); the date shown in search results was unreliable (unverified). Paraguay's next GAFILAT evaluation round would push for monetary sanctions, which would help this idea (unverified, inference).

## Buyers

- **Count.** SEPRELAD's register held **1,642** vehicle-sector obliged subjects (importers, dealers, consignors). That compares with 1,658 real-estate firms, 162 jewellers, 27 pawnshops and 3,529 registered firms in all ([GAFILAT MER 2022](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf), Table 55).
- **Older counts.** In 2019 SEPRELAD counted 1,142 used-car lots, with fewer than half filing ROS ([Última Hora](https://www.ultimahora.com/seprelad-el-50-playa-autos-eluden-el-control-antilavado-n2852685.html)). Ferrere put the number of lot owners at "more than 700", plus distributors of more than 90 new-car brands in CADAM ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-personas-fisicas-o-juridicas-involucrad/)).
- **Segments.**
  - New-car distributors (CADAM members). They are larger and more formal, and likely already advised by law firms.
  - Used-vehicle importers and lots. The used-car importers' association CIVU has only about 110 member lots out of "1,000 and something" ([Última Hora](https://www.ultimahora.com/seprelad-el-50-playa-autos-eluden-el-control-antilavado-n2852685.html)).
  - Paraguay is a leading importer of used cars ([ABC](https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/paraguay-lider-en-importar-vehiculos-usados-1755720.html)).
  - Most of these firms are small and owner-run.
- **How firms comply today.**
  - Reporting is thin. The sector filed only 159 ROS in 2017-2021 ([GAFILAT MER](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf), Table 12), and only 27 external or internal audits reached SEPRELAD for 2021 (Table 49).
  - Compliance seems to be paper-based, with the owner as compliance officer, help from accountants or law firms, and external auditors for the audit (unverified, inference). SEPRELAD trained the sector before the 2021 GAFILAT visit (para. 356).

## Competition

- **SEPRELAD SIRO (free, state).** It takes ROS and negative reports ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/seprelad-amplia-la-implementacion-del-sistema-de-reporte-de-operaciones-sospechosas-para-varias-categorias-de-sujetos-obligados/)) and offers a lookup of registered firms and auditors. I saw no sign that it produces a manual, risk matrix, annual report or KYC file, and the homepage lists no templates ([seprelad.gov.py](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/)). The state tool covers filing only, not the whole job.
- **Compliance Paraguay.** A local consultancy selling a database of more than 9,000 PEPs that names car lots as a target sector. No price was published, and the article does not mention manuals or reports ([La Nación, Aug 2024](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2024/08/13/consultora-presenta-herramienta-que-identifica-a-personas-expuestas-politicamente/)).
- **Law firms.**
  - Ferrere publishes guidance on Res 196 and advises clients ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-personas-fisicas-o-juridicas-involucrad/)).
  - Amaral markets AML training to obliged firms, car dealers included ([InfoNegocios](https://infonegocios.com.py/default/capacitacion-en-prevencion-de-lavado-obligacion-legal-para-algunos-ventaja-estrategica-para-todos)).
  - Neither publishes prices.
- **Training.** FOTRIEM/BNF runs a compliance-officer certification approved by SEPRELAD Res 889/21 ([FOTRIEM](https://www.fotriem.edu.py/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Ref.-17-BNF-Programa-Certificacion-en-Prevencion-de-Lavado-de-Activos-en-Paraguay-Formacion-de-Oficial-de-Cumplimiento.pdf)). The price is not shown.
- **SEPRELAD-registered external auditors.** They do the mandatory audit, and many probably also sell manuals and gap fixes (unverified).
- **Global screening APIs.** Didit and TheKYB cover PEP and sanctions checks only ([Didit](https://didit.me/es/blog/aml-screening-api-paraguay-53831/), [TheKYB](https://thekyb.com/our-data/paraguay/), as noted in the earlier check).
- **Vertical software.** The searches found no product built for Res 196 dealers. None of the 20 searches surfaced a Paraguayan AML-workflow SaaS for small obliged firms. Dealer-management systems might add a KYC module (unverified).

## Willingness to pay

- **What firms pay today.** I found no published price for a Res 196 manual, outsourced compliance officer, external audit or PEP database (unverified). Typical small-firm AML packages in the region run a few hundred USD per manual plus an annual audit fee (unverified, not Paraguay-specific).
- **What they risk.**
  - **Registry suspension.** The real penalty so far is a 60-day suspension from SEPRELAD's register (329 cases), and registration is a condition for bank access ([GAFILAT MER](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf), Table 65, para. 445). For a car lot, losing banking for 60 days is serious, and that is the selling point.
  - **Statutory fines.** These reach thousands of minimum wages but have not been used on this sector ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/inspecciones-aleatorias-de-seprelad-bajo-resolucion-36-21/)).
- **Staff time.** The owner-compliance officer must produce about 4 negative reports, 1 annual report, risk forms, training records and KYC files every year. A guided tool could cut this to a few hours a year (inference).
- **Plausible price.** About USD 150-300 to set up (manual, code of ethics, risk matrix), plus USD 15-30 a month for the KYC register, calendar and report drafts (unverified estimate). An upsell is a bundle with a partner auditor at about USD 500-1,000 a year (unverified).

## Channels

- **Associations.**
  - CIVU (about 110 member lots), which says it has worked with SEPRELAD for years ([Última Hora](https://www.ultimahora.com/seprelad-el-50-playa-autos-eluden-el-control-antilavado-n2852685.html)).
  - Civemup, a used vehicles and machinery chamber, which backs SEPRELAD but does not police its members (same source).
  - CADAM, the new-car distributors' chamber, which may adopt a collective code of ethics ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-personas-fisicas-o-juridicas-involucrad/), [La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2020/06/24/cadam-cumple-55-anos-de-vida-institucional/)).
- **SEPRELAD-registered external auditors** are a natural reseller or referral channel. They need clients with clean files, and the public lookup lists them ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/la-serpelad-crea-un-modulo-de-consultas-para-verificar-el-catastro-de-sujetos-obligados-registrados-en-el-siro/)).
- **Training.** SEPRELAD's own SIRO and sector trainings ([seprelad.gov.py](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/)) and the FOTRIEM/BNF certification cohorts.
- **Direct outreach.** The SIRO public lookup and municipal commercial licences can be used to build a lead list (inference). Banks that must verify registration could refer clients (unverified).

## Risks

- **Small and informal market.** About 1,642 registered firms, with perhaps a few hundred willing to pay. Only 27 audits were filed for 2021 ([GAFILAT MER](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf)).
- **Weak monetary enforcement.** Warnings are the norm and no fines have been seen. Firms may do the minimum.
- **Bundling by consultants or auditors.** Auditors and law firms could bundle a manual for free.
- **Regulator-built tools.** SEPRELAD could add model forms to SIRO. So far it covers filing only.
- **Rule change.** A new vehicle rule, or an amended sanctions law, would force content updates. No repeal has been found.
- **Liability.** A template manual could fail an inspection. Disclaimers and review by a SEPRELAD-registered auditor partner are needed.
- **Language and licensing.** Spanish only, and no licence is needed for software. Acting as an outsourced compliance officer may be limited by the rule that the officer be senior and in-house (unverified).

## First product

- **Version 1 (Spanish, web, mobile-friendly):**
  - An onboarding wizard that writes a Res 196 manual, code of ethics and compliance-officer appointment letter for the firm.
  - A risk self-assessment matrix using SEPRELAD's factors: operation volume, cash share, number of clients, geography ([GAFILAT MER](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf), para. 555).
  - A KYC register per sale, with the simplified route under 15 or 20 minimum wages, PEP and UN/OFAC list checks, and red-flag prompts.
  - A deadline calendar: quarterly negative reports, the 90-day annual evaluation, the 180-day external audit, risk forms, the 2-year risk review and annual training.
  - A draft annual evaluation report and an audit-ready evidence folder.
- **First 30 days:**
  - Get the official texts of Res 196/2020 and 243/2020 and the SIRO forms.
  - Build the manual and report generators and the calendar.
  - Interview 10 lots plus 2-3 registered auditors on prices.
  - Pilot with CIVU members.
  - Plan to reuse the engine for real estate (Res 201/2020) and jewellers (Res 222/2020).
