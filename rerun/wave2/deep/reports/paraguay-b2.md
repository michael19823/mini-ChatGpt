# Paraguay B2: SEPRELAD compliance pack for real-estate agencies and other subjects with no natural supervisor

Research date: 9 October 2026. All figures marked "SEPRELAD stats portal" come from the official public query tool at https://www.seprelad.gov.py/siro/estadisticaExterna/estadistica.xhtml. I ran the queries on 9 October 2026, by year and by sector.

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 5/10.**

The duty is real, recurring and enforced. SEPRELAD Res 201/2020 requires every regular buyer or seller of property to have a compliance officer, a manual, a code of ethics, a risk assessment every two years, KYC files, an annual internal evaluation, an annual external audit, and a negative report for each quarter with no suspicious-transaction report (ROS) ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-empresas-y-personas-involucradas-en-la/)). SEPRELAD's own statistics show the buyer base and the compliance gap. In 2025, 1,451 real-estate firms paid SEPRELAD's annual fee. Real-estate filers sent 7,474 negative reports but only 492 external-audit reports and 683 internal-evaluation reports. In 2024 SEPRELAD issued 1,238 formal warnings to real-estate firms ([SEPRELAD stats portal](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/siro/estadisticaExterna/estadistica.xhtml)). The free SIRO portal only receives filings. I found no local software that runs the KYC, risk-matrix, manual and annual-report workflow. The weak points are a small market (about 1,450 real-estate firms, plus about 850 car dealers and about 140 smaller subjects), low price anchors (SEPRELAD's own annual fee is about US$45), and enforcement that relies on warnings rather than fines. A niche business is possible: about US$100k-250k a year at good penetration, best sold together with the registered external auditors.

## Duty

**Legal basis**
- **Ley 1015/97** is the anti-money-laundering (AML) law. It has been amended by Leyes 3783/09, 6497/19, 6797/21 and 6960/22 ([academic summary via search](https://www.pj.gov.py/notas/22633-instituto-de-investigaciones-juridicas-actualiza-obra-de-lavado-de-activos)). Ley 6960/2022 amends arts. 13, 25 and 28 (same source). I did not read the consolidated text (unverified).
- **SEPRELAD supervises** the subjects that have no other supervisor: real-estate agents, dealers in precious metals and stones, lawyers, accountants, vehicle importers and dealers, non-profits, and virtual-asset providers. It also supervises remitters, pawn shops, cash-in-transit firms and safe-deposit firms ([GAFILAT Mutual Evaluation Report 2022, paras. 104-105](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf)).
- **Res 201/2020 (14 Sep 2020)** is the real-estate rulebook. It covers agencies, agents, brokers, commission agents and developers, and it repealed Res 264/07 and 266/07 ([Ferrere ES](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-empresas-y-personas-involucradas-en-la/); [Ferrere EN](https://ferrere.com/en/news/new-regulations-for-the-prevention-of-asset-laundering-and-financing-of-terrorism-for-companies-and-individuals-involved-in-the/)).
- **Related resolutions:**
  - Res 240/2020 sets the real-estate risk matrix.
  - Res 241/2020 sets the information-capture forms.
  - Res 239/2020 sets SEPRELAD's risk-based supervision method.
  - Res 285/14 sets the off-site and on-site inspection forms.

  All are cited in the [GAFILAT MER 2022, criteria 28.4-28.5](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf).
- **Other sector rulebooks:** Res 196/2020 (vehicle dealers), Res 222/2020 (jewels and precious metals), Res 265/07 and 267/07 (pawn shops), Res 208/14 (cash-in-transit), Res 220/14 (safe-deposit boxes) and Res 314/2021 (virtual-asset providers) (GAFILAT MER 2022, Table 55).

**What a real-estate firm must do under Res 201/2020** ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-empresas-y-personas-involucradas-en-la/))
- Register with SEPRELAD as a subject with no natural supervisor. This costs a registration fee of about Gs 322,000. SEPRELAD collected Gs 104.4m from 324 new real-estate registrants in 2025 ([SEPRELAD stats portal](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/siro/estadisticaExterna/estadistica.xhtml)).
- Pay an annual fee (canon). In 2025 1,451 real-estate firms paid Gs 476.7m in total, about Gs 329,000 (about US$45) each (same portal).
- Appoint a compliance officer at manager or director level. In a one-owner firm the owner can hold the role.
- Write an AML manual and a separate code of ethics.
- Assess risk at least every 2 years and check the method every 4 years. The assessment must cover four factors: clients, products, channels and geography. A new assessment is also needed before launching new products or entering new areas.
- Do KYC: identify the client and the beneficial owner, understand the purpose of the deal, build a profile and check that operations fit it (Art. 18, per [GAFILAT MER 2022, c.10](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf)). Simplified KYC is allowed below 150 minimum wages paid at once or 20 minimum wages a year in instalments. The minimum wage has been Gs 2,899,048 a month since 1 July 2025, under Decreto 4122/2025 ([impuestospy](https://impuestospy.com/impuestos/decreto-n-4122-2025/)). That puts the thresholds at about Gs 435m and Gs 58m.
- Screen clients against the UN and national freeze lists, and freeze assets on a match.
- Keep records for at least 5 years ([GAFILAT MER 2022, para. 461](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf)).
- File ROS through SIRO. File a negative report for each 3-month period with no ROS. SIRO replaced ROS-WEB in 2023 ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/seprelad-amplia-la-implementacion-del-sistema-de-reporte-de-operaciones-sospechosas-para-varias-categorias-de-sujetos-obligado-1/)). SIRO has been the only channel for all reports since 2024 ([SEPRELAD stats portal](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/siro/estadisticaExterna/estadistica.xhtml)).
- Send an annual internal evaluation to SEPRELAD within 90 days of year-end.
- Send an external audit report within 180 days of year-end. The auditor must be on SEPRELAD's public register of external auditors ([abogados.com.ar, 2021](https://abogados.com.ar/prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo/28532)).
- Send periodic objective "Reportes de Operaciones" (monthly or quarterly depending on the sector). Real-estate firms filed 3,385 of them in 2025 and 1,466 by October 2026 ([SEPRELAD stats portal](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/siro/estadisticaExterna/estadistica.xhtml)). The resolution that sets this report, and its threshold, are (unverified).
- Fill in SEPRELAD's information-capture forms (operation types, number of clients, volume, payment mode, geography), which feed its risk matrix. GAFILAT describes these as semi-annual ([MER 2022, para. 441](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf)). Whether they are still separate from SIRO today is (unverified).

**Penalties** (Ley 1015/97 arts. 24-25, per [GAFILAT MER 2022, c.28.4](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf) and [Ferrere on Res 36/21](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/inspecciones-aleatorias-de-seprelad-bajo-resolucion-36-21/))
- **Companies:** a warning note, a public reprimand, a fine of up to 5,000 minimum wages (about Gs 14.5bn), a fine of up to 50% of the transaction, suspension or closure for up to a year, or revocation.
- **Individuals, including directors and staff:** a fine of up to 500 minimum wages or 1-10% of the transaction, and removal from office with a 3-10 year ban.

**Enforcement evidence**
- **Random inspections:** SEPRELAD has run them since Q3 2021 under Res 36/21. Inspectors ask for the manual, proof of the compliance officer and proof of training ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/inspecciones-aleatorias-de-seprelad-bajo-resolucion-36-21/)).
- **Before 2022:** SEPRELAD made 125 on-site and 622 off-site inspections across its sectors from 2015. Real estate had 2 to 25 on-site inspections a year. It issued 421 observations, and real estate received 100 of them in 2019. GAFILAT found that "no economic sanctions have been applied to real-estate firms" and called the sanctions regime not dissuasive ([MER 2022, paras. 581, 599-602](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf)).
- **2024:** SEPRELAD issued **1,238 warning notes (notas de apercibimiento) to real-estate firms** and 456 to car dealers. All are listed as final ("vigente/ejecutoriada") ([SEPRELAD stats portal](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/siro/estadisticaExterna/estadistica.xhtml)). The breaches behind them are (unverified). Their volume suggests a mass action over missing filings.
- **2025:** only 1 sanction in all sectors, a fine on a car dealer that is under appeal. **2026 to date:** none (same portal).
- **Audit gap:** real-estate audit filings were only 30-39 a year in 2015-2021 (MER Table 49). Through SIRO they rose to 589 external and 790 internal in 2024, and 492 external and 683 internal in 2025. That is still about one-third of the 1,451 fee-paying firms (SEPRELAD stats portal).

**How they comply today**
- Firms file through free SIRO.
- The owner usually acts as compliance officer, which the rule allows.
- Firms buy the external audit from a SEPRELAD-registered auditor.
- They use SEPRELAD's published forms and risk matrix ([MER paras. 441, 580](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf)).
- GAFILAT saw real-estate firms keep supporting documents on the source of funds ([MER para. 461](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf)).

## Competition

- **SEPRELAD SIRO (free):**
  - Covers registration, ROS, negative reports, operation reports, and upload of audit and internal-evaluation reports ([SEPRELAD](https://www.seprelad.gov.py); [Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/seprelad-amplia-la-implementacion-del-sistema-de-reporte-de-operaciones-sospechosas-para-varias-categorias-de-sujetos-obligado-1/)).
  - Also offers a public lookup of registered subjects by RUC ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/la-serpelad-crea-un-modulo-de-consultas-para-verificar-el-catastro-de-sujetos-obligados-registrados-en-el-siro/)).
  - Does not keep KYC files, screen clients, build the manual or produce the internal evaluation.
  - SEPRELAD has also published free capture forms and a risk matrix for real estate ([MER para. 580](https://www.pj.gov.py/descargar/ID1-148_informe_de_evaluacion_mutua_de_paraguay_2022.pdf)). This partly covers the risk-assessment step for free.
- **Compliance Paraguay (Gregorio Mayor):** a database of about 9,000 politically exposed persons (PEPs), sold to banks, finance firms, real-estate firms, exchange houses, car dealers and cash carriers. Price not published ([La Nación, Aug 2024](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2024/08/13/consultora-presenta-herramienta-que-identifica-a-personas-expuestas-politicamente/)). It is a possible partner or a feature competitor.
- **Law and accounting firms:** Ferrere and others advise on these rules ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/nuevo-reglamento-de-prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo-para-empresas-y-personas-involucradas-en-la/)). SEPRELAD-registered external auditors sell the yearly audit ([abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/prevencion-de-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo/28532)). Fees are (unverified).
- **Training:** for example the BNF/Fotriem compliance-officer certification ([Fotriem PDF](https://www.fotriem.edu.py/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Ref.-17-BNF-Programa-Certificacion-en-Prevencion-de-Lavado-de-Activos-en-Paraguay-Formacion-de-Oficial-de-Cumplimiento.pdf)).
- **Local software:** six Spanish-language searches across the two earlier checks and this one found no Paraguayan SaaS for small SEPRELAD subjects.
- **Regional vendors:** AML vendors in Chile, Colombia and Mexico that sell to non-financial businesses (DNFBPs) were not checked by name (unverified).
- **Real-estate tools:** ACIP's official tool is Place Analyzer, an appraisal and analytics tool, not AML. ACIP is also building an MLS with Grupo ITTI ([Infonegocios](https://infonegocios.com.py/default/acip-y-place-analyzer-se-unen-para-digitalizar-el-mercado-inmobiliario-permitira-acceder-en-tiempo-real-a-la-oferta-del-sector)). Either could add compliance modules later.

## Willingness to pay

**Price anchors are low**
- SEPRELAD's annual canon is about Gs 329,000 (about US$45) per real-estate firm ([SEPRELAD stats portal](https://www.seprelad.gov.py/siro/estadisticaExterna/estadistica.xhtml)).
- Most firms are small, and many are run by their owners.

**Costs firms already carry**
- A mandatory annual external audit by a registered auditor. Its price is (unverified), probably a few hundred to a few thousand US$.
- Owner or staff time on quarterly negative reports, operation reports, KYC files and the two annual reports.

**Fear factor**
- The legal maximums are very large: up to 5,000 minimum wages, about Gs 14.5bn.
- In practice the real-estate sector has received warnings, not fines. There were 1,238 warnings in 2024, and only 1 fine in all SEPRELAD sectors in 2025 (same portal).
- A warning still creates a record that can lead to a fine on a repeat breach (unverified).

**Plausible pricing**
- About US$25-60 a month (Gs 200k-450k) per firm for software.
- About US$300-600 a year for a "compliance pack": templates, risk matrix, annual internal-evaluation report and calendar.
- A bundled price with a partner auditor is the most credible way to win budget.

**Rough size (my estimate)**
- Real estate: 1,450 firms × US$400 = about US$580k a year in theory.
- Car dealers: 845 firms × US$300 = about US$250k.
- Reaching 15-30% of both gives about US$120k-250k a year.

## Channels

- **SEPRELAD-registered external auditors:** the best channel. Every compliant firm must hire one each year. Auditors want clients whose files are audit-ready. The register itself is (unverified as public online).
- **ACIP (Asociación de Corredores Inmobiliarios del Paraguay):** about 90 agencies and more than 1,000 agents. It runs training, a yearly Agent Day (12 August) and the MLS project. President Daniel Ortiz Suárez ([Infonegocios](https://infonegocios.com.py/default/mercado-inmobiliario-en-transformacion-acip-impulsa-ley-de-corretaje-y-modernizacion-digital-en-el-sector)). Res 201/2020 lets trade associations adopt a collective code of ethics ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/en/news/new-regulations-for-the-prevention-of-asset-laundering-and-financing-of-terrorism-for-companies-and-individuals-involved-in-the/)). That is a natural hook for an association deal.
- **CAPADEI (developers' chamber):** cited on sector trends ([La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2021/03/03/sector-inmobiliario-fue-el-que-mas-avanzo-en-la-pandemia-dice-capadei/)). No AML link found.
- **SEPRELAD's own outreach:** SIRO training sessions, for example "SEPRELAD realizará capacitación sobre el uso del SIRO", 7 Oct 2026 ([SEPRELAD](https://www.seprelad.gov.py)). Also its public RUC lookup of registered subjects, which works as a lead list if used lawfully ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/la-serpelad-crea-un-modulo-de-consultas-para-verificar-el-catastro-de-sujetos-obligados-registrados-en-el-siro/)).
- **Accountants:** the firms that already keep the books of small agencies, and compliance-officer training providers such as Fotriem.
- **Car-dealer associations:** for a second vertical (names unverified).

## Risks

- **SEPRELAD extends SIRO or its free forms.** It already publishes a risk matrix and capture forms, and it collects every report in SIRO. A free KYC or risk module from the state would remove much of the value. It uses donated SAS analytics on its own side ([Hoy, May 2024](https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2024/05/31/seprelad-cuenta-con-software-actualizado-gracias-a-donacion-de-eeuu)).
- **Weak fines.** Enforcement is mostly warnings. Many firms may accept the risk rather than pay.
- **Small market.** About 1,450 real-estate firms. The pack only reaches a sustainable size if car dealers and jewellers are added. Each sector has its own resolution, so each needs its own templates.
- **Rule churn.** Five-plus years after Res 201/2020, a new real-estate rule or a GAFILAT-driven update is likely (unverified). Templates would then need rework.
- **Liability.** If a client is sanctioned after relying on a template, the vendor faces blame. Position the product as a tool, with an auditor or lawyer review.
- **Language and licensing.** Spanish only, which is fine. No vendor licence appears to be needed (unverified).
- **Distribution cost.** Small, owner-run agencies are expensive to reach one by one. The auditor and association channels matter.

## First product

**Version 1, for real-estate firms under Res 201/2020**
1. **Obligation calendar and reminders:**
   - The quarterly negative report (when no ROS was filed).
   - Periodic operation reports.
   - The internal evaluation due 90 days after year-end.
   - The external audit due 180 days after year-end.
   - The 2-yearly risk assessment.
   - The annual canon.
2. **Client and deal register with KYC:**
   - A form per client and beneficial owner, with document upload.
   - Automatic simplified or full KYC based on the 150 / 20 minimum-wage thresholds at the current minimum wage.
   - Records kept for 5 years.
3. **Screening:** check each client against the UN consolidated list, the SEPRELAD or national freeze lists and a PEP list (licence Compliance Paraguay's data or build from public sources). Log each check as audit evidence.
4. **Risk-matrix wizard:** the four factors (client, product, channel, geography), aligned to SEPRELAD's Res 240/2020 matrix and Res 241/2020 forms, giving a printable risk-assessment document.
5. **Document generator:** the AML manual, the code of ethics, the compliance-officer appointment letter and the training log.
6. **Annual internal-evaluation report generator,** plus an "audit-ready" export folder for the external auditor.
7. **SIRO helper:** checklists and pre-filled data for the negative and operation reports. No direct integration unless SEPRELAD allows it.

**First 30 days**
- **Week 1:**
  - Get the full texts of Res 201/2020, 240/2020 and 241/2020, the SIRO operation-report rule and the auditor-register rule.
  - Interview 8-10 agencies (through ACIP) and 3 registered auditors.
  - Learn which warnings were issued in 2024 and why.
- **Weeks 2-3:**
  - Build the calendar, the client/KYC register with threshold logic, UN-list screening and the manual and code-of-ethics templates.
  - Use the Spanish UI with a guaraní-based threshold calculator.
- **Week 4:**
  - Build the internal-evaluation report template and the auditor export.
  - Pilot with 5 agencies through one partner auditor at about Gs 250k a month.
  - Test whether ACIP would endorse it or adopt a collective code.
