# Peru B1: Casino, slot-room and betting SPLAFT-in-a-box (Res. SBS 01015-2026)

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 4/10.**

The duty is real and got stricter in April 2026. Res. SBS 01015-2026 rewrites the anti-money-laundering (SPLAFT) rules for casino and slot-machine operators. It adds supplier due diligence, a 30-day induction for new staff, a tougher annual compliance-officer report (IAOC), an internal audit report, and puts promotional prize winners into the operations register ([PRCP](https://prcp-r2-prd.postedin.com/FT-y-FP-incorporadas-por-la-Resolución-SBS-N°-01015-2026.pdf)). I found no packaged SPLAFT software aimed at Peruvian gaming. Law firms and consultants serve the sector instead. Three problems keep the score low. The market is small: about 714 slot rooms and 19 casinos, run by an estimated 325 firms. The regime is not new, so operators already have compliance officers and manuals. And enforcement is mild: the top fine is about 8 UIT (S/ 44,000), and the last public SPLAFT action I found was 10 firms fined in 2022. Online operators (about 91 licences) are a separate segment that already buys international KYC tools. The idea works best as a cheap workflow tool sold through the consultants who act as outsourced compliance officers. It does not work well as a direct SaaS business.

## Duty

- **Legal basis.** Ley 27693 creates UIF-Perú and the list of reporting entities ([search summary of UIF legal framework](https://busquedas.elperuano.pe/dispositivo/NL/2375685-1)). Res. SBS 01015-2026 is dated 6 April 2026 and was published in El Peruano on 8 April 2026 ([El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/293028-nueva-norma-sobre-lavado-de-activos-para-casinos-y-apuestas-online-verifica-si-eres-sujeto-obligado); [lpderecho](https://lpderecho.pe/norma-prevenir-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo-casinos-tragamonedas-apuestas-deportivas-resolucion-sbs-01015-2026/)). It updates Res. SBS 1695-2016. It also amends the sanctions regulation, Res. SBS 5389-2013, so that it now covers remote gaming and remote sports betting too ([El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/293028-nueva-norma-sobre-lavado-de-activos-para-casinos-y-apuestas-online-verifica-si-eres-sujeto-obligado)).
- **Online operators.** These have their own AML rule, Res. SBS 03622-2025, dated 7 October 2025 and published 14 October 2025 ([lpderecho](https://lpderecho.pe/norma-prevenir-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo-juegos-distancia-apuestas-linea-resolucion-sbs-03622-2025/); [Focus Gaming News](https://focusgn.com/latinoamerica/la-sbs-de-peru-aprueba-norma-para-la-prevencion-de-la-ft-para-casas-de-apuestas-online)). One report says it has applied since 15 October 2025 (unverified). A 120-day adaptation period was proposed in the draft ([Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-sbs-propone-marco-legal-sobre-lavado-activos-para-plataformas-apuestas-deportivas-1021852.aspx)). Whether the final text kept it is unverified.
- **What must be done.** The SPLAFT must be risk-based and have 13 minimum components. These include policies and procedures, a compliance officer, due diligence, training, a manual, a code of conduct, audit, an operations register (RO), detection of unusual operations, suspicious-transaction reports (ROS), record keeping, and answering UIF requests ([El Peruano, Carlos Caro](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/293055-carlos-caro-nueva-norma-contra-lavado-de-activos-implica-menor-tolerancia-ante-fallas-de-cumplimiento)). Changes from 1695-2016 ([PRCP](https://prcp-r2-prd.postedin.com/FT-y-FP-incorporadas-por-la-Resolución-SBS-N°-01015-2026.pdf)):
  - Risk assessment now covers proliferation financing and treats geographic zone as a risk factor. It must be updated at least every three years ([El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/293055-carlos-caro-nueva-norma-contra-lavado-de-activos-implica-menor-tolerancia-ante-fallas-de-cumplimiento)).
  - New workers and directors must get an SPLAFT induction within 30 days, with written proof. Training must cover the lists and the UN asset-freeze mechanisms.
  - Client due diligence asks for more data: marital status, spouse, address, occupation, phone, email, purpose of the relationship, and who benefits from the operation. Enhanced due diligence applies to clients linked to people under AML investigation.
  - Worker and director due diligence must be checked for updates at least once a year.
  - Due diligence on suppliers is new. Their data must be refreshed at least every 2 years.
  - The RO must log every chip or ticket cash-out of US$ 2,500 or more, plus every promotional prize winner at any amount. The old prize-winner register (REGAP) is gone.
  - An Internal Audit Report (IAI) is required. It is written by internal audit, or by a manager other than the compliance officer. An external audit firm must also review the SPLAFT where the law already requires the firm to have one.
  - The IAOC must now include set content: the risk and due-diligence procedures, staff and training counts, the IDs of shareholders and managers, and the location of every room.
  - A corporate compliance officer for a group must work full-time on the role, with exceptions allowed. UIF-Perú may waive some requirements based on a firm's size and risk.
- **Timing.** ROS must be filed within 24 hours of an operation being judged suspicious, through ROSEL ([Focus Gaming News](https://focusgn.com/latinoamerica/peru-endurece-controles-sobre-casinos-y-apuestas-online-para-prevenir-el-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo)). RO records must be kept at least five years and made available to UIF-Perú and the regulator ([same](https://focusgn.com/latinoamerica/peru-endurece-controles-sobre-casinos-y-apuestas-online-para-prevenir-el-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo)). It looks like the RO is kept on file, not filed routinely (unverified). The IAOC goes to the supervisor and UIF-Perú every year ([SiGMA](https://sigma.world/es/news/sbs-norma-prevencion-lavado-activos-apuestas-peru/)). A 15 February deadline was reported in one search summary (unverified). Anonymous betting is abolished ([Focus Gaming News](https://focusgn.com/latinoamerica/peru-endurece-controles-sobre-casinos-y-apuestas-online-para-prevenir-el-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo)).
- **Effective date and adaptation window.** Not found in any source I could read (unverified). The full text in El Peruano was not reachable.
- **Penalties.** Infractions are graded minor, serious and very serious. The worst cases are failing to file a ROS, failing to check UN Security Council lists, and failing to freeze funds. These carry fines of up to 8 UIT ([PRCP](https://prcp-r2-prd.postedin.com/FT-y-FP-incorporadas-por-la-Resolución-SBS-N°-01015-2026.pdf); [Focus Gaming News](https://focusgn.com/latinoamerica/peru-endurece-controles-sobre-casinos-y-apuestas-online-para-prevenir-el-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo)). The 2026 UIT is S/ 5,500 under DS 301-2025-EF ([El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/285208-mef-establece-en-s-5-500-la-unidad-impositiva-tributaria-para-2026)), so the maximum is about S/ 44,000 (about US$ 12,800). The supervisor is MINCETUR's DGJCMT ([Focus Gaming News](https://focusgn.com/latinoamerica/peru-endurece-controles-sobre-casinos-y-apuestas-online-para-prevenir-el-lavado-de-activos-y-financiamiento-del-terrorismo)).
- **Enforcement.** From January to August 2022, MINCETUR made 1,346 inspection visits to more than 700 rooms. It fined 30 firms a total of S/ 690,000, and separately sanctioned 10 firms for not filing the IAOC ([Focus Gaming News, 7 Sep 2022](https://focusgn.com/latinoamerica/peru-multo-a-30-empresas-titulares-de-salas-de-juegos-de-azar)). Most of those fines were not about AML: they were for unauthorised machines, letting in banned people, and video retention. I found no AML sanctions from 2023 to 2026 (unverified). MINCETUR is active against illegal gaming. It closed clandestine rooms in 2026 ([Yogonet](https://www.yogonet.com/latinoamerica/noticias/2026/06/02/109170-el-mincetur-clausuro-seis-salas-de-apuestas-clandestinas-y-decomiso-69-maquinas-de-juego-en-loreto-y-cusco)) and blocked 50 unlicensed online platforms ([El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/305981-mincetur-bloquea-50-plataformas-de-juegos-a-distancia-y-apuestas-deportivas-que-operaban-sin-autorizacion)).

## Buyers

- **Land-based.** Peru has 714 slot rooms and 19 casinos with 72,823 machines ([Focus Gaming News, 2022](https://focusgn.com/latinoamerica/peru-multo-a-30-empresas-titulares-de-salas-de-juegos-de-azar)). A July 2026 statement from the vice-minister gave about the same figures (714 rooms, 19 casinos, about 72,500 machines), per the earlier check (unverified here). The reporting entity is the legal entity, not the room. The earlier challenge check cited an undated MINCETUR presentation to Congress, apparently from 2023, with about 325 licensed firms running 711 rooms ([Congreso PDF](https://www.congreso.gob.pe/Docs/comisiones2023/comercio/files/ppt_mincetur_congreso_-_ica_-_dgjcmt.pdf)). That PDF returned 404 for me, so the count is unverified. Many firms are in the provinces ([Focus Gaming News](https://focusgn.com/latinoamerica/peru-multo-a-30-empresas-titulares-de-salas-de-juegos-de-azar)). Chains with many rooms will have their own compliance staff.
- **Online.** About 91 remote gaming and betting licences had been granted by early 2026 ([El Búho, 1 Jul 2026](https://elbuho.pe/?p=428097)). This is a secondary source (unverified). Many are foreign groups.
- **How they comply today.** Every operator must already have a UIF-accredited compliance officer, designated through SISDEL ([SiGMA](https://sigma.world/es/news/sbs-norma-prevencion-lavado-activos-apuestas-peru/)). MINCETUR authorises compliance officers together with the UIF ([Congreso PDF via search](https://www.congreso.gob.pe/Docs/comisiones2023/comercio/files/ppt_mincetur_congreso_-_ica_-_dgjcmt.pdf)). Law firms such as PRCP and Caro & Asociados sell SPLAFT implementation ([PRCP](https://prcp-r2-prd.postedin.com/FT-y-FP-incorporadas-por-la-Resolución-SBS-N°-01015-2026.pdf); [El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/293055-carlos-caro-nueva-norma-contra-lavado-de-activos-implica-menor-tolerancia-ante-fallas-de-cumplimiento)). Small rooms probably use spreadsheets, paper ID logs and a part-time outside compliance officer (unverified).

## Competition

- **Dedicated Peru gaming SPLAFT software.** None found in about eight searches in Spanish and English. These covered gaming-specific SPLAFT software, casino-management systems with AML modules, list-screening vendors, and PLAFT software pricing.
- **Free state tools.** The SBS publishes "Listas de interés", which include online UN Security Council sanctions list checks, the PEP role list, and an update mail alert ([SBS listas](https://www.sbs.gob.pe/prevencion-de-lavado-activos/listas-de-interes), per search summary; the page would not load for me). ROSEL (for ROS) and SISDEL (for officer designation) are filing channels only. SUCTR monitors machines for MINCETUR and SUNAT and is not an AML tool. No state tool runs the full SPLAFT workflow: due diligence files, RO, training logs, IAOC drafting.
- **Consultants and law firms.** PRCP (Payet, Rey, Cauvi, Pérez) has a compliance team that markets 01015-2026 implementation ([PRCP](https://prcp-r2-prd.postedin.com/FT-y-FP-incorporadas-por-la-Resolución-SBS-N°-01015-2026.pdf)). Caro & Asociados comments publicly on the rule ([El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/293055-carlos-caro-nueva-norma-contra-lavado-de-activos-implica-menor-tolerancia-ante-fallas-de-cumplimiento)). Garrigues covers the sector ([Garrigues](https://www.garrigues.com/es_ES/noticia/peru-aprobada-norma-prevenir-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo-sector-juegos)). No prices found.
- **International KYC/AML vendors (online segment).** GBG says it was the first identity-verification provider registered under MINCETUR's rules ([GBG](https://www.gbg.com/en/blog/igaming-and-kyc-in-peru/)). KYCAID says it is an authorised KYC provider in Peru ([KYCAID](https://kycaid.com/blog/peru-vs-brazil-compliance-comparison/)). Online operators are therefore already served for KYC and screening.
- **Casino-management systems.** IGT Advantage, myACP and ED ROBERTS sell player tracking ([SoloAzar](https://soloazar.com/en/category/casino/igt-advantage-casino-management-system-to-modernize-the-gaming-experience-at-circus-circus); [SoloAzar](https://soloazar.com/en/category/casino/hellenic-casino-corfu-chooses-myacp-system)). I found no Peru deployment with an SPLAFT module (unverified). Large chains may log RO data in their own cage or CMS systems (unverified).
- **General LatAm AML SaaS.** Colombian SARLAFT tools and global screening vendors were not confirmed in Peru gaming (unverified).

## Willingness to pay

- I found no public prices for SPLAFT consultancy, outsourced compliance officers or PLAFT software in Peru.
- **Benchmarks.** Peruvian SME payroll software costs about S/ 75 to S/ 280 a month ([Talana](https://web.talana.com/es-pe/blog/sistema-de-planillas-cuanto-cuesta-para-una-pyme)). Outsourced bookkeeping for a micro firm is about S/ 1,050 a month ([ByB Consultores](https://bybconsultores.pe/servicios/outsourcing-contable-y-tributario/)).
- **Downside avoided.** The maximum fine is about S/ 44,000 per very serious infraction. Ordinary failures, such as a late IAOC or a missing induction record, are lower-tier (amounts unverified). A licence-dependent business may also fear that AML findings will hurt its MINCETUR standing (unverified).
- **Plausible price.** About S/ 150 to S/ 400 a month (about US$ 40 to US$ 110) per single-room operator. About S/ 800 to S/ 1,500 a month for a consultant licence covering 10 to 30 client firms (unverified, an estimate). Small operators already pay a part-time compliance officer and a yearly external review, so software has to cut that cost or make the officer's time go further.

- **Peru Gaming Show.** 17-18 June 2026 at the Jockey exhibition centre in Lima, with more than 50 exhibitors, the vice-minister of tourism and the regulator attending ([Yogonet](https://www.yogonet.com/international/news/2026/06/01/122462-peru-gaming-show-2026-confirms-conference-agenda-and-participation-of-more-than-50-brands-in-lima)).
- **Industry bodies.** SONAJA (president Fernando Calderón Castro) and the Association of Online Sports Betting (president Gonzalo Rosell) both spoke at PGS 2026 ([Yogonet](https://www.yogonet.com/international/news/2026/06/01/122462-peru-gaming-show-2026-confirms-conference-agenda-and-participation-of-more-than-50-brands-in-lima)). SONAJA's full name and membership are unverified. APADELA is a sports-betting association ([ProActivo](https://proactivo.com.pe/la-feria-peruana-del-juego-retorna-en-junio/)).
- **Trade press.** Focus Gaming News, Yogonet, SoloAzar and SiGMA all cover Peruvian regulation closely, so sponsored content or webinars there would reach operators.
- **Registers.** MINCETUR's DGJCMT keeps a public register of authorised rooms and online licences ([El Búho](https://elbuho.pe/?p=428097)). That gives a full prospect list.
- **Outsourced compliance officers and law firms.** This is the most efficient channel. One officer serves many small firms (unverified).

## Risks

- **Small, shrinking-margin market.** About 325 firms, many of them small and in the provinces, plus about 91 online licences. Total revenue at S/ 200 a month even with 100 customers is about S/ 240,000 a year (about US$ 65,000).
- **Not a new duty.** Operators already have manuals and officers under 1695-2016. The rewrite causes a one-time update, mostly sold by law firms.
- **Weak enforcement.** The latest public SPLAFT sanction I found is 10 firms in 2022 ([Focus Gaming News](https://focusgn.com/latinoamerica/peru-multo-a-30-empresas-titulares-de-salas-de-juegos-de-azar)). Fines are capped at about 8 UIT.
- **Unknown adaptation deadline** (unverified). If the deadline is long or already past, the urgency fades.
- **Online segment taken.** GBG, KYCAID and similar vendors already serve it.
- **Reputation and liability.** The tool sits in a money-laundering-exposed sector. A screening miss could be blamed on the vendor. Contracts need clear limits.
- **State tooling.** The SBS could extend the free list-check tools. A full workflow tool from the state is unlikely (unverified).

## First product

A Spanish-language web app, "SPLAFT Sala", for single-room and small-chain operators and their outsourced compliance officers. Version one:

1. **RO register.** Log cash-outs of US$ 2,500 or more and all promo prize winners, with the new client fields (ID, address, occupation, spouse, purpose, beneficiary). Export to Excel/PDF and keep 5-year retention.
2. **List screening.** Check clients, workers, directors and suppliers against the SBS-published UN lists and the PEP role list. Log every check with a timestamp as evidence.
3. **Due diligence files with reminders.** Workers and directors yearly, suppliers every 2 years, risk assessment every 3 years.
4. **Training log.** 30-day induction tracker for new hires, annual training attendance, and counts ready for the IAOC.
5. **IAOC and IAI generator.** Pre-fill the mandatory IAOC sections from the data above, including room locations and shareholder IDs.
6. **Consultant mode.** One login that manages many client firms.

**First 30 days.** Get the full text of 01015-2026 and 03622-2025 from El Peruano and map each article to a feature. Interview 10 outsourced compliance officers and 5 provincial room owners. Build items 1-3 plus a basic IAOC template. Pilot free with 3 compliance officers who each serve several rooms. Exhibit at or contact the PGS and SONAJA network.
