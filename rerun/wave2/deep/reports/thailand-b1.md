# Thailand B1: Work-Permit Renewal Desk for Small Employers of Lao, Myanmar and Vietnamese Workers (e-WorkPermit)

## Re-assessment (owner's criteria)

**Verdict: maybe. New score: 6/10 (old score: 4/10).**

**The case.** Under the owner's criteria, the free portal is not the obstacle it seemed. e-WorkPermit is slow, buggy and backlogged. Middlemen sell queue slots, and documents can sit for months ([Naewna](https://www.naewna.com/local/972023); [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/668193)). The best buyer is not the small employer but the multi-client filer. That means the 17,619 registered proxy filers and 331 licensed import companies, who juggle many employers, cohorts and deadlines ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5314014/)). I found no Thai software that does this job; the near tools are generic expiry add-ons ([ECOSIRE Odoo app](https://ecosire.com/ja/apps/odoo/odoo-visa-passport-expiry)). A back-office tool for these filers, priced per active worker, could plausibly reach about 4-9 million baht a year by year 3. It stays a "maybe" because the price that agents will pay is not confirmed and the rules change with every cabinet resolution.

### Room for improvement over the portal or current practice

- **The portal has documented faults.** The DOE admitted problems with user registration and with employers not finding their own workers' permit data. It let employers file on paper with a screenshot of the error, until 28 Jan 2026 ([InfoQuest](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=542101)).
- **Backlogs are severe.** Capacity was 254,760 queue slots a month in June 2026. The operator wanted 658,955 ([Post Today](https://www.posttoday.com/business/743372)). In April 2026 more than 2 million people were queuing ([Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/657139)). Some documents waited up to 4 months for registrar approval. Daily volume ran above 10,000 sets and some days hit 90,000, against a contract figure of 6,000 ([Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/668193)).
- **Middlemen profit from the friction.** In June 2026 the Labour Minister's visit heard that middlemen charge about 2,000 baht per worker for queue slots. Some Samut Sakhon workers had to book in Mae Sot, and many workers fell out of the system ([Naewna](https://www.naewna.com/local/972023)).
- **The operator is in dispute with the state.** Future Sky says it is owed about 715 million baht and has sued ([Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/668193)). This keeps the portal unstable.
- **What the portal does not do (gaps a tool could fill):**
  - A register across cohorts. One employer can have workers on the 11 Dec 2026 cohort, a 30 Jun 2027 passport and visa deadline, MOU terms and regularisation permits ([Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/policy/40069723); [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/657139)). I found no evidence that the portal sends expiry reminders (unverified).
  - Pre-filing checks per worker: passport or substitute, a health certificate from a DOE-linked clinic, the right type of health or social-security cover by sector, and a power of attorney with the correct stamp duty ([Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/news-update/1250827)). Missing items cause rejections and lost slots (unverified).
  - Multi-client work. A filer who serves 20 employers has to track each client's workers, powers of attorney, fees paid and status by hand (unverified, based on the portal's per-employer design).
  - Section 13 hire and exit notices within 15 days, with fines up to 20,000 baht ([drthawip.com law text](https://www.drthawip.com/book/export/html/3263)).
  - Inspection readiness. The DOE inspected 74,265 workplaces in FY2026 ([Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/1250192)). A clean per-worker file is useful at the gate.
  - Language. Workers speak Burmese, Lao or Vietnamese. Many rely on brokers partly because of language barriers ([BHRRC](https://business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/thailand-migrant-workers-from-myanmar-face-financial-hardships-as-broker-fees-for-work-permit-renewals-soar), search summary).
- **Consultants sell exactly this tracking work** in the expat segment. One firm tells HR teams to keep a central tracker of visa and permit expiries, with alerts at 90 and 60 days ([Issa Compass](https://www.issacompass.com/insights/thailand-work-permit-compliance-for-companies-what-hr-teams-must-know-to-avoid-f)).

### Competitor reality check

- **e-WorkPermit (free).** It handles filing, fee payment and status updates by email, SMS and LINE ([PRD English](https://thailand.prd.go.th/en/content/category/detail/id/2874/iid/429777)). It is a filing channel, not a multi-client register. It has the faults listed above.
- **Licensed import companies and proxies.** They sell a service, not software. Directory listings advertise MOU and document work with no prices ([Yellow Pages, PCT Business](https://www.yellowpages.co.th/catalog/item/บริการรับทำเอกสารต่างด้าว-qaDwNTJ); [JobBKK](https://www.jobbkk.com/company/188599)). They are customers for a tool, not rivals to it.
- **Software.** Four Thai and English searches found no Thai product built for migrant-worker permit cycles. What exists is generic: Odoo visa and passport expiry add-ons from about USD 299 one-off ([ECOSIRE](https://ecosire.com/ja/apps/odoo/odoo-visa-passport-expiry)), and foreign HR systems such as SafeHR ([SafeHR](https://www.safe-hr.com/hr-software/engagement/)). None knows Thai cabinet-resolution cohorts or the e-WorkPermit document set. I did not check Thai payroll vendors such as HumanSoft or Prosoft (unverified).
- **Issa Compass** sells software-backed visa and work-permit services, but for Non-B expat staff, not for the Lao, Myanmar and Vietnamese cohorts ([Issa Compass](https://www.issacompass.com/insights/thailand-work-permit-compliance-for-companies-what-hr-teams-must-know-to-avoid-f)).
- **Bottom line.** No incumbent does the whole job. This is an opening, not a killer.

### Price per customer

- **What the market pays today.** The state fee is 1,000 baht per worker ([Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/news-update/1250827)). Queue middlemen take about 2,000 baht per worker ([Naewna](https://www.naewna.com/local/972023)). Brokers charge workers 10,000-18,500 baht ([BHRRC](https://business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/thailand-migrant-workers-from-myanmar-face-financial-hardships-as-broker-fees-for-work-permit-renewals-soar), search summary). Thai law says recruitment costs should fall on the employer, not the worker ([Five Corridors Project](https://fivecorridorsproject.org/myanmar-thailand/myanmar-thailand-tackling-fraud-abuse)). A lapse costs the employer 10,000-100,000 baht per worker ([drthawip.com](https://www.drthawip.com/book/export/html/3263)).
- **What filers charge employers per renewal** was not found (unverified). This is the biggest pricing gap.
- **Proposed prices (hypotheses, unverified):**
  - Proxy or small agent: about 1,000 baht a month (12,000 a year) for up to 200 active workers, plus 5 baht per extra worker per month.
  - Licensed import company: about 2,500 baht a month (30,000 a year).
  - Direct employer with 20 or more migrant workers: about 500 baht a month (6,000 a year).
- These prices are tiny next to the fees per worker above. A filer with 200 workers pays 60 baht per worker a year.

### Ease of implementation and sale

- **Build: medium-easy.** The core is a register, a rules table per cohort, reminders, checklists and a POA generator. LINE is the right channel; the DOE itself uses LINE OA @doewp ([Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/social-biz/655341)).
- **No portal integration exists** that I found (unverified). Data entry is manual or by spreadsheet import. Scraping the portal would be fragile.
- **Upkeep: medium.** Each cabinet resolution sets new dates and conditions ([Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/508271)). This is also a moat, because a generic tool will not keep up.
- **Sale: medium.** Proxies and import firms are reachable through LINE and Facebook groups and around DOE service centres (unverified). Each one brings many employers. Deadline crunches (Mar, Apr and Dec 2026) create urgency ([Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/657139)).

### Remaining risks

- **Unknown willingness to pay** of proxies and import firms (unverified). Many may work from spreadsheets and resist a monthly fee.
- **Portal change.** The state could add reminders or a bulk employer view. The operator dispute could also cause a system swap ([Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/668193)).
- **Physical queues are the main pain.** Software cannot fix a shortage of biometric slots ([Post Today](https://www.posttoday.com/business/743372)).
- **Policy churn.** Cohorts may be merged or regularisation reopened ([Naewna](https://www.naewna.com/local/972023)).
- **Reputation.** The agent trade is tied to worker exploitation ([Five Corridors Project](https://fivecorridorsproject.org/myanmar-thailand/myanmar-thailand-tackling-fraud-abuse)). A tool should help employers file directly and keep fees transparent.
- **Cambodian workers** were reportedly left out of the 2 Dec 2025 resolution ([Thai PBS Verify](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/verify/content/7424), search summary). Their track needs separate checking.

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 4/10.**

The duty is real, large and enforced. About 770,000 Lao, Myanmar and Vietnamese workers have permits that end on 11 Dec 2026. Their employers, or agents acting for them, must renew on the state e-WorkPermit portal by that date ([InfoQuest](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=615165); [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/policy/40069723)). Hiring a worker without a permit costs 10,000-100,000 baht per worker under section 102 of the Royal Ordinance ([drthawip.com law text](https://www.drthawip.com/book/export/html/3263)). The DOE inspected 74,265 workplaces and prosecuted 1,721 of them in FY2026 ([Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/1250192)). But the filing itself is done on a free state portal, and 17,619 proxy filers plus 331 licensed import companies had already registered on it within its first month ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5314014/)). The real money in this market is in the agent and broker trade, which charges workers 10,000-18,500 baht ([BHRRC](https://business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/thailand-migrant-workers-from-myanmar-face-financial-hardships-as-broker-fees-for-work-permit-renewals-soar)). It is a relationship business, not a software one. A small software tool would be a multi-cohort expiry tracker for employers and small agents. That is a narrow, price-sensitive niche, and its deadlines change with each cabinet resolution.

## Duty

- **Legal basis.** The Royal Ordinance on Foreign Workers Management B.E. 2560 (2017), as amended by No. 2 B.E. 2561 (2018), sets the rules ([drthawip.com law text](https://www.drthawip.com/book/export/html/3263)):
  - Section 9 bars anyone from employing a foreigner who has no work permit.
  - Section 59 requires a foreigner to hold a work permit.
  - Section 13 requires an employer to notify the registrar within 15 days of hiring and within 15 days of a worker leaving.
  - Section 102 sets the employer fine at 10,000-100,000 baht per worker. A repeat offence brings up to 1 year in prison or a fine of 50,000-200,000 baht per worker, plus a 3-year ban on hiring foreign workers.
  - Section 103 fines a failure to notify under section 13 up to 20,000 baht.
  - Section 101 fines the worker 5,000-50,000 baht and leads to deportation.
- **Current cycle.** On 14 Jul 2026 the cabinet approved a one-year extension, to 11 Dec 2027, for workers whose permits were valid to 11 Dec 2026. The cohort is about 770,000 people, counted as of 30 Jun 2026 ([InfoQuest](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=615165); [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/508271); [PRD](https://www.prd.go.th/th/content/category/detail/id/39/iid/521864)). This round continues a cabinet resolution of 11 Nov 2025 ([Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/508271)).
- **What must be filed.** An electronic renewal on eworkpermit.doe.go.th by 11 Dec 2026. Filing closes at 16:30 and fee payment at 20:00 on the last day ([Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/policy/40069723)). The fees are 100 baht per application plus 900 baht per permit ([Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/1247316)). The worker needs a health check at a hospital linked to the DOE system, and social security or approved health insurance. A passport and visa must be completed by 30 Jun 2027, or within 90 days of a passport issued after 1 Aug 2026 ([Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/policy/40069723)). The worker, the employer or an authorised recruitment company may file ([Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/policy/40069723)). The lead file says a stamped power of attorney is also needed (unverified by me).
- **Recurrence.** The deadlines repeat, but on no fixed schedule:
  - Permits expiring by 13 Feb 2026 could renew under the old system ([Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/economy/642991)).
  - A cohort expiring 31 Mar 2026 (cabinet resolution of 2 Dec 2025) had a catch-up window from 7 to 21 Apr 2026 ([Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/657139)).
  - The current cohort expires 11 Dec 2026.
  - Workers regularised in 2025 got permits to 14 Oct 2026 ([Khaosod English via search](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/featured/2025/10/06/thailand-to-legalize-700000-undocumented-workers-from-4-nations/); I could not open the page, so this is unverified).
  - The first check reports an MOU cohort running to 13 Feb 2027 (unverified by me).
  - Each round comes from a new cabinet resolution with its own conditions.
- **Enforcement.** In FY2026 (1 Oct 2025 to 1 Sep 2026) the DOE inspected 74,265 workplaces and prosecuted 1,721. It also inspected 888,620 foreign workers and prosecuted 5,330 ([Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/1250192); [Thairath](https://thairath.co.th/news/politic/2957000)). On 4 Sep 2026 the government ordered strict enforcement against employers who hire illegally ([PRD](https://www.prd.go.th/th/content/category/detail/id/39/iid/538443), search snippet only; the page returned 503). On those numbers, about 2.3% of inspected workplaces were prosecuted.
- **Portal changes.** e-WorkPermit became mandatory from 13 Oct 2025 for new permits, renewals and cancellations ([EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/thailand-launches-online-platform-for-work-permit-applications); [PRD English](https://thailand.prd.go.th/en/content/category/detail/id/2874/iid/429777)). The rollout had glitches. Paper fallbacks ran to 28 Jan 2026 ([Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/news/thailand-extends-grace-period-for-employers-struggling-with-new-e-workpermit-system-525271)), and later to 28 Jul 2026 or "until further notice" for some permit types ([Vialto](https://vialtopartners.com/regional-alerts/thailand-immigration-e-work-permit-processing-update-now-extended-until-28-july-2026); [EIG Law](https://eiglaw.com/thailand-temporarily-allows-manual-work-permit-applications/)).

## Buyers

- **Workers.** The current cohort is about 770,000 Lao, Myanmar and Vietnamese workers ([InfoQuest](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=615165)). The portal held data on 2,323,339 foreign workers in Nov 2025 ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5314014/)). By Apr 2026 it held data on more than 3.6 million, and its operator said about 4 million migrants need renewals ([Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/657139)).
- **Employers.** No official count of distinct employers in the cohort was found (unverified). In the portal's first month, 81,551 employers, 331 licensed import companies, 17,619 proxy filers ("ผู้ดำเนินการแทน") and 999 public bodies or foundations registered ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5314014/)). The DOE inspected 74,265 workplaces employing foreigners in a single year ([Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/1250192)). That suggests the total employer base is well into six figures (unverified estimate).
- **Segments.** Manufacturing, agriculture, fisheries, food processing, construction and domestic work ([Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/thailandnews/thailand-extends-work-permits-for-770000-migrant-workers-to-prevent-labor-shortage-557605)).
- **How they comply today.** Employers file themselves, or use licensed import firms and proxy filers. Many workers rely on brokers because of language barriers, fear of questioning and a complex process ([BHRRC](https://business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/thailand-migrant-workers-from-myanmar-face-financial-hardships-as-broker-fees-for-work-permit-renewals-soar), via search summary; the page returned 429). The 17,619 registered proxies show that the intermediary layer is already large ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5314014/)).

## Competition

- **Free state tool.** e-WorkPermit is free apart from the official fees. It runs 24 hours and supports online payment and live status tracking ([EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/thailand-launches-online-platform-for-work-permit-applications)). It sends updates by email, SMS and LINE OA ([PRD English](https://thailand.prd.go.th/en/content/category/detail/id/2874/iid/429777)). It covers filing and status updates. I found no evidence that it sends proactive expiry reminders (unverified). It also has 40 service centres, 5 border centres and 8 mobile units for biometric capture ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5314014/)).
- **Agents and brokers.** There are 331 licensed import companies and 17,619 proxies ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5314014/)). Small firms advertise MOU visa and permit renewals, new passports and residence certificates, without listing prices ([JobBKK listing](https://www.jobbkk.com/company/188599)). A licence to run an import business needs at least 1 million baht in paid-up capital ([drthawip.com, ministerial rule](https://www.drthawip.com/book/export/html/3269), via search summary).
- **Software.** Thai and English searches found no employer-side SaaS for tracking migrant-worker permit, visa or passport expiries. Results showed only generic HR software ([MiHCM](https://mihcm.com/th/resources/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-hr-compliance-software/)) and generic Odoo passport modules ([Odoo apps](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/17.0/employee_passport_app)). I did not check Thai payroll vendors such as HumanSoft or Prosoft for a migrant module (unverified).
- **Bottom line.** The free state portal covers the filing job. Agents cover the hand-holding. The open gap is narrow: one tracker across several cohorts and deadlines for an employer's whole workforce, plus document checklists.

## Willingness to pay

- **State fees.** 1,000 baht per worker for the renewal itself ([Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/1247316)).
- **What workers pay today.** The Aid Alliance Committee reported 10,000 baht per person for a two-year permit plus visa, against an official cost of 3,700 baht. Brokers charge 11,000-18,500 baht, depending on status. One Samut Prakan factory cut its charge from 9,200 to 6,500 baht ([BHRRC](https://business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/thailand-migrant-workers-from-myanmar-face-financial-hardships-as-broker-fees-for-work-permit-renewals-soar); [DVB](https://english.dvb.no/myanmar-migrant-workers-protest-against-rising-costs-in-thailand/); both via search summary, and the year is unclear). These costs usually fall on the worker, often by wage deduction, not on the employer (unverified).
- **What a lapse costs the employer.** 10,000-100,000 baht per worker, and more for repeat offences, plus a hiring ban ([drthawip.com law text](https://www.drthawip.com/book/export/html/3263)). The risk is real but rare: about 2.3% of inspected workplaces were prosecuted ([Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/1250192)).
- **Plausible price.** A tracker at roughly 990-2,990 baht a year per small employer, or a filing service at 300-800 baht per worker (both unverified hypotheses). An assisted service could undercut brokers by a wide margin. But a tracker alone saves a 5-20 worker employer little, because the portal already reports status.

Its hotline is 1506, option 2 ([Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/policy/40069723)). The DOE will not endorse a private vendor (unverified).
- **Thai Chamber of Commerce.** It has run campaigns urging employers to register migrant workers ([Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/business/economic/1078261); the date on the page is unclear).
- **Federation of Thai Industries.** It has taken part in DOE meetings on bringing in MOU-channel workers ([Bangkok Biznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/social/978456); older item).
- **Partners at the point of need.** Clinics linked to the DOE health-check system, migrant health insurers, and the 17,619 proxy filers ([Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/policy/40069723); [Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5314014/)). The best wedge may be to sell the tool to small agents and proxies as their back office, rather than to employers one by one (unverified).
- **Facebook and LINE groups** of employers in construction, agriculture and restaurants, in Thai and Burmese (unverified).

## Risks

- **The free portal grows.** e-WorkPermit already offers status alerts by email, SMS and LINE ([PRD English](https://thailand.prd.go.th/en/content/category/detail/id/2874/iid/429777)). Adding expiry reminders would be a small step for it.
- **Portal instability.** The contractor, Future Sky, says the DOE owes it about 715 million baht and has sued in the Administrative Court ([Naewna](https://www.naewna.com/n/?p=43928); [Thai Post](https://www.thaipost.net/public-relations-news/1063652/)). It is paid 520 baht per completed permit ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5314014/)). In April 2026 more than 2 million people were queuing ([Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/657139)). This friction creates demand for help, but it also creates platform risk for any tool built around the portal.
- **Rules change every round.** Each cohort runs under a one-off cabinet resolution with its own conditions, such as passport deadlines and health cover ([Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/508271); [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/policy/40069723)). Content upkeep is constant.
- **Incumbents.** Agents control worker relationships, and many employers already outsource to them ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5314014/)).
- **Licensing and liability.** Filing for others may need registration as a proxy, and running a recruitment business needs a licence and capital of at least 1 million baht ([drthawip.com](https://www.drthawip.com/book/export/html/3269)). The exact proxy rules are unverified. A missed renewal exposes the client to fines of up to 100,000 baht per worker.
- **Reputation.** The broker trade is tied to worker exploitation ([BHRRC](https://business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/thailand-migrant-workers-from-myanmar-face-financial-hardships-as-broker-fees-for-work-permit-renewals-soar)).
- **Language.** The product needs Thai for employers and Burmese, Lao and Vietnamese for workers.
- **Cambodian workers.** Their status and timeline are separate and were not checked (unverified).

## First product

The first version should be a LINE-first "migrant workforce register" for small employers and small proxy agents. It should:

1. Import each worker's details: name, nationality, passport number and expiry, visa expiry, permit expiry, cohort, health insurance or social security status, and health-check date.
2. Map each worker to the correct current cabinet-resolution window, such as 11 Dec 2026, 30 Jun 2027 for passport and visa, and the 14 Oct 2026 regularisation cohort.
3. Send LINE and SMS reminders at 60, 30, 14 and 3 days before each deadline, plus a section 13 reminder to report a hire or exit within 15 days.
4. Produce a per-worker document checklist and a fee estimate (100 + 900 baht), and bulk-fill a power-of-attorney template.
