# Uganda: workplace registration and OSH record-keeping service (uganda-a11)

## Summary

**Verdict: maybe. Score: 4/10.**

The legal duty is real and has grown. Since 25 July 2025, the OSH (Amendment) Act No. 20 of 2025 applies to every workplace, not only those with 20 or more staff. Employers now owe registration, a safety committee, a safety officer, a health surveillance plan for the Commissioner, incident notification and annual audits ([KTA Advocates alert](https://www.ktaadvocates.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/KTA-LEGAL-ALERT-ON-OSH-ACT.pdf)). However, the registration step itself goes through a free state portal, OSHMIS ([Enabel, 2025](https://open.enabel.be/en/update/id/3388)). Enforcement is very thin: in 2013 the OSH department inspected about 400 workplaces a year ([MGLSD presentation](https://www.sesric.org/imgs/news/Image/827-p-uganda.pdf)), and in 2025 it was only 70% staffed ([Monitor](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/gov-t-tasks-employers-to-designate-labour-safety-officers-5032054)). The paying market is probably the roughly 33,000 formal employers that actively pay NSSF, not the hundreds of thousands of informal premises ([Monitor commentary](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/oped/commentary/nssf-puzzle-why-just-850-000-active-members--5233588)). The best opening is a low-cost "OSH compliance pack" service for formal SMEs, sold through accountants and business associations. It only becomes a strong business if the pending OSH regulations set deadlines and enforcement rises.

## Duty

**Legal basis**
- Occupational Safety and Health Act No. 9 of 2006, now Cap. 231. Part VII covers "Registration of Workplaces", including the Commissioner's register and the certificate of registration ([Act PDF, Parliament](https://bills.parliament.ug/attachments/Laws%20of%20Uganda%20(Acts)%20-%20THE%20OCCUPATIONAL%20SAFETY%20AND%20HEALTH%20ACT,%202006.pdf)). The PDF is a scanned image, so exact section numbers could not be read (unverified).
- Anyone who intends to occupy or use premises as a workplace must register it with the Commissioner for OSH at the Ministry of Gender, Labour and Social Development (MGLSD). Using a workplace without a certificate is an offence ([KAA Advocates, Aug 2022](https://kaa.co.ug/?p=12709)).
- Building plans for new or altered workplaces must go to the Commissioner for approval before work begins ([KAA](https://kaa.co.ug/?p=12709)).
- OSH (Amendment) Act No. 20 of 2025, in force 25 July 2025. It extends the Act to all workplaces and repeals the 20-employee limit ([KTA Advocates](https://www.ktaadvocates.com/uganda-expands-workplace-safetyobligations-under-the-occupationalsafety-and-health-amendment-act2025/)). DLA Piper dates presidential assent to 10 April 2025 ([DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2025/enhancing-workplace-safety-with-new-health-and-safety-law)).

**What must be filed or kept**
- Application for registration, possible inspection, then a certificate. With the certificate the ministry issues an **incident register**, an **OSH inspection checklist** and a short guide to the Act ([KAA](https://kaa.co.ug/?p=12709)).
- The certificate is renewable every **3 years** ([KAA](https://kaa.co.ug/?p=12709)). The only sources are a law firm and a ministry speech that could not be opened, so the statutory text is unverified.
- New duties since 2025 ([KTA alert PDF](https://www.ktaadvocates.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/KTA-LEGAL-ALERT-ON-OSH-ACT.pdf)):
  - a safety and health committee and a designated safety and health officer;
  - a workplace health surveillance plan submitted to the Commissioner for approval (s. 20(2)(a));
  - informing staff about hazards (s. 20(2)(b));
  - pre-assignment, periodic and exit medical exams for hazardous roles, paid by the employer (s. 20(2)(c), (e), 20(3));
  - occupational health services and wellness programmes (s. 20(2)(f), (4));
  - mandatory notification of accidents, incidents and disease outbreaks (s. 20A);
  - risk assessments for noise, dust and vibration (s. 46(3), (5));
  - **annual** workplace safety and health audits (s. 46(4));
  - certified training for operators of machinery, lifting equipment and electrical installations (ss. 70, 72, 83).
- Regulations are still pending. They are expected to set timelines for risk assessments and audits (s. 46(3)), licensing of safety practitioners (s. 15A(1)), and annual electrical inspections by certified inspectors (s. 83(3)) ([KTA alert](https://www.ktaadvocates.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/KTA-LEGAL-ALERT-ON-OSH-ACT.pdf)).
- Plant and equipment examination is a separate fee-earning service of the department ([MGLSD 2013](https://www.sesric.org/imgs/news/Image/827-p-uganda.pdf)). Statutory intervals for lifts, boilers and pressure vessels were not found (unverified).

**Penalties**
- A currency point is UGX 20,000 under the 2006 Act ([FAOLEX summary](https://leap.unep.org/en/countries/ug/national-legislation/occupational-safety-and-health-act-2006)).
- The penalty for operating an unregistered workplace was not found (unverified).
- A secondary source says breaches of the operator training rule carry up to 1,000 currency points (about UGX 20 million) or 2 years in prison, for both employer and operator ([WageIndicator](https://wageindicator.org/en-ug/ai/work-in-uganda/labour-law/health-and-safety/)). This is unverified against the gazetted text.
- Inspectors can now prosecute with the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions ([DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2025/enhancing-workplace-safety-with-new-health-and-safety-law)).

**Enforcement in practice: weak**
- In 2013 the department had 24 of 42 approved inspector posts filled, inspected "over 400 workplaces" a year, and collected over UGX 100 million a year in fees from workplace registration and plant examination ([MGLSD presentation, 2013](https://www.sesric.org/imgs/news/Image/827-p-uganda.pdf)).
- In May 2025 the OSH department said it was 70% staffed. Most employers were "reluctant" to appoint safety officers, and enforcement was hampered by understaffing ([Monitor, 7 May 2025](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/gov-t-tasks-employers-to-designate-labour-safety-officers-5032054)).
- EPRC (September 2026) found fewer than half of workplaces inspected over two years. It quotes a ratio of 1 inspector to 486,421 workers, but its own counts (234 inspectors, 10.2 million workers) imply about 1:43,600, so the ratio is unclear ([EPRC](https://library.eprcug.org/publication/the-business-case-for-labour-inspection-in-uganda-a-case-of-cocoa-coffee-and-hospitality-subsectors-in-the-albertine-and-rwenzori-subregions)).
- I found no news of fines or closures for unregistered workplaces in 2025-2026 (unverified; the search found none).

**Recent changes:** a secondary source says an Employment (Amendment) Act, assented in April 2026, adds OSH duties: an OSH officer above a threshold, incident reporting, and inspectors asking for training records and risk assessments ([Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/uganda-employment-law-changes-2026/)). Unverified against the official gazette.

## Buyers

- **In principle:** every workplace since July 2025 ([KTA](https://www.ktaadvocates.com/uganda-expands-workplace-safetyobligations-under-the-occupationalsafety-and-health-amendment-act2025/)). UBOS published its 2019/20 Census of Business Establishments in August 2025, but I could not open the totals ([UBOS](https://www.ubos.org/2025/08/14/2019-2020-census-of-business-establishments-cobe-report/)). The census counts fixed-premise businesses with at least one employee ([UBOS censuses page](https://www.ubos.org/?p=4560)). The total is likely several hundred thousand (unverified).
- **Formal, reachable buyers:** URSB had over 850,000 registered entities by February 2025. NSSF has 113,200 registered employers, of which only **33,200 are active** ([Monitor commentary](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/oped/commentary/nssf-puzzle-why-just-850-000-active-members--5233588)). Active NSSF payers are the best proxy for firms that pay for compliance.
- **Number of registered workplaces:** not published anywhere I could find (unverified). The 2013 fee revenue of about UGX 100 million a year suggests a small number of registrations at the time ([MGLSD 2013](https://www.sesric.org/imgs/news/Image/827-p-uganda.pdf)).
- **Segments with the most pressure:** factories and industry (named in the safety-officer directive, [Monitor](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/gov-t-tasks-employers-to-designate-labour-safety-officers-5032054)), construction, energy, agribusiness exporters (Wagagai), breweries and hospitality (OSH award winners, [Enabel](https://open.enabel.be/en/update/id/3388)), and firms with donor, export or lender audits (inference).
- **How firms comply today:** large firms have in-house HSE staff. Mid-size firms use law firms (KAA, KTA publish guides) and small OSH consultancies such as Kashari Consults ([VC4A](https://vc4a.com/ventures/kashari-consults-u-limited/)). Small firms mostly do nothing, given low inspection rates (inference from the [Monitor](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/gov-t-tasks-employers-to-designate-labour-safety-officers-5032054) and [EPRC](https://library.eprcug.org/publication/the-business-case-for-labour-inspection-in-uganda-a-case-of-cocoa-coffee-and-hospitality-subsectors-in-the-albertine-and-rwenzori-subregions) findings). The paper incident register comes from the ministry ([KAA](https://kaa.co.ug/?p=12709)).

## Competition

- **Free state tool: OSHMIS** (oshmis.mglsd.go.ug). It was announced at the 2025 OSH awards ([Enabel](https://open.enabel.be/en/update/id/3388)). A 2023 ministry speech says workplace registration is filed there (seen in search snippet only; the [speech PDF](https://www.mediacentre.go.ug/sites/default/files/media/SPEECH%20BY%20HON.%20GIDUDU%20MAFWABI%20DOMINIC%20MINISTER%20OF%20STATE%20FOR%20ELDERLY%20AFFAIRS.pdf) returned 404). An earlier ministry system lets workplaces report problems ([Monitor, 2022](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/ministry-introduces-online-system-to-monitor-workers-4015048)). The portal returned HTTP 503 when fetched, so its features (renewal, incident reporting, payment) are unverified. **The free state tool covers the filing step.** A private product can only sell help around it.
- **Local digital efforts:** the UNCST-listed "OSH Tracking and Measurement System" (incident tracking, alerts; prototype) ([UNCST](https://www.innovations.uncst.go.ug/innovation/296)); Kashari Consults' digital incident-reporting and compliance system, in development ([VC4A](https://vc4a.com/ventures/kashari-consults-u-limited/)); a Makerere undergraduate HSE app prototype ([Makerere](https://dissertations.mak.ac.ug/handle/20.500.12281/14232)).
- **Regional and international tools:** HSEQ App by HSE Masters, Ghana ([Graphic](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghanaian-company-develops-workplace-safety.html)); DNV Synergi Life Connect ([DNV](https://www.dnv.com/services/mobile-app-for-hse-risk-management-and-incident-reporting-synergi-life-connect-3697/)); Jotform HSSE templates ([Jotform](https://www.jotform.com/app-templates/hsse-incident-management-app)). None is tailored to Ugandan forms. Prices were not found.
- **Training providers:** UMA runs a 2-3 day OHS open course in Kampala ([UMA prospectus 2025](https://uma.or.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/UMA-Training-Courses-Prospectus-2025-1.pdf)). The fee was not found (unverified).
- **Law firms:** KAA, KTA and DLA Piper's Ugandan partners advise on compliance ([KAA](https://kaa.co.ug/?p=12709), [KTA](https://www.ktaadvocates.com/uganda-expands-workplace-safetyobligations-under-the-occupationalsafety-and-health-amendment-act2025/)).
- No commercial Ugandan OSH compliance SaaS was found.

## Willingness to pay

- No published prices were found for Ugandan OSH consultancy, registration help or audits (unverified).
- The official registration fee was not found (unverified). Total fee revenue was about UGX 100 million a year in 2013 ([MGLSD](https://www.sesric.org/imgs/news/Image/827-p-uganda.pdf)).
- The potential fine for one known offence is up to UGX 20 million (1,000 currency points) ([WageIndicator](https://wageindicator.org/en-ug/ai/work-in-uganda/labour-law/health-and-safety/), unverified). The chance of being inspected is low.
- New paperwork costs real staff time: a surveillance plan, committee minutes, risk assessments and an annual audit. KTA tells employers to budget for audits and health surveillance ([KTA alert](https://www.ktaadvocates.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/KTA-LEGAL-ALERT-ON-OSH-ACT.pdf)).
- My estimate (unverified): formal SMEs might pay UGX 300,000-1,500,000 (about USD 80-400) a year for a done-for-you pack, such as registration help, surveillance plan, templates and an annual audit report. Pure software at UGX 50,000-150,000 a month is a harder sell. Buyers who face donor, lender or export audits will pay more than those who face only the state.

## Channels

- **Uganda Manufacturers Association (UMA):** runs OHS courses and has a training calendar ([UMA](https://uma.or.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/UMA-Training-Courses-Prospectus-2025-1.pdf)).
- **Federation of Uganda Employers (FUE):** takes part in OSH training ([SESRIC event](https://www.sesric.org/event-print.php?id=1730)). There is no confirmed current course.
- **MGLSD OSH department:** annual World Day for Safety and Health awards, with Enabel as co-organiser ([Enabel](https://open.enabel.be/en/update/id/3388)); Vision Zero Africa conference, Kampala, August 2025 ([Monitor](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/gov-t-tasks-employers-to-designate-labour-safety-officers-5032054)).
- **Law firms** that publish OSH alerts (KTA, KAA) as referral partners.
- **Accountants and payroll bureaus** that already handle NSSF and PAYE for SMEs (inference; ICPAU membership not checked).
- **Donor projects** such as the Enabel Decent Work project and the ILO ([ILO ToT](https://www.ilo.org/resource/article/training-trainers-tot-workshop-labour-inspectors-and-osh-officers)).

## Risks

- **State tool:** OSHMIS already handles registration and may add incident reporting and plan submission. It is free.
- **Weak enforcement:** low inspection rates and reluctant employers ([Monitor](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/gov-t-tasks-employers-to-designate-labour-safety-officers-5032054), [EPRC](https://library.eprcug.org/publication/the-business-case-for-labour-inspection-in-uganda-a-case-of-cocoa-coffee-and-hospitality-subsectors-in-the-albertine-and-rwenzori-subregions)) mean little fear of fines.
- **Pending regulations:** deadlines for audits and risk assessments are not set yet ([KTA alert](https://www.ktaadvocates.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/KTA-LEGAL-ALERT-ON-OSH-ACT.pdf)), so the urgency is unclear.
- **Licensing:** practitioner licensing under s. 15A may require a licensed safety professional to sign audits or plans. This is a barrier, or a moat if you partner with one.
- **Small market:** only about 33,200 active formal employers ([Monitor](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/oped/commentary/nssf-puzzle-why-just-850-000-active-members--5233588)), and many are tiny. Prices are low.
- **Liability:** a template-driven plan that misses a hazard could be blamed after an accident.
- **Language:** English is the language of law and business, so there is no barrier for documents. Shop-floor training may need Luganda or Swahili.

## First product

**V1: an "OSH Compliance Pack" service with light software. It is not a SaaS-first product.**
- Intake form, about 10 minutes: sector, headcount, premises, machines, hazards.
- It generates:
  - a registration checklist and OSHMIS filing help;
  - a draft health surveillance plan for the Commissioner;
  - a safety committee constitution, officer appointment letter and minutes template;
  - a risk assessment template covering noise, dust and vibration;
  - a digital incident register with s. 20A notification prompts;
  - an annual audit checklist.
- Reminders for the 3-year certificate renewal, annual audit, medical exams and operator training.
- Optional paid add-on: a site visit and audit by a partner safety practitioner.

**First 30 days**
1. Get the gazetted Act No. 20 of 2025 and the consolidated Cap. 231 from Parliament or ULII. Confirm section numbers, penalties and the renewal period.
2. Register a test workplace on OSHMIS, or shadow a client doing so. Record the fee, documents, timing and portal features.
3. Interview 15 formal SMEs: UMA members, hotels and construction subcontractors. Ask what they would pay, and whether a lender, donor or buyer audit is driving them.
4. Build the intake and document generator (Google Forms, document templates, WhatsApp reminders).
5. Sign one partner safety practitioner and one accounting firm. Pre-sell 5 packs.

## Open questions

- Official registration fee, renewal rule and penalty for an unregistered workplace (statutory text).
- What OSHMIS does today: renewals, incident reports, surveillance plan upload, online payment.
- Number of registered workplaces and the trend since July 2025.
- When the OSH regulations will be issued, and what deadlines they set.
- Whether s. 15A licensing will require a licensed practitioner to sign plans or audits.
- Statutory examination intervals for lifts, boilers and pressure vessels, which could be a recurring revenue line.
- The COBE 2019/20 establishment count by size class.
- Whether the April 2026 Employment (Amendment) Act adds OSH duties (unverified secondary source).
