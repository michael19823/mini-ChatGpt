# Uruguay B2: AML/PLAFT compliance kit for small non-financial obliged subjects (SENACLAFT)

## Re-assessment (owner's criteria)

**Verdict: maybe. New score: 5/10 (old score: 3/10).**

**The case.** SENACLAFT's inspections keep finding gaps that simple software would close. Firms skip the written risk analysis, keep no copy of the due diligence, do not identify the beneficial owner, and print list searches without dates ([Ferrere, 2023](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/principales-debilidades-detectadas-por-senaclaft-en-los-procesos-inspectivos-de-sujetos-obligados/)). The state offers only optional Word/PDF forms from 2021 and no online workflow ([SENACLAFT guías](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/comunicacion/publicaciones/guiasformularios-para-debida-diligencia-para-sujetos-obligados-sector-3)). Two local products, Cumplo360 and HADA, already cover the workflow. Neither publishes a full price, and neither is built around the one-person notary or estate agent. A cheap, fixed-price, notary- and agency-first tool could still win a slice of about 11,000 obliged subjects. Light enforcement and two incumbents keep this at "maybe".

### Room for improvement over the portal or current practice

- **The state gives forms, not a system.** SENACLAFT publishes six due-diligence forms (simplified, normal and intensified, for people and companies) plus an instructivo. They are Word and PDF files dated 2021, use is "not mandatory", and there is no online system to fill them in ([SENACLAFT guías](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/comunicacion/publicaciones/guiasformularios-para-debida-diligencia-para-sujetos-obligados-sector-3)). They predate Ley 20.469's new cash threshold, *permuta* and virtual-asset rules ([Ferrere](https://www.ferrere.com/es/novedades/cambios-significativos-al-regimen-plaft/)).
- **Inspection findings match what software does.** At an April 2023 webinar, SENACLAFT listed the main weaknesses it finds in non-financial subjects ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/principales-debilidades-detectadas-por-senaclaft-en-los-procesos-inspectivos-de-sujetos-obligados/)):
  - no written risk analysis to set the due-diligence level;
  - no physical or digital backup of the due diligence done;
  - no beneficial-owner identification or check;
  - list searches with no saved evidence, printed without dates, and matches not analysed or ruled out;
  - no documents for income or source of funds.
  Each of these is a form field, a timestamped search log, a document upload or a required step in a guided file.
- **Proof matters more after Res. 61/2026.** RSM says the main exposure is "not being able to show technically that you complied" ([RSM](https://www.rsm.global/uruguay/es/node/772)). That is an audit-trail and inspection-readiness job.
- **Ley 20.469 forces an update.** Firms must update risk matrices and manuals. Due diligence now reaches shareholders and fund contributors, and the cash threshold drops ([Ferrere](https://www.ferrere.com/es/novedades/cambios-significativos-al-regimen-plaft/)).
- **People pay for help today.** Crowe sells per-client due-diligence files for estate agents, auctioneers and notaries, delivered in 72 working hours with an evidence file and inspection support ([Crowe](https://www.crowe.com/uy/plaft-ag-inmob-y-rematadores)). On 4/09/2026 SENACLAFT warned that subjects may hire due-diligence firms but keep the responsibility, and that it does not register or approve such firms ([SENACLAFT communiqué](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/sites/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/files/2026-09/Comunicado%20SENACLAFT%20-%20DDC%20-%2004SET2026.pdf), via search snippet; the PDF is a scan). The warning suggests an active market for outsourced due diligence (inference, unverified).
- **Notaries complain about the burden.** The notaries' association said it wants controls "rationalised" and that some deals have become harder ([El Observador](https://www.elobservador.com.uy/nota/nueva-norma-antilavado-que-cambio-para-los-abogados-escribanos-y-contadores--201812311224)). Officials considered easing notaries' requirements after their complaints ([Funds Society](https://www.fundssociety.com/es/?p=57209), date unverified).

### Competitor reality check

- **Cumplo360 (Devsys, Montevideo).** It has list search backed by Dow Jones, auditable client files, continuous screening, configurable risk assessment and transaction monitoring. It is web-based and multi-user. The page does not mention ROS drafting. It claims 350+ clients across several countries and 8,000+ users in Latin America. Named clients include an exchange in Paraguay, a Peruvian fintech, Zonamerica, one Uruguayan estate agency, two notary/professional firms and one accounting firm. Pricing is "scalable plans", with no public price ([Cumplo360](https://cumplo360.com/es/uruguay-antilavado/)). **Read:** a capable, regional, finance-grade product. It does the job, but it is probably priced and pitched for mid-size firms (unverified, no price).
- **HADA (GNS Software).** It has three tiers. The Prepaid tier ("from US$10 + tax") is list screening only. Lite adds history and alerts. Only Full adds a risk matrix, simplified/normal/intensified due diligence, a document manager with expiry alerts, self-service client profiles and ROS generation. Lite and Full are annual plans with no public price. The page names no notaries, estate agents or accountants as clients, and does not mention multi-user access or an audit trail ([HADA](https://hada.com.uy/)). **Read:** it covers the full duty list only on the unpriced top tier. The US$10 anchor is for screening, not compliance files.
- **Crowe.** A done-for-you service per client, not software. No public price ([Crowe](https://www.crowe.com/uy/plaft-ag-inmob-y-rematadores)).
- **Notary practice software.** A search found no Uruguayan notary software with a PLAFT module (searched, none found; not exhaustive).
- **Bottom line.** Both incumbents do the core job, so a generic "PLAFT suite" has no gap. The openings are price transparency, a sector-specific flow for notaries and estate agents, and quick Ley 20.469 updates. This is a real but narrow opening, not a killer.

### Price per customer

- **Today's costs.** Fines start at UI 1,000 and go up to UI 20,000,000 ([RSM](https://www.rsm.global/uruguay/es/node/772)). UI was about UYU 6.50 in March-April 2026 ([INE](https://www5.ine.gub.uy/documents/Estad%C3%ADsticasecon%C3%B3micas/PDF/UI/2026/UI%20Marzo%202026.pdf)). So the minimum fine is about UYU 6,500, or about US$160 at roughly UYU 40 per dollar (exchange rate unverified). Crowe's per-file fee and consultants' manual fees are not public (unverified).
- **Suggested pricing.**
  - Solo notary or estate agent: US$20-30 a month (US$240-360 a year), with unlimited client files, a timestamped search log of the free UN, OFAC and SENACLAFT PEP lists, and PDF export of each file (my estimate, unverified).
  - Per-file option for low-volume users: US$5-10 per file (my estimate, unverified).
  - Accountant or consultant running files for several obliged clients: US$60-100 a month per firm, priced per client entity (my estimate, unverified).
  - One-off Ley 20.469 matrix and manual update kit: US$100-200 (my estimate, unverified).
- **Planning figure:** a blended US$300 a year per customer.

  - Core: 3,700 x 3% = 111 customers.
  - Notaries: 7,368 x 2% = 147 customers.
  - Total: 258 customers x US$300 = **about US$77,000 a year**.
- **Upside case:** 5% of all 11,000 = 550 x US$300 = US$165,000, plus 200 update kits x US$150 = US$30,000 once.
- **Downside case:** 1.5% of 11,000 = 165 x US$300 = about US$50,000.
- **Read:** about US$50,000-165,000 a year, with a central case near US$77,000. That is a viable side business or a small team's income. It is not a large one.

### Ease of implementation and sale

- **Build: easy.** The core is guided forms that follow SENACLAFT's six forms, a risk score with stated reasons, a check of the free lists with date and result logged, document uploads, and PDF export. No state integration is needed. The SENACLAFT PEP list is free and updated (latest June 2026) ([lista PEP](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/comunicacion/publicaciones/lista-pep)). Licensed list data such as Dow Jones would add cost; free lists keep costs low.
- **Onboarding: easy.** A single user needs no setup beyond a firm profile and a risk-matrix template.
- **Sale: medium.** Buyers are many one-person firms that are price-sensitive and lightly policed. Channels are the notaries' association, the Cámara Inmobiliaria (800+ firms) and the accountants' college (~7,500 members) (see Channels below). SENACLAFT will not endorse any vendor ([communiqué](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/sites/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/files/2026-09/Comunicado%20SENACLAFT%20-%20DDC%20-%2004SET2026.pdf), via search snippet).

## Summary

**Verdict: no-go. Score: 3/10.**

The duty is real, in force and getting broader. Ley 19.574 art. 13 and Decreto 379/018 require estate agents, developers, accountants, lawyers on listed operations, corporate-service providers and others to register with SENACLAFT. They must also keep a risk matrix, write a risk analysis for each client and run due diligence. Ley 20.469 (2026) and Res. 61/2026 added new obliged subjects and set a new sanctions scale. The idea assumed no local tool existed. That is wrong. At least two Uruguayan SaaS products already cover the core job. Cumplo360 (Montevideo, 350+ clients, real estate and notary customers) and HADA by GNS Software offer screening, a risk matrix, simplified, normal and intensified due diligence, and draft suspicious-activity reports (ROS), with prepaid plans from US$10. Crowe sells a done-for-you service, and SENACLAFT publishes free forms. The market is small: about 3,700 registered subjects outside notaries and free zones in 2019. Enforcement is light: 50 sanctions in 2023, 14 of them fines. A newcomer would be the third software entrant in a small, lightly enforced niche. That is the killer.

## Duty

- **Legal basis.** Ley 19.574 art. 13 lists the non-financial obliged subjects. They are casinos; estate agencies, developers, builders and other property intermediaries; lawyers on the operations in literal C; anyone (other than notaries) on literal D operations; dealers in antiques, art and precious metals; corporate and trust service providers (literal H); and accountants on literal J operations ([gub.uy trámite](https://www.gub.uy/tramites/inscripcion-sujetos-obligados-sector-no-financiero); [IMPO art. 13](https://impo.com.uy/bases/leyes/19574-2017/13)). Decreto 379/018 regulates the non-financial sector ([PwC](https://www.pwc.com.uy/es/acerca-de-nosotros/publicaciones/ViaRapida/decreto-reglamentario-de-ley-de-lavado-de-activos.pdf); [IMPO Decreto 379/018](https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/decretos/379-2018/36)).
- **Registration.** Registration with SENACLAFT is online and free, using a gub.uy user or a digital ID. Changes must be reported within 30 days. Notaries, auctioneers, free-zone operators and non-profits do not register themselves; the Caja Notarial, the free-zone authority, the auctioneers' registry and the MEC supply their data ([gub.uy trámite](https://www.gub.uy/tramites/inscripcion-sujetos-obligados-sector-no-financiero)).
- **Ongoing duties.**
  - Keep a risk matrix covering client, geographic and operational risk.
  - Choose simplified, normal or intensified due diligence from that matrix.
  - Show the regulator a written risk analysis.
  - Keep records for at least 5 years, or 10 if asked ([Ferrere](https://www.ferrere.com/en/news/uruguay-reglamentan-ley-integral-contra-el-lavado-de-activos-para-sector-no-financiero/)).
  - Appoint a compliance officer, who may be the subject itself ([abogados.com.ar / Ferrere](https://abogados.com.ar/uruguay-reglamentan-ley-integral-contra-el-lavado-de-activos-para-sector-no-financiero/22625)).
  - Report unusual or suspicious operations to the UIAF.
  - There is no fixed periodic filing. The work is per client and per transaction, plus upkeep (first check, citing [Ferrere](https://www.ferrere.com/es/novedades/cambios-significativos-al-regimen-plaft/)).
- **Ley 20.469 (promulgated 19/03/2026, published 10/04/2026).**
  - New subjects: non-financial trustees, and business-process outsourcers serving financial activities abroad.
  - Unions and business organisations are added as non-profits.
  - Property deals paid by *dación en pago*, *permuta* or virtual assets are now covered.
  - Due diligence now reaches shareholders, partners, investors and fund contributors.
  - The low-risk presumption for banked operations is removed.
  - The cash threshold falls from UI 1,000,000 to UI 200,000, or 5% of the deal capped at UI 450,000.
  - Directors and senior management can now be sanctioned.
  - Many points still await regulation ([Ferrere](https://www.ferrere.com/es/novedades/cambios-significativos-al-regimen-plaft/); [IMPO](https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/leyes-originales/20469-2026)).
  - No regulating decree was listed on SENACLAFT's normativa page as of this check ([SENACLAFT normativa](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/institucional/normativa)).
- **Res. 54/2026 (12/05/2026)** put the new subjects under SENACLAFT and opened their registration ([Presidencia](https://www.gub.uy/presidencia/comunicacion/noticias/senaclaft-sumo-su-fiscalizacion-dos-nuevos-sujetos-obligados-financieros)). The registration deadline was 10/07/2026 ([SENACLAFT home](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/), via search snippet; unverified on the page itself).
- **Res. 272/024 (01/08/2024)** is a binding resolution on client due-diligence procedures for non-financial subjects ([SENACLAFT normativa](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/institucional/normativa)). I could not read its text (unverified).
- **Penalties.**
  - Res. 61/2026 (26/06/2026) replaces Res. 016/2022 ([SENACLAFT normativa](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/institucional/normativa)). It keeps fines of UI 1,000 to UI 20,000,000 and grades them by income band.
  - Serious infringements include: not registering; not updating data; not setting up an electronic domicile; skipping sector due diligence; not identifying beneficial owners; and not reporting suspicious operations.
  - Serious infringements can bring fines or suspension. RSM's summary: "la principal exposición no estará solamente en incumplir, sino en no poder demostrar técnicamente que se cumplió" ([RSM](https://www.rsm.global/uruguay/es/node/772)).
- **Enforcement.**
  - 2016-2019: 1,680 inspection visits, including 254 to estate agencies, 564 to notaries, 111 to accountants and 62 to developers and builders. In 2019 SENACLAFT issued 15 warnings, 2 observations and 5 fines totalling $U 1,560,724 ([SENACLAFT Memoria 2019](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/sites/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/files/documentos/noticias/Memoria%20SENACLAFT%202019%20web.pdf)).
  - 2023: 50 sanctions (16 warnings, 20 observations, 14 fines) totalling UI 1,310,001. No real estate line was shown ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/resumen-de-actividades-y-sanciones-aplicadas-por-la-senaclaft-durante-2023/)).
  - Common inspection findings: no written risk analysis and no beneficial-owner identification ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/principales-debilidades-detectadas-por-senaclaft-en-los-procesos-inspectivos-de-sujetos-obligados/), via search snippet).
  - The non-financial sector filed 107 suspicious-activity reports in 2025, against 973 from the financial sector ([Presidencia](https://www.gub.uy/presidencia/comunicacion/noticias/senaclaft-sumo-su-fiscalizacion-dos-nuevos-sujetos-obligados-financieros)), and 84 in 2024 ([La República](https://www.larepublica.co/globoeconomia/lavado-de-activos-en-uruguay-aumentaron-los-reportes-de-operaciones-sospechosas-4074157)).
  - On 4/09/2026 SENACLAFT issued a communiqué. Subjects may hire firms to help with due diligence, but responsibility cannot be delegated. SENACLAFT does not authorise, register or endorse such firms. Its inspections never give "more flexible" treatment to any group or list of subjects ([SENACLAFT communiqué](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/sites/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/files/2026-09/Comunicado%20SENACLAFT%20-%20DDC%20-%2004SET2026.pdf)).

## Competition

- **Free state tools.**
  - SENACLAFT publishes sector guides and simplified/normal/intensified forms for people and companies ([guías/formularios](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/comunicacion/publicaciones/guiasformularios-para-debida-diligencia-para-sujetos-obligados-sector-3); [Instructivo Sector Inmobiliario](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/sites/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/files/documentos/publicaciones/Instructivo%20Sector%20Inmobiliario_0.pdf)).
  - It also publishes a PEP list ([lista PEP](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/comunicacion/publicaciones/lista-pep)).
  - Registration itself is free ([gub.uy trámite](https://www.gub.uy/tramites/inscripcion-sujetos-obligados-sector-no-financiero)).
  - No free state tool covers the full workflow (matrix, case files, audit trail).
- **Generic APIs.** Screening APIs such as Didit market to Uruguay ([Didit](https://didit.me/es/blog/aml-screening-api-uruguay-54038/)).
- **Bottom line.** The "no Uruguay-specific tool" premise from both earlier checks is false. Two local SaaS products already sell this exact workflow to estate agents, notaries and accountants.

## Willingness to pay

- No public price was found for Cumplo360, Crowe, or HADA's annual plans (unverified). HADA's prepaid entry point is US$10 plus tax ([HADA](https://hada.com.uy/)), which sets a low price anchor.
- **Cost of non-compliance.**
  - Fines range from UI 1,000 to UI 20,000,000 ([RSM](https://www.rsm.global/uruguay/es/node/772)). UI 1,000 is roughly US$160 (unverified conversion).
  - Actual fines are few: 14 in 2023 across all non-financial sectors, totalling UI 1.31M ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/resumen-de-actividades-y-sanciones-aplicadas-por-la-senaclaft-durante-2023/)).
  - The expected cost of a fine is low for a small agency, so willingness to pay for software is likely modest.
- **Staff time.** Each sale or development deal needs a written risk analysis and a client file. For a small agency with a few dozen covered deals a year, this is hours, not days (unverified estimate).
- **Plausible price.** About US$15-40 per month per small firm, or a per-file fee of US$10-30. This is my estimate (unverified) and is bounded by HADA's prepaid offer.

- **CCEAU.** The accountants' college has about 7,500 members ([GAFILAT](https://biblioteca.gafilat.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/IEM-Uruguay.pdf)) and a technical prevention commission ([La Mañana](https://www.xn--lamaana-7za.uy/?p=57284), via search snippet).
- **Notary bodies.** The Asociación de Escribanos and the Caja Notarial (unverified as channels).
- **SENACLAFT training.** Free courses on EducAntel and SENACLAFT's subscriber mailing list ([SENACLAFT Memoria 2019](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/sites/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/files/documentos/noticias/Memoria%20SENACLAFT%202019%20web.pdf); [LinkedIn listing](https://uy.linkedin.com/in/luciapraino)). SENACLAFT will not endorse vendors ([communiqué](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/sites/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/files/2026-09/Comunicado%20SENACLAFT%20-%20DDC%20-%2004SET2026.pdf)).
- **Law and accounting firms** that write PLAFT manuals could resell or refer a tool. They also compete.

## Risks

- **Incumbents (killer).** Cumplo360 and HADA already offer matrix, due diligence, screening and ROS drafting in Uruguay, with local clients ([Cumplo360](https://cumplo360.com/es/uruguay-antilavado/); [HADA](https://hada.com.uy/)).
- **Small market.** About 3,700 registered core subjects in 2019, plus notaries ([GAFILAT](https://biblioteca.gafilat.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/IEM-Uruguay.pdf)). Many are one-person firms.
- **Light enforcement.** 50 sanctions in 2023 ([Ferrere](https://ferrere.com/es/novedades/resumen-de-actividades-y-sanciones-aplicadas-por-la-senaclaft-durante-2023/)). Low reporting volumes ([Presidencia](https://www.gub.uy/presidencia/comunicacion/noticias/senaclaft-sumo-su-fiscalizacion-dos-nuevos-sujetos-obligados-financieros)).
- **Free state forms** cover the basic paperwork ([SENACLAFT guías](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/comunicacion/publicaciones/guiasformularios-para-debida-diligencia-para-sujetos-obligados-sector-3)).
- **Pending regulation.** Ley 20.469's rules are not yet regulated, so product logic could change ([Ferrere](https://www.ferrere.com/es/novedades/cambios-significativos-al-regimen-plaft/)).
- **Liability.** Responsibility stays with the subject. SENACLAFT warns it does not endorse third-party due-diligence procedures. Any marketing implying "SENACLAFT-approved" is risky ([communiqué](https://www.gub.uy/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/sites/secretaria-nacional-lucha-contra-lavado-activos-financiamiento-terrorismo/files/2026-09/Comunicado%20SENACLAFT%20-%20DDC%20-%2004SET2026.pdf)).
- **Data and lists.** Screening needs licensed list and PEP data (Cumplo360 uses Dow Jones). That is a cost a bootstrapper must buy or limit to free lists.
- **Language.** No barrier. Spanish only.

## First product

Only worth doing as a narrow wedge, not a general PLAFT suite:

1. **Ley 20.469 update kit** for small estate agencies and accountants: a risk-matrix template, a manual template and a checklist for the new cash threshold, *permuta* and virtual-asset deals. Sell it as a one-off of about US$100-200 (unverified price).
2. **Per-deal file builder**: a guided form that maps to SENACLAFT's simplified/normal/intensified forms, searches the free UN and PEP lists, records the risk score with reasons, and exports a PDF that can be shown in an inspection.
3. **First 30 days:**
   - Read Decreto 379/018, Res. 272/024 and the sector guides.
   - Build the form and PDF export.
   - Interview 15 agencies from the Cámara Inmobiliaria and 10 accountants about Cumplo360 and HADA pricing and their pain points.
   - Stop unless clear gaps appear (price, simplicity, Ley 20.469 coverage).
