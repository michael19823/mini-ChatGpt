# South Africa: FICA duties of high-value goods dealers (jewellers, gold, bullion and stone dealers), turned into product requirements

Status: IN PROGRESS (started 2026-10-10). Sections are being filled in. Facts so far are from primary FIC documents downloaded from fic.gov.za.

## Summary (draft notes)

- Item 20 of Schedule 1 to the FIC Act: a person who carries on the business of dealing in high-value goods and receives payment of R100,000 or more for an item valued at R100,000 or more, in one payment or linked payments. "Dealing" means to buy or sell, retail or wholesale. A second-hand dealer that buys a R120,000 unit of scrap gold is in scope ([PCC 58, 28 Mar 2024, paras 1.4, 1.5.4, example 2](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/2024.03-PCC-HVGD-guidance.pdf)).
- Directive 12 (Gazette 55337, Notice 7887, 4 Sep 2026; in force 7 Sep 2026): item 20 dealers must upload their RMCP on goAML by 31 Oct 2026 and by 31 Oct every year; new dealers within 90 days of starting business; an updated and approved RMCP within 10 days of approval ([Directive 12](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Directive-12-On-the-submission-of-risk-management-and-compliance-programmes.pdf)).
- Directive 10 (in force 31 Jul 2026): dealers with more than one location must add head office, branch and subsidiary particulars to their goAML registration; existing registrants within 90 days (29 Oct 2026); changes within 90 days ([Directive 10](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Directive-10-On-information-pertaining-to-geographic-locations.pdf)).
- Draft PCC 126 (30 Sep 2026, comments to 16 Oct 2026): one goAML registration per SADPMR permit or licence; silver and lab-grown stones count; check client permits, source of goods and source of funds ([draft PCC 126](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026.9-PCC-DPMS.pdf)).
