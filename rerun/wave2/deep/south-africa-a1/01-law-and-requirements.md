# South Africa: FICA duties of high-value goods dealers (jewellers, gold, bullion, coin and stone dealers), turned into product requirements

Status: draft, sections being completed (10 Oct 2026). Almost every fact below comes from primary documents on fic.gov.za (the Act, the Regulations, gazetted directives, PCCs, guidance notes, the FIC annual report and published sanction notices). I downloaded and read them myself.

Short names used below:
- **Act** = Financial Intelligence Centre Act 38 of 2001, consolidated text published by the FIC ([Act PDF](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Financial-Intelligence-Centre-Act-2001-Act-38-of-2001.pdf)). The cover page lists amendments only up to 2017, but the text already contains the 2022 changes (proliferation financing, s42(2A)-(2C), s42(2)(qA)), so I treat it as current.
- **Regs** = Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Control Regulations, GN R.1595 of 2002 as amended up to GN R2943 ([Regs PDF](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Money-Laundering-and-Terrorist-Financing-Control-Regulations.pdf)).
- **FIC** = Financial Intelligence Centre. **goAML** = the FIC's registration and reporting platform ([goAML login](https://goweb.fic.gov.za/goAMLWEb_PRD/Home)).
- **HVGD** = high-value goods dealer (Schedule 1 item 20). **DPMS** = dealers in precious metals and stones.
- **RMCP** = risk management and compliance programme (s42). **RCR** = risk and compliance return. **CTR** = cash threshold report (s28). **STR/SAR** = suspicious or unusual transaction or activity report (s29). **TPR** = terrorist property report (s28A). **TFS** = targeted financial sanctions.
- **PCC** = public compliance communication. **GN** = guidance note. The FIC says its guidance is "authoritative" and that a firm that does not follow it must show it complied "in an equivalent manner" ([PCC 58, preface](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/2024.03-PCC-HVGD-guidance.pdf)).

## Summary

- **The duty is national, statutory and now recurs every year.** Item 20 of Schedule 1 has made high-value goods dealers "accountable institutions" since 19 December 2022 ([Act, Schedule 1 item 20](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Financial-Intelligence-Centre-Act-2001-Act-38-of-2001.pdf); in-force date per [FIC sanction notice, para 2](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Administrative-sanction-%E2%80%93-Miller-Gold-House-Pty-Ltd.pdf)). The FIC itself supervises them; item 20 has no other supervisory body in Schedule 2 ([Act, Schedule 2](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Financial-Intelligence-Centre-Act-2001-Act-38-of-2001.pdf)).
- **Who.** Anyone who, as a regular part of business, buys or sells a physical item (or a bundle sold as one unit) for R100,000 or more, paid in any form, in one payment or linked payments. This includes jewellers who sell even one R100,000 ring, Krugerrand and coin dealers, bullion dealers, diamond and coloured-stone dealers, and second-hand or scrap gold buyers who buy a R100,000 lot ([PCC 58, paras 1.4-1.5, 3.1, examples 2-5](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/2024.03-PCC-HVGD-guidance.pdf)). There is no small-firm exemption.
- **What must exist on paper.** A board- or owner-approved RMCP covering the 19 elements of s42(2) (a) to (s), with a written entity-wide risk assessment behind it ([Act s42](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Financial-Intelligence-Centre-Act-2001-Act-38-of-2001.pdf); [GN 7B, paras 180-190CC](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Guidance-Note-7B-%E2%80%93-Implementation-of-various-aspects-of-the-FIC-Act.pdf)). A compliance function or compliance officer (s42A). Ongoing staff training (s43). Employee screening records (Directive 8). CDD files and transaction records kept for 5 years (s22, 22A, 23).
- **What must be filed, and when.**
  - Register on goAML within 90 days of opening; report changes within 90 days ([Regs 27A(3)](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Money-Laundering-and-Terrorist-Financing-Control-Regulations.pdf); [Act s43B(4)](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Financial-Intelligence-Centre-Act-2001-Act-38-of-2001.pdf)).
  - **RMCP upload on goAML by 31 October every year** (first one 31 October 2026), within 90 days of opening for new dealers, and within 10 days of approving any updated RMCP ([Directive 12, Gazette 55337, 4 Sep 2026](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Directive-12-On-the-submission-of-risk-management-and-compliance-programmes.pdf)). Format: one PDF named YYYYMMDD_RMCP.pdf, where the date is the approval date ([FIC "How to submit an RMCP"](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/HTSRMCP.png)).
  - **Geographic locations of head office, branches and subsidiaries** by 29 October 2026 for existing registrants with more than one location, then within 90 days of any change ([Directive 10, in force 31 Jul 2026](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Directive-10-On-information-pertaining-to-geographic-locations.pdf)).
  - **Risk and compliance return** when the FIC issues a directive. The 2026 round (Directive 11) ran from 4 May to 31 July 2026 for item 20 and asked for data for three periods back to 1 July 2023 ([Directive 11, Gazette 54439](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Directive-11-%E2%80%93-Risk-and-compliance-return.pdf)).
  - **Event reports:** cash of R50,000 or more (strictly "above R49,999.99") within 3 business days; suspicious or unusual transactions within 15 business days; property of a sanctioned person within 5 business days ([Regs 22B, 24](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Money-Laundering-and-Terrorist-Financing-Control-Regulations.pdf)).
- **The FIC enforces it, and jewellers are its most-inspected group per head.** In 2025/26 the FIC issued 549 inspection reports, 61 of them to precious metal and stone dealers (17 metals, 12 metals and stones, 32 stones; 43 of the 61 in Gauteng) and 37 to motor dealers. That is 61 inspections on 664 registered jewellery, bullion, coin and stone registrations. It issued 361 "admission of non-compliance" fines (R10,000 each when settled) for missing RCRs or registration. An item 20 dealer was fined R210,000 (R105,000 payable, R105,000 suspended) for an inadequate RMCP, no sanctions screening and a registration failure (under appeal) ([FIC Annual Report 2025/26, pp. 40-47](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FIC-Annual-Report-2025-2026.pdf)).
- **What goAML leaves undone.** goAML only receives filings. It does not draft or version the RMCP, record board approval, keep the CDD file, compute RCR statistics for three periods, track the 3/5/15-day report clocks, keep a training register or produce the evidence pack inspectors ask for. Inspectors' most common findings are exactly these gaps: no RMCP or an inadequate one, failure to send the RMCP when asked, weak CDD on legal entities and PEPs, late registration or stale registration details, and no evidence of sanctions screening ([FIC AR 2025/26, p. 41](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FIC-Annual-Report-2025-2026.pdf)).
- **Upcoming.** Draft PCC 126 (30 Sep 2026, comments close 16 Oct 2026) would require one goAML registration per SADPMR permit or licence, adds source-of-goods checks and permit checks on clients, and confirms silver and laboratory-grown stones are in scope ([draft PCC 126](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026.9-PCC-DPMS.pdf)).
- **One legal trap for the product.** FIC guidance (PCC 53's editable RMCP template, GN 7B) is copyright. It may be reproduced "in an unaltered form only for personal and non-commercial use" ([PCC 53, copyright notice](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/2022.08-PCC-PCC-53-RMCP.pdf); [GN 7B, preface vi](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Guidance-Note-7B-%E2%80%93-Implementation-of-various-aspects-of-the-FIC-Act.pdf)). The product's RMCP text must be original, written to the Act, and only cite the guidance.
