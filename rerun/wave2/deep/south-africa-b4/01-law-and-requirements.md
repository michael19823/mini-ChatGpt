# South Africa: RMCP and FIC filing duties for small Schedule 1 institutions, turned into product requirements

Status: IN PROGRESS (started 2026-10-10). Sections are being filled. Facts carried over from the lead report are marked "(from lead report, re-check)" until confirmed.

## Working notes (confirmed so far)

- **Directive 12, primary text read.** Government Notice No. 7887, Government Gazette No. 55337, 4 Sep 2026, signed by FIC Director Thabiso Thiti. Issued under FIC Act s43A(1). Purpose: the manner and time frame for submitting "a copy of the documentation describing their RMCP" under s42(4) (para 2). Applies to Schedule 1 items 1, 2, 3, 9, 11 (excluding banks, mutual banks and co-operative bank credit providers), 14, 20, 21 and 22 (para 4). Submission "on an annual basis" per Annexure A (para 5), via "the Centre's registration and reporting platform", from Monday 7 Sep 2026 (para 6). New institutions: within 90 days of commencing business (para 7). Update approved under s42(2B) after the Annexure A period: submit within ten (10) days of approval (para 8). Breach: administrative sanction under s42(4) read with s61(c) and s45C (para 9). Annexure A: items 1, 2, 9, 11 by 9 Oct 2026 and by 9 Oct annually; items 3, 14, 20, 21, 22 by 31 Oct 2026 and by 31 Oct annually ([FIC, Directive 12 gazette PDF](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Directive-12-On-the-submission-of-risk-management-and-compliance-programmes.pdf)).


- **FIC Act text read on Acts Online (consolidated, with amendment notes).** s42(1) RMCP duty (as substituted by GLAA Act 22 of 2022, in force 31 Dec 2022, adds proliferation financing); s42(2)(a)-(s) contents, incl. (qA) group-wide programmes; s42(2A) state which paras do not apply and why; s42(2B) board/senior management/highest authority approval; s42(2C) review at regular intervals; s42(3) available to employees; s42(4) on request copy to Centre or supervisory body (Acts Online still shows the words "internal rules", unverified whether the text is current). s61(a)-(c): non-compliance with 42(1),(2),(2A),(2B),(2C),(3),(4) = administrative sanction. s42A governance: board or senior management ensure compliance; legal persons need a compliance function and a competent senior person; non-legal persons other than sole practitioners must appoint a competent person (s42A(4)). s43 ongoing training; s62 sanction. s43A directives (gazette after consulting supervisory bodies; draft and submissions; s43A(5) costs on the institution). s43B registration, changes within 90 days (s43B(4)); s61A sanction. s45B inspections (premises, private residence only with warrant or consent). s45C sanctions: caution, reprimand, remedial directive, restriction/suspension, fine up to R10m natural / R50m legal person; personal liability (4)(b); suspension up to 5 years (4)(c); publication of decisions (11). s23: records 5 years. s24: third-party record keeping allowed if free and easy access; institution liable; must give the FIC "prescribed particulars" of the third party (s24(3); Reg 20 lists them). Schedule 1 items 1-23 and Schedule 2 (only FSCA, SARB/PA, FinSurv remain as supervisory bodies; former bodies deleted by Notice 2800 eff. 19 Dec 2022 and GLAA 2022), so the FIC itself supervises items 1, 2, 3, 9, 11, 14, 20, 21, 22 (my reading of s45(1) with Schedule 2) (https://www.acts.co.za/financial-intelligence-centre-act-2001/).
- **MLTFC Regulations (GN R1595 of 2002) read.** Reg 1A single transaction R5,000; Reg 20 third-party record keeper particulars; Reg 22B CTR threshold R49,999.99 (eff. 14 Nov 2022); Reg 24: s28A TPR within 5 days, s29 STR within 15 days, s28 CTR within 3 days; Reg 27A registration within 90 days of business opening, electronic, no fee, not a licence.
