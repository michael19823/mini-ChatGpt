# South Africa: RMCP and FIC filing duties for small Schedule 1 institutions, turned into product requirements

Status: IN PROGRESS (started 2026-10-10). Sections are being filled. Facts carried over from the lead report are marked "(from lead report, re-check)" until confirmed.

## Working notes (confirmed so far)

- **Directive 12, primary text read.** Government Notice No. 7887, Government Gazette No. 55337, 4 Sep 2026, signed by FIC Director Thabiso Thiti. Issued under FIC Act s43A(1). Purpose: the manner and time frame for submitting "a copy of the documentation describing their RMCP" under s42(4) (para 2). Applies to Schedule 1 items 1, 2, 3, 9, 11 (excluding banks, mutual banks and co-operative bank credit providers), 14, 20, 21 and 22 (para 4). Submission "on an annual basis" per Annexure A (para 5), via "the Centre's registration and reporting platform", from Monday 7 Sep 2026 (para 6). New institutions: within 90 days of commencing business (para 7). Update approved under s42(2B) after the Annexure A period: submit within ten (10) days of approval (para 8). Breach: administrative sanction under s42(4) read with s61(c) and s45C (para 9). Annexure A: items 1, 2, 9, 11 by 9 Oct 2026 and by 9 Oct annually; items 3, 14, 20, 21, 22 by 31 Oct 2026 and by 31 Oct annually ([FIC, Directive 12 gazette PDF](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Directive-12-On-the-submission-of-risk-management-and-compliance-programmes.pdf)).


- **FIC Act text read on Acts Online (consolidated, with amendment notes).** s42(1) RMCP duty (as substituted by GLAA Act 22 of 2022, in force 31 Dec 2022, adds proliferation financing); s42(2)(a)-(s) contents, incl. (qA) group-wide programmes; s42(2A) state which paras do not apply and why; s42(2B) board/senior management/highest authority approval; s42(2C) review at regular intervals; s42(3) available to employees; s42(4) on request copy to Centre or supervisory body (Acts Online still shows the words "internal rules", unverified whether the text is current). s61(a)-(c): non-compliance with 42(1),(2),(2A),(2B),(2C),(3),(4) = administrative sanction. s42A governance: board or senior management ensure compliance; legal persons need a compliance function and a competent senior person; non-legal persons other than sole practitioners must appoint a competent person (s42A(4)). s43 ongoing training; s62 sanction. s43A directives (gazette after consulting supervisory bodies; draft and submissions; s43A(5) costs on the institution). s43B registration, changes within 90 days (s43B(4)); s61A sanction. s45B inspections (premises, private residence only with warrant or consent). s45C sanctions: caution, reprimand, remedial directive, restriction/suspension, fine up to R10m natural / R50m legal person; personal liability (4)(b); suspension up to 5 years (4)(c); publication of decisions (11). s23: records 5 years. s24: third-party record keeping allowed if free and easy access; institution liable; must give the FIC "prescribed particulars" of the third party (s24(3); Reg 20 lists them). Schedule 1 items 1-23 and Schedule 2 (only FSCA, SARB/PA, FinSurv remain as supervisory bodies; former bodies deleted by Notice 2800 eff. 19 Dec 2022 and GLAA 2022), so the FIC itself supervises items 1, 2, 3, 9, 11, 14, 20, 21, 22 (my reading of s45(1) with Schedule 2) (https://www.acts.co.za/financial-intelligence-centre-act-2001/).
- **MLTFC Regulations (GN R1595 of 2002) read.** Reg 1A single transaction R5,000; Reg 20 third-party record keeper particulars; Reg 22B CTR threshold R49,999.99 (eff. 14 Nov 2022); Reg 24: s28A TPR within 5 days, s29 STR within 15 days, s28 CTR within 3 days; Reg 27A registration within 90 days of business opening, electronic, no fee, not a licence.
- **RCR / Directive 11, from FIC page and PCC 60 (read).** Directive 11 gazetted Tue 31 Mar 2026, in force Wed 1 Apr 2026 (PCC 60 paras Objective, 3.1). Window opened Mon 4 May 2026. Deadlines 17:00 on Tue 30 Jun 2026 (items 2, 9 casinos, 11 excl. banks, 14, 21, 22) and Fri 31 Jul 2026 (items 1, 3, 9 non-casino, 20). Data for three periods in one form (1 Apr 2023 or 1 Jul 2023 to 31 Mar 2026). One RCR per Org ID; one Org ID per Schedule 1 item; branches not separately registered do not file. Compliance officer with compliance function must submit; board/senior manager/highest authority stays responsible; "Third-party service providers are not allowed to submit the 2026 RCR on behalf of the accountable institution" (PCC 60 para 4.10). goAML credentials used; sharing credentials prohibited by Directive 2 of 2014 (4.11). Save several times (4.15); download a copy after submission (4.18); no retraction, errors via written compliance query (4.19). The FIC feeds RCR data into a risk-rating tool to pick institutions for its supervisory plan (FIC RCR page). PCC copyright: reproduction only unaltered and for non-commercial use within the institution (PCC 60 cover). Sector questionnaires published May 2026, e.g. legal practitioner questionnaire, 16 pages: Part 1 general risk (1.1-1.63), Part 2 PF (2.1-2.11), Part 3 TF (3.1-3.32), Part 4 sector-specific (4.1-4.3x) (https://www.fic.gov.za/risk-and-compliance-return-submissions/; https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026.6-PCC-60-RCR-_On-RCR-Submission.pdf; https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Legal-practitioner-questionnaire.pdf).
- **FIC "How to submit an RMCP" graphic (uploaded 9 Oct 2026, read).** Only via goAML. Step 1: save the approved RMCP as PDF named YYYYMMDD_RMCP.pdf using the approval date (example 20250304_RMCP.pdf). Step 2: goAML > profile > "My Org Details". Step 3: in comments below the address type "RMCP submission" to activate "Submit Request". Step 4: Attachment > Upload. Step 5: Submit Request > Continue (https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/HTSRMCP.png).
- **Directive 12 consultation feedback note, 4 Sep 2026 (read).** Paras 5-6 one submission per institution if branches are not separate AIs, standalone AI branches file their own; para 7 one RMCP per item registration (Org ID); para 9 no transition; para 11 further directives may cover all other AIs incl. authorised dealers and financial institutions; para 13 only item 11 entities in a bank/mutual bank/co-op bank group are out; para 15 RCRs under Directives 6 and 7 (Microsoft Forms link), Directive 11 RCR (RCR system) and Directive 12 RMCP (goAML) are different obligations; para 16 wrong submissions may be penalised; paras 18-20 upload proves only correct format and naming, not adequacy; para 19 FIC and supervisory bodies will access RMCPs for guidance, monitoring, inspections and enforcement; para 22 submit the RMCP documentation as approved; para 23 commentator notes GN 7B "requires RMCP documentation to be comprehensive and not merely to reference other documents"; para 24 user guide on naming and format; para 28 every approved amendment within 10 days, FIC gives no advice on materiality; para 30 10 days kept; para 33 group-wide RMCP: submit extracts as annexures (https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Consultation-feedback-note-Relating-to-draft-Directive-12-on-the-submission-of-RMCPs.pdf).
- **Directive 10 of 2026 (read).** s43A(1) notice dated 31 Jul 2026, effective on gazette publication; FIC info sheet says in force Fri 31 Jul 2026. Same items as Directive 12. Institutions with more than one location must give, for head office, each SA and foreign branch, each subsidiary head office and each subsidiary branch: name, licence no., registration no., business address, and name and contact of the s42A(2)(b) compliance person (para 5.1). Existing registrants update within 90 days of commencement (para 5.2), i.e. by about 29 Oct 2026 (my calculation); later changes within 90 days (5.3). Sanction under s43B(2) with s61A and s45C (para 6). goAML steps: head-office profile > active organisations > "new delegating organisation" (https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Directive-10-On-information-pertaining-to-geographic-locations.pdf; https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Directive-10-information-sheet-3-1.pdf).
