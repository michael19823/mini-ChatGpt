# South Africa: RMCP and FIC filing duties for small Schedule 1 institutions, turned into product requirements

Status: IN PROGRESS (started 2026-10-10). Sections are being filled. Facts carried over from the lead report are marked "(from lead report, re-check)" until confirmed.

## Working notes (confirmed so far)

- **Directive 12, primary text read.** Government Notice No. 7887, Government Gazette No. 55337, 4 Sep 2026, signed by FIC Director Thabiso Thiti. Issued under FIC Act s43A(1). Purpose: the manner and time frame for submitting "a copy of the documentation describing their RMCP" under s42(4) (para 2). Applies to Schedule 1 items 1, 2, 3, 9, 11 (excluding banks, mutual banks and co-operative bank credit providers), 14, 20, 21 and 22 (para 4). Submission "on an annual basis" per Annexure A (para 5), via "the Centre's registration and reporting platform", from Monday 7 Sep 2026 (para 6). New institutions: within 90 days of commencing business (para 7). Update approved under s42(2B) after the Annexure A period: submit within ten (10) days of approval (para 8). Breach: administrative sanction under s42(4) read with s61(c) and s45C (para 9). Annexure A: items 1, 2, 9, 11 by 9 Oct 2026 and by 9 Oct annually; items 3, 14, 20, 21, 22 by 31 Oct 2026 and by 31 Oct annually ([FIC, Directive 12 gazette PDF](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Directive-12-On-the-submission-of-risk-management-and-compliance-programmes.pdf)).


- **FIC Act text read on Acts Online (consolidated, with amendment notes).** s42(1) RMCP duty (as substituted by GLAA Act 22 of 2022, in force 31 Dec 2022, adds proliferation financing); s42(2)(a)-(s) contents, incl. (qA) group-wide programmes; s42(2A) state which paras do not apply and why; s42(2B) board/senior management/highest authority approval; s42(2C) review at regular intervals; s42(3) available to employees; s42(4) on request copy to Centre or supervisory body (Acts Online still shows the words "internal rules", unverified whether the text is current). s61(a)-(c): non-compliance with 42(1),(2),(2A),(2B),(2C),(3),(4) = administrative sanction. s42A governance: board or senior management ensure compliance; legal persons need a compliance function and a competent senior person; non-legal persons other than sole practitioners must appoint a competent person (s42A(4)). s43 ongoing training; s62 sanction. s43A directives (gazette after consulting supervisory bodies; draft and submissions; s43A(5) costs on the institution). s43B registration, changes within 90 days (s43B(4)); s61A sanction. s45B inspections (premises, private residence only with warrant or consent). s45C sanctions: caution, reprimand, remedial directive, restriction/suspension, fine up to R10m natural / R50m legal person; personal liability (4)(b); suspension up to 5 years (4)(c); publication of decisions (11). s23: records 5 years. s24: third-party record keeping allowed if free and easy access; institution liable; must give the FIC "prescribed particulars" of the third party (s24(3); Reg 20 lists them). Schedule 1 items 1-23 and Schedule 2 (only FSCA, SARB/PA, FinSurv remain as supervisory bodies; former bodies deleted by Notice 2800 eff. 19 Dec 2022 and GLAA 2022), so the FIC itself supervises items 1, 2, 3, 9, 11, 14, 20, 21, 22 (my reading of s45(1) with Schedule 2) (https://www.acts.co.za/financial-intelligence-centre-act-2001/).
- **MLTFC Regulations (GN R1595 of 2002) read.** Reg 1A single transaction R5,000; Reg 20 third-party record keeper particulars; Reg 22B CTR threshold R49,999.99 (eff. 14 Nov 2022); Reg 24: s28A TPR within 5 days, s29 STR within 15 days, s28 CTR within 3 days; Reg 27A registration within 90 days of business opening, electronic, no fee, not a licence.
- **RCR / Directive 11, from FIC page and PCC 60 (read).** Directive 11 gazetted Tue 31 Mar 2026, in force Wed 1 Apr 2026 (PCC 60 paras Objective, 3.1). Window opened Mon 4 May 2026. Deadlines 17:00 on Tue 30 Jun 2026 (items 2, 9 casinos, 11 excl. banks, 14, 21, 22) and Fri 31 Jul 2026 (items 1, 3, 9 non-casino, 20). Data for three periods in one form (1 Apr 2023 or 1 Jul 2023 to 31 Mar 2026). One RCR per Org ID; one Org ID per Schedule 1 item; branches not separately registered do not file. Compliance officer with compliance function must submit; board/senior manager/highest authority stays responsible; "Third-party service providers are not allowed to submit the 2026 RCR on behalf of the accountable institution" (PCC 60 para 4.10). goAML credentials used; sharing credentials prohibited by Directive 2 of 2014 (4.11). Save several times (4.15); download a copy after submission (4.18); no retraction, errors via written compliance query (4.19). The FIC feeds RCR data into a risk-rating tool to pick institutions for its supervisory plan (FIC RCR page). PCC copyright: reproduction only unaltered and for non-commercial use within the institution (PCC 60 cover). Sector questionnaires published May 2026, e.g. legal practitioner questionnaire, 16 pages: Part 1 general risk (1.1-1.63), Part 2 PF (2.1-2.11), Part 3 TF (3.1-3.32), Part 4 sector-specific (4.1-4.3x) (https://www.fic.gov.za/risk-and-compliance-return-submissions/; https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026.6-PCC-60-RCR-_On-RCR-Submission.pdf; https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Legal-practitioner-questionnaire.pdf).

- **Directive 12 consultation feedback note, 4 Sep 2026 (read).** Paras 5-6 one submission per institution if branches are not separate AIs, standalone AI branches file their own; para 7 one RMCP per item registration (Org ID); para 9 no transition; para 11 further directives may cover all other AIs incl. authorised dealers and financial institutions; para 13 only item 11 entities in a bank/mutual bank/co-op bank group are out; para 15 RCRs under Directives 6 and 7 (Microsoft Forms link), Directive 11 RCR (RCR system) and Directive 12 RMCP (goAML) are different obligations; para 16 wrong submissions may be penalised; paras 18-20 upload proves only correct format and naming, not adequacy; para 19 FIC and supervisory bodies will access RMCPs for guidance, monitoring, inspections and enforcement; para 22 submit the RMCP documentation as approved; para 23 commentator notes GN 7B "requires RMCP documentation to be comprehensive and not merely to reference other documents"; para 24 user guide on naming and format; para 28 every approved amendment within 10 days, FIC gives no advice on materiality; para 30 10 days kept; para 33 group-wide RMCP: submit extracts as annexures (https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Consultation-feedback-note-Relating-to-draft-Directive-12-on-the-submission-of-RMCPs.pdf).
- **Directive 10 of 2026 (read).** s43A(1) notice dated 31 Jul 2026, effective on gazette publication; FIC info sheet says in force Fri 31 Jul 2026. Same items as Directive 12. Institutions with more than one location must give, for head office, each SA and foreign branch, each subsidiary head office and each subsidiary branch: name, licence no., registration no., business address, and name and contact of the s42A(2)(b) compliance person (para 5.1). Existing registrants update within 90 days of commencement (para 5.2), i.e. by about 29 Oct 2026 (my calculation); later changes within 90 days (5.3). Sanction under s43B(2) with s61A and s45C (para 6). goAML steps: head-office profile > active organisations > "new delegating organisation" (https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Directive-10-On-information-pertaining-to-geographic-locations.pdf; https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Directive-10-information-sheet-3-1.pdf).

Schedule 1 items 1, 2, 3, 9, 11, 14, 20, 21, 22 read (as substituted by Notice 2800, GG 47596, eff. 19 Dec 2022). Item 20: dealer in high-value goods for any transaction with payment in any form of R100,000 or more (single or linked), "high-value goods" = item valued at R100,000 or more. Schedule 2 lists only FSCA (items 4, 5, 12), SARB/PA and related. Exemption 10 in the MLTFC Regulations exempts item 1 functions from Parts 1 and 2 of Chapter 3 (CDD, record keeping) except listed transactional work; current status (unverified). - **Directive 8 of 2023 (Notice 3257, read on Acts Online).** Applies to all AIs. Screen prospective and current employees for competence and integrity periodically, risk-based (2.3); scrutinise employee info against TFS notices under s26A(3) (2.4); record the manner (2.5) and outcomes (2.6); produce on request (2.7) (https://www.acts.co.za/financial-intelligence-centre-act-2001/n3257_2__directive.php).
- **Directive 10 = Notice 7750, GG 55097, 31 Jul 2026 (Acts Online).** Confirms gazette details (https://www.acts.co.za/financial-intelligence-centre-act-2001/n7750_notice_no__7750_of_2026.php).
- **GN 7B final, 3 Aug 2026, 77 pages (PDF read).** Preface iii: guidance is "authoritative"; not following it requires showing an equivalent level of compliance; enforcement may follow. Preface vi: copyright, reproduction unaltered and non-commercial only. Para 37A: risk-assess new products, services, practices, channels and new technologies before launch and on material change. Para 40A: low-income persons not automatically low risk. Para 60: systems, controls and CDD levels per risk level must be documented in the RMCP. Paras 168-174: records may be electronic or cloud; free and easy access; legible reproduction; off-site third party details to FIC; tamper-proof and access safeguards (172); if stored abroad and access could be restricted, keep copies in SA (173); assess third-party storage providers (174). Chapter 4 paras 180-190CC: RMCP must record all s42 elements (180); acknowledge board/senior management ultimate responsibility (181); approval cannot be delegated, incl. for sole proprietors (181C-E); must address full s42 scope (181F); describe comprehensively, not merely reference other documents (181G); enough detail for approvers to apply their minds (181H-J); RMCP given to FIC must include the approval (181L); inadequate RMCP may lead to sanctions, approvers sanctionable under s61 (181M); Example 5: unapproved revised RMCP in use = non-compliance; three-part minimum structure: Part 1 risk identification and assessment incl. risk appetite, Part 2 mitigation (CDD, reporting, record keeping), Part 3 monitoring of control effectiveness (183A); entity-wide risk assessment first (183B-C), group entities (183D), reflect national and sector risk assessments (183E); documents not referenced are not part of the RMCP (183G); describe the approvers, compliance function, seniority and experience (184); also training, board reporting, risk methodology, CDD decision escalation, escalation for new products, onboarding, monitoring and risk profile changes (184A); proportional to size (185); say which s42 elements do not apply and why or what alternative controls (185A); review at regular intervals, reviews and amendments documented and approved (190); DNFBPs referred to PCC 53 and its template (190A); inspectors check approval and Parts 1-3 (190AA-CC) (https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Guidance-Note-7B-%E2%80%93-Implementation-of-various-aspects-of-the-FIC-Act.pdf).
- **PCC 53 (30 Aug 2022, 44 pages, PDF read).** For DNFBPs not in groups and without advanced or complex structures (1.6). On a s42(4) request give the RMCP document; failure to produce it = non-compliance (1.4); available to every employee (1.5). Nine themes (1.8): governance; risk assessment and risk-rating framework; CDD; TFS-TF; TFS-PF (s26A-C); PIPs; account monitoring; reporting; record keeping. Governance (2.1-2.8): roles of s42A compliance officer, compliance function and approvers; name the compliance officer and show competence and seniority; documented approval; review dates (FIC recommends annually, 2.4); implementation and dissemination; training; escalation of breaches; remediation. Risk framework (3.1-3.5): business-level and business-area risk assessment, new products and services, client-level risk assessment before onboarding and periodically, named indicators, a client risk matrix (Table 1: client, product/service, geography, delivery channel, other; scores 1-3; total 5-15 mapped to SDD/standard/EDD; automatic high-risk e.g. FPPO; automatic decline e.g. TFS-listed), rating intervals, record outcome on client file, document decisions. CDD (4.1-4.12) incl. once-off transactions under R5,000 where suspicious. TFS (5-6): UNSC 1267 lists under POCDATARA s25 and s26A lists; systems; freezing. PIP (7.1-7.4): DPIP/FPPO, family and close associates, senior management approval, source of funds and wealth, data sources. Monitoring (8.1-8.2) under s21C. Reporting (9.1-9.4): end-to-end process for s28, 28A, 29 reports, who submits, periods; written findings to report or not; s27, 32, 34 requests and s35 monitoring orders; tipping-off. Record keeping (10.1-10.4) incl. Reg 20 third-party details. Annexure A: s42 reference table. Annexure B: editable template; "The Centre strongly cautions against accountable institutions copying this document as is"; an uncustomised template = non-compliant. Annexure B review clause: at least annually, within X months of material change, within X of legislative change. Annexure C: risk indicators by product/service, delivery channel, client, other. Copyright: unaltered, non-commercial reproduction only (https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/2022.08-PCC-PCC-53-RMCP.pdf; listing https://www.fic.gov.za/document/pcc-53-rmcp/).
- **Directive 11 of 2026 gazette read (GN 7334, GG 54439, 31 Mar 2026).** Issued under s43A(3)(a) (para 2.1). Items 1, 2, 3, 9, 11 (non-bank), 14, 20, 21, 22 (4.1-4.2). Answer all applicable questions based on current understanding of ML/TF/PF risk and current controls (5.2). Periods: items 1, 2, 3, 9 from 1 Apr 2023; items 11, 14, 20, 21, 22 from 1 Jul 2023; to 31 Mar 2026 (5.3). Completed directly on the RCR platform via the FIC website (5.4). Schedule: items 11, 14, 21, 22, 2 and 9 (casinos) by 30 Jun 2026 17:00; items 20, 1, 3, 9 (non-casinos) by 31 Jul 2026 17:00. Breach: administrative sanction under s62E (para 6). In force 1 Apr 2026 (para 7). Directive covers only "the 2026" return; no recurring frequency stated (https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Directive-11-%E2%80%93-Risk-and-compliance-return.pdf).
- **LPC Advisory Notice AN/FIC/15052026 (15 May 2026, read).** Scope: attorneys (incl. conveyancers, notaries) practising for own account s34(5)(a) LPA; advocates with fidelity fund certificate s34(2)(a)(ii); commercial juristic entities s34(7). RCR via goAML only, no paper/e-mail; valid Org ID needed; one RCR per Org ID; separate RCR per Org ID for multi-item or branch registrations; no withdrawal or amendment after submission. Tells practitioners to make sure the RMCP is current, implemented and documented and to name a responsible person. The LPC (the professional regulator) relays the FIC duty but is not the FIC Act supervisor (https://lpc.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Advisory-Notice-Risk-and-Compliance-returns.pdf).
- **More Act text (Acts Online).** s61 heading now "Failure to comply with duty in respect of RMCP": (a) s42(1),(2),(2A); (aA) approval s42(2B); (aB) review s42(2C); (b) staff availability s42(3); (c) "make a copy of its Risk Management and Compliance Programme available to the Centre or a supervisory body in terms of section 42(4)". s61A failure to register or give s43B information. s62 failure to train. s62E failure to comply with a s43A(3) directive. s45D appeal within 30 days; decided only on the written evidence that was before the FIC when it decided (s45D(3), (3A)). Reg 20(a)-(f) third-party record keeper particulars, "without delay" (https://www.acts.co.za/financial-intelligence-centre-act-2001/61__failure_to_formulate_and_implement_internal_rules.php ; .../62e__failure_to_comply_with_di.php ; .../45d__appeal.php ; .../r1595_20__particulars_of_third_parties_keeping_records.php).

- **PCC 47A (17 Oct 2023, read).** Item 1 covers: (1) attorneys incl. conveyancers and notaries practising for own account with a fidelity fund certificate; (2) advocates under s34(2)(a)(ii) LPA who take briefs directly from the public, with a fidelity fund certificate; (3) commercial juristic entities under s34(7) LPA, incl. where shareholding, partnership or membership is attorneys only (2.2). Risk indicators for lawyers at 4.3.x (e.g. cash into trust account, 4.3.10) (https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/2023.10-PCC-PCC-47A-Guidance-on-the-interpretation-of-Legal-Practitioners.pdf).
