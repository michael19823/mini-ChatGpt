# SA RMCP filing kit: market size, buyers and competition (deep dive 02)

Date: 10 Oct 2026. Builds on [the B4 report](../reports/south-africa-b4.md). Scope: market size, buyers, competition, channels and regional expansion. Law, product and go-to-market detail are covered by the other files.

Status: complete (10 Oct 2026). Searches were in English, plus one in Afrikaans ("risikobestuur- en nakomingsprogram"). The Afrikaans search returned only English pages, e.g. [Property Professional](https://propertyprofessional.co.za/2026/05/26/warning-to-estate-agents-fic-enforcement-is-no-longer-optional/). FIC and trade material on this duty is published in English.

## Summary

- **Who must file.** Directive 12 covers 46,622 FIC registrations (31 Mar 2026). Without estate agents it is 36,927. Only 10,675 RMCPs reached the FIC from these sectors in 2025/26, about 23% ([FIC AR 2025/26](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FIC-Annual-Report-2025-2026.pdf), my calculation).
- **Real firms.** About 9,300 law firms, 75% of them sole practitioners ([LSSA](https://www.lssa.org.za/about-us/about-the-attorneys-profession/statistics-for-the-attorneys-profession/)). Add about 2,600 trust and company service providers, 2,700 credit providers (8,147 on the NCR register), 4,300 motor dealers, 1,300 other high-value goods dealers and 362 crypto firms. The serviceable market is **about 20,500 small firms**, excluding gambling and estate agents.
- **Pain is visible.** "No RMCP" is the FIC's top inspection finding. 549 inspection reports were issued in 2025/26. Only 9.7% of legal practitioners had filed their 2026 RCR two weeks before the deadline. Typical settled fines are about R10,000; R25,000-R175,000 cases are common in the record ([FIC AR 2025/26](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FIC-Annual-Report-2025-2026.pdf); [Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/fic-flags-filing-errors-as-rcr-deadline-closes/)).
- **Willingness to pay.** Buyers already pay R3,500 a year (eFICA Builder), R4,995 once (Moonstone template), R8,500 (eFICA drafted RMCP) and R1,200 for a 2.5-hour LEAD webinar ([efica.co.za](https://efica.co.za/); [Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/new-do-it-yourself-fica-compliance-solution-for-accountable-institutions/); [LEAD](https://www.lssalead.org.za/course/fica-webinar/)).
- **Competition is closer than the lead report said.** eFICA launches a self-service RMCP Builder at R3,500 a year in November 2026, with a free RMCP Manager. VerifyNow gives a basic RMCP generator away free. nCino KYC holds attorneys through the LSSA ([efica.co.za](https://efica.co.za/); [VerifyNow](https://www.verifynow.co.za/tools/rmcp-generator); [GoLegal](https://www.golegal.co.za/docfox-lssa-fica/)).
- **The gap that remains.** No product found serves accountants and consultants with many client entities, and none ties together the RMCP, 10-day amendments, the Directive 10 location register, RCR preparation and an inspection pack. Non-law sectors (credit providers, dealers, crypto) are under-served.
- **Best channels.** Accounting practices (buyers and resellers) via SAICA, SAIPA, SAIT, the Tax Faculty, Accounting Weekly and FISA. New law firms via LEAD's compulsory practice-management course. Credit providers via CASA (1,800+ members). Motor dealers via NADA/RMI. Sources in the Channels table.
- **Regional expansion is weak.** Namibia, Botswana, Kenya and Mauritius have AML duties for lawyers, but I found no yearly RMCP upload duty there. Namibia is the only easy add-on ([FIC Namibia](https://www.fic.na/how-we-do-it/guidance/); details in Regional expansion).
- **Positioning.** Sell "your FIC year, done and provable", not "an RMCP generator". Price at about R2,490 a year for one entity and R900 per entity for accountants (minimum 10), below eFICA.
- **Size.** 2-6% of 20,500 firms at R2,490 gives R1.0m-R3.1m a year before the accountant channel. A good small business, not a large one.

**Reading the numbers.**
- The FIC received only 10,675 RMCPs from the Directive 12 sectors in 2025/26, against 46,622 registrations. That is 23%. The FIC asked everyone for an RMCP in March 2025 ([FIC letter, 4 Mar 2025](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2025.3-GN-RMCP-Letter-Request_250304.pdf)). So about three in four registrations either have no RMCP or did not send it.
- By sector, the RMCP response was lowest for gambling (450 of 4,676, 10%), legal practitioners (4,218 of 21,034, 20%) and estate agents (2,059 of 9,695, 21%). It was highest for crypto (48%), trust and company service providers (40%) and credit providers (40%) (my calculations from FIC AR PDF p.29 and p.36).
- The core market for a product sold to small firms is therefore about **9,300 law firms, about 2,000-2,600 TCSPs (mostly accounting practices), about 2,700 credit providers (with up to 5,400 more on the NCR register), about 4,300 motor dealers, about 1,300 other high-value goods dealers, about 4,600 gambling outlets and 362 crypto firms**. Without estate agents that is roughly **25,000 firms**; with estate agents (9,695 registrations) about 35,000.

## Buyer profile and pain

**Size.** Almost all buyers are micro firms.
- Law firms: 6,969 of 9,307 firms (75%) are sole practitioners and 98% have fewer than 10 attorneys ([LSSA](https://www.lssa.org.za/about-us/about-the-attorneys-profession/statistics-for-the-attorneys-profession/)).
- Credit providers: most new NCR applicants in 2024/25 "were from sole-director businesses", mainly in Gauteng, Limpopo and KwaZulu-Natal ([NCR AR 2024/25, p.29](https://ncr.org.za/documents/pages/Annual%20Reports/NCR%20Annual%20Report%202024_2025_signed.pdf)).
- Motor dealers (4,277 of the 5,581 HVGD registrations) range from franchise dealers to small used-car lots (split not published).

**How they comply today.**
- Many do nothing until pushed. Only 23% of the Directive 12 registrations sent the FIC an RMCP in 2025/26 (my calculation from [FIC AR 2025/26, p.29 and p.36](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FIC-Annual-Report-2025-2026.pdf)).
- The FIC's main inspection findings in 2025/26 were: no RMCP developed or implemented; RMCPs that did not meet the Act; and ignoring the request to submit RMCPs (FIC AR, PDF p.40).
- Those who act use a free template (FIC PCC 53, LSSA guide, REBOSA for estate agents), a paid template (Moonstone), a KYC vendor's service (nCino KYC, eFICA) or a consultant. No survey of the split exists (unverified).
- Regulators and associations keep warning against templates. NADA told motor dealers in March 2025 that the FIC "will not accept standard templates offered in the marketplace" (search summary of [NADA](https://nada.co.za/?p=5097)). The FIC says one-person firms do not need "a 200-page document" ([Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/fic-urges-businesses-to-simplify-compliance-focus-on-risks-not-paperwork/)).

**Software they already use.**
- Law firms: practice and trust-accounting software (AJS, LegalSuite, Lexpro, GhostPractice), some with nCino KYC integrations ([GoLegal](https://www.golegal.co.za/?p=57648)). Prices are not published for South Africa (unverified).
- Estate agents: property CRMs and compliance tools such as Realty Comply ([Realty Comply](https://www.realtycomply.com/)).
- All: goAML for registration, cash threshold reports and RMCP upload. Some use KYC check vendors (VerifyNow, eFICA, nCino KYC).

**Pain, with evidence.**
- *Pressure is rising.* The FIC issued 549 inspection reports in 2025/26: 169 on legal practitioners, 111 on estate agents, 76 on credit providers, 61 on precious metals/stones dealers and 37 on motor dealers (FIC AR, Table 8, PDF p.40). It hosted 33 webinars and 16 roadshows for 14,077 attendees and answered 24,840 phone queries (FIC AR, PDF p.26 and p.34). Accounting Weekly reports 556 inspections in 2024/25, with remedial notices to 59% of those inspected (search summary of [Accounting Weekly](https://www.accountingweekly.com/trending-news/high-court-clips-fic-power-to-backdate-penalties), unverified).
- *Sanctions are real, if usually small.* 361 admission-of-non-compliance notices; 149 settled for R1.49m in total (about R10,000 each); 212 went to the Adjudication Panel; 19 sanctions on non-financial sectors totalled R635,000 (FIC AR, PDF p.43). Named cases in the report include an estate agent fined R175,000 for RMCP failures and legal practitioners fined R25,000-R50,000 for not filing the RCR (FIC AR, sanctions table).
- *Low filing rates.* 2026 RCR, 31 July group: legal practitioners 9.7% filed by 15 July ([Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/fic-flags-filing-errors-as-rcr-deadline-closes/)). Trust service providers: only 417 of 789 had filed their RCR by 10 July 2026, and 493 had uploaded an RMCP (search summary of a [FISA notice](https://www.fisa.net.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/260626_TCSP_Submission-of-2026-RCR-1.docx.pdf), unverified).
- *Confusion over channels.* RMCPs go through goAML; Directive 11 RCRs use a separate system, and earlier RCRs used Microsoft Forms; one does not satisfy the other (search summary of [Moonstone, p=61994](https://www.moonstone.co.za/?p=61994)). The FIC flagged RMCPs uploaded instead of RCRs ([Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/fic-flags-filing-errors-as-rcr-deadline-closes/)).
- *Forums.* I found no Reddit or MyBroadband threads on RMCP filing (two searches). The visible "voice of the customer" is in trade press, association notices and paid webinars, such as IEASA's "How to submit RMCP" webinar ([Quicket](https://www.quicket.co.za/events/395674-ieasa-webinar-how-to-submit-rmcp-the-step-by-step-guidance-on-the-new-directive/)).

**Reading.**
- The market already accepts **R3,500 a year** (eFICA) for a self-service builder and **R5,000-R8,500** for a drafted or template RMCP. A one-off LEAD webinar costs R1,200.
- The free generator (VerifyNow) caps what a plain PDF is worth. A buyer will pay for things the free tools do not give: a document the firm can defend at inspection, the approval and filing record, and reminders.
- Fines of R25,000-R175,000 for filing and RMCP failures are 7-50 times a R3,500 subscription. That is a clear enough sales message for firms that have been inspected or warned.

**Discussion.**

- **The market has three layers.**
  1. *Free content:* FIC PCC 53, the LSSA guide, REBOSA's pro-forma, law-firm templates and the VerifyNow free generator. Together they make a basic RMCP document free.
  2. *Templates and drafting:* Moonstone (R4,995 once), Probeta's hub (price unknown), eFICA RMCP Pro (R8,500) and consultants. These cost R5,000-R10,000 for a document, with little or no help afterwards.
  3. *KYC platforms with an RMCP add-on:* nCino KYC, eFICA, VerifyNow and AML GO. Their money is in ID checks and screening. The RMCP is there to win or keep the KYC customer.
- **The lead report's claim that "no local product does the whole job" is now only half true.** eFICA announces a self-service **RMCP Builder at R3,500 a year from November 2026**. It also has a free **RMCP Manager** with versions, publishing and a change log ([efica.co.za](https://efica.co.za/)). VerifyNow already gives a generator away free ([VerifyNow](https://www.verifynow.co.za/tools/rmcp-generator)). The "questionnaire that writes an RMCP" is therefore not unique. Two vendors already sell or give it away.
- **What is still missing (as far as I can see from public pages):**
  - No product sells an **accountant or consultant view**: one login, many client entities, each with its own Org ID, deadline (9 Oct or 31 Oct), approval date and 10-day amendment clock. eFICA's Manager is for its own clients (unverified whether it handles many entities).
  - No product ties the **whole FIC year** together: the RMCP, the Directive 10 location register (due about 29 Oct 2026), the RCR preparation sheet that mirrors the FIC's sector questionnaire, the 90-day registration changes and a training register. Each tool does one or two of these.
  - No product is built for the **non-law sectors** that the FIC now inspects: 76 inspection reports on credit providers, 61 on precious metals and stones dealers and 37 on motor dealers in 2025/26 ([FIC AR 2025/26, Table 8](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FIC-Annual-Report-2025-2026.pdf)). Most RMCP content and partnerships target attorneys and estate agents.
  - Nothing prepares a firm for **inspection**: one pack with the RMCP, risk assessment, approval minute, upload proof, training records and CDD sample. Inspectors now ask for exactly these documents (search summary of [BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/finance/861026/government-cracking-down-on-estate-agents-in-south-africa/), unverified).
- **Who is dangerous.**
  - *eFICA* is the main threat: same idea, same price band, an existing customer base ("over 500 accountable institutions"), and live from November 2026 ([efica.co.za](https://efica.co.za/)).
  - *nCino KYC* holds the attorney segment through the LSSA. Its RMCP help comes from a compliance team, and it promises firms an RMCP template with an implementation guide ([nCino blog](https://blog.kycafrica.ncino.com/legal-practice-council-warns-members-of-non-compliance)). If it adds a cheap builder for LSSA firms, the law-firm segment shrinks.
  - *Free tools* stop a buyer paying for a plain document. A paid product must give more than a PDF.
- **Who is not dangerous.** Moonstone, Probeta and consultants sell static documents and are natural resellers or content partners. LexisNexis serves large firms. KYC-only vendors could be referral partners.

## Implications for positioning and pricing

**Serviceable market (my calculation from the tables above).** Small law firms (fewer than 20 attorneys) 9,276 + TCSP registrations 2,574 + credit providers 2,697 + motor dealers 4,277 + other high-value goods dealers 1,304 + crypto firms 362 = **about 20,500**. I leave out gambling (the ~4,600 item 9 registrations are mostly outlets of route operators and bookmaker chains, unverified) and estate agents (separate idea). Firm counts for items 2 and 11 are lower than registrations, so treat 20,500 as a ceiling.

| Penetration of 20,500 | Customers | At R2,490 a year excl. VAT |
|---|---|---|
| 2% | 410 | R1.0m |
| 4% | 820 | R2.0m |
| 6% | 1,230 | R3.1m |

(My arithmetic. The channel plan for accountants comes on top; see the lead report.)

**Positioning.**
- **Do not sell "an RMCP generator".** VerifyNow gives one away and eFICA will sell one at R3,500 a year. Sell **"your FIC year, done and provable"**: a tailored RMCP and business risk assessment, an approval minute, the PDF named for goAML, an upload checklist, the 10-day amendment tracker, the Directive 10 location register, an RCR preparation sheet that follows the FIC's sector questionnaire, a training register and a one-click **inspection pack**.
- **Lead with accountants and consultants (item 2).** They are buyers themselves (2,574 registrations) and they serve credit providers, dealers and other small clients. No competitor offers them a multi-client view. Note the limit: goAML credentials may not be shared and third parties may not submit the RCR ([FIC PCC 60](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026.6-PCC-60-RCR-_On-RCR-Submission.pdf)), so the accountant prepares and tracks while each client uploads.
- **Go where nCino is weak.** Sole practitioners who do not use nCino; credit providers; motor and other high-value goods dealers (31 Oct deadline, NADA already warns against templates); crypto firms. Do not fight nCino for LSSA-partner firms.
- **Catch new firms.** New practices must file within 90 days. The LEAD PMT course reaches every new attorney practice.
- **Show the content is defensible.** The FIC and the LSSA reject generic templates. Name the FICA attorney who reviewed the content, map each section to s42(2) and GN 7B, and show which answers changed which paragraph.

**Pricing (my proposal, to test in pilots).**

| Plan | Price excl. VAT | Anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Free check | Free risk-assessment quiz and deadline calendar | Matches VerifyNow's free generator; lead magnet |
| Solo (one Org ID) | R2,490 a year | Under eFICA Builder (R3,500) and Moonstone (R4,995 once); a quarter of a typical R10,000 settled fine |
| Firm (up to 3 Org IDs or branches) | R4,490 a year | Under eFICA Pro + Builder (R10,000) |
| Accountant / consultant | R900 per entity a year, minimum 10 entities | Lets the practice resell at R1,500-R2,500 |
| Optional attorney review | R1,500-R2,500 per RMCP via a partner FICA attorney | eFICA "we draft it with you" R8,500 |

- Charge yearly, not monthly. Demand is seasonal and buyers think in "this year's filing".
- Quote in rand, including VAT for South African buyers.
- Every price is far below the fines in the record: R25,000-R50,000 for not filing an RCR, R175,000 and R7.7m for RMCP failures ([FIC AR 2025/26](https://www.fic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FIC-Annual-Report-2025-2026.pdf); [Moonstone](https://www.moonstone.co.za/r7-7m-fine-stands-as-fic-appeal-board-rules-against-law-firm/)).

**Pressure will stay.** South Africa left the FATF grey list on 24 October 2025 ([National Treasury](https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2025/2025102401%20MEDIA%20STATEMENT-SOUTH%20AFRICA%20EXITS%20THE%20FATF%20GREYLIST%20ON%2024%20OCTOBER%20%202025.pdf)). The FIC still issued Directives 10, 11 and 12 and GN 7B in 2026, and it says further directives may cover all other accountable institutions (FIC consultation feedback note on Directive 12, para 11, see [01-law-and-requirements.md](01-law-and-requirements.md)). Exit from the grey list did not reduce the paperwork.


## Open questions

1. **Real firm counts per item.** The FIC register is not public. Ask the FIC (or use a PAIA request) for Org IDs by item and size. How many of the 21,034 item 1 registrations are active firms?
2. **eFICA RMCP Builder.** What does it do at launch (November 2026)? Does it handle many entities, the 10-day rule and Directive 10? How many of its 500+ clients take it?
3. **nCino KYC.** Is RMCP help included for LSSA firms, and at what price? Is a self-serve builder coming?
4. **Gambling segment.** How many of the ~4,600 item 9 registrations are independent small operators versus outlets of chains?
5. **Credit providers in scope.** How many of the 8,147 NCR-registered credit providers must register under item 11 after PCC 23A?
6. **Consultant fees.** What do FICA attorneys and compliance consultants charge for a small-firm RMCP? No public prices found.
7. **Fines on small non-law firms.** How often are credit providers and dealers sanctioned, and for how much?
8. **Will buyers pay before being inspected?** Test with 10 sole practitioners, 5 accounting practices, 5 dealers and 5 credit providers.
9. **Practice-software partners.** Would AJS, LegalSuite or GhostPractice resell or integrate a non-nCino tool?
10. **Association counts.** Current member numbers for SAIPA, NADA and CASA come from undated or secondary pages.
