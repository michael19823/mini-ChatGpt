# Comoros (Union des Comores): Country Research

**Researched:** 2026-10-04 to 05 · **Search budget used:** 7 of 10 (small economy) · **Languages:** French, English

## Summary verdict

**There is no viable standalone indie-software opportunity in Comoros.** The formal economy is very small. The World Bank's 2025 Enterprise Survey sampled only 153 firms, and the informal economy is estimated at about 36.6% of GDP, with informal jobs making up 48% of self-employed work (2021 Labour Force Survey). The state is not running any digital-compliance push that would give a "why now". As of September 2026 there is no confirmed mandatory fiscalisation, e-invoicing clearance or real-time sales-reporting regime. The AGID (tax authority) still runs a consumption tax, not VAT; VAT appears only in longer-term IMF policy options. The 2025 reform (tax bases broadened from September 2025) brings in about KMF 1.2bn (0.18% of GDP), which means rate and base changes, not a new recurring digital workflow.

Comoros applies OHADA accounting law (SYSCOHADA). That makes it reachable as an **add-on market** for any product built for Francophone OHADA countries such as Senegal or Côte d'Ivoire, or for Madagascar. It does not justify a product of its own.

Accessibility: no US, EU or UK sanctions apply to software sales. Card and payment rails are thin, with 4 commercial banks and a few microfinance institutions and money-transfer firms (2017 count; unverified whether it is current). Collecting payment from a foreign SaaS seller would be awkward but not illegal.

## Industries screened

| Industry | Workflow looked at | Verdict | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accountants / SMEs (tax) | AGID filings, OHADA annual financial statements (DSF) | Reject as standalone; possible add-on | No e-filing or e-invoicing mandate; maybe a few hundred formal firms; covered by Sage / OHADA tools and manual Excel |
| Payroll / employers | Monthly CNSS wage and contribution declarations (due within about 15 days of month-end) | Reject | Real and monthly, but very few formal employers; no digital portal mandate found; Excel and Sage Paie are enough |
| Customs brokers / freight forwarders | Import declarations (Comoros imports most goods) | Reject | Customs system and broker workflows run on UNCTAD's ASYCUDA customs software (assumed; Comoros status not confirmed in searches); only a handful of brokers in Moroni and Mutsamudu |
| Agricultural exporters (clove, ylang-ylang, vanilla) | Organic / fair-trade certification and EU buyer traceability files | Poor distribution / too small | Few dozen exporters (e.g. Bioylang Comores, Vaniacom); UNDP and EU donor projects already fund certification; cloves (71% of exports) go mainly to Singapore and Turkey, which ask little traceability |
| Pharmacies / health | Drug import and controlled-drug reporting | Reject (unresearched depth) | Tiny market, mostly paper, regulator has no digital channel to integrate with |

## Strongest opportunities

None of the ideas reach the quality bar. The best one is recorded below for completeness, as an **add-on to a regional OHADA product**, not as something to build for Comoros.

### Opportunity: SYSCOHADA year-end and AGID filing pack (Comoros module of a regional OHADA product)

**Industry:**
Accounting firms / SME bookkeeping

**Buyer:**
Small accounting firms (cabinets comptables) and in-house accountants at formal SMEs in Moroni and Mutsamudu

**Trigger / Why now:**
Weak. The IMF-backed tax reform (bases broadened from September 2025, a new Tax Policy Unit, planned modernization of income and indirect taxes, possible future VAT) could create filing changes. Nothing in force mandates electronic filing in 2025–2026.

**Current workflow:**
1. Bookkeeping in Sage Comptabilité or Excel.
2. Year-end OHADA financial statements (DSF) built in Excel templates.
3. Tax returns prepared by hand and filed on paper or in person at the AGID.

**Pain:**
Manual reformatting of the same trial balance into OHADA statements and AGID forms. No complaint evidence or measure of hours spent was found.

**Existing solutions:**
Sage Comptabilité (dominant across OHADA countries), OHADA statement tools such as O.E.F. (O. Expert Fin.), local consultants, and Excel templates.

**The gap:**
No Comoros-specific tax-form mapping in regional tools (unverified). Too few firms to justify building one.

**Possible product:**
A Comoros country pack inside an existing or planned OHADA trial-balance-to-DSF product.

**MVP:**
Mapping templates from a trial balance to the Comoros DSF and AGID forms.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
About EUR 15–30 per month per firm (estimate). Total market likely under 100 paying firms (estimate).

**How to find first customers:**
The Comoros order of accountants (Ordre des experts-comptables), if one exists (unverified), and the Moroni chamber of commerce (UCCIA).

**Risks:**
Tiny market, payment collection, and a possible future AGID e-filing portal that changes the forms.

**Kill condition:**
Fewer than 50 formal firms filing OHADA statements, or no regional OHADA product to bolt onto.

**Score:** 2/10

## Rejected after competitor research

- **CNSS payroll declarations:** killed by Sage Paie and Excel plus a tiny employer base. The monthly obligation is confirmed, but there is no portal or integration point. Sources: https://africarrieres.com/comores/fr/guide/droit-du-travail/securite-sociale, https://africarrieres.com/comoros/en/guide/employeur-entreprise/employer-taxes
- **Customs declaration helper for brokers:** killed by the government customs system (assumed ASYCUDA; Comoros status unconfirmed) and only a handful of brokers. Source for ASYCUDA's general reach: https://unctad.org/fr/publication/rapport-asycuda-2025

## Attractive problem, poor distribution

- **Export certification and traceability files for vanilla and ylang-ylang:** the work is real for organic and fair-trade EU buyers. But the buyers are a few dozen exporters, donor projects (UNDP, EU EPA) already subsidise the work, and clove buyers in Asia ask for little. It could only work as an add-on to a Madagascar vanilla traceability product. Sources: https://www.undp.org/fr/node/218981, https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/mauritius/r%C3%A9ussite-commerciale-no16-comores-l%E2%80%99ape-stimulant-les-exportations-de-vanille-biologique-et_fr, https://www.jetro.go.jp/newsletter/osc/2025/CATALOGUE_FILIERE_YLANG-YLANG_V2.pdf, https://fr.tradingeconomics.com/comoros/exports

## Too competitive

- None really. The issue is market size, not competition. General accounting is served by Sage across OHADA countries.
