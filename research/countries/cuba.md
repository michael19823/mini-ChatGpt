# Cuba — Indie-Hacker Opportunity Research

**Researched:** 2026-10-04/05 · **Search budget used:** 4 (inaccessible / sanctioned market)
**Verdict:** **Not accessible for a foreign solo founder. No opportunity recommended.**

---

## 1. Accessibility check (done first, and decisive)

| Barrier | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| US sanctions (CACR, 31 CFR 515) | The May 2024 CACR amendment authorized US persons to provide SaaS/IaaS and other cloud services to Cuba, plus some services to independent private-sector entrepreneurs. That opening has since been narrowed. On 20 Jan 2025 an executive order revoked NSM-29 and brought back the Cuba Restricted List (republished in the Federal Register on 6 Feb 2025). NSPM-5 (30 Jun 2025, effective 14 Jul 2025) expanded the restricted-entity lists. | Kelley Drye, govinfo FR 2024-11618, govinfo FR 2025-02282, Hogan Lovells |
| 2026 escalation | According to search summaries of law-firm and OFAC material, E.O. 14404 (1 May 2026) created new Cuba Sanctions Regulations (31 CFR part 516), effective 30 Sep 2026, and OFAC narrowed CACR authorizations at the same time. I could not read the full text. Whether US persons may still sell SaaS to private Cuban businesses after 30 Sep 2026 is **unverified** and would need sanctions counsel. | Steptoe, Thompson Hine SmarTrade, OFAC |
| Local software certification | MINCOM (Ministry of Communications) keeps an official list of "valid" accounting and financial software, last updated 21 Jul 2025. Every product on it is from a Cuban state entity, for example VERSAT SARASOLA (DATAZUCAR) and Zun Suite (GET). A foreign accounting or tax tool would need this certification to count as valid bookkeeping, and that is out of reach for a solo founder. | mincom.gob.cu PDF lists (Feb 2025, Jul 2025) |
| Payment rails | **Unverified (no search spent):** Stripe, PayPal and US-issued cards do not work in Cuba. Mipymes hold CUP and restricted MLC/USD accounts at state banks, so recurring foreign SaaS billing is impractical. Collecting payment would mean informal channels (diaspora remittances, crypto, Zelle through relatives), which carry legal risk for the founder. | Unverified; well known but not checked this session |
| Internet / state control | **Unverified (no search spent):** ETECSA is the only telecom provider, mobile data is expensive and intermittent, and there have been periodic shutdowns. | Unverified |

**Conclusion.** For a **US person**, Cuba is effectively closed. The SaaS general license is being narrowed (E.O. 14404 / 31 CFR 516 from 30 Sep 2026), the restricted-entity screening burden is rising, and the market is tiny anyway. For an **EU or other non-US founder**, EU law does not stop the sale (the EU Blocking Statute even pushes back against US extraterritorial rules). The practical blockers are still decisive: no payment rail, no legal way to contract and bill in hard currency at scale, state-certified accounting software, and buyers with little foreign currency. Per the agent instructions, research stops here.

## 3. Opportunities

**None.** No opportunity meets the brief's final decision rule. Distribution, payment and legal access fail before pain or competition can even be scored.

---

## 4. Rejected after competitor research

- **ONAT digital-filing and accounting helper for mipymes.** Rejected because of the MINCOM certified-software regime and incumbent state products: **VERSAT SARASOLA (DATAZUCAR)** and **Zun Suite (GET)**, which already run municipal treasuries and ONAT offices. A foreign uncertified product cannot become the bookkeeping system of record.

## 5. Attractive problem, poor distribution

- **Mipyme tax compliance (digital ONAT filing, Res. 8/2024; accounting under Res. 272/2024; tighter audits, with ONAT closing 15 mipymes and investigating 117 for evasion).** The problem is mandatory and recurring, but buyers cannot pay foreign SaaS and the founder cannot legally or practically invoice them.

## 6. Too competitive

- Diaspora remittance and "combo" e-commerce aimed at Cuban families. It is crowded and sanctions-sensitive (not researched further).

## 7. Add-on note

Cuba cannot be served as an add-on to a neighbouring market (Dominican Republic, Mexico, Panama). Their billing, tax and legal stacks do not carry over, and the sanctions exposure would put the parent product at risk.
