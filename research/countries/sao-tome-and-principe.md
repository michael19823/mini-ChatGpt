# São Tomé and Príncipe: opportunity research

**Date:** 2026-10-04 | **Market class:** microstate (population about 230k, estimate; very small formal private sector) | **Search budget used:** 4 of 4

## Bottom line

**No viable standalone indie-software opportunity was found.** The formal economy is too small. Very few businesses are forced into recurring digital compliance workflows, and the two real "why now" triggers can each be served as an add-on to a larger market:

1. **VAT (IVA), introduced 1 June 2023.** A recent master's dissertation (2025) found weaknesses in administrative capacity, technology adoption ("apropriação tecnológica") and practical execution. That is real pain, but the reachable buyer pool is a few hundred VAT-registered firms at most (estimate). Lusophone invoicing/ERP vendors from Portugal and Angola, such as PHC and Primavera, are the natural suppliers. Their presence in STP is unverified.
2. **EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) for cocoa exports.** Cocoa is the main export, and the EU (Portugal, France, Netherlands buyers) is the main destination. But exporters are few and concentrated: the cooperatives CECAB (about 3,000 farmers) and CECAQ-11 (1,135 members), plus a few private exporters. Global traceability platforms (Sourcemap, TraceX, SCS Global, buyer-run systems) already serve this need.

**Accessibility:** no sanctions or internet restrictions found. Portuguese is the language, and the currency is the dobra (STN), pegged to the euro. Market size, not access, is the binding constraint.

## Industries screened

| Industry | Workflow looked at | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| All VAT-registered SMEs / accountants | IVA monthly returns, invoice requirements since 2023 | Reject (standalone) | Real pain, but buyers number in the low hundreds (estimate). Best treated as a module of a Portugal/Angola/Cabo Verde invoicing product. |
| Cocoa exporters / cooperatives | EUDR geolocation + due-diligence statement packages for EU buyers | Reject | Under 10 exporters (estimate). Sourcemap, TraceX and SCS Global, plus EU importers' own systems, already cover this. Organic/Fairtrade certification already imposes traceability. |
| Fisheries | EU fisheries partnership catch reporting / export docs | Reject (not researched in depth) | Done mostly by foreign fleets and the state. There is no SME buyer base. |
| Tourism / hotels | Guest registration, tourist tax | Reject (not researched in depth) | Few dozen properties (estimate). Generic PMS products serve them. |
| Customs brokers / import agents | Import declarations (ASYCUDA, unverified) | Reject (not researched in depth) | A handful of brokers. The government system is the integration point. |

## Opportunities

None meet the bar. In particular, none score 5/10 or above for a standalone business.

## Rejected after competitor research

- **EUDR cocoa traceability package for STP exporters.** Killed by Sourcemap, TraceX and SCS Global, by EU buyer-run traceability (for example Kaoka buying from CECAB), and by a buyer count under 10.
- **IVA compliance / invoicing tool for STP SMEs.** Killed by market size and by Lusophone ERP/invoicing vendors (Primavera, PHC; local presence unverified) plus local accountants doing it manually.

## Attractive problem, poor distribution

- IVA compliance for small STP firms: the pain is documented, but there is no reachable volume. It could only work as a localisation add-on (STP IVA return format) to a product already selling in Angola, Cabo Verde or Portugal.

## Too competitive

- EUDR cocoa traceability (globally contested by funded platforms).

## Add-on note

If a cross-country "Lusophone Africa SME invoicing/VAT compliance" product emerges from the Angola, Mozambique or Cabo Verde research, STP is a cheap localisation add-on. The STP IVA has been in force since 2023, and its tax design is Portuguese-derived.
