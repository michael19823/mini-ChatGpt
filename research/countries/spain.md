# Spain - opportunity research (deep pass, 2026-10-05)

Accessibility: open EU market; no sanctions or payment issues. You need a Spanish-language product (plus Catalan, Basque or Galician for some regional portals). AEAT, regional and municipal portals usually need a digital certificate, Cl@ve or apoderamiento (power of attorney), so automating a filing for a client generally needs a representative certificate or a "colaborador social" arrangement. About 55 searches across both passes. Claims I could not confirm are marked "unverified".

Bottom line: Spain is a rich market for regulatory triggers but a poor one for indie gaps. Almost every high-volume trigger already has a funded vertical vendor or a free government tool. Examples: VeriFactu, B2B e-invoicing, DeCA, SES.HOSPEDAJES, NRUA deposits, time tracking, CAE, waste e-SIR, farm notebooks, solar legalisation and pest control. No opportunity scored above 5/10. The best remaining lead is the multi-jurisdiction tourist-tax filing layer, which is becoming more fragmented in 2026-2027 (Galician municipalities and the three Basque foral territories).

## Opportunities

### Opportunity: Multi-jurisdiction tourist-tax filing and reconciliation for holiday-rental managers

**Industry:**  
Short-term rental property management and small independent hotels or hostels

**Buyer:**  
Owner or administrator of a holiday-rental management company with 20-300 units, especially companies spanning Galicia, the Basque Country, Catalonia and the Balearics. Secondary buyer: gestorías that file for many small rental owners.

**Trigger / Why now:**  
- **Galicia:** A Coruña started on 29 Sep 2025 and Santiago in Oct 2025. Vigo was planned for 1 Apr 2026. Rates are EUR 1-2.5 per night, capped at 5 nights, and Asturias is designing its own tax.  
- **Basque Country:** Foral Norms 3/2026 (Bizkaia), 3/2026 (Gipuzkoa) and 13/2026 (Álava) create a tourist-stay tax from 1 Jan 2027. Each foral treasury sets a framework and each town hall sets its own rates through an ordinance, so the tax is fragmented by territory and by municipality.  
- **Catalonia:** IEET is filed half-yearly on mod. 950 (1-20 Apr and 1-20 Oct), with an aggregated mod. 920 option. Rates rose in 2025-2026.  
- **Market size:** about 330,000 tourist dwellings (INE, Nov 2025); about 300,000 had an NRUA by Jan 2026.

**Current workflow:**  
1. The PMS or check-in tool (Chekin, Avirato, net2rent, Smoobu) collects guest data and, in Chekin's case, calculates and collects the tax at online check-in.  
2. Each period, staff export stays per property and recompute taxable nights. They apply exemptions (minors, 5-night or 7-night caps, low season) and per-municipality rates.  
3. They file per jurisdiction: ATC mod. 950 in Catalonia, Balearic ITS, the municipal e-office in A Coruña, Santiago and Vigo, and from 2027 three foral treasuries.  
4. They reconcile tax collected from guests and OTA payouts against tax declared, chase owners for certificates and powers of attorney, and answer inspections.

**Pain:**  
The penalty exposure and the per-jurisdiction rules are documented. ATC offers a web form "recommended for one establishment" and a downloadable helper program for multi-unit filers, which is a sign of bulk-filing friction. New taxes are being added every year. Hours spent and error rates are unverified; this needs interviews.

**Existing solutions:**  
Chekin (calculation, collection, audit history, "centralised information for declarations"), net2rent and Smoobu (guides and calculations), PMS/channel managers, gestorías that file manually, and the free government web forms and ATC helper program.

**The gap:**  
Filing and reconciliation across several tax authorities, as opposed to calculation and collection. Specifically: one normalised stays ledger produces each jurisdiction's return, including the new Basque municipal-rate matrix. On top of that, a reconciliation of collected vs declared vs OTA-collected tax per property and owner. Whether Chekin or the PMSs already submit returns directly is unverified, and it is the key question.

**Possible product:**  
A "tourist-tax back office" fed by the existing PMS or Chekin exports. It holds a rules table per jurisdiction and per municipality and computes each return. It pre-fills or produces the file for each portal, logs submissions, and reconciles amounts per owner.

**MVP:**  
CSV import from 2-3 PMSs, a rules engine for Catalonia, A Coruña, Santiago and Vigo, a pre-filled mod. 950 dataset and municipal return worksheets, and a reconciliation report. Add the three Basque territories before 1 Jan 2027.

**Pricing hypothesis:**  
EUR 2-4 per unit per month (EUR 60-500 per month per manager), or EUR 10-20 per filing for gestorías.

**How to find first customers:**  
Regional tourist-dwelling registers (public in most regions), holiday-rental manager associations (for example FEVITUR and regional associations), Chekin and PMS partner ecosystems, and gestorías in Galicia and Euskadi.

**Risks:**  
Chekin or a PMS adds direct filing; Chekin is the obvious incumbent and is very active in content on every new tax. Filing needs representation and certificates per owner. Filing is half-yearly or quarterly, not daily. Taxes may be repealed (Valencia repealed its tax). Single-region managers have little cross-jurisdiction pain.

**Kill condition:**  
Interviews with 10 managers show that Chekin or their PMS already files, or that filing takes under an hour per period. Or most managers operate in only one jurisdiction.

**Score:** 5/10

### Opportunity: Funeral-service expediente router (insurer portals plus regional sanitary communications)

**Industry:**  
Death care: independent funeral homes

**Buyer:**  
Owner or office manager of an independent funeral home (roughly 800-900 SMEs out of about 1,100-1,700 funeral companies) that serves decesos policyholders for several insurers.

**Trigger / Why now:**  
No new law. The trigger is structural: about 59-60% of Spanish deaths are covered by decesos insurance (ICEA). The market is concentrated: Santalucía (~30%), Ocaso and Mapfre hold about 65%. Mapfre (Funespaña) and Santalucía (Albia) have negotiated merging their funeral arms, which squeezes independents who rely on insurer work. Regional mortuary-health rules keep moving from prior authorisation to prior communication (Castilla y León, Cantabria), filed through each region's e-office. Madrid requires "comunicación censo funerario" filings.

**Current workflow:**  
1. Insurer assistance line assigns the service to a collaborating funeral home.  
2. The funeral home opens a case in its management software (Gesmemori, Memento Cloud, Tellmebye, Karing).  
3. It files transfer communications or authorisations with the region(s) concerned, with death certificate, burial licence and so on, using a digital certificate in each region's e-office.  
4. It re-enters the service and invoice data into the insurer's provider portal (for example the Ocaso provider portal) and attaches documents to get paid. Whether this is re-keying is unverified.

**Pain:**  
Payment depends on insurer acceptance. Rules differ by region. Evidence of hours spent or rejection rates was not found; pain is unverified.

**Existing solutions:**  
Gesmemori (83+ installations), Memento Cloud, Tellmebye, Karing Funeraria, insurer portals, and manual work by office staff.

**The gap:**  
Integrations between funeral software and insurer provider portals plus regional e-offices (not found in search; unverified).

**Possible product:**  
One case record that generates each region's transfer communication pack and each insurer's claim pack, with a status tracker per insurer.

**MVP:**  
Document pack generator for 3 regions and 2 insurers, plus a payment-status tracker.

**Pricing hypothesis:**  
EUR 99-199 per month per funeral home, or EUR 3-5 per insured service.

**How to find first customers:**  
Regional registers of funeral companies (Madrid, Basque Country and others are public), PANASEF and regional funeral associations, Funermostra trade fair, Revista Funeraria.

**Risks:**  
Small market (around 900 SMEs). Insurers that own funeral chains may push their own tools. Portal automation may breach insurer terms. Existing funeral software may already integrate.

**Kill condition:**  
Funeral homes report that insurer claims take minutes, or that Gesmemori or Memento already integrate with the major insurers.

**Score:** 4/10

### Opportunity: Treatment-record push for phytosanitary contractors (ROPO treatment-services sector)

**Industry:**  
Agriculture: contract spraying companies and agronomic advisors

**Buyer:**  
Contractor firms registered in ROPO (treatment-services sector) that spray for many farms, and the advisors and cooperatives that prescribe treatments.

**Trigger / Why now:**  
Electronic registration of phytosanitary treatments becomes mandatory from 1 Jan 2027 (EU Reg. 2023/564; RD 1311/2012 as amended; RD 34/2025 keeps the rest of the CUE voluntary). Paper is allowed during 2026. Fertiliser application records have been mandatory since 1 Jan 2026.

**Current workflow:**  
1. The contractor applies a product on a client's plot and logs it in its own ERP or a paper log (treatment-services firms must keep their own operations register).  
2. The same treatment must appear in each client farm's digital notebook (CUE or private app), which means the farmer or advisor re-keys it.  
3. The contractor also reports purchases and transactions through RETO.

**Pain:**  
Duplicate entry per treatment per client from 2027. Volume per contractor is unverified.

**Existing solutions:**  
Free regional CUE apps, oSIGris (~EUR 6.90/month, B2B licences to cooperatives), Agroptima, VisualNACert, Hispatec/Efemis (cooperative C3 notebook), Gestion5 "ERP Fitosanitarios", plus regional lists of authorised CUE developers.

**The gap:**  
"Apply once, post to every client's notebook": routing a contractor's records into heterogeneous client notebooks through SIEX. This may already be possible through SIEX interoperability (unverified).

**Possible product:**  
A contractor app that records each job and pushes it to the SIEX-compatible notebook of each client, with a per-client signed treatment certificate.

**MVP:**  
Job logging plus an export per client in CUE import format for one region.

**Pricing hypothesis:**  
EUR 30-80 per month per contractor.

**How to find first customers:**  
Regional ROPO registers (searchable), machinery-contractor associations, cooperatives.

**Risks:**  
Very crowded agri-software market, seasonality, low willingness to pay, and SIEX may make the push trivial for incumbents.

**Kill condition:**  
SIEX or cooperative C3 already lets contractors post records to client notebooks, or incumbents ship it before 2027.

**Score:** 3.5/10

### Opportunity: Titularidad real (beneficial owner) monitor for gestorías

**Industry:**  
Accounting / gestoría services, corporate secretarial

**Buyer:**  
Small and mid-size gestorías and asesorías handling 100-1,000 companies

**Trigger / Why now:**  
The central beneficial-ownership register (RCTR, RD 609/2023) is live. A missing beneficial-owner declaration is now an independent ground for registry closure ("cierre registral"), separate from missing accounts. The declaration is filed with each annual accounts deposit.

**Current workflow:**  
1. Gestor collects ownership data per client.  
2. Checks structure changes manually.  
3. Declares at the Registro Mercantil with the accounts deposit and chases clients for updates.  
4. Discovers a blocked registry sheet only when a filing is rejected.

**Pain:**  
Closure risk and fines; recurs with each deposit. Hours spent are unverified.

**Existing solutions:**  
copilotgestoria (content and product for gestorías), law-firm services, Registradores' own tools, and gestoría suites (details unverified).

**The gap:**  
Portfolio-level tracking of which clients have stale or missing declarations, plus the supporting document pack, tied to deposit deadlines.

**Possible product:**  
A dashboard of ownership changes and deposit deadlines, with checklists and client reminders.

**MVP:**  
CSV import, status per client, reminders.

**Pricing hypothesis:**  
EUR 50-200 per month per gestoría.

**How to find first customers:**  
Colegios de gestores administrativos directories and asesoría associations.

**Risks:**  
Annual and seasonal; close to the generic document-collection trap; suites (A3, Sage) can add it cheaply.

**Kill condition:**  
Gestoría suites already track it, or gestores report no pain.

**Score:** 3/10

### Opportunity: Pay-transparency register and gap-justification pack for payroll bureaus (watch)

**Industry:**  
HR / payroll bureaus

**Buyer:**  
Payroll bureaus and labour consultancies serving firms with 50-250 employees

**Trigger / Why now:**  
The transposition deadline for Directive 2023/970 passed on 7 Jun 2026 without a final Spanish rule. The labour ministry ran a prior consultation from 24 Apr to 8 May 2026 and had a draft RD in July 2026. The Commission may open an infringement procedure. The existing pay register (RD 902/2020) already applies.

**Current workflow:**  
1. Export payroll to Excel.  
2. Build the pay register by category and gender.  
3. Compute gaps, justify any gap of 5% or more, and prepare a joint pay assessment.

**Pain:**  
Consultant-heavy and periodic; the rules are still unsettled.

**Existing solutions:**  
PayFit, Factorial, TeamSystem, Protime and Tramitapp are all marketing it; equality-plan consultancies; Excel templates.

**The gap:**  
A payroll-agnostic register with a justification workflow for multi-client bureaus.

**Possible product:**  
Payroll-export importer that produces the register and gap analysis.

**MVP:**  
CSV import plus the register and gap report.

**Pricing hypothesis:**  
EUR 50-150 per month per bureau.

**How to find first customers:**  
Graduados sociales colleges and payroll bureau associations.

**Risks:**  
Final rules unknown; annual; crowded HR market.

**Kill condition:**  
The final RD prescribes a format that HR suites ship at launch.

**Score:** 3/10

## Rejected after competitor research

- **Waste memoria compliance router** (was 4.5/10 in the first pass). Under the Coordination Commission agreement under Ley 7/2022, hazardous-waste producers whose identification documents are in the e-SIR repository have fulfilled the memoria obligation, and several regions exempt them. Eurofins EcoGestor and others already generate the archive and memoria and integrate e-SIR and DIWASS. Sources: https://medioambiente.castillalamancha.es/sites/default/files/2026-06/PRODUCTOR_obligaciones.pdf, https://www.caib.es/sites/residus/es/acuerdo_comision_memoria_anual_productores_residuos/, https://www.eurofins.com/es-es/servicios-medioambientales/ecogestor/residuos/
- **Rent-a-car SES reporting:** rental software and the net2rent-style automatic SES web-service submission; AECA guidance (https://www.aecarent.com/noticias-ID643.htm, https://net2rent.com/guia-rapida-envio-automatico-de-datos-a-ses-hospedajes/).
- **NRUA annual rental deposit:** the free N2 app from the Colegio de Registradores; Partee and Chekin guides and tools; annual (https://partee.es/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Manual-de-instalacion-y-creacion-de-Depositos-de-Arrendamientos-en-aplicacion-N2.pdf).
- **Solar / shared self-consumption legalisation (RDL 7/2026 "gestor de autoconsumo"):** Wattwin, PylonData, Elecsum "+Legal", Hesstec, SolarDirecto (https://wattwin.com/, https://elecsum.com/es/comunidades-energeticas/legal/, https://cuencasolar.es/blog/el-nuevo-rdl-7-2026-crea-la-figura-del-gestor-de-autoconsumo-para-instalaciones-compartidas/).
- **Pest control / legionella records:** iGEO ERP (800+ companies), EviSane (https://gust.com/companies/igeoerp).
- **Plastic-packaging tax (mod. 592):** AHORA and Goldensoft ERP modules; Circulr for refunds (https://ahora.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/154000210673-erp-impuesto-sobre-el-pl%C3%A1stico-no-reutilizable, https://circulr.es/blog/recuperar-impuesto-plastico-modelo-a22).
- **CBAM / EUDR for Spanish importers:** pan-EU vendors (osapiens, Freyr and others); the 50 t CBAM threshold exempts most SMEs (https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/cambio-climatico/temas/cbam/actores-cbam.html, https://osapiens.com/es/preguntas-frecuentes-eudr).
- **Electrical CIE multi-region filing:** regional systems differ (SERIE, OGE, TECI), but installers mostly work in one region, and CYPE and dmELECT produce the documents (https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/sites/default/files/inline-files/2025/06/Manual_Usuario_TECI.pdf).
- **Electronic DeCA:** bluecmr, logitrack, kaleidotrans, metotrans, plus the free government tool.
- **Vet PRESVET:** clinic software already automates it (~80% of vets report via web services).
- **SILICIE:** AEAT system since 2020; ERP vendors; small producers exempt.
- **Packaging declaration (RD 1055/2022):** Ecoembes and Ecovidrio portals.
- **Digital time tracking:** Factorial, Sage, Tamigo, iAccesos, TeamSystem.
- **CAE contractor documentation:** CTAIMA, Dokify, Nalanda, e-coordina, TÜV SÜD DOCS, PlayCAE.
- **Food-waste law plans (Ley 1/2025):** mostly one-off and generic; consultancies and food banks provide guides.

## Attractive problem, poor distribution

- **Dangerous-goods safety-advisor annual reports** (Orden FOM/606/2018; electronic filing to each region in Q1): annual and a small niche, and ADRLY already automates report generation.
- **Small water utilities (RD 3/2023 SINAC, water safety plans):** buyers are municipalities or provincial councils reached through public procurement.
- **Construction subcontracting-book habilitation per region:** fragmented but low-value; the contractors are fragmented too.
- **Regional pet-ID registries / future state registry (Ley 7/2023):** wait for the RD; vet software vendors control the channel.

## Too competitive

- B2B e-invoicing (RD 238/2026; Order in force 1 Oct 2026, so obligations from 1 Oct 2027 and 1 Oct 2028): free AEAT solution plus b2brouter, Docuten, Sage and others.
- SES.HOSPEDAJES traveller registry (Chekin, Avirato, PMSs).
- FUNDAE subsidised-training notifications.
- Farm digital notebooks for farmers (oSIGris, Agroptima, VisualNACert, Hispatec); only the contractor niche is kept above.
