# Tonga: Opportunity Research

**Classification:** Microstate / tiny economy (population about 100,000; the formal private sector is small and concentrated in Nuku'alofa). Search budget: 4 searches, all used.
**Accessibility:** Not sanctioned, and a foreign founder can sell software there in practice. The market is too small to support a standalone product.
**Bottom line:** I found **no viable standalone indie-software opportunity** in Tonga. The only lead worth tracking is a kava-export compliance pack. It only makes sense as an add-on to a regional Pacific product built mainly for Fiji and Vanuatu, the larger kava exporters.

## Opportunities

### Opportunity: Pacific kava export compliance pack (Tonga module)

**Industry:**
Kava processing and export

**Buyer:**
Owner or manager of a kava processing or export company in Tonga. There are a few dozen at most (estimate, unverified).

**Trigger / Why now:**
On 26 March 2026, PHAMA Plus handed the Tonga Kava Standard over to government authorities. The standard has been incorporated into the Tonga Kava Bill, so it is moving from voluntary guideline to legal benchmark. Exporters are also working toward HACCP to sell into Australia and New Zealand, and Tongan kava exports to Australia are growing (PTI Q1/Q2 2026 data).

**Current workflow:**
1. Processor keeps batch, drying and quality records on paper or in Excel.
2. Exporter requests a MAFF quarantine consignment inspection and a Phytosanitary Certificate (Kava).
3. Exporter files the customs export entry (ASYCUDA) and arranges shipping through an agent.
4. Exporter separately answers the importer's food-safety and quality evidence requests by email.

**Pain:**
The steps are documented on the Tonga Trade Portal: several agencies (MAFF, MTED, Revenue & Customs, the shipping agency, Ports Authority) are involved per shipment. Beyond that, I have no direct complaints or evidence of hours lost.

**Existing solutions:**
Excel/paper; the government trade portal (information only); donor-funded technical assistance (PHAMA Plus until 2026, and now the government); generic HACCP/food-safety apps such as SafetyCulture (not verified for use in Tonga); consultants.

**The gap:**
No tool turns per-batch processing records into the Kava Standard / HACCP evidence plus the shipment paperwork. That gap only matters at regional scale.

**Possible product:**
A batch-to-shipment record keeper for kava processors. It produces evidence that the batch meets the Kava Standard, a draft of the phytosanitary request, and buyer document packs. Fiji and Vanuatu would have their own standards modules.

**MVP:**
A mobile/web batch log plus a PDF export pack mapped to the Tonga Kava Standard clauses.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
USD 30–60/month per exporter (estimate). Tonga alone is under USD 15k ARR even at full adoption.

**How to find first customers:**
MAFF's exporter registrations, the Tonga Kava Council / grower associations, and the PHAMA Plus/PTI exporter networks.

**Risks:**
Too few buyers. Donors or government may provide free tools. Exporters may have low willingness to pay. The Kava Bill's final status is unverified.

**Kill condition:**
Kill it if Fiji and Vanuatu exporters would not pay for the regional product, or if MAFF/donors roll out a free traceability system.

**Score:** 3/10

## Rejected after competitor research

- **Consumption Tax / SME bookkeeping compliance:** Killed by Xero/MYOB plus local accounting firms. 15% CT, TOP 100,000 registration threshold; I found no 2025–26 e-invoicing mandate. Sources: https://taxatlas.io/country/tonga/vat-sales-tax, https://aviaanaccounting.com/accounting-firms-in-tonga/
- **Customs entry automation:** Killed by ASYCUDA World (UNCTAD, government-run) and the Tonga Trade Portal. Brokers are optional and few. Sources: https://tonga.tradeportal.org/procedure/153?l=en, https://matangitonga.to/tag/customs?page=1
- **Quarantine/biosecurity records:** Killed by the donor-funded government Quarantine Management Information System. Source: https://pacerplus.org/tag/committee-on-sps-and-tbt/

## Attractive problem, poor distribution
- Kava export compliance (above): the problem is real but the Tongan buyer pool is tiny. It is viable only as a module of a Fiji/Vanuatu-led regional product.

## Too competitive
- SME accounting/tax (Xero, MYOB, local firms).

## Notes
- Unverified: the exact number of kava exporters, whether the Tonga Kava Bill has been enacted, and the payroll / retirement-fund reporting channels (none found in searches).
- Recommendation: serve Tonga as an add-on market to a Fiji- or Vanuatu-centred Pacific product. Do not research Tonga further as a standalone market.
