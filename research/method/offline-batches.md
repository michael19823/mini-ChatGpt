24 countries in 6 cloud sessions, with 4 agents each and up to 45 searches per agent. The mix
covers rich markets where small firms pay for software, plus large informal economies.
Instructions: `research/method/offline-instructions.md`. Output: `research/offline/<slug>.md`.

# Worldwide extension

The other 175 countries, packed into sessions of at most 190 searches. Search budget per agent:
**40** for larger markets, **20** for mid-sized ones and **8** for microstates and inaccessible or
sanctioned markets. Each agent uses the budget given in its prompt instead of the 45 in the
instructions.
