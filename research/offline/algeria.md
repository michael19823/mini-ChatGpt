# Algeria: offline (quiet) industries pass

Research date: 2026-10-05. Languages: French only. Arabic queries were not run, which is a known gap: many Algerian regulator notices and trade-association pages are in Arabic only. Searches used: 19 of 20. The last three were refused for a usage limit (two of them returned nothing), so the research stopped there, as the instructions require.

**Market access caveat (from the country report, still applies):** the dinar is not convertible. Service imports need prior authorization on `services.mcepe.gov.dz` from 1 August 2026, and foreign digital services carry 19% VAT plus DGI registration. Every idea below therefore needs an Algerian entity or a local reseller billing in DZD. A non-local solo founder **cannot** realistically sell any of these alone. All scores include this penalty.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

Only one idea survived. Algeria's quiet industries are mostly managed through state subsidy programmes such as milk, flour and livestock, and in those programmes the state builds the platform itself.

### Opportunity: Jeweller compliance kit (authorization dossier + digital *registre de police* / customer register + AML due-diligence log)

**Industry:**
Jewellery retail, gold buying (*or cassé*), and artisan jewellery making

**Buyer:**
Owner of a 1–5 person jewellery shop or workshop, often a family business. Secondary buyer: the shop's accountant (*comptable* / *commissaire aux comptes*), who is now an AML-obliged professional too.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- A ministerial order (Algérie-Eco, 24 July 2026; TSA; Maghreb Emergent) sets new conditions for granting and withdrawing the authorization to work with precious metals and stones. Existing operators have **one year from publication** (about July 2027) to comply. Operators must keep an up-to-date *registre de police* and a customer register under the indirect-tax code.
- Law 05-01 was amended on 27 July 2025 and widened due-diligence duties for non-financial professions. The CTRF has publicly named jewellers as a laundering risk. Algeria was grey-listed by the FATF in October 2024; Algérie360 reports a later exit, which was not verified in detail.
- A parallel crackdown targets unhallmarked and illegally imported jewellery (TSA). About 15 tonnes of counterfeit-hallmarked gold are said to circulate (Algérie360).

**Current workflow:**
1. The jeweller puts together the authorization dossier (proof of experience or diploma, premises, tax registration) and files it on paper with the local guarantee service.
2. Every purchase of used gold and every sale is written by hand in the bound *registre de police*: seller ID, item, weight, assay, price.
3. Customer IDs are photocopied or copied out by hand. There is no structured due-diligence or suspicious-transaction record.
4. Inspectors from the guarantee service or the police check the books on visits. The accountant reconciles stock and tax separately.

**Pain:**
Losing the authorization means closing the shop. The one-year deadline creates a compliance rush. Hand-written registers make police inquiries into stolen gold slow. Direct complaints from operators were **not** found in French sources (an Arabic search would be needed).

**Existing solutions:**
- Bound paper registers sold by stationers (the default substitute).
- Generic Algerian retail and stock software (unverified: the jeweller-software search was refused).
- ConformePro (`conformepro.dz`) publishes activity codes and compliance guides and could add this vertical.
- The accountant or a *mandataire* who prepares the authorization dossier for a fee.
- Foreign jeweller POS products, which are unusable because of payment and currency rules.

**Offline evidence:**
The dossier is filed at a counter with the guarantee service. The registers are physical books checked on site. No Algerian jeweller SaaS turned up in the searches that were run.

**Offline channel:**
- Jeweller clusters such as Rue Bab Azzoun in Algiers and the gold souks of Constantine and Oran. Door-to-door visits work in these clusters.
- The jewellers' craft section of the *Chambre de l'artisanat et des métiers* (CAM) in each wilaya.
- Gold and assay-equipment suppliers (scales, acid kits) who visit shops.
- Accountants who file for jewellers.

**Market count:**
Unknown. D&B shows 280 jewellery/luggage/leather retailers, which is a clear undercount. The real number of authorized jewellers is likely in the low thousands (estimate, unverified). The tax administration's *garantie* registrations would be the real source.

**The gap:**
Nothing links the authorization dossier, the daily used-gold register and the AML record. The paper book does not satisfy an inspector who wants searchable seller-ID history, and it does not support a CTRF report.

**Possible product:**
A tablet or phone app, sold with a local partner, that does three things. It scans the seller's ID and records the item, weight and karat. It prints or exports the *registre de police* and customer register in the official layout. It flags transactions above a threshold for due diligence. An optional dossier service handles the authorization renewal.

**MVP:**
A register app that captures the seller's ID by photo, records item and weight, prints a daily register page and searches by ID. It should come with a fixed-fee service to assemble the authorization dossier, sold before the 2027 deadline.

**Willingness to pay:**
The dossier service is easier to sell than software: a one-time fee of perhaps 15,000–30,000 DZD (estimate). Software would be about 2,000–4,000 DZD a month, roughly $15–30 at the official rate. Revenue would come mainly from service plus software, and it is small.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
A one-time dossier fee of 20,000 DZD, then 2,500 DZD a month for the register app. Accountants get a reseller margin.

**How to find first customers:**
Walk the jeweller streets of Algiers, Oran and Constantine. Approach the CAM jewellery sections, and accountants who serve jewellers.

**Founder access:**
**Needs a local founder or partner**: the work is in Arabic and French, the market is cash and DZD only, and trust is built face to face.

**Risks:**
- The exact register format may be fixed by the tax administration, or the state may digitise it itself, as it did for milk.
- Jewellers may resist any digital record of gold buying because of informality and tax exposure.
- The one-time dossier spike fades after July 2027.

**Kill condition:**
Any one of these kills the idea:
- the order requires the official paginated bound register and does not accept electronic registers;
- the DGI announces its own e-register;
- fewer than about 1,500 authorized operators exist nationwide.

**Score:** 4/10. The trigger is real and fresh, and the chance of digitisation through the state is moderate. The count is unknown, willingness to pay is low, the founder must be local, and digital registers may not be legally accepted.

## 3. Rejected

- **Subsidised-milk distributor traceability:** the obligation is real (monthly retailer-stamped quantity sheets, 118 dairies under contract), but the Ministry of Commerce launched its own digital platform for quotas and per-retailer volumes. Distributors' margins are fixed by the state. Sources: [Algérie360, convention ONIL](https://www.algerie360.com/convention-onil-laiteries-nouvelle-clause-tracabilite-lait-sachet/), [Algérie360, plateforme numérique](https://www.algerie360.com/plateforme-numerique-lancee-pour-une-distribution-optimisee-du-lait-subventionnee/), [TSA, marges distributeurs](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/les-distributeurs-de-lait-en-sachet-reclament-une-forte-hausse-de-leur-marge-beneficiaire/).
- **Bakery flour quotas:** about 21,000 bakeries, but the control obligation sits with the mills and OAIC, and bread prices are fixed ([Algérie-Eco](https://algerie-eco.com/?p=26763)).
- **Well-drilling authorizations:** volume is high (26,000 in 2022 and growing), but each filing is one-time per well and the driller-permit rule does not exist yet ([Radio Algérie](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/25423), [Legal Doctrine](https://legal-doctrine.com/edition/agriculture-comment-obtenir-une-autorisation-de-forage-en-algerie-d621ba8954ef9080346e987022c70da8)).
- **Scrap metal:** exports frozen since 2010 and collectors are informal ([Algérie360](https://www.algerie360.com/exportation-des-dechets-ferreux-et-non-ferreux-malaise-chez-les-operateurs/)).
- **Livestock identification:** a state programme run by vets, with a state platform ([TSA, puces moutons](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-mise-sur-les-puces-electroniques-pour-identifier-ses-moutons/)).
- **Domestic-worker employers:** largely undeclared, and the CNAS channels are free ([demarchesdz](https://www.demarchesdz.com/tele-declaration-cnas-employeurs/)).
- **Taxi licences:** the decree is still pending and the pain is about licence ownership ([Algérie360](https://www.algerie360.com/transport-un-nouveau-decret-pour-reorganiser-les-taxis/)).
- **Driving schools:** still a bill, and a state candidate-tracking system is planned ([APA News](https://fr.apanews.net/news/algerie-durcissement-des-regles-du-permis-de-conduire/)).

## 4. Method notes

- **Worked:** French queries naming the specific regulator or programme (ONIL, OAIC, *service de la garantie*, ABH), and the news sites Algérie360, TSA and Algérie-Eco, which report new decrees within days.
- **Didn't work:** queries about vets, beekeepers and operator counts returned French or Moroccan results. Operator counts are almost never published.
- **Main pattern:** in Algeria, quiet industries tied to subsidies end up digitised by the state itself, so the room left for a startup is small.
- **Still to do:** Arabic-language queries, the jewellers' operator count and existing jeweller software were not covered. Two searches were refused for a usage limit.
