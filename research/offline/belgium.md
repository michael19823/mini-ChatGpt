# Belgium: offline-industries pass

Research depth: 36 WebSearch calls in Dutch, French and English, regulator-first. WebFetch was blocked, so all evidence comes from search-result snippets. Anything not seen in a source is marked "unverified" or "estimate". This pass does not repeat the opportunities in `research/countries/belgium.md`: Flemish chain liability, the Parcel Act, the syndic 3.94 pack and EPC kNR.

Belgium's quiet industries are better served than expected. Several regulators run their own digital intake: Sanitel for livestock, eDOV for boreholes, SIGPAA for individual wastewater systems, ANB e-loket for hunting, and eLys for deaths. Belgian field-app vendors already cover the heating attests. The regional split (Flanders, Wallonia, Brussels) is the main source of fragmentation. Most surviving ideas serve small markets.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

### Opportunity: Pesticide-use register for agricultural contractors and green-sector applicators

**Industry:**
Agricultural contractors (loonwerkers / entrepreneurs de travaux agricoles) who spray for many farmers. Also landscapers, public green services, sports-turf and golf-course managers. Belgium-wide (the FAVV rule is federal).

**Buyer:**
The owner-operator of a small spraying contractor or landscaping firm that holds a P2/P3 phytolicence. Secondarily, the greenkeeper or sports-ground manager.

**Trigger / Why now:**
EU Implementing Regulation 2023/564 changes the content of the use register. Belgium applies the new data fields from 1 Jan 2026: authorisation number, application time, crop growth stage and plot reference. After an EU postponement, the register must be electronic from 1 Jan 2027. In 2027 users may still keep paper and convert once a year, by 31 January of the following year. From 2030 all data must be electronic within 30 days of use. When a contractor sprays, the registration duty falls on the contractor: register within 7 days, and give the client access to or a copy of the record within 30 days. FAVV has not yet published the formats it will accept.

**Current workflow:**
1. The operator sprays several fields for several clients in one day and notes product, dose and field on paper or in the cab terminal.
2. Back at the yard, they copy it into a notebook or Excel and look up each product's authorisation number on Fytoweb.
3. They send each farmer a copy (paper, email or WhatsApp photo) so the farmer's own register is complete for audits (Vegaplan, FAVV).
4. During an inspection they dig out the register. From 2027 it must be electronic. A photo or scan is not valid.

**Pain:**
- Mandatory, and done per job.
- The data has to be split by client, and each client must get it within 30 days.
- Inspections by FAVV and Vegaplan exist.
- Every farm and green-sector body has published guidance (Boerenbond, Inagro, Viaverda, Praktijkpunt Landbouw, Departement Landbouw FAQ). This shows confusion.
- VILT reports farmers are "not yet cheering" about the postponement.
- Direct complaints from contractors were not found (unverified).

**Existing solutions:**
- Farm-side tools: Agromanager, Care4Growing, EVA-app, the Vegaplan tool and AgroVision Crops (NL/BE/PL). These are named in official FAQs.
- Boerenbond's digital register offering.
- Excel and Word templates, which FAVV says are acceptable machine-readable formats. This is the biggest substitute.
- Contractor ERP or task software with sprayer-terminal logs (Belgian contractor packages not identified, unverified).

**Offline evidence:**
- The register is legally allowed on paper until the end of 2026.
- FAVV still has to define the accepted formats.
- Guidance is spread through farm unions, trade-press articles and training sessions, not software reviews.
- Contractors and landscapers are absent from the tool lists, which target farmers.

**Offline channel:**
- Phytolicence refresher training (the mandatory additional courses run by Boerenbond, Inagro, Viaverda and others).
- The Agro-Service contractors' federation (membership count unverified).
- Viaverda for the green sector.
- Crop-protection distributors and agricultural stores (for example the Ingro co-op, which republished the FAQ).
- Agriculture shows such as Agriflanders and the Libramont fair.

**Market count:**
About 47,000 phytolicences approved in Flanders by end of 2014, roughly three quarters of all Belgian applications, 60% of them in agriculture (Departement Landbouw). The number of contractors and landscapers within that total is unverified. As an estimate, a few thousand contractors and green firms apply products for third parties.

**The gap:**
Farm tools register one farm's own fields. A contractor needs:
- one entry per job that is split automatically into per-client register extracts;
- each extract sent within 30 days;
- product authorisation numbers looked up from the Fytoweb list;
- a format FAVV will accept.

No tool seen is built for the contractor or green-sector case. This is unverified for the AgroVision and contractor ERPs.

**Possible product:**
A mobile-first job log for spray operators. It holds a Fytoweb product picker with authorisation numbers, plot references and growth stage. The operator's own register is exported in a FAVV-accepted format, and each client gets an automatic register extract by email or link.

**MVP:**
- A PWA form per application.
- A product list imported from Fytoweb's authorised-products data (availability unverified).
- A client address book.
- A monthly per-client PDF/CSV extract and a full-register Excel export.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
EUR 15-40 per month per operator (estimate). Buyers would pay for a cheap tool. Willingness to pay for software is modest, because Excel is legal. A done-for-you conversion service (paper to electronic, once a year, before 31 January) is possible as a low-price add-on.

**How to find first customers:**
- Attend phytolicence refresher sessions and sector fairs.
- Ask Agro-Service and Viaverda for a newsletter mention.
- Approach distributors who sell to contractors.
- Use public lists of contractors from Vegaplan sector-guide participants (unverified).

**Risks:**
- Excel is explicitly accepted.
- FAVV may publish a free template.
- Farm-tool vendors could add a contractor mode.
- Low prices and a seasonal pattern.
- The 2027 date has already slipped once.
- Language: NL and FR are both needed.

**Kill condition:**
- FAVV publishes a free official electronic register or template with a client-copy function.
- Or 10 contractor interviews show they already export from their sprayer terminal or ERP and email clients without effort.

**Founder access:**
A non-local founder could build it, but selling through training sessions and fairs needs Dutch and French and a physical presence. A local is realistically needed.

**Score:** 4/10

### Opportunity: Walloon septic-emptying and SEI-maintenance paperwork router

**Industry:**
Septic-tank emptying (vidange) and maintenance of individual wastewater treatment systems (SEI) in Wallonia.

**Buyer:**
The owner-manager of a small family emptying firm (one to a few vacuum trucks) or an SEI maintenance provider declared to SPGE.

**Trigger / Why now:**
Since 1 Jan 2022 every SEI in Wallonia falls under public management (GPAA). The OAA intercommunales (AIDE, IDELUX, IGRETEC, INASEP, inBW, IPALLE and others) tell owners when emptying is due, and owners then have 3 months. Contracted emptiers bill SPGE directly. Intervention forms and maintenance reports go through the SPGE's SIGPAA platform. This sits on top of the 2015 approval regime: triplicate transport documents and quarterly reporting to SPW. Not a new 2026 trigger, but GPAA has increased volume and administration since 2022.

**Current workflow:**
1. Fill in a triplicate transport document in the truck at each emptying: one copy for the owner, one for the treatment station at discharge, one kept.
2. Within 10 days, enter the intervention form into SIGPAA for the competent OAA.
3. Invoice SPGE, not the owner.
4. At quarter end, send copies of all transport documents to SPW's Department of Environment and Water.
5. For maintenance jobs, send the report to the owner and to SPGE within 15 days.

**Pain:**
- One job feeds 3 to 4 receiving bodies (owner, treatment station, SPGE/OAA via SIGPAA, SPW).
- The paper is triplicate.
- Payment from SPGE depends on correct filing.

No direct complaints were found, so the pain is inferred from the workflow.

**Existing solutions:**
- SIGPAA itself (free; manual entry).
- Carbon-copy document pads.
- Generic field-service apps.
- The intercommunales' own scheduling.

No Belgian software for emptying firms was found (unverified). French ANC software exists but targets French bordereau rules (unverified).

**Offline evidence:**
- Triplicate paper documents are required in each vehicle.
- The quarterly report is "a copy of the transport documents".
- The firms' web presence is limited to brochure sites.

**Offline channel:**
- Call the 209 firms from the official SPW list (ODWB open dataset) and the SIGPAA public lists of contracted emptiers and maintenance providers.
- The 7 or so OAAs, which coordinate the emptiers.
- Fosse-septique installers and suppliers.

**Market count:**
- 209 approved emptiers (per drainup.be's summary of the SPW list: Liège 59, Hainaut 56, Namur 24, Luxembourg 24, Brabant wallon 13).
- SEI maintenance providers: public list on SIGPAA, uncounted.
- About 180,000 homes are in autonomous-sanitation zones (PASH figure).

**The gap:**
Nothing turns one tablet entry into all the outputs:
- the transport document;
- the SIGPAA form;
- the SPGE invoice;
- the quarterly SPW bundle;
- the owner attestation.

**Possible product:**
A tablet app for the truck. The driver fills in one emptying record with signature and volume. The app produces the owner PDF, a station copy, an SIGPAA-ready form (copy-assist, since there is no API) and the quarterly SPW export.

**MVP:**
- A PWA form.
- PDF generation in the official document layout (if digital copies are allowed; unverified).
- A quarterly ZIP for SPW.
- A per-job checklist for SIGPAA entry.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
EUR 30-60 per month per truck (estimate). Small family firms may prefer a done-for-you admin service.

**How to find first customers:**
Phone the ODWB/SPW list and the SIGPAA public list, and ask the OAAs for introductions.

**Risks:**
- The total market is about 200 firms, which is about EUR 100k ARR at best.
- SPGE could extend SIGPAA with a mobile app.
- Whether paper triplicates may be replaced by digital copies is unverified.
- French-only market.

**Kill condition:**
- SPW requires a specific paper form, so digital copies are not accepted.
- Or SIGPAA already offers a mobile entry route for emptiers.

**Founder access:**
Needs a French-speaking founder, physically able to visit yards. Not realistic for a non-local.

**Score:** 3/10

### Opportunity: Field bore-log to eDOV XML for Flemish drilling firms

**Industry:**
Water-well and geothermal drilling, Flanders.

**Buyer:**
Owner or office manager of a VLAREL-recognised drilling firm.

**Trigger / Why now:**
Since 2017 only recognised firms may drill groundwater wells. Each permit-requiring drilling needs a pre-notification at least 2 days ahead. Every bore must be reported with its log and well schema within 2 months via eDOV. Enforcement guidance for groundwater exists (omgeving.vlaanderen.be). Rising interest in private wells during droughts (a July 2026 blog post on own boreholes) is an estimate-level demand signal, not a regulatory trigger.

**Current workflow:**
1. The driller notes lithology by depth on site.
2. The office files the pre-notification.
3. Later, it re-types the bore log and well schema into the eDOV screens, or builds the XML in the DOV exchange format and validates it with the DOV tool.

**Pain:**
Double entry from paper field notes into eDOV, under a 2-month deadline. No complaints were found.

**Existing solutions:**
- eDOV online screens.
- eDOV XML import and the DOV online XML validator.
- Geotechnical logging software (international packages; Belgian use unverified).

**Offline evidence:**
Field logs are written by hand on the rig (unverified). The firms are small family drillers with brochure websites.

**Offline channel:**
The VLAREL list of recognised drilling firms, and the drilling-sector training that recognition requires (provider unverified).

**Market count:**
Unverified. As an estimate, low hundreds of recognised firms.

**The gap:**
A rugged field-entry app that writes valid DOV-format XML directly.

**Possible product:**
A tablet bore-log app with a lithology picker and well-schema builder that exports eDOV XML. It also schedules the pre-notification.

**MVP:**
A form that generates DOV XML, plus the validator call.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
EUR 20-40 per month (estimate).

**How to find first customers:**
Phone the firms on the VLAREL list.

**Risks:**
- A tiny market.
- DOV may improve its own screens.
- Firms with low drilling volume can type it in manually.

**Kill condition:**
Fewer than about 150 active firms, or the typical firm files fewer than about 5 bores a month.

**Founder access:**
Dutch-language, and needs a local presence.

**Score:** 2/10

## 3. Rejected

- **Scrap-metal dealer register.** The cash ban since 31 Mar 2024 removed the ID registration tied to cash payments, so there is no recurring register workflow left (denuo.be, jubel.be).
- **Second-hand, gold and antique dealer registers.** No Belgian equivalent of the Dutch police register was found, so there is no forced workflow.
- **Heating-attest apps (three regions).** Testo Pro+ and EuroSoft Attest already issue Flemish, Brussels and Walloon certificates on site.
- **Livestock-trader movement records.** Sanitel (FAVV) is central, and DGZ/ARSIA mediate.
- **Beekeeper medicine register.** Hobbyists with no willingness to pay. FAVV registration is free.
- **Tattoo studio compliance.** 2,350 registered artists, but no surprise inspections in 2022, so enforcement is too weak to create pain.
- **Flemish market traders.** Deregulated in 2024. The buyer is the municipality.
- **PAB/PVB budget-holder payroll.** Social secretariats, Helpper, Partner in Assistentie and budget-holder associations already do it.
- **Dog and cat breeders.** About 11 recognised associations hold the administration, and the government is specifying a central database in 2026.
- **Hunting cull reporting.** Free ANB e-loket, with volunteer WBEs as the buyer.
- **Flemish taxi operators.** Licensing is one-off, and trips already run through apps.

## 4. Method notes

- What worked: Dutch and French queries naming the exact legal object, such as "vidangeur agréé", "boorbedrijf eDOV", "register gewasbeschermingsmiddelen" and "omzendbrief". Official Walloon pages (wallonie.be, wallex, sol.environnement) and Flemish pages (lv.vlaanderen.be, DOV confluence, FAVV circulars) returned the precise obligations.
- What failed: operator counts. Phytolicence totals, drilling firms, beekeepers and livestock traders rarely appear in snippets. The register usually exists, but only as a portal or list that WebFetch could not open.
- Belgium-specific lesson: regulators here tend to build their own intake portal (Sanitel, eDOV, SIGPAA, ANB e-loket, eLys). That turns the "quiet" gap into thin copy-assist products with small markets.
