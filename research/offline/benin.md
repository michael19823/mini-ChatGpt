# Benin: Offline-Industries Pass

**Date:** 2026-10-05 · **Searches:** 20 of 20 WebSearch calls (budget for this country). Mostly French queries. WebFetch was not used. Anything not backed by a source below is marked **unverified** or **estimate**.

**Context:** the existing country report (`research/countries/benin.md`) found no strong opportunity. Benin's state digitises aggressively and builds its own free portals (DGI e-services, e-MECeF, GUCE, SIGMaP). This pass found the same pattern in the quiet industries. The state has already built a free platform for domestic-worker declarations ("Gens de maison"), for primary-school data (EducMaster) and for moto-taxi registration (Cotonou municipal census with a QR card). The one new trigger with a private-software opening is the **May 2026 driving-licence reform**. It requires driving schools to document training hours in a learning booklet and follow-up sheet. Even that lead is small.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

Only one lead is worth recording, and it is weak.

### Opportunity: Digital training booklet and ANaTT exam-file pack for driving schools

**Industry:**  
Driving schools (auto-écoles) licensed by ANaTT (Agence Nationale des Transports Terrestres)

**Buyer:**  
Owner-operator (*promoteur*) of a licensed driving school with 1–3 instructors and a few vehicles, mainly in Cotonou, Abomey-Calavi, Porto-Novo and Parakou (**estimate** for locations).

**Trigger / Why now:**  
A ministerial order signed on 20 May 2026 (reported as decree/arrêté n°2026-96) reformed the driving licence. Candidates need at least 60 h of theory and 30 h of practice. To access the practical exam they need six months of training or **proof of 120 h of effective driving, documented by a learning booklet and follow-up sheet issued by the driving school**. The reform also adds categories B1 and BEA, and the theory test is now taken on a smartphone with instant results ([Banouto, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.banouto.bj/societe/article/20260720-reforme-du-permis-de-conduire-ce-qui-change-avec-le-nouvel-arrete-de-lanatt-04-choses-a-retenir), [BeninWebTV](https://beninwebtv.com/permis-de-conduire-au-benin-un-parcours-de-formation-renforce), [La Nouvelle Tribune](https://lanouvelletribune.info/2026/07/benin-le-permis-de-conduire-revolutionne-pour-sauver-des-vies/)). ANaTT also says no hours were added compared with the old rules ([Badona](https://badona.bj/bon-a-savoir/article/permis-de-conduire-au-benin-05-choses-a-savoir-sur-la-polemique-des-frais-de-formation)), so the change may be in **proof and enforcement** more than in substance. In parallel ANaTT is cracking down: an auto-école was suspended for fraud in May 2025 ([Le Potentiel](https://lepotentiel.bj/2025/05/16/lutte-contre-la-fraude-dans-les-auto-ecoles-lanatt-suspend-lauto-ecole-perseverant-plus-pour-fraude/)), and more than 30 unlicensed schools were closed in Atlantique in July 2026, with checks extending to the whole country ([Triomphe Mag](https://triomphemag.bj/benin-plus-de-trente-etablissements-dauto-ecoles-clandestines-fermes-dans-latlantique-lanatt-etend-ses-controles-a-tout-le-pays/)).

**Current workflow:**  
1. The school enrols the candidate and collects identity documents and fees (in cash or Mobile Money, **unverified**).
2. The instructor logs each lesson in a paper booklet or follow-up sheet (format set by ANaTT, **unverified**), counting towards 30 h / 120 h.
3. Before the exam, the school compiles the candidate file and booklet and submits it to ANaTT (online or at the counter, **unverified**).
4. ANaTT inspectors check the booklets and files. Fraud leads to suspension.

**Pain:**  
Indirect only. The reform triggered a public fee controversy: promoters say costs must rise because of the higher volume of driving hours, while ANaTT denies any increase ([Badona](https://badona.bj/bon-a-savoir/article/permis-de-conduire-au-benin-05-choses-a-savoir-sur-la-polemique-des-frais-de-formation), [L'Événement Précis on the FAAB](https://levenementprecis.bj/clarification-de-la-federation-des-auto-ecoles-agreees-du-benin-la-faab-dement-toute-hausse-des-frais-du-permis-de-conduire/)). Fraud suspensions show that ANaTT checks the paperwork. No direct complaint about the booklet itself was found.

**Existing solutions:**  
- The paper booklet and follow-up sheet issued by the school (the default substitute).
- ANaTT's own systems for exam booking and the digital theory test (whatever the agency adds next could absorb the booklet).
- French driving-school software with digital booklets (Klaxo, ReservationAutoEcole, Colibri, Drive Scout: [Appvizer](https://www.appvizer.fr/services/auto-ecole/klaxo)), built for the French ANTS/RdvPermis system, priced in euros and not localised for ANaTT. None was found marketing in Benin.
- Generic Excel or WhatsApp scheduling.

**Offline evidence:**  
The booklet is legally a document "delivered by the driving school". No Benin driving-school software listing was found. The schools appear in directories (expat.com, globenin) with phone numbers rather than websites. The associations communicate by press communiqués.

**Offline channel:**  
The two associations: **CoNaPAB** (Conseil National des Promoteurs d'Auto-écoles Agréées du Bénin, president Babatoundé Thomas Djégui, which publicly supported the reform: [Kaweru](https://www.kaweru.com/politique-societe/2026/07/19/benin-le-conseil-des-promoteurs-dauto-ecoles-agreees-reagit-aux-nouvelles-conditions-dobtention-du-permis-de-conduire/), [Nasuba](https://www.nasuba.info/politique-societe/benin-le-conapab-apporte-des-precisions-sur-les-nouvelles-conditions-dobtention-du-permis-de-conduire/)) and **FAAB** (Fédération des auto-écoles agréées du Bénin). Also ANaTT awareness sessions for promoters, and phone or WhatsApp outreach from the directories ([globenin](https://www.globenin.com/Benin/auto-ecoles-conduite), [expat.com](https://www.expat.com/en/business/africa/benin/11_transportation/84_driving-schools/)).

**Market count:**  
Not found. The number of licensed schools is **unknown**; low hundreds nationally is an **estimate**. ANaTT may hold a list of licensed schools (**unverified**).

**The gap:**  
A tamper-evident lesson log (instructor + candidate confirm each lesson on a phone, with time and GPS) that prints or exports the booklet and follow-up sheet in the ANaTT format. It would give the school proof against fraud accusations and a running count towards 30 h / 120 h. Nobody sells this in Benin. The risk is that ANaTT builds it into its own platform, as it did for the theory test.

**Possible product:**  
A mobile-first lesson logbook for Beninese driving schools. Instructors log lessons, candidates confirm by SMS or WhatsApp, the app tracks hours per candidate and category (B, B1, BEA, etc.), and produces the booklet / follow-up sheet and a pre-exam checklist. Add Mobile Money fee tracking per candidate later.

**MVP:**  
An Android web app: candidate list, lesson log with candidate OTP confirmation, hour counters against the 30 h / 120 h thresholds, and a PDF booklet export. No ANaTT integration.

**Pricing hypothesis:**  
XOF 10,000–20,000 per school per month (about USD 17–35), or XOF 2,000–3,000 per candidate file. **Estimate.** The buyer would pay for software only if ANaTT accepts the printout. Otherwise it is a nice-to-have.

**How to find first customers:**  
Present to CoNaPAB and FAAB at a members' meeting; ask ANaTT whether a digital booklet is acceptable; call schools from the public directories in Cotonou and Abomey-Calavi.

**Risks:**  
ANaTT digitises the booklet itself (likely, given the smartphone theory test and the state's habit of building free platforms). A small and poor market. A foreign founder needs a local partner: it is a relationship sale through associations, in French and Fon, with cash or Mobile Money billing. The fee controversy may make promoters resist any added cost.

**Kill condition:**  
ANaTT announces its own digital booklet, or says it only accepts its official paper booklet; or there are fewer than about 150 licensed schools.

**Founder access:**  
Needs a local partner or a local founder. A non-local solo founder could not credibly sell through CoNaPAB/FAAB.

**Score:** 3/10 (pain 4, frequency 8 because it is per lesson, mandatory 7, fragmentation 2, competition 7 as nobody local is in it, incumbent gap 5, buyer accessibility 6, willingness to pay 2, MVP simplicity 8, distribution 5). The regulatory risk and low willingness to pay dominate. Better as a module of a francophone West Africa driving-school product (Côte d'Ivoire also publishes an approved-schools list: [securiteroutiere.gouv.ci](https://securiteroutiere.gouv.ci/liste-auto-ecoles-agreees)).

## 3. Rejected

- **Domestic-worker CNSS declarations.** The obligation is real and enforced (2023 ultimatum, criminal penalties), but CNSS, DGI and ASIN built the free "Gens de maison" platform, linked to ANIP, in Nov 2023. The substitute is the state platform plus the household's own accountant. The leftover (payslips) is a generic payroll app.
- **Private-school EducMaster double entry.** The state platform is free and mandatory, with no known API. School management software is a crowded generic category. Revisit only if EducMaster opens a CSV import that third-party tools could feed.
- **Moto-taxi licensing and professional cards.** A municipal B2G system, and drivers pay XOF 4,800 a year. Nothing for an indie vendor.
- **Scrap metal.** The 2023 export ban removed the export paperwork. No dealer register reported to police was found.
- **Livestock / transhumance.** State vets issue the certificates. Cross-border transhumance is restricted. Herders don't buy software.
- **Pesticide dealers, fuel/LPG stations, used-vehicle parks.** No recurring operator-side report found. Approvals are already online or handled by customs and forwarders.

## 4. Method notes

What worked: French queries combining "Bénin" with the regulator acronym (ANaTT, CNSS, MEMP) and a year, which surfaced Beninese news sites (lanouvelletribune.info, beninwebtv.bj, banouto.bj, kaweru.com) reporting new orders and enforcement campaigns. "Fermées", "suspend", "ultimatum" and "contrôle" were good enforcement keywords. What didn't work: searches for registers or lists of licensed operators (no public lists online), and hotel or police-register queries, which returned European results. sgg.gouv.bj decrees appear in results but snippets are thin without WebFetch.
