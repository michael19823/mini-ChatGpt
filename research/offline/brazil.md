## 2. Opportunities

### Opportunity: "Compra Segura": police-ready purchase register for scrap dealers

**Industry:**
Scrap metal and recycling dealers (ferros-velhos, sucatarias, depósitos) that buy from individuals and catadores.

**Buyer:**
The owner-operator of a small or medium scrap yard. Second buyer: the accountant who issues their NF-e entry invoices (*nota fiscal de entrada*).

**Trigger / Why now:**
- **Federal Lei 15.181/2025** (in force 29 Jul 2025) doubles the penalties for theft and for receiving stolen wires, cables and power or telecom equipment. A yard caught holding stolen cable now faces much higher criminal exposure.
- **A wave of 2025–2026 state and municipal laws, each with its own rules:**
  - **Ceará, Lei 19.268/2025:** registration with the Polícia Civil and Detran-CE, invoices on every transaction, and a record book.
  - **Rondônia, Lei 6.426/2026 (2 Jun 2026):** annual state registration, plus an **electronic** register of every transaction with:
    - the seller's photo ID and CPF;
    - a photo taken at the sale;
    - the material's type, weight and declared origin;
    - a photo of the material.

    Fines go up to 10,000 UPF, plus suspension or loss of the registration.
  - **João Pessoa, Lei 15.893/2026 (3 Jul 2026):** targets cables, manhole covers, gravestone plaques and bronze crucifixes.
  - **Belo Horizonte, 2025:** opening-hour restrictions and "Operação Ferro-Velho", with more than 200 establishments inspected in 2025.
  - **Pending or other local rules:** bills in São Paulo state (ALESP, 2025) and Cuiabá (2025), and rules in São José dos Campos, Juiz de Fora and Jundiaí.

**Current workflow:**
1. A catador or other seller arrives. The material is weighed on the yard's scale. The yard pays in cash or by Pix.
2. The seller's name and RG/CPF go into a paper notebook or record book, or onto the scale ticket. Some yards use a small management system or an Excel sheet.
3. Later, the accountant or the yard issues an NF-e entry invoice for purchases from non-taxpayers. State tax rules require the seller's ID, address and a detailed description of the material (MG Lei 11.817/1995 and Lei 21.138/2014).
4. During police, DETRAN or municipal inspections, the owner shows the book and invoices. In Rondônia, the register must now be electronic and include photos.

**Pain:**
- Receiving stolen cable now carries a doubled sentence.
- Interdictions and closures are documented (e.g., Detran-RJ closing a yard in Itaboraí).
- Rules differ by state and city, and new ones keep arriving (CE 2025, RO June 2026, João Pessoa July 2026).
- Paper books can't produce what Rondônia now requires: photo of the seller, photo of the material, declared origin.

**Existing solutions:**
- Small vertical tools focused on cash, stock and scale tickets:
  - EcoSistema (NF-e, weighing tickets, suppliers);
  - LC Info "Gestor" for recycling and scrap depots;
  - SCARP (purchases, sales, stock by material, receipts);
  - Custom System (scale integration, purchase and sale slips);
  - "Gestão Reciclagem" (Excel spreadsheet product).
- Mid-market ERP: Tecnicon's recycling vertical.
- Paper record books from stationers.
- Accountants who issue the NF-e entry invoices.
- Government side: state registration forms at the Polícia Civil and DETRAN counters. No state portal for the purchase register was found.

**Offline evidence:**
- The Ceará law literally specifies a record book.
- Purchases are cash at the scale, from informal sellers.
- The tools found sell on stock and cash features. None of the result snippets mentioned seller photo-ID capture, declared-origin fields or police-format exports.
- No SaaS review pages and no forums.
- An accounting Q&A forum thread ("Empresa de Ferro-velho/Sucata") shows owners asking basic legalisation questions.

**Offline channel:**
1. The public CNPJ register (Receita Federal open data), filtered by CNAE 4687-7/03 (wholesale of metal scrap) and the recycling CNAEs, then phone or WhatsApp outreach by city. Start in Rondônia, Ceará, João Pessoa and BH, where the new rules apply.
2. Scale (balança) sellers and calibration technicians, who visit every yard. They are a natural reseller with an existing relationship.
3. Inesfa and the state scrap unions [U on exact union names].
4. Accountants who already issue entry invoices for yards.
5. Lists from inspection campaigns ("Operação Ferro-Velho") and the municipal registration lists the new laws create.

**Market count:**
More than 5,600 companies according to Inesfa (search snippet; another snippet says about 3,000 SMEs). Informal depósitos are additional (estimate, unverified). The number of yards in the jurisdictions with the new laws is not counted.

**The gap:**
No product found turns each purchase into a **jurisdiction-specific evidence record**: validated CPF, ID photo, seller photo, material photo, weight from the scale, declared origin, and flags for high-risk items (cable, manhole covers, bronze). Nor does any product found generate the NF-e entry invoice and an inspection-ready export in the format each state or city wants.

**Possible product:**
A tablet or phone app at the scale. It captures the seller and the material in about 30 seconds, issues the NF-e entry invoice through an invoice API, stores an immutable log, and produces the register in the format each jurisdiction requires (e.g., Rondônia, Ceará, João Pessoa). It warns the operator on high-risk items and on repeat sellers.

**MVP:**
Android app plus web panel for **one** jurisdiction (Rondônia's Lei 6.426/2026, which is the most explicit):
- CPF check;
- three photos;
- manual weight entry;
- declared-origin field;
- a PDF/CSV register for the inspector.

NF-e entry invoices come in version 2, through an existing invoice API.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
R$99–199 per month per yard. Alternatively, R$0.50–1.00 per purchase recorded. The buyer likely pays for this as "protection" (software). An accountant-bundled service is the more realistic route for very small yards.

**How to find first customers:**
- CNPJ open data by CNAE, filtered to Rondônia, Ceará and Paraíba first, then WhatsApp or phone.
- Scale technicians as resellers.
- Accountants serving yards.

**Founder access:** A non-local solo founder could only sell this with fluent Portuguese and WhatsApp/phone selling. On-site demos at the scale help a lot. Realistically, it needs a local partner, such as a scale technician or an accountant.

**Risks:**
- Informality: many yards may prefer *not* to record sellers.
- Laws may go unenforced after the headlines.
- Each new jurisdiction needs its own format.
- The small vertical tools listed above could add photo and ID fields quickly.
- Margins are low and the business is cash-based.

**Kill condition:**
- 10 interviews in Rondônia, Ceará or João Pessoa show inspectors never ask for the register, or accept a notebook.
- Or EcoSistema or SCARP already offers jurisdiction-specific seller-ID and photo capture.

### Opportunity: Controlled-drug register and MAPA reporting for field vets and small agro-vet sellers

**Industry:**
Veterinary medicine, specifically holders of controlled veterinary substances (anaesthetics such as ketamine).

**Buyers:**
- Ambulatory large-animal and equine vets, and small clinics without clinic software.
- MAPA-registered agropecuárias and veterinary compounding pharmacies, for the movement report.

**Trigger / Why now:**
**Portaria MAPA 837/2025** (signed 23 Sep 2025, published in the DOU on 2 Jan 2026, later corrected) sets a new special-control regime:
- Purchases require an electronic acquisition notification issued by a vet registered in MAPA's system.
- The register book must be updated **at least every 7 days**.
- Electronic bookkeeping is allowed only through a MAPA-validated system or a future MAPA system.
- Electronic controlled prescriptions are being centralised in the national prescription-control system (SNCR), whose prescription function was expected by June 2026.
- The adaptation deadline is **31 Dec 2026**. Movement reporting becomes mandatory from **Jan 2027**.
- The regional vet councils are training members now: CRMV-RJ manual of 29 Jul 2026, and the CRMV-MG guide.

**Current workflow:**
1. The vet registers in SIPEAGRO or MAPA's system and issues an acquisition notification.
2. The vet buys from a distributor. Stock leaves the seller on an invoice in the vet's name.
3. Each use or loss is written by hand into the paper register book, updated weekly at minimum.
4. Merchants and compounders also file the semiannual movement report in SIPEAGRO by hand (IN 35/2017).

**Pain:**
- Weekly bookkeeping by hand, with "no erasures, amendments or stickers".
- Field vets work from a truck, not a clinic.
- New 2027 reporting is coming on top of existing purchase, prescription and register steps.

**Existing solutions:**
- Paper register books.
- SimplesVet, which has a controlled-drug prescription feature.
- FlyVet, which publishes guidance on the register book and MAPA.
- Other clinic software [U].
- MAPA's own SIPEAGRO and SNCR.
- CRMV manuals.
- Anfarmag guidance for compounding pharmacies.

**Offline evidence:**
- The legal instrument is a physical book.
- MAPA's Q&A states that the minimum characteristics for recognising an electronic system **have not been published, and recognition is currently impossible**.

**Offline channel:**
- CRMV training events and newsletters.
- Distributors and agropecuárias that sell controlled anaesthetics. They see every buyer, since every sale is invoiced to a named vet.
- Equine and rodeo associations [U].
- Anfarmag, for compounders.

**Market count:**
Not found. Number of vets holding controlled drugs: estimate in the tens of thousands, unverified. Number of MAPA-registered merchants: unknown.

**The gap:**
No affordable mobile tool reconciles acquisition notifications, purchase invoices and per-case use into a compliant weekly register and the Jan 2027 movement report for vets who have no clinic system.

**Possible product:**
A mobile log for field vets, with an invoice import and a printable register that matches the book's layout. When MAPA publishes the validation spec, it becomes a validated electronic book.

**MVP:**
- Logs uses per animal and case.
- Imports purchase NF-e XML.
- Shows a weekly reminder.
- Prints a book-format PDF.
- Exports a semiannual-report CSV for agropecuárias.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
R$29–59 per month per vet. R$99 per month per agropecuária. This would be paid as software.

**How to find first customers:**
- Distributors' and agropecuárias' counters.
- CRMV events.
- Equine vet groups.

**Founder access:** It needs Portuguese and vet-sector credibility. A local partner (a vet) is close to essential.

**Risks:**
- MAPA builds its own free electronic book (the Portaria explicitly foresees "a system to be instituted by MAPA").
- The validation spec may never come.
- Clinic SaaS vendors will add this feature.

**Kill condition:**
MAPA announces a free government electronic register, or the clinic SaaS leaders ship a validated book before mid-2027.

## 4. Method notes

- **Worked:**
  - Portuguese queries naming the regulator and the instrument ("Lei nº … 2026 sucata cadastro", "Portaria MAPA 837/2025", "Detran Portaria desmonte"). Legisweb, Normas Brasil and state gazette PDFs surface new state and municipal laws quickly.
  - Searching for "sistema para X" / "software para X" in quotes reliably exposes the small vertical vendors.
- **Weak:**
  - Market counts. Associations rarely publish member numbers in snippets.
  - Competitor feature depth, since WebFetch is not available.
  - Some news sites stamp old articles with the current date; the shooting-club results were 2022 stories.
- **Not done (budget refused at search 27):**
  - LPG resellers under "Gás do Povo".
  - Tattoo studios, cemeteries and funeral homes, beekeepers.
  - A CNAE count for scrap yards from Receita Federal open data.
