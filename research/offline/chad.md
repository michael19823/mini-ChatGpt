# Chad (Tchad): offline-industries pass

*Research date: 2026-10-05. Search budget: 8 WebSearch calls, all used. Languages: French. Arabic (Chadian Arabic is the trade language in livestock and gum markets) was not searched. This is a short report because the market is inaccessible. See the existing report `research/countries/chad.md` for accessibility (XAF and mobile money only, so you need a local partner) and for the FEN e-invoice ideas, which are not repeated here.*

**Bottom line:** Chad's quiet industries are real and large in headcount: livestock trade, gum arabic, artisanal gold, moto-taxis. In every one of them, though, the "regulator" is a tax collector at a market gate or checkpoint, not a register-keeping authority with forms. The workflow is cash, receipts and informal levies ("fausses taxes"), not paperwork a product could route. Only one niche, gum arabic exporters, has a documentary export workflow and paying foreign buyers. Even that is a few dozen firms and needs a local partner. Nothing here meets the build bar.

## 2. Opportunity

### Opportunity: Gum arabic export dossier and buyer-document pack

**Industry:**
Gum arabic (and sesame) export trading

**Buyer:**
The owner or export manager of a Chadian gum arabic exporting firm. These are usually family trading houses in N'Djamena or Abéché that ship via Douala to processors in France and the US.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- An August 2025 Finance Ministry order created a 5% export duty plus a 2% statistical fee on gum arabic.
- Volumes are rising: 52,500 t in 2024, about 61,600 t projected for 2025. In January–April 2026 Chad overtook Sudan as France's largest supplier.
- The Sudan war makes Chadian supply strategic, so foreign processors will ask for more origin and quality documentation (inference, **unverified**).

**Current workflow:**
1. Assemble the Ministry of Commerce special export authorisation dossier (more than 10 documents) and file it in person.
2. Get phytosanitary and origin certificates, plus customs declaration and payment of the 7% levy.
3. Forward the copies by email or WhatsApp to the foreign buyer and the Douala freight forwarder.
4. Repeat for each shipment or campaign.

**Pain:**
- Documented as a non-tariff barrier in an AfCFTA complaint, which was later marked resolved.
- An industry piece describes the sector as showing "striking technical and technological disorganisation".
- No exporter complaints about paperwork itself were found (**unverified** pain intensity).

**Existing solutions:**
- Freight forwarders and transitaires in N'Djamena and Douala (for example AGL, which publicised logistics support for Chad agro-pastoral exports).
- ANIE (the national investment and export agency) organises export campaigns.
- Excel, email and WhatsApp.
- The buyer's own supplier-qualification portals (general knowledge for food-ingredient processors, **unverified**).

**Offline evidence:**
- The authorisation is a paper dossier filed at a ministry counter.
- There is no software listing for Chadian gum exporters.
- The exporters' association is known only through press quotes from its president.

**Offline channel:**
- The Association des exportateurs de gomme arabique du Tchad (its president is named in the press).
- ANIE export campaigns.
- The French processors who buy from Chad. They could push a supplier-document standard down to their suppliers, which would be the realistic wedge.

**Market count:**
A few dozen exporters (**estimate**; no register found). Volume is about 60,000 t/year.

**The gap:**
- A per-shipment dossier: one lot record that produces the ministry authorisation file, the customs and levy computation, and a buyer-ready origin and quality pack.
- **Unverified** whether exporters see this as worth paying for rather than leaving it to their transitaire.

**Possible product:**
A shipment-lot record from which the exporter generates the authorisation checklist, a 7%-levy calculation, and a standard document pack for the foreign buyer (origin, lot, quality certificates). It would be sold as done-for-you through a local partner.

**MVP:**
A checklist plus document vault per lot, with a one-click PDF and zip pack for the buyer. A service operator in N'Djamena would do the filing.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
A per-shipment service fee of about 50,000–150,000 XAF (≈ $80–250) (**estimate**). Exporters would pay for the done-for-you service, not for software.

**How to find first customers:**
Through the association and through foreign processors' supplier lists. A local partner (a transitaire or accounting firm) is needed.

**Willingness to pay:**
Service only. They would pay because shipments earn hard currency, but a transitaire already bundles this.

**Founder access:**
A non-local solo founder could not realistically sell this. It needs a local, Arabic- and French-speaking partner. A better route is the buyer side (France or US processors) offering supplier onboarding.

**Risks:**
- The transitaire already does it as part of its fee.
- The market is tiny.
- The 2025 complaint resolution may have simplified the authorisation.
- Security in the east.
- Collecting payment.

**Kill condition:**
Exporters say their transitaire handles the dossier inside the freight fee, or the authorisation has been reduced to a single form.

**Score:** 3/10

## 3. Rejected

- **Livestock market tax compliance:** The taxes are real and rose in 2025 and 2026 ([sikafinance](https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/tchad-le-gouvernement-augmente-les-taxes-sur-les-ventes-de-betail_50264), [Alwihda, N'Djamena tariffs](https://www.alwihdainfo.com/tchad-nouveaux-tarifs-dans-les-marches-de-betail-de-ndjamena/)). But they are cash collected at the gate, and the pain is "false taxes" ([Alwihda](https://www.alwihdainfo.com/Tchad-dans-les-marches-a-betail-les-fausses-taxes-jouent-sur-les-prix_a79867.html)). That is a governance problem, not a workflow problem.
- **Live-cattle export certificates:** Live export will be banned from 1 Jan 2028 ([Ecomatin](https://ecomatin.net/commerce-le-tchad-interdira-lexportation-de-betail-vivant-a-partir-de-2028), [Alwihda](https://www.alwihdainfo.com/fin-de-lexportation-du-betail-sur-pied-des-2028-le-pari-de-la-transformation-locale-au-tchad/)). The successor meat-export abattoirs are a few PPP projects.
- **Livestock traceability or ID:** I found no Chadian programme for 2025–26. Donor pilots elsewhere in the Sahel are the only substitute.
- **Artisanal gold buyers:** The state is centralising purchase (a 5 bn XAF central counter for 2026–28, and the single window moved to the Ministry of Mines) ([Ecomatin](https://ecomatin.net/mines-un-investissement-public-de-5-milliards-fcfa-pour-centraliser-le-commerce-des-metaux-precieux-au-tchad), [Ecomatin](https://ecomatin.net/tchad-le-gouvernement-reprend-le-controle-de-la-filiere-aurifere-face-aux-reseaux-de-contrebande)). There is no SME buyer, and AML and security make it a no-go.
- **Moto-taxi identification:** It is driven by the ministry–union pact and run by the union, and the riders are poor ([tchadinfos 2024](https://tchadinfos.com/2024/09/17/signature-dun-pacte-entre-le-ministere-des-transports-et-les-syndicats-des-mototaxis/)).
- **Well drillers:** They are paid by donor projects, so the buyer is the PIU ([tchadinfos](https://tchadinfos.com/2026/01/11/salamat-des-forages-reussis-ameliorent-lacces-a-leau-potable-en-zones-de-socle/)).
- **Domestic-worker CNPS, scrap and pawn registers, butchers:** I found no enforced obligation or data.

## 4. Method notes

- **Worked:** French queries on Chadian news sites (Ecomatin, tchadinfos, Alwihda) surfaced the new taxes and decrees. The AfCFTA NTB complaint portal (tradebarriers.africa) was the best "official form" proxy for export paperwork.
- **Didn't work:** Searches for licensing registers (forage agrément, traçabilité, comptoirs) returned nothing Chadian. Chad publishes few registers online.
- **Not done:** Arabic queries were not tried. For quiet trades (livestock, gum) they are probably needed, but within 8 searches they were skipped.
