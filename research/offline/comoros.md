# Comoros: offline (quiet) industries pass

**Researched:** 2026-10-05. **Searches used:** 3 of 8 (stopped early: results were thin and the market is tiny). **Languages:** French, English.

**Verdict: no quiet-industry opportunity worth pursuing.** Comoros has very few formal operators in each group. Registers are either not published online or do not exist. No regulator trigger dated 2024-2027 was found. Most of the screen below rests on absence of evidence and is marked unverified.

## 1. Quiet industries screened

| Industry | Obligation | Evidence it's offline | Rough count (source) | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Household employers (domestic workers) | CNSS affiliation | Search returned only Morocco and Benin material. Comoros-specific rules and any portal were not found. | Unverified | Reject | Enforcement is unproven. Almost no formal domestic employment. |
| Taxi-brousse / minibus operators | Transport licence | Travel guides only. No register, licence rule or enforcement found. | Unverified | Reject | No evidence of any obligation or of a receiving body. |

## 2. Strongest opportunities

None. The best candidate is the money changers and transfer agents group, which has a public Central Bank list. At about 5-6 licensed firms it fails on market count and buyer type. It is not worth a template entry. The existing report's OHADA add-on (score 2/10) remains the only marginal item and is already recorded there.

**Score:** none above 2/10.

## 3. Rejected

All rows above. The common causes are:
- very few formal operators;
- no published licensing registers online;
- donor-run workflows (fisheries, agriculture);
- no regulatory trigger, no enforcement evidence and no receiving portal.

## 4. Method notes

- Regulator-first queries in French did surface one official list (Central Bank intermediaries) and the FAO fisheries profile. These were the only useful returns.
- Domestic-worker and taxi queries returned Morocco, Benin, Madagascar and Mayotte material, not Comoros. This suggests the Comoros online footprint of these regimes is nil. It is not proof that no rules exist.
- Gazette-based searches (Journal Officiel) were not run. The 5 unused searches could test them, but the expected yield is low given the market size.
