# Cyprus: offline (quiet) industries pass

Researched 2026-10-05 with 18 WebSearch calls (full budget) (English and Greek, standard mode). WebFetch was not used. The search engine returned mostly other countries' material for Cyprus-specific regulator queries, so this is a **short, low-evidence report**. Anything not backed by a cited source is marked "unverified". Cyprus is a microstate-sized market (population roughly 0.9-1.0M, estimate), which caps every idea below. See the existing report `research/countries/cyprus.md` for the mainstream opportunities (not repeated here).

## 2. Strongest opportunities

**None meets the bar.** I found no quiet industry in Cyprus where I could verify (a) a recurring regulator-facing workflow, (b) a countable pool of obliged operators, and (c) a reachable offline channel. Scoring any idea above 3/10 would need facts I could not source. The least-bad lead is below, as a hypothesis only.

### Lead (not a full opportunity): household domestic-worker employment paperwork as a done-for-you service

- **Industry:** Households employing third-country domestic workers and carers.
- **Buyer:** Household employers, or the recruitment agencies and accountants who handle their permits (unverified who currently does this).
- **Trigger:** None found for 2025-26. The Cyprus Mail (Nov 2024, Sep 2026) covers migration and labour-shortage debate, not a new filing rule.
- **Existing solutions:** Migration Department direct filing, recruitment agencies, accountants (all unverified as to fees).
- **Offline evidence:** Permits run through the Migration Department and District Immigration Units (Legal 500).
- **Offline channel:** Recruitment agencies. No register of agencies was found.
- **Market count:** Unverified, with inconsistent old figures (about 22,000 to 36,000).
- **Score:** 2/10. Consumer buyer, service model only, no trigger, small country.
- **Sources:** https://www.legal500.com/guides/chapter/cyprus-corporate-immigration/ ; https://cyprus-mail.com/2024/11/20/issue-of-domestic-workers-back-in-the-spotlight ; https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/09/27/cyprus-labour-shortages-who-can-legally-work (all read via search snippets only)

## 3. Rejected

- **Scrap metal dealer register software:** enforcement exists (https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/07/23/over-a-tonne-of-electricity-cables-seized-by-police-overnight), but no purchase-register or reporting duty was found, only 20 premises were inspected, and the trade is small and cash-based. No evidence of recurring regulator filings.
- **Domestic-worker payroll/payslip app:** Social Insurance and the Migration Department are the receiving bodies, but households are consumers who will not pay SaaS prices.
- **Beekeeper and livestock traceability tools:** no Cyprus-specific rule found; the Greek digital sheep/goat system (https://www.tovima.com/society/greece-launches-digital-system-for-sheep-and-goat-tracking/) suggests a Greek-market opportunity, not a Cypriot one.
- **Waste-law compliance for small haulers:** fines went up in 2025 (https://en.politis.com.cy/social-lens/984909/new-law-raises-fines-for-illegal-waste-dumping-to-eur40000), but nothing on a manifest or register workflow was found.

## 4. Method notes

- Used 18 searches in total (budget exhausted). Cyprus-specific hits came from Cyprus Mail, Politis, Fastforward, Sigmalive, businessincyprus.gov.cy and moa.gov.cy/vs. cylaw.org was reached only for unrelated laws; I could not find any Cyprus law on pawnbrokers or second-hand dealers.
- Greek-language queries on Social Insurance Services (domestic employers) and cemeteries returned Greek (e-EFKA, taxheaven) material, not Cyprus. Treat those two as not covered.
- Registers that appear to exist and could give prospect lists: Central Bank of Cyprus bureau-de-change register, DFMR Fleet Register, taxi licence list (all unverified as downloadable).
- Conclusion: Cyprus has no quiet-industry opportunity I can support. A quiet-industry product would only make sense as part of a Greece-plus-Cyprus Greek-language product.
