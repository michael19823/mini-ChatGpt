# Eritrea: Offline-Industries Pass

**Research date:** 2026-10-05
**Budget used:** 6 of 8 WebSearch calls. 2 of those failed with a usage-limit error, so 4 returned results. I stopped early because the market is inaccessible and the errors were recurring.
**Bottom line:** I recommend **no opportunities**. The country report found the market practically closed to a foreign software founder: there are no usable payment rails for recurring subscriptions, EriTel holds a connectivity monopoly with about 20% internet use, the private sector is tiny and the state is hostile to it, and there is no regulatory digitisation trigger. Quiet industries are even more cut off. Eritrea has **no online public business registry**, so this pass's "regulator first" method can't count operators, and every obligation I found is enforced on paper at a ministry counter. The short honest answer is that there is nothing to sell into.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

**None.** No group meets the brief's Final Decision Rule. Every candidate fails on all of these:
- **Market count:** I found no public register or statistic for any group.
- **Payment and willingness to pay:** buyers can't pay foreign recurring subscriptions, and a done-for-you service would need a local presence.
- **Why now:** there is no 2024–2027 regulatory digitisation in any of these industries.
- **Founder access:** a non-local solo founder could not sell here. Even a local founder would face state allocation of licences, currency and fuel, which software doesn't fix.

## 3. Rejected (closest calls)

- **Livestock export certificate packs for Gulf buyers.** This was the only real per-shipment document workflow I found (veterinary certificate, quarantine/isolation record, authenticated certificates). I killed it because the issuing body is the government veterinary service, exporters are few and mostly state or party-linked, and Saudi import bans make shipments episodic. Neighbouring Somaliland, Ethiopia and Djibouti handle far larger livestock volumes, so if this pattern is worth anything it belongs there.
- **Import-licence paperwork service.** The pain is real (reports of more red tape on import licences). The substitute is personal relationships at MOTI and with the state forex allocation, and no software can address that.

## 4. Method notes

- **Didn't work:** register-first queries. Eritrea publishes no online business or licensing registers, gazette text since about 2010 is rarely online, and searches return Ethiopian look-alikes (Addis Chamber, OTRIS). Asking for operator counts returned nothing.
- **Worked somewhat:** foreign-regulator angles (Saudi MEWA livestock import conditions, WOAH documents) and old proclamations mirrored on FAOLEX and Refworld.
- **Not tried:** Tigrinya or Arabic queries. I judged them low-yield given how little the state publishes. Two searches failed on usage-limit errors.
