# Eswatini: offline (quiet) industries pass

**Research date:** 2026-10-05 · **Budget:** 8 WebSearch calls allowed. 4 were sent: 3 returned results and 1 (an extended-mode livestock query) was refused with "usage limit". Following the instructions ("if a search is refused, stop and write up what you have"), research stopped there. WebFetch was not used.
**Market:** about 1.2M people, with a lilangeni pegged to the rand. Most formal businesses run South African software and use South African accountants (see `research/countries/eswatini.md`, which already covers the TaxCore e-invoicing opportunity; it is not repeated here).

**Overall verdict:** I found no quiet-industry opportunity in Eswatini that meets the bar. The market is tiny, the regulators publish very little online (most of the law sits in old Acts on eswatinilii.org, not in fresh gazette notices), and the few obligations I found are either voluntary or not enforced in any visible way. This report is short on purpose. Most rows below are **not verified** because the budget was cut short.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

**None meet the bar.** The best candidate is written up below for the record only.

### Opportunity: Domestic-employer payslip + ENPF contribution service (record only)

**Industry:**
Households as employers (domestic workers, gardeners, herdsmen, caregivers).

**Buyer:**
Middle-class or expatriate household in Mbabane/Manzini that employs 1–3 domestic workers.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- The domestic-employees minimum wage was adjusted from 6 Dec 2024 under the 2022 Wages Order.
- The ENPF ceiling rose to E4,300 for 2026 (maximum contribution E430/month split 50/50).
- The ENPF is campaigning for households to register. A National Pension Fund conversion is pending (from the existing country report). None of these makes household compliance mandatory or enforced.

**Current workflow:**
1. Pay cash or EFT monthly. There is often no payslip.
2. If registered, fill in the ENPF contribution schedule and pay at a branch or by bank (unverified).
3. Work out leave and severance by hand when the employment ends.

**Pain:**
Low. The amounts are small and enforcement is absent. Only 154 households bothered to register in 2023.

**Existing solutions:**
- Doing nothing (the dominant substitute).
- The ENPF counter.
- Labour-office guidance.
- South African household-payroll apps (e.g., SweepSouth/SA-focused tools) that target SA's UIF, not ENPF (unverified fit).

**Offline evidence:**
ENPF publicises registrations through press releases and counts in the hundreds. No software listing for Eswatini household payroll was found.

**Offline channel:**
ENPF outreach campaigns, or the Swaziland Domestic Workers' union/association (name unverified).

**Market count:**
154 registered contributing households (2023, ENPF via Times of Eswatini). The total number of domestic employers is unknown.

**The gap:**
A payslip, ENPF schedule and minimum-wage check. It is technically trivial.

**Possible product:**
A WhatsApp or SMS monthly payslip with an ENPF schedule.

**MVP:**
A form that produces a payslip and the ENPF amount.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
E20–30/month at most. More likely nobody pays. Only a done-for-you service bundled by an accountant would be viable.

**How to find first customers:**
ENPF registrant outreach (they won't share the list), and expat community groups.

**Risks:**
The market is too small. The obligation is voluntary. A non-local founder cannot sell this.

**Kill condition:**
Already met: there are fewer than 1,000 obliged and active buyers.

**Score:** 1/10

**Sources:**
- https://wageindicator.org/salary/minimum-wage/eswatini/42965-domestic-employees
- https://www.pressreader.com/eswatini/times-of-eswatini/20240225/281676849847739
- https://support.payspace.com/portal/en/kb/articles/eswatini-increase-in-enpf-maximum-wage-limit-effective-1-january-2025

## 3. Rejected

- **Domestic-employer payroll/ENPF:** largely voluntary, about 154 formal buyers, and the substitute is doing nothing or the ENPF counter.
- **Kombi/taxi permit management:** no recurring workflow or 2025–26 trigger was found. Permits go through Road Transportation Board hearings under the 2007 Act, which is in-person and association-mediated. Sources: https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/2007/5 , https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/sz/2007/sz-government-gazette-dated-2007-09-20-no-98.pdf
- **Scrap/copper dealer registers:** no Eswatini obligation was found. The results were all about Zimbabwe, South Africa and New Zealand.

## 4. Method notes

- English is the working language of law and the press, so the local-language-first rule does not apply in practice. siSwati queries were not tried.
- The regulator-first approach yields old Acts (eswatinilii.org, gazettes.africa) rather than recent notices or registers. No public licence registers were found.
- Press (Times of Eswatini via PressReader) was the only source that gave counts.
- Livestock (dip tanks, brands, movement permits, FMD controls) is the most plausible quiet industry in Eswatini and was **not screened** because its search was refused. If budget allows, it is the one follow-up worth running, though it will probably end up as a government/donor procurement play rather than an indie product.
