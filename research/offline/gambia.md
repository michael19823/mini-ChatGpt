# Gambia: Offline (quiet) industries pass

**Date:** 2026-10-05. **Budget:** 8 searches allowed, 3 used (results thin; stopped because the market is a microstate, ~2.7M people, and nothing reached the bar). WebFetch not used. Everything not in a search extract is marked unverified/estimate.

**Bottom line:** No opportunity worth scoring above 3/10 was found. The Gambia is a short-honest-report market: tiny operator counts, low willingness to pay, and regulators (CBG, MoTWI, GTU) that work by counter, sticker and union rather than by portal.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

None scored. The only candidate with a trigger is a licence-compliance tracker for recruitment agencies under the Labour (Amendment) Bill 2026; it would score about 2/10 (annual licence, tiny buyer count, no software willingness to pay, a local founder required). Not written up in the template because the evidence is one bill extract.

## 3. Rejected

- **Forex bureaux compliance**: few operators, CBG-supervised, AML software is regional/global.
- **Transport route-licence and sticker management**: free permits, run by GTU, poor operators; would need a local union partnership and would likely only be sold as a service.
- **Domestic-worker payroll and contracts**: no enforcement, 86% have no written contract, no willingness to pay.

## 4. Method notes

- Local-language queries are not useful (English is the official language).
- The CBG site and The Point/Gambiana news were the main indexed sources; ministry sites (MoTWI, Labour, GRA) barely appear in search. Registers exist as bare PDF downloads (cbg.gm/downloads-file/...) that are not parsed by search.
- Not tried due to budget: livestock/abattoir, fisheries licensing (Department of Fisheries), tourism (Gambia Tourism Authority licensing), SSHFC employer registration.
