# Georgia (country, South Caucasus): offline / quiet-industries pass

**Researched:** 2026-10-05. **Budget:** 20 WebSearch calls allowed, 3 attempted. The third call returned "You've hit your usage limit". The instructions say to stop when a search is refused, so this is a **short, incomplete report**. Most rows below are a screen built from background knowledge and marked *unverified*. They are leads for a later pass, not findings.

**Bottom line:** I can't recommend any opportunity at the brief's quality bar. Of the two quiet industries with real evidence:
- **Livestock identification** is run through a free, state-operated system: the National Animal Identification and Traceability System (NAITS), operated by the National Food Agency (NFA).
- **Scrap-metal collection points** exist, but I found no public register or reporting obligation.

The one live lead is a **national pet-registration system** being launched, which could create a per-animal recording duty for vet clinics. It is unverified beyond a headline, so it is listed under "leads", not scored.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

**None.** No idea reached the evidence standard. The single lead worth a follow-up:

- **Vet-clinic pet-registration workflow.** If the national pet-registration system requires clinics to microchip animals and enter them into a state database, a Georgian-language clinic tool could do one entry and send it to both the state registry and the clinic's own records. Before scoring it, verify:
  1. the legal act and its start date;
  2. whether entry goes through a free state portal (which would likely kill the idea);
  3. the number of licensed vet clinics.

  Source: https://georgiatoday.ge/georgia-to-launch-nationwide-pet-registration-system/ (headline only; content not read).
