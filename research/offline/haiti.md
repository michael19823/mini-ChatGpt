# Haiti: offline (quiet) industries pass

**Date:** 2026-10-05. **Searches used:** 4 of 8 (French, extended mode). WebFetch not used. Haitian regulator pages index poorly, so counts are mostly unavailable; unverified items are marked.

**Bottom line:** No opportunity clears the bar. The industries are quiet and regulated, but there is no verified trigger, no register to count operators, and no payment rail for a foreign solo founder (see the main report for PayPal/Stripe/USD limits). A short honest report.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

None. Closest, and still rejected: a **moto-taxi legalisation helper** (plate, DGI, OAVCT), tied to the "Moto pa m legal" programme. It fails on buyer (millions of tiny owner-operators, no payer), payment (cash/MonCash), and a monopoly counterparty. Score about 2/10.

## 3. Rejected

All rows above. Common reasons: state monopolies or telco platforms own the workflow, operators are micro and cash-based, and there is no countable register.

## 4. Method notes

- Worked: searching the OAVCT, ONA/OFATMA and MARNDR names in French surfaced the institutions and the obligations.
- Didn't: no register counts or enforcement stats were returned. No 2025-26 triggers were found beyond the OAVCT moto programme. Four of eight searches were left unused, since the results gave no further leads.
