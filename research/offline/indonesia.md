## 2. Strongest opportunities

### Opportunity: "Faktur-ready gold stock": VAT/PPh provenance and monthly return desk for family gold shops (toko emas)

**Industry:**
Gold jewellery retail (toko emas / toko perhiasan), including shops in traditional markets.

**Buyer:**
The owner or family bookkeeper of a toko emas that is, or is being forced to become, VAT-registered. Secondary buyers: the small tax consultants and accounting firms (KJA) that file for these shops.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- **The rule.** PMK 48/2023 (effective 1 May 2023, amended by PMK 52/2025) requires jewellery traders to register as VAT-registered businesses whatever their turnover. They must charge VAT at a deemed rate: 1.1% of the selling price if they hold complete tax invoices for the stock's acquisition, 1.65% if not. Sales to manufacturers are 0%. PPh 22 at 0.25% applies to sales other than to end consumers.
- **Enforcement in 2025–2026.** Tax offices are calling traders in. Examples:
  - ~35 jewellery traders, organised through the APEPI Denpasar branch, were invited to KPP Denpasar Barat.
  - KPP Subulussalam held a session on 6 May 2026.
  - DDTC reports tax offices "combing traditional markets" for gold traders who have not registered.
- **Returns must now go through e-Faktur/Coretax.** VAT-registered gold traders can no longer use the old 1111 DM form.
- **Coretax does not calculate the deemed-rate VAT for this kind of sale.** The trader must compute the VAT due before creating the invoice (Ortax/DDTC guidance).

**Current workflow:**
1. The shop buys new stock from a manufacturer or wholesaler, sometimes with a tax invoice and often without. It also buys back old gold from consumers, which never comes with a tax invoice.
2. Sales are written on a paper receipt with weight, karat and price. Many shops use generic cashier software or a ledger book.
3. At month-end the owner or an outside consultant sorts sales by acquisition evidence: 1.1% for items with invoices, 1.65% for items without, 0% for sales to manufacturers. They decide where PPh 22 at 0.25% applies.
4. The consultant enters each invoice, or a summary invoice for end consumers (unverified for Coretax), into Coretax using "DPP Nilai Lain / besaran tertentu" and files the monthly VAT return.
5. If a cash transaction reaches Rp500m, the shop should also file an AML transaction report to PPATK in goAML. Low awareness is documented.

**Pain:**
- **Money left on the table.** The 0.55-point VAT gap is real cash: on Rp5bn of annual sales it is ~Rp27.5m a year (estimate, my arithmetic). A shop that cannot prove which stock came with invoices pays 1.65% on everything.
- **Coretax does not help.** It requires a manual pre-calculation for deemed-rate deliveries.
- **The industry says the scheme is too complex.** The producers' association APPI told the Finance Minister on 23 Oct 2025 that it is "complicated". It estimates ~90% of producers operate outside compliant tax channels, which means upstream invoices often don't exist.
- **Enforcement is active.** Tax officers make field visits to shops (DDTC, "Toko Emas Didatangi Lagi oleh Pegawai Pajak").

**Existing solutions:**
- Coretax: the government's free filing tool, with no calculation for deemed-rate VAT.
- Licensed tax-application providers (PJAP): OnlinePajak, Pajakku, Klikpajak. They are general e-Faktur tools. OnlinePajak markets to gold traders, but I found no stock-provenance feature.
- Generic cashier/POS software (e.g., SID Retail Pro on Tokopedia) and foreign jewellery software (wJewel). Neither has Indonesian deemed-rate VAT logic.
- Tax consultants and KJA: e.g., MSM Consulting publishes gold-tax guides.
- Paper receipt books and ledgers.

**Offline evidence:**
- Enforcement happens through in-person KPP sessions and field visits to market stalls.
- Searches for "software toko emas" return only student theses and generic POS listings. There are no review pages and no vendor blogs aimed at the PMK 48 workflow.
- The PPATK reporting study says traders lack awareness, and PPATK outreach to them has been weak or absent.

**Offline channel:**
1. APEPI regional branches (apepi.id). The association already convenes traders for KPP sessions.
2. Sit in the KPP outreach sessions for gold traders that tax offices hold (Denpasar, Pasuruan, Subulussalam, Singkawang), co-presented with a local tax consultant.
3. Wholesalers and manufacturers that supply many shops. They benefit when shops ask for invoices.
4. Walk the gold rows of traditional markets in one city, e.g., Pasar Baru Jakarta or Pasar Atom Surabaya (choice of markets is my suggestion, not sourced).

**Market count:**
- About 30,000 gold shops and 500+ industry players nationally (Kemenperin).
- How many are already VAT-registered is unknown.

**The gap:**
Nothing tracks, item by item, whether stock was acquired with a complete tax invoice. Without that, nothing can split monthly sales into the 1.1%, 1.65% and 0% buckets, flag PPh 22 sales, and produce the Coretax invoice and return data. Consultants rebuild this from paper each month, and shops default to 1.65%.

**Possible product:**
A gold-shop stock ledger: each piece or batch is tagged at intake with its supplier, invoice number (or none, or consumer buyback), weight and karat. The cashier screen prints the receipt and assigns the VAT/PPh treatment automatically. At month-end it produces Coretax-ready invoice data and a VAT working paper for the consultant, plus a flag for goAML threshold transactions.

**MVP:**
- An Android/web app with intake, sale and buyback screens, and a printable receipt.
- A month-end Excel/XML export in Coretax's deemed-rate VAT invoice import format (to be confirmed).
- One-page working papers showing the tax saved from invoiced stock.
- No hardware.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
- Rp300k–750k per shop per month (estimate). Justified by the 1.1% vs 1.65% saving and lower consultant fees.
- Consultant plan at Rp150k per client shop per month.
- Many owners will want it done for them: likely a software + consultant-partner model rather than pure self-serve.

**How to find first customers:**
- APEPI branch rosters, plus KPP outreach-session attendee groups through partner consultants.
- Walk one traditional market's gold row with a printed one-page "your VAT at 1.1% instead of 1.65%" calculation.

**Founder access:**
- Needs a local Indonesian-speaking founder or partner.
- The trade is relationship-driven, cash-heavy and often family- or clan-networked.
- A non-local solo founder could build the software but would need a local consultant partner to sell it.

**Risks:**
- **Policy change.** The Finance Minister has said he is open to changing the jewellery VAT scheme. APPI proposes collecting all ~3% at the producer, which would remove retail VAT invoicing (Kontan, Tirto, Oct 2025). It is unclear whether this has been enacted; PMK 52/2025 only changed PPh 22 exemptions.
- **Owners may prefer staying under the radar** to keeping better records.
- **Consumer buybacks never carry invoices**, so the 1.1% share may be small for shops that rely on recycled gold.
- **Coretax import format and summary-invoice rules for retail** need confirmation.

**Kill condition:**
- A new PMK moves gold-jewellery VAT entirely to producers.
- Or interviews show most shops' stock has no acquisition invoice anyway, so everything is taxed at 1.65% and there is nothing to optimise.
- Or consultants say a spreadsheet already does this in minutes.

**Score:** 6.5/10 (Pain 7, Frequency 8, Mandatory 8, Fragmentation 4, Competition 7 (weak), Incumbent gap 7, Buyer access 6, WTP 5 (service-plus-software), MVP 8, Distribution 6)

### Opportunity: Swiftlet-nest "house-to-container" traceability ledger for GACC-registered processors

**Industry:**
Swiftlet-nest (sarang burung walet, SBW) processing and export, mainly to China.

**Buyer:**
The quality/HACCP manager or owner of a Barantin-registered SBW processing plant (GACC-listed or applying). Secondary buyers: large middlemen (pengepul) who supply them.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- **China's demands.** In mid-2026 China's customs authority (GACC) flagged excess aluminium in some Indonesian nests and asked for stronger traceability down to the swiftlet house.
- **Suspensions.** On 21 Aug 2026 Barantin suspended 19 of 57 GACC-registered companies.
- **Registration standard.** Barantin's swiftlet-house registration service standard is Kepdeputi KH 24/2025.
- **The stated problem.** Barantin officials said it was "unclear which swiftlet house the company sourced from".

**Current workflow:**
1. Registered swiftlet houses harvest nests and sell to middlemen or directly to processors (paper notes, WhatsApp).
2. The processor receives mixed raw nests, grades, cleans and dries them, and records intake in ledgers or Excel (assumed; not verified).
3. For each export, the processor assembles a quarantine dossier: registered-house origin, HACCP, the veterinary control number (NKV), the plant's quarantine registration and lab results, for Barantin's inspection and health certificate.
4. When Barantin or GACC asks for origin proof for a lot, staff rebuild it from paper.

**Pain:**
- Suspension from the China market. China takes 80% of exports, worth US$172m (periskop, Aug 2026).
- Barantin explicitly names the house-level origin gap.
- 2025 exports were 894.86 t (Antara). A suspended plant loses its whole export business.

**Existing solutions:**
- Barantin's own registration and certification systems (state-run, plant-facing; no lot ledger found).
- Farm-side IoT and "one-stop" swiftlet vendors, e.g., PT Lentera Alam Nusantara apps.
- Generic traceability platforms, e.g., Telkom's Agree.
- Student-built stock systems.
- HACCP consultants.
- No processor-side house-to-lot mass-balance product was found.

**Offline evidence:**
- The farm side is buildings in small Kalimantan, Sumatra and Java towns, harvested and sold through middlemen.
- Registration happens through quarantine officers' administrative and technical assessments.
- The only software found was academic projects and IoT farm tools.

**Offline channel:**
- Barantin's published lists of registered processors and houses.
- Barantin outreach and "acceleration" events with SBW firms (Oct 2025 Teropong report).
- The swiftlet-nest business association (PPSBI; the name is unverified, from memory).
- Introductions from HACCP/NKV consultants who already serve these plants.

**Market count:**
- 57 GACC-registered processors, of which 19 are suspended.
- 75 SBW companies registered with Barantin; 50 registered in China's CIFER system and 25 in process.
- 4,204 registered swiftlet houses (Barantin, mid-2026).

**The gap:**
Nothing links each intake (registered house ID and middleman, weight, date) to each processed and exported lot, along with that lot's aluminium and contaminant lab results. Without that link, nothing can produce, in minutes, the origin list and mass balance per container that Barantin and GACC now demand.

**Possible product:**
- A lot-genealogy ledger: an intake app that validates the house registration number against the Barantin list, then tracks batches through grading, cleaning and drying, along with lab results.
- One click produces an export traceability dossier per container in Barantin's format.
- A later add-on: an intake app for middlemen.

**MVP:**
- A web app with intake, batch-split/merge and lab-result screens.
- A PDF/Excel dossier per shipment.
- The registered-house list imported from Barantin data.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
Rp3m–8m per plant per month, or about US$2–5k a year (estimate). It is cheap next to losing China access. Setup and onboarding fee Rp10–25m.

**How to find first customers:**
- Start with the 19 suspended firms, whose names may appear in press and Barantin releases (not collected here), and the 25 CIFER applicants.
- Reach them through Barantin regional offices and HACCP consultants.

**Founder access:**
- Needs a local partner.
- Chinese-language ability helps with the GACC side.
- Plants are concentrated in Java (Surabaya, Semarang, Jakarta areas) and Kalimantan (unverified).

**Risks:**
- Barantin or GACC may mandate a state traceability system. Barantin has a history of building its own.
- The market is about 75 buyers, so the revenue ceiling is ~US$300k/yr.
- Some plants' real problem is adulteration (aluminium), not paperwork.

**Kill condition:**
- Barantin announces a mandatory national SBW traceability app.
- Or interviews show plants source from a handful of their own houses, so the paper trail is already easy.

**Score:** 6/10 (Pain 8, Frequency 7 (per lot), Mandatory 8, Fragmentation 3, Competition 7, Gap 7, Buyer access 8, WTP 7, MVP 7, Distribution 5; capped by market size)

### Opportunity: UU PPRT compliance kit for domestic-worker placement agencies (P3RT) (provisional)

**Industry:**
Domestic-worker placement: yayasan/LPK that place household helpers (ART), babysitters, nannies and elder carers, mostly in Jabodetabek and other big cities.

**Buyer:**
The owner or admin of a placement agency (P3RT; formerly LPPRT under Permenaker 2/2015, which required a provincial SIU-LPPRT licence). Household employers are a secondary, low-paying user.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- **The law.** UU 2/2026 on Domestic Worker Protection was passed 21 Apr 2026 and signed and in force 30 Apr 2026. It requires:
  - work agreements by consent or in writing;
  - a minimum recruitment age of 18, with an adjustment period for existing workers;
  - wages, social security (BPJS contributions by the employer where the worker is not state-subsidised), working hours and leave;
  - a report to the RT/RW giving the worker's identity and the agreement;
  - that P3RT be legal entities with a business licence, sanctionable up to licence revocation;
  - an integrated electronic data system covering workers, P3RT and employers (Art. 30).
- **Rules still to come.** Implementing regulations (data collection, P3RT licensing) are still being drafted.

**Current workflow:**
1. The agency recruits workers from villages through sponsors, houses and "trains" them, and keeps paper files (KTP copy, photo, health letter).
2. It matches a worker to a household and signs a placement agreement with a replacement guarantee (garansi). Worker–employer agreements are rarely written.
3. Under the new law, someone must draft the agreement, give the RT/RW the worker's identity and terms, arrange BPJS registration, prove age 18+, and later report into the government data system.

**Pain:**
- New legal exposure: licence revocation for P3RT.
- Every placement now needs a written agreement and an RT report.
- Agencies are small and paper-based.
- Press commentary already says supervision will be hard without a data system (CNBC Indonesia, Apr 2026).
- Direct operator complaints were not found. This is a regulatory-trigger inference.

**Existing solutions:**
- Consumer matching apps (e.g., "Bantu", from a university thesis, with digital contracts).
- Foreign nanny-agency software (OctopusPro, EngineHire), which has no Indonesian compliance.
- Paper placement-contract templates from the association APPSI (unverified).
- Labour lawyers and notaries.
- The future government system (Art. 30).

**Offline evidence:**
- Agencies advertise through local listings and job boards (Glints listing for LPK Sinar Mentari) and recruit through village networks.
- The receiving body is the neighbourhood RT/RW, plus a provincial licensing office counter (e.g., the South Sulawesi one-stop licensing service lists the "IULPPRT" licence).

**Offline channel:**
- APPSI (Asosiasi Pelatihan dan Penempatan PRT Seluruh Indonesia), whose members are placement agencies.
- Provincial one-stop licensing offices (DPMPTSP) and Disnaker licence lists.
- Kemnaker's UU PPRT outreach events.
- Ads on agency listing pages, contacted by phone or WhatsApp.

**Market count:**
- Agency count unknown: no register total found.
- More than 4 million domestic workers nationally (press on UU PPRT). The agency-placed share is unknown.

**The gap:**
No Indonesian tool turns a placement into the new statutory paperwork set:
- a UU 2/2026-compliant agreement in Bahasa;
- an RT/RW notification letter;
- a BPJS enrolment checklist;
- an age and training evidence file;
- a placement register ready for the future integrated data system.

**Possible product:**
A placement desk for P3RT: worker profile → match → generate the agreement, RT/RW letter and BPJS checklist → keep a register with expiry and replacement tracking that can export to the government system once its format is published.

**MVP:**
Bahasa document templates driven by a short form (worker, household, wage, hours, leave), plus a placement register and a WhatsApp-shareable PDF bundle. Price per agency.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
- Rp150k–400k per agency per month, or Rp15k–25k per placement bundle (estimate).
- Agencies charge households placement fees, so a per-placement fee fits their economics.

**How to find first customers:**
- APPSI branches.
- Agencies listed in provincial licensing directories and on classified/job sites in Jakarta, Bekasi, Tangerang and Surabaya.

**Founder access:**
Local only: this sells through trust and language. A non-local founder cannot realistically sell it.

**Risks:**
- Implementing regulations may take years. Enforcement on households is socially sensitive, and the government says mechanisms must be "simple" to avoid resistance.
- The state's integrated data system may include free contract and registration features.
- Many agencies are informal and may not get licensed.
- Willingness to pay is low.

**Kill condition:**
- The implementing government regulation is not issued, or carries no P3RT reporting duties, by mid-2027.
- Or Kemnaker launches a free portal that generates agreements and registers placements.

**Score:** 5/10, provisional (Pain 5, Frequency 7 (per placement), Mandatory 7, Fragmentation 4, Competition 8 (none), Gap 7, Buyer access 4, WTP 3, MVP 9, Distribution 4)

## 3. Rejected

- **Private pawnshops (OJK).**
  - The trigger is real: POJK 39/2024, then POJK 29/2025 (effective 26 Nov 2025), offered regency/city-level pawnshops a simplified licence window from 1 Dec 2025 to 12 Jan 2026. OJK shut illegal pawnshops in 2026.
  - Killed by existing pawnshop software (SmartGadai; Starfield publishes buying guides for pawnshop software) and a pool of only ~214 licensed firms. OJK reporting goes through its own portals.
  - Sources: https://finansial.bisnis.com/read/20251116/89/1929171/pembiayaan-gadai-tembus-rp93-triliun-per-september-2025-didominasi-emas ; https://keuangan.kontan.co.id/news/ojk-sederhanakan-aturan-pergadaian-simak-rincian-ketentuan-terbarunya ; https://starfield.id/panduan-lengkap-memilih-software-pegadaian-terbaik-untuk-bisnis-anda/ ; https://finansial.bisnis.com/read/20260614/89/1980795/hati-hati-ada-184-perusahaan-gadai-belum-terdaftar-ojk
- **LPG 3 kg base sellers.** NIK recording is mandatory, but only in Pertamina's own free MAP app. There is nothing to integrate.
  - Source: https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/pertamina-catat-57-juta-nik-pembeli-lpg-3-kg-siap-jalankan-subsidi-tepat-sasaran
- **Inter-island cattle traders (NTT/NTB).** Many documents are needed per shipment (health certificate, recommendations, veterinary certificate, quarantine). But the binding constraint is the governor's quota and its allocation. Ombudsman NTT calls it a "tangled thread", and Pergub 37/2025 is the legal basis. Software can't fix discretion.
  - Sources: https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkinternal--benang-kusut-pengeluaran-ternak-kabupaten-kupang ; https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/pwkmedia--ombudsman-ntt-tegaskan-pergub-372025-jadi-payung-hukum-pengeluaran-ternak
- **Used-car and vehicle dealers (PPATK reports).** Transactions of Rp500m or more are rare for small dealers and goAML is free. This is a low-frequency annual-feeling chore.
- **Umrah operators (Siskopatuh).** Not a quiet industry, and the portal is the system. Licensing is being rewritten by Kemenhaj (half of 3,631 operators are inactive).
  - Source: https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/berita-utama/5483391/50-persen-travel-umrah-tak-aktif--kemenhaj-rombak-aturan-dan-perizinan
- **Small-scale fishers (eLogbook/e-PIT).** Free KKP apps with NGO activation drives. No willingness to pay.
  - Source: https://fisheryprogress.org/sites/default/files/documents_actions/12%20October%20-%20Socialisation%20and%20evaluation%20logbook%202023.pdf
- **Furniture small exporters (SVLK).** Certification bodies, consultants and state-funded group certification own the workflow, and the data is old (2020).
- **Groundwater drillers.** Permen ESDM 14/2024 cut requirements from 13 to 3, and the work is mostly one-off per well.

## 4. Method notes

- **Queries that worked best:** Bahasa regulator-first queries naming the instrument (PMK 48 2023, UU 2 Tahun 2026, Kepdeputi KH 24 2025, POJK 29 2025) plus words like "wajib", "sanksi", "jumlah ... terdaftar".
  - Ombudsman regional news (ombudsman.go.id "pwk") was excellent for hidden counter-level pain (livestock).
  - DDTC and pajak.go.id regional news exposed tax-office enforcement sweeps of traditional traders. This is a reusable pattern for other "now must register for VAT" trades.
- **Queries that failed:** counts of informal intermediaries (domestic-worker agencies, money changers, livestock traders) are not published, and the search engine does not reach provincial licensing lists. Competitor searches for niche POS ("software toko emas") return student theses rather than vendors. That absence is weak evidence of a gap, not proof.
- **Fetching:** WebFetch was not used (blocked by instructions). All facts come from search-result summaries.
