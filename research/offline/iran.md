## 2. Opportunities

None. A foreign solo founder cannot legally sell software to Iranian businesses or collect payment from them.

- OFAC's General License D family authorises only communications-related services and software for individuals. A search result summarising it says it does not authorise web hosting for commercial endeavors. B2B compliance software would need a specific OFAC license. See the [Davis Wright Tremaine copy of GL D-1](https://www.dwt.com/files/Uploads/Documents/OFAC%20Iran%20General%20License%20D-1%202-7-14.pdf) and the [Arent Fox alert](https://arentfox.com/perspectives/alerts/calling-all-techies-ofac-issues-new-general-license-allowing-exports-software).
- The search found no 2026 update that loosens this.

The gold-dealer reporting regime is the one genuine offline-industry finding. Obligated dealers must register trades in the Comprehensive Trade System. Registering there removes the need to register again in the Moadian tax system. Sources: [Tabnak](https://www.tabnak.ir/fa/news/1174092/ارسال-پیامک-هشدار-قوه-قضاییه-به-طلافروشان), [Eghtesadnews](https://www.eghtesadnews.com/fa/tiny/news-619111) and [Etemad](https://www.etemadonline.com/بخش-%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF%DB%8C-22/676866-%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%B3%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%87-%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B9-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AA). The portal appears to be domestic-only and state-run. A real product would have to be built and sold by an Iran-based team, which is outside this study's scope.

## 3. Rejected

- **Gold-dealer trade-registration tool.** Real mandate, with a deadline, extensions and inspections reported in the press. Rejected because the buyer cannot be reached or paid from outside Iran, and a state-run portal plus domestic vendors fill the gap (unverified).
- **Any other quiet-industry product.** Rejected for the same reason as the country report.
