## 2. Strongest opportunities

### Opportunity: Multi-regime scrap-purchase ledger (金属盗対策法 + 古物 + prefectural ordinances)

**Industry:**
Scrap-metal and copper-scrap buyers (鉄・非鉄スクラップヤード, 金属くず商). Also 古物商 that buy cable, outdoor AC units and gratings.

**Buyer:**
Owner or office manager (often the owner's spouse) at a family-run yard that buys over the counter from tradespeople, demolition crews and the public.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- 盗難特定金属製物品処分防止法 took full effect on 2026-06-01. Existing buyers had to notify the 公安委員会 by 2026-08-31.
- The seller's ID must be checked: photo ID per the Nikkei report.
- Every purchase needs a record kept for 3 years.
- Prefectural permit ordinances sit on top: 千葉 (2025-01-01), 茨城 (2025-04-01, 5-year permits).
- 古物営業法施行規則 removed the ¥10,000 exemption for cable, outdoor AC units, heat pumps and gratings from 2025-10-01.

**Current workflow:**
1. The seller arrives with a truck. The load is weighed on the yard scale.
2. Staff copy the ID details into a paper 判取帳, or photocopy the ID.
3. They hand-write the item description, quantity, price and payment method. Cash is common.
4. They decide which regime applies (national 特定金属くず, prefectural 特定金属類, or 古物 item) and whether extra fields are needed.
5. Paper is filed for 3 years and pulled out when police ask.

**Pain:**
- Per-transaction, legally mandatory, with police inspection. Yards handle dozens of walk-in sellers a day (estimate).
- ITmedia and NPA committee coverage ties the law to solar-cable theft. The industry press (日刊産業新聞, steelstory) discusses compliance burden and the "囲い込み" effect of the ordinances.
- No published fines found yet: the law is 4 months old.

**Existing solutions:**
- **ScrapID** (ディールコネクト): cloud 判取帳 explicitly for 金属盗対策法 and 改正古物, on a smartphone (日刊産業新聞 via Yahoo).
- **カイトリ番** (Social Bridge): ID check, purchase record and 3-year storage in about 30 seconds.
- Yard weighing/POS systems from scale vendors: unverified whether they added ID capture.
- Paper 判取帳 and police sample forms.
- 行政書士 handle the permit/notification paperwork only.
- Abroad the category is mature (ScrapRight, Scrap Dragon in the US), so the Japanese category will probably consolidate.

**Offline evidence:**
- Police publish paper forms (様式第1–3号) for notification.
- Guidance assumes a written record.
- Yards are not SaaS-listed. Both competitors are new and press-release driven.

**Offline channel:**
- Prefectural police briefing sessions (説明会) for 買受業.
- JISRI regional branches (686 regular members).
- Scrap trade press (日刊産業新聞, 日刊市况通信).
- Scale and weighbridge dealers who visit yards.
- 行政書士 who filed the 茨城/千葉 permits.

**Market count:**
- JISRI: 686 regular members, iron only.
- No national count of 特定金属くず買受業 notifications was found. Total yards including non-ferrous: several thousand (**estimate**).

**The gap:**
Both entrants focus on the national law. The possible gap:
- one record that also satisfies the stricter prefectural ordinance fields (千葉/茨城 and others coming) and the 古物 item rules;
- scale-ticket integration, so staff don't key weights twice;
- a police-request export by period, seller or item.

Unverified whether ScrapID or カイトリ番 already cover these.

**Possible product:**
A tablet counter app:
1. Scan the ID (OCR) and photograph the seller and load.
2. Pull the weight from the scale ticket.
3. Auto-classify the regime per item.
4. Generate a compliant record, with a one-click export for police inspection.

**MVP:**
- ID photo plus OCR, a per-prefecture rules table (national + 千葉 + 茨城 + 古物 items), and a CSV/PDF ledger export.
- No scale integration in v1. Weights are typed in.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
¥5,000–15,000 per month per yard. Software is sold, but yards will expect setup help (a tablet pre-configured on site).

**How to find first customers:**
- Notified/permitted dealer lists are not public. Use JISRI branch directories, the 茨城/千葉 permit lists if disclosed (unverified), and Google Maps "金属スクラップ 買取" in 千葉/茨城.
- Phone outreach, then a yard visit.

**Risks:**
- Two funded entrants already in market.
- Scale vendors may bundle ID capture.
- Yards may settle for a ¥0 paper book.
- A non-Japanese founder needs a local partner: yards expect visits, Japanese phone support and a qualified invoice.

**Kill condition:**
Five yard interviews show ScrapID or カイトリ番 already handle prefectural ordinances and scale tickets at a price yards accept. Or yards report police accept the paper 判取帳 without friction.

**Score:** 5/10 (Pain 6, Frequency 9, Mandatory 9, Fragmentation 6, Competition 4, Gap 4, Buyer access 5, WTP 5, MVP 7, Distribution 5)
- Distribution: police 説明会, JISRI branches and scale dealers.
- WTP: software, with a done-for-you setup.
- Founder access: needs a local partner.

### Opportunity: Sign-permit renewal and safety-inspection router for sign contractors (屋外広告物)

**Industry:**
Outdoor-sign makers and installers (屋外広告業), acting for sign owners such as shops, clinics and small buildings.

**Buyer:**
Owner or office staff at a small sign company (看板屋) that manages renewals for dozens to hundreds of client signs.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- Since the 2015 Sapporo sign-fall accident and the 2016 MLIT guideline revision, permit renewal needs a qualified inspection: 屋外広告士, 点検技能講習 or 建築士.
- Tokyo's revised rule took effect on 2026-04-01. The 「屋外広告物安全点検報告書」 went from 6 to 18 items, with 3-level grading (良好・経過観察・要改善).
- Tokyo wards (港区, 江東区, 豊島区) process permits with their own sample forms.
- Other prefectures may follow MLIT/Tokyo (unverified).

**Current workflow:**
1. Track every client sign's permit expiry: 2 years in 静岡市, 3 years in some areas. Usually Excel or a paper ledger (unverified).
2. Visit, inspect and photograph the sign.
3. Fill in that authority's inspection report and 許可更新申請書 (宮城 様式第2号 / 第6号の2, Tokyo's new form, and so on).
4. Attach photos, a map and the fee stamp. Submit at the counter or by post, or on the e-application where one exists (神奈川).
5. Collect the renewed permit and bill the owner.

**Pain:**
- Missed renewals make a sign illegal and the owner liable.
- The form doubled in length in Tokyo.
- Each authority (prefecture, 政令市, 中核市, delegated municipality) has its own forms and fees.
- Not yet evidenced by complaints: insiders don't post online. Interviews are needed.

**Existing solutions:**
- Generic field-inspection apps: Smart Attack, シンプル点検帳, ミライ工事管理アプリ. They produce photo reports but have no authority-specific forms or expiry logic.
- Free official form downloads.
- 行政書士 offer 屋外広告業登録 (shigyo.co.jp lists per-authority registration).
- 日広連 provides inspection items and training.
- No sign-industry permit-management SaaS was found in 2 searches. Not exhaustive.

**Offline evidence:**
- Each authority posts Word/PDF forms (宮城, 静岡, 岡山 per city, Tokyo wards).
- E-application is partial.
- No SaaS listings for the workflow were found.

**Offline channel:**
- 日広連 and its 46 prefectural associations (2,736 member firms).
- The 屋外広告士 exam and 点検技能講習 training sessions.
- Tokyo's inspection seminars (都市整備局 ran a 2025-07 form-revision seminar).
- Sign-material suppliers.

**Market count:**
- 2,736 association member firms (日広連).
- Total registered 屋外広告業 not found (registration is per authority).
- Signs under permit: hundreds of thousands (**estimate**).

**The gap:**
One inspection record should produce the correct renewal application plus inspection report for whichever of the ~100+ permit authorities holds that sign, with an expiry calendar across the client portfolio. Generic inspection apps stop at the photo report.

**Possible product:**
"Sign register + renewal autopilot":
- an import of the client-sign list;
- an expiry calendar;
- a mobile inspection checklist that matches Tokyo's 18 items and the MLIT guideline;
- output of the filled authority-specific PDF forms.

**MVP:**
- Tokyo 23 wards plus 2–3 large prefectures' forms only.
- Excel import, a mobile checklist with photos, a PDF generator and email reminders 90 days before expiry.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
¥5,000–20,000 per month per sign company, tiered by signs managed. Or ¥1,000–2,000 per renewal filed. Sign companies pass the cost through to owners as an inspection fee. Software is plausible. Smaller firms may prefer a per-filing service.

**How to find first customers:**
- 日広連 member association directories (東京都屋外広告美術協同組合 and others; unverified which publish lists).
- Attendee lists from 点検技能講習 sessions.
- Ward/prefecture registered-contractor lists, where published (unverified).

**Risks:**
- Renewal frequency per sign is low (every 2–3 years), so value depends on portfolio size.
- Authorities may move to e-application with their own forms.
- Small market (about 3,000 firms).
- A Japanese-speaking founder is essential for form mapping and the association channel.

**Kill condition:**
Interviews show typical sign firms manage fewer than 50 permitted signs, or that most renewals are already handled by the owner or a 行政書士.

**Score:** 5/10 (Pain 5, Frequency 5, Mandatory 8, Fragmentation 8, Competition 7, Gap 7, Buyer access 6, WTP 4, MVP 7, Distribution 6)
- Distribution: 日広連 associations and inspection training courses.
- WTP: software, priced per filing.
- Founder access: needs Japanese; a local partner is preferred.

### Opportunity: 改葬 (grave-relocation) paperwork kit for stonemasons

**Industry:**
Monument makers and stonemasons (石材店) doing 墓じまい and grave relocation.

**Buyer:**
Owner of a family-run 石材店 (older, local), or a temple-affiliated cemetery agent.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- 改葬 cases roughly doubled, from about 84,000 (FY2014) to about 176,000 (FY2024, a record), per the MHLW 衛生行政報告例 as reported by 現代ビジネス.
- Some municipalities (長野市) now accept online applications, but originals must still be posted.
- A 2024 地方分権 proposal to MHLW asks to simplify the procedure (content unverified).

**Current workflow:**
1. The family hires a 石材店 to remove the grave.
2. Someone collects the 埋蔵証明 from the current cemetery manager (often a temple) and the 受入証明 from the new site.
3. They obtain the current municipality's own 改葬許可申請書. Remains are listed one by one, with the names and death dates of each.
4. They submit at the counter or by post, receive the 改葬許可証, and present it at the new site.

**Pain:**
- Multi-party document chasing for each job. Old remains often have unknown death dates.
- Forms differ by municipality.
- Families are confused, so the 石材店 or a 行政書士 (paid per job; fee level unverified) ends up doing it.
- Evidence of pain from stonemasons themselves was not found.

**Existing solutions:**
- Municipal forms and guides.
- 行政書士 代行.
- Consumer 墓じまい brokers that bundle paperwork (not individually verified in this pass).
- Municipal cemetery systems (久留米市 procurement) serve the municipality, not the stonemason.

**Offline evidence:**
Counter or postal submission, with originals required even where online exists (柏市, 長野市).

**Offline channel:**
- 全優石 (about 200 member companies, 2026).
- 日本石材産業協会.
- Stone wholesalers and the Japan Stone Show (unverified).
- Temples, which refer families.

**Market count:**
- About 176,000 改葬 cases per year (MHLW FY2024).
- Number of 石材店 not found (**estimate**: several thousand).
- Industry revenue roughly halved, from 4,500億円 to 2,500億円 between the 2000s and 2015 (snippet).

**The gap:**
No stonemason-side tool was found that:
- holds a municipal form library;
- pre-fills one application listing several sets of remains;
- tracks the three certificates per job.

**Possible product:**
Job-based paperwork tracker:
- Enter the family, the remains list and the old and new sites.
- Get the right municipal form pre-filled, the certificate request letters, and a status board.

**MVP:**
Form library for the top 50 municipalities by 改葬 volume, a PDF pre-fill and a checklist.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
¥3,000–5,000 per month, or ¥2,000 per job. WTP is weak: stonemasons may prefer to keep using a 行政書士 or have the family do it. A done-for-you service is the more likely format.

**How to find first customers:**
全優石 member list, prefectural 石材組合, and phone outreach from local listings (itp.ne.jp).

**Risks:**
- Low frequency per shop.
- Shrinking industry.
- Consumer brokers capture the paperwork.
- Municipal online procedures may reduce the need.
- Needs a local founder.

**Kill condition:**
A typical 石材店 does fewer than 3 改葬 jobs a month, or already outsources the paperwork to a 行政書士 at a fee the family pays.

**Score:** 4/10 (Pain 4, Frequency 4, Mandatory 8, Fragmentation 8, Competition 6, Gap 6, Buyer access 5, WTP 3, MVP 8, Distribution 4)
- Distribution: 全優石 and stone wholesalers.
- WTP: mostly a service.
- Founder access: needs a local.

## 3. Rejected

- **Pet breeder ledgers and 定期報告:** あんしんペット台帳 (全国ペット協会) and PSDB 第一種動物取扱・管理 already produce the ledgers, breeding-limit checks and 定期報告.
- **Light-freight 業務記録 (2025-04):** not a quiet industry. CarryNote (¥800/month) and other 日報 and 点呼 apps already exist.
- **Livestock 定期報告:** annual, with a free form and hygiene-centre support.
- **Individual-taxi records:** 26,788 ageing operators, but their associations already do the admin and WTP is tiny.
- **宗教法人 annual filings:** annual, guided by sect headquarters, and a sensitive buyer.
- **Domestic workers:** only a 2024 guideline. No binding trigger.
- **Pesticide-seller books:** low pain. POS and JA hold the data.
- **Bounty claims for problem wildlife:** the municipality is the payer, which means procurement. Apps are being introduced.
- **TAC catch reports:** filed by the co-op on a government system.
- **General 古物台帳:** mostly dormant or online resellers. Only the metal sub-segment is live (kept above).
- **Pickle-maker licences:** a one-time licence.

## 4. Method notes

What worked:
- Queries naming the regulator's own form or document returned official PDFs and counts quickly. Examples: "届出 様式", "定期報告届出書", "許可更新申請 点検結果報告書", "施行 令和8年".
- So did police and prefecture pages for 公安委員会-regulated trades.

What didn't work:
- Counts of obliged businesses are rarely in snippets. NPA and MoE statistics PDFs exist, but snippets give only old or partial numbers.
- Competitor discovery needs product-phrase queries ("対応 アプリ", "台帳 ソフト"). These found the new scrap and pet tools at once.
- English-language queries returned US scrap software rather than Japanese vendors.

Gaps left by the usage-limit cutoff:
- the scrap competitor scan (scale vendors, pricing);
- a national count of sign permits;
- the count of 石材店.

Research model: Opus
