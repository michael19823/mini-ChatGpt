**Bottom line:** No quiet-industry opportunity reaches the bar. Mauritania has a population of about 5 million, a small formal sector and weak SaaS payment rails (see the country report). The quiet industries that exist there are mostly informal, or the state runs the whole chain. The one lead worth a later check is livestock export health certification, which is listed in the table below. Even that lead is unverified and probably state-run.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

None. I won't pad this section. The only Mauritania opportunity on record is the country report's EU CATCH and sanitary-certificate pack for seafood exporters, scored 4/10, and I am not re-reporting it here.

The livestock export certificate is the only quiet-industry lead worth a follow-up. If budget becomes available, these searches would settle it:

- "certificat zoosanitaire exportation bétail Mauritanie" (French)
- "شهادة صحية تصدير المواشي موريتانيا" (Arabic)
- "Office National de Recherche et de Développement de l'Élevage" (ONARDEL)
- PRAPS livestock-corridor documentation (World Bank)

If the certificate is issued by hand at a state counter and the traders are informal, kill the lead.

## 3. Rejected

- **Domestic-worker payroll:** informal, with no portal confirmed. The substitute is cash and nothing filed.
- **Gold buying:** the state chain (Maaden and the Central Bank) is the substitute.
- **Hajj agencies:** the market is too small and seasonal.
- **Transport and street trade:** no recurring regulated filing, and no money in it.
- **Fishmeal plants:** about 8 plants remain (per the country report, citing Mongabay, Jan 2026).
