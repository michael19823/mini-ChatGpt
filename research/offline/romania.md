# Romania: Offline-Industries Pass

Research date: 2026-10-05. I used 38 of 40 WebSearch calls, all in Romanian, and no WebFetch, so every finding comes from search-result summaries. Anything I could not confirm against a primary source is marked "unverified" or "estimate". This pass does not repeat the opportunities in `research/countries/romania.md`, which are packaging EPR, the RSVTI/ISCIR workbench and the REGES-ONLINE exception desk.

**Overall verdict:** Romania's quiet industries are regulated heavily, but the state usually supplies a free mandatory tool. Examples are the Inspecția Muncii app for day labourers, the ANOFM platform for domestic workers, SNIIA for livestock, and SIATD for waste traceability. That leaves little room for a product in many sectors. Three narrow openings remain, and all are weak to moderate. None reaches the brief's "build" bar without interviews.

1. Scrap-metal collection yards got a new per-transaction withholding obligation on 1 Aug 2025.
2. Small țuică distilleries that distil for households under a fiscal-warehouse authorisation keep paper client registers and file monthly.
3. Beekeepers' associations prepare APIA files for two annual aid streams, and one of them is new for 2026–2028.

A non-local founder would struggle in every case. The work is Romanian-language, the channels are face-to-face, and the buyers are older and rural.

## 2. Opportunities

### Opportunity: Scrap-yard withholding and borderou ledger (10% + 3% per seller, D100/D205-ready)

**Industry:**
Scrap-metal and recyclables collection yards that buy from individuals. These are mostly CAEN 4677/3832 SRLs authorised by the county environmental protection agency (APM).

**Buyer:**
The owner-operator of a single yard or a small chain (1–5 sites), or the external accountant who keeps their books.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- Law 141/2025, from 1 Aug 2025, makes income an individual receives from handing over ferrous or non-ferrous metals taxable at 10%, withheld at source by the yard.
- The yard declares it on D100 by the 25th of the following month and reports it per beneficiary (CNP) on the annual D205, due at the end of February.
- The 3% AFM environmental contribution is still withheld on the same transaction.
- ANAF antifraud is using e-Factura data to target yards. One Cluj yard allegedly failed to declare 11.8M lei of revenue.

**Current workflow:**
1. The seller arrives and the load is weighed.
2. The clerk fills in a borderou de achiziție with the seller's ID data, the sworn statement that the waste comes from the seller's household, material, kg and price.
3. The clerk deducts 3% for AFM and 10% for income tax and pays the net amount in cash.
4. The borderou pile goes to the accountant, who re-keys it into the accounting software.
5. The accountant calculates the monthly totals for the AFM declaration and D100, and builds D205 per CNP at year end.

**Pain:**
- High fiscal-risk sector, with antifraud raids and Garda de Mediu fines (one Galați yard got about 100,000 EUR).
- The borderou must carry particular fields and a sworn statement.
- Per-CNP aggregation across a year of cash transactions is error-prone.
- Mismatches between the D100 totals and the D205 per-person totals are an audit trigger.

**Existing solutions:**
- ExpertAccounts "Reciclare" / "Program gestiune deșeuri", which generates the borderou, delivery notices, Anexa 3 transport forms and the environmental-fund handling.
- registrul-deseurilor.ro (waste register for collectors).
- WinMentor's "Taxa de mediu pentru deșeuri" module.
- Weighbridge (cântar) software from scale suppliers.
- Accountants re-keying paper borderouri.
- SIATD, the free AFM traceability app, covers packaging, WEEE and similar streams but not the withholding itself.

**Offline evidence:**
- The core document is a signed paper borderou at the scale.
- Payment is mostly cash.
- Owners are not active online. The only discussion is accountants' Q&A on fiscalitatea.ro, contabilul.manager.ro and infotva.
- There are no SaaS review pages for the niche.

**Offline channel:**
- APM county lists of authorised collectors (public authorisations).
- Weighbridge and scale suppliers and service technicians who visit yards.
- Accountants who already keep several yards' books, found through CECCAR county branches.
- Phone outreach to yards listed on colectaredeseuri.ro.

**Market count:**
- About 1,904 active CAEN 4677 companies and about 485 CAEN 4687 companies (termene.ro).
- An estimated 1,000–2,000 actually run yards that buy from individuals (estimate).

**The gap:**
- I could not verify whether ExpertAccounts or registrul-deseurilor.ro updated their products for the 10% withholding and the D205 per-CNP export after Aug 2025.
- If they did not, the gap is a tablet-at-the-scale borderou that captures the ID by photo or OCR, applies both withholdings, and exports to the accountant's software and D205.
- If they did, there is no gap.

**Possible product:**
A tablet app at the weighbridge. It reads the ID card, prints or e-signs the borderou with the sworn statement, and calculates net pay after 10% and 3%. It produces the monthly AFM/D100 totals and a year-end D205 per-CNP file for the accountant.

**MVP:**
- Borderou PDF with ID capture.
- Withholding calculator.
- Monthly CSV for the accountant.
- D205 XML export.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
- 30–60 EUR/month per yard.
- An accountant tier at 100–200 EUR/month for 10 yards.
- Willingness to pay is for software only if the accountant recommends it. Many owners would prefer that the accountant just handles it.

**How to find first customers:**
- Accountants who serve yards.
- Weighbridge suppliers as resellers.
- APM authorisation lists.

**Risks:**
- Incumbents (ExpertAccounts, WinMentor) can patch quickly.
- Yards with informal cash practices resist traceability.
- ANAF may introduce its own e-borderou.
- It needs a Romanian-speaking local founder. A non-local solo founder realistically cannot sell this.

**Kill condition:**
- ExpertAccounts or the accounting suites already export D205 rows for scrap withholding, or
- accountants say the yearly per-CNP aggregation takes less than a day per yard.

**Score:** 5/10

### Opportunity: Client register and monthly return for small țuică distilleries

**Industry:**
Small distilleries authorised as fiscal warehouses, including stills that distil fruit brought in by households as a paid service ("prestări servicii").

**Buyer:**
The owner of the still, often a rural family business or a PFA/SRL, or their accountant.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- The 2025 Senate vote, made at the President's request, kept the 50 l exemption and rejected 200 l.
- The government has said it will move to household self-declaration of production and excise, which would add declaration steps on top of the existing distillery records.
- Authorisation of small distilleries has moved to territorial customs (ANAF orders up to OMF 389/2026).
- Whether a new 2026 obligation is in force is unverified.

**Current workflow:**
1. A household brings fruit mash in the autumn season.
2. The still owner writes into a paper register: household name, raw material quantity, product obtained, quantity taken, date and signature.
3. The owner works out which part falls within the 50 l exemption and which part owes excise (50% of the standard rate).
4. The owner sends the monthly report by the 25th to customs or the fiscal authority.

**Pain:**
- Fines run up to 100,000 lei (orange.ro / press).
- Hundreds of household clients pass through in a few weeks of the season.
- The register has to reconcile with the monthly report.
- The rules change frequently.

**Existing solutions:**
- A paper register (stationers).
- The accountant.
- Customs' own forms.
- No Romanian distillery-register software was found. This absence is unverified.
- Larger excise software for alcohol warehouses (EMCS-connected) exists for bigger producers (unverified for small stills).

**Offline evidence:**
- Rural, seasonal, cash business.
- Forum traces exist only on portalcontabilitate.ro.
- Authorisation is at the territorial customs office.
- No vendor listings for this niche.

**Offline channel:**
- Still manufacturers and sellers (e.g. metalgroup.ro, which writes about distilling legislation) as a reseller channel.
- County customs authorisation lists. Whether these are public is unverified.
- Rural accountants.
- Agricultural fairs (e.g. Indagra).

**Market count:**
- Unknown nationally.
- Press reports county counts in the tens (timponline.ro, county figure, unverified).
- National estimate: low thousands (estimate).

**The gap:**
- A phone or tablet register at the still: one entry per household, with automatic split of exempt and excisable litres, a client receipt, and the month-end return pre-filled.

**Possible product:**
A mobile register for the season. Each household's batch becomes an e-signed register line and receipt. The month-end report is produced as PDF/XML for customs.

**MVP:**
- Register entry form.
- Exemption calculator.
- Printable receipt.
- Monthly summary PDF.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
- 50–100 EUR per season, or about 10 EUR/month.
- Willingness to pay is low. It would sell better bundled with an accountant service.

**How to find first customers:**
- Still sellers.
- Accountants in the fruit-growing counties (Vâlcea, Argeș, Bistrița, Maramureș, Satu Mare).

**Risks:**
- Strongly seasonal revenue.
- Customs may require a specific format.
- Political volatility.
- The market may be too small.
- Needs a local seller.

**Kill condition:**
- Fewer than about 1,000 authorised small stills nationally, or
- customs accepts a simple paper register with no reconciliation burden.

**Score:** 4/10

### Opportunity: APIA file kit for beekeepers' associations (ISA intervention plus the 15 EUR/colony aid)

**Industry:**
Beekeeping.

**Buyer:**
County branches of ACA (Asociația Crescătorilor de Albine) and beekeeping cooperatives. They help members prepare APIA files and can themselves file as associative forms. Individual beekeepers are a secondary buyer.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- A state-aid law gives 15 EUR per colony per year for 2026–2028. Eligibility requires colonies registered in the ANZ database at 31 Dec 2025 and a DSVSA-registered apiary. One claim per beneficiary is filed at the APIA county office by 31 Jul.
- The ISA sector intervention under PS 2023–2027 runs in parallel. It has a budget of 12.16M EUR for 2026 and pays a percentage of invoiced spending (varroa medicines, equipment, transhumance, queens, honey analysis), also due 31 Jul.
- 2025 was a crisis year, with production at about one third of normal, so beekeepers depend on the aid.

**Current workflow:**
1. The beekeeper collects the ANZ certificate, DSVSA registration and invoices dated from 1 Jan.
2. The beekeeper fills in the APIA forms, often helped by the association.
3. The beekeeper submits in person, or through a notarised representative, at the APIA county centre.
4. In parallel, the beekeeper keeps the paper apiary logbook and treatment register for DSVSA.

**Pain:**
- Two claims with different rules.
- Ineligible invoices and missing documents cut the payment.
- Older, rural applicants.
- The deadline is fixed.

**Existing solutions:**
- APIA's information guide and forms.
- Association volunteers and staff.
- Agricultural consultants who charge per file.
- Apiary-logbook apps such as international hive trackers. Their Romanian APIA support is unverified, and I did not find any.

**Offline evidence:**
- Filing is in person at the APIA counter.
- Paper logbooks.
- Information spreads through association meetings and regional press (bzi.ro, alba24).

**Offline channel:**
- ACA county branches and its magazine "România Apicolă" (I did not confirm the title as an ad channel in search).
- Bee-equipment and medicine suppliers.
- Beekeeping fairs.

**Market count:**
- 35,000–37,000 registered beekeepers and 2.35M colonies. About half are ACA members (ACA president via economica.net).

**The gap:**
- A per-member file checker for associations: invoice eligibility per ISA measure, the 15 EUR claim, a document checklist and pre-filled forms, plus an apiary-logbook export for DSVSA.

**Possible product:**
A web tool for association staff. Enter a member's apiary and invoices once and it produces both APIA claim packs and flags ineligible items.

**MVP:**
- Invoice-to-measure eligibility rules.
- PDF pack generator for one county branch.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
- 300–800 EUR per year per county branch, or 5–10 EUR per member file.
- Buyers would pay for a done-for-you service rather than for software.

**How to find first customers:**
- ACA county branches.
- Cooperatives listed in APIA beneficiary lists.

**Risks:**
- Annual-only workflow.
- APIA may digitise the claim.
- The aid scheme still needs EC authorisation (stated in the law).
- Low budgets.
- Needs a local founder.

**Kill condition:**
- APIA moves the claims to its online portal with pre-fill from ANZ, or
- associations say volunteers handle it at no cost.

**Score:** 3.5/10

## 4. Method notes

What worked:
- Romanian queries naming the specific form or term: "borderou de achiziție", "registru electronic zilieri", "antrepozit fiscal mic producător țuică", "intervenția sectorială apicultură".
- Accountants' Q&A sites (fiscalitatea.ro, contabilul.manager.ro, infotva, portalcontabilitate) were the best way to see the real workflow.
- Regional press (alba24, bzi.ro) was the best source for enforcement and APIA deadlines.

What didn't work:
- National counts of obliged businesses were hard to find. termene.ro CAEN pages helped. BNR, ANAF and APM registers did not come up in search.
- Queries on hunting and the electronic producer atestat drifted to Moldova (gov.md, particip.gov.md). Adding "România" or a Romanian institution name is needed.
- Software-vendor queries came back mostly empty, which fits the "quiet" thesis but leaves competitor diligence unverified.

Research model: Opus
