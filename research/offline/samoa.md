# Samoa: Offline-Industries Pass

**Market type:** Microstate. The population is about 220,000 (approximate, not re-verified), and formal business is concentrated in Apia.
**Search budget:** 8 allowed. I made 4 calls: 3 returned results and the 4th was refused with "You've hit your usage limit". As the instructions require, I stopped searching at the refusal, so this is a light screen.
**Bottom line:** **No quiet-industry opportunity qualifies in Samoa.** Every seed group fails for one of three reasons:
- the obliged population is too small (dozens of operators, not thousands);
- the obligation is handled at a government counter, so there is no private software market;
- or the obligation could not be verified with the searches available.

This matches the existing country report, whose only watch item is TIMS-to-ledger reconciliation as a Fiji add-on, scored 3/10. That item is not repeated here.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

None. No industry met the bar of a mandatory recurring workflow, enough buyers and an offline channel. A solo founder from outside Samoa could not sell to these segments without a local partner, and even with one, the counts are in the tens to low hundreds.

## 3. Rejected

- **Household-employer SNPF payroll:** the obligation exists (household workers are covered at 7% employer contribution), but few households employ formally. The substitute is paying at the SNPF counter or a family-run arrangement.
- **Taxi and bus operator compliance:** LTA permits are renewed at the counter. There are only hundreds of operators, and no recurring filing was found.
- **Scrap metal:** there are trade flows but no dealer register or reporting regime was found.
- **All other seed groups:** the operator populations are too small, and the obligations are either unverified or government to government.

## 4. Method notes

- Regulator-first English queries return mostly *other* countries' regimes (Trinidad, Botswana, Fiji, South Africa). Samoan regulators (LTA, MCIL, MAF, MNRE) publish little that search engines index.
- The SSA "Social Security Programs Throughout the World" profile was the most useful official source.
- Samoan-language queries were not attempted because the budget was cut off.
- For microstates, this pass adds little beyond the original report. A better approach is to treat Samoa as an add-on to a Fiji (or NZ/Australia seasonal-labour) product.
