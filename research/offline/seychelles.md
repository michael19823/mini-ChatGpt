# Seychelles: Offline-Industries Pass

**Research date:** 2026-10-05
**Searches used:** 4 of 8. Three returned results; one (artisanal fisheries) was refused with "usage limit hit", so research stopped there as the instructions require. One of the three that returned (the Seychelles Licensing Authority licence categories) gave only New Zealand and UK pages, so it was useless.
**Market type:** Microstate (population roughly 100k, estimate not verified). English is an official language. Laws are published on SeyLII (seylii.org).

## Bottom line

**No quiet-industry opportunity in Seychelles reaches the bar for an indie software business.** All the quiet industries that exist here are tiny in absolute numbers. Most have a single receiving body (the Seychelles Licensing Authority, SRC, the Seychelles Pension Fund or the Seychelles Fishing Authority), so there is no fragmentation to build on. The obligations I found are flat-rate and low-value, for example a household-worker social security contribution reported as SCR 50/month. Like the existing country report, this pass concludes that Seychelles is at most a country module inside a wider Indian Ocean product, for example one built for Mauritius.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

None. No candidate scored above 3/10, and I am not presenting one for the sake of having one.

## 3. Rejected

- **Domestic-worker payroll/contributions service.** The obligation is real, but contributions are flat-rate and small, and there is one receiving body (the social security fund and the Seychelles Pension Fund). No severance fund or visa-renewal complexity was verified. The substitute is paying at the counter or having the employer's accountant handle it. Sources: [SSA 2011](https://ssa.gov/policy/docs/progdesc/ssptw/2010-2011/africa/seychelles.html), [SSA 2009](https://www.ssa.gov/policy/docs/progdesc/ssptw/2008-2009/africa/seychelles.html).
- **Taxi/minibus operator compliance.** The only recurring touchpoint found is licensing, and the operator base is small. There is an association channel (STOA), but nothing for software to do. Sources: [nation.sc 2021](https://nation.sc/articles/10342/adverts), [nation.sc 2021](https://nation.sc/articles/8557/adverts), [SeyLII SI 16/2015](https://seylii.org/akn/sc/act/si/2015/16).
- **All other seed groups.** Either unverified (budget) or presumed too small for a microstate.

## 4. Method notes

- English-language regulator queries work (SeyLII, nation.sc), but generic "licensing authority + category" queries return foreign (NZ/UK) results. Anchor queries to "seylii" or "nation.sc".
- Register counts for Seychelles are not easy to find through search. They would need the SLA directly.
- The search cap was hit after 4 queries. Not researched: SFA artisanal fisheries, dealer registers, and SLA licence counts.
