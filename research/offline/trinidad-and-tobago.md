### Opportunity: Scrap Metal Act 2022 transaction register (rejected)

**Industry:** Scrap metal dealers and exporters
**Buyer:** Owners of licensed scrap yards
**Trigger / Why now:** The Scrap Metal Act, 2022 is only partly proclaimed. Licences under the old Old Metal and Marine Stores Act are deemed valid under s.39 and have been extended for a 5th time, to 14 Oct 2026. If the Act is fully proclaimed and new licences are issued, register-keeping duties could follow (unverified: exact record duties not confirmed).
**Current workflow:** Unknown. Not researched because searches were refused.
**Pain:** Policy uncertainty and repeated extensions (MTI releases from 2024–2025 "clarifying operations" in the industry).
**Existing solutions:** Not researched.
**Offline evidence:** MTTI publishes industry notices only as PDF media releases. No licences have been issued under the new Act.
**Offline channel:** MTTI's list of the 31 licensed sites. Direct visits or calls are feasible because the count is small.
**Market count:** 31 licensed sites (MTTI).
**Score:** 2/10. Too few buyers, and the trigger is not in force.
