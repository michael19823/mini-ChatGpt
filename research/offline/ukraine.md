# Ukraine: offline (quiet) industries pass

Researched 2026-10-05 with 20 WebSearch calls (the full budget), mostly in Ukrainian. WebFetch was not used. Facts taken only from search snippets, without a check against the primary document, are marked *unverified*. Counts not found in a register are marked *estimate* or "not found".

Context from the country report: Ukraine's state digitises fast and gives tools away free (Дія, ЕкоСистема, е-ТТН). The same thing happened in several quiet industries here. The free state tool "Є-Бджільництво" and a free NGO service already cover pesticide notifications to beekeepers. The state animal register is free. The one quiet field with a fresh, multi-part, recurring paperwork load and no free state tool is **veterinary practice**. Two changes drive it: licensing became mandatory from 31 Jan 2026, and a new regime for veterinary medicines and prescriptions took effect on 1 Mar 2026.

## 2. Opportunities

### Opportunity: Prescription and antimicrobial compliance kit for rural / farm-animal vets

**Industry:**
Veterinary practice, focused on mobile and rural vets (ФОП and small clinics) who treat cattle, pigs, poultry and small ruminants for households and small farms.

**Buyer:**
A licensed vet who is a ФОП, or the head of a small rural vet clinic. Secondary buyer: the farm or food-business operator, who must keep treatment and withdrawal-period records for food-producing animals.

**Trigger / Why now:**
- **31 Jan 2026:** the declaration route for vet practice closed. Every practitioner needs a ДПСС licence, and the transition ended 30 Apr 2026.
- **1 Mar 2026:** Law №1206-IX «Про ветеринарну медицину та благополуччя тварин» and наказ Мінагрополітики №752 took effect for medicines. Brought under prescription: all antibiotics, products for productive animals, euthanasia products and medicated feed. Electronic prescriptions are signed with КЕП. Remote sale of Rx products is banned. A prescription for an antimicrobial is valid for 5 days.
- Already in force: the annual report by vet specialists on the volumes of antimicrobials they used (наказ Мінекономіки №1177-21 of 30.12.2021).

**Current workflow:**
1. Visit the farm or household and examine the animal.
2. Write a paper prescription (or an e-prescription in the competent authority's system with КЕП). Copy it by hand into journal form №1, or form №2 for narcotics, psychotropics and euthanasia products.
3. Note the diagnosis, product, quantity, course length and withdrawal period. The farm owner must keep matching treatment records.
4. Keep originals and copies for 5 years and show them to ДПСС at inspection.
5. Once a year, total the antimicrobial volumes used and submit the report to ДПСС.

**Pain:**
- New obligations stacked in one quarter: the licence, the Rx scope expansion and the e-Rx requirement.
- The records are per visit and must survive 5-year inspections.
- Paper journals are still sold (marazm.org.ua sells form-2 journals) and bought through Prozorro, which shows paper is the norm.
- ДПСС oblast offices and associations (Vet Alliance, UACBBC "checklist for vet pharmacies") publish explainers, which signals confusion.
- Direct complaints from vets were **not found**. Pain is *inferred* from the obligations.

**Existing solutions:**
- **ENOTE:** cloud CRM/ERP for vet clinics and pharmacies, used by "hundreds" of clinics, with ПРРО integration. It is aimed at pet clinics and was not confirmed to issue e-Rx or produce the antimicrobial report.
- The state information system for electronic vet prescriptions (free, but *unverified* how usable it is in the field).
- Paper journals from stationers.
- Accountants and consultants for licence applications (e.g. law firms advertising vet-licence services).

**Offline evidence:**
- Paper journal forms are sold and tendered.
- The rules are communicated through ДПСС oblast pages and hromada websites, not vendor blogs.
- Rural vets work from cars and phones.
- No software listings target farm vets.

**Offline channel:**
- ДПСС oblast licence registers (public licensee list) give phone outreach and Viber/Telegram contact lists.
- Regional vet associations (Vet Alliance publishes for practitioners).
- Distributors of veterinary medicines. These wholesalers visit rural vets weekly and now must check prescriptions, so they share the pain. They are the best resellers.
- Agrarian and veterinary university CPD courses.

**Market count:**
Not found. The ДПСС licence register is the source. *Estimate:* low thousands of licensed practitioners, of whom perhaps 1,000–3,000 are rural or farm-focused (unverified).

**The gap:**
Pet-clinic software is built around appointments, stock and the till. Nothing found serves the mobile farm vet:
- an offline-capable phone Rx pad that writes the form-1/2 journal automatically;
- tracking withdrawal periods per farm client and printing the farmer's treatment record;
- auto-totalling antimicrobial volumes into the annual report.

**Possible product:**
A Ukrainian-language mobile app for rural vets: "write the prescription once". It produces the journal entry, the farmer's treatment and withdrawal-period sheet, the PDF/e-Rx data to key into (or submit to) the state system, and the year-end antimicrobial report.

**MVP:**
An Android app with an offline prescription form, a product list (seeded from the state register of vet medicines), an auto-generated printable form-№1 and form-№2 journal, a per-farm treatment log and an annual antimicrobial-volume export. No integration in v1.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
UAH 300–600/month per vet (~$7–15). Distributors could bundle it for free as a loyalty tool, paying $1–3k/year per distributor. Vets would pay for software only if it is cheap. A done-for-you service makes no sense here.

**How to find first customers:**
ДПСС licensee register → phone and Viber calls in two oblasts with dense livestock (e.g. Vinnytsia, Khmelnytskyi). Pitch the top 2–3 vet-drug distributors as a channel.

**Risks:**
- ДПСС's own e-Rx system may become good enough, or ENOTE may add a mobile farm module.
- Rural vets have very low incomes.
- Enforcement in villages may be lax under martial law.
- A local Ukrainian speaker is needed. A **non-local solo founder could not realistically sell this** without a Ukrainian partner.

**Kill condition:**
- The state e-Rx system already writes the journals and computes the annual antimicrobial report.
- Or interviews show farm vets still do not record antimicrobial prescriptions in practice.

**Score:** 5/10

### Opportunity: Acceptance-act and cash-purchase register for scrap-metal reception points

**Industry:**
Scrap-metal collection (заготівля металобрухту): reception points (приймальні пункти) of specialised companies.

**Buyer:**
The owner or manager of a scrap company with 1–20 reception points.

**Trigger / Why now:**
The Ministry of Economy approved a new form of information for the Перелік of scrap operators (order №6782 of 16.06.2026, amended by №7468 of 13.07.2026). Entries are submitted electronically, with a Дія guide. The existing per-purchase rules stay in force:
- an acceptance act per purchase, kept for 1 year;
- purchases from individuals count as settlement operations under the cash-register law;
- video surveillance able to identify the seller.

**Current workflow:**
1. A citizen brings scrap. The point weighs it and writes a free-form acceptance act (company, quantity, origin).
2. The point pays cash through an expense cash order (видатковий касовий ордер) and runs the operation through РРО/ПРРО.
3. Video is recorded and kept.
4. The office compiles volumes and keeps the company's Перелік information up to date.

**Pain:**
- Penalties exist for violating scrap-operation rules (legalaid.wiki).
- Under the cash-register law each purchase is a fiscal operation.
- Evidence of operator pain was **not found**. GMK Center reports that the de-shadowing law "never worked" and the market remains largely in the shadow, which suggests weak enforcement.

**Existing solutions:**
- Generic ПРРО apps (Checkbox, Cashalot and others, *not checked for scrap-specific features*).
- BAS/1C accounting.
- Paper act pads.
- Video-surveillance vendors.

**Offline evidence:**
Acts are free-form paper documents. The rules reach operators through regional state administration notices (Vinnytsia ОДА, Kyiv district administrations), not vendor blogs.

**Offline channel:**
The Ministry's published Перелік (names and addresses) for phone outreach. The steel mills that buy scrap (Метінвест, АМКР etc.) and their supplier networks. The scrap association УАВтормет (*unverified name*).

**Market count:**
Not found. The Перелік is public but no count was seen.

**The gap:**
A tablet app at the scale: weight → act → ПРРО receipt → photo of the seller and the scrap, in one step, plus a monthly volume summary for the Перелік and the buyers. This is a hypothesis.

**Possible product:**
A "scale-side" acceptance app that produces the legally required act and fiscal receipt per purchase and keeps a searchable archive for police and inspection queries.

**MVP:**
An Android app with an act template, a ПРРО integration through an existing ПРРО API, photo capture and a CSV export.

**Pricing hypothesis:**
UAH 500–1,000/month per point (estimate).

**How to find first customers:**
Call operators from the Ministry's Перелік. Get introductions through the mills' scrap-procurement departments.

**Risks:**
- The shadow economy means operators may avoid records.
- Exports are under zero quotas.
- Generic ПРРО apps are good enough.
- A local founder is required.

**Kill condition:**
Operators say police and inspection never ask for the acts, or a ПРРО vendor already offers a scrap template.

**Score:** 3.5/10

## 3. Rejected

- **Pesticide-spraying notifications to beekeepers.** This was a perfect "one job, many receivers" pattern: notify all councils and beekeepers within 10 km at least 3 days ahead, with UAH 30–50k fines. It was killed by two free tools. The state's **Є-Бджільництво** offers a spray journal, an 8 km beekeeper alert and council notification. **Grand Expert** sends a free SMS to apiaries within 10 km. ## 4. Method notes

What worked:
- Ukrainian queries on regulator and community sites (dpss.gov.ua oblast pages, hromada websites, me.gov.ua) and accountant media (dtkt, ligazakon, 7eminar) gave triggers and dates quickly.
- Searching for paper-journal sellers and Prozorro tenders proved offline workflows.

What didn't:
- No query returned operator counts. ДПСС and Ministry registers exist but their totals don't show up in search snippets, and WebFetch was not allowed.
- Every "obvious" quiet-industry router idea (beekeeper notification, animal ID) had already been built by the state or an NGO for free. Ukraine needs a check of the free-state-tool alternative first.
