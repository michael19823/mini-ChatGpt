# Albania: accountants (generic consultant category, no URL)

Idea it killed: C0005 Short-term-rental DIVA income declaration helper.

## Verdict: beatable

Local accountants and tax consultants are the default alternative, but no accountant was found offering a productised, cheap DIVA service for short-term-rental hosts. Based on 4 WebSearch calls; no specific firm was evaluated.

## Evidence
- Since 1 Jan 2026, hosts on Airbnb/Booking.com must declare rental income via DIVA (annual individual income declaration), 15% tax. No NIPT needed. No obligation under 50,000 ALL/year. Deadline was 31 Mar 2026 (sources: english.gazetatema.net "Airbnb and Booking may have to report income in Albania"; himara.net/es/blog/airbnb-albania-2026; eurofast.eu "Your Last Chance: April 30 Deadline for Albania's DIVA Tax Declaration" mentions a 30 April deadline, a discrepancy, unverified which applies).
- Enforcement is real: authorities began checking Booking.com hosts (100 persons/businesses, about EUR 24.9M, Oct 2022 to Dec 2025), and platforms must share transaction data (english.gazetatema.net "Booking hosts face tax crackdown"). This creates demand for help.
- Scale: about 26.3k apartments and about 21k hosts on Airbnb in 2025 (shqiptarja.com), a large pool of small taxpayers.
- Complaints: none found. No reviews, forum threads or app-store reviews about accountants' DIVA service for hosts surfaced in English or Albanian searches.
- Momentum: not applicable to a generic category; firms such as Eurofast publish DIVA explainers (visible activity, but aimed at businesses/expats, unverified for host service).

## Pricing
No accountant fee for DIVA or rental declarations was found. Unverified. Searches explicitly returned nothing on prices.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- Accountants are typically oriented to NIPT businesses; a no-NIPT individual host with a few bookings is a small, possibly unattractive client.
- Foreign owners need English-language help; Eurofast-type firms cater to them but price is unknown.
- Nothing found on automated import of Airbnb/Booking payout data into DIVA.
- DIVA itself is a government e-filing, so a helper competes with free self-filing plus an accountant's one-off fee.

## Opening
Low-cost, English/Albanian guided DIVA prep for individual hosts (payout data to declaration) fits a gap, but the deadline-driven, once-a-year demand and free self-filing limit the ceiling.
