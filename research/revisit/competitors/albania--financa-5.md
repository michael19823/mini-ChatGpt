# Financa 5 (Albania)

Dropped idea: C0002, payroll and tax-declaration automation for accounting firms.

## Verdict: weak

Only a general-ledger accounting package with an established user base. I found no evidence it does payroll or tax-declaration automation. Evidence is thin overall: 5 of 6 searches used (the sixth was not needed).

## Evidence
- Epoka University described "Financa 5" as a well-known accounting software broadly used for company financial administration, and ran a six-week training on it starting 1 April 2017. Source: https://www.epoka.edu.al/news-training-on-financa-5-software-3446.html
- A job posting surfaced (balfin.al, https://balfin.al/?p=13059) that, per the search summary, seeks knowledge of Financa 5 and/or Microsoft Dynamics NAV. I did not read the page itself, so treat this as unverified.
- No vendor website, developer name, or product page found. Vendor identity: unverified.
- Searches in Albanian and English for payroll or tax-declaration features (pagat, deklarimi tatimor) returned only generic tatime.gov.al and PwC material and nothing on Financa 5. Whether Financa 5 exports or files declarations: unverified.
- Albanian tax filings are mandatory electronic filings (per the tatime.gov.al results), so any product serving this need must integrate with that. Nothing shows Financa 5 does.

## Complaints
None found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit, forum or news complaints surfaced. That is probably because the product has little online presence, not because users are satisfied.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of payroll, social-insurance or tax-declaration automation (unverified either way).
- Appears to be desktop-era accounting software; the only dated reference is 2017. Cloud, mobile and multi-client accounting-firm workflow support: unverified.

## Momentum
Unknown. The only dated source is 2017 and no recent releases, news or website were found. Possibly stagnant (unverified).

## Opening
Little visible competition from Financa 5 for payroll and declaration automation aimed at accounting firms. The real competitors are likely other local products and the state e-filing portal, which this check did not cover.
