# HotelBee (Albania)

Killed ideas: C0004 (E-invoicing / fiscalization connector), C0009 (Hotel PMS with fiscalization).
Searches used: 4 of 6 (English and Albanian). No URL for the vendor site was found, so none is given.

## Verdict: beatable (for C0004 and C0009)

## Evidence
- Cloud PMS (bookings, housekeeping, invoicing, accounting, POS, channel manager) based in Tirana, per Capterra/GetApp listings: https://www.capterra.com/p/227957/HotelBee/ and https://www.getapp.ca/software/2067670/hotelbee
- Search results show nothing confirming HotelBee supports Albanian fiscalization (NIVF / DPT real-time e-invoice). Unverified either way; it should be checked directly with the vendor.
- Cloudbeds publishes an Albania government-compliance page, so at least one international PMS targets the same need: https://cloudbeds.com/government-compliance/albania
- Complaints: none found. Searches returned only listing pages with no user review text. Absence of complaints reflects a small review base, not proof of satisfaction.
- Momentum: still listed on Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice with 2026 pages. No acquisition or shutdown found. Development pace is unverified.

## Pricing
- Listings say "starting from about EUR 40/month". One snippet mentions a free tier for under 5 rooms and another mentions plans from $400/month. These conflict and are unverified.

## Fit gaps (unverified)
- Fiscalization support not shown in any source found.
- Few reviews, so the evidence base is thin.
- Multi-entity, offline and mobile use were not checked.

## Opening
A fiscalization connector (or a PMS with built-in NIVF filing) is open if HotelBee lacks it, and it could sit alongside HotelBee. Confirm its fiscalization status first.
