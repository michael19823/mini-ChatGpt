# Pagero (Albania) - dropped idea C0004: E-invoicing / fiscalization connector

## Verdict: weak (close to not-a-real-competitor in Albania)

## Evidence
- No source found showing Pagero offers or markets Albanian fiscalization (NIVF/QR, real-time XML to the tax authority's central platform). A search for Albanian-language material (Pagero + Shqiperi + faturim elektronik + fiskalizim) returned only tax-authority and law pages, no Pagero page.
- A Pagero/Zuora supported-country list for AR invoicing (via a Zuora docs search result, https://docs.zuora.com/zuora-billing/bill-your-customer/leverage-advanced-capabilities/e-invoicing/supported-countries-by-e-invoicing-vendors) does not list Albania. AP coverage is described as wider and "contact them"; Albania coverage is unverified.
- Albania's regime (Law 87/2019, certified software, NIVF identifier, QR code, real-time reporting; see https://eurofast.eu/albania-new-fiscalization-tax-reform-requires-e-invoicing/) requires certified local software and registered software producers (per tatime.gov.al / Udhezim 16/2020 results). Pagero listing for that certification is unverified.
- Momentum: Thomson Reuters took majority control of Pagero in Jan-Feb 2024 (about USD 800m, per https://thomsonreuters.com/en-us/posts/innovation/thomson-reuters-corporation-acquires-majority-interest-in-pagero-a-world-leader-in-e-invoicing and sharedserviceslink.com). It is being rebranded into Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE Pagero, with 75-80+ countries claimed. Actively developed, but oriented to global enterprises.
- Complaints: none found specific to Albania. Generic aggregator (rfp.wiki, a secondary source of unclear reliability) mentions some onboarding frustration, long ERP-dependent setup, and cost vs lighter tools; its rating figures were not verified.

## Pricing
Not published in anything found (unverified). Enterprise sales model; likely overpriced for Albanian SMEs (inference, not verified).

## Fit gaps
- Albania apparently not in public supported-country lists.
- Enterprise/multinational focus; ERP-integration-led onboarding; no evidence of Albanian-language support or local certified-software registration.
- No evidence of SME self-serve or local accountant/POS integrations.

## Opening
A cheap, locally certified, Albanian-language fiscalization connector for SMEs and local accounting/POS software faces no visible Pagero presence; the real competition is local certified vendors (not researched here).
