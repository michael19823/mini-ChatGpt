# PMS Expert (Albania) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0009 (Hotel PMS with fiscalization). Searches used: 4 of 6 (English and Albanian).

## Verdict: weak

Only a Capterra listing was found. It claims Albania availability, but no site, no Albanian fiscalization evidence, and almost no market footprint. Several better-evidenced PMS options exist for Albania (see below).

## Evidence
- Capterra listing "pms expert": founded 2009, cloud/SaaS/web, Android/iOS apps, channel manager with 100+ OTAs, CRM, maintenance management, available in Albania, 24/7 chat support. Source: https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/179198/pms-expert
- Capterra shows 5.0/5 from only 2 reviews (very thin signal; not independently verified).
- No evidence found that PMS Expert is certified for or integrates with Albanian fiscalization. Unverified.
- No complaints found: no app-store, Reddit, Trustpilot or local forum reviews. A search for the product's reviews returned only unrelated premenstrual-syndrome pages.
- Momentum: unverified; no news, release notes or acquisition found.
- Competitors that did show up for Albanian fiscalization: Clock PMS+ (via S-Systems/Sconnect, https://www.clock-software.com/products/integrations/fiscalization-s-systems), Logic (https://www.logic.al/en/clients/), MiniHotel (claims fiscalization ready in 50 countries, per App Store listing https://apps.apple.com/app/id1454930137). Cloudbeds states it has no automated Albania solution yet (https://www.cloudbeds.com/?p=42961).

## Pricing
- Capterra: starting price EUR 15.00, per-feature model, free trial. Unit (per month/property) not verified.

## Fit gaps
- Albanian fiscalization support: unverified, not confirmed anywhere found.
- Unknown whether it supports Albanian language, small guesthouses, or offline use. All unverified.

## Opening
Little evidence PMS Expert is a real obstacle; the actual competition for an Albanian fiscalized hotel PMS is Clock/S-Systems, Logic and local custom vendors, so check those rather than this one.
