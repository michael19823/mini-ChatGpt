# Albania -- Voxel (Voxel Group, Barcelona)

Idea it killed: C0004 E-invoicing / fiscalization connector.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Voxel Group appears to be a Spanish/Caribbean B2B e-invoicing vendor. In Albania it only publishes an informational guide. I found no evidence of an Albanian product, customers or local certification. (4 of 6 searches used.)

## Evidence
- Voxel's Albania page is a regulatory explainer, not a product page: https://voxelgroup.net/normativa/guias/albania
- It describes the Albanian CIS / NIVF / QR-code model and UBL 2.1. No Albania-specific product, price or customer is named in the results.
- Its platform, Bavel (Bavel Billing, Pay, Procurement), is aimed at tourism and large corporates, with a Spanish and Dominican Republic focus (600 clients in the DR, per revistamercado.do interviews): https://www.voxelgroup.net/bavel-billing-solution/
- The Albanian-language search (Voxel fiskalizimi, faturë elektronike) returned only tatime.gov.al documents and no mention of Voxel. Whether Voxel is in the tax authority's registry of software providers is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. I found no Trustpilot, app-store or forum reviews of Voxel, for Albania or generally. This is an absence of evidence, not evidence that customers are happy.
- Momentum: the company is active (blog, Bavel platform), but nothing indicates Albanian activity.

## Pricing
The only figure found was "from 6000 EUR" for an electronic transaction platform solution. This came from a search summary, the exact source page is unverified, and it is not Albania-specific. If accurate, it is far above what a small Albanian business would pay.

## Fit gaps
- No visible Albanian localisation, support or reseller.
- It targets large and tourism-sector enterprises, not Albanian SMEs.
- Albanian-language support and DPT/CIS certification: unverified.

## Opening
Voxel does not occupy the Albanian SME e-invoicing connector niche. The real competitors are local certified providers and the free tax authority tools, which should be checked separately.
