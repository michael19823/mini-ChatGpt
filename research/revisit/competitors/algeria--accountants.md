# Algeria: accountants (consultant, no URL)

Ideas killed: C0017 (general accounting), G50 (local accounting packages, accountants).
Searches used: 4 of 6 (French, English, Arabic, French software query).

## Verdict: beatable

Accountants and local accounting software are an established, mandatory service for Algerian businesses under the real tax regime, but the searches found no evidence of a well-loved, cheap, digital incumbent. The only product-like competitor found is an Odoo module priced high for small buyers.

## Evidence
- G50 is a monthly declaration (VAT, TAP, IRG, IBS) for real-regime firms, due around the 20th/21st of the following month, and filing is mostly through the Jibayatic platform. Late filing carries a 10% penalty plus 3% per month, capped at 25%. Sources: https://www.upgrowth.dz/en/g50-monthly-tax-return-algeria , https://www.upgrowth.dz/en/company-vat-algeria
- One result says SMEs spend heavily on VAT-related staff time and use "low cost software" to limit compliance cost. The figures came from a search summary, and I did not verify them at source. Source: https://www.bankofscotlandtrade.co.uk/en/market-potential/algeria/accounting (unverified figures).
- Academic work (Ghardaia, Msila) points to shortcomings in G50 bookkeeping practice, often from insufficient training, and to barriers to accounting adoption in SMEs. Sources: https://dspace.univ-ghardaia.edu.dz/items/036aa04c-4bb3-4efc-ad99-dd58f59f5ba9/full , https://depot.univ-msila.dz/bitstreams/a2bdeb98-4b96-4998-9ef6-009130371bff/download
- Software competitors found: Odoo localisation modules (dz_accounting, Digitaliac) with SCF, G50 and ERA/Jibayatic reports. Source: https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/dz_accounting
- Complaints: I found no consumer reviews or complaints about Algerian accountants (no app-store reviews, Reddit or forum posts in the results). This is a gap in the search results and does not show customers are satisfied.

## Pricing
- Odoo dz_accounting module: listed at $1141.46 on the Odoo app store. I could not confirm whether this is one-time or annual. It is high for a micro-business, and it needs Odoo.
- Accountant fees in Algeria: not found. The results were French-market only, so I give no figure. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Odoo-based solutions need an ERP setup and are likely too heavy for micro-businesses and freelancers.
- Evidence of weak G50 practice and limited training suggests demand for guided, simple, Arabic/French tools. This is inferred and not confirmed by user complaints.
- Not verified: Arabic UI, mobile use, direct Jibayatic filing.

## Momentum
Odoo modules are maintained for versions 16 to 19 (actively developed). Individual accountants are fragmented and have no single product roadmap.

## Opening
Little visible competition in the lightweight segment: a simple G50/Jibayatic-oriented tool for small real-regime firms, in French and Arabic, priced far below the Odoo module. Validate fee levels and complaints locally, because the searches here could not.
