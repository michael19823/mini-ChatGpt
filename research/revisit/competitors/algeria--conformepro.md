# ConformePro (Algeria) - competitor check for C0013 (Law 25-11 data-protection compliance)

## Verdict: beatable

## Evidence
- Real, live Algerian SaaS at conformepro.dz (https://conformepro.dz/). It is a broad administrative-compliance platform, not a dedicated Law 25-11 tool. Modules listed in search snippets: RC/NIF authentication of customers, customs-tariff filing for import authorisation, pre-filled administrative forms with tracking, and "personal data processing management" (processing and consent).
- Personal-data is one module among four; the site mentions Law 18-07. A mention of Law 25-11 specifically (mandatory DPO, DPIA, 5-day breach notification) was not confirmed (unverified).
- Claimed clients on its site: Faras International, DP World Djazair, KPMG, ECCBC, Promedal, Froidis, El Wedjdene, Adara. Skews to larger or corporate names (self-reported, unverified).
- Reviews: only testimonials on its own site (https://conformepro.dz/reviews), all positive. No independent reviews or complaints found (6 searches in French and English; none in Arabic; no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum hits). No complaints found.
- Momentum: has a press page and terms of service in French and Arabic; a trustmrr.com listing ("conforme pro") exists, suggesting a small startup. Actively maintained looks likely, but dates and funding were not found (unverified).

## Pricing
Not published in anything found. Terms of service (https://conformepro.dz/terms-of-service) exist but their contents were not read. Pricing is unverified; it appears to be demo/quote-based.

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified)
- Data-protection module appears to be a general processing/consent register, not a full Law 25-11 workflow (DPO tooling, DPIA, breach-notice handling, ANPDP filings) - unverified.
- Broad multi-purpose focus and enterprise-style client list suggest SMEs, small professionals and DPO-as-a-service buyers are less served.
- Arabic site exists; depth of Arabic content in the product is unverified.

## Opening
A focused, low-cost Law 25-11 toolkit (DPO workflow, DPIA, 5-day breach notification, ANPDP filings) in Arabic/French for SMEs can beat ConformePro's generalist data module.
