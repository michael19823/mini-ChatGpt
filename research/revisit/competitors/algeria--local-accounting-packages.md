# Algeria: local accounting packages (ideas C0017 General accounting, G50)

## Verdict: weak

## Evidence
- Searches used: 4 of 6 (one returned a usage-limit error; the rest were not run). Languages: Arabic, English, French.
- An Algeria tech news piece says Algeria's 1.3M SMEs, 98% of them micro (<10 staff), "remain almost entirely without dedicated business software" ([algeriatech.news](https://algeriatech.news/?p=28642)). Secondary source, not verified.
- The same source says about 64 local SaaS startups are building ERP/HR/CRM with Arabic-French UIs and built-in Algerian tax compliance, DZD pricing, and an e-invoicing mandate delayed to 2027. Claims unverified; no specific vendor names found.
- G50 results were academic theses, tax-office pages and explainers (e.g. [upgrowth.dz](https://www.upgrowth.dz/ar/g50-declaration-algerie)). They show G50 is a monthly VAT/withholding return filed manually or electronically. No product that automates G50 was identified.
- An Ouedkniss listing appeared (https://www.ouedkniss.com/store/17937/it-device/annonce/52516333); content not seen, so unverified.
- The French-language search returned only French-market results (Sage, EBP, Ciel, PC Compta). Nothing Algeria-specific.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Reddit or forum reviews of Algerian accounting packages surfaced. This is a lack of evidence, not proof of satisfaction.
- Momentum: unknown for specific packages.

## Pricing
No Algerian published prices found. Any figures seen were French euro prices (e.g. Sage from 15 EUR/month, EBP from 12 EUR/month per search summaries); these are not Algerian prices and are unverified for the local market.

## Fit gaps (partly inferred, unverified)
- No identifiable local package targeting micro-SMEs with G50 automation.
- Foreign packages are euro-priced and not clearly localized to SCF/G50.
- Local firms appear to rely on accountants and bookkeeping firms rather than software.

## Opening
Micro-SMEs and accountants appear underserved for simple, DZD-priced, SCF/G50-aware, Arabic-French accounting; no concrete local product was found, so verify with a manual check of Ouedkniss and local vendors.
