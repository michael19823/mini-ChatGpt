# Mercans (Angola) - competitor check for C0022 (IRT/INSS payroll compliance tool)

## Verdict: beatable

## Evidence
- Mercans has a dedicated Angola payroll page and a Portuguese-language payroll software page. It claims IRT calculation, INSS contributions, statutory filings and reports, as Managed or SaaS, for "enterprises and SMEs" (https://mercans.com/global-payroll/angola/, https://mercans.com/payroll-software/angola/portuguese/). Company claims, not independently verified.
- It claims alignment with Angola's General Labour Law (Law 12/23) and with INSS and AGT requirements, and says it is ISO 27001 certified (same pages).
- Ratings (via an aggregator summary, not checked at source): G2 4.8/5 from 27 reviews, Glassdoor 4.1, Trustpilot 4.0 from 4 reviews (https://www.gloroots.com/blog/mercans-reviews, https://es.trustpilot.com/review/mercans.com).
- Complaints found were generic and not Angola-specific: dated UX, limited customization, poor support in some cases, no published prices (gloroots and employerrecords.com review summaries; unverified at the original reviews). Smaller firms without multi-country needs may find it more than they need.
- No Angola-specific complaints, outages or failed-filing news were found. No app-store reviews or local Portuguese forum complaints were found (3 searches run; Reddit/Facebook not surfaced).
- Momentum: the site is active, with 2026 content (2026 minimum wage and IRT exemption threshold of AOA 150,000). Appears actively maintained. No acquisition or shutdown news found.

## Pricing
- Payroll pricing is not published and there is no self-serve quote. EOR is reported at about $600 per employee per month (third-party claim, unverified). This is likely out of reach for a small local Angolan business.

## Fit gaps
- Positioned as a global multi-country payroll provider. It is probably sales-led and aimed at multinationals, with managed service and quote-based pricing.
- No evidence of a self-serve, low-cost tool for local SMEs, accountants or small employers, or of AOA-priced plans.
- Offline and mobile use, and local-bank or filing-format specifics: unverified.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, Portuguese-first IRT/INSS calculator and filing helper for local SMEs and accountants, priced in AOA, sits below Mercans' enterprise/managed-service positioning.
