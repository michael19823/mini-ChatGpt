# Ontop (Angola) - competitor to C0022 (IRT/INSS payroll compliance tool)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (for a local IRT/INSS compliance tool)

Ontop is a Miami/Bogota global EOR, contractor and payroll platform for foreign companies hiring in 150+ countries. Its Angola page covers INSS and IRT-style calculations, but only as a service for foreign employers paying Angolan staff. It is not a domestic compliance tool for Angolan SMEs.

## Evidence
- Ontop has an Angola payroll page. It claims AI-powered calculations, local INSS handling, compliant payslips, and no local entity needed. Source: https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/angola (search snippet; page not opened).
- Company background (founded 2020 in Bogota, Miami-based, global payroll and workforce platform). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontop
- Reviews are mixed. Search snippets report Trustpilot 3.7 from about 100 reviews and Capterra 4.4 from 32 reviews. G2 is reported as about 4.6, though that figure may be a G2 score rather than a count (unverified). Sources: https://www.capterra.ca/reviews/1023440/ontop, https://subscribed.fyi/ontop/reviews/
- Reported concerns are negative employee and contractor reviews and a lack of transparency about services. Sources: https://subscribed.fyi/ontop/reviews/, https://www.techrepublic.com/article/ontop-payroll-review/. I did not read the individual reviews, so I quote none.
- No Angola-specific complaints found (no Angolan forums, Portuguese-language reviews or news of failed filings turned up).

## Pricing
- Reported by review sites: Contractor Starter $29/mo (up to 10 contractors), Contractors $49/mo, EOR $499/mo (per worker, unverified). Sources: https://www.techrepublic.com/article/ontop-payroll-review/, https://subscribed.fyi/ontop/reviews/
- I did not find a published Angola-specific price. All plans are in USD and aimed at cross-border hiring.

## Fit gaps
- It serves foreign firms hiring in Angola through EOR or contractors. It is not for Angolan companies running their own payroll.
- It does not appear to offer AGT/INSS filing workflows for local entities, local-language (Portuguese) bookkeeping, or Kwanza-based local pricing (unverified; not confirmed on its site).
- EOR pricing is far above what a small Angolan firm could pay for a payroll tool.

## Momentum
- It looks active and growing (2026 review pages, Angola page live). Not shut down or stagnant.

## Opening left
A Portuguese-language, Kwanza-priced IRT/INSS calculator and filing tool for Angolan SMEs is untouched by Ontop, which targets foreign employers.
