# Competitor: Pebl (Angola) - killed idea C0022 (IRT/INSS payroll compliance tool)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Pebl (formerly Velocity Global, site hellopebl.com) is a global employer-of-record (EOR) service. It is not a self-serve IRT/INSS payroll tool for local Angolan firms. It only overlaps when a foreign company hires staff in Angola through Pebl.

## Evidence
- Search results describe Pebl as an "AI-first platform and global employer of record" that provides payroll and compliance for Angola. It has an Angola payroll-tax blog page (https://hellopebl.com/resources/blog/payroll-tax-in-angola), which is content marketing, not a local product.
- The searches (English and Portuguese) found no Pebl product for local Angolan SMEs. The Portuguese query returned no Pebl-specific Angola material. Actual local-payroll alternatives surfaced were Odoo l10n_ao_payroll modules (apps.odoo.com), Mercans payroll software for Angola (mercans.com/payroll-software/angola/) and Ontop (getontop.com/payroll-in/angola). These are the closer competitors to C0022 and are not assessed here.
- Complaints (Trustpilot, https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/hellopebl.com): TrustScore 2.5/5 from 6 reviews. Themes reported: very high fees, currency risk placed on the employee, unstable leadership and layoffs, contract risk on the employee, and onboarding/compliance communication problems. Pebl had not replied to the negative reviews. The sample is tiny, and the complaints are about the EOR model, not Angola or IRT/INSS. I did not find Angola-specific complaints.
- Momentum: rebranded from Velocity Global to Pebl and still active, with 2026 pricing coverage. Not shut down.

## Pricing
- Per-employee-per-month (PEPM) EOR model. Third-party sources (e.g. gloroots.com, anywherer.com, hireinsouth.com pricing articles) cite a promotional price of about $399 and a standard price of about $599 per employee per month. These are third-party figures and I did not verify them on Pebl's own site (unverified).
- The same sources say that setup, FX and offboarding fees push the real cost 30-50% higher (unverified).
- That is far above what a small Angolan firm would pay for payroll software.

## Fit gaps (for C0022's buyer: local Angolan SMEs and accountants)
- It is an EOR that employs the worker on Pebl's behalf, not software the local firm runs on its own staff.
- The price is aimed at foreign employers hiring abroad.
- No evidence of Portuguese-language self-serve tooling, AGT/INSS filing formats or offline use (unverified).

## Opening
Pebl leaves the local-SME IRT/INSS payroll tool segment untouched; the real checks for C0022 are Mercans, Odoo modules and local Angolan payroll or accounting software.
