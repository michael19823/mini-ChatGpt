# Primavera (PRIMAVERA BSS) - Angola

Dropped idea: C0022 (IRT/INSS payroll compliance tool)

## Verdict: strong

Primavera is the market-leading ERP/accounting vendor in Angola with a local office and a small-business tier. No complaints were found. Only 2 of 3 searches returned results (one hit the usage limit), so the evidence is thin, and payroll specifics are unverified.

## Evidence
- Reported to be used by 34% of companies in Angola, with the most customers of any ERP in Angola, Mozambique and Cape Verde. Sources: https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/072015/Tecnologia/1694/Primavera-BSS-tecnológica-portuguesa-é-líder-no-mercado-angolano.htm ; https://hrportugal.sapo.pt/?p=32055
- Local operation in Luanda (about 30 employees, per the cited coverage). Source: https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/102015/Tecnologia/2675/Primavera-BSS-operação-em-Angola-cresceu-23-por-cento-até-Setembro-“muito-acima”-do-esperado.htm
- Angolan revenue was expected to reach about 5 million euros in 2019, up almost 80%, driven by the introduction of VAT. This indicates active momentum. Source: https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/112019/Economia/17189/IVA-em-Angola-ajuda-Primavera-a-facturar-mais-80-por-cento-este-ano.htm
- A package for micro and small companies was launched in 2017. Source: https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/052017/Tecnologia/8293/Primavera-BSS-lança-pacote-de-software-para-apoiar-micro-e-pequenas-empresas.htm
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, forum or review-site results surfaced. The AGT portal outages in the results concern the tax authority, not Primavera. The search for Primavera reclamações returned nothing on Primavera itself.
- Payroll (IRT/INSS) module coverage in Angola: unverified. The searches did not confirm it.
- Recent news (2024-2026): none found. Momentum evidence is from 2015-2019 articles, so current status is unverified.

## Pricing
- Subscription model (1 or 2 year renewable, pay-as-you-go) with no large upfront cost. Source: Ver Angola 2019 article above.
- Three free invoicing programs (Starter Express, PSSS Express, Tlim Express), free until billing reaches 15 million kwanzas. These are invoicing, not payroll. Source: https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/122019/Tecnologia/17645/Primavera-BSS-cria-software-de-facturação-gratuito-para-empresas.htm
- Free technical support for 30 days after installation.
- Published price list for payroll: not found (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- The free tier is invoicing only, so very small firms with payroll needs may not be covered (unverified).
- Full ERP is likely heavy for micro firms or accountants who only need IRT/INSS calculation and filing (inference, unverified).
- No evidence found of gaps in language, filing format, mobile or offline use.

## Opening
Little visible: a standalone IRT/INSS tool would have to beat an entrenched, locally supported ERP, so it would only fit as a cheap add-on for micro firms or accountants who don't want a full ERP.
