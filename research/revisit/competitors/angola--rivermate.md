# Angola: Rivermate (dropped idea C0022: IRT/INSS payroll compliance tool)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Rivermate is a global Employer-of-Record (EOR) and payroll provider for foreign companies hiring abroad. It is not a local IRT/INSS compliance tool, and its own page appears to say it does not currently serve Angola.

## Evidence
- Search snippet of Rivermate's Angola EOR page (https://www.rivermate.com/employer-of-record/angola) shows 499 EUR per employee per month, but the same result notes Rivermate "currently does not offer services" in Angola. Page text not read directly (WebFetch blocked), so treat as unverified.
- Rivermate guides for Angola (https://rivermate.com/pt/guias/angola/salario) are informational content, not a local filing product.
- Rivermate was formed by the rebrand of Eos, Serviap and Hightekers EOR (banner on rivermate.com pages), so it is actively developed and consolidating.
- Ratings reported in a search summary (unverified, not opened): G2 4.9/5 (73 reviews), Capterra 4.9/5 (44), Trustpilot 3.9/5 (24), Gartner Peer Insights 4.4/5 (21). Source: https://peoplemanagingpeople.com/tools/rivermate-review/ and G2 listing https://www.g2.com/products/rivermate/discuss.
- Complaints: none found specific to Angola or to IRT/INSS. The only general caveat seen was that reporting options feel limited. Two of six planned searches (reviews/complaints, Angola compliance) failed on a usage-limit error, so complaint coverage is thin; no Reddit, Facebook or Portuguese-language forum results were obtained.

## Pricing
- 499 EUR per employee per month for Angola EOR (an older figure of 399 EUR also appeared). Unverified, from search snippets. Far above what a small Angolan firm would pay for payroll software, and it is an EOR fee, not a software subscription.

## Fit gaps (for an IRT/INSS tool buyer: local SMEs and accountants)
- Targets foreign firms hiring in Angola, not Angolan employers running their own payroll.
- Apparently not offered in Angola at present (unverified).
- No evidence of AGT/INSS filing formats, Portuguese-language local payroll software, or self-serve tooling.

## Opening
A self-serve IRT/INSS payroll and filing tool for Angolan SMEs and accountants is untouched by Rivermate, which sells expensive EOR services to foreign companies.
