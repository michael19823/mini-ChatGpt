# Certronic (Argentina)

Ideas it killed: C0029 (contractor documentation control), C0033 (contractor control).

## Verdict: strong

Established local SaaS aimed at mid-to-large firms, with good ratings and no complaints found. Beating it head-on is unlikely. Any opening is in the small-buyer segment.

## Evidence
- Argentine SaaS from Bahia Blanca, founded by labor lawyer Francisco Costa, who also founded the audit firm Laborem. It is described as modular and scalable and as chosen by "more than 160 leading companies". Features: contractor documentation control, AI document reading, delivery requirements with due dates, and access control for premises and plants. Source: https://www.itsitio.com/ar/software/certronic-el-software-argentino-que-transformo-el-control-de-contratistas-en-el-mundo/
- Capterra lists it at 4.7/5 from 7 users (a small sample). Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/252221/Certronic/
- Momentum: it is still marketed, and itsitio.com ran an AI-in-contractor-management article (https://www.itsitio.com/ar/inteligencia-artificial/inteligencia-artificial-en-la-gestion-de-contratistas-que-estan-haciendo-las-empresas-lideres-de-la-region/). That article is only a hint of activity, and its content was not checked. Acquisition or shutdown: none found.
- Complaints: none found. A Spanish-language review search returned unrelated companies. App-store, Reddit, Trustpilot and forum complaints were not surfaced in 6 searches or were not checked. Finding none is a valid result.

## Pricing
Not published. Capterra says to contact the vendor and that no free trial is available. No price or minimum is verified.

## Fit gaps
- The "160 leading companies" positioning suggests a large-enterprise focus (unverified).
- No free trial and quote-only pricing probably put it out of reach for small buyers (inferred, not verified).
- The site was not checked for offline or mobile use, other languages or any hardware requirement (unverified).

## Opening
Self-serve, low-cost contractor document control for SMEs or small contractors who cannot get quotes from or afford an enterprise vendor. This is unproven.
