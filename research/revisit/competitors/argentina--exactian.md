# Exactian (Argentina) - competitor check

Killed ideas: C0029 (Contractor documentation control), C0033 (Contractor control; also Certronic).

## Verdict: strong

Established Argentine vendor (Exactian Consulting S.A.), a regional leader in contractor control, aimed at large multinationals.

## Evidence
- Product is WebCont / "Acceso Contratista": contractors upload a file per document per period; automated alerts, due-date controls, unlimited users, plant access control, integrations. Source: https://exactian.com/?p=472 (search snippet, not fetched).
- Scale: more than 35,000 resources managed; one major oil company uses it across Argentina, Ecuador, Mexico and Colombia (45,000 resources). Same source.
- Described as a Latin America leader used by 50+ multinationals (search summary of a result on connectamericas.com; exact page unverified).
- Market context: a CONA RH survey reported by Forbes Argentina says only 27% of companies control contractor documentation monthly and 70% call their controls superficial, so the need exists. https://www.forbesargentina.com/today/litigiosidad-70-control-documentacion-contratistas-realizan-suficiente-n24603
- Complaints: none found (no app-store, G2, Capterra, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced in 5 searches). Absence of reviews is not evidence of satisfaction.
- Momentum: no news of acquisition, shutdown or stagnation found. Unverified either way.

## Pricing
Not published; none found. Unverified. Enterprise, quote-based positioning is implied.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- Focus on large multinationals and oil/industrial clients; likely heavy for small contractor-hiring firms.
- No evidence of self-serve signup or a published small-business tier.
- Mobile/offline support not confirmed.

## Opening
Small and mid-size Argentine firms that cannot afford enterprise contractor-control suites (Exactian, Certronic) with self-serve, low-cost tooling, though the incumbents are entrenched.
