# IfsinRem (Argentina)

Dropped ideas: C0031 (HyS / PPE digital records under SRT Prevencion 4.0), C0035 (HyS/PPE apps: IfsinRem, SafetyCulture, insurers' tools).

## Verdict: weak

Tiny, very early startup (about 200 PPE deliveries digitised per the press piece), with no complaints or reviews found anywhere. Its pricing is aimed at mining/construction/industry firms, so it is not a close fit for small buyers.

## Evidence
- Only one source found: an iProUP article on a Salta startup that digitises PPE delivery. Search summaries attribute to it: founder Marcelo Ifran Singh, based in General Guemes, Salta; targets SRT Resolution 299/11 documentation; features are employee management, unlimited PPE deliveries, automatic spreadsheets, electronic signature, QR validation, traceability, history. Sources: https://www.iproup.com/startups/71259-startup-saltena-digitaliza-entregas-de-elementos-de-seguridad-y-se-expande-por-el-mundo
- Traction claim: "over 200 deliveries digitised" (as summarised from that article). The article's date is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. A search for reviews and opinions returned nothing relevant (it was mistaken for ISDIN). No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit hits. This means little public footprint, not necessarily a good product.
- The same article's summary mentions voice and natural-language AI for creating PPE records (unverified beyond that summary).
- Only 3 searches were used. Product site, app-store listings and SRT Prevencion 4.0 integration were not located.

## Pricing
- SaaS monthly subscription, varying with organisation size and features. The article summary says plans start "from $200,000 monthly" (currency presumably ARS; exact figure and currency unverified). That is a high floor for a small employer or a freelance HyS technician, though it is not clearly a published price list.

## Fit gaps
- Positioned at mining, construction and industry firms, not micro/small firms or independent HyS professionals.
- No evidence found of: offline/mobile use, multi-entity support for HyS consultants serving many clients, or integration with SRT Prevencion 4.0 or insurer (ART) systems. All unverified.
- Scope appears limited to PPE delivery records (Res. 299/11), not the wider HyS paperwork.

## Opening
A low-priced, multi-client tool for small employers and HyS consultants, covering PPE plus broader HyS records and ART/SRT filing, is not served by this early-stage, enterprise-priced player.
