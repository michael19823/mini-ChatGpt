# Argentina: insurers (ART) tools

Killed idea: C0031, HyS / PPE digital records under SRT Prevención 4.0.

## Verdict: beatable (low confidence; research cut short by a search usage limit after 2 of 6 searches)

## Evidence
- SRT Resolution 48/2025 sets up the "Prevención 4.0" ecosystem. Employers, ARTs, ART-mutuales and self-insured employers can meet Occupational Risk System obligations with certified digital tools. Providers need certification, cybersecurity risk management and a named regulatory responsible party. Sources: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-419733/texto , https://www.argentina.gob.ar/prevencion-40-digesto
- The ecosystem lets ARTs register establishment visits through digital visit certificates. Source: search summary of the argentina.gob.ar / blogdelcontador pages above.
- SRT Resolution 299/11 lets employers record PPE and work clothing delivery receipts as digital records. Standards for digital tools were approved (a DISP SRT 1/2023 PDF was in the results, plus https://www.ignacioonline.com.ar/resolucion-299-11-srt-elementos-de-proteccion-personal-constancia/). Third-party providers are therefore explicitly allowed, and the ART is not the only channel.
- No evidence found that any specific ART offers a PPE digital-records product. Unverified: ARTs probably offer some portal or app, but I did not confirm any.
- Complaints: none found. The one search aimed at ART portal and app reviews failed (usage limit), so this is not a finding that none exist. App-store, Reddit and forum reviews were not checked.

## Pricing
Unverified. ART-provided prevention services are normally bundled into the insurance premium that employers already pay, so a standalone tool would compete with "free". No published prices were found.

## Fit gaps
- Unverified, but ART tools are likely tied to one insurer and not neutral. They likely cover the ART's own obligations (visits, reports) more than the employer's day-to-day PPE signatures.
- The regulation requires third-party tools to be certified. That is a barrier to entry, and it favours incumbents who certify first.

## Opening
Small, employer-side PPE-receipt and HyS record keeping that works with any ART, if the Prevención 4.0 certification cost is manageable. ART bundling makes pricing hard.
