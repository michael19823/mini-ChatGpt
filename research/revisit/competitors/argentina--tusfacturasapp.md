# TusFacturasAPP (Argentina)

Dropped idea: C0030 (obras sociales / prepagas billing and claim rejections)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

TusFacturasAPP is a general ARCA/AFIP electronic-invoicing tool and REST API. Nothing found shows obras sociales/prepagas billing, claim submission or rejection (debito) handling.

## Evidence
- Described as billing software and an AFIP/ARCA e-invoicing REST API by VOUSYS, an Argentine company, started in 2015. It issues invoices, credit/debit notes, receipts, payment orders, budgets and stock control. Sources: https://tusfacturas.app/api-factura-electronica-afip-en.html, https://www.capterra.com/p/213128/TusFacturas-APP/
- 4 searches (Spanish and English), including one pairing it with obras sociales/prepagas/clinics, found no health-payer billing features. Unverified beyond search snippets; site not fetched.
- Complaints: none found. Capterra shows only positive reviews from December 2020 (stability, support). No Reddit, forum or outage reports surfaced. The sample is small and old.
- Momentum: apparently still active (Capterra "2026" listing, current API page). No acquisition or shutdown found.

## Pricing
Not found in search results. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of payer-specific billing formats, padron/eligibility checks, authorizations, or debito/rejection management.
- Generic invoicing and API only; no clinic or provider claims workflow seen.

## Opening
Provider-to-payer claims and rejection handling is not addressed by TusFacturasAPP, so it does not block C0030 (other competitors such as Traditum are separate).
