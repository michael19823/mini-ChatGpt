# YoFacturo (Argentina)

Dropped idea: C0030 (obras sociales / prepagas billing and claim rejections)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

YoFacturo is a general ARCA/AFIP e-invoicing and small-business management tool. Nothing found shows any obras sociales/prepagas billing, claim submission or rejection handling.

## Evidence
- Described as a cloud platform for Argentine SMEs: AFIP/ARCA invoicing, point of sale, inventory, sales tracking, e-commerce integrations. Users: accountants, finance teams, SMEs. Source: Capterra/GetApp listings (https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1102700/YoFacturo).
- Own site targets freelancers and professionals issuing ARCA invoices: https://yo-facturo.com/facturacion-electronica/ and https://yo-facturo.com/freelancers/
- 3 searches (Spanish and English) found no mention of obras sociales, prepagas, healthcare, or claim rejections. The first search returned only generic regulatory pages. Unverified beyond what search snippets showed; site not fetched.
- Complaints: none found. The search results had no user ratings or review text, so no review content could be assessed.
- Momentum: active blog with 2026-dated content (e.g. "como-hacer-una-factura-modelo-ejemplo-paso-a-paso-2026"), so apparently maintained.

## Pricing (per search snippet of the vendor site, unverified)
- Punto de Venta: AR$ 20,000/month
- Basico: AR$ 50,000/month
- 1-month free trial, no card.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of payer-specific (obra social/prepaga) billing formats, padron checks, authorization or debit/rejection (debitos) management.
- Generic invoicing only; no clinic/provider claims workflow seen.

## Opening
Medical-provider billing to obras sociales/prepagas and rejection handling appears unserved by YoFacturo; it is not a blocker for C0030.
