# AlayaCare (Australia)

Idea it killed: C0045 (Support at Home claiming and evidence).

## Verdict: strong

AlayaCare was first to a live Support at Home claim, has government API certification, and says it serves about 30% of Australian home care. It is also still acquiring local rivals. The only gap found is its price and complexity for small providers.

## Evidence
- AlayaCare says it was the first technology provider to submit a successful invoice under Support at Home, with customer Resthaven, hours after the Services Australia APIs went live on 5 November. Source: https://alayacare.com/en-au/press-release/alayacare-first-to-submit-successful-claim-under-new-support-at-home-program/
- It received Notice of Intent certification from Services Australia for the Support at Home APIs. Sources: https://insideageing.com.au/alayacare-announces-support-at-home-api-certification/ and https://www.theweeklysource.com.au/alayacares-software-one-of-the-first-to-receive-approval-for-support-at-home-systems
- Momentum: it acquired Nightingale Software (ANZ aged care) and says it now serves around 30% of the Australian home care market. Source: https://www.theweeklysource.com.au/aged-care/alayacare-acquires-nightingale-software-to-deepen-its-aged-care-footprint-in-anz. A past layoff of about 14% of staff is reported (https://betakit.com/alayacare-lays-off-14-percent-of-employees-slows-ma-plans/). The year of that layoff was not verified, and no 2026 layoffs were found.
- Complaints (general, not Australia-specific): Capterra shows 4.1/5 from 188 reviews and G2 shows 4.0/5 from 75. Reported issues are weak reporting and data exploration, search limits (apostrophes and hyphens in names), slow pages on large datasets, and some downtime. Sources: https://www.capterra.com/p/147424/AlayaCare/reviews/ and https://www.g2.com/products/alayacare-alayacare/reviews. I saw only the search summaries, not the individual reviews. No complaints about Support at Home claiming specifically were found.
- Only 6 searches were allowed, and I did not search in a local language because Australia is English-speaking. App-store, Reddit and Facebook complaints were not checked.

## Pricing
- AlayaCare publishes no price and directs visitors to "Get a Quote".
- Third-party listings (via search summary, unverified) say plans start at about $1,000/month, with implementation of $5,000 to $25,000, data migration of $3,000 to $15,000 and integrations of $2,000 to $10,000. Sources: https://www.capterra.com/p/147424/AlayaCare/ and https://softwarefinder.com/emr-software/alayacare/pricing. These figures are unverified, and the currency is unclear.

## Fit gaps
- It is a full home care platform covering scheduling, clinical and billing. Small providers may find it more than they need, and the cost and training hard to justify (per third-party summary, unverified).
- No evidence-capture or audit-evidence gap was verified.

## Opening
A narrow, cheap claiming and evidence tool for small Support at Home providers that cannot afford or configure a full platform. It would be head-to-head with a certified incumbent and several other vendors, so the opening is unproven.
