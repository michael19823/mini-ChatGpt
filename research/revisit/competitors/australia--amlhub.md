# AMLHub (Australia / New Zealand) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0043 (AML/CTF Tranche 2 for accountants, real estate and lawyers)
Searches used: 4 of 6 (English only; the search results gave no sign that a local-language search would add anything, since Australia is English-speaking).

## Verdict: strong

It is an established NZ and AU vendor aimed at exactly the Tranche 2 buyers (law firms, accountants, real estate). It has low published prices, active Australian go-to-market and no complaints found.

## Evidence
- Vendor profile: end-to-end AML/CTF software, consulting, training and outsourcing for AU and NZ reporting entities. It says it launched in 2016, with 12+ years of AML consulting. It names law firms, accountants, financial services and real estate as targets. Source: https://www.ntaa.com.au/aml and the Capterra/GetApp listings, e.g. https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1023911/amlhub
- Claimed scale (vendor's own marketing, unverified): "10,000 users", 400,000+ individuals onboarded, 220,000 deals managed. Source: Capterra listing above.
- Features listed: face-to-face and remote CDD, deal and customer monitoring, staff assurance and training, suspicious activity records, report and document management, regulatory updates.
- Momentum (active): partnerships with RE/MAX (Tranche 2 training) and REIP for Australian real estate agencies, plus a video campaign with comedian Jimmy Rees. Source: https://www.realestatebusiness.com.au/industry/30916-remax-starts-aml-training-ahead-of-tranche-2-reforms and https://eliteagent.com/?p=279435. Tranche 2 obligations started 1 July 2026 (https://cgw.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/AML-CTF-Reforms-2026-Guide-for-Advisers-Cooper-Grace-Ward-3469-0389-9209-v1.pdf), so it is positioned for the launch window. No acquisition or shutdown news found.
- Complaints: none found. Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice listings showed no user reviews ("rated by 0 users"). No Reddit, forum, app-store or news complaints surfaced. This is an absence of data, not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing
- Published "from" prices: Standard $100/month (single-user office), Premium $150/month (up to 5 admin users), Enterprise $250/month (tailored). Source: Capterra/Software Advice listings. Currency is unverified (one summary said NZ$) and the vendor says additional charges may apply.
- Cheap for small firms, so it is not overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps
- None verified. Could not check the product itself (WebFetch is blocked).
- Unverified possibilities to test later: depth of AUSTRAC-specific reporting (e.g. SMR/TTR/IFTI filing formats), whether it is NZ-first in design, and integrations with Australian practice-management tools (accounting, legal, property).

## Opening
Little room in a generic Tranche 2 compliance platform. Any opening is a narrow niche (e.g. a specific profession's workflow or integrations, or AUSTRAC filing automation) that AMLHub's AU/NZ-first product doesn't cover, and that needs product-level checks to confirm.
