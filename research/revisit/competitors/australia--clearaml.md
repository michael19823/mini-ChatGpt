# ClearAML (Australia)

Idea it killed: C0043, AML/CTF Tranche 2 for accountants, real estate and lawyers.

## Verdict: strong

It is a purpose-built, live, cheap SaaS product aimed at exactly the dropped idea's buyers. No complaints were found, but there are also no reviews at all.

## Evidence
- Capterra describes it as Australia's purpose-built AML/CTF platform for Tranche 2 entities (accountants, lawyers, conveyancers, real estate agents), cloud SaaS with email, phone and knowledge-base support. https://www.capterra.com/p/10045616/ClearAML/
- It lists 0 user reviews on Capterra, so no verified user feedback exists. https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1100946/ClearAML
- Tranche 2 took effect 1 July 2026. Accountants had to enrol with AUSTRAC by 29 July 2026, and AUSTRAC began issuing s167 notices on 28 Aug 2026, so demand is live. https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/08/austrac-takes-compliance-action-in-relation-to-tra
- Other competitors exist: easyAML, from $179/month +GST for 5 users (https://easyaml.com); PEXA Clear, launched for property (https://cfotech.com.au/story/pexa-clear-launches-aml-compliance-tool-for-property); NameScan; Alpma.
- Complaints: none found. Searches covered Capterra and the general web. A Reddit and forum search hit the usage limit and was not retried. Local-language search is not applicable (English-language market). Absence of complaints is not evidence of quality, because the product has few or no reviews.

## Pricing
- Per Capterra search snippets: Basic A$149/month, Professional A$299/month (up to 25 users, unlimited client risk assessments), plus about A$5 per KYC check (DVS and biometric). Free trial. Exact tier details are unverified beyond these snippets.
- This is already priced for small firms, so it is not overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. No evidence of gaps in language, filing format, or multi-entity support. Whether it covers AUSTRAC reporting such as SMRs, and how deep its programme templates go, is unverified.

## Momentum
- Active and recently launched or marketed around the 1 July 2026 start date. The company's founding date and ownership are unverified.

## Opening
- Little. The only angle is a niche vertical (e.g. a single profession) or better integration with practice-management software, and the crowd of competitors with low prices makes this weak.
