# Australia: Domestic Payroll (household employer payroll) - competitor check

Idea killed: C1175 Household employers.
Searches: only 2 of 6 returned results (usage limit hit). The first English query failed, and no local-language search was run. Evidence is thin.

## Verdict: beatable (low confidence)

"Domestic Payroll" is a category with no single named product. Niche services exist (NannyPay, Care.com guidance), alongside generic small-business payroll outsourcing.

## Evidence
- NannyPay (nannypay.com.au) is an Australian service for families employing nannies. It handles tax, super, payslips and leave. Source: https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/nannypay.com.au and https://www.getapp.com.au/software/2077602/nannypay
- The search summary says most reviews of NannyPay are positive. Complaints it listed: a slow, unclear onboarding; having to double-check work, with figures and names referenced wrongly in emails; and no support for nanny-share families. The nanny-share complaint mentions HMRC, which is a UK body. It may come from the UK NannyPaye listing rather than the Australian one, so treat it as unverified. I did not open the pages and have no direct quotes.
- Care.com Australia publishes an employer-obligations guide for nanny and caregiver hiring. That is content, not payroll software. Source: https://www.care.com/c/en-au/hiring-a-nanny-or-caregiver-are-you-meeting-your-obligations-as-an-employer/
- Super rule: for domestic workers, super is compulsory only above 30 hours a week. This is a household-specific rule that generic payroll tools may not handle. Source: search summary of the sprintlaw/sleek results (unverified detail).
- No app-store, Reddit, Facebook or news complaints were found. That reflects the searches I ran, not proof that none exist.

## Pricing
- NannyPay price: not found (unverified).
- Generic outsourced payroll for comparison: Lawpath $50 per employee per pay run with a 2-employee minimum (https://lawpath.com.au/payroll-services). Sleek from A$25 per payslip (https://sleek.com/au/?p=78256). Digit from $12.50 per person per pay run (https://digit.business/outsourced-payroll-services-australia).
- These per-pay-run prices, and Lawpath's 2-employee minimum, are poorly suited to a household with one nanny or cleaner.

## Fit gaps
- Generic payroll targets businesses and has minimums or per-run fees that are expensive for one employee.
- Nanny-share, multi-family employment looks poorly served (unverified).
- Household-specific rules, such as the 30-hour super threshold, are not obviously handled by generic tools (unverified).

## Opening
A cheap, simple per-household payroll and super tool covering one or two domestic workers, including nanny-share, could fit. The competitors are NannyPay and generic payroll, and incumbents' pricing and quality are only partly verified.
