# EasyAML (Australia) - competitor to C0043 (AML/CTF Tranche 2 for accountants, real estate, lawyers)

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- easyAML is built for exactly this buyer: SME lawyers, accountants, conveyancers, real estate agents, DPMS and TCSPs, ahead of the 1 July 2026 reforms. Covers AML program, risk assessment, KYC/KYB, monitoring, AUSTRAC reporting, 7-year record retention and training. Source: https://easyaml.com/platform/features/ and https://easyaml.com
- Backed by Scantek (ISO 27001, hosted in Australia, claims 15+ years in ID/compliance tech and 1,000+ Australian businesses; company claims, unverified). Source: https://easyaml.com/about-easyaml/
- Has a mobile app for face-to-face KYC (App Store listing): https://apps.apple.com/md/app/easyaml/id6752552128
- Endorsed or listed by professional bodies: https://www.alpma.com.au/partners/easyaml/ and https://www.qlsproctor.com.au/?p=25920 (only seen as search results; nature of the partnership unverified).
- Offered the full platform free until the Tranche 2 deadline, with free risk assessments and training (vendor claim, via search summary of easyaml.com).
- Complaints: none found. Searches for reviews, Reddit and complaints returned only vendor and partner pages. No independent reviews seen (Play, App Store ratings, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot not surfaced). This is absence of evidence, not a clean record.
- Momentum: active and recent (app listing, blog content, partner pages, Tranche 2 go-live passed 1 July 2026). Not stagnant.
- Local-language search was not needed (Australia is English); no extra searches used.

## Pricing
- Search summary of https://easyaml.com/pricing: Starter $179/month, Professional $449/month, Enterprise $999/month, all plus GST. Plan contents not verified.
- For comparison, easyAML cites the AGD impact analysis estimates: under $200k turnover about $4,040 upfront and $6,020 a year; over $200k about $28,650 upfront and $33,230 a year (via search summary of its FAQ, unverified).
- $179/month is about $2,150 a year, so not obviously overpriced for small firms, though very small sole practitioners may still find it heavy.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed from evidence. Possible gaps (all unverified): depth for non-trivial structures, integrations with practice-management systems (Xero, LEAP, Actionstep, property CRMs), per-sector workflow depth, price for sole traders.

## Opening
Little visible opening for a generic Tranche 2 tool; any entry would need a narrow niche (e.g. sole practitioners at under $179/month, or integration with a specific practice-management system) and first-hand checking of reviews.
