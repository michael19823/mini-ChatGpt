# Flagship AML (Australia)

Idea killed: C0043, AML/CTF Tranche 2 for accountants, real estate and lawyers.

## Verdict: strong (on price and target segment)

It targets the same buyers as the dropped idea at a small-firm price. The searches found no complaints, so the product's quality is unknown.

## Evidence
- Capterra lists it as AML/CTF compliance software for Australian Tranche 2 designated service providers. It targets SME professional firms: law firms, accountants, conveyancers and real estate agents. Sizes listed: self-employed, 2-10 and 11-50 employees. [Capterra AU](https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1097498/Flagship-AML)
- Features listed: client onboarding, beneficial ownership tracing, KYC with identity verification, sanctions and PEP screening, enhanced due diligence escalation, ongoing monitoring and review reminders, alerts for AUSTRAC reporting, training modules, a Tranche 2 dashboard, audit trails and cloud hosting. [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10042681/Flagship-AML/)
- Complaints: none found. Four searches (3 English, 1 comparison) returned only Capterra listing pages. No user reviews, Reddit threads, forum posts or news items about the product appeared. I did not search in a local language other than English, since Australian English is the local language. Review content on the Capterra pages was not visible in the results (unverified).
- Momentum: Capterra lists it with 2026 pricing, and Tranche 2 obligations start 1 July 2026 (per the search summaries). It looks active, but I found no company site, funding or launch news (unverified).
- Competing products in the same results: AML Simple (from A$79/month, real estate focus), ClearAML (from A$149/month plus A$5 per ID check), easyAML, overseer AML, and consultancy and form-software guides (CCP, Formslive, Scantek). The market is crowded and cheap.

## Pricing
- Flat A$66.70 per month (Capterra).
- Annual plan A$800 per year, including a tailored business risk assessment report (Capterra).
- No per-check identity verification fees were found. Whether any apply is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. The searches found no evidence of missing features.
- Unverified possibilities: depth of the AUSTRAC reporting workflow (it only "alerts" users per the listing), integrations with practice management software, multi-entity or multi-office support, and whether identity verification is included.

## Opening
Little opening on price or segment. Any gap would have to come from a vertical workflow (for example, practice-management integration for lawyers or accountants) that this generalist tool does not cover, and that needs checking with real users.
