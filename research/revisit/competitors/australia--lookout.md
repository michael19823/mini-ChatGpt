# Lookout (The Lookout Way), Australia

Idea killed: C0045 Support at Home claiming and evidence

## Verdict
strong

Lookout is an active, Australian-built, end-to-end home care platform that claims it is live on Support at Home and integrated with Services Australia APIs. Claiming is built in.

## Evidence
- Vendor and trade-press claims (unverified independently): Lookout says it is live with Support at Home and has integrated with Services Australia APIs. Source: https://insideageing.com.au/lookout-launches-ai-powered-platform-overhaul-aimed-at-transforming-home-care-delivery/ and search snippets of thelookoutway.com.
- Scope: scheduling, rostering, claims and statements, compliance, reporting, client/worker apps, remote monitoring. Source: search snippets (thelookoutway.com, softwareadvice.com.au).
- Momentum: launched 2020 (tech from Five Good Friends); reported AI overhaul, "Lookout Assist", available from 1 June 2026; claims over 1,000,000 hours of support delivered (vendor claim, unverified). Active development.
- Reviews: Software Advice / Capterra AU listing shows 4.7/5 from 38 users (ease of use 4.6, support 4.7, value 4.4, features 4.6), per search summary. https://www.softwareadvice.com.au/software/370276/the-lookout-way
- Complaints: only one found, a reviewer saying "It has promise, but is not NDIS ready" (NDIS, not aged care; summarised from search result, full review not read). No complaints about Support at Home claiming found. Only 2 of 6 searches ran; the tool then hit a usage limit, so Reddit, Facebook and app-store checks were NOT done.

## Pricing
Custom/quote-only; no published price or minimum found. Cannot judge whether it is overpriced for small providers (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Weak NDIS support per one review.
- It is a full platform, so a provider wanting only a lightweight claiming/evidence tool must adopt the whole system (inference).
- Self-managed participants are not an evident target (unverified).

## Opening
Little: only a small provider or self-managed participant wanting a cheap standalone claiming/evidence tool, and that gap is unconfirmed.
