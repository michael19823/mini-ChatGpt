# Pay The Nanny (Australia) - competitor check for C1175 (Household employers)

## Verdict: beatable

Small, human-assisted payroll service (a 3-person team per its vendor case study) built on Employment Hero. It is not a self-serve product, and it does not cover wider household-employer needs. (3 WebSearch calls used; no URL was known beforehand.)

## Evidence
- Founded 2019 by Mark Hudson, an accountant, after a client asked how to pay their nanny. It covers Australia and NZ and runs "thousands of payrolls a year". Team of three. Source: https://employmenthero.com/case-studies/pay-the-nanny/
- Handles PAYG, leave and super for families employing nannies. It runs on Employment Hero, with a claimed 50% cut in processing time. It is a service backed by off-the-shelf payroll software, not its own product. Same source.
- Complaints: none found for Pay The Nanny in the searches run. No app-store, Reddit or forum complaints surfaced. Not exhaustive: only 3 searches were run, and no local-language search was needed or run (Australia is English-speaking).
- Possibly related, unverified: a separate "NannyPay" (nannypay.com.au) shows 3.5/5 from 6 users on Capterra AU (https://www.capterra.com.au/software/31890/nannypay) and positive Trustpilot reviews (https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/nannypay.com.au). I could not confirm this is the same company as Pay The Nanny, so treat it as a different or unverified competitor.
- Other nanny payroll options exist: ReadyTech markets nanny payroll software in Australia (https://readytech.io/what-we-do/workforce/markets/nanny-payroll-systems-software-australia). Care.com AU publishes employer-obligation guidance (https://www.care.com/c/en-au/hiring-a-nanny-or-caregiver-are-you-meeting-your-obligations-as-an-employer/).
- Momentum: apparently active, since the case study is recent and it expanded to NZ. Exact date unverified.

## Pricing
Not found. No published price located for Pay The Nanny. Generic outsourced payroll in Australia is cited at roughly $5-$20 per employee per pay run (source: https://digit.business/outsourced-payroll-services-australia, search summary only). That is not Pay The Nanny's price.

## Fit gaps
- Service model with a 3-person team: likely limited scale, and likely limited self-serve or app experience (inferred, unverified).
- Appears nanny-focused. Other household employees (carers, cleaners, gardeners, aged-care or NDIS helpers) and tax or compliance tooling beyond payroll are not shown (unverified).
- Relies on third-party payroll software (Employment Hero), so it has limited control of the product.

## Opening
A self-serve, low-cost household-employer tool (payroll plus compliance for all domestic workers, with the domestic super rule of more than 30 hours per week) could undercut a manual 3-person service. Pay The Nanny has no evident dissatisfaction to exploit, so the case rests on scale and price, not on complaints.
