# ShiftCare (Australia) - competitor check for C0045 (Support at Home claiming and evidence)

## Verdict: strong

ShiftCare already ships Support at Home (SaH) rostering, claiming and co-contribution invoicing, with a dedicated product page and help article. It is well reviewed and actively developed.

## Evidence
- Dedicated SaH product pages: https://shiftcare.com/support-at-home ("Manage Support at Home From Rostering to Claiming") and https://shiftcare.com/go/support-at-home.
- Help article on SaH invoicing: https://help.shiftcare.com/en/articles/13134964-support-at-home-sah-invoicing. Per search snippet: co-contribution invoicing for Independence and Everyday Living items is only possible once Services Australia marks the claim 'Paid'. Co-contribution rates are set on the client's SaH tab.
- Per the same results, ShiftCare can connect to quickclaim to automate subsidy claiming and co-contribution invoicing with Services Australia. So direct claiming is via a third-party integration (details unverified).
- Capterra: about 4.6 from 145 reviews (per search summary), https://www.capterra.com/p/233684/ShiftCare/reviews/. Cross-platform average about 4.7 (per search summary; unverified).
- Complaints seen in review summaries (Capterra; I did not read individual reviews):
  - Xero integration formatting errors and no automatic sync of staff details.
  - Billing feature described as poor.
  - Small mobile app bugs; shifts sometimes not showing after restarts.
  - Awkward date picker; shift times limited to 15 or 30 minute increments.
  - Not recommended for larger businesses.
- No Reddit or Software Advice complaints surfaced. I found no outage or failed-filing news. I did not run local-language searches because the market is English-speaking.

## Pricing
Australia (shiftcare.com/pricing, per search snippet): Essentials $9, Growth $15, Intelligence $25 per user per month. 5-staff minimum; up to 20% off for annual billing. It is priced per staff member, not per client. Whether the SaH features need a higher tier is unverified. Pricing is affordable for small providers.

## Fit gaps
- SaH claiming appears to depend on a quickclaim integration rather than being native (unverified).
- "Evidence" (audit-ready proof of service delivery) was not seen as a specific feature (unverified).
- Reviewers call billing weak and say it suits small teams rather than larger ones.
- Xero sync issues.

## Momentum
Active. It has current SaH content, a 2026-dated Capterra listing, and multi-country pricing pages (AU, US, CA).

## Opening
Little room for a generic SaH claiming tool. The only opening is a narrow one: deeper claim reconciliation and evidence for larger or multi-entity providers, or better billing and Xero accuracy.
