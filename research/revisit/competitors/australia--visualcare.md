# Visualcare (Australia, commercial)

Idea killed: C0045 Support at Home claiming and evidence

## Verdict
strong. Visualcare already sells Support at Home rostering, evidence capture and claiming in one product, including a direct Services Australia API export. No complaints were found to counter this (see below for search coverage).

## Evidence
- Visualcare has a dedicated Support at Home (SAH) page and a resource article on SAH rostering, reporting and claims. Sources: https://visualcare.com.au/support-at-home and https://visualcare.com.au/resources/how-to-make-support-at-home-rostering-reporting-and-claims-easier (vendor claims, taken from search summaries, not independently verified).
- Per the search summary of those pages: SAH scheduling, documentation and claiming are in one workflow; workers capture notes, time tracking, sign in/out, photos and incidents in the Worker app, linked to the rostered service as an "audit-ready record"; SAH claims export direct to Services Australia via an API; finance can see upcoming, submitted and reconciled claims.
- It is an Aged Care Online Web Services Integration Partner. Source: https://visualcare.com.au/resources/visualcare-achieves-aged-care-online-web-services-integration-partner-status
- Other SAH-ready rivals appear in the same results: CareVision and ShiftCare (https://shiftcare.com/go/support-at-home), so the space is crowded.
- Capterra: the Visualcare listing (https://www.capterra.com/p/238548/Visualcare/) shows 0 user reviews. An older "visual care" listing (https://www.capterra.com.au/software/204981/visual-care) has 5.0/5 from 3 users, mostly praise, with one reviewer saying it "hangs and crashes very frequently". It is unverified that this is the same product.
- Complaints: none found. Reddit, Google Play, App Store, Facebook and outage-news searches did not return anything, or failed with a usage-limit error. This is not evidence that none exist. A news search for acquisition or funding returned only unrelated "vision care" results.

## Pricing
Not published in anything found; Capterra/GetApp listings show no usable price (appears quote-based). unverified. The listings say it targets teams from 2-10 up to 10,000+ employees.

## Fit gaps
None confirmed. It covers rostering, evidence and claiming for aged care and NDIS. A gap for a small provider (price, minimums, complexity of a full suite versus a claiming-only tool) is unverified.

## Momentum
Active: it has shipped SAH features since the November 2025 start of the program and holds an integration partner status. No acquisition or shutdown news found.

## Opening
Only a lightweight claiming and evidence tool for small providers who do not want a full rostering suite, and that depends on Visualcare's price, which is unverified.
