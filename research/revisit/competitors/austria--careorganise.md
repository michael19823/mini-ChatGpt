# CareOrganise (Austria) - competitor check for C0055 (24-hour care agency admin)

## Verdict: weak (low confidence)

Confidence is low. Only marketing material turned up, and no current product site was found (5 WebSearch calls used).

## Evidence
- The vendor describes it as cloud software for 24-hour care agencies. It covers admin from contact management to billing and is DSGVO and OeQZ compliant. Source: [CareOrganise PostMailing 02/2020 (PDF)](https://i27ez-2iaaa-aaaas-ajqeq-cai.icp0.io/files/uploads/downloads/CareOrganise_PostMailing_022020-web.pdf)
- The vendor material says customers have passed OeQZ certification using its standardised processes. This is a vendor claim and unverified independently. Same PDF.
- Search summaries credit it with 2nd place at the EuroCloud Awards 2017 (Europe). I did not open the page, so this is unverified. A [futurezone.at article](https://futurezone.at/amp/science/digitaler-helfer-soll-24-stunden-betreuung-verbessern/400726563) appeared in results, but I did not confirm it covers CareOrganise.
- Complaints: none found. There were no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews in German or English. This is absence of evidence, not evidence of satisfaction.
- Momentum: the newest dated source is a Feb 2020 mailing. The site careorganise.at did not come up in search. Activity after 2020 is unverified, and it may be stagnant, small or dormant.
- Searches for competing Austrian agency software surfaced no direct peers. Results were mostly unrelated agency tools.

## Pricing
Not published in anything found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- It is aimed at the Austrian OeQZ and DSGVO context, so it is probably weak for other DACH markets or for the Romanian, Slovak or other source-country side of agencies.
- No evidence was found of a mobile app, caregiver-facing portal, or multi-language support.
- No public reviews, pricing or self-serve signup were found, which suggests a sales-led product for established agencies.

## Opening
A small Austrian agency would likely find a cheap, self-serve, mobile-first tool with transparent pricing and caregiver-side features more reachable than a low-visibility, apparently 2020-era product. Verify first that CareOrganise is not still active under another site or name.
