# enox share (Austria) - competitor check for C0066 (EEG billing)

## Verdict: strong

Backed by Salzburg AG, live since 2022, with a real user base in the exact niche. Four searches were used (of 6); no complaints surfaced.

## Evidence
- enox.share is described as the established software for all Austrian energy-community types (joint generation, EEG, citizen communities). It covers member management, energy allocation, optimization and billing in one responsive web app. Source: https://a.storyblok.com/f/139490/x/dcef7c1e6f/produktinfo-enox_share-dezember-2025.pdf (search snippet; PDF not opened)
- Reported scale: 150+ active communities and 2,600+ metering points since the 2022 launch (same source, December 2025 product sheet; self-reported).
- It is the digital innovation brand of Salzburg AG (same source), so it has utility backing.
- Momentum: the product sheet is dated December 2025, which suggests active maintenance. No acquisition, shutdown or outage news found.
- Other Austrian players surfaced in results (not checked in depth): Energyfamily GmbH (online tool for founding, administration and billing), and a free LEADER-funded software from an association in Grieskirchen (https://www.tips.at/nachrichten/grieskirchen/wirtschaft-politik/579207-verein-entwickelt-mit-unterstuetzung-von-leader-eine-kostenlose-software-fuer-energiegemeinschaften). Free competition pressures pricing.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of enox share appeared in English or German searches. This is not proof of satisfaction; the product is a B2B web app with little public review surface.
- Generic pain point (not about enox): general sources say community admin and billing of exchanged energy is among the biggest EEG challenges (Klimafonds workshop doc, https://www.klimafonds.gv.at/wp-content/uploads/sites/16/Zentrale-Diskussionspunkte-aus-den-Workshop.pdf; snippet only).

## Pricing
- Search summary says billing is a flat fee per active metering point, independent of energy volume and community size. The exact price was not found; unverified. A pricing sheet may be in the product sheet PDF, but it was not opened (WebFetch blocked).

## Fit gaps
- None verified. Possible gaps, all unverified: Austria-only scope; per-metering-point fee may be poor value for very small communities; dependence on a utility brand may deter communities that want vendor neutrality.

## Opening
Little visible; only a vendor-neutral or cheaper/free niche tool for tiny communities, or a non-Austrian market, looks open, and the free Grieskirchen tool already contests the low end.
