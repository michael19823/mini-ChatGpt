# Austria: farm-record apps (Farmdok and similar)

Idea killed: C0068, electronic pesticide records from 1 Jan 2027.
Searches used: 3 of 6 (German and English wording). Checked 2026-10-06.

## Verdict: strong

Established Austrian players already cover electronic field records, Farmdok in particular. The 2027 obligation is demand for them, not a gap.

## Evidence
- Search results (a Saxony PDF and a summary) say records must be electronic and machine-readable from 1 Jan 2027, and that "professional farm management systems or electronic Ackerschlagkarteien" can be used. The Saxony sources are German; the Austrian rule itself is unverified by me. Source: https://landwirtschaft.sachsen.de/download/20251219_Fachbeitrag_PSM_Aufzeichnungspflicht_elektronisch.pdf
- Farmdok: Austrian app with over 30,000 users in Austria, Germany and Slovakia, automatic GPS field-work recording, expanding to other European countries. Source: https://futurezone.at/start-ups/oesterreichische-agrar-app-farmdok-expandiert-nach-europa/400086668
- Farmdok is listed by the Lower Austria Chamber of Agriculture: https://noe.lko.at/farmdok-aufzeichnungen-feldarbeiten-landwirtschaftskammer-niederösterreich+2400+3262954
- Farmdok was named "App des Jahres 2018": https://futurezone.at/amp/apps/farmdok-ist-app-des-jahres-2018/400323690
- Other apps seen in results (not examined): Farmable (fruit growers), Ackerchef.
- Momentum: actively funded and expanding (futurezone article; date of the funding unverified).

## Complaints
None found. The searches returned no app-store reviews, Capterra review text, forum or Reddit complaints. I did not search specifically for them, so this is "none found", not "none exist".

## Pricing
- Capterra lists a starting price of EUR 66.34, usage-based, with a free version and free trial. Period (month or year) unverified.
- A search summary says app and recording are free and data processing is chargeable. Unverified.
- Source: https://www.capterra.ie/software/203519/farmdok
- Farmdok targets small and medium Austrian farms, so it is not overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps
None verified. Nothing found showing a missing filing format, language or offline mode. Possible gaps (unverified): specialty crops such as fruit, vine and vegetables, and non-arable users.

## Opening
Little room for a generic pesticide-record app. Any opening would be a niche (specialty crops, contractors) or a free official tool, and no free Austrian tool was found in these searches.
