# Austria: GRC tools (NISG 2026 / NIS2), competitor to idea C0067

Searches used: 4 of 6 (German and English). Verdict is based on search snippets only; no vendor site was read.

## Verdict: beatable

The market is crowded with generic German or EU NIS2/ISMS/GRC platforms, but they are priced for mid-market firms. The only Austria-specific tool found is new, with no reviews. A cheap, guided, Austria-specific tool for small affected firms still looks open.

## Evidence
- NISG 2026 went into parliamentary proceedings after a Ministerrat circulation resolution on 20 Nov 2025. Sources say mandatory measures apply from 1 Oct 2026, with fines up to EUR 15m or 2.5% of turnover. The scope is stated as 50 or more employees or turnover above EUR 10m in critical sectors. Sources: https://www.proliance.ai/en/blog/nisg-2026 , https://www.boerse-express.com/news/articles/nis2-compliance-ab-oktober-drohen-bussgelder-bis-15-millionen-euro-922634 , https://kpmg.com/at/de/media/press-releases/2025/nis-2-gesetzesentwurf-stellt-weichen-fuer-hohes-cybersicherheitsniveau-in-oesterreich.html
- "NISG Plattform" is listed on Capterra as an Austria-specific NIS2 tool. Its modules cover a sector and size classification questionnaire, registration data, control self-assessment, incident reporting and an audit log, with EU hosting. Capterra shows 0 user reviews. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/10047203/NISG-Plattform/
- Proliance (proliance.ai) has an Austria page and ISMS product aimed at SMEs. Orbiq, SECJUR, Kertos and Legiscope publish NIS2/GRC comparison guides aimed mainly at Germany. Their Austria coverage is unverified. Sources: https://www.proliance.ai/en/nis2-austria , https://www.secjur.com/blog/nis2-software , https://www.kertos.io/en/compliance-hub/nis2-software-comparison
- An academic source (FH St. Pölten) describes an ISMS framework built for Austrian SMEs. It notes that the Austrian law names 13 topic areas but gives no concrete requirements, leaving SMEs unsure how to implement them. This is a gap indicator, not a complaint about a product. Source: https://phaidra.fhstp.ac.at/api/object/o:7205/get
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum complaints turned up in German or English. The one Capterra listing has 0 reviews. This is a valid "no complaints found" result and is not evidence that users are happy.
- Momentum: the NISG Plattform listing looks newly added, so it is likely a new entrant (unverified). The generic vendors are actively publishing 2026 content, so the category is active.

## Pricing
- NISG Plattform: from USD 2,500 per year flat, with a free trial and a free version noted on Capterra. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/10047203/NISG-Plattform/
- General market, from a search summary (unverified, no primary source checked): about EUR 5,000-40,000 per year for SME-focused platforms and EUR 50,000-200,000+ per year for enterprise GRC suites.
- Most vendors quote only on request (per the search summary, unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Generic GRC and ISMS suites are built for Germany, DORA or ISO 27001 and are heavy for firms with no security team. This is inferred from the comparison guides' framing, not verified per product.
- The Austrian law gives no concrete requirements, so a tool that translates the 13 topic areas into step-by-step tasks for small firms is a gap (per the FH St. Pölten source).
- Austria-specific registration, with the regulator's process and German-language Austrian guidance, is covered by only one tool found. That tool is unreviewed.
- Pricing of EUR 5,000+ per year is likely steep for firms near the 50-employee threshold (inferred).

## Opening
A low-priced, Austria-specific NISG 2026 guided-compliance tool for small affected firms and their consultants, with the 13 topic areas turned into concrete tasks and evidence templates, is open. The new NISG Plattform is the one to watch.
