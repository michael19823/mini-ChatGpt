# HerzensManagement (Austria)

Killed idea: C0055, 24-hour care agency admin.

## Verdict: strong (provisional, thin evidence)

It is a purpose-built admin suite for Austrian 24-hour care agencies, made by a funded-looking Graz startup with a second product (a comparison portal) feeding it. It is not a weak or generic tool. Only 4 of 6 searches were used and the results were thin.

## Evidence
- HerzensApp GmbH is an Austrian company (Graz-based social startup per press) building a digital ecosystem for agencies, carers, families and care recipients. Its agency product, HerzensManagement, covers client and carer administration, automated rota planning (Turnusplanung), invoicing, digital documents with e-signature and AI-assisted search. Source: search summaries of https://at.trustpilot.com/review/herzens.app and https://www.trendingtopics.eu/herzensportal-oesterreich-bekommt-erstes-vergleichsportal-fuer-pflegeagenturen/
- Co-founders named in press: Oliver Wimmer and Konstantin Pollanz. It launched HerzensPortal (2024), which compares 400+ Austrian care agencies. Source: trendingtopics.eu article above. This gives it a distribution channel into agencies.
- Trustpilot shows a TrustScore of 4.0/5 for HerzensApp GmbH (https://at.trustpilot.com/review/herzens.app). Review count and content were not visible, so I do not know whether the rating covers the agency software, the portal or the app. Unverified.
- Complaints: none found. I did not find app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints about outages, bugs or pricing. This reflects limited search depth and a small user base, not proof of satisfaction.
- Momentum: appears active (AI search feature, portal launch in 2024). Exact release dates and funding were not found. Unverified.

## Pricing

## Fit gaps
No gaps confirmed. Possible but unverified areas to check: pricing for very small agencies and sole traders, Slovak/Romanian-language carer interfaces, links to Austrian Personenbetreuung billing and subsidy rules, and the Trustpilot review text itself.

## Opening
