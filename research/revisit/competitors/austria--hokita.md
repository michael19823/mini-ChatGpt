# HOKITA (Austria) - competitor check for C0056 (private kindergarten subsidy reporting)

## Verdict: weak (provisional, thin evidence)

HOKITA shows up as a Kita management system with a parent portal, but every hit placed it in Germany, not Austria. No evidence was found that it handles Austrian subsidy reporting. The searches stopped at 4 of 6 because of a usage limit.

## Evidence
- A parent-portal PDF from a German municipality (Buettelborn, dated 14.01.2022) describes HOKITA as the Kita management system used in the daycare. It covers messages and facility information to parents, and parents entering absences. It is described as GDPR-compliant. Source: https://www.buettelborn.de/dokumente/kitas/informationen-aus-den-kitas/hokita-eltern-portal-14.01.2022.pdf?cid=dfi
- Searches in German and English found no Austrian customers, no Austrian subsidy (Foerderabrechnung) feature, and no Austrian vendor page. Austria-focused results were other products (OKIDS, SOKRATES KiB), not HOKITA.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, forum, Reddit or news complaints surfaced. This reflects the thin search, not proof of satisfaction.
- Momentum: unverified. The only dated source is from 2022.

## Pricing
Unverified. No published price found. The pricing search was blocked by the usage limit.

## Fit gaps
- Appears to be a German-market product. Austrian Laender subsidy formats (for example Wien MA10) are not mentioned anywhere in what was found (unverified).
- The visible features are parent communication and absence reporting, not subsidy billing or reporting.

## Opening
HOKITA looks like a German parent-portal and admin tool with no visible Austrian subsidy reporting, so it is not what blocks C0056. The Austrian blockers are the other competitors (OKIDS and others).
