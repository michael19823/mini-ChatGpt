# neoom KLUUB (Austria) - competitor check for C0066 Energy-community (EEG) billing

## Verdict: strong

neoom KLUUB is a full-stack, actively marketed EEG/BEG service (matchmaking, founding, registration, billing) bundled in the neoom app. It is aimed at community members and operators. A standalone billing tool would be competing with a bundled, end-to-end service.

## Evidence
- Product page describes KLUUB as a feature of the neoom APP: community matchmaking, founding or joining a community, consumption and income overview, billing and invoicing. https://neoom.com/en/produkte/app/kluub
- neoom's own claim (unverified marketing figure): 1000+ locations (households, municipalities, SMEs) in 80 energy communities, 800,000+ kWh traded. Same page as above.
- Search snippets say neoom handles the legally required steps (authority registration), quarterly billing, and member self-signup via link or QR code. Source: KLUUB flyers, https://neoom.com/hubfs/Produkt_Downloads/KLUUB/neoom_KLUUB_Flyer_2503-DE_web.pdf and https://neoom.com/hubfs/Partner_Downloads_KLUUB/KLUUB_KLUUB%2B_Flyer.pdf (only seen as search-result summaries, not read in full).
- Active: a 2025 flyer and a municipality info-event blog post exist (https://neoom.com/en/blog/infoveranstaltung-energiegemeinschaften-für-gemeinden). There is a neoom community discussion thread at https://www.energiesparhaus.at/forum-neoom-energiegemeinschaft-wer-ist-schon-dabei/75958 (contents not read).

## Complaints

App Store reviews of the neoom app (https://apps.apple.com/at/app/neoom-app/id6443656138) and the forum thread were not read. Absence of complaints is not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing (from search summary of the flyer, unverified in full)
- Energy price traded among members: about 10 ct/kWh (regionally dependent).
- KLUUB service fee on consumed and supplied energy, tiered: up to 500 kWh 2.4 ct/kWh; 501-1500 kWh 1.8 ct/kWh; from 1501 kWh 1.2 ct/kWh.
- Operating cost per location (max 3 metering points), decreasing as regional member count grows (amount not found).
- Deposit: 50 EUR per consumption metering point.
- Per-kWh fees on both sides look heavy for small households. Whether this is overpriced for the segment is unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred, mostly unverified)
- Tied to the neoom app/ecosystem; not found whether it works for communities that want their own branding or an independent operator.
- Fee is per-kWh to neoom, so a community that wants to keep the margin, or a small volunteer-run EEG, may prefer a cheaper flat-fee tool.
- No evidence found about support for other billing needs (custom tariffs, multi-community operators); not verified.

## Opening
Possibly an independent, flat-fee or white-label billing tool for small community operators who dislike per-kWh fees to a platform; this is unproven, and neoom is well established.
