# VDKF-LEC (Austria) - dropped idea C0063: F-gas logbooks

## Verdict: strong

VDKF-LEC is a real, established F-gas logbook product (since 2004, still developed), but it comes from the German trade association VDKF. Its Austria-specific fit is unverified. Searches found no Austrian version, no Austrian users and no complaints.

## Evidence
- VDKF-LEC (Leakage & Energy Control) is the industry software of the Verband Deutscher Kaelte-Klima-Fachbetriebe (VDKF). It covers system logbooks, tightness tests, refrigerant inventories and specialist-company certificates. Sources: https://www.baulinks.de/webplugin/2025/0975.php4 ; https://www.baulinks.de/webplugin/2026/1116.php4
- Scale: about 67,000 system operators and about 307,000 systems recorded (2026 release). An earlier 2025 release says over 1,100 specialist companies and operators use it in Germany and the EU, with about 65,000 operators and 293,000 systems. Source: same baulinks pages.
- Momentum: first launched in 2004, won a 2009 award in the "Refrigerant Emission Reduction" category, and is still cited in 2026. Described as available in nine languages (from search summaries of the baulinks pages; I did not check the product site).
- Austria: none of 4 searches (German and English) turned up an Austrian edition, an Austrian customer or Austria-specific compliance content. Whether it handles Austrian rules is unverified. The "EU" claim above suggests some use outside Germany.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Capterra, G2, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced. The review-oriented search returned only unrelated results. This is an absence of evidence, not evidence of satisfaction.

## Fit gaps (all unverified or inferred)
- It is positioned around association member firms and operators in Germany. Austrian fit and Austrian-language or legal specifics are unverified.
- The tool is aimed at refrigeration contractors, so a small operator may only reach it through a contractor.
- Whether it offers a mobile or offline app is unverified.
- Association-run software can mean membership or licence gating (unverified).

## Opening
A small Austria-focused, self-serve, mobile-first F-gas logbook for Austrian contractors and operators could undercut it, but only if Austrian use of VDKF-LEC turns out to be thin. That needs a check on the vendor's own site, which I could not reach in this search budget.
