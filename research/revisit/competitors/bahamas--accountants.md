# Competitor: accountants (Bahamas, consultant, no URL)

Idea it killed: C0076 Short-term rental tax compliance.

## Verdict: weak

Accountants are a generic, manual, per-engagement substitute, not a product. Research was limited: 2 of 3 attempted searches returned results (one hit a usage limit), and no local-language search was possible, since Bahamian English is the local language.

## Evidence
- Foreign owners of short-term rentals must register for VAT regardless of turnover. Bahamian individuals register only above $100,000 turnover. The VAT rate on short-term rentals is 12%. Owners can authorize platforms such as Airbnb to collect VAT for them. Source: search summary citing https://ewnews.com/airbnb-hosts-subject-to-vat/ and bnbcalc.com pages. The bnbcalc.com pages were previously judged low quality, so treat the figures as unverified.
- A government portal for registering short-term rental properties has been required since 1 March 2023 (same sources, unverified).
- Larger firms advertise VAT services, for example Crowe Bahamas (https://crowe.com/bs/services/vat). These are general VAT services, not specific to short-term rentals; I could not check their detail.
- An accounting-firm directory page exists (https://aviaanaccounting.com/accounting-firms-in-bahamas/). I did not verify its content.
- Complaints: none found about accountants serving rental hosts. Results showed only industry-level concerns about VAT filing deadlines and compliance cost for small business (Tribune242, Bahamas Chamber of Commerce). No app-store, Reddit, Trustpilot or forum reviews turned up. Finding none is not proof that none exist, because the search was shallow.

## Pricing
No published prices found. Unverified. Per-engagement fees at established firms are likely to be high for a single-property host, but I have no source for this.

## Fit gaps
- The advice is manual and per-client. I found nothing offering a self-serve tool for hosts.
- Mid-size and large firms appear aimed at businesses, not individual foreign owners of one rental.
- I found no evidence of rental-specific automation such as platform-report reconciliation or VAT return preparation.

## Opening
A cheap self-serve VAT-registration and filing helper for foreign owners of rentals is possible, but the market is small and need was already scored low (3). Rules are unverified and need confirmation from the Bahamas Department of Inland Revenue.
