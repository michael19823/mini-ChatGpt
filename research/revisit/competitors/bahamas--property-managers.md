# bahamas -- property managers (consultant type, no URL)

Dropped idea: C0076 Short-term rental tax compliance.

## Verdict: beatable (low-medium confidence; only 3 searches were run, English only)

Property managers exist and are paid for full-service management, but no search found any that sells tax compliance as a product. They are a bundled service for owners who hand over the whole property, not a tool for self-managing hosts.

## Evidence
- Airbtics lists Bahamas Airbnb managers such as Exuma Exclusives (21 listings), On Beach Time Vacation Rentals (15) and Anchor Down Destinations (12). Listings, occupancy and revenue figures are scraped estimates. https://airbtics.com/airbnb-management-companies/exuma-exclusives-2078810
- Managers are small, island-based, and aimed at high-end villas (the same source shows average daily rates of about $485 to $2,031). Smaller or local hosts are probably not their customers. This is an inference from the listings.
- Complaints: none found for Bahamas managers. The search results said they did not contain Bahamas-specific complaints. Only generic complaints about managers (inconsistent cleaning, slow responses) turned up, and those are not about Bahamas firms or taxes. Local-language search is not applicable (English-speaking market). Facebook groups and Reddit were not searched.
- Tax rules, from third-party guides (bnbcalc.com, not official; rates unverified and inconsistent across pages): 12% VAT, 10% guest tax, mandatory property registration via the Inland Revenue portal since March 2023, VAT registration for Bahamian owners only above roughly $100,000 of income. Owners are responsible for reporting and paying. https://www.bnbcalc.com/blog/short-term-rental-regulation/Staniel-Cay-Bahamas-Guide
- No search result showed a manager offering VAT or guest-tax filing as a service. Absence of results is not proof they don't. Unverified.
- Momentum: not found for any of these firms. Unverified.

## Pricing
- A third-party aggregate (wpreset.com, unverified) says Bahamas managers charge 10-15% of rental income for half-service and 20-30% for full service, with premium firms up to 40-45%. Tax filing is not shown as a separate priced item. https://wpreset.com/how-much-do-airbnb-property-managers-charge/
- For a small host, 20-30% of revenue is expensive just to get tax handled.

## Fit gaps (partly inferred)
- Full-service only; a host who self-manages and only needs VAT/guest-tax calculation, filing and reconciliation is not served.
- Villa-focused and few in number, so small or local-island hosts are likely underserved.
- No evidence of software or automated filing.

## Opening
A low-cost host-side tool for VAT/guest-tax calculation and filing prep is not blocked by property managers, which sell bundled service at 20-30% of revenue. The market size is small, and the official rules need confirming first.
