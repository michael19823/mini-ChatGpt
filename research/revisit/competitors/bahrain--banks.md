# Bahrain: banks (generic) vs C0080 WPS payroll file generation (LMRA)

## Verdict: weak

Banks only execute the payment. Under the enhanced WPS, the salary file is uploaded to the LMRA portal (EMS), and the bank pays after LMRA validates it. No bank was found offering payroll-file generation as a product for small employers. The real overlap is bank corporate portals (e.g. Al Salam) and payroll vendors, not banks as a class. The 6-search budget was not fully used (4 searches run); the run was interrupted by a usage limit and resumed.

## Evidence
- LMRA made the enhanced WPS mandatory for private-sector employers from February 2026. Salary payments go through the LMRA WPS portal only, with no direct transfers. A standardised salary file is uploaded monthly through the Expatriate Management System, even with no changes. Sources: EY tax news (taxnews.ey.com/news/2025-2235), KPMG regulatory alert (assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/bh/pdf/2025/11/regulatory-alert-lmra-to-launch-mandatory-enhanced-wages-protection-system.pdf), Mondaq summary.
- Payroll files are validated by LMRA before salaries are released, and errors block payment (greythr.com compliance guide, a vendor blog, so treat it as secondary).
- Each employer needs a Wages Responsible Person with an advanced eKey, plus up to five maker/checker roles (greythr.com, Mondaq; not checked against LMRA).
- Banks advertising WPS support: Al Baraka Islamic Bank (gccbusinessnews.com/al-baraka-islamic-bank-joins-bahrain-wps/) and Al Salam Bank (corporate digital banking portal, per its own site, alsalambank.com). These are bank self-descriptions, not independent reviews.
- Bank readiness was a problem: LMRA extended the filing deadline to the end of Q1 2026 so employers could open WPS-compliant bank accounts (EY, globaltaxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0217). This points to uneven bank readiness.
- Other vendors in the space: Cercli, greytHR, Mercans, Sovereign (payroll services) and an Odoo Bahrain payroll app listing. Their quality and pricing were not verified.
- Complaints: none found about banks' WPS or payroll file tools (app-store, forum, Reddit or Arabic-language searches returned only legal and vendor articles). No complaints found is not proof of satisfaction. The searches did not reach review sites.

## Pricing
Not found. No bank published payroll or WPS fees in the results. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Banks do not generate the LMRA salary file. The employer or its payroll software does, and the bank executes payments afterwards.
- Banks do not obviously cover employer-side validation, maker/checker role setup or justification documents for late payment. This is unverified.
- Banks that are not WPS-ready force employers to switch accounts. Bank-by-bank format differences were not confirmed.

## Opening
A small-employer tool that produces LMRA-valid monthly salary files and pre-validates them (with maker/checker support) fits a gap banks do not fill. The competition to check next is the vendors listed above, not the banks. Whether the file format is simple enough to make this a thin product is unverified.
