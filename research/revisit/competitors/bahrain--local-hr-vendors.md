# Bahrain: local HR vendors (killed C0080: WPS payroll file generation, LMRA)

## Verdict: weak

Bahrain WPS file generation is already a checkbox feature in cheap SME payroll tools (Zoho, greytHR, Odoo add-on). No single local vendor was identified as dominant, and the file generator alone is commoditised. The opening is narrow.

## Evidence
- Zoho Payroll has a Bahrain edition advertising wage file generation and Bahrain gratuity/pension compliance (search snippet of zstatic.zohostatic.com/en-bh/payroll/pricing/; claim not opened in full, unverified detail).
- greytHR advertises WPS-compliant SIF generation for Bahrain/GCC (https://www.greythr.com/middle-east/pricing/ ; https://www.greythr.com/middle-east/blog/bahrain-enhanced-wps-2026-compliance-guide/). Indian vendor, not strictly local.
- Odoo add-on "l10n_bh_wps_sif" generates the LMRA SIF (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/l10n_bh_wps_sif).
- Local-ish names surfaced in a search snippet: InfoRise HRMS and AeroHR, both described as having WPS output (details unverified, no URLs opened, pricing by quote).
- Regulatory driver: WPS 2.0 requires a payroll-responsible person (WRP) by 1 Feb 2026 and a monthly standardised file via LMRA portal, validated before payment release (https://www.tamimi.com/our-knowledge/publications/eyes-on-2026/articles/bahrains-wage-protection-system-and-labour-law-compliance-reforms/; EY: https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0217-bahrain-announces-filing-timeline-extension-and-new-compliance-updates-under-its-wages-protection-system). This increases demand and vendor supply alike.
- Complaints: none found about local HR vendors' WPS output (searches in English and Arabic). Found only worker wage-delay complaints (2,300+ over four years, per DLA Piper summary), which concern employers, not vendors.
- Momentum: vendors are actively updating for WPS 2.0 (greytHR 2026 guide; Odoo module versions for 18 and 19).

## Pricing
- Zoho Payroll Bahrain: USD 10 per org per month incl. 5 employees, +USD 2 per extra employee (search snippet, annual billing).
- greytHR: Essential USD 50/month incl. 10 employees, +USD 2 each (snippet).
- Odoo WPS module: USD 128.58 (v18) / USD 116 (v19) one-off, requires Odoo.
- InfoRise / AeroHR: custom quote, unverified.
A small buyer is already well served at low cost, so a standalone WPS tool cannot undercut meaningfully.

## Fit gaps
- Not verified that any tool handles the new WPS 2.0 portal validation (CPR/IBAN/work-permit match against LMRA data) or WRP workflow end to end; marketing claims only.
- Local vendors sell by quote with implementation bundles, likely poor for micro firms (inference, unverified).
- Unknown: Arabic UI quality, bank-specific formats.

## Opening
Only a very cheap, WRP-focused pre-validation and filing checker for micro-employers (no full HR suite) leaves room, and the pricing floor of about USD 10 per month makes that thin.

Searches used: 5 of 6.
