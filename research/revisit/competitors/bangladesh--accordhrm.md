# AccordHRM (Bangladesh) vs C0082: PF administration for factories with 100+ workers

## Verdict: beatable

AccordHRM is a real, active, cheap cloud HR/payroll product with a provident fund module. PF is one feature inside a general HRM, not a dedicated product. Nothing found shows depth on factory-scale PF administration. The evidence is thin, so treat this as a lean, not a certainty.

## Evidence
- Described as cloud HR and payroll software for Bangladesh. Features listed: attendance, leave, loans and advances, provident and gratuity fund management, employee self-service, web and mobile apps. Sources: https://softwarefinder.com/hr/accordhrm ; https://ibos.io/best-provident-fund-pf-loan-management-software-in-bangladesh/ (a vendor-comparison blog, so possibly biased).
- In the ibos.io PF software comparison, AccordHRM's PF and gratuity management is listed with custom-quote pricing. Same source as above.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in English and Bengali returned no app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints.
- Reviews are almost nonexistent. G2 shows one review (5.0), which praises record security and available support and mentions poor internet connections at times (https://www.g2.com/sellers/accordhrm). It is unverified whether that G2 listing is the same product as the Bangladesh AccordHRM, because the product URL slug points to a different vendor (intelliob / Accord eHR). SoftwareFinder says it has no reviews, though it notes the UI "may feel overwhelming for first-time users".
- Momentum: marketed through 2026 listicles and listed on SaaSBrowser and Elioplus. No evidence of acquisition or shutdown. Development pace and client list are unverified.
- Search results did not name garment-factory clients. Industries named were generic: tech, manufacturing, logistics, energy, finance.
- Demand context: a Bengali-language result says firms with more than 100 permanent workers must now set up a provident fund automatically (employee 7-8% of basic, employer matches). This is from a search summary, so verify the rule before relying on it. If true, it is a tailwind for the idea.

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified, inferred from absence of evidence)
- No evidence of PF trust-fund features: trustee reporting, NBR recognition filings, fund investment and interest allocation, or member loan and withdrawal workflows at factory scale. Verify with a demo.
- No evidence of garment-specific features such as piece-rate or overtime-heavy payroll, or Bengali UI.
- Positioned as a general HRM, so a PF-only buyer pays for a full suite.
- One UI complaint (overwhelming for first-time users) comes from SoftwareFinder's editorial summary, not from user reviews.

## Opening
A focused PF and trust-fund administration tool for 100+ worker factories, priced per fund member and usable alongside any HR system, could undercut a full-suite module. First confirm AccordHRM's PF module depth through a demo or its feature page.
