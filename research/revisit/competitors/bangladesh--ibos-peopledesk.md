# iBOS PeopleDesk (Bangladesh)

Dropped idea: C0082, provident fund administration for factories with 100+ workers.

## Verdict: strong

PeopleDesk is an active, Bangladesh-specific HR and payroll suite from AKIJ iBOS Limited. It claims provident fund contributions and factory customers. Competing on PF alone looks hard. A dedicated PF fund-administration product (trust accounts, member ledgers, interest allocation, withdrawals) is not confirmed inside it.

## Evidence
- Vendor pages say PeopleDesk handles provident fund contributions, gratuity, NBR tax, festival bonus and overtime. Source: https://ibos.io/?p=91493 (search snippet). The vendor's own marketing, so treat it as unverified.
- Vendor pages say it serves factories, retail chains and corporations, with biometric integration, mobile/GPS attendance, multi-branch support, and bank and mobile-banking salary disbursement. Source: https://ibos.io/peopledesk/
- It claims 50+ organizations and 30,000+ employees. Self-reported and unverified. Source: https://ibos.io/?p=80627
- Momentum: the vendor publishes many 2026 SEO and comparison pages, so it is actively marketed. Source: https://ibos.io/top-10-hr-payroll-software-in-bangladesh-2026-why-peopledesk-is-1/
- G2 rating of 4.5/5 was reported in a search summary (https://www.g2.com/de/products/peopledesk/reviews, exact count not seen). A SoftwareFinder review gave 4/5 and praised payroll and leave handling (https://softwarefinder.com/hr/peopledesk/reviews).
- Complaints: the only one found is a single reviewer saying the product is simple to use but rigid, with limited workflow and report customization. It was relayed through a search summary, and I did not see the original text. No outage, failed-filing or app-store complaints were found. Searches of Google Play, Reddit and Bangla sources returned nothing specific.

## Pricing
- No published price. Custom quotes by employee count, modules and implementation scope; a free demo or trial is offered. Source: https://ibos.io/?p=94815 and the G2 pricing page. No BDT figures verified.

## Fit gaps
- Verified: none.
- Possible and unverified: depth of PF trust administration (member ledgers, interest allocation, trustee reporting, NBR-recognized fund compliance) may be shallow compared with a dedicated product. It may also be bundled and priced for a whole HR suite, which a PF-only buyer might not want. Customization rigidity was noted once.

## Opening
Only a narrow opening: a PF-trust-only tool that sits beside or integrates with existing HR and payroll systems, and only if PeopleDesk's PF module proves shallow. Confirm that with a demo.
