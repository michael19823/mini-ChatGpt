# Bangladesh: payroll / HRMS vendors (generic)

Idea killed: C0087 Payroll / HRMS with PF. Searches used: 3 of 6 (English, Bengali, reviews).

## Verdict: strong

Many local vendors (PeopleDesk by iBOS, PiHR, AccordHRM, Shomvob, MiHCM, an Odoo l10n_bd_payroll module, IceHrm) already sell Bangladesh-specific payroll with NBR tax slabs and provident fund handling, at low prices.

## Evidence
- PeopleDesk (iBOS) is described as built around Bangladesh tax and labour law, with NBR slab tax and automatic TDS. Source: https://ibos.io/best-payroll-software-in-bangladesh-complete-guide/ (vendor's own content, so promotional).
- PiHR is described as a popular cloud HR system in Dhaka with payroll and tax management. Same source.
- AccordHRM is positioned as cloud payroll supporting local tax and labour compliance. Source: https://softwarefinder.com/hr/accordhrm
- Other options: Odoo l10n_bd_payroll (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_bd_payroll), MiHCM (https://mihcm.com/bn-bd/solutions/payroll/), Mercans.
- Provident fund and gratuity tracking is described as a core feature of most of these (vendor guides; unverified per product).
- Complaints: only generic ones found, in a vendor blog (Shomvob): technical errors, complex UI, slow response. Source: https://shomvob.com/blog/hris-software-bangladesh-problems (competitor-authored, not user reviews).
- G2 shows positive reviews for "HRMS Software" (a seller page), citing good support and simple UI: https://www.g2.com/sellers/hrms-software
- No Play Store or App Store reviews were found. No named user complaints, outage or failed-filing news were found.

## Pricing
- PiHR: about BDT 2,500/month for up to 25 members, about BDT 6,000/month for 100 members (from search summary of ibos.io; unverified against PiHR's own site).
- IceHrm: $2,499 one-time for unlimited employees (search summary; unverified).
- Others: unknown.
- This is already cheap for small businesses.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. Possible gaps are unverified: small-firm PF trust or fund accounting, offline use, Bengali-language UI.
- Legal note from search: firms with 100+ permanent staff must set up a PF (unverified), so the market for PF-specific need is mainly mid-sized firms, which the vendors serve.

## Momentum
- Active. iBOS publishes 2026 content, Odoo has a Bangladesh payroll module for versions 18 and 19, MiHCM offers a Bengali page.

## Opening
Little: the market is crowded and cheap. Only a narrow niche (micro firms, or PF trust accounting) might remain, and no evidence of it was found.
