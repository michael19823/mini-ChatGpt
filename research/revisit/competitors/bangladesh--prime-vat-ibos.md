# Prime VAT (iBOS / AKIJ iBOS), Bangladesh

Dropped idea: C0081, NBR VAT management software (Mushak 6.3 invoicing, 6.1/6.2 registers, 9.1 return).

## Verdict: strong

It is a real, NBR-recognised, actively marketed product that covers exactly the dropped idea's scope. I found no complaints, but the search tool is weak for local review sites.

## Evidence
- iBOS markets Prime VAT as NBR-approved, cloud-based VAT software under the VAT & SD Act 2012, from iBOS Limited (part of AKIJ Resource). Its pages say it generates Mushak 6.1, 6.2.1, 6.3 and 9.1 with annexes, plus adjustments, inventory and reports. Source: https://ibos.io/no-1-vat-software-in-bangladesh-prime-vat/ and https://ibos.io/primevat-details/ (vendor claims, unverified independently).
- The vendor claims setup takes about 3 hours (unverified).
- iBOS publishes many SEO pages ("top 10 vat software", "best vat software in bangladesh", NBR-approved guide), which points to active marketing. Source: https://ibos.io/top-10-vat-software-in-bangladesh/
- NBR requires an enlisted digital VAT system above BDT 5 crore annual turnover (vendor-stated, unverified). That makes the segment mandatory-buying.
- Other options exist: Odoo Bangladesh Mushak VAT module (listed at $118.01 for v19, https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_bd_mushak_vat), an ECOSIRE ERPNext app (from about $399, https://ecosire.com/apps/erpnext/erpnext-bangladesh-vat-mushak), and a Frappe VAT compliance app that does not yet auto-generate 9.1 (https://cloud.frappe.io/marketplace/apps/vat_compliance).
- Government threat: Bangladesh media reported NBR is building a free software for small and micro traders to keep accounts and file returns (https://www.bd-pratidin.com/last-page/2026/05/09/1248460; the search summary only, I did not read the article). This would undercut a paid product for small buyers.
- Complaints: none found in 6 searches (English and Bangla). No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or Facebook reviews surfaced. This is absence of evidence, not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing
Not published. Custom quote with free demo, per vendor pages and search summaries. Whether it is overpriced for small buyers is unverified.

## Fit gaps (all unverified)
- Likely aimed at mid-to-large firms (the BDT 5 crore mandatory threshold, ERP-backed sales), so small VAT-registered shops below the threshold may be underserved.
- No public price, so small buyers cannot self-serve.
- Unknown: mobile or offline use, Bangla UI, and direct IVAS submission.

## Opening
A low-cost, self-serve, Bangla-first Mushak tool for sub-BDT 5 crore VAT filers is the only possible gap. It is narrow, and the planned free NBR small-trader software threatens even that.
