# Prism ERP (Bangladesh), commercial

Killed idea: C0082, provident fund administration for factories with 100+ workers.
Searches used: 3 of 6 (2 English, 1 Bengali). WebFetch not used.

## Verdict: strong

Prism ERP has a dedicated provident fund module inside an established ERP with RMG-industry pages. It is the incumbent for mid and large factories. It looks beatable only for small factories that cannot afford a full ERP.

## Evidence
- Its docs describe a provident fund module: PF configuration, employee PF setup, PF slab and type, PF income distribution, and PF refund at final settlement. Source: https://docs.prismerp.net/human-capital/payroll-compensation/provident-fund/
- Its HR product lists payroll, attendance, leave, provident fund and gratuity, loans, and tax. Source: https://www.prismerp.net/blog/best-human-resource-management-software-in-bangladesh
- The vendor claims 300+ businesses and 35+ business types (vendor claim, unverified). It has an RMG industry page: https://prismerp.net/industries/rmg
- A named customer exists: Bashundhara adopted PrismERP of Divine IT. Source: https://www.tbsnews.net/node/231229
- Independent comparison sites position it for medium to large enterprises (summarized from search results, e.g. https://ibos.io/top-10-best-performing-erp-software-in-bangladesh).
- Complaints: the only negative signal is a Capterra/Software Advice listing. Per the search summary it shows about 2.8/5 from about 10 users, with value for money 1.9/10, ease of use 2.7/10, and customer service 2.1/10. The scale of these figures was not verified, and I did not read individual reviews. Sources: https://www.capterra.com/p/147399/PrismERP/reviews/ and https://www.softwareadvice.com/erp/prismerp-profile
- No complaints about outages, failed filings, app-store reviews, Reddit, Facebook groups or Bengali forums were found. The Bengali search returned no complaint content.

## Pricing
- Capterra lists a starting price of $5,000 one-time (per the search summary, not confirmed on the page). Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/147399/PrismERP/
- Pricing is not published on its own site, as far as the searches showed. Per-seat, support and customization costs are unverified.
- The low value-for-money score above suggests price sensitivity, but there is too little data to call it overpriced.

## Fit gaps
- PF is one module of a full ERP, so a factory wanting only PF administration has to adopt or integrate the wider suite (inferred, not stated by the vendor).
- It is positioned at medium and large firms, so factories at the 100-worker threshold may find it heavy.
- It is described as web and mobile based. Offline use, Bengali-language worker self-service, and Bangladesh Labour Act or NBR-approved PF trust reporting formats were not verified either way.
- Customization is a selling point, which suggests implementation effort and cost (inferred).

## Momentum
Active. Its blog and docs site are maintained, it has a user-experience recognition post, and it has recent 2026 Capterra listings. No acquisition or shutdown was found. Exact dates of recent releases are unverified.

## Opening
A lightweight, low-cost, standalone PF and gratuity tool for 100-500 worker factories that do not run a full ERP, or one that sits alongside their existing payroll, is the gap. Competing head-on with Prism for factories that already run it is unlikely to work.
