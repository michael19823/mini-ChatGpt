# SMAC IT ProFund (Bangladesh)

Killed idea: C0082, provident fund administration for factories with 100+ workers.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Four searches (English and Bangla) found no evidence that a product called "SMAC IT ProFund" exists in Bangladesh. Product existence is unverified. No URL was found.

## Evidence
- English search for "SMAC IT ProFund provident fund software Bangladesh": no page mentions the product. Results were government procurement notices for provident fund software (tea estates PF, e.g. https://bangla.bppa.gov.bd/advertisement-services/details-7975.html), which are public-sector tenders, not a commercial product for factories.
- Search for "SMAC IT" Bangladesh: the only match was a YourStory profile (https://yourstory.com/companies/smac-it) describing a "SMAC IT" as a digital service provider (social, mobile, e-commerce, cloud; web, apps, digital marketing). The snippet did not tie it to Bangladesh or to provident funds. Whether it is the same company is unverified.
- Bangla search (provident fund software for garment factories): returned only unrelated garment ERP news (Prydesys IT), no ProFund or PF administration product.
- Search for PF software for factory workers: the generic payroll vendors Mercans, MiHCM and iBOS mention PF tracking inside payroll. No SMAC IT or ProFund result.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, forum or news reviews exist for this product in the results.
- Momentum: unknown, no evidence of active development.

## Pricing
None published or found (unverified).

## Fit gaps
Cannot assess. The real alternatives for the buyer are payroll/HRM suites with a PF module (Mercans, MiHCM, iBOS, per search snippets only). Whether any covers PF trust-fund administration (interest allocation, member ledgers, withdrawals, trustee reporting) was not verified.

No identifiable dedicated competitor was found, so C0082 is not blocked by this name. Payroll suites with PF modules are the real substitutes and should be checked.
