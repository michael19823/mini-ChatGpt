# Appreo (Belgium) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0104, cleaning-sector attendance registration (PC 121). Other killers: the free RSZ app and CleanManag.

## Verdict: weak

Appreo is a general Dutch workforce platform (planning, work orders, time registration). I found no evidence that it does RSZ check-in/out for PC 121, and its Belgian presence is only weakly indicated. Searches used: 5 of 6.

## Evidence
- A Dutch cleaning-software comparison page describes Appreo as cleaning software with graphical planning, digital work orders, time registration and team communication. It also says three cleaning companies tested it for two weeks. Source: https://www.facto.nl/onderwerp/schoonmaaksoftware (seen only as a search snippet; I did not open the page).
- Searches for "Appreo" with Belgium, RSZ, PC 121 or "aanwezigheidsregistratie" returned nothing connecting Appreo to the Belgian RSZ "Check in and out at work" service. The English query "Appreo Belgium app" returned no results about it.
- The Belgian cleaning-software results were other products. FacilityApps is described as a market leader for cleaning companies in Belgium and acquired M.I.D. Software Engineering in October 2024 (https://www.facilityapps.com/en/blog/press-release-facilityapps-acquires-m-i-d-software-engineering-and-strengthens-its-position-in-t). This shows the Belgian niche is served by others, not by Appreo.
- A fifth search (Dutch, Belgium) gave a vakbeursfacilitair.nl company profile (https://en.vakbeursfacilitair.nl/bedrijfsprofiel/softwareplatform-voor-stroomlijning-werkzaamheden/). The search summary describes an all-in-one platform for cleaning, security, landscaping, property management, catering and more, and hints at Benelux availability. I did not open the page, so Belgian availability is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Trustpilot, Capterra, G2 or Reddit reviews of Appreo turned up. This does not mean it is good; the product barely shows up in search.
- Momentum: unverified. I found no release, funding or acquisition news.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- RSZ check-in/out integration for PC 121: not shown (unverified).
- Belgian presence, Belgian-language (FR/NL) support and Belgian customers: unverified.
- The product appears aimed at planning and work orders, not at legal attendance registration.

## Opening
Appreo does not block C0104. The real competitors for the idea are the free RSZ app, CleanManag and FacilityApps.
