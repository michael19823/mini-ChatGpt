# AutoCheckIn (Belgium) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0103, Check In and Out at Work in construction (from 1 Apr 2027).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (unverified identity)

No product named "AutoCheckIn" turned up in 4 searches (English, Dutch). Searches only surfaced the official government Checkinatwork (CIAW) service and several unrelated third-party vendors. The competitor name could not be confirmed.

## Evidence
- Checkinatwork is the government (RSZ/ONSS) service for attendance registration on construction sites of EUR 500,000 or more excl. VAT; it has an official free app. Sources: https://www.socialsecurity.be/site_nl/employer/applics/checkinatwork/index.htm, https://www.socialsecurity.be/foreign/working_in_belgium/en/checkinatwork.html
- Commercial CIAW-compatible vendors did appear (not AutoCheckIn): Targa Telematics https://targatelematics.com/be-en/check-in-at-work-simplify-time-tracking-on-construction-sites/ , Traxxeo https://traxxeo.com/en/worksite-management/check-in-at-work-mobile-application-presence-recording , Traxgo https://www.traxgo.be/en/checkinatwork/ . One app listing says it only works with an active Suivo IoT platform account (https://apps.apple.com/py/app/check-in-at-work/id1560606755).
- Complaints: none found. Searches returned only official/vendor pages, no app-store reviews, forum or news complaints about AutoCheckIn.

## Pricing
Nothing found for AutoCheckIn. A web-application listing for CIAW showed only "op aanvraag" (on request) and was not AutoCheckIn (unverified relevance). Official CIAW registration is free.

## Fit gaps
Unknown for AutoCheckIn (unverified). The wider market: government app is free, and several vendors already sell integrated CIAW tooling, which is the real pressure on the idea.

## Opening
AutoCheckIn itself leaves no verifiable barrier; the real obstacle for C0103 is the free government app plus existing vendors (Targa, Traxxeo, Traxgo, Werfc), not this name.
