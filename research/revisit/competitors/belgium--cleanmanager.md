# CleanManager (Belgium) - competitor check for C0104

Idea: Cleaning-sector attendance registration (PC 121). Competitors named: free RSZ app, CleanManager, Appreo, Flexsys.

## Verdict: weak (in Belgium)

CleanManager is a real product, but I found no evidence that it is sold or supported in Belgium.

## Evidence
- Danish SaaS for cleaning firms: planning, time registration, quality control, cost calculation, mobile app. Described as trusted in Denmark, Germany and the UK. Sources: https://cleanmanager.dk/en/about-us/story , https://cleaningmag.com/news/cleanmanager-software-now-available-in-the-uk
- Operates in Danish, English and German. No Dutch or French found, and no Belgian presence or Belgian PC 121 / RSZ attendance-registration feature found (unverified either way). Source: https://tynmagazine.com/zvoove-acquires-cleanmanager/
- Momentum: acquired by zvoove (Munich, staffing/security/cleaning software) in August 2025. Reported 425+ customers and 15,000 users. Active, now backed by a larger group; the integration may bring a Belgian push (unverified). Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/nl/persberichten/zvoove-neemt-cleanmanager-over-snellere-groei-dankzij-toegang-tot-de-scandinavische-markt-en-uitbreiding-van-de-softwareportfolio-voor-schoonmaak-302538682.html
- Complaints: none found. A Trustpilot search in Dutch returned nothing about this product. Capterra shows a single review (Oct 2023, 5/5 overall, 4/5 support), too thin to tell anything. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/249080/CleanManager/

## Pricing
Modular, per user type, in Danish kroner (from https://cleanmanager.dk/en/pricing): administrator 299 kr/month, supervisor 199 kr/month, employee with login 69 kr/month, plus add-on modules. Examples on the site: 10 employees with 1 add-on is 1,488 kr/month; 32 employees with 3 add-ons is 3,714 kr/month; 134 employees with 5 add-ons is 10,760 kr/month. No Belgian/EUR pricing found. The per-employee fee (about 69 kr, roughly EUR 9) is not cheap for a small cleaning firm with many part-time staff (conversion is my own estimate).

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of Belgian legal fit (PC 121, RSZ/ONSS attendance registration, Dutch/French UI). Unverified.
- Per-employee-login pricing penalises firms with many part-timers.
- No Belgian reviews or local support found.

## Opening
A Dutch/French-language, Belgium-specific attendance tool for PC 121 is not covered by CleanManager as far as visible. Watch for zvoove expanding it into Benelux.
