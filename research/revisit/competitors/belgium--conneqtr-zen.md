# Conneqtr Zen (Belgium)

Killed idea: C0103 Check In and Out at Work in construction (from 1 Apr 2027): AutoCheckIn, Werfc.

## Verdict: strong

Conneqtr Zen is an established, industry-backed construction-site compliance platform that already covers CIAW (Check In At Work), so it is a real competitor for C0103. Only 3 of the 6 searches ran. The 4th hit a usage limit and the 5th and 6th were not run, so pricing and complaints are unchecked.

## Evidence
- Zen is described as combining smart document management, CIAW rules, chain structure and access control in one environment. It was developed with BESIX and the ADEB-VBA federation. The same page says it is used on 2,299 sites and approved by over 3,500 contractors and subcontractors. These figures come from a search-result summary of a Sept 2024 press release, not from the page itself, so treat them as unverified. Source: https://besix.storychief.io/conneqtr-besix-and-socogetra-unveil-digital-construction-roadshow-and-zen-application-on-9-october-and-7-november (also on besix.com and ffgb.be).
- Backing from a large contractor (BESIX) and sector federations (ADEB-VBA, SOCOGETRA) gives it distribution. The 2024 roadshow shows it was active then. No later momentum data was found (unverified).
- Complaints: a Dutch-language search for reviews, ervaringen and klachten returned only unrelated products with similar names. No complaints about Conneqtr Zen were found. This does not show it has no problems, only that the search found none. App-store, G2, Capterra and Reddit were not searched specifically.

## Pricing
Not found. The pricing search was not run because of the usage limit. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
Unverified, since no source covered them. Plausible gaps to check:
- Zen looks aimed at main contractors and large sites (document management, chain structure), so it may be heavy for small subcontractors and self-employed workers who only need simple check-in and check-out.
- Whether it covers the new 2027 daily working-time registration (obligation reported from 1 Jan 2027) is unknown.
- Offline use and hardware needs are unknown.

## Opening
A lightweight, cheap check-in/out and working-time tool for small subcontractors could fit alongside Zen, but this is unconfirmed until its pricing and small-firm fit are checked.
