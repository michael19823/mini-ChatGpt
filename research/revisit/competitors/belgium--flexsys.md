# Flexsys Belgium (belgium) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0104 cleaning-sector attendance registration (PC 121).

## Verdict: weak

Flexsys Belgium appears to be a tiny generic workforce-management vendor. I found no evidence of a cleaning-sector or Check-in-at-Work product. 4 searches used (NL and EN).

## Evidence
- Directory listing (bsearch.be): Flexsys Belgium BVBA, Brussels, founded 1993, 1-5 employees, website www.flexsysbelgium.com (not fetched). Listed services: interactive planning, absence registration, attendance registration, time registration software, personnel planning software. Source: https://bsearch.be/bedrijfsinfo/2/4051185025323420/G10/SE/flexsys-belgium-brussel-1-nl.html
- No result linked Flexsys to PC 121, the RSZ/ONSS "Check in at Work" API, or cleaning companies. Unverified whether it offers any such integration.
- Context: attendance registration in cleaning has been mandatory via the free RSZ "Check In and Out at Work" service (https://www.socialsecurity.be/citizen/nl/nieuws/registratie-schoonmaakactiviteiten-verplicht-vanaf-1-september-2024). A generic time-and-attendance vendor is not a specific answer to this need.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Trustpilot, G2, Capterra or forum reviews surfaced. A search on "Flexsys reviews" returned only unrelated products named "Flex". Finding none is a valid result.
- Momentum: unverified. No news found, and a 1-5 person firm suggests a stagnant niche vendor.

## Fit gaps
