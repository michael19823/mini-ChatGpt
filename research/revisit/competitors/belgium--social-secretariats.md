# Belgium: social secretariats (Securex, Acerta, Partena, Liantis and others)

Idea killed: C0103, Check In and Out at Work in construction (from 1 Apr 2027): AutoCheckIn, Werfc.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Social secretariats are payroll and HR-compliance advisors. In 3 searches I found them explaining the new check-in/out rules, not selling a site check-in/out tool. Fit for this idea is unverified (no product page checked; WebFetch was not allowed).

## Evidence
- Securex publishes news and guidance pages on the mandatory check in/out in construction from 2027 and on attendance registration. This is information for employers, not a product. Source: https://www.securex.be/en/lex4you/employer/news/towards-mandatory-check-in-and-check-out-in-construction-from-2027
- Per Securex's summary, the 2027 rules require both arrival and departure registration, ban pre-registration, and require each person to register in real time. The measure was still a draft law and not yet official in that source (status may have changed since; unverified).
- Lexgo also covers the change as a news item: https://www.lexgo.be/en/news-and-articles/15016-actief-op-belgische-werven-vanaf-2027-wordt-in-en-uitchecken-verplicht
- Registration has been mandatory on construction sites since April 2014 (Check-in-at-Work, per Securex search summary). Existing registration channels are therefore the official system, not secretariats (the official platform operator was not confirmed in my searches).
- Momentum: Securex, Acerta, Partena and Liantis are all large, active incumbents (comparison pages at bobex.be and accountable.eu).
- Complaints: none found. The one search for reviews (Trustpilot, Dutch) returned only comparison and marketing pages, no reviews. No app-store, Reddit or forum searches were run for this check-in angle, so "no complaints" covers only that search.

## Pricing
Secretariat pricing is for payroll, not check-in. Comparison sites say roughly EUR 15-45 per employee per month, one-off setup EUR 50-150, and management fees of about 3-4.25% of social contributions for the self-employed (Securex 3.9%, Acerta 3.05%, Partena 4.25%). Source: bobex.be and accountable.eu comparison pages (secondary, unverified against the providers). No published price for any check-in/out feature found.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence they offer a site-level, real-time check-in/out app, QR/geofence or hardware, or subcontractor and self-employed self-registration. Unverified, since only news and guidance pages were seen.
- Their customer is the employer's payroll department, not the site foreman or subcontractor who must check in on the day.

## Opening
Secretariats only inform employers of the rule, so a lightweight site check-in/out tool for small contractors and subcontractors faces no direct secretariat competition on what I found; the open question is how the official Check-in-at-Work channels and existing site-access vendors compete (not checked).
