# Traxgo / ConstruBadge (Belgium)

Idea killed: C0103, Check In and Out at Work in construction (from 1 Apr 2027): AutoCheckIn, Werfc.

## Verdict
**strong** for the hardware-based, large-site segment. Beatable only in a narrow niche (small sites with no hardware).

## Evidence
- ConstruBadge is the sector's official worker ID card. It is issued automatically to NSSO-registered construction blue-collar workers by the sector's occupational security fund. It carries a barcode, so it is not a Traxgo product. Source: https://www.traxgo.be/en/construbadge/ and https://constructiv.be/en-US/Werkgevers/ConstruBadge.aspx
- Traxgo (traxgo.be) sells track-and-trace systems and a mobile time clock for the site. They read ConstruBadge and LisaBadge and forward the data to Checkinatwork automatically. It claims 20+ years of experience (self-reported). Sources: https://www.traxgo.be/en/management-of-attendance and https://www.traxgo.be/en/attendance-recording-in-the-construction-sector
- Checkinatwork applies to sites above EUR 500,000 excl. VAT. Source: search summaries of traxgo.be pages.
- Traxgo has a dedicated "automatic Checkinatwork" offer and has been at Matexpo (https://www.matexpo.com/en/automatically-register-checkinatwork-on-construction-site). That suggests active marketing, but I found no dated release or news. Momentum: apparently active, unverified.
- Complaints: none found. Five searches in English, Dutch and French returned only official and marketing pages. There are no app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews in the results. Absence of reviews is not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing
Not published in the results (unverified). Quote-based, and it involves hardware.

## Fit gaps (inferred from the marketing pages, unverified)
- It is built around a physical time clock or track-and-trace unit. A software-only or phone-only tool for small subcontractors is not what it offers.
- It targets main contractors on large sites. Small subcontractors and the self-employed are not shown as a focus.
- I did not check whether it supports the new rules from 1 Apr 2027.

## Opening
A cheap, no-hardware, phone-first check-in aimed at small subcontractors, possibly combined with ConstruBadge barcode or QR scanning, but Traxgo already owns the incumbent badge-reader integration.
