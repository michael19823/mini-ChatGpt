# Werfcheck (Belgium) - competitor check

Idea(s) killed: C0103 Check In and Out at Work in construction (from 1 Apr 2027): AutoCheckIn, Werfc...

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

"Werfcheck" is not a product I could find. It looks like a placeholder or misread name from the earlier rejection text. The real incumbent for this idea is the government's free Checkinatwork service (socialsecurity.be), plus commercial hardware/time-clock vendors.

## Evidence
- 3 searches (Dutch and English), including an exact-match search on "Werfcheck". No product, company, app-store listing or review turned up. The exact search returned only unrelated results (Wren-check, Checkmk "Werks"). Existence of a product by this name: unverified, probably none.
- Checkinatwork in construction has been mandatory since April 2014. From 2027, check-in and check-out will both be mandatory, with no advance registration; each person registers in real time (Securex: https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/werkgever/nieuws/naar-een-verplichte-check-in-en-out-in-de-bouw-vanaf-2027). The official registration channel is socialsecurity.be (https://www.socialsecurity.be/site_nl/employer/applics/check-in-and-out-at-work/general/how-to-register.htm).
- Commercial adjacent vendor found: All-Connects "CheckinATwork" time clocks at the site entrance, reading Limosa, Dallas, Construbadge, LISA, RFID or ID cards (listing: https://dev.entreeding.com/bedrijven/552806/te-koop-item/62897757/all-connects-n-v/webapplicatie-voor-checkinatwork). This is a different named competitor that needs its own check.
- Complaints: none found, because there was nothing to review. No complaint search was possible for a product that doesn't appear to exist.
- Momentum: not applicable.

## Pricing
None found for Werfcheck. All-Connects pricing is "upon request" (unverified beyond that listing).

## Fit gaps
Not assessable for Werfcheck. For the real incumbents: the government service is free, and hardware vendors require a clock at the site (a hardware dependency).

## Opening
No opening from this competitor, since it doesn't appear to exist. The idea should be judged against the free government tool and hardware vendors such as All-Connects, not against Werfcheck.
