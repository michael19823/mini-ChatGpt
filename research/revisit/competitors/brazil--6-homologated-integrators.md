# Brazil: "6+ homologated integrators" (RENAVE for used-car lots, idea C1199)

## Verdict: beatable (low confidence; search quota ran out after 4 of 6 searches)

## Evidence
- RENAVE is the Denatran/Serpro national vehicle-stock registry. Denatran accredits private providers/systems that integrate with it, and resellers must contract an accredited provider for their software. Sources: https://motonline.com.br/noticia/renave-facilita-comercio-de-veiculos-usados/ , https://tecnoblog.net/408100/detrans-recebem-renave-sistema-transferencia-propriedade-veiculos/
- Access needs a digital certificate and Sistema Credencia registration. Source: https://autopapo.com.br/noticia/renave-como-fazer-transferencia-eletronica-veiculos/
- Recent news (Sept 2026) says RENAVE became mandatory nationwide for resellers from 28 September, which suggests a demand shock for small lots. It also mentions integration challenges among stock, fiscal documents and traffic systems. Sources: https://www.vrum.com.br/mercado/2026/09/7506809-renave-agora-e-obrigatorio-o-que-muda-na-compra-e-venda-de-carros.html , https://www.portaldotransito.com.br/noticias/renave-torna-se-obrigatorio-nacionalmente-saiba-o-que-muda-para-lojistas-e-consumidores/amp/ . ## Momentum
RENAVE itself is active: the mandate began 28 Sept 2026. Individual integrators' momentum is unknown.

## Opening
The mandate pushes many small lots to buy accredited software, and integrators are generic vendors (not a single dominant player), so a cheap small-lot-focused product could fit. But a new entrant would itself need Denatran accreditation, which is the main barrier. Verify pricing and complaints before relying on this.
