# Brazil: "7+ vertical tools" (killed C1202: dumpster-rental e-CTR router)

## Verdict
not-a-real-competitor (low confidence). No named competitor was identifiable, and the search budget was cut short.

## Evidence
- The competitor has no name or URL in the brief, so it is a generic bucket of vertical tools. I could not identify any specific Brazilian caçamba (dumpster) rental product that issues MTR/CTR.
- 3 of 6 allowed WebSearch calls were attempted. One returned nothing usable because of a usage-limit error, so 2 searches were productive and 1 failed.
- Result quality: the Portuguese search returned only regulator pages and manuals. These are the CETESB SIGOR manual (https://mtr.cetesb.sp.gov.br/assets/pdf/Manual_SIGOR.pdf), the Minas Gerais Sistema MTR page (https://www.mg.gov.br/servico/emitir-o-manifesto-de-transporte-de-residuos-mtr) and a Joinville MTR guide for construction waste (https://www.joinville.sc.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Manual-Orientacoes-para-Preenchimento-de-MTR-para-Residuos-da-Construcao-Civil-Gerador-Pessoa-Juridica.pdf).
- The English search returned legal alerts on the federal MTR/SINIR, which has been mandatory since 1 Jan 2021 (Demarest, Mayer Brown, Trench Rossi).
- Only generic US dumpster-rental software glossary pages (curbwaste.com) appeared. They are not Brazil-specific and not evidence of a Brazilian competitor.
- Regulatory context: MTR issuance is state-run (SIGOR in SP, Sistema MTR in MG) and federal (SINIR). That fragmentation is a real friction a router could address. The regulators' own free portals are the de facto incumbents.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, Reclame Aqui or forum results surfaced. Absence of complaints here reflects the thin search, not product quality. All of it is unverified.

## Pricing
Unverified. No published prices found for any Brazilian caçamba-rental software. The state MTR portals are free to use (stated generally, not confirmed on a specific page).

## Fit gaps
Unverified. If caçamba-specific tools exist, the likely gaps are multi-state MTR integration (SIGOR, MG, SINIR) and fit for small operators. No source confirms either.

## Opening
Per-state MTR/CTR fragmentation looks open for a small-operator router, but this needs a follow-up search for named Brazilian caçamba-management products (e.g. in Portuguese: "sistema para locadora de caçambas") before the idea is revived.
