# Alterdata (Brazil) - competitor to C0137 "Crédito do Trabalhador" payroll-loan deductions

## Verdict: strong

Alterdata's payroll products already ship Crédito do Trabalhador import and automatic payroll deduction. Searches turned up no complaints and no clear gap for the small buyer.

## Evidence
- Alterdata's help center documents "FP3205 - Crédito do Trabalhador - Como realizar a importação do arquivo de empréstimo". It describes two routes: automatic API import from Dataprev (batch or individual), and manual JSON file import for clients with an A3 certificate or who download the file from the Emprega Web portal. Installments are then calculated automatically in payroll. Sources: https://ajuda.alterdata.com.br/prosoftecontador/fp3205-credito-do-trabalhador-como-realizar-a-importacao-do-arquivo-de-emprestimo-292260876.html and https://ajuda.alterdata.com.br/prosoftsocial/fp3205-credito-do-trabalhador-como-realizar-a-importacao-do-arquivo-de-emprestimo-265262766.html
- It is sold to accounting firms (Prosoft eContador) and to companies (Social/payroll), so it reaches small employers through their accountants.
- Capterra shows Alterdata Pack at 4.6/5 from 54 users, with mostly positive comments about reliability and integration. Minor concerns: training, and some tasks needing direct database access. These are not about Crédito do Trabalhador. Source: https://www.capterra.in/software/197060/alterdata-pack
- Complaints: none found. A Reclame Aqui search returned no Alterdata page, only unrelated companies. This is not evidence of no complaints, because Reclame Aqui was not checked directly (unverified). No app-store, Reddit or news complaints were found within the 6-search budget.
- Momentum: the 2025-26 help articles show active development; the date of the FP3205 article was not checked (unverified).
- Other payroll vendors (TOTVS RM Labore, Keevo) also surfaced in the results, which suggests the feature is common.

## Pricing
Not published for Pack Folha or the Prosoft products. Third-party sites say it is quote-based and modular, by users and modules (unverified, from aggregator pages). No price or minimum could be compared with a small buyer's budget.

## Fit gaps (none confirmed)
- Only usable inside Alterdata payroll. Companies on other payroll software or a spreadsheet, or microbusinesses with no payroll system, are not served.
- Mobile or employee-side use was not found. Alterdata's help covers only the employer side (unverified).
- Quote-based pricing may put it out of reach for very small employers (unverified).

## Opening
Little: the core deduction workflow is already built in. Any opening is a standalone tool for employers outside the Alterdata/TOTVS base, and it is thin.
