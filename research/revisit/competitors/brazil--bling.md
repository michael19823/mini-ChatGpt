# Bling (Brazil, commercial ERP) - competitor to C0134

Dropped idea C0134: 2026 tax-reform invoice changes (CBS/IBS on NF-e, national NFS-e). URL not given; domains below come from search results.

## Verdict: strong

Bling already ships CBS/IBS auto-fill for NF-e/NFC-e and supports the national NFS-e flow. It is cheap and widely used by micro and small firms.

## Evidence
- Bling's help center has articles on the reform. It says Bling prepared the system to automate IBS/CBS filling on invoices. For products (NF-e model 55, NFC-e model 65) it auto-fills the fixed transition rates, but only if the company and the operation nature are set to "Regime Normal". Accountants can customize the taxes under Operation Natures. Source: https://ajuda.bling.com.br/hc/pt-br/articles/35705278410775 and https://ajuda.bling.com.br/hc/pt-br/articles/35704385019031 (as quoted in search results; I did not open the pages).
- NFS-e: the search results describe the national model (DPS sent, national Sefin validates, XML issued with IBS/CBS). They do not confirm in detail how Bling handles it. Treat Bling's NFS-e national support as unverified.
- Momentum: active. The help center has recent reform content, plus an article on sending invoices in the background (https://ajuda.bling.com.br/hc/pt-br/articles/35864097628311) and a price-update post (https://blog.bling.com.br/?p=13179).

## Complaints
- B2BStack shows Bling at 4.4/5 from 21 reviews (support 4.7, value 4.7). Source: https://www.b2bstack.com.br/product/bling-erp/avaliacoes
- Reported problems are about the Shopify integration: automatic export errors, prices not updating, and fiscal data not reaching Shopify after authorization. Source: Shopify app listing https://apps.shopify.com/app76902 (as summarized by search).
- No complaints found about the CBS/IBS or NFS-e reform features. I did not find Reclame Aqui, Reddit or app-store data in the 6 searches allowed (3 used), so this is not conclusive.

## Pricing
Per a secondary source (search summary of the Bling blog and HostGator pages, so unverified against Bling's own page): Cobalto R$55/mo, Mercurio R$110, Titanio R$185, Platina R$450, Diamante R$650. All invoice types are available from Cobalto up. This is cheap for small buyers.

## Fit gaps
- The auto-fill is limited to the Regime Normal setup. Simples Nacional and MEI users may need manual setup (inferred from the Regime Normal requirement; unverified).
- Marketplace/e-commerce integration bugs, mainly Shopify.
- Full ERP scope may be more than a user needs who only wants a reform-compliance tool.
- Nothing found on offline use or multi-municipality NFS-e edge cases.

## Opening
Little room for a general invoicing tool. A narrow opening is a reform-readiness or validation layer for Simples/MEI sellers or accountants, or for firms on non-Bling ERPs.
