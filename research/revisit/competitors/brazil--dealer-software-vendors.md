# Brazil: dealer software vendors (dropped idea C1199: RENAVE for used-car lots)

## Verdict: weak (borderline not-a-real-competitor for a small-lot niche)

Reason: RENAVE filing is legally done only through a SENATRAN-authorized private "integradora"; the vendor market exists and is the incumbent, but searches surfaced no vendor-specific complaints, and the mandate is brand new, so the field is open for a cheap small-lot integrator. Note: becoming an integradora is itself an authorization barrier (see fit gaps).

## Evidence
- RENAVE became mandatory nationwide on 28 September 2026 for covered resellers. Source: [Portal do Transito](https://www.portaldotransito.com.br/noticias/renave-torna-se-obrigatorio-nacionalmente-saiba-o-que-muda-para-lojistas-e-consumidores/amp/), [Vrum](https://www.vrum.com.br/mercado/2026/09/7506809-renave-agora-e-obrigatorio-o-que-muda-na-compra-e-venda-de-carros.html).
- The store must contract an integradora authorized by SENATRAN; choice of integradora is the store's. Fine for non-compliance is gravissima, R$ 293,47 per vehicle (CTB art. 330). Source: Portal do Transito (search summary; fine amount not checked on the page itself).
- General dealer-management software exists, e.g. AutoSDR (CRM, WhatsApp, listing publishing) listed from R$ 297/month. Source: [AutoSDR blog](https://blog.autosdr.com.br/blog/melhor-software-concessionarias-veiculos-seminovos) (vendor's own marketing; the search result did not tie that price to RENAVE).
- Complaints: none found about dealer software vendors or RENAVE integradoras. The Reclame Aqui hits were complaints about used-car dealers themselves, not software. No app-store, G2, Capterra or Reddit reviews were found in 4 searches. Unverified whether complaints exist; the mandate is only days old.
- Momentum: sector was reported preparing in July 2026 ([Jornal do Comercio](https://www.jornaldocomercio.com/economia/2026/07/1257684-setor-automotivo-acelera-preparacao-para-nova-regra-de-venda-de-veiculos.html), title only seen). Specific vendor names, acquisitions or outages: not found.

## Pricing
- No published RENAVE integration price found. Only AutoSDR's R$ 297/month (general CRM, not confirmed to include RENAVE). Everything else unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Not established from sources; no vendor-level gap data found.
- Likely hypotheses (unverified): small lots may be underserved by enterprise DMS packages; the integradora authorization requirement is a barrier to entry for a new product (a startup would need to partner with or become an authorized integradora).

## Opening
Small lots now face a compulsory, fined obligation; a low-cost, simple front end (possibly white-labeling an authorized integradora) is open, but check SENATRAN integradora requirements and incumbent pricing first.
