# Dominio / Thomson Reuters (brazil) - commercial

Idea: C0137 "Crédito do Trabalhador" payroll-loan deductions

## Verdict: strong

It is the incumbent accounting and payroll suite in Brazil. It has announced consignado integration through a partner, and it holds the accounting-office distribution channel. Complaints are about general support and module quality, not about this niche. Searches turned up no complaints about consignado specifically.

- It launched a digital bank (with Modal) embedded in Domínio. Payroll-deductible credit (consignado) was named as a next launch, via the fintech Paketá, aimed at SMEs and their employees. Source: https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/noticias-sobre-fintechs/com-modal-thomson-reuters-lanca-banco-digital-integrado-a-software-contabil

## Pricing
Not found. The product is quote-based, with no published price (unverified). Domínio Conta PJ (the digital bank) is described as having no monthly fee, per search snippets. Source: https://jornalcontabil.com.br/noticia/thomson-reuters-anuncia-servico-de-banco-digital-integrado-a-solucoes-contabeis/

## Fit gaps
- It is sold mainly to accounting offices, so employers outside that channel may not be served directly (unverified).
- No evidence found of a consignado workflow for small employers who run their own payroll.
- Support and reliability complaints suggest weak execution on new modules.
- The consignado feature depends on partner lenders (Paketá), and I found no evidence it is live.

## Opening
A lightweight payroll-deduction tool for small employers outside the accounting-office channel is possible. It would have to beat a bundled, channel-backed incumbent that already announced this feature.
