# Fortes (Fortes Tecnologia), Brazil

Idea it killed: C0137 "Crédito do Trabalhador" payroll-loan deductions

## Verdict: strong

Fortes is an established Brazilian payroll/accounting software vendor (Fortaleza, about 30 years old) with a payroll product (Fortes Folha). The Crédito do Trabalhador deduction is a mandatory eSocial/payroll rule that suites like this are expected to handle. I found no evidence that Fortes lacks it. I also found no page confirming that Fortes ships it, so that coverage is unverified.

## Evidence
- Reclame Aqui lists Fortes Tecnologia em Sistemas with a "Good" reputation, 7.6/10 over the last 6 months, 63 complaints, 100% answered, 76.7% resolved. Source: https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/fortes-tecnologia-em-sistemas/lista-reclamacoes
- Complaint themes per the search summary: invoice import fails to fill tax retentions, phone support delays, billing problems during a system migration. Source: same page, plus https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/fortes-tecnologia-em-sistemas/problemas-recorrentes-no-sistema-fortes-com-importacao-de-notas-fiscais-e-emissao-de-darfs_51HzlfVpuYlI_glF/. None of these concern consignado deductions.
- Capterra has a Fortes Contabil listing (https://www.capterra.com/p/197101/Fortes-Contabil/). I saw no review text, so there are no quotes.
- Program background: the employer must read the loan file on the Brasil Emprega portal and deduct via eSocial events S-1200/S-2299/S-2399 (rubric nature 9253). Since Oct 2025 eSocial cross-checks these automatically. This is a compliance rule that payroll vendors have to implement. Sources: https://meutudo.com.br/blog/noticias/2025/04/09/desconto-em-folha-do-consignado-clt-inicia-em-maio-veja-proximos-passos/ and https://www.senior.com.br/blog/econsignado-o-que-e-beneficios-e-as-principais-mudancas
- Searches found no Fortes-specific page, release note or complaint about Crédito do Trabalhador. Fortes support for it: unverified. Complaints specific to this feature: none found.

## Momentum
- It has a cloud product (Fortes Contábil Web) and opened a new São Paulo office (itforum.com.br, baguete.com.br; dates not checked). The company looks active, not stagnant or shut down. Unverified beyond the news headlines.

## Pricing
- No public price list. Quotes only, via the site. Source: search summary of Capterra and the Fortes site. Whether it is overpriced for small buyers: unverified.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed for this idea.

## Opening
Little. A standalone tool would only win as a cheap add-on or integration for small employers on suites that skip the feature, and nothing found shows Fortes skips it.
