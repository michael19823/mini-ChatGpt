# Omie (Brazil, commercial) - competitor check for C0134 (2026 tax-reform invoice changes: CBS/IBS on NF-e, national NFS-e)

## Verdict: strong

Omie is a live, actively developed cloud ERP that already ships the CBS/IBS and national NFS-e changes, so it covers the gap that C0134 targeted.

## Evidence
- Omie has a dedicated tax-reform product page and help article ("Configurando o IBS e o CBS no Omie"). Search summaries say NF-e is already filled automatically with the new IBS/CBS fields, including the symbolic test-phase rates. https://www.omie.com.br/reforma-tributaria/produto/ , https://ajuda.omie.com.br/pt-BR/articles/13291407-configurando-o-ibs-e-o-cbs-no-omie
- Omie says its NFS-e is ready for the national standard and that it keeps companies compliant in any municipality, including those with their own issuers. This is Omie's own claim; I did not verify it independently.
- Omie says its APIs carry the new tax fields without breaking existing endpoints.
- Omie has a blog post on the obligation to include IBS/CBS on service documents starting 1 Oct 2026, so it is tracking the deadline. https://www.omie.com.br/blog/outubro-obrigatoriedade-ibs-cbs-documentos-fiscais-servicos/
- Complaints, from Reclame Aqui (search summary only; I did not open the pages):
  - Reputation is RA1000 (the top tier), with a 6-month average of 8.7/10.
  - 223 complaints, 93.2% resolved, 97.8% answered, average response time about 14 days.
  - Main themes are poor customer support (SAC), product quality and system slowness.
  - One complaint is titled "problemas recorrentes na emissão de notas de serviço com o sistema Omie". I did not read its content, so the details are unverified. https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/omiexperience/lista-reclamacoes/
- Momentum: active. It publishes frequent tax-reform updates and has an in-ERP loan product (Baguete) and accountant-focused products (Brazil Journal). I did not check dates, so recency is only loosely supported.
- I searched for Reddit, app-store and Facebook-group complaints and outage or failed-filing news. I found none, so none are claimed.

## Pricing
Published at https://www.omie.com.br/precos (per search summaries, not opened):
- Pricing scales with monthly gross revenue, from bands up to R$ 6,750 per month to above R$ 400,000.
- ERP Padrão starts at R$ 309 per month.
- Omie Multivarejo starts at R$ 419 per month.
- Signup is online with no credit card required.

The entry price is reasonable for small firms, but it is not the cheapest. Free or cheaper invoicing tools (for example MEI tools) are not compared here.

## Fit gaps
- Slow support and system slowness, per Reclame Aqui summaries.
- Priced as a full ERP, which is likely more than a micro-seller who only needs invoice emission wants to pay. This is an inference, not verified.
- Recurring service-invoice emission problems are reported, but the details are unverified.
- No evidence found of a lack of CBS/IBS, national NFS-e or API support.

## Opening
Narrow only: a lightweight, cheap invoice-compliance tool or add-on for micro and small firms that find a full ERP too heavy or too slow to support. A general tax-reform invoice tool for ERP users is already covered by Omie.
