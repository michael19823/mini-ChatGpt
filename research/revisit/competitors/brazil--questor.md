# Questor (Questor Sistemas Inteligentes), Brazil

Killed idea: C0137, "Crédito do Trabalhador" payroll-loan deductions. No URL was supplied. Type: commercial.
Searches used: 3 of 6 (Portuguese and English mix).

## Verdict: beatable

Questor is an established (since 1985) accounting and payroll suite sold to accounting firms and outsourcers. I found no evidence that it ships a dedicated Crédito do Trabalhador / e-Consignado module. That absence is unverified, because the searches did not reach Questor's own product pages. The government handles the e-Consignado flow through eSocial and the DET, and a payroll vendor will probably absorb it as a feature. Reclame Aqui complaints show weak support and implementation.

## Evidence
- Reclame Aqui reputation page, as summarized by search: "Boa", average 7.4/10 over 6 months, 30 complaints, 100% answered, 75% resolved, average response time 8 days 7 hours, 66.7% would do business again. https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/questor-sistemas-inteligentes/lista-reclamacoes/ (figures come from the search summary, not from the page itself).
- Complaint themes in the same summary:
  - slow support, with waits over a day and tickets auto-closed after 30 minutes of inactivity;
  - inadequate implementation and training, for example a 20-minute fiscal training;
  - promised features that don't work, such as receiving invoices and boletos in the app;
  - instability and crashes.
- A complaint thread titled "Falhas e lentidão no Questor Cloud após migração causam prejuízo operacional e insatisfação" exists. https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/questor-sistemas-inteligentes/falhas-e-lentidao-no-questor-cloud-apos-migracao-causam-prejuizo-operacional-e-insatisfacao_FtlALst_tqTQ_0F7/ I saw only the title.
- A complaint thread titled "aviso prévio de 90 dias por um serviço que não foi prestado" exists. https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/questor-sistemas-inteligentes/aviso-previo-de-90-dias-por-um-servico-que-nao-foi-prestado_thmXFLpW-ctSwWhx/ I saw only the title and did not open it. It points to a 90-day notice for contract termination.
- Regulatory context: Portaria MTE 435/2025 sets up e-Consignado, integrated with eSocial. Workers apply through CTPS Digital, and employers get notices via DET between the 21st and 25th of each month, with deductions capped at 35% of salary. https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego/pt-br/assuntos/legislacao/portarias-1/portarias-vigentes-3/PDFPortariaMTEn435de20demarode2025compiladaem14.10.2025.pdf
- Momentum: the company describes processing 1.5 million payroll documents a month, with products including Gestão Contábil, Questor Cloud, Quiu (automation robot) and the Zen modules. This looks active, not stagnant, but I found no dated news (unverified). https://www.b2bstack.com.br/product/questor-gestao-contabil
- No Capterra, GetApp, G2 or Reddit reviews turned up. A contabeis.com.br forum thread, "sistema questor", exists but I did not read it.

## Pricing
Not published. B2Bstack lists Questor Gestão Contábil at "R$ 0,00 / month" as a placeholder and Solução Fiscal as "under consultation". Neither is a real price. Real pricing is unverified. It is sold through accounting channels, and I found no small-buyer self-serve price.

## Fit gaps
- It is aimed at accounting firms and outsourcers, not at employers wanting a lightweight consignado view or at workers and lenders.
- No evidence of a dedicated e-Consignado reconciliation or payroll-deduction module (unverified).
- Complaints about support and implementation suggest small customers are poorly served.

## Opening
A narrow, low-touch e-Consignado/DET reconciliation tool for small employers or small accounting firms could beat Questor on support and simplicity. The risk is that Questor, or any payroll vendor, adds this as a standard feature.
