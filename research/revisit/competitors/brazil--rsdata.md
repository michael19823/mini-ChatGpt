# RSData (Brazil) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0135 (eSocial health-and-safety events and NR-1 psychosocial-risk programmes).
Research limit: only 2 of 6 searches returned results (usage limit hit on the others), so most findings are unverified.

## Verdict: strong

Established, specialised SST/eSocial vendor with long tenure and direct eSocial XML generation. Not a gap a small newcomer can easily beat on core eSocial SST events.

## Evidence
- Official site rsdata.com.br (found by search; https://www.rsdata.com.br/software-de-seguranca-e-saude-do-trabalho/) markets an SST software for companies, clinics and occupational-medicine consultancies. It was built for eSocial and generates the XML events, with an "inconsistency center" that checks data before sending (vendor's own claim). https://www.rsdata.com.br/software-esocial/
- Vendor claims 100,000+ companies and 20+ years in the market; its CEO (Rogério Luiz Balbinot) is named as coordinator of the pilot-companies working group for SST in eSocial (vendor claim, unverified independently).
- Publishes content on eSocial security-standard changes (https://www.rsdata.com.br/esocial-seguranca-digital-sistemas-integrados/), suggesting active maintenance (unverified beyond that).
- Complaints: none found. A Reclame Aqui search returned no RSData page (only unrelated companies); no app-store, G2, Capterra, Reddit or forum results were obtained. This is absence of evidence given the limited searches, not proof of satisfaction.
- NR-1 psychosocial-risk coverage: not verified.

## Pricing
Not found; unverified. Not seen published on the pages surfaced.

## Fit gaps (all unverified inference, not confirmed)
- Positioned at companies, clinics and consultancies; the small-employer / micro-business segment is not confirmed to be served or priced for.
- Whether it has a dedicated NR-1 psychosocial-risk (GRO/PGR) module or mobile/offline use was not checked.

## Momentum
Appears active (recent blog content on eSocial changes); no sign of shutdown or acquisition found. Unverified.

## Opening
Only a narrow one, if any: a cheap, simple NR-1 psychosocial-risk tool for micro/small employers, pending confirmation that RSData lacks it and is priced above that segment.
