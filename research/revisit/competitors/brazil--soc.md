# Brazil: SOC (SOC Software de Saude e Seguranca do Trabalho, AGE Technology)

Idea killed: C0135 (eSocial health-and-safety events, NR-1 psychosocial-risk programmes). Searches used: 5 of 6.

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- SOC is a dominant occupational health and safety (SST) platform. A Senior Sistemas page (senior.com.br/noticias/soc) reports it manages 200k+ companies and that the developer estimates about 70% of SST service providers use it (vendor claim, unverified independently). Whether Senior owns SOC was not confirmed (unverified).
- It sends SST events to eSocial online and targets service providers and SESMT units (b2bstack.com.br/product/soc-software-de-saude-e-seguranca-do-trabalho).
- Complaints: none found. Searched Reclame Aqui, G2 and Capterra in Portuguese and English. The only review seen was one on B2BStack rating ease of use, support and cost-benefit 5.0 and features 4.0. That is one review, so not meaningful. No outage or failed-filing news found.
- NR-1 psychosocial-risk module: not confirmed. Searches did not show SOC's NR-1 psychosocial features (unverified). The rule is live: the Ministry of Labour published Q&A on NR-1 chapter 1.5 in May 2026 (findes.com.br PDF).
- Momentum: Senior Sistemas bought Salu, an occupational-health HRtech, for R$ 318.7M in June 2026 (baguete.com.br). This shows consolidation in the segment; its link to SOC is unverified.

## Pricing
Not published. B2BStack lists "R$ 0,00" starting price, which probably means quote on request (unverified). No minimums found.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at SST providers and SESMT, so likely heavy for micro and small employers (inference, unverified).
- No evidence found of gaps in eSocial filing, language or mobile use.
- Psychosocial-risk (NR-1) depth unknown.

## Opening
Little in eSocial SST events; any opening is a lightweight NR-1 psychosocial-risk tool for small employers, and that is unproven.
