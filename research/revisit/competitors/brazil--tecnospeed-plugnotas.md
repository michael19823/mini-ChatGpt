# TecnoSpeed / PlugNotas (Brazil)

Dropped idea: C0134, 2026 tax-reform invoice changes (CBS/IBS on NF-e, national NFS-e).
Searches used: 3 of 6.

## Verdict: strong

PlugNotas already ships CBS/IBS calculation inside its NF-e/NFC-e flow, has a large installed base, and targets software houses. It is not a thin competitor for an API/integration play.

## Evidence
- PlugNotas is an API for NF-e, NFC-e, NFS-e, NFCom and MDF-e. It says it is used by more than 4,100 integrated software houses, covers more than 2,200 municipalities and has 20 years in the market (self-reported). Source: https://tecnospeed.com.br/en/plugdfe/plugnotas/ and https://blog.tecnospeed.com.br/ (search snippets).
- TecnoSpeed integrated the Tax Reform Calculator into PlugNotas for automatic CBS/IBS calculation, with no need for the customer to build tax logic or call the Federal Revenue API. It reports 61,880 test-environment documents (61,300 NFC-e, 580 NF-e) from 153 CNPJs and 21 software houses. Source: https://convergenciadigital.com.br/mercado/reforma-tributaria-mais-de-61-mil-notas-fiscais-foram-emitidas-no-ambiente-de-testes/
- It publishes tax-reform content (NFS-e Nacional mandatory for Simples Nacional in November 2026, debit/credit notes, automation). Source: https://blog.tecnospeed.com.br/tag/reformatributaria/
- Momentum: actively developed (NFCom, tax-reform calculator, iVia).
- Complaints, Reclame Aqui for TecnoSpeed as a whole: reputation "Regular", 6.6/10 over 6 months, 103 complaints, 100% answered, 70.7% resolved, consumer score 4.76 (41 ratings), 53.7% would do business again, average response time 12 days. Source: https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/tecnospeed/lista-reclamacoes/
  - Complaint titles mention service instability and improper charges, tickets open since November without a solution, delayed support responses, and a Bradesco boleto integration failure (a different product, not PlugNotas). Sources: https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/tecnospeed/reclamacao-de-cliente-cnpj-instabilidade-de-servicos-cobrancas-indevidas-e-falta-de-solucao-pela-tecnospeed_upc21N97RxpBtDeb/ and https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/tecnospeed/tecnospeed-falta-de-retorno-e-solucao-para-tickets-abertos-desde-novembro-impacta-clientes-e-operacao_v19pyqXlyjJHKaEa/
  - I did not read the complaints themselves, only the titles and the search summary. Which complaints are about PlugNotas specifically is unverified.
- No app-store, G2, Capterra, Reddit or Facebook-group results were searched.

## Pricing
Not found in search results. Unverified. Pricing is not published in the pages I saw. It looks quote- or plan-based and aimed at software houses.

## Fit gaps
- It is B2B for software houses and integrators. A direct small-business buyer (small merchant or accountant) is not its main audience (inferred).
- Support is slow, with an average response time of 12 days on Reclame Aqui, and there are stability complaints.
- Price transparency for small volumes is unverified.

## Opening
A tax-reform engine as an API is covered. The opening is only support and price for small direct buyers (accountants, micro-businesses) who are poorly served by a software-house-focused vendor, and that is weak.
