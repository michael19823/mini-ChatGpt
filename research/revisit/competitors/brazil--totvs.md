# TOTVS (Brazil)

Dropped idea: C0134, 2026 tax-reform invoice changes (CBS/IBS on NF-e, national NFS-e).
Searches used: 3 of 6 (Portuguese and English).

## Verdict: strong

TOTVS is the incumbent ERP vendor for mid and large Brazilian firms and is shipping tax-reform updates to its whole ERP line. The gap it leaves is the small buyer and the bad support experience, which is real but narrow.

## Evidence
- TOTVS is adapting Protheus, RM, Datasul, Winthor and Consinco to the new fiscal model, with releases and update schedules. It also launched a tax-reform chatbot and uses its assistant "Carol" for customer questions. Source: https://www.reformatributaria.com/brasil/totvs-lanca-chatbot-especialista-para-apoiar-empresas-na-transicao-da-reforma-tributaria/
- Deadline context: IBS/CBS groups on NF-e/NFC-e become mandatory for Normal Regime companies from 2026-08-03. A later notice reports rejection of notes without IBS/CBS was postponed or made flexible. Sources: https://sovos.com/pt-br/blog/tributos/nf-e-nfc-e-sem-ibs-cbs-rejeitadas/ and https://contadores.cnt.br/projetos/54/noticias/tecnicas/2026/08/04/reforma-tributaria-rejeicao-de-notas-sem-ibs-e-cbs-e-adiada.html
- Complaints on Reclame Aqui (search snippets only, not read in full; the third is a customer cancelling over support quality and cost):
  - Poor post-implementation experience and migration to Cloud Prime: https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/totvs/experiencia-extremamente-negativa-com-totvs-cloud-protheus-e-migracao-para-cloud-prime_1RdhARLDuDL5CuIo/
  - Cancellation complaint over falling support quality and high monthly fee for limited functionality: https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/totvs/reclamacao-sobre-o-atendimento-e-processo-de-cancelamento-do-contrato-totvs_B3O-R5YyBEikjGsy/
- B2BStack reviews summarise that cost can be high for smaller firms and support could be faster: https://www.b2bstack.com.br/product/totvs-erp/avaliacoes
- No complaints specific to the tax-reform or CBS/IBS updates were found. None found does not mean none exist.

## Pricing
- Capterra lists a "starts at R$1.00" placeholder and says to contact TOTVS (not a real price): https://capterra.com/p/191104/TOTVS-BI/
- One snippet cites a SaaS tier at R$ 99 per user per month. The source and product were unclear, so unverified.
- Core ERP pricing is quote-based and typically needs implementation work (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at medium and large companies (per Capterra/GetApp style listings).
- Costly for micro and small firms; support is described as slow.
- Requires an ERP install or migration. A lightweight tool that only fixes invoice issuance for CBS/IBS, or that works with whatever ERP or accounting software the firm already has, is not covered.
- Customers on other ERPs are not served.

## Opening
Small firms, accountants and users of non-TOTVS software who need a cheap, simple CBS/IBS NF-e and NFS-e compliance layer, without buying or migrating an ERP.
