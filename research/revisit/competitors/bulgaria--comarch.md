# Comarch (Bulgaria) - competitor check for C0147 (mandatory e-invoicing, NISSEF, 2028)

## Verdict: beatable

Comarch is a large international EDI/e-invoicing vendor aimed at enterprises. Its Bulgarian offer is B2G and EDI based, with no visible small-business NISSEF product. The 2028 mandate is still only a bill.

## Evidence
- Comarch says it has an operational Bulgarian solution that sends e-invoices to public administration, supports EDI, and is "monitoring" the planned mandate. Source: https://www.comarch.com/trade-and-services/data-management/e-invoicing/e-invoicing-in-bulgaria/
- It has held Bulgarian conferences on EDI and e-invoicing (e.g. https://comarch.com/trade-and-services/news/other/e-invoice-in-bulgaria-join-us-at-the-conference-edi-for-e-invoicing-strategic-decision-immediate-effect). This shows interest, not a shipped NISSEF integration. I found no evidence of one (unverified either way).
- Regulatory status: a bill for mandatory structured e-invoicing from 1 Jan 2028 via NISSEF (run by the NRA, with third-party software allowed to transmit to it). It is in public consultation until 23 Oct 2026 and is not yet law. Source: https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/6021-bulgaria-proposes-mandatory-e-invoicing-and-real-time-vat-reporting-from-2028 and https://marosavat.com/vat-news/e-invoicing-bulgaria
- Complaints: a search-result summary of G2 says Comarch EDI and e-Invoicing has 4.4/5 from 17 reviews. Reviewers praise ease of use and global compliance. Minor complaints: complex pricing for smaller businesses and a cluttered UI. Source: https://g2.com/products/comarch-edi-and-e-invoicing/reviews (I read only the search summary, not the reviews). No Bulgaria-specific complaints, outages or failed filings found. No Bulgarian-language complaints found.
- Momentum: active (conferences, maintained pages, continuing G2 reviews). Not stagnant, not acquired.

## Pricing
Not published for Bulgaria. G2 pricing page exists (https://www.g2.com/products/comarch-edi/pricing) but I did not see figures. Unverified. Complexity for small buyers is flagged in reviews.

## Fit gaps
- Enterprise EDI orientation (130,000+ connected entities, 60+ countries per Comarch), so likely overkill and costly for micro and small Bulgarian firms (inference).
- No evidence of a NISSEF-specific, Bulgarian-language, SME-priced product (unverified).
- Complex pricing and cluttered UI noted by some G2 reviewers.

## Opening
A cheap, Bulgarian-language, plug-and-play NISSEF/EN 16931 tool for micro and small VAT-registered firms and accountants is not served by Comarch's enterprise EDI, though the law is not yet final and local accounting software vendors were not checked here.
