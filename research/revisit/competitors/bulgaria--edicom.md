# EDICOM (Bulgaria) - idea C0147: Mandatory e-invoicing (NISSEF, 2028)

## Verdict: weak
(Moderate-low confidence: all 6 searches used; no Bulgaria-specific EDICOM material found.)

## Evidence
- EDICOM is a SaaS platform for e-invoicing, tax reporting and B2B data exchange aimed at multinationals that need country-specific compliance (source: https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/edicom).
- Review volume is tiny: G2 4.5/5 from 2 reviews, Gartner Peer Insights 4.3/5 from 4 reviews. Feedback is sparse and positive (EDI integration, time saved) (same source). No complaints found.
- Bulgaria's NISSEF mandate is still a draft: Ministry of Finance published the VAT Act amendment 23 Sep 2026, consultation open to 23 Oct 2026, start 1 Jan 2028, EN 16931 format. Invoices can be issued in NISSEF directly or via third-party software that transmits to NISSEF in real time (https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/6021-bulgaria-proposes-mandatory-e-invoicing-and-real-time-vat-reporting-from-2028; https://sovos.com/regulatory-updates/global-vat/bulgaria-proposes-mandatory-e-invoicing-for-domestic-supplies/).
- Bulgarian-language and English searches (6 total) found no Bulgarian page, local office, Bulgarian customer or NISSEF connector for EDICOM; results said nothing specific about EDICOM in Bulgaria. Local presence: unverified.
- EDICOM is a Peppol Access Point and eIDAS trusted service provider, and an official e-invoicing provider in Croatia (https://www.edicomgroup.com/b2b-einvoicing-spain and search summary; Croatia claim from search snippet, unverified in detail). It could extend to Bulgaria, but nothing found says it has.
- Bulgarian rollout: no phased roll-out by company size from 1 Jan 2028; B2G e-invoicing already mandatory since Nov 2019 (https://digtechs.com/bulgaria-e-invoicing/?lang=en). Other international providers (e.g. Editel, econnect) publish Bulgaria pages, so EDICOM is not alone in the field.
- Momentum: unverified from searches; no outage or failed-filing news found.

## Pricing
Not published in anything found (unverified). Enterprise/quote-based positioning is implied by the multinational target, not confirmed.

## Fit gaps
- Targets multinationals, not Bulgarian micro/small VAT payers (inferred from vendor description).
- No evidence of a Bulgarian-language UI, local support or NISSEF integration (unverified).
- The mandate is not yet law, so no vendor can have a live NISSEF integration yet.

## Opening
A cheap, Bulgarian-language, NISSEF-ready invoicing tool for small VAT-registered firms looks unserved by EDICOM, though local competitors (not checked here) may matter more.
