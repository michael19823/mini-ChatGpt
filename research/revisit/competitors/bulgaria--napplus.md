# NAPplus (Bulgaria) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0144, SAF-T reporting for SMEs and accounting firms.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (low confidence; the search was cut short)

Only 1 of the 6 allowed searches returned results. The second failed with a usage-limit error, so the remaining searches were not run.

## Evidence
- The search "NAPplus SAF-T България" returned no product, vendor or page called NAPplus. Every result was about NAP, the Bulgarian National Revenue Agency (Национална агенция за приходите). "NAPplus" may be a mislabel of NAP's own government SAF-T service. This is unverified.
- NAP offers a free electronic service for submitting SAF-T, "Подаване на стандартен одитен файл за данъчни цели (SAF-T)". It is in the SAF-T category of NAP's e-services portal and requires a qualified electronic signature (QES). Source: https://kik-info.com/novini/nap/NAP-vnedri-nova-elektronna-usluga-za-podavane-na.15190.php
- Rollout, per the search summary and not checked against NAP's own page:
  - It is phased from 2026 to 2030.
  - Phase 1, from January 2026, covers large enterprises with turnover over 300 million BGN or tax liabilities over 3.5 million BGN.
  - Monthly files are due by the 14th of the following month.
  - Annual files are submitted with the annual return.
  - Sources: https://kik-info.com/novini/nap/NAP-podgotvya-vavezhdaneto-na-mezhdunarodniya-standart-za-schetovodno.14658.php and https://www.ey.com/bg_bg/insights/tax/ey-workshop--saf-t---the-new-mandatory-standard-for-data-exchang
- Complaints: none found. No reviews, outage reports or forum threads were searched for or seen, because the searches ran out.

## Pricing
- No commercial pricing found for any product called NAPplus.
- The NAP e-service appears to carry no fee beyond the QES. This is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- If NAPplus means the NAP portal, it is a submission channel only. It does not appear to produce SAF-T from an SME's books, which accounting software and ERP vendors must do. This is unverified.
- The portal needs a QES.
- Phase 1 targets large taxpayers, so SMEs are not yet obliged to file. Their later phases, expected up to 2030, are the window for a tool.

## Opening
Nothing under the name NAPplus blocks this idea. The real field to check is Bulgarian accounting and ERP vendors that add SAF-T export, and a free NAP submission service that would still need generated files.
