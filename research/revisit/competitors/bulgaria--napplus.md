# NAPplus (Bulgaria) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0144, SAF-T reporting for SMEs and accounting firms.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Four searches returned results, in English and Bulgarian. None found a product, vendor or page called NAPplus. One more search failed with a usage-limit error. The remaining searches were not used.

## Evidence
- "NAPplus SAF-T България", "NAPplus счетоводен софтуер отзиви" and "NAPplus НАП данъци програма" found no product named NAPplus. The results were about NAP, the Bulgarian National Revenue Agency, and unrelated "NAP" or "NonProfitPlus" products. "NAPplus" is probably a mislabel of NAP's own SAF-T service (unverified).
- NAP runs its own electronic service for submitting SAF-T, in the "SAF-T" category of its e-services portal, with a qualified electronic signature (QES). Source: https://kik-info.com/novini/nap/NAP-vnedri-nova-elektronna-usluga-za-podavane-na.15190.php
- Rollout phases, per search summaries (not checked against NAP's own page):
  - Large taxpayers (turnover over 300 million BGN or tax liabilities over 3.5 million BGN) from January 2026.
  - Medium enterprises (net revenue over 15 million BGN or tax liabilities over 1.5 million BGN) from January 2028.
  - All remaining taxpayers, including small VAT-registered businesses, from January 2030, with some exceptions such as micro-enterprises.
  - Sources: https://www.comarch.com/trade-and-services/data-management/legal-regulation-changes/bulgaria-to-mandate-saf-t-reporting-starting-in-2026/ and https://www.meridianglobalservices.com/blog/2023/10/04/bulgaria-saf-t
- Other vendors seen in results, not assessed:
  - EY SAP SAF-T solution: https://www.ey.com/bg_bg/insights/tax/new-ey-sap-saf-t-solution
  - Comarch.
- Complaints: none found, because there is no product to complain about.

## Pricing
- No NAPplus pricing exists in the results.
- The search for SAF-T software prices for small firms found none.
- The NAP portal service appears to have no fee beyond the QES (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- If NAPplus means the NAP portal, it only receives files and does not appear to generate SAF-T from SME books (unverified).
- It needs a QES.
- SMEs have no obligation until 2028 (medium) or 2030 (small VAT-registered), so demand arrives later.

## Opening
No NAPplus product blocks this idea. The real competition to check is accounting and ERP vendors adding SAF-T export (such as EY's SAP solution and Comarch). SMEs are only obliged from 2028 or 2030, so timing matters more than competition.
