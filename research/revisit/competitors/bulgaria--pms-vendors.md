# Bulgaria: PMS vendors (generic, no URL)

Dropped idea: C0148 (SAF-T, sales-management software under Ordinance Н-18 / СУПТО, likely ЕСТИ).
Searches used: 5 of 6 (Bulgarian and English).

## Verdict: beatable (low confidence)

Generic PMS vendors are a real but fragmented set of competitors. Small-property compliance gaps are plausible, but the searches found no direct evidence of them. Several of the drop reasons are regulatory, not competitive (see below).

## Evidence
- ЕСТИ is mandatory electronic guest and stay reporting for all accommodation, regardless of size. Several PMS and guest-compliance vendors already offer automated ЕСТИ reporting: Chekin, Charge Automation and others. Sources: https://support.chekin.com/en/bulgaria-unified-tourist-information-system-esti , https://chargeautomation.com/guest-compliance/bulgaria-esti-live/
- Local PMS vendors exist (Anolla, https://anolla.com/bg/softuer-za-xoteli ) and so do international ones (Clock Software has a Sofia hotel customer page, https://clock-software.com/customers/hotel-central-sofia ). They are active; I did not verify product details or release cadence.
- СУПТО (Н-18) requires sales software that issues fiscal receipts to be declared on an НАП public list. This is a compliance and certification barrier, not a competitor. A PMS that issues fiscal receipts must be declared. Sources: https://kik-info.com/novini/novini-i-akcenti/Otnosno-softuerite-za-upravlenie-na-prodazhbite-i-deklariraneto.143029.php , https://www.minfin.bg/upload/46676/02+Motivi_Naredba+N-18.pdf . I did not find hotel-specific guidance.
- SAF-T is phased in: large taxpayers from January 2026, medium from 2028, everyone else from 2030. Small firms are not obliged in 2026. Sources: https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/bg/pdf/bg-TN-2025-03-27-SAFT-BG.pdf , https://www.ey.com/bg_bg/insights/tax/_-_-saf-t--_-_-_------ . I did not open these pages; this is from search summaries.
- Complaints: none found specific to Bulgaria or to Bulgarian PMS vendors. Capterra reviews of international PMS products (Cloudbeds support, eZee, SMS|Host integration) exist, but none mention ЕСТИ, СУПТО or Bulgaria. Not relevant evidence for this market. Example source: https://www.capterra.com/p/158839/Cloudbeds/reviews/?page=7
- No Bulgarian-language forum, Facebook group or app-store complaints surfaced.

## Pricing
Published Bulgarian pricing not found. Anolla's site says price depends on room count, modules and integrations, with no figures in the search results. Pricing: unverified.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. Possible gaps, all unverified: weak ЕСТИ support at the cheapest tiers, no СУПТО declaration for fiscal-receipt flows, and enterprise orientation of SAF-T tooling (SAP and EY solutions target large firms).
- SAF-T is not required for small properties until 2030, so there is little near-term demand.

## Opening
A small-property tool bundling ЕСТИ reporting with fiscal-receipt compliance could fit, but ЕСТИ is already covered by existing vendors and SAF-T/СУПТО are regulatory or certification barriers. The opening is thin and unproven.
