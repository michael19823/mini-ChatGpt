# SAF-T Bridge (Bulgaria) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0144 (SAF-T reporting for SMEs and accounting firms). Searches run: 4 of 6.

## Verdict
not-a-real-competitor

## Evidence
- A search for the exact phrase "SAF-T Bridge" returned no product, vendor or site of that name. The results were an unrelated Philippine news story and generic SAF-T explainers. The name is unverified and may be a generic label rather than a real product.
- No app-store reviews, G2/Capterra/Trustpilot entries, forum threads or outage news were found for it. No complaints found, because there is no product to complain about.
- The real Bulgarian SAF-T offerings that surfaced are SAP-focused add-ons and advisory services:
  - SNI SAF-T Bulgaria (SAP-integrated): https://platform.softwareone.com/product/sni-saf-t-bulgaria/PCP-2907-6568
  - KGT add-on for SAP: https://www.sap.com/products/financial-management/partners/key-group-tax-performance-advisory-bv-kgt-addon-for-saft-bulgaria-seamless-compliance-validation-and-automated-reporting.html
  - SB consulting and filing service: https://sb-bg.com/saf-t-reporting/
- Rollout timeline, from search summaries (not checked against NAP primary sources, so partly unverified):
  - 2026: very large taxpayers (about 500 firms).
  - 2028: medium enterprises.
  - 2030: all remaining taxpayers.
- Small firms are therefore not yet obliged to file. The SME need is still years away, which weakens the idea's urgency.
- A search for local accounting software and SAF-T modules (Microinvest, Business Navigator, Firma.bg) found general mentions only. An apideck page says Plus Minus and Ajur hold the largest share and Microinvest and Eltrade dominate POS segments. None of these had pricing or reviews in the results. Their SAF-T readiness is unverified.

## Pricing
No published pricing found for any SAF-T product. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
Not assessable for "SAF-T Bridge". The SAP add-ons found (SNI, KGT) target ERP users and large firms, not SMEs or accounting firms. This is inferred from the product descriptions and is unverified.

## Opening
No named competitor blocks C0144. The SME and accounting-firm SAF-T segment looks open, but demand is gated by the 2028-2030 phase-in and by whether the incumbent local accounting suites add SAF-T modules.

## Momentum
Unknown. No evidence the product exists.
