# Bulgaria: SAF-T vendors (idea C0148: SAF-T, СУПТО under Н-18, likely ЕСТИ)

Research limited: only 1 of 6 searches succeeded (the others returned "usage limit"). Most findings are unverified.

## Verdict: weak (low confidence)

No specific vendor was identified, and the SAF-T obligation for small firms is years away. The phase-in dates come from one search summary, not from a page I read.

## Evidence
- Phased SAF-T rollout in Bulgaria, as stated in the search summary (unverified beyond that): large taxpayers (turnover over 300m BGN or tax liabilities over 3.5m BGN) from January 2026; medium firms (over 15m BGN) from January 2028; all others from January 2030. Sources: https://www.ey.com/bg_bg/insights/tax/saf-t-is-introduced-with-the-state-budget-act-for-2025 , https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/bg/pdf/bg-TN-2025-03-27-SAFT-BG.pdf , https://nra.bg/wps/portal/nra/actualno/nap-yvedomqva-golemi-danukoplatci-za-promeni-pri-deklariraneto-ot-dogodina
- Large ERP vendors reportedly ship their own SAF-T modules (unverified; a search summary, no page read). Big-4 advisers (EY, KPMG) cover the topic for large firms.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, forum or review-site complaints were retrieved, and the searches did not cover them.
- СУПТО (Н-18) software vendors and ЕСТИ: not researched (searches failed). Unverified.

## Pricing
Not found. No published vendor prices were retrieved.

## Fit gaps
- Unverified. The likely gap is that SAF-T tooling targets the large firms obliged first, and small firms do not have to file until 2030.
- Small-business SAF-T tooling probably does not exist yet, but this is not confirmed.

## Opening
The mandate hits small firms only from 2030 (unverified), so any SAF-T-for-SMEs product has a long wait. Н-18/СУПТО and ЕСТИ need a re-check before judging them. A re-run with working search is advised.
