# CheckinMe (Cambodia)

Dropped idea: C0164, generic Cambodian payroll and NSSF calculation.

## Verdict: strong

CheckinMe is a real, funded, award-winning Cambodian product. It already does payroll, payroll tax and NSSF to Cambodian rules, bundled with attendance, at a low price for small firms.

## Evidence
- Its payroll page says it calculates salary, overtime, late/early deductions, payroll tax and NSSF "compliant with Cambodian law". It also says it makes digital payslips (app or Telegram) and bank-ready payment files for major Cambodian banks. Source: https://www.checkinme.app/payrolls (search-result summary, not opened directly).
- The main site claims "5,000+ companies" as customers. This is a self-claim, unverified. Source: https://checkinme.app/
- Momentum: it has Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications support, won the Minister's Award for Digital Startup 2022 at the Cambodia Digital Awards, and joined Plug and Play. Cambodia Investment Review (2026-06-20) reports it reached the Asia-Pacific final of a JCI Creative Young Entrepreneur competition. A 2023 interview with the CEO and co-founder, Ley Kamthong, covers 10x customer growth and regional expansion. A Lao version exists at lao.checkinme.app. Sources: https://cambodiainvestmentreview.com/2026/06/20/cambodian-startup-checkinme-reaches-asia-pacific-final-as-kingdoms-tech-ecosystem-gains-regional-momentum/ and https://cambodiainvestmentreview.com/2023/07/18/rising-giants-e93-ley-kamthong-discusses-10x-customer-growth-regional-expansion-plans-for-checkinme/
- Complaints: none found. Four searches (English and Khmer) turned up no app-store, Reddit, Facebook or news complaints, and no outage reports. A search result lists an App Store page (https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/checkinme-hr-solution/id1579633736), but I did not read its reviews. Absence of evidence only; review sites were not checked directly.

## Pricing (from the search summary of the official site)
- Basic: $15/month, $50 setup, up to 30 members.
- Standard: $30/month, $100 setup, up to 40 members.
- Both include attendance, payroll and sales activity. Prices are not independently verified.
- This is already cheap for small buyers, so there is little room to undercut it.

## Fit gaps (all unverified inference)
- It is bundled with attendance, so a firm that wants only standalone payroll/NSSF calculation, with no check-in app, may find it bundled. Per-member caps of 30 and 40 may not suit larger SMEs.
- No evidence found on direct NSSF portal filing, which looks like calculation only.
- No evidence found on multi-entity support or on accountant/bureau use across many clients.
- It is a QR/mobile attendance product first, with payroll as a feature.

## Opening
A narrow opening exists only for accountant and bookkeeper firms running payroll for many clients, or for NSSF filing and reconciliation. A generic payroll/NSSF calculator is already covered by CheckinMe at $15-30/month.
