# EDICOM (Cambodia) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0161 CamInvoice e-invoicing connector for SMEs.
Searches used: 3 of 6 (English and Khmer).

## Verdict: beatable

EDICOM is a real, active vendor with a Cambodia page. It is an enterprise and multinational platform, and nothing found shows it targeting Cambodian SMEs.

## Evidence
- EDICOM publishes a Cambodia e-invoicing page and blog posts in several languages. They say its platform connects by API to the CamInvoice portal to issue and receive e-invoices, and that it converts ERP data into the national format. Source: https://edicomgroup.com/electronic-invoicing/cambodia and https://edicomgroup.com/blog/cambodia-clearance-electronic-invoicing-system
- Search summaries say EDICOM acts as a CamInvoice service-provider member. I did not see the official provider list, so this is unverified. Source: the same EDICOM pages, via search summary.
- The same summaries say EDICOM covers PDF with QR code delivery and 10-year archiving (3 years for small taxpayers).
- Background on CamInvoice: B2G soft launch in December 2024, B2B voluntary now and expected to become mandatory for select businesses in 2026, with wider expansion (including B2C) by 2027. Source: https://sharedserviceslink.com/news/cambodia-launches-centralized-e-invoicing-caminvoice (from a search summary, not read in full).
- Global reviews: a search summary gives EDICOM 4.5 on G2 and 4.3 on Gartner Peer Insights. The same summary says fit is clearest for mid-to-large multinationals, and that implementation complexity and a learning curve can slow smaller IT teams. Sources: https://www.gartner.com/reviews/product/edicom-b2b-cloud-platform and https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/edicom. Both are secondhand search summaries and I did not read the pages.
- Complaints: none found specific to Cambodia, in English or Khmer. I found no app-store, Reddit or Facebook reviews and no outage news. This is an absence of evidence, not a clean record.
- Momentum: active. It has multilingual Cambodia content, and the CamInvoice mandate is still rolling out.

## Pricing
- No published price. A search summary describes quote-based subscription pricing by volume and modules, and says opaque pricing frustrates buyers. I found no Cambodia price or minimum, so any SME price comparison is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Enterprise and multinational positioning; no SME self-serve offering seen (unverified for Cambodia).
- No evidence found of Khmer-language UI or support, or of local SME onboarding. This is unverified; absence in a search is not proof.
- The government's own CamInvoice portal and other local providers may already cover small taxpayers. I did not check this.

## Opening
A cheap, Khmer-first, self-serve CamInvoice connector for small taxpayers is not served by an enterprise vendor like EDICOM. The risk is the free government portal and local providers, which this check did not cover.
