# MiHCM (Cambodia)

Dropped idea: C0164, generic Cambodian payroll and NSSF calculation.

## Verdict: strong

MiHCM is a real, actively marketed HCM and payroll product. It has a dedicated Cambodia payroll page and guide that cover NSSF, PIT and seniority pay. Gaps for a small local buyer exist, but they are not proven.

Research note: 4 of the 6 searches were used. The 4th (a Khmer-language search) failed with a usage limit error, so no Khmer-language results were gathered.

## Evidence
- MiHCM's Cambodia payroll page says it handles NSSF, PIT and seniority pay. It claims NSSF Pension, Risk and Health rates are applied with the KHR 1,200,000 wage ceiling, resident and non-resident PIT, and semi-monthly pay runs. This is the vendor's own claim, not independently verified. Sources: https://mihcm.com/mm/en-kh/solutions/payroll/ and https://mihcm.com/resources/guides/cambodia-payroll-guide/
- Capterra: 3.8/5 from 26 users, per a search summary. One verified 1-star review (Philippines context) complained that support took 2 weeks to over 1 month to resolve issues, and that reports use jargon uncommon locally. Source: https://www.capterra.co.uk/reviews/138958/microimage-hcm-cloud
- G2: 4.8 from 5 reviews. One reviewer said it can be slow. Source: https://www.g2.com/products/mihcm/reviews
- No Cambodia-specific complaints, outage reports or failed-filing news were found. This is a valid result, but coverage was thin and no Khmer search ran. Unverified.
- Momentum: actively maintained. It has localized guides for many countries, and the G2 profile showed a review about two months before the search. No acquisition or shutdown found.

## Pricing
- No public price. It is quote-based, scaled by employee count and modules. Implementation and support are billed separately. A secondary aggregator summary says there is a 20-employee minimum (unverified; source: https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/mihcm).
- The price for small Cambodian businesses is unknown. A minimum and a quote-only model likely exclude micro-SMEs.

## Fit gaps (partly unverified)
- Aimed at mid-size and regional firms, not micro-SMEs (inferred from the quote-only model and the 20-employee minimum).
- Support responsiveness complained about (one review, not Cambodia).
- Full HCM suite, probably heavier than a simple payroll and NSSF tool. Unverified.
- No evidence found of Khmer-language UI, a Khmer-format NSSF filing export, or an offline or mobile-first option. Unverified.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, Khmer-language NSSF and payroll tool for firms under about 20 staff could fit below MiHCM's quote-only floor. This depends on verifying its Khmer support and pricing first.
