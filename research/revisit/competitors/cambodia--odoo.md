# Odoo (Cambodia) - competitor check

Killed idea: C0164 Generic Cambodian payroll and NSSF calculation.
Searches used: 2 of 6 (the third failed with a usage-limit error, so research is incomplete).

## Verdict: weak (low confidence)

Odoo has no Cambodian payroll or NSSF localization that I could find. It is a generic ERP and does not directly serve a small Cambodian employer's NSSF needs.

## Evidence
- An English search for Odoo Cambodia payroll/NSSF returned only dedicated payroll tools (MiHCM, CheckinMe, Playroll and others). None of the results mentioned an Odoo Cambodian payroll module. Source: https://mihcm.com/resources/guides/cambodia-payroll-guide/ , https://checkinme.app/payrolls
- A Khmer-language search surfaced an Odoo community app, "l10n_kh_vat_return" (Odoo 19.0), which covers VAT return filing and not payroll. Source: https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_kh_vat_return
- That search also returned payroll modules for other countries (Oman, Philippines) but none for Cambodia. Absence from search results is not proof that no module exists. Unverified.
- Complaints: none found. I did not reach app-store, G2, Capterra, Reddit or Facebook sources because searches ran out.
- Momentum: the Cambodia VAT module is listed for Odoo 19.0, which suggests some active local accounting localization. Unverified beyond that listing.

## Pricing
Not verified. No Odoo pricing was retrieved. Odoo's payroll is a paid Enterprise module, and I did not confirm Cambodian-specific pricing or minimums. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred, mostly unverified)
- No evidence of an NSSF contribution, Tax on Salary or seniority pay rule set for Cambodia.
- A general ERP implementation likely needs a paid partner and is probably heavy for a small Cambodian business. Unverified.
- Khmer-language payslip and NSSF filing formats were not confirmed. Unverified.

## Opening
A lightweight, Khmer-friendly payroll tool with built-in NSSF, Tax on Salary and seniority rules is not covered by Odoo. The real competition is dedicated tools such as MiHCM and CheckinMe, not Odoo.
