# Ontop (Cambodia) - competitor check for C0164 (generic Cambodian payroll and NSSF calculation)

## Verdict: weak

Ontop is a global EOR/contractor-payment platform for foreign companies hiring in Cambodia. It is not a local payroll tool for Cambodian SMEs. Its Cambodia page covers NSSF, but the product is built for cross-border hiring.

## Evidence
- Ontop has a Cambodia payroll page claiming AI payroll calculation, NSSF handling, payslips, seniority indemnity and garment CBA rules, "without setting up a local entity" (https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/cambodia). This is vendor marketing and the claims are unverified.
- Its model is hiring in 150+ countries via contractor or EOR plans (same page; TechRepublic review https://www.techrepublic.com/article/ontop-payroll-review/). This targets foreign employers, not Cambodian businesses running their own staff payroll.
- Ratings reported by a search summary (unverified, not checked at source): Trustpilot 3.7/5 (about 100 reviews), Capterra 4.6/5 (160), G2 4.1/5 (64). Summary says Trustpilot skews to contractor/employee experience, with some negative employee reviews and a perceived lack of transparency. I did not see individual review text.
- Reddit and forum complaint search returned nothing specific about Ontop. No Cambodia-specific complaints, outages or failed filings found.
- Local alternatives appear in the same searches: CheckinMe (https://checkinme.app/payrolls) claims NSSF-compliant payroll, Telegram/app payslips and 5,000+ companies (unverified vendor claim). MIHCM (https://mihcm.com/mm/en-kh/solutions/payroll/) is enterprise-focused. These are the real competitors to a local SME payroll idea, not Ontop.
- Khmer-language search returned no Ontop results.
- Momentum: active marketing in 2026, with 2026 pricing and reviews published. No sign of shutdown or acquisition.

## Pricing
- Reported by review sites (unverified): Contractor Starter $29/month (10 or fewer contractors), Contractors $49/month, EOR from $499/month (per employee, per the usual EOR model; not confirmed). Source: TechRepublic review, Subscribed.fyi (https://subscribed.fyi/ontop/reviews/), Capterra listing.
- A $499/month EOR fee is far above what a small Cambodian firm would pay for local payroll software. The contractor tier is cheap but is not payroll with NSSF filing.

## Fit gaps
- Serves foreign companies hiring in Cambodia, not local employers with their own entity.
- Requires USD-based EOR/contractor payments flow; no evidence of Khmer language UI, NSSF portal filing format, or bank payment files for local banks (unverified, absent from sources seen).
- No evidence of Telegram/mobile payslip delivery of the kind local tools offer.
- No evidence of multi-branch or factory (garment) attendance/overtime integration.

## Opening
A Khmer-language, low-cost payroll and NSSF calculator for local SMEs is not served by Ontop. The competition to check is CheckinMe and similar local tools.
