# RTC Suite (Cambodia) - competitor check

Killed idea: C0161, CamInvoice e-invoicing connector for SMEs.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

RTC Suite is a global, ERP-centred tax-compliance cloud platform. In the searches run, its only Cambodia connection was blog and tag pages about Cambodia e-invoicing. I found no sign of a Cambodian SME product or local customers.

## Evidence
- Its site describes a cloud platform for global tax compliance for organisations using various ERP systems. It automates the creation and submission of compliance documents. It also says it is an SAP partner and a Peppol/GENA member. Sources: https://rtcsuite.com/es/empresa/ , https://saasbrowser.com/hi/saas/5471/rtc-suite
- It has Cambodia tag pages, including coverage of the Voluntary B2G e-invoicing system introduced 12 Dec 2024. These look like content marketing, not evidence of a live Cambodian product. Sources: https://rtcsuite.com/tag/cambodia-e-invoicing-system/ , https://rtcsuite.com/tag/b2g-e-invoicing-in-cambodia/ , https://rtcsuite.com/tag/digital-tax-compliance-cambodia/
- Complaints: none found. The search results had no G2, Capterra, app-store or forum reviews. A G2 pricing page exists (https://www.g2.com/products/rtc-suite/pricing), but the search snippet showed no reviews.
- Search coverage: one Khmer query and one English query returned results. A third query (English, "RTC Suite Cambodia e-invoicing CamInvoice") failed on a usage limit. Local-language coverage is thin.

## Pricing
Not published (G2 says contact the vendor). Unverified beyond that.

## Fit gaps (inferred, partly unverified)
- It targets ERP users such as SAP-centric mid-market and enterprise firms, so it is unlikely to suit a Cambodian SME.
- I did not verify whether it has a certified connector to the Cambodian tax authority's (GDT) system, or Khmer-language support. Both are unverified.

## Opening
The SME segment of Cambodian e-invoicing looks unserved by RTC Suite, since it is an enterprise ERP-integration vendor. The caveat is that the GDT system itself and local accounting vendors are the real competition for C0161, not RTC Suite.
