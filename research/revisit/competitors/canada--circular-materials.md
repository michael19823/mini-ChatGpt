# Circular Materials (Canada)

Dropped idea: C0168, Multi-province packaging EPR workbench. 5 of 6 searches used.

## Verdict
beatable

## Evidence
- Circular Materials is a producer responsibility organization (PRO), not a software vendor. It runs the "WeRecycle" portal, where producers register, report annual packaging supply and pay fees. It describes this as a national one-stop-shop that covers several provinces. Source: https://www.circularmaterials.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Circular_Materials-Report_to_Producers-2025.pdf (via search summary; not opened).
- It is a PRO in Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Ontario, Alberta and Yukon, and also operates in Saskatchewan alongside SK Recycles. The search summaries are not fully consistent about this. Source: https://rwdi.com/?p=6598 and https://ppwrconnect.com/selling-to-canada-packaging-epr
- Quebec is handled by Eco Entreprises Quebec (EEQ), the sole designated PRO there, and British Columbia is handled by Recycle BC (the Recycle BC part is unverified). So a producer selling in several provinces still has to deal with more than one PRO. Source: https://circularmaterials.ca/producer-province/quebec/
- Reporting thresholds differ by province. BC is CA$1M turnover and 1 t. Manitoba is CA$750k. Saskatchewan is CA$1M and 1 t. Ontario is CA$2M with per-material tonnage thresholds. All 2025 supply data was due to a PRO by 31 May 2026. Source: https://rev-log.com/us/canada-packaging-epr-may-31-reporting-and-compliance-steps/ and https://ppwrconnect.com/selling-to-canada-packaging-epr (secondary sources; unverified against regulations).
- Momentum: it is active, with Reports to Producers for 2023, 2024 and 2025 and a 2026 reporting cycle running. Ontario fees and budgets are shared through an advisory group and the portal because Ontario is a competitive PRO market. Source: the 2025 Report to Producers above.

## Complaints
None found. Two searches aimed at complaints (portal problems, small-business fees, Reddit and forums) returned nothing about Circular Materials. The one complaint-style result was about UK EPR billing errors, which is unrelated. This does not show that complaints don't exist. Search coverage was thin, and app-store and G2 reviews do not apply to this kind of product.

## Pricing
- Producers pay fees to the PRO, set per material and weight, as part of compliance. No separate software price was found.
- The search results did not give a fee schedule. I did not read Circular Materials' fee pages, so the amounts are unverified.

## Fit gaps
- It is a single-PRO channel, not a cross-PRO workbench. It does not cover Quebec (EEQ) or BC (Recycle BC), and in Ontario it competes with other PROs.
- It is a filing and payment portal. It is not a tool for mapping SKUs to materials, comparing provinces, working out thresholds, or consolidating data across several PROs. This is inferred from the search summaries, not tested.
- Large producers have their own EPR staff, and consultants fill the gaps. The idea's buyer would be a small or mid-size multi-province producer.
- French support and Quebec filing are handled by a different body.

## Opening
A PRO-neutral prep layer would sit upstream of every PRO portal, with one SKU-to-material dataset, threshold checks per province and exports for each portal. It would suit mid-size brands selling in more than one province.
