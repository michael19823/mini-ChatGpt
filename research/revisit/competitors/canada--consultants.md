# canada -- consultants (EPR compliance consultants; no single URL)

Idea it killed: C0168 Multi-province packaging EPR workbench

## Verdict: beatable

Consultants are a fragmented, service-based crowd, not one product. Software vendors are entering, but nothing found shows a dominant incumbent for small producers.

## Evidence
- Many firms offer Canadian packaging EPR reporting help, including H2 Compliance / cGlobal, Rev-Log, RWDI, Deloitte Canada and EcoPackables (guide). Sources:
  - https://h2compliance.com/canadian-packaging-epr-a-compliance-guide-for-businesses/
  - https://rev-log.com/us/canada-packaging-epr-may-31-reporting-and-compliance-steps/
  - https://rwdi.com/?p=6598
  - https://www.deloitte.com/ca/en/Industries/consumer/perspectives/extended-producer-responsibility.html
- Multi-province work is real and recurring. Annual supply reports are due May 31 in BC (Recycle BC) and Ontario (RPRA plus a PRO such as Circular Materials, Landbell, Ryse or EnviroFocus). Independent verification of packaging data is coming in Alberta and Ontario (reports due June 30, 2027). Quebec runs through Eco Entreprises Quebec (in French, "REP" for selective collection, in force July 2022). Source: https://rev-log.com/us/canada-packaging-epr-may-31-reporting-and-compliance-steps/
- Software vendors are moving in: Assent launched an EPR packaging solution in Sept 2025 (with Lorax EPI), and Repurpose Global offers packaging compliance. Sources:
  - https://www.wastedive.com/press-release/20250918-assent-unveils-extended-producer-responsibility-packaging-solution-to-simpl/
  - https://repurpose.global/packaging-compliance
- Complaints: none found. Three searches (English and French) returned no reviews or Reddit threads about Canadian EPR consultants. This is a valid null result, not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing
- No Canadian consultant price lists were found. Unverified.
- One vendor claim (US-focused, from search summary): professional help cuts first-state compliance from 2+ months to about 2 weeks. Unverified marketing claim.
- Manitoba exempts producers under CAD 750,000 revenue or under 1 tonne of material (search summary, not independently checked).

## Fit gaps
- Consultants are bespoke and per-engagement. They do not give a small producer a self-serve tool to reuse each year.
- Enterprise software (Assent) targets large manufacturers. Small and mid-size brands are likely underserved (inference, unverified).
- French-language, Quebec-specific support is not evident in the results (unverified).

## Opening
A cheap self-serve annual workbench that does multi-province data collection, reconciliation and verification-ready records for small and mid-size producers, which consultants and enterprise software both price or scope out. Caveat: the idea's original need score was only 4, and the PROs' own portals handle the filing itself.
