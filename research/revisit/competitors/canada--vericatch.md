# VeriCatch (Canada) - dropped idea C1208: Fishing ELOGs

## Verdict
strong

## Evidence
- Product is FisheriesApp2 ELOG. Marketing text (App Store listing, https://apps.apple.com/app/id1576603812) calls it "Canada's only independent commercial fishing ELOG", DFO-qualified. The "thousands of harvesters" claim is the vendor's own and unverified.
- DFO qualification covers lobster, crab, whelk/bivalves/squid/scallops, groundfish, small pelagics, salmon, shrimp, urchins/sea cucumbers/marine plants, and tuna/swordfish (vendor listing). DFO lists qualified ELOG applications at https://dfo-mpo.gc.ca/fisheries-peches/sdc-cps/nir-nei/qualified-elog-client-applic-cliente-jbe-qualifie-eng.html (I did not open it, so how many other qualified vendors it names is unverified).
- Regulatory tailwind: ELOGs are mandatory in the Gulf Region for lobster and snow crab from 1 Jan 2025, in Quebec for lobster, snow crab, herring, mackerel and bait fish from 1 Jan 2025, and in Newfoundland and Labrador for Unit 1 Redfish, lobster and snow crab in 2025 (https://www.canada.ca/en/fisheries-oceans/news/2024/12/government-of-canada-announces-changes-to-make-fisheries-catch-data-faster-and-more-accurate.html; https://www.glf.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/en/node/20431).
- Momentum: active. About 20 years in business, offices in Vancouver, Halifax and St. John's, 7-day seasonal support in English and French, and a hiring ad for customer support (search snippets). No acquisition or shutdown news found.
- Complaints: none found about VeriCatch's app quality. No app-store review text was visible in the results, so I make no claim about ratings. The complaints that exist are about the DFO mandate in general, from the union FFAW-Unifor (https://ffaw.ca/dfo_elog/, https://ffaw.ca/ffaw-unifor-calls-for-mp-intervention/):
  - paper logs are free, but ELOGs cost at least $60 per season per species;
  - older harvesters are uncomfortable with smartphones or tablets;
  - cell coverage is poor in many areas, and the 72-hour submission window is hard to meet on week-long trips;
  - speedboat lobster fishing makes data entry hard and risks losing the device overboard.
  These are not specific to VeriCatch and affect any ELOG vendor.
- Searches in French returned no additional local-language complaints or reviews.

## Pricing
- $60 per year, from search summaries of the vendor and DFO material. Exact tiers and whether it is per species are unverified. The union says $60 per season per species. This is already low for the segment, so there is little room to undercut.

## Fit gaps
- None clearly found. It already covers many fisheries, works offline, supports English and French, and includes a dockside-monitor trip summary.
- Possible gaps, all unverified: no hardware or device support for older harvesters, species or fisheries DFO has not yet qualified, and non-Canadian markets.
- A new entrant also needs its own DFO qualification, which is a certification barrier. The method says such barriers lower the score.

## Opening
Narrow: a rival would need DFO qualification and a clear advantage for low-connectivity or low-tech harvesters against a cheap ($60) incumbent that is already mandated.
