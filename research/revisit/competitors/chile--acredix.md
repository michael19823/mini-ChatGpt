# ACREDIX (Chile) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C1440, Chile contractor accreditation pack.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (for ACREDIX itself; the segment has other real players)

## Evidence
- 4 WebSearch calls (Spanish and English, incl. "Acredix" with mining/contractor terms) returned nothing on a product named ACREDIX. No site, app-store listing, review, news item or pricing found. Existence of ACREDIX is unverified.
- Searches surfaced Chilean health/education accreditation bodies (e.g. Acredita Norte Chile, Acredita Consultores), which are unrelated to contractor accreditation.
- Real players in Chilean contractor/worker accreditation that the idea would actually face:
  - MINPASS / Mine Pass (digital worker credential for mining): https://www.reporteminero.cl/noticia/entrevistas/2026/06/mine-pass-busca-digitalizar-y-simplificar-la-acreditacion-en-mineria
  - SICEP (AIA, Antofagasta; 4,100+ suppliers, 26 mining/industrial users): https://www.mch.cl/sicep-llama-empresas-proveedoras-y-contratistas-realizar-proceso-de-actualizacion-2015/
  - Codelco SIIRLL-VP portal: https://www.codelco.com/proveedores/portal-de-acreditacion-para-las-empresas-contratistas-de-vicepresidencia
  - Job ads mention WebControl, SIGA, Pronexo, ControlDoc (names only, not researched).
- Complaints: none found for ACREDIX (nothing to find). No complaints researched for the other platforms (outside search budget).
- Momentum: unknown/unverified for ACREDIX.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
Cannot assess; no product found. Gap against the wider field is unverified, though mining-focused platforms are largely mandated by large mandantes (Codelco, AIA members), which may leave small non-mining contractors less served (unverified inference).
