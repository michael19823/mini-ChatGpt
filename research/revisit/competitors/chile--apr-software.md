# APR Software / Octocelio (Chile)

Ideas it killed: C1211 (rural sanitary services (SSR) management).

## Verdict: beatable

A real, active local competitor, but it reaches only about 12% of Chile's rural water systems. No complaints were found, and the IoT and sensor focus leaves room for a cheap, administration-only tool. The verdict is tentative: only 4 of the 6 allowed searches ran (2 hit a usage limit and 1 returned unrelated results), and no pricing was found.

## Evidence
- Founded in 2019 by Edgardo Fuentes and Víctor Reyes. It manages commercial and financial aspects (billing, financial control) of SSR (formerly APR), and combines software with IoT sensors for tank level, chlorine, pressure, extraction and consumption. It reportedly works with more than 300 of about 2,600 SSR in Chile. Source: https://www.df.cl/df-lab/sostenibilidad/apr-software-la-startup-que-creo-un-sistema-para-gestionar-el-agua-en
- Momentum: it has evolved into "Octocelio", a SaaS platform covering operational, administrative and community management. It reportedly benefits more than 5,000 families in over 50 connected communities (Antofagasta, Valparaiso, Los Rios, San Pedro de Atacama, San Felipe, Socaire). Sources: https://g5noticias.cl/2026/01/01/octocelio-una-nueva-plataforma-para-la-gestion-del-agua-rural/ and https://ecosistemastartup.com/?p=55156. It is active as of Jan 2026. No acquisition or shutdown found.
- Other players exist: SNAP (geoXite, via Esri partners page), described as management and operations software for rural water communities. Source: https://www.esri.com/partners/geoxite-spa-a2T70000000TOijEAG/snap-a2dUU000007MGIDYA4
- Market context: about 2,836 APR systems exist in Chile, and about 20% of committees and cooperatives reportedly report supply interruptions at least once a year, which search summaries attribute to management gaps. There is widespread dissatisfaction with the State-mandated technical units (not with this product). Sources: https://www.infraestructurapublica.cl/?p=49942 and https://www.elmostrador.cl/agenda-pais/2021/05/07/apr-sin-tecnologia-no-hay-agua/
- Complaints: none found about APR Software or Octocelio itself. Spanish searches for reviews and complaints returned only market-level material. App-store, Reddit, Facebook group and forum reviews were not surfaced or not checked. Finding none is a valid result.

## Pricing
Not found. Octocelio pricing and minimums are unverified. A search for opinions and prices returned unrelated products.

## Fit gaps
- Penetration is about 300 of 2,600 SSR, so most committees are not customers (reported figure, not independently checked).
- The sensor and IoT emphasis suggests hardware-linked deployment, which may be too heavy or costly for tiny committees (inferred, unverified).
- Offline or mobile use, and compliance reporting formats for the state sanitary regulator, were not checked (unverified).

## Opening
A very low-cost, hardware-free billing and member-management tool for small SSR committees that cannot afford or do not need IoT. This is unproven, and price comparison is impossible until Octocelio pricing is found.
