# Buk (Chile) - competitor check for C0187 (Chile payroll and attendance compliance)

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Buk is a Chilean cloud HR suite covering payroll, attendance, recruiting and performance. It says its calculation engine is "100% adapted to Chilean regulations" and that it handles Dirección del Trabajo and Previred compliance automatically (vendor claim, unverified). Source: https://www.buk.cl/blog/software-rrhh-integral-remuneraciones-asistencia-buk-chile
- Momentum is high. A $50M Series B in January 2025 was reported at a $850M valuation, led by Headline, with Workday Ventures participating. Reported scale is about 7,000 companies. Sources: https://multiples.vc/private-comps/buk and https://seedtable.com/companies/buk/funding-rounds/series-b-2025-01 (third-party aggregators; the figures are unverified).
- Buk has a free tier aimed at small firms, "Buk Starter", for fewer than 20 employees. Buk and press report about 2,000 to 4,000 SMEs using it (the figures differ by source and are unverified). Source: https://www.mediabanco.com/buk-starter-ha-beneficiado-a-mas-de-2-000-pymes/
- Complaints: none found. Five searches in English and Spanish returned mostly Buk marketing pages and funding news. I found no Google Play, App Store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews, and no news of outages or failed filings. Absence of results does not mean absence of complaints.

## Pricing
- Buk Starter is free for fewer than 20 employees, per Buk marketing and press.
- Paid plans are billed per active employee per month, with the unit price falling as headcount rises (Buk site). A third-party guide (https://www.guiadesoftware.com/blog/mejor-software-rrhh-nomina-chile) puts it at roughly CLP 4,000-8,000 per employee per month, depending on modules (unverified). It also says Buk may be overkill and expensive for pure payroll (unverified).
- I did not confirm any published minimum.

## Fit gaps
- Pure-payroll or compliance-only buyers may find the full suite heavy. Implementation is reported at about 4 months on average (third-party, unverified).
- The free tier is capped at 20 employees. Above that, paid pricing is quote-based.
- I found no evidence of a missing filing format, language, or offline or mobile gap in Chile. Buk lists geolocated mobile attendance and biometrics.
- I could not verify the quality of its small-firm support.

## Opening
Little room against a well-funded, Chile-native suite with a free micro-business tier. A gap exists only for a very narrow niche, such as an ultra-cheap, standalone, compliance-only tool for firms above 20 employees that find suites too heavy.
