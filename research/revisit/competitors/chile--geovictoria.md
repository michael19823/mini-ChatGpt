# GeoVictoria (Chile) - competitor check for C0187 (Chile payroll and attendance compliance)

## Verdict: strong

Established, Chilean-founded attendance vendor with broad hardware and app coverage, public-sector framework listing, and a payroll partnership (Nubox). Attendance is covered well. Payroll itself is not its product.

## Evidence
- Self-described as 16+ years in attendance control, 40+ countries, 5,000+ clients (vendor claim, unverified). Another vendor page says 10,000+ private companies and 100+ public bodies (vendor claim, unverified). https://www.geovictoria.com/es-cl/
- Marking by app, clock, web, biometric/facial/fingerprint, phone; reports, overtime calculation, shifts, leave/vacation modules, access and canteen control. https://www.geovictoria.com/es-cl/ ; https://www.geovictoria.com/es-cl/blog/modulo-de-vacaciones-como-funciona-en-chile/
- Available via Convenio Marco (Chilean public procurement); markets to the Contraloria attendance requirement. https://www.geovictoria.com/es-cl/industrias/gobierno/
- Marketing around the Direccion del Trabajo requirement and the 40-hour law (Ley 40 horas); Nubox alliance feeds attendance data into payroll calculation. https://blog.nubox.com/empresas/alianza-geovictoria-ley-40-horas ; https://transtecnia.cl/articulos-de-opinion/cumpla-la-nueva-exigencia-de-la-direccion-del-trabajo/
- Momentum: active (expansion to the US reported by DF; Nubox alliance on the 40-hour law). https://www.df.cl/startup-chilena-de-control-de-asistencia-geovictoria-aterriza-en-eeuu-y
- Complaints: none found. 6 searches (Spanish and English, including Capterra/G2) returned only vendor-authored pages. No user reviews, app-store reviews, Reddit or forum complaints surfaced. Absence of results is not evidence of satisfaction; Google Play / App Store ratings were not checked via search and are unverified.

## Pricing
- No published prices found in search results; the vendor appears to use quote-based sales (contact sales). Unverified. Price for a micro-business could not be compared.

## Fit gaps (inferred, partly unverified)
- Core is attendance, not payroll: payroll (liquidaciones, Previred, finiquitos) depends on a partner such as Nubox; no evidence GeoVictoria computes Chilean payroll itself.
- Likely oriented to mid/large employers and public bodies (hardware clocks, Convenio Marco); fit for micro-businesses with 1-10 staff is unverified.
- Quote-based pricing suggests small buyers cannot self-serve (unverified).

## Opening
Only a combined, self-serve, low-cost payroll-plus-attendance product for micro/small Chilean employers is left open, and Nubox's partnership with GeoVictoria already addresses part of that, so the gap is narrow.
