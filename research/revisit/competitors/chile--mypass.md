# MyPass (MyPass Global), Chile

Killed idea: C1440 Chile contractor accreditation pack.

## Verdict: strong

Well-funded, active digital-passport platform already live in Chile mining, with large-owner contracts (BHP, Codelco-adjacent processes, Chevron/Shell elsewhere).

## Evidence
- Australian SaaS operating in Chile, Canada, US; workflow for Chilean contractors (RUT validation, vendor reps, profile validation requests) is documented in Spanish. Source: https://www.mypassglobal.com/hubfs/003%20GCS_C%C3%B3mo%20crear%20a%20un%20contratista%20en%20MyPass%20(SPC).pdf
- Reported raise of 75 million (currency not verified in snippet) for global expansion. Source: https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/construction-tech-startup-mypass-raises-75-million-for-global-expansion-0878ee11-f175-40ae-982f-8edfe9243ab5/
- Reporte Minero (Expomin 2025) says 1,300+ companies and 110,000+ workers on platform; BHP awarded it the Global Contractor System. Source: https://www.reporteminero.cl/noticia/expomin-2025/2025/04/mypass-global
- Capterra: 4.4/5 from 28 users (as relayed by search summary). Source: https://www.capterra.in/software/210507/mypass
- Momentum: actively developed, present at Expomin 2025. No acquisition or shutdown news found (unverified beyond that).
- Complaints: none found specific to MyPass in Chile (app stores, Reddit, forums, news). Only an unrelated 2020 reclamos.cl item, not about MyPass. Coverage was limited to 6 searches, so absence is not proof.

## Pricing
- Subscription per active worker up to 49 workers, then flat tiers; 1-4 tier annual billing only; 5-49 monthly or annual; prices in AUD ex GST; card payment under $5,000. Sources: https://support.mypassglobal.com/hc/en-au/articles/9712258844047-Pricing-FAQ , https://mypassglobal.com/pricing
- Exact amounts not seen in results: unverified. Per-worker pricing could be heavy for small contractors, unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Appears aimed at mining and energy owners and their contractor networks (owner-driven, owner pays/mandates); small contractors are the vendor side.
- Pricing in AUD, support from Australia/Philippines/India/Colombia teams (per search summary).
- No evidence found of gaps in Spanish, offline or mobile use: unverified.

## Opening
Little: only a thin niche serving small contractors outside MyPass-mandating owners (e.g. non-mining sectors, Codelco-specific tooling), and even that is unproven.
