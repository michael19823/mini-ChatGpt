# Rex+ (Chile) - competitor check for C0187 (Chile payroll and attendance compliance)

## Verdict: strong

Rex+ is a live, Chile-specific HCM product that covers payroll, attendance control certified by the Dirección del Trabajo (DT) and Previred. It covers the whole of idea C0187. The research was limited: 1 of 4 searches hit a usage limit, and no independent reviews were found.

## Evidence
- Rex+ is described as a People Operations/HCM system covering payroll, vacations, ATS and internal surveys, with attendance tracking. It works with Chile's Previred. Source: Capterra listing https://www.capterra.com/p/232879/Rex-/ (vendor-provided description).
- Rex+ says its attendance platform is certified by the DT and complies with DT ruling 2927-58 on digital marking. It also says it lets users create shifts and schedules and assign them in bulk, with traceability through to payroll. Source: Rex+ blog https://rexmas.com/blog/control-de-asistencia-obligacion-o-el-mejor-aliado-para-calcular-remuneraciones/ (vendor marketing, unverified independently). Related pages: https://rexmas.com/conexion-dt/ and https://rexmas.com/control-de-asistencia-bnovus/ (the latter suggests hardware partner integration, unverified).
- The vendor claims 17+ years of experience (vendor claim, unverified).
- Complaints: none found. The searches (English and Spanish) returned only vendor content and the Capterra listing page. Review text was not retrieved. Absence of complaints here reflects thin search coverage, not proof the product is well liked.
- Momentum: an active blog and a current Capterra listing (2026) suggest active development. Unverified beyond that.

## Pricing
Not published. Capterra says pricing requires contacting the vendor, with no free trial mentioned. Whether it is overpriced for micro or small buyers is unverified.

## Fit gaps
All unverified, with no evidence found either way:
- Likely sold through quotes and sales contact, which may be a poor fit for very small employers.
- Hardware or biometric attendance may rely on partners.
- Offline or mobile marking and multi-entity support were not checked.

## Opening
None confirmed. Any opening would be a low-price, self-serve product for micro-employers, and that would still need a pricing check on Rex+ and other Chilean vendors.
