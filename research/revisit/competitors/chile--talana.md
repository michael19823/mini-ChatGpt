# Talana (Chile)

Dropped idea: C0187, Chile payroll and attendance compliance.

## Verdict: strong (low confidence)

Talana is an established, multi-module Chilean HR platform. It covers payroll, attendance and shifts, digital signature, recruitment and training, and it also operates in Peru. I could not complete the research. The WebSearch tool hit its usage limit after one successful search, so no complaints data was gathered. The verdict rests on how the product describes itself.

## Evidence

It is built in Chile and also serves Peru. Source: https://web.talana.com/ (search snippet, not read in full).
- Talana's own blog positions it for "medium and large companies" in Chile and Peru. Source: https://web.talana.com/blog/software-de-remuneraciones-cuanto-cuesta-para-una-pyme (search snippet).

## Pricing
- No fixed published price. Pricing is custom, based on the number of active workers and the modules contracted, and a demo is required for a quote. Source: Talana blog, search summary.
- That blog's general market range for Chilean SME payroll software is "1 UF to 3 UF" per month, from a free basic plan up to fuller plans. This is a generic range for the market, not a confirmed Talana price. Unverified for Talana.
- Minimums: unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred from positioning, unverified)
- It is aimed at medium and large firms, so micro and small businesses may be underserved or find it too expensive.
- Quote-only pricing and a demo requirement are friction for very small buyers.
- Unverified: offline use, hardware-free attendance, and the quality of the mobile app.

## Opening
Only a narrow opening is plausible: a self-serve, transparent-price compliance tool for micro and small Chilean employers. Re-run this check with more searches before relying on it.
