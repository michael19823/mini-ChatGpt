# Chile: unnamed consultants (Ley 21.719 data-protection compliance), idea C0190

## Verdict: beatable

Consultants are real and the law is imminent, but the offerings found target mid-market and enterprise. No complaints were found, and no published small-buyer pricing. The SME segment looks underserved. (4 WebSearch calls used.)

## Evidence
- Law 21.719 takes effect 1 Dec 2026; SMEs reportedly get a one-year warnings-only grace period (Dec 2026 to Dec 2027). Sources: https://www.diarioconstitucional.cl/2026/06/12/la-ley-21-719-entra-en-vigor-el-1-de-diciembre-y-expone-vacios-en-regulacion-de-pequenas-empresas/ , https://preyproject.com/es/blog/ley-21719-pymes-proteccion-datos
- A search snippet says 72% of Chilean firms report not being ready (source: https://www.emol.com/noticias/Economia/2026/09/23/1212212/pymes-ley-proteccion-datos-personales.html; snippet only, not verified in full).
- Alaya Digital Solutions (alayiatrust.com) sells law-firm counsel plus OneTrust, with separate enterprise and SME pages. It claims 1-4 months for SMEs and 3-6 months for enterprise. Source: https://alayiatrust.com/en/ley-21719-pymes
- Other players seen: Prey Project (security tool with SME compliance guides, https://preyproject.com/es/resources/guia-de-proteccion-de-datos-para-pymes-cumplimiento-de-ley-21-719), RSM Chile and Prieto (law firm) publications, and a U. de Chile course.
- A letter to the editor argues compliance should be designed for pymes rather than via lawyers: https://www.diarioconstitucional.cl/cartas-al-director/respuesta-a-la-ley-que-no-se-cumple-con-abogados-la-proteccion-de-datos-tambien-debe-pensarse-desde-las-pymes/ (snippet only; shows a perceived gap, not a complaint about a named firm).
- Complaints: none found (searched Spanish and English; no reviews or forum threads surfaced).

## Pricing
No published prices found for consultancy or Alaya plans. Unverified. OneTrust is generally enterprise-priced (unverified here).

## Fit gaps
- Counsel-plus-OneTrust model is likely heavy and costly for micro and small firms (inference, unverified).
- Timelines of 1-4 months for SMEs suggest a project, not self-serve.
- Many SMEs have no staff to maintain compliance; grace period reduces urgency until late 2027.

## Opening
A cheap, Spanish-language, self-serve compliance kit for Chilean pymes (data inventory, privacy policy, rights-request handling) fits the gap, but demand may stay soft until the grace period ends.
