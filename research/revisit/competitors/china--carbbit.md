# Carbbit (China) vs C0193: Generic CBAM calculator for EU importers

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Carbbit is aimed at Chinese exporters and manufacturers, not EU importers. Its customers sit on the other side of the CBAM relationship. Overlap is partial: it does calculate embedded emissions and estimate CBAM cost.

## Evidence
- Shanghai Carbbit Digital Technology Co., Ltd. (碳硕), founded 2022, has offices in Shanghai, Nantong and Singapore. It sells a CBAM compliance platform, carbon accounting tools, emission factor databases and consulting. Source: https://netzerocompare.com/organizations/shanghai-carbbit-digital-technology-co-ltd and https://netzerocompare.com/software/carbbit (search-result summaries; I did not open the pages).
- Main users are steel, aluminium, fertilizer and cement exporters selling into the EU. Same sources.
- Features: carbon footprint calculation, monitoring plan management, automated CBAM report generation, and CBAM cost estimates using EU ETS prices. Same sources.
- Complaints: none found. The English and Chinese searches returned no app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints about Carbbit. I ran 3 searches, and the third returned only generic CBAM pages. This is absence of evidence, not a clean record.
- Momentum: the platform is listed and described as active. I found no acquisition or shutdown news. Dates of recent releases are unverified.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (for an EU importer buyer)
- It is built around the exporter or producer workflow: collecting plant emissions data, monitoring plans and verification preparation. It is not built around the importer's declarant tasks such as CBAM certificate purchase and quarterly declarations. This is inferred from the product description and is unverified.
- It is China-centred, with Shanghai and Nantong offices. Whether an EU-importer UI, EU-language support or EU-based support exists is unverified.

## Opening
An importer-side tool that handles declarant filing, supplier data requests and default-value cost exposure is not served by Carbbit. Carbbit is more of a possible data source from the Chinese supplier side.
