# China: e-invoicing incumbents (idea C1214, reverse invoicing for recyclers, 反向开票)

## Verdict: strong

Reverse invoicing is now a national tax policy, and the tax authority's own platform provides the function. The search found no complaints. The research was cut short (see below), so the verdict rests on thin evidence.

## Evidence
- The policy exists and is large. Resource-recycling firms can issue invoices directly to natural-person sellers. The tax authority reports more than 500 billion yuan invoiced under it. Source: https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20250812/c670294601.shtml
- It solves the "first invoice" gap. Recyclers buying from individuals without invoices could not claim input VAT or deduct costs for corporate income tax. Source: https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20250812/c670294601.shtml
- Sellers can claim small-scale-taxpayer relief (VAT exemption under 100k yuan per month, 3% rate cut to 1%). A seller whose reverse-invoiced sales pass 5 million yuan over 12 months can no longer be reverse-invoiced. Source: same article.
- A named example: Chery's recycling subsidiary has reverse-invoiced 4,000+ individuals for roughly 8 million yuan. Source: https://www.news.cn/fortune/20250812/82a0f1ceb2d9459d95ad8086922581b8/c.html
- Individuals can register as sole proprietors and file through the electronic tax bureau in one step. Source: same Xinhua article.
- Complaints: none found. Searches on reviews, forums and outages for this niche returned only policy news. Not verified: that no complaints exist, only that none surfaced.
- Named incumbents (Baiwang, Aisino and similar): not researched. The third search, which targeted them, failed on a usage limit. I did not find or verify any vendor product, review or momentum data.

## Pricing
Not found. No published price for vendor tools. The policy function appears to be built into the state electronic tax bureau and tax-control systems, so it is likely free to the user (unverified).

## Fit gaps
None established. Possible gaps, all unverified guesses: bulk or mobile collection of seller identity at the scrap yard, and ledger or weighing integration. No source found for either.

## Opening
Little. The government platform supplies the core feature, so any opening would be only a workflow layer (yard intake, seller ID capture, reconciliation) on top of it, and that is unproven.
