# Kolum (china) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0193 Generic CBAM calculator for EU importers.

## Verdict: strong (provisional; research was cut short)

Only 1 of 6 searches ran. The second (Chinese-language) search failed with a usage-limit error, so no local-language or complaint search was done.

## Evidence
- Kolum (kolum.earth) is a Berlin-based trade compliance platform, founded 2023, not a China company. The "china" label on the candidate looks wrong or refers only to supplier-side use. Source: https://cbamguide.com/software/kolum/ , https://tech.eu/2024/07/11/carbon-compliance-platform-kolum-raises-2-1m-pre-seed/
- Its EU CBAM module covers the importer chain end to end: import classification, supplier emissions data collection (guided workflows in 20+ languages), embedded-emissions calculation from default or actual values, quarterly declaration prep with exports formatted for the EU registry, and certificate cost forecasting. Source: cbamguide.com review above.
- It offers a free on-page CBAM cost calculator, so a generic calculator is already given away. Source: search summary, cbamguide.com.
- Momentum: raised EUR 2.1M pre-seed (tech.eu, July 2024) and was still listed in a 2026 software review. That suggests it is active.
- Complaints: none found. Not searched for in depth (no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit queries ran). Unverified.

## Pricing
Quote-only, no public tiers (as of July 2026, per cbamguide.com). The free cost calculator is public. Whether it is overpriced for small importers is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Quote-only pricing may deter very small importers (inference, unverified).
- Nothing found on missing features. No evidence on China-specific gaps such as Chinese-language support or Chinese exporter data formats. The 20+ language claim may already include Chinese (unverified).

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, published-price tool for small importers or Chinese exporters could undercut a quote-only platform. Complaint evidence is missing, so this is not confirmed.
