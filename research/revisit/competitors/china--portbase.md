# Portbase (country tag: china) vs C0193 Generic CBAM calculator for EU importers

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (for the China market); weak-to-beatable as a general CBAM tool

## Evidence
- Portbase is the Dutch (Rotterdam/Amsterdam) non-profit port community system, founded 2009 by the two port authorities. It is not a China-based company and has no found China presence. Source: https://www.portbase.com/en/?p=3262 (search snippet)
- It lists "The Carbon Pricing Platform" on its marketplace: a B2B web app for EU CBAM declarants (data upload, supplier emissions collection, shipment emissions tracking, CBAM report generation, financial-risk management), plus tools for non-EU exporters to produce emissions data. Source: https://www.portbase.com/en/marketplace/the-carbon-pricing-platform/
- The search snippets point to Carbon Glance as the partner service provider (not verified beyond the snippet), so it is a marketplace listing and not Portbase's own product.
- No Chinese-language Portbase CBAM material found. A Forvis Mazars China CBAM webinar page appeared but is unrelated to Portbase.
- Complaints: none found about the CBAM platform. A Portbase customer survey (Integron, rated 7.8) noted generic PCS issues: maintenance/outage communication, usability, service desk follow-up, complaint handling. Source: https://www.portbase.com/en/?p=3262. This is Portbase's own page, and it concerns the port system, not CBAM.

## Pricing
- Subscription (quarterly or annual) and/or transaction fee. No figures published in what I saw; contact required. Unverified beyond that.

## Fit gaps
- Targets declarants with Dutch port/logistics ties. Not a free generic calculator, and no public price.
- No Chinese-language product or China-side tooling found (unverified; limited search).
- Platform appears aimed at data collection and reporting workflows, not a quick standalone calculation for small importers (inferred).

## Opening
Portbase does not block a lightweight, transparent-price generic CBAM calculator, and it has no China footprint; the real competition lies in other CBAM tools.
