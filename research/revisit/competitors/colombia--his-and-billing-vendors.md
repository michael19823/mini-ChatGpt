# Colombia: HIS and billing vendors (killed C0196, FEV-RIPS JSON generator/validator)

Searches: 5 of 6 used, English and Spanish. Result snippets only; no pages fetched. No reviews were read.

## Verdict: strong

Many Colombian HIS and clinic-software vendors already bundle RIPS JSON generation and FEV-RIPS validation as a standard feature. The obligation is mandatory and fully in force. I found no complaints and no pricing gaps that would let a standalone tool displace them.

## Evidence
- Resolution 2275 of 2023 (strengthened by Resolution 1884 of 2024) requires RIPS in JSON, validated electronically and tied to the FEV-Salud invoice. Mandatory phases: 1 Feb 2025, 1 Apr 2025, and full adoption on 1 Jun 2025. Source: https://consultorsalud.com/ministerio-salud-fev-rips-optimiza-registros/ and https://www.medesk.net/es/blog/rips-fev-salud-cambios-colombia/
- A search snippet says Resolution 948 of 2026 updated the RIPS 2025 technical rules and tightened the FEV-RIPS validator's cross-checks. I could not verify this beyond the snippet.
- Dinámica Gerencial (EHR plus ERP, in the market since 1991) says it updated its application for the new FEV and RIPS rules. Sources: https://syac.net.co/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/BROCHURE-DINAMICA-GERENCIAL-2025.pdf and https://syac.net.co/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Boletin-Sep-2024.pdf
- Medifolios (cloud EHR and billing) lists RIPS validation. Source: https://www.capterra.in/software/1237788/Medifolios
- Medesk markets automatic RIPS generation and validation and FEV support. It also publishes a "free RIPS generator" blog post and a RIPS page. Sources: https://www.medesk.net/es/blog/programa-generador-de-rips-gratis/ and https://www.medesk.net/es/rips
- AgendaPro, Siigo and Athenea (HIS) also publish RIPS content or release notes. Sources: https://agendapro.com/blog/nueva-resolucion-rips/ and https://help.atheneasoluciones.com/news/202/novedades-version-2_0_0_00294-de-athenea.aspx
- Ministry technical documents and validation rules are public, so vendors have the specification. Source: https://www.minsalud.gov.co/Anexos_Normatividad_Nuevo/Documento%20tecnico%201-FEV-RIPS.pdf
- Complaints: none found. The searches returned vendor and regulatory pages only, with no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews.
- Weak signal, from a regulator-related snippet in the second search: the Ministry reportedly found that about 70% of entities did not comply and that over 80% lacked the capacity to implement by the original deadline. This suggests early difficulty, but I did not verify it. It is also dated, since full adoption is now mandatory.

## Pricing
- No vendor prices were found in the results, so all vendor pricing is unverified.
- Medesk says it has a free trial with no card, no extra charge for regulatory updates, and DIAN invoicing included. This is unverified marketing copy from the snippet.
- Medesk also has a "precios software médico Colombia" page (https://www.medesk.net/es/blog/precios-software-medico-colombia/). I did not read it.
- The Ministry offers a free validation service (unverified, from background knowledge). This weakens the case for charging for validation alone.

## Fit gaps
- Nothing verified. A possible gap is the small provider that has no HIS, such as an independent professional or small practice with spreadsheets. Medesk and AgendaPro already target consultorios, so this is partly covered.
- Another possible gap is the large IPS that runs its own legacy HIS and needs a bridge or pre-validator. This is unverified.

## Opening left
Little to none as a standalone RIPS JSON generator or validator, since it is a bundled, mandatory feature of every Colombian clinic HIS. Any opening would be a niche add-on, such as a pre-submission validator for legacy systems.
