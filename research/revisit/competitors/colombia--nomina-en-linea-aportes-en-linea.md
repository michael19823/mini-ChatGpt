# Nomina en Linea (Aportes en Linea), Colombia

Dropped idea: C1215, domestic-worker employers (Ley 2466/2025).

## Verdict: weak (low confidence, thin evidence)

Aportes en Linea is a PILA payment operator (aportesenlinea.com), not a household-employer product. Research was cut short: 2 of 6 searches ran, the third failed with a usage-limit error. Everything below is limited to those two searches.

## Evidence
- A search result states that aportesenlinea.com lets users generate a PILA payroll number and pay through PSE, then obtain a contribution certificate. This came from the search summary, not a fetched page; unverified in detail.
- A PILA form exclusive to part-time domestic service exists in Colombia (same search summary; source: El Espectador article on affiliating part-time domestic workers, https://www.elespectador.com/economia/como-afiliar-a-su-trabajadora-domestica-a-tiempo-parcial-article-474916/). The government PILA infrastructure therefore already covers the basic filing.
- Complaints: none found. The Spanish-language search for opiniones/quejas returned only unrelated results (Chile, Mexico, Argentina). Absence of results is not proof of absence of complaints; app-store, Trustpilot and Reddit were not checked.
- Momentum: not determined.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- It is a payment and filing channel. Nothing found suggests payroll calculation, contract or severance guidance, or Ley 2466/2025 compliance help for household employers.

## Opening
An employer-friendly layer (contract, prestaciones, Ley 2466/2025 changes, PILA reminders) on top of existing PILA operators appears open, but this rests on thin evidence and should be rechecked.
