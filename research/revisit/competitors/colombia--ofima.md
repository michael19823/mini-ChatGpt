# Colombia: Ofima (killed idea C0198: Colombia carrier RNDC manifest)

## Verdict: beatable

Ofima is a real, established Colombian ERP vendor that publishes RNDC manifest content and lists transport among its sectors. Its RNDC feature depth is unverified, it is a broad ERP rather than a focused manifest tool, and no complaints or prices were found.

## Evidence
- Ofima is a Colombian ERP vendor aimed at industrial, commercial, pharma-chemical, transport and services firms (https://ofima.com/, https://www.colombia.com/top/ofima-erp-la-solucion-tecnologica-colombiana-que-moderniza-la-gestion-empresarial-538880).
- A promotional article claims over 1,000 client companies (colombia.com link above). This is a vendor-sourced claim and unverified.
- Ofima published a 2026 article on the manifest and RNDC changes (Decree 1017 of 2025; unified manifest formats mandatory from 1 May 2026): https://www.ofima.com/?p=24940. It frames the issue as data feeding the manifest needing to be integrated with accounting. That signals content marketing to carriers. The search snippets did not confirm a dedicated RNDC-reporting module or web-service integration. Unverified.
- Complaints: no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of Ofima surfaced in 5 searches, English and Spanish. No complaints found. This is not evidence of satisfaction, because coverage is thin.
- Momentum: active. It is still publishing 2026 regulatory content. No sign of acquisition or shutdown.
- Other RNDC tooling exists (e.g. an Odoo l10n_co_rndc module on apps.odoo.com), so the niche is not exclusive to Ofima.

## Pricing
Not found. No published prices or minimums surfaced. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
All unverified, inferred from positioning:
- A general ERP (accounting, inventory, HR), likely heavier and costlier than a small carrier needs for manifests alone.
- Likely sold through sales and implementation, not self-serve.
- Mobile or offline use for drivers and dispatchers is not evidenced.

## Opening
A lightweight, self-serve RNDC manifest and compliance tool for small carriers and owner-operators that does not need a full ERP.
