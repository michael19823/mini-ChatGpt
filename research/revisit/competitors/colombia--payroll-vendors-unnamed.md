# Colombia: Payroll vendors (unnamed)

Idea that this competitor killed: C0199, Colombia labour reform Ley 2466 payroll.
Research limits: 2 of 6 WebSearch calls returned results. The third hit a usage limit, so no complaint search ran.

## Verdict: strong

Colombia has many established payroll vendors (Siigo, Alegra, Ofima, Softland, Aleluya, MiRemuner). They are already publishing Ley 2466 guidance and updating for it. A standalone Ley 2466 payroll tool has no clear gap against them.

## Evidence
- Ley 2466 de 2025 was signed on 25 June 2025. Its changes phase in until July 2027. The Sunday and holiday surcharge goes 75% to 80% (July 2025), 90% (1 July 2026) and 100% (July 2027). Night work starts at 7 PM from 25 Dec 2025. Fixed-term contracts are capped at 4 years. Habitual commissions count as salary. Source: https://siemprealdia.co/colombia/recursos/guia-de-reforma-laboral/ and https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/colombia/pago-de-domingos-y-festivos-sube-al-90-desde-hoy-1-de-julio-de-2026-en-colombia/
- Ofima publishes a payroll guide to the reform: https://www.ofima.com/blog/reforma-laboral-colombia/
- Softland publishes reform content: https://softland.com/co/reforma-laboral-colombia-actual-gobierno/
- Siigo markets DIAN-certified electronic payroll for SMEs, with cloud updates (self-description): https://www.siigo.com/blog/tecnologia-innovacion/programas-para-hacer-nomina/
- Alegra offers payroll inside an integrated cloud suite for small businesses (self-description): https://blog.alegra.com/colombia/alegra-vs-siigo-colombia/
- Aleluya is payroll-only and claims unlimited DIAN electronic payroll transmission and an AI payroll check (self-description): https://aleluya.com/nomina-facil
- Complaints: none found. No review-site, Reddit or app-store search returned results. Unverified either way.

## Pricing
Alegra and Aleluya publish prices (https://aleluya.com/precios). I did not see the figures in the search snippets, so amounts are unverified. Alegra advertises a 15-day free trial with no card.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. Segments such as micro-employers or household employers may be thinner, but that is unverified.
- Ley 2466 is a rule change that every vendor must implement, so it is not a durable differentiator.

## Opening
Little. A gap would only come from a complaint pattern that was not searched, such as the micro-employer or domestic-worker segment or reform-update bugs.
