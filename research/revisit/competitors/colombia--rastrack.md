# Rastrack (Colombia) - competitor check for C0198 (Colombia carrier RNDC manifest)

## Verdict
weak (low confidence). Rastrack looks like a GPS/satellite fleet-tracking vendor, not an RNDC manifest-filing product. No evidence found that it files RNDC manifests.

## Evidence
- Rastrack S.A.S is based in Bogota and describes itself as "Administracion Satelital de Vehiculos": GPS tracking with sensors such as door open/close. Source: https://expeditiorepositorio.utadeo.edu.co/bitstreams/0c0f6a99-cf84-4e8c-8e51-7be8bb2138fa/download (academic case study on its use in fleet distribution in Bogota).
- It is hiring (Computrabajo listings for "Coordinador operativo", e.g. https://co.computrabajo.com/trabajo-de-coordinador-operativo-en-montebello). Employer identification from search snippets only, so unverified. This suggests it is still operating.
- A search for "Rastrack" together with RNDC / manifiesto de carga returned only regulation pages (Decreto 1079/2015, Resolucion 377/2013, Ofima RNDC article) and nothing linking Rastrack to RNDC filing.
- Complaints: none found. Searches for opinions, reviews and quejas returned no app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews. The absence is partly a search-tool limit, since I could not reach app stores or Facebook groups directly.
- Momentum: no news found on acquisition, shutdown or product releases. Unverified. Its tracking business dates to 1993 per a search summary (unverified).
- Context: the RNDC manifest rules are changing. Decreto 1017/2025 gave the electronic manifest executive merit, and unified manifest formats became mandatory 1 May 2026 (per search summaries of https://www.ofima.com/?p=24940). Established ERP and TMS vendors such as Ofima appear to be the more relevant RNDC competitors.

## Pricing
No published pricing found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Core product is tracking hardware plus platform, so it is probably not a manifest or RNDC-compliance tool (inferred, not confirmed on the vendor's own site, which I could not fetch).
- Likely requires installed GPS hardware (inferred).
- Whether it has any RNDC integration is unknown. Unverified.

## Opening
A small carrier needing RNDC manifest and fulfillment filing with the new 2026 formats and no hardware is not served by Rastrack. The real competition is RNDC-focused software and ERPs, which this check did not cover.
