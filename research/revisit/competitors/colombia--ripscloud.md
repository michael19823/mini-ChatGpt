# RipsCloud (Colombia)

Idea killed: C0196, Colombia FEV-RIPS JSON generator/validator. No URL known.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (unverified)

Five searches (English and Spanish) found no product, site, review or news item called "RipsCloud". It may exist under another spelling or be a small regional vendor with no web footprint. Its existence and quality are unverified, so it can't be called a strong competitor on this evidence.

## Evidence
- Searches for "RipsCloud", "RipsCloud RIPS facturación electrónica Colombia" and "RipsCloud opiniones reseñas precio RIPS JSON" returned no page about such a product. Results were unrelated (RISCO Cloud, R-Cloud, CloudRip) or general RIPS regulation.
- Complaints: none found (no app-store, Trustpilot, Capterra, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced).
- Context that matters for the idea: Minsalud itself publishes a free FEV-RIPS validator (installation guide, JSON converter user manual, validation rules and technical docs on minsalud.gov.co). That is a government free-tool substitute for the validation step, independent of RipsCloud.
- Regulation is moving: search results report Resolución 948 de 2026 regulating RIPS as FEV support and repealing Resoluciones 2275/2023, 558 and 1884 of 2024; the validator reached v1.4.4 (consultorsalud.com). Any generator would need to track those changes.
