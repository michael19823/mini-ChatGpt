# SmartQuick (Colombia)

Idea killed: C0198 Colombia carrier RNDC manifest. Searches used: 4 of 6.

## Verdict
**not-a-real-competitor**. SmartQuick appears to be the route-optimization and delivery-tracking platform of Quick, a Colombian last-mile and logistics operator. No source found links it to RNDC or electronic cargo manifest (MCE) filing. The identification is from search summaries only, so treat it as unverified.

## Evidence
- Search summaries describe SmartQuick as a portal and app that uses analytics and AI for route planning, delivery monitoring and operations optimization. Quick is a Bogota-based logistics platform founded in 2014. It runs a network of "Quickers" (transporters) for last-mile, distribution, long-haul and warehousing. Sources: https://www.descubre.vc/quick ; https://www.valoraanalitik.com/?p=112662 ; https://www.diarioestrategia.cl/texto-diario/mostrar/3231620/quick-plataforma-tecnologica-revoluciona-industria-logistica
- Quick reportedly started operating in the US (thelogisticsworld.com, dated 4 Aug 2026 in the results; details unverified): https://thelogisticsworld.com/actualidad-logistica/quick-la-plataforma-logistica-colombiana-inicia-operaciones-en-eeuu
- A search for SmartQuick together with RNDC or manifiesto returned only official RNDC regulation and Ofima content. None of it mentioned SmartQuick as a manifest or RNDC tool.
- Context: Decree 1017 of 2025 reportedly makes the electronic manifest executable, blocks issuance below SICE-TAC minimum freight, and from 2026 requires logistics-time reporting. Source: https://www.ofima.com/?p=24940 . Firms without their own software can use the Ministry's free RNDC program (rndc.mintransporte.gov.co).
- Complaints: none found. Searches turned up only employer ratings for Quick (4.4/5 from 437 reviews, on a job site). I found no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of SmartQuick.

## Pricing
Not published or not found (unverified). It looks like B2B shipper-side software tied to Quick's own services.

## Fit gaps
- It is built for shippers and fleet operators doing delivery routing. It is not an owner-operator carrier tool for issuing or reporting manifests.
- No evidence of RNDC web-service integration or manifest issuance.

## Opening
It does not block C0198: a simple RNDC manifest tool for small owner-operator carriers has no SmartQuick competition. The remaining blockers are the other listed competitors and the free Ministry RNDC tool.
