# Symplifica (Colombia) - competitor check for C1215 (domestic-worker employers, Ley 2466/2025)

## Verdict: strong (provisional; search budget cut short by a usage limit after 3 of 6 searches)

## Evidence
- Symplifica is a dedicated Colombian platform for household employers: social-security affiliation, contract generation, monthly payroll and contribution (PILA) calculation, with Symplifica Hogares (employer) and Symplifica Trabajadores (worker) apps. Source: https://www.enter.co/especiales/emprende/empleados-domesticos-emprendimiento
- Press coverage says it had formalised more than 4,000 domestic workers (undated article, likely early years; current scale unverified). Source: https://www.elespectador.com/economia/la-plataforma-digital-que-ha-formalizado-mas-de-4000-empleados-domesticos-en-colombia-articulo-809410
- It also appears in sponsored content on formalising domestic workers and avoiding sanctions: https://www.portafolio.co/contenido-patrocinado/como-formalizar-a-los-empleados-domesticos-y-evitar-sanciones-586079
- Demand tailwind: Ley 2466/2025 requires written contracts and PILA registration for domestic workers, day-rate minimums for 2026, and a 42-hour weekly cap from 15 July 2026; the sector is 700,000+ workers. Source: https://www.infobae.com/colombia/2026/03/19/empleadas-domesticas-por-dias-el-minimo-que-deben-recibir-en-2026-y-los-nuevos-derechos-que-los-empleadores-estan-obligados-a-cumplir/ . This helps Symplifica as much as any entrant.
- Complaints: none found. One Spanish-language complaint search returned only unrelated companies (Sympla, Simplifica Soluciones in Brazil), and the English reviews search failed on the usage limit. Only a Glassdoor employer page for Symplifica surfaced (https://www.glassdoor.ca/Reviews/Symplifica-Reviews-E4086780.htm); its content was not read. App-store, Trustpilot and Reddit reviews: unverified / not checked.
- Momentum: no 2026 news on the company itself found; status unverified. Funding, acquisition and shutdown: unverified.

## Pricing
- Per an undated article (old, likely stale): two plans, no lock-in; the plan for employers not yet affiliated to social security starts at COP 28,500 and varies with days worked per week (from 1 day/week); "Plan Cero Estres" for those already affiliated. Source: https://www.enter.co/especiales/emprende/empleados-domesticos-emprendimiento . Current prices: unverified.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. Possible but unverified: cost for 1-day-a-week cleaners, no coverage of the 42-hour/day-rate changes, or no WhatsApp-first flow. No evidence found either way.

## Opening
- Little visible opening; a challenger would need a clearly cheaper or simpler product aimed at by-the-day employers, and should first check current Symplifica pricing and app-store reviews.
