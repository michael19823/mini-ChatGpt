# Alegra (Costa Rica) - competitor check for C0211 (TRIBU-CR reconciliation for accountants)

## Verdict: strong

Alegra is a live, commercial, Latin American cloud accounting and invoicing vendor. Its own marketing (via search snippets) says it already ships TRIBU-CR-integrated invoicing 4.4, accounting, and automatic bank reconciliation for Costa Rican accounting firms, and a free accountant tier. Searches turned up no customer complaints to exploit.

## Evidence
- Alegra's Costa Rica blog page for accounting-firm software describes accounting software "integrated and validated with TRIBU-CR", with automatic bank reconciliation for BAC/BCR/BN per company. Source: https://blog.alegra.com/costa-rica/software-contable-para-despachos/ (vendor claim, from search snippet only; not independently verified).
- A related Alegra-owned portal (Siempre Al Dia) says Alegra integrates invoicing 4.4, accounting and reconciliation with TRIBU-CR on one platform, and that it connects to bank accounts, matches movements to invoices and expenses, and flags inconsistencies. Source: https://siemprealdia.co/costa-rica/ (vendor claim, unverified).
- A separate Alegra blog page mentions bank connections (BAC, BCR, Banco Nacional, Scotiabank) with AI-assisted automatic reconciliation. Source: https://blog.alegra.com/costa-rica/software-contable-crecimiento/ (vendor claim, unverified).
- "Alegra Contador": accountants' tool described as 100% free, managing the accounting of clients who use Alegra, with support for accountants. Source: Alegra blog / guiadesoftware.com search results (unverified detail).
- Momentum: active. Content on TRIBU-CR, a 2026 accountant guide and AI-for-accountants posts are current. Alegra is Colombia-based and hiring (Indeed listing, co.indeed.com/cmp/Alegra).
- Context: Colegio de Contadores Privados filed a precautionary measure over failures in TRIBU-CR (https://observador.cr/colegio-de-contadores-privados-presenta-medida-cautelar-por-fallas-en-tribu-cr/). This is a platform pain point for accountants, not a complaint about Alegra.

## Complaints
No complaints about Alegra in Costa Rica were found. Third-party reviews seen in search results were positive (Capterra Spain 4.5/5 from only 4 users; "simple", "stable"). The only mild criticisms were limited report customization and a wish for more advanced tutorials. No Google Play, App Store, Reddit or Facebook complaints surfaced. Employee reviews on Indeed (3.9/5) are not product complaints.

## Pricing
- Electronic invoicing from USD 4.99/month; prepaid and postpaid plans from about CRC 6,600/month plus VAT (search summary of Alegra pages; verify before use).
- Entrepreneur plan USD 9.99/month with 25 invoices; SME plan USD 19.99/month with unlimited invoices and inventory (search summary; unverified).
- Accountant tool free; 15-day trial.
- Cheap for the segment, so not overpriced.

## Fit gaps
- Reconciliation is tied to clients who are on Alegra. It is unverified whether it works for accountants whose clients use other ERPs or spreadsheets.
- It is unverified whether reconciliation covers TRIBU-CR filing statuses and ledgers beyond invoicing, or multi-entity batch work across many clients.
- Mild: report customization limits.
- All gaps are unverified; no direct evidence found.

## Opening
Narrow: only an ERP-agnostic reconciliation layer for accountants whose clients are not on Alegra, and that is unproven demand.
