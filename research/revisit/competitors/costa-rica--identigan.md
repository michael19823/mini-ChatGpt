# Identigan (Costa Rica) - competitor check for C1217 (herd-management app for ranchers)

## Verdict: beatable

Identigan is a real, live mobile herd-management app, but I found no evidence of Costa Rica-specific
presence, traction, reviews or pricing. The state-run Trazar-Agro system is the more important rival in Costa Rica.
(4 WebSearch calls used; WebFetch and GitHub not used.)

## Evidence
- Apple App Store listing "Identigan: App Ganadera" (https://apps.apple.com/co/app/identigan/id6449440032)
  describes a mobile livestock app: per-animal records, health/production history, purchases/sales/transfers,
  RFID electronic ID, Bluetooth scale integration, reproduction/health/production modules.
  Targets small, medium and large ranchers, technicians and vets. The listing is on the Colombian store
  (/co/), so its home market may be Colombia (unverified).
- No Costa Rica-specific mention of Identigan found in news or forums (unverified whether it markets there).
- Complaints: none found for Identigan. No app-store review text, Reddit, Facebook or forum complaints surfaced.
- Costa Rica context: MAG/OIRSA's Trazar-Agro is the government traceability platform (RFID ear tags,
  digital movement guides, auction module); MAG promised free tags and registration for micro and small
  producers (https://semanariouniversidad.com/pais/mag-promete-aretes-y-registro-de-trazabilidad-de-ganado-sin-costo-alguno-a-micro-y-pequenos-productores/).
  Ranchers' reported complaints are about Trazar-Agro, not Identigan: poor rural internet/cell coverage
  blocking digital movement guides, low tech skills among older ranchers, and resistance to mandatory ear tags
  (https://observador.cr/herramienta-permitira-darle-trazabilidad-al-ganado-de-costa-rica/ and the MAG/Semanario
  Universidad pages in search results; details not read in full).

## Pricing
Listed as free with in-app purchases on the App Store. Specific tiers and prices not found (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- No evidence it integrates with Trazar-Agro or Costa Rican MAG/SENASA movement-guide formats (unverified).
- No evidence of offline-first operation, which matters given the rural coverage gaps ranchers report (unverified).
- RFID/Bluetooth-scale features imply hardware for the full workflow.
- No Costa Rica local presence, support or distribution found.

## Opening
A Costa Rica-specific, offline-capable herd app that works with Trazar-Agro tagging and movement guides is
not served by what I could verify of Identigan; the risk is more the free government platform than Identigan.
