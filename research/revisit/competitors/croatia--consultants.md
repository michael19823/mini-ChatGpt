# Croatia: Consultants (NIS2 compliance) - competitor check for C0219

## Verdict: beatable

Consultants are real and numerous, but they sell bespoke, quote-only projects. No self-serve
tooling for small entities turned up in the searches (3 searches run, EN + HR).

## Evidence
- Croatia transposed NIS2 as the Zakon o kibernetičkoj sigurnosti (NN 14/2024) plus a Uredba (NN 135/2024). Scope is reported at roughly 8,000-10,000 organisations; some sources conflict on deadlines. Source: https://advisera.com/hr/articles/zakon-o-kibernetickoj-sigurnosti-hrvatske-naspram-nis-2/
- Active providers seen in results: Copla (https://copla.com/nis2-hr/), Advisera (templates/training; https://advisera.com/articles/croatia-nis2-cybersecurity-regulation/), A1 Digital (https://www.a1.digital/cybersecurity/compliance-certification/nis2-consulting), plus law-firm commentary (MTR Legal: https://www.mtrlegal.com/hr/provedba-nis-2-direktive/), FOI board-education offering (https://www.foi.unizg.hr/sites/default/files/Flyer-edukacija-uprave-1.pdf).
- A public procurement notice for NIS2 services in Zagreb had an estimated value of 400,000 EUR (https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/316290-2026/pdf); this is a large-buyer data point, not a small-firm price.
- Momentum: active. Multiple vendors market NIS2 services in Croatian.
- Complaints: none found. The searches returned no reviews, forum threads or news about consultant failures (unverified whether any exist on Reddit/Facebook/local forums, which were not specifically reached).

## Pricing
- No published consultant prices found (quote-only). Only the 400,000 EUR tender above, and generic cost-guide figures from non-Croatian sources, which I did not verify. Small-buyer affordability unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Project-based, bespoke work, likely priced for large or public-sector entities (inference, unverified).
- No evidence of a cheap self-serve, Croatian-language product for small important entities.
- Advisera sells templates/training, which is a partial substitute.

## Opening
A low-cost Croatian-language self-serve NIS2/ZKS workflow for small entities, priced well below consultant quotes.
