# Croatia: Generic GRC platforms (killed C0219, Croatia NIS2 compliance tooling)

## Verdict: beatable

"Generic GRC platforms" is a category, not one product. No Croatian-specific GRC vendor was found. The competing offers are foreign GRC/compliance-automation tools with Croatian localisation, plus documentation packages and consultants.

## Evidence
- Croatia transposed NIS2 through the Zakon o kibernetičkoj sigurnosti (NN 14/2024) and the Uredba (NN 135/2024). Compliance deadline is reported as Feb 2026, with full application by Jan 2027. Source: https://presencis.com/regulations/nis2/national/hr and https://dnevnik.hr/vijesti/tech/strucnjakinja-za-sigurnost-upozorava-ne-postoji-univerzalno-carobno-rjesenje-za-kiberneticku-sigurnost---874489.html (the figures come from search summaries, unverified in the pages themselves).
- Copla (copla.com) has Croatian-language NIS2 pages (https://copla.com/nis2-hr/), so a localised GRC offer exists. Its Croatian pricing was not found (unverified).
- Advisera sells a Croatian-language NIS2 documentation package (74 editable documents, AI wizard, 24 months of updates): https://advisera.com/hr/paketi-dokumentacije/nis-2-paket-dokumentacije/. Price not shown in results (unverified).
- Other EU-native NIS2 tools (Legiscope, Secjur, Orbiq, Kertos, Secfix, ComplianceHive) appear in searches. No evidence was found that they support Croatian or the Croatian regulator's specifics (unverified).
- Complaints found concern Vanta, a US tool, not Croatian products. Per a 6clicks write-up, small-startup users cite pricing, rigid contracts and difficult cancellation, and say it caters to mid-size and enterprise firms (https://www.6clicks.com/resources/blog/understanding-vantas-limitations-insights-from-real-user-experiences). This is a vendor-written secondary source. Vanta is US-built for SOC 2.
- No Croatian-language reviews, forum posts or app-store complaints about GRC tools were found in 5 searches. Finding none is a valid result.

## Pricing
- SME-focused platforms: roughly EUR 5,000-40,000/year. Enterprise GRC suites: EUR 50,000-200,000+/year. This is from an aggregator search summary, unverified.
- Legiscope: from EUR 400/month for SMEs (search summary, unverified).
- OneTrust raised its minimum ACV to $10,000/year from March 2026 renewals (search summary, unverified).
- Advisera documentation package and Copla Croatian pricing: not found.
- Likely too expensive for micro and small Croatian entities newly in NIS2 scope.

## Fit gaps
- Most tools are built for SOC 2, ISO 27001 or enterprise use, not Croatian NIS2 specifics.
- Croatian-language coverage is thin: only Copla and Advisera were seen.
- Not verified: whether any tool covers Croatian authority reporting formats (incident notification, self-assessment or categorisation of entities). The Croatian regulator's process needs a check.
- Small and mid-size entities face enterprise-priced tools, or consultants plus documentation templates.

## Opening
A cheap, Croatian-language NIS2 tool for SMEs (entity categorisation, measures checklist, incident reporting to the national authority) looks open. Copla is the main localised rival, so check its price and depth before committing.
