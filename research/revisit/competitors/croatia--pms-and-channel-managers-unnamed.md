# Croatia: PMS and channel managers (unnamed), eVisitor for private hosts (C0220)

## Verdict: strong

Several local and international PMS/channel managers already file to eVisitor automatically, and the market leader is highly rated with a low per-unit price. The official free mVisitor app covers the simplest case.

## Evidence
- Rentlio (PMS + channel manager + booking engine) offers an eVisitor integration for guest registration and check-out. Source: https://rentl.io/en/blog/industry-related/evisitor-croatia-vacation-rental-guest-registration-guide
- Rentlio + Chekin integration: online guest check-in, then automatic filing to eVisitor within the legal window. Source: https://chekin.com/en/blog/chekin-rentlio-integration/
- WebBookingPro lists eVisitor credentials and property ID in its settings and sends guests automatically at check-in. Source: https://webbookingpro.com/support/pms-general/adding-modifying-guest-book-and-evisitor/
- SWEN states it integrates with eVisitor for automatic check-in and reporting. Source: https://www.hiswen.com/blog/swen-integrations-channels-seamless-connectivity-for-hospitality-businesses
- Official mVisitor mobile app (Croatian Tourist Board) lets owners register guests by manual entry or document scan. Source: https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/mvisitor/id1619947820
- Complaints: none found for the eVisitor function in these searches. Rentlio's Capterra page shows 4.9/5 from 54 reviews. The minor concerns were that the mobile app lags the web version and that some customisation is limited. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/178760/Rentlio/reviews/ (as summarised by search; I did not read the page). No forum or local-language complaints about eVisitor tools surfaced; the Croatian forum search returned only official guidance.
- Momentum: Rentlio claims 17,000+ units in 40+ countries, which suggests active development (vendor claim, unverified independently). Chekin's blog content is recent.

## Pricing
- Rentlio vacation rentals: EUR 12 per unit, EUR 10 per unit at 4+ units, contact sales above 15 units, plus a 2% + VAT booking engine fee. Hotels: EUR 23 per room. Source: Capterra/GetApp listings (https://www.capterra.co.za/software/178760/rentlio). Chekin, WebBookingPro and SWEN prices: unverified (not found).
- mVisitor / eVisitor itself is free.

## Fit gaps
- PMS products are pitched at units that take bookings, and a host with 1-3 apartments and no channel manager may find a full PMS more than they need. Unverified, as no source states this.
- Chekin is a separate add-on to Rentlio, so the filing automation may cost extra (price unverified).
- No evidence found of missing language or filing-format gaps.

## Opening
Narrow at best: an eVisitor-only filer for tiny hosts competes with the free mVisitor app and with PMS tools that already file automatically, and none of the searches found complaints to exploit.
