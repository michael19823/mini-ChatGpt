# Cyprus: Greek ERPs (SoftOne/EntersoftOne, Epsilon Net/Pylon) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0226, Cyprus payroll and TFA filing.

## Verdict: strong (unverified on TFA specifics)

Established Greek vendors are firmly present in Cyprus with payroll modules and local subsidiaries. No complaints found, but also no evidence on whether they actually file via TFA.

## Evidence
- EntersoftOne (Entersoft + SoftOne merger, 2025) says it serves 90,000+ businesses in Greece, Cyprus, Bulgaria and Romania, with ERP, HCM/Payroll and Accounting products. Cyprus SMB/mid-market presence described as prominent (apideck.com Cyprus accounting integrations listing; job ads at apply.smartcv.co/entersoft-softone).
- Epsilon Net Cyprus Limited is a 100% subsidiary of Epsilon Net S.A. Pylon and Epsilon Net payroll apps are said to serve 30,000+ businesses and a large share of tourism/hospitality (fastforward.com.cy "Why Greek IT Giants Are Targeting Cyprus"; cbn.com.cy 2025/7/18 article). Company-sourced claims.
- Context: from 2025 the monthly TD7/PAYE return and payments moved to the TFA portal (eurofast.eu, KPMG Cyprus, Andersen Cyprus). Employers must register valid TINs for all employees.
- Complaints: 5 of 6 searches in English and Greek found no reviews, app-store ratings, forum posts or outage news about these vendors' Cyprus payroll. No complaints found, which is not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified. No published prices for payroll or TFA integration.

## Fit gaps (all unverified)
- Whether their payroll modules produce TFA-ready TD7 files or only export data for manual upload is unknown.
- Likely sold via resellers and partners, which suggests accountant-oriented, quote-based pricing that may be heavy for micro-employers.
- Desktop and ERP heritage; Greek-law payroll logic had to be adapted to Cyprus (social insurance 8.8%, GESY 2.65% / 2.9%).

## Momentum
Active: the 2025 merger and Greek IT groups explicitly targeting Cyprus (fastforward.com.cy, cbn.com.cy).

## Opening
Only a narrow gap: a cheap TFA-first TD7 filing tool for micro and small employers or accountants, if the Greek ERPs turn out to lack direct TFA submission. Verify with a direct check of their Cyprus product pages before betting on it.
