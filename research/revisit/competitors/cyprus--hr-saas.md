# Cyprus: HR SaaS (generic, no URL known)

Dropped idea: C0226 Cyprus payroll and TFA filing.

## Verdict: weak
"HR SaaS" is a generic label, not one product. No Cyprus-specific payroll/HR SaaS with TFA (Tax For All) filing was identified in 5 searches (English and Greek).

## Evidence
- Context: from 1 Jan 2025 monthly PAYE/TD7 (ΤΦ7) declarations are mandatory via the TFA portal, and the annual return needs every employee's TIN. Sources: https://eurofast.eu/cyprus-employers-tax-return-t-d-7-transitioning-to-the-tax-for-all-tfa-portal/ , https://kpmg.com/cy/en/home/insights/2025/05/extension-for-filing-of-td7-2024-and-faqs.html
- Results are mostly accountant/advisory blogs (ibccs.tax, rightax.com.cy, Accace payroll services https://accace.com/locations/cyprus/payroll-services), which suggests the work is largely done by outsourced providers, not self-serve software.
- Global players (Remote, Deel) are EOR/global payroll, not local TFA-filing tools.
- A snippet mentioned an unnamed cloud HR/payroll platform that files with Cypriot tax, health, labour and social institutions (https://een.ec.europa.eu/partnering-opportunities/cloud-based-software-service-platform-hr-payroll-and-shift-management). It is a partnering listing; the product is unverified.
- Whether any vendor files TD7 to TFA directly (API/export) is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews surfaced.

## Pricing
- Remote.com: $29 per employee per month (payroll), per search summary; Deel payroll from $599/month management fee; Gloroots EOR $199 per employee per month (https://www.gloroots.com/blog/best-eor-in-republic-of-cyprus). These are not verified on vendor pages and are for global/EOR products.
- No published Cyprus-local SaaS pricing found.

## Fit gaps
- Global tools are costly per head for a small local employer and are EOR-oriented.
- No evidence of Greek-language, TFA-specific filing support (unverified).
- Local advisers fill the gap manually.

## Momentum
Unverifiable for a generic label. The regulatory change (monthly TD7 since 2025) is a live demand driver.

## Opening
Small Cyprus employers face new monthly TFA filings with no found self-serve, Greek-language, affordable tool, but this is based on thin evidence; validate whether local ERP/payroll vendors already offer TFA export.
