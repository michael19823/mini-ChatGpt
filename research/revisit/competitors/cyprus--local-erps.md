# Cyprus: Local ERPs (idea C0226: Cyprus payroll and TFA filing)

## Verdict
beatable (low confidence: 5 of 6 searches used, thin evidence, no pricing found)

## Evidence
- "Local ERPs" is a category, not one product, and no URL was known. Search found one concrete Cyprus-specific payroll vendor: Codeworks Ltd, which makes PayrollPlus and a TD7 Editor. Sources: https://ergodotisi.com/en-CY/companies/codeworks-ltd-94824383/about , https://www.carierista.com/en/partners/827/codeworks-ltd
- The TD7 Editor is described as part of PayrollPlus or standalone. It prepares the TD7 as an .XML file for the Tax Department's Taxisnet. The search snippets do not say whether it files directly to TAX FOR ALL (TFA). Unverified.
- The legal context moved against desktop and XML tools. Monthly TD7 filing and payment now go exclusively through TFA, mandatory from January 2025. Sources: https://cy.andersen.com/news/cyprus-news-cyprus-tax-update-employer-td7-filing-obligations-new-deadlines/ , https://rightax.com.cy/2025/09/14/tf7-tfa-employer-obligations-2025/
- Other results were Greek vendors (Soft1, Epsilon Net) and international or EOR vendors (Playroll, Expanship). None are local Cyprus ERPs. A web.hr page about cloud HR software in Cyprus appeared but was not examined.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of Cyprus payroll tools turned up, in English or Greek. Absence of reviews is not evidence that users are satisfied.
- Momentum: unverified. Codeworks was still listed as a partner on Cypriot job and portal sites, but nothing shows release activity or any TFA integration roadmap.

## Pricing
No published prices found for PayrollPlus or any other local ERP. All unverified. Likely quote-based, which suggests micro employers are not the target.

## Fit gaps (inferred, mostly unverified)
- Products appear to be desktop or ERP-style and aimed at accountants and mid-sized firms, with no evidence of a micro-employer self-serve offering.
- TFA direct filing or integration was not confirmed. The XML export workflow predates TFA.
- No evidence of mobile, English/Greek self-serve onboarding, or published small-business pricing.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, TFA-ready monthly TD7, social insurance and GESY payroll tool for micro employers looks open. Verify that the local ERPs' TFA support is missing and what they charge before committing.
