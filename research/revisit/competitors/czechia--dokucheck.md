# DokuCheck (Czechia) - https://www.dokucheck.cz/

Idea killed: C0228, Czech Portal stavebnika submission validator.

## Verdict: strong

DokuCheck is a live product that does what the dropped idea proposed: pre-submission checks of files for the Portal stavebnika. Search turned up no complaints, no pricing and no momentum data, so the verdict rests on direct overlap only. Treat it as provisional.

## Evidence
- The site title reads "DokuCheck: Vase jistota pri kazdem podani na Portal stavebnika." Source: https://www.dokucheck.cz/ (seen only as a search-result title and summary; WebFetch was not allowed).
- Per the search summary of that page, it does batch checks of PDF/A-3, electronic signatures (PAdES), qualified time stamps and basic metadata errors.
- It is described as having no editing, signing or document-creation functions. It is a pure checker, which is what C0228 proposed.
- It comes in two modes: a desktop "Agent" app for local use, and a web interface. The page says the Agent is a free download from Microsoft Store, with a licence available after installation.
- In local mode only metadata about format, signature and stamp goes to the verification server. No project data leaves the office. This is a privacy selling point.
- The page says it was made by engineers and aims to avoid returns of submissions.
- Related context (not about DokuCheck): the Czech Chamber of Authorised Architects (CKAIT) ran an article in March 2026 on unexpected risks in documentation checks after digitalisation: https://zpravy.ckait.cz/vydani/2026-03/digitalizace-stavebniho-rizeni-prinesla-necekana-rizika-pri-kontrole-dokumentace/ (title only; unverified relevance).
- The Ministry for Regional Development (MMR) publishes a "Metodika k Verifikatoru podpisu BPP". This suggests an official signature verifier exists, but I did not confirm what it covers or whether it overlaps with DokuCheck. Unverified.

## Complaints
None found. Searches of Czech and English terms returned no app-store, forum, Reddit, Trustpilot or news items about DokuCheck. The product name is also easy to confuse with unrelated tools (DocuCheck, DocuChecker, Doku), which hid any reviews that may exist. Absence of reviews most likely means a small user base, not satisfied users.

## Pricing
Unverified. The only fact found: the Agent app is free to download from Microsoft Store and needs a licence. The licence price, tiers and minimums were not found.

## Fit gaps
Only inferred, so unverified:
- It checks format, signatures, time stamps and metadata. It does not appear to check content: required document set, drawing completeness, or the form fields and structure of the Portal's submission.
- The Agent app is on Microsoft Store, so it likely runs on Windows only. Mac and mobile are unconfirmed.
- Nothing seen on digital technical map (DTM) or geodetic submission formats, which is a separate validation area handled by CUZK.

## Momentum
Unknown. No dated releases, news or acquisitions found. The site is live and the new construction law and Portal stavebnika are current in 2026, so demand is plausible.

## Opening
A validator that goes beyond file format, checking content, completeness and the submission package against the Portal's rules, could differentiate. Any real gap needs confirming through the DokuCheck site and pricing, which this check could not do.
