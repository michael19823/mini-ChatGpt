# Czechia: multiple vendors (unnamed), for C0233 Czech electronic construction diary

## Verdict: strong

The market already has several Czech-language e-diary products, at least one with a free tier. Searches found no complaints and no mandate that would create demand.

## Evidence
- Several Czech vendors exist: Stavario (online diary, attendance, equipment, tasks, investor communication) and STAVEE (mobile app on Android/iOS meeting basic legislative requirements). Sources: https://www.ceskestavby.cz/firmy/stavario.html, https://www.estav.cz/cz/8454.prejdete-na-elektronicky-stavebni-denik-snizite-naklady-a-usetrite-spoustu-casu
- Free or cheap App Store apps in Czech also exist: "Můj stavební deník", StavX and "Deník Stavebníka". The search summary described them as offline-capable, but I did not check that on the store pages (unverified). Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/m%C5%AFj-stavebn%C3%AD-den%C3%ADk/id6504241953
- Czech legal and trade press (epravo, estav) already presents online diaries as legally acceptable and normal. Source: https://www.epravo.cz/top/aktualne/stavebni-denik-z-pohledu-legislativy-a-moznosti-jeho-vedeni-online-112923.html
- Complaints: none found for any product (no store, G2 or forum reviews surfaced). The only criticism was generic: some users treat the diary like a social network and expect replies to every comment, which floods communication with messages (estav.cz, https://www.estav.cz/cz/8454.prejdete-na-elektronicky-stavebni-denik-snizite-naklady-a-usetrite-spoustu-casu).
- Regulation: the searches did not show that the new Building Act mandates a specific e-diary system. I found no state-run diary system. The state-run Builder's Portal (Portál stavebníka) covers permit communication only. Source: https://arws.cz/en/news-at-arrows/czech-new-building-act
- Momentum: unverified. I found no dates, acquisitions or shutdowns.

## Pricing
- Stavario: per its listing, the basic tier is free for individuals with small projects. Larger projects are priced by number of users, projects and cloud storage. No figures found (unverified): https://www.ceskestavby.cz/firmy/stavario.html
- STAVEE: no price found.
- Small apps: described as free. The store pages were not checked (unverified).
- The only price in the results was for a Swiss product, JUURLY Baujournal, at CHF 39 per month. It is not a Czech competitor.

## Fit gaps
- No clear gap found. Free tiers cover small self-builders. The product category is commoditised.
- Possible gaps, all unverified: integration with the Builder's Portal, an official-format export, or a mandated format. No mandate was found.

## Opening
Little. A new entrant would face free incumbents, unless the new Building Act later mandates a specific diary format or portal integration, which was not found.
