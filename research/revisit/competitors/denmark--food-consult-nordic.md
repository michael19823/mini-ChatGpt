# Food Consult Nordic (Denmark) - competitor check for C0240 (Denmark food egenkontrol temperature logging)

## Verdict: beatable

A small Aalborg consultancy that resells third-party sensor hardware (ThermIT). It is not a software product company. Searches were cut short: only 2 of 6 WebSearch calls ran, and the rest failed on a usage limit. The verdict is therefore moderately confident.

## Evidence
- Listed by Fodevarestyrelsen (Danish Food Agency) as an approved food advisor (radgiver pa fodevareomradet), Engtoftevej 21, 9000 Aalborg. The listed approval expiry was 17 Feb 2025, so current status is unverified. Source: https://foedevarestyrelsen.dk/Media/638699349603565832/Godkendte rådgivere på fødevareområdet efter 1. april 2008.pdf
- Owned by food consultant Marco Imhof. It says it has helped more than 600 companies since 2020 and is moving into digital services. Source: https://nordsoeposten.dk/nyt-nordjysk-samarbejde-om-digital-foedevaresikkerhed
- Partnership with ThermIT (wireless temperature monitoring). The offer is consultancy, service and equipment. The electronic egenkontrol covers 24/7 automatic fridge/freezer monitoring, smartphone/PC view, deviation alerts, automatic documentation and technical support. Same source as above.
- Complaints: none found. No reviews (Trustpilot, app stores, forums) were reached. The review search was not run because of the usage limit. Absence of complaints is not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing
Not found in the results (unverified). The model appears to be consultancy plus hardware, probably quote-based. Nothing is published.

## Fit gaps (partly inferred, mark as unverified)
- Hardware-dependent: the monitoring runs through ThermIT wireless sensors, so there is no hardware-free or manual/app-only logging option (unverified).
- Local one-person-led consultancy (Aalborg), with service-heavy delivery. This is unlikely to scale or to offer self-serve onboarding (unverified).
- No evidence of its own app, multi-site tooling or other languages.

## Opening
A self-serve, low-cost, hardware-optional egenkontrol and temperature-log app for small Danish food businesses leaves room beside a consultant-plus-sensor bundle. Verify pricing and reviews before relying on this.
