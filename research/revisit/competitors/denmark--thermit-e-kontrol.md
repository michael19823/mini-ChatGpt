# ThermIT E-Kontrol (Denmark)

Idea it killed: C0240 (Denmark food egenkontrol temperature logging).
Searches used: 5 of 6 (2 hit a usage-limit error and were re-run; 1 result set was irrelevant).

## Verdict: strong (provisional, thin evidence)

Reason: it is a real, Danish, HACCP/egenkontrol-specific product from an established temperature-logging vendor, and it bundles egenkontrol software with wireless logging. Direct coverage of the dropped idea. No complaints were found, but I also found no independent reviews at all.

## Evidence
- ThermIT ApS (thermit.dk) is a Danish temperature monitoring/logging vendor serving kitchens, supermarkets, healthcare, pharmacies, labs, transport. Source: https://brandfetch.com/thermit.dk, https://funnelfeedr.com/no/company/dk/thermit-aps-31063914
- E-Kontrol is described (search summaries of the vendor's pages) as egenkontrol software with flow diagrams, company profile, procedure descriptions, HACCP-based risk assessment, and control parameters for goods receipt, hot holding, cooling, buffets, plus cleaning and maintenance plans. Temperature monitoring is integrated. Source: search result summaries (the vendor page itself was not opened).
- Wireless 24/7 automatic logging, web portal and mobile app, alerts by email, notification or SMS. Same source.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced in English or Danish. This is absence of evidence, not evidence of satisfaction.
- Momentum: unverified. No news, acquisition or shutdown info found.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified. Likely quote-based with hardware sensors, which suggests a cost for very small buyers, but this is an inference only.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- Appears sensor/hardware-led, so a hardware-free or manual-entry user may be underserved.
- Likely sold B2B via the vendor; small kiosks, food trucks, and home-based producers may be overlooked. Not confirmed.

## Opening
A low-cost, hardware-free (phone-only) egenkontrol logbook for micro food businesses could undercut ThermIT, but only if pricing research confirms ThermIT is priced out of reach.
