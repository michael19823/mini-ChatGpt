# Alegra Contador (Dominican Republic)

Idea killed: C0252 (606/607 from e-CF XMLs for accountants). Searches: 5 of 6 used. Search snippets only; no pages were fetched.

## Verdict: strong

Alegra is DGII-certified for e-CF and generates 606/607/608 automatically, and it has an accountant multi-client mode. Those are the core features of C0252.

## Evidence
- Alegra says it is DGII-certified for e-CF. It says reports 606, 607 and 608 are generated automatically from the recorded vouchers, and also the IT-1. Source: https://blog.alegra.com/republica-dominicana/facturacion-electronica-rd/ (search snippet).
- Alegra's accountant plan (Plan Contador / Espacio Contador) lets one accountant manage multiple client companies under one subscription. I saw this for Mexico (unlimited RFCs) at https://blog.alegra.com/mexico/alegra-vs-contalink/. I did not confirm that the same plan is offered in the Dominican Republic (unverified).
- Alegra's accountant-facing content for the Dominican Republic is active, and it was published through siemprealdia.co. Examples: https://siemprealdia.co/republica-dominicana/contabilidad/gestion-contable-con-alegra/ and https://siemprealdia.co/republica-dominicana/impuestos/software-para-emitir-factura-electronica/. A Dominican Republic strategy director is named in press (Haydee Cabrera). Momentum: active, with AI automations mentioned in reviews.
- Complaints: none found. Reviews seen were positive: Capterra Spain 4.5/5 from 4 users (https://www.capterra.es/software/196363/alegra-erp), and GetApp/Software Advice praising ease of use. Suggestions were for more tutorials and more report and letterhead customization. None of these were Dominican Republic specific, and I found no Dominican Republic app-store, Reddit or forum complaints.

## Pricing
- No Dominican Republic price was found (unverified).
- Other countries, from a search snippet: Mexico 2026 MXN 499 to 1,999 per month; Colombia 2026 COP 74,900 to 250,900 per month. There is no contract lock-in and no per-document charges.

## Fit gaps
- Alegra generates 606/607 from vouchers recorded inside Alegra. It is not clear that it ingests third-party e-CF XMLs received from suppliers or from other invoicing systems. This is unverified and is the main possible gap.
- It is a full accounting and invoicing suite. An accountant who only wants a lightweight XML-to-606/607 converter, without switching clients to Alegra, may not be served.
- Multi-client DR pricing is unknown.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: a standalone, system-agnostic tool that takes e-CF XMLs from any source, from clients not on Alegra, and produces 606/607. Alegra already covers the use case for clients who are on Alegra.
