# ContApp Digital (Dominican Republic)

Killed idea: C0252, Dominican Republic 606/607 from e-CF XMLs for accountants.

Research was cut short. WebSearch returned "usage limit" on 2 of 3 calls, so only 1 search succeeded. Findings are thin and mostly unverified.

## Verdict
**strong** (provisional, low confidence). It is a real, live product that appears to target the same buyer. Only a store listing description was seen, so its 606/607 coverage is unverified.

## Evidence

It covers finances, ITBIS control, invoices with DGII-authorized sequences, inventory, financial reports and easy connection with accountants. (Source: search-result summary of the listing; I did not open the listing.)

- Complaints: none found. No reviews were retrieved (the review searches failed), so this is "not checked", not "no complaints exist". Google Play, Reddit, Facebook and local forums were not searched.
- Other DR tools mentioned in the same results: Digisoft ERP (advertises automatic 606/607/608 formats) and Siempre al Día content on e-CF automation. They show the space is crowded.
