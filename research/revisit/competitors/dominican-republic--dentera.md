# Dentera (Dominican Republic) - competitor check for C0253

Idea killed: C0253, Dominican Republic ARS claims plus e-CF for clinics.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

No evidence that a Dentera dental or clinic software product exists in the Dominican Republic. 4 of 6 searches used (English and Spanish).

## Evidence
- Search "Dentera software dental clínica República Dominicana" found no Dentera product. It returned other dental software (Denteo, Dr.DENTES, DentoD, Dentech, Dentactil, Dentem), none tied to Dentera.
- Search "Dentera dental practice software Dominican Republic ARS claims e-CF" found no Dentera. It surfaced Dentalcore, which the search summary says operates in the Dominican Republic with insurance billing. This was not verified on its own page. It is a different product and may be a more relevant competitor to the idea.
- Search `"Dentera" dental` found only Dentera LLC (Salt Lake City, US), an oral-care products company selling the FlossQuick system (https://gust.com/companies/dentera-llc). That is a consumer product, not software, and has no Dominican link. Otherwise "dentera" is a Spanish/Tagalog dictionary word meaning teeth on edge.
- Search on Dominican dental software with ARS claims and e-CF found only generic e-CF material (DGII e-invoicing, grace period ending 15 Nov 2026 per beancount.io) and general invoicing software such as Eduka (https://www.edukasoftware.com/?p=11188). It found nothing on dental ARS claims.
