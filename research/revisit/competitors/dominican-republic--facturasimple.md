# FacturaSimple (Dominican Republic)

Killed idea: C0252, Dominican Republic 606/607 from e-CF XMLs for accountants.

## Verdict: beatable (low confidence)

It is a real, accountant-focused e-CF product. But the searches found no evidence that it generates 606/607 files, and no published price. No reviews were found. 3 of 6 searches were planned; one hit a usage limit and two returned results, so the evidence is thin.

## Evidence
- A Contadores en Red article describes FacturaSimple as a platform for issuing electronic vouchers for businesses plus a client dashboard for accountants. It was built to replace the fiscal printer, cut accountant-client communication time and help with e-CF compliance. https://contadoresenred.com/factura-imple-emision-de-comprobantes-electronicos-para-empresas-y-tablero-de-clientes-para-contadores/
- The same source says accountants get a "Control Panel" to manage all their client companies for free: business setup, point-of-sale setup and certificate management for WebServices. (Search-summary wording, not read in full.)
- Nothing found says it builds 606/607 from e-CF XMLs. Unverified either way.
- Complaints: none found. A search for reviews returned only unrelated products: Contasimple (Spain) and an Argentine "FacturaSimple" on Tiendanube. The Argentine product is probably a different company, so it is ignored here.
- Momentum: unknown. No dates, funding or update information found. Unverified.
- Alegra (alegra.com blog) publishes 606/607 content for the Dominican Republic, so other vendors already cover those formats. Not verified as direct competition.

## Pricing
Not found. The accountant dashboard is described as free. Business plan prices are unverified. No URL for the official site was found.

## Fit gaps
- Focuses on issuing e-CFs. Importing existing e-CF XMLs from received documents into 606/607 is not evidenced. Unverified.
- Tied to its own issuing platform, so it probably doesn't help accountants whose clients use other e-CF providers. Inference, unverified.

## Opening
A standalone tool that turns any provider's e-CF XMLs into 606/607 files for accountants is not clearly covered by FacturaSimple, but a free accountant dashboard and Alegra make the space contested.
