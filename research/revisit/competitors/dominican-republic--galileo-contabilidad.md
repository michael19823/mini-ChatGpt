# Galileo Contabilidad (Dominican Republic)

Killed idea: C0252 - Dominican Republic 606/607 from e-CF XMLs for accountants.
Searches used: 4 of 6 (Spanish and English-style queries).

## Verdict: strong

It markets itself at exactly this buyer (DR accounting firms doing 606/607/IT-1 for multiple clients) with a free tier. I found no complaints and no gaps that I could verify.

## Evidence
- Capterra listing describes it as cloud accounting software for Dominican SMBs (MIPYMES) needing DGII e-CF compliance, and for accounting firms managing taxes, payroll and 606/607/IT-1 reports for multiple clients. It lists AI document extraction, bank reconciliation, fiscal reminders, POS and payroll. Source: https://www.capterra.co.za/software/1111377/Galileo-Contabilidad (same listing on other Capterra country domains).
- Momentum: no news, funding, acquisition or shutdown found. A Capterra listing exists, but I saw no date or update info. Momentum is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. Searches for reviews and complaints returned only the Capterra listing and an unrelated Trustpilot page for "galileofx" (a different company). I found no app-store, Reddit, Facebook or forum reviews. Absence of reviews suggests a small user base, not a good product.
- Other DR players appear in the same results (Alegra, Softland, Siempre al Dia content), so the segment is crowded. None were investigated here.

## Pricing
- Capterra shows a flat-rate model, a free version, a free trial and a starting price of US$0.00. I found no published paid-plan prices or minimums (unverified). Competing against a free tier on price is hard.

## Fit gaps
- None verified. Could not confirm whether it imports third-party e-CF XMLs to build 606/607, or whether it only generates them from invoices issued in its own system (unverified). This is the one plausible gap.
- Offline or desktop use, multi-entity depth and accountant-specific workflows are also unverified. Its feature list suggests a general SMB suite, not an accountant tool.

## Opening
Only a narrow tool that ingests e-CF XMLs from any issuer (including clients on other systems) into 606/607 could differ from it. That depends on the XML-import gap, which is unconfirmed.
