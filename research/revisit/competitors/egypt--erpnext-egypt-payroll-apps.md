# ERPNext Egypt payroll apps (Egypt)

Dropped idea: C0268, Egypt payroll / Labour Law 14/2025.

## Verdict: weak

It is a custom-implementation add-on, not a self-serve product. It needs an ERPNext install and a scoping call, so it does not reach a small Egyptian employer who has no ERP.

## Evidence
- The only concrete listing found is "erpnext egypt payroll" by ECOSIRE (https://ecosire.com/apps/erpnext/erpnext-egypt-payroll). It covers progressive income-tax brackets, employer/employee social insurance, end-of-service settlement and Arabic RTL payslips, with tax and insurance reports. Per the listing, ECOSIRE builds, installs and supports it on ERPNext v15/v16 after a scoping call, and it is "not an off-the-shelf download".
- A sibling Odoo product from the same vendor (https://ecosire.com/apps/odoo/odoo-egypt-payroll) has similar scope. This shows the vendor sells Egypt payroll as per-ERP add-ons.
- No mention of Labour Law 14/2025 changes was found in the listing text. Whether it is updated for the law is unverified.
- Searched in Arabic (Labour Law 14/2025 payroll software). The results were only law texts, with no competing payroll product surfaced.
- Complaints: none found. I found no app-store, G2, Reddit or forum reviews of it. No community Frappe HR Egypt app was found in these searches (GitHub was out of scope).
- Searches used: 3 of 6.

## Pricing
- ERPNext add-on: indicative "from $249 USD", with a quote after scoping (ECOSIRE listing). Exact terms are unverified.
- Odoo sibling: one-time licence from $299 USD, including 12 months of updates and support (ECOSIRE listing).
- ERPNext hosting or implementation costs come on top and were not checked.

## Fit gaps
- It requires an ERPNext/Frappe deployment, so it is unsuitable for micro or small firms and accountants who run no ERP.
- It is sold as a services engagement, not as self-serve SaaS.
- It does not appear to include e-filing with the Egyptian tax authority or social insurance authority, only exportable report layouts. This is unverified.
- Mobile and offline use is not mentioned.
- There is no evidence of 14/2025-specific features.

## Opening
A standalone, self-serve Arabic-first Egypt payroll tool that tracks Law 14/2025, aimed at small firms without an ERP, is not served by this offering.
