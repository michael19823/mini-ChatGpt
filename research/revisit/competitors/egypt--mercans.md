# Mercans (Egypt)

Dropped ideas: C0268 (Egypt payroll / Labour Law 14/2025), C0272 (Egypt HR/payroll).

## Verdict: strong (for enterprise/multinational buyers); beatable only for small local SMEs

Caveat: only 2 of the 6 allowed searches returned results (the usage limit cut off the complaints and Arabic searches). Complaint search is incomplete, not "none found".

## Evidence
- Mercans runs a dedicated Egypt offering: payroll outsourcing, PEO/EOR and payroll software. It claims in-country compliance teams and direct ETA (tax) and NOSI (social insurance) relationships. These are the company's own claims, unverified. Sources: https://mercans.com/global-payroll/egypt/ , https://mercans.com/payroll-software/egypt/ , https://mercans.com/employer-of-record-payroll-peo/egypt/
- It publishes Egypt payroll outsourcing alerts, so it is active on Egyptian regulatory content (a 2020 PDF is the one I saw: https://mercans.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Mercans-Egypt-Payroll-Outsourcing-Alert-Updated.pdf). Whether it has covered Law 14/2025 is unverified.
- Third-party review sites position it for mid-market and enterprise multi-country payroll in 160+ countries (e.g. https://eorhq.com/reviews/mercans/ , https://softwarefinder.com/hr/mercans ). I did not read their review text, so I cite no specific complaints.
- Complaints (app stores, Reddit, Trustpilot, Arabic forums): not found. The searches did not run, so this is unverified.
- Momentum: no acquisition or shutdown news found. The company appears actively marketed. Detailed development activity is unverified.

## Pricing
- Quote-only. A third-party estimate (unverified, from aggregator results) puts global payroll at about $25-60 per employee per month, with implementation of $2,000-15,000 and EOR from about $400-600 per employee per month.
- That is likely too high and too heavyweight for a small Egyptian SME with a local payroll need.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at mid-market and enterprise multinationals that need multi-country consolidation, not Egyptian SMEs.
- Managed-service and quote-based, so there is no self-serve, low-cost option (unverified).
- Arabic-first UX, local-payment/WhatsApp workflows and mobile-first use are unverified. Nothing seen on them.

## Opening
A cheap, Arabic-first, self-serve Egypt payroll/HR tool for local SMEs (Law 14/2025 aware) is not served by Mercans' enterprise, quote-based model, but any multinational-facing product would face a strong incumbent.
