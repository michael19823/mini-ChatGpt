# ZenHR (Egypt)

Dropped ideas: C0268 (Egypt payroll / Labour Law 14/2025), C0272 (Egypt HR/payroll).

## Verdict: strong

Well-funded, Egypt-localised, bilingual cloud HR and payroll product with high review scores and few complaints. Searches (4 of 6 used) found no Egypt-specific complaints.

## Evidence
- Capterra: 4.7/5 from 161 reviews (ease of use 4.8, support 4.6, value 4.6, features 4.6). Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/157129/zenHR/
- G2: 4.6/5 from 55 reviews. Source: https://www.g2.com/products/zenhr-solutions-zenhr/reviews
- Complaints are minor and not Egypt-specific (from search summaries of Capterra/G2; the review text itself was not opened): missing punch in some cases, errors entering bank details, wrong locations, and poorly organised onboarding training with no follow-up after go-live (https://www.capterra.com/p/157129/zenHR/reviews/).
- Complaints in Arabic or on Reddit, Facebook, app stores or local forums: none found. The Arabic search returned only funding and profile pages.
- Egypt fit: ZenHR's own Egypt page claims compliance with Egyptian Labor Law and Social Insurance Law (income tax brackets, social insurance, Martyrs Tax, EOSB, overtime), in Arabic and English (https://www.zenhr.com/hr-payroll-software/egypt). This is a vendor claim. It has a labour-law reference page at https://www.zenhr.com/en/egypt-labor-law. Whether it has fully implemented Law 14/2025 (effective 1 Sept 2025) is unverified.
- Momentum: raised a $10M growth round led by Rua Growth Fund, with Endeavor Catalyst participating (https://www.wamda.com/en/2022/10/zenhr-successfully-raises-10Million-to-fuel-expansion-plans-arabic-english). It operates in 12 MEA markets and is actively developed (2026 listings). Founded 2017.

## Pricing
Capterra lists a starting price of US$5.00 (unit not confirmed, probably per employee per month) and a free trial. Detailed pricing requires contacting sales. Targets companies from 11-50 to 10,000+ employees. Egypt-specific price in EGP: unverified.

## Fit gaps
- No clear gap found for SMEs; it already serves 11-50 employee firms.
- Possible gaps (all unverified): depth of Law 14/2025 features (annual 3% increment, tiered sick pay, 120-day maternity leave, the 30-day employee statement to authorities), government filing integration, micro-businesses under 10 staff, onboarding and training quality.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: ultra-small Egyptian firms or a Law 14/2025 filing and compliance workflow that ZenHR does not cover. A general HR/payroll product would face a funded, well-reviewed incumbent.
