# Equatorial Guinea: Excel template (generic)

Idea killed: C0281, VAT-on-every-invoice compliance.

## Verdict: weak

A generic Excel or Word invoice is the status quo, but it is not a compliance product. The rule only requires VAT to be shown on invoices. No format or e-channel is mandated, so a spreadsheet already satisfies it. The opening exists only if the buyer sees value beyond compliance.

## Evidence
- A search for Equatorial Guinea VAT invoice requirements returned an e-invoice.app country page and EY tax news. Via the search summary, these say there is no e-invoicing mandate and no announced programme. Orden Ministerial 04/2025 (5 Sep 2025) requires every resident company to show VAT on all sales invoices, and neither text prescribes a format or electronic channel. Sources: https://www.e-invoice.app/country/GQ and https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2019-2230 (the EY page is from 2019; I did not open either page).
- A Spanish-language search on IVA, factura electrónica and Excel returned nothing specific to Equatorial Guinea, only pages on other African countries.
- Complaints: none found. I found no reviews, forum posts or news about Excel templates failing for Equatorial Guinea VAT invoicing. I ran only 2 of the 6 allowed searches, so absence of evidence is weak.
- Momentum: not applicable. This is a generic tool, not a product.

## Pricing
Free or already owned. I found no published price for any Equatorial Guinea-specific template (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- No VAT validation, no numbering control, and no audit trail or tamper protection.
- Manual calculation errors are possible.
- No tax-return (VAT filing) output and no multi-user use.
- These gaps matter only if the buyer cares about more than showing VAT on the invoice.

## Opening
Compliance alone is a weak pitch against a free spreadsheet, so a product would need to sell invoicing and bookkeeping convenience and VAT-return support, not the display rule.
