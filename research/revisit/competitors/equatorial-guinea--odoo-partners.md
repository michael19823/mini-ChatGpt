# Equatorial Guinea: Odoo partners

Dropped idea: C0284 (Equatorial Guinea SYSCOHADA accounting and payroll).

## Verdict: weak (low confidence)

No evidence of an active, visible Odoo partner or implementation in Equatorial Guinea. Only 2 of 6 planned searches returned results, because the search tool hit its usage limit. The French-language query and all later queries were never run.

## Evidence
- Odoo's partner site has a country page for Equatorial Guinea (https://www.odoo.com/es_ES/partners/country/83). The search snippet did not show any partner names, so whether it lists any partner is unverified.
- Odoo lists Equatorial Guinea on its "Francophone Africa (EUR low)" pricelist, billed in EUR (https://oec.sh/odoo-pricing/equatorial-guinea, a third-party page).
- A Spanish search for Odoo implementations in Malabo found no local implementation case, partner or review. The nearest results were generic articles on Odoo in Guinea and Guinea-Bissau (dasolo.ai), which are not about Equatorial Guinea.
- Complaints: none found. This is a gap in the search, not evidence that there are none. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum results turned up for this market.
- Momentum: Odoo the product is actively developed (the pricelist page is dated 1 Aug 2026). Local partner activity is unverified.

## Pricing
- Odoo's published pricelist for the country is "PL223 EUR" (source above). I did not see the per-user figures, so they are unverified.
- Partner implementation fees are unknown.

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified inference)
- No sign of a local partner offering SYSCOHADA configuration, local payroll rules or Spanish and French support. Odoo's out-of-the-box localization for Equatorial Guinea is unverified.
- Payroll is likely the weakest area. Odoo payroll is an Enterprise feature that normally needs a local rules setup, which I could not confirm.

## Opening
Odoo partners are not visibly serving Equatorial Guinea, so a small-business SYSCOHADA accounting and payroll product could face little local competition. Before relying on this, re-check the Odoo partner page and search for French-language and local-forum complaints.
