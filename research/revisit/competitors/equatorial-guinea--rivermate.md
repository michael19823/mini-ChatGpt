# Rivermate (Equatorial Guinea) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0282, Equatorial Guinea INSESO payroll filing.
Searches used: 4 of 6 (the first search failed on a usage limit and returned nothing).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Rivermate is a global Employer of Record (EOR). It employs people on the client's behalf and handles their payroll. It is not a self-serve INSESO filing tool for a local firm that runs its own payroll. The EOR price and model do not suit a small local employer.

## Evidence
- Rivermate describes itself as an EOR for 160+ countries. Its Equatorial Guinea pages are informational guides and a cost calculator. They state INSESO employer contributions of 21.5% of gross, and 1% for the Labor Protection Fund. Sources: https://rivermate.com/en/guides/equatorial-guinea, https://rivermate.com/es/guias/guinea-ecuatorial/calculadora-costo-empleado
- Rivermate absorbed Eos, Serviap and Hightekers EOR (banner on its site), so it is actively consolidating. Source: the same rivermate.com pages.
- No filing software for INSESO was found. There is no sign of an INSESO filing format or workflow.
- Complaints (second-hand summaries from search snippets, unverified against the original reviews):
  - Trustpilot is reported at 3.2/5 in one snippet and 3.9/5 (24 reviews) in another, so the figures conflict.
  - Themes: health insurance weaker than prior providers, few HRIS integrations, no mobile app, basic reporting, and slow replies to negative reviews. Sources: https://www.gloroots.com/blog/rivermate-reviews, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/rivermate.com?page=2
  - None of these complaints is specific to Equatorial Guinea or INSESO filing.
- Reported 4.9/5 on Capterra and G2 (71 reviews). Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/229765/Rivermate/reviews/Capterra___6676267/

## Pricing
- Capterra lists a starting price of EUR 299 per employee per month (EOR). Not verified on Rivermate's own site.
- This is far above what a small local firm would pay for filing-only software.

## Fit gaps
- It is an EOR, not payroll or filing software. The client cannot run its own payroll or submit INSESO filings with it.
- It targets foreign firms hiring in the country. It does not serve local SMEs that already employ staff.
- No evidence of INSESO filing formats, offline or mobile use, or multi-entity filing tools.

## Opening
A cheap filing and compliance tool for locally employed staff is left open. Rivermate only offers an expensive per-head EOR service and free information guides, and it does not file INSESO returns for self-run payrolls.
