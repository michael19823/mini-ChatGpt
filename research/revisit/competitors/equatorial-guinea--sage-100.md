# Sage 100 (Equatorial Guinea) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0284 Equatorial Guinea SYSCOHADA accounting and payroll.
Searches used: 3 of 6 (English, Spanish, French). No URL was known for the competitor.

## Verdict: beatable

Sage 100 is a real, established, SYSCOHADA-capable product in the OHADA region, but it is a
mid-market, partner-deployed ERP. Nothing found shows it serving micro/small Equatorial Guinea
buyers, and reviews show cost and usability complaints.

## Evidence
- Sage Comptabilite is described as one of the most widely used accounting packages in the OHADA
  space, and a training book exists on running SYSCOHADA accounting with it
  (https://www.ohada.com/actualite/3793/parution-de-louvrage-intitule-mettre-en-place-et-tenir-sa-comptabilite-syscohada-avec-sage-comptabilite.html).
- Certified partners deploy Sage 100 Cloud (accounting, commercial management, payroll/HR, fixed
  assets) in OHADA countries (https://www.erpresearch.com/sage-partners/hestia). Equatorial Guinea
  specifically: unverified; no local partner or customer found.
- A freelance request exists to adapt Sage 100 / X3 / Odoo to SYSCOHADA
  (https://www.codeur.com/projects/459083-application-sage-adapte-syscohada), suggesting the
  SYSCOHADA fit can need custom work. Single data point, weak evidence.
- Capterra (the product listing is the US/French Sage 100 line, not an OHADA edition; relevance to
  Equatorial Guinea is indirect): 4.1/5 overall; value for money 3.8/5, support 3.8/5, ease of use
  3.9/5 (https://www.capterra.fr/software/156947/sage-100cloud). The Capterra summary lists
  complaints: frequent technical glitches/slowdowns, high and rising costs with extra module and
  support fees, cumbersome transaction handling, dated interface
  (https://www.capterra.com/p/156947/Sage-100/reviews/). These are AI-summarised percentages from
  search output; I did not read individual reviews.
- No Equatorial Guinea-specific complaints, outages or failed-filing news found.

## Pricing
- Capterra lists a start price of about US$7,500 (custom quote, France-focused listing). Unverified
  for Equatorial Guinea; no local price found.
- Targeted at companies with roughly 30 to 500 employees (Capterra.fr/Appvizer summary), so likely
  overpriced for micro and small firms.

## Fit gaps
- Mid-market focus; partner-led implementation and licensing cost for small buyers.
- Spanish-language support for Equatorial Guinea (Spanish is an official language; also French and
  Portuguese) not confirmed; the OHADA material found is French. Unverified.
- Equatorial Guinea is in CEMAC/OHADA only via SYSCOHADA adoption; local tax and payroll (INSESO,
  IRPF) coverage by Sage unverified.
- Desktop/server heritage with a dated interface per reviews.

## Momentum
Actively sold and developed (Sage 100 Cloud, 2026 listings). Not stagnant.

## Opening
A cheap, Spanish/French, cloud or mobile SYSCOHADA accounting plus payroll tool with Equatorial
Guinea tax and social-security rules for small firms that cannot afford a partner-deployed Sage
100, though it would still compete with Sage Comptabilite and local accountants on trust.
