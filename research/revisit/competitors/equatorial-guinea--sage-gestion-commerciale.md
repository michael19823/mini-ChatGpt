# Sage Gestion commerciale (Sage 100c / 100cloud) - Equatorial Guinea

Idea killed: C0281, Equatorial Guinea VAT-on-every-invoice compliance.

## Verdict: beatable

Sage Gestion commerciale is a generic French-market SME invoicing/ERP module. I found no evidence it is localised for Equatorial Guinea's VAT rules. Evidence is thin, though: 5 of 6 searches were used (2 were cut off by a usage limit).

## Evidence
- Searching "Sage Gestion commerciale Guinée Équatoriale" returned no Equatorial Guinea-specific product page or local Sage partner. Only generic Sage partner listings (erpresearch.com) and country trade profiles came back. Local availability or support: unverified.
- Capterra FR and Appvizer list Sage 100cloud Gestion Commerciale as a modular ERP for French SMEs. One result summary quoted 4.1/5 from 407 users (capterra.fr/software/156947/sage-100cloud). I did not read individual reviews.
- Equatorial Guinea context: Orden Ministerial 04/2025 (5 Sept 2025) requires VAT to be shown on all invoices. There is no e-invoicing mandate and no prescribed format (e-invoice.app/country/GQ, taxatlas.io). So the need is basic VAT-compliant invoicing, which any generic tool could meet if it were set up locally.
- Complaints: none found. The searches did not surface Google Play, Trustpilot, Reddit or local forum reviews. Not searched in depth; absence of complaints is not proof of satisfaction.

## Pricing
Price on request only (Capterra/Appvizer summaries). No published price or minimum found. Sage 100 is typically sold through partners and is aimed at SMEs, so a micro-merchant in Equatorial Guinea probably cannot cheaply buy it. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- No visible localisation or support for Equatorial Guinea (no CEMAC/GQ VAT template, Spanish-language use, or local partner found). Unverified.
- Desktop/ERP-oriented, partner-sold and quote-based, which suggests a poor fit for micro-businesses.
- Offline or mobile use for small sellers: unverified.

## Opening
Cheap, Spanish/French, mobile-first VAT-on-invoice tooling for small Equatorial Guinea businesses that Sage's partner-sold ERP does not reach.
