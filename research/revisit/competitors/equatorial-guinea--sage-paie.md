# Sage Paie (Equatorial Guinea)

Ideas it killed: C0282 (INSESO payroll filing), C0284 (SYSCOHADA accounting and payroll).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Four searches, in French, English and Spanish, found no evidence that Sage Paie is sold, localized or supported in Equatorial Guinea. Sage's generic OHADA and French payroll products exist, but nothing ties them to INSESO rules or local users.

## Evidence
- A search for Sage Paie with INSESO returned only generic payroll guides (Rivermate, Playroll, G-P, PwC). None mention Sage. Sources: https://rivermate.com/fr/guides/guinee-equatoriale/salaire , https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/equatorial-guinea/individual/other-taxes
- A Spanish search for INSESO nómina software found only EOR and payroll guides. These list INSESO contributions (employer 21.5%, employee 4.5%, monthly filing) but no local software vendor. Source: https://www.globalization-partners.com/es/globalpedia/equatorial-guinea/payroll
- A Kolonell comparison says Sage 100 Cloud has an official SYSCOHADA module with multi-company and multi-currency support and Sage Paie integration. It calls the product expensive and complex, suited to SMEs with more than 50 staff and multiple OHADA subsidiaries. The snippet refers to the OHADA region generally, not Equatorial Guinea. Source: https://kolonell.com/en/blog/syscohada-accounting-software-senegal-sme-comparison-2026
- Nothing found says Sage has a presence, partner or INSESO filing format in Equatorial Guinea. Equatorial Guinea is a CEMAC member, but whether it uses SYSCOHADA is unverified here.
- Complaints: none found specific to Equatorial Guinea. The only generic French-market criticisms were unclear pricing and no mobile app, for Sage Business Cloud Paie (https://culture-rh.com/solution-rh/sageone-avis-test-prix-logiciel-paie-rh/ , via search summary). These are not about this market.
- Momentum: Sage 100 Cloud is actively sold in France (rated 4.1/5 by 404 users per a search summary, https://www.capterra.fr/software/156947/sage-100cloud). Nothing on Equatorial Guinea momentum.

## Pricing
- Equatorial Guinea: no published price (unverified).
- France only, for reference: Sage Business Cloud Paie is about 15 EUR per employee plus 5 EUR a month for 1-3 employees, 10 EUR plus 25 EUR for 4-10, and 8 EUR plus 45 EUR for 11-100 (per a search summary, not checked on Sage's site). This is not relevant to local pricing.

## Fit gaps (inferred, partly unverified)
- No evidence of an INSESO filing format, Spanish-language support, or local support.
- Sage 100 is described as aimed at larger SMEs (more than 50 staff), which leaves micro and small firms unserved.
- No evidence of mobile or offline use.

## Opening
Small employers in Equatorial Guinea appear to have no local INSESO-aware payroll tool, so the dropped ideas look viable. Caveat: the web evidence is thin, and Sage may be sold through local partners that online search doesn't show.
