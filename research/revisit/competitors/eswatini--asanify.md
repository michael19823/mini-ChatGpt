# Asanify (Eswatini) — competitor check for C0293 (Eswatini payroll PAYE + ENPF)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Asanify has an Eswatini content page, but it is aimed at foreign employers using its Employer of Record (EOR) service. No evidence was found of a local-payroll product for Eswatini SMEs.

## Evidence
- Asanify's Eswatini page is an EOR/employer-guide page ("Salary Structure in Swaziland (Eswatini): A Complete Employer Guide"). It sits under the `global-employer-of-record` path and describes SRA PAYE and ENPF obligations. Source: https://asanify.com/global-employer-of-record/swaziland/salary-structure/
- Asanify's core payroll product is India-focused. Its pricing pages are split into India and International. Sources: https://asanify.com/pricing/ and https://asanify.com/pricing-international/
- A search for Eswatini payroll software returned mostly EOR and managed-payroll vendors (Ontop, TopSource, Playroll, PEBL, Rivermate) and free calculators (AfroTools). Asanify appeared only through its EOR content page. No local Eswatini SaaS vendor was identified. Sage (via its ENPF limit announcements) and Workpay (via its 2026 statutory updates) also surfaced, which suggests some regional software coverage. Unverified: whether either serves Eswatini SMEs well.
- Complaints: Capterra shows 4.7/5 from 69 reviews and G2 shows 4.9/5 from 370 reviews (search snippets). The one negative snippet was a general bug complaint ("leave management, attendance tracking, payroll, everything has a bug"), with some users reporting slow support. No Eswatini-specific complaints, app-store reviews, forum or Facebook posts, or Trustpilot reviews were found. Sources: https://www.capterra.com/reviews/206742/Asanify and https://www.g2.com/products/asanify/reviews
- No siSwati-language results were found. A search that included siSwati terms returned only English pages.

## Pricing
- Published: Essential $7.99 per employee per month, VIP $17.99 per employee per month (unverified whether these apply to Eswatini). Source: https://asanify.com/pricing-international/
- EOR: about $199 per employee per month (from a search snippet; unverified). Country-specific African pricing is not published, so buyers must contact sales.
- The EOR price is far above what a small Eswatini employer would pay for payroll alone.

## Fit gaps
- EOR-oriented, so it targets foreign firms hiring in Eswatini, not local businesses running their own payroll.
- No evidence of SRA PAYE or ENPF filing formats, SZL bank payment files or a local-language UI.
- No local presence or support was found. Unverified.

## Opening
A cheap local payroll tool that produces SRA PAYE and ENPF returns in SZL is not served by Asanify. The gap is the same one that Asanify's EOR page implies but does not fill.
