# EOR vendors (Eswatini) - competitor check for C0296 (Eswatini payroll and statutory filings)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

EOR vendors (TopSource, Rivermate, Africa HR, Playroll, G-P, and others) sell a legal-employer service to foreign companies hiring in Eswatini. They do not sell payroll software to local SMEs that already employ people. They are a different product for a different buyer.

## Evidence
- Search results list EOR and payroll providers covering Eswatini: TopSource, Rivermate, Africa HR, Playroll, Globalization Partners (G-P), Hire Borderless, and Ontop. Source: https://topsourceworldwide.com/employer-of-record/eswatini/ ; https://rivermate.com/employer-of-record/eswatini ; https://africa-hr.com/swaziland-employer-of-record/ ; https://www.playroll.com/compliance-hub/paying-employees-in-eswatini ; https://www.globalization-partners.com/globalpedia/eswatini/payroll/
- These pages pitch hiring without a local entity (for example Playroll's "local entity required to hire in eswatini" page), so the buyer is a foreign employer. A local firm already has an entity and does not need an EOR.
- Africa HR and TopSource also advertise payroll outsourcing for Eswatini (https://africa-hr.com/swaziland-payroll-outsourcing/ ; https://topsourceworldwide.com/global-payroll/eswatini/). I could not check what a local SME would pay or whether they serve local SMEs. Unverified.
- Complaints: I found none specific to Eswatini. General EOR feedback (not Eswatini-specific) from one aggregator, summarised in search results: Playroll reviewers report salary or wallet-transfer delays, inconsistent support and unexpected fees; Rivermate has basic reporting. Source: https://www.gloroots.com/blog/rivermate-reviews . I did not read the underlying review pages. Treat as weak, indirect evidence.
- Momentum: Rivermate states that Eos, Serviap and Hightekers EOR are now Rivermate (a merger or rebrand; search snippet, date unverified). The market looks active but consolidating.

## Pricing
- Search snippets say EOR can start from $399 per employee per month (Playroll). Deel is quoted at $599 and Oyster at $699 per employee per month in general (not Eswatini-specific) via aggregator sites such as https://costbench.com/compare/deel-vs-oyster-hr/ . Aggregator figures are unverified.
- No Eswatini-specific price was found for Deel, Remote or Oyster. Unverified.
- Even the low figure of $399 per employee per month is far above what a small local employer would pay for payroll software. Employer taxes, the provident-fund contribution and other costs are extra. This is overpriced for the SME segment, but the vendors are not targeting that segment anyway.

## Fit gaps
- Built for foreign employers hiring a few staff in Eswatini, not local SMEs running their own payroll.
- The employer relationship transfers to the vendor. The local firm does not keep control of its own payroll and filings.
- Per-employee monthly pricing does not suit a small firm with many low-paid staff.
- No evidence found of a self-serve tool for local PAYE or provident fund returns. Unverified.

## Opening
A cheap self-serve local payroll and statutory-filing tool for Eswatini SMEs is not blocked by EOR vendors. The real competition for C0296 is PaySpace, Sage and local payroll bureaus, which are checked in separate files.
