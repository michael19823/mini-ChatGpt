# PaySpace (Eswatini) - commercial
Ideas it killed: C0293 (Eswatini payroll PAYE + ENPF), C0296 (Eswatini payroll and statutory filings)

## Verdict: strong
Actively maintained, covers Eswatini statutory rules in detail, and is now part of Deel (a "Deel Local Payroll" brand). Searches found no complaints. 4 of 6 searches used; the 2 not used were not needed to reach the verdict.

## Evidence
- PaySpace publishes current Eswatini compliance content: ENPF rate increase notices, PAYE calculation changes (August 2025), a new monthly PAYE schedule, and an Eswatini Payroll Tax Guide 2026-2027 (https://support.payspace.com/portal/en/kb/articles/eswatini-payroll-tax-guide-2026-2027, https://payspace.com/blog/eswatini-paye-calculation-changes-august-2025).
- Branding now reads "Deel Local Payroll" (powered by PaySpace) on payspace.com and G2, which suggests it has been acquired by or merged into Deel (inferred from the branding; the acquisition is not confirmed by any source I read).
- Reviews: Capterra shows 5.0 from 1 reviewer (minor note: report formatting could look better); G2 shows 4.9 from 5 reviews, all positive (https://www.capterra.com/p/161486/PaySpace/, https://www.g2.com/products/deel-local-payroll-powered-by-payspace/reviews). These are tiny samples. No Eswatini-specific reviews seen.
- Complaints: none found in the search results (no Play Store, App Store, Reddit, Facebook or local-language results surfaced). The Eswatini search was in English only; siSwati has no meaningful software-review corpus, so I did not run a separate local-language search. Absence of complaints is not proof of satisfaction.
- Other Eswatini options surfaced: Ontop, TopSource (managed payroll), Playroll and Pebl (EOR-type), plus free calculators at AfroTools. These are mostly global/EOR players, not local SME tools.

## Pricing
- No published Eswatini price. Capterra lists a "from" figure of GBP 2.99 (unit not stated, unverified). Vendor requires a quote for most plans.
- A 2016 article cited R11.18 to R24.60 per payslip in South Africa (old, unverified for today).
- PaySpace LITE exists as a small-business/startup tier (from search summary; details unverified).

## Fit gaps (all unverified, inferred from positioning)
- Positioned as an enterprise/multi-country HR+payroll suite; likely heavy and quote-based for a very small Eswatini employer.
- Not confirmed whether it files directly to ERS TaxEase or only produces schedules/reports for upload.
- No evidence of mobile-first, offline or siSwati-language use.
- No evidence of an accountant/bureau-oriented multi-client tool for Eswatini.

## Opening
Little room on compliance accuracy; the only plausible wedge is a cheap, transparent-priced, mobile-first tool for micro employers or bureaus with direct ERS TaxEase submission, and that wedge is unproven.
