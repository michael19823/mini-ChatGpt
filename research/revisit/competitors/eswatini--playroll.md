# Playroll (Eswatini) - competitor check

Killed idea: C0293 Eswatini payroll PAYE + ENPF. Searches used: 5 of 6 (2 failed on a usage limit and were rerun). Local-language search (siSwati) returned only English results.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Playroll is a global Employer of Record (EOR) for foreign companies hiring remote staff in Eswatini. It is not local payroll software for Eswatini employers.

## Evidence
- Playroll's Eswatini pages cover hiring without a local entity, paying remote employees and severance rules. The EOR becomes the legal employer and handles PAYE, ENPF and filings. Sources: https://www.playroll.com/payroll/eswatini , https://playroll.com/global-hiring-guides/eswatini , https://playroll.com/compliance-hub/paying-employees-in-eswatini
- The search snippet also mentions a "Global Payroll" service for companies with a local presence. I did not read the page, so the detail is unverified.
- Other global players cover Eswatini the same way: TopSource (https://topsourceworldwide.com/global-payroll/eswatini/), Ontop (https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/eswatini), Deel/PaySpace (https://payspace.com/blog/eswatini-enpf-rate-increase), Workpay (https://www.myworkpay.com/blogs/eswatini-2026-statutory-updates). These are also mostly foreign or EOR oriented. I did not verify whether any is a self-serve local payroll tool.
- Complaints, general (not Eswatini-specific): a review roundup reports G2 4.7 from 95 reviews and Trustpilot 3.0 from 12 reviews. Reported themes are slow support in some regions, payroll or wallet transfer delays, limited HRIS integrations, basic reporting, refundable deposits and withdrawal fees. Sources: https://www.gloroots.com/blog/playroll-reviews , https://eorhq.com/reviews/playroll/ . I did not open the underlying reviews, so these are secondhand.
- No Eswatini-specific complaints found.
- Momentum: Playroll's Eswatini pages mention 2025 and 2026 rules (minimum wage SZL 2,500 from 1 Jan 2025), so the content is maintained. Beyond that, momentum is unverified.

## Pricing
- EOR fee from $399 per employee per month (from search snippet and review roundups; the "per month" part is unverified). This is far above what a small Eswatini employer paying local wages could use.
- No published local-payroll-only price found.

## Fit gaps
- It serves foreign companies hiring in Eswatini, not Eswatini-based SMEs running their own staff.
- It does not offer self-serve payroll software for local firms (unverified beyond its pages).
- Per-employee EOR pricing is a poor fit for local small businesses.
- No evidence of ERS or ENPF filing-format tooling that a local employer controls.

## Opening
A cheap, local, self-serve PAYE + ENPF payroll tool for Eswatini SMEs is not contested by Playroll. Check local accountants and payroll bureaus as the real competition (not researched here).
