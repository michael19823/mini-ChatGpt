# Rivermate (Eswatini) - competitor check for C0293 (Eswatini payroll PAYE + ENPF)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Rivermate is a global Employer of Record (EOR) and contractor-payroll service for foreign companies hiring in Eswatini. It is not payroll software that a local small business can run. It publishes Eswatini guides, but no local payroll product.

## Evidence
- Rivermate has an Eswatini country guide covering taxes and an employment-cost calculator. These are content pages that explain PAYE, ENPF, and the 7th-of-month remittance deadline. Source: https://rivermate.com/guides/eswatini/employment-cost-calculator and https://www.rivermate.com/guides/eswatini/taxes
- Rivermate says that Eos, Serviap and Hightekers EOR are now Rivermate (a rebrand or merger, per its site banner). Source: https://rivermate.com/en/pricing
- The Rivermate Eswatini guide states an ENPF employer rate of 5% with an E10,000 ceiling. Another source (Workpay) cites a ceiling of E4,300 for 2026. The two differ and I did not resolve which is right, so the Rivermate figure is unverified. Source: https://www.myworkpay.com/blogs/eswatini-2026-statutory-updates
- Other players in the same search results are also EOR or managed-payroll providers: Topsource Worldwide, Playroll, Asanify. Source: https://topsourceworldwide.com/global-payroll/eswatini/ and https://www.playroll.com/compliance-hub/paying-employees-in-eswatini
- Complaints: none found. One search (Trustpilot/G2 reviews) failed with a usage-limit error, so review sites were not checked. A Capterra listing for Rivermate exists (https://www.capterra.com/p/229765/Rivermate/) but I did not read its reviews. Complaints are unverified, not confirmed absent.
- Momentum: it appears active and expanding (rebrand, 180+ countries claimed, multi-language site). Not stagnant.
- Searches in SiSwati found nothing. Only 4 of 6 allowed searches were used, and one of them errored.

## Pricing
- EOR: $319-$639 per employee per month, per an aggregator summary, or from EUR 299 per employee per month. Contractors: EUR 199 per month. Sources: https://rivermate.com/en/pricing and https://www.capterra.com/p/229765/Rivermate/. I did not confirm the exact figures on the official page.
- This is far above what a local small business with a few staff would pay for payroll software.

## Fit gaps
- It serves foreign companies hiring in Eswatini through an EOR, not Eswatini-based employers who run their own payroll.
- It is not self-serve payroll software: it offers no payslip tool, no ERS or ENPF filing outputs, and no SiSwati or offline or mobile use for local SMEs (all unverified, since no product was found).
- Per-employee EOR fees make it uneconomic for local SMEs.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve local payroll tool (PAYE, ENPF, payslips, ERS and ENPF return outputs) for Eswatini SMEs faces no direct Rivermate competition. Other local payroll vendors were not checked and could still matter.
