# Eswatini: Sage (payroll and statutory filings)

Idea killed: C0296 (Eswatini payroll and statutory filings).

## Verdict: beatable (low confidence)

Sage supports Eswatini payroll as a regional add-on to its South African products. The research was cut short: only 2 of 6 searches ran, because the third hit a usage limit. Complaint, pricing and momentum evidence is missing.

## Evidence
- Sage's South African community hub has Eswatini/Swaziland tags and announcements for Sage VIP Payroll & HR and Sage Business Cloud Payroll Professional. Recent updates cover the pension deduction cap rising from 10% to 15% (effective 1 July 2024), the repeal of Graded Tax (2023), a new Monthly PAYE Reconciliation report and an amended Monthly PAYE Declaration Form. This shows Sage actively maintains Eswatini tax rules. Sources:
  - https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-payroll-professional/f/announcements/tags/Eswatini
  - https://www.sagecity.com/za/sage-vip-payroll-hr/f/announcements/tags/Swaziland
  - https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-payroll-professional/sage-payroll-professional-year-end-centre/w/sage-payroll-professional-year-end-articles/2898/africa-taxation-summaries-and-calculators/revision/27
- Sage publishes Eswatini taxation summaries and tax calculators in its Africa year-end resources (same hub, URL above).
- Other payroll vendors and employer-of-record providers also list Eswatini (PaySpace, Playroll, Rivermate, Topsource, Africa-HR outsourcing). These were seen in search results only; their features were not checked.
- Complaints: none found. No complaint-specific search ran. Complaints are unverified, not absent.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred from the search results, unverified)
- Eswatini seems to be a side-market for Sage's South Africa-centred desktop and cloud payroll. Local language and support, e-filing to the Eswatini Revenue Service (ERS) and ENPF submission formats were not checked.
- The sources show filings as reports and forms, for example the Monthly PAYE Declaration Form. This suggests the filing is manual rather than a direct integration. Unverified.
- Small-employer pricing and fit were not checked.
- Momentum: actively updated through 2024-2025 per the Sage community hub.

## Opening
A lightweight, locally priced payroll tool with direct ERS and ENPF filing could be pitched at small Eswatini employers. This needs confirming with the missing pricing and complaint checks before relying on it.
