# Demoz (Ethiopia)

Killed idea: C0297, Ethiopia payroll/PAYE + POESSA pension. Type: commercial, no URL known.

## Verdict: weak

Demoz appears only as a directory listing with a feature blurb. I found no website, reviews, pricing or sign of ongoing development. Searches used: 4 of 6. The 5th (Amharic) failed on a usage-limit error and the 6th was not run, so there is no local-language coverage.

## Evidence
- A SaaS directory listing (https://saasbrowser.com/ar/saas/1531667/demoz) describes Demoz as a payroll and HR system. It lists salary processing, tax compliance, leave, attendance and pension contributions, Ethiopian calendar support, multi-format reports and data export. It says it targets small to large businesses. The listing is the only source I found, and it reads like auto-generated directory copy. Its feature claims are unverified.
- No Google Play or App Store page, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit result for Demoz appeared in two searches. No outage or filing-failure news was found either.
- Complaints: none found. This reflects the absence of any visible user base or review footprint, not evidence that users are satisfied.
- Other Ethiopian payroll options that did show up (not the assigned competitor, listed for context): Odoo Ethiopian payroll modules (apps.odoo.com l10n_et_payroll), Lenvica, HST-ET, and global EOR and payroll vendors such as Ontop, Mercans and Asanify. These are more visible than Demoz.
