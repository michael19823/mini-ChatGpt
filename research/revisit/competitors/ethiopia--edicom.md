# EDICOM (Ethiopia)

Dropped idea: C0301, Ethiopia ERP e-invoicing migrations. Competitor type: commercial, no URL known (edicomgroup.com found).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

EDICOM is a Spanish multinational. In Ethiopia it has only published an explainer blog on the new e-invoicing rules. The searches found no Ethiopian product, certification, office or customer. (5 searches used: English x4, Amharic x1, plus one on domestic-vendor policy. The Amharic search returned only English or generic results.)

## Evidence
- EDICOM publishes a blog explaining Ethiopia's Directive No. 1142/2026 and the phased rollout (large VAT taxpayers targeted by end of December 2026). Source: https://edicomgroup.com/blog/mandatory-electronic-invoicing-ethiopia. This is content marketing. The searches did not show that EDICOM is an approved Ethiopian provider.
- The directive allows approved service providers, approved software, approved cloud systems or self-developed systems, all authorised by the tax authority. No list of approved vendors was found (unverified whether any exist yet). Source: search summaries of the EDICOM blog and Thomson Reuters regulatory updates (https://europe.thomsonreuters.com/compliance/regulatory-updates/ethiopia).
- EDICOM has a Dynamics 365 e-invoicing integration for other countries (https://edicomgroup.com/edicom-microsoft). No Ethiopia-specific connector was found.
- Policy risk for foreign vendors: Shega reported in July 2024 that the Ministry of Innovation and Technology made government software projects exclusive to domestic companies (https://shega.co/news/government-software-projects-now-exclusive-to-domestic-companies). Only the headline was visible. Whether this covers e-invoicing providers is unverified.
- Complaints (global, not Ethiopia): G2 (https://www.g2.com/products/edicom-edi/reviews) shows few reviews. Reported themes are a very technical product, hard to resolve without help from EDICOM support, and complex initial setup (https://www.g2.com/products/edicom-b2b-cloud-platform/reviews). rfp.wiki (https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/edicom) cites pricing opacity and implementation complexity for smaller IT teams. No Ethiopia-specific complaints found. Capterra and Trustpilot returned nothing relevant.

## Pricing
Not published. Custom quotes only (per rfp.wiki). No Ethiopian price found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Enterprise and multinational focus, with complex implementation.
- No evidence of local presence, Amharic support, or a tax-authority-approved Ethiopian product.
- Offline invoicing (26 sectors must support it) and local ERP migration work are not addressed in anything found.
- Possible domestic-only procurement preference.

## Momentum
Active globally. In Ethiopia it is only at the awareness/content stage.

## Opening
EDICOM does not close the gap. A local, authority-approved, ERP-migration-focused offering for mid-size Ethiopian firms stays open. The real competitors to check are local vendors and the ministry's own system.
