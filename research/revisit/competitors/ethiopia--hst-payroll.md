# Ethiopia: HST payroll (hst-et.com)

Killed idea: C0297 (Ethiopia payroll/PAYE + POESSA pension).

## Verdict: beatable

HST is a real, local, Amharic-capable payroll product, but it looks like one service line of an IT services firm, with no public pricing and no public reviews found.

## Evidence
- HST runs an online "Payroll Management System" page at https://hst-et.com/services/technology/payroll-management-system. Search snippets describe: custom formulas, deductions, allowances and bonuses; compliance with Ethiopian labor and tax law; reports exportable to Excel/PDF/CSV; cloud access; Amharic support; employee self-service portal for payslips.
- Its page sits under "services/technology", so it is probably bundled with other IT services (inference, unverified).
- HST's site reportedly lists Telebirr, CBE, CBE Birr, Awash and COOP as payment options (from search summary, unverified in detail).
- Complaints: none found. Searches in English and Amharic, including for Reddit/Facebook/forum complaints, returned no reviews of HST at all. This is absence of evidence, not proof of satisfaction.
- Market context (not about HST): general sources say some Ethiopian organisations still run payroll in decentralised Excel, and that many providers wrongly compute pension on gross instead of basic salary (https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/ethiopia, https://dsr.mercans.com/?p=831). Rival options include an Odoo Ethiopia payroll module (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_et_payroll), global EOR/payroll vendors (Mercans, Ontop, Multiplier), and local HR software lists (https://www.peoplehum.com/blog/top-10-hr-software-in-ethiopia). Several Ethiopian orgs issue payroll-system RFPs, which shows demand.
- Momentum: could not verify (no dated releases or news found). Site appears live.

## Pricing
Not published; no prices found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified)
- No visible self-serve pricing or trial, so small buyers must contact sales.
- No evidence of POESSA or Ministry of Revenue filing-format exports, mobile app, or offline use; the page lists only generic reports.
- Whether it handles the 2025-26 PAYE reform and pension-on-basic-salary rule is not stated.
- No public reviews or case studies found.

## Opening
A cheap, transparent-price, self-serve payroll tool with correct PAYE/POESSA calculations and ready filing exports for small firms, mobile and Amharic-first, faces a vendor with no visible pricing or review presence.
