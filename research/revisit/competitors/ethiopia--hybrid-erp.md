# Hybrid ERP (Ethiopia, commercial)

Idea it killed: C0301 Ethiopia ERP e-invoicing migrations.
Searches used: 4 of 6 (English and Amharic).

## Verdict
weak

Hybrid ERP PLC is a small Addis Ababa ERP consultancy, not a productised e-invoicing vendor. I found no e-invoicing or EIMS offering from it.

## Evidence
- The Manifest lists "HYBRID ERP PLC" as a small ERP consulting and systems-integration firm in Addis Ababa, founded 2013, rated 5.0 from 4 reviews (https://themanifest.com/company/hybrid-erp-plc). I did not open the page, so the details come from the search summary only.
- The same summary mentions an Odoo deployment for a personal-development company and an HR and inventory web platform for a petrochemical importer. It also mentions "Hybrid Systems PLC", which may be the same firm. Unverified.
- No website was found. An Amharic search returned only generic "hybrid ERP" articles.
- I found no mention of Hybrid ERP with EIMS, the Ministry of Revenue e-invoicing system, or Directive 1142/2026. Marakisoft, by contrast, advertises "Marakisoft e-Invoice" (https://linkedin.com/company/marakisoft).
- Complaints: none found (no app-store, Reddit, forum or news results about it). Absence of results is not proof of good service.
- Momentum: unknown. No recent activity found.

## Pricing
The Manifest summary says projects start around $3,000 (unverified, secondhand). No published price list was found.

## Fit gaps
- No visible e-invoicing or EIMS product or migration service.
- Appears to be a custom, project-based Odoo shop, with no sign of a repeatable migration offer.
- Very small web footprint, no website found, and only 4 reviews.

## Opening
A repeatable, fixed-price EIMS e-invoicing connector or migration package for Odoo and other ERPs would face little visible competition from this firm. The other listed names, such as Marakisoft, need their own checks.
