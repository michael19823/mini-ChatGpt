# Marakisoft (Ethiopia)

Killed idea: C0301 Ethiopia ERP e-invoicing migrations.

## Verdict: strong

It is a real, local, established ERP/POS vendor that already markets e-invoice workflows. It is the incumbent for exactly this niche. No complaints were found, so there is no evidence of a weakness to exploit.

## Evidence
- Marakisoft Technologies is an Addis Ababa ERP/POS vendor, founded 2007, claiming 17+ years and 5,000+ clients (self-reported, unverified). Sources: https://linkedin.com/company/marakisoft , https://www.briter.co/companies/marakisoft-technologies
- It says it serves SMEs and enterprises across retail, manufacturing, construction and distribution, with implementation, training and after-sales support, and "e-Invoice and payment-enabled workflows". Same sources.
- Ethiopia's Electronic Invoicing System Administration Directive No. 1142/2026 allows approved service providers, approved software, cloud systems or self-developed systems. Source: https://lookuptax.com/tax-changes/ethiopia/electronic-invoicing-directive-1142-2026 . Whether Marakisoft is formally approved by the tax authority is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. Searches (English and Amharic) returned no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews, and no outage or failed-filing news. The Amharic search returned nothing relevant.
- Momentum: unverified. No recent news, acquisition or shutdown was found. The LinkedIn and directory listings suggest it is active.

## Pricing
Not found. No published prices or minimums (unverified). Its stated audience includes SMEs.

## Fit gaps
None evidenced. Offline or mobile support, multi-entity support, and whether its e-invoice module covers the new directive are all unverified.

## Opening
Little evidence of an opening. At most, a neutral migration or compliance tool for firms on other ERPs, which is not Marakisoft's own base. Verify its tax-authority approval status and pricing directly before relying on this.
