# Ethiopia: modern_ethiopian_payroll (generic, no URL)

Dropped idea: C0297, Ethiopia payroll/PAYE + POESSA pension. Searches used: 3 of 6.

## Verdict: beatable

No single incumbent. The "competitor" is a loose set of foreign cloud HR suites, an Odoo module, EOR providers and local outsourcers. None was shown to be a dominant, well-loved SME payroll tool. Verdict is based on thin evidence.

## Evidence
- Search results show no user complaints (app stores, Reddit, forums, Trustpilot) for any Ethiopian payroll product. No complaints found. The Amharic search returned only irrelevant salary-site pages, so local-language coverage is effectively empty (unverified either way).
- Products surfaced: SeamlessHR and WebHR (cloud HR/payroll, marketed as Ethiopia-ready, per a PeopleHum and a SeamlessHR blog, both vendor/marketing content: https://www.peoplehum.com/blog/top-10-hr-software-in-ethiopia, https://seamlesshr.com/blog/best-hr-management-software-in-ethiopia-for-businesses/); Odoo module l10n_et_payroll (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_et_payroll); Mercans and Ontop (global payroll/EOR, https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/ethiopia); Deel/Multiplier/Asanify (EOR); local outsourcers HST (https://hst-et.com/services/technology/payroll-management-system) and YES (https://techbehemoths.com/company/yes-your-employment-soluitons).
- Statutory rules are in flux: a 2025-26 reform reset the PAYE schedule (ETB 2,000 tax-free floor, six bands up to 35%) per Mercans/Ontop snippets; pension 11% employer + 7% employee via POESSA. A rule change favours tools that update quickly.
- Momentum: foreign vendors are actively marketing to Ethiopia; no local product with visible active development found. Unverified.

## Pricing
- Deel: from USD 599/month per employee (EOR, search snippet; unverified on vendor page).
- Odoo Ethiopia payroll module: $59.05 one-off (snippet), plus Odoo licence/hosting and setup skill.
- SeamlessHR, WebHR, HST, YES: no published prices found.
- EOR and global-payroll tools are far too expensive for small Ethiopian firms.

## Fit gaps
- Foreign tools are priced in USD, aimed at multinationals/NGOs/EOR, not local SMEs paying in birr.
- Odoo needs technical setup; no Amharic/mobile-first focus indicated.
- Local outsourcers are services, not self-serve software.
- Unverified: whether any tool files directly to ERCA portal or POESSA formats.

## Opening
A cheap, birr-priced, Amharic/English self-serve payroll with current PAYE and POESSA rules and ERCA/POESSA-ready outputs for SMEs looks open, but validate demand and filing-format needs locally since no complaint evidence was found.
