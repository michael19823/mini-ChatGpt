# Ethiopia: Peachtree payroll (commercial, no URL known)

Killed idea: C0297, Ethiopia payroll/PAYE + POESSA pension.

## Verdict: weak

Peachtree (now Sage 50) is a generic accounting package with a payroll module. The searches found no evidence of an Ethiopia-specific payroll product, PAYE/POESSA localisation, or active Ethiopian marketing. It is an incidental tool Ethiopian accountants may use, not a dedicated payroll competitor.

## Evidence
- Searches (4 WebSearch calls, English plus Amharic) returned no page showing Peachtree/Sage 50 payroll configured for Ethiopian PAYE or POESSA. The search tool itself said there was no clear evidence it is marketed or customised for Ethiopia. Sources: [Odoo Ethiopian payroll](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/17.0/modern_ethiopian_payroll), [Sage 50 on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sage_50).
- Peachtree is the older name for Sage 50 (Wikipedia). Peachtree tutorial and course pages show up for the query, but none mention Ethiopia. Sage 50 payroll tax updates are sold separately from the software, per the Sage community results. Those updates are for US/Canada tax tables, so Ethiopian rules would have to be set up by hand (inference, unverified for Ethiopia).
- Other Ethiopia-specific payroll options surfaced: Odoo-based Ethiopian payroll modules, [HST-ET payroll management system](https://hst-et.com/services/technology/payroll-management-system), WebHR, and EOR providers (Multiplier, Asanify). These are the more relevant competitors for the idea.
- Ethiopia rules are in flux: one result says the 2025-26 reform reset PAYE to a 2,000 ETB tax-free floor and six bands up to 35%, with pension at employer 11% and employee 7%. A generic foreign package would not track this automatically. Source: [Asanify guide](https://asanify.com/global-employer-of-record/ethiopia/payroll/). The reform claim is unverified from a single secondary source.
- Complaints: none found about Peachtree payroll in Ethiopia (no app-store, forum or review results). That is not evidence of satisfaction, only that nothing surfaced. Local-language search returned nothing relevant.

## Pricing
Unverified. No Ethiopia price found. Sage 50 is a paid licence/subscription, and payroll tax updates are a separate purchase (Sage community results). No figures were verified.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of built-in Ethiopian PAYE brackets, POESSA pension filing or Amharic support.
- Desktop-style accounting package, not mobile-first. Unverified.
- Foreign vendor with no confirmed local support. Unverified.

## Opening
A small-business Ethiopian payroll tool with current PAYE and POESSA rules, Amharic and mobile use is not blocked by Peachtree. The real rivals are Odoo localisations and local vendors such as HST-ET.
