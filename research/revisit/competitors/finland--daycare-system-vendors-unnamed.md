# Finland: daycare system vendors (unnamed), killed idea C0314 (Varda reporting for private daycares)

## Verdict: weak (low confidence, thin evidence from 6 searches)

The "competitor" is an unnamed group of daycare information-system vendors. Searches found no named product with published pricing, no reviews and no complaints. The real obstacle is that Varda accepts both manual entry and automated transfer from a provider's own system, so a small daycare can already comply for free by hand. This is closer to "not a real competitor" than to "strong", but it is not proven either way.

## Evidence
- Varda is the national early childhood education data repository, run by Opetushallitus (Finnish National Agency for Education). Private providers (private daycares, private family daycare) must report into it. Source: https://www.oph.fi/fi/palvelut/varhaiskasvatuksen-tietovaranto-varda
- Providers can enter data manually in the Varda web UI or through automatic transfer from their own information system. Deadlines are the 15th and the last day of the month for manual entry, and within one day for automatic transfer. Source: https://www.oph.fi/fi/palvelut/varhaiskasvatuksen-tietovaranto-varda (also cited in municipal privacy notices, e.g. https://www.imatra.fi/sites/default/files/2026-01/tietosuojaseloste-varda.pdf)
- Private providers have had to record children since 1 Jan 2020 and guardians since 1 Sep 2020, per a municipal Varda notice. Source: https://siilinjarvi.fi/en/education-and-learning/early-childhood-education-and-care/early-childhood-education-and-care-information-resource-varda/
- Daisy is a daycare information system. Municipal privacy notices say it transfers data to Varda. These notices are municipal, so Daisy's fit for private daycares is unverified. Source: https://kaavi.fi/content/uploads/2024/07/tietosuojaseloste-varhaiskasvatuksen-tietojarjestelma-daisy.pdf
- Touhula (a private daycare chain) has published a Varda notice for guardians, which suggests chains handle Varda in-house. Source: https://touhula.fi/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/varda_bulletin-for-guardians.pdf
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, forum, G2 or Capterra reviews of any Finnish vendor surfaced in Finnish or English searches. Finding none is a valid result, but coverage was thin (6 searches, results dominated by municipal PDFs).

## Pricing
- No published prices found for any Finnish daycare system. Unverified.
- Generic non-Finnish tools list per-child prices (MyKidReports $0.99/child/month, Storypark $1.59, Tadpoles $2.25, per https://mykidreports.com/blog/guide-to-childcare-management-software-pricing). None were confirmed to support Varda, so they are not comparable.
- The free manual route through the Varda UI sets a price ceiling of zero for a very small daycare.

## Fit gaps (hypotheses, unverified)
- Municipal-oriented systems such as Daisy may be sold as large municipal contracts, leaving small private daycares with manual entry. Not confirmed.
- No evidence found about missing languages (Swedish) or multi-unit support.
- Momentum of any vendor: unknown.

## Opening
A thin, cheap Varda-reporting layer for small private daycares could work only if manual entry is a real pain. That pain was not evidenced here, and the free manual option plus vendor and chain in-house solutions weaken the case. A follow-up should check what Varda's API documentation requires and survey a few private daycares directly.
