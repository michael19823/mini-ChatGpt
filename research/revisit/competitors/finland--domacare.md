# Domacare (Finland)

Killed idea: C0309, Finland Kanta client-record system for social care.

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- DomaCare is a cloud ERP and client-record tool for private health and social care providers. Its vendor, Invian Oy, was founded in 2007. A Visma press release says it serves over 100 private customers. Sources: https://domacare.fi/ratkaisut, https://publish.ne.cision.com/v2.2/Release/ViewReleaseHtml/D61B1C3105C083E2
- Visma acquired Invian Oy and DomaCare to broaden its cloud welfare ERP offering. The date is unverified (not shown in the snippets). Source: https://publish.ne.cision.com/v2.2/Release/ViewReleaseHtml/D61B1C3105C083E2
- Per a search-result summary of the Finnish Journal of eHealth and eWelfare (2020 survey of social-care professionals), Domacare got the best rating among client information systems. Pro Consona was the best-rated of the widely used systems. The summary says Domacare is used in limited, smaller social care settings. Source: https://jyx.jyu.fi/handle/123456789/81006 (I did not open the paper, so details are unverified).
- Kanta's Social Welfare Client Data Repository is the national archive that these systems feed. Source: https://www.kanta.fi/web/guest/ammattilaiset/sosiaalihuollon-asiakastiedon-arkisto
- Whether Domacare is connected to Kanta is unverified. I did not confirm it.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in English and Finnish returned no Finnish user reviews. The Finnish query only surfaced unrelated UK "Domicare" results. Absence of reviews is not evidence of satisfaction.
- Momentum: backed by Visma, with an active domacare.fi site. No shutdown found.

## Pricing
Not published in anything I found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- It targets private providers and is now part of a large group, so it is probably less suited to very small or niche units. This is an inference, not verified.
- No concrete gaps were evidenced.

## Opening
Little: a Visma-backed, well-rated, incumbent system for this buyer. Only a niche too small for Visma pricing might be open, and that is unproven.
