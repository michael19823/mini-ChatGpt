# Nappula (Finland) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0309 (Finland Kanta client-record system for social care). Type: commercial. URL: not known.
Searches used: 4 of 6 (Finnish and English).

## Verdict: strong

Nappula (now myneva.nappula, i.e. Myneva-owned - ownership unverified from search results) is an established social-care client information system. It has completed Kanta registry-access testing and ranks among the best-rated systems in Finnish user surveys.

## Evidence
- Social-work professionals rated it among the best social-care client systems in a 2020 survey (Nappula and Domacare scored best; they are used in narrower social-welfare environments). Source: https://jyx.jyu.fi/handle/123456789/81006
- Satakunta wellbeing area notice lists myneva.nappula as having passed Kanta registry-access implementation tests. Source: https://satakunnanhyvinvointialue.fi/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Tiedote-Kanta-palvelujen-liittymismallista.pdf
- One search summary describes Nappula as developed for child-protection services (summary only; unverified).
- Kanta context: social-care providers, private ones included, must join Kanta by 1 Sept 2026 if they use an electronic client system. Source: https://www.kanta.fi/en/professionals/client-data-repository-for-social-welfare-services
- Private providers must buy, train on and develop their own client system, and some wellbeing areas require use of their own system. Source: https://hyvinvointiala.fi/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/yksityinen-palveluntuottaja-sosiaalihuollon-asiakastiedon-kirjaajana_validia.pdf
- Complaints: none found specific to Nappula. The searches turned up only general complaints about social-care client systems (e.g. Yle on rollout problems, https://yle.fi/a/74-20134054, which concerns other systems).
- Momentum: active, since it is Kanta-tested ahead of the 2026 deadline.

## Pricing
Not found. No published price or minimum (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Positioned toward municipal and wellbeing-area and child-protection use. Fit for very small private providers is unknown (unverified).
- Small private providers are reported to lack information and support for joining Kanta (Hyvinvointiala Hali / Satakunta sources above). This is a gap in the ecosystem, not specifically in Nappula.

## Opening
Little: the incumbent is Kanta-ready and well rated. Any opening is limited to a low-cost, easy Kanta-connected tool for micro private providers, and pricing evidence is missing.
