# Finland: property-management suites (unnamed), dropped idea C0316 (housing-company share register, HTJ)

## Verdict: weak (as a competitor). The real blocker is the state register, not the suites.

Used 3 of 6 WebSearch calls (Finnish and English). No specific suite was identified, so nothing below is product-specific.

## Evidence
- Finland's housing-company share register (huoneistotietojärjestelmä, HTJ) is run by the National Land Survey (Maanmittauslaitos). It has been live since 1 Jan 2019. New housing companies are registered in it automatically, with no paper share certificates. Older companies could transfer their share lists from 1 May 2019 and then stop maintaining them. Sources: https://valtioneuvosto.fi/en/-/1410837/osakehuoneistojen-tiedot-uuteen-rekisteriin-vuodesta-2019-alka-1 and https://www.borenius.com/2019/04/08/new-electronic-register-for-housing-company-information
- The transfer deadline was extended to 31 Dec 2023, and transfer inside the deadline was free. Later transfers carry a fee. Source: https://valtioneuvosto.fi/-/1410837/taloyhtioiden-maaraaikaa-osakeluetteloiden-siirtamiselle-huoneistotietojarjestelmaan-jatketaan-vuodella
- The Land Survey opened technical interfaces for property-management systems on 8 Dec 2020. Companies that use such a system are advised to transfer mechanically from it. Source: https://www.taloustaito.fi/koti/2019/huoneistotietojarjestelma-avautui-vanhoille-taloyhtioille--osakeluetteloiden-siirtoa-kannattaa-kuitenkin-lykata/ (search snippet). So the suites are integrators into HTJ, not owners of the share-register function.
- Small housing companies find it hard to get a property manager (isännöitsijä) at all, and they are offered light packages with extra billing. Source: https://www.taloustaito.fi/koti/pieni-taloyhtio-tuskailee-mista-isannoitsija/ (search snippet). Optimate is a Finnish platform for self-managed housing companies (energy procurement, tendering, meetings). It was listed as a G2 seller (https://www.g2.com/sellers/optimate), and I saw no sign that it offers share-register maintenance. This is unverified beyond the snippet.

## Complaints
No complaints about property-management software or its share-register features were found. The complaints that came up were about property managers as people, such as slow replies and too many clients per manager (Yle, Taloustaito). They are not about software, so they say nothing about a product gap.

## Pricing
No published suite prices were found (unverified). The only price signal is that HTJ transfer was free until the end of 2023 and carries a fee afterwards. I did not find the fee amount.

## Fit gaps
- None identifiable for the share-register use case, because the state register has replaced it.
- Small and self-managed housing companies are under-served by professional management. That is a gap in management, not in share-register software.

## Opening
Little to none for a standalone share-register product, since the Land Survey register is the legal record. Any opening is in lightweight tools for self-managed small housing companies that connect to HTJ, such as meeting, minutes or fee handling, which is a different idea from C0316.
