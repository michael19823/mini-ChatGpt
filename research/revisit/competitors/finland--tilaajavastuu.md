# Tilaajavastuu (Finland), now Vastuu Group

Killed idea: C0315 Finland construction-site reporting.

## Verdict: strong

Tilaajavastuu is now the Vastuu Group brand (the company was formerly Suomen Tilaajavastuu). It is the incumbent in Finnish contractor-liability compliance, with a paid, mandatory-in-practice product and no sign of decline in product activity. Verdict rests on limited evidence: 4 of 6 searches ran (usage limit hit on one, and one returned only off-topic results).

## Evidence
- Rebrand and product: the "Luotettava Kumppani" service gathers the data the contractor-liability law requires, compiles it into a report and keeps it current. Source: https://www.vastuugroup.fi/hubfs/VG-website/luotettava-kumppani/pdf-valtakirjat/Luotettava_Kumppani_VK_FI-FI_28.1.2026.pdf (terms updated 28.1.2026, so actively maintained) and https://vastuugroup.fi/en/asiakastiedotteet/reliable-partner-service-becomes-even-more-comprehensive
- Complaints: the only criticism found concerns pricing and transparency. Yle reported that Kela required service providers to buy the service, and Vastuu Group invoiced 300 EUR a year. A cheaper 160 EUR option was reportedly harder to find on the company site, and a law professor questioned the arrangement. Source: https://yle.fi/a/74-20143754 (I only saw the search summary, not the full article). The Yle story is not about app quality.
- No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit complaints were found. Searches did not reach these sites, so this is "no complaints found", not "no complaints exist".
- Momentum (unverified, third-party aggregator summary): Asiakastieto/PitchBook-style listings show Vastuu Group Holding Oy with 2025 revenue of about 7.8 M EUR, 21 employees, and revenue reported as down 60.8%. This may reflect a restructuring and I could not verify the cause. Source: https://www.asiakastieto.fi/yritykset/fi/vastuu-group-holding-oy/23273271/tilaajavastuu and https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/508976-29
- Regulatory background: a working group on reforming the contractor-liability law exists, and the Entrepreneurs of Finland (Suomen Yrittäjät) commented on it. Source: https://www.yrittajat.fi/lausunnot/lausunto-tilaajavastuulain-uudistusta-valmistelevan-tyoryhman-mietinnosta

## Pricing
- Reported main plan: 300 EUR/year plus 25.5% VAT. Cheaper plan: 160 EUR/year excl. VAT. Source: Yle article above. The official price list is said to be at www.vastuugroup.fi/hinnasto (not opened, so current prices are unverified).
- This is cheap for most firms, so price is not a strong wedge, though the cheaper plan reportedly is not prominent.

## Fit gaps
- The core product is a contractor-compliance report (the buyer checks partners), not on-site daily reporting or site diaries. Whether Vastuu Group also covers on-site worker registers or ID06 reporting was not verified (unverified).
- No evidence found about missing languages, offline or mobile use, or multi-entity support (unverified).
- Reports of a hard-to-find cheaper tier suggest an upsell-oriented sales approach.

## Opening
Little opening if the idea is compliance reporting itself (the incumbent is entrenched and cheap); a gap exists only if C0315 is about on-site operational reporting that Vastuu Group does not cover, which still needs checking.
