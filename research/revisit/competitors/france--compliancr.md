# CompliancR (France)

Killed idea: C0326 (France multi-REP producer declarations).

## Verdict: beatable

CompliancR (compliancr.io) is a real product aimed at the same job as the dropped idea: AI-assisted calculation of eco-contributions and preparation of declarations across French REP schemes. But it has almost no public footprint: no reviews, no published pricing, no news. It looks early-stage and unproven. The verdict is a judgement from thin evidence, and the product's depth is unverified.

## Evidence
- Search snippets describe CompliancR as automating REP compliance with an AI assistant that calculates eco-contributions, detects product categories and prepares declarations for various eco-organisms. Source: https://www.pubrio.com/ko/companies/c5c9411f-1c27-41fd-8cd6-d77bee05e55a/compliancr-io (a company-data aggregator; the descriptive text was relayed through search summaries, not read directly).
- Website given by the coordinator: https://www.compliancr.io/en. Not opened (WebFetch not allowed), so product details are unverified.
- Incumbent alternatives exist in the same space, for example consultancy services such as Leyton (https://leyton.com/fr/progres-environnemental/declarations-eco-contribution-filieres-rep/), which sells expert review of eco-contribution declarations across many REP filieres. This shows the need is real and the market is served mostly by consultants.
- Complaints: none found. Five searches (French and English, covering reviews, pricing, Reddit, Trustpilot, launch/funding news) returned nothing about CompliancR beyond the description above. Absence of complaints reflects absence of public users or coverage, not satisfaction.
- Momentum: unverified. No news, funding or release information found.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified. A search for "ForSURE" EPR software returned a figure of up to $3000/month for a different product (not CompliancR), source: https://alternativeto.net/software/forsure/about . That is only a rough market reference.

## Fit gaps
All unverified, since the site was not read:
- Unknown which of the 25 REP filieres and which eco-organisms are actually covered.
- Unknown whether it files directly with eco-organism portals or only prepares data.
- Unknown pricing for small producers, and whether it offers a human-reviewed or accountant/consultant-assisted workflow.
- No evidence of references, customers or certifications.

## Opening
A multi-REP declaration tool for small French producers can differentiate on verified filiere coverage, transparent small-business pricing and trust (references, human review), since CompliancR shows no visible traction or reviews.
