# Leyton (France) - competitor to C0326: France multi-REP producer declarations

## Verdict: beatable

Leyton is a real, established consultancy that sells REP eco-contribution declaration services. It is a high-touch advisory firm, not a self-serve tool, so a cheap software product for small producers is not directly covered.

## Evidence
- Leyton has a dedicated French REP offer: "Sécurisez vos filières REP et optimisez vos éco contributions". It covers analysis of products, data and declarations, mapping which REP streams apply, and avoiding omissions and double declarations across streams. Source: https://leyton.com/fr/progres-environnemental/declarations-eco-contribution-filieres-rep/
- Its own claim (marketing, unverified independently): it finds declarative discrepancies in 90% of cases and achieves up to 30% savings on eco-contributions. Same page.
- It also sells a one-day REP training (Leyton Academy) and has packaging-specific content: https://leyton.com/fr/progres-environnemental/eco-contribution-emballage/
- The model is expert-led audit and optimisation (it looks like a consulting engagement). I found no evidence of a self-serve declaration platform. This is inferred from the pages above and is unverified.
- Complaints: none found specific to its REP service. Trustpilot shows about 4.5/5 from roughly 206 reviews, mostly praise for R&D tax-credit work, and Leyton replies to negative reviews. Source: https://fr.trustpilot.com/review/leyton.com. No app-store, Reddit or forum complaints surfaced in my searches.
- Momentum: the REP pages are actively maintained, including an item on a new REP obligation. Leyton is an international firm and looks active. I found no acquisition or shutdown news, but did not search for it.

## Pricing
Not published. I found no price or minimum, so the pricing is unverified. Consultancies of this type often work on a share of the savings or on a project fee. That is an assumption, not a finding.

## Fit gaps
- It is advisory and project-based, so it is likely a poor fit for small producers who need a recurring, low-cost declaration tool (inferred, unverified).
- It optimises and audits declarations. I did not see a product that files declarations with each eco-organisme on a continuing basis.
- It is part of a large R&D-tax and incentives consultancy, and REP is a side line there.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve multi-REP declaration tool for SMEs and marketplace sellers can undercut Leyton's consulting model. Other software players such as Recyda and Ecoveritas already appear in this space (named in search results only; not assessed here).
