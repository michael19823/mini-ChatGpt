# Competitor: Balance (HR/payroll modules), Georgia

Idea killed: C0338 Georgia payroll and HR.

## Verdict: strong (for SMEs already on Balance); beatable for stand-alone HR/payroll buyers

Balance is an established accounting/ERP product, so it is a real competitor, but payroll is a bundled module and not a dedicated HR product.

## Evidence
- Balance is an accounting and ERP package on the 1C 8.3 platform, owned by BDO Solutions. It is sold as on-premise licence and SaaS, with a web interface and a mobile app for key figures. Source: https://balance.ge/why-choose-balance-accounting-software
- It claims 3,000+ companies in Georgia (the vendor's own claim, unverified). Same source and https://www.entrepreneur.com/ka/siakhleebi-da-tendentsiebi/balansi-10-tseli/504631
- A page on balance.ge says it automates bank statement entry, payroll and tax declaration preparation. Source: https://balance.ge/digitron (other pages name LLC Digitron as provider; the relationship to BDO was not clarified).
- It is actively developed: a "Balance Assistant" smart digital agent has been released. Source: https://balance.ge//balance-assistant-accounting-automation-en
- Dedicated Georgian HR/payroll rivals also appeared in the searches: SMART Payroll for Georgia (Business Central add-on, RS.GE integration), Profesto, Smartchoice.ge, Aisitec/selfservice.ge. They were not researched further.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in English and Georgian returned no app-store, forum, Reddit or review-site complaints. This does not show that users are satisfied.

## Pricing
- Subscription SaaS with "small monthly fees". No published price or minimum was found (unverified).

## Fit gaps (inferred and unverified)
- Payroll is a module inside an accounting suite, so a buyer who only needs HR (contracts, leave, staffing table, time tracking, employee self-service) must adopt the whole accounting package.
- The 1C platform and the accountant-centred interface suggest HR-manager and employee self-service workflows are weak. No source confirms this.
- The vendor's examples are sector-based (retail, wholesale, construction and others), with no sign of a standalone HR focus.

## Opening
A lightweight, HR-first product with employee self-service and RS.GE integration could win firms that don't want a full accounting suite. It would face Profesto and SMART Payroll, and would need to be sold to firms that don't use Balance.
