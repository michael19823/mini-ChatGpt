# Georgia: Local ERPs (competitor to C0338, Georgia payroll and HR)

## Verdict
beatable (low confidence, research incomplete)

Only 1 of 6 WebSearch calls returned results. The other searches, including the Georgian-language ones, failed with a usage-limit error. The verdict is therefore mostly unverified.

## Evidence
- Balance.ge is a Georgian cloud "mini ERP". It bundles accounting, warehouse, HR, payroll, production, financial reporting, and bank and tax-authority integration, and advertises scheduled accruals, payroll processing and declaration preparation. Payroll and HR therefore exist as modules inside a bigger accounting product, not as a standalone product. Source: https://balance.ge/why-is-balance-an-outstanding-erp-system-and-what-does-it-offer-to-georgian-businesses (the vendor's own marketing, not independent evidence)
- Business Central partner SMART IT markets a "SMART Payroll for Georgia" add-on. Payroll for Georgia is therefore also sold as a localisation for foreign ERPs. Source: https://bc.smart-it.com/en/news-and-articles/smart-payroll-for-georgia-perform-hr-accounting-and-payroll-easily-accurately-and-efficiently/ (snippet only)
- Profesto appeared in the results as an HR product listing: https://softwarefinder.com/hr/profesto. Whether it is Georgian or relevant is unverified.
- Other local ERPs such as Oris and Fina were not confirmed by any search result. Their features and momentum are unverified.
- Complaints: none found. This is because the searches failed, not because the products were checked and found fine. App-store, Reddit, forum and Georgian-language reviews were not checked.
- Momentum: unverified. Balance.ge appears to be actively marketed, but no dates were seen.

## Pricing
Not found. The one successful search found no published prices. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
All of these are unverified inferences. None came from the sources.
- Payroll and HR are bundled into accounting or ERP suites, so a small firm that only needs payroll and HR may be pushed into a heavier product.
- Several of these products are sold through partners and quotes, which is typical for small-market ERPs.

## Opening
A standalone, lightweight payroll and HR tool for small Georgian employers may have room next to the bundled ERP modules. This needs verification of pricing and complaints before relying on it.
