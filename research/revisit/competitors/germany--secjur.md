# SECJUR (Germany) - competitor check for C0355: Germany NIS2

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Active, NIS2-focused German vendor: secjur.com has several NIS2 landing pages (including a self-check "betrifft Ihr Unternehmen direkt/vermutlich nicht") and a NIS2 cost blog. Sources: https://www.secjur.com/blog/nis2-kosten , https://www.secjur.com/nis2/2
- A Handelsblatt special "NIS2-Schnellstarter Programm 2026" (July 2026) shows current marketing activity. Source: https://www.secjur.com/produkte/nis2-schnellstart-handelsblatt-special-juli-2026
- Own claims (vendor marketing, unverified independently): up to 70% effort reduction, 100% NIS2 requirement coverage, 80% faster compliance, over 50% cheaper than consulting.
- Complaints: none found. One German-language search ("SECJUR Erfahrungen Bewertung Kritik") returned only irrelevant SEC results. No G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or app-store reviews were checked or found. A third search (funding/acquisition/news) failed because of a usage limit, so ownership and funding status are unverified. Only 2 of 6 allowed searches succeeded.

## Pricing
- Per search summary of SECJUR materials: ISMS platform "starts at 10,000 EUR" (period, per-year vs one-off, unverified). Positioned as the "Digital Compliance Office". Exact source page for the figure not confirmed.
- Generic market context (third-party, Legiscope blog): NIS2 tools roughly EUR 5-30k/yr focused, 30-100k/yr mid-market. https://www.legiscope.com/blog/nis2-compliance-tools-comparison.html

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified)
- 10k EUR entry price is likely high for micro-businesses and very small suppliers pulled into NIS2 via supply chains.
- Likely German-first; other languages and multi-country support unverified.
- No evidence found of missing filing formats or hardware requirements.

## Opening
Only a low-cost, self-serve tool for micro/small firms (under about 10k EUR) leaves room, and SECJUR's own NIS2 self-check and Schnellstart program already target that entry point.
