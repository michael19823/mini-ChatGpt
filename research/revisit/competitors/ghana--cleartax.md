# ClearTax (Ghana) - competitor check for C0359

Idea killed: C0359, E-VAT sales-invoice clearance / POS-ERP integration for SMEs.

## Verdict: beatable (low confidence; only 2 of 6 searches returned results, the third hit a usage limit)

## Evidence
- ClearTax runs a Ghana e-invoicing page and positions itself as an E-VAT compliance tool. It generates GRA-format invoices, embeds the SDC code, timestamp and QR code, and transmits data to GRA. Source: https://www.cleartax.com/gh/e-invoicing-ghana
- Ghana's E-VAT is a clearance model through GRA's VSDC. It applies to all VAT-registered businesses, including Flat Rate Scheme ones, so the SME market is mandated. Source: https://www.fonoa.com/resources/blog/ghana-e-vat-e-invoicing-2026
- Complaints found were general (ClearTax overall, mostly India/enterprise), not Ghana-specific. Reviewers cite ERP compatibility gaps (including Dynamics 365 F&O), login/access friction, and mixed comments on advanced filtering and multi-user concurrency. Ratings reported by an aggregator: G2 3.8/5 (19 reviews), Capterra 4.5/5 (30), Software Advice 4.5/5 (49), Trustpilot 1.7/5 (30). Source: https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/cleartax (secondary aggregator, figures not checked at the original sites). The Trustpilot figure is probably mostly consumer/India filing feedback (unverified).
- No Ghana-specific complaints, outage news or failed-filing reports were found. No Ghana user reviews found either way.
- Momentum: ClearTax is actively expanding a global e-invoicing product with a Ghana page, so it is not stagnant (inferred from the page existing; no dates verified).

## Pricing

## Fit gaps (mostly inferred, unverified for Ghana)

## Opening
