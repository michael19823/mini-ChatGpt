# DDD Invoices (Ghana) - idea C0359 (E-VAT sales-invoice clearance / POS-ERP integration for SMEs)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (for Ghana)

## Evidence
- DDD Invoices is a Ljubljana, Slovenia startup founded in 2023. It sells a single API for e-invoicing and fiscalization to ERPs, SaaS, POS vendors and marketplaces, in 30+ countries. Sources: https://fintech.global/2026/05/14/ddd-invoices-raises-e1-31m-seed-for-e-invoicing-api/ , https://tech.eu/2026/05/14/ddd-invoices-raises-eur131m-to-simplify-global-e-invoicing-compliance/
- Its coverage focus is Central and Eastern Europe: Poland KSeF, Hungary NAV, Romania, Serbia SEF, Croatia, Montenegro. Same sources.
- Seed round of EUR 1.31M in May 2026 (Fil Rouge Capital, 500 Global), so it is actively developed and funded. Source: https://vestbee.com/blog/articles/ddd-invoices-secures-1-31-m-to-build-global-tax-compliant-e-invoicing-infrastructure
- Three searches (English only) found no evidence that DDD Invoices supports Ghana's GRA E-VAT. Ghana results were GRA/Deloitte/ClearTax pages and an Odoo module (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_gh_evat_einvoice), none naming DDD Invoices. Ghana support is unverified; it may have been a mistaken match.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced. No Ghana-specific news. Local-language search was not useful (Ghana's business language is English).
- Searches used: 3 of 6.

## Pricing
Not published in anything found (unverified). It is a B2B API for software vendors, not priced for individual SMEs.

## Fit gaps
- Ghana E-VAT is not in any listed coverage (unverified).
- Targets software vendors, not SMEs directly.
- Europe-focused; no Ghana presence found.

## Opening
No Ghana competition from DDD Invoices today; the risk is only that it adds Ghana later as a vendor API, and the Ghana SME POS/ERP E-VAT connector niche remains open to it.
