# EDICOM (Ghana) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0359, E-VAT sales-invoice clearance / POS-ERP integration for SMEs.

## Verdict: beatable (low confidence)

EDICOM is a Spain-based global EDI/e-invoicing vendor. Its Ghana offer is an ERP-file connector to the GRA E-VAT portal, aimed at ERP-using firms. I found nothing showing a POS or SME product. Only 2 searches ran: the first returned results and the second hit the usage limit. Evidence is thin.

## Evidence
- EDICOM publishes Ghana E-VAT pages saying its platform is "ready to connect" with the GRA. The flow is: extract a data file from the customer's ERP, EDICOM converts it to the GRA standard, validates it and sends it to the GRA portal. Source: https://edicomgroup.com/electronic-invoicing/ghana and https://edicomgroup.com/blog/b2b-evat-electronic-invoicing-ghana
- The same material describes the GRA flow: each invoice gets an SDC code with timestamp, hash and QR code, and the PDF must carry it. Documents must be archived for 6 years. Phases: large taxpayers from Oct 2022, medium in 2023, all in 2024. These are the vendor's and a Deloitte page's descriptions (https://www.deloitte.com/gh/en/services/tax/perspectives/electronic-invoicing-regime-update-on-implementation.html). The actual current rollout status is unverified.
- The pages are marketing in many languages, probably the same template for many countries. That suggests a generic global offer rather than a Ghana-specific product. This is inference.
- Complaints: none found. I did not reach app stores, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or Ghanaian forums, so "none found" means not searched, not "none exist".
- Momentum: EDICOM is an active, established global vendor (inference from its many localized pages). Ghana-specific traction is unverified.

## Pricing
No published pricing found (unverified). Vendors of this type usually quote by enterprise contract. That is likely out of reach for a small shop (unverified).

## Fit gaps (mostly inferred, unverified)
- Built around ERP integration. No evidence of POS, mobile or offline support, or a self-serve SME plan.
- Likely a sales-led, large-firm sale. No evidence of local-language or local support.
- No evidence of a Ghana-specific entity or local team.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve POS/mobile E-VAT connector for small Ghanaian merchants looks open against EDICOM. The bigger risk is GRA-approved local providers and the GRA's own tools, which I did not check (the second search failed).
