# Enerpize (Ghana) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0359, E-VAT sales-invoice clearance / POS-ERP integration for SMEs.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Enerpize is a generic cloud ERP sold globally. I found no evidence of a Ghana presence or a GRA E-VAT integration.

## Evidence
- Review-site listings (Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice, in South Africa, Canada, UK, Australia, Singapore) describe it as a cloud ERP for SMEs: accounting, inventory, HR, payroll, sales and POS. Sources: https://www.capterra.co.za/software/209024/enerpize , https://www.getapp.co.uk/software/2082129/enerpize
- The only rating found is 5.0/5 from 1 user, a negligible sample. Source: https://www.getapp.ca/software/2082129/enerpize
- Search for "Enerpize" with GRA E-VAT, certified invoicing system or Ghana returned nothing linking it to Ghana or to GRA certification. Searches were in English only. No Ghana-specific local-language query was run, because English is the business language in Ghana. This is unverified, not proven absence.
- Searches for the GRA E-VAT regime returned only Ghana-wide material, none naming Enerpize. The GRA offers a free certified web invoicing app and an API for integrating billing systems with the CIS. Sources: https://www.deloitte.com/gh/en/services/tax/perspectives/electronic-invoicing-regime-update-on-implementation.html
- Competing E-VAT connectors exist, such as ECOSIRE's ERPNext/Odoo Ghana E-VAT apps, from USD 499 (build to order). Source: https://ecosire.com/apps/erpnext/erpnext-ghana-evat
- Complaints: none found. No Ghana-specific reviews were found either.

## Pricing
Listings disagree and are unverified against the vendor's own site. One gives Basic from USD 14.95/month, Advanced 39.95, Premium 64.95. Another gives 9.99 / 29.95 / 41.64. A free trial is reported. Source: https://techjockey.com/detail/enerpize

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of GRA E-VAT / CIS integration, so it does not serve this need.
- No evidence of Ghana localisation (GRA VAT/levies, GHS, mobile money). Unverified.
- Momentum: unclear. Not determined from the searches.

## Opening
Enerpize does not close the E-VAT clearance / POS-ERP gap for Ghanaian SMEs. The real rivals are GRA's free tool and local ERP resellers or connectors, which this check did not assess.
