# Ghana: payroll packages (unnamed) — killed C0365 (Statutory payroll)

## Verdict: beatable

Ghana has several statutory-payroll products, but no single dominant one, and the sources found are mostly vendor marketing. A narrow, cheap, SME-focused statutory payroll tool still has room.

## Evidence
- Products that advertise Ghana PAYE/SSNIT Tier 1/Tier 2 calculation: Odoo l10n_gh_payroll module (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/l10n_gh_payroll), Lenvica (https://lenvica.com/?p=43565), Cohrus HRMS and PayrollGhana (named in a Faciotech/search summary, https://blog.faciotech.com/payroll-compliance-ghana-business), SeamlessHR (https://seamlesshr.com/blog/top-hr-payroll-software-in-ghana-for-2026/), Kolonell (https://kolonell.com/en/blog/hr-payroll-app-smes-ghana-2026), Cadana, Aketua (MoMo payouts). Several of these are vendor blogs, so quality is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, Trustpilot or forum reviews appeared in 3 searches. Not searched further (6-search cap, 3 used); local-language search gave nothing useful.
- Pain that exists without the software: SMEs on Excel make errors on PAYE brackets, the SSNIT basic-vs-total salary split and Tier 1/2 routing, and late SSNIT payments attract a 3% monthly penalty (vendor blog claims, unverified).

## Pricing
- Odoo Ghana payroll module: $129.02 (listing; one-off or per-version unclear, unverified).
- Kolonell: 24,900 FCFA/month for 1 to 15 employees (a West African, FCFA-priced vendor; Ghana applicability unverified).
- XenFlex (earned wage access, adjacent): employers GHS 10-15 per enrolled employee per month.
- Others: no published prices found.

## Fit gaps
- Odoo needs an ERP and technical setup; poor for micro-SMEs.
- HRMS suites (SeamlessHR, Lenvica) target mid-size firms; pricing not published.
- Cadana, Aketua and XenFlex focus on payment or wage access rather than filing-ready GRA/SSNIT/Tier 2 returns.
- Whether any produces GRA/SSNIT/NPRA filing-format files: unverified.

## Momentum
Active: 2026 content from several vendors, and Cadana is reported as growing. No shutdowns found.

## Opening
A cheap, MoMo-friendly payroll tool for firms with under 20 staff that outputs filing-ready PAYE, SSNIT and Tier 2 returns.
