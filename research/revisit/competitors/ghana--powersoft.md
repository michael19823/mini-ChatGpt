# Powersoft (Powersoft System, Ghana) - competitor check for C0359 (E-VAT clearance / POS-ERP integration for SMEs)

## Verdict: weak

Powersoft System appears to be a local reseller and implementer of third-party software (ERPNext, QuickBooks). I found no evidence of an own E-VAT clearance product. 4 searches were used (of 6 allowed).

## Evidence
- Jobberman company page: Powersoft System is a Jobberman partner since 2014 and offers ERPNext, QuickBooks, hotel/restaurant management, HR/payroll and custom SaaS, with licensing, implementation, training and support. https://www.jobberman.com.gh/company/powersoft-system
- No search result tied Powersoft System to GRA E-VAT integration. A search combining "Powersoft System", ERPNext, QuickBooks and GRA E-VAT returned only other vendors (e.g. ECOSIRE's build-to-order ERPNext Ghana E-VAT app). https://ecosire.com/apps/erpnext/erpnext-ghana-evat
- Do not confuse with Powersoft 365 (a Cyprus-linked POS/ERP product seen in results). Any link to the Ghana company is unverified.
- Context: Ghana E-VAT is reported as mandatory from January 2026 for VAT-registered businesses; Odoo E-VAT modules exist for POS, e-invoice and inbound. https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/l10n_gh_evat_pos
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of Powersoft System surfaced (English search only; no local-language results of use).
- Momentum: unverified. No news, releases or dates found.

## Pricing
- No Ghana pricing published. Only a Powersoft 365 (international, not confirmed to be the same company) price appeared: PocketPro EUR 20.23/month per user plus Stock Control EUR 46.41/month (source: search snippet, unverified for Ghana).
- Reference point for the market: ECOSIRE's ERPNext Ghana E-VAT app is from USD 999 (one-off, build-to-order, 2-4 weeks).

## Fit gaps
- Services/reseller model, so likely custom, per-project work and not a self-serve SME product (inferred, unverified).
- No evidence of a packaged E-VAT connector, offline POS fiscalisation or mobile use.
- Depends on ERPNext/QuickBooks, so a QuickBooks-based shop would need separate E-VAT integration (unverified).

## Opening
Powersoft is not a real E-VAT competitor on current evidence; the real rivals for C0359 are Odoo/ERPNext E-VAT modules and integrators like ECOSIRE, so a low-cost, self-serve SME E-VAT bridge still has room.
