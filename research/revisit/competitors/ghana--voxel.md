# Voxel (Voxel Group / baVel, an Amadeus company) - Ghana

Idea killed: C0359 E-VAT sales-invoice clearance / POS-ERP integration for SMEs.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

## Evidence
- Voxel Group's Ghana page is an informational "digital reporting requirements" guide on E-VAT (XML/JSON, real-time reporting), not a Ghana E-VAT product listing. https://www.voxelgroup.net/normativa/guias/ghana/
- Voxel is a Spanish company founded 1998, now an Amadeus company, focused on invoicing, payment and procurement for tourism (hotels, travel companies, restaurants) via its baVel platform. https://www.voxelgroup.net/blog/en/past-present-and-future-of-voxel-group-by-economic/
- Searches found no Ghana clients, no GRA E-VAT certification claim and no Ghana-specific reviews (unverified that none exist; just none found). Only 2 of 6 allowed searches ran (one hit a usage limit, so some searches were not run). No local-language search done (Ghana official language is English).
- Complaints: none found.
- Momentum: active as an Amadeus-owned tourism e-billing vendor; no sign of Ghana development.

## Pricing
- baVel eBilling Full Package listed at 159 EUR per month per property (hospitality marketplace listing, https://store.apaleo.com/apps/bavel-voxel-group per search snippet; unverified for Ghana). Far above typical Ghana SME budgets and tourism-specific.

## Fit gaps
- Tourism/hotel focus, not general SME POS/ERP.
- No evidence of GRA E-VAT integration, Ghana presence or local support.
- Priced per hotel property in EUR.

## Opening
Voxel does not serve Ghanaian SME POS/ERP E-VAT clearance, so the idea's real competition lies elsewhere (GRA-certified local providers).
