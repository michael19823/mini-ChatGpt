# Greece: PMS products (e.g. Vezpa AMA guide)

Idea killed: C0369 (short-term-rental per-stay declarations and AMA compliance)

## Verdict: strong

Existing tools already automate AADE per-stay declarations. This is a feature set, not a gap.
Searches: 5 of 6 WebSearch calls ran; the 6th hit a usage limit.
"Vezpa" itself returned nothing (unverified as a product).

## Evidence
- Chekin documents an e-invoicing and short-term-rental compliance flow for Greece (support.chekin.com, "e invoicing short term rental compliance in greece"; chekin.com/en/blog/mydata-guide-for-vacation-rental-managers-in-greece/).
- Hostaway's blog (hostaway.com/blog/airbnb-rules-in-greece/) describes PMS-driven compliance. Its search summary says PMSs generate short-term stay declarations, track submission status, handle cancellations and changes, and map AMA identifiers. Whether Hostaway itself ships this is unverified.
- GuestArrive is Greece-specific. It handles guest data collection, AADE short-term-stay declarations, climate-crisis-tax receipts and myDATA integration. strspecialist.com rates it 3/5 (60/100). Weaknesses cited: Greece-only, and it needs PMS integration for full automation (strspecialist.com/reviews/guestarrive-greece-compliance-review).
- Market size: AADE recorded 2,466,075 short-term stay declarations in 2025, with rents of about EUR 973.7M (same strspecialist.com search result, quoting AADE data). Demand is large and enforcement is tightening: a fine of 50% of gross income, minimum EUR 5,000, applies for non-registration (search summaries of tovima.com and rewize.com).
- Rewize publishes an AMA owner guide (rewize.com), so content and tooling are crowded.

## Complaints
None found. No app-store, Reddit, Trustpilot or forum complaints surfaced; the Greek-language complaint search was cut off by the usage limit. Absence of complaints is not proof of satisfaction.

## Pricing
- Lodgify: from USD 16/month on an annual plan, Professional from USD 40, Ultimate from USD 59 (search summary of Lodgify/Capterra comparisons).
- Hostaway: not published; sources estimate about USD 35 per listing per month, with enterprise contracts and a USD 500 setup fee (unverified).
- GuestArrive and Chekin: prices not found (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- GuestArrive is Greece-only and relies on a PMS for full automation. Hosts with no PMS, or a single listing, may be underserved (inferred from the review, not verified).
- Hostaway is aimed at professional managers, and per-listing fees plus setup are heavy for owners of one or two flats.
- Hosts of one or two properties who just want to file declarations are not clearly served. This is an inference; not verified.

## Opening
A thin, cheap, PMS-free declaration tool for 1-3 property owners is the only visible gap, and AADE's own free portal plus GuestArrive and Chekin already cover most of it.
