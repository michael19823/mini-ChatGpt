# Greece: tax accountants (consultant, no URL) vs C0369 (short-term-rental per-stay declarations and AMA compliance)

## Verdict: beatable

Accountants are a human-service substitute, not a software product. The per-stay declaration is monthly, repetitive work. Greek hosts can now buy software that automates it, so the real competition is those tools plus the accountants.

## Evidence (3 WebSearch calls used of 6)
- Each rented property, or each separately rented room, needs its own AMA. The Short-Term Accommodation Declaration is due by the 20th of the month after guest checkout. Source: https://www.newmoney.gr/roh/palmos-oikonomias/oikonomia/ti-prepi-na-xeroun-osi-nikiasoun-akinito-meso-platformas-airbnb-o-oktalogos-tis-eforias/ and https://www.hostaway.com/blog/airbnb-rules-in-greece/
- Not registering is penalised with a fine of 50% of gross rental income, with a minimum of EUR 5,000. Source: https://www.tovima.com/finance/greece-cracks-down-on-undeclared-airbnb-income-with-hefty-fines-and-data-audits/amp/. This gives hosts a strong reason to pay for compliance.
- Software already automates the declarations. GuestArrive (a Hostaway marketplace integration) says it submits declarations, collects guest data and issues receipts and the climate tax. Source: https://www.hostaway.com/marketplace/guestarrive/. Its claims are unverified.
- AADE says registration and AMA issuance are done electronically and immediately, so the filing itself is simple. Source: search summary of AADE guidance (lifo.gr/parianostypos.gr results).
- Complaints: none found. I did not search for reviews of accountants specifically, and no review sites turned up.

## Pricing
Accountant fees for per-property or per-declaration STR work: not found, unverified. No published price list appeared in the results.

## Fit gaps
- Accountants work on a monthly or annual cycle and rarely handle per-stay guest data capture.
- Multi-property hosts, who need a separate AMA per room, face a per-unit service cost that scales linearly (unverified).
- The accountant's work is manual and not integrated with Airbnb or Booking calendars.
- Existing software (GuestArrive and PMS integrations) already fills part of this gap, which narrows the opening.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve per-stay declaration and AMA tool for small hosts, undercutting accountant hourly fees and priced below full PMS suites. GuestArrive-type tools are the competitor to check next.
