# Guinea: EOR platforms (generic) vs C0389 (general payroll SaaS for Guinea)

## Verdict
not-a-real-competitor

## Evidence
- EORs (Rivermate, Playroll, Globalization Partners) legally employ staff in Guinea on behalf of foreign companies that have no local entity, and run payroll, CNSS and tax as part of that. They are not a payroll tool a local firm uses for its own staff. Sources: https://rivermate.com/employer-of-record/guinea , https://playroll.com/employer-of-record/guinea-conakry , https://www.globalization-partners.com/globalpedia/guinea
- Rivermate says Eos, Serviap and Hightekers EOR are now Rivermate (consolidation, so active; exact deal unverified): https://rivermate.com/en/guides/guinea/taxes
- A French-language search for a Guinea/Conakry SME payroll tool returned only French products (PayFit, Sage, EBP) and no Guinea-specific payroll software. Its summary said as much. No local SaaS payroll competitor surfaced (absence in one search, not proof).
- Complaints: none found specific to Guinea. Generic global EOR complaints (Deel, Remote, Oyster) were slow support, processing delays and extra fees. These are secondary summaries (G2 comparison pages, blogs) and say nothing about Guinea: https://www.g2.com/compare/oyster-oyster-vs-remote-vs-deel-payroll

## Pricing
- Guinea-specific EOR prices not found (unverified). Global list prices from comparison sites: Deel about $599, Remote and Oyster about $699 per employee per month (https://www.costbench.com/compare/deel-vs-remote/). Oyster reportedly asks for a deposit of about 1 month's gross salary per employee (https://eorhq.com/guides/oyster-pricing/). Far above what a local SME would pay for payroll software.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at foreign employers hiring a few remote or expat staff, not local firms with their own payroll.
- Hundreds of dollars per head per month, so unusable for a local SME's whole workforce.
- Not a software product the local firm controls. It is a legal-employment service.

## Opening
Local payroll SaaS for Guinean employers (CNSS, 18% employer contribution, monthly cycle, French-language) is not contested by EORs, which serve a different buyer. Local-software competition was not checked beyond one search.
