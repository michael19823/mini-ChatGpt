# PaySpace (Guinea)

Idea it killed: C0389 (general payroll SaaS for Guinea).

## Verdict: beatable

PaySpace has a Guinea Conakry product guide, so a localized payroll engine probably exists. But it is an enterprise-oriented multi-country platform now owned by Deel, with no evidence of local support, French-first UX, or a micro-business focus.

## Evidence
- PaySpace's support portal has a "Guinea Conakry Product User Guide" (the search result titles for the Guinea and Equatorial Guinea guides appear swapped, so check which is which). Source: https://support.payspace.com/portal/en/kb/articles/equatorial-guinea-product-user-guide . The guide's contents were not read; depth of the Guinea localization (CNSS filing formats, PAYE) is unverified.
- Deel acquired PaySpace in 2024 for an undisclosed sum. PaySpace covers 44 countries, with a 14,000-client base of large names (Coca-Cola, Puma, Heineken) per press. Sources: https://techpoint.africa/2024/03/05/deel-acquires-south-africas-payspace , https://www.fintechfutures.com/accounting-payroll/deel-acquires-africa-based-payroll-company-payspace
- Momentum: actively developed and backed by Deel, a large funded company (not stagnant). The focus is likely Deel's EOR and multinational customers, not local SMEs (inference).
- Capterra shows a single review (5.0/5, 1 user). Source: https://www.capterra.co.za/software/161486/payspace
- Complaints: none found on Capterra, app stores, forums or news, in English or French. No Guinea-specific reviews exist. This is a valid "none found" result, not evidence of satisfaction.
- No evidence of a Guinea-based reseller, French-language UI, mobile app or offline use (unverified).
- Local alternatives seen: Africa HR Solutions (payroll outsourcing, claims 400+ clients across Africa), plus EOR and payroll providers Playroll and Ontop. These are services for foreign firms rather than SaaS for local SMEs (inference from snippets). Source: https://africa-hr.com/guinea-payroll-outsourcing/

## Pricing
- Capterra lists a starting price of ZAR 1.31 per payslip per month, with a free trial (per search summary, unverified; the figure is South African and may not apply to Guinea). Source: https://www.capterra.co.za/software/161486/payspace
- Guinea pricing and minimums: not published (unverified). Enterprise sales-led pricing is likely, which would put it out of reach for micro firms.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- Aimed at mid and large enterprises and multinationals; no evidence it serves micro or small Guinean firms.
- No evidence of a Guinea-based sales or support presence, a French-first product, mobile-money (Orange Money, MTN MoMo) salary payout, or offline use.
- Sold via enterprise sales; a self-serve small-business tier for Guinea is not shown.

## Opening
A cheap, French-language, mobile-first CNSS and PAYE payroll tool for Guinean SMEs looks open, since PaySpace targets larger multi-country customers. Caveat: evidence is thin and the Guinea module's depth was not checked.
