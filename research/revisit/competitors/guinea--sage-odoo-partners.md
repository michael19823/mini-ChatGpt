# Guinea: Sage / Odoo partners (dropped idea C0389: general payroll SaaS for Guinea)

## Verdict: weak

Sage and Odoo partners in Guinea are bespoke ERP integrators, not self-serve payroll SaaS. They are a weak competitor for a cheap, simple payroll product for small firms.

## Evidence
- Search 1 (Sage paie Guinee) hit the usage limit and returned nothing. 5 of the 6 allowed searches were completed, including one retry.
- erpresearch.com lists two Sage partners in Conakry: Hestia (Sage 100 Cloud, X3, 1000; listed as working in HR/payroll) and Eti SA / Digitalis (founded 1984, telecom operator and integrator; Sage 100). Source: https://www.erpresearch.com/sage-partners/hestia and https://erpresearch.com/sage-partners/eti-sa-digitalis. This is a directory listing, so the depth of their payroll work is unverified.
- Odoo: a vendor blog (Dasolo, an Odoo implementer with an interest in selling) says Odoo HR/payroll suits Guinea and names Dasolo and Louis Dresse SRL as partners. Source: https://www.dasolo.ai/blog/odoo-around-the-world-7/odoo-implementation-in-guinea-793. No evidence of a Guinea-specific payroll localization (CNSS, RTS), and no evidence of local Odoo partners in Guinea.
- Searches for Guinean payroll software (French, CNSS/RTS, "paie PME Conakry") found no dedicated Guinean payroll SaaS and no Guinea-specific software reviews. Payroll job postings in Conakry suggest demand and manual or in-house payroll work (unverified as to tools used).

## Complaints
None found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews for these partners in Guinea turned up in the searches.

## Pricing
Not published. Sage 100 and X3 are typically quote-based, with licence, implementation and consulting fees; I found no Guinea price data (unverified). Odoo has public pricing, but I did not find a source for it in these searches.

## Fit gaps
- Quote-based, project-style sales aimed at mid and large firms (inferred, not verified).
- No evidence of a self-serve, mobile or low-price product for small firms.
- No evidence of Guinean payroll rules (CNSS, RTS) pre-built in the products.

## Momentum
Unknown. The Sage partner listings are directory entries with no recent-activity signal.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve payroll product with Guinean CNSS and RTS rules built in, aimed at small firms that cannot afford a Sage or Odoo integration project.
