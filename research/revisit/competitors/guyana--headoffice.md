# HeadOffice (Guyana) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0391, payroll filing (GRA PAYE Form 2/5 CSV plus NIS schedules).

## Verdict: strong (provisional; thin evidence)

HeadOffice appears to be a real, Caribbean-focused accounting and payroll product that markets itself for Guyana. It targets this exact need. No complaints were found, but that mostly reflects how little independent coverage exists.

## Evidence
- A marketing site, hosted at a laravel.cloud staging-style domain (headoffice-marketing-site-production-a7kpwu.laravel.cloud), has Guyana pages: payroll tax and tax forms blog posts, and a download page for GRA Form 5 (monthly PAYE return). Its search snippet says HeadOffice "can help you manage payroll for your Guyanese business and stay compliant". Sources:
  - https://headoffice-marketing-site-production-a7kpwu.laravel.cloud/guyana/blog/guyana-payroll-tax
  - https://headoffice-marketing-site-production-a7kpwu.laravel.cloud/guyana/blog/guyana-tax-forms
  - https://headoffice-marketing-site-production-a7kpwu.laravel.cloud/guyana/download-form-form-5-monthly-paye-return
- A Jamaica page says it combines payroll with accounting, tax returns, invoicing, inventory and HR, with automatic NIS, NHT, Education Tax and HEART calculations (https://headoffice-marketing-site-production-a7kpwu.laravel.cloud/jamaica/blog/best-payroll-software-jamaica-2026). This is a multi-country Caribbean play and self-promotional content.
- Search snippets did not show whether HeadOffice generates the GRA eServices CSV upload or NIS schedules. Unverified.
- Complaints: none found. No Trustpilot, Capterra, G2, app-store or forum reviews surfaced in 5 searches (English only; the local language is English). Not evidence of satisfaction, only of no visible reviews.
- Momentum: 2026-dated content suggests it is active. Unverified beyond that.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified. (Other Guyana options seen: Odoo l10n_gy_payroll module at about $1,511 per the Odoo apps listing; EOR services such as Remote, Ontop and Rivermate; Celery; SalisFlow; PeoplePay. None are HeadOffice.)

## Fit gaps
- Unknown whether it produces GRA CSV and NIS schedule files; to be checked by reading its product pages directly (WebFetch not permitted here).
- Likely a full accounting suite, so possibly heavier than a payroll-filing-only tool.
- No evidence of offline or mobile use, or of local-language needs (Guyana uses English).

## Opening
Only a narrow one: a cheap, filing-only tool that outputs GRA-ready Form 2/5 CSV and NIS schedules, if HeadOffice proves to be bundled and pricier. Without complaints or pricing data, there is no evidence this gap is open.
