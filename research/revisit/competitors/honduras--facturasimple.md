# FacturaSimple (Honduras) - competitor check for C0404 (E-invoicing exception desk, SAR)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

No evidence that a product called FacturaSimple operates in Honduras. The only FacturaSimple found is an Argentine app (ARCA/AFIP).

## Evidence
- Four WebSearch calls (Spanish and English) found no Honduran product, site, review or news item for "FacturaSimple" / "Factura Simple" (queries: SAR e-invoicing, opiniones/precios, CAI/SAR software, app reviews).
- The only match is an Argentine Tienda Nube app named FacturaSimple, which issues invoices with CAE through ARCA (AFIP). It is not Honduran. Source: https://www.tiendanube.com/tienda-aplicaciones-nube/facturasimple
- Honduras context: mandatory SAR-cleared e-invoicing is reported as not yet live. The regime is CAI-based, with an optional electronic self-printing modality under Acuerdo 481-2017. Sources: https://www.e-invoice.app/country/HN , https://www.ey.com/es_ce/technical/tax/tax-alerts/honduras-el-servicio-de-administracion-de-rentas-inicia-una-encuesta . The search summary also warned that some commercial sites overstate Honduras as having live e-invoicing. Treat as secondary-source, not verified against SAR.
- Complaints: none found, because no Honduran product was found. App-store, Reddit, Facebook and local-forum coverage was not found by search and is unverified.

## Pricing
Nothing for Honduras (unverified / not found). The Argentine app lists a 15-day trial, then ARS 19,900 per month, but that is not relevant to Honduras.

## Fit gaps
Cannot assess. There is no Honduran product to compare against C0404. Momentum is unknown.

## Opening
FacturaSimple does not block C0404. The real question for the idea is whether SAR mandatory e-invoicing exists yet, since it appears still in design.
