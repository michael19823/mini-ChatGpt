# Honduras: local payroll software (generic, no URL known)

Idea killed: C0403 Payroll contributions (IHSS / RAP / INFOP)

## Verdict: weak

The generic "local payroll software" label did not resolve to one dominant product. Searches found one Honduras-focused vendor (Softland HCM, a regional ERP/HR suite), global EOR/payroll vendors, and no complaints or reviews about any of them. Evidence is thin: no prices, no user reviews.

## Evidence
- Searches run: 5 successful WebSearch calls (English and Spanish) plus 1 that failed on a usage limit and was re-run after reset. Within the 6-call cap.
- Softland HCM is presented on its own site as cloud payroll/HR software for Honduras, "over 40 years of experience in Latin America" (vendor claim, unverified independently). Source: https://softland.com/hn/software-de-nomina-costa-rica/ (page title mentions Costa Rica, so Honduras fit is unclear) and https://softland.com/hn/contratar-software-de-nomina-costa-rica-2025/
- Global vendors covering Honduras payroll (IHSS, RAP, INFOP, aguinaldo, 14th month): Deel, Rivermate (absorbed Eos, Serviap, Hightekers EOR), Activ Payroll, Ontop, Globalization Partners, Pebl, Niural. These are EOR/multi-country offers rather than small local tools. Sources: https://www.rivermate.com/guides/honduras , https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/honduras , https://www.activpayroll.com/global-insights/honduras , https://www.deel.com/employees/Honduras
- Rate context from Rivermate-type guides: employer IHSS 5.0% + 3.5%, RAP 1.5% + 4.0%, INFOP 1.0% (secondary source, not checked against law).
- IHSS itself ran a 2023 tender for HR/ERP software for its own staff (not a competitor): https://www.elheraldo.hn/honduras/ihss-licita-software-para-gestionar-a-su-personal-AM15912147
- A bank (BAC Credomatic) publishes a monthly IHSS planilla PDF template, suggesting banks/spreadsheets are a common workflow (inference, unverified): https://www.baccredomatic.com/sites/default/files/2025-09/Planilla_IHSS_2025_Septiembre.pdf
- Complaints: NONE found. Spanish and English searches returned no app-store, Capterra, G2, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum complaints about any Honduran payroll product. Only IHSS institutional news (finances, outages from an ISP fault), unrelated to payroll tools.

## Pricing
Not found for any product (Softland, Ontop, Rivermate etc. prices not shown in results): unverified. A Spanish pricing search returned no prices.

## Fit gaps
Unverified. Likely gaps (inference only): global EOR vendors target hiring/employing via a provider, not a small local employer filing its own IHSS/RAP/INFOP; Softland is a mid-market ERP/HR suite and probably sales-led. No evidence on offline use, mobile, or filing formats.

## Momentum
Softland site shows 2025 content (active). Rivermate recently consolidated acquired EORs. No local product's momentum could be assessed.

## Opening
No evidenced low-cost, filing-focused tool for small Honduran employers; the main alternatives are enterprise HR suites, global EOR platforms and spreadsheets, but pricing/fit is unverified.
