# Mercans (Honduras) - competitor check for C0403 (payroll contributions IHSS / RAP / INFOP)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (for a small-buyer product)

Mercans is a real, capable global payroll outsourcer that does cover Honduras. It sells managed multi-country payroll and EOR to multinationals, not a self-serve contributions tool for local small employers. It is not a direct substitute for a small-business product.

## Evidence
- Mercans publishes a Honduras payroll page covering IHSS (employer 8.5% / employee 5% on a capped base), RAP (1.5% each above the ceiling), INFOP (1% employer), and 13th/14th month bonuses, "on a single proprietary stack". Source: https://dsr.mercans.com/?p=869 (search snippet; figures are Mercans' marketing text, unverified against law).
- Described as a Dubai-based firm with 500+ HR consultants serving multinationals; named a leader in ISG Provider Lens 2023 multinational payroll. Sources: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231211493923/es and https://nearshoreamericas.com/?p=81260 (snippets). Expanded in Latin America (Costa Rica, Bogota offices).
- Ratings: G2 about 4.8/5 (27 reviews), Glassdoor about 4.1, Trustpilot about 4.0 (4 reviews), per https://www.gloroots.com/blog/mercans-reviews (third-party blog, unverified).
- Complaints found (mild, generic, not Honduras-specific): dated UX, limited customization needing vendor involvement, some poor-support mentions on HR Blizz product. Sources: gloroots blog above; G2 pages https://www.g2.com/products/mercans-hr-blizz/reviews and https://www.g2.com/products/mercans-mercans-peo/reviews (not read in full).
- No Honduras-specific customer reviews or complaints found (including a Spanish-language search).
- Momentum: active, expanding, no sign of shutdown or acquisition found.

## Pricing
- No published pricing. A third-party blog says EOR starts around $600 per employee per month with no self-serve quote (unverified). Payroll-only pricing unknown. Clearly out of reach for a small local employer.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at multinationals with distributed headcount; no self-serve or small-business tier found.
- Managed service rather than a lightweight tool; quote-based sales.
- No evidence of local-language (Spanish) self-service product or filing UI for small Honduran firms (unverified).

## Opening
A cheap, Spanish-language, self-serve IHSS/RAP/INFOP calculator and filing helper for small Honduran employers is not served by Mercans; Mercans does not compete at that price or segment.
