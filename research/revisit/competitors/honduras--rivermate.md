# Rivermate (Honduras) - dropped idea C0403: payroll contributions (IHSS / RAP / INFOP)

## Verdict
not-a-real-competitor

Rivermate is a global Employer of Record (EOR). It hires staff on a foreign company's behalf. It is not a tool a Honduran employer uses to calculate or file its own IHSS / RAP / INFOP contributions. Its Honduras pages are informational guides, plus a cost calculator.

## Evidence
- Rivermate's Honduras pages describe it as an EOR that legally hires employees without a local entity. The pages are at https://www.rivermate.com/guides/honduras and https://rivermate.com/guides/honduras/taxes. They list IHSS, RAP and INFOP rates and say monthly filings fall due in the first ~10 days of the next month. This is guidance content, not a filing product (as seen in search snippets; pages not opened).
- Momentum: Eos, Serviap and Hightekers EOR were rebranded as Rivermate (site banner on rivermate.com pages). It is actively marketing and expanding.
- Complaints: reviews found are about the EOR service in general, not Honduran contributions. The sources are the aggregator summaries at https://www.gloroots.com/blog/rivermate-reviews and https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/rivermate. They report Trustpilot ~3.9 (24 reviews; another source says 3.2), G2 4.9 (73), Capterra 4.9 (44), Gartner 4.4 (21). Noted weaknesses: basic reporting and analytics, and slow replies to negative Trustpilot reviews (over 1 month, per Trustpilot summary). I did not read the individual reviews.
- Spanish-language search (Honduras planilla / opiniones) found no local reviews or complaints about Rivermate in Honduras.
- No Honduras-specific complaints, outages or failed filings found.

## Pricing
- Per its pricing page (https://rivermate.com/en/pricing, per search snippets), EOR is from EUR 299 per employee per month. A third-party snippet gives $319-$639 per employee per month, varying by country, headcount and expat vs local hire. The Rivermate pricing page says it has no minimums or contracts (snippet). Honduras-specific price: unverified.
- This is far above what a small Honduran employer would pay for payroll-contribution software, and it is the wrong product for them anyway.

## Fit gaps
- It serves foreign companies hiring in Honduras, not local SMEs that run their own payroll.
- It offers no standalone IHSS / RAP / INFOP calculation or filing tool for local employers. The only tools seen are guides and a cost calculator.
- It requires the employee to be on Rivermate's payroll (the EOR model).
- Whether it supports IHSS / RAP electronic filing formats: unverified.

## Opening
A local-language payroll-contribution calculator and filing helper for Honduran SMEs is not addressed by Rivermate. It only touches foreign employers using an EOR.
