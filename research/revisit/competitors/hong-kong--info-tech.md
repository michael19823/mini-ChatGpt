# Info-Tech (Hong Kong, commercial) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0408, 468 continuous-contract tracking and MPF offsetting split calculation.
Searches used: 6 (English and Traditional Chinese). The first draft of this file said "not-a-real-competitor". That was wrong, because the vendor could not be identified at the time. It is replaced by this version.

## Verdict
beatable. Info-Tech is a real, established vendor, but nothing found shows it doing 468 tracking or an MPF-offsetting split. It is a general payroll and HRMS suite, and a dedicated tool for this niche is not covered by it.

## Evidence
- Identity: Info-Tech Systems Integrators is headquartered in Singapore, with operations in Malaysia, Hong Kong and India. It claims 20+ years in HK, 30,000+ customers and 1M+ employees paid monthly (vendor claim, unverified). Sources: https://www.info-tech.com.hk/blog/best-payroll-software-solutions-in-hong-kong/ and https://investhk.gov.hk/fr/client-profiles/info-tech-systems-integrators-hk-limited
- Product scope: a cloud payroll and HRMS suite with IR56B/E/F/M forms, MPF contribution tables, bank autopay, a mobile app, and attendance, leave and claims modules (same vendor blog). Search summaries mention no continuous-contract (468) tracking, average daily wage, long service payment or offsetting calculation. This is absence of evidence only, because the product pages were not read.
- Momentum: active. It won 1 Gold and 1 Bronze at the 2024 HR Vendor Awards in Hong Kong (Best HRMS and Best Payroll Software), per https://www.info-tech.com.hk/news/excelling-in-hr-innovation-info-tech-secures-1-gold-and-1-bronze-at-2024-hr-vendor-awards-in-hong-kong/ . It has a regularly updated blog.
- Complaints: none found. Reviews seen were positive. Capterra India shows 4.9/5 from 173 users (https://www.capterra.in/software/1028858/hrms-all-in-one-software). Those are Indian-market reviews, so they do not speak to HK. No complaints were found on Reddit, local forums or app stores. That does not prove there are none, since the searches were limited.
- Regulatory context: MPF offsetting was abolished on 1 May 2025, so the offsetting split matters only for service before that date, and that shrinking legacy case fades over time. Source: https://bcthk.com/en/mpf-orso/employer/how-to-administer-your-mpf-plan/contribution/333-english-content/what-s-new/latest-news/mpf-offsetting-arrangement . The 468 rule (17 hours a week, or 68 hours over 4 weeks) applies from 18 January 2026. Source: https://www.lewissilkin.com/en-hk/insights/2025/02/07/hong-kong-employment-outlook-2025-what-employers-and-hr-specialists-should-know . Local HR vendor Workstem writes that good payroll software should track continuous hours, average daily wage and long service payment (https://www.workstem.com/hk/?p=23265). That implies a competing HRMS vendor sets this expectation, though it does not show that Info-Tech meets it.

## Pricing
A search summary says HK$15 per employee per module (payroll, leave, claims, attendance and so on), with a possible 1-year minimum contract for at least 10 employees. This is unverified, and I did not confirm it on a vendor page. It is cheap per head, but modules add up.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of 468 tracking or an offsetting split (unverified, not checked on product pages).
- It is a broad suite rather than a specialist legal-entitlement calculator, so it is a bundle purchase for a buyer who only needs the calculator.
- Possible minimum contract and employee count (unverified).

## Opening
A standalone or lightweight 468 and long-service-payment entitlement tracker (with a legacy pre-May-2025 offsetting split) that works alongside any payroll is open. The risk is that Info-Tech or other HRMS vendors such as Workstem add the feature. Offsetting alone is a fading niche, so the idea rests on 468 and long service payment.
