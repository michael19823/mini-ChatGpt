# EY (Hungary) - competitor check for C0412 (EPR quarterly reporting)

## Verdict: weak

EY is a Big Four consultancy, not a software product. Searches found no EY EPR product or published EPR offering aimed at small producers. Its relevance is as a high-touch, bespoke advisory option. Evidence is thin because searches were limited (one query hit a usage limit, two ran).

## Evidence
- Search "EY Hungary EPR extended producer responsibility compliance service" returned Schoenherr, Mondaq, Grant Thornton Hungary, Marosavat, Regsurance and KPMG Hungary pages on Hungarian EPR. None was from EY and none described an EY EPR service. Sources: https://grantthornton.hu/en/news/the-extended-producer-responsibility-fee-scheme-will-start-on-1-july-there-is-only-one-month-left-to-register , https://kpmg.com/hu/en/insights/2023/06/taxalert-2023-06-08.html
- Hungarian-language search (EPR bejelentes szoftver kisvallalkozas MOHU negyedeves jelentes) returned adozona.hu pages. They describe quarterly data reporting (by the 20th of the month after the quarter) and a small-business web tool, "Korforgo", offered at a minimal fee. No EY result appeared. Source: https://adozona.hu/brandContent/EPRdij_olcso_egyszeru_megoldas_39IVTK
- The same search surfaced law firm and other consultancy content (Jalsovszky, KPMG, Grant Thornton) but nothing from EY. Those firms appear to be the more visible EPR advisers in the search results.
- Complaints: none found. No reviews of any EY EPR service were found. Unverified whether EY offers one at all.

## Pricing
Not published. Big Four advisory is typically quoted per engagement. Unverified for EPR specifically. Do not assume a figure.

## Fit gaps
- Advisory/consulting model, not self-serve software. Likely aimed at large clients (inference, unverified).
- No evidence of a product, app, or recurring quarterly-filing tool for micro/small producers.
- Small buyers are already served by cheaper tools such as Korforgo, per adozona.hu.

## Opening
EY does not appear to compete for small producers needing cheap, recurring quarterly EPR filing; the real pressure comes from low-cost tools like Korforgo, not EY.
