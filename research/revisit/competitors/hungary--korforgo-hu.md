# korforgo.hu (Körforgó) - Hungary

Killed idea: C0412, EPR quarterly reporting. Competitor type per triage: consultant. In fact it is a SaaS web app, not a consultant.

## Verdict: strong

It is a purpose-built, cheap web app aimed at the same small and micro firms the idea would target.

## Evidence
- Sponsored article on adozona.hu (brand content, so marketing, not independent) describes Körforgó as a web app for EPR record-keeping and quarterly data reporting. It says it is meant for "tens of thousands of small and micro-enterprises", can be filled from other systems, and gives a good price-to-value ratio. https://adozona.hu/BrandContent/EPRdij_olcso_egyszeru_megoldas_39IVTK
- Other adozona.hu coverage of EPR quarterly reporting appears in the same searches. It did not give more detail on Körforgó. https://adozona.hu/2023_as_adovaltozasok/Kozeleg_az_EPR_kovetkezo_negyedeves_adatszo_VE7RVE
- Searches found no customer reviews, app-store ratings, forum threads or complaints about it. The 4 searches that targeted it turned up none. Finding none is a valid result. It also means the product is low-profile, not proven loved.
- Context: sources say manual entry in OKIRkapu is slow and error-prone (Andersen, EY, Novitax material). That is a pain point Körforgó already targets. EY also offers an EPR upload tool, which is a larger-firm alternative. https://assets.ey.com/content/dam/ey-sites/ey-com/hu_hu/topics/tax/ey_epr_adatfeltolto_tool.pdf

## Pricing
- Free for 30 days, then 8,000 HUF + VAT per quarter. An add-on for preparing the environmental product fee (EPR) return costs another 4,000 HUF + VAT per quarter. Source: search summary of the adozona.hu brand-content page. I could not open the page directly, so treat the figures as unverified and possibly out of date.
- That is cheap for a small buyer. It is not overpriced.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. No evidence of missing filing formats, mobile or offline use, or multi-entity support. I could not inspect the product (WebFetch blocked), so feature gaps are unverified.
- Possible gaps to test: direct submission to OKIRkapu (unclear whether it only exports data), ERP or accounting integrations, accountant multi-client use, English-language support.

## Momentum
- Unverified. The only dated material found is the 2023-2024 era brand content. No news of shutdown or acquisition was found, and no recent product updates were found.

## Opening
Little: only a differentiated angle (accountant multi-client workflow, ERP/invoicing auto-fill, or direct OKIRkapu submission) would beat a 8,000 HUF/quarter incumbent built for the same small buyers.
