# Novitax (Hungary) - competitor to C0412 (EPR quarterly reporting)

## Verdict: weak

Novitax is an accounting/payroll software vendor, not a consultant. Its EPR feature is a side module that only produces a list; filing is manual on OKIRkapu.

## Evidence
- Novitax's own knowledge base says the EPR module (menu "Feladások, bevallások/EPR adatszolgáltatás") works from the EPR content of outgoing invoices. It cannot send XML automatically because no test interface is available. It prepares a list, and the user keys KF codes and quantities into OKIRkapu by hand. Source: https://tudastar.novitax.hu/epr-adatszolgaltatas/?pdf=18343
- Novitax also has a knowledge-base page on product-fee (termékdíj) invoices with NAV XML 2.0: https://tudastar.novitax.hu/termekdijas-szamla-nav-xml-2-0-tukreben/?pdf=11194
- Active development: the knowledge base lists regular version releases (e.g. ntax 2023/24, raxla 2023/24, ber 2023/24) and 2026-era quarterly 2658 reporting content. Source: https://tudastar.novitax.hu/2658-negyedeves-adatszolgaltatasok/?pdf=23505
- Other Hungarian EPR competitors seen in results (not researched here): EY EPR upload tool (https://assets.ey.com/content/dam/ey-sites/ey-com/hu_hu/topics/tax/ey_epr_adatfeltolto_tool.pdf), WTS Klient, KPMG, Forvis Mazars.
- Complaints: none found. The searches (Hungarian and English) returned no user reviews, forum threads or outage news for Novitax. Absence of reviews does not mean users are satisfied.
- The vendor's official site was not visited (WebFetch not allowed). A search noted the contact address Gitár utca 4, Budapest, novitax@novitax.hu. This comes from the knowledge-base PDFs, so treat it as unverified.

## Pricing
Not found. No published price list or review in the searches. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- No automated EPR filing: manual re-keying into OKIRkapu is required.
- Needs a Novitax accounting licence, so it is usable only by existing Novitax customers (mostly accountants and bookkeeping firms). It is not a standalone EPR tool for producers.
- It derives quantities from invoices, so it presumably does not cover the product-weight and KF-code mapping. Unverified, inferred from the doc.
- The claim that automation is blocked by the lack of a test interface may have changed since the page was written. The page date is unknown.

## Opening
A standalone EPR tool that maps products to KF codes and weights and produces or submits the quarterly filing would beat Novitax's manual list. The better route to Novitax's accountant users is likely a partnership or complement rather than a head-on fight.
