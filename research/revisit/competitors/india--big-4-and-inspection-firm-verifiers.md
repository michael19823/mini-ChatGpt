# India: Big-4 and inspection-firm verifiers (SGS, Bureau Veritas, TUV SUD, Big-4 advisers)

Killed idea: C0428, EU CBAM reporting for steel/aluminium MSME exporters.

## Verdict: beatable (partly complementary)

Accredited verification is a legal requirement that a software product cannot replace. The prep and reporting layer for MSMEs is not served at their price point. Only 2 of the 6 planned searches returned results (usage limit hit), so evidence is thin.

## Evidence
- Only auditors accredited by EU-recognised national bodies can do CBAM verification; SGS, Bureau Veritas and TUV SUD are named as verifier options, with SGS and BV also offering pre-verification, gap assessment, training and reporting support. Limited EU-accredited verifier capacity in India is flagged as a possible bottleneck. Source: https://www.thesustainabilitycloud.com/blog/eu-cbam-verifiers-complete-guide-and-checklist-for-indian-exporters-2026-ready/
- Certification reported at around Rs 10 lakh per company; small exporters asked for TRACE-style reimbursement. Source: https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/govt-may-reimburse-cbam-certification-costs-for-small-steel-exporters-under-export-scheme
- Reports say MSMEs may face Rs 15-20 lakh in total compliance cost (carbon accounting, monitoring, verification, digital reporting); the government is considering covering 90% for MSMEs. Sources: https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/eus-cbam-poses-major-compliance-challenge-for-indian-msmes-experts-seek-urgent-government-support/ and https://www.basispointinsight.com/Story/-india-s-metal-exports-to-eu-plunge-24--as-cbam-reporting-hits-msmes-_30e1360663c9.html
- Complaints: no direct user reviews of these firms found. Only the indirect signal above (cost burden, capacity bottleneck). The Hindi search surfaced no firm-specific complaints.

## Pricing
No published price lists found. Reported figures (Rs 10 lakh verification, Rs 15-20 lakh total) are press estimates and unverified as to source methodology.

## Fit gaps
- Bespoke, project-based consulting; cost looks high for micro and small firms.
- Verification capacity is limited.
- Verifiers must stay independent, so they cannot also do the exporter's data prep and reporting for the same entity (general CBAM verifier-independence principle, not confirmed by my searches; unverified).
- No evidence of a recurring, low-cost, self-serve tool from these firms.

## Opening
Sell a cheap emissions-data and report-prep tool that produces verifier-ready files, positioned as a complement that lowers MSMEs' verification cost, not a replacement for the accredited verifier.
