# Ecobillz (India) - competitor check

Idea it killed: C0421 Hotel / homestay Form C (now Form-III) automation.

## Verdict: strong

It is an active, established vendor that has built exactly this product (Form C automation for hotels). The one gap I found is homestay and budget fit, and I have no evidence of unhappy customers.

## Evidence
- Ecobillz sells a Form C automation product for hotels: it scans photo ID and visa, auto-fills the C-form and submits it to the FRRO site in real time. Source: https://bwhotelier.com/article/as-part-of-govt-compliance-ecobillz-offers-c-form-automation-solution-for-hotels-to-manage-foreign-guests-458690
- It also ships a "Ecobillz C-Form Assistant" Chrome extension that automates arrival and departure submissions on the FRRO portal: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/cjhkkoncpnhoggbgkkdhaeoahmjpcpjc
- Company profile: founded 2016, claims 300+ hotel chains as customers, offices in India, London and Dubai. Sources: https://yourstory.com/2022/10/ecobillz-saas-simplifies-hotel-check--check-out, https://cxotoday.com/corner-office/ecobillz-spearheading-a-new-age-change-to-digitally-drive-hospitality-industry-in-india/
- Momentum: a profile page cited a filing date of 30 March 2025 and the last disclosed funding as 2017 (https://inc42.com/company/ecobillz/funding/). I found no news of a shutdown or acquisition. Whether the product is still being actively developed is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. The only review found was one 5.0 G2 review of "Ecobillz POS" (a different product), which mentioned minor issues when the interface is combined with other software and said support resolved them. Source: https://g2.com/products/ecobillz-pos/reviews. No app-store, Reddit, forum or Hindi-language complaints were found in 6 searches (English and Hindi). Absence of results is not proof that customers are satisfied.

## Pricing
- No published price found. A press article said it planned to sell SaaS licences in three tiers, from a full suite for 5-star hotels to a minimal version for budget hotels (bwhotelier link above). Actual prices unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Marketing focuses on hotels and hotel chains; I found nothing aimed at homestays, guesthouses or single-owner properties (unverified, based on limited search).
- Hindi or regional-language support for small owners: not found.
- The articles found refer to "C-form". I found no mention of the newer Form-III naming, so whether it has been updated is unverified.

## Opening
Little room to compete on hotel automation itself; any opening would be a low-cost, simple tool for homestays and tiny properties, if Ecobillz's budget tier turns out to be too costly or too complex for them (unverified).
