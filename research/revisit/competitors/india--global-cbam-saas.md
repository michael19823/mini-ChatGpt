# Competitor: Global CBAM SaaS (India) - dropped idea C0428

Idea killed: EU CBAM reporting for steel/aluminium MSME exporters. Searches used: 5 of 6.

## Verdict: strong

CBAM software aimed directly at Indian exporters already exists, including an India-based vendor with MSME-priced plans, so the "global SaaS is too big or too dear for MSMEs" gap is mostly closed.

## Evidence
- The Sustainability Cloud (TSC) sells CBAM software for Indian exporters: installation-level emission mapping, automated CBAM report generation, a CBAM Communication module for EU importer data exchange, verifier access and consultancy. It claims to have helped over 1,000 non-EU exporters (vendor claim, unverified). Source: https://www.thesustainabilitycloud.com/blog/cbam-software-in-india/
- A press release describes new CBAM software "for SMEs and enterprises" at a lower cost. Source: https://alcircle.com/press-release/new-cbam-software-offers-cost-effective-carbon-reporting-for-smes-and-enterprises-113394
- CBAMBOO (London, founded 2023) serves both EU importers and their non-EU suppliers. Source: https://cbamguide.com/software/cbamboo/
- Other global tools reviewed by cbamguide.com: Coolset, CarbonChain, Kolum, Greenly (CBAM module added 13 Feb 2025), Dubrink. Source: https://cbamguide.com/software/
- Momentum is active: CBAM entered its payment phase on 1 Jan 2026, the market is crowded, and there are many 2026 vendor listicles.
- Demand and pain are real. Indian MSMEs report cost and paperwork burdens, and one MSME paid a consultant about Rs 2 lakh a year. Reports say the government is considering covering 90% of MSME compliance costs, which could subsidise using existing tools. Sources: https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/eus-cbam-poses-major-compliance-challenge-for-indian-msmes-experts-seek-urgent-government-support/ and https://www.kotakneo.com/news/market-news/india-msme-compliance-costs-eu-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-2026/
- Complaints: none found. No G2, Capterra, Reddit, app-store or Hindi-language user complaints about CBAM software turned up in 5 searches (English and Hindi). That is a null result, not proof of satisfaction.

## Pricing
- TSC (India, published): Starter Rs 0 to start, pay per report generated. Basic Rs 3,599/month (1 installation, 4 reports, 2 users). Standard Rs 7,199/month (1 installation, 20 reports, 5 users). 20% discount for annual payment. Source: https://www.thesustainabilitycloud.com/tsc-cbam-exporter-pricing/ (figures from a search summary, not read on the page directly).
- CBAMBOO importer plans: EUR 9,000 Basic and EUR 19,000 Pro, per cbamguide.com. These are for EU importers; supplier-side pricing is unverified.
- Wider compliance cost estimates for MSMEs range from Rs 2-5 lakh a year (audits and reporting) to Rs 15-20 lakh (full setup). These are industry estimates and vary by source.

## Fit gaps
- Verification: the verifier-access modules are still software, and costly third-party verification remains the main MSME burden.
- Language: no Hindi or regional-language product found (unverified).
- Offline, mobile or WhatsApp-first use: not evidenced (unverified).
- Multi-entity support: TSC plans are priced per installation or entity, so multi-plant MSMEs pay more.
- Input data: the hard part is getting installation-level data from small units with no metering. Software does not fix that, though a data-capture or consultancy layer might.

## Opening
Little room for generic CBAM reporting software. The only opening is a very cheap, vernacular, mobile-first data-capture and verifier-prep service for micro and small units, ideally bundled with the expected government subsidy. TSC already prices at Rs 3.6k a month.
