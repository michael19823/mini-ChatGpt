# greytHR (India) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0426 Payroll and labour-codes rollout (with Keka, Zoho Payroll, RazorpayX Payroll).

## Verdict: strong

Established, actively developed, cheap, with a free tier and a labour-codes response already published. Only a narrow wedge exists.

## Evidence
- Capterra: 4.3/5 from 296 reviews; one summary says 88% positive (aggregated via search snippets, not read directly). Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/150850/greytHR/reviews/
- Complaints (Capterra/G2 review summaries): payroll support agents often lack payroll-rule knowledge; slow support on urgent or complex cases; slow loading and occasional server hang-ups; limited report customization; tech team slow to ship improvements. Sources: https://www.capterra.com/p/150850/greytHR/reviews/ , https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/greythr-review-5007090
- Momentum: active. It has a blog, a webinar and a help-center guide on the Labour Codes (in force 21 Nov 2025) and Code on Wages. Sources: https://www.greythr.com/blog/labour-codes-2025/ , https://www.greythr.com/webinars/parichay-webinars/new-labour-codes-2025 , https://www.greythr.com/help-admin/statutory-compliance-reports/code-on-wages-2019-guide/ . How well its payroll engine actually handles the codes was not verified.
- Hindi-language search returned no Hindi-specific complaints. No Reddit/Facebook/app-store evidence was found within the search limit (unverified).

## Pricing (from search summaries of greytHR's pricing page, https://www.greythr.com/pricing; verify before relying)
- Starter: free up to 25 employees.
- Essential: Rs 2,495/month base, includes 50 seats, then Rs 45 per employee per month.
- Growth: Rs 4,495/month base, includes 50 seats, then Rs 85 per employee per month.
- Premium: custom quote. 18% GST extra.
- Not overpriced for small buyers.

## Fit gaps
- Weak payroll-specific support and expertise (per reviews).
- Limited custom reports.
- Performance issues.
- No evidence of gaps in language, offline use, or small-firm coverage; it serves micro to large firms.

## Opening
Only a narrow wedge: a labour-codes salary-restructuring/compliance tool with expert human support for small firms, or a layer on top of greytHR. A full payroll replacement faces a free, cheap, well-reviewed incumbent.
