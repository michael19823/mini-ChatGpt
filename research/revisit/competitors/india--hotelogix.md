# Hotelogix (India) - dropped idea C0421: Hotel / homestay Form C (Form-III) automation

## Verdict: weak (as a Form C competitor)

Hotelogix is a real, active hotel PMS, but no source found shows a Form C / FRRO filing feature. As a general PMS it is a neighbour, not a direct competitor for this idea. Caveat: absence of evidence in 5 searches is not proof; check Hotelogix's own site (unverified).

## Evidence
- Founded 2008, HQ Noida; targets small and mid-market hotels, B&Bs, hostels. Most users are in India (137 users per search summary). Source: https://www.capterra.in/software/76374/hotelogix , https://www.hoteltechreport.com/operations/property-management-systems/hotelogix
- Two searches for Hotelogix + Form C / C-Form / FRRO returned nothing linking them. Unverified whether it has any such module.
- A different vendor, ECOBILLZ, is reported to offer C-Form automation for hotels (https://bwhotelier.com/article/as-part-of-govt-compliance-ecobillz-offers-c-form-automation-solution-for-hotels-to-manage-foreign-guests-458690). That is the more relevant competitor and should be checked separately.
- Ratings: Capterra India 4.4/5 (251 reviews), G2 4.3/5 (10 reviews), per search summary. Source: https://www.capterra.in/reviews/76374/hotelogix
- Complaints (paraphrased from search summaries of Capterra reviews; not read directly):
  - Access denied over a payment issue and ignored support emails; "support disappears" after initial period.
  - Double bookings (9 reservations / 15 rooms) on day 2 of channel integration; vendor did not accept responsibility.
  - A recent review says sales and implementation promises were not kept.
  - None relate to Form C.
- Momentum: active. Multi-Property Manager launched 30 June 2026; a Philippines hotel migrated to it in June 2026 (per search summary; source link not confirmed, unverified). No acquisition or shutdown found.

## Pricing
- Reported: $3.99 per room per month, or $0.20 per occupied room night, plus $350 sign-up fee (setup, training, booking engine). Source: https://www.roommaster.com/blog/hotelogix-pricing (third-party; verify on vendor site). Roughly affordable for small hotels, but a full PMS replacement is a heavy ask for a homestay that only needs Form C.

## Fit gaps
- No confirmed Form C / FRRO e-filing, passport/visa OCR, or police-station reporting.
- Full PMS: a homestay or small guesthouse that just needs compliance must adopt a whole system and a $350 setup.
- Support quality complaints for small customers.

## Opening
A lightweight, standalone Form C filer (ID scan to FRRO submission, check-in and check-out) for homestays and small hotels not on a PMS, possibly sold as an add-on or integration for PMS users like Hotelogix's, is not blocked by Hotelogix.
