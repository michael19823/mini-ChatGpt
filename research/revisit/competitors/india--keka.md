# Keka (India, commercial HRMS/payroll)

Killed idea: C0426 (payroll and labour-codes rollout; greytHR, Keka, Zoho Payroll, RazorpayX Payroll)

## Verdict: strong

Established, widely reviewed HRMS and payroll product. It is weak only for very small buyers on price. Only 3 of 6 searches returned results (one hit the usage limit), so evidence is thin.

## Evidence
- G2 rating 4.5 from 1,956 reviews; Capterra 4.4 from 87 reviews, per the rfp.wiki aggregator (https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/keka). Not checked on G2/Capterra directly.
- Praised for clean UI, low training burden, payroll, attendance and self-service (same source).
- Recurring complaints: support responsiveness and follow-up, mobile app and occasional lag, limited customisation and report flexibility (same source). Aggregated, no individual reviews verified.
- Labour codes: all four codes were notified effective 21 Nov 2025, with rules still being finalised through 2026 (https://blog.ipleaders.in/labour-codes-2026-implementation-complete-guide-to-what-is-in-force-what-changed-and-what-to-do-now/). Whether Keka has shipped labour-code payroll support: unverified. No Keka-specific source found.
- No app-store, Reddit, Trustpilot or Hindi-language complaint results found; the local-language search failed (usage limit).
- Momentum: not verified. No acquisition or shutdown news seen.

## Pricing
Per a third-party blog (https://blog.petpooja.com/payroll/top-10-payroll-software-in-india-for-small-businesses/ and related results; not confirmed on Keka's own site, so unverified): flat monthly bands covering the first 100 employees. Foundation Rs 9,999, Strength Rs 12,999, Growth Rs 15,999. Extra employees Rs 90, 120 and 150 each per month. A 15-person team on Foundation pays about Rs 666 per head per month. Not priced for headcounts under about 30.

## Fit gaps
- Poor price fit for micro and small firms (under about 30 staff) because of the flat floor.
- Limited report and customisation depth; complex rules need admin tuning.
- Mobile app and support complaints.
- Labour-code readiness unverified.

## Opening
A cheap, labour-codes-focused payroll and compliance tool for firms under 30 staff, which Keka's price floor and support complaints leave underserved. This competes with the other payroll vendors too, so it is not a clear gap.
