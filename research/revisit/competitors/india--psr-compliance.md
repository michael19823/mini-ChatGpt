# PSR Compliance (India) - competitor check for C0422 (PIBO plastic-EPR compliance software)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (for software); it is a service consultancy

## Evidence
- Search results list psrcompliance.com (contact support@psrcompliance.com, +91 87961 04190) with pages on EPR credit procurement, annual return filing, registration, brand-owner EPR, and blog posts on EPR consulting (https://www.psrcompliance.com/epr-credits-for-plastic-waste, https://www.psrcompliance.com/epr-annual-compliance-plastic-waste, https://www.psrcompliance.com/blog/epr-consultant-in-india-cpcb-compliance-and-waste-management-services).
- It describes itself as helping PIBOs buy CPCB EPR plastic credits from registered recyclers, validate certificates on the CPCB portal and file annual returns. That is human-run, end-to-end service, not self-serve software. No product, app or login was seen in the results (unverified beyond search snippets).
- Complaints: none found. Searches (English and Hindi) returned no reviews, app-store listings, Trustpilot/Reddit/forum posts or news about it. This is absence of evidence, not a clean record.
- Market context: other consultancies (GreenSutra, Gravita India, Afleo) sell the same service, so the consultant space is crowded. As of 25 June 2026, 62,175 PIBOs were registered on the CPCB portal (per a search-result summary; unverified at source), suggesting a large buyer base.

## Pricing
Not published in anything seen. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of software: no dashboard, no automated target calculation, no multi-entity tracking, no certificate inventory tool.
- Service model means per-engagement fees and manual turnaround; small PIBOs may find that costly or slow (inferred, not verified).

## Momentum
Actively publishing 2026-dated blog content (renewal for F&B brands 2026, Category 3 process 2026), so it is active as a marketing/service operation.

## Opening
Consultants like this do not close the gap for a self-serve, low-cost PIBO compliance tool (target calculation, credit tracking, annual return prep); the bigger risk is other consultants and CPCB portal changes, not this firm.
