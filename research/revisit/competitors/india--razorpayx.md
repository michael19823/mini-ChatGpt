# RazorpayX Payroll (india)

Idea killed: C0426, payroll and labour-codes rollout. URL from search: https://razorpay.com/payroll/ (the brief gave none).

## Verdict: strong

Well-funded, actively developed, automates TDS/PF/ESI/PT filings, and priced for small firms. No substantial complaints were found.

## Evidence
- Software Suggest lists 4.3/5 from 9 reviews, praising ease of use and PF/ESI compliance. https://www.softwaresuggest.com/razorpayx-payroll/reviews
- Reviewers say support responsiveness is inconsistent, that report generation and compliance updates could be better, and that they want more HRMS features. This is from a search summary; I did not see the review text, so treat it as unverified. Sources: https://techjockey.com/reviews/razorpayx and the Software Suggest page above.
- Reviewers say the mobile app lacks features the desktop version has, and that customization is limited. Same caveat: unverified summary. Source: https://techjockey.com/reviews/razorpayx
- Trustpilot and Capterra results for Razorpay broadly mention complaints about unexpected charges and slow refunds. These concern the payments product, not Payroll. Source: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/razorpay.com?page=4 (unverified summary).
- Momentum: Razorpay keeps a Payroll changelog (https://razorpay.com/docs/payroll/changelog.md) and says over 40,000 businesses use the product (company claim, from a search summary). I did not verify whether labour-code (50% wage rule) updates have shipped. The search results did not confirm this.
- Only the first search surfaced review sites. The Reddit/forum search and the labour-code search returned no user complaints. No Hindi-language complaint results appeared. I used 4 of 6 searches.

## Pricing
Source: https://razorpay.com/payroll/pricing, via search summary, so unverified.
- Prime: Rs 3,499/month, up to 20 employees, then Rs 150 per extra employee.
- Elite: Rs 6,499/month for 50 employees, then Rs 150 per extra employee.
- Enterprise (100+ employees): custom quote.
- 18% GST is extra.
- The QuickBooks integration was quoted at Rs 100 per employee per month.
- The minimum monthly spend is high for micro-businesses with fewer than 10 employees.

## Fit gaps
- It is a payroll tool, not a labour-code advisory or rollout service.
- Reviewers report limited customization and a thin mobile app (unverified).
- The Rs 3,499 minimum is steep for micro firms.
- I found no evidence of gaps in regional languages or multi-state coverage. This is untested, not confirmed.

## Opening
Little room in a generic payroll product. A gap remains only in a low-cost or free micro-business tier, or in labour-code restructuring advice, such as salary re-structuring for the 50% wage rule.
