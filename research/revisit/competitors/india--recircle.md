# ReCircle (India) - competitor check

Killed ideas: C0422 (PIBO plastic-EPR compliance software), C0423 (SWM Rules 2026 EBWGR bulk-waste-generator compliance).
Searches used: 6 of 6 (English and Hindi). No URL was given for the competitor.

## Verdict: beatable

For C0422, ReCircle is mainly a credit seller and service provider (waste collection plus EPR certificates) with a reporting dashboard. It is not a standalone compliance-software product. For C0423 no evidence was found that it serves bulk waste generators at all, so it is effectively not-a-real-competitor there.

## Evidence
- ReCircle is a brand of Swachh Sustainable Solutions. It runs a digital platform (ClimaOne) that aggregates dry-waste collection and processing and sells EPR credits to businesses. Named clients include Hindustan Unilever, Dabur, Hindustan Coca-Cola Beverages and Nestle. Sources: [GSMA blog](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/blog/building-a-national-circular-supply-chain-platform-recircles-climaone-platform-in-india/), [Acumen](https://acumen.org/blog/recircle-taps-the-hidden-potential-of-waste-and-waste-workers-in-india/), [YourStory](https://cwv.yourstory.com/2024/11/trash-treasure-recircle-cracked-indias-waste-management).
- Scale: it operates collection and sorting facilities in Mumbai, has partnerships covering over 270 sites, and had recovered over 169,000 tonnes as of March 2024 (GSMA / Recycling Today coverage: [Recycling Today](https://recyclingtoday.com/news/recircle-closing-the-plastics-loop-in-india)). Momentum looks active and growing.
- Its EPR programme is described as handling CPCB portal registration, plastic footprint measurement, end-to-end implementation support and reporting via the ClimaOne dashboard (search summary of its own materials; the page itself was not opened).
- Complaints: none found. Searches for reviews on Reddit, Trustpilot and Glassdoor, plus a Hindi search, returned nothing about ReCircle. This is a valid "no complaints found" result, not proof of satisfaction.
- SWM Rules 2026 / EBWGR: the search returned only articles on the rules. None mentioned ReCircle offering bulk-waste-generator compliance or software (e.g. [Waste Recycling magazine](https://www.wasterecyclingmag.com/swm-rules-2026-push-polluter-pays-digital-compliance-and-four-way-segregation)).

## Pricing
- Unverified: one search summary of a Scribd FAQ document ([link as returned](https://www.scribd.com/document/1077018387/FAQs)) attributed to ReCircle rates of Rs 0.93/kg + 18% GST for EOL credits and Rs 0.95/kg + 18% GST for recycling credits. I could not confirm the document is current or official.
- No published software subscription price or minimum order quantity was found.

## Fit gaps (for the dropped ideas)
- Its model is selling credits and fulfilment, so a PIBO buys tonnage from it rather than software. It is not a neutral tool for managing multiple suppliers, certificates or CPCB filings across vendors (inference, unverified).
- Its client list is large FMCG brands; small-PIBO fit and minimums are unknown (unverified).
- No sign of an EBWGR product for bulk waste generators or local-body certificate management.

## Opening
A vendor-neutral PIBO filing and reconciliation tool, or an EBWGR compliance tool for bulk generators, is not blocked by ReCircle, which sells credits and collection rather than compliance software.
