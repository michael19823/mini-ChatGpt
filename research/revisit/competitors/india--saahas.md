# Saahas (Saahas Zero Waste) - India
Killed idea: C0422 PIBO plastic-EPR compliance software.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor
Saahas is a waste-management service company and a CPCB-registered PRO that sells EPR fulfilment (collection, recycling, certificates). It is not a self-serve compliance software product for PIBOs. The 4 searches I ran (of 6 allowed) found no evidence of such a product.

## Evidence
- Registered as a Producer Responsibility Organisation by CPCB; offers end-to-end EPR plastic compliance, a "standardized workflow", "real-time IoT tracking", MIS reporting and onsite audits. Source: https://saahaszerowaste.com/solutions/epr-plastic and https://saahaszerowaste.com/saahas-successfully-registered/
- The workflow and MIS appear to be part of a managed service. No standalone software product, login or SaaS pricing was found. A search for one returned nothing (unverified beyond that).
- Momentum: revenue Rs 66.7 Cr in FY25, down 12.3% from Rs 76.1 Cr in FY24, per a search summary (https://yourstory.com/2025/08/saahas-zero-waste-circular-economy). A Rs 6 Cr round was led by C4D Partners (https://www.indiaglobalbusiness.com/igb-archive/c4d-partners-backs-waste-management-firm-saahas-india-global-business). Active, with no sign of acquisition or shutdown.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in English and Hindi, and for reviews, returned no app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints about Saahas. The Hindi query returned only general PIBO and CPCB portal articles.
- Context: 62,175 PIBOs were registered on the CPCB EPR portal as of 25 June 2026 (https://www.downtoearth.org.in/environment/daily-court-digest-major-environment-orders-july-6-2026). That is a large market that Saahas's service model is unlikely to cover for small PIBOs.

## Pricing
Not published (unverified). Service-based and quote-driven.

## Fit gaps
- It is a managed service, not software.
- It is tied to buying Saahas's own EPR fulfilment.
- It is aimed at brands that need reverse logistics. A small PIBO that only wants filing and portal tooling is not served (inferred, not verified).

## Opening
A cheap self-serve filing and tracking tool for the many small PIBOs, which Saahas does not sell.
