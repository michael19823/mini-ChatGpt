# Simpliance (India)

Dropped idea: C0426, payroll and labour-codes rollout. The searchers named greytHR, Keka, Zoho Payroll and RazorpayX Payroll. Simpliance appears to be a different category of product.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Simpliance is a labour-law compliance calendar and register tool, not payroll. It does not run payroll or implement the labour codes in payroll engines. It overlaps only on the compliance side (registers, PF/ESIC/PT/LWF tracking, labour-code advisory).

## Evidence
- Simpliance Classic is positioned as cloud software for labour law compliance. It auto-configures applicable compliances by state, headcount and industry, with recurring activities, evidence management, risk scores and email alerts. Source: https://aparajitha.com/products/labour-law-compliance-software/
- It also covers "payroll statutory compliance" (PF, ESIC, PT, LWF), register generation and vendor/contractor compliance, but this is compliance tracking, not salary processing (search summaries only). Source: https://simpliance.in/simpliance-registers
- It offers expert advisory and implementation support for the four labour codes, effective 21 Nov 2025 (from search summary; unverified in detail). Source: https://aparajitha.com/products/labour-law-compliance-software/
- It is a product of Aparajitha Corporate Services, which says it is on an acquisition path to consolidate compliance platforms. This suggests active momentum. Source: https://www.aparajitha.com/aparajitha-is-set-for-its-acquisition-journey-of-consolidating-robust-compliance-platforms/
- Complaints: none found. The searches (English and Hindi) returned no customer reviews. The review-site hits were employee reviews of Aparajitha (AmbitionBox, Indeed), which are not product complaints. A Capterra listing exists (https://www.capterra.com/p/10005493/Simpliance/), but I saw no review content. A pricing-complaint query returned only unrelated products (Simplifi, Simpli.fi) and was discarded.

## Pricing
- Capterra lists a start price of Rs 20,000 per annum (from a search summary; unverified on the vendor site). The vendor describes a "pay as you use" model. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/10005493/Simpliance/
- This looks aimed at mid-size and multi-state firms with a compliance function. It is probably too much for micro or small employers (unverified).

## Fit gaps (relative to the payroll and labour-codes idea)
- No evidence it processes payroll, payslips or salary restructuring for the Code on Wages (the 50% wage definition).
- No evidence it integrates with payroll tools such as greytHR, Keka, Zoho or RazorpayX.
- Appears to be a tracker with a consultant-style service layer, so it is not self-serve payroll for small firms.
- No evidence of vernacular-language or mobile-first support (unverified).

## Opening
Simpliance does not block a small-business payroll and labour-codes tool. The real competitors are the payroll vendors, and this file does not assess them. At most it is a complementary compliance-calendar product.

Searches used: 5 of 6. Search limitation: the tool is US-only, so local-language coverage was thin.
