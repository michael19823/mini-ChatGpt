# Stayflexi (India) - competitor to C0421 (Hotel/homestay Form C / Form-III automation)

## Verdict: beatable (low confidence; research cut short)

Only 3 of 6 searches returned usable results. One hit a usage limit, so complaint research is incomplete.

## Evidence
- Stayflexi is a general hotel PMS / booking engine / channel manager (flexible check-in/out model). Source: https://business.stayflexi.com/news/stayflexi-raised-1-6-million-to-provide-the-modern-os-for-hospitality-and-fundamentally-change-the-painful-2-pm-to-11-am-hotel-booking-model-to-anytime (search-result title only; funding figure not verified further).
- Searches for Stayflexi plus Form C (English and Hindi) found no evidence of a built-in Form C / FRRO filing feature. Absence in search results is not proof it does not exist: unverified.
- A specialised rival, ECOBILLZ, is marketed for Form C automation (scan ID/visa, auto-fill, submit to FRRO): https://bwhotelier.com/article/as-part-of-govt-compliance-ecobillz-offers-c-form-automation-solution-for-hotels-to-manage-foreign-guests-458690
- Complaints: the review search (Capterra/G2/Play Store) failed on the usage limit. No complaints found or verified. Capterra pages exist (https://www.capterra.com/p/193259/Flex-PMS/pricing/) but their contents were not read.
- Momentum: unverified. No recent-activity data gathered.

## Pricing
- Per a SoftwareSuggest snippet (https://www.softwaresuggest.com/stayflexi/pricing): Starter $28/month, Pro $449/month, Flex $999/month, in USD. Unverified against the official site; no INR pricing found. The Pro and Flex tiers are far above what a homestay would pay.

## Fit gaps
- Form C / Form-III filing is not a documented feature (unverified).
- It is a full PMS, so a homestay owner wanting only Form C filing would have to adopt the whole suite.
- Pro and Flex pricing is out of reach for small homestays; Starter may be reachable.

## Opening
A cheap standalone Form C / Form-III filer for homestays and small hotels is not served by Stayflexi, but ECOBILLZ and other PMS vendors may be closer rivals. Check them before reviving C0421.
