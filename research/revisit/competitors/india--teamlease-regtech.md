# TeamLease RegTech (India, commercial)

Idea killed: C0426 payroll and labour-codes rollout (greytHR, Keka, Zoho Payroll, RazorpayX Payroll, TeamLease RegTech).

## Verdict: weak (as a competitor for small-buyer payroll/labour-codes tooling)

It is a real, active company, but it sells enterprise compliance outsourcing and SaaS. It is not a self-serve payroll product for small firms, so it barely overlaps with the small-buyer idea.

## Evidence
- Positioned as an "AI-enabled compliance SaaS" and the compliance business of TeamLease Services, partnering with "enterprises" (https://teamleaseregtech.com/services/labourcompliance/labour-law-compliance-services/).
- RegTrack: cloud compliance management tool on a database of 1,536 Acts and 69,233 compliances, with over 8,000 changes a year. Also offers compliance management and payroll outsourcing with a team of 175+ experts for 450+ clients (search summary of group.teamlease.com pages; the figures differ across pages, so treat as unverified).
- Another page claims 3,300+ entities and 25M compliance instances (https://group.teamlease.com/services/regulatory-compliance/). Counts are self-reported and inconsistent, so unverified.
- Active content on labour-codes transition challenges (https://teamleaseregtech.com/blogs/181/labour-codes-in-india-8-challenges-organisations-face-during-transition-and-implementation) and a whitepaper covered by CXOToday. It is actively marketing on the labour codes.
- Complaints: none found about the product or service from customers. Searches in English and Hindi returned only company marketing pages and Glassdoor employee reviews (3.7/5 from 26 reviews; mentions of long hours). Employee reviews are not product complaints. Two voxya.com complaint pages were for TeamLease Services Ltd (parent), and I did not read their contents.

## Pricing
Not published. Quote-based and aimed at enterprises (unverified; no price page found). It is unlikely to suit a micro or small buyer.

## Fit gaps
- Enterprise and multi-entity focus, with managed-service delivery. No evidence of a self-serve plan for small firms.
- No evidence of a low-cost payroll product for small employers.
- Hindi or regional-language product support: nothing found (unverified).

## Momentum
Active: current labour-codes content and marketing. No sign of shutdown or stagnation.

## Opening
Small employers (roughly under 100 staff) who cannot buy an enterprise managed-compliance service are left to the self-serve payroll tools, so any opening is against those, not TeamLease.
