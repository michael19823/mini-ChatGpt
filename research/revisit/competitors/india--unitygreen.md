# Unitygreen (India, consultant) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0423, SWM Rules 2026 bulk-waste-generator (EBWGR) compliance.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Four WebSearch calls (English and Hindi) found no company called Unitygreen or Unity Green working on EBWGR or bulk-waste compliance in India. It may be a generic or placeholder name from the earlier pass. Unverified either way.

## Evidence
- Searches for "Unitygreen", "Unity Green" and a Hindi spelling, combined with EBWGR, bulk waste generator, consultancy and reviews, returned no page about such a firm. No website, app, reviews or complaints were found.
- Complaints: none found. This is not evidence that the firm is good, only that it has no visible online presence.
- Real context found: the SWM Rules 2026 (published 28 Jan 2026) introduce Extended Bulk Waste Generator Responsibility. Bulk waste generators must obtain EBWGR certificates, with registration through the CPCB portal. Sources: https://www.cseindia.org/waste-management-by-the-bulk-waste-generators-bwgs-and-establishing-a-decentralized-waste-management-ecosystem-12600 and https://enviliance.com/regions/south-asia/in/report_15091
- Other visible consultants in the EPR space (general EPR, not shown to cover EBWGR): Corpseed, Enviraj, Agile Regulatory. Their EBWGR coverage and quality are unverified. See https://www.corpseed.com/knowledge-centre/top-10-epr-plastic-waste-consulting-companies-consultants-in-india
- Municipal agencies (e.g. Gurugram, Delhi MCD) are empanelling agencies and pushing registration of bulk waste generators. This points to a real need, but it is unrelated to Unitygreen.
