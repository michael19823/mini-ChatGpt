# Zoho Payroll (India)

Dropped idea: C0426 (payroll and labour-codes rollout). Searches used: 4 of 6.

## Verdict: strong

Zoho Payroll is a free-to-cheap, actively maintained product from a large vendor that already markets labour-code compliance. A small-buyer payroll entrant would struggle to beat it on price or features.

## Evidence
- Zoho's own FAQ and academy pages say the product is auto-updated for statutory changes as the new Labour Codes roll out (https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/payroll-faq/). This is vendor-claimed and unverified independently.
- Zoho publishes a 2026 payroll-software checklist for Indian businesses, which suggests active content and product investment (https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/academy/payroll-operations/14-point-checklist-choose-payroll-software-2026.html).
- Capterra India: 3.7/5 from 10 users. Praise for ease of use and tax management. One review was titled "Horrendous Customer Support" (1.0/5); another complaint cited slow, understaffed support (https://www.capterra.in/software/186299/zoho-payroll).
- G2 summaries: customization is limited for complex pay structures, and support response time could be faster (https://www.g2.com/products/zoho-payroll/reviews). The search summary gave no verbatim quotes, so I quote none.
- Reddit and Hindi-language searches found nothing specific to Zoho Payroll. The Hindi query returned only generic Zoho community threads on support quality. No outage or failed-filing news was found.
- Momentum: active. No sign of acquisition, shutdown or stagnation.

## Pricing
Per Zoho's FAQ: a free plan for up to 10 employees. Standard is Rs 1,000/month for 25 employees (Rs 40 per extra employee). Professional is Rs 3,000/month for 50 employees (Rs 60 per extra). Premium is Rs 4,000/month for 50 employees (Rs 80 per extra). A reseller summary quoted Rs 40-50 and Rs 60-75 per employee per month for Standard and Professional, depending on billing cycle. Zoho reportedly does not strictly enforce the minimums. This is not overpriced for small buyers. Prices should be re-checked on the live page.

## Fit gaps
- Limited customization for complex pay structures (G2, summary-level).
- Thin and slow customer support (Capterra, G2).
- Whether Zoho handles state-specific labour-code rules as they are notified is unverified.
- No evidence found of a gap in language, mobile use or filing formats.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: hands-on support and complex or state-specific labour-code configuration for firms that find Zoho's support slow. It is not a price opening.
