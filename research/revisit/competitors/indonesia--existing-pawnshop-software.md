# Indonesia: existing pawnshop software (idea C1269, private pawnshops under OJK)

## Verdict: weak

5 WebSearch calls were used. No named vendor of pawnshop management software for Indonesian private gadai companies turned up, so there is no identifiable product to evaluate. This is closer to not-a-real-competitor, but the weak verdict is kept because OJK-licensed firms must already run some internal system (unverified which) and vendors may exist that web search did not surface.

## Evidence
- No vendor, product page, pricing page or software review for Indonesian pawnshop back-office software appeared in any of the Indonesian or English queries. Results were all consumer pawn apps, regulator pages, news and academic papers.
- The only digital product in the results is Pegadaian's own consumer app (Pegadaian Digital Service, now Tring!). It is not sold to other pawnshops, so it is not a competitor. One source says its Google Play rating is 2.8 and reports login and transaction failures. This is a consumer complaint about a state-owned lender's app, not about B2B software. Source: https://journals.unpad.ac.id/sawala/article/download/49512/pdf (academic paper, secondhand).
- Market: as of Nov 2024, 184 OJK-licensed pawn companies, 183 of them private. They hold only 3.04% of pawn lending. Source: https://finansial.bisnis.com/read/20250205/89/1837119/pangsa-pasar-gadai-swasta-masih-mini-cuma-3-dari-total-pinjaman-industri (figures as summarised in the search result).
- Bisnis.com says private players need to adapt digitally, with data security, regulatory compliance and integrated, accurate collateral valuation. Source: https://finansial.bisnis.com/read/20251011/89/1918761/gadai-swasta-perlu-dorong-adaptasi-digital-untuk-saingi-dominasi-pegadaian
- Regulatory pull: POJK 39/2024 on pawn businesses covers licensing, certified appraisers, loan quality assessment and lending limits. Pawn companies with fiduciary-collateral financing must become SLIK reporters by Dec 2025 at the latest. Sources: https://finansial.bisnis.com/read/20250304/89/1845022/perusahaan-gadai-dan-pembiayaan-koperasi-umkm-wajib-jadi-pelapor-slik and the Bisnis.com article above. SLIK reporting needs a data pipeline, so the need for software is plausible.
- Complaints about existing pawnshop software: none found. Absence of evidence only; search is weak for B2B vendors.
- Momentum: the sector is growing, with new licences still being granted in 2026 (e.g. https://finansial.bisnis.com/read/20260911/89/2003410/ojk-beri-izin-pt-gadai-elektronik-jakarta-perluas-wilayah-usaha-jadi-nasional). Nothing was found on any vendor's own momentum.

## Pricing
Unknown. No published price, minimum or subscription found for any pawnshop system. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
Cannot be assessed without a named product. Possible gaps, all unverified: SLIK and OJK report formats, sharia (syariah) pawn products, certified-appraiser workflows, multi-branch support, and use by very small firms. The tiny addressable market (about 184 firms) is a risk in itself.

## Opening
The opening is a low-cost, OJK/SLIK-compliant pawn back-office tool for small private gadai firms, but the buyer pool is only about 184 licensed companies and the incumbent landscape is unverified. Interview a few operators before committing.
