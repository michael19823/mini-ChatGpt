# Klikpajak (Mekari), Indonesia

Dropped idea: C0431 (Coretax wrappers). Researched 2026-10-06 with 5 WebSearch calls.

## Verdict: strong

It is an official DJP-registered PJAP (tax application service provider). It says it has fully integrated Coretax. It is backed by Mekari, which also owns Jurnal. It has published pricing and a clear product ladder, and it is actively marketing Coretax content.

## Evidence
- Klikpajak describes itself as a DJP-registered PJAP that has fully adopted and integrated Coretax since 1 Jan 2025 (https://klikpajak.id/blog/pjap-era-coretax/).
- Features listed: e-Faktur API, e-Bupot API, e-Billing API and online SPT reporting. It claims ISO 27001 (https://klikpajak.id/aplikasi-pajak-online/).
- It runs a blog post on Coretax errors (https://klikpajak.id/blog/solusi-coretax-error), so it is actively positioned as the fix for Coretax pain.
- It is integrated with Mekari Jurnal accounting (https://www.jurnal.id/id/blog/kepatuhan-pajak-dan-solusi-menyeluruh-dari-klikpajak-by-mekari/).
- Coretax itself drew heavy complaints: errors, slowness, login and OTP problems, and legacy data not recognised (CNBC Indonesia, Kontan, Hukumonline, found in search). This shows demand for a wrapper, but it is also the pain Klikpajak already targets.
- Complaints about Klikpajak itself: none found. The Capterra page shows 0 reviews. The searches did not surface app-store, Reddit, Kaskus or Trustpilot reviews. This does not prove there are none, only that these searches did not find any.

## Pricing (per month, from a search summary of klikpajak.id/harga; I could not open the page, so treat as unverified)
- Starter: Rp250,000, with up to 100 e-Faktur and 100 e-Bupot uploads, 3 users, unlimited multi-NPWP.
- Pro: Rp500,000 (Rp450,000 with a 10% discount), with 300 uploads of each, 5 users, and reconciliation and API add-ons.
- Enterprise: Rp999,000 (Rp899,100 with a 10% discount), with 750 uploads of each and 10 users.
- Monthly quotas are tight for a high-volume small business. The entry price is likely steep for micro-SMEs and freelancers. This is an inference, not a sourced fact.

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified or inferred)
- Priced and packaged for companies, with a quota per upload type and a user cap. It looks less suited to individuals, micro-SMEs, or very low-cost per-filing needs.
- No evidence found of offline or mobile-first use.
- No complaints found that name a specific missing feature.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: a much cheaper or pay-per-filing tool for micro-SMEs and individuals, or a niche workflow. A general Coretax wrapper competes head-on with an incumbent that already holds the official PJAP status.
