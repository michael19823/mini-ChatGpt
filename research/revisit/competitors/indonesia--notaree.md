# Notaree (Indonesia)

Idea it killed: C0429, Notary/PPAT monthly multi-agency reports.
Searches used: 4 of 6 (English and Indonesian).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

No evidence that a product called "Notaree" exists for Indonesian notaries or PPAT. The name is probably a misspelling or a placeholder. It could also be a very small, undocumented product.

## Evidence
- Searches "Notaree aplikasi notaris", "Notaree notary software Indonesia", "Notaree notaris PPAT laporan bulanan software review" and "notaree.id OR notaree Google Play app notary" returned nothing about a product named Notaree.
- Results for the Indonesian queries were academic papers on cyber notary and custom-built notary information systems. Sources: https://jurnal.umb.ac.id/index.php/JSAI/article/download/8634/5198/35653 and https://jtsiskom.undip.ac.id/article/view/12676
- The nearest name match is "Noturee", a US notary marketplace app (https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.noturee). It is unrelated to Indonesia and to reporting.
- A real adjacent product exists: Sinotis PPAT, sold on Tokopedia, which says it generates monthly PPAT reports (https://www.tokopedia.com/alumnisoftware22/sinotis-ppat-software-notaris-ppat-edition-v2). It is a separate listing in the batch, not Notaree.
- Demand signal for the idea: the tax authority (DJP) says some notaries and PPAT still do not report monthly to the KPP. The reports are due by the 10th of the following month, and some KPP accept spreadsheets by email (https://ikpi.or.id/en/djp-temukan-masih-ada-notaris-dan-ppat-belum-rutin-lapor-bulanan-ke-kpp/).
