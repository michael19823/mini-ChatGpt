Only 1 of 6 allowed WebSearch calls returned results. The other 4 calls I made failed with "You've hit your usage limit". The verdict rests on that single search plus the fact that the caller had no URL for Notarius. Re-run this check if more certainty is needed.

## Evidence
- I found no product, company, app-store listing or website named "Notarius" serving Indonesian notaries. I could not confirm that it exists as an Indonesian product (unverified). The only query that returned results was in Indonesian (notaris laporan bulanan wasiat daftar protes SABH AHU PPAT BPN pajak aplikasi keluhan), and it returned no mention of "Notarius".
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints about Notarius surfaced. Absence here is not evidence that the product is good, because the searches were too few.
- The search did surface the state of the underlying problem:
  - Notaries must report wills (Daftar Pusat Wasiat) monthly, including nil reports, online via the AHU application. Many still file manually and late. Source: https://ejournal.wiraraja.ac.id/index.php/SNAPP/article/download/3168/1907/ (as summarised by the search tool; I did not open it).
  - PPAT monthly reports go to the tax office in the PPAT's jurisdiction. The e-PHTB application is accessed through pajak.go.id. Sources: https://www.pajak.go.id/en/node/110896 and https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/berita/pajak-bengkulu-dua-gandeng-notaris-optimalkan-penerimaan-pajak
  - A regional rule (a Cirebon regency regulation, https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/230371/PERBUP%2020%202019%20KAB.CIREBON.pdf) lets the regional revenue head temporarily deactivate a PPAT's booking menu for missing monthly reports, with a Rp1,000,000 fine per month. I did not verify the details beyond the search summary.
  - These filings go to separate government portals, which suggests the pain is real and handled by the government systems themselves. No third-party aggregator showed up.
