# OnlinePajak (Indonesia) - competitor check

Killed idea: C0431 (Coretax wrappers). Searches used: 5 of 6.

## Verdict: strong

OnlinePajak is an established, DJP-authorised tax service provider (PJAP) that already ships Coretax-integrated e-Faktur and e-Bupot, so a Coretax wrapper is not an open gap. I found no customer complaints about OnlinePajak itself.

## Evidence
- Company: developed by PT Achilles Systems since September 2014; one source says it has over 700,000 users (dailysocial.id; the figure is unverified and may be stale). https://dailysocial.id/post/onlinepajak-helps-smes-tax-management ; https://rencanamu.id/perusahaan/onlinepajak
- Product: its own blog announces "e-Faktur Coretax Tersedia di OnlinePajak" and describes e-Bupot Coretax for PPh 4(2), 15, 22, 23 and 26, plus e-Faktur. https://www.online-pajak.com/tentang-efaktur-ppn/e-faktur-coretax-tersedia-di-onlinepajak-temukan-solusi-mudah-kelola-pajak-bisnis-anda/
- Momentum: it is actively shipping Coretax features (2025-2026), and it has an Android app (OnlinePajak Mobile) per search summaries. No sign of shutdown or stagnation.
- Complaints: none found about OnlinePajak specifically. No Google Play, App Store, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews turned up in the results (this does not mean none exist). The complaints I found are about the government Coretax system itself (login failures, slow access, Error 404/502, deadline-time outages), e.g. https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/satu-bulan-meluncur-megaproyek-coretax-masih-banjir-keluhan ; https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1818923/wp-ramai-keluhkan-gagal-akses-coretax-error-404-djp-jawab-begini . That pain also supports a wrapper product, but it is not a complaint about OnlinePajak.

## Pricing
- Not verified for OnlinePajak. Search did not return its price list.
- A "Starter Rp250.000/bulan" plan (100 e-Faktur, 3 users, Coretax access) appeared at https://klikpajak.id/harga . That is Klik Pajak, a different competitor, not OnlinePajak. It is only a market reference for the price band.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed from sources. Unverified guesses: it targets businesses, so it may be heavy or costly for individual taxpayers and micro-sellers, and may be weak on individual SPT help.

## Opening
Little room against OnlinePajak in business e-Faktur/e-Bupot; any opening is at the individual or micro-seller end, and that needs a pricing and review check (Play Store, Reddit) that these searches did not cover.
