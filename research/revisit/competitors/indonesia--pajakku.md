# Pajakku (Indonesia) - competitor check

Killed idea: C0431 Coretax wrappers. Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Indonesian).

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Pajakku (PT Mitra Pajakku) describes itself as a DJP-appointed PJAP (Penyedia Jasa Aplikasi Perpajakan) since 2005, with ISO 9001, ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 20000-1. These are its own claims, from its own site. Source: https://pajakku.com/artikel/tak-cuma-coretax-ini-aplikasi-pajak-resmi-djp-yang-terjamin-aman
- It positions itself as a layer that connects to Coretax rather than replacing it. Source: https://pajakku.com/artikel/perbedaan-coretax-dan-pjap-dalam-kelola-pajak-perusahaan-anda
- Products listed in search summaries: e-Faktur, e-Bupot, e-Billing, NPWP verification, e-Meterai. Source: https://pajakku.com/artikel/rekomendasi-software-pajak-djp-terintegrasi-coretax
- DJP says invoices issued through PJAP channels are compiled in the eFaktur menu in Coretax (via a Bisnis.com report on DJP sync). Source: https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20250212/259/1839034/penerbitan-faktur-lewat-coretax-dan-e-faktur-ditjen-pajak-sinkronisasi-data-setiap-hari
- Complaints: none found about Pajakku itself. The only review-like summary (a Gartner Peer Insights page appeared in results, https://www.gartner.com/reviews/product/pajakku, content not read) said it follows DJP rules closely and has responsive support, though slower at peak filing periods. Treat that as unverified paraphrase. No Play Store, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews surfaced.
- Market context: Coretax itself drew many complaints (server errors, invoice issuance blocked, DJP admitted 22 problems), e.g. https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20250204/259/1836552/sistem-pajak-coretax-terus-bermasalah-pengusaha-ritel-minta-kejelasan . That is pain with Coretax, and PJAPs are the officially sanctioned workaround, which helps Pajakku.
- Momentum: active content and Coretax-integration marketing in 2025-26. No sign of shutdown or acquisition. Other PJAPs (OnlinePajak, Klikpajak, Pajak.io) compete in the same space.

## Pricing
Not found. One search returned unrelated results. Pricing is unverified; I did not see a public price list or minimums.

## Fit gaps
- None verified. Focus appears to be companies and enterprise-style needs; whether it serves small/UMKM buyers well is unverified. Note that e-Faktur is not mandatory for UMKM under the 4.8 billion turnover threshold, per a search summary of an academic source (unverified).

## Opening
A "Coretax wrapper" is a thing that DJP-certified PJAPs like Pajakku already legally provide with an 20-year head start; any opening is only a cheap, simple UMKM-focused UX layer, and that is unproven.
