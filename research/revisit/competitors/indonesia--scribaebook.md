# ScribaeBook (Indonesia) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0429 Notary/PPAT monthly multi-agency reports.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

## Evidence
- 3 of 6 searches used (English and Indonesian). None found a product called "ScribaeBook" for Indonesian notaries/PPAT. No website, app-store listing or reviews found. Existence: unverified.
- The only near-name hits were unrelated "Scriba" products (Capterra listing, an iOS note app); none tied to Indonesia or notaries. Not verified as related.
- Real adjacent tools found:
  - Sinotis PPAT Software Edition v2, sold on Tokopedia; described as generating monthly PPAT reports (https://www.tokopedia.com/alumnisoftware22/sinotis-ppat-software-notaris-ppat-edition-v2). This is a more plausible incumbent for the idea.
  - Government channels: e-PHTB on pajak.go.id (tax) and the Kemenkumham APN reporting web app (http://apn.kemenkumhamjatim.com).
- Complaints: none found (nothing to review for ScribaeBook).
- Momentum: unknown/unverified.

## Pricing
ScribaeBook: none found. For reference, Sinotis PPAT v2 listed at Rp650,000 (Tokopedia listing; one-off vs subscription not verified).

## Fit gaps
Cannot assess ScribaeBook. Sinotis appears to be a desktop/one-off product (unverified); whether it handles multi-agency reports (BPN, DJP, Kemenkumham/MPD) was not confirmed.

## Opening
No evidence ScribaeBook blocks the idea; the real check should be against Sinotis and the government portals (e-PHTB, APN).
