# Indonesia: SIMRS / clinic software vendors (unnamed) — killed C0434 (SATUSEHAT integration for clinics)

Searches: 2 of 6 succeeded (one hit a usage limit); results are thin. Everything below is from search summaries only.

## Verdict: strong

Many commercial EMR/SIMRS vendors already bundle SATUSEHAT integration, and the Ministry publishes the integration route for them. The only open angle is a thin gap for tiny clinics.

## Evidence
- eClinic advertises full SATUSEHAT integration (listed on dealroom: https://app.dealroom.co/companies/eclinic).
- DIGMAN is described as connected to SATUSEHAT, HL7 FHIR compliant, and used by 400+ facilities (as reported in the search summary; unverified at source).
- Zi.Care offers data mapping, transformation and cleansing services for routing EMR data to SATUSEHAT (https://www.intersystems.com/sg/news/news/zi-care-partners-with-intersystems-to-accelerate-digital-healthcare-deployment-in-indonesia/).
- Kemenkes publishes how facilities and EMR providers integrate: https://www.kemkes.go.id/id/begini-cara-terintegrasi-ke-satusehat-bagi-fasyankes-dan-penyedia-rekam-medis-elektronik
- Complaints found were academic/policy rather than customer reviews: integration is complicated; as of 19 Oct 2023 only 565 of 44,081 facilities (1.28%) had integrated; vendors differ in data design, causing mismatches with Kepmenkes HK.01.07/MENKES/1423/2022; frontline staff are overwhelmed by data entry (sources: https://www.ejurnalmalahayati.ac.id/index.php/manuju/article/download/19624/Download%20Artikel, https://kebijakankesehatanindonesia.net/arsip-pengantar/23-agenda/4930-kolaborasi-multisektoral-dalam-integrasi-layanan-kesehatan-di-indonesia). Those figures are dated 2023 and may be outdated.
- No app-store, G2, Reddit or forum customer complaints about specific vendors were found.

## Pricing
Not published in anything found; vendors work on subscriptions/quotes. Unverified. A 2026 blog post on free vs paid EMR exists (https://legawa.com/2026/07/10/emr-gratis-atau-berbayar-kerangka-keputusan-untuk-manajer-rumah-sakit-dan-klinik/) but I did not read its contents.

## Fit gaps
- Vendor inconsistency in data variables/metadata versus the Ministry standard (from the sources above).
- Low adoption suggests small clinics find integration hard; unclear whether the cause is vendor products or clinic readiness.
- Gaps for a tiny clinic's price or workflow: unverified.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: a very cheap, simple SATUSEHAT-ready layer for tiny clinics, if vendor pricing proves prohibitive. That is unconfirmed, and the integration is government-defined and widely offered.
