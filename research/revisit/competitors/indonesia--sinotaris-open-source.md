# Sinotaris (open source) - Indonesia

Idea killed: C0429, Notary/PPAT monthly multi-agency reports.

## Verdict: weak

A small, commercial, one-off-purchase desktop-style product (marketed as "Sinotis PPAT Software, Notaris PPAT Edition v2") on Tokopedia. It is not open source as far as I could verify. It is a minor tool, not an entrenched competitor.

## Evidence
- Name appears as "Sinotis" in the only listing found, not "Sinotaris". The match to the dropped competitor is probable but unverified. Source: https://www.tokopedia.com/alumnisoftware22/sinotis-ppat-software-notaris-ppat-edition-v2
- Per search summaries of that listing, it records client orders, manages files, generates letters and deeds, tracks file delivery to other institutions, and produces a monthly PPAT report. Same source.
- Search summaries said no customer reviews existed on the Tokopedia listing. I did not open the page, so this is unverified.
- Search results found no complaints, app-store reviews, forum threads, outage news or open-source repository. Finding none is a valid result. Two searches were run (Indonesian), covering monthly reports and complaints. The remaining 4 searches were not used because the early results showed little presence.
- Non-product results were mostly academic theses and journals on notary/PPAT administration apps, which suggests the need is real but fragmented. Examples: https://jurnal.umb.ac.id/index.php/JSAI/article/download/8634/5198/35653 and https://jptam.org/index.php/jptam/article/download/8925/7288/16744 (not read in detail, unverified).

## Pricing
- Rp650,000 on Tokopedia, per search summary of the listing. Reportedly includes a manual by email and no training or implementation (unverified, snippet only). It is cheap and one-time, so not overpriced for small buyers.

## Fit gaps (inferred from the listing description, partly unverified)
- Reports only the monthly PPAT report. No sign of multi-agency submission (tax office, land office/BPN, other agencies) or online filing integration.
- Appears to be an order-tracking and letter-template tool, not a cloud or mobile service.
- No evidence of active development, versions beyond "v2", or support.

## Opening
A cloud tool that compiles and files the notary/PPAT monthly reports across agencies is not covered by this cheap order-tracking template product.
