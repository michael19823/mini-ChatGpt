# Sinotis Fusion (Indonesia) - competitor to C0429 (Notary/PPAT monthly multi-agency reports)

## Verdict: weak

## Evidence
- No product named "Sinotis Fusion" was found. An exact-phrase search returned only unrelated results (e.g. Synopsys Fusion Compiler). Whether a "Fusion" edition exists is unverified.
- The closest real product is "Sinotis PPAT Software - Notaris PPAT Edition v2", sold as a software listing on Tokopedia by the seller "alumnisoftware22": https://www.tokopedia.com/alumnisoftware22/sinotis-ppat-software-notaris-ppat-edition-v2
- Per the listing, it records client orders, manages files, generates letters and deeds, tracks file delivery to other institutions, and produces a PPAT monthly report. Document templates include cover letter, certificate-check letter, power of attorney and others.
- Nothing found indicates multi-agency submission (BPN, tax office, INI, Kemenkumham/SIEMON) or e-filing. It appears to be a desktop/document tool producing a report, not a filing integration. This is inferred from the limited listing text, so treat it as unverified.
- Momentum: no website, news, vendor updates or company info found. It looks like a small reseller listing, stagnant or unknown.
- Complaints: none found (app stores, forums, Reddit, news). No reviews were located; this is not evidence of satisfaction.
- Context: the monthly PPAT report to the tax office is a real obligation (https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/berita/pajak-bengkulu-dua-gandeng-notaris-optimalkan-penerimaan-pajak), and tax e-PHTB exists as a government portal (https://pajak.go.id/id/berita/laksanakan-sosialisasi-pajak-purwodadi-ajak-ippat-implementasi-e-phtb). These are government systems, not competitors.

## Pricing
- Rp650,000 on the Tokopedia listing (single price, manual delivered by email, no training or implementation included). Not verified whether it is a one-time or recurring price. Cheap, so price is not a gap.

## Fit gaps
- Appears not to handle multi-agency report formats or direct submission (unverified).
- No evidence of cloud, mobile, multi-user or update support (unverified).
- Sold via a marketplace listing, with no visible support or compliance updates when regulations change.

## Opening
A cloud tool that auto-generates and tracks monthly reports across BPN, tax office and Kemenkumham for small notary/PPAT offices faces no visible real competitor here.
