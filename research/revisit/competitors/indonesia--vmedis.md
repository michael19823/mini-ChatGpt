# VMedis (Indonesia) - competitor check

Idea(s) it killed: C0430 SIPNAP pharmacy narcotics/psychotropics report.

## Verdict: beatable (low confidence)

VMedis is a real, small, commercial pharmacy-software vendor. Searches found nothing showing it does SIPNAP filing. They also found no complaints and no pricing. One of the 4 searches hit a usage limit and returned nothing, so coverage is thin.

## Evidence
- Search snippets describe VMedis as pharmacy software for sales, purchasing, drug inventory and finance, with stock-out alerts. The vendor is said to be based in Surabaya, founded 2010, with 11-50 employees. Source: sgpgrid.com/company/vmedis-ID0000 (directory listing, secondary source). Details are unverified against the vendor's own site.
- A search for "VMedis SIPNAP" returned only generic SIPNAP papers and no VMedis page. Unverified whether VMedis has a SIPNAP export or report feature.
- The SIPNAP papers returned say narcotics reporting is monthly, due by the 10th of the following month. One study found about 29.87% of sampled pharmacies reported via SIPNAP, and 85.42% of the non-reporters said they did not understand how to make and send the report (a snippet from search results, source paper not opened). That suggests an unmet need.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, forum or news results about VMedis surfaced.
- Momentum: unknown. No news, release or acquisition information found.

## Pricing
Not found (unverified). A search for "harga paket" returned nothing about VMedis.

## Fit gaps
- Unverified: no evidence of a SIPNAP-format report or upload.
- Product appears to be general pharmacy ERP/POS, not a narcotics-reporting specialist.
- Small vendor, so likely limited reach beyond Java.

## Opening
A standalone or add-on tool that turns narcotics and psychotropic stock movements into a SIPNAP-ready monthly report, aimed at small pharmacies that don't understand the process, looks open. First confirm whether VMedis already exports to SIPNAP.
