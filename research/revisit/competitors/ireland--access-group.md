# Access Group (Ireland) - competitor check for C0447 (Home support HIQA readiness)

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Access Group has an Irish site and an Ireland-specific home care page covering scheduling, care planning and electronic care monitoring. Source: https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-ie/health-social-care/sectors/home-care/
- Access Group has a partnership with HCCI (Home & Community Care Ireland), the national body for home care providers, so it has a channel into the exact buyer. Source: https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/health-social-care/care-management-software/care-partners/hcci/
- It already markets HIQA-aligned products, mainly residential and nursing-home focused: Access Care Planning, Medication Management (eMAR) and Access Care Compliance (mock HIQA inspections, governance tracking). The home-support-specific coverage of the compliance module is unverified. Source: https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-ie/blog/hsc-hiqa-compliance-software-for-residential-care/
- It publishes content on Ireland's home support regulation, so it is tracking the Health (Amendment) (Home Support Providers) Act 2026 (signed 1 July 2026 per a third-party source, learnsignal.com, not independently verified). Source: https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-ie/blog/hsc-irelands-home-support-regulation-and-the-ai-opportunity/
- Momentum: active (new Irish content, partnerships, multiple products).
- Complaints (search summary, group-wide and not Ireland- or home-support-specific; individual reviews not seen): slow or hard-to-reach customer support, features removed without clear justification, little online guidance on new systems. Ratings summarised as Trustpilot 4.6/5 (4,700+ reviews, group-wide) and G2 4.1/5 (350+). Sources: https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/theaccessgroup.com , https://www.g2.com/products/access-domiciliary-care-software
- No complaints found specific to Irish home support or HIQA readiness.

## Pricing
Not published; no price found in searches (unverified). Likely quote-based, bundled across modules.

## Fit gaps (likely, mostly unverified)
- Irish HIQA content is centred on nursing/residential homes; the home-support compliance workflow for newly regulated providers may be thin.
- Broad multi-module suite is probably heavy and costly for small home support providers (inference, unverified).
- Support responsiveness complaints (group-wide).

## Opening
A lightweight, low-cost HIQA-registration-readiness tool for small home support providers is possible, but Access's HCCI channel and existing HIQA products make it a hard incumbent to displace.
