# Applied Systems (Ireland)

Idea killed: C0452 (Consumer Protection Code 2025 for brokers). Searches used: 4 of 6 (English only; local-language/Irish-market queries returned mostly vendor pages).

## Verdict: strong

Applied Systems is the incumbent broker-management vendor in Ireland (a 40-year Irish presence), bought Relay Software in Ireland, and publishes its own Irish compliance content. A thin CPC 2025 add-on would compete with a vendor that already ships compliance updates inside the broker's system of record.

## Evidence
- Applied marks 40 years supporting Irish insurance (press release 2025): https://prod-rebrand.appliedsystems.com/en-uk/news/press-releases/2025/applied-systems-celebrates-40-years-of-supporting-irish-insurance
- Acquired Relay Software in Ireland (Insurance Age): https://insuranceage.co.uk/insurance-age/news/2467394/applied-systems-buys-relay-software-in-ireland
- Updated its Irish products (Motor, Home, Commercial Vehicle, eQuote) for the Consumer Insurance Contracts Act in summer 2021, so it has a track record of shipping regulatory updates: https://www1.appliedsystems.com/en-ie/blog/posts/compliance-confidence-irish-brokers/ (the page appears in results; the CICA details are from the search summary, and I did not open the page)
- Applied Epic launched for Northern Ireland in 2019: https://www1.appliedsystems.com/en-us/news/press-releases/2019/applied-systems-launches-applied-epic-for-the-northern-ireland-insurance-market/
- Momentum caveat: Insurance Age reports Applied is exiting Epic from the UK market (https://insuranceage.co.uk/insight/7956795/insurers-and-mgas-line-up-to-support-brokers-amid-applied-epic-exit). Whether this affects the Irish product is unverified. If it does, brokers there may be looking for alternatives.
- I did not find a specific CPC 2025 product from Applied in these searches (unverified either way).

## Complaints
- No Irish-specific complaints found (no Trustpilot, Reddit or forum hits).
- Capterra UK lists Applied Epic at 4.2/5 from 141 reviews, 80% recommend. Reviewers mention a learning curve, occasional performance issues on simple tasks, and cost and implementation concerns for smaller brokerages (summary from search; I did not read the page): https://www.capterra.co.uk/reviews/70671/applied-epic

## Pricing
- Capterra lists Applied Epic from GBP 1,000 (unit not stated, unverified); other pricing is by quote.
- A third-party blog (kolonell.com, a vendor with a commercial interest, unverified) puts broker management packages at EUR 150 to 400 per user per month: https://kolonell.com/en/blog/insurance-broker-software-cost-dublin-2026

## Fit gaps
- Cost and implementation burden for small brokerages (from the Capterra review summary).
- Core system of record; a lightweight, CPC-specific evidence layer (fact-find, suitability, CPD tracking) sitting on top of any broker system is not confirmed as covered.

## Opening
A small-broker, system-agnostic CPC 2025 evidence and workflow tool is possible, but only if it integrates with Applied rather than replacing it. Direct competition is hard.
