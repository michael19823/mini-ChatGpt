# BCR Comply (Ireland) - competitor check for C0451 (BCAR ancillary certificate collation)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Four WebSearch calls (English only; the Irish BCAR context is English-language) found no product called "BCR Comply". It could not be verified as an existing product, so there is no evidence it is a live competitor. "BCR" most likely stands for Building Control Regulations, but this is unverified.

## Evidence
- Searches for "BCR Comply BCAR Ireland software" and "BCR Comply building control regulations" returned only unrelated products (a Capterra "Comply" VAT tool, a Canadian bank-capital "BCAR") and generic UK/Irish building-control pages. No vendor page, app-store listing or review of "BCR Comply" appeared.
- Searches on BCAR ancillary certificate collation and certificate-management software found only service providers and public guidance. Catalyst Group (job posting, https://catalyst-group.factorialhr.com/job_posting/assigned-certifier-248011) says it uses its own obi(R) software for inspections and BCAR administration. This is an in-house tool of a certifier firm, not a standalone product (details unverified). Other results: NBCO presentation on collation of ancillary certificates (https://nbco.nbco.localgov.ie/sites/default/files/4567776/2025-10/Presentation%203%2020250923%20Dermot%20Mullally%20collation%20of%20Ancillary%20Certificates.pdf), Engineers Ireland page (https://www.engineersireland.ie/Professionals/News-Insights/Campaigns-and-policies/Building-Regulations-and-Ancillary-Certificates).
- Complaints: none found, because no product was found.
- Momentum: no evidence either way.

## Pricing
No published pricing found (unverified / nothing to report).

## Fit gaps
Not assessable, since the product could not be found. Separately, obi(R) appears to be tied to Catalyst's own certification work, so it is probably not offered to independent certifiers (unverified).

## Opening
Dropping C0451 because of "BCR Comply" is not supported by evidence; the only visible tooling is proprietary to certifier firms, leaving standalone ancillary-certificate collation open (a manual search of the Irish market for the exact name is still advisable).
