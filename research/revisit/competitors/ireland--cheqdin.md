# Cheqdin (Ireland) - dropped idea C0449: Childcare NCS/ECCE

## Verdict: strong

Cheqdin is a live commercial childcare management product with a dedicated NCS/ECCE feature set, a funding calculator and a very cheap tier including a free plan. Little room for a me-too product. 5 of 6 searches used.

## Evidence
- Cheqdin's own site has pages on NCS and ECCE compliance for Irish providers (https://cheqdin.com/childcare-software-for-ncs-and-ecce-compliance-reporting-in-ireland, https://cheqdin.com/how-cheqdin-can-help-irish-providers-stay-compliant-with-ncs-and-ecce). Claimed features (vendor marketing, unverified independently): digital attendance registers with timestamps, NCS subsidy tracking per child, live ratio dashboards.
- Has a support article "NCS ECCE funding calculator, attendance compliance tracking" (https://support.cheqdin.com/support/solutions/articles/48001264419-ncs-ecce-funding-calculator-attendance-compliance-tracking), so the feature is documented and shipped.
- Listed on Capterra, G2, GetApp, Software Advice and SaaSworthy (listing URLs surfaced in search; review content was not visible in the results).
- Another Irish competitor surfaced: CrecheHQ (Capterra listing), aimed at crèches with 15-150 children claiming ECCE/NCS, from EUR 49/month (per search snippet, unverified).

## Complaints
None found. Searches for reviews/complaints returned only an unrelated company (CHEQ, cheq.ai), so no Cheqdin-specific complaints are verified. Review text on Capterra/G2 was not seen; this is a gap, not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing (from search snippets of Capterra/G2 listings, unverified)
- Free "Rise" plan for 15 children; free trial.
- Lite about USD 12/month for 25 children (online registrations only); Prime about USD 33/month for 25 children (all features). Another source showed lower ranges (Lite 8-10, Prime 23-28). Scales with number of children.
- This is cheap for a small provider; overpricing is not an angle.

## Fit gaps
- No verified gaps found. Possible but unverified: prices quoted in USD suggest a multi-country product, so Irish-specific depth (direct Pobal/CCMS submission, Irish payroll, Tusla inspection forms) is unconfirmed.
- Lite tier is limited to online registrations; fuller features sit in Prime.

## Momentum
Active: recent blog and support content on NCS/ECCE and 2026 Capterra listing. No sign of shutdown or acquisition found.

## Opening
Little to none on core NCS/ECCE admin; only a niche deeper Irish integration (e.g. CCMS submission, staff rosters/qualification compliance) could differentiate, and that is unverified.
