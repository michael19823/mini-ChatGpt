# Ireland: compliance consultants (type: consultant, no URL known)

Killed idea: C0452, Consumer Protection Code 2025 for brokers.

## Verdict: beatable

Consultants are a real, service-based alternative, but they sell people-hours and training, not a product. Searches found no named software competitor. 4 of 6 WebSearch calls ran (3 were lost to a usage-limit error).

## Evidence
- The Central Bank of Ireland finalised the CPC on 24 March 2025. It takes effect on 24 March 2026. Sources: [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/ie/en/insights/consulting/consumer-protection-code-2025.html), [Maples](https://maples.com/knowledge/new-consumer-protection-code-recommended-steps-for-firms-to-comply). Today (Oct 2026) the code is already in force.
- Large firms offer CPC 2025 advisory content: KPMG, PwC, Simmons & Simmons, Maples ([PwC](https://www.pwc.ie/services/audit-assurance/insights/consumer-protection-code-what-firms-must-prioritise.html), [Simmons & Simmons](https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/cmkb0i21706bcvlz09vjk21fe/consumer-protection-code-2025)). These are thought-leadership pages. I did not verify any fixed-price broker product.
- RegSol runs a "Broker Bootcamp: Compliance" training covering CPC 2025, vulnerable customers, digitalisation and AML ([listing](https://happeningnext.com/event/broker-bootcamp-compliance-eid1ef0l1hk8pra)).
- A Mason Hayes & Curran survey (330+ professionals) found 31% of Irish financial firms not ready for the revised code. 44% named digital compliance as the top challenge ([Law Society Gazette](https://www.lawsociety.ie/gazette/top-stories/2025/july/one-third-of-finance-firms-not-ready-for-code/), [Funds Europe](https://funds-europe.com/irish-financial-firms-struggle-with-consumer-code-implementation/)). This is a demand signal, not a complaint about consultants.
- Complaints: none found. No reviews of consultants (Google, Trustpilot, Reddit, forums) surfaced. No Irish-language search was run, since the topic is English-language regulation, and no reviews were checked directly.

## Pricing
- RegSol Broker Bootcamp: EUR 549 per person (from the listing above).
- Consultancy day rates and retainers: unverified, not published in the results.

## Fit gaps
- Training and advice, not ongoing tooling: no evident self-serve gap analysis, policy templates, or evidence/audit tracking for a small broker (inference, unverified).
- Big-firm advisory is likely priced for large regulated entities (unverified).
- Small brokers lack dedicated compliance resources (per the survey commentary).

## Momentum
Active: the CPC 2025 drove a wave of consultant content and training in 2025.

## Opening
A low-cost, self-serve CPC 2025 toolkit (gap assessment, policies, evidence log) for small brokers who cannot afford a big-firm retainer, with consultants as possible channel partners.
