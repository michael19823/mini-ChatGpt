# CrecheHQ (Ireland) - competitor check for C0449 (Childcare NCS/ECCE)

## Verdict: strong (on fit); beatable only on reach and maturity

CrecheHQ is purpose-built for exactly the dropped idea: Irish crèche billing with ECCE and NCS deductions. It leaves little functional gap. The one caveat is that it looks young and unproven (no reviews found). Based on 3 of the 6 allowed searches, so evidence is thin.

## Evidence
- Capterra listing describes it as childcare software for Irish crèches with ECCE and NCS billing, live room ratios, kiosk check-in, rosters and parent messaging. Source: https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1240886/CrecheHQ
- Target user per listing: owner-managers of Irish crèches, community/private preschools and afterschool services with 15 to 150 children, especially those claiming ECCE/NCS via the Early Years Hive. Same source.
- Handles ECCE and NCS scheme deductions on each invoice and imports from Early Years Hive with registration matching (per search summary of the same Capterra page).
- Cloud/SaaS, Android app, English only, available in Ireland only. Integrates with Stripe and WhatsApp. Support via email, FAQs, phone. Same source.
- Complaints: none found. Capterra shows no verified user reviews. No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or boards.ie complaints about CrecheHQ itself surfaced (boards.ie results were returned but not confirmed to mention it; unverified).
- Sector context (not CrecheHQ-specific): providers complain the Hive portal is "not fit for purpose" and NCS admin is cumbersome. Source: search result listing, e.g. https://www.anglocelt.ie/?p=94745 (unverified detail). This supports demand for tooling but also means the Hive pain is what CrecheHQ already targets.
- Another Irish rival exists: Cheqdin, https://cheqdin.com/childcare-software-for-ncs-and-ecce-compliance-reporting-in-ireland (market is not empty).
- Momentum: could not determine launch date, funding, or release cadence. Unverified. No Irish-language search was run (limited to English; 3 of 6 searches used).

## Pricing
- Starts at EUR 49.00, usage-based, free trial (Capterra listing). Exact tiers and per-child rates not found. Unverified beyond the starting price.
- Low entry price suits small crèches; not overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps
- Ireland only, English only (no Irish-language UI).
- Android listed; iOS not mentioned (unverified).
- Zero public reviews: unproven reliability, no social proof.
- Targets 15 to 150 children; very small childminders or large chains may be less served (inference, unverified).
- No evidence about offline use or multi-site/multi-entity support (unverified).

## Opening
Little functional room on ECCE/NCS billing itself; the only opening is trust and distribution (no reviews, apparent young product), so a rival would need to win on a differentiated angle (e.g. Hive automation depth, multi-site, childminders) rather than on the core idea.
