# Ireland: HR vendors (unnamed) - idea C0455 Pay transparency

## Verdict: beatable
(Low confidence: only 2 of 6 searches ran. The 3rd hit a usage limit, the rest were not run. No Irish-language or review-site searches were done.)

## Evidence
- Ireland missed the 7 June 2026 transposition deadline for the EU Pay Transparency Directive. Implementation is phased, and employers will not be penalised for missing elements in June 2026. A separate Pay Transparency Bill is planned and not yet drafted. Sources: https://www.mccannfitzgerald.com/knowledge/employment/eu-pay-transparency-directive-ireland-confirms-it-will-not-meet-7-june-2026-transposition-deadline ; https://www.williamfry.com/knowledge/ireland-postpones-pay-transparency-whats-next/
- Gender pay gap reporting threshold lowered from 150 to 50 employees. A Gender Pay Gap Portal is expected for the November 2026 cycle (per search summary; verify at source). Source: https://global.lockton.com/us/en/news-insights/ireland-updates-gender-pay-gap-reporting-regulations
- A survey reports only 4% of Irish SMEs fully prepared. Source: https://itbrief.co.uk/story/irish-smes-unprepared-for-eu-pay-transparency-rules
- HR vendors found publishing pay-transparency guides: BrightHR IE (https://www.brighthr.com/ie/blog/industry-news/eu-pay-transparency-directive-a-guide-for-employers-in-ireland/), Zellis (https://www.zellis.com/resources/blog/ireland-misses-eu-pay-transparency-deadline/), HRlocker (https://www.hrlocker.com/blog/pay-transparency/), Payslip (https://payslip.com/resources/blog/eu-pay-transparency-directive-what-it-means-for-ireland). These are blog content; whether each has a dedicated pay-transparency product is unverified.
- Complaints: none found (reviews were not searched before the limit hit). Not evidence of absence.

Not found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Law is not fully transposed, so vendors are mostly selling guidance and general HR suites, not a finished compliance product (inference, unverified).
- Full HR suites are likely heavier than a small employer needs (unverified).

## Opening
A lightweight pay-range, job-ad and pay-gap-report tool for 50-250 employee Irish firms is plausible, but the legal spec is still unsettled and vendors are already marketing to the topic.
