# Rentalize (Ireland) - competitor check for C0442 (RPZ/RTB compliance desk for letting agents)

## Verdict: strong

Rentalize is an active Irish/UK property-management platform that already automates RTB registration and RPZ calculations, and it serves letting agents directly. No complaints were found, but only because it has almost no public reviews.

## Evidence
- Described as property management software for the Irish and UK rental markets. It automates RTB registration, Rent Pressure Zone calculations and Residential Tenancies Act compliance. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/10048576/Rentalize/ and https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/rentalize/ (search-result summaries; page text not opened).
- Three products (per search summaries): Select (housing bodies, AI applicant scoring), Core (portfolios of 50 to 2,000 units), 360 (mobile-first app for smaller portfolios with digital leases and e-signatures). Targets landlords, letting agents, property managers, local authorities and approved housing bodies.
- Claims over 50,000 units managed and paying customers, including Meath County Council (self-reported, unverified).
- Complaints: none found. Capterra and GetApp list no reviews (https://www.capterra.ie/software/1103956/Rentalize, https://www.getapp.ie/software/2102032/rentalize). No Reddit, app-store or forum complaints surfaced in 3 searches. This is absence of evidence, not evidence of quality.
- Momentum: active. It describes an "August 2026" launch with a waiting list and a 50% list-price rise at launch, so it is in a growth or launch phase.
- Searches used: 3 of 6, English only. No Irish-language queries were run, and Irish-language results are unlikely to matter here.

## Pricing
- Listed starting price EUR 15.00, usage-based (Capterra/GetApp summary). Waiting-list users keep their rate; list prices rise 50% at the August 2026 launch (so roughly EUR 22.50 starting, unverified arithmetic). Full tier details were not found.
- This is cheap and in line with TenantSync (EUR 20/month for 10 properties). It is not overpriced for small landlords.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. It already covers RTB registration, RPZ and agents.
- Possible gaps (unverified, inferred): it is a broad property-management suite rather than a dedicated compliance desk. It has no apparent human or advisory service, and its enterprise and housing-body focus may thin out support for small agents. Its newness means little track record.

## Opening
A thin one: only a human-assisted, compliance-only service (RTB filings and RPZ checks done for agents) next to a cheap software suite. A software-only competitor would not have a gap here.
