# TenantSync (Ireland) - competitor check for C0442 (RPZ/RTB compliance desk for letting agents)

## Verdict: beatable

TenantSync is a real, Irish-specific product, but it is a self-serve landlord tool with no public user reviews. It does not serve the "compliance desk for letting agents" service angle.

## Evidence
- Product listed on Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice (Ireland and international sites). The listings describe: PSD2 Open Banking to AIB, Bank of Ireland and Revolut for rent matching, pre-drafted RTB Form 1s, Part 4 date tracking, RPZ caps (HICP formula), compliance reminders, Daft/MyHome enquiry handling. Source: https://www.capterra.ie/software/1079790/TenantSync , https://www.getapp.ie/software/2082763/tenantsync
- Review count: the listings showed 0 verified user reviews (as of April 2026, per search summary). Source: https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/tenantsync/
- Complaints: none found. Searches (English) returned no Reddit, forum, Trustpilot or news items about TenantSync. The Irish-language search was not run (usage limit); 3 of the 6 searches were used successfully, and 2 were blocked by the limit. Absence of complaints is not evidence of satisfaction, because the product has almost no public footprint.
- Momentum: listed in 2026 on Capterra ("Pricing, Alternatives & More 2026"). Release cadence, funding and company details are unverified.

## Pricing
- Search summary reports roughly EUR 20-29/month for 10-15 properties, up to EUR 99-149/month for 50-100 properties. The earlier research note recorded EUR 20/month (10 properties) to EUR 199/month (200). Exact tiers not verified against the vendor site; treat as unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Software-only, no human compliance service or advisory layer (unverified from listings, but none described).
- Banks supported appear limited to AIB, Bank of Ireland and Revolut (other Irish banks not listed).
- No reviews, so quality of RPZ/RTB automation and support is unproven.
- Multi-client (agent-of-many-landlords) workflow, white-label or agent-level features: not mentioned in the listings (unverified).

## Opening
A human-backed compliance service for agents and small landlords, or an agent-focused multi-landlord workflow, sits beside a thinly reviewed self-serve tool whose entry price (about EUR 20/month) is already low.
