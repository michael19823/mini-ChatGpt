# Unique IQ (Ireland) - competitor to C0447: Home support HIQA readiness

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- UK-based vendor (founded 2003) selling IQ:caremanager, a cloud home care platform covering rostering, call monitoring, eMAR, payroll, HR, compliance and reporting. Says it has served Irish home care providers for over a decade. Source: https://www.uniqueiq.co.uk/?p=4783 , https://www.uniqueiq.co.uk/our-solutions/care-management
- Explicitly markets HIQA-aligned record keeping, audit trails and inspection readiness for the new home support regulation (HIQA as regulator under the Health (Amendment) (Home Support Providers) Bill 2025; the search summary said it passed the Oireachtas in June 2026, not independently verified). Includes IQ:careaudit for automated audit of visit notes. Sources: https://www.uniqueiq.co.uk/?p=4783 , https://www.uniqueiq.co.uk/our-sectors/domiciliary-care-software/
- Reviews: Trustpilot about 4.5-4.6/5 from 18-24 reviews (counts differ between pages); the company replies to negative reviews. Source: https://de.trustpilot.com/review/uniqueiq.co.uk
- Complaints: none found. One review mentioned occasional glitches, partly attributed to mobile signal. No Reddit, Facebook, app-store or Capterra complaints surfaced in 5 searches (Irish-language query returned nothing relevant). Capterra/GetApp listings exist for IQcaremanager but their reviews were not seen.
- Momentum: active. Current marketing and blog content, AI-enabled features, a pricing page. Competing Irish option: Access Group (HCCI partner), per the earlier rejection note.

## Pricing
Per active carer per month, three tiers plus optional extras. No amounts published in what I could see, so quote required (exact figures unverified). Source: https://www.uniqueiq.co.uk/our-solutions/pricing-packages/

## Fit gaps
- Unverified, no evidence found of gaps. Possible ones to test: UK-centred product with Irish support via marketing rather than a local entity; full-suite platform may be heavy for very small or single-owner home support providers; no published price.
- No Irish-language or HIQA-specific self-assessment/inspection-prep tooling claim seen beyond general audit features (unverified).

## Opening
Little room against it as a full care-management suite; a narrow, cheap HIQA-registration/policy-pack tool for tiny providers is the only plausible gap.
