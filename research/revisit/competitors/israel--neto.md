# Neto (Israel) - competitor check for C0460

Idea: foreign-worker employer compliance (deposits, insurance, PIBA notices).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

## Evidence
- 4 of 6 searches used (Hebrew and English). No URL was known for "Neto".
- The only entity found by that name is NETO (Bareket IT Ltd), listed in the SHRM vendor directory as a licensed Israeli Employer of Record / payroll provider. It lets foreign companies employ and pay workers in Israel without a local entity. It reportedly holds manpower-contractor licence no. 1565. Source: https://vendordirectory.shrm.org/company/940886/neto-bareket-it-ltd (via search snippet; page not opened).
- That is a different buyer (foreign companies hiring Israeli staff) from the idea's buyer (Israeli households and businesses employing foreign workers). No evidence it handles foreign-worker deposits, the foreign-worker health insurance obligation or PIBA notices.
- It is unverified that this is the "Neto" that was originally flagged. Hebrew searches for a Neto payroll or consulting service for foreign-worker employers returned only generic government and Kol Zchut pages, with no Neto-specific hits.
- Complaints: none found. The SHRM listing shows no reviews. No app-store, forum or news complaints surfaced.

## Pricing
Not found (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Appears to target foreign companies using an EOR, not Israeli foreign-worker employers.
- No sign of a foreign-worker deposit, insurance or PIBA-notice workflow.

## Momentum
Unknown (unverified); no news found.

## Opening
The idea's niche appears unserved by Neto. The real market context is that the deposit obligation is being extended to all sectors (May 2024 government decision, per Meitar/lawoffice.org.il search snippets), which suggests growing demand for deposit and compliance tooling.
