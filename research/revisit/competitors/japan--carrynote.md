# CarryNote (Japan) - competitor check

Idea killed: C1284 (Japan light-freight records, 2025-04). Competitor type: commercial, no URL supplied.

## Verdict: strong (low confidence, thin evidence; only 3 of 6 searches ran)

CarryNote is a real, purpose-built, cheap app for exactly this segment (individual light-freight drivers). No complaints were found, and no fit gaps could be confirmed.

## Evidence
- Developer is F Works (エフワークス). Official release 2025-09-26 on iOS and Android. Sources: https://www.lnews.jp/2025/09/r0926501.html , https://www.logi-today.com/850661 , https://voix.jp/?p=56639
- App Store listing exists: https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/carrynote/id6747877997 (title "CarryNote - 軽貨物専用日報作成アプリ"). Its rating and review count were not seen. Unverified.
- Features reported by the press: chat-style daily report (日報), tap start/end, GPS location capture, automatic distance, multi-vehicle management, inspection/maintenance alerts with photos, per-job sales entry, expense management, income/expense calendar, monthly summary, export for tax filing, PDF export of reports, cloud sync.
- Stated motive: over 80% of light-freight drivers do not create daily reports. This is the same need the dropped idea targeted.
- Complaints: none found. Searches (Japanese and English) returned only launch articles and the store page. No Reddit, forum, X or review-site complaints surfaced. This is absence of evidence, not evidence of satisfaction.
- Momentum: very new (about 1 year old as of 2026-10). Recent articles also at https://www.logi-today.com/898376 (content not confirmed). Ongoing development unverified.

## Pricing
- 800 yen/month including tax, with the first 30 days free and all features available (lnews.jp, logi-today.com). Cheap for a sole operator. No small-buyer overpricing gap.

## Fit gaps (all unverified; inferred from absence in the feature list)
- Individual-driver focus. Fleet or multi-driver dispatcher use, delivery-company (運送会社) compliance filing formats and driver-roster features were not mentioned.
- No mention of invoicing, qualified-invoice (インボイス) support or full bookkeeping beyond sales/expense export. Unverified.
- Subscription only; no free tier mentioned after the trial.

## Opening
Little room against sole drivers at 800 yen/month. Any gap would be small-fleet or office-side compliance features (multiple drivers, roster and filing formats, invoicing), and that needs confirming before reviving.
