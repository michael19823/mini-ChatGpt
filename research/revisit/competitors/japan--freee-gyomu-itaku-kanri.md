# freee 業務委託管理 (freee Gyomu Itaku Kanri, formerly pasture) - Japan

Idea revisited: C0482 取適法 compliance ledger. Searches used: 3 of 6.

## Verdict: strong

It is an actively developed, mainstream SaaS aimed at 取適法 (from Jan 2026), with a free tier and published prices. Reviews show no serious complaints, only minor usability and learning-curve remarks.

## Evidence
- Launched a free plan in Dec 2025 explicitly for 取適法 (旧下請法) compliance, covering contract, order, invoice and payment for one partner per month. Source: https://digitalpr.jp/r/124909 and https://www.techno-edge.net/release/prtimes2/20251218/22404.html
- Free plan limits per a search summary (unverified in detail): 1 working partner ID per month, up to 10 partner IDs registered, 3 company users, 3 projects. Source: https://support.freee.co.jp/hc/ja/articles/53242093251865
- Product states support for 取適法, the Freelance Act, the Invoice System and electronic bookkeeping law. Source: https://www.freee.co.jp/partner-management/features/
- Reviews: 49 reviews on ITreview (https://www.itreview.jp/products/pasture/reviews), mostly IT, advertising and media firms. Praise: intuitive UI, contracts built by filling in fields, copy-paste for repeat orders, bulk withholding-tax entry. Complaints (from a search summary, I did not read the pages): hard to learn new features or find training material, and changing orders or invoices is awkward until learned. No outage or failed-filing complaints found.
- Momentum: the 2025 free plan launch and the legal-change positioning show active development. The product was renamed from pasture, so it is a freee line, not an independent startup.

## Pricing
- Free: 1 partner per month (basic features).
- Paid: 5 IDs at 16,000 yen/month (annual billing); 10 IDs at 32,000 yen/month (annual billing); 200 IDs or more by quote. Source: https://digitalpr.jp/r/124909
- Per-partner cost is about 3,200 yen/month on the paid tiers (my arithmetic). That is expensive for a small buyer with many small subcontractors, and the jump from free (1 partner) to 5 IDs at 16,000 yen is steep. The ID definition (monthly active partner) is unverified beyond the summaries.

## Fit gaps
- Volume cliff: free covers one partner a month, and the cheapest paid plan is 16,000 yen/month.
- It is built around contracting and paying freelancers, not a lightweight 取適法 ledger of payment deadlines (60-day rule), delayed-payment interest and price-negotiation records for a manufacturer or small firm. Not verified.
- Whether it works without the rest of the freee ecosystem, and whether it fits small firms with their own accounting software, is unverified.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: a cheap, per-transaction or flat-priced 取適法 ledger for firms with many small subcontractors, which sits between the free 1-partner tier and 16,000 yen/month. freee can close it by changing its plans.
