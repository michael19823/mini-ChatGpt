# freee Sign (freeeサイン) - Japan

Dropped idea: C0482 (取適法 compliance ledger)

## Verdict: strong

freee Sign itself is only an e-contract tool, but freee's sibling product freee業務委託管理 targets 取適法 compliance directly, has a free tier and is made by the same listed vendor. That makes it a strong competitor for this idea.

## Evidence
- freee Sign is a cloud e-contract service (create, review, execute, store) from freee K.K. [freee support pricing page](https://support.freee.co.jp/hc/ja/articles/28984485968921-freee%E3%82%B5%E3%82%A4%E3%83%B3%E3%81%AE%E3%83%97%E3%83%A9%E3%83%B3-%E6%96%99%E9%87%91), [boxil](https://boxil.jp/service/10840/)
- freee runs a 取適法 (旧下請法) knowledge page and a press release on a free plan of freee業務委託管理 "to streamline 取適法 compliance from Jan 2026". [freee KB](https://www.freee.co.jp/kb/kb-deals/toritekihou/), [press release](https://digitalpr.jp/r/124909)
- freee業務委託管理 covers contract, order, invoice and payment management, with payment due-date reminders, and claims compliance with フリーランス法/下請法/インボイス/電帳法. [features](https://www.freee.co.jp/partner-management/features/)
- Momentum: active. The free plan was launched in Dec 2025 and plan structures were revised (freee Sign ID limits removed).
- Complaints: none found specific to freee Sign. The search returned only general freee accounting-software reviews on it-trend and minhyo (poor support, slow phone/chat, some price complaints, one report of outages). I did not verify these and they are not about freee Sign. No G2 or Capterra reviews of freee Sign turned up (results were for an unrelated product, SignFree).

## Pricing
- freee Sign (per freee support page and search summary; yearly billing): Free 0 yen (1 e-signature/month, 3 stored docs/month); Starter 5,980 yen; Standard 29,800 yen (100 signatures/month, contract check); Advance/Enterprise by quote. The exact billing unit (monthly or annual) is unverified.
- freee業務委託管理 (per search summary of the freee site): free plan covering 1 partner/month; 5-ID plan 17,600 yen and 10-ID plan 35,200 yen (annual billing); larger plans by quote. [pricing source in search results](https://www.freee.co.jp/partner-management/features/) - the exact pricing page URL was not confirmed, so treat the prices as unverified.

## Fit gaps
- freee Sign alone has no 取適法 ledger (payment-term, order-document or deadline tracking).
- freee業務委託管理 is per-ID priced and built around freee's ecosystem. Whether it covers the full 取適法 duty set (e.g. the 60-day payment rule, record-keeping detail, 協議 records) is unverified.
- The free tier is limited to 1 partner, so a small firm with several subcontractors must pay about 17,600 yen/year or more.

## Opening
Little room against freee for a general 取適法 ledger. Only a very cheap, standalone tool for non-freee users with several subcontractors, or deeper rule checking, is a possible gap.
