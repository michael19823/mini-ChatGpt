# Jordan: accountants (consultant) — SSC/payroll filing (C0504)

## Verdict: weak

Accountants are a generic human service, not a product. Searches found no complaints about them, but SSC filing in Jordan is already covered by cheap, well-reviewed software and by outsourcers, so the gap is thin. 4 of 6 searches used.

## Evidence
- No complaints about accountants doing SSC/payroll filing were found (Arabic and English searches). Finding none is a valid result and does not prove satisfaction.
- The Social Security Corporation asks establishments to submit monthly wage data for all insured staff. Source (Arabic news snippet, details unverified): https://www.erembusiness.com/economy/zsupevx
- Software already automates Jordan SSC payroll and filing:
  - ZenHR says it covers Jordan labour law, SSC and income tax: https://www.zenhr.com/en/hr-software-jordan
  - Ontop says it handles SSC contributions and statutory filings: https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/jordan
  - Odoo has a Jordan payroll localization: https://www.odoo.com/documentation/master/applications/hr/payroll/payroll_localizations/jordan.html
- Outsourcers also offer it: Yomly (https://www.yomly.com/payroll-management-outsourcing-services-jordan/) and TopSource (https://topsourceworldwide.com/global-payroll/jordan/).
- ZenHR is rated 4.7/5 from 161 reviews on Capterra, according to the search snippet (unverified beyond that): https://www.capterra.com/p/157129/zenHR/
- Momentum: ZenHR, Ontop and others are active. A 2026 guide to HR systems in Jordan exists (https://mozon-tech.com/?p=20739).

## Pricing
- Accountant fees: nothing published, so unverified.
- ZenHR: the snippet says "starting price US$5.00" with quotes based on headcount, and does not give the unit. Treat as unverified: https://www.zenhr.com/en/zenhr-pricing
- Other vendors: unverified or quote-only.

## Fit gaps
- Nothing evidenced. Accountants are manual and per-client, so they probably do not scale to micro-employers. This is an inference, not a sourced finding.
- Unknown whether the SSC portal has an API or bulk-upload format that third parties can use. Not verified.

## Opening
Thin: the opening is only a very-small-employer, accountant-assisted filing workflow, and it competes with ZenHR-type tools already used in Jordan.
