# Jordan: HR/payroll suites (unnamed)

Killed idea: C0504 (SSC/payroll filing)

## Verdict: strong

Jordan already has several local and regional HR/payroll products that explicitly handle Social Security Corporation (SSC) contributions, labor-law rules and income tax. No complaints were found, so there is no visible gap to exploit.

## Evidence
- ZenHR markets HR and payroll software that it says is compliant with Jordan Labor Law and SSC, with automated payroll, tax and end-of-service calculations. Source: https://www.zenhr.com/hr-payroll-software/jordan (vendor claim, unverified).
- Ontop advertises payroll with local SSC handling. Source: https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/jordan (vendor claim).
- Yomly offers payroll outsourcing in Jordan covering SSC compliance, end-of-service indemnity and income tax reporting. Source: https://www.yomly.com/payroll-management-outsourcing-services-jordan/ (vendor claim).
- Odoo has a Jordan payroll localization covering progressive income tax and employee and employer social security. Source: https://www.odoo.com/documentation/master/applications/hr/payroll/payroll_localizations/jordan.html
- VOLT HRMS appears in a channel-partner profile as a Jordan-based HR package. Source: https://elioplus.com/markdown/profiles/channel-partners/127912/volt-hrms (not verified further).
- Mozon publishes comparison guides to Jordan HR systems, which shows an active market. Source: https://mozon-tech.com/en/blog/the-best-hr-and-payroll-software-for-companies-in-jordan-ultimate-guide/
- Complaints: none found. Three searches (English and Arabic) returned no Capterra, G2, app-store, Reddit or news complaints specific to Jordan payroll or SSC filing. This is an absence of evidence, not evidence that users are satisfied.
- Not found: whether any of these products files directly to the SSC e-portal or only produces the figures for manual entry. This is unverified and is the main open question.

## Pricing
No published prices were found in the search results (unverified). The Arabic query surfaced SSC rates only: 7.5% employee and 14.25% employer contribution, per tradingeconomics.com, which is not product pricing.

## Fit gaps
Possible gaps, all unverified: direct SSC portal submission, micro-businesses with fewer than 10 staff, and Arabic-first or mobile-first use. None was confirmed from the search results.

## Opening
Only a narrow one, if any: a very cheap Arabic-first SSC filing tool for micro-businesses, and only if the incumbents turn out not to file directly to the SSC portal.
