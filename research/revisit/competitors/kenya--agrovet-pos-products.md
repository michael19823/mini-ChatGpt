# Kenya: Agrovet POS products (no single URL; category of commercial products)

Idea it killed: C1290, Kenya agrovet multi-regulator compliance.
Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Swahili).

## Verdict: weak

The products found are generic POS and inventory tools, or small agri-input POS pilots. None of them showed multi-regulator compliance features (PCPB, Kenya Veterinary Board, PPB, KEPHIS) in these searches. Absence from search results is not proof that the features do not exist (unverified).

## Evidence
- Generic Kenyan POS and inventory tools with M-Pesa exist and are pitched to shops, including agrovets: SokoniPOS, Paylink, InventoryMS, ProPos. The sources did not mention agrovet-specific regulatory records.
  - https://shocpcms.vps.webdock.cloud/
  - https://paylink.it.com/
  - https://nairobi-biz-growth.lovable.app/products/mystocksystem
  - https://www.producthunt.com/products/propos-business-operating-system
- Agri-input specific POS products:
  - MTela: a mobile POS for agro-input stores that also trains staff on product information. It is described as building a database of agricultural inputs sold in Kenya. https://vc4a.com/ventures/mtela
  - agriPOS: stock management and performance analysis, creates digital footprints for access to finance, and is said to serve over 100 agriHUBs. https://learningnetwork.ictforag.com/innovation/agripos
  - Both are oriented to stock, sales and finance access, not regulator filings, from what the snippets show.
- eTIMS (KRA e-invoicing) is mandatory for businesses, including non-VAT-registered ones, per the sources found. Generic POS vendors (Odoo module, TallyPrime and others) advertise eTIMS integration. So tax compliance is covered by the market, but regulator compliance specific to agrovets was not found.
  - https://hapakenya.com/2026/01/23/understanding-etims-a-guide-to-kenyas-electronic-tax-invoice-management-system/
  - https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/15.0/pos_etims_auto_invoice
- Market context: about 10,000 agrovets in Kenya, only about 4,000 registered. Many lack technical knowledge on vet medicines. ILRI ran capacity building for agrovet operators on compliance and business management. This shows a compliance need that is being met by training, not software, in the sources found.
  - https://www.ilri.org/knowledge/publications/capacity-building-agrovet-operators-compliance-and-business-management
  - https://www.thebrooke.org/news/bridging-gap-empowering-agrovets-through-mentorship
- Complaints: none found. No app-store reviews, forum threads or news about outages were returned for any of these products. The Swahili search returned only NGO and programme material, no product pages or complaints. Review sites (Google Play, Trustpilot) were not surfaced, so this is a gap in coverage and not evidence of satisfaction.
- Momentum: unverified for all products. MTela and agriPOS pages appear to be venture or programme profiles with no dated recent activity seen. Several generic POS pages look like small or template-site vendors (for example a webdock-hosted page and a lovable.app page).

## Pricing
- SokoniPOS: Duka (Starter) plan at KES 1,500 per month (per search summary; unverified beyond that).
- Paylink: Free to KES 4,000 per month across tiers (Basic 1,000, Professional 2,000, Business 3,000, Enterprise 4,000), per search summary.
- InventoryMS: monthly subscription, price not shown.
- MTela, agriPOS: pricing not found.
- At roughly KES 1,000 to 1,500 per month, generic tools are affordable for a small agrovet. Price is not the gap.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of product-register or batch/expiry records tied to PCPB (pesticides), Kenya Veterinary Board, or PPB requirements.
- No evidence of prescription or controlled-product logging, or inspection-ready reports.
- No evidence of license-renewal reminders across several regulators.
- Agri-input POS products target distributors and finance access, not the regulator burden on the shop owner.
- Offline use and Swahili interface not verified for any product.

## Opening
No dominant competitor on agrovet regulatory compliance appeared; a POS or record-keeping add-on covering the multiple regulators, with eTIMS and M-Pesa handled by integration, looks open, though demand willingness to pay is unproven.
