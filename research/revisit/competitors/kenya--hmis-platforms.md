# Kenya: HMIS platforms (vs C1444, Kenya SHA claims/rejections desk)

## Verdict: beatable (low confidence, thin research)

Only 2 of 6 searches ran. The 2nd search hit a usage limit, so the Swahili search did not run and no app-store, Reddit or review-site checks were done.

## Evidence
- HMIS platforms are the incumbents, and SHA is now making an accredited HMIS mandatory for providers in the 2026/28 contracting cycle. The compliance deadline was extended to 30 Sep 2026. Sources: https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/124812-sha-makes-accredited-hmis-mandatory-all-healthcare-providers ; https://allafrica.com/stories/202609010210.html
- The HMIS is meant to cover real-time patient verification and electronic claims submission. That overlaps with a claims desk, so platforms will bundle claims submission. Source: https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/sha-begins-transition-to-integrated-digital-healthcare-management-system/
- The pain is large and sits mostly on the payer side. SHA reportedly rejects about 1 in 5 county facility claims (https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-07-04-sha-rejects-one-in-five-claims-from-hospitals). Claims worth Sh16.03bn of Sh181.9bn were rejected as of 30 Apr 2026 (https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-05-15-sha-under-fire-over-delayed-payments-system-failures). SHA has also rejected about 80% of Emergency and Chronic Illness Fund claims (https://k24.digital/411/mercy-mwangangi-explains-why-sha-declined-80-of-hospitals-claims).
- Reported complaints name no vendor. They concern the SHA platform (frequent downtime, no clear resubmission or feedback on rejected claims, delays past the 90-day period). Source: https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/digital health records/212975/mps-raise-alarm-over-failing-digital-health-system-in-kenya
- No vendor-specific user complaints (app stores, G2, Capterra, Reddit, Facebook groups) were found. None were searched for in depth.
- Momentum: the SHA mandate is driving demand and activity. Individual vendors' development status is unverified.

## Pricing
- Q-AFYA HMIS lists annual license and maintenance fees of KES 119,000 (up to 50 users and 30 workstations), KES 244,000 (50-100 users) and KES 368,000 (101-200 users). Secondary source, via search summary: https://ova.it.com/hmis-pricing/what-is-the-cost-of-hmis
- Other vendors' prices: unverified.
- The cheapest tier is likely out of reach for very small clinics. Whether it is overpriced for them is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Nothing found says HMIS vendors handle claim rejection analysis, appeals or resubmission well. Unverified.
- Likely gap (inference, unverified): HMIS is an accredited all-in-one system, while a claims desk could serve facilities that use a different or basic HMIS, or handle rejection triage and appeals across vendors.
- Risk: accreditation and integration with SHA/DHA are mandatory, so a standalone tool may have to work through certified HMIS vendors.

## Opening
A vendor-agnostic rejection-triage and resubmission layer for small facilities, if SHA and DHA integration allows it, is the opening. HMIS vendors are not shown to serve this need well, and the evidence is thin.
