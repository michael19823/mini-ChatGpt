# Kenya: local payroll SaaS (unnamed)

Idea killed: C0513, statutory payroll (PAYE, SHIF, Housing Levy, NSSF).
Searches used: 3 of 6 (English, plus one Swahili-keyword query; the Swahili query returned English results).

## Verdict: strong

Many Kenyan-built products (and global ones) already do statutory payroll. Several have free or low-cost entry tiers aimed at small businesses.

## Evidence
- Named Kenya payroll products found:
  - FaidiHR offers free payroll for businesses with 5 employees or fewer (https://blog.faidihr.com/our-latest-news/FaidiHR+to+offer+Free+Payroll+Software+to+Small+Businesses+In+Kenya).
  - Payroll.ke says to "start today for free" (https://www.payroll.ke/about).
  - WorkPay offers a 30-day free trial (https://myworkpay.com/blogs/cloud-based-payroll-software-for-smes-in-kenya).
  - Sage One Payroll was listed at "as little as Ksh100 per employee per month" (Business Daily; the date and the current price are unverified).
  - Deel/PaySpace local payroll covers PAYE, NSSF tiers, SHIF and the Housing Levy (https://payspace.com/kenya).
  - Odoo and ERPNext Kenya payroll modules exist (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/l10n_ke_payroll).
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced. The searches returned only vendor blogs, so none of this is user-review evidence.
- Vendor blog posts (FaidiHR, which has a commercial interest) say that systems with outdated SHIF or NSSF rules cause under-remittance and KRA non-compliance flags. This is vendor marketing, unverified as a customer complaint.
- KRA iTax portal outages and missing PAYE credits were reported near deadlines (https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/124706-kra-explains-itax-portal-delays-kenyans-rush-beat-june-30-deadline). This is a government portal problem, not a payroll vendor problem.

## Pricing
- Free tier for 5 or fewer employees (FaidiHR); other free-start claims (Payroll.ke).
- Sage One at about Ksh100 per employee per month (unverified, possibly dated).
- Odoo module $99.08 and ERPNext module from $249 (one-off module prices, per the search summary).
- Deel/PaySpace: no price published in the results.
- Small buyers are not overcharged; the low end is effectively free.

## Fit gaps
- None evidenced. Statutory rate changes (SHIF 2.75%, Housing Levy 1.5%, NSSF upper limit) appear to be tracked by active vendors.
- Possible but unverified gaps: very large rate-change lag at weaker vendors, and informal micro-employers (household staff) that these products may not target.

## Opening
Little to none for generic statutory payroll. Only a niche (for example household or casual-worker payroll via M-Pesa) might remain, and that is unverified.
