# Al Tamimi & Company (Kuwait) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0523, UBO register compliance (MOCI Res. 37/2026, 15-day window, KD 1k-10k fines, licence renewal condition).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Al Tamimi is a large regional law firm. It publishes legal updates on UBO rules but, in what I found, sells no product or software for UBO register compliance. It serves large clients, not the small and mid-size companies that would buy a self-serve tool.

## Evidence
- Al Tamimi has published a legal-update article on UBO disclosure in Kuwait, effective 1 April 2023: https://tamimi.com/news/kuwait-effective-as-of-1st-april-2023-disclosure-of-ultimate-beneficial-ownership-in-kuwait-companies. This is thought leadership, not a product.
- Its Kuwait office (https://www.tamimi.com/offices/kuwait/) is described as having about 40 staff, over 20 of them lawyers, in Burj AlShaya. Its listed work is M&A, banking, finance and disputes. Named clients include Al Ahli Bank of Kuwait, National Bank of Kuwait and Blackstone.
- Legal 500 client testimonials for the firm are positive and cover litigation and Kuwaiti-law expertise. They say nothing about UBO.
- Res. 37/2026 (16 April 2026) is confirmed by Deloitte, BDO and others: no licence granted or renewed unless UBO requirements are met, and fines of KD 1,000-10,000. The Al Tamimi mention in the results was only as a source of guidance. No Al Tamimi UBO service for Res. 37 turned up.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in English and Arabic found no app-store, forum or news complaints about Al Tamimi's Kuwait practice. No complaints is a valid result.

## Pricing
Not published. Law-firm hourly or project fees are unverified, and no source gave a figure. Fees are very likely far above what a small buyer would pay for a filing tool.

## Fit gaps
- Bespoke advisory by lawyers, not a self-serve tool or register-maintenance product (unverified, since no such product was found).
- Targets large corporate, bank and PE clients, so small and mid-size Kuwaiti companies are poorly served.
- No evidence of Arabic-first workflow software, deadline tracking or multi-entity dashboards.

## Momentum
The Kuwait office is active and moved to new premises in 2023. It is not a software vendor, so product momentum does not apply.

## Opening
Offer an affordable, self-serve UBO register and 15-day-deadline filing tool for small and mid-size Kuwaiti companies. Al Tamimi leaves that segment unserved and is more likely a referral partner than a rival.
