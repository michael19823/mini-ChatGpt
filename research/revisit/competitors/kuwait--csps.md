# Kuwait: CSPs (corporate service providers / consultants)

Idea killed: C0523, UBO register compliance (MOCI Res. 37/2026, 15-day update window, KD 1,000-10,000 fines, licence renewal blocked).
Searches used: 4 of 6 (a usage limit interrupted one; no further searches were made after the reset).

## Verdict: beatable

Reason: the competitor is a loose group of consultants and advisory firms, not a product. Only light evidence of what they charge or how they are rated was found, and no software-led UBO filing tool for small Kuwaiti firms was seen in the results.

## Evidence
- MOCI Ministerial Resolution No. 37 of 2026 (16 April 2026) amends Decision 4/2023. No commercial licence is granted or renewed unless UBO identification requirements are met. Penalties run KD 1,000 to 10,000. It covers all entity types registered with MOCI, including sole proprietors. Sources: [Deloitte Middle East](https://www.deloitte.com/middle-east/en/services/tax/perspectives/kuwait-updates-to-ultimate-beneficial-owner-regulations.html), [Regfollower](https://regfollower.com/kuwait-tightens-ubo-rules-with-licensing-conditions-new-penalties/), [BDO](https://www.bdo.global/en-gb/insights/tax/world-wide-tax/kuwait-companies-now-required-to-identify-ultimate-beneficial-owners).
- Per the BDO and search summaries, the register must be updated within 15 days of an ownership change and resubmitted at licence renewal.
- Big-4 and mid-tier firms (Deloitte, BDO) publish advisory notes on the rule. Their sales offer is consulting, not a self-serve tool.
- Commenda, a global corporate services provider, has a Kuwait UBO filing page ([commenda.io/kuwait/ubo-filing](https://www.commenda.io/kuwait/ubo-filing)). It targets international companies entering the market, which looks like a service offer. No price was visible in the search results (unverified beyond that).
- The Arabic search returned no Kuwait-specific commercial UBO tools or reviews.

## Complaints
None found. No reviews or forum threads about Kuwaiti CSPs' UBO work appeared in the 4 searches. This is absence of evidence only, not evidence that customers are satisfied.

## Pricing
No published prices found. Unverified: consultants most likely quote per engagement.

## Fit gaps (inferred, partly unverified)
- Consultants and advisory firms work case by case, so they probably suit SMEs poorly. A small firm is unlikely to want a retainer for a 15-day update filing.
- No evidence of a self-serve register or reminder tool for sole proprietors and small companies. That segment is now covered by the rule.
- Arabic-first tooling was not seen.

## Opening
A cheap self-serve UBO register and 15-day-change reminder tool for small MOCI-registered firms looks open. The main risk is that the filing goes through MOCI's own portal, so the value would lie in preparing the data, keeping records and sending alerts.
