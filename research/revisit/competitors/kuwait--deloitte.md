# Deloitte (Kuwait) - competitor check for C0523 (UBO register compliance)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Deloitte Kuwait is a Big Four advisory firm. It publishes a client alert on the UBO rules and offers bespoke compliance support. It does not sell a self-serve UBO product.

## Evidence
- Deloitte Middle East has a perspective, "Kuwait updates to ultimate beneficial owner regulations". It summarises MOCI Resolution 37/2026 (16 April 2026): no licence granted or renewed unless the requirements are met, and fines of KD 1,000-10,000. It says Deloitte can help companies assess the impact and offers "compliance support". Source: https://www.deloitte.com/middle-east/en/services/tax/perspectives/kuwait-updates-to-ultimate-beneficial-owner-regulations.html
- No software, portal, app or fixed-scope product was found in the search results. The offering is a bespoke advisory engagement.
- Other competitors that surfaced and are NOT Deloitte: BDO and Tamimi publish alerts. Commenda has a Kuwait UBO filing page (https://www.commenda.io/kuwait/ubo-filing); it was not examined and is unverified as a product. These may merit their own checks.
- Complaints: none found. The English and Arabic searches returned no reviews or complaints about Deloitte Kuwait. The Arabic search returned nothing relevant on Kuwait. Review platforms were not surfaced, so this is absence of evidence only.
- Momentum: Deloitte Kuwait is established (since 1949; Bader Al Wazzan & Co. joined in 2010 per its site). It is active on the topic, with a 2026 alert. https://www.deloitte.com/middle-east/en/about/governance/network-brand-alliances/deloitte-kuwait.html

## Pricing
No published pricing found. Unverified, but Big Four advisory work is typically quoted per engagement and is likely out of reach for a micro business.

## Fit gaps
- Targets mid-size and large clients; not a self-serve tool for small firms (inferred, not stated by Deloitte).
- No evidence of recurring tooling such as the 15-day change updates, UBO tracking or reminders.
- Arabic-language product: none found.

## Opening
A cheap self-serve UBO register and 15-day change-tracking tool for the many small MOCI-registered entities that Deloitte's advisory model does not serve.
