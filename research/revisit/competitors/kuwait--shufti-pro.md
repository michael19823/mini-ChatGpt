# Shufti Pro (Kuwait) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0526, generic KYC/sanctions screening APIs (Shufti Pro, Sumsub, LexisNexis, Focal).
Searches used: 4 of 6 (English and Arabic).

## Verdict: strong

Shufti Pro is an active, established, global KYC/AML API vendor with a dedicated Kuwait offering and a licensed Kuwaiti customer. Complaints found are mostly from end users, not buyers.

## Evidence
- Shufti runs a Kuwait page claiming KYC/KYB/AML under Law No. 106 of 2013, Central Bank of Kuwait supervision and KWFIU reporting; it lists Civil ID (PACI), passport and driving licence support. Vendor claims, unverified independently. Source: https://shuftipro.com/supported-countries/kuwait/
- Named Kuwaiti customer: AZ eWallet (CBK-licensed e-money provider). Source: https://shuftipro.com/press-release/shufti-is-empowering-az-ewallet-the-fastest-growing-fintech-company-in-kuwait/
- Trustpilot: TrustScore 4.8 with about 3,681 reviews, per search summary. Complaints in the summary are consumer-side: rejected verifications, unclear remediation, slow or poor support, delays activating purchased features. I did not read individual reviews. Source: https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/shuftipro.com
- No Kuwait-specific complaints, outages or failed-filing news found. No complaints found in Arabic sources; the Arabic search returned only other vendors (Didit, Focal) and generic KYC explainers.
- Momentum: apparently active; pricing aggregator shows 2026 data and many localized country pages. Not acquired or shut down as far as found.

## Pricing
- Aggregator (costbench) lists free tier and "Essentials" at $0.95 per check as of Aug 2026; real pricing is not public and is sales-quoted. Third-party figure, unverified. Source: https://costbench.com/software/kyc-aml/shufti-pro/
- Older (2017) published packs: $1.20 and $0.75 per verification with $2,500 setup fee; historical only. Source: https://shuftipro.com/news/shufti-pros-prices
- Some users say per-check cost is high at volume (aggregator summary, unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Generic global API, not Kuwait-specific workflow (e.g., local goAML/KFIU filing, Arabic compliance workflow). Unverified whether it files with KWFIU or just supplies checks.
- Small local buyers may face sales-led quotes. No evidence found of hardware requirements or lack of Arabic support.
- Competes also with Sumsub, Focal (has a Kuwait KYC page), Didit (Kuwait page), which suggests a crowded space.

## Opening
Only a thin one: a Kuwait-local, Arabic-first, goAML/KWFIU-filing-aware compliance workflow for small regulated firms, not a raw KYC/screening API.
