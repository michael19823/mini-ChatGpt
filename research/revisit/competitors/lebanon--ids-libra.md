# IDS Libra (Libra Salary Declaration / Payroll Junior), Lebanon

Idea killed: C0541 Payroll and NSSF declarations.

## Verdict: strong (for the narrow MoF + NSSF declaration job); beatable only on delivery model and price transparency

## Evidence
- IDS (Integrated Digital Systems) has operated since 1991; Libra Financials became a full ERP in 1999. Source: https://www.ids.com.lb/media/3916/company_profile.pdf and https://libra.ids.com.lb/salary-declaration/
- "Libra Salary Declaration" is a Windows-based app for Lebanese private-sector firms and accounting firms. It prepares payroll tax (MoF) and NSSF declarations, supports multiple companies/employees/fiscal years, batch runs, and says it is integrated with the Ministry's online submission system. Source: https://libra.ids.com.lb/salary-declaration/
- It explicitly targets SMEs, accounting firms and large enterprises (as an ERP add-on). Source: same page.
- "Payroll Junior" (cheaper, simplified edition with official MoF/NSSF reports) was announced 12 May 2016. Source: https://www.ids.com.lb/news/payroll-junior-released-standard-payroll-with-official-reports/
- Momentum: an active product site with 2025-26-style blog content (step-by-step MoF + NSSF guide, "top 5 mistakes"), so it appears actively marketed. Release dates of the blog posts were not verified. Sources: https://libra.ids.com.lb/blog/
- Other local options exist (e.g. Ontop, a foreign payroll platform claiming Lebanon NSSF handling; https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/lebanon), but I did not verify their depth.

## Complaints
None found. Searches (English and Arabic, 6 total) returned no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit, forum or news complaints about Libra. Absence likely reflects a small, niche, B2B product with little public review footprint, not proof of satisfaction.

## Pricing
Not published. Searches found no price or license figures. Unverified. Payroll Junior was described as "cheaper" than the full module, without numbers.

## Fit gaps (inferred from IDS's own description; mostly unverified)
- Windows desktop application: no mentioned mobile, web or cloud version (unverified whether one exists).
- Sales-led, no public pricing or self-serve trial found.
- Arabic UI support not confirmed either way.
- Positioned for accountants and firms; the smallest employers (few staff) may prefer outsourcing or spreadsheets.

## Opening
A cloud/mobile, self-serve, transparently priced tool for micro-SMEs and freelance accountants could undercut IDS, but IDS already covers the core MoF + NSSF filing formats, so the product must win on delivery and price, not on compliance coverage.
