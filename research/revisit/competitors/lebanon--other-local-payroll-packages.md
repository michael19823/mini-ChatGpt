# Lebanon: other local payroll packages (idea C0541: Payroll and NSSF declarations)

## Verdict: strong

Local, purpose-built, regulator-aligned products already exist (IDS Libra Salary Declaration, Payroll Junior), with a cheaper tier aimed at small firms. No complaints were found, but that mostly reflects thin public review coverage. 5 searches used, English and Arabic.

## Evidence
- IDS markets "Libra Salary Declaration" as a Windows-based app for private companies and accounting firms. It claims MoF and NSSF compliance, multi-company and multi-year records, and integration with the Ministry's online submission system. Source: https://libra.ids.com.lb/salary-declaration/
- IDS also sells "Payroll Junior", a cut-down payroll module with official MoF and NSSF reports, described as cheaper, faster and easier. Source: https://www.ids.com.lb/news/payroll-junior-released-standard-payroll-with-official-reports/
- Libra's site also mentions a Web Payroll option with attendance and "Libra on the Cloud". Source: https://libra.ids.com.lb/accounting (the search snippet only; unverified in detail).
- IDS publishes active content (step-by-step salary-declaration guides, a "Top 5 Mistakes" post), which suggests ongoing marketing and maintenance. Source: https://libra.ids.com.lb/blog/ (publication dates not verified).
- Foreign EOR/payroll providers (Ontop, Mercans) list Lebanon NSSF handling, but they target outsourced or multinational payroll, not local declaration filing. Sources: https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/lebanon, https://dsr.mercans.com/payroll-dossiers/lebanon/
- Complaints: none found. The Arabic search returned only general NSSF news (law 319 of 2023, fund finances), nothing on payroll software. No app-store, forum or review-site complaints about Lebanese payroll packages surfaced.

## Pricing
Not published for Libra Salary Declaration or Payroll Junior (unverified; likely quote-based). No local competitor price could be compared.

## Fit gaps (inferred from the vendor's own descriptions, not from user complaints)
- Libra Salary Declaration is described as Windows-based, so it is probably weak on mobile or browser-only use. A web payroll offering exists but looks tied to the larger Libra suite.
- IDS seems aimed at companies and accounting firms. The micro-business or self-serve segment is less clear (unverified).
- Arabic UI or support was not verified either way.
- Other small local vendors and Excel-based workflows may exist, but the searches did not surface them.

## Opening
Only a thin one: a low-cost, browser or mobile, self-serve payroll and NSSF/MoF filing tool for micro-employers, if IDS pricing proves to be quote-based or enterprise-priced. That would need verifying with IDS pricing and customers.
