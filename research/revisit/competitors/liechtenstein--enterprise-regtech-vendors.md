# Liechtenstein: enterprise RegTech vendors (generic, no URL)

Idea killed: C0555, bank and fund AML monitoring and EU AMLR readiness for banks.
Searches used: 3 of 6 (English and German). No complaint-site hits.

## Verdict: strong

For banks, the market is served by established enterprise vendors. The gap is for small buyers, and a small founder is unlikely to sell to banks.

## Evidence
- LGT, the Liechtenstein private banking group, was reported to have chosen NICE Actimize for AML and markets surveillance. https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/nice-actimize-chosen-by-lgt (date not checked)
- Fenergo transaction monitoring was reported at PROVEN Bank. https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2024/08/228311-regtech-fenergo-transaction-monitoring-solution-to-streamline-proven-banks-aml-compliance-processes/ (unverified that this bank is Liechtenstein-based; the search returned it for this query)
- Hypo Investment Bank (Vaduz) uses mlds name matching from Innovations Softwaretechnologie for sanctions-list checks. https://www.moneycab.com/?p=1285807
- The PwC Liechtenstein AML survey 2026 says 86% of respondents name transaction monitoring as their largest technology investment area (as quoted in the search summary; the page was not opened). https://www.pwc.ch/en/insights/risk/liechtenstein-aml-survey-2026.html
- Third-party comparison text describes Fenergo and Actimize as enterprise-first, with material implementation and integration effort, and with less obvious value for smaller firms. https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/fenergo/nice-actimize (secondary source, quoted only as summarised by the search tool)
- A Celent report on AML solutions for smaller institutions exists, so the small-institution segment is known to analysts. https://www.celent.com/insights/428842331 (content not read)

## Complaints
None found for Liechtenstein banks or vendors. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit hits. Only generic industry complaints about cost and data quality appeared (Euromoney, Bank Systems & Technology; not read in detail).

## Pricing
No published prices found for any vendor. A generic search snippet says AML transaction monitoring systems cost $50K to $500K+ to build. The source is unclear, so treat it as unverified.

## Fit gaps
- The enterprise tools seem aimed at large banks. This is the only gap evidenced, and only from a secondary source.
- Nothing found on gaps specific to Liechtenstein, such as EU AMLR readiness or fund managers.
- Liechtenstein has few banks, and they are large and well resourced (LGT, among others). The addressable market is tiny.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: lightweight AML or AMLR tooling for small Liechtenstein asset managers, fund managers or trustees, which the evidence does not show to be underserved. Banks are not a realistic target.
