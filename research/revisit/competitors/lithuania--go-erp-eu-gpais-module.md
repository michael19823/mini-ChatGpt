# go-erp.eu GPAIS module (Lithuania)

Dropped idea: C0559, GPAIS packaging/product supply accounting for producers, importers and e-shops.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

GO-GPAIS is an add-on for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations (and older AX). It is not a standalone product, so it does not serve the small producers, importers and e-shops that C0559 targets. Only 3 of the 6 allowed searches were run, because the first three already settled the question.

## Evidence
- AppSource lists "go erp.gpais" under Dynamics 365 for Operations. It is described as a data-transfer solution for packaging and products, built for D365 F&O and earlier AX. https://appsource.microsoft.com/en-my/product/dynamics-365-for-operations/go-erp.gpais
- Cheaper ways to meet GPAIS reporting already exist inside other ERPs:
  - an Odoo module, lr_gpais, which generates XSD-compliant XML: https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/17.0/lr_gpais
  - an SAP add-on from UAB Innoforce: https://www.sap.com/products/financial-management/partners/uab-innoforce-gpais-waste-and-packaging-reporting-for-lithuania.html
- Small e-shops are served by outsourced GPAIS bookkeeping services (paslaugos.lt listings). I did not verify any specific provider's price.
- Complaints: none found for GO-GPAIS, in English or Lithuanian. No reviews were found.
- Momentum: unverified. The AppSource listing was not dated in the snippet.

## Pricing
Not published in the results (unverified). It is presumably sold with a D365 implementation, so it is out of reach for small buyers. The only price figure found was for general accounting services, 50-100 EUR/month, average 71 EUR/month, according to a paslaugos.lt snippet. That figure is not specific to GPAIS.

## Fit gaps
- It requires a D365 F&O or AX licence and a partner implementation, so it fits only mid-size or large firms.
- It is not usable by a small e-shop or importer without an ERP.
- No standalone web or SaaS version was found (unverified).

## Opening
Small producers, importers and e-shops without a D365, SAP or Odoo setup have no standalone GPAIS tool here. The real alternatives are Odoo or SAP modules and outsourced bookkeepers, so check those before reviving C0559.
