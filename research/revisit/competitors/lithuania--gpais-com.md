# gpais.com (Lithuania) - competitor check for C0559

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

"gpais.com" is not a product I could find. GPAIS is the Lithuanian state Unified Product, Packaging and Waste Accounting Information System, which producers and importers must report to. It is a regulator system, not a vendor. The real commercial competitors are ERP add-ons and accounting services that feed it (listed below). Those are the ones C0559 would actually face, and they are ERP-bound.

## Evidence
- A search for "gpais.com" returned nothing relevant (only GPAI, the AI partnership). No site found. URL unverified.
- GPAIS is a state system. Since 2018, producers and importers must keep packaging and product accounting in it. Source: https://www.sap.com/products/financial-management/partners/uab-innoforce-gpais-waste-and-packaging-reporting-for-lithuania.html and https://lappa.org/guides/epr/lithuania-epr/
- The EPR guide says distance sellers and e-shops placing packaged goods on the Lithuanian market count as producers/importers. This confirms the e-shop buyer exists. Source: https://lappa.org/guides/epr/lithuania-epr/
- Real vendors found:
  - GO-GPAIS by GO-ERP UAB, a Dynamics 365 F&O localization add-on: https://appsource.microsoft.com/en-my/product/dynamics-365-for-operations/go-erp.gpais
  - BC GPAIS (oixio) for Dynamics 365 Business Central, manual v5.2 dated Oct 2025, so it is actively maintained: https://oixio.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BC-GPAIS-User-Manual_v5.2.pdf
  - UAB InnoForce GPAIS XML generator for SAP S/4HANA: https://www.sap.com/products/financial-management/partners/uab-innoforce-gpais-waste-and-packaging-reporting-for-lithuania.html
  - Odoo module lr_gpais: https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/17.0/lr_gpais
  - Local accountants and freelancers offering "GPAIS apskaitos tvarkymas" on https://paslaugos.lt/gpais-apskaitos-tvarkymas-vedimas/neringos-sav, plus job ads for in-house GPAIS compliance specialists (cvbankas.lt).
- Complaints: none found. The one Lithuanian-language complaint search returned irrelevant results. This is a null result, not proof the products are good.

## Pricing
- gpais.com: none (no such product).
- Odoo lr_gpais module: listed at $1,365.54 (apps.odoo.com listing, one-off as shown; terms unverified).
- GO-GPAIS, BC GPAIS, InnoForce: no public prices found (unverified).

## Fit gaps (of the add-ons, for a small producer/importer/e-shop)
- All are tied to an ERP (Dynamics 365 F&O or Business Central, SAP, Odoo). A small e-shop on Shopify, WooCommerce or spreadsheets is not served.
- Enterprise-oriented pricing and sales model (inferred; unverified).
- The gap left over is a standalone tool for small firms. It would take sales or order data, work out packaging weights by material, and produce the GPAIS XML or reports.

## Opening
No dedicated standalone SMB or e-shop GPAIS tool surfaced. The competition is ERP add-ons plus accountants, so C0559 stays open if it targets non-ERP small sellers. These searches did not test the Lithuanian market for other standalone tools.
