# gpais.lt (Lithuania)

Dropped idea: C0559, GPAIS packaging/product supply accounting for producers, importers and e-shops.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

gpais.lt is the national government Unified Product, Packaging and Waste Accounting Information System (GPAIS), the system producers and importers are legally required to report into. It is the regulator's filing portal, not a commercial product. The real competitors are the third-party tools that sit in front of it (see below). Its government status means the idea was dropped because a free official tool exists, so complaints would not reopen the gap, per method.md Phase 2 scoring rules.

## Evidence (4 searches used, 2 English, 2 Lithuanian)
- GPAIS is the mandatory Lithuanian system. Since 2018, producers and importers must keep packaging and product accounting in it. Source: https://oixio.eu/digital/finance-and-compliance/gpais-ppwis/
- E-commerce and distance sellers placing packaged goods on the Lithuanian market must register in the Producers and Importers Register via GPAIS, keep packaging records and file reports there. Source: https://lappa.org/guides/epr/lithuania-epr/
- Commercial ERP add-ons already generate GPAIS XML reports:
  - Oixio "BC GPAIS" for Dynamics 365 Business Central: https://oixio.eu/digital/finance-and-compliance/gpais-ppwis/
  - UAB Innoforce for SAP: https://www.sap.com/products/financial-management/partners/uab-innoforce-gpais-waste-and-packaging-reporting-for-lithuania.html
  - go erp.gpais for Dynamics 365: https://appsource.microsoft.com/en-my/product/dynamics-365-for-operations/go-erp.gpais
- A job listing for a "GPAIS assistant" suggests firms hire staff for the filing work (unverified as a market signal): https://www.cvbankas.lt/aplinkosaugos-gpais-asistentas-e-vilniuje/1-14107565
- Complaints: none found. Searches in Lithuanian and English returned no user reviews, forum posts or outage news about GPAIS. This is an absence of evidence in a few searches, not proof that none exist.
- Momentum: the system is active. The official site is gpais.eu (a 2025-08-28 regional reporting guidance PDF is hosted there), and the Oixio manual was updated to v5.2 in October 2025. The gpais.lt domain itself was not confirmed (no URL was known).

## Pricing
- GPAIS itself: no price found. It is a legal obligation run by the state, assumed free to use (unverified).
- Oixio, Innoforce and go erp add-ons: prices not found (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Third-party tools found are ERP add-ons (Business Central, SAP, Dynamics 365), so they serve firms already running those ERPs. Small firms and e-shops on Shopify, WooCommerce or spreadsheets did not appear to be served. This is an inference from the search results and is unverified.
- No evidence was found of what GPAIS itself lacks for small buyers, such as import from e-shop orders or simplified packaging-weight entry.

## Opening
Small e-shops and importers without an ERP could use a lightweight tool that turns orders or invoices into GPAIS-ready packaging data. It would sit in front of a mandatory free government system, so willingness to pay is unproven.
