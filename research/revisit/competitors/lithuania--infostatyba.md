# Infostatyba (Lithuania) - competitor check for C0556 (electronic construction work journal, ESDŽ)

## Verdict
**not-a-real-competitor** (as a commercial rival). Infostatyba is the state construction-permit and supervision information system, run by the State Territorial Planning and Construction Inspectorate under the Ministry of Environment. It is the mandatory public registry the ESDŽ lives in, not a commercial product. The brief listed it as "commercial" with no URL; that looks wrong. Confidence is moderate, because only 4 of 6 searches were used (the first failed with a usage-limit error, and one returned only irrelevant electrostatic-discharge results).

## Evidence
- Infostatyba is described as Lithuania's construction permits and state construction supervision information system. It is administered by the State Territorial Planning and Construction Inspectorate and offers "one-stop-shop" e-services. Source: https://finonamai.lt/informacija/infostruktura-boruzeles-l-d-statybos-leidimas/ and the search summaries of https://www.tv3.lt/gaires/infostatyba
- The electronic construction work journal is required for contractors, technical supervisors and clients. It covers registration, completion and closing of the journal, plus notices of construction start. Source: https://finonamai.lt/informacija/statybos-zurnalas-nuosavam-namui/
- A private market exists around the state system. Freelancers advertise filling in the electronic journal and handling construction notices (for example https://paslaugos.lt/donatas-ds1330). That points to a usability or time burden that people pay to outsource. This is inferred and unverified; no source states the reason.
- statyboszurnalas.lt turned up in results. I did not open it and do not know what it is. Unverified; it may be a private journal tool or a guide.
- Complaints: none found. The Lithuanian-language searches for complaints returned nothing relevant. No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced. That is a search-coverage limit, not evidence that users are happy.

## Pricing
- No published price for the journal system. It appears to be a state e-service. This is unverified, and the search results did not state it.
- Outsourced help is priced by freelancers: from 50 EUR for a construction-start notice and from 200 EUR for completion declarations. Consultations run 10-100 EUR/hour. Source: search summary of paslaugos.lt listings, and I did not verify the individual listings.

## Fit gaps (all unverified; no source found)
- Likely a government-portal workflow with no field-friendly UX, such as mobile, offline or photo capture. No source confirms this.
- It probably does not cover a contractor's internal project management (crews, materials, subcontractor coordination). No source confirms this.
- Neither the state portal nor any tool built on it was checked for mobile or offline use, so treat both as open questions.

## Momentum
Actively maintained state system. It appeared in EU tender notices (ted.europa.eu results), which suggests ongoing public procurement. I did not open those notices. Unverified.

## Opening
The journal itself is a legal filing that must go through the state system, so the opening is a convenience layer on top of it (mobile, offline, photo-based entries, guided filling, assistance for small contractors and self-builders) rather than a replacement. Dropping the idea because of Infostatyba was likely a mistake in framing. The real question is whether the state system exposes an API or accepts third-party submissions, and I did not establish that.
