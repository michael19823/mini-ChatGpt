# Kidizz (Luxembourg)

Ideas it killed: C0562 (private-crèche billing for the 2026 CSA reform), C0565 (crèche CSA billing).

## Verdict
**beatable**. Kidizz is mainly a parent-communication app (news feed, photos, messages, menus). I found no evidence that it handles Luxembourg CSA billing.

## Evidence
- Kidizz is described as a parent app for crèches, kindergartens and leisure centres, free for parents. Features listed: news feed, private messages, photos and videos, calendar, menus, documents. Source: https://www.roubaixxl.fr/kidizz-lappli-pour-des-parents-zen
- France-focused: partners named are Babilou, UCPA and the city of Paris. Source: search summary of Kidizz pages (no direct URL verified).
- Luxembourg presence: a Luxembourg crèche (KidsCare) offers Kidizz to its parents. Source: https://www.lesfrontaliers.lu/jeux-concours/cheque-service-accueil-offrez-a-votre-enfant-lexperience-kidscare-et-tentez-de-gagner-de-superbes-cadeaux/
- App Store rating of 4.4/5 from about 3,300 ratings was reported in a search summary (unverified; the exact app listing was not checked).
- One listicle (https://tool-advisor.fr/blog/logiciel-petite-enfance/) mentions Kidizz as childcare management software with modules, including billing. The search summary did not confirm CSA or Luxembourg billing (unverified).
- Complaints: none found in 4 searches. No Trustpilot, G2, Capterra or Reddit results for Kidizz surfaced. This is not proof that none exist.
- Momentum: apparently active, with many white-label apps (e.g. "Hopla Kids by Kidizz", "Ma crèche & Vous by Kidizz", "Kidizz EPN") on the App Store. No acquisition or shutdown news found.
- Other player: Kidola is reported to run in France, Belgium, Luxembourg and Spain (https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1112579/Kidola). It is a more likely CSA-billing competitor than Kidizz.

## Pricing
Free for parents. Pricing for crèches: not found (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of CSA (chèque-service accueil) billing, the state-reimbursement workflow or the 2026 reform rules in Luxembourg.
- Product focus and brand are French; Luxembourg localisation (FR/DE/LU, CSA) is unverified.
- Strength is parent communication, not billing.

## Opening
A Luxembourg-specific CSA billing tool could sit beside or replace Kidizz's billing module. The real rivals to check are Kidola and local software.
