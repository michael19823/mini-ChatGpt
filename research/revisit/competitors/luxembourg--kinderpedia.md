# Kinderpedia (Luxembourg) - commercial, no URL given

Ideas it killed: C0562 (private-crèche billing for the 2026 chèque-service accueil reform), C0565 (crèche CSA billing).

## Verdict: weak

Kinderpedia is a well-reviewed general childcare and school management platform. I found no evidence that it handles Luxembourg CSA billing or the 2026 reform rules. It is a generic competitor, not a CSA-specific one.

## Evidence
- Capterra lists it with Luxembourg pages (capterra.lu/software/174857/kinderpedia), so it is sold or visible in the country. This is from search results only. I did not open the page.
- Search results show a rating of 4.8/5 from 165 users on Capterra (163 positive, 2 neutral, 0 negative) and 4.9/5 on G2. Sources: capterra.com/p/174857/KINDERPEDIA/reviews/, getapp.com listings.
- Complaints (mild, from search-result summaries, not verified against the full reviews):
  - some notifications do not arrive;
  - some UI buttons and options misbehave;
  - users want more customisation of filters, sorting and reports;
  - it is awkward to work in several windows for different locations;
  - one user reported a translation problem in Icelandic.
  No complaint about billing, Luxembourg or CSA was found.
- Searches in French ("logiciel gestion crèche Luxembourg facturation CSA") and German did not surface Kinderpedia in the Luxembourg crèche context. Local-market tools named in the results were Meeko (crèche management including billing), LIGIEC (payroll) and Zigy (attendance hours). These came from a search summary and are unverified.
- Background: the 2026 CSA reform introduces uniform billing rules and payment based on actual hours rather than flat fees (men.public.lu press kit, Jan 2026). I found no sign that Kinderpedia has adapted to it. This is unverified.
- Momentum: reviews are current to 2026 and the product appears actively sold. I found no news of acquisition or shutdown.

## Pricing
- The Starter package is listed at EUR 61 per centre per month, including up to 70 students. Extra students cost EUR 0.90 each per month. Annual billing gives a 20% discount. Business Plus (over 500 students) has custom pricing. Source: search-result summary of getapp/softwareadvice pages. Unverified against the vendor site.
- This is affordable for a small crèche. It is not overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of CSA support: no CSA voucher or hours-based reconciliation with the state, and no 2026 reform rules. Unverified, because absence of evidence from 6 searches is not proof.
- The UI languages shown are mostly Romanian, English and others. French and German support in Luxembourg is unverified.
- Customisable reporting and filters are limited, per reviewers.

## Opening
A CSA-specific billing tool for Luxembourg crèches (hours-based invoicing under the reform, and state reconciliation) is still open against Kinderpedia. The more direct local competitor to check is Meeko, which was not researched here.
