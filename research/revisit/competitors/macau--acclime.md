# Acclime (Macau) - competitor check for C0566 (Macau payroll statutory-filing automation)

## Verdict: beatable

Acclime is a real payroll outsourcer in Macau, but it sells a managed, quote-based service. It is not self-serve filing software. Evidence is thin because only 3 of 6 searches were run, and one of those failed on a usage limit.

## Evidence
- Acclime runs a Macau payroll page offering outsourced payroll and benefits calculation, tax processing, payments, reporting and compliance filings, plus bundling with HR, accounting and tax. Source: https://macau.acclime.com/hr/payroll/ (search-result summary only; the page was not fetched).
- The summary mentions no software, app or self-serve product, and no specific FSS (Social Security Fund) contribution workflow. Unverified beyond the summary.
- Momentum: unverified. Acclime appears to be a multi-country firm (job listings in Thailand and the Philippines), which suggests it is active, but I did not confirm this.
- Complaints: none found. The one review search returned only unrelated companies (Aclima, ACS Clima), so no Acclime reviews turned up. This does not show the product is good, only that nothing was found.

## Pricing
Not published. No prices were found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred from the service model, unverified)
- It is a service-led, human-delivered outsourcer, probably priced by headcount or on a quote. That is likely a poor fit for micro and small Macau employers who want cheap self-serve FSS and non-resident-worker filings.
- No evidence of a self-serve tool or mobile use.

## Opening
A low-cost self-serve FSS and non-resident filing tool for small employers can sit beneath Acclime's managed, quote-based offer. This needs confirmation that Acclime has no product and that its pricing excludes small firms.
