# AscentHR (Macau)

Idea killed: C0566, Macau payroll statutory-filing automation (FSS quarterly contributions, non-resident worker fees).

## Verdict
**weak** as a competitor for a small Macau buyer. It is an India-based enterprise and multinational payroll vendor. Macau is one country page in a global-payroll catalogue. It is not a local product, and I found no sign it targets Macau SMEs.

## Evidence
- AscentHR's Macau page lists Professional Tax, FSS contributions, quarterly FSS filing, annual Professional Tax reporting, final settlements and non-resident worker employment fee workflows "where applicable". It is a feature list on a country-compliance page. I found no Macau customers, local office or local-language materials. https://ascent-hr.com/en-ae/solutions/global-payroll/country-specific-payroll-compliance/macau/
- Company profile: India-based HR and payroll technology and managed-services firm aimed at multinational enterprises. Its platform is PowerPay, described as multi-country enterprise payroll. https://www.briter.co/companies/ascenthr and https://ascent-hr.com/solutions/global-payroll/
- A Chinese-language search found no Macau-specific AscentHR content or FSS-integration claims. It did surface a local competitor, Workstem (https://www.workstem.com/mo/?p=29), which advertises Macau social-security and provident-fund calculation tied to payroll. Workstem is the more relevant competitor and was not checked further.
- Aggregated reviews (rfp.wiki, a secondary aggregator) show a 3.7 score. Per that page: G2 4.3 (2 reviews), Capterra 5.0 (1), Software Advice 5.0 (1), Trustpilot 3.1 (30). Reported complaints are payroll errors or delays, inconsistent support responsiveness, a clunky many-click UI, mobile app login reliability, limited self-service depth, and opaque commercial and exit terms. Praise is for payroll accuracy, compliance help and the combined HR and payroll platform. https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/ascenthr
- Caveat: the Trustpilot count may include a different company with a similar name. I could not verify this, and I did not read the reviews themselves. None of the complaints are Macau-specific.
- Momentum: the site is active, with en-ae and en-my regional pages and press content, and PowerPay is described as having AI-enabled features. I found no acquisition or shutdown news. The claim that PowerPay natively supports 28 countries, with partners covering 34-35, comes from rfp.wiki and is unverified.

## Pricing
Not published. Sales-led enterprise engagement, and rfp.wiki says commercial and exit terms are opaque. No minimum employee count was found (unverified). Positioning suggests it is out of reach for a small Macau firm, but no price evidence exists.

## Fit gaps
- Enterprise and multinational focus, so there is no sign of an SME or self-serve tier.
- No evidence of Chinese or Portuguese UI or support for Macau.
- No evidence of direct FSS e-filing integration. The Macau page only says it supports contributions and filing workflows.
- The non-resident worker fee item is listed with a hedge ("where applicable"), so depth is unverified.
- Managed-service model, so a small employer would pay for a service rather than get a cheap tool.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, Chinese-language Macau FSS and non-resident-fee filing tool for SMEs is not served by AscentHR. The nearer competitor to check is local vendors such as Workstem.
