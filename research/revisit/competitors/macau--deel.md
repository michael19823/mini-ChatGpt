# Deel (Macau)

Ideas it killed: C0566 (Macau payroll statutory-filing automation: FSS quarterly contributions, non-resident workers), C0571 (payroll / HR compliance).

## Verdict: beatable

Deel is a global EOR and payroll platform aimed at foreign companies that hire in Macau. It is not a local SME payroll or FSS-filing tool. Its Macau coverage is real but generic, and evidence on FSS filing specifics is thin (see Limits).

## Evidence
- Deel runs a Macau EOR page. It says Deel handles local laws, tax, benefits and international payroll, and that staff can start in as little as 7 days. It also offers PEO-style payroll and compliance for firms that already have a Macau entity. Source: https://www.deel.com/hiring/employees/macau/
- The page is a template that appears in many languages (nl, pt-pt, fr, es, de) with the same English title. No Chinese or Portuguese Macau-specific page turned up. Unverified whether it handles FSS contribution filings, non-resident worker rules or Chinese-language payslips.
- Complaints (Capterra, aggregated search summary, not Macau-specific): higher exchange fees than some rivals, tedious double authentication, difficult initial setup and support, limited integrations with external tools. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/205015/Deel/reviews. I did not verify individual reviews.
- No Macau-specific complaints, outage reports or failed-filing news were found. The Trustpilot, G2, Reddit and local-forum searches either returned nothing relevant or were not run (see Limits).
- Momentum: actively marketed, with 2026 pricing guides and Capterra reviews listed. No sign of shutdown or stagnation.

## Pricing
Third-party guides, not Deel's own page, so unverified:
- EOR from $599 per employee per month.
- Global payroll $29 per employee per month.
- Contractor management $49 per contractor per month.
- Employer taxes and statutory benefits are extra.
- Volume discounts above about 20 heads.
Sources: https://www.pin.com/blog/deel-pricing/, https://www.compono.com/articles/deel-hr-pricing-guide-2026
The $599 EOR price is far too high for a small Macau employer just filing FSS contributions. The $29 payroll tier is cheaper, but it is unverified that it covers Macau.

## Fit gaps
- Built for cross-border hiring and foreign firms, not for local SMEs with an existing Macau payroll.
- No evidence of a Chinese or Portuguese interface or Macau-specific statutory forms (FSS quarterly filings, non-resident worker levies). Unverified.
- Per-head USD pricing is poor value for micro-businesses.
- Generic third-party complaints about support and setup.

## Opening
A cheap, Chinese-language, local-first tool for FSS quarterly contribution and non-resident worker filings for small Macau employers. Deel serves foreign companies hiring through EOR, not this buyer.

## Limits
Only 3 of 6 searches returned results. A local-language search (Chinese) hit a usage limit and returned nothing. The Trustpilot, Reddit and app-store checks were not done, so the "no Macau complaints found" result is weak.
