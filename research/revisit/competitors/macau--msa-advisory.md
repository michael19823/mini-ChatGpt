# MSA Advisory (Macau) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0566, Macau payroll statutory-filing automation (FSS quarterly contributions, non-resident worker items).

## Verdict: weak

MSA Advisory is a multi-country outsourced advisory and payroll service, not a self-serve filing-automation product. It would compete for the same buyer's budget but not for the same product category.

## Evidence
- MSA runs a Macau page for "Payroll Outsourcing" listing salary calculation, tax filings, mandatory social security contributions, leave and benefits administration, and regulatory reports. It mentions "secure, cloud-based payroll systems" as a back-end. Source: https://macau.msadvisory.com/service/payroll-outsourcing/
- Same service template appears on its Taiwan, Thailand and China sites (e.g. https://taiwan.msadvisory.com/service/payroll-outsourcing/, https://thailand.msadvisory.com/service/payroll-outsourcing/). This points to a generic regional advisory offering, not Macau-specific software (inference).
- No evidence found of a customer-facing software product, app, or login for FSS filing.
- Complaints: none found. A search for reviews and complaints returned only unrelated Macau government complaint-system pages. Absence of reviews is not evidence of quality; it may reflect low public visibility.
- Momentum: pages are live; no dates, acquisition, shutdown or product-update news found. Unverified.
- Searches were run in English and Traditional Chinese (4 of 6 used). Chinese search returned only FSS and government documents, no MSA-specific local-language content or reviews.

## Pricing
Not published. Searches found no per-employee or minimum fee. Unverified. As an outsourced service, it is likely a quote-based monthly fee (assumption).

## Fit gaps
- Service-based and outsourced: the employer hands off payroll rather than gaining a tool. Unsuited to firms wanting in-house control or low per-filing cost.
- No evidence of self-serve software, FSS-format file generation, or non-resident worker fee tracking.
- Multi-country consultancy; Macau micro/small businesses may be below its target size (unverified).

## Opening
A cheap self-serve tool for small Macau employers that generates FSS contribution and non-resident worker filings leaves room, since MSA only sells quote-based outsourcing.
