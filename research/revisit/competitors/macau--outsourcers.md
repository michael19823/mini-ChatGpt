# macau -- outsourcers (payroll / HR outsourcing consultants)

Killed idea: C0571 Payroll / HR compliance. Competitor has no single URL; it is a category of consultancies. 5 searches used (English and Traditional Chinese).

## Verdict: beatable

A fragmented set of service firms (not software) serves Macau, and none was found to be a software-first product for small employers. Their strength is human service and local-law expertise, so a tool would not easily replace the full-service end of the market.

## Evidence
- Providers found offering Macau payroll/HR outsourcing: Acclime (macau.acclime.com/hr/payroll/), MSA (macau.msadvisory.com/service/payroll-outsourcing/), INS Global (EOR/PEO, ins-globalconsulting.com/countries/macau/employer-of-record/), Remote People/remotepad (EOR/PEO listing), and Galaxy Payroll Group (Nasdaq: GLXG, subsidiary Galaxy HR (Macau)).
- Galaxy Payroll, per press coverage (barchart.com, streetinsider.com): half-year to 31 Dec 2025 revenue about HKD14.0M (about 2% growth), customers 196 to 210. In Feb 2026 it renewed a 5-year deal with Nike China Holding HK (Macau Branch) covering payroll, leave, social security and tax filings. This implies a stable but slow-growing, enterprise-leaning, group-wide business (Macau is one of several markets).
- Workstem (workstem.com/mo) has a Macau page in results; it is HR software but its details were not verified here.
- Complaints: none found specific to Macau payroll outsourcers (no app-store, Reddit, forum or news hits). The only complaint-type content was unrelated, generic HR software reviews on Capterra (non-Macau). Macau news hits concerned employers falsifying local-employee data to Social Security Fund / Labour Affairs Bureau for foreign worker quotas, which indicates compliance risk but not outsourcer failures.
- Search limits: the Chinese-language searches returned mostly Hong Kong providers (fastlane-global, RSM HK, Forvis Mazars HK, Kaizen CPA). Local Facebook groups and forums were not surfaced. Treat the absence of complaints as weak evidence.

## Pricing
- No Macau price published by any provider found. Quote-based; HK-style pricing is typically tiered by headcount (unverified for Macau, source: HK provider pages only). A PEO/EOR charges a percentage of salary (general statement, no figure found).

## Fit gaps
- Quote-only, headcount-tiered service. Likely poor value for very small employers (under about 20 staff) (inference, unverified).
- Enterprise and multinational orientation (Nike client; EOR/PEO for foreign entrants).
- No evidence found of self-serve software, Chinese/Portuguese/English UI, or mobile access from these firms (unverified; not checked).

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve Macau payroll and Social Security Fund / tax-filing tool for SMEs could undercut quote-based consultancies, but the human service and compliance advice they bundle limits how much it displaces them.
