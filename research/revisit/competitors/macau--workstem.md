# Workstem - Macau

Dropped ideas: C0566 (Macau payroll statutory-filing automation: FSS quarterly contributions, non-resident tax), C0571 (payroll / HR compliance)

## Verdict: strong

Workstem is a cloud HR and payroll platform with a dedicated Macau edition that already advertises FSS and tax filing support, mobile app, SME pricing and a free tier. No complaints were found. 4 WebSearch calls were used.

## Evidence
- Macau edition describes a payroll engine that complies with Macau labour law, covering onboarding, scheduling, attendance, leave, expenses, payroll, social security and tax reporting, and a mobile app. Sources: https://www.workstem.com/mo/solutions ; https://www.workstem.com/mo/en/
- Dedicated "FSS & Tax Management System" page: one-click FSS registration, auto-compiled pay items for FSS contributions, one-click import/export of tax files, bulk FSS import with auto-reminders. Source: https://www.workstem.com/mo/en/?p=141
- Claims over 2,000 companies use it (vendor claim, unverified; not specific to Macau). Source: Workstem Macau pages above.
- Official release in Macau; natively supports Hong Kong, Australia, Taiwan and Macau. Source: https://www.hr.software/reviews/workstem
- Help-centre article on submitting contribution data to the EMPF platform (Hong Kong MPF), which shows active development of filing integrations, though for Hong Kong. Source: https://help.workstem.com/en/articles/12007086-how-to-submit-contribution-data-to-the-empf-platform-through-workstem
- Whether it submits directly to the FSS portal or only produces files for import: unverified.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in English and Chinese for reviews returned only vendor pages and a listing site, plus unrelated products (Workstream, WorkStep). No app-store, Reddit, forum or outage reports surfaced.
- Momentum: active, with current help-centre and product pages; no acquisition or shutdown news found.

## Pricing
- Reported to start from HK$165 per month, with a free trial and a free version; Attendance Plus from HK$33 per employee per month; pay-as-you-go. Source: https://us.fitgap.com/products/workstem (third-party listing, current Macau pricing unverified).
- This is affordable for small employers.

## Fit gaps
- No clear gaps found for small Macau employers.
- Non-resident worker tax specifics and exact FSS submission method: unverified.
- Likely targets F&B, retail and construction; accountants or payroll bureaus serving many clients may be less well served (inference, unverified).

## Opening
Little: Workstem already covers FSS and tax filing at SME prices, so a new tool would need a narrow angle, such as a multi-client accountant workflow or non-resident tax handling, and that is unverified.
