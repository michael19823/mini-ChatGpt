# Madagascar: Sage 100 / Sage Paie via SRA Madagascar

Idea killed: C0575 (payroll and accounting for formal mid-market firms).

## Verdict: strong (for formal mid-market firms; leaves a gap below that)

## Evidence
- SRA Madagascar (formerly Interface Technologie, founded 2000, rebranded October 2013 after joining the French SRA Group) is a certified Sage integrator in Antananarivo. It sells and supports Sage 100 Comptabilité, Gestion Commerciale, Paie & RH, and Sage X3, and offers implementation, maintenance, training and support to Malagasy SMEs and mid-market firms. Source: https://erpresearch.com/sage-partners/interface-technologie-sra-madagascar (directory listing, not the vendor's own site).
- Complaints: none found specific to Madagascar or to SRA Madagascar (6 searches budgeted, 3 used; no local-language review, forum or news hits). Capterra's summary for Sage 100 generally (369 reviews, 4.1/5, 77% positive, 6% negative) cites a dated interface and complex pricing. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/156947/Sage-100. These are general, not Madagascar-specific.
- Momentum: Sage 100 is actively sold and has a Paie & RH module; SRA has had a local presence for 20+ years. Not stagnant.

## Pricing
- No Madagascar price found; integrators quote on request (unverified).
- Capterra lists Sage 100 at "$7,500 per user, per month". That looks like a US listing artefact and is unverified. It is not usable as a Madagascar price.
- Canada prices for the cloud Sage Paie/Comptabilité (from $24.50 and $23 per month) are for a different product and market and do not apply.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified for Madagascar)
- Integrator-led model: implementation, training and maintenance contracts. This is likely too heavy and costly for micro and small firms.
- Dated interface and complex pricing (Capterra, general).
- No evidence found of Malagasy-specific payroll items (CNaPS, OSTIE, IRSA filings) or Malagasy-language support. Not verified either way, so check with SRA before relying on this.

## Opening
Strong in the formal mid-market, so avoid competing head-on there; the opening is small firms and informal-adjacent businesses that cannot afford an integrator-led Sage deployment.
