# HeadOffice (Malawi) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0578 Malawi payroll/PAYE. Searches used: 4 of 6 (English; a Chichewa-keyword query returned nothing local).

## Verdict: weak

HeadOffice shows up only as a marketing and content site (Malawi tax guides, a P12 form download page, tax-forms list). I found no evidence of a payroll product with real users, reviews or pricing.

## Evidence
- Pages found are blog or guide content on a laravel.cloud staging-style domain: https://headoffice-marketing-site-production-a7kpwu.laravel.cloud/malawi/blog/malawi-payroll-tax , https://headoffice-marketing-site-production-a7kpwu.laravel.cloud/malawi/download-form-p12-monthly-paye-return , https://headoffice-marketing-site-production-a7kpwu.laravel.cloud/malawi/forms . The search summary described it as offering a "free service to help manage business taxes" (from search snippet; unverified by me, since pages were not opened).
- Content covers PAYE bands, pensions, TEVETA levy, P12 deadline (14th), P9, Msonkho Online. This is SEO-style guidance, not proof of a working payroll engine.
- No reviews on Google Play, App Store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or local forums turned up.
- Other Malawi payroll options that did surface: PaySpace (https://support.payspace.com/portal/en/kb/articles/malawi-product-user-guide), Sage VIP, Ontop, TopSource, Remote, Pebl (hellopebl.com). These are the stronger competitors.

## Complaints
None found. This reflects a lack of any visible user base, not a clean record.

## Pricing
Not found. Described as free in a search snippet (unverified). No MWK pricing found for any small-business payroll tool in the searches.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of a downloadable or app-store product, offline or mobile use, Chichewa support, or direct Msonkho Online filing (all unverified, not confirmed absent).
- Appears to be informational rather than a filing tool.

## Momentum
Unverified. Site looks recently built (modern hosting domain) but I found no company details, funding, or release history.

## Opening
Little competition from HeadOffice itself; the real rivals are PaySpace, Sage and EOR/global-payroll vendors that serve mid-size firms, leaving cheap small-employer PAYE/P12 tooling open.
