# Ontop (Malawi) - competitor check for C0578 (Malawi payroll/PAYE)

## Verdict: weak

Ontop is a cross-border contractor-payment and employer-of-record (EOR) platform. It has a Malawi payroll marketing page, but it is built for foreign companies that hire in Malawi without a local entity. It is not a local PAYE/MRA filing tool for Malawian SMEs. It is therefore a weak competitor for a local-employer payroll product.

## Evidence
- Ontop has a Malawi page claiming AI payroll calculations, local MASM/pension handling and compliant payslips, "avoiding setting up a local entity" (search snippet of https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/malawi). The "no local entity" framing indicates a foreign-employer EOR audience. I did not see any MRA e-filing integration; that is unverified.
- Trustpilot: average about 3.0 out of 5 on ~123-131 reviews. The company reportedly replies to about half of negative reviews, often after more than a month (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/getontop.com?page=2 and regional pages). The ratings quoted from other sources were inconsistent (3.0 vs 3.7 on Trustpilot).
- Complaints seen in snippets: "hidden fees", "continuous errors", payment discrepancies between received and invoiced amounts, payment delays, and "okay until you get problems that need to be solved asap". These are generic, not Malawi-specific.
- Praise: fast payouts to Colombia and good support from some contractors. G2 about 4.1 and Capterra about 4.6 per search summaries (https://www.capterra.com/p/241309/Ontop/reviews/).
- No Malawi-specific complaints, Chichewa-language material or Malawi forum threads were found. The search returned only English results.
- Momentum: active. Sacra reports about $15M annualized revenue (https://sacra.com/research/ontop-at-15m-annualized-revenue/); date and figures not checked.

## Pricing
- Contractors: from $49/month per contractor; another source says $29/month plus 1% of disbursements (https://us.fitgap.com/products/ontop, https://sacra.com/c/ontop). Conflicting; unverified.
- EOR: about $499/month per seat in one source, $209 per employee per month in another (same sources). Unverified.
- Review sites mention FX spread, setup/deposit and off-boarding fees. Malawi-specific pricing was not found.
- At USD pricing of this size, it is far too expensive for a typical Malawian SME with local-currency payroll.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at foreign companies employing remote staff, not at local employers running their own payroll.
- USD pricing per head; no evidence of a small-business or local-currency plan.
- No evidence found of MRA PAYE return (P12) or TEVETA/pension return filing formats for local employers (unverified).
- No local-language support evident (unverified).
- Other Malawi payroll options appear in the same searches: Playroll, Mercans, TopSource, HeadOffice (not assessed here).

## Opening
A cheap, local-currency PAYE/pension/TEVETA calculator and filing-prep tool for Malawian SMEs is not served by Ontop's USD, EOR-oriented offer.
