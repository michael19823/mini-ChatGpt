# Playroll (Malawi)

Dropped idea: C0578 Malawi payroll/PAYE (alongside HeadOffice, Workpay, Ontop, TopSource, Sage).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Playroll is a global EOR and payroll vendor for foreign companies hiring in Malawi. It is not a local-SME payroll or PAYE tool.

## Evidence
- Playroll has a Malawi EOR page and a Malawi payroll page. The EOR fee starts from $399 per employee. Source: https://playroll.com/employer-of-record/malawi , https://playroll.com/payroll/malawi
- Its direct payroll product needs a local entity and is aimed at multinationals (180+ countries). Source: https://playroll.com/payroll/malawi (search snippet)
- G2 rating is reported as 4.7/5. Reviewers praise support and onboarding. Source: https://www.g2.com/products/playroll/reviews (search snippet only; I did not open it)
- Complaints: none found in the searches. No Malawi-specific reviews found. Local-language search (ma malipiro, MRA PAYE) returned no Playroll results.
- Momentum: the site is actively maintained (Malawi compliance pages, 2026 public holidays). Source: https://www.playroll.com/compliance-hub/public-holiday-regulations-in-malawi

## Pricing
- EOR from $399 per employee per month. Contractors $49 per month.
- Global Payroll Services from $10 per employee per month. Payroll analytics from $2.07 per employee per month. Source: marketplace/review sites (toolradar, peoplemanagingpeople), unverified against Playroll's own page.
- Far above what a Malawian SME could pay for in-country payroll (unverified; no local price benchmark found).

## Fit gaps
- Serves foreign firms hiring in Malawi, not Malawian SMEs.
- Direct payroll needs a local entity. EOR is per-head USD.
- No evidence of MRA Msonkho Online filing (P12 return) support. Unverified.
- No evidence of local-language UI or mobile-money payouts. Unverified.

## Opening
A cheap local-currency (MWK) PAYE, P12 and pension payroll tool for Malawian SMEs is untouched by Playroll. Other vendors on the list (HeadOffice, Workpay) still need their own checks.
