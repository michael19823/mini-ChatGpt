# Competitor: Sage resellers (Malawi)

Type: commercial. No URL known. Dropped idea: C0578 (Malawi payroll/PAYE).
Searches used: 3 of 6 (English only; no Chichewa-specific results surfaced, none added).

## Verdict: weak

Sage supports Malawi PAYE through South African-built payroll products. No Malawi reseller could be identified, and no Malawi-specific complaints were found.

## Evidence
- Sage community pages carry Malawi tag announcements: PAYE tax-table updates (for example 2020/21 and 1 April 2022) for Sage payroll products, plus P16 and Fringe Benefit reports for Malawi. Source: https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-payroll-professional/f/announcements/tags/Malawi and https://www.sagecity.com/tags/Malawi
- Sage Pastel Payroll release notes list Malawi among the countries supported for tax-year-end processing. Source: https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-pastel-payroll/w/year-end-centre/2882/tax-year-end-processing-by-country
- The product is run from the South African Sage community hub (/za/), so Malawi looks like a secondary market served by tax-table updates. This is my inference.
- Searches found no named Malawi resellers, no Malawi reviews on Google Play, Capterra, Reddit or local forums, and no outage or failed-filing news. Complaints: none found. Generic Sage payroll complaints (lag, costly support) appeared for other regions only and are not relevant.
- Other players showing up for Malawi payroll are international EOR and payroll services (Ontop, TopSource, Mercans). They are not Sage resellers.

## Pricing
Not found. No published Malawi price or minimum. Unverified: licensing is probably per-seat or annual and sold through partners, so quotes would be on request.

## Fit gaps
All unverified, inferred from the product type.
- Desktop or server software with reseller-led setup, so likely a poor fit for small employers.
- No evidence of direct MRA e-filing or mobile or offline-first use. Outputs seem to be reports and CSV templates.
- Not confirmed: local-language support, or statutory items such as the TEVETA levy and pension handling beyond PAYE.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve Malawi payroll and PAYE tool for small employers, with MRA-ready outputs, is not clearly served by Sage resellers. Sage appears to be reachable mainly through partners at unpublished prices.
