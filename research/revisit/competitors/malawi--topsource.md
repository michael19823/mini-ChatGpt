# TopSource (Malawi) - competitor check for C0578 (Malawi payroll/PAYE)

## Verdict: weak

TopSource is a multi-country managed payroll and EOR provider with no published pricing. It is not aimed at small Malawian employers who want self-serve PAYE software.

## Evidence
- TopSource Worldwide has a Malawi page for fully managed payroll (130+ countries), with a named account manager, local specialists and one consolidated monthly invoice: https://topsourceworldwide.com/global-payroll/malawi
- It also offers an Employer of Record service in Malawi: https://topsourceworldwide.com/employer-of-record/malawi/
- It is a managed service for multinationals, not self-serve local software. Its own Malawi page does not mention MRA Msonkho Online filing specifics. The search summary only described filing generically (unverified for Malawi).
- Third-party reviews (secondary sources, not verified directly):
  - G2 is reported at 2.8 from 4 reviews and Gartner Peer Insights at 5.0 from 1 review, per https://www.gloroots.com/blog/topsource-worldwide-reviews. The sample is tiny.
  - Complaints reported there: setup fees, about $2 per payment transaction, and "inaccurate quote" cases with costs 30-50% over estimate.
  - Another review roundup says integrations and self-service are limited: https://peoplemanagingpeople.com/tools/topsource-review/
- No Malawi-specific complaints, app-store reviews, Reddit or local-forum posts found. Search results showed limited Trustpilot and Reddit volume.
- Searching in Chichewa/local terms returned no TopSource content. The only local payroll item found was a student project, "Malipiro Payroll System" (https://mwnation.com/poly-students-invent-payroll-system/), which is not a commercial competitor.
- Momentum: the site is actively publishing content (2026 comparison pages), so it is active. No acquisition or shutdown found.

## Pricing
Not published. A scoping call is required, so no rate card is available (per gloroots.com review above). Pricing for a small Malawi employer is unverified, but a quote-based multi-country service is likely out of reach for micro and SME employers.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at multinationals paying staff in many countries, not local SMEs.
- No self-serve product for a small employer to run PAYE or P12 themselves.
- Quote-only pricing, with reported setup and per-transaction fees.
- Limited self-service and integrations (secondary source).

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve MWK PAYE/P12 payroll tool for local SMEs sits outside TopSource's market. The other competitors named in the dropped idea (HeadOffice, Sage and others) were not checked here.
