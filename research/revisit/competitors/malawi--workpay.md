# Workpay (Malawi) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0578 Malawi payroll/PAYE.

## Verdict: weak

Workpay is a Kenya-based multi-country HR/payroll/EOR provider. In Malawi it appears only as a
hire-through-us (EOR/contractor) service, not as local payroll or PAYE-filing software for Malawian SMEs.

## Evidence
- Workpay's own Malawi page offers hiring contractors, remote staff or full-time employees in Malawi
  through its service: https://myworkpay.com/country-guide/hire-employees-in-malawi (this is an EOR
  offering aimed at foreign employers, not a local payroll tool).
- Founded 2019, HQ Nairobi; expanded to Nigeria and Uganda (funding coverage: https://thecondia.com/workpay-pre-series-a-kenyan-hr/,
  https://citizen.digital/tech/kenyan-hr-payroll-start-up-workpay-to-expand-across-africa-after-securing-ksh342m-n315144).
  Momentum: funded ($2.7M pre-Series A) and active; no Malawi-specific product or local presence found.
- Complaints: none found. G2 says there are not enough reviews to give buying insight
  (https://www.g2.com/sellers/workpay). No app-store, Reddit, Facebook or Chichewa-language results
  surfaced (3 searches, mostly English; no local-language hits). Absence of complaints reflects a lack of
  data, not proof of satisfaction.

## Pricing
- Third-party aggregator reports EOR from about $300 per employee per month and contractors at about $40
  per contractor per month; overall range $4-$400 (https://www.linktly.com/sales-software/workpay-review/,
  https://pricingsaas.com/companies/myworkpay). Unverified against Workpay's own page.
- Far above what a small Malawian employer would pay for local payroll software.

## Fit gaps
- Serves foreign companies hiring in Malawi, not local SMEs needing PAYE/pension calculation and filing.
- No evidence of MRA PAYE filing formats, MWK-native local pricing, Chichewa, or offline/mobile use (unverified).

## Opening
A cheap, MWK-priced local PAYE/NPS payroll tool for Malawian SMEs is not contested by Workpay.
