# kumoDoc (Malaysia) - competitor check for C0580 (GP panel/TPA claims reconciliation)

## Verdict: beatable

kumoDoc is a real, established clinic management system, but nothing found shows dedicated panel/TPA claims reconciliation. Its published feature list covers billing, not panel claim matching.

## Evidence
- kumoDoc is a cloud clinic management system from Kumo (operating since 2015), launched July 2023. Features cited: appointment scheduling with WhatsApp notification, inventory, telehealth, EMR, "fraud detection" and "secure billing", multi-branch dashboard. Kumo claims 1,000+ clinics in Southeast Asia. Sources: https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2023/07/20/kumodoc-champions-the-path-to-tech-savvy-clinical-healthcare/ , https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2023/07/20/a-star-is-born-kumodoc-champions-the-path-to-tech-savvy-clinical-healthcare/80719 , https://newswav.com/article/malaysia-based-kumo-sees-regional-clinic-adoption-as-south-east-asia-pushes-A2601_X9d1Xq
- Momentum: a Newswav article (title references regional clinic adoption and SEA digitalisation) suggests recent activity. Date and details unverified (only the title was seen).
- None of the search snippets mention TPA reconciliation, remittance matching or panel claim tracking for kumoDoc. Absence in snippets is not proof it lacks them (unverified).
- Competing GP software lists in Malaysia (MedicalMet, MagSys, Docspe, CxSYS, MocDoc) are described as handling panel price lists and claim reporting, so panel billing is a common feature, but reconciliation of payments against TPA remittances was not shown for any (unverified). Source: https://www.medicalmet.advisoryapps.com/blog/best-clinic-management-software-malaysia-2026
- Context on the pain point: MPCAM (doctors' coalition) has raised complaints about TPAs, e.g. panel appointment fees. Source: https://www.malaysiakini.com/letters/328416 (about TPAs, not kumoDoc).
- Complaints: none found about kumoDoc (no app-store, G2, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced; a G2 seller page exists but no review content was seen). Finding none does not mean none exist.

## Pricing
Not published in anything found (unverified). General Malaysian clinic software market range cited by a third-party blog: roughly RM45 to RM1,000+/month (not specific to kumoDoc).

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of TPA-by-TPA claim reconciliation / short-payment detection (unverified).
- Positioned as a full-suite system; a small GP would have to adopt the whole system rather than add a reconciliation layer.

## Opening
A standalone or add-on tool that reconciles TPA/panel remittances against clinic invoices (and works alongside any clinic system) is not shown to be covered by kumoDoc.

Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Malay terms; local-language results were thin).
