# MedicalMet (Malaysia) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0580 GP panel/TPA claims reconciliation

## Verdict: strong

MedicalMet is an actively marketed Malaysian clinic management system with insurance-panel billing and panel-specific billing reports for claims and reconciliation, at low prices. It is a general clinic system, though, and nothing found shows a dedicated TPA remittance-matching or rejection-chasing tool.

## Evidence
- KL-headquartered cloud clinic management system for GP, dental, aesthetic and other clinics across SEA. Claims 3,000+ providers (vendor claim, unverified). Source: https://www.medicalmet.advisoryapps.com/faq/what-is-medicalmet/ and https://www.medicalmet.advisoryapps.com/faq/where-is-medicalmet-based/
- Insurance panel module: per-panel price lists applied automatically at invoicing; "panel-specific billing reports for claim submissions and reconciliation" (vendor claim). Sources: https://www.medicalmet.advisoryapps.com/features/insurance-panel/ , https://www.medicalmet.advisoryapps.com/faq/how-to-manage-insurance-panel-pricing-for-different-insurers-in-a-clinic/
- Vendor blog claims panel billing across all major Malaysian TPAs, LHDN MyInvois e-invoicing, AutoCount/Xero integration (marketing, unverified). Source: https://www.medicalmet.advisoryapps.com/blog/best-clinic-management-software-malaysia-2026
- Momentum: 2026-dated content, e-invoicing, AI notes and WhatsApp features suggest active development.
- Complaints: none found. Searches returned only vendor content. A G2 alternatives page exists (https://www.g2.com/products/medicalmet/competitors/alternatives) but showed no review text. No app-store, Reddit, Trustpilot or Malay-language user complaints surfaced.
- Buyer pain is real and separate from the vendor: GPs report delayed TPA payments, late claim rejections, 10-15% deductions and panel fees up to RM5,000. Sources: https://codeblue.galencentre.org/2024/06/health-care-costs-will-rise-further-if-tpas-remain-unregulated-mma/ , https://codeblue.galencentre.org/2025/11/the-double-dipping-scandal-how-tpas-are-exploiting-both-corporations-and-clinics-dr-james-jeremiah/ . These are not complaints about MedicalMet.

## Pricing
Conflicting search summaries: one said from RM49/month (Solo) up to RM999/month, another said from US$12/month up to US$245/month (unlimited users/branches). The pricing page is https://medicalmet.advisoryapps.com/pricing (not read; exact figures unverified). Free 30-day trial and no setup fee are vendor claims. Either way it is cheap for a small GP.

## Fit gaps (all unverified inference)
- Reconciliation appears to be billing reports per panel, not automated matching of TPA payment remittances against submitted claims or tracking rejections and ageing across TPAs.
- No evidence of TPA portal or API submission, or of rejection-reason analytics.
- Nothing found on multi-clinic panel-group analytics.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: a standalone remittance-matching and rejection-chasing layer that sits beside any clinic system, if MedicalMet's reconciliation proves to be report-level only. Verify with a demo or user interviews first.
