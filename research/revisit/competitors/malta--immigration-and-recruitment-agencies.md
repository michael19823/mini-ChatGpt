# Malta: immigration and recruitment agencies (type: consultant, no single URL)

## Verdict
beatable (low confidence; research was cut short, see Limits)

Reason: these are human service providers that file permits for a fee, not a software product. A self-serve tracker for employers or workers does not compete head-on with them. They are more likely a channel or an alternative than a product rival.

## Evidence
- 3 of 6 WebSearch calls were attempted. Two failed with "You've hit your usage limit". One returned results (the Maltese-language query). Searches 4 to 6 were not attempted.
- Official process: the Single Permit is run by Identity Malta and combines the employment licence and residence permit in one application. The employer must endorse the application and a job offer is required first. Normal processing is about 2 to 3 months. (Sources: https://www.identita.gov.mt/single-permit/ , https://identita.gov.mt/?p=1537 , https://www.expat.com/en/guide/europe/malta/13290-resident-and-work-permit-in-malta.html)
- A Malta labour-migration reform write-up exists at https://www.mamotcv.com/insights/maltas-labour-migration-framework-reform/ (a law firm). I only saw the title and did not read the content, so its contents are unverified.
- Specific named agencies, their products and their reviews were not found in the searches that ran.
- Complaints: none found. I did not reach review sites (Trustpilot, Google reviews, Reddit, Facebook groups), so "no complaints found" reflects missing coverage, not a clean record.

## Pricing
- Government fees, as reported in the search summary and not verified on the official page: first Single Permit EUR 600, renewal EUR 150 per year, change of employer EUR 600, change of designation EUR 300, healthcare and elderly-care sector flat EUR 150.
- Agency or consultant service fees on top of government fees: unverified. No published prices found.

## Fit gaps (hypotheses, unverified)
- Agencies sell per-case human service, not a self-serve status tracker or dashboard.
- Likely weak on multi-permit visibility for employers with many TCN staff, and on worker-facing status or expiry reminders in several languages. This is unverified.

## Opening
A cheap self-serve permit and expiry tracker for small employers and workers can undercut per-case agency fees, and agencies could be a distribution channel. Re-check with review-site searches before relying on this.
