# BDO EBS (from MUR 1,850/month), Mauritius

Dropped idea: C0607, MRA e-invoicing (EBS fiscalisation) for Phase 2/3 SMEs.

## Verdict: beatable

BDO's offer is a real, priced, MRA-certified product aimed at SMEs. But it is a thin accounting-firm
service layered on QuickBooks, Xero, Odoo and legacy systems. No complaints were found, and the
market is open to other certified EBS providers.

## Evidence
- A search result summary says BDO offers an MRA-compliant solution from MUR 1,850/month, an
  MRA-certified EBS with real-time fiscalisation and no setup fees. The summary says it works with
  QuickBooks, Xero, Odoo and legacy systems, with go-live in about 5 business days for supported
  systems. Contact details given: ebsmauritius.com, oubindro.ghose@bdo.mu. The source page was not
  opened, so these details are unverified beyond the search snippet.
- MRA rules: EBS providers register after self-certifying against MRA specs on the developer portal
  (https://www.mra.mu/e-invoicing). Many providers can therefore be certified, so there is no
  monopoly.
- Rollout (Edicom, https://edicomgroup.com/blog/mauritius-electronic-invoicing-system): Phase 1
  started 15 May 2024 for turnover above MUR 100m. Phase 2 was planned for 2025-26 for turnover above
  MUR 80m. The Phase 2/3 timing may have changed (unverified).
- Complaints: none found. A search for reviews of ebsmauritius.com or BDO's e-invoicing returned
  nothing relevant. No reviews, app-store listings or outage news were found. This is a valid
  result, not proof of satisfaction.
- Other providers appear in results, such as Webtel (https://webtel.in/E-invoicing-mauritius). I did
  not assess them.

## Pricing
- From MUR 1,850/month, no setup fee (search snippet, unverified). Plan tiers, volume limits and
  extras are unknown.
- This is moderate for a small shop, and likely a floor price for a basic tier.

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified inference)
- It looks like an integration layer over existing accounting packages. A micro-SME without
  QuickBooks, Xero or Odoo, or one using a POS or local billing tool, may not be served.
- No evidence of a mobile or offline app, Creole or French UI, or a self-serve product. Onboarding
  appears to go through an accounting firm contact (a phone and email), not self-serve signup.
- The BDO brand suggests mid-market and audit clients rather than the smallest SMEs (inference).

## Opening
A self-serve, cheaper or free-tier, mobile-first MRA-certified invoicing app for micro-SMEs without
accounting software could undercut BDO's MUR 1,850/month. The MRA certification process is the main
hurdle.
