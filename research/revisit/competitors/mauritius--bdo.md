# Competitor: BDO (Mauritius)

Idea killed: C0609, MRA e-invoicing/fiscalisation connectors. Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and French).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

BDO is an audit and advisory firm. Five searches found no BDO Mauritius e-invoicing product, connector or pricing. Any product claim stays unverified.

## Evidence
- Searches for BDO Mauritius with MRA e-invoicing, fiscalisation, EBS, pricing and advisory (English and French) returned no BDO page. The results were MRA pages, EY, Edicom, ClearTax, Comarch, Orbitax and MCCI material.
- One search summary credited BDO with a QuickBooks/Xero/Odoo e-invoicing solution, "no setup fees" and "5 business days" onboarding. No cited source supports it. The listed sources were Odoo app-store modules (ERP Heritage, Pokutsoft), MRA pages and others. The text looks like a mix-up with those Odoo modules, so it is **unverified** and not counted.
- The real MRA EBS connector competition, seen in results but not BDO:
  - Odoo modules: https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/eh_l10n_mu_einvoicing and https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_mu_ebs_einvoice
  - Sage add-on from Greytrix: https://www.greytrix.com/middle-east/product/other-solutions/e-invoicing-solutions/e-invoicing-for-mauritius-mra/
  - Webtel, which publishes e-invoicing guidance: https://webtel.in/Blog/A-Guide-to-Generating-E-Invoices-as-per-MRA-Guidelines-in-Mauritius/3355
- Complaints: none found about BDO. No app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints about BDO e-invoicing turned up. Only generic MRA mandate information came back.
- Mandate context: https://www.mra.mu/e-invoicing says EBS developers must register, customise, test and self-certify. Rollout is phased by turnover (Phase 1 from 15 May 2024 above MUR 100M; Phase 2 in FY2025-26 above MUR 80M, per Edicom). Smaller firms are encouraged to adopt voluntarily.
