# Greytrix (Mauritius)

Ideas it killed: C0607 (MRA e-invoicing/EBS fiscalisation for Phase 2/3 SMEs), C0609 (MRA e-invoicing/fiscalisation connectors).

## Verdict: beatable

Greytrix is a Sage-partner services firm. Its MRA e-invoicing offer is an add-on for Sage ERP customers. It does not look like a general SME fiscalisation connector. This is based on 3 searches; page contents were not read in full.

## Evidence
- Greytrix lists an "E-invoicing for Mauritius MRA" product. It claims MRA compliance and integration with Electronic Billing Systems (EBS): https://www.greytrix.com/middle-east/product/other-solutions/e-invoicing-solutions/e-invoicing-for-mauritius-mra/
- A separate page, "e-invoicing for Sage ERP Mauritius", ties the product to Sage X3 and Sage 300: https://www.greytrix.com/africa/product/other-solutions/e-invoicing-solutions/e-invoicing-for-sage-erp-mauritius/
- Greytrix describes itself as a certified Sage partner offering implementation, development and integration work (https://www.greytrix.com/sage-x3/solution/). Its core business is consulting on mid-market Sage ERPs.
- Odoo alternatives exist, so other ERPs have competing connectors: ERP Heritage's `eh_l10n_mu_einvoicing` module and a POS module (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/eh_l10n_mu_einvoicing).
- MRA's own e-invoicing page, for context: https://www.mra.mu/e-invoicing
- Complaints: none found. Searches returned only Greytrix marketing and testimonials. No app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints surfaced, and I found nothing in French. No outage or failed-filing reports found.

## Pricing
Not published in anything I found (unverified). Sage X3 and Sage 300 projects are typically sized for mid-market firms, so the cost is probably above what a Phase 2/3 micro or small SME would pay. That is an inference, not a verified price.

## Fit gaps (largely inferred, unverified)
- It is Sage-only (X3 and 300). SMEs on Odoo, Tally, QuickBooks, Excel, custom billing or small POS tools are not covered.
- It is sold through a partner-services model, so small buyers are unlikely to get self-serve onboarding.
- No evidence of French or Creole UI, mobile use, or multi-entity support for small firms.

## Momentum
Greytrix is active: its blog had posts as recently as Sept 2025 and it maintains regional product pages. It is not shut down or stagnant. The MRA-specific product appears to be a small part of its catalogue.

## Opening
A self-serve, ERP-agnostic MRA fiscalisation connector for small non-Sage SMEs (Odoo, POS, spreadsheets) is not served by Greytrix. The Odoo modules are the nearer competitors.
