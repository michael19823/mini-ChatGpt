# Mauritius: MRA EBS provider list (https://www.mra.mu/download/eInvoicing/EBSSolutionProviders.pdf)

Idea killed: C0607 (MRA e-invoicing / EBS fiscalisation for Phase 2/3 SMEs). Searches used: 3 of 6 (English, French).

## Verdict
not-a-real-competitor

The PDF is a directory published by the regulator, not a product. It lists vendors who self-certified an Electronic Billing System (EBS). The real competitors are the listed vendors, which this check did not assess one by one.

## Evidence
- Search results describe the list as EBS Solution Providers who "expressed their willingness" and self-certified compliance with MRA requirements. It is published on the MRA e-Invoicing portal (search snippets citing https://mra.mu/download/eInvoicing/EBSSolutionProviders.pdf and https://www.mra.mu/e-invoicing).
- The snippets name some listed providers: SMARTSOFT LTD (ABS), STREAK TECHNOLOGIES LTD (web-based invoicing), CYBERNAPTICS LTD (Dynamics 365 Business Central). The full list was not read. I had no WebFetch, so the count and the rest of the names are unverified.
- Self-certification means MRA does not vouch for quality, so provider quality probably varies (inference, unverified).
- Timeline per search results (sources differ): Phase 1 started 15 May 2024 for turnover over Rs 100m. Phase 2 was reported as FY2025-26 for turnover over Rs 80m. Phase 3 (Rs 50-80m) and Phase 4 are "to be confirmed" (https://edicomgroup.com/fr/facture-electronique/ile-maurice, https://www.cleartax.com/mu/e-invoicing-mauritius). A KPMG/MRA-linked snippet gave different thresholds (Rs 100m, then 50-100m, then all VAT-registered). Current dates are unverified. Today is 2026-10-05, so Phase 2 may already be live.
- Penalty cited: Rs 10,000 per month for non-compliance (cleartax snippet, unverified).
- Complaints: none found. The searches returned explainers and vendor blogs, with no app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints about EBS providers or outages. This is a null result, not proof that there are none. Local press (lemauricien.com "Adapt or Perish?") and MCCI pages surfaced but were not read.

## Pricing
No pricing found for any listed provider. The list itself carries no prices. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- The list gives no SME-specific fit information. Providers are mostly ERP/accounting vendors (e.g. Dynamics 365 Business Central), which suggests they target larger firms. This is an inference from the three names above, unverified.
- Whether any provider offers a cheap, mobile or offline tool for Rs 50-80m firms and below is unknown.

## Opening
Compliance is mandatory and there is no single incumbent. The opening is a low-cost, SME-focused EBS that gets onto this list, but it would compete with every listed vendor, so check the main vendors' pricing before reviving C0607.
