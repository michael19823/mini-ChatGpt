# Webtel (Mauritius) - competitor check

Ideas it killed: C0607 (MRA e-invoicing/EBS fiscalisation for Phase 2/3 SMEs), C0609 (MRA e-invoicing/fiscalisation connectors).

Research limit: only 1 of 6 searches returned results (the second hit a usage limit). Most points below are unverified.

## Verdict: strong (provisional, low confidence)

Webtel is a real, MRA-listed-looking EBS vendor with a Mauritius-specific product that matches both dropped ideas, including ERP connectors. Evidence is thin because of the search limit.

## Evidence
- Webtel runs a dedicated Mauritius e-invoicing page and several blog posts on MRA e-invoicing and EBS, so it is actively marketing to this market (https://webtel.in/E-invoicing-mauritius ; https://webtel.in/Blog/Understanding-E-Invoicing-in-Mauritius-An-Intermediate-Guide/3437).
- It claims 100% MRA compliance and integration with EBS; offers on-premise and cloud (SaaS); components are an e-invoicing application, a connector to accounting ERPs, and a connector to MRA for real-time fiscalisation (same page). This is vendor marketing, not independently verified.
- The MRA publishes an EBS solution providers list (https://mra.mu/download/eInvoicing/EBSSolutionProviders.pdf). It appeared in results but I did not read it, so whether Webtel is on it is unverified.
- Blogs are recent-looking and numerous, suggesting active marketing; the product's development momentum is unverified. The site is webtel.in, which suggests an India-based compliance vendor serving Mauritius.
- Complaints: none found (app stores, Trustpilot, Reddit, forums, local-language French searches were not run because of the search limit). This means not searched, not proven absent.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (all unverified)
- Likely India-based vendor with no confirmed local Mauritian presence; SME-tier pricing and onboarding unknown.
- Pitches ERP/accounting connectors, so it may favour firms with existing ERPs rather than micro/small businesses using spreadsheets or no software.
- Mobile/offline POS use and French/Creole support not confirmed.

## Opening
A cheap, simple, SME-first EBS (mobile/POS, French-language, local support) for Phase 2/3 firms could still fit if Webtel's pricing or ERP-centric focus is shown to leave small buyers unserved; this needs a follow-up search on pricing and reviews.
