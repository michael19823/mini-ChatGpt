# Aspel (Mexico) - competitor check for C0628 (generic electronic time clocks for SMEs)

## Verdict
not-a-real-competitor (low confidence; evidence thin, searches cut short by a usage limit after 3 of 6 searches)

## Evidence
- Aspel is known in Mexico mainly as accounting/payroll/admin software (e.g. Aspel NOI). One search result (syscom.mx, "Integración de relojes checadores con la nómina: Acceso 3 TITANIUM") describes Aspel NOI as a payroll package that integrates with third-party time-and-attendance products. That suggests Aspel integrates with time clocks rather than selling its own. Source: https://syscom.mx/blog/integracion-de-relojes-checadores-con-la-nomina-acceso-3-titanium (based on search summary only, not read in full; unverified).
- No Aspel-branded time clock or attendance product was found in the search results (unverified; absence in 1 search is not proof).
- The actual attendance competitors in the results are others: GeoVictoria, Factorial, Cegid, Fulltime Reloj Virtual (app, no biometrics, geolocation), plus many hardware clocks on MercadoLibre. These are not Aspel and were not assessed here.
- Complaints: none found. The one search for Aspel NOI complaints returned unrelated companies (Asus, Aspire, Aspen). Finding none here reflects a poor search, not a clean record.

## Pricing

## Fit gaps
- Likely no native time-clock/attendance product; it relies on integrations with other vendors' clocks (unverified).
- Any attendance feature would probably be tied to its payroll suite, which is desktop/licence software aimed at accounting firms (unverified).

## Opening
Aspel does not appear to sell a generic SME time clock, so the opening is a standalone, cheap clock/app that exports to Aspel NOI and similar payroll tools; the real rivals are GeoVictoria, Factorial and similar apps.
