# Buk (Mexico) - competitor check

Killed idea: C0628 Generic electronic time clocks for SMEs.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Buk is a cloud HR/payroll suite with an attendance module. It is not a generic time-clock product, and it is well funded. It competes for the same budget but not for the "simple clock" product.

## Evidence
- Buk Mexico sells attendance control as a module that links to payroll and reports. Source: https://www.buk.mx/control-de-asistencia-laboral
- Capterra shows 4.8/5 from 354 reviews, with praise for ease of use and support. No complaint content was surfaced in the snippets I saw. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/208706/Buk/reviews/
- Funding: US$50M round in Jan 2025 led by Headline, valuation about US$850M. It plans to double its Mexico team. Source: https://www.theclinic.cl/2025/01/06/los-planes-de-buk-la-startup-chilena-de-rrh-en-vias-de-convertirse-en-unicornio-lo-primero-es-sellar-nuestra-llegada-a-brasil/
- Complaints: none found about the attendance or software product. One Indeed Mexico employee review says its Mexican payroll "se queda por debajo" (falls short). Indeed rates Buk 2.8/5 as an employer, which is not a product signal. Source: https://mx.indeed.com/cmp/Buk
- The Mexico-specific pages and any Reddit or forum complaints were not directly read (no WebFetch). The Capterra review text is unverified beyond the search summaries.

## Pricing
- Mexico pricing is not published. I found none.
- The Chilean site gives an example for 350 employees (Essential UF 9.73 + VAT per month, Pro UF 13.63 + VAT per month). The Capterra listing says "starting $95/month". Both are unverified for Mexico. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/208706/Buk/pricing/
- Pricing is per employee and quote-based. The minimum size for Mexico is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- It is a full HR suite sold through demos, aimed at mid-size firms. Very small firms (under about 20 staff) are probably not its focus (unverified).
- Whether it supports a dedicated physical clock or biometric hardware was not verified. Its Mexican payroll depth was criticized in one employee review.

## Opening
Buk does not target a cheap standalone clock for micro and small businesses. Any opening is a low-cost, no-demo, self-serve attendance tool, and Buk's funding means it could move into that space.
