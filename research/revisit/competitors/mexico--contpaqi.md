# CONTPAQi (Mexico)

Dropped idea: C0628, generic electronic time clocks for SMEs.

Search coverage: 3 of 6 WebSearch calls ran. The third (English query) failed on a usage limit, so
momentum and English-language review sites were not checked.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (for this idea)

CONTPAQi is accounting, payroll and ERP software. The search results show it integrating with
third-party time clocks (Syscom's Acceso 3 TITANIUM, per a Syscom blog post) rather than selling its
own. No CONTPAQi-branded time clock was found. It is a payroll back end that a clock product would
feed, not a rival clock.

## Evidence
- Syscom's blog describes integrating time clocks with payroll via Acceso 3 TITANIUM, with
  compatibility named for NOMIPAQ, CONTPAQi Nóminas and iNóminas.
  https://syscom.mx/blog/integracion-de-relojes-checadores-con-la-nomina-acceso-3-titanium
- Runa's integration page lists CONTPAQi as an integration target (page not read in full).
  https://runahr.com/br/integraciones/contpaqi/
- Comparasoftware (a comparison site, secondhand): CONTPAQi Nómina is strong on fiscal precision
  (SUA, IDSE, INFONAVIT, SAT) and preferred by accounting firms. Its weaknesses are local
  installation rather than cloud, and one reviewer's report of critical errors after updates that
  halted operations. This is a single reported complaint, not a pattern.
  https://www.comparasoftware.com/contpaqi-nominas
- Other results (Capterra CONTPAQi Contabilidad, Indeed Mexico, elcontribuyente.mx) appeared but
  their content was not read. Complaint volume is unverified.
- No complaints about time-clock or attendance functionality were found, because CONTPAQi doesn't
  appear to offer any.

## Pricing
- 2026 CONTPAQi price list (PDF) exists; the search summary says annual licensing starts at about
  $8,890 MXN plus per-user network costs. Not verified against the PDF.
  https://www.contpaqi.com/hubfs/Listas%20de%20precios/Lista_de_Precios_Sistemas_CONTPAQi_2026.pdf
- Comparasoftware quotes a basic Nómina license at about $4,390 MXN per user, with extra users at
  $1,390 MXN. Secondhand and possibly dated; unverified.
- Third-party biometric clocks on the Mexican market were summarized as roughly $1,196 to $7,485 MXN
  (from MercadoLibre listings; unverified).

## Fit gaps
- No own time-clock hardware or attendance product found.
- Desktop and local-install heritage, so it is awkward for small firms without IT.
- Payroll tooling aimed at accountants, not owners of micro and small shops who just need
  clock-in.

## Momentum
Actively maintained (2026 price list, recent CFDI Nóminas release posts on elconta.mx). Not
stagnant. Not checked further.

## Opening
A cheap, cloud or mobile clock-in product that exports attendance into CONTPAQi payroll would
complement it rather than compete with it. The real competitors for this idea are the clock vendors
and cloud HR tools (GeoVictoria, Runa, CheckRH, Syscom hardware), not CONTPAQi.
