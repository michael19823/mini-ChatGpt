# Factorial (Mexico) - competitor check for C0628 (generic electronic time clocks for SMEs)

## Verdict: strong

Factorial is an active, Mexico-localized HR suite (factorial.mx) whose time tracking/attendance module is cheap and already targets SMEs. It integrates with third-party biometric clock hardware rather than selling its own, so generic clocks are not blocked, but the software side is well covered.

## Evidence
- factorial.mx has a dedicated control horario / reloj checador page and a turnos-asistencia page; it advertises a "reloj checador con integracion de biometricos" (https://factorial.mx/control-horario, https://factorial.mx/turnos-asistencia-paid).
- Biometric hardware is via integrations, e.g. Phuc and Innux (https://factorial.mx/apps/integracion/phuc, https://factorial.es/apps/integracion/innux).
- Claims Mexico/LATAM compliance, local support, offices in Mexico (search snippet of vendor/aggregator pages; vendor claim, unverified independently).
- Reviews: Capterra 4.4/5 from 312 users (global, not Mexico-specific; per search snippet, Capterra Factorial page). Praise: time tracking easy and intuitive. Complaints seen in snippets: weak/immature payroll module, mixed support responsiveness, limited customization/reporting, some slowness, one G2 complaint about data retrieval after termination (https://capterra.com/p/168685/Factorial-HR-Software/reviews/, https://www.g2.com/products/factorial/reviews). None were specific to time clocks or Mexico.
- Mexico-specific complaints (app stores, Reddit, local forums, outages): none found in the 3 searches run. Not exhaustively searched (search usage was cut off; 3 of 6 used).
- Momentum: actively developed, active Mexican blog/marketing and new integrations; "thousands of clients in 60+ countries" (vendor claim, unverified).

## Pricing
- From $8/user/month (aggregator snippet; currency and whether USD/MXN unverified; time tracking module price not separately verified).
- Promo seen in snippet: Factorial One free first year, 80% discount second year (unverified, may be time-limited).
- Hardware is extra via third-party integrations; price unverified. Minimums not found.

## Fit gaps
- No own hardware: buyer must separately buy/integrate a biometric clock (Phuc, Innux etc.).
- Per-user SaaS pricing may be heavy for very small or high-headcount, low-margin SMEs (unverified).
- Payroll module reported as immature; customization/reporting limited (global reviews).
- Offline operation and IMSS-specific features not verified.

## Opening
Only a bundled, low-cost clock-plus-simple-software offering for very small Mexican businesses that don't want a full HR suite or per-seat SaaS; the software alone is already well served.
