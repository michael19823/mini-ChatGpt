# Mexico: Generic field-service apps (idea C1320, Mexico pest control NOM-256)

## Verdict: beatable

Generic field-service apps (Jobber, ServiceTitan, PestPac and similar) are not built around NOM-256. The one niche, Spanish-language pest-control product the searches surfaced is EviSane, which appears to be Spain-oriented. Whether it covers Mexico's NOM-256 formats is unverified. Search budget note: 5 of 6 WebSearch calls were used and 1 returned a usage-limit error, so coverage is thin.

## Evidence
- NOM-256-SSA1-2012 is in force. Providers need a sanitary licence and a sanitary responsible person, and must give the customer a copy of the "Service Report". Sources: [platiica.economia.gob.mx](https://platiica.economia.gob.mx/normalizacion/nom-256-ssa1-2012/), [normasoficiales.mx](https://www.normasoficiales.mx/nom/nom-256-ssa1-2012). The "Service Report" detail came from a search summary of government procurement documents, so treat it as unverified.
- Capterra Mexico carries a PestPac listing (3.9/5, 255 reviews per search summary) and Jobber is listed at 4.6/5 (1463 reviews). Neither listing was shown to mention NOM-256 or Mexican filing formats. Source: [capterra.mx PestPac](https://www.capterra.mx/software/23130/pestpac).
- EviSane is described on Capterra Spain as a Spanish-language, cloud pest-control and environmental-health platform that generates reports in "official format" and handles legal compliance. It is rated 4.9/5 by 35 users. The searches do not show which country's official format it means (Spain is likely), and Mexican support is unverified. Source: [capterra.es EviSane](https://www.capterra.es/software/179537/evisane).
- The one critique found for EviSane is an initial learning curve and heavy training needed.
- Complaints: no Mexico-specific complaints about generic field-service apps or EviSane were found in the searches. No Reddit, Facebook, Play Store or App Store review data was reached. This is a valid null result, not proof that none exist.
- Momentum: PestPac, ServiceTitan and Jobber are actively marketed (commercial, well funded). EviSane shows a 2026 Capterra presence. No shutdown or acquisition news was found.

## Pricing
- Jobber Core: from $29.00 per user (search summary, unverified currency and period). Source: search result summary.
- EviSane: from EUR 29.90 per user per month, with a free trial (search summary; Capterra Spain). Source: [capterra.es](https://www.capterra.es/software/179537/evisane).
- PestPac and ServiceTitan: no published price found; unverified. Both are typically sold to larger pest operators.
- Compared with a small Mexican fumigator, per-user pricing near US$30 or EUR 30 is modest, so price alone is not a strong opening.

## Fit gaps
- Generic and US-centric tools (Jobber, ServiceTitan, PestPac) show no evidence of NOM-256 service-report templates, cholinesterase-exam tracking, sanitary responsible-person records or COFEPRIS-style documents. Unverified, as only listing-level information was seen.
- Spanish-language and local-format support is probably thin in the US tools. Unverified.
- EviSane may fill the language and compliance gap, but its Mexican coverage is unverified. It is the real risk to this idea.
- Offline and mobile use by field technicians in Mexico: not checked.

## Opening
A Mexico-native tool that outputs the NOM-256 service report and tracks sanitary licences and staff exams, at a small-operator price and in Mexican Spanish, is open unless EviSane already localises for Mexico, which should be checked first.
