# Grant Thornton Mexico (Mexico) - killed C0612 (LFPIORPI avisos and KYC for vehicle dealers)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Grant Thornton Mexico is a big-firm audit/tax/advisory practice. It is not a self-serve product for vehicle dealers. The real competitors for this idea are dedicated PLD software vendors (for example KYC Systems, which has a vehicle-sales page).

## Evidence
- Grant Thornton Mexico lists anti-money laundering and anti-corruption among its advisory services (https://www.grantthornton.mx/en/site-map and search summary of grantthornton.fr fast-facts PDF). Nothing found showing a dealer-specific LFPIORPI product, app or subscription. Unverified beyond that.
- Dedicated vendor exists: KYC Systems has "Software PLD para Venta de Vehiculos" (https://kyc-systems.com/actividades-vulnerables/software-antilavado-vehiculos.html). It is the more direct competitor. I did not inspect its features or pricing.
- Market context: vehicle sales are a vulnerable activity (LFPIORPI art. 17 fr. VIII). Per search summaries, identification starts at 3,210 UMA (about MXN 376,565 in 2026) and the SAT notice at 6,420 UMA (about MXN 753,130). Since July 2025, art. 18 fr. X requires automated transactional profiling and monitoring, which favors software over consulting (https://elconta.mx/enajenacion-automoviles-materia-prevencion-de-lavado-de-dinero/, https://www.cuatrecasas.com/resources/enajenacion-de-vehiculos-uif-redefine-actividad-vulnerable-690d15e91c3a6067710025.pdf).
- Complaints: none found. Searches (3, Spanish and English) turned up no reviews of Grant Thornton Mexico's AML service. Absence of reviews is expected for a private advisory firm.
- Momentum: active, with offices in Mexico City, Monterrey, Querétaro, León, Tijuana and Puerto Vallarta. Careers and people pages are live.

## Pricing
Not published. Engagement-based advisory fees are likely out of reach for a small dealer, but that is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Consulting and advisory, not a productized dealer tool.
- Serves mid-market and large clients (unverified), with no sign of a per-dealer self-serve plan.
- No evidence of automated notice filing or ongoing client monitoring for dealers.

## Opening
A cheap, dealer-specific, software-first LFPIORPI tool (KYC, notices, transactional profiling) is not blocked by Grant Thornton. The competitive check should focus on PLD software vendors such as KYC Systems.

Note: only 3 of 6 searches were used.
