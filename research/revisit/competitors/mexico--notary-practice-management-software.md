# Mexico: notary practice-management software (generic; killed C0611, LFPIORPI avisos and KYC for notaries)

## Verdict: strong

Notary-specific software with UIF alert handling already exists (TotalNot, KYC Systems), and the SAT offers a free filing tool, Declaranot. Only 2 of 6 planned searches returned results (one search hit a usage limit), so evidence is thin.

## Evidence
- TotalNot is notary practice software. Its brochures describe budget, file, protocol and index management, digital document management, third-party expense checks with CFDI, UIF alerts, and the Digital Notarial Seal (SDN). Source: https://www.totalnot.mx/documentacion/formatos/BrochureTotalNot2024.pdf (also the 2022 edition).
- KYC Systems sells "Software PLD para Notarios y Fe Pública", a dedicated anti-money-laundering product for notaries. Source: https://kyc-systems.com/actividades-vulnerables/software-antilavado-fe-publica.html
- Declaranot is the free SAT electronic system notaries use to file LFPIORPI avisos, integrated with the SAT PLD portal. Sources: https://kyc-systems.com/blog/declaranot and https://www.elcontribuyente.mx/2023/01/declaranot-la-herramienta-con-la-que-los-notarios-informan-al-sat/
- Edifact has pages aimed at notaries on SAT matters (https://www.edifact.com.mx/masinfo/sat-notarios), which suggests more vendors in the space. Their contents were not verified.
- Regulatory pressure creates real demand. Reported fines for unfiled avisos run 10,000 to 65,000 UMA. The 2026 real-estate alert threshold is 8,025 UMA. Identification is mandatory with no minimum threshold. Source: kyc-systems.com pages above.
- Complaints: none found. The searches returned no app-store, Reddit, forum or G2/Capterra reviews of these products. One item noted that notaries cannot verify whether failures come from the SAT portal (https://idconline.mx/fiscal-contable/2018/07/30/como-probar-anomalias-en-el-buzon-tributario). That is a general complaint about the SAT portal, not about a vendor. Not searched in depth.

## Pricing
Not found. TotalNot gives a contact route (ventas@totalnot.mx), and no list price was seen. KYC Systems pricing is unverified. Declaranot is free.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence either way on small or rural notarías, mobile or offline use, or multi-notary offices (unverified).
- TotalNot appears to be a full practice suite, so it may be heavy for a notary who wants only KYC and aviso preparation (inference, unverified).

## Momentum
TotalNot published brochures in 2022 and 2024, so it looks maintained. KYC Systems is active, with blog posts on avisos. Both are unverified beyond that.

## Opening
Little. A narrow, cheap KYC/aviso helper would have to beat free Declaranot plus two established vendors, and only a clear small-notaría pricing or usability gap found through real user interviews would justify it.
