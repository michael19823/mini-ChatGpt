# Runa HR (Mexico)

Dropped idea: C0628 (generic electronic time clocks for SMEs)

## Verdict: weak

Runa is a strong payroll/HR platform, but attendance is only an add-on module. It integrates with third-party clocks (e.g. ZKTeco) and sells no clock of its own that was found. It is a weak blocker for a generic SME time-clock product. Caveat: the "biometric clock" in its attendance page may mean a partner device (unverified).

## Evidence
- Runa HR is a cloud payroll platform for Mexican SMEs. Attendance control is offered via app, web and "biometric clock", with overtime calculation and payroll integration (search snippet of https://runahr.com/mx/landing/control-de-asistencias/).
- It integrates with external clocks such as ZKTeco: https://runahr.com/mx/integraciones/zkteco/ and https://runahr.com/en/integraciones/reloj-laboral/
- Ratings: 4.9/5 on Capterra (152 reviews) and 4.9/5 on G2 (79 reviews), per search snippets (https://www.capterra.com/p/194791/Runa/, https://www.g2.com/products/runa). Both are unverified beyond snippets. The 4.9 average on Trustpilot comes from Runa's own site (https://runahr.com/mx/runa-vs-la-competencia/).
- Complaints: none found. Searches (Spanish and English) returned only positive feedback. G2 notes the profile had no new review for about two months (snippet).
- Momentum: active. The site has 2026 content and says it is "ready for Mexico 2027" labor reform.

## Pricing (search snippet of runahr.com pricing, unverified, MXN per employee per month, Pro plan, modular)
- Payroll 32.90; IMSS 7.52; Employees 28.20; Absences 8.46; Attendance 20.68; Document Management 4.70.
- Another snippet mentions 110 MXN per employee per month for software and 250 MXN for payroll outsourcing (unverified).
- Attendance alone is about 20.68 MXN per employee per month, plus any hardware. Hardware pricing was not found.

## Fit gaps
- Attendance is a module of a payroll suite, not a standalone clock product.
- No Runa-branded clock hardware found; relies on partner devices.
- Likely poor fit for micro-businesses that want only a simple clock with no payroll (inference).

## Opening
A cheap, standalone clock, or one bundled with hardware, for SMEs not buying a payroll suite is left open. The risk is that Runa's attendance module is cheap at about 21 MXN per employee per month.
