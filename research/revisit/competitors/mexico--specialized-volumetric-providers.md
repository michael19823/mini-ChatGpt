# Mexico: specialized volumetric providers (SAT volumetric controls, idea C0630)

## Verdict: strong

Established vendors already cover the large fuel-station market. The SAT rules also demand certified hardware and software, which makes entry costly. Any gap is in small or non-station segments, and the evidence found is thin.

## Evidence
- ATIO Group's ControlGAS is described as installed in about 12,000 Mexican gas stations, with up to a third of the market (Xataka Mexico via search snippet; figures vary by source, other pages say 2,700 of 9,200 Pemex-franchise stations or 5,000+ stations, so treat counts as unverified). Source: https://www.xataka.com.mx/otros-1/rastrillo-software-que-gasolineras-mexico-venderian-huachicol-enganando-a-pemex-sat
- ControlGAS is sold in licence tiers: Master, Station and Express (a basic version). Source: https://www.edifact.com.mx/masinfo/control-gas
- 1G Volumetrico is a cloud product for fuel transporters and is listed on Capterra. Source: https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1098230/1G-Volumetrico
- Regime: Annexes 30, 31 and 32 set technical specs, verification services and fuel-quality reports. The obligation also covers non-permit firms with autoconsumption of 75,714 litres a month or more (PwC). Source: https://www.pwc.com/mx/es/impuestos/novedades-fiscales/controles-volumetricos-2025.html
- Since 2022 SAT has not required provider authorization (the 2018 rule was dropped), so many providers can enter. Source: https://energiaadebate.com/sat-no-ha-dado-prorroga-para-cumplir-con-controles-volumetricos-advierte-ampes-a-sus-asociados
- Capacity bottleneck: roughly 60 certifying third parties for more than 13,500 permits, and fewer than 30 calibration labs. Source: same energiaadebate.com article.
- Non-compliance: AMPES says about 40% of its client stations lack compliant controls, and up to 80% of autoconsumption sites do. Fines are cited at 36,000 to 69,000 pesos a month, and up to 5 million pesos for missing a certificate. Source: https://vanguardia.com.mx/noticias/alertan-rezago-contra-huachicol-40-de-gasolineras-sin-controles-y-autoabastos-hasta-80-IK19025915
- Complaints: no user reviews or complaints about specific providers turned up in 6 searches (Spanish and English). Capterra and app-store review content was not visible in the results. None found does not mean none exist.

## Pricing
No published prices found for ControlGAS or other providers. Unverified. Only licence tiers are known, which suggests a basic Express option exists for small stations.

## Fit gaps
- Unverified, but the gap is probably in autoconsumption and transport firms above the 75,714 L/month threshold. Only 1G Volumetrico was found targeting them.
- Certified hardware and calibration remain a barrier for a software-only entrant.
- The high non-compliance rate may reflect cost and service capacity, not a lack of software. This is an inference, not a verified finding.

## Momentum
Active. The Annex updates in 2025 and the continued SAT enforcement keep demand alive. Nothing found on acquisitions or shutdowns.

## Opening
A narrow one: low-cost, compliant volumetric reporting for small stations and autoconsumption firms. It needs a certified partner and pricing evidence to confirm.
