# Symplifica (Mexico) - competitor check

Idea(s) it killed: C1318 (Mexico domestic-worker employers, IMSS).
Searches used: 5 of 6. WebFetch not used.

## Verdict: beatable

A real, funded, active competitor with a Mexico launch, but no public Mexico pricing and no complaint evidence were found. Thin traction signals and the free IMSS tool leave room.

## Evidence
- Colombian startup (founded 2016) that expanded to Mexico as its second market; web platform that handles IMSS affiliation and labor formalization for domestic workers. Top 10 of MassChallenge Mexico. Source: https://www.forbes.com.mx/symplifica-la-app-para-la-gestion-de-las-empleadas-del-hogar/
- Launched a worker-side app (Symplifica Trabajador@s) offering payroll advances, insurance and education. Source: https://www.elfinanciero.com.mx/opinion/de-jefes/2022/10/12/lanzan-app-de-recursos-humanos-para-los-trabajadores-del-hogar/
- Funding: USD 3m pre-series A in late 2021 led by Soros Economic Development Fund, with ALIVE Ventures and InQLab. Source: https://www.lavca.org/feature/deal-case-highlight-alive-ventures-symplifica/
- Search snippet says it has a presence in Mexico and a team of 40 (unverified, no date; may include Colombia staff).
- Glassdoor shows 4.2 from 6 reviews (employee reviews, not customer reviews). Source: https://www.glassdoor.ca/Reviews/Symplifica-Reviews-E4086780.htm
- No shutdown or acquisition news found. Momentum: appears active, but the newest Mexico-specific item found is from 2023; recent activity is unverified.
- Market context: IMSS registration of domestic workers has been mandatory since 2023 and IMSS offers its own online process and fee calculator; IMSS warns of fines up to about 41k pesos (https://www.elimparcial.com/dinero/2026/05/30/imss-recuerda-que-asegurar-a-trabajadoras-del-hogar-es-obligatorio-y-advierte-multas-de-hasta-41-mil-pesos-para-empleadores-que-incumplan/).

## Complaints
None found. Searches in Spanish and English returned no app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of Symplifica Mexico. This is a gap in search coverage, not proof of satisfaction.

## Pricing
Mexico pricing: not found (unverified). Colombia reference only: about COP 16,500 to 29,500 per month depending on days worked (older press, not Mexico). Source: https://lanotaeconomica.com.co/?p=21673 (Colombia).

## Fit gaps
- Mexico plan prices are not public in what was found, so the price comparison for small buyers cannot be made.
- Free IMSS portal covers basic affiliation, so Symplifica must justify a fee through payroll, payments and worker benefits.
- Focus is Colombia first; Mexico looks secondary (unverified).
- No evidence of offline, WhatsApp-first or low-end-device support (unverified).

## Opening
A WhatsApp-first, low-cost IMSS affiliation and payment-reminder service for single-household employers could undercut Symplifica, which has little visible Mexico traction.
