# Worky (Mexico) - killed C0628: Generic electronic time clocks for SMEs

## Verdict: strong

Worky is not a generic hardware clock. It is a funded, actively developed cloud HR, payroll and attendance
platform aimed at operational SMEs in Mexico. It includes a digital time clock with facial recognition.
It is priced low and rated highly. Treat it as a strong competitor to the dropped idea.

## Evidence
- Cloud HR and payroll for Mexican SMEs in operative sectors (manufacturing, retail, logistics). Modules
  include attendance and time clock, payroll, recruitment and benefits, with SAT/IMSS compliance. It
  reports 40,000+ users. Sources: https://www.descubre.vc/worky ; https://www.comparasoftware.com/worky
- Capterra rating is 4.8/5 from about 171 reviews (Worky Nomina 4.9/5 from 19), per search snippets. Reviewers
  praise ease of use, payslip distribution and clock integration, and mention a "zero fines" guarantee.
  Source: https://www.capterra.es/software/195463/worky
- Complaints are few and minor, per the snippets (not verified on the page): slow support response at times,
  and the tablet disconnecting from the platform twice for one reviewer. No outage, filing-failure or news
  complaints found. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/195463/Worky/reviews/?page=2
- Momentum is strong. A $6M Series A led by Atlantico with ADP Ventures was reported in May 2024
  (https://fintech.global/2024/05/15/mexicos-worky-bags-6m-series-a-to-transform-payroll-solutions/).
  A further $3.1M round in Nov 2025 appeared in one search summary; the label and date are unverified and
  conflict with an earlier QED-led $3M round (https://www.fintechfutures.com/?p=15211969).
- No Reddit, Facebook-group or app-store reviews were surfaced in the 4 searches used.

## Pricing
The figures below come from aggregator snippets and are unverified against Worky's own site.
- Roster (attendance): Essential MXN 39/month, Business MXN 50/month. The unit is not stated, probably per employee.
- Human Resources: Standard MXN 79, Advanced 119, Advanced Pro 139 per month.
- Payroll service: custom pricing.
Source: https://softwarefinder.com/hr/worky
This is very cheap for SMEs and leaves little room to undercut.

## Fit gaps
- It is a software suite sold with payroll and HR. A buyer who wants only a standalone, simple clock may be
  pushed to a bundle.
- Facial recognition and tablet use depend on tablet connectivity (the reported disconnects suggest
  offline robustness is a possible weak spot, unverified).
- It targets operative SMEs with enough staff for payroll. Micro-businesses (under about 10 staff) and
  clock-only needs are not clearly served (unverified).
- Support responsiveness was occasionally criticised (a single reviewer).

## Opening
Little. A narrow opening may exist only for clock-only, offline-capable, micro-business tools that Worky's
payroll-centred bundle does not target.
