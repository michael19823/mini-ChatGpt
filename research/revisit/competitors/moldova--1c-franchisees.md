# Moldova: 1C franchisees (commercial, no URL known)

Dropped idea: C0637, e-Factura B2B mandate in general (issuing and ERP integration).

## Verdict: strong (for the ERP-integration part; beatable only for businesses not on 1C)

1C franchisees are entrenched incumbents in Moldovan accounting software. They already ship e-Factura support, and the B2B mandate adds demand for their products. Only 4 of the 6 searches were used.

## Evidence
- Official 1C franchise partners in Moldova include TopSoft and Modern Sistem SRL, selling localized 1C Accounting 8 (1C Contabilitate) on platform 8.3. Source: search result summary, unverified beyond that (no page opened).
- 1C-Account Timbal, a developer of standard Moldovan solutions on 1C Accounting 8, says it is working to let users get e-Factura-format files directly from 1C without converters. Source: https://monitorul.fisc.md/en/auditors_applications/sdayte_elektronnye_deklaracii_pryamo_iz_1s_prosto_i_udobno.html. That page appears to be about declarations, so e-Factura details are partly unverified.
- e-Factura B2B mandate between VAT-registered taxpayers is scheduled from 1 Oct 2026, per https://invoicedataextraction.com/blog/moldova-e-factura-requirements. Today is 2026-10-05, so the mandate is at or just past its start date.
- 1C is deeply embedded in the state sector (many tenders on storage.mtender.gov.md). A news item says Moldova cannot abruptly drop 1C (unverified, title only: "Tofan: Moldova nu poate renunța brusc la programul rusesc 1C", https://logos-pres.md/noutati/tofan-a-declarat-ca-moldova-nu-poate-renunta-la-programul-rus-1c/).
- Complaints: none found. Searches in Romanian and Russian returned no user reviews or forum threads about 1C and e-Factura in Moldova. The one forum hit (forum.infostart.ru) concerned Kazakhstan and is irrelevant. This is an absence of evidence from 4 searches, not proof that there are none.

## Pricing
- No public list prices found for licences or ITS subscriptions.
- One tender document mentions maintenance of 1C Contabilitate Enterprise 8 at 11,400 MDL/month excl. VAT (13,680 with VAT), for a large state customer. It is not representative of small-business pricing. Source: a storage.mtender.gov.md procurement file (exact URL not identified among results; unverified).
- Small-buyer pricing: unknown.

## Fit gaps (mostly inferred, unverified)
- Customers must already run 1C, usually through a franchisee, with per-partner service and update fees.
- Desktop or server product, so little fit for micro-businesses or freelancers who just need to issue invoices.
- Integration quality and update timing depend on each local vendor (Timbal, TopSoft, etc.); this was not verified.
- Geopolitical risk from a Russian-origin platform was raised in the news, but nothing shows it hurting sales.

## Opening
A light, standalone e-Factura issuing and sync tool for micro and small VAT payers not on 1C, or an add-on that works with several accounting packages, is the only gap. Competing head-on for 1C users is weak.
