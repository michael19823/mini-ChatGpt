# Moldova -- AIFactura (idea C0637: e-Factura B2B mandate, issuing and ERP integration)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

## Evidence
- 4 searches (English, Romanian) found no Moldovan product called "AIFactura". Not verified to exist.
  The name may be a mislabel of the state e-Factura system, or an unindexed small vendor.
- "AI Factura" / Factura.ai on Capterra, G2 and SoftwareAdvice is a US/Canada accounts-payable OCR tool by The Deposit Exchange. It is unrelated to Moldovan e-invoicing.
  Sources: https://www.g2.com/products/factura-ai/pricing , https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1019702/facturaai
- The real incumbent is the state e-Factura (SFS taxpayer cabinet). It was voluntary from 2014, mandatory for B2G from 2023, with B2B mandatory from 1 Oct 2026 (pilot Jan 2026).
  Sources: https://www.vatcalc.com/moldova/moldova-2025-mandatory-e-factura-consultation/ , https://www.flick.network/en-md/e-invoicing-moldova-efactura , https://monitorul.fisc.md/en/electronic_services/sia-e-factura-destinaie-avantaje-mod-de-utilizare.html
- Complaints: none found for AIFactura (no product found). Nothing found on ERP/1C integration problems either (searches returned only general mandate guides).
- Momentum: unknown for AIFactura (unverified).

## Pricing
No published pricing found (unverified). The state e-Factura portal itself is free to use as far as the sources show, which sets the price floor for any add-on.

## Fit gaps
Not assessable. No product found. The general gap with the state portal is ERP/accounting integration (XML with digital signature) for SMEs.

## Opening
AIFactura does not block C0637. The real rival is the free state e-Factura portal plus local ERP/accounting vendors (e.g. 1C partners, unverified), so check those instead.
