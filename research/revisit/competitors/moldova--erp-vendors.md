# Moldova: ERP vendors (generic) vs idea C0637 (e-Factura B2B mandate: issuing and ERP integration)

Search budget note: 2 of 4 searches hit a usage limit and returned nothing. Only 2 searches returned usable results, and one of those was mostly about Romania. Evidence is thin.

## Verdict: weak (low confidence)

No specific vendor was identified or checked. The "competitor" is a category, and the strongest force on this idea is the government's free e-Factura platform and API, not any ERP vendor.

## Evidence
- Moldova's e-Factura is run by the State Tax Service (SFS). It was voluntary from 2014, mandatory for B2G from 2023, and is scheduled to be mandatory for B2B from 1 October 2026, after a pilot in January 2026. Sources: https://www.flick.network/en-md/e-invoicing-moldova-efactura , https://rtcsuite.com/moldovas-e-factura-the-2026-pivot-from-choice-to-mandate/ , https://invoicedataextraction.com/blog/moldova-e-factura-requirements
- The platform can be integrated with internal accounting systems through an API, with an Integration Guide for external accounting systems. So ERP vendors can connect, and so can any new entrant. Source: same pages as above (search summary; the guide itself was not read).
- Complaints: none found about Moldovan ERP vendors' e-Factura support. The one complaints search returned only Romanian (ANAF RO e-Factura) results, which are a different country and system, and I do not count them as Moldovan evidence.
- Names of local ERP vendors (for example 1C partners) and their e-Factura modules: unverified, not found in the searches.
- AmCham Moldova published proposals on e-Factura to SFS in 2020 (https://www.amcham.md/st_files/2020/06/24/74_2020_AmCham_propuneri_E_Factura_SFS.pdf). I did not read it and cannot say what it says.

## Pricing
Not found. No published ERP or e-Factura module prices for Moldova were found (unverified).

## Fit gaps
Not verified. Plausible but unconfirmed: large ERPs are aimed at mid-size and large firms, so micro and small firms with a spreadsheet, a simple invoicing tool or no software may be underserved. This is an assumption, not a finding.

## Opening
Possible niche is a cheap e-Factura issuing and API-connector tool for micro and small firms, but it competes with the free SFS portal and needs a follow-up search for named Moldovan vendors and complaints before reviving.
