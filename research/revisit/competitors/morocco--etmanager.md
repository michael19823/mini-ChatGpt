# ETmanager (Morocco) - dropped idea C0655: Law 69-21 (payment deadlines) for single companies

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

ETmanager could not be verified as an existing product in Morocco. Five WebSearch calls (French and English) returned nothing about it.

## Evidence
- "ETmanager Maroc logiciel comptabilité" returned no mention of ETmanager. The Moroccan accounting tools named were Sage, KHABIR, Cegid, EBP, Experio and ComptaCom. Source: https://upsilon-consulting.com/logiciel-de-comptabilite-que-choisir/
- `"ETmanager" software` matched only unrelated products: E Transport Manager (UK crane-hire system), the "etm" Event and Task Manager, and ETAP. None relates to Morocco or Law 69-21.
- The searches for ETmanager together with Law 69-21 and SIMPL returned no ETmanager page. They returned only general coverage of the law.
- Complaints: none found, because no reviews or forum posts about ETmanager turned up.
- Momentum: unverified. No product page, release or company was found.
- Vendors that do address Law 69-21:
  - Sage Maroc launched a compliance solution (reporting, automation, electronic declaration) in October 2023. Sources: https://medias24.com/2023/10/27/sage-maroc-lance-sa-solution-de-conformite-sur-les-delais-de-paiement-de-la-loi-69-21/ and https://fr.hespress.com/337076-337076.html
  - The Ministry of Finance runs a supplier-complaints platform for public bodies. Source: https://www.finances.gov.ma/fr/Pages/plateforme-dediee-reclamations-fournisseurs.aspx
- Law context: declarations are quarterly or annual, filed electronically via SIMPL, with a detailed invoice statement certified by an auditor or accountant. The law applies to firms with turnover above 2M MAD. Penalties run from 5,000 to 250,000 MAD. Sources: https://upsilon-consulting.com/loi-69-21-delais-paiement/ and https://upsilon-consulting.com/delais-de-paiement-au-maroc/
