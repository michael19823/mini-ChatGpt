# Hisab (hisab.ma), Morocco

Dropped idea: C0653, DGI e-invoicing compliance (medium and large firms).

## Verdict: weak (for the medium/large segment); strong only against the small-business segment

Hisab targets SMEs, freelancers (auto-entrepreneurs) and accounting firms. It is not aimed at medium and large firms. For C0653 it is therefore a partial, adjacent competitor, not a direct one. Confidence is low: only 3 of 6 searches ran (1 failed on a usage limit), and no primary pages could be opened.

## Evidence
- Capterra listing describes Hisab as a Moroccan invoicing platform that creates DGI-ready invoices in French and Arabic, calculates VAT and exports UBL 2.1 XML. Source: https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1102799/Hisab
- Features reported by the search summaries: French, Arabic and English invoices; ICE, IF and VAT fields filled in automatically; sequential numbering; quotes, credit notes, recurring invoices, expenses, payment tracking; multi-organization dashboard for accounting firms and groups; bulk import. Source: Capterra pages above (search summary, not read directly).
- Context: Morocco's e-invoicing mandate is expected around 2026, and Sage is marketing to Moroccan firms for it (https://medias24.com/2026/04/16/sage-accompagne-les-entreprises-marocaines-dans-leur-preparation-a-la-facturation-electronique-1660745/). Established ERP vendors are the more relevant competition for large firms.
- Complaints: none found. Searches (French and English) surfaced no app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of Hisab. The Capterra listing showed no substantive reviews in the snippets. No news on outages or failed filings found.
- Momentum: unverified. A live Capterra listing and DGI-ready positioning suggest active development, but no release or funding data was found.

## Pricing
- Capterra lists a starting price of US$15.00, flat-rate, with a free trial. Tier details and MAD prices were not found (unverified). Cheap for small buyers; not overpriced.

## Fit gaps (for the medium/large buyer)
- Positioned at SMEs, freelancers and accountants, not large firms (inferred from positioning).
- Unverified: ERP/accounting integration, direct DGI platform transmission (it exports UBL 2.1 XML; whether it transmits to DGI was not confirmed), approval workflows, SSO, and ERP connectors for large firms.
- Multi-entity support exists via the multi-organization dashboard, so that is not a gap.

## Opening
Medium and large firms needing ERP-integrated DGI e-invoicing (connectors, audit, volume) are not clearly served by an SME invoicing tool; Hisab does not block C0653 there, but the small-business end is contested.
