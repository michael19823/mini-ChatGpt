# Morocco: international e-invoicing players (generic) vs C0653 (DGI e-invoicing compliance, medium and large firms)

## Verdict: weak

No international vendor was found with a Morocco-specific certified product. The DGI runs a state clearance platform, so there is no commercial market yet for third-party clearance. The opening is a software layer (ERP connector and UBL formatting) rather than a competing platform. Searches used: 4 of 6.

## Evidence
- The DGI mandate rests on Article 145-9 of the General Tax Code (2018 Finance Law). The model is centralised clearance with UBL 2.1 / CII formats. Sources: [Edicom](https://edicomgroup.com/fr/blog/maroc-obligation-facturation-electronique), [Comarch](https://www.comarch.com/trade-and-services/data-management/legal-regulation-changes/morocco-confirms-2026-mandate-for-electronic-invoicing/), [Banqup](https://www.banqup.com/resources/blog/e-invoicing-in-morocco-a-digital-leap-towards-tax-compliance).
- Reported phases are large IS-liable firms on 1 Jan 2026, mid-size on 1 Jul 2026, and small firms with turnover under 10M MAD on 1 Jan 2027. Source: [Upsilon](https://upsilon-consulting.com/facturation-electronique-maroc-2026/), [Le360](https://fr.le360.ma/economie/facturation-electronique-le-detail-du-calendrier-de-mise-en-place_2EQSNG2DTVB6PGBBHQ6OCFT6CY/).
- The sources disagree on status. One summary (Upsilon Arabic page) says the implementing decree went to the General Secretariat of the Government in April 2026 and was not yet published. Another says dates depend on that decree. Whether the mandate is actually live as of Oct 2026 is unverified.
- The DGI platform is built by the local firm xHub. The DGI is the initial operator and clearance agent. Certified Service Providers are said to come in later phases (one source, unverified).
- Edicom, Comarch, Banqup and a SAP partner (SNI Technology) publish Morocco explainer content. This is advisory and marketing content, and I found no confirmed Morocco-certified product from them.
- A search naming Sovos, Avalara, Pagero and Basware found nothing tying them to Morocco.
- Sage Morocco has blog content on the mandate (a local ERP vendor, so likely to bundle compliance). Its product capability is unverified.

## Complaints
None found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of any international vendor's Morocco product turned up in French, English or Arabic searches. This is because no such product was confirmed, not proof that users are satisfied.

## Pricing
No published prices found for any international vendor in Morocco (unverified / none).

## Fit gaps
- No confirmed Morocco-certified connector from the international players. They are advisory-led and enterprise-oriented.
- Likely enterprise-only focus (assumption, unverified). Mid-size firms are the target for C0653.
- Local ERPs (Sage Maroc and others) and the DGI's own portal will likely cover many firms.
- Rules are in flux (decree and Certified Service Provider regime unclear), so international vendors may wait.

## Opening
A cheap, French/Arabic-language UBL/CII ERP connector and compliance layer for mid-size firms, before the CSP regime is defined. The main risk is that the DGI portal and local ERPs (Sage, xHub) make it unnecessary.
