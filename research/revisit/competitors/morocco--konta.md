# KONTA (Morocco) - competitor check

Killed idea: C0655 (Law 69-21, single companies). 4 WebSearch calls used (French and English).

## Verdict: weak

KONTA is an accounts-payable (supplier invoice) automation / small-business finance tool. Nothing found shows it issues or transmits Law 69-21 / DGI e-invoices (UBL) for single companies. It is adjacent, not a direct competitor.

## Evidence
- Casablanca fintech "Kontatech", founded 2019 by Issam Dahman, El Arbi Aboussoror and Mehdi Zirari; AI capture and audit of supplier invoices, approval workflows, accounting integration, plus a mobile payment account for freelancers and small businesses. Sources: https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/solutions/maroc-konta-utilise-l-ia-pour-numeriser-les-factures-fournisseurs-des-entreprises ; https://www.cbinsights.com/company/konta
- Another source describes it as a SaaS invoice-to-pay platform (attributed there to "Mosaiclab"): https://bcrpub.com/news/moroccan-start-launches-invoice-processing-platform/ . The company naming is inconsistent across sources (unverified).
- Funding round reported (Azur Innovation Fund): https://www.ecoactu.ma/kontatm-levee-de-fonds-azur-innovation-fund/ (details not read).
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Trustpilot, Capterra, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced. This does not mean there are none.
- Mandatory e-invoicing in Morocco is expected in 2026, UBL format, DGI-led (https://edicomgroup.com/fr/blog/maroc-obligation-facturation-electronique). I found no sign of KONTA supporting it (unverified).

## Pricing
Not published in anything found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Focus is payables (incoming supplier invoices), not issuing compliant sales e-invoices.
- No evidence of DGI/UBL transmission or 69-21 compliance (unverified).
- Aimed at SMEs and finance teams, not tiny single-company filers, though that is not confirmed.

## Opening
A cheap, compliance-first issuing and DGI-transmission tool for micro and single companies is not covered by KONTA, based on public information found.
