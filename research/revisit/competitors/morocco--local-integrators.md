# Local integrators (Morocco) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0653, DGI e-invoicing compliance (medium and large firms). No single competitor URL was known (type generic). Searches used: 4 of 6 (French, English, Arabic; the 4th was French). Results were mostly regulatory explainers and vendor blogs; no review-site or forum content surfaced.

## Verdict: beatable

"Local integrators" is a loose category, not one product. The state's own platform is being built by a Moroccan firm (xHub) and ERP and consulting vendors are already marketing readiness services. But the mandate's timing is unsettled and no product or pricing for the medium segment was found, so the space is not closed.

## Evidence
- The DGI picked the Moroccan software firm xHub as technology partner for the national platform, with a reported budget of 6.3 million MAD and a 12-month build from October 2024 (reported by search summary of TelQuel / Medias24 coverage). Sources: https://telquel.ma/2024/12/05/2026-lannee-du-big-bang-de-la-facturation-electronique-au-maroc_1907224 ; https://medias24.com/2026/04/16/dgi-facturation-electronique-lancement-en-preparation-les-details-avec-younes-idrissi-kaitouni-1660967
- The model is described as clearance (prior validation by the DGI platform), with a differentiated setup: simple tools for small firms and automated integrations for large ones. The mandate also reportedly allows delegated invoicing through certified service providers (CSPs). Sources: https://www.comarch.com/trade-and-services/data-management/legal-regulation-changes/morocco-confirms-2026-mandate-for-electronic-invoicing/ ; https://upsilon-consulting.com/en/electronic-invoicing-morocco-2026/
- Players visibly marketing in Morocco on this topic: Sage (fr-ma blog), Inyad (invoicing software blog, calendar and software pages), Upsilon Consulting (guide), SNI Technology (SAP partner), Comarch and Banqup (international e-invoicing vendors). Whether any is a certified provider is unverified. Sources: https://www.sage.com/fr-ma/blog/facturation-electronique-maroc-2026/ ; https://inyad.com/blog/logiciel-facturation-electronique-maroc ; https://snitechnology.net/morocco-plans-for-mandatory-e-invoicing-by-2026 ; https://www.banqup.com/fr-fr/resources/blog/e-invoicing-in-morocco-a-digital-leap-towards-tax-compliance

Some say wave 1 (over 50M MAD revenue) from January 2026 and wave 2 (10 to 50M MAD) from 1 July 2026; a later search summary says rollout is progressive "from the end of 2026" and that the measure was meant to apply this year. Treat all dates as unverified until checked against the DGI. - Complaints: none found about any specific integrator. No app-store, Reddit, Facebook or forum reviews appeared. This is absence of evidence, not proof of satisfaction. An Arabic Hespress report on challenges of e-invoices in tax-fraud control exists but was not read (title only): https://www.hespress.com/%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%B5%D8%AF-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86-1469855.html
- Momentum: the government platform is in preparation and vendors are actively publishing; no shutdown or acquisition found.

## Pricing
Not found. No integrator or vendor price list, per-invoice fee or minimum surfaced. Unverified. Integration for large ERP estates is likely project-priced (inference, not sourced).

## Fit gaps
All inferred, none confirmed:
- ERP vendors and consultancies (SAP partners, Sage) likely favour large and ERP-using firms; mid-size firms with bespoke or legacy accounting may be underserved.
- No evidence found of a ready, DGI-certified, low-cost connector for mid-size firms.
- Rules and the certified provider (CSP) regime are still moving, which creates delivery risk for any entrant and incumbent alike.

## Opening
A fixed-price, quick-to-deploy DGI connector for mid-size firms on non-SAP or legacy accounting tools is plausibly open, but it depends on the CSP certification rules and final dates, which need checking with the DGI before committing.
