# Sage (Morocco) - https://www.sage.com/fr-ma/

Ideas it killed: C0653 (DGI e-invoicing compliance for medium/large firms), C0655 (69-21 payment-delay compliance for single companies).

## Verdict: strong (for mid/large firms, C0653); beatable (for single/small companies, C0655)

## Evidence
- Sage Maroc markets DGI e-invoicing readiness: Sage 100, Sage Business Cloud X3 and other products are described as configured for DGI requirements. Sources: https://medias24.com/2026/04/16/sage-accompagne-les-entreprises-marocaines-dans-leur-preparation-a-la-facturation-electronique-1660745/ and https://medias24.com/2026/04/11/abdellah-marrakchi-sage-la-fiscalite-numerique-peut-devenir-un-veritable-levier-de-competitivite-pour-les-pme-marocaines-1658089/. The search summary is a vendor claim. I did not verify certification or a live DGI filing format.
- Sage Maroc launched a law 69-21 payment-delay compliance solution in Oct 2023, with reporting, automation and "Intuit-EDI" tele-declaration. Sources: https://telquel.ma/2023/10/24/sage-maroc-lance-sa-solution-de-conformite-sur-les-delais-de-paiement-de-la-loi-69-21_1837365 and https://medias24.com/2023/10/27/sage-maroc-lance-sa-solution-de-conformite-sur-les-delais-de-paiement-de-la-loi-69-21/
- Sage's own survey (reported by TelQuel) found 80% awareness of 69-21 overall, 93% in large firms and 41% in TPE. This suggests small firms are underserved. Source: https://fr.hespress.com/337076-337076.html
- The law covers firms with turnover above 2M MAD, so many small companies are in scope. Same Hespress source.
- Momentum: active. It issued e-invoicing and 69-21 press and blog content in 2023-2026, and it runs a Sage Maroc blog on these laws.

## Complaints
No Morocco-specific complaints found (no Trustpilot, app-store, forum or news items on outages or failed filings for Sage Maroc). Generic Sage 100 reviews on Capterra FR (4.1/5, 404 reviews) show ease of use 3.9, support 3.8 and value for money 3.8, which is mediocre but not damning. They are not Morocco-specific. Source: https://www.capterra.fr/reviews/156947/sage-100cloud. Also a Sage Online (FR) access incident in April 2025 (France, not Morocco).

## Pricing
No Morocco pricing is published (unverified). Generic US/UK figures: Sage 50cloud from about $61.92/month, Sage Business Cloud Accounting from about $15/user/month (https://g2.com/products/sage-50cloud-accounting/pricing). These are not applicable to Morocco. Sage 100 and X3 are sold via integrators, with implementation costs. This is likely heavy for micro or single companies, but unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Sage's Moroccan focus is on PME/ETI (the fr-ma page for "moyennes et grandes entreprises"), with ERP-based, integrator-led deployment. This is a poor fit for single companies, freelancers and TPE that don't run Sage.
- The 69-21 solution is tied to Sage's own stack, so firms on other accounting tools (Excel, Odoo, local software) are not covered. Inference, not verified.
- No evidence found of a lightweight standalone 69-21 declaration tool from Sage.

## Opening
Medium/large e-invoicing (C0653) is well covered, so a challenger would need a different angle. A cheap, software-agnostic 69-21 and DGI tool for single or small companies (C0655) stays open, because Sage targets its own installed base of larger firms.
