# Mozambique: Payroll vendors (idea C1329, small-employer INSS)

## Verdict: weak

"Payroll vendors" is a generic category, not one product. Searches (5 of 6 used, English and Portuguese) found no Mozambique-specific payroll product aimed at small employers. The vendors found are global EOR/payroll outsourcers and a small Odoo add-on. None targets the 45-50% of ~200k firms that don't pay INSS regularly.

## Evidence
- Global managed-payroll providers list Mozambique: Ontop (https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/mozambique), Playroll (https://playroll.com/employer-of-record/mozambique), TopSource Worldwide (https://topsourceworldwide.com/global-payroll/mozambique), Mercans (https://dsr.mercans.com/?p=859). These are aimed at multinationals and employer-of-record use, not local micro or small firms. (Segment fit inferred from the positioning; unverified.)
- Mercans says it has direct relationships with AT and INSS and models the INSS rates of 4% employer and 3% employee (https://dsr.mercans.com/?p=859).
- Odoo community add-on "Mozambique Payroll IRPS" by Pokutsoft, listed at $62.00 (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/l10n_mz_payroll). It computes IRPS and INSS and produces a salary map and remittance summary. It still needs an Odoo install and does not appear to file or pay anything.
- PHC Software has an ISCAM protocol in Mozambique (https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-phc-software-activates-protocol-with-iscam/). That is an academic tie-up. Whether PHC, Sage or Primavera sell a Mozambique payroll module with INSS output is unverified.
- The non-compliance problem persists despite the vendors:
  - INSS says 45-50% of ~200,000 registered firms pay regularly (https://aimnews.org/2026/03/05/inss-intensifica-mobilizacao-para-empresas-canalizarem-contribuicoes-dos-trabalhadores/).
  - Contributor debt exceeds 6.1 billion MZN, and a penalty-forgiveness regime was set by decree 20/2025 (same source, plus https://www.rtp.pt/noticias/economia/empresas-de-maputo-devem-mais-de-36-me-a-seguranca-social-mocambicana_n1674268).
- INSS runs its own SISSMO platform and is modernising it. Employers must pay by the 15th of the following month (aimnews.org results). The searches did not confirm details of its employer submission function.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced for any Mozambique payroll vendor.
- Momentum: unverified for all of them. The global EOR brands are active. The Odoo module is listed for Odoo 18 and 19, so it is maintained.

## Pricing
- Odoo module: $62.00 (listed), plus the Odoo hosting or licence cost, which is unverified.
- Global providers: no published prices found. Enterprise or EOR pricing is likely out of reach for small firms (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- No local, low-cost, mobile-first payroll plus INSS tool for micro and small employers was found.
- The global providers are EOR or outsourcing and cater to foreign firms.
- The Odoo add-on calculates only. There is no evidence of INSS filing or payment, mobile money or reminders.
- Note: this is a limited search. Unverified whether local accounting firms or software houses cover this segment informally.

## Opening
A cheap, simple INSS and IRPS calculator-and-reminder tool for small Mozambican employers has no clear incumbent. The risk is that the cause of non-payment is cash flow and enforcement, not software, and that INSS's own SISSMO platform may absorb the filing step.
