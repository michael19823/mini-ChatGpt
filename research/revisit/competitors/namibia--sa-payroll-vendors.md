# Namibia -- SA payroll vendors (C0666: Payroll / PAYE)

## Verdict: strong

Mature, locally supported payroll products already cover Namibian PAYE/SSC, and the searches found no complaints to exploit.

## Evidence
- Sage Pastel Payroll ships regular Namibia-specific updates (FNB Namibia bank file layout, SSC reports, PAYE reporting fixes) per its release-history PDFs: https://communityhub.sage.com/cfs-file/__key/communityserver-discussions-components-files/382/Sage-Pastel-Payroll-_2600_-HR-2025-Update-2a-_2D00_-History-of-Changes.pdf (actively maintained, 2025 update).
- PaySpace publishes Namibia payroll content and sells there: https://payspace.com/blog/payroll-software-in-namibia-why-you-need-it
- Premium Pay (cloud payroll for Namibian SMEs to large firms; Grans IT with Namibia-based support team): https://www.gransnamibia.com/?p=13954
- Mercans covers Namibia payroll (global provider): https://dsr.mercans.com/?p=862
- Africa HR Solutions offers outsourced payroll in Namibia, claims 400+ clients: https://africa-hr.com/Namibia-payroll-outsourcing/
- A payslip product listing mentions Afrikaans support (TrustRadius): https://www.trustradius.com/products/payslip/details (unverified whether it covers Namibia).
- Complaints: 3 searches (English + Afrikaans term) found no customer complaints, reviews or outage/failed-filing news. No complaints found; this is not proof of satisfaction.
