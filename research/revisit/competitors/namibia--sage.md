# Namibia -- Sage (Sage Pastel Payroll, Sage VIP, Sage 300 People)

Idea killed: C0665 PAYE/IT14E and SSC payroll filing. Searches: 5 of 6 used (English only; no useful local-language hits for Namibia, where English is the business language).

## Verdict: strong (for firms that already run Sage payroll); beatable only for micro/small firms using spreadsheets or no payroll software

## Evidence
- Sage supports Namibia as a tax country in Sage Pastel Payroll and Sage VIP; the Namibian reported Sage Pastel as statutorily compliant there (https://www.namibian.com.na/sage-pastel-payroll-software-increases-african-footprint).
- Sage VIP has a Namibia Monthly Employee Tax (ETX) report that writes an export file (ETX_<MonthYYYY>.xls) plus a reconciliation report, for NamRA submission (https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-vip-payroll-hr/f/general-discussion/124328/namibia-monthly-employee-tax-etx-report-for-release-5-2a). Sage 300 People has a Namibia ETX return statutory update (https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-300-people/f/announcements/193894/statutory-update-namibia-employee-tax-etx-return---tax-deducted).
- A Sage Pastel Payroll update added a Namibia SSC Stimulus Package report (per search summary; source page: Sage Pastel Payroll "History of Changes" PDFs on communityhub.sage.com).
- Filing itself is still done by the employer on the NamRA ITAS portal; Sage produces files/reports. Whether Sage directly generates IT14E/IT12E in ITAS-ready format: unverified.
- Sage community forum has a user asking when the changed Namibia tax-table patch would be available (https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-payroll-professional/f/general-discussion/218645/namibia-new-tax-tables/552748), suggesting tax-table updates can lag. Date and outcome not verified. Another thread mentions an issue affecting Namibia companies licensed for the Employment Equity module (details not visible).
- Complaints: no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or local-press complaints about Sage in Namibia found. Only the two forum threads above.
- Momentum: statutory updates for Namibia appear regularly over several years (2020-2023 update PDFs, ETX and SSC reports); actively maintained. The listed sage.com/fr-ma page is Morocco/French and not relevant to Namibia.

## Pricing
- No Namibia-specific price found (unverified). Sage sells via local partners; contact required.
- South Africa reference only: Sage Payroll "from R90/month", Sage 50cloud Pastel "from R730/month" (search summary; not Namibian pricing, not verified on page).

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified inference)
- Output is spreadsheet/report exports, not clearly direct ITAS or SSC portal submission; the employer still re-keys or uploads manually.
- Desktop-heavy products (Pastel, VIP) and South Africa-centric; Namibia is a secondary tax country with patch-based updates.
- Full payroll suite is more than a business needs if it only wants filing/reconciliation.
- No evidence on mobile/offline or multi-entity limits.

## Opening
A lightweight, ITAS/SSC-ready filing and reconciliation layer (IT14E/IT12E, monthly PAYE, SSC) that works with any payroll or spreadsheet could serve small employers not on Sage, but Sage users already have ETX/SSC exports, so the gap is narrow there.
