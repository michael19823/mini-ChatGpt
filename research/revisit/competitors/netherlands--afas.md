# AFAS (Netherlands), competitor for C0679 (Wtta staffing compliance, agencies and inleners)

Searches used: 5 of 6 (Dutch and English). Search results were thin; most claims below are unverified beyond what is cited.

## Verdict: beatable

AFAS is a large Dutch ERP/HR/payroll suite (Profit) with a staffing-sector module ("Uitzendbranche (Flex)"). No evidence was found that it ships Wtta licence-compliance, inlener-checking or register-verification features. It is a general suite, not a Wtta compliance tool, so it leaves the compliance-workflow niche mostly open.

## Evidence
- AFAS Profit has a documented Flex module for the temporary-staffing sector (hours declarations leading to payslip and customer invoice). Source: https://help.afas.nl/help/NL/SE/Pro_Flx.htm
- Searches for AFAS plus Wtta returned no AFAS Wtta feature, page or announcement. Only Wtta explainers from law and advisory firms surfaced (e.g. https://www.stibbe.com/nl/publications-and-insights/de-wtta-introduceert-toelatingsstelsel-voor-uitzendarbeid-ook-inleners). Absence in search results is not proof of absence. Unverified.
- Wtta context: adopted by the Eerste Kamer on 11 Nov 2025; licensing replaces Waadi registration; in force 1 Jan 2027; licence applications 1 May to 30 Jun 2027 (https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/004501/2025/11/wet-toelating-terbeschikkingstelling-van-arbeidskrachten-aangenomen). Inleners must check a public register and can be liable or fined (https://www.grantthornton.nl/insights/themas/wtta-terbeschikkingstelling-van-arbeid/wtta-voor-opdrachtgevers/). That inlener duty falls outside a payroll/ERP vendor's core.
- Complaints: no substantive customer complaints about AFAS found. Capterra lists AFAS Software at 4.2/5 from 9 users (https://capterra.com/p/186725/AFAS-Software/); review text was not retrieved. A search snippet said the Profit 1 rollout "did not go smoothly for all customers", but no primary source was seen. Unverified.
- Outages: status-tracker entries exist for bank-coupling outages in March and April 2026 and an old 2014 AFAS Online disruption (https://pingoru.io/providers/afas/incidents/615929). Minor and not Wtta-related.
- A Radar forum thread titled "Misleiding AFAS boekhoudsoftware" exists (https://radar-forum.avrotros.nl/viewtopic.php?p=3025242). Content not read. Unverified.

## Pricing
Not found. AFAS publishes no verified price in the results seen. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- Serves its own customers' payroll/HR/finance data; not a standalone Wtta tool for agencies that run other systems.
- No sign of an inlener-side product (supplier licence-register checks, chain-liability monitoring) for companies that are not AFAS customers.
- Likely too heavyweight as a purchase just for Wtta compliance.

## Momentum
Actively developed (Profit 1 renewal, 2026 status entries show ongoing operation). No acquisition or shutdown seen.

## Opening
A lightweight, ERP-agnostic Wtta tool (licence-register checks for inleners, licence-application readiness for small agencies) is not covered by AFAS, but a Wtta add-on in AFAS or other payroll suites before 2027 is a risk to watch.
