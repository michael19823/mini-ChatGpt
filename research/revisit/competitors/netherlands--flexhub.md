# Flexhub (Netherlands) - https://flexhub.nl/wtta/

Idea it killed: C0679 Wtta staffing compliance (agencies and inleners).

## Verdict: weak

Flexhub is a backoffice outsourcing and consulting provider for staffing entrepreneurs. Its Wtta page is
content marketing and lead generation, not a compliance product. Searches found no Wtta software or
self-service tool from Flexhub, and nothing aimed at inleners.

## Evidence
- Search result titles show flexhub.nl/wtta/ ("Wtta 2027: regels, eisen en stappen voor uitzendbureaus") and a
  knowledge base: Wtta stappenplan, SNA-keurmerk and Wtta, Wtta and foreign agencies, 2026 rule changes.
  Source: https://flexhub.nl/wtta/ , https://flexhub.nl/kennisbank/wtta-stappenplan-wat-moet-je-regelen-voor-2027/
- A directory profile describes Flexhub as a backoffice service provider: backoffice outsourcing, help starting an
  agency, and reselling staffing software (ATS and backoffice packages). It claims 20+ years in the flex industry.
  Source: https://mtsprout.nl/partners/bedrijfsprofiel/flexhub-de-backoffice-dienstverlener-voor-flexondernemers-met-ambitie
- Content is aimed at agencies and detachers (uitzendbureau and detacheringsbureau start-up pages). Nothing found
  for inleners checking suppliers against the Wtta register.
- Another Wtta-specific vendor surfaced: Beeple ("Wtta ready with Beeple"), https://www.beeple.eu/es/post/wtta-ready-with-beeple-from-compliance-pressure-to-a-workable-process .
  Not researched further (search budget); it is a separate competitor to check.
- Context: Wtta takes effect 1 Jan 2027, transition registration 1 Nov to 31 Dec 2026, enforcement from 1 Jan 2028.
  Fines up to EUR 90,000 per violation were reported in search results (Kiwa, ABU guide), which is demand context, not Flexhub evidence.

## Complaints
None found. Searches for Flexhub reviews returned only unrelated products (FlexiHub USB software, Indeed Flex).
No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of Flexhub were found. 5 of 6 searches used.

## Pricing
No published price found for Wtta help (unverified; likely quote-based backoffice services). No pricing seen in
search results.

## Fit gaps
- No Wtta software or tool found; appears to be advice plus outsourced backoffice services.
- No inlener-side offering found (supplier checks, register monitoring, chain liability).
- Service model likely does not suit very small agencies or inleners wanting a cheap self-service tool (unverified).

## Momentum
Actively publishing Wtta content in 2026 (knowledge base articles on Wtta, SNA, 2026 regulation changes).
Acquisition or shutdown: nothing found.

## Opening
A self-service, low-cost Wtta readiness and supplier-check tool, especially for inleners, is not covered by Flexhub's
advice-and-outsourcing model; the real rival to check is Beeple.
