# Competitor: quality assurers tools (Netherlands)
Killed idea: C0685 Wkb consumer dossier. Searches used: 5 of 6 (Dutch and English).

## Verdict: weak
The "competitor" is a generic category, not one named product. Searches found no named product that is clearly focused on the consumer dossier (consumentendossier). The tools that appeared are inspection/quality-assurance software for kwaliteitsborgers, and no specific product, price or reviews could be verified.

## Evidence
- The Wkb has applied in phases since 1 Jan 2024. The contractor must hand over a consumentendossier at completion. Sources: https://iplo.nl/regelgeving/regels-voor-activiteiten/technische-bouwactiviteit/kwaliteitsborging/privaatrecht-wet-kwaliteitsborging/consumentendossier-opleverdossier-wkb/
- Kiwa and NVKL material says digital inspection lists can be imported into inspection software, and that several software providers exist. No provider names were captured. Sources: https://www.kiwa.com/nl/nl/specials/wkb/hoe-kunt-u-in-de-praktijk-werken-met-de-wkb/ and https://nvkl.nl/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/NVKL-Factsheet-Wkb.pdf
- The tools are approved "instruments" for kwaliteitsborgers. The municipality checks that the tool is approved for the relevant consequence class. Their purpose is the quality assurer's risk assessment, not the contractor's consumer dossier.
- Autarco has an article on its software and the new construction law, which suggests niche vendors such as solar installers. Contents not read. Source: https://www.autarco.com/en/updates/autarco-software-and-the-new-construction-law
- Sector complaints concern the Wkb itself, not any tool. They cite extra administrative burden and stricter proof requirements for single-plot builders and small contractors, and a "checklist culture". Sources: https://omgevingsweb.nl/beleid/de-wkb-stelsel-draait-kwaliteit-nog-niet-bewezen/ and Binnenlands Bestuur. The Eerste Kamer received a letter on 2026-09-08 about the system being "fundamentally" questioned: https://www.eerstekamer.nl/brief_in/20260908/het_wkb_stelsel_fundamenteel/f=/vn0tdrxcdovg.pdf. Contents not read, so the policy risk is unverified.
- Product complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews turned up for any specific tool.

## Pricing
Not found. No published prices for any tool (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Tools target kwaliteitsborgers, not the small contractors and installers who must assemble the consumentendossier.
- Small contractors carry the paperwork burden, and no consumer-dossier-specific product was found for them (unverified, based on limited search).

## Momentum
Unknown. Wkb policy is unstable: the Eerste Kamer letter of Sept 2026 questions the system.

## Opening
A cheap consumer-dossier assembler for small contractors and installers is not visibly served by quality-assurer tools. The main risk is Wkb policy changes, and a real named competitor may exist that these searches missed.
