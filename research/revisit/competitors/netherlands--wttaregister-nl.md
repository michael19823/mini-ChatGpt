# wttaregister.nl (Netherlands) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0679, Wtta staffing compliance (agencies and inleners).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

The name appears to be the public Wtta register run by the regulator (NAU). That is a lookup list of licensed agencies, not compliance software. Unverified: the domain itself did not appear in any search result, so the link between "wttaregister.nl" and the NAU register is an inference.

## Evidence
- A search for the domain returned a summary saying the NAU (Nederlandse Autoriteit Uitleenmarkt) manages a public Wtta register where clients can check that an agency is approved. The summary said it goes live in summer 2027 (not yet live as of this check). Sources: KVK (https://www.kvk.nl/en/rules-and-laws/wtta-stricter-rules-for-employment-agencies/), Kiwa (https://www.kiwa.com/nl/nl/over-kiwa/nieuws/wtta-nieuwe-toelatingsplicht-voor-de-uitleenmarkt/). The summary text was not matched to a specific page, so treat the go-live date as unverified.
- Wtta takes effect 1 Jan 2027. Existing agencies register 1 Nov to 31 Dec 2026 and apply for approval 1 May to 30 Jun 2027 (same search summary, unverified in detail).
- Inleners must consult the register before engaging an agency (search summary, Dutch-language query; sources included ABU guide https://www.abu.nl/app/uploads/2026/04/Praktische-gids-klaar-voor-de-Wtta-ABU.pdf).
- Existing commercial tools in the same space are separate: Beeple markets "Wtta ready" (https://www.beeple.eu/fr/post/wtta-ready-with-beeple-from-compliance-pressure-to-a-workable-process); Werva is general staffing software for small Dutch agencies (https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1112105/Werva). I did not check their features, so they may deserve their own competitor check.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced; a government register would not normally have such reviews.

## Pricing
None for the register as a software product. Costs listed for agencies (unverified summary): up to EUR 3,611 annual fee, EUR 100,000 financial guarantee, inspection certificate, VOG. These are regulatory costs, not a competing product's price.

## Fit gaps
It is a public lookup, not a workflow tool. It would not cover: preparing the application, inspection-readiness records (contracts, time tracking, client data), inleners' due-diligence records or monitoring of their suppliers, or multi-agency tracking.

## Opening
The register is a data source to integrate with, not a rival; the real competition is commercial Wtta tools such as Beeple, which should be checked separately.
