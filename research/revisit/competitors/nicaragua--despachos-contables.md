# Nicaragua: despachos contables (accounting firms)

Idea killed: C0706, DGI VET withholding and declaration file generator.

## Verdict: beatable

Many small firms sell monthly DGI filing as a service, but it is a manual, per-client service and not a software tool. Nothing found suggests a competing generator tool.

## Evidence
- Accounting firms advertise preparing and submitting monthly declarations (DMI) through VET-DGI. Example: Encuentra24 listing "Despacho contable y fiscal", Los Robles: https://www.encuentra24.com/nicaragua-en/classified-listings-services-business-services-consulting/despacho-contable-y-fiscal/32570358
- Since January 2013, withholding agents (IR) and non-VAT-collectors registered in VET must upload their withholding declarations to the DGI. Source (administrative provision text): https://nicaragua.justia.com/nacionales/disposiciones-administrativas/declaraciones-de-retenciones-de-ir-a-cuenta-y-definitivas-y-autotraslacion-para-no-recaudadores-del-iva-jan-30-2013/gdoc
- Complaints found concern the VET itself, not the firms. They cover technical failures that kept taxpayers from paying, and interruptions attributed to the system being unable to handle the number of users. These are 2013-2017 La Prensa articles, for example https://www.laprensani.com/2016/03/30/economia/2009770-plazo-declarar-pagar-impuestos-ante-la-dgi-nicaragua-recta-final
- No complaints about despachos contables themselves (reviews, forums) were found in these searches. Unverified whether any exist.
- Momentum: not applicable, since this is a fragmented service category and not a single product.

## Pricing
- One Encuentra24 listing shows $100.00 for accounting and tax services (the listing does not clearly say whether this is per month). Other firms are said to adjust prices to client needs. Treat as unverified and incomplete.

## Fit gaps
- Per-client manual labour. It does not scale for a small business that wants to prepare its own filing cheaply.
- Nothing found about file-generation tooling. This is based on a very limited search (3 searches), so it is inconclusive.
- A tool could also serve the firms as a productivity aid, since they do many filings.

## Opening
A cheap self-service or batch file generator for VET withholdings could undercut a roughly $100 manual service, or be sold to the despachos themselves. Neither segment was validated here.
