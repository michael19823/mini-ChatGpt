# Nicaragua: local/ERP systems (killed C0706: DGI VET withholding and declaration file generator)

## Verdict
beatable (low confidence). Only 2 of 3 searches ran. The first, in Spanish on VET, hit a usage limit and returned nothing. No complaint, review or pricing evidence was found.

## Evidence
- Competitor is generic (no URL). Searches only surfaced ERP localizations, not a dedicated VET file generator.
- Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central "Localizacion Nicaragua" by LLB Solutions. Its listing says it covers DGI billing complement, IR withholding at source, IVA, ISC, withholding books and withholding certificates. It does not mention VET or declaration file output. Source: https://appsource.microsoft.com/en-us/product/dynamics-365-business-central/PUBID.llbusinesssolutionsllc1580159119255%7CAID.llb_loca_ni_bc%7CPAPPID.a6324ee6-c73c-4396-be1c-89a413d793c0
- AlfaPeople has a Nicaragua BC offering (PDF, contents not read). Source: https://alfapeople.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/AlfaPeople_Nicaragua-BC_V2_en.pdf
- Odoo has an l10n_ni module that gives only a minimal chart of accounts, not tax filing. Source: https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/17.0/l10n_ni
- DGI requires prior authorization for computerized accounting and billing systems. Source: https://nicaragua.justia.com/nacionales/disposiciones-tecnicas/requisitos-para-uso-de-sistemas-de-facturacion-computarizadas-jul-16-2007/gdoc (the 2007 date appears in the URL; the page contents were not verified).
- Complaints: none found. Momentum: unverified. The AlfaPeople PDF URL suggests a 2026 upload, which is not confirmed.

## Pricing
Unverified. No prices were found for any Nicaraguan ERP or localization. Business Central is a mid-market product, so it is likely overpriced for micro and small firms. This is an inference, not a finding.

## Fit gaps
- The ERPs target mid-size firms that already run Business Central or Odoo.
- No evidence that any of them generates the DGI VET declaration or withholding upload file. This is unverified, and the searches were shallow.
- Small businesses and accountants who work in Excel or a basic system are not served by these products.

## Opening
A cheap standalone VET withholding and declaration file generator for small firms and accountants, one that doesn't require adopting an ERP, appears open. The main risk is that the accountant-service competitor ("despachos contables") bundles this work.
