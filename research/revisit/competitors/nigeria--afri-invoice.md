# Afri Invoice (Nigeria)

Killed idea: C0713 (Nigeria SME MBS e-invoicing). Researched on 2026-10-05.

Only 2 of the 6 allowed searches ran. The third hit a usage limit, so the research is partial. Everything below comes from search-result snippets and headlines. I did not open any page.

## Verdict: strong (provisional)

It is a real, locally built SaaS aimed squarely at SMEs. It claims FIRS e-invoicing integration, has a bank distribution partner (Kuda), and is investing in sales. I found no complaints, but my search for them was thin.

## Evidence
- BusinessDay reports that Afri Invoice debuted in Nigeria for SME digital invoicing. https://businessday.ng/news/article/digital-invoicing-for-smes-gets-boost-as-afri-invoice-debuts-in-nigeria/
- ThisDay (2025-07-08) describes it as a pioneering solution for the FIRS e-invoicing mandate. https://www.thisdaylive.com/2025/07/08/firs-e-invoicing-mandate-afri-invoice-pioneers-novel-solution-for-nigerian-businesses/
- Coverage says it integrates with FIRS' e-invoicing system and offers mobile-first tools aimed at full compliance with the FIRS mandate. I did not verify this against FIRS or any accreditation list, so treat it as unverified.
- Listed features include:
  - instant invoicing
  - payment tracking
  - multi-currency
  - analytics
  - customer and inventory databases
  - offline invoice draft mode
  - mobile-responsive design
- Kuda Bank partnership, per Techeconomy: https://v2.techeconomy.ng/afri-invoice-kuda-bank-unveil-digital-invoicing
- Momentum is active. In August 2025 it announced 100 to 150 marketing roles to drive the mandate.
  - Guardian: https://guardian.ng/business-services/afri-invoice-launches-over-100-marketing-roles-to-drive-nigerias-e-invoicing-mandate/
  - AllAfrica: https://allafrica.com/stories/202508140340.html
- Complaints: none found. A Google Play review search returned nothing about this app. I did not check the Play Store or App Store listings directly. I made no Nigerian-language or Pidgin searches, and no Reddit, Facebook or Trustpilot searches. Absence of complaints is not evidence of satisfied users.
- I have no 2026 data on status, outages or acquisition. Current momentum is unverified.
