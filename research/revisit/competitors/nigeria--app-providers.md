# Nigeria: APP providers (FIRS/NRS-certified Access Point Providers)

Idea killed: C0715, emerging-taxpayer e-invoicing Nigeria 2027-28.

## Verdict: beatable (tentative)

Many certified APPs exist, but they are enterprise- and integration-oriented. The emerging-taxpayer phase is not live until July 2027. Evidence on small-buyer fit is thin (5 of 6 searches used; 1 search was a duplicate-angle query).

## Evidence
- FIRS certified 16 firms as system integrators / APPs: Pasca, Hoptool, eTranzact, Telepac, Cryptware, Namiri, Ace of Spades, Jureb, Qucoon, Courteville, Softrust, Westmetro, Arca Payments, Elara, Interswitch, Remita. Taxpayers may switch providers; the list is to be updated. Source: https://msmeafricaonline.com/firs-unveils-16-tech-firms-to-drive-e-invoicing-adoption-as-onboarding-deadline-extended-to-november/
- Timeline for emerging taxpayers (turnover below NGN 1bn): pilot Apr-Jun 2027, go-live 1 Jul 2027, review Oct-Nov 2027, enforcement Jan-Mar 2028. Source: https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/02/nigeria-phased-rollout-e-invoicing-electronic-fiscal-system.html
- Penalty for an invoice not processed through MBS: NGN 200,000 per invoice plus VAT and interest, which gives small firms strong motivation to comply. Source: https://techcabal.com/2026/04/16/all-you-need-to-know-about-e-invoicing-in-nigeria/ (as summarized in search result)
- Interswitch and Remita are among the APPs: https://interswitchgroup.com/products/e-invoicing ; https://guardian.ng/news/remita-deployed-as-digital-platform-to-drive-nigerias-e-invoicing-system/
- Some SME-focused entrants exist, for example Afri Invoice (invoicing, payment tracking, tax compliance for SMEs): https://businessday.ng/news/article/digital-invoicing-for-smes-gets-boost-as-afri-invoice-debuts-in-nigeria/ . Whether it is a certified APP is unverified.
- Commentary says small firms face more difficulty than large firms with ERPs, and that mandatory e-invoicing may raise the cost of formalisation. Source: https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/06/13/how-e-invoicing-will-reshape-nigerias-tax-system/ (as summarized in search result)
- Large-taxpayer deadlines were extended because of onboarding and integration complexity; data quality and process issues were cited as the main hurdles, not the technology. Source: https://beancount.io/nl/blog/2026/09/26/nigeria-e-invoicing-medium-taxpayers-nrs-mbs-guide (as summarized in search result)

Complaints: no app-store reviews, Reddit/Facebook posts, Trustpilot or G2 reviews of specific APPs were found, and no local-language (Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo) results surfaced. Complaints about APPs for small buyers: none found. This is not evidence that none exist.

## Pricing
No published APP pricing found (unverified). One search result mentions generic SME invoicing tools such as Naira Invoice from about US$4/month (Capterra), but these are not shown to be certified APPs.

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified inference)
- The certified list is dominated by payment and enterprise-integration firms (Interswitch, Remita, eTranzact), likely priced and built for ERP-connected large and medium taxpayers.
- No evidence found of offline/mobile, low-literacy, or multi-language support aimed at micro-businesses (unverified).
- Emerging-taxpayer market opens only in 2027, so there are no incumbents with proven small-buyer products yet.

## Opening
A cheap, mobile-first, possibly offline-tolerant invoicing-plus-MBS layer for sub-NGN 1bn firms, or white-labelled on top of an existing APP, ahead of the July 2027 go-live. The risk is that the firm would depend on, or compete with, certified APPs; certification is a requirement.
