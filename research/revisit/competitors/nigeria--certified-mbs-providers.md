# Nigeria: certified MBS providers (no single URL, commercial)

Dropped idea: C0716 MBS e-invoicing issuance Nigeria (dup).

## Verdict: beatable (not a single competitor; a certification-gated category)

Issuing through the NRS/FIRS Merchant Buyer Solution (MBS) needs a certified System Integrator / Access Point Provider (APP), and certification is the barrier. A new entrant would probably have to resell or integrate with an existing certified provider rather than compete head-on. Per method.md, certification barriers score lower and complaints do not reopen the gap. The segment itself looks under-served.

## Evidence
- Redtech Limited was certified by FIRS as System Integrator and Access Point Provider for MBS: https://techcabal.com/2025/10/30/redtech-certified-by-firs-as-system-integrator-and-access-point-provider-for-nigerias-national-e-invoicing-platform-mbs/
- FIRS approved eTranzact International Plc as a certified provider: https://businessday.ng/business-economy/article/firs-approves-etranzact-for-nationwide-e-invoicing/
- Selection criteria reportedly include nationwide capacity, security, neutrality and scalability, with NITDA input (search summary of the BusinessDay/TechCabal coverage; unverified in detail). The full official provider list was not retrieved; the portal einvoice.firs.gov.ng was suggested by the search summary (unverified).
- Rollout: large taxpayers (turnover above N5bn) first; medium taxpayers (N1bn to N5bn) from 1 July 2026; emerging taxpayers (below N1bn) expected from 1 July 2027 (KPMG, Deloitte and BusinessDay snippets via search; https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/06/nigeria-e-invoicing-expected-july-2025.html).
- Momentum: active. Providers are certifying, and the medium-taxpayer wave went live in July 2026.
- Complaints: no user reviews, app-store reviews, Reddit, Nairaland or Trustpilot complaints about specific providers were found in the searches. Only general commentary that costs and technical capacity could burden micro and small firms (businessday.ng and Zikoko pieces surfaced; contents not read in detail). I searched in English only; the language in Nigeria is English, so no local-language search was needed.

## Pricing
- Published provider prices were not found. One third-party guide (afrotools / kolonell / beancount style blogs, unverified) estimates 2026 SME platform costs of N5,000-15,000/month (entry), N15,000-40,000 (active SME), N40,000-120,000 (high volume). These are estimates, not verified provider price lists.
- Non-compliance penalties are reported as above N1,000,000 (unverified, same source type).

## Fit gaps
- Certified providers (Redtech, eTranzact type) appear aimed at large and medium firms and system integrations; micro/emerging taxpayers (below N1bn, due 2027) are a likely gap (inference, not verified).
- Small firms with weak technical capacity may need low-cost, mobile-first, simple issuance (inference, unverified).
- No evidence found on offline or mobile support or multi-entity support in either direction.

## Opening
Build a low-cost, simple SME invoicing front end on top of a certified APP (white-label or partnership) aimed at the sub-N1bn wave due July 2027, instead of seeking own certification.
