# Cryptware (Cryptware System Limited), Nigeria

Idea killed: C0713, Nigeria SME MBS e-invoicing.

## Verdict
**weak** (thin public footprint; one of many accredited providers, not a visible SME product)

## Evidence
- Search results describe "Cryptware System Limited" as one of 16 firms certified by NITDA as accredited service providers (system integrators / access point providers) for Nigeria's National E-Invoicing Solution (Merchant-Buyer Model). Sources: https://msmeafricaonline.com/firs-unveils-16-tech-firms-to-drive-e-invoicing-adoption-as-onboarding-deadline-extended-to-november/ and https://newtelegraphng.com/firs-unveils-companies-accredited-to-support-e-invoicing-adoption/ (the summary named Cryptware; I did not open the pages, so the exact wording is unverified).
- Searches for its own website, product, app listings or reviews returned nothing. No website URL found.
- Nigeria's wider MBS rollout is phased, with small businesses in later waves (per https://econnect.eu/en/docs/knowledge/regulations/nigeria; wave dates in the summary were inconsistent, so unverified).
- Momentum: unverified. It was accredited recently; no news of product releases, acquisition or shutdown.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints surfaced (searched in English; Nigeria's business language is English, so no local-language search was run).

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified. (The USD 350/year and USD 6 per authentication figures from search results relate to the separate CBN Form M/trade e-invoicing scheme, not Cryptware.)

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of an SME-focused product, self-serve signup, mobile app or pricing. This is unverified, and may reflect an integrator-style, enterprise-led offering.
- Accredited-provider status means it competes with 15 other accredited firms (e.g. Taxaide Technologies, per https://businessday.ng/technology/article/taxaide-technologies-secures-firs-licence-as-accredited-e-invoicing-service-provider/), so the real competitive pressure is the accredited set, not Cryptware specifically.

## Opening
Cryptware has no visible SME-facing product or pricing, so a low-cost, self-serve SME MBS onboarding tool is open, provided the builder gets accredited or partners with an accredited provider.
