# Doftwerks (Nigeria)

Killed idea: C0713 (Nigeria SME MBS e-invoicing). Researched on 2026-10-05.

4 of the 6 allowed searches were used. Everything below comes from search-result snippets. I did not open any page. The coordinator's brief gave no URL for the competitor.

## Verdict: beatable

Doftwerks is a real, accredited player, but its product is an ERPNext integration. It reaches only the ERPNext user base and says nothing about SMEs outside ERPNext. A standalone, SME-first MBS product is not directly covered. This is provisional because pricing, user counts and complaints were not found.

## Evidence
- Doftwerks West Africa Limited is described as the technology subsidiary of Stransact Chartered Accountants. It is accredited by the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) as both Systems Integrator (SI) and Access Point Provider (APP) for the mandatory e-invoicing programme.
  - Daily Trust: https://dailytrust.com/doftwerks-secures-dual-nrs-accreditation-for-nigerias-e-invoicing/
  - AllAfrica (2026-06-23): https://allafrica.com/stories/202606230322.html
  - Vanguard: https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/06/doftwerks-secures-dual-nrs-accreditation-for-e-invoicing/amp/
- Its product ("doftwerks_nrs") sends Sales Invoices from ERPNext to the NRS. On submit, invoices are validated, signed and transmitted through the Doftwerks E-Invoice Access Point. The IRN, receipt status and verification QR code are written back to the invoice. It also has a pre-flight data check and multi-entity support (one NRS credential set per ERPNext Company). Source: https://cloud.frappe.io/marketplace/apps/doftwerks_nrs
- Momentum is active. The accreditation news is from June 2026, and the integration was described as already live.
- Complaints: none found. I searched for reviews and complaints and found nothing about Doftwerks. I made no Play Store, App Store, Reddit, Facebook or Trustpilot checks, and no Pidgin or Yoruba/Hausa searches. Absence of complaints is not evidence of satisfied users.
- A search for the bare name "Doftwerks" returned only unrelated German companies. The firm has a small web footprint beyond the press coverage above.

## Pricing
Not found. The Frappe Cloud Marketplace lists the app as "Free", but the Access Point transmission fees are not stated in what I saw. Pricing for the service is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Tied to ERPNext, which is a niche choice among Nigerian SMEs. Businesses on Excel, Tally, QuickBooks, Sage or paper are not served, as far as the snippets show (unverified).
- No evidence of a mobile-first or offline invoicing app for micro and small merchants.
- Accountant-firm parentage suggests a services-led model with implementation support. That may be a poor fit for self-serve, low-ticket SMEs (inference, unverified).
- No evidence of a published self-serve price list.

## Opening
A self-serve, mobile or offline-capable MBS product for SMEs that do not run ERPNext is untouched by Doftwerks. Its strengths are dual accreditation and ERPNext multi-entity support.
