# Duplo (Nigeria) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0713, Nigeria SME MBS e-invoicing. Searches used: 5 of 6 (English only; no local-language results surfaced, and Nigerian business usage is largely English).

## Verdict: strong

Duplo is an NRS-licensed Systems Integrator and Access Point Provider (reported March 2026). It is well funded and bundles e-invoicing with payments and reconciliation. It is a real, active competitor for the MBS e-invoicing idea.

## Evidence
- Duplo is a Nigerian B2B payments and invoicing startup, founded September 2021 by Yele Oyekola and Tunde Akinnuva (spelled "Akinnuwa" in one source), with $5.6M raised in total (seed of $4.3M, YC-backed). Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2022/08/31/duplo-digitizes-payment-flows-for-african-b2b-enterprises-gets-4-3m-seed-funding ; https://thecondia.com/duplo-4-3-million-seed
- Reported March 2026: NRS granted Duplo both the Systems Integrator and Access Point Provider licences. This lets it connect business systems to the MBS and transmit validated invoices to the NRS engine, ahead of the 1 July 2026 deadline for medium taxpayers. Sources: https://techcabal.com/2026/03/16/duplo-secures-nrs-licence/ ; https://www.itnewsafrica.com/2026/03/duplo-secures-dual-licenses-to-bridge-e-invoicing-unified-payments-for-businesses-in-nigeria/
- Its pitch is "compliance-to-commerce": generate NRS-standard e-invoices, route them to the tax authority, then settle and reconcile payments in the same platform. Source: the itnewsafrica article above.
- An August 2026 techeconomy.ng headline says Duplo also holds CBN licences (title only; details unverified). Source: https://www.techeconomy.ng/duplo-taps-dual-cbn-licenses-to-automate-tax-compliance-and-curb-500bn-revenue-loss
- Momentum: active and growing as of 2026 (new licences, vendor management portal per BusinessDay). It is not stagnant or shut down.
- Complaints: none found. Searches for Trustpilot, Reddit, Nairaland and app-store reviews returned no user reviews or outage reports. This is an absence of evidence, not proof of satisfaction. Google Play and App Store pages were not checked directly.

## Pricing
Not found. The search results had no published e-invoicing price, per-invoice fee or minimum. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
All are inferred from positioning, not confirmed:
- Its focus is mid-market and B2B finance teams, consumer goods manufacturers and multi-branch businesses. Micro and small SMEs may be underserved (unverified).
- It is a full payments platform, so a buyer who only wants cheap or simple compliance filing may find it heavier than needed.
- Offline or low-connectivity use and local-language support: no evidence either way.

## Opening
Little room for a general MBS e-invoicing tool. Any opening is a narrow, low-price micro-SME niche, such as simple mobile or offline compliance for very small traders. Even that is unproven without Duplo's pricing and SME tier.
