# eTranzact (Nigeria) - competitor check for C0713 (Nigeria SME MBS e-invoicing)

## Verdict: strong

eTranzact is a listed Nigerian fintech and an NRS-certified e-invoicing provider. It already markets same-day onboarding to taxpayers. Fit gaps for small SMEs are unverified.

## Evidence
- NRS/FIRS approved eTranzact International Plc as a certified provider for the nationwide MBS e-invoicing rollout, building on earlier VAT automation work. Source: https://businessday.ng/business-economy/article/firs-approves-etranzact-for-nationwide-e-invoicing/
- It pledges same-day onboarding and 24-hour technical support for uploading e-invoices to the NRS portal. Sources: https://nannews.ng/etranzact-promises-easy-onboarding-e-invoicing-to-tax-payers/ , https://www.nigeriahousingmarket.com/news/etranzact-nrs-einvoicing-taxpayer-onboarding-2026
- It ran an e-invoicing compliance workshop with the NRS and stakeholders and reported over N103 trillion processed in 2025. Source: https://www.arise.tv/etranzact-plc-processes-over-n103-trillion-in-transactions-through-platform-in-2025
- Rollout context: large taxpayers (N5bn+ turnover) had to comply by 31 July 2026. Medium taxpayers (N1-5bn) are onboarding in 2026, and smaller "emerging" taxpayers come in later phases. Sources: the nigeriahousingmarket.com page above and https://allafrica.com/stories/202609100373.html
- Momentum: active and well publicised in 2026. It is a certified provider with a visible stakeholder push.
- Complaints: none found. Searches (English; one Hausa-keyword attempt) returned nothing on eTranzact e-invoicing complaints, outages or failed filings. No eTranzact app-store or Trustpilot reviews surfaced. The Trustpilot hits were for Tranzfar, a different product, and are not counted. This does not prove satisfaction, only that the searches found nothing.

## Pricing
Unverified. No published e-invoicing price, per-invoice fee or minimum was found.

## Fit gaps
All unverified. The marketing and rollout focus is on large and medium taxpayers. Nothing was found on small-business pricing, a mobile or offline app, or multi-entity support. The "user-friendly regardless of literacy level" line is a marketing claim only.

## Opening
A cheap, mobile-first or offline-capable service for micro and small businesses could still fit, because the emerging-taxpayer phase comes later. It would have to compete with a certified, well-known incumbent. Verify eTranzact's SME pricing and product before committing.
