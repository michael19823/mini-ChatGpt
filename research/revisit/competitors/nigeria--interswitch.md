# Interswitch (Nigeria) - competitor check

Idea(s) it killed: C0713 Nigeria SME MBS e-invoicing. Competitor URL was not given; searched 5 of 6 allowed WebSearch calls (English; one Yoruba-language query returned only English payment-outage pages). WebFetch not used.

## Verdict: strong

Interswitch is a FIRS/NRS-accredited Access Point Provider and System Integrator for the MBS e-invoicing system, it markets to SMEs explicitly, and it has a large existing merchant base. No e-invoicing-specific complaints were found, but that is mostly because small taxpayers are not mandated until later.

## Evidence
- Interswitch announced it was accredited by FIRS as Access Point Provider and System Integrator for mandatory e-invoicing under MBS; the solution is stated to support corporates and SMEs. Source: https://interswitchgroup.com/news/interswitch-named-firs-access-point-provider-for-mandatory-e-invoicing
- Its product page says it keeps you compliant "whether you process 10 invoices or 10,000" (as reported in search snippet). Source: https://interswitchgroup.com/products/e-invoicing
- Rollout timeline (per search summaries of NRS news): large taxpayers (over N5bn turnover) live Nov 2025; medium (N1bn-N5bn) live 1 July 2026; small/emerging (under N1bn) scheduled July 2027. Sources: https://beancount.io/blog/2026/09/26/nigeria-e-invoicing-medium-taxpayers-nrs-mbs-guide , https://msmeafricaonline.com/nrs-sets-july-31-e-invoicing-deadline-for-large-taxpayers/ . Treat the 2027 date as unverified (secondary source).
- Momentum: actively developed, with e-invoicing also run in Kenya (Interswitch newsletter on Kenya e-invoicing) and recent public-sector news (techeconomy.ng, Sept 2026). Active, not stagnant.

## Complaints
- None found about Interswitch's e-invoicing product (no reviews, forum posts or news of failed filings surfaced).
- Adjacent, not e-invoicing: Quickteller app scored 3.8 on Google Play (per a third-party index, https://index.interceptd.com/detail/app/com.quickteller.app); a reported complaint about SMS OTP codes not arriving; Trustpilot has very few reviews (https://de.trustpilot.com/review/www.interswitchgroup.com).
- Adjacent payments reliability: Flutterwave status incidents in 2026 for Interswitch card collections (about 15h and 23h disruptions, plus May and June incidents), e.g. https://isdown.app/status/flutterwave/incidents/659960-ngn-service-alert-disruption-on-ngn-card-collections-through-interswitch . These concern card payments, not e-invoicing; dates and durations come from search summaries (unverified in detail).

## Pricing
- No published e-invoicing price or minimum found. Unverified / "contact sales". Cannot judge whether it is overpriced for small buyers.

## Fit gaps (all unverified; no direct evidence found)
- Likely built around ERP/system integration and payments customers; a very small, non-technical business may find a lightweight self-serve invoicing app a better fit. Not confirmed.
- No evidence found on offline/mobile use, local-language support or multi-entity handling. Not confirmed.
- Small taxpayers are not mandated until the later wave, so demand for SME tools is still ahead; Interswitch's real SME depth is untested.

## Opening
Little proven opening against Interswitch itself; the only plausible gap is an ultra-cheap, self-serve, mobile-first invoicing app for micro-businesses ahead of the small-taxpayer deadline, and it would need to partner with or be accredited alongside access point providers.
