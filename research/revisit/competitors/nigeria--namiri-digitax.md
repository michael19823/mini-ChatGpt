# Namiri / DigiTax (Nigeria) - competitor check for C0713 (Nigeria SME MBS e-invoicing)

## Verdict: strong

DigiTax (Namiri Technology Ltd) is already FIRS/NRS-accredited as both Access Point Provider (APP) and System Integrator (SI) for the MBS platform, has a local Country Director, and says it plans to serve SMEs signing up in 2026. It is a real, funded, accredited incumbent in exactly the idea's space. (Searches used: 5 of 6, English only; no Nigerian-language results surfaced.)

## Evidence
- Accredited by FIRS (now NRS) as APP and SI for MBS: https://businessday.ng/?p=1016022 , https://dabafinance.com/en/news/digitax-nigeria-firs-e-invoicing-approval
- Appointed Olumide Akinsola as Nigeria Country Director: https://guardian.ng/business-services/business/digitax-appoints-akinsola-country-director/
- Claims 800+ clients, mostly large firms (retail, pharma, manufacturing); integrates with SAP, MS Dynamics, Oracle, Sage, Zoho, QuickBooks, Odoo and custom APIs (search summary of the above sources, https://www.e-invoice.app/vendors/digitax).
- Has a Frappe/ERPNext marketplace app "Nigeria compliance via DigiTax": https://cloud.frappe.io/marketplace/apps/nigeria_compliance_via_digitax
- Positioning for SMEs signing up in 2026: https://techcabal.com/2025/06/03/digitax-chases-africas-rising-demand-for-tax-tech/
- Momentum: active (accreditation 2025, expansion news through 2025-26). Not stagnant, shut down or acquired as far as found.
- Rollout timeline (secondary source, unverified against NRS): large taxpayers (>N5bn) live Nov 2025; medium (N1-5bn) live 1 Jul 2026; small (<N1bn) July 2027, enforcement 2028 (https://beancount.io/blog/2026/09/26/nigeria-e-invoicing-medium-taxpayers-nrs-mbs-guide). So the small-business segment is not yet forced to comply.

## Complaints
None found. No Google Play, App Store, Reddit, Nairaland, Trustpilot or G2 reviews surfaced; no outage or failed-filing news about DigiTax specifically. Absence of results is not evidence of satisfaction; review sources were not directly searchable (WebFetch blocked).

## Pricing
Not published. TechCabal reports a mix of annual subscription, one-off integration fee and per-transaction pricing, with tiers undisclosed. Unverified for SMEs. Penalty for non-compliant invoices (context for willingness to pay): N200,000 per invoice plus 100% of VAT due plus interest (BusinessDay/TechCabal reports).

## Fit gaps (inferred, mostly unverified)
- Customer base is large enterprises with ERP/API integration; SME-grade UX, mobile-first or offline use is not evidenced.
- No published SME price or free tier found.
- Other accredited APPs/SIs likely exist (not checked), so the segment may be crowded beyond DigiTax.

## Opening
Small-taxpayer mandate is not until 2027-28, so a very cheap, mobile/offline-first SME tool could still win that segment, but only by out-executing an accredited incumbent that is already courting SMEs.
