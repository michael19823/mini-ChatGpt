# Qucoon (Nigeria), competitor check for C0713 (Nigeria SME MBS e-invoicing)

## Verdict: weak

Qucoon is on the accredited list as a System Integrator / Access Point Provider, but I found no evidence of an SME-facing product, a price list, or any customer complaints. Treat it as a low-visibility integrator, not a strong SME competitor. This rests on 5 searches that found little, so it is not proof of absence.

## Evidence
- Qucoon Limited appears on the list of companies accredited (via NITDA) as a System Integrator or Access Point Provider for Nigeria's e-invoicing system. Source: https://newtelegraphng.com/firs-unveils-companies-accredited-to-support-e-invoicing-adoption/ (also https://itweb.africa/article/nigeria-names-16-firms-to-support-e-invoicing-onboarding/WnpNgq21g9jMVrGd). The search summary said this; I did not open the pages, so the exact role is unverified.
- The only reviews found are Glassdoor employee reviews (3.9/5 from 28 reviews, per the search summary). They say nothing about the product. Source: https://www.glassdoor.ca/Reviews/Qucoon-Reviews-E5079404.htm
- Customer complaints (app stores, Trustpilot, Reddit, Nairaland, outage or failed-filing news): none found. No Nigerian-language searches were run, since Nigerian business is mostly in English.
- Visible SME-oriented rivals in the same results (more visible than Qucoon): Afri Invoice (dual-licensed APP and SI), Interswitch, Doftwerks (ERPNext), Payeet, DigiTax, Duplo.

## Pricing
Not found. No published price or minimum for any Qucoon e-invoicing offering. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of an SME self-serve product. It looks like an integrator, probably aimed at larger firms (unverified).
- Product features, mobile or offline use, and language support: not found.

## Momentum
Accredited, so likely active. No news of acquisition or shutdown found. Product activity is unverified.

## Opening it leaves
A self-serve, low-cost SME invoicing and MBS/NRS compliance product is not blocked by Qucoon. The real competition is the other accredited providers, such as Afri Invoice and Payeet.
