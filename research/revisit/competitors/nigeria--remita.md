# Remita (Nigeria)

Ideas it killed: C0717 (multi-state payroll remittances Nigeria), C0713 (Nigeria SME MBS e-invoicing).

## Verdict: strong

Remita (SystemSpecs) is the payment rail under Nigerian public finance, so any remittance idea has to pay through it. It also sells an SME suite with payroll. It is entrenched, though it is a payment rail and not a multi-state payroll tax tool. The only e-invoicing link found is a search-query match, so for C0713 it is closer to not-a-real-competitor.

## Evidence
- Remita is owned and operated by SystemSpecs, founded 2009. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SystemSpecs
- A 2026 review says it processed more than N100 trillion in payments in 2025 and underpins the public finance system. Source: https://brands.ng/remita-review-2026-is-it-safe-legit-and-worth-using-in-nigeria/ (a third-party review, figure unverified).
- SystemSpecs launched the Remita SME Suite in 2018, covering collections, vendor payments and payroll for SMEs. Source: https://thisdaylive.com/index.php/2018/10/25/systemspecs-launches-remita-sme-suite-for-businesses
- PAYE is remitted to the State Internal Revenue Service of the state where the employee works, so multi-state teams deal with several state authorities. Source: https://www.remotepass.com/country/nigeria/payroll. Remita is the payment channel, and the search results did not show it automating multi-state PAYE computation or filing (unverified).
- Complaints: reports of RRR (Remita Retrieval Reference) payment disruptions into federal agencies. The Remita MD attributed them to bank branch network failures, application glitches or transmission breaks. Source: https://thenigerialawyer.com/?p=368943. Another thread shows users getting "error generating RRR". Source: https://myschool.ng/questions/view/school-based-questions/137895. Premium Times reported a House of Representatives panel querying payments to Remita. Source: https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/top-news/681857-reps-panel-queries-accountant-generals-office-for-payment-to-remita.html. No app-store, G2, Capterra or Trustpilot reviews were surfaced, and no Reddit or Facebook content was checked.
- E-invoicing: FIRS's Merchant Buyer Solution (MBS) is a government platform. Large taxpayers (turnover of N5bn and above) were mandated from 1 August 2025, then extended to 1 November 2025, with medium and small businesses to follow. Source: https://www.deloitte.com/ng/en/services/tax/perspectives/FIRS-announces-e-invoicing-mandate-for-large-taxpayers-in-Nigeria.html. No result tied Remita to MBS e-invoicing.
- Momentum: active and large, with the 2026 review and 2025 volume figures above. No acquisition or shutdown found.

## Pricing
Not published in what was found. The only fee found is a 1% transaction fee on Federal Government TSA transactions, shared among Remita, banks, the CBN and other operators (from a search summary, not a primary page). SME payroll tier pricing: unverified.

## Fit gaps
- The SME Suite payroll appears to be generic salary processing. Multi-state PAYE handling by state SIRS was not confirmed (unverified).
- Remita does not appear to be an MBS e-invoicing provider (unverified). The government platform is the main barrier on that idea, not Remita.
- Reliability complaints concern RRR and bank-side failures on government payments.
- Offline, mobile, language and multi-entity support were not checked (unverified).

## Opening
A layer above Remita that computes and files PAYE by state for multi-state employers and then pays through Remita, which is complementary rather than head-on. The e-invoicing idea is blocked by the government MBS platform and not by Remita.
