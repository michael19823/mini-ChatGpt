# SeamlessHR (Nigeria)

Killed idea: C0717 Multi-state payroll remittances Nigeria. Searches used: 3 of 6 (all English; no local-language results of value).

## Verdict: strong

SeamlessHR is a well-funded, active HR and payroll vendor. It already advertises multi-state PAYE and pension, NHF and NSITF remittance handling for Nigerian employers, so it covers the dropped idea directly.

## Evidence
- Funding: $10M Series A in 2022, then a $9M Series A extension in Jan 2025 led by the Gates Foundation and Helios Digital Ventures, about $25M raised in total. It plans to expand across Africa, possibly through acquisitions. Sources: https://dabafinance.com/en/news/seamlesshr-raises-9m-en-serie-a-extension-pour-lexpansion-de-lafrique , https://techcrunch.com/?p=2256522
- Product claims: native Nigerian PAYE logic, PFA pension remittance file generation, NHF, ITF and NSITF calculations, and "multi-state PAYE". These come from a search snippet of a third-party page, which is probably vendor-influenced. Treat as unverified against the product itself. Source: https://seamlesshr.com/?p=41250 and the search result set.
- Customers: PwC, Sterling Bank and Lagos Business School, in Nigeria, Ghana and Tunisia. It targets medium to large enterprises.
- Complaints: none found. Trustpilot shows 3.5 TrustScore with 0 reviews in the last 12 months (https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/seamlesshr.com). G2 has too few reviews to report (https://www.g2.com/products/seamlesshr/reviews). No app-store, Reddit or forum complaints surfaced, and these were not searched directly.
- Momentum: active and growing. No acquisition or shutdown found.

## Pricing
- Reported: Pro at N2,000 per employee per month, Premium at N3,000 per employee per month. Payroll, recruitment and performance modules usually cost extra, plus a one-time onboarding fee that varies. The source is a third-party search snippet and the figures are unverified (date unknown). Source: https://icehrm.com/blog/best-hr-software-for-nigerian-businesses-q-18/ (unconfirmed which result held this).
- Per-seat pricing plus add-ons and an onboarding fee probably suits mid-size and large firms better than micro-SMEs.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at medium and large enterprises. Small firms and accountants serving many small clients may be underserved (inference, unverified).
- Whether it files directly with each state revenue service, as opposed to producing schedules, is unverified.
- No evidence found on offline use or low-cost pay-per-filing options.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: a cheap, filing-focused tool for micro-SMEs or accounting firms that need state-by-state remittance without an enterprise HR suite, and only if direct state filing is something SeamlessHR lacks (unverified).
