# Zoho Invoice (Nigeria)

Dropped ideas: C0715 (emerging-taxpayer e-invoicing 2027-28), C0713 (Nigeria SME MBS e-invoicing)

## Verdict: strong

Zoho launched a Nigeria edition of Zoho Books with NRS/MBS e-invoicing via Remita (Sept 2026). It is a well-funded incumbent already doing what these ideas propose. Note that the e-invoicing is in Zoho Books, not the free Zoho Invoice product (unverified whether Zoho Invoice itself gets it).

## Evidence
- Zoho Books Nigeria edition launched around 17 Sept 2026 with NRS-compliant e-invoicing through Remita, an approved access point provider for the Merchant Buyer Solution. Invoices, credit notes and debit notes are pushed to the NRS portal and get a UUID. Sources: https://brandspurng.com/2026/09/17/zoho-launches-nigeria-edition-of-zoho-books/ ; https://www.zoho.com/en-ng/books/help/e-invoicing/ ; https://www.zoho.com/books/academy/taxes-and-compliance/nigeria-einvoicing.html
- Older third-party page (ecosire.com/apps/zoho-one/zoho-nigeria-firs-einvoicing) claimed Zoho Books lacked Nigerian e-invoicing localisation. This appears outdated given the launch above.
- Momentum: active. Zoho Books product updates continue (Sept 2026), a local country manager exists, and there is a Monnify integration for Nigerian payments. Source: https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/community/topic/zoho-books-product-updates-september-2026 ; https://www.itedgenews.africa/monnify-for-zoho-books-automates-invoicing-and-payments-for-nigerian-smes/
- Complaints: the only one found is price. Zoho raised Nigerian prices in Aug 2024 after naira devaluation (about N350 to N1500 per $), and the report says startups are considering local alternatives. Source: https://techpoint.africa/2024/07/04/zoho-price-increase-nigerians/ ; https://technext24.com/90w9
- No app-store, G2, Capterra, Reddit or Trustpilot complaints about Nigerian e-invoicing were found in the searches (searches limited; local-language search not productive for Nigeria, English is the working language). Finding none is a valid result.

## Pricing
- Local naira pricing was fixed in 2020 (Zoho One at N7,500, about $6 then) and raised in Aug 2024. Exact current naira prices: unverified. Zoho Invoice has historically had a free tier (unverified for Nigeria).

## Fit gaps
- E-invoicing sits in Zoho Books and depends on a third-party access point (Remita). Fees and the Zoho Invoice free tier coverage: unverified.
- Likely generic for micro and emerging taxpayers; no evidence of offline, WhatsApp or Pidgin/Yoruba/Hausa support (unverified).

## Opening
Little: only a thin, ultra-low-cost or offline/mobile-first niche for micro-businesses priced out after the 2024 increase, and even that is unproven.
