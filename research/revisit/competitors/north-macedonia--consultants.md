# north-macedonia--consultants

Idea killed: C0720 (CBAM emissions packages for exporters). Competitor: generic consultants (no URL known). 4 WebSearch calls used (English and Macedonian).

## Verdict: beatable

## Evidence
- No North Macedonia-specific CBAM consultancy was identified in the searches. Results were general: Big 4 firms, boutique carbon consultancies, customs brokers and verifier-affiliated consultants (https://cbamguide.com/compliance/cbam-consultants/). Local firms may exist but were not found (unverified).
- Foreign providers sell CBAM advisory remotely: SCS Global (https://hi.scsglobalservices.com/consulting/services/cbam-advisory-services), NECC (https://www.necc.co.uk/cbam-2), TUV Austria Hellas verification (https://tuvaustriahellas.gr/services/verification-of-carbon-footprint-reports-under-eu-regulation-956-2023-cbam-verification/?lang=en), plus Fiverr gigs.
- Local awareness material is policy-level (World Bank policy note on CBAM and North Macedonia; IDSCS policy brief; WB6 CIF CBAM paper at https://wb6cif.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/CBAM.pdf), not product-level.
- Complaints: none found. Searches returned no reviews or forum posts about consultants in this market.
- Momentum: CBAM advisory demand is active; per search results, from 2026 operator data must be independently verified.

## Pricing
Published day rates or retainers were not found for most firms (per cbamguide.com summary). Local prices: unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- Big 4 and international firms probably target large exporters; small and mid-size North Macedonian exporters are likely underserved.
- No Macedonian-language packaged tool or fixed-price offer was found.
- Verification still needs an accredited verifier, so a product cannot replace that step.

## Opening
Fixed-price, Macedonian-language emissions-data packages for small exporters (steel, aluminium, fertilizer, cement supply chains), partnering with accredited verifiers.
