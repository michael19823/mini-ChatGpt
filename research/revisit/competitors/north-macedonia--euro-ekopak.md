# Euro-Ekopak (North Macedonia) - competitor check for C0719 (Packaging EPR reporting evidence)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (as software); it is a real incumbent PRO that the idea would have to sit beside

Euro-Ekopak is a non-profit producer responsibility organisation (collective scheme), not a software vendor. It takes over members' legal obligations and does their reporting itself. It competes for the same buyer's compliance budget, but not with a reporting-evidence tool. A tool would be complementary or aimed at companies that self-comply or want an audit trail. Searches used: 5 of 6 (one standard search hit a usage limit and returned nothing).

## Evidence
- Founded June 2011 as a non-profit PRO, Skopje (DOO). Claims 620+ member companies, 90,000+ t packaging waste collected, 2,000+ collection equipment units, and "number 2 on the market". Source: Euro-Ekopak presentation, Tirana, June 2026: https://closetheglassloop.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Irina-Stojanovska-Euro-Ekopak-presentation-Tirana-June-2026.pdf (figures are the company's own claims, unverified independently).
- Same source describes regulatory spending rules: at least 65% of income on collection/infrastructure, 5-15% on public awareness, 24/7 ministry oversight access.
- Same source: before recent reforms, members reported and paid irregularly (monthly to yearly, paying 4-8 months late). This is a market-wide compliance weakness, and the reforms appear to tighten it.
- Trademark registration exists (Justia, "Drustvo za upravuvanje so pakuvanje i otpad od pakuvanje EURO-EKOPAK DOO SKOPJE"): https://trademark.justia.com/owners/drustvo-za-upravuvanje-so-pakuvanje-i-otpad-od-pakuvanje-euro-ekopak-doo-skopje-2910553
- Market context: several licensed collective operators exist (e.g. Pakomak, first licensed 2011). Not Euro-Ekopak-specific.
- Momentum: active; presenting at a June 2026 regional event.

## Complaints
None found. Searches in Macedonian (Cyrillic) and English returned no reviews, forum posts, news of failed filings or outages about Euro-Ekopak. No app-store or review-site listings found. Absence of results does not mean absence of complaints (coverage of this niche is thin).

## Pricing
Not found. No published membership fee or tariff located (unverified). Results for "Ekopak" with per-tonne prices (KM/ton) concern the Bosnia and Herzegovina organisation, a different entity, and must not be attributed to Euro-Ekopak.

## Fit gaps
- Provides no evidence/reporting software that is visible in search results (no members portal found; unverified).
- A PRO-membership model means the producer outsources obligations; it does not serve firms wanting their own audit-ready data, multi-scheme comparison, or own-evidence trails.
- Gaps in features, language or mobile use of any member portal: unknown.

## Opening
Little direct threat: the idea only competes if it targets producers wanting their own verifiable EPR data or cross-scheme reporting, since the PRO handles filings for members.
