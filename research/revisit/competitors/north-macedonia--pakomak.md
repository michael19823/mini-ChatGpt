# Pakomak (North Macedonia)

Idea killed: C0719 Packaging EPR reporting evidence

## Verdict
not-a-real-competitor (as a software rival). Pakomak is a non-profit collective EPR scheme (PRO), not a reporting tool. It is a channel or partner, and also the incumbent way producers discharge the obligation.

## Evidence
- Pakomak is a non-profit founded in 2010 by 11 manufacturers, licensed by the Ministry of Environment for packaging waste since 2011. It is a collective scheme and a member of PRO EUROPE. Sources: [amcham.mk](https://amcham.mk/member-news/45-000-tons-of-packaging-waste-per-year-recycled-by-pakomak/), [pro-e.org](https://pro-e.org/country/2105/macedonia)
- Scale: about 45,000 t/year reported by members, more than 350 member companies (per search summary of amcham.mk/pro-e.org). Recycled over 20,000 t in 2022, per a 2023 company report quoted in search results.
- It runs a reverse-vending / "Green Points" smartphone app for PET bottles and cans. This is consumer-facing, not producer reporting. Source: [amcham.mk](https://amcham.mk/?p=31426)
- Its membership service page exists at https://pakomak.com.mk/PakomakSite/en-uslugi-clenstvo.html. Its contents were not read (no fetch).
- Complaints: none found in 6 searches (Macedonian and English). No app-store, forum or news complaints surfaced. Absence of results is not proof none exist.

## Pricing
Fee per tonne, as reported in search-result snippets (unverified, exact source page not opened): the state fee is cited as 320 EUR/t for plastics versus Pakomak 26.10 EUR/t. Pakomak says it is non-profit and calculates fees without profit. No minimum or software price found.

## Fit gaps
- It is a service that takes over the obligation, not software producers use for their own evidence. Whether it gives members audit-ready data exports is unverified.
- Producers who join a PRO may need little separate reporting tooling, which is the real threat to C0719 demand.
- Other schemes or self-compliers may need evidence; unverified.

## Momentum
Active: 2022 results reported, RVM and app initiatives, PRO EUROPE membership. Not stagnant. Dates of latest activity were not verified.

## Opening
A producer-side or multi-scheme evidence/reporting layer is possible, but demand is limited because Pakomak already handles filings for its 350+ members at low fees; best treated as a partner channel.
