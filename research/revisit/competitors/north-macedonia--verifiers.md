# north-macedonia -- verifiers (type: consultant, no URL known)

Idea dropped because of it: C0720 CBAM emissions packages for exporters.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor
"Verifiers" is a generic category, not a named product. Searches (4 of 6 used, English and Macedonian) found no named North Macedonian CBAM verifier or consultancy selling to exporters. Verifiers perform an audit; they do not prepare the emissions data package. That is a different service from the dropped idea.

## Evidence
- Actual-emissions data must be verified by an independent verifier accredited by an EU national accreditation body. Verifiers outside the EU may apply to any NAB offering the service. Source: https://www.epa.ie/our-services/licensing/climate-change/eu-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/accreditation-and-verification/
- The Commission issued CBAM Verification and Accreditation Guidelines (reported as 24 Aug 2026). Verification of actual emissions is expected from 2027, with first accreditations around September 2026. Sources: https://carbon-pulse.com/543968/ and https://www.sluicebox.ai/news/cbam-verification-arrives-2027
- Reported verifier shortage in the EU (Belgium only two, six member states none). Source: https://gmk.center/en/posts/the-european-cbam-how-the-verification-of-reports-will-work/ (snippet only, unverified in detail). This supports a gap rather than a competitor.
- Global verifiers (DNV, DEKRA, SCS) advertise CBAM verification. Sources: https://www.dnv.com/services/environmental-and-energy-certification/cbam-verification-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/ and https://sustainability.dekra.com/en/cbam-verification-the-accreditation-framework-takes-shape/. Whether they serve North Macedonia was not found (unverified).
- North Macedonia context: World Bank and IDSCS analyses say cement, aluminium, iron and steel exporters are exposed. Source: https://idscs.org.mk/en/?p=27260
- Complaints: none found (no reviews or forum posts about verifiers in North Macedonia).

## Pricing
Only a generic estimate was found: EUR 5,000-15,000 (small firms), 15,000-50,000 (medium), 50,000-150,000+ (large), per a search summary of the SMM article https://news.metal.com/en/newscontent/104031152-smm-analysis-cbam-costs-are-taking-effect-verification-capacity-lags-what-stainless-steel-exporters-need-to-know (not checked in the full text, unverified). No North Macedonian published prices found.

## Fit gaps
- Verification is an audit, not a data-preparation package. Exporters still need monitoring plans, installation emissions data and report templates.
- No local-language (Macedonian) CBAM service found.
- Large international verifiers likely have minimums that suit large firms (unverified).

## Opening
Sell the pre-verification data package (monitoring plan, emissions calculation, report templates in Macedonian) to small exporters, and make it verifier-ready; verifiers are complements, not rivals.
