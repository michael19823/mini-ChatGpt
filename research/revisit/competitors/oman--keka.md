# Keka (Oman)

Killed ideas: C0728 (Oman payroll SPF/WPS/Omanisation), C0732 (Oman payroll WPS SIF/SPF).
Searches used: 4 of 6 (English and Arabic).

## Verdict: strong

Keka has a dedicated Oman site and payroll-compliance page that claim the exact features of the dropped ideas: SIF/WPS file generation, SPF calculation, gratuity and multi-CR payroll.

## Evidence
- Keka's Oman pages claim it converts payroll data into WPS-aligned SIF files with built-in validations, and calculates SPF contributions with contribution reports. Sources: https://www.keka.com/om/payroll-compliance , https://www.keka.com/om/payroll-software
- Keka runs a separate payroll per commercial registration on one platform and calculates gratuity and end-of-service under Oman Labour Law (same Oman pages). These are vendor claims, not independently verified.
- Keka has a help article on social insurance for GCC countries: https://help.keka.com/hc/en-us/articles/39946695342353-Understanding-Social-Insurance-Contributions-for-GCC-Countries
- Keka also has pages for Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Saudi Arabia, and a GCC promotion page. It claims 10,000+ Middle East businesses (vendor claim, unverified). This suggests active investment in the region.
- Ratings: G2 4.4 with about 2,197 reviews; Capterra 4.4 with 88 reviews (search-result summaries). Reviewers praise ease of use and support.
- Complaints: none found about Oman, WPS or SPF. Generic ones were minor (limited integrations, mobile time tracking is hard to stop). Gulf-specific review content was not found in these searches (unverified).
- Omanisation tracking: not mentioned on the pages found (unverified).
- The Arabic search returned nothing about Keka. It returned Zoho Payroll Oman, which is another competitor.

## Pricing
- No Oman or OMR price found.
- Generic Keka pricing from third-party aggregators: about $111/month for a Foundation plan of up to 100 employees, flat, then per employee (https://www.softwaresuggest.com/keka/pricing). Unverified for Oman.
- The flat fee for 100 employees is not obviously expensive for a small buyer. A micro-business with under 10 staff may be overpaying.

## Fit gaps
- Positioned at "growing enterprises"; small and micro firms may not be its focus.
- No Arabic-language support evidence found.
- No Omanisation quota tracking evidence.
- No evidence of a local Omani presence or bank-specific SIF formats (unverified).

## Opening
Only a narrow one: Arabic-first, very small Omani firms needing Omanisation tracking and bank-specific WPS files at a very low price. Keka already covers core SIF/SPF.
