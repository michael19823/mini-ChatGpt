# Mercans (Oman) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0728, Oman payroll SPF/WPS/Omanisation. Type: commercial. URL not given in the brief; Mercans pages found in search are on mercans.com.
Searches used: 4 of 6 (English and Arabic).

## Verdict: beatable

Mercans does cover Oman payroll (SPF, WPS, Omanisation tracking), but it is a multi-country payroll/EOR provider aimed at mid-size and large multinationals, not at small local Omani employers.

## Evidence
- Mercans' own pages claim Oman payroll covering SPF registration and contributions, WPS salary file integration and Omanisation quota tracking, as managed or SaaS service. Sources: https://mercans.com/global-payroll/oman , https://mercans.com/glossary/oman-social-protection-fund-spf-contributions/ , https://dsr.mercans.com/payroll-dossiers/oman/ (vendor claims, not independently verified).
- Review aggregators (via search summary, pages not opened): about 4.8/5 on G2 from 27 reviews, 4.1/5 on Glassdoor, 4.0/5 on Trustpilot from 4 reviews. Source: https://www.gloroots.com/blog/mercans-reviews (third-party blog; figures unverified).
- Complaints seen in that summary: interface less polished than big rivals, customization needs vendor involvement, limited customization in HR Blizz, some users report slow support response. None are Oman-specific. Mostly positive overall.
- Arabic search found no Mercans-specific Oman complaints or news (results were generic WPS/legal pages). No Oman-specific complaints found.
- Momentum: actively marketed, with current statutory alerts (e.g. January 2025 PDF) and 2026 review pages. Not stagnant. No acquisition or shutdown found.

## Pricing
- Not published. Third-party estimates only (unverified): global payroll SaaS/managed roughly $25-60 per employee per month; EOR from roughly $400-600 per employee per month; implementation $2,000-15,000 for standard setups. Sources: https://softwarefinder.com/hr/mercans , https://stealthagents.com/alternatives/mercans-alternatives , https://employerrecords.com/?p=17578 (all unverified).
- Quote-based with no self-serve quoting, so likely poor fit and overpriced for a 5-50 employee Omani SME.

## Fit gaps
- Targets multinationals and multi-country payroll; small local firms are not the ideal customer.
- No self-serve sign-up or published rates.
- Implementation fees and sales-led onboarding.
- Arabic-language product or local bank WPS file formats: unverified.
- No evidence found of a low-cost, Oman-only, self-serve tool inside Mercans.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, Arabic/English Oman-only payroll tool (SPF, WPS file, Omanisation tracking) for local SMEs is not served by Mercans' enterprise, quote-based model; check local Oman payroll vendors separately.
