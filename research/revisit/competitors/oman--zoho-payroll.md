# Zoho Payroll (Oman)

Dropped idea: C0728, Oman payroll SPF/WPS/Omanisation. 4 of 6 searches used.

## Verdict: strong

Zoho launched a dedicated Oman edition with WPS, SPF and EOSB, English and Arabic with RTL, at a low published price. A generic Oman payroll tool would be hard to differentiate. Omanisation support is unverified.

## Evidence
- Zoho launched Zoho Payroll for Oman, tailored to Omani labour and social security law. Source: https://timesofoman.com/article/166042-oman-sees-launch-of-automated-payroll-system-tailored-to-labour-and-social-security-laws
- Oman-specific pages exist at https://www.zoho.com/en-om/payroll and https://www.zoho.com/ar-om/payroll/ (Arabic). They describe automatic WPS wage file generation and a bilingual interface with RTL.
- SPF help page for Oman: https://www.zoho.com/en-om/payroll/help/employer/social-security/spf.md . It lists employer 11.00% and employee 7.50% for wages of OMR 180-3,000, plus occupational disease, employment security and maternity contributions.
- EOSB/gratuity for expatriates is supported, per the search summary.
- Omanisation quota tracking: no evidence found in search results (unverified, likely absent).
- Complaints: only general ones, not Oman-specific. Capterra shows 3.7/5 from 10 users, with support responsiveness as the main complaint and one 1.0/5 review titled "Horrendous Customer Support" (https://www.capterra.com/p/186299/Zoho-Payroll/reviews/Capterra___6639843/). No G2 data surfaced. No Oman app-store, Reddit or forum complaints were found, and no outage or failed-filing news.
- Momentum: active. Oman edition recently launched and the same help structure is rolled out across GCC countries.

## Pricing (Oman page, https://www.zoho.com/en-om/payroll/pricing/, USD, per search summary)
- Standard: $10/month billed annually ($12.5 monthly), includes 5 employees, +$2/employee annually.
- Professional: $15/month annually, includes 5 employees, +$3/employee.
- Premium: $20/month annually, includes 5 employees, +$4/employee.
- 14-day free trial. This is cheap for small buyers, so it is not overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps
- Omanisation (nationalisation quota/ratio tracking and reporting): not found, unverified.
- Customer support responsiveness is weak in general reviews.
- Not verified: direct SPF/WPS bank-file format coverage per Omani bank, filing integration vs. file export only, and multi-entity handling.
- Not verified: Omani-specific local support or partners.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: Omanisation compliance and local-bank/SPF filing workflows plus responsive local support, if Zoho indeed lacks them. Core WPS/SPF payroll is already covered cheaply.
