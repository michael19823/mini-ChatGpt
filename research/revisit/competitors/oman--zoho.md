# Zoho Payroll (Oman) - competitor check for C0732 (Oman payroll WPS SIF/SPF)

## Verdict
strong

Zoho has a dedicated Oman edition of Zoho Payroll that covers WPS wage files and SPF contributions. It has an Arabic RTL interface and costs very little. Searches found no Oman-specific complaints.

## Evidence
- Zoho launched Zoho Payroll for Oman. It is described as automating payroll to match Omani labour and social security law, with WPS wage-file generation, SPF calculation, and English and Arabic (RTL) interfaces. Source: https://timesofoman.com/article/166042-oman-sees-launch-of-automated-payroll-system-tailored-to-labour-and-social-security-laws
- Zoho has Oman product pages (https://www.zoho.com/en-om/payroll, https://www.zoho.com/ar-om/payroll/) and an Oman SPF help page (https://www.zoho.com/en-om/payroll/help/employer/social-security/spf.md). The Oman page was seen only through search snippets and was not opened.
- SPF rates quoted in the search summary: employee 7.5%, employer 11% on wages of OMR 180-3,000, plus extra contributions. Treat as unverified against the Zoho page.
- Search results did not mention SIF support (the older social-insurance scheme). Unverified either way.
- Ratings for Zoho Payroll overall (not Oman-specific): Capterra UAE 3.7/5 from 10 users; G2 4.4 from 19 reviews. Sources: https://www.capterra.ae/software/186299/zoho-payroll and https://www.g2.com/products/zoho-payroll/reviews. A G2 notice said the profile had not received a new review in two months.
- Complaints found (generic, not Oman): support is slow and under-staffed ("Engaging and taking help from customer support is not easy due to low headcount of people and response time", quoted in a search summary of the G2/Capterra reviews). Customisation is limited for complex pay structures, and some advanced features need manual workarounds.
- No Oman-specific complaints, outage reports or failed-filing news were found. No Reddit, Facebook-group or Trustpilot results appeared. Searches were in English and Arabic.
- Momentum: active. Oman is one of 8 Zoho Payroll country editions per the Arabic page, so Oman is a recent addition. The Oman edition was probably launched in the last year or two (date unverified).

## Pricing
From https://www.zoho.com/en-om/payroll/pricing/, shown in USD (per organisation per month, includes 5 employees):
- Standard: $10 billed annually or $12.5 monthly; extra employees $2 annually or $2.5 monthly.
- Professional: $15 annually or $17.5 monthly; extra employees $3 annually or $3.5 monthly.
- Premium: $20 annually or $25 monthly; extra employees $4 annually or $5 monthly.
This is very cheap for small Omani firms. It is not overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps
- No confirmed gaps for a small buyer.
- Unverified: SIF coverage for legacy cases, bank-specific WPS file formats for all Omani banks, direct submission (versus file download), and depth of Omanisation or PASI reporting.
- Slow support and limited customisation are generic complaints.
- Zoho Books being limited to USA/India was an older community complaint and is irrelevant to Payroll.

## Opening
Little room for a generic payroll tool. Any opening would be a narrow niche, such as bank-specific WPS and SIF/PASI filing automation for Omani accountants or PRO firms. That would need verification of Zoho's actual WPS bank-format coverage first.
