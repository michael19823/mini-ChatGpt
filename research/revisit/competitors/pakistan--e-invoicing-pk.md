# e-invoicing.pk (Pakistan)

Dropped ideas: C0738 (provincial sales tax POS integration), C0734 (FBR B2B digital invoice for SMEs).

## Verdict: beatable

A real, active FBR digital-invoicing vendor, but priced above what a micro or small buyer would pay. Its published model is a setup fee plus a monthly fee, with plans that scale with invoice volume. No user complaints were found.

## Evidence
- Active commercial product: an "AI powered" web-based FBR digital invoicing system. It extracts data from uploaded invoices, supports bulk spreadsheet upload, validates against the FBR API and submits with one click. Claims compatibility with Excel, SAP, Tally, Dynamics 365, Odoo and others. Source: https://e-invoicing.pk/ and https://www.e-invoicing.pk/features.html (search snippets only; not fetched).
- It has a separate page for FBR Income Tax POS integration (https://www.e-invoicing.pk/fbr-income-tax-pos-integration.html). Custom pricing is offered for multi-branch and franchise chains. Provincial sales tax (SRB/PRA etc.) POS coverage is unverified; the snippet only mentions FBR/Income Tax POS.
- Momentum: the site warns about imposter look-alike sites, which suggests a known brand. Its FAQ and contact pages exist. I found no date or news on funding, acquisition or shutdown (unverified).
- Complaints: none found. A search for reviews and complaints returned nothing specific to this vendor, and I found no app-store, Trustpilot, G2 or Reddit reviews. No Urdu-language complaints were found either.
- Market-level pain, not specific to this vendor (source: Manager.io forum thread https://forum.manager.io/t/pakistan-digital-invoicing-solution/61431 and a vatcalc/e-invoice.app article, snippets only):
  - PRAL API problems reported by some users.
  - Certificate management is confusing.
  - Smaller registered persons lack the IT capability to integrate.
  - PRAL helpdesk is slow.
- Competitors in the same niche include InvoiceFBR, ComplyStream (Shopify app), the Ecosire Odoo/D365/SAP modules (e.g. a Shopify FBR app at a one-time USD 299), FastAccounts and Oscar POS. All are from search snippets only.

## Pricing (from the search-result summary of https://e-invoicing.pk/pricing.html; not fetched, so treat as unverified until checked)
- One-time setup fee: Rs. 15,000 on all plans.
- Basic: Rs. 5,000/month, up to 50 invoices.
- Starter: Rs. 8,000/month, up to 100 invoices.
- Professional: Rs. 20,000/month, up to 300 invoices.
- Business: Rs. 30,000/month, 500+ invoices.
- Enterprise: custom, 1,000+ invoices.
- Effective cost is about Rs. 100 per invoice at the Basic tier, plus setup. That is steep for a tiny shop or SME with few invoices.

## Fit gaps
- Cost: the Rs. 15,000 setup plus Rs. 5,000/month minimum is high for micro and small sellers.
- Built around uploading invoices from existing files or ERPs (an overlay on top of existing tools). It is not a full POS or billing tool for a small retailer.
- Offline or mobile use, Urdu interface and SRB/PRA/KPRA/BRA provincial integration are unverified. I found no evidence either way.
- Per-invoice tiers penalise low-volume users.

## Opening
Undercut it with a cheap or free-tier, mobile or offline-capable POS-plus-invoicing app for micro and small sellers, with built-in FBR and provincial sales tax reporting and simple certificate/token onboarding.
