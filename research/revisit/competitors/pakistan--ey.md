# EY (EY Ford Rhodes), Pakistan
Dropped idea: C0734, FBR B2B digital invoice for SMEs, Pakistan. Searches used: 4 of 6 (English and Urdu).

## Verdict
weak (as an SME competitor). EY is a licensed FBR integrator, but it is a Big-4 firm selling SAP-centred enterprise integration, and PRAL, the state integrator, offers free integration.

## Evidence
- EY (Pvt) Ltd is one of four FBR-licensed digital invoicing integrators, alongside PRAL, Haball and WebDNAworks. Sources: https://www.dawn.com/news/1903690/fbr-notifies-four-firms-to-register-retailers ; https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/04/13/fbr-approves-four-companies-for-retailers-digital-invoice-integration/
- EY's own listing and job post describe the work as SAP and non-SAP integration with FBR, with iFlows and APIs on SAP BTP Integration Suite. This is enterprise-oriented. Source: https://careers.ey.com/ey/job/Karachi-Indirect-Tax-Assistant-Manager-E-invoicing-Karachi%2C-Pakistan-75600/1431750133/
- EY publishes tax alerts on the mandate, which now covers non-corporate persons too (deadlines 1 June 2025 corporate, 1 July 2025 non-corporate). Source: https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/pakistan-extends-scope-of-electronic-invoicing-to-corporate-and-noncorporate-registered-persons
- PRAL has stated it will provide integration at no cost. Source: https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40362590
- Momentum: EY is actively hiring for e-invoicing roles in Karachi (job post above). The date of that post is unverified.
- Complaints: none found about EY specifically. No app-store, Reddit or forum reviews were surfaced. One FTO investigation into FBR's forced integration of businesses exists, but it is about FBR, not EY: https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/04/07/fto-orders-investigation-into-fbrs-forceful-online-integration-of-businesses/

## Pricing
Not published. Unverified. Other (non-PRAL) integrators may charge for integration, but no figures were found. A free state option (PRAL) caps what any integrator can charge small buyers.

## Fit gaps
- Positioned at SAP and larger corporate environments, not micro or small firms (inferred from EY's own service description).
- No evidence of an SME self-serve product, Urdu interface or mobile or offline use (unverified).
- It is an integrator service, not a product. The SME buyer must supply or buy its own invoicing or ERP software.

## Opening
SMEs need cheap, simple, Urdu-friendly invoicing software that plugs into FBR. EY serves enterprises, PRAL is free but is a gateway rather than SME software, so the opening is the SME software layer. The risk is that the free PRAL route and other integrators crowd it.
