# Pakistan: global traceability platforms (TrusTrace, Retraced, TextileGenesis, FibreTrace, Tracextech, Green Story, etc.)

Idea killed: C0737 (Textile DPP/HREDD traceability). No single URL; category of commercial platforms. 6 WebSearch calls used (English plus one Urdu query).

## Verdict: beatable

Credible global vendors exist and some are already in Pakistan, but they target brands and large vertically integrated mills. No evidence found of a low-cost, local, SME-focused DPP/HREDD tool, and no public price or complaint data was found.

## Evidence
- DPP under ESPR is expected to apply to textiles from about 2027; one result says roughly 75% of Pakistan's textile exports go to the EU, UK and US (aptma.org.pk/?p=12361).
- Pakistan's Commerce Coordinator met Octans Digital about introducing a DPP for textile exports, a sign of government interest but not a deployed product (same APTMA source).
- A DPP awareness, HREDD feasibility and traceability assessment project, including a National Cotton Traceability System with National Textile University, is under way (gopa.eu/en/stories/pakistan-paris-advancing-transparency-sustainability-textile-sector). This is donor-led and may crowd out or complement a private product.
- Large Pakistani firms are adopting tools: Sapphire's Diamond Denim and Artistic Apparels use FibreTrace (textileworld.com 2022). Interloop is building its own tool, Looptrace, and the discussion asks who benefits given the data-gathering cost (aptma.org.pk/?p=10153). Large players are covered or self-built.
- Smaller manufacturers were reported far from ready for EU requirements (WWF SMEP success-story PDF, wwfasia.awsassets.panda.org).
- Tracextech and Green Story market textile compliance/DPP offerings (tracextech.com, search snippets). Their Pakistan presence is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or Urdu-forum reviews surfaced about these platforms in Pakistan. Absence of results is not evidence of satisfaction.
- Momentum: category is active (regulation-driven, funding and pilots); specific vendor momentum not verified.

## Pricing
- TextileGenesis: yearly SaaS licence tiered by supplier count and fibre volume; no public price list, demo first (per search summary of netzerocompare.com / ki-syndikat.de). No figures found.
- FibreTrace: "Mapped" tier described as no fee; the verified tier needs pigment plus a handheld Bluetooth scanner; cost unverified.
- TrusTrace, Retraced: no pricing found (unverified).
- Typically these are paid by brands, so suppliers may get free or sponsored access, but that is unverified.

## Fit gaps (mostly inferred, unverified)
- Built for brands and tier-1 mills; SME sub-suppliers (spinners, dyers, stitching units) likely get thin support.
- No evidence of Urdu-language UI or local-data/Pakistani cotton-ginner integration.
- FibreTrace needs physical pigment and scanners (hardware).
- Brand-led platforms push data collection onto many suppliers, each with a different system, creating fatigue; a supplier-side multi-buyer tool is not evidenced.

## Opening
A cheap, supplier-side, buyer-agnostic DPP/HREDD data tool for Pakistani SME textile units (one data entry, many brand formats), but the market is donor- and brand-driven and demand timing depends on 2027 DPP rules, so validate willingness to pay first.
