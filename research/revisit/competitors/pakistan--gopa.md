# GOPA (Pakistan) - competitor check for C0737 (Textile DPP/HREDD traceability)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

GOPA Worldwide Consultants is a consultancy running a donor-style awareness, feasibility and assessment project. It does not sell a software product. (Based on 4 searches; the project's funder and timeline are unverified.)

## Evidence
- GOPA's ECP team runs a "Digital Product Passport Awareness, HREDD Feasibility and Traceability Assessments Project in Pakistan". It supports manufacturers and the National Textile University in developing a National Cotton Traceability System. Source: https://www.gopa.eu/en/news/pakistan-paris-advancing-transparency-sustainability-textile-sector/
- The search results did not show a commercial GOPA DPP or traceability app, a product page, or a pricing page. This is a project and advisory engagement. The NTU cotton system is a national-level effort, not a SaaS product for SMEs.
- Related Pakistani DPP training and awareness activity shows demand and a market gap, not a product incumbent: https://fpcci.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/7.0-Training-of-Trainers-ToT-on-Digital-Product-Passport-Pakistan.pdf (contents not verified in detail).
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, review-site, Reddit or forum material exists for GOPA in this context. Searching in Urdu returned nothing GOPA-specific.
- Momentum: the project is active and recent (the pages appeared in 2025-2026 results), but its end date is unverified. Project-based work ends when funding ends.

## Pricing
None published. Consultancy and project work, with no price list found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- It is not a product, so there is nothing for SMEs to buy or subscribe to.
- It serves manufacturers and institutions via awareness and assessment, not ongoing DPP or HREDD data tooling.
- About 80% of Pakistan's textile value chain is fragmented, with many standalone SMEs (reported in the GOPA page). A project engagement is unlikely to cover them at scale.

## Opening
GOPA builds awareness and the national cotton traceability groundwork, which creates demand. An affordable SME-focused DPP/HREDD tool is still open, and GOPA's outputs could be partners or data sources rather than rivals.
