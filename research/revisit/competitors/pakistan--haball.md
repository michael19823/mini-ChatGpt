# Haball (Pakistan) - competitor check for C0734 (FBR B2B digital invoice for SMEs)

## Verdict: strong

Haball is an FBR-licensed e-invoicing integrator and already serves about 8,000 SMEs. It is well funded.
Its invoicing product is a side line to its supply-chain finance business, so a narrow SME tool may still find room (see the opening below).

## Evidence
- FBR granted M/s Haball (Pvt.) Ltd., Karachi a licence to integrate electronic invoicing (June 2024): https://propakistani.pk/2024/06/27/fbr-grants-license-to-firm-for-integration-of-electronic-invoicing/
- Licensed integrators named in search results: PRAL, Haball, EY, WebDNAworks. PRAL says it will serve taxpayers at no cost: https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40362590
- Haball's own product page describes real-time FBR registration, pre-submission validation and an FBR QR code per invoice: https://haball.pk/wasl/ (as summarised by search; page not opened)
- Funding: US$52M raised (about $5M equity and $47M financing), led by Zayn VC and Meezan Bank, announced March/April 2025. Claims about 8,000 SMEs and up to 70 multinationals. Expanding to Saudi Arabia: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250331198096/en/Haball-Secures-US%2452-Million-Funding-Led-By-Zayn-VC
- Business model: payments aggregator, digital invoicing and supply-chain financing (Dealroom/Profit summaries). The customer base skews to corporate supply chains such as Coca-Cola, with SMEs reached through those chains.
- Momentum: active and growing. No sign of shutdown, acquisition or stagnation.

## Complaints
- No complaints about Haball itself found. Searches covered Google Play reviews, English and Urdu. Search results returned nothing relevant (one search returned only unrelated apps). Not verified in the app stores directly.
- Market-level pain (not specific to Haball): PCDMA and other trader bodies called the August 2025 e-invoicing rollout abrupt and "disastrous for SMEs" because of missing infrastructure, training and resources: https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40375982 and https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40370109

## Pricing
- Haball's price list for e-invoicing: not found (unverified).
- Price benchmarks: PRAL offers integration at no cost. Reporting says the invoice charge is capped at Rs10 per transaction, and the integration fee is capped by FBR order. Sources: https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40362590 and https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/04/04/fbr-expands-digital-invoicing-network-with-two-new-private-integrators/
- Any competing SME tool faces a free government integrator and capped fees.

## Fit gaps
- Focus is corporate supply chains and financing. An SME with no anchor corporate may not be well served (inferred, unverified).
- The mandate is phased: it applies first to large taxpayers and to those with high turnover (Rs1 billion or more per Urdu news reports) and sales-tax-registered importers. Small buyers have a later and uncertain timeline.
- Urdu-language UI, offline use and mobile use: unverified.

## Opening
Little room for a basic FBR submission tool, because integration is free or capped and Haball is funded. The opening is a simple Urdu-first, mobile or offline invoicing and bookkeeping tool for micro-SMEs that the integrators ignore, charged for the software rather than for the integration.
