# PBC (Pakistan) - competitor check for C0737 (Textile DPP/HREDD traceability)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

PBC is the Pakistan Business Council, a business-policy and research body. Its DPP work is a published research report with policy recommendations, not a software product. (Based on 3 searches; I did not need the other 3.)

## Evidence
- PBC published the report "Woven In or Locked Out? The Future of Pakistan Textiles Exports Under the EU's New Standards" (reported as June 2026). It covers ESPR, DPP and PFAS, and compares measures taken by China, Bangladesh, India and Turkey. Source: https://www.pbc.org.pk/research/woven-in-or-locked-out-the-future-of-pakistan-textiles-exports-under-the-eus-new-standards/
- It recommends fast-tracking and funding a National DPP Platform / dashboard run by the National Compliance Centre, integrated with the Pakistan Single Window "at minimal transaction cost to SMEs". This is advocacy for a government platform, not a PBC product. Source: https://www.pbc.org.pk/wp-content/uploads/Woven-In-or-Locked-Out-The-Future-of-Pakistan-Textiles-Exports-Under-the-EUs-New-Standards.pdf (summary from search results only).
- The report says EU importers file the DPP but factories must supply the data, so the compliance burden falls on Pakistani factories. This supports demand for a tool.
- Real traceability activity exists elsewhere, for example Sapphire and Artistic Apparels with FibreTrace (physical tracer). That is a different vendor, not PBC. Source: https://apparelresources.com/business-news/sustainability/pakistans-textile-manufacturers-adopt-traceability-solution-fibretrace/
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, review-site, forum or news material about a PBC product. An Urdu search returned nothing PBC-specific.
- Momentum: active as a research and policy voice (2026 report). It does not ship a product.

## Pricing
None. No product or price list exists. Unverified beyond search results.

## Fit gaps
- Nothing to buy or subscribe to.
- It argues for a state DPP platform. If that is built and free for SMEs, it could undercut a private tool, but that risk comes from the government, not from PBC.

## Opening
PBC documents the problem and the SME data burden but offers no tool. A low-cost factory-side DPP/HREDD data tool is open. The main risk to watch is the proposed National DPP Platform.
