# Pakistan: POS vendors (idea C0738: provincial sales tax POS integration)

Verdict: strong

The market is crowded with vendors that already ship FBR, PRA, SRB and KPRA integration, some at very low prices. A new entrant would be competing with a commodity feature.

## Evidence
- Many vendors sell FBR/provincial-integrated POS. Examples in search results: Oscar POS (oscar.pk), Mint POS, Tier3 "FBR POS" (Capterra), e-invoicing.pk, cornpos.com (has an SRB integration page). Sources: https://oscar.pk/blog/fbr-pos-integration-pakistan-guide , https://www.capterra.com/p/10003238/FBR-POS/ , https://cornpos.com/blog/pos-integration-with-srb , https://www.e-invoicing.pk/fbr-pos-integration.html
- A published PRA POS component and eIMS manual exists, so there is an official integration path: https://e.pra.punjab.gov.pk/templates/POS_COMPONENT_and_eIMS_User_Manual.pdf
- Vendor-neutral write-up says integration is enforced at PRA, SRB and KPRA level as well as FBR (search summary; the underlying source is a vendor blog, so treat as unverified).
- Complaints (these are about the FBR/PRAL system rather than the vendors): the Chainstore Association of Pakistan cited POS failures, "exorbitant integration costs", profile disconnections, missing invoice uploads and poor FBR/PRAL coordination. Sources: https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/?p=190179 , https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/?p=201924 , https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40368868
- Past vendor-quality problem: a blacklisted entity was found among 42 FBR-approved POS integrators (2020): https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2020/07/12/blacklisted-entity-among-42-fbr-approved-vendors-providing-pos-integration-to-retailers
- Regulatory churn: the FBR draft of 18 Feb 2026 proposes a new e-invoicing framework with a licensing regime for integrators (via search summary; details unverified). Provincial authorities opposed SRO 288(I)/2026: https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40411087
- No app-store, Reddit or Facebook reviews of specific vendors surfaced in the 6 searches (4 used), so no vendor-level complaints found. Urdu search returned only news about crackdowns and FBR system faults.

## Pricing
- Tier3 "FBR POS": Rs 1,000/month, free signup (Capterra listing; price as shown in search summary, not independently checked): https://www.capterra.com/p/10003238/FBR-POS/
- Oscar, Mint POS and others: no price found (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Complaints concern the FBR/PRAL backend, not an unmet vendor feature. Nothing found showing small buyers cannot be served.
- Possible gap (unverified, inferred from the news): multi-authority filing (FBR plus PRA/SRB/KPRA at once) for businesses in several provinces. Not confirmed as a real unmet need.

## Opening
Little: integration is a cheap, bundled commodity, so the only opening would be a multi-province compliance layer or integrator-licensing support once the 2026 FBR rules settle.
