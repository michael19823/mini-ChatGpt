# Ramco Payce (Pakistan) - competitor check for C0736 (EOBI/PESSI/SESSI contribution compliance)

## Verdict: weak

Ramco Payce is an enterprise global payroll product from Ramco Systems. Nothing found shows a Pakistan-specific EOBI/PESSI/SESSI offering, and it is not aimed at small Pakistani buyers.

## Evidence
- Searches for "Ramco Payce" with EOBI/PESSI/SESSI returned no Ramco page that mentions Pakistan statutory contributions. Results were Odoo and Ontop pages. Pakistan-specific coverage by Ramco is unverified.
- Ramco Payce is described on zoftwarehub as a unified global payroll solution deployable on cloud or as a managed service (https://zoftwarehub.com/ar-sa/products/ramco-payce/overview).
- Complaints (about Ramco Global Payroll / Ramco HCM generally, not Pakistan-specific): per search summaries of Gartner and GetApp, support is a weak point (timelines not followed, issues not fully resolved) and the system is very flexible but hard to configure. Ratings: 4.3/5 (9 reviews) on GetApp; 3.5 (8 validated reviews) on Gartner. I did not read the individual reviews, and the numbers come from search summaries.
  - https://www.getapp.com/hr-employee-management-software/a/ramco-global-payroll/
  - https://www.gartner.com/reviews/product/ramco-hcm-integrated-with-global-payroll
- No Pakistan-specific complaints, forum threads or local-language (Urdu) results found. No news on outages or failed filings found.
- Momentum: not established. No data found either way, so unverified.

## Pricing
- Capterra lists Ramco Global Payroll as starting at $8 (search summary says "per month"; unit and minimums unverified). Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/158667/Ramco-Global-Payroll/alternatives/
- For comparison, local Pakistani tools, per search snippets: HysabOne from PKR 3,499/month (https://hysabone.com/products/payroll-attendance/); a Pakistani vendor quotes standalone payroll software from PKR 80,000 (one-time, apparently) (https://softvirtue.com/software/payroll-software). Both are vendor claims, unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Enterprise/global positioning, with implementation and configuration overhead. Likely poor fit for small Pakistani firms (inference).
- No evidence of EOBI, PESSI, SESSI or provincial filing formats. Unverified.
- Local alternatives (Odoo Pakistan payroll, HysabOne, SoftVirtue and others) explicitly advertise EOBI/PESSI/SESSI, so they are the more relevant competitors for this idea.

## Opening
Ramco does not visibly occupy small-firm Pakistani EOBI/PESSI/SESSI compliance, but local payroll tools (HysabOne, Odoo localisation, SoftVirtue) do, so the real check for C0736 is those, not Ramco.
