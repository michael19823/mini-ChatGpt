# Bisan NGO module (Palestine) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0744 Palestine NGO grant accounting. Searches used: 4 of 6.

## Verdict: strong

Bisan is a long-established local vendor (Ramallah, since 1988) with a dedicated NGO fund-accounting product that Palestinian NGOs already use.

## Evidence
- Bisan NGO is a dedicated ERP product for NGOs covering budgeting, fund accounting, and revenue and expenditure tracking per project, category, activity or cost center. Its marketing says it was built with funders' reporting requirements in mind. Source: https://www.bisan.com/storage/products/Bisan%20NGO.pdf (vendor material, so promotional).
- Bisan Systems Ltd. is based in Ramallah, founded 1988, with offices in Jerusalem, Jordan and the USA. Source: https://bisan.com/about-us and https://craft.co/bisan-systems.
- It is widely named in Palestinian NGO accountant and finance-officer job postings, which shows real adoption. Examples on https://jobs.ps and https://en.pngoportal.org/post/3686/Near-East-Council-of-Churches-DSPR-Gaza-Job-Vacancy-Accountant.
- The vendor claims a 2021 "Best Cloud ERP Solution Company" award in the Middle East and Africa. This is unverified and comes from its own site.
- Momentum: the vendor is active, with current brochures and cloud ERP. No sign of shutdown or acquisition. This is unverified beyond the vendor's own material.

## Complaints
None found. Searches in English and Arabic returned only vendor material and job postings. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced. This is not evidence that the product is good, only that no public complaints turned up.

## Pricing
Not published. Unverified; probably quote-based.

## Fit gaps
All unverified, because no independent sources were found.
- Likely aimed at mid-size and large NGOs, as a full ERP.
- It is probably a heavy implementation for very small NGOs and community groups.
- No evidence either way on donor-specific templates, offline use, or multi-language reports.

## Opening
Only a possible niche: very small NGOs priced out of a full ERP. That is unproven, and Bisan already holds the trust and installed base among Palestinian NGOs.
