# Bisan NGO (Palestine) - competitor check

Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Arabic). Idea it killed: C0745 NGO donor financial reporting Palestine.

## Verdict
**strong** (on available evidence; confidence moderate, because no user reviews were found either way).

## Evidence
- Bisan Systems Ltd. is based in Ramallah with offices in Jerusalem, Jordan and the USA. It sells "Bisan ERP NGO", a fund-accounting product with budgeting, per-project/category/activity/cost-center tracking, extensive donor reports, multi-currency, cloud deployment and 365-day support. Source: vendor pages https://www.bisan.com/bisan-erp-ngo and https://www.bisan.com/non-governmental-sector (as shown in search snippets; vendor marketing, not independent).
- Vendor says it was built with input from NGOs and funders on reporting requirements. Source: https://www.bisan.com/storage/products/Bisan%20NGO.pdf (vendor claim).
- Entrenched in the local market: many Palestinian job ads for finance officers/accountants list Bisan experience, e.g. https://www.jobs.ps/en/jobs/financial-officer-65397 and https://www.jobs.ps/en/jobs/accountant-60870. Arabic search likewise surfaced job ads (jobs.ps) referencing the Bisan system for civil-society finance roles. I only saw search snippets for these, so the exact wording is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of Bisan surfaced in English or Arabic searches (the Capterra results were for other products; one Arabic result was about a different product, "Busy"). Absence of reviews is not evidence of satisfaction; it likely reflects a local, direct-sales product with little public review footprint.
- Momentum: appears active (cloud and multi-currency features advertised; current vendor site with several subdomains). Exact release dates, acquisitions or shutdown news: not found/unverified.

## Pricing
Not published in anything found. Unverified. Likely quote-based enterprise/implementation sale (unverified inference); no evidence either way on small-NGO affordability.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed from evidence. Possible gaps to test by interview, all unverified: heavy ERP implementation for very small NGOs; donor-specific report formats (e.g. USAID/EU templates) beyond the generic donor reports; Excel/mobile-first workflows; Gaza connectivity and offline use.

## Opening
Little visible: Bisan is a local, NGO-specific incumbent with donor reporting already built in; any gap would be a lightweight, cheap layer for very small NGOs or donor-template-specific report generation that sits on top of Bisan or Excel, and needs customer interviews to confirm.
