# Bisan Systems (Palestine) - competitor check for C0742 (Palestine SME accounting/VAT)

## Verdict: strong

Established local incumbent (since 1988) with a broad ERP and an SME-capable Trade & Industry edition, a large local user base and a university training pipeline. No complaints found.

## Evidence (4 searches, English and Arabic)
- Bisan Systems Ltd., IT solutions provider founded 1988 in Ramallah. Claims 35+ years and 12,000 users (company's own claims). Source: https://bisan.com , https://bisan.com/about-us
- Products: Government Edition, NGO, Trade & Industry ("ERP for small, medium and large enterprises"), Loan Tracking, others. Source: https://www.bisan.com/storage/products/Bisan%20T&I.pdf and sibling brochures on bisan.com
- Company claims: 365-day support, zero hardware investment, continuous upgrades at no extra cost (company marketing, unverified independently).
- Local ecosystem: Birzeit University runs student training on the "Bisan accounting system" and signed an MoU; Palestinian job ads ask for Bisan skills. Source: https://www.birzeit.edu/ar/news/kly-lhqwq-wldr-lm-tnzm-tdryban-hwl-nzm-bysn-lmhsby , https://jobs.ps/jobs/accountant-4994
- Complaints: none found. Only client testimonials on the vendor site (positive, vendor-curated). No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced.
- Palestine VAT-specific filing support: not confirmed or denied in results (unverified).
- Momentum: active marketing and brochures; current development cadence not verified. No sign of shutdown or acquisition.

## Pricing
Not published; contact-sales only. Search results contained no prices. Unverified whether it suits micro or small businesses.

## Fit gaps (all unverified inferences, not found in sources)
- Positioning is ERP across government, NGO and large firms; may be heavy or costly for micro-SMEs and sole traders.
- Unclear whether there is a mobile-first, self-serve or low-priced tier.
- Unclear Palestinian VAT e-filing or invoicing integration.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, mobile-first VAT and bookkeeping tool for micro and small shops could sit below Bisan's ERP, but it must overcome Bisan's incumbency and accountant familiarity.
