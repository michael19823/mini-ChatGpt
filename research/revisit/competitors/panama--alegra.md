# Alegra (Panama)

Killed idea: C0746, Panama e-invoicing for SMEs off the free DGI tool. Competitor type: commercial. URL: not given in the brief. Alegra publishes a Panama blog at blog.alegra.com/panama/.

## Verdict: strong

Alegra is a DGI-authorized PAC, it targets exactly this SME segment, and it is actively marketing in Panama. Complaint evidence is thin.

## Evidence
- Search snippets from the Alegra Panama blog say Alegra is authorized by the DGI as a PAC. It sends invoices to the PAC, gets the CUFE and emails the receipt to the client. Source: https://blog.alegra.com/panama/alegra-contabilidad-facturacion-electronica/ (self-published, so treat as marketing).
- Alegra has a Panama blog post titled "alternativas al facturador gratuito", aimed squarely at SMEs leaving the free DGI tool: https://blog.alegra.com/panama/alternativas-al-facturador-gratuito/ . This is the idea's target segment.
- The same blog says that from 1 Jan 2026 the free DGI tool is limited to taxpayers with annual gross income up to B/.36,000 and 100 documents a month. Anyone above that must use a PAC, which widens Alegra's market. This is a search-engine summary of the blog; verify against the DGI.
- The blog's firma-electronica-panama post covers the electronic signature requirement, so Alegra also guides users through that step.
- Panama-specific content also covers ITBMS registration and MEF reports.
- Momentum: it is active. There is recent Panama content, an article on avoiding DGI sanctions from e-invoicing errors (portalerp.com), and a mobile app. Not stagnant, not acquired, not shut down (as far as the search showed).
- Apple App Store listing, "Alegra: Factura y Contabilidad", Panama storefront: https://apps.apple.com/pa/app/alegra-factura-y-contabilidad/id1084859936 . A search summary gives 4.2/5 from 82 ratings on that listing and 4.5/5 from 3.4K reviews on another source (unverified which store). Positive comments: simple, intuitive, easy for non-accountants. One suggestion: more customizable report formats with letterhead.
- Complaints: Capterra (https://capterra.com/p/196363/Alegra-ERP/) shows only 3 reviews. One is 3/5, titled "Lacking features and support": support does not help with simple issues and communications are slow and unprofessional. Reviewers like the interface and reports. That is one anecdote and not Panama-specific.
- No Panama-specific complaints were found. No Reddit, Facebook or local-forum complaints surfaced, and no outage or failed-filing news. Finding none is a valid result. The one Spanish search for opinions and complaints returned irrelevant results (unrelated Panama news), so local-language complaint coverage is weak. One search call failed on a usage limit, so the Panama pricing search never ran.

## Pricing
Not verified. No Panama price was retrieved. The app is described as free with in-app purchases and a 15-day free trial of all functions. Check the Alegra Panama pricing page before relying on any number. No evidence either way that it is overpriced for micro-businesses.

## Fit gaps
- Nothing confirmed. It is a general SME accounting suite with e-invoicing, so a lighter invoice-only tool might suit micro-businesses. That is speculation, not evidence.
- Support responsiveness, from a single Capterra review (unverified for Panama).
- Report format customization, from one app review.

## Opening it leaves
Narrow at best: an invoice-only tool for the smallest taxpayers near the B/.36,000 and 100-documents-a-month free-tool limit, or better support in Panama. Alegra already owns the PAC route with SME-focused content.
