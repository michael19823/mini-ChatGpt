# Competitor: CargoWise partners (Panama), killed C0754 (Panama CUSCAR manifest filing)

## Verdict: beatable

CargoWise (WiseTech) is a real general forwarding platform, but no search result showed a native Panama SIGA/CUSCAR filing module or a Panama partner. It targets large forwarders and is costly and complex for small carriers and agents. 4 of 6 searches were used (the rest were unneeded).

## Evidence
- No result confirmed CargoWise integration with Panama's SIGA customs system. The search tool itself said Panama-specific integration is not documented in what it found (unverified either way). Searched English and Spanish.
- Panama manifests go through SIGA (built by CrimsonLogic). Per the search summary, from 23 March 2026 shipping companies and agents must file manifest and transport documents in CUSCAR 11A, at least 24h in advance (source: La Estrella, https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/aduanas-panama-nueva-regla-de-trazabilidad-maritima-sera-obligatoria-desde-marzo-PD20891079). I did not read the article itself, so verify.
- Dedicated Panama CUSCAR filing services already exist for small/mid carriers and agents (e.g. Global eTrade Services: https://globaletrade.services/blogs/panama-cargo-manifest-filing-solutions-mid-small-size-carriers-and-cargo-agents). This is a competitor to check separately.
- Complaints about CargoWise generally (secondhand, via aggregator/competitor pages, not read in full; treat as unverified): G2 4.3 (35 reviews), Capterra 2.9 (32 reviews); complaints about support (1.9/5 on Capterra/Software Advice), complex interface, pricing opacity and unexpected increases. Source: https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/cargowise ; Magaya page https://www.magaya.com/magaya-vs-cargowise-what-the-industry-is-actually-saying/ (a rival vendor, biased).
- No Panama-specific complaints, app-store reviews, Reddit or forum posts found. No complaints found for Panama.
- Momentum: active. WiseTech moved CargoWise to per-transaction "Value Packs" on 1 Dec 2025; forwarders report modelled cost rises of 20-50% (https://breakbulk.news/forwarders-face-a-high-stakes-test-as-wisetech-shifts-cargowise-costs-onto-shippers/ ; trade press, via search summary).

## Pricing
- Since 1 Dec 2025, per-transaction rather than per-seat. Examples from search summary: $19.95 full import container with inland, $9.95 standalone import customs entry (unverified; I did not open the source; source listing: https://theloadstar.com/wisetech-ceo-on-cargowise-pricing-we-knew-this-would-be-disruptive/). No published minimum found. Implementation/partner fees not found.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at the largest forwarders and 3PLs; heavy overhead and training for small firms.
- No evidence of Panama CUSCAR 11A / SIGA submission out of the box; likely needs local partner customization (unverified).
- Complex UI, weak support per reviews, opaque pricing.
- Does not serve a small Panamanian agent wanting only manifest filing.

## Opening
A lightweight, Spanish-language, pay-per-filing CUSCAR 11A tool for small Panama shipping agents and carriers is not served by CargoWise, but check Global eTrade Services and CrimsonLogic/SIGA tooling before committing.
