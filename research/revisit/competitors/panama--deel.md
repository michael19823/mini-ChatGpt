# Panama: Deel (commercial) vs C0748 (Panama CSS reform payroll)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor
Deel is a global employer-of-record (EOR) and contractor platform for foreign companies hiring in Panama. It is not local payroll software for Panamanian employers who run their own SIPE/CSS filings.

## Evidence
- Deel's Spanish EOR page says that under EOR, CSS registration, calculation and monthly remittance are automatic and the client has no direct CSS obligations. The model is Deel employing the worker, not software for a local employer. Source: https://www.deel.com/es/blog/css-panama-seguridad-social-eor/ and https://www.deel.com/es/hiring/employees/panama/
- A search result about Panama payroll said Deel, Remote and Rippling run through a partner network in Panama. That is a third-party summary and unverified; I did not confirm it on Deel's own pages. Source: https://dsr.mercans.com/payroll-dossiers/panama/
- Local payroll software exists (e.g. nominaHQ, which claims SIPE export files and Ley 462 updates). It is the more relevant competitor for the dropped idea. Source: https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1107667/nominaHQ
- Complaints found (global, not Panama-specific), from review-aggregator summaries rather than primary pages: payroll errors at scale, employees omitted from runs, and support that slows as headcount grows. Sources: https://www.gloroots.com/blog/deel-reviews and https://www.trustpilot.com/review/deel.com?page=7. These are secondhand summaries and I read no individual reviews.
- No Panama-specific complaints or CSS filing failures found. Searches in Spanish returned no Deel opinions from Panamanian small businesses.
- Momentum: active, with 150+ countries claimed and Trustpilot about 4.7 per a secondhand summary (unverified). Source: https://www.gloroots.com/blog/deel-reviews

## Pricing
- Third-party sources cite EOR at $599 per employee per month, with negotiated rates of about $400-500 at 20+ headcount. Sources: https://www.pin.com/blog/deel-pricing/ and https://www.deel.com/pricing. I did not verify the figure on Deel's own page. Statutory costs come on top; the employer CSS rate is 13.25% plus 1.50% educational insurance, per a Deel blog result.
- Deel's non-EOR payroll pricing for Panama was not found (unverified).
- $599 per head is far above what a small Panamanian employer would pay for local payroll software.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at foreign firms hiring in Panama, not Panamanian companies managing their own planilla.
- Per-employee EOR pricing is uneconomic for a local small business with many staff.
- Does not appear to give a local employer direct SIPE file export or control of CSS filings (unverified).
- Support complaints at scale (secondhand).

## Opening
Local Panamanian SMEs that file their own SIPE/CSS planilla are outside Deel's model, so the open ground is cheap local payroll software tracking Ley 462. Local tools such as nominaHQ are the real comparison.
