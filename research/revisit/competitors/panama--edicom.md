# EDICOM (Panama) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0746, Panama e-invoicing for SMEs off the free DGI tool.

## Verdict: weak (as a competitor for this SME segment)

EDICOM is a real, accredited PAC, but it is enterprise/ERP-integration focused and not aimed at micro and small businesses. The free-tool ceiling that began in 2026 creates a new group of SMEs that must pay a PAC.

## Evidence
- EDICOM is an accredited PAC in Panama, accredited 14 Nov 2022 by the DGI. Source: https://edicomgroup.com/blog/edicom-certified-as-a-qualified-authorization-provider-panama
- Its Panama offer centres on ERP integration and workflow automation for the SFEP. Source: https://edicomgroup.com/es/factura-electronica/panama
- From Jan 2026, the free DGI invoicer is limited to businesses with gross income up to B/.36,000 and no more than 100 documents a month. Above either limit, a business must use a paid PAC or a DGI-certified own system. Sources: https://beancount.io/es/blog/2026/09/21/panama-free-e-invoicing-ceiling-pac-migration-guide (secondary source), https://edicomgroup.com/blog/state-electronic-invoicing-panama
- PACs are described as aimed mainly at firms needing accounting-system integration, sold as folio packages priced monthly or yearly by document volume. Source: https://blog.alegra.com/panama/facturador-gratuito-dgi/
- Company scale: 2023 revenue EUR 219M, about 17,000 clients in 80+ countries, growth driven by large corporate accounts. Source: https://valenciaplaza.com/valenciaplaza/cuentas-edicom-2023
- Momentum: active and growing, with global expansion and Panama content kept up to date. Not stagnant, and no sign of acquisition or shutdown.

## Complaints
No complaints specific to EDICOM Panama were found. Searches in Spanish and English returned no Panama reviews or Google Play/App Store reviews. The only review data was global: G2 has 2 reviews averaging 4.5, and Gartner Peer Insights has 4 reviews averaging 4.3. Those summaries mention pricing opacity and implementation complexity for smaller IT teams (secondary summary, unverified at the review level). Source: https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/edicom

## Pricing
No published EDICOM Panama price was found. Pricing is by quote through sales, which makes it opaque. Unverified: it is likely overpriced and over-engineered for a micro or small business.

## Fit gaps
- Enterprise and ERP-integration positioning. There is no evidence of a self-serve plan for sole traders or small shops.
- No published pricing, so there is no small-buyer entry point.
- No evidence of a mobile-first or offline product (unverified).

## Opening
Panama SMEs pushed past the free DGI tool's B/.36k and 100-document caps need a cheap, self-serve, Spanish-language PAC-backed invoicer. EDICOM targets bigger accounts. The real competitors for this niche are the SME-focused PACs, such as Alegra, and they were not checked here.
