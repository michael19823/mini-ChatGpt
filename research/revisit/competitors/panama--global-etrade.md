# Global eTrade Services (GeTS) - Panama

Dropped idea: C0754 Panama CUSCAR manifest filing.

## Verdict: strong

It is a real, CrimsonLogic-owned vendor that markets Panama CUSCAR filing directly to small and mid-size carriers and agents. CrimsonLogic also built Panama's SIGA customs system. I found no complaints.

## Evidence
- GeTS publishes Panama-specific pages aimed at "mid small size carriers and cargo agents": https://globaletrade.services/blogs/panama-cargo-manifest-filing-solutions-mid-small-size-carriers-and-cargo-agents , https://globaletrade.services/blogs/cargo-manifest-panama , https://globaletrade.services/blogs/panama-cargo-manifest-cuscar-and-iata-standards-and-how-they-affect-carriers
- GeTS is a CrimsonLogic subsidiary (search summaries), and CrimsonLogic developed Panama's SIGA customs system (per GeTS blog text as summarised in search results; unverified beyond that).
- GeTS also sells an H2H eDeclaration service for the Colon Free Zone: https://globaletrade.services/en-gets-cfz
- Regulatory tailwind: per search summaries, ANA requires electronic CUSCAR 11A manifests at least 24h before arrival, mandatory from 23 March 2026 (La Estrella: https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/aduanas-panama-nueva-regla-de-trazabilidad-maritima-sera-obligatoria-desde-marzo-PD20891079 ; ANA spec: https://www.ana.gob.pa/w_ana/images/ANA_pdf/siga_portel/CUSCAR_SIGA_D11A_V1.4.7.pdf). Not read in full.
- Reviews: Gartner Peer Insights lists 5 reviews, average 4.6, mostly for its CALISTA product: https://external.pi.gpi.aws.gartner.com/reviews/vendor/global-etrade-services
- Complaints: none found in English or Spanish searches (app stores, Reddit, forums, Trustpilot). Not a proof of absence; the search was limited to 6 queries and none targeted Panama user forums directly.

## Pricing
- Panama CFZ H2H eDeclaration: $50/month including 20 transactions, then $2.50 each (search summary of the GeTS CFZ page).
- Panama CUSCAR manifest pricing: not found (unverified).
- Cross-border eManifest (US/Canada, not Panama): Self-File from $19/month and Managed from $50/month, no contract (search summary of GeTS site). This shows its general low-cost SaaS model.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of gaps for small carriers and agents; it targets exactly that segment.
- Possible gaps (all unverified): Spanish-language UI and support, local Panama presence, and integrations with local freight-forwarder or port community systems.

## Opening
Little room for a generic CUSCAR filer. Any opening would be a Spanish-first, local-agent product bundling manifests with other Panama workflows, and only if GeTS's local support or language coverage proves weak (unverified).
