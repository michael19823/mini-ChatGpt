# Panama: PAC providers (Proveedor Autorizado Calificado)

Killed idea: C0752 (Panama e-invoicing PACs). Type: commercial. No single URL; this is a category of DGI-authorized vendors.

Note: 4 of the 6 allowed searches returned results. One hit a usage limit, so the search for per-PAC pricing and reviews was not completed. Treat the verdict as provisional.

## Verdict
**strong** (provisional). The PAC layer is legally mandatory for anyone above the free tier and has many licensed, established vendors. A new entrant cannot replace the PAC role without DGI authorization, and no complaint evidence was found to show an opening.

## Evidence
- Law 256 of 2021 and Executive Decree 766 of 2020 require electronic documents to be validated by a PAC. Invoices are pre-authorized by a PAC before reaching the recipient. Sources: https://edicomgroup.com/es/blog/estado-factura-electronica-panama , https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/220527/factura-electronica-decision-acertada-progreso-empresa-pais
- From 1 Jan 2026 (DGI Resolution 201-6299) the free DGI invoicer is limited to taxpayers with up to B/. 36,000 annual income and at most 100 documents per month. Everyone above that must use a PAC or a self-built DGI-certified system. This pushes many small and mid-size firms into paid PACs. Sources: https://blog.alegra.com/panama/facturador-gratuito-dgi/ , https://www.forvismazars.com/pa/es/quienes-somos/noticias/nuestras-publicaciones/tax-legal/actualizacion-de-deberes-de-sfep-en-panama , https://beancount.io/blog/2026/09/21/panama-free-e-invoicing-ceiling-pac-migration-guide
- PACs named in search results: HKA, WEBPOS, EBIPAC. The DGI also publishes PAC authorization documents, for example one for Soluciones de Punto de Venta S.A. (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/_7facturaelectronica/pdf/201-9720 SOLUCIONES DE PUNTO DE VENTA S,A..pdf). The full PAC list was not retrieved.
- ERP and accounting vendors such as Alegra publish Panama e-invoicing content and appear to integrate with PACs (unverified which PAC they use). An Odoo module for Panama e-invoicing exists (https://apps.openerp.com/apps/modules/16.0/factura_electronica_pa).
- Decree 25 of 27 June 2022 allows exceptions for taxpayers with poor connectivity or special activity type (https://edicomgroup.com/es/blog/estado-factura-electronica-panama). This is a regulatory carve-out, not a product gap.
- Momentum: the active market and fresh 2026 rule change suggest vendors are growing. No acquisition, shutdown or stagnation evidence was found (unverified).

## Complaints
None found. Searches in Spanish and English for complaints, outages, reviews and Reddit discussion returned only regulatory and vendor-marketing pages. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or forum reviews of any PAC were surfaced. This is not proof they have none, since coverage was limited.

## Pricing
- Not verified from a primary source. One search summary mentioned an "EDocs" account at a 550 USD one-time fee plus 160 USD annual renewal, with signing plans sold separately. The vendor and source URL were not confirmed, so treat this as unverified.
- Other sources say PACs charge by volume tier, subscription or per-document fee, with no concrete small-buyer prices found.

## Fit gaps
- Not established from evidence. Plausible, but unverified: the 2026 free-tier cap creates a new pool of micro-businesses (above 100 documents per month or B/. 36,000 per year) that may find per-PAC onboarding fees or minimums heavy.
- Possible gaps for small buyers (all unverified): onboarding cost, digital signature purchase, no offline or mobile-first option.

## Opening
Little room to compete as a PAC itself; the only plausible opening is a cheap, simple front end or integration for micro-businesses just above the free-tier cap that routes through an existing PAC, and that needs pricing and complaint evidence this check did not find.
