# Propiedata (Panama) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0753 Panama PH/condo administration. Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Spanish).

## Verdict: beatable

Propiedata is a real, multi-country PH SaaS, but its Panama presence is only claimed in its own marketing, and its app rating is weak. A Panama-specific, small-building product still has room.

## Evidence
- Propiedata is a Colombia-origin SaaS for PH administration. Its own and third-party copy says it has a presence in Mexico, Panama, Ecuador, Peru and Chile. Modules: virtual assemblies (claims 5,000+ simultaneous participants), virtual intercom/visitor management, accounting, payments, reservations, mail and parking control. Panama presence is vendor-claimed only; local traction is unverified. Sources: https://www.g2.com/products/propiedata/discuss , https://www.enter.co/?p=509499
- Origin and assembly track record (600+ non-in-person assemblies) come from Colombian press: https://www.elheraldo.co/tecnologia/2021/06/04/crean-app-para-que-asambleas-de-propietarios-dejen-de-ser-un-dolor-de-cabeza-822915 , https://www.lafm.com.co/tecnologia/la-aplicacion-que-permite-gestionar-asambleas-virtuales-de-propiedad-horizontal
- Complaints: one search summary stated a Google Play average of about 2.30 stars from 230 ratings. This is unverified because I could not open the listing (developer listing: https://www.appbrain.com/dev/Propiedata+SAS/ ). No review text was seen. A Trustburn page exists (https://trustburn.com/reviews/propiedata) but its content was not verified. Other generic positive claims in the summary could not be tied to this product. No Panama-specific complaints, outage or failed-filing news found.
- Panama context: Ley 284 of 2022 allows in-person, virtual or hybrid owner assemblies, and a 2026 bill (112) would change fee and virtual-assembly provisions. This is a regulatory moving target that a local product could track faster. Sources: https://www.prensa.com/economia/que-cambiaria-en-los-ph-cuotas-remanentes-y-poderes-entran-al-debate-legislativo/ , https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/proteccion-de-datos-personales-que-rol-asumen-los-ph-en-panama-EC20000576
- Momentum: appears active (modern marketing copy, App Store and Play apps). No news of acquisition or shutdown found. Recency of releases is unverified.

## Pricing
Not published in anything found. Unverified. For reference only, a different product (pHKondo Cloud) lists from $6/month: https://phalkaline.gumroad.com/l/phkondocloud . Enterprise-style positioning (5,000-participant assemblies, intercom integration) hints at a larger-building focus, but this is inference.

## Fit gaps (mostly inferred, unverified)
- Multi-country product; no evidence of Panama-specific features: Ley 284 fee rules by unit area, remanentes, powers/proxies, Panama payment rails (Yappy, ACH, Clave), local accounting norms.
- Low app rating suggests mobile UX or reliability issues (unverified).
- Probably aimed at mid/large PHs and administrators; small buildings and self-managed PHs may be underserved.

## Opening
A Panama-native, Ley 284-aware, low-priced tool for small and self-managed PHs, with local payments, could beat a generic LatAm platform with a weak app rating; confirm demand first, since Panama pricing and traction for Propiedata are unknown.
