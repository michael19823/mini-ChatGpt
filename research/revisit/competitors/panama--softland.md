# Softland (Panama) - competitor check for C0746 (Panama e-invoicing for SMEs off free DGI tool)

Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Spanish). WebFetch and GitHub not used.

## Verdict: beatable

Softland is a real regional ERP vendor with a Panama office. In Panama it sells an ERP and HCM suite aimed at mid-sized firms and does not appear to target the micro and SME e-invoicing segment. I found no evidence that Softland is a qualified authorized provider (PAC). The bigger obstacle to the idea is the free DGI invoicer, not Softland.

## Evidence
- Softland has a Panama site (softland.com/pa) with news and blog posts that explain e-invoicing requirements and how to avoid DGI penalties. The same site lists support and partner pages. These are marketing content. https://softland.com/pa/?p=14071 , https://softland.com/pa/?p=14729
- Softland describes itself as present in 20+ countries with 35,000+ clients and 40+ years of experience (company marketing, unverified independently). https://www.comparasoftware.com/softland-suite-empresarial
- Market context from a DPL News article summarized in search: about 13,200 taxpayers registered for e-invoicing, 91% use the free DGI invoicer and only 9% go through a PAC. This is a search-result summary and I did not open the article. https://dplnews.com/panama-la-facturacion-electronica-una-estrategia-de-oportunidades/
- I did not find Softland on the DGI PAC documents returned by search. This is unverified, because I could not check the full PAC list. https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/_7facturaelectronica/fpreguntas
- Capterra lists Softland ERP at 4.5/5 from 6 reviews. This is a global listing and not Panama-specific. The one positive review quoted in search is from an online-business owner. https://www.capterra.com/p/196365/Softland-ERP/reviews/

## Complaints
- No Panama-specific complaints found in app stores, Reddit, forums or news. This is a valid null result. Search coverage was thin and mostly returned Softland's own pages.
- Two Chile-only complaints exist on reclamos.cl: poor post-sale service (2019) and an unannounced platform change (2021). Both are from another country and old, so they are weak evidence for Panama. https://www.reclamos.cl/softland/reclamo/2019/dec/softland_p_simo_servicio_post_venta , https://www.reclamos.cl/softland/reclamo/2021/jan/softland_cambio_de_plataforma

## Pricing
- No published prices for Softland in Panama found (unverified). Pricing appears to be quote-based through sales or partners.
- Capterra's page may show pricing but I did not see it in the results.

## Fit gaps (inferred, mostly unverified)
- Positioned as a full ERP (accounting, inventory, payroll, projects, production). That is likely too heavy and too costly for micro-SMEs that only need invoicing.
- Sold through sales contact and partners, not self-serve signup.
- No evidence of a standalone low-cost e-invoicing product or mobile-first invoicing.

## Momentum
- Active. The Panama site has news posts dated 2025 (January, June, November), and the Costa Rica site has a September 2025 post. No acquisition or shutdown found.

## Opening
Softland does not serve the tiny-SME invoicing segment, so it leaves the gap open. The binding constraint is that 91% of taxpayers already use the free DGI tool, so any opening depends on value beyond filing.
