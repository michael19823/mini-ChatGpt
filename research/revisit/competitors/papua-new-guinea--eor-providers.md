# EOR providers (PNG) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0757, PNG payroll SWT and super. Searches run: 5 of 6 (English only; no useful local-language/Tok Pisin sources expected, none found).

## Verdict: weak (for the dropped idea)

EOR/global-payroll players are not a real fit for a local-employer SWT + super payroll tool. They target foreign companies hiring in PNG, not local SMEs running fortnightly payroll.

## Evidence
- Providers listing PNG EOR/payroll: TopSource Worldwide, Mercans, Ontop, Pebl, Globalization Partners, Rivermate, Borderless (all found via search; their pages describe SWT, Nasfund/Nambawan Super, IRC filing). https://topsourceworldwide.com/global-payroll/papua-new-guinea/ , https://rivermate.com/en/guides/papua-new-guinea , https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/papua-new-guinea
- Remote.com's own country page says its EOR is not yet live in PNG (contractors only), per search summary. https://remote.com/country-explorer/papua-new-guinea
- Local option: PwC PNG offers web-based payroll services covering tax and super remittance. https://www.pwc.com/pg/en/services/tax/payroll-services.html (premium, large-firm positioning; pricing unverified)
- Odoo has an l10n_pg_payroll module computing fortnightly SWT and super. https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_pg_payroll (generic ERP, needs implementation)
- Complaints: none found specific to PNG. General EOR complaints (not PNG-specific): hidden fees, slow support, no dedicated account manager for Deel; Oyster reported by one user to rely on sub-vendors in APAC. Source: aggregator summaries (G2/Trustpilot/Capterra via search), e.g. https://www.g2.com/compare/oyster-oyster-vs-remote-vs-deel-payroll . Individual reviews not verified.

## Pricing
- Generic EOR: Deel and Remote from about $599/employee/month, Oyster about $699 (costbench/eorhq summaries, https://www.costbench.com/compare/deel-vs-oyster-hr/). Plus employer costs and possible deposit. No PNG-specific price found (unverified for PNG). Far beyond a small local employer's payroll budget.

## Fit gaps
- Built for foreign employers hiring individuals, not for local businesses with their own staff.
- Per-employee USD pricing is unsuitable for kina-denominated SMEs.
- Several major EOR brands not live in PNG; PNG delivered via partners/local entities.
- No evidence of self-serve software for local SWT returns, super fund file formats, or IRC filing.

## Opening
A cheap, kina-priced, self-serve local payroll tool (SWT, Nasfund/Nambawan Super schedules, IRC returns) is untouched by EOR firms; the real rivals are PwC-type outsourcers, Odoo, and spreadsheets.
