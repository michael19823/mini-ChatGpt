# Competitor check: local payroll bureaus, Papua New Guinea

Idea killed: C0757 (PNG payroll SWT and super). No single URL; competitor type is consultant.

## Verdict: beatable

Bureaus are human services (large accounting firms, EOR providers), not software. No named small-bureau pricing or complaints were found.

## Evidence
- PwC PNG advertises payroll services (processing, accounting records, tax remittances, compliance). Source: https://www.pwc.com/pg/en/services/tax/payroll-services.html
- Global EOR/payroll providers (Ontop, Playroll, Topsource, Rivermate, Globalization Partners) cover PNG payroll, including SWT, Nasfund/Nambawan super and IRC filing. They are aimed at foreign employers, not local small businesses. Sources: https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/papua-new-guinea, https://topsourceworldwide.com/global-payroll/papua-new-guinea/
- The only PNG-specific software found is an Odoo community module (l10n_pg_payroll) computing fortnightly SWT, super and remittance schedules. Source: https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_pg_payroll
- Compliance pain is real: super deducted but never remitted (Nasfund blog: https://www.nasfund.com.pg/blogs/5-common-superannuation-mistakes-png-workers-make); late super penalties; IRC 20% penalty interest on SWT shortfalls (per search summaries, unverified in detail).
- Local-language (Tok Pisin) search returned nothing relevant.
- Complaints: none found. Searches for local bureau reviews or complaints returned nothing (6 searches max rule; 4 used).

## Pricing
Not found. No published bureau prices (unverified). Big-firm and EOR pricing is likely out of reach for small employers (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Big-firm services target corporates and expats; small local employers are probably underserved.
- Global EORs serve foreign employers hiring in PNG, not local payroll.
- No found local self-serve SaaS handling fortnightly SWT, dual super funds and IRC/fund remittance schedules.

## Opening
A cheap self-serve PNG-specific SWT and super payroll tool for small and mid-size local employers, as no local software competitor surfaced; bureau and PwC services are manual and likely pricey.
