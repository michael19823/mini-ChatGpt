# EDICOM (Paraguay) - competitor check

Killed ideas: C0758 (Paraguay SIFEN SME e-invoicing), C0762 (SIFEN issuance all segments).
Searches used: 4 of 6 (English and Spanish). No URL was supplied for the competitor.

## Verdict: beatable

EDICOM is a real, active vendor with a dedicated Paraguay SIFEN page. It is an enterprise-style global platform, and I found no evidence it targets small Paraguayan SMEs. It is not a strong barrier for an SME-focused product.

## Evidence
- EDICOM publishes a Paraguay page and blog posts (Spanish and other languages) on SIFEN / e-kuatia and DTE issuance, validation and archiving. Its solution is described as streamlining DTE generation, validation and submission. Sources: https://edicomgroup.com/es/factura-electronica/paraguay ; https://edicomgroup.com/electronic-invoicing/paraguay ; https://edicomgroup.com/es/blog/como-es-la-factura-electronica-en-paraguay
- Momentum: marketing content is current and covers newly designated taxpayer groups, so it is actively maintained. Unverified: local presence, number of Paraguayan clients, and SET provider-approval status. Search results did not confirm any of these.
- Market context: SIFEN rollout continues through 2027 (https://www.sharedserviceslink.com/news/paraguay-extends-rollout-through-2027). Other vendors are also present, for example Sovos and Fonoa coverage, and a Uruguayan system noted by Infonegocios. These are not checked here.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in Spanish ("opiniones reclamos") and English ("reviews") returned no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of EDICOM in Paraguay. This is not evidence that it is well liked, only that no public feedback was found.

## Pricing
Not published in anything found. Unverified. EDICOM generally sells by quote as an enterprise platform, which is an assumption, not a finding.

## Fit gaps
Mostly inferred and unverified:
- Positioning is a global multi-country platform for companies with ERP integration needs, not a low-cost tool for micro and small businesses.
- Nothing found on a lightweight or mobile-first SME offering, or on a free tier.
- Nothing found on offline use.
- SET's own free e-kuatia tool already covers the smallest taxpayers, which squeezes the low end for any paid vendor.

## Opening
A cheap, simple, Spanish and Guarani-friendly SIFEN issuance tool for Paraguayan SMEs, with transparent pricing, is not contested by EDICOM. The main competition is the SET's free e-kuatia and local providers, not EDICOM.
