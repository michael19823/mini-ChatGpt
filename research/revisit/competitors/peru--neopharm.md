# NEOPHARM (Peru)

Idea it killed: C0764, Peru pharmacy DIGEMID reporting.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

No pharmacy-software or DIGEMID-reporting product called NEOPHARM was found. Only 3 of 6 allowed searches ran: one hit the usage limit and returned nothing. The verdict is therefore low-confidence.

## Evidence
- Search "Neopharm Peru" found only unrelated entities: Grupo Neolpharma (Mexican drug manufacturer with a presence in Peru), Neopharma E.I.R.L. (a registered company in Arequipa, https://tramitesperu.com/empresas/neopharma-e-i-r-l-20610989064/; its business activity is unverified), and Neopharm Labs (a Canadian manufacturer). None is shown to sell reporting software.
- A search for pharmacy software for DIGEMID price reporting found no commercial product. It showed that reporting is a legal duty: establishments must report prices monthly to the Observatorio Peruano de Productos Farmacéuticos (OPPF/SNIPPF) under DS 014-2011, art. 30 (https://www.elperuano.pe/noticia/214572-observatorio-de-precios-ayuda-a-acceder-a-medicamentos-de-calidad-y-al-menor-costo). DIGEMID trains pharmacy staff to file the reports on its own platform.
- The government platform is the real substitute for the idea. It is free.
- Complaints found: none. No app-store reviews, forum threads or news about NEOPHARM turned up. Nothing was searched on Google Play, Trustpilot or Reddit specifically.
