# Sysfarma (Peru) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0764, Peru pharmacy DIGEMID reporting. Searches used: 4 of 6 (Spanish and English).

## Verdict: strong

It targets the same buyer and already advertises DIGEMID reports at a low price. No complaints were found, but only because almost nothing is published about it.

## Evidence
- Listed on Comparasoftware and Capterra as cloud pharmacy software for independent pharmacies, drugstores and small chains in Peru: SUNAT e-invoicing, POS, FEFO inventory by batch and expiry, "reports for DIGEMID", valued kardex, expiry alerts, multi-branch (https://www.comparasoftware.com/sysfarma, https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1110090/Sysfarma).
- Web plus Android app, no installation, Spanish; migration from the previous system and WhatsApp support during onboarding (same listings).
- Complaints: none found. The listings say no reviews have been published. No Reddit, Facebook, Play Store or news hits about outages or failed filings turned up in these searches. Absence of reviews also means little customer traction is visible.
- Detail on what the "DIGEMID reports" actually cover (for example SNIPPF/OPPF price reporting) is unverified. The listings only say "reports for DIGEMID".
- Momentum: unverified. No dates, release notes or company news found. I did not find the company's own site in the results.

## Pricing
From S/69 per month, free demo (per the Comparasoftware and Capterra listings). Cheap for the segment, so not overpriced for small buyers. Higher-tier prices are unverified.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. It already serves small pharmacies and chains with a DIGEMID report feature.
- Unverified possible gaps: depth of DIGEMID price-reporting automation (direct upload versus export), controlled-substance books, and whether the reports work for non-pharmacy establishments.

## Opening
Little: only a narrow DIGEMID-reporting specialist, or a better-documented and better-supported product, could compete, and only if testing shows Sysfarma's DIGEMID reports are shallow.
