# Akrivia HCM (Philippines)

Dropped idea: C0770 (SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG remittance files). Type: commercial. Searches used: 4 of 6.

## Verdict
strong

## Evidence
- Akrivia sells a native Philippines payroll module that calculates SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and withholding tax, plus 13th-month, night differential and overtime rules (vendor's own page, so marketing, not independent): https://akriviahcm.com/products/payroll-philippines
- Part of a wider hire-to-retire suite with multi-entity and multi-country (SG, MY, UAE, India, GCC) payroll. Vendor claim: https://akriviahcm.com/blog/best-hr-software-philippines-2026
- Reviews are positive: Saasworthy lists 4.8/5 from 69 ratings (https://www.saasworthy.com/product/akrivia/reviews). G2 reviews are at https://g2.com/products/akrivia-automation-pvt-ltd-akrivia-hcm/reviews/akrivia-hcm-review-10888371.
- Complaints found were mild and not about the Philippines. G2 reviewers mention: notifications could be better, slow initial setup of workflows, rigid reporting, and a mobile app that lags the web version. One review noted missing German-specific compliance, with no DATEV connector and no German hosting.
- No complaints found about SSS, PhilHealth or Pag-IBIG remittance files, failed filings or outages. Searches did not reach Reddit, Facebook groups or Filipino-language sources (no Filipino-language results surfaced; unverified whether any exist).
- Momentum: active. The vendor publishes 2026 Philippines content. Acquisition or shutdown: none found.

## Pricing
Not published. Per-user pricing varies by module and headcount, with a custom quote required (Capterra and elearningindustry listings; exact figures not seen, unverified). Positioned as mid-market.

## Fit gaps
- Full HCM suite and quote-based sales suggest it is oversized for micro and small Philippine employers who only need remittance files (inference, unverified).
- Reviewers cite setup effort and a weaker mobile app.
- Whether it generates the actual SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG upload or remittance files, rather than just computing contributions, is unverified.

## Opening
A small-business, low-cost, remittance-file-only tool could undercut it. Akrivia's reviews show no Philippine-specific failures, so the gap is price and simplicity, not product quality.
