# BigSeller (Philippines) - competitor check for C0773 (e-commerce seller e-invoicing PH)

## Verdict: beatable

BigSeller is a marketplace-seller ERP (orders, inventory, listings) that treats e-invoicing as a side feature. No evidence found that it files in the BIR EIS JSON format. It is a real adjacent player with a free tier and many seller users.

## Evidence
- BigSeller markets itself as a free e-commerce ERP for Southeast Asian sellers covering Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop and 20+ platforms. Source: https://www.bigseller.com/blog/articleDetails/4485/best-erp-for-philippine-online-distribution-2026.htm (vendor's own blog; self-promotional).
- Its own blog guide says Philippine Shopee sellers can print e-invoices via BigSeller, with one-click invoice generation after an order and integration with multiple accounting software. Source: https://www.bigseller.com/blog/articleDetails/4285/how-can-shopee-philippines-sellers-print-and-upload-einvoice.htm. The search snippet describes printing and uploading to Shopee. Whether it generates BIR EIS-compliant JSON and submits to the BIR within 3 days is unverified.
- BigSeller publishes BIR e-invoicing explainers (RR 26-2025, deadline 31 Dec 2026, micro e-commerce taxpayers exempt). Source: https://www.bigseller.com/blog/articleDetails/4936/bir-e-invoicing-philippines-online-sellers-2026.htm. This shows it is aware of the mandate and is targeting the topic; the product itself is unverified.
- Momentum: actively publishing 2026 content, with several comparison blogs including BigSeller vs Ginee. This looks actively developed and marketed. Source: https://www.bigseller.pro/blog/articleDetails/4411/bigseller-vs-ginee-philippines-which-erp-should-filipino-sellers-use.htm
- Complaints: none found. Two searches (general review/complaint and Google Play/Trustpilot/Reddit) returned only BigSeller's own blog content or unrelated companies. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Reddit or Facebook reviews were retrieved. Local-language (Filipino) search also returned only vendor and news pages. Absence of complaints reflects thin search coverage, not proof of satisfaction.

## Pricing
- Per vendor-sourced blog posts: permanent free plan, up to 1,500 orders/month across 3 stores. Paid plans from SGD 39/month (about PHP 1,650). Higher tiers up to 300,000 orders/month and 500 stores. Source: https://www.bigseller.pro/blog/articleDetails/4578/top-5-inventory-management-systems-for-ph-ecommeerce-sellers-2026-.htm. Not verified against the official pricing page.
- A small seller can use it free, so BigSeller is hard to undercut on price. A standalone invoicing tool would need to be cheap or free for the invoicing part.

## Fit gaps
- It is an ERP/order tool, not a tax-compliance product. Verified BIR EIS filing (JSON format, submission, registration with BIR) is unconfirmed (unverified).
- Invoice flow seems built around marketplace (Shopee) orders. Sellers on their own sites, social selling (Facebook/Instagram), or B2B invoicing are likely not served (unverified).
- Tool is broad and may be heavy for a seller who only needs compliant invoices.
- Relies on third-party accounting software integrations for accounting.

## Opening
A narrow, BIR-EIS-certified invoicing and filing tool for small/medium PH sellers (including non-marketplace channels) can beat BigSeller if BigSeller only prints invoices rather than filing to the EIS.
