# ClearTax (Philippines) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0773 (E-commerce seller e-invoicing PH)

## Verdict: beatable

ClearTax runs a real, active Philippines e-invoicing product (cleartax.com/ph), but it is ERP-integration and enterprise oriented, with no public pricing, so it does not obviously serve small marketplace sellers. (Verdict based on 5 searches, mostly its own marketing pages; fit gaps are inferred, not confirmed.)

## Evidence
- Active PH product: ClearTax markets itself as a BIR EIS e-invoicing platform. It pulls data from an ERP, builds BIR JSON, applies a JWS signature, sends within the 3-day window, and offers consultants for the BIR Acknowledgement Certificate / Compliance Dossier. Source: https://www.cleartax.com/ph/en and https://www.cleartax.com/ph/e-invoicing-software
- Momentum: multiple PH pages for the 2026 timeline, penalties, FAQ and exemptions. Actively developed, which suggests it is investing in the market. Source: https://www.cleartax.com/ph/philippines-e-invoicing
- Mandate: RR 11-2025; e-commerce firms are among the first groups; BIR fine up to PHP 50,000 per violation (per BigSeller, a secondary source; unverified). Source: https://www.bigseller.pro/blog/articleDetails/3977/philippines-will-fully-mandate-einvoicing-december-31-2026.htm
- Seller-side tools: Shopee does not issue invoices for sellers, so sellers must do it themselves (per BigSeller). BigSeller recommends Taxumo as the most PH-focused tax tool; QuickBooks, Autocount and Odoo are mentioned as EIS-connectable. Source: https://www.bigseller.pro/blog/articleDetails/4576/top-5-financial-software-recommendations-for-philippines-ecommerce-sellers.htm
- Complaints: none found specific to ClearTax Philippines. Complaints found relate to ClearTax India (not PH): Trustpilot 1.5/5, reports of renewal costs doubling yearly, and ERP compatibility gaps (e.g. Dynamics 365) and login friction. Aggregated at https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/cleartax and https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/cleartax.in. Treat as weak, indirect evidence for PH.
- No Filipino-language (Tagalog) results turned up for ClearTax or small-seller e-invoicing.

## Pricing
- No public pricing for ClearTax e-invoicing (G2 pricing page says none provided). Quote or demo only. Source: https://www.g2.com/products/cleartax-e-invoicing/pricing
- A secondary source says entry-level cloud PH tools cost about PHP 131-551/month (unverified; source was a search summary, not checked). Quote-based enterprise pricing is likely well above that for a micro seller (inference).

## Fit gaps (inferred unless noted)
- Workflow is ERP-to-EIS; no evidence of Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop order import for individual sellers.
- Quote-based pricing and consultant-led onboarding point to mid-size and large taxpayers.
- Micro-taxpayers are exempt from the mandate unless they already issue e-invoices (per ClearTax's own exemptions page), so ClearTax has little reason to target them.
- No evidence of mobile-first or Tagalog UX.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, marketplace-order-to-BIR-e-invoice tool for small Shopee/Lazada/TikTok sellers is not served by ClearTax's enterprise ERP pitch. The real rivals there are Taxumo and bookkeeping tools, not ClearTax.
