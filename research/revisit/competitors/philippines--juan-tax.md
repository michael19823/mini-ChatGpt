# Juan(Tax) - Philippines (dropped idea C0772: BIR 2307 issuance and SAWT/QAP)

## Verdict: strong

JuanTax is a live, BIR-accredited (eTSP) cloud filing platform that already covers SAWT, QAP and SLSP submission and Form 2307. A small-buyer gap exists only if it is priced or designed for larger firms, and the searches did not show that.

## Evidence
- Describes itself as the first BIR-accredited tax platform (eTSP program) with tax automation and e-filing/payment. Source: https://juan.tax/blog/submit-qap-sawt-slsp-using-juantax
- Has a dedicated blog post on submitting QAP, SAWT and SLSP: https://juan.tax/blog/submit-qap-sawt-slsp-using-juantax and a SAWT page: https://juan.tax/sawt/
- Positions itself as a replacement for Alphalist Data Entry and eBIRForms (same blog post/help center).
- Active help center with release notes (help.juan.tax, e.g. https://help.juan.tax/en/articles/6-september-01-2022). Latest dated item I saw was 2022; the blog covers up to 2021-2022. I found no 2025/2026 news, so current momentum is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. A search for Google Play, Reddit and Trustpilot reviews returned only unrelated pages. A local-language search (Tagalog "reklamo") returned only JuanTax's own pages. No independent reviews were found, which is not the same as there being none. App-store ratings were not checked and are unverified.
- Late-filing policy: forms must be submitted 3 hours before the deadline and JuanTax disclaims delays not caused by its own system (https://help.juan.tax/en/articles/383-late-filing). This is a policy, not a complaint.

## Pricing
- Since 1 Sept 2022, Fast File forms cost PHP 100 each (per the search summary of https://help.juan.tax/en/articles/6-september-01-2022). Full subscription plan prices were not retrieved: unverified.

## Fit gaps (all unverified inference unless noted)
- The searches did not show whether it supports issuing 2307 certificates to payees in bulk or tracking 2307s received. They only showed that it files alphalists.
- The help article "my company uses eFPS, can I use JuanTax" (https://help.juan.tax/en/articles/413-my-company-uses-efps-can-i-use-juantax) suggests eFPS filers may be limited. Details were not read.
- No offline, mobile-first or multi-entity details were confirmed. A "JuanTax mobile" blog post exists.

## Opening
Thin: only a narrow tool for a segment JuanTax underserves (for example, bulk 2307 issuance/reconciliation for SMEs or bookkeepers, or eFPS filers) would be viable, and it would need to be verified first.
