# Juan (Philippines) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0770 SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG remittance files.
Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Filipino). WebFetch not used, so only search snippets were seen.

## Verdict: weak (for this idea)

Juan, as far as the search results show, is "Juan Accounting Software" by JuanTax. It is a BIR-accredited accounting, invoicing and tax-compliance product, not a payroll or remittance tool. None of the results showed it generating SSS, PhilHealth or Pag-IBIG remittance files. That is unverified, not confirmed absent. The real competitors for this idea are the actual payroll suites (Sprout, Omni HR, PayrollHero, Akrivia, ERPNext PH), not Juan.

## Evidence
- A search for "Juan" payroll returned PayMongo's partner post describing "Juan by JuanTax" as accounting, invoicing and BIR tax-compliance software with PayMongo payments in its free plan: https://www.paymongo.com/blog/paymongo-and-juan-by-juantax-partner-to-simplify-accounting-and-payments-for-philippine-smes and https://www.paymongo.com/company/partners/juan-accounting-software
- The search tool's own summary noted Juan appears to be primarily accounting software rather than a dedicated payroll or HR app. This is the search tool's inference, not a Juan statement.
- No SSS, PhilHealth or Pag-IBIG remittance feature of Juan appeared in any result.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of Juan surfaced in 5 searches, including a Filipino-language one. Finding none is a valid result, but it also means the search was shallow.
- Momentum: the PayMongo partnership suggests it is actively marketed, but I saw no date, so this is unverified.

## Pricing
- Juan: the PayMongo post mentions a free plan. Paid tiers and any payroll add-on were not found (unverified).
- For context, other PH payroll tools that do cover remittance, per search snippets and not checked on their own sites: Omni HR payroll add-on from about $2/employee/month on top of core HR from $3; PayrollHero from about $4/user/month; Sprout Solutions about PHP 7,900 to 10,000/month for 10 employees (Starter). Source: https://www.omnihr.co/ and https://www.hono.ai/blog/best-payroll-software-philippines via search snippets.
- That shows remittance-capable payroll for small firms already exists at fairly low per-head prices, and this applies to the idea's other killers, not to Juan.

## Fit gaps
- Juan: no evidence of payroll or remittance-file output (unverified). Primary focus is accounting and BIR compliance.
- Other incumbents' pricing and coverage were not examined in depth here.

## Opening
Juan itself does not appear to block a remittance-only tool for small employers. The idea's viability depends on the payroll suites (Sprout, Omni HR, PayrollHero, Akrivia), which are covered by separate checks.
