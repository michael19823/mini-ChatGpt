# Kaiser (Kaiser International Healthgroup, Inc.) - Philippines

Dropped idea: C0768 Philippines HMO LOA/claims hub. Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Filipino).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Kaiser is an HMO, meaning a payer and plan seller. It is not a software vendor. It would be a customer or integration partner for an LOA/claims hub, not a rival. The only caveat is that an HMO may run its own in-house LOA process (unverified).

## Evidence
- Kaiser International Healthgroup is a Philippine HMO, registered with the SEC in 2004 and licensed by the Insurance Commission. It is headquartered in Cebu City. It is unrelated to the US Kaiser Permanente. Sources: https://www.clinicfinderph.com/blog/kaiser-hmo-philippines-plans ; https://www.insurance.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/List-of-HMOs-with-CA-Issued-by-the-Insurance-Commission-as-of-31-January-2025.pdf
- It sells Short Term Care and Long Term Care plans to corporate, group, family and individual buyers (clinicfinderph.com, same page).
- LOAs are requested through hospital HMO desks or hotlines, for example the Kaiser coordinator's clinic in a hospital building listed on https://www.makatimed.net.ph/about-us/hmo/. This is a manual, hospital-side process.
- Complaints: none specific to Kaiser found. The searches returned only generic HMO claim-denial and complaint guides (e.g. https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/how-to-file-a-complaint-against-an-hmo-for-unpaid-reimbursements). Reddit results were about US Kaiser Permanente and are irrelevant. No Kaiser PH app-store reviews were found (unverified whether a member app exists).
- Momentum: no news found on acquisition, shutdown or product launches. Status is unknown.

## Pricing
Not published. Quotes come through an agent or live chat. One example from a state university benefit package: a maximum benefit limit of PHP 75,000 per illness, including pre-existing conditions (https://csu.edu.ph/news/2025/053125-hmo-medical-package). This is a plan price, not a software price, so it does not compare with a hub subscription.

## Fit gaps
- It does not sell LOA or claims software. The gap relative to the idea is total, because it is a different category.
- A hub serving hospitals and clinics across many HMOs would sit alongside Kaiser, not replace it.

## Opening it leaves
Kaiser leaves the whole hospital-side, multi-HMO LOA/claims workflow open. It is a potential integration partner or accredited-network customer, not an obstacle.
