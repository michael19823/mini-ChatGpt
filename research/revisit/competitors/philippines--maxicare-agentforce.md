# Maxicare Agentforce (Philippines)

Idea it killed: C0768, Philippines HMO LOA/claims hub.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Maxicare Agentforce is a captive, in-house Salesforce Agentforce deployment by one HMO (Maxicare). It is not a product sold to other HMOs, clinics or employers. It does not cover other HMOs' LOAs or claims.

## Evidence
- Maxicare is the first Philippine HMO to adopt Agentforce in Service Cloud. Partner dental clinics (over 720 in the network) self-serve LOA requests through an AI agent and get automatic notification. Source: https://www.salesforce.com/ap/customer-stories/maxicare-healthcare-corporation/ and https://www.salesforce.com/ap/news/press-releases/2025/09/10/maxicare-agentforce-dental-care/
- Claimed results, as reported by Salesforce (vendor claims, unverified independently): 20-minute manual process cut to 30 seconds, about 40,000 requests automated monthly, under 3% escalated to humans, 500% projected ROI. Same sources. Also reported by https://newsbytes.ph/2025/09/12/maxicare-adopts-agentforce-to-streamline-dental-care-processes/
- Scope is dental LOAs only, per the sources above. Maxicare's wider LOA flow is the Member Gateway portal, with a stated turnaround of up to 24 hours and 30-day validity. Source: https://www.maxicare.com.ph/?p=1573 and https://maxicare.com.ph/member-section/member-gateway
- Maxicare also offers LOA kiosks at some Southstar Drug branches. Source: https://www.maxicare.com.ph/latest-announcements/maxicare-launches-convenient-loa-requests-at-southstar-branches/
- Complaints: a Trustpilot page for maxicare.com.ph (https://de.trustpilot.com/review/maxicare.com.ph, about 23 reviews) was summarised by search as reporting long LOA waits (one about 6.5 hours), about 2 hours for hospital approval, and agents issuing LOAs with missing procedures. I did not read the individual reviews, so treat this as unverified. These complaints are about Maxicare's general LOA process, not about Agentforce. No complaints found about Agentforce itself, and no app-store reviews were found in the searches.

## Pricing
Not sold. No price applies to buyers. Salesforce Agentforce pricing is not published in my results (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Single-HMO only. It does nothing for clinics juggling LOAs across several HMOs, or for employers, brokers or HR teams.
- Dental LOA focus. Hospital, lab and claims reimbursement flows are not shown as covered.
- Not available to competitors' networks.

## Opening
A multi-HMO LOA/claims layer for clinics and employers remains open. Maxicare's move validates demand and shows HMOs will build their own agents, so the risk is more HMOs doing the same, not a third-party rival.
