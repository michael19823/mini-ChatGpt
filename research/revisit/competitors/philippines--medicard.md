# Medicard (Philippines)

Idea it killed: C0768 (Philippines HMO LOA/claims hub), together with SeriousMD, Kaiser and Maxicare Agentforce. URL: none in brief. Type: commercial (HMO and insurer, not a software vendor). Searches used: 4 of 6 (2 of the first attempts hit the usage limit and were repeated).

## Verdict: beatable (as a hub competitor); strong as an HMO

Medicard is one of the HMOs a neutral LOA/claims hub would connect to. It is not a multi-HMO hub, so it does not close that gap. It does run its own LOA channels and app, so a hub needs Medicard integrations rather than a replacement for them.

## Evidence
- App complaints: unstar.app, a third-party review aggregator, lists MediCard GO at 3.0 stars and as the most-complained-about app in the Philippines, with 479 negative reviews (App Store and Google Play). The search summary gave no complaint details, and I did not see the original store reviews: https://unstar.app/worst-apps/in/philippines (aggregator figure, partly unverified).
- Reddit r/phinvest (Nov 2024, "What's the best HMO"): one summary says Medicard was named among the top 3 HMOs for quick LOA approval. A contrasting comment reads "medicard is the worst lol", with no detail. Mixed, thin signal: https://lr.in.psf.lt/r/phinvest/comments/1h17cj9/whats_the_best_hmo
- LOA channels: app, website, hotline, and walk-in LOA desks (listed hours Mon-Fri 7am-7pm, Sat 7am-5pm at a Tower 1 office). Digital LOA (dLOA) is standard across Philippine HMOs: https://www.makatimed.net.ph/?p=1027
- Scale: reported about 58,000-59,000 accredited doctors and 18 own clinics (secondhand, from a search summary).
- Momentum: active. AIA Philippines and Medicard launched a joint HMO plus life insurance product in July 2026: https://mb.com.ph/2026/07/22/aia-philippines-medicard-launch-joint-plan-as-healthcare-costs-soar
- Integration: SeriousMD already integrates with Medicard for LOA upload, verification and claim submission (see philippines--seriousmd.md). The Medicard side is therefore already partly served by a third party.
- Complaints about outages, failed filings or billing disputes: none found beyond the above. No Facebook group or local forum threads surfaced.

## Pricing
- Individual plans 2026, per a third-party blog: from P1,545/year (Health Plus, outpatient only) to P25,379+/year (VIP). My MediCard P3,600/year, H.E.R.O. P6,000/year, Standard from P10,739/year: https://www.clinicfinderph.com/blog/medicard-plans-prices (third-party, unverified against Medicard).
- Small-group AIA-Medicard product: groups as small as 5 employees, reported "around P50 to P200 per employee" (unit unclear in the summary, unverified).
- Pricing is for health plans. Medicard does not sell an LOA/claims tool to clinics, so there is no software price to compare.

## Fit gaps (for the C0768 buyer: clinics and employers handling several HMOs)
- It covers only Medicard members. A clinic that sees patients from many HMOs still needs a separate workflow for each.
- App rated poorly (3.0, aggregator figure).
- It is an HMO rather than a neutral provider-side tool, so it has no incentive to unify other HMOs.

## Opening
A provider-side multi-HMO LOA and claims layer for clinics outside SeriousMD's base remains open. Medicard should be treated as an integration target, not a blocker.
