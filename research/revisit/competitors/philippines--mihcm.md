# MiHCM (Philippines) - killed idea C0770: SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG remittance files

## Verdict: strong (for mid/large employers); beatable only for micro/small buyers

## Evidence
- MiHCM's PH payroll page says it automates income tax, SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contributions, with "statutory contribution reports" and SSS/HDMF/PHIC/tax remittance reports. Source: https://mihcm.com/en-ph/solutions/payroll/ and https://mihcm.com/th/?p=44858
- It lists 40+ ready-made reports, including BIR 1601-C, 2316 and Alphalist schedules. Same sources. Whether it exports files in the exact portal upload formats of SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG is unverified.
- It positions itself as "enterprise-grade" and built for complex payroll configurations (vendor claim).
- Capterra: 3.8/5 from 26 reviews. One reviewer complained that support took 2 weeks to over 1 month to resolve issues. Another said reports use jargon not common in the Philippines. Minimal customization was also cited. Source: https://www.capterra.co.uk/reviews/138958/microimage-hcm-cloud
- G2: 4.8 from 5 reviews, praising ease of use and responsive support. One user mentioned occasional slowness. Source: https://www.g2.com/products/mihcm/reviews
- Momentum: the PH pages and a PH cloud-payroll blog post are live and in 2026 search results, and G2 shows no new review in about two months. Active, but I found no funding or acquisition news (unverified).
- No complaints were found about failed SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG filings or outages.
- Only 4 of the 6 allowed searches were run. No Filipino-language independent complaints turned up.

## Pricing
Quote-based. No public per-employee price. Aggregator sites list a "$2.00/month" starting price that looks like a placeholder (unverified). Implementation and support appear to be billed separately (aggregator claim, unverified). The price is probably out of reach for a micro business.

## Fit gaps
- Enterprise focus, so likely heavy or expensive for small employers and bookkeepers (inferred).
- Slow support, per Capterra.
- Jargon in the reports, not localized for the Philippines.
- Quote-only pricing means a small buyer cannot self-serve.
- No evidence of a standalone, cheap remittance-file tool, since it is a full HCM suite.

## Opening
A low-cost, self-serve tool that only produces correct SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG remittance files, for small employers and accountants who cannot afford a full HCM suite. Against mid/large buyers MiHCM already covers the idea.
