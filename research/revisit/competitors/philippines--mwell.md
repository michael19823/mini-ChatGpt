# mWell (Philippines) - competitor check for C0769 (YAKAP/eKonsulta replacement clinics)

## Verdict: strong

mWell (Metro Pacific Health Tech, backed by MPIC) launched a YAKAP claims platform for clinics in April 2026, aimed squarely at the eKonsulta-replacement gap. It is well funded, actively developed and award-winning.

## Evidence
- mWell is an all-in-one health app from Metro Pacific Health Tech Corp. (MPIC subsidiary): teleconsults, e-pharmacy, wellness programs, OFW access. Source: https://www.sunstar.com.ph/cebu/lifestyle/mpic-launches-health-wellness-app-mwell-in-ph
- Apr 2026: "mWell launches new platform to simplify PhilHealth claims for clinics" (the mWell YAKAP Claims Platform: less paperwork, fewer errors, faster claims). Source: https://context.ph/2026/04/20/mwell-launches-new-platform-to-simplify-philhealth-claims-for-clinics/ (I only saw the search summary; the article body was not read).
- mWellMD, a doctor-side app with patient data, billing, referrals and professional fees, was launched in 2025. Source: https://malaya.com.ph/lifestyle/living/mwell-launches-mwellmd-app-empowering-doctors-to-serve-patients
- Momentum: GLOMO award in 2023 (https://news.abs-cbn.com/business/03/08/23/mpics-mwell-app-wins-in-global-mobile-awards), a Tech4Good award at MWC 2026, and reported foreign investor interest (https://context.ph/2026/04/09/foreign-investors-show-strong-interest-in-mwell-metro-pacific-health-app/). Actively developed.
- Context: eKonsulta is being shut down on 31 Dec 2026 and clinics must migrate YAKAP data. Source: https://seriousmd.com/yakap/what-is-yakap/. SeriousMD is another player in this space (unverified how it compares; not researched here).
- Complaints: none found. Searches in English and Tagalog returned no negative reviews. The Apple App Store listing showed 5.0 from 15 reviews (per an aggregator snippet; unverified). This reflects thin search coverage (Google Play and Reddit were not reachable via search), not proof of satisfaction. The claims platform is new, so there is little review history.

## Pricing
- Consumer: Healthsavers Plan at P399 with one consult plus P20,000 accident insurance (per a search summary; the source page was not read, so unverified).
- YAKAP Claims Platform pricing for clinics: not found / unknown.

## Fit gaps
- None verified. Possible gaps, all unverified: the claims platform may favor clinics inside the mWell/Metro Pacific network or partners; it may not serve independent solo clinics, offline use, or full clinic management (EMR, billing, inventory); the pricing model is unknown.
- Complaints from small clinics about the claims platform could not be found.

## Opening
Little, unless a follow-up finds small independent clinics priced out or poorly served by mWell's claims platform; otherwise a well-funded incumbent already occupies the eKonsulta-replacement gap.
