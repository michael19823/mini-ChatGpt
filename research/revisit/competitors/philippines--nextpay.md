# NextPay (Philippines) - competitor to C0772 (BIR 2307 issuance and SAWT/QAP)

## Verdict: beatable

## Evidence
- NextPay (nextpay.world) is a real, Philippine fintech for MSMEs (payouts, AP/AR, invoicing, payroll), serving customers since 2019. Source: https://nextpay.world/customers (via search summary).
- It does handle BIR 2307: its blog describes AR/AP tooling that eases 2307 and uses a vendor's BIR 2303 to decide whether to withhold and at what rate. Sources: https://nextpay.world/blog/certificate-of-registration-bir-2303-and-tax-withholding-bir-2307 and https://nextpay.world/blog/save-time-on-bir-forms-with-nextpay
- 2307 is a feature inside a payments/AP platform, aimed at the payor side. Search results showed no SAWT or QAP (BIR alphalist/summary file) generation. Absence in snippets only: unverified.
- Complaints: none found. G2 has too few reviews to give buying insight (https://www.g2.com/products/nextpay/discuss). Searches for Reddit and local-language (Tagalog/English mix) complaints returned nothing about NextPay. Play Store and App Store reviews were not reached: unverified.
- Momentum: appears active (pricing change tied to a "flex wallet" launch, current help-centre and docs articles, e.g. https://help.nextpay.world/en/articles/10066933-price-adjustment-flex-wallet-launch). Dates not verified.

## Pricing (from nextpay.world docs/help search results; not opened directly)
- Starter PHP 499/mo (PHP 399/mo billed annually); Business PHP 1,499/mo (PHP 1,199/mo annual); Enterprise custom.
- Starter: 5 team members, 1 workspace. Business: 25 members, 5 workspaces.
- Transaction fees are separate (https://docs.nextpay.world/guide/nextpay/pricing/transaction-fees); amounts unverified.
- Whether 2307 features are on every tier: unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Payor-side tool bundled with a payments wallet; a payee wanting only 2307 chasing/SAWT/QAP must adopt the whole platform and its subscription.
- No evidence of SAWT/QAP filing-format output (unverified).
- Payee-side tracking of missing 2307s is not clearly covered (unverified).

## Opening
A standalone, cheap payee- and bookkeeper-focused 2307 collection plus SAWT/QAP export tool leaves room, since NextPay bundles 2307 into a payments platform with no found complaints but no clear SAWT/QAP.
