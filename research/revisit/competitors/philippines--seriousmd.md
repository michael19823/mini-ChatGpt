# SeriousMD (Philippines)

Ideas it killed: C0769 (YAKAP/eKonsulta replacement clinics), C0768 (Philippines HMO LOA/claims hub). URL: seriousmd.com (found in search; none given in brief). Type: commercial.

## Verdict: strong (for C0769); beatable only on the multi-HMO breadth of C0768

## Evidence
- Reviews are overwhelmingly positive. Endorsal page shows 5/5 across 186 reviews with 98% 5-star (self-hosted testimonial aggregator, so likely curated): https://endorsal.io/reviews/seriousmd
- Capterra shows SeriousMD Doctors at 4.8/5 from 20 reviews: https://capterra.co.za/reviews/152369/seriousmd-doctors
- Complaints: none found in 4 searches (English and Tagalog). No Reddit, Facebook or app-store complaint threads surfaced. One review snippet noted there is no Android doctor app (secondhand, unverified; I did not see the original).
- PhilHealth YAKAP certified: clinics can import their patient masterlist, encode the first patient encounter, consultations, labs and medicines, and submit YAKAP reports directly to PhilHealth: https://news.seriousmd.com/129774-seriousmd-is-now-philhealth-yakap-certified and https://seriousmd.com/yakap/
- Actively running a eKonsulta-to-YAKAP migration campaign. Search snippets conflict on dates: one says eKonsulta shuts down July 1, 2026, another says it operates until December 31, 2026 (treat the date as unverified). Today is 2026-10-05, so the migration window is live now. Pages: https://seriousmd.com/yakap/accreditation/ and https://seriousmd.com/yakap/what-is-yakap/
- HMO: integrates directly with Medicard and Kaiser (LOA upload, verification, claim submission). Other HMOs get only an HMO billing tracker, not LOA integration: https://help.seriousmd.com/en/articles/10364326-track-hmo-billing and https://intercom.help/seriousmd/en/articles/4541469-medicard-integration
- A "Billing 2.0 HMO payment workflow" is in beta, which signals momentum toward HMO claims: https://help.seriousmd.com/en/articles/10934807-billing-2-0-hmo-payment-workflow-beta
- Momentum: active (recent YAKAP product, blog, help centre, comparison/SEO content).

## Pricing
- Pro plan P1,950/month or P22,788/year, with unlimited clinics, devices and patients (via search summary; confirm on the pricing page). Group/enterprise plan is custom. A free start tier is mentioned (terms unverified). This is plausible for a doctor, but heavy for a small YAKAP clinic or a barangay-level provider.

## Fit gaps
- HMO LOA integration is limited to 2 HMOs (Medicard, Kaiser). Other HMOs and a multi-HMO claims hub are not covered.
- The product is doctor-centric EMR/practice management. It is not a hub for employers, HMOs or clinic networks.
- Possibly no Android doctor app (unverified).
- It targets doctors, not facility-level YAKAP operations (primary-care clinic chains, pharmacies, labs). Unverified.

## Opening
Little room to beat SeriousMD on YAKAP/eKonsulta EMR (certified, loved, in-market now). The only possible gap is a neutral multi-HMO LOA/claims layer across the HMOs it does not integrate with, or a cheap tier for tiny clinics.
