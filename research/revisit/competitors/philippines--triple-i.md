# Triple i (Triple i Consulting Inc.), Philippines

Idea killed: C0772, BIR 2307 issuance and SAWT/QAP.

## Verdict
not-a-real-competitor

## Evidence
- Triple i is a Makati-based consultancy (founded 2007, per its own site) offering business registration, visas, accounting outsourcing, tax compliance and 30+ other services. Source: https://tripleiconsulting.com/
- Its only link to the idea is educational blog content, such as "How to Submit the SAWT With the Philippines BIR": https://www.tripleiconsulting.com/how-submit-sawt-with-philippines-bir/. The search results showed no software or tool for generating 2307, SAWT or QAP.
- Complaints: none found. A JobStreet listing shows 6 reviews at 5.0 (https://ph.jobstreet.com/companies/triple-i-consulting-168556145829230), which are probably employer reviews (unverified). Local-language search (Filipino) surfaced no complaints.
- Real software competition for this idea is elsewhere: Odoo's Philippines localization generates 2307, SAWT and QAP .DAT files, and juan.tax and invoicedataextraction.com have related content. These are not assessed here.
- Momentum: the site is actively publishing content (unverified beyond the search results).

## Pricing
Third-party listing (softwaresuggest.com): under $25/hr, minimum project $1,000+ (unverified, aggregator data). No published price for 2307 or SAWT work.

## Fit gaps
It is a done-for-you service, not self-serve software. Small buyers who want automation, in-house issuance or batch .DAT generation are not served. A $1,000+ project minimum is high for a micro-SME.

## Opening
Triple i leaves the self-serve 2307/SAWT/QAP tooling space open, so the idea should be judged against software competitors such as Odoo and juan.tax, not this firm.
