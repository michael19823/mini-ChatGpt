# Comarch Optima (Poland) - competitor check for C0779 (Poland deposit-return small shops)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (for a small-shop deposit-return tool); strong as the incumbent back-office ERP

Optima is a general ERP/accounting suite, not a deposit-return tool. It does handle the Polish deposit system
(system kaucyjny) in its trade module, so it is the product a small shop already using Optima would reach for first.
A shop that does not use Optima has no reason to buy it for this one task.

## Evidence
- Comarch's help pages say automated handling of the deposit system arrived in Comarch ERP Optima 2026.0.1.
  Covered packaging: plastic bottle, metal can, glass bottle. Packaging schemes are assigned to products and the deposit
  is calculated automatically at sale. Deposits are not subject to VAT at sale.
  Sources: https://pomoc.comarch.pl/optima/pl/2026/?p=50901 , https://pomoc.comarch.pl/optima/pl/2026_5/dokumentacja/obsluga-opakowan-zwrotnych/
  (found via search snippets; pages not opened).
- Comarch also documents deposit-packaged beverages for its e-shop integration:
  https://pomoc.comarch.pl/optima/pl/2025_5/dokumentacja/jak-przygotowac-oferte-do-comarch-e-sklep-w-przypadku-sprzedazy-napojow-w-opakowaniach-objetych-systemem-kaucyjnym/
- Momentum: active. Versions through 2026 and 5-series docs exist; a source says over 70,000 firms keep accounts in Optima (secondary, unverified).
- Complaints: none found for the deposit feature. No Google Play, App Store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced.
  Capterra and G2 search returned only unrelated products called "Optima". The only critique was an academic paper
  (https://ruj.uj.edu.pl/entities/publication/95cbffad-19e9-49f6-b2d4-e21e61572601): users wanted automatic updates and a night mode, and
  noted stock reconciliation differences between trade and accounting. Not specific to deposits.
  The search was limited (4 of 6 searches used), so user complaints may exist that I did not find.

## Pricing
Per a reseller site (kluczesoft.pl, e.g. https://kluczesoft.pl/wiedza/aplikacje/cennik-comarch-optima; not official Comarch, treat as unverified):
perpetual licences per workstation, net PLN: Trade (Handel) from 1,490; General Ledger from 1,490; Desktop Start from 1,290;
Desktop Standard from 1,890. There is also a monthly "Optima 365" subscription (price not found).
This is a heavy purchase for a corner shop that only needs deposit tracking.

## Fit gaps (for the dropped idea's buyer)
- Full ERP/accounting scope and cost, with per-workstation licensing and a reseller or implementation setup. Overkill for a tiny shop.
- Handles recording deposits at sale and purchase. Not found in the search: deposit-return reverse vending or return-to-supplier reconciliation workflows,
  or reporting against the operator (KAUCJA / the system operator). Unverified.
- Small shops on a simple POS or cash register or no ERP are not served.

## Opening
A lightweight, cheap, POS-agnostic deposit-return tracker/reconciliation tool for small shops not on Optima; Optima users are already covered.
