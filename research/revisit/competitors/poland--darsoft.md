# Darsoft (Poland, commercial) - killed C0775: Poland BDO waste paperwork

## Verdict: weak

## Evidence
- Darsoft sells Windows desktop programs for waste records: "Ewidencja odpadów" and a "Mała firma" (small business) edition, plus "Skup Surowców Wtórnych" (scrap and secondary-raw-material buying points). Source: https://www.instalki.pl/download/programy/producent/darsoft-pl/ (download catalogue listing).
- The listed version there is a demo, last updated 2023-11-16. That suggests slow or stagnant development, but it is only a download-site date and the vendor site was not checked (unverified).
- Features listed: registers of contractors, goods, waste codes, waste transfer cards and waste records, and warehouse transfers. The search snippets did not confirm direct BDO API integration or submission of KPO/KEO cards (unverified).
- Other solutions do advertise BDO sync: RLGSOFT, https://www.wiadomoscihandlowe.pl/opakowania-i-recykling/system-kaucyjny-i-rop/rlg-stworzylo-system-do-automatyzacji-bdo-2520002 ; All for One BDO (SAP add-on), https://www.all-for-one.pl/en/?p=32573 . These are the stronger competitors, not Darsoft.
- Complaints: none found. Searches "Darsoft BDO opinie" returned only unrelated BDO (accounting firm) results. No app-store, Reddit, forum or outage reports surfaced. This does not prove users are happy; there is just no public review footprint.

## Pricing
- Not found in the searches. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- Desktop Windows software with a demo download, so probably no mobile or cloud use.
- BDO integration unconfirmed.
- Appears aimed at waste-processing firms and scrap buyers rather than generic small waste producers, although a "Mała firma" edition exists.

## Opening
- A cloud or mobile BDO-native tool for small waste producers faces little web-visible competition from Darsoft; the real rivals are BDO-synced platforms such as RLGSOFT.
