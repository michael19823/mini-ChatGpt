# Poland: deposit-system retail tool vendors (C0789)

## Verdict: strong

Many vendors cover Polish deposit (kaucja) handling for shops. Established POS/ERP vendors, the licensed operators themselves and a franchise-focused platform have all shipped tools, so the generic "retail tool" gap is mostly filled.

## Evidence
- Comarch POS added deposit support (automatic deposit charge at sale, deposit documents). Comarch ERP Optima added automated deposit support in 2026.0.1, and Comarch B2B and e-Sklep have deposit guides. Sources: https://pomoc.comarch.pl/pos/cee/documentation/nowosci-i-zmiany-w-wersji-2025-1-1/ , https://pomoc.comarch.pl/optima/pl/2025_5/dokumentacja/jak-przygotowac-oferte-do-comarch-e-sklep-w-przypadku-sprzedazy-napojow-w-opakowaniach-objetych-systemem-kaucyjnym/ , https://pomoc.comarch.pl/b2b/pl/?p=3310
- Shoper (e-commerce) has a deposit app/module. Source: https://www.shoper.pl/aplikacje/system-kaucyjny
- Kaucyjni.pl platform integrates with fiscal registers, POS and RVMs. Per a trade-press report, 11,000 shops are rolling it out, mostly via franchisors (ABC, Groszek, Euro Sklep, Lewiatan and others). Source: https://www.wiadomoscihandlowe.pl/franczyza-w-handlu/franczyza-spozywcza/11-tysiecy-sklepow-w-polsce-wdraza-system-kaucyjny-z-platforma-kaucyjni-pl-2531668
- Polski System Kaucyjny S.A. offers a free-to-shops app, "PSK Punkt Zbiorki", so shops can take returns without RVMs or POS integration (Android; iOS announced). Source: https://www.wiadomoscihandlowe.pl/retailtech-i-nowe-technologie/praktyczne-narzedzie-dla-malego-handlu-aplikacja-ulatwiajaca-wejscie-w-system-kaucyjny-2531037
- Kaucja.pl (an operator) publishes an OpenAPI quick-start for shops, producers and RVMs. Source: https://cdn.kaucja.pl/gcdeposits/media/2025OpenAPIKaucjaplszybkistartdlasklepwiproducentwRVM.pdf
- Hardware: TOMRA won a 3,000-RVM order from Dino Polska. Source: https://www.tomra.com/news-and-media/news/2025/large-rvm-order-received-from-retail-chain-in-poland
- Momentum: the system has run since 1 Oct 2025. Vendors are still shipping updates (Comarch POS 2026.5, July 2026). Source: https://pomoc.comarch.pl/pos/cee/documentation/nowosci-i-zmiany-w-wersji-2026-5-14-07-2026/
- Complaints: I found no vendor-specific reviews (app store, forums). Press describes market-level pain, not tool failures. Small shops are "paralysed" by operational chaos, must pre-fund refunds from their own cash, and settle with operators in two-week cycles. Poor operator contracts, costly equipment and penalty risk are also reported. Outdated software may not integrate. Sources: https://kb.pl/aktualnosci/promocje/kaucja-za-butelki-rozwscieczyla-sklepy-teraz-pojawilo-sie-rozwiazanie-ktore-moze-wszystko-zmienic_fg/ , https://www.wiadomoscihandlowe.pl/opakowania-i-recykling/system-kaucyjny-to-chaos-w-sklepach-anita-sowinska-sklepy-sa-sercem-systemu-2532655 , https://www.wiadomoscihandlowe.pl/prawo-i-polityka/system-kaucyjny-w-jednostkach-handlowych-nieznajomosc-przepisow-moze-kosztowac-wiecej-niz-obsluga-zwrotow-2535259 . These articles were seen only as search snippets, not read in full.
- Seven licensed operators exist: Zwrotka, PolKa, OK Operator Kaucyjny, Eko-operator, Reselekt, Kaucja.pl, Polski System Kaucyjny (from search summary, unverified in full).

## Pricing
- Published pricing was not found for Kaucyjni.pl or the Comarch deposit features (unverified).
- The operator app (PSK) appears free to shops. Source does not state a price; unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Franchise-chain focus: Kaucyjni.pl is sold through franchisors, so independent shops may be less served (inference).
- Operator tools lock the shop to one operator. Shops deal with several operators, and a neutral cross-operator reconciliation tool (refund cash float, 2-week settlements, inspection records) was not seen (unverified).
- Comarch is a full ERP/POS, so it is heavy for micro shops.

## Opening
Little room for a generic deposit tool. At most, a niche cross-operator reconciliation/compliance-records helper for independent shops, and only if complaint evidence emerges, which I did not find.
