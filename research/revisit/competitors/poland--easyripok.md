# easyRipok (Poland, commercial) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0775 (Poland BDO waste paperwork).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (low confidence, existence unverified)

No evidence found that a product called easyRipok exists as a live, marketed BDO tool.

## Evidence
- 6 WebSearch calls were budgeted. Two hit a usage-limit error before returning results. Four returned results: "easyRipok BDO", "EasyRipok program do ewidencji odpadów BDO opinie cena", "\"EasyRIPOK\" odpady program" and a generic Polish query on BDO software for small firms.
- None of the four returned a product page, app-store listing, review, price list, news item or forum thread for easyRipok. All four returned unrelated results, such as Black Desert Online (also abbreviated BDO), DVD rippers, PARP training listings and Slovak and Czech legal texts.
- The only related thing the searches surfaced was the term RIPOK. This is a Polish waste-sector term (Regionalna Instalacja Przetwarzania Odpadów Komunalnych, regional municipal waste processing installation), which appeared in an AGH thesis result. So the name may refer to a niche tool for RIPOK operators, not for general BDO filing. This is unverified.
- The generic BDO search surfaced RLGSOFT (by RLG) as an existing external waste-registry system. It was mentioned in a search summary only; I did not verify its details, so it is a lead and not a finding. The state BDO API reportedly allows third-party software to integrate with BDO.
- Complaints: none found. Finding none here mostly reflects that the product could not be located.

## Pricing
Not found (unverified). The only price that appeared was for BDO training courses (about 490 PLN net for a 6-hour beginner course, from a PARP listing). That is not software pricing.

## Fit gaps
Cannot assess. If easyRipok is a RIPOK-operator tool, it likely does not serve small waste producers or holders who file their own BDO records. This is a guess, not a finding.

## Momentum
Unknown. No sign of active development, acquisition or shutdown.

## Opening
easyRipok is not a verified blocker for C0775. The idea should be judged against other Polish BDO software, such as RLGSOFT (a lead, not verified), and against the free state BDO system, not against this name.
