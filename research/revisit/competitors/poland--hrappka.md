# HRappka (Poland)

Ideas it killed: C0777 (Poland foreign-worker legalisation agencies), C0788 (Poland foreign-worker legalisation).
Searches used: 4 of 6 (Polish and English).

## Verdict: strong

It already ships a dedicated foreign-worker legalisation module aimed at temporary work agencies, and no independent complaints turned up.

## Evidence
- Own site describes a "Legalizacja pracy cudzoziemców" module: generates work permits and work-assignment statements (pulls data from worker profile), integrates with praca.gov.pl for submission, tracks validity dates of permits, residence cards, entry dates and passport/visa scans, and reminds about start/end-of-work reporting. Sources: https://hrappka.pl/legalizacja-zatrudnienie-cudzoziemcow , https://hrappka.pl/legalizacja-cudzoziemcow , https://hrappka.pl/?p=20256
- Bundled with recruitment, HR/payroll and housing modules; positioned for temp agencies: https://hrappka.pl/?p=14383
- Momentum: active. Multilingual site (PL/EN/DE/RU/UK), blog and content in place; Capterra 2026 listing exists. No acquisition or shutdown found. Sources: https://hrappka.pl/opinie/ , https://www.capterra.com/p/209096/HRappka/
- Complaints: none found. Only testimonials on HRappka's own site (vendor-published, so biased). Capterra shows 0 user reviews. No G2, Trustpilot, Reddit, GoWork or forum complaints surfaced. Absence is not proof of satisfaction; the search coverage was limited.

## Pricing
Per HRappka's offer page as summarised by search: All-HR (all modules) 135 PLN/month per administrator, free employee accounts; time tracking and leave requests 6 PLN/employee/month, minimum 10 employees. Whether the legalisation module is included in All-HR or priced separately: unverified. Source: https://hrappka.pl/oferta/ . This is affordable for small agencies.

## Fit gaps
- Appears to be a full HR/payroll suite; a small legalisation-only agency or law/consulting firm may not want or need it (unverified).
- No evidence found of: multi-client case management for outside legalisation consultancies (it is aimed at employers/agencies handling their own workers), residence-permit (karta pobytu) application filing beyond tracking, or mobile/offline use. All unverified.
- Coverage of the Polish rules after the 2025 foreigner-law changes: unverified.

## Opening
Little room for a generic agency tool; the only gap is a standalone, case-based tool for outside legalisation service providers serving many client employers, and even that is unverified.
