# Maas Loop (Poland) - competitor check for C0779 (Poland deposit-return for small shops)

## Verdict: beatable (medium-low confidence; hardware vendor, little evidence on reviews, price or small-shop fit)

## Evidence
- 6 WebSearch calls (English, Polish, Dutch-spelling variant). The name is "Maas Loop" (maasloop.com), a Polish hardware startup, not software. Founded 2020 by Pawel Ciesielski, Szczepan Wantusiak and Lukasz Swiech, based in Jasionka, Poland (https://www.cbinsights.com/company/maas-loop).
- Products: Rev Loop Retail, described as "the smallest reverse vending machine that you can fit between shelves" (https://www.maasloop.com/rev-loop), and Glass Loop HoReCa, a bottle crusher. So it does target small-format retail with a physical machine.
- Funding: EUR 1.2M (over 5M PLN) round led by Aper Ventures, with AIP Seed, ASP Capital and Aleksander Szalecki, as of March 2024 (https://www.vestbee.com/blog/articles/polish-recycling-hardware-startup-maas-loop-secures-1-2-m-led-by-aper-ventures). Small, hardware-focused company.
- Distribution: SUUS EXPERT provides warehousing, delivery to chains and smaller stores across Poland, and assembly of Maas Loop machines (via search snippet; original source page not opened, details unverified).
- Context: Poland's deposit system started 1 Oct 2025. Shops over 200 m2 must take returns; smaller shops are voluntary, and voluntary small shops are said to be more than half of all collection points (gov.pl, snippet: https://www.gov.pl/web/climate/the-deposit-refund-system-has-been-launched). Press says small shops are hit hard by space and capital limits (https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/System-kaucyjny-pograzy-male-sklepy-Handlowcy-apeluja-Rossmann-rezygnuje-8996649.html; https://kb.pl/aktualnosci/promocje/kaucja-za-butelki-rozwscieczyla-sklepy-teraz-pojawilo-sie-rozwiazanie-ktore-moze-wszystko-zmienic_fg/). Another vendor guide for small food shops choosing a machine exists at wiadomoscihandlowe.pl, so the segment has several hardware options.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews of Maas Loop located. General consumer complaints about broken return machines and vouchers exist (e.g. https://www.gov.pl/web/klimat/co-zrobic-gdy-nie-dziala-butelkomat-sprawdz-jak-odzyskac-kaucje) but none name Maas Loop.

## Pricing
Unknown, unverified. No published price or lease terms found.

## Fit gaps
- It sells hardware only; the shop still needs space, capital and installation. Software or service layers (handling the deposit accounting, claims against the system operator, manual-return workflows for shops without a machine, staff processes) are not part of what was found. Unverified whether it offers any of these.
- Not verified: leasing, integration with POS, or support for tiny shops below 200 m2.

## Momentum
Active as of last evidence (funding 2024, logistics partnership and press presence around the 2025 launch). No sign of shutdown or acquisition found.

## Opening
Maas Loop only covers the machine; a software/service or low-capex manual-return layer for tiny shops is not blocked by it, so C0779 is not killed unless the idea was itself a machine.
