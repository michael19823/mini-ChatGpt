# Polski System Kaucyjny (Poland) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0779, Poland deposit-return small shops.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (for a software/service layer aimed at small shops)

Polski System Kaucyjny S.A. is not a product for shops. It is one of the state-licensed deposit-system operators, alongside OK Operator Kaucyjny, Reselekt, Kaucja.pl, Krajowy System Kaucyjny Zwrotka, PolKa (joined 23 Oct 2025) and Eko-Operator (from 1 Aug 2026). It collects and settles deposits and pays shops a handling fee. It does not sell shops a tool to manage them. It is more a counterparty than a rival. The system launched 1 Oct 2025.

## Evidence
- Polski System Kaucyjny S.A. is listed as one of the authorised operators. Source: https://kb.pl/aktualnosci/promocje/kaucja-za-butelki-rozwscieczyla-sklepy-teraz-pojawilo-sie-rozwiazanie-ktore-moze-wszystko-zmienic_fg/ and search summaries of https://www.wiadomoscihandlowe.pl/opakowania-i-recykling/system-kaucyjny-i-rop/operatorzy-systemu-kaucyjnego-zaczynaja-wspolprace-piec-firm-podpisalo-umowe-ktora-ma-regulowac-m-in-zasady-rozliczania-kaucji-2522247
- Shops of 200 m2 or less may join voluntarily and must sign with at least one operator that approaches them. Larger shops must sign with all operators that approach them. Source: https://www.wiadomoscihandlowe.pl/opakowania-i-recykling/system-kaucyjny-i-rop/system-kaucyjny-w-polsce-nadciaga-sprawdz-jak-sie-do-niego-przygotowac-poradnik-2527721
- Small-shop pain is documented, but it is aimed at the system and operators in general, not at this operator specifically:
  - Weak negotiating position and costly equipment.
  - Penalty risk.
  - Shops must pre-fund deposit refunds from their own cash before the operator reimburses them.
  - Bureaucracy, IT difficulties and settlement problems.
  - Contracts with multiple operators.
  Sources: https://www.prawo.pl/podatki/system-kaucyjny-maly-sklep-a-butelkomat-i-handling-fee,534937.html ; https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/System-kaucyjny-pograzy-male-sklepy-Handlowcy-apeluja-Rossmann-rezygnuje-8996649.html ; https://kb.pl/aktualnosci/promocje/kaucja-za-butelki-rozwscieczyla-sklepy-teraz-pojawilo-sie-rozwiazanie-ktore-moze-wszystko-zmienic_fg/
- Operators reportedly offered handling fees of about 0.10-0.12 zl per PET bottle collected automatically and about 0.03-0.10 zl manually, which is said to be below real cost (prawo.pl link above; figures come from the search summary, so treat them as unverified).
- Scale: more than 1 billion containers returned by May 2026 across about 57,000 return points. Voluntary small shops are more than half of the points, and RVMs are about 22% of points but about 81% of volume. Source: https://packaging-journal.de/en/?p=108035 and https://www.gov.pl/web/climate/valuable-raw-materials-are-being-recycled-the-deposit-return-system-has-now-been-in-operation-for-three-months (summary only, unverified in detail).
- Kaucja.pl uses the Omecon DRS system (https://itwiz.pl/kaucja-pl-postawilismy-na-system-omecon-drs-ze-wzgledu-na-jego-uniwersalnosc-i-funkcjonalnosc/). This shows operator back-office IT is bought in, not aimed at shops.
- No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews specific to Polski System Kaucyjny S.A. were found. No complaints specific to this operator were found.

## Pricing
- Shops pay no price for the operator service. The operator pays the shop a handling fee per container. Per-container figures are quoted above and are unverified. No published fee schedule for this operator was found.

## Fit gaps (for the dropped idea's buyer, the small shop)
- It offers no shop-side tooling that was found: no multi-operator reconciliation, no cash-float or deposit-liability tracking, no POS or checkout helper, and no help comparing operators' handling fees.
- Each operator settles only its own flows. A shop dealing with several operators has to reconcile them itself (inferred, unverified).
- Small shops decide on thin margins and have little negotiating leverage.

## Momentum
- The operator is active and licensed. The system is growing. Regulation is still moving (Eko-Operator joined in Aug 2026). No acquisition or shutdown news was found.

## Opening
- A neutral, shop-side tool for the small shop (multi-operator contract and fee comparison, deposit cash-float and reconciliation tracking, POS or checkout handling). The operator is not a competitor there, but a possible integration or channel. Check the other tool vendors separately: Omecon, RVM makers, POS vendors.
