# Poland: Softlab ERP (Asseco Business Solutions)

Idea it killed: C0775, Poland BDO waste paperwork.

## Verdict: weak (as a competitor for this idea)

Softlab ERP is a real, actively sold ERP, but it targets medium and large firms. I found no evidence of a BDO waste-records module, so it is a weak competitor for a small-business BDO tool. This rests on 3 searches and is not conclusive.

## Evidence
- Softlab ERP is a modular ERP for medium and large enterprises (sales, logistics, finance, production, warehouse, HR). Source: https://assecobs.pl/softlab/?lang=en
- Asseco Business Solutions acquired Softlab and merged it in 2007. It is listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange. Source: https://www.parkiet.com/gospodarka/art24627761-softlab-dolaczyl-do-grupy-asseco
- Momentum: the product is active. It has an iPKO biznes banking integration (https://www.pkobp.pl/aktualnosci/bankowosc-elektroniczna/system-softlab-erp-firmy-asseco-bs-w-ipko-biznes) and Asseco is hiring Softlab ERP implementation consultants (pracuj.pl listings).
- Complaints: none found. A search for "Softlab ERP Asseco opinie" returned no user reviews. This is not proof that none exist. Review sites (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, forums) did not surface, and I did not search them further.
- BDO: a search for "Asseco Softlab ERP BDO ewidencja odpadów" returned only government BDO pages and no Softlab BDO feature. Whether Softlab has such a module is unverified.

## Pricing
Not published. Search results gave no prices (unverified). Asseco positions Softlab for larger firms and its Wapro line for small firms and accounting offices, so a small buyer is unlikely to buy Softlab ERP for this task alone.

## Fit gaps
- It targets medium and large firms, not small waste holders or small firms with BDO duties.
- No BDO waste-records feature found (unverified).
- It is a full ERP, so adopting it for BDO paperwork alone would be heavy.

## Opening
A lightweight, low-cost BDO paperwork tool for small waste holders is not served by Softlab. The other Asseco lines (Wapro) and BDO-capable ERPs were not checked here and may be the real competitors.
