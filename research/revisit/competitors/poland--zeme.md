# ZEME (Poland) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0775 (Poland BDO waste paperwork). Searches used: 4 of 6.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

ZEME is a waste-collection contractor for large retail and industrial clients. Its software (ZEME 360) is bundled with its own collection service. It is not a standalone BDO paperwork tool for small waste producers.

## Evidence
- Founded in Warsaw in 2017. It describes itself as combining IT and industrial waste management, and collects cardboard, foil, wood, plastics and hazardous waste. Source: https://app.dealroom.co/companies/zeme_sp_z_o_o_
- ZEME 360 is its platform. It automates collection orders, integrates with the BDO database and generates documents such as KPO (waste transfer cards). Source: https://app.dealroom.co/companies/zeme_sp_z_o_o_
- It claims about 40 million BDO queries per month and about 300,000 collections in 2024 (company claims as reported by aggregators, unverified).
- Named clients are Żabka, Eurocash and Action S.A. Source: https://www.wiadomoscihandlowe.pl/zielony-handel/byly-szef-action-w-polsce-i-byly-dyrektor-finansowy-pepco-inwestuja-w-firme-obslugujaca-eurocash-zabke-i-dealz-2525832
- Momentum is strong. It plans over PLN 200 mln of acquisitions, expects revenue of about PLN 140 mln in 2026, is seeking PLN 35-40 mln from investors, and plans Brazil entry in 2027. Source: https://wbj.pl/polish-waste-management-company-zeme-plans-pln-200-mln-for-acquisitions/post/150717
- Complaints: none found. The searches (Polish and English) returned no app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews. Absence of results is not evidence of satisfaction.
- A different vendor, All for One Poland, offers BDO services (https://all-for-one.pl/en/offer/bdo). I did not assess it.

## Pricing
Not published in anything I found. ZEME 360 appears to be sold as part of a collection contract (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- It serves large retail chains and industry. Small waste producers are not its stated target.
- It appears to require using ZEME as the waste-collection provider, so it is not a neutral paperwork tool (inferred, unverified).
- No evidence of a self-serve product for small firms.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve BDO paperwork tool for small waste producers and independent collectors is not addressed by ZEME. Other BDO software vendors may still compete there.
