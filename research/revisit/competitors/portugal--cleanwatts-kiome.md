# Cleanwatts / Kiome (Portugal)

Killed idea: C0802 (Portugal energy communities EGAC management). Searches used: 3 of 6 (English and Portuguese).

## Verdict: strong

Cleanwatts is an established, funded originator and manager of renewable energy communities (CER) in Portugal. Kiome is one of its management tools. It is not a thin software vendor.

## Evidence
- Cleanwatts says it manages over 100 energy communities in Portugal, built by its "REC Factory". Kisense, Kiplo Energy Communities and Kiome are its monitoring and management tools. Sources: https://pv-magazine.com/press-releases/cleanwatts-paves-the-way-for-the-deployment-of-energy-communities ; https://www.enlit.world/companies/cleanwatts
- Search snippets elsewhere gave 80+ and 100+ for the count. The figures are inconsistent, so the exact number is unverified.
- Model: energy as a service with zero upfront investment. An anchor client hosts solar, and surplus is sold to nearby members at discounted tariffs. Source: https://esgpost.com/dws-acquires-cleanwatts-to-expand-energy-communities-in-portugal/
- Momentum: DWS reportedly acquired Cleanwatts in February 2026 and plans to invest in solar (snippets say EUR 50M until 2030 and "150 million"; the amount is unverified). Sources: https://esgpost.com/dws-acquires-cleanwatts-to-expand-energy-communities-in-portugal/ ; https://onestopesg.com/esg-news/dws-invests-150-million-in-cleanwatts-to-expand-portugal-s-renewable-energy-communities-1772119575147
- A management buyout in December 2025 spun out "Cleanwatts Digital" as an independent AI-powered software company, with Celtis Venture Partners investing (seed reported as about $2.5M). Source: https://www.privsource.com/acquisitions/deal/celtis-invests-in-cleanwatts-digital-following-management-buyout-9zSb5V
- Earlier funding: Verdane-led round of about EUR 15M (2022). Source: https://elreferente.es/?p=169274
- Reported customer outcomes: at least 10% bill reduction (Miranda do Douro) and about 50% for 20 disadvantaged families (Braga). Source: https://www.jornaldenegocios.pt/empresas/energia/detalhe/cleanwatts-e-associacao-de-futebol-de-braga-criam-comunidade-de-energia-para-20-familias
- Complaints: none found. The searches returned no app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews. This is not proof there are none; the Portuguese search did not reach local forums.

## Pricing
No published software price found (unverified). The model is mostly bundled energy-as-a-service with no upfront cost to anchor clients.

## Fit gaps
- Cleanwatts mostly originates its own communities (anchor clients, municipalities, IPSS). There is no evidence it sells a self-serve tool to independent community managers (the EGAC role). This is an inference and unverified.
- The newly independent Cleanwatts Digital may start selling software more widely, which would close that gap.
- No evidence found about support for other languages, offline use or hardware requirements.

## Opening
A neutral, low-cost management tool for independent or small EGACs that Cleanwatts does not serve is possible, but it competes with a well-funded, DWS-backed incumbent that is moving into software.
