# Energy Ring (Portugal) - competitor check for C0802 (EGAC management)

## Verdict: strong (provisional, low evidence)
It is a purpose-built EGAC and energy-community platform backed by an operating company with a funded rollout. Research was cut short: only 1 of 6 searches completed, because the search tool hit a usage limit.

## Evidence
- Search result snippet describes Energy Ring as a SaaS platform for launching and managing self-consumption projects: renewable energy communities, collective self-consumption and individual self-consumers, with tools for aggregators. (Source: web search summary, not independently verified.)
- Planning phase: sizes the UPAC(s) from average consumption patterns, proposes total capacity and quantifies potential savings.
- Post-implementation: separate access and features for the EGAC and for the self-consumers, with management of allocation coefficients when members join or leave. This is directly the C0802 use case.
- Operated by CSide as the "EnergyRing" service. CSide aims to support 60 renewable energy communities in Portugal with municipalities and companies, with an investment over EUR 32 million. Source: https://echoboomer.pt/?p=95498 (article title: "CSide quer dar energia renovável a mais de 33 mil casas em todo o país até 2023"). This article is dated around 2022-23, so it is old and its current status is unverified.
- Other pages that surfaced and are not directly about Energy Ring: https://goldenergy.pt/blog/autoconsumo/comunidades-de-energia, https://e-redes.pt/sites/eredes/files/2024-08/E-redes_autoconsumocoletivo.pdf, https://www.idealista.pt/news/node/52629

## Complaints
None found. This is not evidence of none existing, because no review-site, app-store, Reddit or forum searches were run.

## Pricing
Not found (unverified). No published price or minimum was seen.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- It appears to be tied to CSide's own project pipeline, targeting municipalities and companies, so it may not serve independent small EGACs, condominium managers or cooperatives. This is a guess.
- Current activity, momentum and any recent acquisition or shutdown: not checked (unverified).

## Opening
Possibly an independent, low-cost EGAC tool for small communities or condominiums that don't want to depend on a developer's platform. This needs confirmation of Energy Ring's pricing and openness before relying on it.
