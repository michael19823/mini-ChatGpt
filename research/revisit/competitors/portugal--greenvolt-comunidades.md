# Greenvolt Comunidades (Portugal) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0802, Portugal energy communities EGAC management software.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Greenvolt Comunidades is an energy-community developer and operator. It installs solar, signs up consumers and runs the communities as a service. It does not sell management software to other EGACs (community managing entities). The most it does is compete for the same communities, and it does that by running them itself.

## Evidence
- Greenvolt Comunidades, S.A. is part of the Greenvolt Group. Its model has a producer share surplus solar with nearby consumers (companies or families) at lower prices. Source: https://eco.sapo.pt/entrevista/greenvolt-multiplica-por-seis-comunidades-de-energia-ativas-crescimento-ainda-vai-ser-maior-a-medida-que-o-setor-publico-se-abra-diz-ceo/
- Scale reported in the press: 37 to 72 operating communities in Portugal (12 in June 2024), with a target of 120. A later snippet says more than 110 operating (45 MWp). Source: https://www.essential-business.pt/2025/07/16/greenvolt-plans-to-create-120-energy-communities/
- Consumer offer "Energia Unida": open to anyone in continental Portugal, as producer or consumer, with a solar installation within 2-4 km. Source: https://www.idealista.pt/news/node/52491
- The company is actively expanding, with corporate deals (Hovione, M.A.Silva) and a social programme ("Riqueza Energetica", an EUR 80,000 donation per year). Sources: https://dinheirovivo.dn.pt/empresas/greenvolt-e-hovione-lanam-comunidades-de-energia-que-prometem-poupanas-superiores-a-dois-milhoes and https://comunidades.greenvolt.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/regulamento_riqueza-energetica_2024-2025.pdf
- Momentum: strong and growing.
- Complaints: none found. One Portuguese-language search for complaints returned only promotional and press coverage. No app-store, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews turned up. This is an absence of results from limited search, not proof that none exist.
- Other tools seen in the searches but not examined: Digital-CER (a Portugal 2020 project platform) and Tools4AgriEnergy (an agri-sector ACC/CER platform). Spain's Communit-e is a free tool. The searches did not cover these, so their fit is unverified.

## Pricing
No published software pricing, because Greenvolt does not sell software. Participants buy energy at reportedly up to 33% savings (press claim, unverified). Pricing for any EGAC tool was not found.

## Fit gaps
- It is a vertically integrated operator, not a SaaS for independent EGACs, municipalities, cooperatives or condominium administrators.
- Its communities are tied to Greenvolt-owned or Greenvolt-contracted generation, so independent communities have no reason to use it.
- Whether it exposes any EGAC-facing tooling is unverified.

## Opening
Independent EGACs (cooperatives, municipalities, SMEs, condominium managers) are not served by Greenvolt. A neutral management tool for them is still open, though Greenvolt's scale will pull many prospective communities into its own service.
