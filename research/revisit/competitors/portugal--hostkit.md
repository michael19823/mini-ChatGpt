# Hostkit (Portugal) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0791 Portugal tourist-tax router for AL (alojamento local). Searches used: 3 of 6 (English and Portuguese).

## Verdict: strong

Hostkit already automates the tourist tax (TMT) as part of a full AL compliance suite, and users rate it very highly.

## Evidence
- Hostkit is a Portugal-specific compliance and invoicing tool for holiday rentals. Its integration pages say it is certified by the Portuguese Tax Authority (no. 2549) and has more than 40 integrations. Source: https://hostkit.pt/en/about-us/ and https://hostex.io/integrations/hostkit/
- Its feature list includes automated calculation, processing and submission of municipal tourist taxes (TMT) across Portugal. It also covers certified invoicing (AT), SIBA/SEF-AIMA guest reports, and INE and Banco de Portugal statistics. Source: https://hostex.io/integrations/hostkit/
- It integrates with the main channel managers and PMSs (Cloudbeds, Guesty, Beds24, Hostaway, Hostify, Hostfully, Uplisting, Avaibook, Hostex, Lodgify). Sources: https://www.lodgify.com/pt/integracoes/hostkit/ and https://www.hostfully.com/pt-pt/software-de-gestao-de-propriedades/integracoes/hostkit/
- Trustpilot (search snippet): about 4.9/5 from about 90 reviews. Source: https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/hostkit.pt
- Software Advice: 5.0 with all 5-star ratings (snippet). Capterra: 5.0 from only 1 user, so thin. Sources: https://www.softwareadvice.com/vacation-rental/hostkit-profile and https://capterra.com/p/10009366/Hostkit/
- Momentum: it is still listed in 2026 and is integrated by many PMSs. I did not find any news of a sale, shutdown or outage (not searched in depth, so unverified).

## Complaints
No real complaints found. The only criticisms in the snippets were a learning curve and that images cannot yet be uploaded to reservations. Reviewers praised the support and the pricing as fair. I did not read the full review pages (no WebFetch), so this rests on search snippets.

## Pricing
- Capterra lists a starting price of EUR 5.00, usage-based, with a 30-day free trial. The unit is not stated (unverified, probably per property or per month).
- This is cheap, so a small host is not overcharged.

## Fit gaps
- No clear gap for small AL hosts: it has Portuguese and English, is cloud-based, and is built for AL.
- Possible gaps, all unverified: coverage of the tourist tax for individual municipalities; hosts who do not use any PMS; languages other than PT and EN.
- A standalone tourist-tax router has little to add beyond what Hostkit's TMT module does.

## Opening
Very little. Only a tourist-tax-only niche, such as municipality-side collection or reconciliation for platforms or councils, might remain, and that would need separate validation.
