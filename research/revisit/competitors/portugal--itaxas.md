# iTaxas (Portugal)

Idea it killed: C0791 Portugal tourist-tax router AL. Type listed as commercial, but see below.

## Verdict
**not-a-real-competitor** (as a commercial rival). iTAXAS is a municipal platform that municipalities
provide for hosts to register, settle and pay the tourist tax. It is not a commercial product that
competes for the host's money. The real commercial competitor for this idea is Hostkit (see below).
Confidence: medium. Only 5 searches ran, and the page text came through search summaries, not the pages.

## Evidence
- Municipal FAQ and guide documents describe iTAXAS as an electronic platform made available by
  municipalities, for exclusive use by tourism establishments, AL and other sector entities, to
  register, settle and pay the tourist tax. Sources:
  https://taxadecidade.cm-gaia.pt/downloadentityassets/2024110808434g72dcf40338hjj ,
  https://taxaturistica.cmcalheta.pt/downloadentityassets/2024110808434g72dcf40338hjj ,
  https://www.cm-terrasdebouro.pt/images/noticias/2025/06/13/FAQTxtur2025.pdf ,
  https://taxaturistica.cm-evora.pt/cmevora-wirepaper/uploads/writer_file/document/184/faq__1_.pdf ,
  https://www.cm-obidos.pt/cmobidos/uploads/document/file/4678/faq_taxa_turistica_online.pdf
- Per those FAQs, entities must register within 30 days of starting activity, each AL must be
  registered individually even for the same owner, and payment is in a single instalment with an
  invoice-receipt. This is a per-municipality portal, so a host with units in several municipalities
  faces several separate portals (inferred from the per-municipality URLs; the multi-portal pain is unverified).
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, Trustpilot or forum reviews of iTAXAS turned up. Not
  proof that none exist; the searches were limited.
- Momentum: municipal documents dated 2024 and 2025 show it is in active use. Developer or vendor: unverified.
- Adjacent commercial competitor, Hostkit (https://hostkit.pt/en/about-us/): says it is certified by the
  tax authority (no. 2549), automates TMT calculation and submission in dozens of municipalities, and
  does INE reports. Trustpilot summary shows 4.9/5 from 93 reviews (https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/hostkit.pt)
  and GetApp 5.0 (https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/hostkit/). No negative reviews surfaced. Chekin also
  mentions tourist-tax features (https://chekin.com/pt/?p=31266). These are the real threat to the idea,
  not iTAXAS.

## Pricing
No fee for iTAXAS found (unverified). The tax itself is municipal, e.g. Lisbon EUR 4 per night per guest over
13, up to 7 nights; Sintra EUR 2, up to 3 nights (idealista.pt and cm-sintra.pt results). Hostkit pricing: not checked.

## Fit gaps
- Single-municipality portals: no cross-municipality dashboard or router (inferred, unverified).
- Hosts must still collect the tax from guests and file manually; no guest-facing flow (inferred).
- Portuguese-language municipal portals; foreign-language support unverified.

## Opening
iTAXAS does not kill the idea; it is the filing endpoint a router could submit to, but a "router" must beat
Hostkit, which already automates TMT submission with strong reviews.
