# Poupa Energia (Portugal)

Dropped idea: C0802 Portugal energy communities EGAC management.

## Verdict
**not-a-real-competitor**

## Evidence
- Poupa Energia is a free electricity and gas tariff comparison and switching platform run by ADENE (the national energy agency). Sources: https://eportugal.gov.pt/en/servicos/consultar-e-mudar-de-tarifario-de-eletricidade-e-gas-natural-atraves-do-poupa-energia and https://www.gov.pt/servicos/consultar-e-mudar-de-tarifario-de-eletricidade-e-gas-natural-atraves-do-poupa-energia
- A search summary says it launched in November 2017 and lists about 320 tariffs from 26 retailers (secondary summary, unverified against the original).
- Three searches (Portuguese and English) found nothing linking Poupa Energia to energy communities, collective self-consumption or EGAC management. A name match on "poupa energia" is the likely cause of the original flag.
- Complaints: none found. Results had no app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews for it. The Portal da Queixa result was for a different brand (Energia Simples).
- Momentum: no news on development found. It is a state-run service, so shutdown risk is low, but this is unverified.

## Pricing
Free to consumers (per the gov.pt page). No EGAC-related product or price exists.

## Fit gaps
It does not do community or collective self-consumption administration at all: no member and quota allocation, no sharing coefficients, no E-REDES/DGEG filing support, and no billing between members.

## Opening
The EGAC management niche is not occupied by Poupa Energia. Other EGAC tools were not checked here.
