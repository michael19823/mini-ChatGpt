# Sinersol (Portugal)

Dropped idea: C0802, Portugal energy communities EGAC management.

## Verdict
not-a-real-competitor

## Evidence
- Four searches (Portuguese and English) found no EGAC or energy-community management software from Sinersol. Searches run: "Sinersol comunidades de energia EGAC", "Sinersol energy communities management platform", "Sinersol Lda autoconsumo coletivo entidade gestora EGAC opiniões" and "Sinersol Penafiel fotovoltaico empresa". The EGAC ERSE and E-Redes results did not mention it.
- The only Sinersol hit is a public-procurement listing for SINERSOL, LDA. It describes a Portuguese contractor in renewable energy, energy efficiency and project management, with 3 awarded contracts, including a PV installation at a Santa Casa da Misericórdia de Penafiel site. Source: https://bidsfactory.com/en/companies/sinersol-lda
- This looks like a PV installer and engineering firm, not a software product. Unverified: the original reason it was listed as a competitor.
- Platforms that do exist for Portuguese energy communities, and are the more relevant competitors if C0802 is revisited: INESC TEC's RECreation, licensed to Capwatt (https://bip.inesctec.pt/?p=49822), and the Digital-CER project. Neither was assessed here. Large utilities (EDP, Iberdrola) are also creating communities, per the search results.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, forum or Trustpilot content about Sinersol turned up.
