# Republic of the Congo: local payroll consultants

Dropped idea: C0810, Congo payroll 2026 ITS/CNSS/CAMU. Competitor type: consultant, no URL known.
Searches: 4 of 6 used (French and English). Remaining searches were not needed.

## Verdict: beatable

(Low confidence. The search found no named local consultant, so this verdict is mostly inferred.)

## Evidence
- No named local payroll consultancy or accounting firm in Brazzaville or Pointe-Noire turned up in the results, and no reviews, complaints or forum threads about any. No complaints found, which is a valid result and not proof that customers are satisfied. Web coverage of small local firms is thin, so absence of evidence is weak.
- The visible outsourcing offer is mostly international or pan-African providers:
  - Africa HR Solutions (africa-hr.com/republic-of-congo-payroll-outsourcing/), which describes itself as serving over 400 clients across Africa. This is its own claim and is unverified.
  - Mercans (dsr.mercans.com/?p=889), which says it runs all compliance elements on its own stack. Its page notes that Congo payroll needs a CNSS engine with branch-specific ceilings, the 2026 ITS scale (Law 42-2025), CAMU solidarity-threshold logic and the 7.5% unique salary tax.
  - Remote, Deel and Rippling, which per a Playroll/Mercans-style comparison operate through partner networks and don't own the entity.
  These are vendor pages, not independent reviews.
- Payroll rules are complex and changed for 2026 (new ITS scale, CAMU rollout, biometric CAMU cards handed out from March 2025 per allAfrica). That supports demand for either consultants or software.
- The search results only suggested "a rigorous payroll system with suitable software". They named no local payroll software.

## Pricing
- No local consultant fees were found. Unverified.
- For comparison, Remote's page lists payroll at 27 USD per employee per month for Congo-Brazzaville (remote.com/fr-ch/explorateur-de-pays/congo). That is a global platform price, not a consultant price, and it is probably high for a small local firm.

## Fit gaps (largely inferred, unverified)
- Consultants are manual, per-client, and likely hard to scale. Software that automates ITS, CNSS and CAMU calculation, payslips and monthly filings may undercut them on price and speed.
- International providers are priced and structured for employer-of-record or multinational clients, not small local SMEs.
- No evidence found on whether local firms handle the 2026 ITS scale and CAMU thresholds correctly.

## Opening
Small and mid-size Congolese employers who rely on an accountant or consultant for monthly payroll could be served by a cheap, correct-by-default ITS/CNSS/CAMU payroll tool. Local consultants look fragmented and unreviewed rather than strong, but this needs ground-truth checks (fees, quality, willingness to adopt software).
