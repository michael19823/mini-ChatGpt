# Mercans (Republic of the Congo) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0810 Congo payroll 2026 ITS/CNSS/CAMU. Searches used: 4 of 6 (English and French).

## Verdict: beatable

Mercans is a real, credible payroll provider with a Congo offering. It is an enterprise-oriented managed service for multinationals, not a tool for local small and mid-size firms (PME).

## Evidence
- Mercans publishes a Congo-Brazzaville payroll page. It claims a CNSS engine with branch-specific ceilings, the 2026 ITS scale (Law 42-2025), CAMU threshold logic, the 7.5% TUS and in-country staff. Source: https://dsr.mercans.com/?p=889 (marketing copy, not independently verified).
- That page also says most providers handle only part of this (for example CAMU threshold logic done manually). This is Mercans' own claim.
- Ratings found via search snippets (unverified, not opened): G2 4.8/5 (27 reviews), Trustpilot 4.0 (4 reviews), Glassdoor 4.1 (employee reviews). Source: https://www.gloroots.com/blog/mercans-reviews
- Complaints: no payroll-accuracy or Congo-specific complaints found. Generic criticisms from review aggregators are UX that trails modern tools, no published EOR pricing, thin independent review coverage and 5-15 day onboarding. Sources: https://www.gloroots.com/blog/mercans-reviews, https://es.trustpilot.com/review/mercans.com
- Momentum: active. It publishes 2026-specific content and is described as founded in 2000, operating in about 160 countries (G2N Nova and HR Blizz platforms). Source: https://peoplemanagingpeople.com/tools/mercans-review/. No shutdown or acquisition found.
- No local-language (French) complaints or local app-store reviews found.

## Pricing
- No official price found. Third-party estimates (unverified): global payroll about $25-60 per employee per month, EOR about $400-600 per employee per month, implementation about $2,000-15,000. Source: https://softwarefinder.com/hr/mercans (snippet).
- Quote-based sales model. This is far above what a small Congolese firm would pay.

## Fit gaps
- Targets multinationals running multi-country payroll or EOR, not local PME.
- Managed service with sales-led onboarding and implementation fees.
- Enterprise pricing and minimums (unverified).
- No evidence of a self-serve, mobile or low-cost local product, or of French-first local filing tooling. Not verified.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, French-language CNSS/CAMU/ITS payroll and filing tool for local PME sits below Mercans' enterprise and managed-service market.
