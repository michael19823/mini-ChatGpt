# Sage Paie (Republic of the Congo) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C0810 Congo payroll 2026 ITS/CNSS/CAMU. Type: commercial. 4 WebSearch calls used (English and French).

## Verdict: beatable

Sage is a real vendor with a local partner, but no search result showed a Congo-localised payroll product (2026 ITS scale, CNSS ceilings, CAMU). The French-market Sage Paie is not built for Congo, and any Congo version looks like a partner-led, quote-priced implementation.

## Evidence
- A Sage partner, MTI Congo, is based in Pointe-Noire. It implements Sage solutions for Republic of the Congo firms, is described as specialising in Sage 100, and lists Paie/RH work. Source: erpresearch.com/sage-partners/mti-congo. Whether it covers Congo statutory payroll is unverified.
- Sage Business Cloud Paie reviews are 4.6/5 from 36 users on Capterra France. These are French-market reviews, not Congo. Source: capterra.fr/software/215768/sage-business-cloud-payroll
- Only complaint found: a Sage community post about users unable to log in to Sage Paie / Business Cloud Paie. It is a French-market incident and its date and scope are unverified. Source: communityhub.sage.com (Sage Business Cloud Paie, announcements).
- No Congo-specific complaints, reviews or outage news were found. Finding none is a valid result, not proof of satisfaction.
- No result confirmed that any Sage product implements the Congo 2026 ITS scale (Law 42-2025), CNSS branch ceilings or CAMU logic. Unverified.
- Other Congo payroll options in the results are Mercans and Africa HR Solutions (outsourced/service) and free calculators such as afrotools.com. They show the niche is served by services and calculators rather than cheap SME software.
- Momentum: Sage is actively developed in France. Momentum in Congo is unknown (unverified).

## Pricing
- French market only, per a review site (culture-rh.com / companeo.com summaries, not Sage's own page): Business Cloud Paie from about 22 EUR HT/month for micro firms, with a base fee plus a per-employee fee (example: 15 EUR per employee plus a 5 EUR subscription for 1-3 employees). Sage 100 Paie & RH from about 120 EUR HT/month.
- Congo pricing: not found. Sage 100 via a local integrator is likely quote-based, which would suit mid-sized firms better than micro SMEs (inference, unverified).

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified)
- No evidence of out-of-the-box Congo ITS/CNSS/CAMU rules or CNSS filing formats.
- Implementer-led (Sage 100 via partner) suggests cost and setup that a small buyer may not accept.
- The French SaaS version is French-law payroll.
- Offline and mobile support: not checked.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve Congo-specific payroll tool (2026 ITS, CNSS ceilings, CAMU, CNSS declaration outputs) for small firms that cannot afford Sage 100 plus an integrator. Check what MTI Congo actually ships before committing.
