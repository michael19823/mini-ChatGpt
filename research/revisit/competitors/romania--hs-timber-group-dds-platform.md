# HS Timber Group DDS platform (Romania) - dropped idea C0814: Romania EUDR timber DDS

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

HS Timber Group is a large sawmill operator. What it runs is an in-house traceability system (TIMFLOW), not a due-diligence-statement product sold to other firms. I found no evidence that it sells a DDS or EUDR filing product.

## Evidence
- HS Timber Group (formerly Holzindustrie Schweighofer) runs sawmills in Sebes, Radauti and Reci, and a panel plant in Siret (g4media.ro, "grupul holzindustrie schweighofer isi schimba numele in hs timber group").
- Since 2017 it has run TIMFLOW, a GPS-based log-tracking system that works alongside the national SUMAL system. Its data on transports to HS sawmills is published on a public portal (Cesefor Rosewood page "TimFlow | Wood Tracking System"; search summary of ro.linkedin.com/in/adrian-lucan).
- TIMFLOW tracks log transports to HS's own gates. I found no sign it generates EUDR DDS filings or is offered to third parties (unverified: absence of evidence from 4 searches, not confirmed with the company).
- Searches in Romanian and English found no HS-specific EUDR DDS product, no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews, and no outage or failed-filing news.
- Complaints about HS are about sourcing and governance, not software: EIA/WWF illegal-logging allegations, an FSC investigation, and an OCCRP story on free timber demanded from suppliers (occrp.org).
- Wider Romanian context: SUMAL has documented fraud, with about 2,400 fake or copied images flagged from Apr 2022 to Dec 2023 (OCCRP). WWF Romania has pushed for SUMAL to interoperate with the EU EUDR information system (wwf.ro letters, 2026). ASFOR has asked for SUMAL and existing certifications to be recognised as an alternative to EUDR due-diligence documents (agerpres.ro, 2025-06-02). The actual competitive threat for C0814 is the state SUMAL system and the EU TRACES/EUDR information system, not HS.

## Complaints
None found about HS Timber's platform as a product.

## Pricing
None published. Unverified whether any price exists, since the system appears to be internal.

## Fit gaps
Not a product for outside buyers. It is tied to HS's own supply chain and its own sawmills' gates. It is not a multi-client DDS tool and there is no evidence of EU information-system submission features.

## Momentum
Operating since 2017. I found no recent product news. Status as a commercial offering: unverified.

## Opening
HS does not block a DDS tool for small Romanian forest owners, harvesters and traders. The real gaps to check are SUMAL/EU-system interoperability and whether state tools make a third-party DDS product redundant.
