# Karisimbitech (Karisimbi Technology Solutions Ltd), Rwanda

Killed ideas: C0824 (Rwanda clinic HMS/EMR), C0829 (Rwanda hospital/clinic HMS)

## Verdict: beatable

A small Kigali startup with real but modest traction. No complaints found, but also little evidence of momentum since about 2020. The bigger competitors in Rwanda are the free government-backed OpenMRS and OpenClinic, not Karisimbitech.

## Evidence
- Kigali health-tech startup. Products: XanaHealth (web EHR linking providers, insurers and policy-makers) and IvuliroTech (paperless hospital EMR). Sources: [VC4A](https://vc4a.com/ventures/karisimbi-technology-solutions-ltd), [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/600623-56), [techbuild.africa](https://techbuild.africa/e-health-startup-karisimbi-hospitals-digital/)
- Sources disagree on founding year (2016 vs 2018). Backers named: Reinnovate, Healthtech Hub, Hatcher+. See [africa.com](https://africa.com/top-10-news/digitizing-rwandas-healthcare-paper-trail)
- MVP released Sept 2019. Reported: more than a dozen hospitals onboarded and more than 30,000 patients treated (company-era press, undated, so possibly stale).
- Momentum: I found no 2024-2026 news, funding, or product releases. Treat this as stagnant or low-visibility (unverified). A VC4A XanaHealth profile exists but gave no recent detail.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in English and Kinyarwanda returned no app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints about Karisimbitech. No reviews exist to cite.
- Market context: public facilities use OpenMRS (35 of 42 district hospitals) and OpenClinic, which are free. Only about a third of health centers used any EMR. Reported barriers were electricity, internet and software glitches ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/10.1186/s12911-026-03534-w), [UR repository](https://dr.ur.ac.rw/handle/123456789/243)). These are general EMR findings, not about Karisimbitech.

## Pricing
Model is a monthly subscription plus an upfront installation fee (per techbuild.africa). No published price found, so the amounts are unverified. The upfront fee likely suits hospitals better than very small clinics.

## Fit gaps
Mostly inferred and unverified:
- Targets hospitals and insurer connectivity, so it is probably heavier than a solo or small private clinic needs.
- It is web-based, and offline use is not documented.
- Kinyarwanda support is not documented.
- No evidence of mobile-first use or of integration with Rwandan insurers such as RSSB/Mutuelle.

## Opening
A cheap, offline-capable, Kinyarwanda-friendly EMR/billing tool for small private clinics, with no installation fee, is not clearly served by Karisimbitech.
