# MocDoc (Rwanda)

Killed ideas: C0824 (Rwanda clinic HMS/EMR), C0829 (Rwanda hospital/clinic HMS).
Type: commercial. Searches used: 4 successful (a 5th hit the usage limit). Evidence is thin and mostly vendor-authored.

## Verdict: strong (low confidence)

An active, India-based cloud HMS/EMR vendor with a Rwanda-targeted marketing presence, a high third-party rating, and no complaints found. Whether it has real Rwandan customers is unverified.

## Evidence
- MocDoc runs Rwanda landing pages (Kigali, Huye, Musanze, Rubavu, Rwamagana, Kayonza, Nyagatare) claiming EMR/EHR, billing, appointments, pharmacy, lab, OPD/IPD, multi-location, and English/Kinyarwanda/French prescriptions and reports. Source: https://mocdoc.com/rwanda (vendor claim). These look like templated SEO pages, so actual Rwandan installs are unverified.
- The vendor claims HIPAA and Rwanda MOH compliance and "1500+ institutions". Both are unverified vendor claims, and the MOH compliance claim has no regulator source.
- Capterra lists MocDoc HMS at 4.9/5 from 44 reviews, with ease of use 4.9, value for money 4.8 and support 4.9. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/161290/MocDoc-HMS/. The one review I could see (October 2024) is positive. I could not tell where the reviewers are based, and most are likely Indian.
- Other review sources (SaaSWorthy, GoodFirms, MocDoc's own success stories) are positive. Sources: https://www.saasworthy.com/product/mocdoc-hms/reviews, https://mocdoc.com/success-stories.
- Complaints: none found in Capterra, SaaSWorthy or the Rwanda-related searches. No Rwanda-specific user reviews were found, and no Kinyarwanda-language results mentioned MocDoc.
- Momentum: active. It has a current blog, Capterra 2026 pages, comparison posts and country-targeted pages. No sign of acquisition or shutdown.

## Pricing
Not published. Capterra says to contact the vendor. I found no Rwanda price or minimum, so affordability for a small Rwandan clinic is unverified.

## Fit gaps (all inferred, unverified)
- No local Rwanda office, support or implementation partner was found. Support is probably remote from India.
- The MOH and RSSB insurance or claims integration is not documented, and the CBHI/Mutuelle billing flow is unknown.
- Offline operation and mobile money (MTN MoMo) payment support are not stated.
- The Rwanda pages are generic templates, so depth of localization is doubtful.
- Rwanda's public sector uses government-backed EMRs (OpenMRS-based). I did not verify this in this run, and the planned search for it hit the limit.

## Opening
A small-clinic-focused HMS with local support, RSSB/insurance claims handling, offline use and MoMo payments could win where MocDoc is only a remote, generic product. Its high ratings and apparent lack of complaints make a head-on feature fight hard.
