# Rwanda: RRA-licensed EBM providers (killed C0828: Rwanda EBM-integrated POS/ERP/PMS)

## Verdict: weak

No single competitor exists. RRA runs the EBM 2.1 scheme and the free certified SDC/VSDC rail. Integration vendors exist but are fragmented, and the only ones found are Odoo add-ons. The gap is thin but real, and it is an integration play rather than a rival product.

## Evidence
- RRA accepts several EBM 2.1 forms: Sales Data Controller (integrated apps embedded in the taxpayer's accounting software), Virtual SDC (offline-capable integrated apps), e-invoicing software, mobile e-invoicing and online e-invoicing. Private systems integrate via a certification request to RRA (cis_sde_certification@rra.gov.rw). Source: https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/EBM_2.1_Booklet_EN.pdf and https://europe.thomsonreuters.com/uk/compliance/regulatory-updates/rwanda
- The search results showed no published list of certified software vendors (unverified whether RRA publishes one elsewhere).
- Odoo marketplace add-ons exist: "Rwanda EBM POS Fiscal" (Pokutsoft), the "RRA EBM Suite" (AminiTech), and "Rwanda EBM e-Invoice". Sources: https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_rw_ebm_pos , https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/aminitech_rra_suite/
- These add-ons require the buyer to own a certified VSDC and to run Odoo. Both are barriers for small shops.
- RRA is exporting the EBM system (Kenya, Uganda, Madagascar, Zambia and Chad were named). Source: https://allafrica.com/stories/202603310046.html . This signals that the government rail is strong and expanding.
- Complaints: none found about vendors or vendor software. Only general merchant issues surfaced: cost for small businesses, need for stable internet, limited skills, and resistance to EBM. Source: https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/184853/Opinions/how-mandatory-ebm-invoices-affect-your-business . No app-store, Reddit or Facebook reviews were found. Searching in Kinyarwanda returned only English news.
- Enforcement has been tightened since August 2024 and consumers get VAT rewards (10% of VAT), which keeps demand for compliance high. Source: https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/02/rra-disburses-record-frw1-3-billion-in-vat-rewards-to-strengthen-tax-compliance/

## Pricing
- Pokutsoft Odoo POS fiscal module: $69 (v19) or $89 (v18), one-off, from the Odoo Apps listing.
- AminiTech full RRA suite for Odoo: about $1,615 (v17 to v19), from the Odoo Apps listing.
- No pricing found for other vendors (unverified).
- Odoo licence and a VSDC are extra costs (unverified amounts).

## Fit gaps
- The add-ons are Odoo-only, so there is no lightweight POS for micro-retail.
- They need the buyer's own certified VSDC.
- Nothing found for PMS or hospitality, or for non-Odoo ERPs (absence of evidence only).
- No Kinyarwanda or mobile-first offering evident (unverified).
- EBM 2.1 is aimed at large and medium taxpayers. Small taxpayers are served mainly by RRA's own free or low-cost tools (unverified in detail).

## Opening
A small gap exists for a simple, mobile or offline-capable, Kinyarwanda-friendly POS/PMS with built-in certification for small and mid-size businesses. It is limited by RRA's own free rail and by the certification barrier, so it only works as a thin compliance integration.
