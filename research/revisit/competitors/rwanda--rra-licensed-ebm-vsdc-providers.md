# Rwanda: RRA-licensed EBM/VSDC providers (idea C0822: Rwanda EBM/VSDC invoice integration)

## Verdict: beatable
Searches used: 5 of 6. No customer complaints about any specific provider were found.

## Evidence
- No public RRA list of licensed EBM/VSDC providers surfaced. The search only returned generic RRA pages. Providers as a group are unverified. Source: rra.gov.rw search results.
- RRA offers EBM 2.1 software free to VAT-registered taxpayers. It replaced the hardware devices, which cost about $200-300 (New Times, "Revenue body replaces EBM devices"; New Times, "EBM Version 2 uptake on the rise"). One snippet says EBM 2.1 is designed for large and medium taxpayers, and others may apply. That is a free government baseline, and it weakens the case for a paid integration.
- Commercial integrators found are mostly Odoo modules:
  - AminiTech Solutions on apps.odoo.com: "RRA EBM Base" (free VSDC connection setup), "RRA EBM Pro" and "RRA EBM Suite" (invoicing, POS, stock, VSDC, QR/fiscal signature). Odoo versions 17-19. https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/aminitech_rra_suite/
  - AD Finance (Odoo partner) offers the Odoo-IntuitFlow RRA-EBM integration. https://odoo.com/partners/adfinance-7090846
  - Coverage looks limited to Odoo users. I found no integrations for QuickBooks, Zoho, Tally or other common small-business tools (unverified, from limited search).
- Complaints are about the EBM system, not about providers. Small-business burdens include internet connectivity needs, device cost under the old hardware (New Times; ktpress), merchants adding markups for receipts, and tax evasion/non-issuance (isurape.rw, New Times). These are demand-side problems and say nothing about integrator quality.
- Momentum: Madagascar became the fifth country to adopt Rwanda's EBM tax system (allAfrica, 2026-03). That points to active, continuing investment and possible regional reuse. https://allafrica.com/stories/202603310046.html
- No app-store, Trustpilot, G2 or Reddit reviews were found. The Kinyarwanda search returned only English results. No outage reports were found.

## Pricing
- RRA EBM 2.1 software: free (New Times). 
- AminiTech Odoo modules: prices not seen in search snippets. Unverified.
- AD Finance: no pricing found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (partly inferred, unverified)
- Integrator coverage is Odoo-centric, so non-Odoo SMEs, simple POS and mobile-first sellers look underserved.
- Mandatory VSDC and EBM 2.1 are free from RRA, so the paid-integration niche is narrow. The value would be in connectors to local POS, accounting and mobile money, not in the EBM itself.
- No evidence either way on Kinyarwanda UI, offline use or multi-branch support.

## Opening
A small gap exists for lightweight non-Odoo connectors (POS, accounting, mobile-money flows) bridging to the free RRA VSDC. Nothing found suggests unhappy customers, and the free government tool caps pricing power, so need is real but the gap is narrow.
