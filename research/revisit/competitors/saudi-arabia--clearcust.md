# Clearcust (Saudi Arabia) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0852 Saudi FASAH declaration prep for small brokers.
Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Arabic).

## Verdict: weak

Clearcust is a B2B API middleware that links enterprise importers and ERPs to licensed brokers. It is not a declaration-prep tool for small brokers.

## Evidence
- Clearcust describes itself as a Logtech company founded by licensed customs brokers and IT engineers. It offers an API-based SaaS "customs middleware" that syncs importers, exporters and brokers across several countries. Source: https://clearcust.com/ (search snippet).
- Its "Customs Broker Connection" is an API that moves data between a shipper's ERP and customs brokers. It is aimed at shippers and logistics operators. Source: https://www.clearcust.com/en/logistics-operators/saruh/saudi-arabia/riyadh/customs-brokers/all
- It has directory-style pages for brokers in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam, plus UAE and Oman ports. This suggests it lists or connects brokers rather than equipping them. Sources: the clearcust.com pages for Riyadh (saruh), Jeddah (sajed) and Dammam (sadmm).
- A Crunchbase entry exists (https://crunchbase.com/organization/clearcust). I did not see its contents. The coverage "A former unicorn founding member raised funding to revolutionise the legacy customs management software industry" (salestechstar.com) may be about Clearcust, but the snippet does not say so. Unverified.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews turned up. An Arabic search returned nothing on Clearcust. Absence of reviews suggests low adoption among small brokers, but it is not proof.
- Whether it integrates directly with FASAH is unverified. Whether it has an Arabic UI is unverified.

## Pricing
Not published in anything I found. Unverified. Enterprise API integration is likely quote-based, but that is an inference.

## Fit gaps
- Targets shippers, ERPs and logistics operators, not small brokers who prepare declarations.
- Integration-first (API), so there is no evident self-serve declaration-prep UI for a small broker. Unverified.
- No evidence of Arabic-first workflow, HS-code assistance or small-broker pricing.
- It may act as a lead source for brokers rather than a rival to a broker tool. Unverified.

## Momentum
Site is live and has localized (English, Spanish) pages, so it is somewhat active. No dated news, acquisition or shutdown found.

## Opening
A cheap, Arabic-first declaration-prep tool for small Saudi brokers is not addressed by Clearcust. Clearcust could be a distribution partner instead.
