# InstaHMS (Insta by Practo) - Saudi Arabia

Killed idea: C0860 NPHIES claims/RCM clinics.

## Verdict: strong

A real, Practo-backed vendor with a NPHIES-compliance claim, a Saudi office and a dedicated Saudi GM. It is a full HMS/clinic suite, so it covers claims as a module. It is not claims-specialist software. Rated "strong" because of backing and NPHIES presence, but with real gaps for small buyers.

## Evidence
- Vendor blog and landing pages say Insta is "soon to be NPHIES compliant" / "100% compliant", with a NPHIES demo and webinar funnel: https://www.instahms.com/blog/insta-hms-soon-to-be-nphies-compliant , https://go.instahms.com/nphies/free-demo (the two wordings differ, so the exact status is unverified).
- Practo appointed a General Manager for Saudi Arabia, and a Riyadh office is listed (search summary only, address unverified): https://www.linkedin.com/showcase/insta-by-practo
- Claims 18,000+ daily users, 1,250+ centers, 22+ countries (vendor claim, unverified).
- Competing GCC vendor MocDoc also markets NPHIES integration: https://mocdoc.com/nphies-integration
- Complaints (Capterra/GetApp via search snippets, ~3.7/5 over 33 reviews, 50% recommend): needs more functionality inside modules, not user-friendly for doctors, somewhat expensive, learning curve, support doesn't return voice messages. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/130501/Insta-HMS/reviews/ . These are global reviews, not Saudi- or NPHIES-specific. I found no Saudi-specific or NPHIES-specific complaints, no Arabic-language complaints, and no outage news.

## Pricing
- Listed starting at USD 25 per user (Capterra/GetApp listing). Per-user, free trial.
- For UAE/Oman/Qatar/Kuwait, per search summaries: clinics USD 40/user/month with a 5-user minimum; hospitals USD 50/user/month with a 20-user minimum (source: https://zoftwarehub.com/products/insta/plans ; secondhand, unverified).
- No Saudi SAR price published. Per-user pricing with minimums is costly for solo or 1-3 seat clinics.

## Fit gaps
- Generic HMS, so claims/RCM (rejection management, resubmission, denial analytics) is likely shallow. Unverified.
- Reviewers report weak doctor UX and modules that lack depth.
- Per-user minimums and a sales-led process disadvantage micro-clinics.
- Arabic language/RTL support and offline use: not confirmed in searches (unverified).

## Opening
A lightweight, claims-first NPHIES tool for small clinics (rejection fixing, resubmission, cheap flat price, Arabic-first) that sits beside or replaces the clinic's existing system, rather than competing with a full HMS.

Momentum: active (Saudi GM hire, NPHIES webinars, 2026 reviews listed).
