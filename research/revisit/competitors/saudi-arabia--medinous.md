# Medinous (MentisSoft, Bengaluru) - Saudi Arabia - killed C0860 (NPHIES claims/RCM clinics)

## Verdict: beatable

Medinous is a real, NPHIES-certified HMS, but it is a full hospital system sold through sales, aimed at mid/large hospitals. A claims/RCM-only tool for small clinics is not what it sells (though it has a clinic edition, Fusion).

## Evidence
- Press release: Medinous HMS certified on NPHIES (eligibility, authorization, claims, payment test cases passed); markets automated claim coding/validation. Sources: https://www.einpresswire.com/article/866213077/medinous-hms-joins-the-nphies-network-in-the-kingdom-of-saudi-arabia ; https://www.eyeofriyadh.com/news/details/medinous-hms-joins-the-nphies-network-empowering-hospitals-with-connected-compliant-systems
- Described as web-based HMS for large and mid-size hospitals and clinics; Fusion = clinics, Spectrum = mid-size hospitals (technologycounter.com/products/medinous, snippet only).
- An Arabic search result claims 26+ Saudi hospitals since 2008; the page's link to Medinous is unclear (unverified).
- Momentum: NPHIES certification announced in 2025/2026 per the press-release listing, so actively developed.
- Complaints: none found. Searches of English and Arabic surfaced no reviews on G2, TrustRadius, app stores, Reddit or forums. G2 and TrustRadius pages exist but showed no review content in the snippets (0 ratings per Slashdot snippet).

## Pricing
Not published; sales-led. A third-party estimate of $50-150/user/month plus $1,000-5,000 onboarding appears on an aggregator page and is unverified benchmark data, not vendor pricing.

## Fit gaps
- Full-suite HMS (registration, EMR, pharmacy, lab, billing), so a clinic must adopt a whole system to get NPHIES claims; no evidence of a standalone claims/RCM or claim-rejection-management layer for clinics already running another system.
- Sales-led with implementation cost; likely heavy for micro and small clinics (unverified).
- No evidence found on Arabic UI quality, rejection analytics or denial-appeal workflows.

## Opening
A lightweight, self-serve NPHIES claims and rejection-management layer that works alongside any clinic's existing system leaves room beside Medinous's all-in-one, sales-led HMS.
