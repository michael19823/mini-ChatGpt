# Omniful (Saudi Arabia) - competitor to C0862 (Trucking TGA/Wasl compliance)

## Verdict
weak (for this idea). Omniful is a real, funded Saudi OMS/WMS/TMS vendor, but searches found no TGA/Wasl compliance feature, so it does not occupy the trucking-compliance niche.

## Evidence
- Riyadh-based (Al Malqa), founded about 2022, sells OMS, WMS, TMS and POS for retailers, brands and 3PLs. Source: zoftwarehub listing https://zoftwarehub.com/ar-sa/products/omniful/overview (third-party aggregator).
- Raised a $5.85M seed in Dec 2023, led by VentureSouq with 500 Global. Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2023/12/05/omniful-a-supply-chain-and-e-commerce-enablement-startup-emerges-from-stealth-with-5-85m/ and https://www.wamda.com/en/2023/12/omniful-raises-5-85-million-seed-mena-expansion. One search summary said no later round was found (unverified).
- Its TMS pages describe AI route optimization, driver tracking and "adherence to local transport laws" in generic terms (https://www.omniful.ai/best-tms-software/warehouse). I found no mention of Wasl or TGA integration, permits or carrier licensing on Omniful's own pages. A separate blog on Wasl-TGA integration for Saudi 3PLs came from another vendor (https://www.shiprexnow.com/blog/how-to-automate-wasl-tga-integration-saudi-3pl/), not from Omniful. Lack of a Wasl feature is unverified, not confirmed.
- Lokad's 2026 review calls it a real but early-stage execution stack, not a planning engine (https://www.lokad.com/review-of-omniful-ai/).
- Complaints: none found. Capterra shows no published user reviews (https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1067254/Omniful). No G2, app-store, Reddit or Arabic-forum complaints surfaced in 4 searches (English and Arabic). Not an indicator of quality, only of low review volume.
- Momentum: actively marketing (many localized TMS pages, including Iraq), expanding in Saudi, UAE and India.

## Pricing
Published partner price sheet (https://docs.omniful.ai/partners/omniful-pricing-packages.pdf), as summarized by search; figures not independently checked:
- OMS: $4,000 one-time setup, $900/month (up to 25K orders) to $1,500/month (up to 50K orders), plus per-order fees.
- WMS: $4,000 setup, $500/month (5 users) or $800/month (10 users).
- TMS pricing: not found.
This is well above what a small trucking operator would pay for a compliance-only tool.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at retailers, 3PLs and e-commerce fulfilment, not trucking carriers or fleet owners who must keep TGA licences, permits and Wasl registration current.
- No visible Wasl/TGA filing, licence-expiry or driver/vehicle-registry workflow.
- Setup fee and monthly price suit mid-size and larger firms.

## Opening
A cheap, compliance-first tool for small Saudi trucking firms (Wasl/TGA registration, permit and licence expiry tracking) is not covered by Omniful today, though it could add a module.
