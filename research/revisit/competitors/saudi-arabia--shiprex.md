# Shiprex (Saudi Arabia) - competitor check for C0862 (Trucking TGA/Wasl compliance)

## Verdict: beatable

Shiprex is a real, bilingual shipping/courier/3PL ERP that claims Wasl/TGA sync, but it targets courier and 3PL firms, not trucking fleets. It shows no verified trucking-compliance depth, and no independent reviews were found.

## Evidence
- Shiprex is cloud-hosted, bilingual shipping ERP for shipping/courier startups in Egypt and Saudi Arabia; free "Lite" tier, paid "Pro" (wallets, general ledger, inventory, SMS, webhooks, 20+ plugins). Source: https://www.shiprexnow.com/lite/
- Vendor blog claims Wasl-compatible software that stays synced with government servers for TGA compliance in Saudi 3PL. Vendor claim only, unverified: https://www.shiprexnow.com/blog/how-to-automate-wasl-tga-integration-saudi-3pl/
- Positioning is order management, multichannel/e-commerce integration, zones, driver assignment, COD, courier management. Not fleet/vehicle/driver-permit/trip-document management. Sources: https://www.shiprexnow.com/blog/essential-features-shipping-erp-software-shiprex/ and https://shiprexnow.com/en/?p=6587
- Momentum: blog and site content appears recent and active (multiple posts in search results), but exact dates not verified.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in English and Arabic (6 budget, 5 used) returned no app-store, Reddit, forum, Trustpilot/G2/Capterra reviews or outage news for Shiprex. Absence of reviews likely reflects a small user base; this is not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing
- Lite: free, "zero upfront fees and zero costs on any order" (vendor claim, https://www.shiprexnow.com/lite/).
- Pro: price not published in anything found (unverified).

## Fit gaps (inferred from positioning; unverified)
- Built for couriers/3PL/e-commerce shippers, not trucking operators needing Wasl vehicle/trip registration, driver and permit tracking, TGA waybill/e-document rules.
- Wasl claim appears only in marketing blog content; depth of integration (which TGA transaction types) unknown.
- No evidence of offline/mobile driver app for trucking.

## Opening
A trucking-first Wasl/TGA compliance tool for small fleet owners can beat Shiprex, which treats Wasl as a side feature of a courier/3PL ERP.
