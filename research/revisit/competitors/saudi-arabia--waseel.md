# Waseel (Saudi Arabia) - competitor check for C0860 (NPHIES claims/RCM clinics)

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Waseel was incorporated in 2003 as the first medical-insurance ASP in the Gulf. Its own site says it serves 11 payers and more than 1000 providers, with e-transactions, e-approvals and e-claims. Source: https://waseel.com/insurance-companies (search snippet; not opened).
- A search result attributes to Al-Taawuniya the founding of Waseel in 2003 and a link to over 95% of providers with 14 insurers. Source: https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1167060 (snippet only; unverified).
- Waseel has a "Connect" product page for NPHIES integration: https://waseel.com/connect/
- Complaints: 4 searches (English and Arabic) found none. The Arabic search returned only news items, and the review-site search returned unrelated "Wasel" apps. No app-store, Reddit or news complaints were found. That is an absence of data, not proof that users are satisfied.

## Pricing
- One search summary said the Waseel Connect bundle for small clinics costs 1,499 SAR/month. I could not tie it to a source page, so treat it as unverified. If true, it is a high monthly cost for a one-doctor clinic.

## Fit gaps (all unverified; no evidence found)
- Likely built around large providers and payers (the site pitches payers and 1000+ providers). Whether it has a lightweight offering for micro-clinics is unknown.
- Whether it offers a clinic-level RCM layer (denial analytics, resubmission workflow, Arabic-first UX) is unknown.

## Momentum
- Long-running incumbent with an NPHIES connect product. Company status in 2026 (acquisitions, shutdowns) was not checked.

## Opening
- Little, unless a cheap, simple clinic-side RCM layer can sit on top of NPHIES or Waseel connectivity. An incumbent with deep payer and provider links is hard to displace as a connectivity layer.
