# Sage (Senegal) - competitor check for C0867 (Senegal DGID e-invoicing for SMEs)

## Verdict: beatable

Sage is the incumbent accounting vendor in Senegal (Sage 100, Saari, Sage 50, sold via resellers such as Ages Logiciels Services). It is priced and positioned for accounting firms and mid-size or large companies. No product dedicated to DGID e-invoicing for small SMEs was found. Sage does publish content on the Senegal e-invoicing obligation, and it launched an e-invoice compliance solution in Cote d'Ivoire, so it could extend one to Senegal.

## Evidence
- Sage's Morocco site has a blog post on Senegal's new e-invoicing obligations, so Sage is paying attention to the mandate: https://www.sage.com/fr-ma/blog/senegal-les-nouvelles-obligations-de-la-facturation-electronique/ . Snippet only; I did not find a named Senegal DGID module.
- Sage launched a normalized e-invoice compliance solution in Cote d'Ivoire (Dec 2025): https://www.financialafrik.com/2025/12/09/sage-lance-sa-solution-de-conformite-a-la-facture-normalisee-electronique-en-cote-divoire/ . A Senegal equivalent is plausible but unverified.
- Market position: kolonell.com (a competing Senegal vendor blog, so treat as biased and unverified) says over 70% of Senegalese accounting firms use Sage 100 or Saari, and that Sage 100 has about 60% share: https://kolonell.com/fr/blog/logiciel-cabinet-expert-comptable-senegal-2026
- Dakar reseller: Ages Logiciels Services, listed as an authorized Sage partner (Sage 100cloud, Sage 1000, X3): https://www.erpresearch.com/sage-partners/ages-logiciels-services
- Complaints: none specific to Senegal found. Generic, non-Senegal reports on Software Advice and the Sage community forum cite crashes after the 2024 update, bank-feed disconnects and duplicated transactions, and high cost for advanced features: https://softwareadvice.fr/software/190564/sage-business-cloud-accounting and https://communityhub.sage.com/ca/sage_50/f/soutien-technique/215938/bug-2024/535095 . These concern other Sage products and markets and may not apply to Senegalese users.
- Momentum: actively marketing on e-invoicing across Francophone Africa (Morocco, Cote d'Ivoire). Not stagnant.

## Pricing (all figures from kolonell.com, unverified, not from Sage)
- Sage 100: more than 2,500,000 FCFA per year for a 5-person firm; range 2.5M-3.8M FCFA.
- Saari: about 45,000 FCFA per month.
- Sage 50: about 38,000 FCFA per month.
- Cheaper alternative cited: EBP from 850,000 FCFA.
- No official Sage Senegal price list was found.

## Fit gaps
- Sage 100 is priced and sized for accounting firms and mid-size companies, not micro or small VAT-registered merchants or artisans.
- No evidence of a Senegal DGID-specific e-invoicing module, and no evidence of mobile-first or offline invoicing. Both are unverified.
- Licence-and-reseller model, which suggests heavier onboarding than a self-serve app.

## Opening
A cheap, mobile-first, DGID-compliant invoicing tool for small VAT-registered SMEs and artisans is not served by Sage's accounting-suite pricing. The risk is that Sage or its resellers ship a DGID compliance add-on, as happened in Cote d'Ivoire.
