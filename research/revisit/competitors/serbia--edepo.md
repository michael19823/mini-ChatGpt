# eDepo (Serbia)

Dropped ideas: C0868 (Serbia waste DKO records NRIZ), C0874 (Serbia waste record-keeping).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

I could not verify that eDepo exists as a product in Serbia. No URL was known beforehand.

## Evidence
- Four searches (English and Serbian: "eDepo Srbija otpad evidencija DKO NRIZ", "eDepo software Serbia waste record keeping", "eDepo.rs otpad aplikacija", "\"eDepo\" Serbia") returned nothing about a product called eDepo. One further search (a 5th) in Serbian on waste-record software for businesses also found nothing. One search call (the first) failed on a usage limit and was retried with other wording.
- The only name close to it was EDePro, a Belgrade defence manufacturer. It is unrelated: https://dbpedia-live.demo.openlinksw.com/page/EDePro
- The Serbian waste-record context is state-run: annual waste reports go online through the SEPA portal and the NRIZ (National Register of Pollution Sources) information system. Sources: https://balkangreenenergynews.com/rs/rok-za-predaju-izvestaja-o-otpadu-31-mart/ and https://leap.unep.org/en/countries/rs/national-legislation/regulation-form-daily-records-and-annual-report-waste
- Other tools that turned up are not commercial waste-record software for businesses. UpOl is a system for local-community waste records (https://www.znrfak.ni.ac.rs/SE-Journal/Archive/2011-12/Safety%20Engineering%201/PDF/13%20-%20Review%20of%20technical%20solution.pdf). "Ukloni divlju deponiju" is a citizen app for reporting illegal dumps (https://www.undp.org/sr/node/105991).
- No complaints, reviews or app-store entries found (none, because no product was found).

## Fit gaps
