# Sierra Leone: accountants (generic consultant category, no URL)

Idea it killed: C0879 Sierra Leone payroll PAYE/NASSIT.

## Verdict: beatable

Local accountancy firms offer payroll as part of bookkeeping packages, but no productised, priced, self-serve payroll tool for small employers was found. Based on 4 WebSearch calls (English only; no Krio-language results expected or found); no specific firm was evaluated in depth.

## Evidence
- Accountants and outsourcers do market payroll: Aviaan (aviaanaccounting.com, "Accounting Services in Sierra Leone", "Bookkeeping Services in Sierra Leone") lists payroll management among its services; SLONEC (slonec.com), a Freetown accountancy/consultancy firm, offers accounting and consultancy; africa-hr.com has a "Sierra Leone payroll outsourcing" page. Descriptions are marketing copy; quality unverified.
- Obligations are clear and monthly: PAYE and NASSIT due by the 15th of the following month, NASSIT 15% total (10% employer, 5% employee), annual PAYE return due 31 March; filings go through the NRA online portal (sources: asanify.com, playroll.com, nra.gov.sl/node/131 and PaySpace tax guide, as surfaced in search results; not independently verified).
- Global EOR/payroll vendors (Deel-type, getontop.com, playroll.com, rivermate.com, asanify.com) serve foreign employers hiring remotely or via EOR, not small local employers.
- Compliance pressure exists: NASSIT has publicly warned non-compliant employers (africa-press.net) and news covered unpaid NASSIT contributions (awokonewspapersl.com, Sierra Leone Embassy in Liberia). This is enforcement, not complaints about accountants.
- Complaints: none found. No reviews, forum posts or app-store reviews about accountants' payroll or NASSIT/PAYE service in Sierra Leone surfaced. Search on NASSIT portal problems returned no employer complaints.
- Momentum: not applicable to a generic category; Aviaan appears to publish content actively (unverified as a real service level).

## Pricing
No fees found for payroll or accounting services. Searches explicitly returned nothing on prices. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- Accountants likely serve registered, larger SMEs and NGOs; a micro-employer with a few staff may be underserved or pay a disproportionate monthly fee.
- No evidence of self-serve software that computes PAYE bands and NASSIT split and produces NRA/NASSIT schedules and payslips.
- Mobile, offline or low-bandwidth tooling not seen (infrastructure inference, not evidence).
- Global vendors require a local entity or EOR model and are priced for foreign employers.

## Opening
A cheap, simple PAYE/NASSIT calculator plus monthly schedules and payslips for micro-employers fits an unserved niche, but the evidence is thin: demand, willingness to pay and portal-integration feasibility are unverified.
