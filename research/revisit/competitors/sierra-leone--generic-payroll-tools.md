# Sierra Leone: generic payroll tools (killed C0879, payroll PAYE/NASSIT)

## Verdict: weak

No dominant local payroll product turned up. The market is served by foreign EOR/payroll platforms that charge per employee in USD or EUR, plus free calculators and a local outsourcing firm. No user complaints were found.

## Evidence
- Global platforms advertise Sierra Leone PAYE/NASSIT payroll: Ontop (https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/sierra-leone), Playroll (https://playroll.com/compliance-hub/paying-employees-in-sierra-leone), Remote (https://remote.com/en-ie/country-explorer/sierra-leone). Their pages are aimed at foreign firms hiring remote staff, not local SMEs (inferred from the pages; unverified for each vendor).
- PaySpace publishes a Sierra Leone tax guide summary for 2024 (https://www.payspace.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/SL-PAYROLL-SOLUTION-TAX-GUIDE-SUMMARY-2024.pdf). That suggests a South African payroll product covers the country; details unverified.
- AfroTools offers free NRA-compliant PAYE and NASSIT calculators (https://afrotools.com/sierra-leone/). These are calculators only, with no payslips, filings or records.
- Africa HR Solutions is a local-facing payroll outsourcing provider that claims 400+ clients (https://africa-hr.com/sierra-leone-payroll-outsourcing/). The claim is self-reported and unverified.
- Rates found: PAYE is progressive, 0 to 30%. NASSIT is 5% employee and 10% employer. PAYE is due by the 15th of the following month. Penalties of up to 25% were cited by aggregator sites.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews, and no news on outages or failed filings, surfaced in 5 searches. Sierra Leone's working language is English, so the searches were in English. No Krio-language results were expected, and none were found. No search returned NRA or NASSIT portal problem reports.
- Momentum: the vendors' pages look current, but this is unverified, and none was checked for recent releases.

## Pricing
- Remote: payroll from about EUR 27 or $29 per employee per month; EOR EUR 645 per employee per month (from search snippets, https://remote.com/en-ie/country-explorer/sierra-leone). This is expensive for a local firm paying in Leones.
- Other vendors' prices: unverified.
- AfroTools calculators: free.

## Fit gaps
- Foreign platforms target remote hiring and EOR, not local small employers.
- Pricing is in hard currency and per employee, which is steep against Freetown salaries.
- The free tools only calculate. They don't generate payslips, keep records, or prepare PAYE/NASSIT schedules.
- Whether any of them supports NRA/NASSIT filing formats, mobile use or offline use is unverified.

## Opening
A cheap, Leone-priced SME payroll and payslip tool with NASSIT and PAYE schedules is open, though demand and willingness to pay are unproven.
