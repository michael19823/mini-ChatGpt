# Singapore: guard-tour systems (generic category)

Killed idea: C0884, Singapore security agency rostering and PWM payroll.
Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Chinese).

## Verdict: weak

Guard-tour systems are patrol and checkpoint tools. None of the listings found mention Singapore PWM payroll, so they are not a direct competitor to rostering plus PWM payroll. Rostering-plus-payroll tools from abroad exist (see below), but nothing found is Singapore-specific.

## Evidence
- Guard-tour and patrol products are listed on Singapore software directories, so the category exists locally:
  - JWM Guard Tour System: https://www.capterra.com.sg/software/1096027/JWM-Guard-Tour-System
  - Guard Patrol (Lattice Gulf): https://www.getapp.sg/software/2096587/guard-patrol and https://www.capterra.com.sg/software/1098991/Guard-Patrol
  - PatrolSync: https://www.capterra.com.sg/software/1095872/PatrolSync
- Their listed features are GPS, QR, NFC and BLE checkpoints, incident logging with photos, patrol reports and schedule management. Search snippets mention nothing on PWM wage tiers, payroll or SIA licensing.
- Broader guard-management products that include rostering and payroll are marketed abroad. Their Singapore fit is unverified:
  - SoftPatrol (https://www.capterra.com.sg/software/1103420/SoftPatrol): its snippet mentions "SIA licence compliance" and payroll. SIA may refer to the UK Security Industry Authority, so treat it as unverified for Singapore.
  - SecurityTime, RosterElf and Guardhouse are Australian-style tools (SecurityTime's Trustpilot page is .com.au). Each is described as covering rostering, GPS clock-in and payroll export. None was shown to support PWM.
- Complaints: none found for Singapore guard-tour products. The only complaints found were for TracTik Guard Tour, a different product with no Singapore link. They were forced background location permissions, crashes and photo-capture data loss (https://marlvel.ai/apps/tracktik-guard-tour/reviews). The search tool summarised these and I did not open the page, so they are unverified.
- Chinese-language search returned only MOM/MHA PWM wage news. It found no local software vendors or complaints.
- Context: PWM is a licensing condition for security agencies, and the entry-level outsourced wage rises to $3,530 by 2028 (https://www.mom.gov.sg/maintenance/employment-practices/progressive-wage-model/security-sector/). That makes wage-compliance payroll a recurring need.
- Momentum: not assessed. No development dates were found.

## Pricing
- JWM Guard Tour System: starting price US$14.99, flat rate, per a directory snippet (unverified; the billing period is not stated).
- Reflexion Guard Patrol: starting price US$50,000 per a directory snippet (unverified; the basis is not stated).
- Nothing was found on pricing for the rostering-plus-payroll tools.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of PWM wage-tier calculation, training-credit tracking or SIA/PLRD licensing for Singapore agencies (absence in snippets only, not confirmed from vendor pages).
- Patrol tools generally need NFC, QR or BLE tags and sometimes hardware.
- Foreign rostering tools are probably built around other countries' payroll rules (unverified).

## Opening
Singapore-specific rostering and PWM payroll for small and mid-size security agencies appears unserved by guard-tour vendors. Verify against local HR and payroll vendors, which were not searched.
