# Singapore: Hubble (Hubble.Build / Hubble Safety Management System, Hubble Pte Ltd)

Idea killed: C0882 (Singapore construction ePTW / WSH docs). 4 WebSearch calls used; local-language search was not useful (Singapore construction works in English).

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Hubble Safety Management System is listed as a pre-approved ePTW solution on the Singapore government GoBusiness / Productivity Solutions Grant (PSG) repository, so SMEs can get up to about 50% funding. Sources: https://www.gobusiness.gov.sg/productivity-solutions-grant/solutionrepo/201606906Z-Hubbl-Sfty-MGT-Systm-G , https://gobusiness.gov.sg/browse-all-solutions-Building-Construction/e-Permit-to-work--e-PTW-
- Features per search summaries: ePTW module with GPS-verified records, qualified worker/equipment checks, automated isolation-zone identification, conflict detection, safety inspection module, SME checklists. Also appears in a facilitybot list of Singapore ePTW tools (https://blog.facilitybot.co/blog/best-e-permit-to-work-software-insingapore/).
- Momentum: Singapore-HQ, Series B of about US$8.5M (S$11.35M) led by Tin Men Capital with Kajima Ventures, Taronga, Seeds Capital, OSK; a US$5M debt round dated 27 Jun 2024 per a data aggregator (https://www.dealstreetasia.com/?p=280355). Appears actively developed; no acquisition or shutdown found. 2025 product or customer news: not found.
- Reviews: Capterra lists Hubble.Build at 4.7/5 from 15 reviews (https://www.capterra.com.sg/software/1036373/hubblebuild); G2 page exists (https://www.g2.com/products/hubble-build/reviews). I could not read their contents.
- Complaints: none found for Hubble.Build or its safety system. Complaints surfaced in search (slow reports, expensive licenses, rude support, charger issues) belong to other products also called Hubble (Hubble Connected baby monitors, a different Hubble reporting tool) and must not be attributed to this company.

## Pricing
- Per a search summary of listing data: packages from S$9,900 (1 project, 1 year) to S$32,250 (15 projects, 1 year). Currency and exact terms unverified, as the pricing page was not opened. Hubble.Build general platform shows a free version on Capterra (unverified). PSG can fund up to about 50% for SMEs.

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified inference)
- Per-project pricing of about S$9.9k/year is heavy for a small subcontractor or tiny site, though PSG subsidy softens it.
- Broad suite (documents, workforce, quality, payments) may be more than a small buyer wants. Mobile and offline behaviour, languages for foreign workers, and WSH document formats beyond ePTW: not verified.

## Opening
Little: a funded, PSG-approved local incumbent already owns ePTW; only a cheap, narrow tool for very small contractors or a niche (e.g. multilingual worker-facing forms) leaves room.
