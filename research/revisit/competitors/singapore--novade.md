# Novade (Singapore) - competitor check

Ideas it killed: C0882 (Singapore construction ePTW / WSH docs), C0891 (Singapore construction site safety and permits-to-work).

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Market leader in Singapore ePTW. Novade's own marketing claims 70% of A1 contractors in Singapore use it and more than 1 million permits a month globally (vendor claim, unverified independently). Sources: https://www.novade.net/permit-to-work/ , https://www.novade.net/?p=233397
- It has a dedicated page on complying with the new Singapore WSH ePTW regulations, so it is built for the buyer's compliance need. Source: https://www.novade.net/comply-with-wsh-regulations-singapore-eptw
- It is listed in the Singapore government Productivity Solutions Grant (PSG) and CSG solution repository, so grant funding can offset its cost. Source: https://www.gobusiness.gov.sg/productivity-solutions-grant/solutionrepo/201325667M-Novd-SLNs-G
- Momentum: actively developed. Release notes exist for April 2025, including a sync fix. Source: https://www.novade.net/uk/release-note-16-april-2025-2/
- Funding: a snippet mentions a Series B led by SIG and Vulcan Capital. Date and amount were not shown and it is probably older, so treat as unverified. No acquisition or shutdown found.
- Complaints: none found. Six searches covered app stores, G2 and Capterra, and local-language terms. They returned no independent user reviews of Novade. The only competitor-written comparison was a FacilityBot blog listing alternatives (https://blog.facilitybot.co/blog/best-e-permit-to-work-software-insingapore/). The absence of reviews in search does not prove there are no complaints.

## Pricing
- Standard plan: S$29 per user per month in Singapore, per a search summary. Plan pricing reportedly changed on 1 Sep 2025, so the current rate is unverified. Source: https://website-dev.novade.net/pricing
- PSG-listed ePTW packages: A (1 project, 1 year) $16,100; B (2 projects) $21,400; C (unlimited projects) $29,400. Source: https://www.gobusiness.gov.sg/productivity-solutions-grant/solutionrepo/201325667M-Novd-SLNs-G
- Premium and Enterprise tiers are quote only.
- The package prices are heavy for a small subcontractor, but the per-user plan and PSG funding soften that. Overpricing for small buyers is plausible but not evidenced.

## Fit gaps (all unverified inference, no evidence found)
- Aimed at A1 and large contractors, with project-based packages. Small subcontractors and sole traders are probably underserved.
- Broad platform (quality, safety, logistics, maintenance), so it is likely heavier than a lean WSH-docs or permit-only tool.
- Other small Singapore ePTW vendors already exist (Hubble, FacilityBot, SafePermit, PermitFlow SG, iSafe, per the FacilityBot blog), so the low end is not empty.

## Opening
Only a cheap, simple ePTW / WSH-docs tool for small subcontractors, probably PSG-eligible, with no per-project packages. Without evidence of complaints and with other small vendors already present, the opening is narrow.
