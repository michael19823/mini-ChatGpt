# Slovakia: certified providers (77), "Digitálny poštár" (e-invoicing access points)

Idea killed: C0894, Slovakia e-invoicing digitalny postar for SMEs. Searches: 5 successful WebSearch calls (English and Slovak). No WebFetch, no GitHub.

## Verdict: strong

This is a crowded, state-accredited provider market, not one competitor. A new entrant also has to pass accreditation itself. The only open space is the SME end of the market, and that space is already filled with free and low-cost tiers.

## Evidence
- Slovakia makes B2B/B2G e-invoicing mandatory from 1 Jan 2027 on a Peppol-based 5-corner model. Providers ("digitálni poštári") must be Peppol-certified and accredited by the Financial Administration. Sources: [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/sk/en/insights/publications-and-events/newsletters/q-a-mandatory-e-invoicing), [eConnect](https://econnect.eu/en/docs/knowledge/regulations/europe/slovakia).
- The first official list of certified providers was published 11 March 2026. About 30 firms had expressed interest at that stage (per the search summary of [PIKON/fiscal-requirements](https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/5023-slovakia-publishes-accreditation-rules-for-e-invoicing-service-providers), not verified directly).
- A later article reports 62 accredited providers ([podnikajte.sk](https://www.podnikajte.sk/pripravovane-zmeny-v-legislative/efaktura-2027-firmy-varuju-pred-rizikami-ziadaju-odklad)). The "77" in the competitor name is the task's figure and I did not verify it.
- Named providers seen: Doklado, Qvalia (certified, [Qvalia](https://qvalia.com/qvalia-certified-for-slovakia-e-invoicing-under-the-efaktura-framework/)), and invoicing tools such as Billdu and Fakturoid, which publish Slovak eFaktúra/Peppol pages.
- The state portal lets a taxpayer pick a provider from the certified list ([TASR/teraz.sk](https://www.teraz.sk/ekonomika/svojho-digitalneho-postara-si-uz-vyb/969521-clanok.html)).
- Providers are reported to be preparing free or cheap tiers for small senders, and small web-based tools are expected to be used first ([same TASR article](https://www.teraz.sk/ekonomika/svojho-digitalneho-postara-si-uz-vyb/969521-clanok.html)).
- Every VAT non-payer, including sole traders (živnostníci), must be able to receive e-invoices, so each needs a provider ([pravnenoviny.sk](https://pravnenoviny.sk/elektronicka-faktura-povinnost-prijimat/)).
- Momentum is high: there is a voluntary pilot in 2026, the mandate starts in 2027, and cross-border scope is added from 2030.

## Complaints
No user complaints, app-store reviews or Trustpilot/Reddit items about specific providers turned up in my searches. The search results state that they contain no user-experience material. The only criticism found is general and not about providers:
- The Association of Entrepreneurs of Slovakia warns of state-system outages, setup costs, data-security risks and an overly tight deadline ([podnikajte.sk](https://www.podnikajte.sk/pripravovane-zmeny-v-legislative/efaktura-2027-firmy-varuju-pred-rizikami-ziadaju-odklad)).
- A Doklado CEO article says switching providers is hard once chosen ([touchit.sk](https://touchit.sk/vyber-postara-e-faktury-je-rozhodnutie-ktore-zmenite-tazko-ceo-doklado-radi-ako-to-urobit-spravne/897837/)). This is a lock-in point that a competitor is making itself, not a complaint from users.

## Pricing
- Doklado: free up to 7 invoices a month, and a higher tier at 9.90 EUR a month, per the search summary of [touchit.sk](https://touchit.sk/vyber-postara-e-faktury-je-rozhodnutie-ktore-zmenite-tazko-ceo-doklado-radi-ako-to-urobit-spravne/897837/). Prices not verified at the source.
- Several providers reportedly offer small senders (up to about 5 invoices a month) a free tier ([podnikajte.sk](https://www.podnikajte.sk/zakonne-povinnosti-podnikatela/ako-si-vybrat-digitalneho-postara)).
- The Financial Administration expects simple provider apps to cost no more than the EU average of about 5 to 12 EUR a month.
- The SME segment is therefore not overpriced.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed from evidence. Free tiers and web apps already cover micro-businesses.
- Possible gaps, all unverified: Slovak-language onboarding quality, accountant or multi-client workflows, integration with small accounting software, and reliability under the load at the January 2027 deadline.

## Opening
Little room at the SME end. The only plausible angle is a non-provider layer, such as an accountant-facing or onboarding/compliance helper on top of existing providers. A new provider would need to pass accreditation, enter a market of 60+ players and compete with free tiers.
