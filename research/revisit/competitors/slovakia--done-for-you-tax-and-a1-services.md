# Slovakia: Done-for-you tax and A1 services (consultant, no URL known)

Idea killed: C1393, Slovak 24-hour caregivers in Austria.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (as a single identifiable competitor); the category is fragmented and unverified

No specific firm, product or price could be identified in 5 searches (Slovak and English). The "competitor" is a generic set of accountants, tax advisors and agencies. Treat as weak-to-unknown rather than strong.

## Evidence
- No customer complaints about done-for-you tax or A1 advisors for carers were found. The complaint search returned only informational guides. This is a null result, not proof that customers are satisfied.
- Complaints found concern the placement agencies, not tax advisors. A Slovak public broadcaster article reports many of the roughly 30-40,000 Slovak carers in Austria were victims of shady agencies that did not tell them beforehand they would be self-employed. Source: https://enrsi.stvr.sk/articles/topical-issue/138143/slovak-carers-in-austria-often-victims-of-work-agencies
- Slovak-language guides say carers must prepare their own annual income statement (Einnahmen-Ausgaben-Rechnung) and file a tax return. Several results also say agencies dominate the market and carers are practically self-employed. Sources: https://ddadsstornala.sk/aktuality/opatrovatelka-rakusko-nitra/ , https://zssambra.sk/blog/opatrovanie-hranice-rakusko/ . These are content sites; their business behind the guides is unverified.
- Austrian rules create recurring paperwork: SVS social insurance, the 20% flat-rate expense option for small businesses, E106 and PD A1 forms. Source: https://ddadsstornala.sk/aktuality/ministerstvo-prace-zoznam-agentur-pre-opatrovanie-v-rakusku/ . The need is real.
- Agencies are advised to compare their services. Some may bundle admin help (unverified).
- Slovak Social Insurance Agency issues PD A1 for self-employed persons posted abroad. This is a government process, not a commercial service. Source: https://admin.socpoist.sk/node/6426 (title only; content not verified).

## Pricing
No published price for done-for-you tax, A1 or bookkeeping services aimed at carers was found. Unverified. Only the carers' own costs appeared: Austrian SVS minimum contributions of roughly 140 to 170 EUR per month (sources disagree).
