# AllWage (South Africa) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0919, South Africa bargaining council compliance payroll.
Searches used: 5 attempted (4 returned; the 5th hit a usage limit). Language: English plus one Afrikaans-term query. No Afrikaans or other local-language sources turned up.

## Verdict: strong

AllWage ships a dedicated bargaining-council module, so it directly covers the dropped idea. I found no complaints and no pricing, so I can't call it beatable on evidence.

## Evidence
- AllWage's own help docs describe a "councils" module. You enable the council for the company, enrol employees with council details (membership number, skill grade), run payroll with council deductions picked up, then generate and mark the monthly council submission as filed. Source: https://help.allwage.com/modules/councils
- Platform bundles biometric clocking, payroll, document management and WhatsApp comms. It supports UIF, SDL, provident fund, union and medical aid deductions, loans, savings and bulk payment files. Sources: https://www.biznews.com/good-hope-project/payroll-tech-company-allwage and https://help.allwage.com/modules
- Momentum: rebranded from Agrigistics to AllWage and is expanding from agriculture into construction and manufacturing. Search snippets say 200+ customers and about 40,000-50,000 workers, with plans to double. These are the company's or press claims and are unverified. Source: BizNews link above.
- Search snippets suggest a time-and-attendance page for agriculture on allwage.com (https://www.allwage.com/industries/agriculture).

## Complaints
None found. Searches for reviews, Hellopeter and Google Play returned nothing about AllWage. This is a weak signal: it is a small, young vendor and the search tool is US-only. Not checked: Hellopeter directly, App Store, Capterra and G2 listings.

## Pricing
Not found (unverified). A pricing search returned only unrelated products (Wagepoint). No AllWage prices are cited here.

## Fit gaps
- Which councils are supported is unverified; the docs say each council has its own rules and file format, so coverage is likely partial.
- Focus is wage-heavy, labour-intensive firms (agriculture, construction, manufacturing). Other bargaining-council sectors (for example clothing, motor, security, restaurants) are unverified.
- Hardware (biometric clocking) is part of the pitch, but it is not clear it is required for payroll or council filing.
- Small employers with few staff may not be its target (unverified).

## Opening
Little visible: a single product already files council submissions. Any opening would be a council or sector it doesn't support, a small-employer price point, or a standalone filing tool for firms already on other payroll software. All three need checking against its actual council list and pricing.
