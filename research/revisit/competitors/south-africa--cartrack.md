# Cartrack (South Africa) - competitor check

Ideas it killed: C0915 (telematics-style diesel logbooks), C0917 (diesel-refund telematics and pump automation).

## Verdict: strong

Cartrack is a large, established, actively marketed telematics vendor in South Africa. It already sells a GPS logbook, fuel management and SARS diesel-refund content aimed at fleets. It is weak on customer satisfaction and billing, but that does not cover the product gap for farm and mining diesel-refund buyers.

## Evidence
- Trustpilot: TrustScore 1.5/5 across 134 reviews. Complaints are missed technician appointments, faulty units, hard-to-reach and unhelpful support, and billing disputes. A few reviewers praised driver-behaviour monitoring. Source: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/cartrack.com?page=4
- Regulator action: the National Consumer Commission settled with Cartrack after about 210 complaints. Cartrack agreed to a R5 million fine, more than R5.1 million in refunds, cancellation of affected contracts without a fee, and amended T&Cs. Sources: https://www.ewn.co.za/2026/04/01/consumer-complaints-result-in-cartrack-agreeing-to-pay-r10-million-in-client-refunds-and-fines , https://thepost.co.za/news/2026-03-27-cartrack-to-refund-over-r51-million-to-167-customers-after-consumer-complaints/ . The settlement figures vary between outlets, so treat the totals as approximate.
- Warranty claims: Sowetan (Nov 2025) reported clients upset over rejected recovery-warranty claims. Source: https://www.sowetan.co.za/consumer/2025-11-17-cartrack-clients-aggrieved-at-rejected-warranty-claims
- HelloPeter: complaint threads cover billing, unauthorised debits, cancellation and warranty rejection. Source: https://www.hellopeter.com/cartrack . I did not open individual reviews beyond their search-result titles.
- Product: Cartrack publishes fuel management pages, a GPS logbook for tax claims, and a SARS diesel-refund logbook-audit guide. Sources: https://cartrack.co.za/blog/sars-diesel-refund-how-to-pass-a-logbook-audit-and-get-your-100-percent-refund , https://cartrack.co.za/blog/best-fuel-management-system-in-south-africa
- Momentum: active marketing and content in 2025-2026, so it is not stagnant. Acquisition and shutdown status were not checked (unverified).
- Searches in Afrikaans turned up only English results. I found no Reddit or Facebook-group complaints.

## Pricing
- Search results quote vehicle-tracking packages from R99 per month, with tiers at R149, R199 and R219 (source: Cartrack fuel-management blog result, via search summary; unverified in detail).
- Fuel management and pump automation are not priced publicly. They need a custom quote, probably tied to an enterprise contract (unverified).
- Hardware and contract lock-in are likely, and the NCC action concerned contract terms.

## Fit gaps
- Vehicle-centric (GPS and trip logbooks). I found no evidence of bulk diesel storage and usage logbooks in the SARS format for farms, mines or generators, or of pump/tank automation. This is unverified, because absence from search results is not proof.
- It requires installed hardware, with the installation and support complaints noted above.
- Fuel features appear aimed at fleets, not at primary producers who claim the rebate for off-road use.
- Its marketing says that over 60% of diesel-refund audits lead to partial or full rejection (a Cartrack blog claim, unverified). That signals real demand but also shows the vendor is already positioned on the topic.

## Opening
A hardware-free, low-cost tank-and-usage logbook for small primary producers, with no contract lock-in and a SARS-format export, targets the people Cartrack serves badly. The opening only works if the product avoids the vehicle-tracking space where Cartrack is strong.
