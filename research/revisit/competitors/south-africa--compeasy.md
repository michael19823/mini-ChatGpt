# CompEasy (South Africa) - competitor check for C0913 (COIDA injury-on-duty medical claims)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

CompEasy is the Compensation Fund's own government claims/ROE portal (SAP-based), not a commercial product. It is the system any COIDA claims tool has to submit into, not a rival. (Note: Capterra's "Compease" is an unrelated US salary-admin tool.)

## Evidence
- CompEasy is the Fund's electronic system, live since October 2019, SAP-based, reported cost R285m; used for online registration, claim logging and Return of Earnings. Employers, medical providers and authorised intermediaries (third parties) can log in. Source: https://www.da.org.za/2020/07/compensation-fund-should-outsource-covid-19-coida-claims-to-private-sector ; https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/32727/
- Third parties can submit on behalf of employers/providers only with documented authorisation (proxy appointment letter plus certified ID for medical providers). Source: https://www.ourpower.co.za/tools/company-registration/register-for-coida (search snippet) ; https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/33138/
- Complaints (found, mostly 2020 news): launch described as faulty; practitioners unable to submit claims or get paid; one industry survey claimed under 3% of injured workers' claims submitted and under 1% paid. Injured workers could not navigate registration. Sources: https://www.sowetanlive.co.za/business/money/2020-04-09-double-pain-for-thousands-after-injury-at-work/ ; https://www.fanews.co.za/article/people-and-companies/12/news/1163/sa-s-injured-workers-crippled-by-failed-compensation-fund-system/28381 ; https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/group-launched-to-tackle-compensation-fund-issues-2020-02-24
- No app-store, Reddit or local-language (Afrikaans/Zulu) reviews surfaced in 6 searches; recent (2025-26) complaint data unverified. Fraud flagged at the Fund in 2025: https://allafrica.com/stories/202502180014.html
- Momentum: state-run and ongoing (2026 ROE window 1 Apr - 30 Jun reported); current system health unverified.

## Pricing
Free to use (government portal); no published price. Private compliance firms (e.g. ClearComply, SERR) sell filing help, not verified here.

## Fit gaps
- Single-purpose government portal: no workflow for small employers or clinics, no claim tracking/chasing, no payment-delay management.
- Reported navigation difficulty and delayed adjudication/payment to medical providers.
- Intermediaries need authorisation, so a third-party tool must work through CompEasy rather than replace it.

## Opening
A thin layer that helps small employers and medical practices prepare, submit and chase claims through CompEasy is not blocked by it, but depends on its access rules and stability.
