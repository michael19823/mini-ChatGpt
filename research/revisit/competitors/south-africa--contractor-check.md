# Contractor Check (South Africa) - competitor check

Idea dropped because of it: C0916 (South Africa principal-side contractor compliance portals).
Searches used: 5 of 6.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (for South Africa)

Reasoning: "Contractor Check" appears to be Alcumus ContractorCheck, a Canadian-origin contractor prequalification product that Alcumus bought in 2020. It shows up on the Capterra South Africa directory, but I found no sign of a South African operation, SA-specific compliance content (COIDA letter of good standing, SHEQ file, CIDB, B-BBEE) or local customers. Confidence is moderate. The identification and the lack of local presence are not fully verified.

## Evidence
- Capterra South Africa lists "contractor check" (Alcumus ContractorCheck), rated 5.0/5 by 2 users. That is a very thin review base, and no review text was seen. https://www.capterra.co.za/software/219267/contractor-check and https://www.capterra.co.za/reviews/219267/contractor-check
- Alcumus agreed to acquire ContractorCheck on 30 June 2020, as a North American (Canadian) health and safety prequalification business. https://www.on-sitemag.com/construction/u-k-based-alcumus-adds-to-canadian-operations-with-purchase-of-contractorcheck/1003969002/ and https://www.link2build.ca/news/posts/contractorcheck-purchased-by-alcumus/
- Alcumus is moving its Canadian legacy brands under the SafeContractor brand (launched May 2024). This suggests ContractorCheck is being folded into another brand rather than built out for South Africa. A search-result summary reported this; I did not read the source page. https://marketing.verdantix.com/blog/alcumus-paints-a-fresh-target-on-the-vast-north-american-contractor-safety-market-with-the-acquisition-of-cognibox
- A search for South African contractor compliance portals (COIDA, letter of good standing, SHEQ) returned only local informational and compliance-service sites, such as ClearComply and Govchain, and no ContractorCheck pages. The SA requirements are real: tenders need a valid COIDA letter of good standing, and sites need an approved health and safety file. https://www.clearcomply.co.za/blog/eskom-tender-coida-letter-of-good-standing-2025
- A different, unrelated consumer product, ContractorCheck.com (Experian, US), also appeared in results. It is not relevant to South Africa.

## Complaints
None found. No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or local-forum complaints surfaced for ContractorCheck in South Africa. Finding none is a valid result here, not proof of satisfaction. The only reviews are 2 on Capterra, rated 5.0.

## Pricing
Capterra says pricing is based on the size of the contractor database, with per-feature pricing. No published prices or minimums were found, so ZAR pricing is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of SA-specific checks: COIDA good standing, CIDB grading, SARS tax compliance, B-BBEE, or SA construction regulations. Unverified.
- Alcumus product positioning is UK, Canada and North America; the SA listing looks like a directory entry, not a local offering.
- Likely aimed at large principals buying enterprise-style, per-feature, database-size pricing. Unverified.
- No ZAR pricing or local support evidence was found.

## Momentum
Owned by Alcumus (backed by private equity). The legacy ContractorCheck brand is being consolidated into SafeContractor in Canada. No sign of active SA development. Unverified beyond the search summaries.

## Opening
A South Africa-native principal-side portal that automates COIDA, SHEQ file and CIDB checks has no visible direct rival from this product. The real competitors are likely local firms such as ClearComply (unverified as a portal) and manual SHEQ-file processes.
