# Gilbarco (Gilbarco AFS) - South Africa

Ideas it killed: C0915 (telematics-style diesel logbooks), C0917 (diesel-refund telematics and pump automation).
Searches used: 5 of 6.

## Verdict: strong for mines and large fleets, beatable for small and mid farms

## Evidence
- Gilbarco Veeder-Root bought Automated Fuel Systems (AFS, Johannesburg) in 2013. It now trades as Gilbarco AFS and has regional offices in South Africa. Sources: https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/gilbarco-afs-sign-purchase-agreement-2013-04-17 , https://gilbarco.com/eu/blog/gilbarco-veeder-root-finalises-purchase-afs-south-africa
- It sells fuel management with RFID vehicle identification at the pump, automatic tank gauging, and the eNhanceOne controller. Its customers are mines, fleets, rail, transport and government. Source: https://www.miningweekly.com/article/gilbarco-veeder-root-champions-fuel-efficiency-clean-fuels-and-diesel-rebate-solutions-in-mining-2024-03-25
- It claims its telematics data produces SARS-compliant diesel rebate reports for any tax period. It has a dedicated page, "SARS diesel rebates simplified": https://mea.gilbarco.com/sars-diesel-rebates-simplified
- It is still active. A 2026 Electra Mining press release pitches "diesel rebate compliance starts with fuel visibility": https://www.electramining.co.za/exhibitor-press-releases/diesel-rebate-compliance-starts-with-fuel-visibility-why-intelligent-fuel-management-matters-more-than-ever
- Market context: SARS moved diesel rebate claims to a standalone digital platform with automated validation. The rebate rises to 100% from 1 April 2026. Many companies still use manual logbooks and spreadsheets, which is an audit risk. Sources: https://www.energize.co.za/article/opinion-diesel-rebate-overhaul-shifts-compliance-from-paperwork-to-data , https://www.farmersweekly.co.za/agri-news/south-africa/diesel-refunds-why-farmers-struggle-to-claim-whats-rightfully-theirs
- Other players exist, such as Cartrack, which has a diesel-refund logbook blog post: https://cartrack.co.za/blog/sars-diesel-refund-how-to-pass-a-logbook-audit-and-get-your-100-percent-refund

## Complaints
None found. Searches in English and Afrikaans turned up no customer reviews, app-store reviews, forum threads or outage news for Gilbarco AFS in South Africa. Only Glassdoor employee ratings appeared, and they are not relevant. This is not evidence that customers are happy. Review coverage for this niche B2B product is simply thin.

## Pricing
Not published. Searches found no price list or minimums. Unverified: it is quote-based and hardware-led (controllers, tank gauges, RFID tags, installation).

## Fit gaps (inferred from positioning, not from reviews)
- Its marketing targets mining, large fleets and government. Small and mid farms and forestry operators are not its stated focus.
- The solution needs hardware at the tank or pump (controller, RFID, gauges), so it costs more and takes longer to deploy than a software-only logbook.
- It does not appear to offer a low-cost, phone-first, offline-capable logbook. Unverified, because there was no product detail to check.
- No evidence was found of support for the new SARS standalone platform filing flow. Unverified.

## Momentum
Active. It is part of Vontier, is marketing diesel rebate features in 2024 to 2026, and exhibits at Electra Mining. It is not stagnant.

## Opening
A software-only, phone-based diesel logbook and rebate-claim tool for small and mid farms and contractors, which Gilbarco's hardware-first, enterprise-priced offer leaves underserved. The opening is weaker for mines and large fleets.
