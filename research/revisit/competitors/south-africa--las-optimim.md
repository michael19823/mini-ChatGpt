# LAS OptiMIM (South Africa)

Dropped idea: C0915, South Africa telematics-style diesel logbooks.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

OptiMIM is enterprise fuel-dispensing management software from Liquid Automation Systems (LAS). It is sold to mines, refineries, terminals and large fleets. It is not a SARS diesel-rebate logbook for small farmers or operators.

## Evidence
- LAS is a Johannesburg-based systems integrator for measuring and controlling fuel and high-value liquids. It has operated since 1992 and works with mining, rail, aviation, fleet and petrochemical customers. It bundles Hectronic, Satam and Sensile hardware with Opti-MIM software. Sources: https://www.plantautomation-technology.com/suppliers/liquid-automation-systems-pty-ltd ; https://www.newswire.com/opti-mim-integrated-fuel-and-lubricated/191872
- A search for OptiMIM as a diesel logbook, telematics, review or complaints product returned nothing relevant. No reviews were found on Capterra, G2 or any other site in the results.
- No complaints found. Searches were run in English only. South African English covers the local market, so no separate local-language search was made.
- The real competitors in the SARS diesel-logbook space (from the search results, not deeply investigated):
  - Cartrack GPS logbook: https://cartrack.co.za/blog/sars-diesel-refund-how-to-pass-a-logbook-audit-and-get-your-100-percent-refund
  - iOCO Diesel Rebate Solution: https://ioco.tech/?p=74069
- Market context: SARS announced a modernised diesel refund platform with a simplified logbook in December 2025. The rebate rises to 100% from 1 April 2026. Farmers struggle with logbook compliance. Sources: https://www.farmersweekly.co.za/agri-news/south-africa/diesel-refunds-why-farmers-struggle-to-claim-whats-rightfully-theirs
- Momentum: LAS appears established and active. The newswire and acukwik items date from around 2009 to 2012, so recent momentum is unverified.

## Pricing
Not published. It is quote-based, enterprise and hardware-integrated (unverified). It is not aimed at small buyers.

## Fit gaps
- Targets large sites with fixed dispensing infrastructure.
- Requires hardware (Hectronic, Satam and Sensile controllers, tank gauging).
- No evidence that it produces SARS diesel-refund logbooks.
- No evidence of a mobile or offline app for small operators (unverified).

## Opening
OptiMIM leaves the small-farm and small-operator SARS diesel-logbook segment open. The real rivals to check are Cartrack and iOCO.
