# LinkSafe (South Africa)

Dropped ideas: C0916, C0918 (South Africa principal-side contractor compliance portals).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

LinkSafe is an Australian product, now owned by Civica. The searches found no evidence of a South African entity, local customers, a ZAR price or SA-specific compliance content. Its only SA footprint is a listing on Capterra South Africa (https://www.capterra.co.za/software/207817/linksafe), and a Capterra listing is not a local presence. The searches covered 4 queries in English and Afrikaans. It could still be sold to SA buyers, but this is unverified.

## Evidence
- Capterra lists LinkSafe as contractor pre-qualification, online/site inductions, permits, visitor management, sign-in/out and incident reporting: https://www.capterra.com/p/207817/LinkSafe/
- Civica hosts the product pages and PDFs under its Australian (au-docs) sections: https://www.civica.com/en-au/product-pages/linksafe-contractor-management-software/
- All case studies and industry pages found are on linksafe.com.au and are Australian (e.g. Simonds, Cotton On, Lactalis): https://linksafe.com.au/case-studies/simonds-case-study
- Search results say Civica has acquired LinkSafe (no date seen): https://linksafe.com.au/industries/services/
- Complaints: none found. Capterra shows 5.0/5 from only 2 reviews, which is too few to mean anything: https://www.capterra.ca/reviews/207817/linksafe
- No app-store, Reddit, forum or news results about SA use, outages or failures. Nothing found in Afrikaans.

## Pricing
Not published. Capterra says to contact the vendor, with no free trial. No ZAR pricing was found.

## Fit gaps (inferred from absence; unverified)
- No evidence of SA legal and compliance content (e.g. OHS Act, mine health and safety, B-BBEE, COIDA/letter of good standing, CIPC).
- No SA support presence or local reseller found.
- Likely oriented to AU/NZ enterprise and government buyers (local government, utilities, construction, health).

## Opening
A principal-side SA contractor compliance portal faces no entrenched LinkSafe presence. The competition would be local vendors, which this check did not cover.
