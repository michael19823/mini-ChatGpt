# mai Contractor Portal (south-africa)

Dropped idea: C0916, South Africa principal-side contractor compliance portals.
Searches used: 4 of 6 (English only; no local-language results surfaced for this product).

## Verdict: weak

It is a generic, Ireland/UK-oriented product with no verified South African footprint. Its SA presence amounts to directory listings on Capterra ZA and similar sites. Nothing found ties it to SA rules (CIDB, SARS tax compliance, DEL/COIDA, mining contractor onboarding).

## Evidence
- Listed on Capterra South Africa (https://www.capterra.co.za/software/191465/mai-contractor-portal), rated 4.3/5 by 18 users. The same listing appears on the UK, AU, NZ, IL and IN Capterra sites and on GetApp and Software Advice, so it looks like a global syndicated listing, not an SA-specific one.
- A search summary said it is made by EHASoft, founded 2002 (from search-result text; unverified beyond that). The earlier summary says it is "trusted by Fortune 500 companies" (vendor marketing claim, unverified).
- Features listed: compliance management, contract/licence management, contractor database, document management, job and project tracking, qualification tracking, subcontractor management, workflows, automated alerts for document renewals and expiries.
- Target industries listed: pharma, biopharma, food, medical devices, electronics, oil and gas, aviation, retail, life sciences. Mining and construction, the SA core, are not prominent.
- Complaints: none found. The search snippets showed only positive reviews ("easy to use, easily accessible and user friendly"; good support contact). I did not see the full review text, so negative reviews may exist on the Capterra page. No Reddit, Trustpilot, app-store, forum or news complaints surfaced.
- Momentum: unknown. The Capterra title carries "2026", so the listing is current, but no release, acquisition or shutdown news was found.

## Pricing
Capterra lists a starting price of EUR 340 per user per year, with a free trial and cloud SaaS delivery. Per-user pricing in euros is likely steep for an SA SME principal (roughly R6,000+ per user per year at typical exchange rates, my own estimate and unverified). Whether there are minimums or tiers is unknown.

## Fit gaps (mostly inferred, unverified)
- No evidence of SA-specific checks: CIDB grading, SARS tax clearance, B-BBEE certificates, COIDA letters of good standing, or Mine Health and Safety Act induction.
- No evidence of local-language support, local payment or invoicing, or SA-based support.
- Positioned at EHS, pharma and enterprise buyers, not small SA principals.
- Offline or mobile use for site gate access: not mentioned.

## Opening
A cheaper, SA-rule-aware contractor compliance portal (CIDB, SARS, COIDA, mining induction) for small and mid-size principals is not covered by this foreign per-user product.
