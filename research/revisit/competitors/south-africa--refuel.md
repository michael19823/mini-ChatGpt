# Refuel (South Africa)

Idea killed: C0915, South Africa telematics-style diesel logbooks. Competitor URL: none known. Type: commercial.
Searches used: 6 of 6 (English and Afrikaans). Only search snippets were read, not the full pages.

## Verdict
beatable (low confidence). Refuel is a real, active player, but the evidence shows an enterprise and Deloitte-channel offer, with no published pricing, no small-farmer product and no review data.

## Evidence
- Refuel is a South African app and service that delivers fuel by truck to a customer's location, in Johannesburg and Pretoria. It charges the pump price per litre plus a R20 service fee per fill-up, and has extended to small business, fleet and generator owners. Sources: [Briefly](https://briefly.co.za/89919-mzansi-buy-petrol-delivered-doorsteps.html), [Engineering News, 2020](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/hate-stopping-for-petrol-theres-a-new-app-for-that-2020-11-04).
- Momentum: Deloitte Africa and Refuel have partnered on SARS diesel rebate claims, as a "tech plus tax" offer ("Refuel Bridge") for mining, agriculture, forestry, fisheries and other fuel-intensive industries. It captures fuel-use data in the format the tax team needs. Sources: [Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/deloitte-africa-refuel-bridge-diesel-tracking-and-tax-compliance-078245a), [IT News Africa, Aug 2026](https://itnewsafrica.com/2026/08/deloitte-africa-and-refuel-partner-to-simplify-sars-diesel-rebate-claims/), [Deloitte placemat PDF](https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-zone1/za/en/docs/alliances/2026/za-diesel-rebates-placemat.pdf). The Deloitte route points at larger firms. That is my inference and is unverified.
- Market context: Grain SA, SARS and private technology providers are piloting a digital diesel logbook (pilot planned Oct-Dec 2025, proposal to SARS by March 2026). SARS is moving rebates to a standalone digital platform with automated validation. Sources: [Grain SA](https://www.grainsa.co.za/news-headlines/latest-news/lastest-developents-regarding-the-diesel-rebate-system), [Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/sars-diesel-rebate-system-overhaul-paves-way-for-automated-fuel-monitoring-847223a). Refuel's role in that pilot is unverified.
- Other vendors are also in this space, for example Cartrack with a [logbook audit guide](https://cartrack.co.za/blog/sars-diesel-refund-how-to-pass-a-logbook-audit-and-get-your-100-percent-refund).
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Hellopeter or Trustpilot reviews appeared in any search. This is absence of evidence, not proof of satisfaction. One news piece called the delivery app "a solution in search of a problem" (from the search summary; the full article was not read, so this is unverified).

## Pricing
The only published figure is the R20 per fill-up delivery fee on top of the pump price (delivery product, 2020 to 2023 articles). Pricing for the diesel-tracking product: unverified and not found.

## Fit gaps
- Appears aimed at mining, agriculture, forestry and fisheries firms through a Deloitte partnership, so likely larger firms (inference, unverified).
- Delivery coverage is limited to Johannesburg and Pretoria (older sources). Whether the tracking product needs hardware is unverified.
- No evidence of a small-farmer, offline or Afrikaans offer. Unverified.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve SARS-format diesel logbook for small farmers could fit below Refuel and Deloitte's enterprise offer, provided it matches the SARS draft logbook and the Grain SA digital pilot.
