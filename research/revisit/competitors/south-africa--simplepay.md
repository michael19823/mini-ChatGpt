# SimplePay (South Africa) - competitor check for C0919 (bargaining council compliance payroll)

## Verdict: strong

SimplePay is an established, South Africa-specific online payroll product that already has a documented bargaining council setup feature. 4 WebSearch calls used (English plus one Afrikaans-mixed query).

## Evidence
- SimplePay has a help page "Payroll Setup > Company Setup > Bargaining Councils": https://simplepay.co.za/help/payroll-setup/company-setup/bargaining-councils . Search summaries say it lets users set up payroll per the council agreement. I could not open the page (no WebFetch), so the depth of support (council-specific rates, levies, returns filing) is unverified.
- Its site lists dedicated sections for specific bargaining councils (per search summary; specific councils not verified). Tag page: https://www.simplepay.co.za/blog/tag/compliant
- Positioned as fully online and built for South Africa, with claimed automatic legislation updates (search summary of simplepay.co.za; unverified).
- Complaints: none found for SA. Reviews found (Capterra 4.0 from 8 reviews, https://www.capterra.com/p/202444/SimplePay/reviews/ ; Trustpilot https://ch.trustpilot.com/review/simplepay.ie) appear to be for the Irish product (simplepay.ie) or are unclear. Capterra product 202444 is also listed on capterra.co.za, so the SA link is unverified. Themes there: price "a little high" for payroll administrators with many businesses, "light for complex payroll needs", limited customisation, one Trustpilot review about leave-approval permissions and staff confidentiality. Treat as weak evidence for SA.
- No HelloPeter, Reddit or Afrikaans-language complaints surfaced. TrustRadius has a SimplePay reviews page (https://trustradius.com/products/simplepay/reviews) which I did not read.
- Momentum: no acquisition, shutdown or stagnation news found; the help pages and blog look maintained (unverified beyond search listings).

## Pricing
- Per active employee per month, starting from R89.50, sliding scale with a small monthly base fee per company (via Capterra/GetApp search summaries; the exact current SA tariff is unverified: check simplepay.co.za). A small firm with e.g. 10 staff would pay roughly R900+ per month at the entry rate, which is not clearly overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps
- Not verified whether it covers council-specific levies, returns, or agreement-based minimum wage tables and rate updates for each council; the help page suggests configuration is largely manual setup by the user.
- Possible gap for payroll administrators or bureaus managing many small businesses (price complaint from a review of unclear region).
- Possibly light for complex payrolls (same caveat).
- No evidence of offline, hardware or language gaps.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: deep, automated council-specific compliance (rates, levy returns, inspection-ready records) if SimplePay's support turns out to be manual setup, but this needs verification before reviving C0919.
