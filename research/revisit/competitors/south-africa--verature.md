# Verature (South Africa) - competitor check

Killed ideas: C0916, C0918 (dup): South Africa principal-side contractor compliance portals.
Searches used: 5 of 6 (English, plus one Afrikaans query that returned nothing useful).

## Verdict: weak (in South Africa)

Verature is a real, maintained product, but it is a UK product with no verified South African
localisation or customer base. Its feature set is generic principal-side contractor management.

## Evidence
- Verature is a contractor management platform from Target Information Systems Ltd, operated by Heresafe Limited
  (Barnsley, South Yorkshire, incorporated 30 March 2010). Source: https://open.endole.co.uk/insight/brand/309383-verature
- Features: onboarding/assurance, project bookings, RAMS approvals, contractor portal, inductions, mobile check-in
  and badges, fire register, digital permits. Source: https://www.capterra.co.za/software/1033719/verature
- "RAMS" and the Digital Marketplace (G-Cloud) listing are UK terms and channels. Source:
  https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/632904087414671
- Rated 4.4/5 by 45 users on Capterra/GetApp. The same listing appears on regional sites (capterra.co.za,
  getapp.za.com), which looks like a regional mirror of one global listing. Whether any of the 45 reviews
  come from South African users is unverified.
- No SA-specific content found: nothing on CIDB, SARS tax clearance, COIDA letters of good standing,
  B-BBEE certificates or OHS Act Section 37(2) agreements. Absence in search results, not proof of absence.
- No South African customer, partner or local office was found. Unverified.
- Momentum: no acquisition, shutdown or news found. The Capterra listing is titled "2026", suggesting it is still
  active. Not confirmed beyond that.

## Complaints
Only two, from the review aggregator snippet (Capterra/Software Advice, UK-centred); no SA-specific complaints:
- Cannot add multiple companies or individuals to one booking, which breaks cases where a sub-contractor on one
  job is the main contractor on another.
- Cost is "quite expensive", especially with add-ons.
No complaints were found on Reddit, Facebook groups, local forums or in news. Source for both:
https://www.softwareadvice.com.au/software/363395/verature

## Pricing
Listed from GBP 549.00 per month (Capterra/Software Advice listing, 2026). Add-ons cost extra per user reviews.
At the current exchange rate (not checked) that is several thousand rand a month. Likely too high for small SA
principals and SME contractors. No rand pricing was found.

## Fit gaps
- No verified SA compliance document types or SA regulatory workflows (see above).
- No local-language support found (the Afrikaans and other-language search returned nothing).
- Multi-company or sub-contractor chains on one booking are a known limitation.
- Priced in GBP and aimed at multi-site corporates (hospitals, facilities, estates), not SMEs.
- Offline use and mobile-data-light operation: unverified either way.

## Opening
A rand-priced, SA-compliance-native (CIDB, SARS, COIDA, B-BBEE, Section 37(2)) contractor portal for mid-size
principals is not contested by Verature; the idea should not have been dropped on its account.
