# South Korea: CertiCos (CDRI, Cosmetic Data Research Institute)

Killed idea: C0929 South Korea K-beauty multi-market regulatory registration. Searches used: 5 of 6 (Korean and English).

## Verdict: strong

CertiCos is a real, purpose-built, AI-automated multi-market cosmetics registration platform aimed at exactly this buyer, and I found no complaints or fit gaps that a newcomer could exploit.

## Evidence
- CertiCos is described as an AI-based overseas cosmetics certification automation platform from CDRI for Korean brands. Source: https://www.cosmorning.com/mobile/article.html?no=42854
- Claimed coverage: 35 countries (EU, China, Japan, US and others) from a single set of documents. Same source.
- Claimed speed: EU CPNP in an average of about 10 days from contract, against 3-6 months normally. One-time registration auto-generates the CPSR, and a standardised PIF goes to the EU Responsible Person. Same source. These are the vendor's claims as reported by trade press, unverified independently.
- It bundles 13 extra services: ingredient restriction and ban screening, label and claim text screening, clinical-test consulting and notarisation. Same source.
- Other coverage: https://core.asiae.co.kr/article/2022110213332206828 (2022) and cosmorning tag page https://www.cosmorning.com/news/tag_list_all.html?tag=CDRI
- Momentum: a trade-press article appeared in 2026 (cosinkorea, https://www.cosinkorea.com/news/article.html?no=46368, dated 2026.08.27 in the result header). I could not read it, so its content is unverified. CDRI has job postings (rememberapp) and a Jobplanet page, which suggests an operating company. Funding, acquisition or shutdown news: none found.
- Complaints: none found. Searches for reviews and drawbacks in Korean returned only promotional or news coverage. No app-store, Reddit, forum or Trustpilot reviews were found. Absence of complaints probably reflects a small B2B product with little public review footprint, not proof of satisfaction.

## Pricing
Not published in anything I found. Unverified. The 2026 sources say service is contract-based, so it is likely quote-led.

## Fit gaps
- No concrete gap verified. The searches did not show whether it serves very small or single-SKU brands.
- Unverified possibilities: price minimums that exclude micro-brands, thin coverage and support for non-Korean brands, and depth on non-EU markets such as the China NMPA animal-testing and filing regime. Treat all of these as hypotheses.
- It is Korea-centric, so non-Korean K-beauty-adjacent sellers and non-Korean-language users may be underserved. Unverified.

## Opening
Little visible. The only plausible angle is a cheap self-serve tier for micro-brands, or markets and languages CertiCos does not cover, and that needs pricing evidence first.
