# Spain: billing and accounting software (generic; killed C0949 Spain VeriFactu)

## Verdict: strong

A crowded, mature market with many VeriFactu-ready vendors, a free AEAT tool, and entry prices of about 0 to 10 EUR/month. A new generic VeriFactu product would be hard to differentiate.

## Evidence
- Searches: 4 of 6 used. No individual review pages (Trustpilot, app stores, Reddit) were surfaced.
- Complaints: none found. The Holded Trustpilot search returned unrelated pages. This is not evidence that none exist.
- VeriFactu deadlines were postponed one year: 1 Jan 2027 for corporate income tax payers, 1 Jul 2027 for everyone else, including self-employed and SMEs. The vendor deadline of 29 Jul 2025 stayed. Sources: https://www.fonoa.com/resources/blog/spain-delays-verifactu-compliance-deadlines-to-2027 ; https://www.rsm.global/spain/sites/default/files/media/VeriFactu%20postponed%20until%202027_0.pdf
- Awareness is low. One article cites 26.52% of businesses knowing the details and 39.13% not knowing it exists. It is a secondary source, so treat it as unverified. https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/nacional/2025/12/02/2523773/verifactu-sistema-facturacion-digital-pymes-autonomos-aplaza-ano.html
- Existing vendors named in comparison articles: Quipu, Billin, Holded, Sage, Contasimple, Factorial. Holded claims more than 900,000 users (its own claim). https://www.holded.com/es/facturacion-online
- The AEAT publishes its own free invoicing app, which sets a floor price of zero (per comparison sites). https://guiafiscal.es/comparativas/mejor-programa-facturacion-autonomos/

## Pricing (from comparison sites, unverified against vendor pages)
- Quipu: 17 / 30 / 59 EUR per month, with a 15-day free trial.
- Billin: about 6.6 EUR per month.
- Sage: from about 39 EUR per month.
- AEAT app and Contasimple free tier: 0 EUR.
- Other entry plans: about 9.95 to 10 EUR per month.
- Source: https://guiafiscal.es/comparativas/quipu-vs-billin/

## Fit gaps
- Nothing concrete found. The search was not deep enough to identify gaps in offline use, multi-entity support, or language coverage.
- Possible gaps to test (unverified): Quipu is described as stronger on accounting and Hacienda tax forms, Billin as stronger on e-invoicing. Neither appears to be missing anything essential for the mainstream buyer.

## Opening
Little room for a generic VeriFactu product. The only plausible opening is a narrow vertical niche or legacy-software bolt-on, which would need separate validation.
