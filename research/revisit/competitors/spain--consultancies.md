# Spain: consultancies (food-waste law Ley 1/2025, idea C0948)

## Verdict: beatable

Generic consultancies and law firms exist, but the searches found them selling advice, not a product. Free
or cheap tools from industry bodies and a small startup are the stronger competition (see below).
Searches used: 5 of 6. The 6th hit a usage limit and returned nothing.

## Evidence
- Law 1/2025 general obligations apply from 3 April 2026. Operators must have a prevention plan, and
  fines for lacking one reach EUR 60,000 (serious) and EUR 500,000 (very serious, repeat).
  Sources: https://rtvc.es/la-ley-contra-el-desperdicio-incluye-multas-de-hasta-500-000-euros/ ,
  https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/spains-new-food-waste-law-key-obligations-for-businesses-under-new-law-12025
- Law firms and advisers publish alerts and guides: EY, Andersen, Garrigues, Fieldfisher, Baker Tilly,
  TUV SUD, Intertek (a training course). They are informational; no pricing was found.
  Sources: https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/es-es/technical/alertas-fiscal-legal/ey_alerta-informativa_desperdicio_alimentario.pdf ,
  https://es.andersen.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Andersen-Ley-de-desperdicio-alimentario_alerta.pdf ,
  https://www.intertek.es/contentassets/9d467e0585554ae099fd613fbe715400/curso-desperdicio-alimentario.pdf
- Non-consultancy substitutes, which are the real threat:
  - NODA (Ecoavantis and Honu Projects) automates plan creation, claimed to take under 2 hours:
    https://www.corresponsables.com/actualidad/nace-noda-la-primera-solucion-digital-que-automatiza-el-cumplimiento-de-la-ley-1-2025/
  - Hostelería de España's free-looking portal hosteleriacircular.es, with a plan template and donation
    agreement models (price unverified): https://forbes.es/economia/896701/hosteleria-de-espana-crea-una-herramienta-para-facilitar-el-cumplimiento-de-la-ley-de-desperdicio-alimentario/
  - PDApp digital tool (an operational group): https://www.agronewscastillayleon.com/gopdapp-herramienta-digital
  - FIAB sector guides on prevention plans: https://fiab.es/es/archivos/documentos/Guia_planes_prevenci%C3%B3n_desperdicio_Alimentario_iab.pdf
  - Over 100 agrifood companies had already produced plans (Bolsamania/Europa Press, date unverified).
- Complaints: none found. No reviews of consultancies for this service turned up (no Trustpilot, Reddit or
  forum hits). This is a valid "no complaints" result, not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing
No consultancy prices found. Unverified. NODA and Hostelería de España pricing was not seen in the
search snippets (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Big firms publish alerts, not products, and are probably priced for large operators (inference, unverified).
- The obligation covers every food-chain operator, but a shop under 1,300 m2 is exempt from the plan.
  Small buyers therefore mostly rely on free templates.
- Recurring needs (annual measurement and reporting, donation-agreement tracking) look underserved by
  one-off advice, but this was not confirmed.

## Momentum
High regulatory activity: the law is in force and publishing from law firms is heavy. Specialist
tools (NODA) launched recently.

## Opening
Little room against the free sector-association tools and NODA, though a product for ongoing measurement
and donation tracking could still beat one-off consultancy advice.
