# CYPE (Spain) - competitor check for C0941 (Spain electrical CIE multi-region filing)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

CYPE (CYPE Ingenieros) is a design/calculation/BIM software vendor. Searches found no CYPE product that files CIE certificates with the autonomous-community registries. It is adjacent, not head-to-head. Only 3 searches were run, so this is a moderate-confidence verdict.

## Evidence
- CYPE develops CYPELEC (design and calculation of electrical installations) and, with Schneider Electric, a protection-device selection tool for Open BIM projects. These are engineering design tools, not filing tools. Sources: search results on learning.cype.com and info.cype.com (CYPE's own pages, via search snippets; not opened).
- Searches for "CYPE CIE telematic filing across autonomous communities" returned no CYPE page about it. The results were Certicalia (a marketplace for CIE certificates), Junta de Andalucia's TECI user manual (https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/sites/default/files/inline-files/2025/06/Manual_Usuario_TECI.pdf) and generic explainers. This shows filing is done through each region's own portal (e.g. Andalucia's TECI) with the installer's digital certificate.
- Complaints: none found. A Spanish-language search for opinions, complaints and pricing returned only CYPE documentation and training pages, plus a university/training listing. No reviews or complaints were found, and none are claimed.
- Momentum: CYPE is active (courses, Open BIM integrations, an AI-powered positioning on its site as shown in search summaries). Recent development is unverified beyond that.

## Pricing
Not found. Search results mentioned a CYPE price list and online shop but gave no figures. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of multi-region CIE filing or submission to regional registries.
- Aimed at engineers and architects designing projects, not installers who issue CIEs.
- Closest real alternatives appear to be Certicalia-style marketplaces and regional portals (not assessed here).

## Opening
CIE multi-region filing is not covered by CYPE as far as the searches show, so the idea is not blocked by it. Check Certicalia and installer-focused tools instead.
