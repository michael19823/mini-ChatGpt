# Elecsum (Spain) - competitor check for C0937 (Spain solar self-consumption legalisation gestor)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Elecsum appears to be a Catalan digital management and billing platform for energy communities and collective self-consumption. It is not a legalisation gestor for individual installations. (5 of 6 searches used; no official site found, so all of this rests on secondary pages.)

## Evidence
- Elecsum is described as a Catalan solar-energy company chosen by Energia del Prat (an energy community, up to 1.2 MW PV, about 900 homes and businesses) to help commercialise local energy. Sources: https://www.sostenible.cat/print/noticia/energia-del-prat-comptara-amb-el-suport-delecsum-en-la-comercialitzacio-denergia and https://www.elprat.cat/sites/default/files/ndp_energia_del_prat_compta_amb_el_suport_delecsum.pdf
- Services listed in those pages: a branded member web portal, a member app (savings, consumption/self-consumption, invoices, environmental data), management of adhesion contracts and energy-sharing agreements, distribution of available power among participants, and invoicing on behalf of the promoter.
- None of the pages found mention permit paperwork, technical memoria/projects, certificates or regional registration, which is what the dropped idea covers.
- Complaints: none found. Searches for "Elecsum opiniones reclamaciones" returned only unrelated companies (Elecrenov, Elecplanete, etc.). No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews for Elecsum were located. Absence of reviews suggests a small B2B player (unverified).
- Momentum: unverified. The only dated-looking material is the Energia del Prat announcement; no date was confirmed from the snippets.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified. It appears to be sold to community promoters (B2B), not to individual installation owners.

## Fit gaps (relative to C0937)
- Serves energy-community promoters and collective schemes, not individual homeowners or installers needing legalisation.
- No evidence of any legalisation, paperwork or administration filing service.
- Catalonia-centred footprint (Energia del Prat, Electra Caldense), unverified beyond that.

## Opening
Elecsum does not occupy the legalisation-gestor niche; the idea is not blocked by it (it could at most become a partner or adjacent player for collective schemes).
