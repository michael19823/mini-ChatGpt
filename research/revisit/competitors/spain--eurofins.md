# Eurofins (EcoGestor Waste) - Spain
Killed idea: C0934 Spain waste memoria compliance router

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Eurofins owns EcoGestor Waste, a 100% online waste software for producers, managers, transporters, agents and dealers. It is integrated with the main regional platforms, eSIR and DIWASS. Sources: https://www.eurofins.com/es-es/servicios-medioambientales/ecogestor/residuos/ , https://www.ecogestor.com/?p=4948
- Features listed: treatment contracts, transfer notifications, identification documents, DeCA electronic control document, automatic waste labels, and a "memoria anual" feature. Sources: https://www.eurofins-environment.es/?p=31219 , https://www.eurofins.com/es-es/servicios-medioambientales/ecogestor/residuos/
- It comes with consultants for onboarding, training and support, and can request characterisation analyses from Eurofins labs. Source: https://www.eurofins.com/es-es/servicios-medioambientales/ecogestor/residuos/
- Momentum: actively marketed in Spanish and English, free updates for subscribers, and a training course is offered. Source: https://formacion.eurofins-environment.es/img/cms/PROGRAMAS/CURSOS MANEJO/Programa curso manejo EcoGestor Residuos.pdf . The date of the latest release is unverified.
- Complaints: none found for EcoGestor Waste. Capterra lists only the EcoGestor GMAO (maintenance) product, with 0 reviews (https://www.capterra.in/software/211655/ecogestor-gmao). Trustpilot results (TrustScore 2.5) concern Eurofins clinical and testing sites, not the waste software, so they are not relevant. No app-store, Reddit or forum complaints were found within the search budget.

## Pricing
- No published license price found (unverified). A pages snippet mentions a one-time payment, no licensing fees, or SaaS options (https://www.softwaredoit.es/ecogestor/ecogestor.html), but the amounts are unverified. The user training course costs EUR 250 (training program PDF above).

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified inference)
- Probably sold through consultative, quote-based sales to industrial producers and managers. A micro-SME may find it heavy or costly (unverified).
- Tied to the Eurofins ecosystem and its labs.
- Coverage of every regional register format is unverified. It lists eSIR and DIWASS, but not all regions.
- Mobile and offline use is unverified.

## Opening
Thin: only a cheap, self-serve router for tiny producers in regions or formats EcoGestor does not cover, and the memoria feature already exists there.
