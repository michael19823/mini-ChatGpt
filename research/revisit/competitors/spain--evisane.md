# EviSane (Spain) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0938 (Spain pest control / legionella records)
Searches used: 4 of 6 (Spanish and English). WebFetch is blocked, so the product and vendor site were not checked directly.

## Verdict: strong

It is a purpose-built, cheap, well-rated cloud product for Spanish pest control (DDD) and legionella companies. It is the exact buyer of the dropped idea, and no meaningful complaints were found.

## Evidence
- Vendor and product: EviSane is cloud software from Evirom for pest control and environmental health companies. It covers diagnosis, certificates, work orders, contracts, appointments, billing, food safety and lab samples, and it has interactive geolocated maps with technician geotracking. Pest and legionella control are both covered. Sources: https://www.capterra.es/software/179537/evisane, https://www.appvizer.es/servicios/control-plagas/evisane, https://www.softwaredoit.es/evirom-evisane/evirom-evisane.html
- Ratings: 4.9/5 from 35 users on Capterra, with sub-scores of ease of use 4.8, support 4.9, value 4.7 and features 4.7 (aggregator summary). Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/179537/EviSane/reviews/
- Complaints: the only criticism found was a significant initial learning curve because the system is complex (aggregator summary, not verified against the individual reviews). No Reddit, forum, app-store or news complaints (outages, failed filings) surfaced. Little other evidence was found.
- Momentum: listed on Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice, Appvizer and GoodFirms with 2026 review pages. No news of acquisition, shutdown or product launches turned up, so the product looks live but its release cadence is unverified.
- Markets: Spain, Mexico, Colombia, Chile and Argentina; Spanish and English; phone and email support in Spanish business hours.

## Pricing
- From EUR 29.90 per month, with a free trial (Capterra.es and aggregator summaries). Higher tiers and the user or technician limits were not found (unverified). This is not overpriced for small firms.

## Fit gaps
- None verified. Possible gaps, all unverified:
  - a steep learning curve for very small operators;
  - no support for the legionella record-keeping that a client or in-house facility manager keeps (the product targets service companies, not building owners);
  - the depth of regional (autonomous community) report formats.

## Opening
Little room against EviSane for pest and legionella service companies. Any opening is in a different buyer (building owners and facility managers keeping their own legionella logs) or in a simpler, lighter tool for very small operators, and both would need checking.
