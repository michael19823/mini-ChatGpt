# Spain: food banks (FESBAL and member bancos de alimentos)

Killed idea: C0948, Spain food-waste law plans (Ley 1/2025).

## Verdict
not-a-real-competitor

Food banks are charities that receive surplus food. They are not a software or compliance vendor, so they are a donation recipient and channel partner, not a rival. The real competitor for this idea is Too Good To Go "Donations+" (see below).

## Evidence
- Only 3 of the 6 allowed searches were attempted: the first (Spanish, on Ley 1/2025 and food banks) failed with a usage-limit error, and I stopped after the other two. Coverage is therefore thin.
- FESBAL (Federación Española de Bancos de Alimentos) uses the "Tribal" management system from Grupo CMC, reported in use at 29 affiliated food banks. It covers warehouse, distribution and EU food-programme administration. It is internal tooling, not sold to donors. Source: https://www.cio.com/article/2079014/la-federacion-espanola-de-bancos-de-alimentos-implanta-un-nuevo-sistema-de-gestion.html (article date not checked; may be old).
- FESBAL has a "Plan B" digital platform for surplus and donation management. Source: https://www.eurofoodbank.org/plan-b-the-innovative-project-spanish-food-bank-fesba-developed-to-fight-food-waste . Whether it is open to outside donor businesses is unverified.
- Too Good To Go has a collaboration agreement with FESBAL (monetary donations via the app). Source: https://www.toogoodtogo.com/es/press/too-good-to-go-firma-un-acuerdo-de-colaboracion-con-la-federacion-espanola-de-bancos-de-alimentos
- Too Good To Go launched "Donations+" in Spain. It connects food businesses with charities and handles traceable, legally compliant donations, tax-incentive documentation (deduction of up to 5%) and compliance with the law. Source: https://www.toogoodtogo.com/es/press/too-good-to-go-lanza-donations (as summarised by the search result; page not opened).
- Other tools named for Ley 1/2025 compliance: Leanpath (https://blog.leanpath.com/spain-new-food-waste-law) and Oreka Circular Economy. These were not examined.
- Ley 1/2025 applies to food-chain companies that are not micro-enterprises. It sets a hierarchy: prevent, donate, reuse, recycle, dispose. Source: same Leanpath blog.
- Complaints: none found. No reviews of FESBAL or food-bank software turned up, and the search result itself said it found no user feedback. Review sites (Google Play, Reddit, local forums) were not searched.

## Pricing
Not applicable for the food banks: donations are free for the donor. Donations+ pricing: unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Food banks offer no donor-side compliance tooling that is verified (plan templates, traceability records, tax documents). Unverified beyond "Plan B".
- Capacity and logistics limits at food banks (pickup windows, cold chain) are plausible but not evidenced here.

## Opening
A donor-side compliance and logistics tool for small and mid-size food businesses can treat food banks as the destination, but it must beat Too Good To Go Donations+ (and Leanpath-type tools), which is the real competitor to check next.
