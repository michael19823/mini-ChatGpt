# Hesstec (Spain) - competitor check for C0937 (Spain solar self-consumption legalisation gestor)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

## Evidence
- Hesstec (HESStec, Hybrid Energy Storage Solutions) is a Seville-based energy-storage company founded 2018. It sells proprietary hardware and software for optimising and monetising storage, and integrates storage for the grid. Customers are TSOs/DSOs, developers, utilities, generators and C&I. Sources: https://www.cbinsights.com/company/hesstec ; https://www.energias-renovables.com/almacenamiento/la-espanola-hesstec-capta-2-3-ma-20210524
- A search for "Hesstec legalización autoconsumo fotovoltaico" returned nothing linking Hesstec to self-consumption legalisation or permit paperwork; the results were generic articles on Spanish self-consumption regulation.
- Funding: a CB Insights snippet says Series B, about $16.39M total (unverified, snippet only).
- Complaints: none found. I did not search app stores or forums, because the company does not serve this buyer.

## Pricing
Not published. Unverified. Enterprise and project-based storage sales, not a small-buyer paperwork service.

## Fit gaps
Does not appear to offer legalisation, permitting or gestor services for residential or small-business PV self-consumption. It targets utility-scale and C&I storage and needs hardware.

## Momentum
Active and funded (2021 raise reported by energias-renovables; later round per CB Insights, unverified).

## Opening
No overlap with a self-consumption legalisation gestor, so Hesstec did not legitimately kill C0937.
