# iGEO ERP (Spain) - competitor check for C0938 (Spain pest control / legionella records)

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Vertical ERP/SaaS built specifically for pest control and environmental health, by INTIME Desarrollos Informaticos, since 2014 (Capterra.es summary via search): https://www.capterra.es/software/161389/igeo-erp
- Includes a Legionella module for regulatory compliance, CRM, technician route optimiser, alert-to-service conversion, certificates and reports: https://www.softwaredoit.es/igeo-erp-cloud-platform/igeo-erp-cloud-platform.html
- Offices in Madrid, Mexico City, Bogota; present in Portugal, Italy, Peru, Chile, Argentina (same source, search snippet).
- Reviews: 4.8/5 from 55 users on Capterra (ease of use 4.5, support 4.6, value 4.3, features 4.7, 90% recommend): https://www.capterra.com/p/161389/iGEO-ERP/reviews/
- Complaints: none found beyond one mild gap. A reviewer says traceability is lacking: goods-in cannot be correlated with products each technician takes from stock (Capterra snippet). No outages, failed filings, or app-store complaints found in 3 searches (6 allowed; search tool is US-biased). Unverified beyond this.
- Momentum: listed on GetApp, Capterra, G2, Software Advice, Appvizer in 2026; looks actively marketed. No sign of acquisition or shutdown. Release cadence unverified.

## Pricing
- Starting price about 60 EUR/month flat (from Capterra/aggregator snippet); some portals say on request. Unverified minimums/per-user terms. Affordable for small firms, so not overpriced.

## Fit gaps
- Stock/product traceability weak (one review).
- Offline/mobile behaviour, per-user pricing, and Legionella record depth not verified.
- Aimed at pest control firms as a full ERP; a lightweight legionella-only records tool for non-pest-control water-treatment/maintenance firms is not clearly served (inference, unverified).

## Opening
Narrow: only a legionella-only or cheaper/simpler tool for small maintenance/water-hygiene firms outside a full pest-control ERP.
