# PylonData (Spain)

Dropped idea: C0937, Spain solar self-consumption legalisation gestor. Searches used: 4 of 6.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

PylonData sells PV monitoring and a sales/quote calculator for installers. It does not appear to process legalisation filings, so it does not directly kill a legalisation gestor.

## Evidence
- Described as photovoltaic monitoring software for self-consumption, based in Andalucia, which standardises data across inverter brands. Source: https://www.energias-renovables.com/autoconsumo/pylon-data-el-papel-crucial-de-la-20240123
- Its "Solar Calculator" does lead pre-qualification and personalised quotes from consumption, geolocation and energy prices. Source: https://www.interempresas.net/Quimica/537926-Pylon-en-Genera-innovacion-y-eficiencia-en-la-gestion-energetica.html?R=537833
- No result connects PylonData to legalisation or permit paperwork (searched in Spanish and English).
- The actual legalisation-platform competitors surfaced instead: SolarDirecto (SolarTradex plus the Holtrop law office; https://www.energias-renovables.com/fotovoltaica/solardirecto-se-encarga-del-papeleo-20160628), Autosolar's legalisation service (https://autosolar.es/informacion-legalizacion) and Certicalia.
- Momentum: covered in trade press in Jan and Feb 2024 and exhibited at Genera. I found nothing on acquisition or shutdown (unverified).
- Complaints: none found. No reviews of any kind turned up, and I did not find app-store, Trustpilot or Reddit pages.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- It is a monitoring and sales tool, not a filing or permit service.
- It targets installers and solar companies, not homeowners.
- It has no regional paperwork handling (the autonomous communities, the distributor, the municipality).

## Opening
A legalisation gestor is not blocked by PylonData. The real rivals are SolarDirecto, Autosolar and Certicalia, which need their own check.
