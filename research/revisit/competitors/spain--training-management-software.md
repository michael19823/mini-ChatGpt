# Spain: training management software (generic) - killed C0952 (FUNDAE subsidised-training notifications)

## Verdict: strong

A category of purpose-built FUNDAE management tools already exists, and one entry-level product is priced for small buyers.

## Evidence
- Gesforma: online tool specialised in FUNDAE. It communicates data to Fundae, automates documents, sends communications, invoices and collects digital signatures. It targets independent training managers, training organisations and company HR. Native Moodle integration. Source: Capterra/GetApp listing https://www.capterra.com/p/234904/Gesforma/ and https://www.getapp.com/education-childcare-software/a/gesforma/
- Iformalia: training management for private and subsidised training aligned with FUNDAE. Includes FUNDAE workflows, virtual classroom, digital signature and reporting. Source: https://softwarefinder.com/lms/iformalia and https://www.capterra.es/software/1061700/iformalia
- Other vendors cover FUNDAE as part of broader offerings (Woffu, Factorial content, SEIDOR blog on automating subsidised training). Not checked in depth; their FUNDAE depth is unverified.
- Context: FUNDAE 2024 annual report says 347,442 companies used bonified training for 5.8M participants (via search summary of fundae.es data; unverified directly). The market is large.
- Group start must be communicated on the FUNDAE platform at least 2 calendar days ahead (search summary, unverified directly), which is the notification workflow the dropped idea targeted.

## Complaints
None found. Capterra shows Gesforma 5.0 from 1 review and Iformalia 5.0 from 2 reviews. The only criticism seen: Iformalia's interface could be improved, and mapping fields to the LMS is the hardest part of implementation. No app-store, Reddit, Trustpilot or forum complaints surfaced in 6 searches. Review volume is very low, so absence of complaints is weak evidence.

## Pricing
- Gesforma: from EUR 150/month, free trial (Capterra listing). Verified from listing only.
- Iformalia: custom quotes; one listing (SAP partner page / marketplace) shows advanced hosting at EUR 20,000/year plus EUR 7,000 setup, minimum 1 year. That figure is for an enterprise hosting tier; cheaper tiers unverified.
- EUR 150/month is affordable for a training company but likely too much for a tiny company that only bonifies a few courses a year.

## Fit gaps
- Iformalia is oriented to larger organisations (hosting plans, LMS integration). Gesforma is aimed at training providers and HR, not tiny single-company self-managers.
- Possible gap: very small companies self-managing bonificada training, or a low-cost/free notification-and-reminder layer. Unverified; not confirmed by user complaints.
- No evidence on mobile, offline or multi-entity support.

## Momentum
Gesforma has a 2026 Capterra listing and users cite continuous feature improvements (1 review). Iformalia is listed on the SAP partner site and several review sites. Both appear active; no shutdowns or acquisitions found.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: a sub-EUR 150/month or free tool for micro-companies self-managing FUNDAE communications, since existing tools already do the notifications for training providers.
