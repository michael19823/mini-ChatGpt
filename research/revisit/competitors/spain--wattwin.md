# Wattwin (Spain)

Idea it killed: C0937, Spain solar self-consumption legalisation gestor.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Wattwin is B2B sales and project-management software for solar installers. It does not appear to be a legalisation or paperwork service for homeowners. (Searches: 4 of 6 used.)

## Evidence
- Wattwin is described as a platform that digitises the sale and management of self-consumption, efficiency and electrification projects, for installers, energy companies and engineering firms. Sources: https://capital-riesgo.es/en/articles/el-cdti-innovaci-n-y-suma-capital-invierten-en-wattwin-la-plataforma-que-digitaliza-los-servicios-de-venta-de-proyectos-fotovoltaicos/ , https://www.energias-renovables.com/autoconsumo/wattwin-la-herramienta-a-todo-en-unoa-20220725 , https://elreferente.es/startup/wattwin
- Funding: CDTI Innovación (Invierte programme) committed EUR 540,000, alongside Suma Capital's SC ENION I ENERGY FUND (same capital-riesgo.es source). The search snippet did not give the date.
- Search results did not show a legalisation or filing feature in Wattwin. Unverified: the product may include permitting documentation for installers, which a direct look at its site would settle.
- Real legalisation competitors turned up in the searches. SolarDirecto is an online platform for processing and legalising self-consumption installations (https://www.energias-renovables.com/fotovoltaica/solardirecto-se-encarga-del-papeleo-20160628). Certicalia lists legalisation services (https://www.certicalia.com/legalizar-instalacion-fotovoltaica-aislada). These are the competitors to check for this idea instead.
- Complaints: none found. A search for "Wattwin opiniones" returned only unrelated brands (Wattbike, Watt Utilities). No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews were found.

## Pricing
Not published in any search result. Unverified. Certicalia-type sources quote legalisation at roughly EUR 600 to 3,000 depending on power and region. That figure is not Wattwin's.

## Fit gaps
- Its customer is the installer, not the end homeowner or business that needs the legalisation done.
- It is a software subscription, not a done-for-you gestor service.

## Momentum
It was active in 2022 (energias-renovables article dated 2022-07-25 in the URL) and had public-backed investment. Current status is unverified.

## Opening
Wattwin does not do the homeowner-facing legalisation filing, so the gap is open to a consumer gestor service. Any installer that uses Wattwin could still be a customer or a channel for it. SolarDirecto and Certicalia are the competitors to assess.
