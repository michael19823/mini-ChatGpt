# AgriGEN ERP (Sri Lanka)

Dropped idea: C0954, Sri Lanka tea bought-leaf factory payouts and Tea Board returns.

## Verdict: weak

Only one thin source says the product exists, and I found no public site, pricing, reviews or customers. It looks like a bespoke or in-house factory ERP, not a marketed product. A "weak" call is tentative, because lack of web presence does not prove lack of use inside factories (unverified).

## Evidence
- The only hit is a LinkedIn profile of a QA person, which describes AgriGEN ERP as an ERP for tea factories covering green-leaf procurement, supplier accounting and factory store. It lists variable monthly leaf-rate updates, multi-tier approval for advances and store items (fertilizer, equipment), and automated deductions at monthly supplier payout. Source: https://lk.linkedin.com/in/dilan-sanuja. This is a self-description, so the claims are unverified.
- Searches (English and one Sinhala query) for AgriGEN returned no website, app-store listing, review, news item or customer. A search for "AgriGEN" plus tea leaf collection, pricing or review also returned nothing relevant.
- Related product: "Smart Tea", a tea leaf procurement system using NFC officer cards and weight receipts. It came up in search but I did not verify the vendor or whether it is linked to AgriGEN (unverified).
- Larger ERPs are in use in Sri Lankan plantations and exporters (Aitken Spence plantERM, IFS, SAP). These target big firms, not small bought-leaf factories: https://www.ft.lk/Business/aitken-spence-planterm-powers-elpitiya-plantations/34-240500
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints or outage reports surfaced.
- Momentum: unknown. No dated release, funding or acquisition information found.

## Pricing
Not published. Nothing found (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- Per the LinkedIn description it covers the factory-side payout logic, so it overlaps the core of C0954.
- Nothing found on Tea Board return/filing formats, Sinhala or Tamil UI, offline or mobile use, or supplier-facing apps. Not verified either way.
- Seems aimed at factories running a full ERP, not small factories wanting a lightweight payout tool (inference).

## Opening
If AgriGEN is only a private or bespoke ERP with no public sales, a lightweight, affordable payout plus Tea Board returns tool for small bought-leaf factories is still open. Confirm with direct factory interviews that they do not already run it.
