# consentstack (Sri Lanka) - killed idea C0958: Sri Lanka PDPA compliance

## Verdict: weak

ConsentStack is a generic website cookie-consent banner tool with a Sri Lanka PDPA regulation page. It is not a PDPA compliance product (no DPIAs, records of processing, data-subject-request handling, DPO workflow). It does not cover the broader PDPA compliance idea.

## Evidence
- Product is a cookie/consent management platform: geo-aware banners, script blocking, GA/Meta/TikTok consent signals, single script tag, CMS plugins (Squarespace, Webflow, WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Framer) and a React package. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/10042008/ConsentStack/ and https://www.consentstack.io (via search snippets; pages not fetched).
- Has a Sri Lanka PDPA page (https://www.consentstack.io/regulations/lk-pdpa). Search summary says it applies consent standards to Sri Lankan visitors "ensuring readiness" for enforcement. This is banner-level only; unverified beyond the snippet.
- Legal context: PDPA No. 9 of 2022; core enforcement provisions were scheduled for 18 March 2025 but the date was reportedly repealed days before, leaving uncertainty (per search summary of consentstack.io/flexyconsent sources; unverified independently). Weak enforcement lowers buyer urgency for any vendor.
- Listed on Capterra/GetApp/Software Advice, about 4/5 stars per search summary. No review text seen.
- Trustpilot pages exist (e.g. https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/consentstack.io), contents not seen.
- Momentum: appears actively marketed (2026 listings, comparison pages such as /compare/complianz). Exact dev activity unverified.
- Complaints: none found. Searches returned no negative reviews, forum or Reddit complaints, or outage news. Sinhala-language search (PDPA software) returned only legal guides, no mention of ConsentStack or any local compliance tool.

## Pricing
- Pro listed at US$29/month, flat rate; a free tier/trial exists (Capterra/GetApp snippets). Affordable for small sites, so not overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps (for a PDPA compliance buyer)
- Covers only website cookie consent; no PDPA records, DPIA, breach notification, DSAR workflow, controller/processor registers, or DPA registration support (inferred from product descriptions; unverified).
- No evidence of Sinhala or Tamil banner/UI support or local support (unverified).
- Not aimed at Sri Lankan organisations handling non-web data (HR, customers, offline).

## Opening
A Sri Lanka-specific, Sinhala/Tamil-capable PDPA programme tool (registers, DPIA, DSAR, breach) is untouched by ConsentStack; it only competes on the cookie banner layer, at $29/month.
