# EY Colombo (Sri Lanka) - competitor to C0958 (Sri Lanka PDPA compliance)

## Verdict: beatable

EY Colombo is a Big Four consultancy selling bespoke advisory, not a self-serve product. It is not aimed at small buyers who want a cheap tool.

## Evidence
- EY Colombo advertises PDPA-related privacy and data protection roles: privacy impact assessments, DPIAs, consent management frameworks, gap and maturity assessments. Source: https://careers.ey.com/ey/job/Colombo-AssociateExecutive-Privacy-&-Data-Protection-Services/1287126501 (job posting, so the services are inferred from it).
- An EY Sri Lanka partner (Hiranthi Fonseka) has spoken publicly on PDPA compliance and annual audits. EY also worked with CFA Society Sri Lanka on a financial-sector impact assessment. Source: https://www.ft.lk/financial-services/CFA-Society-Sri-Lanka-collaborate-with-EY-to-assess-the-impact-of-the-Sri-Lankan-Data-Protection-Law-on-the-Financial-Services-Industry%C2%A0/42-732819
- Momentum: active. EY is hiring privacy analysts in Colombo. A Sunday Times article (https://www.sundaytimes.lk/260906/business-times/xapi-launches-practical-pdpa-compliance-playbook-655571.html) says the PDPA's core obligations take effect 1 January 2027, so demand is about to rise.
- Other players already sell PDPA tooling (ISMS.online, Securiti) or guides (Xapi playbook, Echelon Studio). These are alternatives to EY, not evidence about EY.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in English and Sinhala returned no reviews, forum posts or news about EY Colombo's PDPA work. The Sinhala search returned mostly Singapore PDPA pages. Reviews of this kind are rarely public for Big Four firms.

## Pricing
Unverified. No published price or minimum found. Big Four engagements are scoped and quoted per client, so they are almost certainly out of reach for SMEs, but this is an inference, not a sourced fact.

## Fit gaps (largely inferred)
- Service-led, per-engagement consulting. There is no evidence of a productised, self-serve or subscription tool.
- Likely serves large corporates and financial services (the CFA collaboration points that way).
- No evidence of Sinhala or Tamil deliverables or SME-sized packages (unverified).

## Opening
A low-cost, self-serve PDPA readiness toolkit for Sri Lankan SMEs (RoPA, DSAR, consent, templates, possibly in Sinhala and Tamil) is not covered by EY. The real rivals there are ISMS.online, Securiti and local boutiques.
