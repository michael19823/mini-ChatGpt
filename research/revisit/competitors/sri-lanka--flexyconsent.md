# flexyconsent (Sri Lanka) - idea C0958: Sri Lanka PDPA compliance

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (for broad PDPA compliance); strong only for a cookie-banner-only product

## Evidence
- FlexyConsent is a generic cookie consent management platform (WordPress plugin, Shopify app, JS snippet), marketed for GDPR, CCPA and IAB TCF. Not Sri Lanka specific. Sources: https://flexyconsent.com, https://wordpress.org/plugins/flexyconsent/, https://apps.shopify.com/flexy-consent
- It publishes a blog guide on Sri Lanka PDPA cookie consent (content marketing, not a localised product): https://flexyconsent.com/blog/sri-lanka-pdpa-cookie-consent-compliance-guide/
- Search summaries of its Shopify listing show ratings of about 4.7-4.9 out of 5, mostly positive. Minor complaints: settings take time to figure out, wanted better docs or video tutorials, one user needed support to reinstall. No serious complaints found.
- No Reddit, Trustpilot, Facebook or Sinhala/Tamil-language complaints found. The local-language search (Sinhala) returned only English PDPA guides and other vendors (consentstack.io, Securiti, CookieConsent). No complaints is a valid result here.
- Other tools appear in the same results (consentstack.io, Securiti, CookieConsent): unverified details, not checked.

## Pricing (from search summaries, unverified against the vendor page)
- Free plan (unlimited impressions, basic features); Basic about $2/month ($20/year); Advanced about $4/month ($40/year). Very cheap for small buyers, so not overpriced.

## Fit gaps
- Cookie/consent banner only: no records of processing, DPIA, DPO workflow, data-subject request handling, breach notification, cross-border transfer records, or policy/templates for PDPA (not seen in results; unverified).
- No Sinhala or Tamil localisation seen (unverified; the Basic plan lists "multiple languages" generically).
- Not aware of Sri Lanka Data Protection Authority formats or local support.

## Momentum
- Active: 2026 PDPA blog post, 2026 G2 listing, current WordPress and Shopify listings.

## Opening
- Cookie banners are a solved, near-free commodity; the gap is the non-cookie PDPA workload (registers, DSARs, breach, DPO, Sinhala/Tamil templates) for Sri Lankan SMEs, which FlexyConsent does not address.
