# ISMS.online (Sri Lanka) - competitor check for C0958 (Sri Lanka PDPA compliance)

## Verdict: strong (for the generic PDPA-software slot), with a narrow local opening

## Evidence
- ISMS.online has a dedicated Sri Lanka PDPA framework page (Act No. 9 of 2022): pre-written policies, RoPA, DSAR management, DPIA, integrations with 5,000+ platforms. Source: https://www.isms.online/frameworks/framework-pdpa/
- G2: about 4.5/5 from 288 reviews; praised for support and clean UI. Only criticism found: the supplier module relies on free text with no logic controls. Source: https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/isms-online-review-7629335
- Capterra: 4.5/5 from 8 users. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/147558/ISMS-online
- Other PDPA tooling exists: Securiti (https://securiti.ai/sri-lanka-personal-data-protection-act/), Xapi PDPA playbook (https://www.sundaytimes.lk/260906/business-times/xapi-launches-practical-pdpa-compliance-playbook-655571.html), consent tools (flexyconsent.com).
- Core PDPA provisions reportedly come into force January 2027 (from a search summary; unverified against the Act).
- Complaints: none found in the searches (no Trustpilot, Reddit, local forum or Sinhala-language complaints surfaced). No Sri Lanka-specific reviews found.
- Momentum: actively marketed, with 2026 listings; appears actively developed.

## Pricing
- Capterra lists a starting price of GBP 375 per month (about GBP 4,500 per year). Not verified on the vendor site. Likely high for Sri Lankan SMEs (unverified; no LKR pricing found).

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- It is an ISO 27001-oriented ISMS suite, so likely heavy for a small firm needing only PDPA basics.
- No evidence of Sinhala or Tamil UI, or of LKR pricing.
- No evidence of local DPA-registration or local-regulator workflows.

## Opening
A lightweight, low-cost, Sinhala/Tamil-capable PDPA kit for Sri Lankan SMEs could sit below ISMS.online's roughly GBP 375/month entry price, but no customer complaints were found, so the gap rests on price and fit inference only.

Searches: 3 of 6 used (English and one Sinhala-mixed query).
