# Sri Lanka: local payroll and HRIS packages

Dropped idea: C0955, Sri Lanka EPF/ETF monthly e-return automation.
Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Sinhala).

## Verdict: strong

Many local and regional payroll/HRIS products already calculate EPF/ETF/APIT and produce statutory forms, at low entry prices. No complaints were found to show a gap.

## Evidence
- Humanised (local SME HR/payroll): described as auto-calculating EPF, ETF and APIT and building bulk salary transfer files in ATPS-compliant formats, with a 30-day free trial. Source: https://www.hr.software/reviews/humanised (vendor-friendly review site; unverified independently).
- Gallery HR: markets auto-calculation of EPF 8% and 12% and monthly C Form generation, plus semi-annual Form 2 reports. Source: https://galleryhr.com/pages/region-sri-lanka (vendor marketing).
- Other packages found: MiHCM (https://mihcm.com/en-lk/solutions/payroll/), IceHrm (https://icehrm.com/blog/top-hr-software-options-for-sri-lankan-businesses/), an Odoo Sri Lanka payroll module (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_lk_payroll).
- Whether any of these actually files the EPF e-return text files by bank or EPF format, rather than only producing the C form: unverified.
- Official process: the EPF e-Return system takes text files in formats set by the EPF/licensed banks. Employers with over 50 staff must register, and those with over 10 may. Source: https://epf.lk/?p=171
- Government context: the EPF system dates from about 2000. The Central Bank (EPF) and the Labour Department (ETF) run separate systems, and a project to integrate them into one application is planned. If it ships, it could remove the need for third-party e-return tooling. Source: search result summary of parliament.lk and veriteresearch.org material (unverified detail).
- Compliance: about 10,000 employers default on ETF payments each month (Sunday Times, 2012, so dated). Source: https://www.sundaytimes.lk/120923/business-times/some-10000-sri-lankan-employers-default-etf-payments-12917.html
- Complaints: none found for the local packages. Only a vendor comparison page quoted an unnamed competitor's review ("No help when we face issues"). It is marketing and cannot be attributed. No app-store, Reddit or forum complaints surfaced, including in Sinhala.
- Momentum: Gallery HR, Humanised and MiHCM all publish active marketing content. No sign of shutdown or stagnation (unverified beyond website activity).

## Pricing
- Humanised: starts from about LKR 10,000 per month, flat tiers for companies under 200 staff, no setup fee (per https://www.hr.software/reviews/humanised, unverified).
- Odoo l10n_lk_payroll module: USD 79 (apps.odoo.com listing).
- Gallery HR, MiHCM, IceHrm: no prices found.
- Global platforms bill in USD, roughly 5 to 12+ per user per month (secondary source).
- Entry pricing is low enough that small buyers are not clearly overcharged.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. Possible gaps, all unverified: direct generation of bank-specific e-return files, filing for micro-employers using spreadsheets only, and Sinhala/Tamil interface.
- Servicing firms with no payroll system who just need a monthly return file may fall outside these suites, which are sold as full HR and payroll.

## Opening
Narrow: only a standalone, near-free e-return file generator for micro-employers on spreadsheets, and the planned EPF/ETF integration may close even that.
