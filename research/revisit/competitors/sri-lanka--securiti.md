# Securiti (Sri Lanka, idea C0958: Sri Lanka PDPA compliance)

## Verdict: beatable

Securiti has a dedicated Sri Lanka PDPA page, but it is an enterprise data-security platform. It is not aimed at the small and mid-size Sri Lankan firms a local PDPA tool would serve.

## Evidence
- Securiti publishes a Sri Lanka PDPA (Act No. 9 of 2022) solution page. It lists PI data discovery, DSR automation, documented accountability and AI-process automation. Sources: https://securiti.ai/solutions/sri-lankas-personal-data-protection-act-2022 and https://securiti.ai/sri-lanka-personal-data-protection-act/
- It is a global platform (Data Command Center). The Sri Lanka page looks like a generic regulation landing page. I found no evidence of a local office, local partner, Sinhala/Tamil support or PDPA-specific templates (unverified).
- Momentum: Veeam announced a $1.725B acquisition of Securiti AI in Oct 2025, expected to close Q4 2025. Securiti is well funded and active, though its focus is DSPM and AI security for large enterprises. Sources: https://www.channelinsider.com/security/managed-services/veeam-acquisition-securiti-ai/ and https://siliconangle.com/2025/10/21/veeam-acquire-securiti-accelerate-safe-ai-scale/
- Complaints: G2 shows about 4.6/5 from about 101 reviews, and Gartner Peer Insights about 4.7/5. A search summary of Gartner reviews mentions OneDrive/SharePoint scanning pod crashes that support could not pin down for weeks, and support hours on an Indian time zone. I did not open the reviews, so treat these as unverified. I found no complaints from Sri Lankan users or about PDPA features specifically.
- Local market: other PDPA offerings exist, including ISMS.online, Xapi's PDPA playbook (Sunday Times, Sri Lanka), and consent tools such as FlexyConsent and ConsentStack. Enforcement is reported to start 1 Jan 2027, with the Data Protection Authority as the enforcer. Source: https://www.sundaytimes.lk/260906/business-times/xapi-launches-practical-pdpa-compliance-playbook-655571.html
- No Sinhala-language results for Securiti turned up.

## Pricing
- No public price list; quote only. Vendr reports a median buyer paying about $49,841 per year (https://vendr.com/marketplace/securiti-ai). This is a third-party aggregate, so treat it as unverified.
- G2 summaries say about 74% of reviews are from enterprise users. That is far above what a Sri Lankan SME could pay.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at large enterprises, with a high price and a sales-led process.
- No sign of a lightweight SME PDPA toolkit: gap assessment, DPO and records-of-processing templates, consent and breach-notice forms in Sinhala/Tamil, and LKR pricing (all unverified absent).
- Heavy data-discovery deployment (connectors, scanning pods) is overkill for small firms.
- Support is reported to run on an Indian time zone (unverified).

## Opening
A cheap, local-language, template-driven PDPA readiness tool for Sri Lankan SMEs ahead of the 2027 enforcement date. Securiti is priced and built for enterprises that would not buy it.
