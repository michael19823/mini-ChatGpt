# Xapi PDPA Compliance Playbook (Sri Lanka)

Dropped idea: C0958 (Sri Lanka PDPA compliance). Searches: 5 of 6 used (English; one Sinhala query returned nothing relevant).

## Verdict: beatable

The Playbook is a roughly 160-page guide, not software. The company behind it sells an enterprise governance platform. Neither addresses small buyers who want a self-serve tool.

## Evidence
- Xapi launched the "Xapi Personal Data Protection Compliance Playbook". It is described as a practical implementation guide with a six-layer framework, a four-level maturity model, an enterprise case study, a leadership checklist and a "PDPA Gap and Readiness Audit". Core PDPA provisions take effect January 2027. Sources: https://www.sundaytimes.lk/260906/business-times/xapi-launches-practical-pdpa-compliance-playbook-655571.html and https://www.newswire.lk/whp5
- Xapi is a Singapore-based unified data, API and AI governance platform that has expanded to Sri Lanka. It targets regulated sectors: financial services, healthcare, government and logistics. Sources: https://hirunews.lk/goldfmnews/440804/singapore-based-xapi-expands-to-sri-lanka-with-global-push-in-data-and-ai-governance and https://www.dailymirror.lk/business/Xapi-unveils-new-brand-identity-and-strategic-vision-for-global-governance-innovation/215-330449
- Other PDPA tools appear in results (Securiti, ISMS.online, ConsentStack, IBM Guardium). They are global or enterprise products. I did not verify their Sri Lanka-specific fit. Sources: https://consentstack.io/regulations/lk-pdpa and https://www.isms.online/frameworks/framework-pdpa/
- Complaints: none found. The Playbook is new (September 2026), and no reviews, forum threads or app-store entries turned up. The Sinhala-language search also returned nothing relevant.
- Momentum: active. It had a rebrand and a recent launch tied to the January 2027 deadline. Funding and traction are unverified.

## Pricing
Unverified. No price for the Playbook or the platform was published in the search results.

## Fit gaps
- The Playbook is static content. It does not appear to automate records, consent, data subject requests or breach logging (inferred from the press descriptions, not verified).
- The marketing is aimed at "organisations", leadership and enterprises. The platform targets regulated sectors, so SME fit looks poor (inferred).
- Sinhala or Tamil localisation: unverified, and not mentioned in the sources.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, SME-focused PDPA toolkit (templates, registers, DSR and breach workflows, possibly in Sinhala and Tamil) fits below Xapi's enterprise framework and platform, ahead of the January 2027 deadline.
