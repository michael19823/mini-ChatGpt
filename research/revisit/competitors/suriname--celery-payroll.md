# Celery Payroll (Suriname)

Killed idea: C0962 Suriname payroll compliance.

## Verdict: strong

Celery is a live, Suriname-specific, cloud payroll product with local launch, local partners, APF and bank-file integrations. No complaints were found, so there is no evidence of a weakness to exploit.

## Evidence
- Suriname payroll was added to Celery, which already covered Curacao, Sint Maarten, Aruba and the BES islands. Source: https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=1858
- Official launch events in Paramaribo (Torarica, Ramada), November 2018. Local partners: Kenswil & Co. and Baboem N.V. Source: https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=1878
- Suriname features: 4 employee categories incl. Supervisory Director; accrual of vacation allowance and bonus with statutory untaxed maximums; APF (Algemeen Pensioenfonds) integration. Source: https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=4173 and https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=25085
- Payment-file export for Finabank, Hakrinbank, DSB and Republic Bank Suriname. Source: https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=15683
- Support knowledge base has Suriname-specific articles, e.g. AOV employer contribution handling: https://support.celerypayroll.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000193803-what-does-celery-do-with-a-employer-contribution-in-de-aov-premium-in-a-suriname-payroll-
- Delivered via browser, iOS/Android app and desktop (per their country guide: https://support.celerypayroll.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000285120-celery-payroll-hrm-country-guide-platform-differences-per-jurisdiction).
- Complaints: none found. English and Dutch searches (6 searches max) returned no reviews or outage/filing-failure news for Celery. App-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot and Reddit were not surfaced by search; absence is not proof of satisfaction.
- Momentum: a post-launch feature article ("Are you taking advantage of all the awesome features in your Suriname payroll?", https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=9625) and a 2026-era support guide suggest active maintenance. Exact recency unverified.

## Pricing
Billed per payslip per month (per search summary of their site). Exact Suriname price: not found / unverified. For reference only, Remote.com lists $29 per employee per month for its Suriname payroll (different provider, EOR-style; not Celery).

## Fit gaps
- Nothing verified. Possible but unverified: filing format for Belastingdienst monthly wage-tax statements/annual returns, SZF, and Dutch/Surinamese-language UI were not confirmed either way.
- Pricing per payslip may not suit very small employers (unverified).

## Opening
Little visible: only a niche play (very small employers, or a filing/compliance-calendar layer alongside Celery) after verifying Celery's tax-filing output and price directly.
