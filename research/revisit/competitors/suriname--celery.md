# Celery (Suriname payroll)

Dropped idea: C0965 Suriname payroll. Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Dutch). Findings come from search-result snippets only; no pages were fetched.

## Verdict: strong

Celery is an established, actively developed, Suriname-specific payroll product with local partners, bank and pension integrations, and low per-payslip pricing. I found no complaints.

## Evidence
- Celery is online payroll/HRM software for Aruba, BVI, BES, Curacao, Guyana, Sint Maarten and Suriname. It claims 2,000+ Caribbean businesses (unverified self-claim). Source: https://celerypayroll.com/en/?p=1267
- Official Suriname launch was in Nov 2018 (Paramaribo events, partners Kenswil & Co. and Baboem N.V.). Source: https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=1878
- Suriname-specific features: four employee categories, vacation allowance accrual with the statutory untaxed maximum. Source: https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=1858
- Integrations: FinaBank payment files (https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=15683) and Algemeen Pensioenfonds Suriname (APF) pension calculation plus the P45 periodic declaration (https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=25916).
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or local-forum reviews surfaced in English or Dutch searches. The only feedback seen is on Celery's own site (praise for support and ease of use), which is vendor-curated. Absence of complaints could reflect search limits, not satisfaction.
- A search for Suriname payroll software returned only generic Dutch and EOR results (Rivermate, Deel-type sites); no other local competitor surfaced.

## Pricing
Published on Celery's site (snippet, USD): 17.25 per employer, 2.75 per payslip, 2.75 per employee, with lower rates for 20+ employees. No contract, 30-day free trial. APF integration is a flat USD 25 per month extra. Sources: https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=960, https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=25916. It is unclear whether the 2.75 figures are monthly or per run (unverified). Pricing is low and not overpriced for small employers.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. Possible but unverified gaps: Suriname tax authority (Belastingdienst) e-filing and SVB filing formats, Dutch/Sranan-language staff self-service, offline use, and pricing in SRD (it is USD-quoted).
- The product is cloud-only, so offline use is not available (inferred from "online payroll", unverified).

## Opening
Little room for a general payroll rival; any opening would be a narrow add-on (for example local tax-authority/SVB filing or SRD-native, offline or mobile use) and only if Celery is confirmed to lack it.
