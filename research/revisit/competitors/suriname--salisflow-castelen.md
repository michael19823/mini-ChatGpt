# SalisFlow (Castelen Systems) - Suriname

Dropped idea it killed: C0962 (Suriname payroll compliance). 4 of 6 searches used.

## Verdict: weak

Real, local, free product, but young and thinly evidenced; the free local-only model leaves a paid compliance/filing niche open. (Verdict rests on thin evidence; see unverified items.)

## Evidence
- SalisFlow is payroll and HR software from Castelen Systems, Paramaribo, aimed at Suriname, Guyana, Aruba, Curacao, Caribbean Netherlands, Sint Maarten and BVI. Free browser app (Windows/Mac) or Windows desktop app; interface in English and Dutch. Source: https://sourceforge.net/software/product/SalisFlow/ and https://slashdot.org/software/p/SalisFlow/ (directory listings, likely vendor-supplied text).
- Features listed: employee records, payroll changes, earnings/deductions review, payslips, reports, "available payment or journal exports". No mention found of wage-tax/AOV filing formats or Belastingdienst submission.
- Data stays in local device/browser storage with encrypted backup export/import; optional paid "SalisFlow Cloud" for multi-device and encrypted recovery copies (same sources).
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced (a review-oriented search returned only unrelated products). No news about outages or failed filings found. Absence suggests a very small user base (inference, unverified).
- Other payroll tools serving Suriname that appeared in results (the more serious competition): Celery (https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=1878, wage tax and AOV calculation), plus EOR/payroll providers Ontop, Rivermate, Playroll.

## Pricing
- Local payroll: free, no account or credit card (sources above).
- SalisFlow Cloud: paid; price not found (unverified).
