# SalisFlow (Suriname) - competitor check for C0965 (Suriname payroll)

## Verdict: beatable

SalisFlow is real and targets exactly this market, but it is a tiny, local-first product with a free tier and no visible traction. 4 of 6 searches used.

## Evidence
- Listed on SourceForge and Slashdot as payroll and HR software from Castelen Systems, Paramaribo. Targets Suriname, Guyana, Aruba, Curacao, Caribbean Netherlands, Sint Maarten, BVI. Sources: https://sourceforge.net/software/product/SalisFlow/ and https://slashdot.org/software/p/SalisFlow/ (search-result summaries only; pages not opened).
- Free browser app (Windows/Mac) or Windows desktop app. Local payroll needs no account. Handles employee records, payroll changes, earnings/deductions, payslips, reports, payment/journal exports. English and Dutch UI.
- Data is stored locally in the device/browser, with encrypted backup export/import for manual transfers. Optional paid SalisFlow Cloud syncs approved devices and stores encrypted recovery copies.
- Complaints: none found. Searches (English and Dutch) returned no reviews, forum threads, app-store entries or news about SalisFlow. A "reviews" search returned only unrelated companies. This means little public adoption or feedback, not that users are happy.
- Momentum: unverified. No dates, release notes or customer references found.

## Pricing
- Local version: $0 (per listing). Paid Cloud: price not found (unverified).
- Other payroll options seen for Suriname: Celery Payroll (https://www.celerypayroll.com/en/?p=4173) and EOR/payroll providers (Rivermate, Deel, Asanify, BDO). These are priced for foreign employers, not a free local tool.

## Fit gaps (inferred from the listing, partly unverified)
- Local-first storage: multi-user, multi-device or accountant-client collaboration needs the paid Cloud tier.
- Not verified: statutory filing outputs (loonbelasting, AOV/AWW, SZF returns), mobile app, WhatsApp/payslip delivery, bank file formats, multi-entity support.
- Windows/Mac desktop focus; no mobile app mentioned.

## Opening
A free local tool already covers the basics, so a new entrant can't win on payroll calculation. The opening is in filing-ready Surinamese tax and social-security outputs, cloud and mobile collaboration, and accountant multi-client workflows, if those turn out to be missing.
