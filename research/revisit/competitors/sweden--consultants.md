# Sweden: climate-declaration consultants (type: consultant, no URL)

Killed idea: C0981 Sweden klimatdeklaration 2027 limit values.

## Verdict: beatable

Consultants and in-house LCA staff are the default way to produce declarations, but the work is manual and costly for small builders. Boverket-approved software (One Click LCA) already automates part of it, so the opening is narrower than "consultants only".

## Evidence
- Boverket proposes limit values from 2027 for modules A1-A5, tightened in 2035 and 2043, plus an expanded declaration from January 2027 (Boverket publication, boverket.se/en/start/publications/2023/limit-values-for-climate-impact-from-buildings/; search summaries, details unverified).
- Cost per declaration: cited estimates of 30,000-40,000 SEK for a small house, and later Boverket estimates of 40,000-60,000 SEK for a 150 m2 serial-built house (search summary of riksdagen.se / regeringen.se consultation material; original figures unverified).
- Remittance responses: costs for small houses are about three times larger as a share of build cost than for multi-family housing. Larger firms benefit from economies of scale, which disadvantages smaller actors (regeringen.se consultation responses, e.g. Villaagarnas riksforbund; paraphrased from search summary).
- One Click LCA is Boverket-approved and markets automated LCA for climate declarations (oneclicklca.com/sv/klimatdeklaration/). It is a software competitor, not a consultant.
- Complaints: no user reviews of specific consultants found (no app-store, Trustpilot or Reddit hits). The only criticism found is policy-level, from industry consultation responses.
- Momentum: the demand driver is regulatory and growing (2027 expansion). I did not verify any consultant firm's activity.

## Pricing
- Published consultant prices: none found. Only the Boverket/government cost estimates above (30-60k SEK per small house). Per-project consultant quotes: unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Per-building manual work does not scale down well to small builders and one-off house projects.
- Quantities for non-standard houses must be volume-determined by hand before they go into LCA software, which adds cost (search summary of the cost analysis).
- Limit-value compliance checking and "what-if" optimisation is likely sold as billable consulting time (unverified).

## Opening
A cheap self-serve tool for small builders that does quantity take-off, 2027 limit-value checking and Boverket filing can undercut 30-60k SEK per house, but it must beat One Click LCA on price and simplicity.
