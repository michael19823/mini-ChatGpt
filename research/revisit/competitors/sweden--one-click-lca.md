# One Click LCA (Sweden) - competitor check

Idea killed: C0981, Sweden klimatdeklaration 2027 limit values.

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Boverket-approved Swedish klimatdeklaration tool, included in all Swedish licences; applies Boverket's 25% conservative factor automatically (https://oneclicklca.com/sv/klimatdeklaration/, https://help.oneclicklca.com/en/articles/275711).
- Actively developed: June 2025 update added per-m2 result tables (help.oneclicklca.com article above). Has a Swedish-language academy course and community thread.
- Boverket proposes limit values from 2027 for A1-A5, tightened in 2035 and 2043, plus more modules (B2, B4, B6) (government consultation responses on regeringen.se, e.g. https://www.regeringen.se/contentassets/eee4559302dc4ac5a904c92df5e836de/malmo-kommun.pdf). The search did not confirm that One Click LCA already supports the limit-value check (unverified), but it is the incumbent best placed to add it.
- Complaints: none significant found. G2 4.5/5 (72 reviews) and Capterra 4.6/5 (18 reviews), per search summaries. Minor cons: initial setup time, graphics not presentation-ready, limited data export flexibility (Capterra/G2 via search summary; not read directly). No Swedish-language complaints found.
- A Swedish SBUF guide notes simple tools such as BM 1.0 and One Click LCA are user-friendly but less flexible/transparent than GaBi or SimaPro (https://vpp.sbuf.se/Public/Documents/InfoSheets/PublishedInfoSheet/c2855b93-4ff1-41b8-854c-e1d96fad3d5d/).

## Pricing
- Building LCA business licence: EUR 3,030/year named user, EUR 5,180/year concurrent (3 users per seat), billed annually (https://shop.oneclicklca.com/building-lca/business-licenses). Region-specific; the Swedish price was not confirmed (unverified).
- Product LCA/EPD plans from EUR 400/seat/month (https://oneclicklca.com/pricing/product-lca-and-epd-pricing).
- Expensive for a very small consultancy or single builder, but modest for firms that declare regularly.

## Fit gaps
- Little found beyond minor UX points (setup time, exports, graphics).
- Likely less suited to occasional or one-off declarants who cannot justify an annual licence (inference, unverified).
- Not confirmed whether 2027 limit-value and expanded-module (B2/B4/B6) support is shipped.

## Opening
Narrow: a cheap, pay-per-declaration limit-value checker for small builders; otherwise One Click LCA is entrenched and well reviewed.
