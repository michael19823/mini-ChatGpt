# EKZ Gemeinsamstrom (Switzerland)

Killed idea: C0992, ZEV/vZEV/LEG billing for property managers. Searches used: 4 of 6 (German and English).

## Verdict: weak

Gemeinsamstrom is a new venture inside the utility EKZ (Canton Zurich). It is aimed at municipalities, producers and residents. It is not a billing tool for property managers. It is also tied to EKZ's own grid area, and I found no evidence of a mature product.

## Evidence
- Search snippets describe Gemeinsamstrom as a venture "built within EKZ" that aims to connect people, municipalities and producers for local energy sharing. The first LEGs (local electricity communities) in Switzerland start in 2026. Source: results surfaced from the EKZ Weinland municipal information PDF (https://www.ekz.ch/dam/ekz/landing/gemeindeninformation/weinland/aktuelles-aus-der-energiepolitik-2025-claudio-maag.pdf). I could not open it, so this rests on the search summary and is partly unverified.
- A jobup.ch listing for "Business Developer:in Account Management Gemeinden" came up for the venture (https://www.jobup.ch/en/jobs/detail/43605b72-4d50-4676-a148-bde19a5f79eb). Its focus is municipality accounts, and the venture is still hiring for business development. This is a snippet-level inference, not verified.
- Search summaries say EKZ handles metering and billing for its own "(v)Eigenstrom X" and vZEV model, for about 1 ct/kWh of self-consumption. This is from a municipal info presentation (https://www.einsiedeln.ch/libraries.files/Praesentation_Infoanlass_Solarstrom_fuer_alle_Neue_Modelle_neue_Moeglichkeiten.pdf). It is not clearly the Gemeinsamstrom product, and it is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. I found no app-store reviews, Trustpilot, Reddit, forum or news items about Gemeinsamstrom. This may be because the product is too new. The only Trustpilot hit was for a different company, Solar Invoice, which I did not assess.
- Momentum: new and in build-up in 2026. It is backed by a large cantonal utility, so it has funding and customer access.

## Pricing
No published Gemeinsamstrom price was found. The only figure is the roughly 1 ct/kWh EKZ measurement and billing fee above, which is unverified and may belong to a different EKZ product. Other utilities publish vZEV price sheets (for example https://www.shpower.ch/fileadmin/images/private/energie/eigenverbrauch-vzev-leg/Preisblatt-vZEV.pdf). I did not read them, so they do not support any claim about EKZ.

## Fit gaps
- It is utility-locked. LEG members must be in the same municipality and have smart meters, and the sharing runs over the EKZ grid. It is therefore likely limited to EKZ's supply area. Properties elsewhere in Switzerland would need another provider.
- It targets municipalities and producers, not property managers who run many buildings and need tenant billing and portfolio tools. This is an inference and unverified.
- I found no sign of tenant-level utility-bill integration, multi-owner portfolio views or a property-management software interface. Unverified.
- A utility has little reason to be neutral across other grid operators' data.

## Opening
A grid-operator-neutral billing and portfolio tool for property managers across many Swiss utilities, which a single-utility venture like Gemeinsamstrom is unlikely to cover.
