# ElektroForm (Brunner Informatik) - Switzerland

Dropped idea: C0985 (Switzerland electrician and PV grid-operator notifications)

## Verdict: strong

It is a real, established incumbent that does exactly this job. It is free to installers, and grid operators adopt it as the standard channel, so it is hard to beat head-on.

## Evidence
- ElektroForm online is a web app at www.elektroform.ch/online, built for installation notifications (Installationsanzeige), connection requests (Anschlussgesuch), equipment orders and safety certificates (SiNa). It has separate access portals per grid operator (VNB). Sources: https://ebl.ch/hubfs/EBL/_EBL%20Website/_Dateien%20f%C3%BCr%20Downloads_Deutsch/Netzdienstleistungen/Kundeninstallationen/Factsheet_Elektronisches_Meldewesen.pdf ; https://www.wetzikon.ch/stadtwerke/strom/Download-Bereich/Haustechnik-Installationskontrolle/Papierloses-Meldewesen-ElektroForm15.pdf
- EBL (Baselland) introduced it from 2 June 2020. There is also a desktop program, ElektroForm15 Installateur. Source: EBL factsheet above.
- Stadtwerke Wetzikon offers it and says it is developed by Brunner Informatik. Source: Wetzikon PDF above.
- Some grid operators run their own portals instead. AEW Energie AG launched its own web portal in June 2025. Source: https://www.aew.ch/sites/default/files/2025-06/250603_MM_AEW_Meldwesen-Portal_lanciert.pdf
- Momentum: it is actively used and was still being adopted in 2020, and AEW's 2025 portal launch shows the market is moving. I found no news of an acquisition or shutdown (unverified beyond that).
- Complaints: none found. The searches returned no app-store, forum or review-site complaints. Searches in German and English turned up no reviews at all, so that is an absence of data, not evidence of satisfaction.
- Caveat: I found no official Brunner Informatik site in the results, so the company details come from utility documents only.

## Pricing
Free to the installer, per the Wetzikon document. The utilities presumably pay for it (unverified). A competitor cannot undercut free on price.

## Fit gaps
- Each utility configures its own portal. Some operators (such as AEW) run proprietary portals, which splits the market and creates a multi-operator headache.
- Coverage is likely limited to operators that license ElektroForm (unverified).
- No evidence either way on PV-specific workflows, mobile or offline use, or integration with the installer's quoting and invoicing tools. These are unverified gaps that need checking.

## Opening
Only a thin one: a single cross-operator layer for installers who deal with many grid operators, including those with proprietary portals. Against a free incumbent that grid operators mandate, it is unlikely to be worth building.
