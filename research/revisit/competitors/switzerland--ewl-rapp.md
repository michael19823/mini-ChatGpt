# switzerland / ewl + Rapp (commercial, no URL known)

Killed idea: C0992, Switzerland ZEV/vZEV/LEG billing for property managers.
Searches used: 5 of 6 (German and English). WebFetch not used.

## Verdict: beatable

ewl (the Lucerne utility) offers ZEV billing as a utility service and uses Rapp as its billing partner. It is a regional utility add-on. It is not a standalone product built for property managers. I found no complaints, but my search coverage was thin.

## Evidence
- ewl's 2026 price sheet describes a "Verrechnungsdienstleistung ZEV". The service covers solar-power distribution to ZEV participants, direct invoicing including collection, an online portal for consumption and invoices, ZEV administration such as tenant changes, meter plausibility checks and annual statements. Source: https://www.ewl-luzern.ch/content-media/documents/ewl_PB_EDL_Verrechnungsdienstleistung_ZEV_2026.pdf
- The search snippet says ewl relies on its partner Rapp for the ZEV billing solution. Rapp's own product page was not found, so Rapp's standalone offering is unverified.
- ewl publishes ZEV application forms (2025). The service looks tied to ewl's own grid area and customers (unverified whether outside customers can use it). Source: https://www.ewl-luzern.ch/content-media/documents/ewl/02-Energie/Antrag-Zusammenschluss-zum-Eigenverbrauch_ewl_20251216_Formular.pdf
- Crowded alternatives exist: zevvy (ewz is integrating its platform), Blockstrom, Gerber AG (ZEV, vZEV and LEG billing), Honegger Elektro Telecom, smart-me, ista, Techem, BKW, WWZ and SH Power. Sources: https://www.lucerne-business.com/en/news/ewz-to-integrate-zevvy-ag-platform-for-billing-16801 , https://marketplace.allthings.me/en/solutions/blockstrom-zev-billing
- Complaints: none found. Searches for Rapp ZEV reviews returned only Rapp-the-agency employer reviews and unrelated items, which are not relevant. No app-store, Reddit or forum complaints about ewl or Rapp ZEV billing surfaced. This is absence of evidence, not proof of satisfaction.
- Momentum: the 2026 price sheet and a December 2025 form suggest ewl keeps the service current. This is inferred and no outage, acquisition or shutdown news was found.

## Pricing
- ewl's price sheet exists (2026 PDF), but the figures were not visible in search snippets. Price: unverified.
- Benchmark: zevvy is reported at 1 CHF per month per household (source: search summary of https://www.startup.ch/zevvy and solarimpulse.com listings, not independently checked).

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified, inferred from the service description)
- It is bundled with the utility and its region. A property manager with buildings across several grid operators would need another tool.
- It is a billing service with the utility in the loop, not self-serve software the manager controls.
- LEG support was not confirmed in the snippets. vZEV is mentioned only in general context.
- No evidence of manager-oriented features such as multi-property dashboards or integration with property-management software.

## Opening
A utility-agnostic, self-serve ZEV/vZEV/LEG billing tool for property managers with buildings in several grid areas is still open. The main pressure comes from zevvy and the other independents, not from ewl/Rapp.
