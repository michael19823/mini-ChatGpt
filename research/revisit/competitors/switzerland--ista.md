# ista (ista swiss ag), Switzerland

Idea killed: C0992, Switzerland ZEV/vZEV/LEG billing for property managers.
Searches used: 5 completed, 1 failed (usage limit). Evidence is thin.

## Verdict: strong

A real, commercial, actively developing incumbent that already sells ZEV, vZEV, LEG, VEWA and e-mobility billing to property managers on one platform. No Swiss-specific complaints were found.

## Evidence
- ista swiss ag offers a ZEV service covering ZEV, vZEV, LEG, VEWA and e-mobility, with a metering concept, device installation, digital consumption capture, automated billing and a portal for managers, owners and tenants. Sources: https://www.ista.com/ch/dienstleistungen/zev/ and https://www.ista.com/fileadmin/media_ista/switzerland_ch/Documents/de/Brochures/ista_swiss_ag_-_ZEV_Angebot.pdf
- It has an LEG news page, so it is active on the 2026 LEG rules: https://www.ista.com/ch/news/lokale-elektrizitaetsgemeinschaften-leg/
- Momentum: it acquired Blockstrom AG (Bern, ZEV and e-mobility billing software) on 4 March 2026, per the ista and Moneycab announcements. Sources: https://www.moneycab.com/energie/ista-swiss-ag-hat-die-blockstrom-ag-uebernommen/amp/ and https://www.ista.com/ch/unternehmen/presse/uebernahme-zev-pionier-blockstrom/
- The company says it has operated in Switzerland since 1973, with over 100 employees at Oftringen and Bussigny (from the company's own material).
- It has a ZEV reference case, Wylerhof Bern: https://www.ista.com/ch/cases/wylerhof-bern-zev/

## Complaints
- No Swiss ista complaints were found about ZEV or billing. The search returned only ista Deutschland reviews: Trustpilot about 2.2 of 5 from about 2,708 reviews, and ErfahrungenScout with 32% would choose again, 12 of 34 complaining about customer service. These cover the German entity and are not directly applicable to Switzerland. Sources: https://de.trustpilot.com/review/ista.com/de/location/79aebe17-44e6-441a-bbd3-8d6f7d87d188 and https://erfahrungenscout.de/dienstleistungen/ista-bewertungen?page=2
- Swiss-specific complaints may exist but were not found within the search budget (unverified).

## Pricing
- No ZEV price was found. The ZEV brochure and a general device and services price list exist (https://www.ista.com/fileadmin/twt_customer/countries/content/Switzerland/Broschueren/Allgemein/ista_87005-2-24_Preisliste_CH_Geraete_Dienstleistungen_RZ_WEB_DS.pdf), but I did not read their contents. Pricing is unverified.
- The offer starts with a consultation and an individual metering concept, which suggests quote-based pricing.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- Hardware- and installation-led offer, with ista fitting its own metering, so it may suit small managers or existing metering setups poorly.
- Quote-based and likely aimed at larger portfolios.
- Its generic HLKSE billing business and a just-acquired Blockstrom product may mean an integration period.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: a lightweight, software-only, self-serve ZEV/vZEV/LEG billing tool for small property managers who use their utility's existing meters. ista is a strong, well-funded incumbent here, and no evidence of customer unhappiness was found.
