# SH Power (Switzerland)

Killed idea: C0992, Switzerland ZEV/vZEV/LEG billing for property managers.

## Verdict: weak

SH Power is the local utility of Schaffhausen (Schweizersbildstrasse 71, 8200 Schaffhausen; shpower.ch). Its ZEV/vZEV/LEG billing is a grid-operator service for customers inside its own supply area. It is not a standalone product for property managers.

## Evidence
- SH Power publishes ZEV, vZEV and LEG pages with a ZEV registration form, a checklist and price sheets (Preisblatt vZEV, Preisblatt LEG). Sources: https://www.shpower.ch/fileadmin/images/private/energie/eigenverbrauch-vzev-leg/Anmeldung-ZEV.pdf , https://www.shpower.ch/fileadmin/images/private/energie/eigenverbrauch-vzev-leg/Checkliste-ZEV.pdf , https://www.shpower.ch/fileadmin/images/private/energie/eigenverbrauch-vzev-leg/Preisblatt-vZEV.pdf , https://www.shpower.ch/fileadmin/images/private/energie/eigenverbrauch-vzev-leg/Preisblatt-LEG.pdf
- Per the search summaries, it offers optional modules from customer management through consulting to billing. It can read meters, work out each participant's consumption, issue bills and handle collection for the ZEV operator. This is based on search-result summaries of the PDFs; I did not read the PDFs in full.
- Contact for ZEV questions is emi@shpower.ch (from the same results).
- Momentum: LEG and vZEV price sheets exist, so it is keeping up with the 2025/2026 law changes. Other dates and activity are unverified.

## Complaints
None found. Four searches in German and English returned only SH Power's own documents and general explainers. They turned up no app-store reviews, forum posts or news about problems. This does not show customers are happy. Little is published about it.

## Pricing
Only the ZEV figures were stated in the search results. Verify them against the current price sheet.
- One-time setup of CHF 350.
- Meter fee of CHF 40 per meter for the first 5 meters, then CHF 20 per meter from the 6th.
- Recurring fee of CHF 7.00 per meter per month.
- vZEV and LEG prices were not visible in the snippets (unverified).

At CHF 7 per meter per month, a 20-unit building would pay roughly CHF 1,680 a year in recurring fees, which is a modest price.

## Fit gaps
- Tied to the Schaffhausen grid area. It is no use to property managers with buildings elsewhere in Switzerland.
- It is a utility offering with modules, not self-service software for managers running many buildings and several utilities.
- It relies on SH Power-supplied meters (stated in the search results for ZEV).
- Other gaps (portfolio view, tenant portal, multilingual support, API) are unverified.

## Opening
A portfolio-wide, utility-independent billing tool for property managers with buildings across several grid areas. SH Power is a local utility service and covers only its own area.
