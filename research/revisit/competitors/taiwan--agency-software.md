# Taiwan: "agency software" (generic; no named product) - killed idea C1001 (Taiwan migrant-worker broker paperwork)

## Verdict: weak
No specific commercial broker-management software product was found. The only visible "competitor" is the government's free Ministry of Labor online application system, which is an official filing channel, not a broker workflow tool.

## Evidence (5 searches used of 6; Chinese and English)
- No named vendor, product, price list, app-store listing or review for Taiwan foreign-labour broker management software surfaced in any query (zh: broker management system / ERP / reviews on PTT and Dcard; en: Taiwan migrant worker broker agency management software). Product existence is unverified; any in-house or niche vendor tools may exist but were not found.
- Complaints: none found. No user reviews of broker software were returned.
- Ministry of Labor (MOL) runs a free online application system since 11 May 2016 covering manufacturing, ocean fishing, household helpers/care workers and institutional care: employment permits, departure check-out, missing-worker reports, initial recruitment, transfers, re-recruitment, reissuance. Reported average review is 4 days online vs 12 days on paper, with online payment and email notices. MOL added online-application rate as an evaluation indicator for brokers. Source: https://www.mol.gov.tw/1607/1632/1640/43717/ (search summary; page not opened).
- Policy momentum: in Oct 2021 the Minister of Labour announced a multilingual platform so workers/employers can self-file and avoid broker document withholding (https://hrmasia.com/?p=76146, https://taiwaninsight.org/2022/01/19/taiwans-migrant-workers-versus-labour-brokerage-system/). This is a risk to the broker-paperwork market itself, not a software rival.
- Market size context: more than 1,700 active registered broker firms and about 710,000 migrant workers (Taiwan Insight, 2022 figures). MOL runs annual broker quality evaluations (https://news.tvbs.com.tw/life/805660).

## Pricing
Unverified; no software prices found. Government system is free. Broker service fees are legally capped at NT$1,800 / 1,700 / 1,500 per month for years 1 / 2 / 3 (search summary of MOL-related sources).

## Fit gaps
Unverified for commercial tools since none was identified. For the MOL portal: it is a government filing portal, so it does not appear to cover broker back-office needs (multi-employer case tracking, document expiry reminders, worker-language communication, billing). This is inference, not verified.

## Opening
Brokers have many cases across 1,700+ firms with a free but filing-only government portal and no visible commercial rival; the main risk is regulatory (self-filing reforms shrinking broker paperwork), not competition.
