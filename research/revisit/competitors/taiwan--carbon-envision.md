# Carbon EnVision (Systex / 精誠資訊), Taiwan

Killed idea: C0999 (Taiwan SME GHG inventory and supplier carbon-data SaaS)

## Verdict: beatable

Carbon EnVision is a real, active SaaS, but the evidence shows it aimed at large manufacturers and financial firms and sold bundled with consulting. SME fit is unproven. This verdict rests on only 3 working searches (1 hit the usage limit), so confidence is moderate.

## Evidence
- Cloud carbon management system by Systex (精誠資訊), Taiwan. Won the 2025 Taiwan Excellence Silver Award. Follows ISO 14064-1 and has BSI AUP verification. Source: https://www.cio.com.tw/2024/12/83318/ and https://ec.ltn.com.tw/article/breakingnews/4375413
- Features: organisational inventory boundary and baseline setup, supplier data exchange across upstream and downstream, integration of internal activity data, real-time emission calculation. Claims to cover about 70% of upstream and downstream value-chain footprint and to cut data-collection labour by about 50% (vendor claims, unverified). Source: https://www.cio.com.tw/2024/12/83318/
- Languages: Chinese, English, Vietnamese. Source: same search results.
- Reported customers are large manufacturers (semiconductor, medical devices, energy) and finance. No SME customers were found. Source: search summaries of cio.com.tw and ctee.com.tw (https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20241216700804-430201).
- Delivered as SaaS together with Systex consultants.
- Momentum: active, with 2024-2025 press coverage and an award.
- Complaints: none found. No user reviews (G2, Capterra, app stores, forums) turned up. The search that targeted Chinese-language reviews returned only press and marketing pages.

## Pricing
Not published. Unverified. Nothing found on SME pricing or minimums. The consulting-bundled model suggests enterprise-level contracts.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at large enterprises. No evidence of an SME tier, self-serve signup or a low price (unverified).
- Consultant-led delivery, which is likely too heavy and costly for small suppliers.
- Supplier data exchange exists, but whether small suppliers can join cheaply or for free is unknown.
- No evidence of mobile or offline use, or of the Taiwan MOENV or Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) disclosure formats (unverified).

## Opening
A low-cost, self-serve inventory and supplier-data tool for Taiwan SMEs and small suppliers sits below Systex's large-enterprise, consultant-bundled offer. Check the price and SME packaging directly before relying on this.
