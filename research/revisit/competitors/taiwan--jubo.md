# Jubo (智齡科技, Jubo Health) - Taiwan

Ideas it killed: C1000 (Taiwan long-term care home-service billing and records), C1003 (Taiwan long-term care home-service management).
Searches used: 5 of 6 (English and Traditional Chinese).

## Verdict: strong

Jubo is a well-funded, market-leading care SaaS vendor in Taiwan that lists home-service units among its customers. No complaints or fit-gap evidence was found to offset that.

## Evidence
- Self-described as Asia's largest senior-care technology company, founded 2018 and spun out of a National Taiwan University project (Dealroom/PitchBook-type profiles via search; Chanchao ATLife product page: https://www.chanchao.com.tw/ATLIFE/en/visitorProductDetail.asp?no=210223).
- Scale claims, which are vendor or press figures and unverified: about 30% Taiwan market share and 1,200+ care institutions (Cheers/CommonWealth, https://www.cw.com.tw/article/5134851). A later CommonWealth piece says 2,700+ long-term care, day-care and home-service units (https://www.cw.com.tw/article/5127666).
- Momentum: Compal's subsidiary Compal Health is merging into Jubo (effective 2026-01-01). Compal is expected to hold about 11.1% of Jubo (https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/8376235019). The company is hiring actively (Yourator job listings) and plans overseas expansion.
- Taiwan's Long-term Care 3.0 push from 2026 is a tailwind for it.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, PTT or Dcard complaints about Jubo surfaced. The one Chinese forum-style query returned only news and government documents. This is absence of evidence, not proof of satisfaction.

## Pricing
Not published in anything found: unverified. The searches returned no price or minimum.

## Fit gaps
- Product emphasis found is institutional: residential care homes, day care, EHR, vitals, wound AI, handover records, tablet and cart hardware (Care Cart), and a family app. Home-service-specific billing, claim filing or caregiver dispatch features were not confirmed. That is unverified, and Jubo's own materials say it serves "home service units".
- It looks aimed at mid-to-large operators, since it sells an enterprise dashboard and hardware. A solo or small home-care agency may be underserved or priced out, but this is unverified.
- Other Taiwan home-service tools exist and were not checked in depth. One example from results is a "聯護管理系統" that claims automated dispatch through claim settlement (派工到核銷) and a 75% time saving.

## Opening
A cheap, mobile-first billing and claims tool for small home-service agencies could work, but only if Jubo's home-service module proves thin or expensive. That needs checking before the idea is revived, because Jubo's share, funding and Compal backing make it hard to displace.
