# Taiwan: listed IT firms (carbon / GHG inventory platforms) - dropped idea C1002

## Verdict: beatable

Several listed IT firms sell carbon inventory platforms, but the searches show them aimed at large and mid-size enterprises, mostly sold as integration projects. They publish no prices, and I found no user complaints. The SME segment looks underserved but is not empty.

## Evidence
- Listed vendors named in Taiwanese press (moneyweekly, ctee, uanalyze, cnyes): Systex/精誠 (6214) "Carbon Envision", 叡揚 (6752) "NetZero 零碳雲", 倍力 (6874) "碳管家", 東捷 (6697) "碳捷流", and eCloudvalley/伊雲谷 (6689). Sources: https://www.moneyweekly.com.tw/_Article?AID=125950 , https://ctee.com.tw/news/tech/905931.html , https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/235359016
- Per search summaries, 精誠 and 東捷 serve mainly large enterprises as system integrators. 叡揚 sells a SaaS subscription but reportedly lacks consulting staff. 東捷 positions itself as the cheaper alternative to SAP's carbon module. The search tool's summary gave these points without quoting the article text, so treat them as lightly verified.
- Carbon Envision lists multi-site inventory, OCR for electricity bills and ISO 14064-1 aligned reports (search summary).
- Other Taiwan vendors: Sustaihub (DCarbon), TPIsoftware (GreenSwift, ISO 14064-1 verified), Galaxy Software Services (Vital NetZero, described as suited to SMEs; https://technews.tw/2022/10/24/everbiz-gss-vital-net-zero/ ). These are not necessarily the listed firms, but they crowd the segment.
- Market context: SME demand is large (industry estimate of 100k to 200k companies) and SMEs struggle with where to start, data provision and keeping evidence (https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202310270176.aspx ; one survey cited 78.6% of firms don't know how to calculate a carbon footprint).
- Complaints: none found. Searches for reviews on PTT, Dcard and app stores returned only news and surveys. No app-store or G2 reviews were surfaced. The result is "no complaints found", not "no complaints exist".

## Pricing
Not published for any of these products (unverified). The sources say only that subscriptions exist (叡揚) and that 東捷 is cheaper than SAP. A small buyer would likely need a sales quote.

## Fit gaps
- Sales motion is enterprise and integrator-led (2023 sources), which suggests small firms get little attention (unverified for 2026).
- No evidence of self-serve onboarding, transparent SME pricing or a fixed tier for SMEs.
- Consulting and verification support is thin at some vendors (叡揚).
- No evidence either way on mobile or offline use.

## Momentum
Active in 2023 when carbon-fee and carbon-exchange news drove launches. The 2026 carbon fee (NT$300/t, only for emitters above 25,000 t) is not aimed at SMEs. I found no 2026 revenue or order data, so current momentum is unverified.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, Chinese-language GHG inventory tool for SME suppliers (customer-driven Scope 1/2 reporting) fits where the listed firms' enterprise and integrator focus leaves room, though SME-leaning rivals (Vital NetZero, Sustaihub) exist.
