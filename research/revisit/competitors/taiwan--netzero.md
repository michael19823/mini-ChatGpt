# NetZero (Taiwan) - competitor check for C0999

Idea killed: C0999, Taiwan SME GHG inventory and supplier carbon-data SaaS.

Note: "NetZero" has no known URL and is ambiguous. Search found no single Taiwan product called plain "NetZero". The likely candidates are NetZeroPro (歐碳科技, EuroCarbon Technology) and Vital NetZero 零碳雲 (叡揚資訊, Galaxy Software Services). Both are assessed below. Salesforce Net Zero Cloud and the UK "NetZero Lite" also surfaced but are not Taiwan SME products.

## Verdict: beatable

Both candidates are real, active Taiwan products, but they look like enterprise or integrator-sold tools. I found no public SME self-serve pricing and no user complaints.

## Evidence
- Vital NetZero 零碳雲 (叡揚資訊): modules for compliance management and net-zero indicator management. Integrates ISO 14064 and ISO 50001. Pitched at SMEs. Listed on Microsoft AppSource and sold via Taiwan's Cloud Marketplace. Sources: https://technews.tw/2022/10/24/everbiz-gss-vital-net-zero/ , https://technews.tw/?p=977091 , https://appsource.microsoft.com/es-ec/product/saas/galaxysoftwareservices.vitalnetzero
- Vital NetZero (English listing): supports ISO 14064-1, CBAM reporting and Taiwan GHG inventory guidelines. Includes more than 20,000 emission factors from ecoinvent. Source: https://www.tmts.tw/en/product/2159
- NetZeroPro (歐碳科技, founded 2023 per press): supports GHG Protocol, ISO 14064-1, ISO 14067 and IFRS S1/S2. SGS AUP in Oct 2022. Named customers are Shin Kong Financial, Shin Kong Steel and Tsing Hua University, which are large or institutional. Source: https://n.yam.com/Article/20251202621313
- Momentum: NetZeroPro had a Dec 2025 launch press release, and Vital NetZero has press from 2022 to later. Both appear active. Not shut down or acquired as far as I found.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in Chinese and English across app stores, forums, PTT/Dcard and review sites returned only press releases and vendor pages. This is a valid "no complaints found" result, not evidence of satisfied users.

## Pricing
- NetZeroPro: no public price found (unverified).
- Vital NetZero: no list price found. The Cloud Marketplace subsidy reportedly covers 50% of cost, up to 30,000 points, per the search summary (unverified at the primary source). This implies a price band where a subsidy matters.
- NetZero Lite (UK, GBP 49 per month) is unrelated to Taiwan and is cited only as a price reference point.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence found of supplier-facing carbon data collection that is cheap for tiny suppliers (the "supplier carbon-data" side). Unverified, since the search had no product detail.
- NetZeroPro's references skew to large firms. Vital NetZero comes from an IT integrator, so likely sold with services.
- No public self-serve pricing, so a small buyer cannot easily try either.
- Mobile or offline use and multi-entity support: not found (unverified).

## Opening
Taiwan has at least two funded local vendors with standards support, so the opening is narrow: a transparent low-price, self-serve tool for SME suppliers that responds to buyer requests from large customers, not a general inventory tool.
