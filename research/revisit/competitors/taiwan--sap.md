# SAP (Taiwan) - competitor check

Ideas it killed: C0999 (Taiwan SME GHG inventory and supplier carbon-data SaaS); C1002 (Taiwan carbon / GHG inventory platforms).

## Verdict: beatable

SAP does have a Taiwan-specific GHG inventory product and it targets SMEs in its marketing. But it is an enterprise ERP-adjacent vendor with block-based pricing and complex setup. The segment of small suppliers that need a cheap, simple tool looks underserved.

## Evidence
- SAP Taiwan launched a GHG inventory solution for Taiwanese companies. Press coverage says it is SGS-validated, follows Ministry of Environment guidelines, and is meant to help local SMEs speed up inventories. Planned additions: auto-generated reports and generative-AI mapping of emission factors (so some of this is roadmap, not shipped; unverified which parts are live). Sources: https://insidesap.asia/sap-taiwan-opens-esg-and-ai-research-center-in-kaohsiung/ , https://finance.technews.tw/2024/09/04/sap-esg-ai/ , https://news.sap.com/taiwan/?p=2245
- SAP opened an ESG and AI research center in Kaohsiung (2024), so there is real local investment. Source: https://insidesap.asia/sap-taiwan-opens-esg-and-ai-research-center-in-kaohsiung/
- SAP Taiwan also lists a partner offering (ITIS CarbonX smart cloud) for carbon data and certification reports, so the SME channel runs through partners. Source: https://www.sap.com/taiwan/products/technology-platform/partners/information-technology-total-services-carbonx-smart-cloud.html
- Complaints: the G2 listing for SAP Sustainability Footprint Management (4.2/5, about 70 reviews per the search summary) shows recurring themes of complex setup and data integration needing technical expertise, and cost described as prohibitive for smaller businesses. One reviewer reportedly says it is not cost-efficient for SMEs. This is a search-result summary, not read directly. Source: https://g2.com/products/sap-sustainability-footprint-management/reviews
- No Taiwan-specific user complaints (PTT, Dcard, app stores, Facebook groups) were found in the searches run. Not found does not mean none exist.
- Context: one news source cites 78.6% of surveyed Taiwan firms not knowing how to calculate carbon footprint, and about 190,000 of 1.5M+ SMEs needing inventories because of supply-chain or export demands (secondary figures, unverified). Source: https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202310270176.aspx
- Momentum: actively developed and investing in Taiwan (2024 to 2025 coverage). Not stagnant.

## Pricing
- SAP Sustainability Footprint Management is priced per block of 50,000 records with a minimum of three blocks; contract terms are on request. Source: https://www.sap.com/korea/products/scm/sustainability-footprint-management/pricing.html (Korea page; Taiwan price unverified).
- No published TWD price for the Taiwan GHG inventory solution found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Enterprise-sized buying model (minimum block quantities, implementation effort), a poor fit for micro and small suppliers.
- Sold largely via SAP and partners, so SMEs likely need an integrator.
- Focus is on the company's own inventory; a lightweight supplier data-collection flow for small suppliers that do not run SAP is not evident (unverified).
- Some AI and report-generation features were announced as upcoming.

## Opening
A very low-priced, self-serve, Chinese-language tool for small Taiwanese suppliers to produce an inventory and share data with buyers (including buyers who run SAP) is still open.
