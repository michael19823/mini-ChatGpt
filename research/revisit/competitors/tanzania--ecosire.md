# Ecosire (Tanzania) - competitor check

Ideas it killed: C1009 (Tanzania ERP to VFD/EFDMS sales fiscalisation connector), C1016 (Tanzania VFD/EFD sales-side fiscalisation).

## Verdict: beatable

Ecosire sells a build-to-order custom project, not a self-serve product. It covers only Odoo and ERPNext, and nothing found shows it is a Tanzania-focused vendor.

## Evidence
- Ecosire lists "odoo tanzania tra efd" and "erpnext tanzania tra efd" pages. Source: https://ecosire.com/apps/odoo/odoo-tanzania-tra-efd and https://ecosire.com/apps/erpnext/erpnext-tanzania-tra-efd
- The ERPNext page describes a Frappe app that fiscalises Sales Invoices against TRA EFD/VFD and returns the fiscal receipt number, verification code and QR to the invoice. It is built, installed and supported by Ecosire for ERPNext v15/v16 after a scoping call.
- The Odoo page covers Odoo 17, 18 and 19 and says go-live takes about 2-4 weeks, with a post-launch support window. It claims to remove re-keying of invoices into a separate terminal or portal.
- Ecosire is a multi-platform ERP app and services shop (Odoo, ERPNext, Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, Dynamics 365), claiming 2,000+ apps. The same site listing is cloned into many languages. This looks like a templated catalogue, and I could not verify any Tanzanian customer, local presence or live TRA certification. Unverified.
- The search results did not state which TRA interface it uses (EFDMS or VFD API).
- Momentum: the website appears actively maintained (2026 comparison pages). Beyond that, unverified.

## Complaints
None found. Searches for Ecosire reviews on Trustpilot, Reddit and similar sites returned nothing about this company, only unrelated firms with similar names. Absence of reviews is not evidence of quality. It more likely means a small or new vendor.

## Pricing
Indicative price from $499 USD for the ERPNext app, per the Ecosire ERPNext page. A quote is required after a scoping call. The Odoo page also shows "from $499" in the search snippets. The $499 figure is a floor, so the final price is unverified. It is per-site and per-ERP, with no published subscription.

## Fit gaps
- It is only for businesses already running Odoo or ERPNext, so it excludes small Tanzanian merchants on Tally, QuickBooks, Zoho, Sage, spreadsheets or local POS software.
- It is bespoke and slow (2-4 weeks, scoping call), so it does not suit a self-serve small buyer.
- No sign of Swahili language support in the product. The site translates, but nothing says the product UI does.
- No evidence found of offline or mobile use, or of multi-entity or multi-branch handling.
- No public documentation, demo or reviews to verify that it works.

## Opening
A self-serve, low-price or subscription fiscalisation connector for non-Odoo and non-ERPNext accounting and POS tools (or standalone invoicing) with Swahili UI is still open.
