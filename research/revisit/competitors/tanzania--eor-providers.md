# Tanzania: EOR providers (Deel, Multiplier, Gloroots, Rivermate, Remote and similar)

Idea dropped: C1008, Tanzania payroll statutory filings (PAYE, SDL, NSSF, WCF, HESLB).

## Verdict: weak

EOR providers do cover Tanzanian statutory filings, but only bundled with employment of record. They cost USD 199-599 per employee per month, so they are not a real option for a local SME running its own payroll. Local payroll software (HONO, Enerpize, Odoo l10n_tz_payroll, Ontop) is the more relevant competition. It was surfaced in search but not assessed here.

## Evidence
- EOR providers handle PAYE, SDL, NSSF and WCF as part of the service (no HESLB mention found). Sources: https://www.gloroots.com/blog/best-eor-in-tanzania, https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/tanzania, https://www.anywherer.com/eor-in-tanzania/
- Rivermate says Eos, Serviap and Hightekers EOR are now Rivermate, so the market is consolidating. Source: https://rivermate.com/de/leitfaden/tansania/personalkosten-rechner
- Tanzania-specific complaints: none found. Searches in English and Swahili returned only vendor and guide pages.
- Generic EOR complaints, not Tanzania-specific: Deel support slow in early months and handoffs on complex legal questions; Multiplier communication and invoicing issues. Source: https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/multiplier/deel. This is an aggregator, so treat it as unverified.
- Local payroll tools exist: HONO (https://www.hono.ai/solutions/africa/tanzania), Enerpize (https://www.enerpize.com/hub/hr-payroll-software-tanzania), Odoo module (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/l10n_tz_payroll). Quality and pricing unverified.
- Momentum: active and well funded. Deel is rated 4.9 on Trustpilot (2,507 reviews) per the aggregator, which is unverified.

## Pricing
- EOR range quoted as USD 199-599 per employee per month. Source: https://www.getontop.com/payroll-in/tanzania (search summary).
- Deel is quoted at USD 599, Multiplier at USD 400 and Gloroots at USD 299 per employee per month. Prices come from search summaries and are unverified.
- That is far too expensive for local-staff payroll at an SME, since it includes employment liability and contracts the buyer does not need.

## Fit gaps
- The buyer must hand over employment to the EOR, which is not wanted by a firm that already employs staff locally.
- Pricing is aimed at foreign firms hiring a few people, not at local SMEs with 5-50 staff.
- No evidence of HESLB loan-deduction handling, or of Swahili-language filing support. Both are unverified.
- No evidence of TRA, NSSF or WCF portal filing for a firm's own payroll as a standalone product.

## Opening
A cheap standalone Tanzanian payroll-filing tool for local SMEs (PAYE, SDL, NSSF, WCF, HESLB, in Swahili) is not served by EOR providers. The real check is the local payroll tools.
