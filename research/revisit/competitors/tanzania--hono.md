# HONO (Tanzania) - competitor check

Dropped ideas: C1008 (Tanzania payroll statutory filings PAYE SDL NSSF WCF HESLB), C1015 (Tanzania payroll).

## Verdict: strong (on features and enterprise segment); small-employer segment unproven

## Evidence
- HONO runs a dedicated Tanzania page claiming automation of NSSF, PAYE, SDL and TRA filings. It also claims WCF and HESLB in the same payroll run, plus mobile employee self-service (payslips, leave). Source: https://www.hono.ai/solutions/africa/tanzania (as shown in search snippets; page not fetched).
- Positioned as an "AI-native" enterprise HCM for Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Nigeria, Zambia and Mauritius, with multi-entity and multi-country payroll. It claims 300+ clients, ISO 27001 and SOC2 Type 2, and cloud, hybrid or on-premise deployment. Sources: https://www.hono.ai/solutions/africa and https://www.hono.ai/hr-software/africa (search snippets).
- Third-party listings exist (GetApp, SpotSaaS comparison pages, e.g. https://www.getapp.za.com/software/2079637/hono), but no review content was seen.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum complaints surfaced in 6 searches, including a Swahili query. This is a lack of evidence, not evidence of satisfaction.
- Momentum: the site is current (2026 event banner for People Matters TechHR India '26), which suggests active marketing. Funding, acquisition and release cadence were not found (unverified).

## Pricing
Not published; "contact us". Enterprise positioning. The price for a small Tanzanian employer is unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred from positioning, unverified)
- Enterprise and multi-country focus, so likely overkill or too costly for small and medium employers and accountants with a few clients.
- No evidence of a self-serve signup, a free tier, or a Swahili interface.
- No evidence of offline or lightweight use.
- Not verified: actual filing accuracy, whether files are uploaded to TRA/NSSF portals or only exported, and local support.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, Swahili-friendly statutory payroll tool for small Tanzanian employers or payroll bureaus, with transparent pricing. HONO targets enterprises and gives no public price.
