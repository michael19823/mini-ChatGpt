# Odoo (Tanzania) vs C1015 Tanzania payroll

## Verdict: beatable (low confidence; thin evidence)

## Evidence
- A third-party Odoo module "l10n_tz_payroll" exists on the Odoo Apps store for v18 and v19 (Community and Enterprise). It computes PAYE (TRA bands 0/8/20/25/30%), NSSF 10%/10%, SDL 3.5% and WCF 0.5%, and produces payroll registers (CSV/Excel) and remittance summaries by agency. Source: https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/l10n_tz_payroll and https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_tz_payroll
- Per the search summary, the vendor is listed as https://pokutsoft.com/ (not Odoo S.A.). I found no official Odoo Tanzania payroll localization (unverified; absence of evidence only).
- The module produces figures and files only. The user files via TRA, NSSF and WCF with their own credentials, so there is no direct e-filing (per listing text). Source: same app pages.
- Odoo's general payroll localization coverage is thin outside the US and Europe, and the full payroll engine is Enterprise-only (user forum posts, not Tanzania-specific). Sources: https://www.odoo.com/forum/help-1/payroll-localization-238635 and https://www.odoo.com/forum/help-1/payroll-for-v17-and-v18-257034
- Complaints: none found specific to Odoo in Tanzania. Searches in English and Swahili returned no Tanzanian reviews or complaints. Generic Odoo Capterra rating is 4.2/5 from 1,307 reviews, but I did not read the review text. Source: https://capterra.com/p/135618/Odoo/pricing/
- Other Tanzania payroll options surfaced: Lenvica (https://lenvica.com/payroll-software-for-tanzania-with-paye-nssf/) and Enerpize (https://www.enerpize.com/hub/hr-payroll-software-tanzania). Not assessed.
- Momentum: the module is current (2026 PAYE bands, v18/v19), so it is actively maintained by a small third party. No Odoo S.A. Tanzania push found.

## Pricing
- Odoo Enterprise: Standard $31.10 per user per month (annual billing), Custom $61.00 per user per month. Source: https://capterra.com/p/135618/Odoo/pricing/
- The Community edition is free, and the module claims to work on it. The module's own price: not found (unverified).
- Odoo is an ERP, so a small firm that needs only payroll pays for a much bigger system and an implementer (implementation cost unverified).

## Fit gaps
- No e-filing or direct TRA/NSSF/WCF submission; output is registers and summaries only.
- Needs Odoo setup, hosting and a partner, which is heavy for a small employer that only wants payroll.
- No evidence of mobile or WhatsApp payslips, mobile-money salary payouts, or a Swahili interface (unverified).
- Support depends on a small third-party module author.

## Opening
Odoo's Tanzania payroll is only a community-style add-on that calculates figures and does no filing, so a lightweight, standalone, Swahili-friendly payroll with statutory returns and payslip delivery for small employers still has room.
