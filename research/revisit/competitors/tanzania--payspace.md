# PaySpace (Tanzania) - competitor check

Killed ideas: C1008 (Tanzania payroll statutory filings PAYE SDL NSSF WCF HESLB), C1015 (Tanzania payroll).

## Verdict: strong (for mid/large employers); beatable only for micro/small buyers

## Evidence
- PaySpace is a commercial cloud payroll/HR product with built-in compliance for 40+ African markets plus UK, Brazil, UAE, 19,000+ customers, acquired by Deel in 2024 (kolonell.com blog, https://kolonell.com/en/blog/payroll-saas-africa-2026 - secondary source). Now listed on G2 as "Deel Local Payroll, powered by PaySpace".
- Search did not confirm a Tanzania-specific PaySpace page; Tanzania coverage (PAYE/SDL/NSSF/WCF/HESLB) is unverified, inferred from pan-African claim.
- Complaints: very few. G2 (https://www.g2.com/products/deel-local-payroll-powered-by-payspace/reviews): ~4.9/5 from 5 reviews; one mentions "integration issues" and support delays on technical glitches; another wants better regional automation and configurable mandatory onboarding fields. Capterra: 1 review, 5.0, says report formatting could be better. No Tanzania-specific complaints, outage or failed-filing news found. No app-store, Reddit, Facebook or Swahili-language complaints found (Swahili search returned only generic guides and other vendors).
- Momentum: active, backed by Deel since 2024 (as reported in the secondary source).

## Pricing
Not published; quote-based. One aggregator-type source estimates ~$5-15 per employee per month (unverified estimate, likely with implementation minimums). 

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified)
- Sales-led, quote-only: poor for tiny firms and accountants wanting self-serve.
- Likely priced/oriented for mid-market and multinationals.
- No evidence of Swahili UI, mobile-money (M-Pesa/Tigo Pesa) payout, or direct TRA/NSSF/WCF/HESLB portal filing formats; unverified.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, Swahili-friendly Tanzania payroll with TRA/NSSF/WCF/HESLB-ready filings for SMEs and accountants; not competitive against PaySpace/Deel for mid-large or multinational employers.
