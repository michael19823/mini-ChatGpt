# POWERHMS (Tanzania) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C1010, Tanzania NHIF claim submission module.
Searches used: 4 of 6 (English and Swahili). No URL was known for the competitor.

## Verdict
**weak** (low confidence). I found no verifiable evidence that POWERHMS is an active NHIF-claims competitor.
The more serious blockers for C1010 are other systems, listed below.

## Evidence
- Searches for "POWERHMS", "PowerHMS" and "Power HMS" with NHIF or Tanzania returned no product page, review, news item or complaint naming POWERHMS and NHIF together. Unverified: whether it has any NHIF claims feature.
- Search summaries mention "Powerweb's Hospital Management Software", said to be owned by PowerComputers Telecommunications Ltd in Tanzania, commercial and not free to customize. Source: https://dspace.nm-aist.ac.tz/bitstream/20.500.12479/826/1/JA_CoCSE_2018%20.pdf. Whether this is the same product as POWERHMS is unverified.
- SoftwareSuggest has comparison pages for an Indian "Power HMS" product (https://www.softwaresuggest.com/compare/ehospital-hospital-management-vs-power-hms). It may be a different product with the same name. Unverified.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints about POWERHMS turned up in English or Swahili.
- Momentum: unknown. No release, acquisition or shutdown information found.

## Larger threats to C1010 (not POWERHMS, from the same searches)
- GoTHOMIS is the government HMIS. It exchanges data with the NHIF claims system. Source: https://uatweb.usaid.gov/sites/default/files/2022-05/PS3_Case_Brief_-_GoTHOMIS.pdf
- A presidential directive reportedly required all Ministry of Health systems to be integrated by 15 Jan 2025. Source: https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-s-deputy-health-minister-urges-system-integration-to-address-nhif-losses-and-medical-cost-inefficiencies-4852688
- Open-source options exist: HMS Tz (ERPNext, has NHIF claims) https://github.com/av-dev2/hms_tz and a Laravel NHIF package https://packagist.org/packages/omakei/laravel-nhif.
- Claim rejections from documentation, coding and compliance errors are a stated pain point. Source: https://iskm.issa.int/node/5667. This supports demand for claim-prep tooling, but not a gap in POWERHMS.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
Unknown. Nothing was found on POWERHMS features, buyer size, offline or mobile use, or language support.

## Opening
POWERHMS itself does not block C1010. The real question is whether GoTHOMIS integration and the open-source NHIF modules leave room for a claim-quality or rejection-reduction tool. That belongs to the GoTHOMIS and HMS Tz competitor checks, not this one.
