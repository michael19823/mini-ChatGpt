# Tanzania: Tally (TallyPrime, Tally Solutions)

Ideas killed: C1009 (ERP to VFD/EFDMS connector), C1016 (VFD/EFD sales-side fiscalisation)

## Verdict: beatable

Tally is a real, actively marketed accounting/ERP product with Tanzania VFD/EFDMS content, but it only covers Tally users. It does not cover a standalone connector for other ERPs or small shops. The depth of its integration is unverified.
