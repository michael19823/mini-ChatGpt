# Thailand - law firms (consultants), dropped idea C1027: Thailand PDPA compliance

## Verdict: beatable

Law firms and consultancies are the incumbent route, but they focus on large clients and enforcement is rising. No small-business-priced, self-serve option was found in the 4 searches I ran (4 of 6 used).

## Evidence
- Thai and regional firms sell PDPA services (data mapping, gap analysis, training, privacy policies, consent forms, transfer agreements). Examples named in results: Tilleke & Gibbins, Rajah & Tann Asia, DFDL, OneAsia Legal, plus consultancies PDPA Thailand and PDPA Core. Source: https://www.tilleke.com/insights/advising-the-worlds-largest-companies-on-pdpa-compliance/ , https://pdpathailand.com/en/about-en/
- Firm marketing leans on large-client credentials (Tilleke headline: "advising the world's largest companies"; Rajah & Tann: Lazada). Suggests enterprise focus. Source: URLs above and https://rajahtannasia.com/?p=36564
- Software competitors exist: PDPA Core (services plus software, bundled Cookie Wow, Consent Wow, PDPA Form), D-PDPA by OSD, Seers (14-day free trial), OneTrust (enterprise). Source: https://techindex.law.stanford.edu/companies/pdpa-core , https://seers.ai/regulation/thailand-personal-data-protection-act-pdpa/
- Demand/need signals: a readiness survey reported only 8% of ~4,000 businesses fully compliant and 31% not started (secondary report, unverified). PDPC fines: IT retailer 7M baht, private hospital over 1.2M baht. Source: https://mondaq.com/privacy-protection/1696208/thailands-pdpc-signals-tougher-enforcement-with-multi-million-baht-fines
- Government help for SMEs: DEPA runs a PDPA training programme for small firms. Source: https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/2334393/agency-begins-pdpa-training-programme-for-small-firms
- SME and small-organisation exemptions from record-keeping (ROPA) took effect in 2025 per search summaries (details unverified), which reduces the compliance workload a tool must cover.
- Complaints: none found. No reviews or forum criticism of law firms' PDPA services surfaced (search coverage was shallow; Thai-language searches returned mostly enforcement news and Singapore material).

## Pricing
No published prices for Thai law-firm PDPA work found; firms appear to quote on request (unverified). Do not assume a figure. (Singapore's SME legal advice scheme at S$500 per hour appeared in results but is a different country.)

## Fit gaps
- Bespoke, quote-based engagements suit large firms; no evidence of a packaged small-business offer (inferred from marketing, unverified).
- Law firms do not offer ongoing self-serve tooling; software players exist but are partly enterprise or English-first (unverified for Thai UI).
- Small buyers can't verify what they need given the SME exemptions and unclear definitions.

## Opening
A cheap, Thai-language, self-serve PDPA kit for SMEs (policies, consent, breach and data-subject-request workflows) sits below law-firm pricing, but PDPA Core and Seers already occupy part of that space, so it needs a clear price or niche edge.
