# Thailand: privacy tools (PDPA compliance) — competitor check

Killed idea: C1027 Thailand PDPA compliance. Competitor type: generic (no URL known). 5 WebSearch calls used (English and Thai).

## Verdict: strong

Several established vendors already cover the segment, including a local one with published low SME prices. No complaints found, so there is no evidence of an unserved or dissatisfied buyer.

## Evidence
- OneTrust has a dedicated Thailand PDPA solution page covering data subject requests, records, PIAs and incident response. https://onetrust.com/solutions/thailand-pdpa-compliance/
- Local vendors exist: PDPA Core (services plus Cookie Wow, PDPA Form, Consent Wow) https://techindex.law.stanford.edu/companies/pdpa-core ; Cookie Wow https://cookiewow.com/en ; bePDPA (search summary describes it as a privacy information management platform with cookie consent, data subject rights and breach notification; its own site was not seen, so unverified).
- Foreign cookie/consent platforms also market Thailand PDPA pages: CookieHub https://www.cookiehub.com/pdpa-thailand , Secure Privacy https://secureprivacy.ai/laws/thailand-pdpa , Cookie-Script.
- Demand context: an NSTDA-linked study of 384 SMEs found very low compliance (average 6.19 out of 100). https://www.nstda.or.th/openarchive/nstda-research-publications/personal-data-protection-compliance-assessment-a-privacy-policy-scoring-approach-and-empirical-evidence-from-thailand-s-smes/ . The PDPC had recorded 2,672 complaints as of January 2026 (per search summary; source page not opened). So the need is real, but it is already being served.
- Complaints: none found. Searches for reviews, "too expensive" and Thai-language complaints returned only vendor and law-firm pages. No app-store, G2, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews were surfaced.
- Momentum: not established. No acquisition or shutdown news found. Cookie Wow has a WordPress plugin listing, so it appears active (unverified).

## Pricing
- Cookie Wow (per search summary of its site): Small 259 THB/month or 2,400 THB/year incl. VAT; Medium 1,249 THB/month or 11,900 THB/year; Large 2,999 THB/month or 28,700 THB/year.
- OneTrust: no Thailand-specific price published. Third-party sources cited in search results say entry about USD 1,620/year, median about USD 11,500/year (unverified, general OneTrust figures).
- PDPA Core and bePDPA: no prices found.

## Fit gaps
- OneTrust is priced for mid-size and large firms.
- Cookie Wow covers cookie consent and consent records, not the full set of a small firm's obligations. The wider workflow (records of processing, breach handling, DPO tasks) appears to be sold as services or bundles by PDPA Core (unverified).
- A Thai SME records-of-processing exemption exists (HSF Kramer note), which may shrink the need for some tooling.
- No gap could be confirmed from reviews, because none were found.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: a cheap, Thai-language, all-in-one SME workflow (records, requests, breach) priced below OneTrust and bundled services, but cookie consent is already commoditised at 2,400 THB/year.
