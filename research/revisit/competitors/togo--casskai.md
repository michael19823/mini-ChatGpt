# CassKai (Togo) - dropped idea C1035: Togo e-invoicing

## Verdict: weak

## Evidence
- Listed on Capterra as accounting software for France, West and North Africa; supports PCG, SYSCOHADA, IFRS, SCF; currencies EUR, XOF, XAF, MAD, DZD; invoicing module cites Factur-X and MECeF. Modules: CRM, inventory, HR, payroll, projects, AI assistant. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/10045631/CassKai/
- Togo has no named CassKai presence in results: searches (French) for CassKai + Togo + OTR/certified invoice returned only generic coverage of the Finance Law 2026 certified e-invoicing obligation (B2B between VAT-registered entities; OTR may build a national platform or approve private ones; progressive rollout discussed 10 Sep 2026). No source says CassKai is approved by the OTR. Sources: https://edicomgroup.com/fr/blog/facture-electronique-certifiee-togo , https://beancount.io/fr/blog/2026/09/16/togo-finance-law-certified-e-invoicing-b2b-otr-platform-guide
- Togo-specific format support: MECeF is a Benin standard, not Togo's; no evidence CassKai supports Togo's OTR format (unverified, as the format is not yet finalized).
- Complaints: none found. Trustpilot/review search returned nothing for CassKai (only unrelated businesses). Capterra snippet showed no review content. Not evidence of quality either way.
- Momentum: unverified; a Capterra listing with 2026 in the title suggests it is current, but no news, funding or release data found. Small and obscure.

## Pricing
Capterra lists a starting price of EUR 29/month flat rate. Per the Capterra snippet only; unverified on vendor site.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of OTR approval or Togo certified-invoice integration (rules still being rolled out).
- Generalist ERP-style suite, not an e-invoicing/compliance specialist; likely heavy for micro-merchants.
- Euro pricing; no evidence of local payment (mobile money) or local support in Togo (unverified).

## Opening
Togo's certified e-invoicing mandate is brand new and CassKai shows no Togo-specific compliance or presence, so a lightweight OTR-compliant invoicing tool for small VAT-registered firms is still open.
