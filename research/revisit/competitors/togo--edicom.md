# EDICOM (Togo) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C1035 Togo e-invoicing.
Searches used: 4 of 6 (English, French).

## Verdict: weak

EDICOM only publishes marketing and explainer content on Togo. The Togo mandate is not yet operational, so there is no live Togo product to beat. EDICOM positions itself for multinationals, not Togolese SMEs.

## Evidence
- EDICOM has a Togo e-invoicing page and blog posts in many languages about certified e-invoicing under the 2026 Finance Law. Sources: https://edicomgroup.com/electronic-invoicing/togo and https://edicomgroup.com/blog/certified-e-invoicing-togo
- Per those pages (via search summaries, not read in full): the scope is B2B between VAT-registered firms plus advance payments. The OTR may create a national platform or approve private platforms. Standardized paper invoices ("factures normalisées") stay the main format until the system is built. It is not confirmed whether transmission will be real-time or deferred, or whether there will be prior validation.
- I found no evidence that EDICOM is an OTR-approved provider in Togo (unverified; no approval list exists in what I saw). The wording says it "enables companies to anticipate" compliance, which reads as positioning, not a live local service.
- Reviews are global only: G2 4.5 (2 reviews), Gartner Peer Insights 4.3 (4 reviews), via search summary. Sources: https://www.rfp.wiki/vendors/edicom and https://www.g2.com/products/edicom-edi/reviews (not read in full). Review snippets cite opaque quote-based pricing, fit mainly for mid-to-large multinationals, and implementation complexity for smaller IT teams.
- Complaints specific to Togo: none found, in English or French. No app-store, forum or outage news was found. Absence of evidence, not a clean record.
- Momentum: active content marketing; the Togo market itself is still pre-launch.

## Pricing
- No published price. Quote-based, per search summaries. Any comparison with Togolese SME budgets is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Enterprise and multinational focus; no SME self-serve offering seen.
- No evidence of Togo-based support, French-first SME onboarding, or mobile/low-bandwidth use (unverified).
- Not checked: the OTR's own platform (the real threat if it is free) and regional vendors such as CassKai, named in the original rejection text.

## Opening
EDICOM leaves the Togolese SME segment open: a cheap, French-language, self-serve invoicing tool that connects to the OTR platform once its specification is published. The risks are the OTR's free platform and the unconfirmed mandate timeline, neither covered by this check.
