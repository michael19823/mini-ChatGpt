# Global EOR providers (Deel, Remote, Oyster etc.) - Trinidad and Tobago

Idea killed: C1042 Trinidad and Tobago SME payroll / statutory deductions.
Searches used: 3 of 6 (English only; local language is English in T&T).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Global EORs serve foreign companies hiring a few remote workers in T&T. They do not serve local SMEs running their own payroll (NIS, PAYE, Health Surcharge, Green Fund Levy, monthly remittance by the 15th, TD4 by end of February). The better-matched alternatives are local payroll modules, for example an Odoo add-on (VedTech tt payroll), which are a separate competitor set.

## Evidence
- EOR pages for T&T describe the product as legally employing a worker on behalf of a foreign client without a local entity. Source: https://www.teamed.global/compare/best-eor-in-trinidad-and-tobago , https://www.atlashxm.com/countries/trinidad-tobago/employer-of-record
- A T&T-specific payroll engine exists for local employers (PAYE, NIS, Health Surcharge, monthly returns) as an Odoo module: https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/vedtech_tt_payroll (feature claims unverified beyond the listing text).
- Complaints found were generic, not T&T-specific: slow support on Remote (24-48 hour tickets) and hidden fees on Deel and Oyster, per an aggregator (https://www.costbench.com/compare/deel-vs-remote/). Aggregator claims, unverified against primary sources. Ratings quoted there are high (Deel 4.7 Trustpilot, Remote 4.6, Oyster 4.5). No T&T-specific complaints found.

## Pricing
- Deel EOR from $599 per employee per month; Oyster EOR from $699 per month (as quoted in search snippets from G2/comparison sites; unverified on vendor sites). Remote Payroll from $29 per month (G2 snippet, unverified).
- Far above what a local SME would pay for payroll compliance, but SMEs are not the target buyer.

## Fit gaps
- Requires the buyer to be a foreign employer; cannot run payroll for a locally registered SME's own staff.
- Priced per employee at hundreds of USD per month.
- No evidence of BIR e-filing or local statutory return support for local firms (unverified).

## Opening
The EOR set leaves the local-SME payroll and statutory deduction niche open; the real competition to check is local payroll software and accountants.
