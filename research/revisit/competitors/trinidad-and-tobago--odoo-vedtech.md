# Odoo VedTech (Trinidad and Tobago)

Idea it killed: C1042 Trinidad and Tobago SME payroll / statutory deductions.

## Verdict: beatable

A real, T&T-specific payroll product, but it is a paid add-on module that sits on top of Odoo, so it needs an Odoo install and an implementer. It is not a turnkey SME tool. (3 searches used; no local-language search, English is the working language in T&T.)

## Evidence
- Odoo App Store lists a VedTech T&T suite: payroll, accounting, overtime, pension, loans, TD4 (PAYE annual return), final settlement. Source: https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/vedtech_tt_payroll (and sibling vedtech_tt_* pages).
- Payroll covers PAYE, NIS and Health Surcharge with editable statutory rates; TD4 generation is included as a separate module. Same source.
- Accounting module covers chart of accounts, statutory taxes and fiscal positions for BIR. Source: https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/vedtech_tt_accounting
- A live demo with a sample T&T company is offered (per search summary).
- Momentum: modules are published for Odoo 19.0, so actively maintained as of the search (release dates unverified).
- Other local options named by a third-party guide: Paymaster, TTPay, Payroll123 (https://www.acciyo.com/trinidad-payroll-software-the-complete-guide-to-local-global-options-for-tt-business-compliance/). Not checked further.
- Complaints: none found. No reviews of VedTech turned up. Searches only returned generic Odoo reviews, which say nothing about this product.

## Pricing
- Base payroll module listed at $850 per the search summary. Currency, one-time vs recurring, and add-on prices are unverified.
- Odoo licence/hosting and implementation costs come on top (not verified for T&T). Total cost of ownership is likely high for a very small firm.

## Fit gaps
- Needs the Odoo platform (hosting, setup, upgrades), which is heavy for a 3-30 person SME. Whether it works on Odoo Online vs only self-hosted/Odoo.sh is unverified.
- No evidence of a standalone mobile app, employee self-service in T&T context, or direct e-filing/remittance to BIR/NIB (only form generation noted; unverified).
- Add-ons sold separately (overtime, pension, loans, TD4, final settlement).

## Opening
A standalone, low-cost, no-ERP payroll tool for T&T SMEs (PAYE, NIS, Health Surcharge, TD4, payslips) could undercut an $850+ module that requires Odoo.
