# Paymaster (Trinidad and Tobago, commercial, URL unknown)

## Verdict: beatable

Paymaster is a legacy local desktop/on-premise payroll package. It handles NIS, PAYE and Health Surcharge, but it appears aimed at larger firms or buyers who want long-standing local support. No cloud or mobile product was found.
