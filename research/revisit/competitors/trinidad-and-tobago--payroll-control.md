# Payroll Control (Trinidad and Tobago)

Killed idea: C1042, Trinidad and Tobago SME payroll / statutory deductions.
Searches used: 2 of 6 (English only; the local language is English).

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Payroll Control appears to be an Odoo implementation partner in Port of Spain, not a self-serve SME payroll product.

## Evidence
- Odoo partner/customer listing "payroll control 13446918" gives the address 23 Stone Street, Port of Spain. It describes an Odoo technical team with regional payroll expertise, deployments in Jamaica, Barbados, T&T and OECS, and rollout, configuration, testing and training services. Sources: https://www.odoo.com/partners/payroll-control-13446918 and https://www.odoo.com/customers/payroll-control-13446918
- Search found no own website, app-store listing, G2/Capterra/Trustpilot page or Reddit/forum thread for a product called Payroll Control. (Searches were limited to two queries; absence is not proof.)
- Related products that do compete for the same buyer (not Payroll Control): VedTech's Odoo T&T payroll module (PAYE, NIS, Health Surcharge, TD1 relief, monthly returns; https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/vedtech_tt_payroll), and local tools named in a guide as Paymaster, TTPay and Payroll123 (https://www.acciyo.com/trinidad-payroll-software-the-complete-guide-to-local-global-options-for-tt-business-compliance/). I did not verify these.
- Complaints: none found.

## Pricing
No published pricing found (unverified). Odoo implementation services are typically quoted per project; not confirmed for this firm.

## Fit gaps
- Appears to be a services/implementation firm requiring an Odoo deployment, so it is likely poorly suited to micro and small employers wanting self-serve payroll (inferred, unverified).
- Context: the combined NIS rate rose from 13.2% to 16.2% on 5 Jan 2026 per a guide (https://www.acciyo.com/trinidad-payroll-software-the-complete-guide-to-local-global-options-for-tt-business-compliance/, unverified), which creates demand for rate-updated tools.

## Opening
Payroll Control does not block the idea. Check the real products (Paymaster, TTPay, Payroll123, VedTech Odoo module) before re-scoring C1042.
