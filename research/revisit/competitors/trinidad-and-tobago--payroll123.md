# Payroll123 (Trinidad and Tobago)

Ideas it killed: C1042 (T&T SME payroll / statutory deductions), C1043 (T&T payroll engines).
Searches used: 5 of 6 (English only; no local-language results exist beyond English for T&T).

## Verdict: weak

Real and locally specific, but nearly invisible online: no website found, no reviews, no published pricing. Probably desktop software. Evidence is thin, so the verdict has low confidence.

## Evidence
- A third-party guide (acciyo.com) lists Payroll123 with Paymaster and TTPay as local T&T payroll software. It says it covers NIS, PAYE and Health Surcharge, targets micro to medium SMEs (1-50 employees), and claims "100% guaranteed local compliance" for NIS, PAYE, TD4 and NI187. It also says such local tools can lack advanced HR/international features or be desktop-based (a generic statement, not specific to Payroll123). Source: https://www.acciyo.com/trinidad-payroll-software-the-complete-guide-to-local-global-options-for-tt-business-compliance/ (secondary, possibly marketing; unverified).
- ANSA McAL advertises a "Payroll System Administrator - Payroll 123" job in Port of Spain. This shows the product is used by a large T&T conglomerate. Source: https://ansamcal.com/careers/job/payroll-system-administrator-payroll-123. Unverified whether it is a commercial product or an in-house or customised system.
- No official site, app-store listing, G2/Capterra page, Trustpilot page, Reddit thread or news item surfaced.
- Complaints: none found. This is not evidence of satisfaction, only of low web presence.
- Momentum: unknown (unverified). The job ad suggests the product is still in use in at least one large firm.

## Pricing
No published pricing found (unverified).

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- No visible web presence or self-serve signup, so small buyers may struggle to discover or buy it.
- May be desktop-based (generic claim in acciyo guide); likely no mobile or cloud multi-user access.
- Positioned for 1-50 employees but is also used at a very large firm, so the SME focus is unclear.

## Opening
A modern, cloud or mobile T&T payroll tool (NIS, PAYE, Health Surcharge, TD4, NI187 filings, Green Fund Levy) with transparent pricing faces a weak, barely visible incumbent. The other local options (Paymaster, TTPay, the VedTech Odoo module) still need checking.
