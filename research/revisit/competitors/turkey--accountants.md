# Turkey: accountants (competitor to C1053, Turkey GEKAP declaration tool)

## Verdict: beatable

Accountants (mali müşavirler, SMMM) are the incumbent channel because they file the GEKAP return for clients through the official GIB declaration system, but the calculation work (product and packaging scoping, weights, import vs domestic) is a separate task that they do not obviously automate. Search turned up no named software tool aimed at small firms.

## Evidence
- Licensed accountants with e-filing authority send clients' GEKAP returns ("POSET" format) through the official BDP program. This is the filing channel, so a tool cannot replace it. Source: https://www.alomaliye.com/2020/08/05/gekap-geri-kazanim-katilim-payi-sikca-sorulan-sorular/amp/ (search summary, page not opened).
- Scope grew from plastic bags (2019) to about 45 product groups with subcategories from 2020. Filers must file even when nothing is to declare. Sources: https://www.alomaliye.com/2020/03/07/gekap-geri-kazanim-katilim-payi-beyannamesi/ and https://www.cottgroup.com/en/component/zoo/item/gekap-beyannamesi-verme-sureleri-yeniden-belirlendi (search summaries only).
- Monthly filing for corporate taxpayers, quarterly for others (search summary of the sources above).
- Big-firm competition: KPMG Turkey sells a "GEKAP Calculator" that handles domestic production, import and packaging calculations and drafts the return. Source: https://kpmgvergi.com/alt-hizmetler/vergi-teknolojileri/kpmg-gekap-teknoloji-cozumu-1041. This is an enterprise offering. Its price is not published (unverified for any figure).
- Complaints: none found. Across 6 searches (Turkish and English) I found no app-store reviews, forum threads or news complaining about accountants and GEKAP. The one thing I saw on muhasebenews and muhasebetr was professional commentary, which I did not open or read in full.

## Pricing
- No published price for accountant GEKAP filing or for the KPMG tool was found (unverified). TURMOB and chamber minimum fee schedules exist (e.g. https://ismmmo.org.tr/dosya/4606/Uye-Dosya/08012024-tavsiye-edilen-asgari-ucret-tarifesi-2024-social.pdf), but I did not see whether they list GEKAP and did not read any figures.

## Fit gaps
- Accountants file, but the buyer (a small manufacturer or importer) must supply the product-level data. Classification and weight calculations are a likely pain point (inference, not verified by reviews).
- KPMG's tool is aimed at large corporates, so small importers are probably not served (inference).
- A tool must hand off to the accountant or produce BDP-compatible output, because filing runs through accountants and the official system.

## Opening
A small-importer GEKAP calculator that produces a filing-ready draft for the client's accountant (rather than replacing the accountant) is the opening; the main risk is that accountants are the entrenched filing channel and buyers may not pay separately.
