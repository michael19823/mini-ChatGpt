# Akkaş Karbon (Turkey) - competitor check for C1063 (CBAM/SKDM carbon software)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

## Evidence
- 4 searches (Turkish and English) found no CBAM/SKDM software or carbon-accounting product under the name "Akkaş Karbon" or "Akkas Karbon". Unverified whether such a vendor exists at all.

Their quality and fit were not assessed; they may deserve their own check.
