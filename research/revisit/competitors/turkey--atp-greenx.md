# ATP GreenX (Turkey)

Dropped idea it killed: C1063, CBAM/SKDM carbon software.

## Verdict: weak

ATP GreenX is mainly a digital carbon-credit and green-certificate marketplace sold through energy companies. The searches found no dedicated CBAM/SKDM filing product for exporters. The CBAM reporting mentioned in the press appears to come through a consultancy partnership.

## Evidence
- ATP GreenX is a brand of ATP Yazılım ve Teknoloji A.Ş., described as a digital carbon-market platform for buying and selling carbon credits and environmental products, built on blockchain and digital wallets. Sources: https://www.webtekno.com/turkiye-ilk-dijital-yesil-enerji-pazar-yeri-greenx-h139801.html, https://www.getmidas.com/midasin-kulaklari/atp-yazilim-climeco-ile-is-birligi-anlasmasi-imzaladi-p-158743
- On 24 July 2025 ATP Yazılım signed a cooperation agreement with ClimeCo (reported through KAP). The scope covers ESG consulting, carbon and water footprint calculation, and TSRS and SKDM (CBAM) compliant reporting, with ClimeCo using GreenX's software and digital infrastructure. This reads as a services partnership rather than a self-serve CBAM tool. Sources: https://kap.org.tr/en/Bildirim/1466378, https://www.foreks.com/haber/detay/68834aa3109d8d77a01670a4/PICNEWS/tr/atp-yazilim-ile-climeco-arasinda-bir-is-birligi-olusturmak-amaciyla-sozlesme-imzalandi-25-07-25/
- Press says energy companies' corporate customers can manage their carbon footprint end to end through GreenX (partnership with Aydem Enerji). The channel is B2B through energy firms. Source: https://www.ekonomigazetesi.com/sirket-haberleri/aydem-enerji-ile-atpden-karbon-donusumunde-is-birligi-76788
- The press coverage also mentions sponsorship of the Istanbul Carbon Summit (https://www.benguturk.com/ekonomi/atp-greenxten-istanbul-karbon-zirvesine-destek-196651h) and the platform's carbon-market positioning (https://paratic.com/atp-yazilimdan-atatp-karbon-piyasasinda-stratejik-adim/).
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced in English or Turkish searches. The product appears to have few public users, so the absence of complaints says little.
- Momentum: active. It is developed by a listed company (KAP disclosures), with 2025 partnerships and events. Specifics on a 2026 CBAM module: unverified.

## Pricing
No published pricing found (unverified). It is likely quote-based and enterprise-oriented.

## Fit gaps
- Focus is carbon trading and certificates, not a purpose-built CBAM declaration or embedded-emissions workflow for exporters (not confirmed either way; no product documentation found).
- Go-to-market is via energy companies and consultancies, so a small exporter has no obvious self-serve entry.
- No evidence of SME-oriented packaging, supplier data collection, or EU importer-facing CBAM reports (unverified).

## Opening
A self-serve, low-priced CBAM/SKDM emissions-data and report tool for Turkish SME exporters is not visibly covered by ATP GreenX, which targets carbon trading and enterprise or energy-company channels.
