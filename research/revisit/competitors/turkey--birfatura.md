# BirFatura (Turkey)

Killed idea: C1060 SME e-Fatura / e-Arşiv / e-İrsaliye. Type: commercial. URL: https://birfatura.com (found in search, not given in candidates).
Searches used: 5 of 6.

## Verdict: strong

It is a GİB-licensed special integrator aimed at SME e-commerce sellers, with low per-invoice pricing and wide marketplace integration. No complaints were found.

## Evidence
- Described as a licensed special integrator with GİB, able to issue e-Fatura, e-Arşiv and e-İrsaliye on its own infrastructure; also e-SMM and e-MM per its pricing page. Sources: https://birfatura.com/e-fatura/ , https://birfatura.com/e-fatura-kontor-fiyatlari-e-fatura-fiyatlari/
- Founded July 2016 as SaaS for SMEs; dashboard consolidates orders from over 75 marketplaces and platforms; dealer network launched 2023 (per Dealroom/OSTIM summaries in search snippets, secondary sources). Sources: https://app.dealroom.co/companies/birfatura , https://www.ostim.org.tr/erc-soft-bilgi-teknolojileri-danismanlik-ic-ve-dis-tic-ltd-sti/birfatura
- Momentum: the company publishes a changelog and says it updates monthly (https://birfatura.com/guncellemeler); it has integrations with e-commerce platforms (e.g. Ideasoft help page https://www.ideasoft.com.tr/yardim/birfatura-app-entegrasyonu/), cargo integrations, and a WordPress plugin. Actively developed.
- A rating of 9.5/10 appears in a search summary; the source was not identified. Unverified.

## Complaints
None found. The search returned no third-party reviews: no Şikayetvar page for BirFatura, no App Store or Google Play rating for the main app, no Reddit or Ekşi Sözlük threads. BirFatura runs its own complaint form (https://birfatura.com/birfatura-sikayetvar/). This does not show that customers are happy, only that nothing was found. Search coverage was limited (US-only search tool, 5 queries).

## Pricing
Published e-kontör (per-invoice credit) packages: 500 credits at 0.800 TL each (400 TL), tiers of 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 / 5,000 down to about 0.600 TL, and 10,000 credits at 0.523 TL each (5,230 TL). It also mentions monthly or annual subscription fees; amounts not captured (unverified). Source: https://birfatura.com/e-fatura-kontor-fiyatlari-e-fatura-fiyatlari/. Prices may be out of date. This is cheap for small sellers, so it is not overpriced.

## Fit gaps
No gaps confirmed. Possible but unverified: focus is on e-commerce sellers with marketplace and cargo integrations, so non-e-commerce SMEs (services, field businesses, accountants managing many clients) may be less well served. Nothing found on multi-entity or offline use.

## Opening
Little on the generic invoicing function itself; any opening would be a non-e-commerce niche (for example accountant-led multi-client workflows), and that needs separate evidence.
