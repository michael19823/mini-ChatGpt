# Turkey: ERP modules (killed C1053, Turkey GEKAP declaration tool)

## Verdict
weak (leaning not-a-real-competitor for small buyers). Only a few ERP vendors have been shown to offer GEKAP features, and the main dedicated tool found is a consultancy product aimed at large firms. Limited evidence (5 searches), so the verdict is provisional.

## Evidence
- Loganites (an ERP vendor/partner) published training content on a GEKAP declaration print screen and parameter screens in its ERP, under the statutory books menu. Source: https://yandex.com.tr/yaozet/t/erp (search summary only; vendor and exact product unverified).
- Logo Netsis documentation mentions a "Sustainability 360" report with GEKAP-related indicators. This is a sustainability report, not clearly a filing tool. Source: https://docs.logo.com.tr/download/attachments/83493511/Netsis%20S%C3%BCrd%C3%BCrebilirlik%20360.pdf?api=v2
- KPMG GEKAP Calculator: a commercial tool covering domestic production, import and packaging calculations and a draft declaration. It is a consultancy product, not an ERP module. Source: https://kpmgvergi.com/alt-hizmetler/vergi-teknolojileri/kpmg-gekap-teknoloji-cozumu-1041
- TÜRMOB offers a free Excel GEKAP calculation table, which is the free baseline for small firms. Source: https://www.alomaliye.com/2020/07/30/gekap-hesaplama-tablosu
- Obligations are declared to the Tax Administration electronically, quarterly since 2021. Sources: https://cottgroup.com/tr/mevzuat/item/gekap-beyannamesi-verme-sureleri-yeniden-belirlendi , https://www.alomaliye.com/2020/03/07/gekap-geri-kazanim-katilim-payi-beyannamesi/
- Complaints: no user reviews of any ERP GEKAP module were found (no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit hits). The only complaints found concern GEKAP itself. Commentary says the application was complex and poorly understood, and TÜRMOB said the infrastructure was incomplete and problems had emerged. Sources: https://www.muhasebetr.com/yazarlarimiz/alidenizinan/020 , https://www.ekonomist.com.tr/haberler/turmobtan-poset-beyannamesinin-ertelenmesi-talebi.html . Vendor-specific complaints: none found.

## Pricing
No published GEKAP module prices found. Logo Netsis ran module and user discount campaigns in Aug-Sep 2025, but with no GEKAP price. KPMG pricing is not published (unverified). The free TÜRMOB Excel table is the baseline.

## Fit gaps
- ERPs (Logo/Netsis and similar) are heavy for micro and small firms. Adding a module means buying the ERP first.
- Features appear to be calculation and print screens within an ERP. Packaging-material mapping per SKU (plastic, metal, glass, wood) is the user's data burden. Whether any module covers it is unverified.
- Firms outside those ERPs, and e-commerce sellers (cargo bags and cartons count as packaging), have no obvious dedicated option except Excel or consultants.
- The KPMG tool targets corporates.

## Opening
A standalone, cheap, SKU/packaging-aware GEKAP calculator and declaration-prep tool for SMEs and e-commerce sellers not on a big ERP, going beyond the free Excel table.
