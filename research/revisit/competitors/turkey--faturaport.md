# Faturaport (Turkey)

Dropped idea: C1060, SME e-Fatura / e-Arşiv / e-İrsaliye.
Site found by search: faturaport.com (the brief had no URL).

## Verdict: strong

It is a cheap, mobile-first, SME-targeted private GİB integrator. It already covers the idea's whole scope and is actively developed. No complaints were found to attack.

## Evidence
- Private integrator working with GİB. Offers e-Fatura, e-Arşiv and e-İrsaliye, 10-year archiving, and Trendyol and Hepsiburada marketplace integration. Source: https://faturaport.com/blog/e-donusum/e-fatura-entegrator-degisikligi-nasil-yapilir and https://faturaport.com/e-fatura
- Gaziantep-based pre-accounting app (cash flow, receivables and payables, cheques and notes, quotes, orders). It claims thousands of users in every province. Source: https://webrazzi.com/2025/11/12/yenilenen-faturaport-un-one-cikan-verileri/
- Its own site claims 10,000+ businesses (unverified, self-reported). Source: https://faturaport.com/fiyatlar
- App Store TR rating is 4.6/5 from 95 ratings, as reported by a search summary. The site says 65% of mobile users are on iOS and 35% on Android. Source: https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/faturaport/id1520478336
- Free migration of old e-invoices from another integrator, plus a 30-day refund guarantee. Source: https://faturaport.com/para-iade-garantisi
- Complaints: none found. Searches in Turkish (şikayet, yorum) and English returned nothing about Faturaport itself. Şikayetvar results were for an unrelated bill-payment site. Google Play reviews and Reddit were not seen. This is not proof that no complaints exist.

## Pricing (from the faturaport.com/fiyatlar search snippet; the page was not opened, so unverified in detail)
- Avantaj: 1.000 TL/year, e-Fatura and e-Arşiv, 100 kontör (credits) included.
- Esnaf: 5.000 TL/year, adds e-İrsaliye, 250 kontör included.
- Sınırsız: 6.500 TL/year, all modules, 1000 kontör included.
- Extra kontör: 500 for 960 TL, 1000 for 1.560 TL, 2500 for 3.000 TL.
- Prices are not overpriced for the SME segment.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. It targets small businesses, is mobile and cloud based, and has marketplace integration.
- Possible gaps, all unverified: offline use, multi-entity support, and depth of accounting beyond pre-accounting.

## Momentum
Active. It was relaunched or renewed in November 2025 (Webrazzi) and publishes 2026 content, such as the "2026 guncel rehber" blog posts.

## Opening
Very little. A new entrant would need a clear niche, such as a vertical workflow or offline use, rather than a generic SME e-invoice tool.
