# Hursoft (Turkey) - killed C1067: Apartment/site management

## Verdict: weak

## Evidence
- Hursoft Apartman ve Site Yonetim Programi is a downloadable Windows desktop program (5.26 MB, listed as trial version, about 12,833 downloads on Tamindir). Source: https://www.tamindir.com/indir/hursoft-apartman-ve-site-yonetim-programi/
- Features per the listing: fee income and expense entry, bank/fund/advance, personnel, meter and check tracking, address labels, accounting reports (balance sheet, trial balance, late-fee and debt-collection reports), built-in knowledge of the Turkish Condominium Law. Same source.
- Same vendor sells other desktop programs (sports club, dormitory, pre-accounting) on Tamindir, so it looks like a small shovelware vendor. No official website found in 3 searches.
- No evidence of a mobile app, resident portal, online payments or cloud use in the search results (not confirmed either way: unverified).
- Complaints: none found (searched Turkish "sikayet yorum" and English). No app-store, forum or review-site results.
- Momentum: no release dates or news seen; appears stagnant or low-visibility (unverified).

## Pricing
Not published in search results (unverified). Trial version exists.

## Fit gaps
- Desktop-only as far as visible: no resident-facing mobile access, online dues collection or notifications (unverified).
- Accounting-centric for the manager, not for residents.

## Opening
A cloud/mobile resident-and-manager app with online dues payment would face little from Hursoft itself; the real Turkish competition is other site-management apps, not checked here.
