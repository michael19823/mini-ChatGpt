# Turkey: KPMG GEKAP solution (killed idea C1053: Turkey GEKAP declaration tool)

## Verdict: beatable

KPMG's GEKAP Calculator is a real product, but it is sold as part of an advisory engagement. No price, self-serve signup or SME offer was found, and no user complaints turned up.

## Evidence
- KPMG Turkey has a "KPMG GEKAP Teknoloji Çözümü" page. It describes software for end-to-end GEKAP calculations, with a data pool, calculation modelling and reporting, built by customs specialists. Source: https://kpmgvergi.com/alt-hizmetler/vergi-teknolojileri/kpmg-gekap-teknoloji-cozumu-1041
- A KPMG PDF brochure (2021) describes the same software. Functions listed: automatic split of packaging into primary, secondary and tertiary layers by product and model, automatic period declaration drafts, group-based packaging calculation for similar products, and export of results to reporting systems. Source: https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/tr/pdf/2021/05/kpmg-gekap-cozumu.pdf
- KPMG also sells GEKAP consulting: product analysis and categorisation, monthly declaration support and staff training. Source: https://kpmgvergi.com/alt-hizmetler/gumruk-ve-dis-ticaret-hizmetleri/geri-kazanim-katilim-payi-gekap-hizmetlerimiz-1037
- The software is positioned as a companion to those services, so it looks like a consultancy-led product for corporate clients. This is my inference, not a stated KPMG position.
- Complaints: none found about the KPMG product (6 searches, Turkish and English). No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews surfaced. The only issues found were about the government system. On 27 July 2020 the Tax Administration's Defter Beyan Sistemi (DBS) locked up under heavy load and some declarations could not be approved. The administration treated queued declarations as timely. Source: https://www.alomaliye.com/2020/07/29/defter-beyan-sistemi-sorunu-hakkinda-mesleki-kamuoyuna-duyuru/
- Other commentary says the GEKAP rollout had many problems and low awareness among the affected businesses. Source: muhasebetr.com columns, found in the search results (exact article unverified).
- Momentum: unverified. The only dated KPMG material found is the 2021 brochure. The product page carries no visible date in the search results, and nothing found shows recent releases, an acquisition or a shutdown.
- Competing tools: no other Turkish GEKAP software vendor surfaced in these searches. This does not prove none exist.

## Pricing
Not published (unverified). The searches found no price, minimum or SME tier. Contact-only advisory pricing from a Big Four firm is likely well above what a small importer or producer would pay, but that is an assumption.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at companies that buy KPMG advisory. No self-serve or SME tier was found.
- ERP integration or API: unverified. The brochure only says results can be exported to reporting systems.
- Mobile or offline use, multi-entity support, and languages other than Turkish: no information found.
- The tool prepares declaration drafts. Whether it files directly with the tax portal is unverified.

## Opening
A low-cost, self-serve GEKAP calculator and declaration-draft tool for SMEs (importers, producers, retailers) could undercut KPMG. It would need to be priced publicly and work without a consulting engagement.
