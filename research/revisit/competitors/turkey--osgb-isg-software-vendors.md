# Turkey: OSGB / ISG software vendors (generic, no URL) - idea C1065

## Verdict: weak (low-confidence; searches were thin)

## Evidence
- No complaints found. 5 searches (Turkish and English) returned no app-store, forum, Reddit or review-site criticism of any named OSGB software vendor. Finding none is a valid result, but it also means the market was barely seen. WebSearch is US-focused and returned mostly regulatory pages.
- The vendor set is fragmented and has no clear leader in what I found. Named vendors that surfaced: Parsek (cloud/mobile ERP + ISG + OSB platform, per Cumhuriyet and Karar news items), and Turkguven (ISG software, via a CB Insights investor page). Neither was checked in depth, so their strength is unverified.
- Official state system ISG-KATIP (Ministry of Labour, csgb.gov.tr) already handles contracts and assignments for OSGBs and employers. Contracts between an OSGB and an employer are made and approved there. This is a free government tool, so it caps what a vendor can charge for filing-type features (method.md says to score such gaps lower).
- Sources indicate OSGBs must use ISG software, and that employers served by an OSGB don't have to buy their own. The OSGB is the paying software buyer (per search summary of ministry text; exact legal wording unverified).
- A star.com.tr column ("1 temmuz yaklasiyor osgbler cozum bekliyor") shows OSGBs are under regulatory pressure, so there is demand. I did not read its content.
- Momentum: unverified for every vendor.
