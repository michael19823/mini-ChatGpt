# Paraşüt (Turkey): killed C1060 (SME e-Fatura/e-Arşiv/e-İrsaliye)

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Paraşüt's own site says it covers e-Fatura, e-Arşiv, e-SMM, e-İhracat and e-İrsaliye for SMEs, with the e-invoice integration free for subscribers (https://www.parasut.com/e-belge, https://www.parasut.com/e-irsaliye). Self-reported, so treat with care.
- Self-reported rating of about 4.6-4.7/5 from about 2,097 reviews (https://www.parasut.com/on-muhasebe-programi). Not independently verified.
- G2 listing shows 4.0/5 from 43 reviews (https://www.g2.com/sellers/parasut). G2 itself says this is too few reviews for buying insight. I found no Capterra or Trustpilot reviews.
- A Kimola summary of Google Play reviews for the Turkish mobile app reports mixed feedback: missing features, mobile app errors, weak customer support, invoice visibility problems, and pricing and usability concerns (https://kimola.com/reports/explore-customer-insights-for-parasut-software-google-play-tr-144845). This is a third-party summary. I saw no individual reviews and quote none.
- No outage or failed-filing news turned up in the searches.
- Momentum: unverified. The site is active, with a mali mühür price dated August 2026, but I did not search for acquisition or ownership news.

## Pricing
- Search snippets from parasut.com give conflicting figures, so both are unverified until checked on the pricing page:
  - e-Fatura + ön muhasebe bundle: 940 TL + KDV per month, or 11,280 TL + KDV per year (https://www.parasut.com/on-muhasebe-fiyatlari).
  - e-Fatura program: 150 TL + KDV per month, 1,800 TL + KDV one-time setup, 120 free e-kontör (page not identified).
- Mali mühür certificate with card reader, 3 years: 1,700 TL + KDV, as of August 2026 (https://www.parasut.com/kullanim-kilavuzu/elektronik-fatura-hizmetinin-maliyeti).
- Not clearly overpriced for SMEs. Integration is free for subscribers.

## Fit gaps
- Complaints about mobile app quality and customer support (Google Play summary).
- Only a bundled ön muhasebe product. A buyer who wants only e-document issuing (no accounting) is possibly poorly served. Unverified.
- Mali mühür hardware and cost, and per-document e-kontör fees, are required for the e-document services (as the pricing snippets suggest).
- No evidence found of gaps in filing formats, language or multi-entity support.

## Opening
Little. Paraşüt is an established, well-rated, SME-priced provider with e-Fatura, e-Arşiv and e-İrsaliye built in. The only visible gaps are mobile app quality and support, which are weak grounds for reviving C1060.
