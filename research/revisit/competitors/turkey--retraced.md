# Retraced (Turkey) - competitor check

Idea killed: C1068 Textile supplier due diligence.

## Verdict: beatable

Retraced is a funded, active platform, but it targets fashion brands (buyers) with enterprise-style sales. It does not appear to serve Turkish small suppliers or small buyers directly.

## Evidence
- Raised EUR 15M (Series A, Sept/Oct 2024); more than 150 fashion brands and 15,000+ suppliers connected, including Desigual, Victoria's Secret, Pangaia, Calzedonia. Sources: https://www.ecotextile.com/2024091049493/news/materials-production-news/new-funding-to-drive-textile-traceability-platform/ , https://fashionunited.de/nachrichten/business/retraced-uber-traceability-nicht-textile-lieferketten-und-15-millionen-euro-finanzspritze/2024102158638/
- German company with Spanish operations. Its site now describes an "AI-first platform for sourcing, product compliance and supplier lifecycle collaboration" (https://retraced.com/).
- Complaints: none found. Capterra listing (https://www.capterra.com/p/219363/retraced/) showed 0 user reviews. I did not find G2, app-store, Reddit or Turkish-language complaints. The Turkish-language search returned nothing specific to Retraced in Turkey.
- Momentum: active as of the 2024 funding. No 2025-2026 acquisition, shutdown or layoff news found (search returned only generic results; unverified either way).

## Pricing
Not published. Typically subscription licences for brands with supplier-portal access (the search summary's wording; unverified). Likely quote-based and aimed at mid-size and large brands.

## Fit gaps (partly inferred, unverified)
- Brand-side, enterprise sales model; small importers, small brands and Turkish suppliers doing their own due diligence are probably not the target.
- No evidence of a Turkish-language interface or Turkey-specific compliance content.
- No complaint data to confirm the gaps. These come from positioning, not customer reports.

## Opening
A cheap, Turkish-language due-diligence tool for small brands and Turkish suppliers, below Retraced's enterprise price point.
