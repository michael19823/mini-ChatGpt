# Seyir Mobil (Turkey)

Dropped idea: C1052, Turkey environmental-consultancy multi-client tracker (TABS / MoTAT / Sifir Atik).

## Verdict
not-a-real-competitor

## Evidence
- Seyir Mobil is a Turkish vehicle-tracking and fleet-management company (HQ Derince, Kocaeli; founded 2012 per its own site, ISO 9001/10002/14001/27001). Source: https://seyirmobil.com/?p=5736
- Its products are GPS vehicle tracking, fleet management, cold-chain tracking, tachograph download and U-ETDS reporting (transport authority filings). Sources: https://seyirmobil.com/en/u-etds-data-generation-and-reporting/ , https://seyirmobil.com/en/fleet-management-system
- Its only environment-related feature is fuel/CO2 and carbon-footprint analysis of vehicles. Source: https://seyirmobil.com/?p=6069 . Nothing found on TABS, MoTAT, Sifir Atik, waste filings or consultancy client management.
- Likely a name-collision or loose match with the dropped idea, not a substitute for it.
- Momentum: active (BSEC-URTA associate member from 1 June 2025). Source: https://bsec-urta.org/2025/05/28/welcome-to-the-family-seyir-mobil/
- Complaints: none found. The iOS app (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id736015353) showed 5.0 from only 2 ratings in search results. No reviews on forums or review sites surfaced (searched English and Turkish, 3 searches).

## Pricing
Not published in anything found (unverified). Fleet service is quote-based as far as seen; the iOS app itself is free.

## Fit gaps
Does not address the buyer (environmental consultancies managing many clients' waste/TABS/MoTAT/Sifir Atik obligations): no multi-client environmental compliance tracking found.

## Opening
Open: no sign Seyir Mobil competes in environmental-consultancy compliance tracking, so it does not block C1052.
