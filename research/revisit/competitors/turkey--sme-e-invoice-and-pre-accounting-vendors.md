# Turkey: SME e-invoice and pre-accounting vendors (generic; idea C1064)

Note: only 2 of 6 allowed searches returned results (the others hit a usage limit). Only Parasut was examined; Logo, Mikro, Zirve, Uyumsoft, Foriba, Nilvera etc. were not checked (unverified).

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Parasut (parasut.com) markets an e-invoice + pre-accounting product for SMEs, claims 4.7/5 from 2,097 reviews (vendor's own page, self-reported, unverified independently). Source: https://www.parasut.com/on-muhasebe-fiyatlari
- Vendor says no setup/integration fees, 14-day free trial, prepaid e-invoice packs at cents per invoice. Source: same page and https://www.parasut.com/e-fatura
- Complaints found (aggregated by Kimola, an AI review summariser; not individual quotes): Google Play reviews mixed; mobile app issues with incoming invoices not showing, profit not displayed, missing product quantity tracking, performance problems, hard-to-reach support, pricing concerns. Source: https://kimola.com/reports/explore-customer-insights-for-parasut-software-google-play-tr-144845 and https://kimola.com/reports/in-depth-parasut-app-feedback-report-elevate-your-business-app-store-tr-144840
- Momentum: product pages and docs are current; appears actively developed (unverified beyond that).
- No outage/failed-filing news found (not searched in depth).

## Pricing
- Parasut e-invoice + pre-accounting: 940 TL/month + VAT, 11,280 TL/year + VAT (vendor page, as retrieved; date of page unverified). Prepaid e-invoice packs extra. Other vendors: unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Mobile app weaknesses (incoming invoices, reports, inventory quantities) per aggregated reviews.
- Support responsiveness complaints.
- Price may be heavy for micro-businesses/sole traders (unverified for them specifically).
- No evidence of language, filing-format or hardware gaps.

## Opening
Narrow: a cheaper, mobile-first tool for micro-merchants could exist, but the market is crowded with well-rated, well-funded vendors, so the gap is thin.
