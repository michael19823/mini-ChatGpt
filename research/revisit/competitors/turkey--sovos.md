# Sovos (Turkey) - competitor check

Idea killed: C1060 SME e-Fatura/e-Arşiv/e-İrsaliye. Competitor type: commercial. 5 WebSearch calls used.

## Verdict: beatable

Sovos is a real, large, actively marketed GİB-approved integrator, but its Turkish business (Foriba, Digital Planet) is enterprise-oriented. It does not publish SME prices, and the SME e-invoice market has many cheaper rivals. Evidence of customer unhappiness is thin, so "beatable" rather than "weak".

## Evidence
- Sovos bought Foriba in May 2019 and Digital Planet (Istanbul, GİB-approved private integrator, NetInvoice platform) afterwards, so it consolidated two Turkish integrators. Sources: https://sovos.com/news/sovos-acquires-foriba-bringing-together-worldwide-pioneers-of-e-invoicing-compliance/ , https://sovos.com/tr/basin-bulteni/sovos-istanbul-merkezli-e-donusum-sirketi-digital-planeti-satin-aldi/
- Sovos Turkish site markets e-Fatura, e-Arşiv, e-İrsaliye, e-Defter with 400+ ERP integrations and 24/7 support; case studies are large firms (e.g. DHL Express). Sources: https://sovos.com/tr/kdv/urunler/turkiye-e-irsaliye-uyumlulugu/ , https://sovos.com/tr/basari-hikayeleri/kdv/dhl-express-sovos-cozumleri/
- Complaints: a Şikayetvar page exists titled (per URL/search snippet) about unauthorized receipt/e-invoice and data use from Sovos. I could not read its content, so the details and count are unverified. Source: https://www.sikayetvar.com/sovos-turkiye/sovosdan-gelen-yetkisiz-makbuz-ve-veri-kullanimi-sikayeti
- No G2, Capterra or Trustpilot reviews, no app-store reviews and no Reddit/forum threads found in the searches. No outage or failed-filing news found.
- Turkish press frames e-invoice as costly for SMEs generally ("e-fatura basılı faturadan pahalı", Sözcü, Ekonomi Gazetesi), not specifically about Sovos. Unverified relevance to Sovos.

## Pricing
- No published SME price found. Sovos pages direct to sales/contact. Unverified: any minimums.
- Other SME-focused providers appear to advertise free tiers or monthly fees (one comparison snippet mentioned a plan from about ₺300/month and 20 free invoices/month); these are not Sovos and not verified here. Source: https://kobitime.com/e-fatura-entegrator-2026/

## Fit gaps
- Positioning and references are enterprise/ERP-integration led, not self-serve micro-business.
- Opaque pricing, a likely friction for small buyers (inference, unverified).
- 2026 expansion of e-Arşiv to nearly all invoices to unregistered buyers widens the micro-SME segment that enterprise integrators serve less directly (from search summary of a third-party blog, unverified).
- No evidence either way on mobile/offline or multi-entity support.

## Momentum
Active: regularly published Turkish content (2024 mandate posts, error-correction guides) and acquisitions. Not stagnant.

## Opening
A transparent, cheap, self-serve SME/micro-business e-Fatura/e-Arşiv/e-İrsaliye front end (possibly as a GİB-approved integrator's reseller) beats Sovos on price clarity and onboarding, but needs integrator status or a partner, and many other SME integrators already exist.
