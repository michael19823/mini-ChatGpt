# TraceX (Uganda) - competitor check

Killed idea: C1075, EUDR traceability for coffee.

## Verdict: strong

TraceX is a real, actively marketed EUDR platform with a Uganda-specific coffee offering. Uganda also has a government-approved traceability provider (Dimitra) and a state-led farmer mapping drive, which narrows the space further.

## Evidence
- TraceX has dedicated Uganda coffee pages: mobile farm mapping and GPS registration of smallholders, cooperative/washing station/exporter linkage, auto-generated Due Diligence Statements per batch. Sources: https://tracextech.com/traceability/coffee-supply-chain-uganda/ , https://tracextech.com/?p=22655 , https://tracextech.com/uganda-coffee-supply-chain/
- The company is Bengaluru-based, launched 2019, with a blockchain-backed platform and an AI compliance tool called Clara (secondary summary: https://letsdatascience.com/news/tracex-technologies-offers-blockchain-traceability-platform-3484c84c).
- Third-party listings: Capterra/GetApp/Software Advice profiles; one summary reports 5.0/5 from 6 reviews. I did not open the listings, so treat the rating as unverified. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/240693/TraceX-Tech/
- Market context: NTV Uganda reports about 60% of farmers mapped, with the rest due by April, ahead of the December 2026 deadline (https://ntv.co.ug/news/coffee-sector-eudr-readiness-over-60-complete). A Dimitra post says Uganda approved Dimitra as an official traceability provider (https://dimitratech.medium.com/uganda-approves-dimitra-as-official-traceability-provider-e2be12ed2f62), which is a stronger local rival than TraceX itself and is unverified beyond the title.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, forum or news complaints surfaced. No Luganda or Swahili searches returned anything relevant (searches were mostly English; local-language coverage was limited).
- Momentum: active. Content is current, with 2026-dated Capterra listing titles. No reports of shutdown, layoffs or acquisition found.

## Pricing
- Capterra-derived listing says a starting price of US$300 per month (unverified; the plan scope and minimums are not stated). That is plausibly affordable for an exporter but not clearly for a small cooperative. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/240693/TraceX-Tech/

## Fit gaps (all inferred, unverified)
- Likely exporter/enterprise-led selling; a Bengaluru vendor probably has no local Uganda presence or local-language (Luganda) farmer UX. Not confirmed.
- No evidence found on offline use, mobile money integration, or cooperative-level multi-entity pricing.
- Generic SaaS plus a government-endorsed rival (Dimitra) leaves little room for a standalone traceability product.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: low-cost, offline-first, local-language tooling for small cooperatives and mid-size exporters that sits on top of government farmer maps. A full platform play is poorly timed.
