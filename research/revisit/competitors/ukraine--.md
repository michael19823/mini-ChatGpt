# Вчасно.ТТН (Ukraine) - competitor check

Idea killed: C1079, e-TTN for carriers and shippers. No URL was given. Search results point to vchasno.ua/en/ttn/.

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Vchasno ("ВЧАСНО СЕРВІС", platform "Вчасно.ТТН") is named as one of the authorized providers in the state e-TTN system, with M.E.Doc and EDI Network (ATС). Source: epravda.com.ua, thepage.ua (search snippets).
- Its own site describes Vchasno.TTN as covering consignors, carriers, drivers, forwarders and consignees: create, sign, exchange and archive. It also claims interoperability with other providers' services. Source: vchasno.ua/en/ttn/ (search snippet, vendor claim, unverified).
- About 20 providers take part in the state project (vendor page claim). Other named providers include AgriChain, Deals, Zozengo, Comarch, InnLogist and Doc.Smart-Era. Source: news.dtkt.ua (snippet).
- Momentum: Vchasno is actively publishing e-TTN content, including one piece on 2026 and one on e-TTN becoming part of state digital infrastructure (vchasno.ua/en). A news snippet says e-TTN becomes mandatory from 2027. Another says it is not yet mandatory until the Law "On Motor Transport" is amended. The two are inconsistent, so this is unverified.
- Kernel is reported to have run e-TTN with 1,000 carriers and 6,000 drivers (thepage.ua). The provider they used is not stated in the snippet.
- Complaints: none found. The searches (Ukrainian and English) returned no app-store reviews, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum complaints about Вчасно.ТТН. The only complaint-like content is generic paper-TTN problems, such as missing driver signatures. That is not a complaint about this product. The absence of results does not show customers are satisfied. Search coverage was thin.

## Pricing
Not found. No published tariffs or minimums surfaced. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
Nothing verified. Possible gaps, all unverified and not found in the sources: small-carrier and driver mobile UX, price for small fleets, and TMS/freight-exchange integration. The market is a state-run central system with many interoperable providers, so a new entrant must also become an authorized provider or build on one.

## Opening
Little visible opening in the core e-TTN filing and exchange. Any gap would be a thin layer for small carriers or drivers (mobile, cheap, workflow) on top of the existing providers. Needs direct tariff and review research to confirm.
