# M.E.Doc (Ukraine) vs C1079: e-TTN for carriers and shippers

Searches: 5 of 6 used (Ukrainian and English). WebFetch and GitHub not used.

## Verdict: strong

M.E.Doc is one of three government-authorized e-document operators connected to the national e-TTN system. It has a large installed base of accountants who already use it for tax filings. A new e-TTN-only product would compete with an incumbent that sits inside the buyer's existing workflow.

## Evidence
- M.E.Doc ("M.E.ДОК") is listed with Vchasno.TTN and EDI NETWORK (ATS) as an authorized operator for issuing e-TTN. Source: https://news.dtkt.ua/accounting/primary-documents/106393-perelik-program-e-dokumentoobigu-shho-pidkliuceni-do-sistemi-e-ttn
- M.E.Doc has an "Accounting of TTN" module for creating waybills, sending them to the tax service and exchanging them with counterparties. Source: https://news.dtkt.ua/accounting/automation/56310-iak-skladati-elektronni-ttn-v-medos (the article's date is unverified, and the module may predate the state e-TTN system).
- Official e-TTN registration started 1 Dec 2025 with legal force equal to paper. Another source says 7 operators are in production and 11 more are in testing. That means many platforms are entering the market. Sources: https://buhplatforma.com.ua/news/109532-v-ukraini-ofitsiino-zapratsiuvala-e-ttn-koly-potribna-paperova-versiia and https://censor.net/biz/news/4007245/v-ukrayini-zatverdyly-bezperervne-vykorystannya-e-ttn-dlya-biznesu
- Roadmap per search summaries: TTN correction acts by May 2026, special e-TTN forms and system modernization by end of 2026. This is a state-system timeline, not an M.E.Doc one. Source: https://buhplatforma.com.ua/news/108980-yaki-platformy-edo-vzhe-v-systemi-e-ttn
- Momentum: M.E.Doc is actively integrating with e-TTN as of early 2026. A 2025 Prozorro tender covers M.E.Doc network-version licensing and support, which suggests ongoing institutional use. The tender is at https://prozorro.gov.ua/tender/UA-2025-09-30-010332-a/print/open/pdf (details unverified).

## Complaints
- Few found, and none specific to e-TTN. vidhuk.ua has mixed reviews of M.E.Doc generally. One reviewer reports an integration module stopping after an update, and others praise support. Source: https://www.vidhuk.ua/uk/programma-medok.html?rating=5 (I did not verify the individual reviews or their dates).
- A 2017 press story alleges M.E.Doc leadership had access to State Fiscal Service systems. This is a trust and reputation issue, not an e-TTN complaint. Source: https://fakty.com.ua/ua/ukraine/20170720-skandal-u-sferi-podatkovoyi-zvitnosti-m-e-doc-pidozryuyut-u-vtruchanni-u-robotu-informatsijnyh-system-dfs/
- No Google Play, App Store, Reddit or Facebook complaints about M.E.Doc e-TTN turned up. No outage or failed-filing news found.

## Pricing
- No published e-TTN price found. I did not find a price for the TTN module or its licence.
- General licences are sold through dealers, typically per year. Amounts are unverified and were not found.
- Vchasno.TTN pricing was also not found.

## Fit gaps
- M.E.Doc is accounting and reporting software, desktop-centric, with e-TTN as an add-on module. This is an inference from its product type, unverified for the TTN module.
- Mobile or driver-side use (a driver signing or showing the TTN on a phone) is not evidenced in what I found. This is a possible gap, but unverified.
- Small carriers who do not use M.E.Doc for accounting have no reason to adopt it. This is also an inference.
- Search results did not show whether M.E.Doc supports multi-entity carriers or forwarders.

## Opening
Little room to beat M.E.Doc as a platform. The opening, if any, is a mobile, driver-friendly e-TTN workflow for small carriers who are not on M.E.Doc, and it would still face Vchasno and several other operators.
