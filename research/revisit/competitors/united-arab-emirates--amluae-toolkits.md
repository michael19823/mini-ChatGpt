# amluae toolkits (United Arab Emirates) - competitor check for C1083 (goAML for DNFBPs: DPMS and real estate)

## Verdict: weak

amluae.com looks like a content-marketing and consulting site, with an AML software pitch behind it. It is not a visible self-serve goAML filing tool. 5 searches used (of 6).

The author archive slug is "pathiktechnovisors-com", so the site is probably run by a firm called Pathik Technovisors (inferred from the slug, unverified).

The snippets describe modules such as identity verification, screening, case management, KYC/CDD, risk assessment, transaction monitoring and regulatory reporting. That is a full AML suite, not a lightweight goAML filing tool.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in English and Arabic, and for Capterra/G2/Reddit-type reviews, returned no reviews or complaints about amluae. The Capterra and Software Advice results were for other AML products. The absence is partly a sign the product has little public footprint.
- Momentum: the site is actively publishing, with recent-looking posts such as the annual goAML checklist. It is active as marketing. Product development is unverified.
- Other goAML-related players surfaced (Signzy, Focal, Fluxforce, Arnifi, FlyingAML). They are not the assigned competitor. They show the niche has several vendors with similar content marketing.

## Pricing
Not published in anything I could see. Unverified, and the site is likely quote-based.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of a self-serve, small-firm DPMS/REAR XML generator with a visible price.
- The positioning looks consultancy-led (case studies, "guides the implementation"), so it is probably not aimed at a single small jeweller or broker filing a few reports a year. This is an inference, unverified.
- Arabic support, mobile use and multi-entity use: unverified.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve DPMSR/REAR/STR XML builder with XSD validation and published pricing for small dealers and brokers. amluae appears to sell consulting-led AML suites, not that.
