# Citadel365 (united-arab-emirates) - commercial, URL: https://citadel365.com

Idea it killed: C1083 goAML for DNFBPs (DPMS and real estate).

## Verdict: strong

Citadel365 markets directly to UAE DNFBPs and lists goAML-ready STR reporting among its features. It is the same buyer as C1083 and it is actively marketing. No complaints turned up, but that reflects a lack of public reviews more than a lack of problems.

## Evidence
- Search snippet of Citadel365 site: it describes helping UAE DNFBPs implement AML/CFT/CPF compliance with sanctions, PEP and adverse-media screening, CDD/UBO workflows, TFS monitoring, goAML-ready suspicious transaction reporting, and policy and risk-assessment support. Source: https://citadel365.com/?p=2671 (as surfaced in search results; page not opened).
- It offers a free interactive "UAE DNFBPs AML/CFT Compliance Navigator" self-assessment tool, a lead-generation signal that it is actively pursuing this segment. Same source.
- Software listings describe an all-in-one AML platform (onboarding, screening, risk assessment, case management, transaction monitoring, workflow automation) with a free trial. Sources: https://topbusinesssoftware.com/products/Citadel365/reviews/ and https://sourceforge.net/software/product/Citadel365/alternatives
- Complaints: none found. Slashdot comparison pages show 0 ratings. No Trustpilot, Reddit, Google Play, App Store, G2 or Capterra reviews surfaced, in English or Arabic. Absence of reviews is not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing
Not published in anything found (unverified). A free trial is mentioned on listing sites. Whether it is affordable for a small DPMS or broker is unknown.

## Fit gaps
- Unverified in all cases, since no product pages were opened and no reviews exist.
- Looks like a broad AML compliance suite, so it may be heavier than a small DPMS or real-estate broker needs and may not be centred on goAML XML filing alone. This is an inference from the feature list.
- No evidence found about Arabic language support, mobile or offline use, or the exact goAML XML formats it supports.

## Momentum
Active: current marketing content and a free tool. No sign of shutdown or acquisition. Exact release cadence unverified.

## Opening
Only a narrow, cheap, goAML-filing-first tool (XML STR/LCTR generation for small DPMS and brokers, with transparent pricing) could differentiate. Citadel365 already covers this buyer, so any entry would need to beat it on price and simplicity.
