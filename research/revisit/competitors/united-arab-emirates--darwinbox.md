# Darwinbox (United Arab Emirates)

Idea killed: C1081, WPS salary-day monitor (Res. 340/2026).

## Verdict: weak

Darwinbox is a real enterprise HCM and payroll suite with UAE WPS support, but it does not sell a standalone salary-day monitor. It targets large and fast-scaling firms, so it is weak against a small-buyer WPS monitoring tool.

## Evidence
- Darwinbox publishes a blog post on running UAE payroll to meet Resolution 340, describing WPS compliance as a continuous operating state inside its payroll engine. It also lists WPS integration and EOSB/gratuity calculation. Sources: https://darwinbox.com/blog/darwinbox-uae-payroll-resolution-340 ; https://explore.darwinbox.com/org/ae/hr-payroll-software-uae
- Ratings (via search snippets, not checked on the pages): G2 4.4/5 from 173 reviews, Capterra 4.3/5 from 43 reviews, Gartner Peer Insights 4.7 from 627 reviews. Sources: https://www.capterra.ca/software/150606/darwinbox-hr ; https://www.g2.com/products/darwinbox/pricing
- Complaints found:
  - One reviewer says support is "very poor", with slow response and delayed fixes (G2/Capterra snippet; I did not open the page, so the wording is unverified).
  - Gartner reviews, as summarised in the results, mention payroll implementation communication problems, bank/account setup or update delays, and long implementation timelines. This is secondhand and unverified.
  - Vendor-run alternative pages (yomly.com, keka.com, zinghr.com) say GCC buyers look for cheaper, simpler and more localised options. These are competitor marketing, so treat them as weak evidence. Example: https://www.yomly.com/ar/top-darwinbox-alternatives/
- No Reddit, Trustpilot or UAE-specific complaint threads turned up. The Arabic search returned only general WPS news and nothing about Darwinbox.
- Momentum: active. Darwinbox is a unicorn with 900+ enterprise customers and is expanding in the Middle East. Source: https://themiddleeastinsider.com/2026/03/02/darwinbox-review-middle-east-enterprise-hr-2026/

## Pricing
Not published. G2 lists no pricing, and I found no minimums. It is quote-based and aimed at enterprises, so it is probably out of reach for micro and small employers (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- It is a full HCM suite with long implementations, not a lightweight add-on.
- It is aimed at large and fast-scaling enterprises.
- It handles WPS inside its own payroll. A firm that runs payroll elsewhere (another system, a bank portal or an accountant) would not get a monitor from it, and I found no evidence of a standalone or cross-system salary-day monitor.
- It offers no visible WPS alerting for non-customers.

## Opening
A cheap, payroll-agnostic WPS salary-day and deadline monitor for SMEs and accountants who do not use enterprise HCM is still open.
