# DPMS Global (United Arab Emirates) - competitor check for C1083 (goAML for DNFBPs: DPMS and real estate)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

"DPMS Global" could not be identified as a product or company. 4 WebSearch calls (English and Arabic) returned no vendor of that name. "DPMS" is the regulatory term for Dealers in Precious Metals and Stones, so the name looks like a segment label, not a competitor. Unverified: a small vendor of that name may exist but was not found.

## Evidence
- No page, app-store listing, review or news item for a product called "DPMS Global" appeared in any search ("DPMS Global UAE goAML" returned only generic goAML/DPMS regulatory pages).
- goAML is the UAE FIU's official reporting portal (UNODC-developed). Registration is free; DPMS file a DPMSR for cash or wire transactions of AED 55,000 or more. https://amluae.com/portfolio/dealer-in-precious-metals-and-stones-report-dpmsr-under-uae-aml-regulations/ , https://avyanco.com/blog/aml/goaml-registration-uae/
- Market size per a search summary: 8,191 DPMS registered on goAML as of 30 June 2025, and 1,448,825 DPMSRs filed July 2021 to June 2025 (source: https://www.signzy.com/regulation-glossary/UAE-goAML-reporting-platform , the figure is a snippet and unverified beyond that).
- The visible "competition" is consultancies selling goAML registration, DPMSR filing and AML packages (for example amluae.com, avyanco.com, RAKEZ VAS). Services, not software.
- Complaints: none found. No reviews of any DPMS-specific goAML tool surfaced (Google Play, App Store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit and Arabic forums were not hit by the searches).
