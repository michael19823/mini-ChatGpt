# HR and payroll suites (United Arab Emirates) - dropped idea C1092: WPS payroll compliance and Emiratisation tracking

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Many vendors already ship WPS SIF file generation. Zoho Payroll generates it on every pay run; Bayzat generates WPS/GOSI files; greytHR also (https://www.zoho.com/en-ae/payroll/academy/payroll-operations/wps-payroll-software.html, https://www.hono.ai/blog/9-best-hr-and-payroll-software-in-dubai-2026). Most of these are vendor-written or listicle sources, so treat claims as marketing.
- Emiratisation tracking is also sold. ZenHR markets Emiratisation tracking, Nafis enrolment, quota reporting and a free calculator (https://www.zenhr.com/en/emiratisation-calculator). Mercans claims real-time Emiratisation rate tracking and Nafis dashboards (https://mercans.com/glossary/emiratization/). Zimyo claims quota monitoring and MoHRE/NAFIS reports (https://www.zimyo.com/middle-east/?p=153877). Bayzat is described as only "partial" Emiratisation support (hono.ai listing above); this is a secondary source.
- The WPS deadline reportedly tightened: salary due by the 1st of the month with no grace period from 1 June 2026 (per the hono.ai blog; unverified against MoHRE). This raises demand for the feature, and for incumbents.
- Complaints: no genuine user complaints found. One yomly.com blog claims Zoho lacks UAE localisation depth (WPS, DEWS); it is a competitor-style listicle, not a user review (https://www.yomly.com/zoho-payroll-alternatives-for-uae-enterprises/). No app-store, G2, Reddit or Arabic-forum complaints surfaced in the 4 searches used (2 in English, 1 in Arabic, plus a tool-name search).
- Momentum: all named vendors appear active, with 2026-dated content.

## Pricing
- Zoho Payroll UAE Standard: from AED 35/month billed annually, including 5 employees, then AED 7 per extra employee (https://www.zoho.com/en-ae/payroll/academy/payroll-operations/payroll-software-pricing.html).
- greytHR Essential: USD 50/month including 10 employees, then USD 2 per employee per month (https://www.greythr.com/middle-east/pricing/).
- Bayzat and ERA: quote-based (per the hono.ai listing). Mercans, ZenHR and Zimyo: not checked.
- Entry prices are very low, so a small buyer is not overcharged.

## Fit gaps
- Bayzat's Emiratisation support is reportedly partial (secondary source).
- Enterprise suites such as ERA and Mercans are quote-based and probably oriented to larger firms (inference, unverified).
- No evidence found of a gap in WPS file output itself.

## Opening
Little room for a standalone product. The only gap might be a lightweight Emiratisation and Nafis tracker for firms whose payroll tool lacks it, but ZenHR and others already cover it.
