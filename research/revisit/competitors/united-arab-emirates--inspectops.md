# InspectOps (United Arab Emirates) - competitor check

Killed idea: C1088 Fire-safety AMC reporting. Searches used: 4 of 6.

## Verdict: beatable

InspectOps is a real, cheap, NFPA-oriented fire-inspection SaaS, but nothing found shows UAE localisation (Civil Defence formats, Arabic) or any UAE traction. Evidence is thin because it is a new listing with no reviews.

## Evidence
- Cloud fire-protection inspection software: QR-coded asset tracking (extinguishers, alarm panels, sprinklers, suppression, backflow), NFPA-aligned checklists (ten built-in NFPA standards), PDF reports, deficiency tracking, scheduling, work orders, customer portal, mobile capture with photos and signatures. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/10037555/InspectOps/ ; https://www.capterra.ae/software/1086776/InspectOps
- It has a Capterra.ae listing, but this only shows the directory is regional. It is not evidence of UAE customers.
- Complaints: none found. Capterra shows 0 reviews. No app-store, Reddit, Trustpilot or forum reviews turned up. Complaints were not found in any search, not proven absent.
- Arabic-language search (AMC fire system reporting UAE) returned no InspectOps-specific results, only generic AMC and job listings.
- Momentum: listed on Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice with a "2026" pricing page, so it appears active. Founding date, funding and release cadence: unverified.

## Pricing
- Starter $29/month: 1 user seat, unlimited assets, inspections, deficiency tracking, QR scanning, PDF reports, customer portal.
- Growth $79/month: up to 5 seats, adds proposals with e-signature, compliance dashboard, smart scheduling, work orders, priority support.
- A third plan exists but its price was not captured (unverified).
- Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/10037555/InspectOps/
- Cheap for small buyers. Price is not the gap.

## Fit gaps (all unverified; searches showed no mention)
- No evidence of UAE Civil Defence (or Dubai/Abu Dhabi/Sharjah authority) report formats. It is NFPA-centric.
- No evidence of Arabic UI or Arabic/bilingual reports.
- No evidence of AMC contract management (contract value, renewals, visit schedules per AMC), AED invoicing or VAT.
- Offline mode not confirmed.
- No UAE support presence or local reviews found.

## Opening
A UAE-specific AMC reporting tool with Civil Defence-format, bilingual Arabic/English reports and AMC contract tracking could undercut a generic US/NFPA tool, but the $29 price point means it has to win on localisation, not price.
