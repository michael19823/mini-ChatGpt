# KYCK (united-arab-emirates) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C1083 goAML for DNFBPs (DPMS and real estate)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

KYCK is a Singapore-based, first-level KYC / remote-onboarding startup (founded 2016, IBM blockchain pilot) aimed at banks and crypto fintech. I found no evidence it offers goAML filing or serves UAE DNFBPs. Confidence is moderate: 4 searches, no official site found.

## Evidence
- KYCK is described as a Singapore start-up with face recognition and OCR remote onboarding on an IBM blockchain network. Sources: https://www.fintechfutures.com/blockchain-crypto-digital-assets/ibm-and-kyck-go-blockchain-for-kyc ; https://www.biia.com/kyck-launches-know-your-customer-platform-on-ibms-high-security-business-network-on-secured-linuxone-servers ; https://www.cbinsights.com/company/kyck
- Searches for "KYCK" with UAE, goAML, DNFBP, jewellers or real estate returned nothing linking KYCK to the UAE or goAML. UAE DNFBP results were other vendors and consultancies (amluae.com, icomplyis.com, binderr.com).
- Arabic search returned nothing about KYCK. It did show the UAE Central Bank building a national e-KYC platform with Norbloc (https://www.wam.ae/ar/article/bzq8zf0-المصرف-المركزي-يعلن-تطوير-منصة-اعرف-عميلك-الرقمية), a separate KYC-layer risk, not goAML filing.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews surfaced.
- Momentum: unverified. Only 2017-era launch coverage found; no recent activity seen, so it may be stagnant.

## Pricing
Not published or not found (unverified).

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of goAML XML/STR/SAR/DPMSR/REAR filing support.
- Aimed at banks and crypto onboarding, not small jewellers or real-estate brokers.
- No UAE presence found.

## Opening
KYCK does not occupy the goAML-for-DNFBP space; the real competition is local AML consultancies and compliance software vendors (names only seen in results, not assessed here), plus the Central Bank's planned e-KYC platform on the KYC side.
