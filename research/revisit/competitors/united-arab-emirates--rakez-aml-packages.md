# RAKEZ AML packages (United Arab Emirates) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C1083 goAML for DNFBPs (DPMS and real estate).
Searches used: 3 of 6 returned results (English x2, Arabic x1); the 4th hit a usage limit and returned nothing.

## Verdict: beatable

RAKEZ sells a human-delivered, per-engagement consultancy service (via partner A&A Associate), not software. It is tied to RAKEZ free-zone clients and says nothing about ongoing goAML filing tooling.

## Evidence
- RAKEZ Value Added Services portal lists "AML Compliance & goAML Registration", delivered with partner A&A Associate. Process: consultation and risk assessment, then implementation (AML framework built, goAML registration, documentation). Enquiry-form based. Source: https://vas.rakez.com/service/aml-compliance-goaml-registration/
- Packages are described as exclusive rates for RAKEZ clients, so the offer appears limited to RAKEZ free-zone companies (inferred from the search summary; unverified on the page itself).
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, Trustpilot or forum material surfaced. The Reddit/complaints search was not completed (usage limit), so absence of complaints is weakly supported.
- Momentum: page is live on the RAKEZ VAS portal; no date, acquisition or shutdown info found. Momentum unverified.
- Other UAE sellers of the same service exist (e.g. avyanco.com, amluae.com, kpi.co, meydanfz.ae), so the market is crowded with consultants, but this is outside RAKEZ itself.

## Pricing (as shown in search summaries of the RAKEZ page; verify on page)
- AML Survey Support: from AED 1,999 (Survey-290 questionnaire)
- AML Audit Assistance: from AED 4,999 (businesses up to AED 3M annual revenue)
- AML Package - Silver: from AED 3,999
- "Starting from" prices only; contents of Silver package and recurring/annual cost not verified.

## Fit gaps
- Service, not software: one-off setup/registration/documentation, no evidence of an ongoing tool for STR/SAR/DPMSR/REAR filing or monitoring (unverified; page not read in full).
- Limited to RAKEZ clients; mainland and other free-zone DNFBPs (most Dubai real-estate brokers and DPMS dealers) are not served.
- No evidence of Arabic-language product, multi-entity support or workflow tooling.
- Not specific to DPMS or real estate report types.

## Opening
A self-serve, low-cost goAML filing and compliance workflow for mainland and non-RAKEZ real estate brokers and DPMS dealers, which RAKEZ's enquiry-based, RAKEZ-only consultancy does not cover.
