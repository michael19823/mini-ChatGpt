# RapidAML (AML UAE), united-arab-emirates

Idea revisited: C1083, goAML for DNFBPs (DPMS and real estate). Type: commercial. Searches used: 4 of 6 (English and Arabic).

## Verdict: weak

RapidAML is a general AML/KYC/screening/case-management SaaS. No source found says it produces goAML XML filings for DPMS or real estate. It has no public pricing and no reviews.

## Evidence
- Capterra lists RapidAML as cloud SaaS available in the UAE, covering sanctions/PEP/adverse media screening, case management, KYC, risk assessment, transaction monitoring and "regulatory reporting". It shows 0 user reviews and "contact vendor for pricing". https://www.capterra.com/p/10031130/RapidAML/
- Stanford CodeX TechIndex: founded 2021, Dubai HQ, subscription model, target market law firms, corporate legal and legal service providers. Funding is blank. https://techindex.law.stanford.edu/companies/rapidaml
- Searches did not surface any RapidAML goAML-specific feature, DPMS or real estate product, or any press, funding or acquisition news. Unverified whether its "regulatory reporting" covers goAML XML.
- Complaints: none found (no app-store, Reddit, Trustpilot or forum results, in English or Arabic).
- Demand context: goAML registration is mandatory for DNFBPs. The Ministry of Economy suspended 50 DNFBP enterprises in Q3 2023 for failing to register (per a Gulf News-linked search summary, not independently verified). https://gulfnews.com/business/banking/uae-tightens-measures-to-combat-money-laundering-1.81642919

## Pricing
Not published ("contact vendor"). Cannot compare with small-buyer budgets. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Positioned at legal and corporate-legal firms, not at gold dealers or real estate brokers.
- No evidence of a goAML-format filing tool, a simple one-person-shop workflow, or Arabic UI (all unverified).
- Quote-only pricing suggests a sales-led product, which is likely a poor fit for micro DPMS and agency buyers.

## Momentum
Unclear. The company exists (founded 2021) with a thin public footprint, and I found no recent news.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve goAML STR/DPMSR/REAR filing helper for small gold dealers and real estate brokers is not served by RapidAML on the evidence found. Other UAE compliance vendors (for example the AML consultancies in the results) were not assessed here.
