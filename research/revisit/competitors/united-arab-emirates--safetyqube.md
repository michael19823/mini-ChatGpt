# SafetyQube (United Arab Emirates) - dropped idea C1088: Fire-safety AMC reporting

## Verdict: beatable

SafetyQube is a general, configurable EHS platform (no-code), not a fire-safety AMC reporting tool. It aimed at mid-sized organisations, so a narrow AMC-contractor product is not directly covered. Based on 6 or fewer searches only; thin evidence.

## Evidence
- Rated about 4.8/5 by 54-58 users on Capterra/GetApp/Software Advice, with >90% "would recommend" (search summaries of https://www.capterra.ae/software/1022650/safetyqube, https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1022650/safetyqube). Review count is small. Not independently verified.
- Described as a cloud, no-code EHS platform, deployable in under a week (zoftwarehub listing: https://zoftwarehub.com/ar-ae/products/safetyqube/overview).
- Operated by Quber Technologies (per Capterra UAE search summary). Whether it has a UAE office is unverified.
- Target users listed: mid-sized organisations in food, chemicals, pharma, auto, manufacturing, retail, logistics, construction, telecom, field services, healthcare, education, facilities (Capterra UAE summary).
- Complaints found: only mild ones in the review summaries: long startup/setup time, and long timeframes to implement added features (https://softwarefinder.com/facility-management-software/safetyqube/reviews, Capterra pages). No outages, filing failures, Reddit or forum complaints found. No Arabic-language complaints found.
- Momentum: still listed on many review sites with discount listings (spendbase); no sign of shutdown or acquisition. Release cadence unverified.
- No search result tied SafetyQube to fire-safety AMC contractors or Civil Defence (UAE) reporting formats.

## Pricing
- Per-site flat subscription, free version and free trial mentioned on Capterra summaries. No actual prices found; unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Horizontal EHS tool, not built for AMC contractors who report on client-site fire equipment inspections (unverified, inferred from positioning and lack of any fire-AMC mention).
- Aimed at mid-sized organisations; small fire-AMC firms not an evident target.
- Setup effort for customisation is a reported drawback.
- No evidence of Civil Defence / UAE-specific report formats or Arabic UI (unverified).

## Opening
A lightweight, ready-made fire-equipment AMC inspection/report tool for small UAE contractors (with Civil Defence-style reports and Arabic) is not served by a configure-it-yourself EHS platform.
