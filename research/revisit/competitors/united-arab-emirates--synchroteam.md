# Synchroteam (United Arab Emirates) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C1088 Fire-safety AMC reporting.

Note: only 3 of 6 planned searches ran (the usage limit cut the rest off). Complaints were not searched successfully.

## Verdict: beatable

Synchroteam is a generic field-service tool. The searches found nothing that ties it to UAE fire-safety AMC work. It is not a fire-safety product.

## Evidence
- Synchroteam is a cloud field-service management app for scheduling, dispatch, work orders and mobile technicians, with iOS and Android apps. Its listing says it serves construction, facilities services and industrial engineering, in 92 countries with multilingual support. Based in Issy-les-Moulineaux, Paris; founded 2000. Sources: https://capterra.com/p/129634/Synchroteam/ , https://www.softwareadvice.com/field-service/synchroteam-profile/
- A search for Synchroteam with fire safety AMC in the UAE returned only generic AMC service pages. Nothing links Synchroteam to fire-safety AMC reporting or to UAE Civil Defence formats (unverified; absence of search results only).
- The Arabic-language search returned no UAE-specific Synchroteam content. Arabic UI support and a UAE presence are unverified.
- Complaints: none found. The review search hit the usage limit, so this is "not checked", not "no complaints exist". A Capterra reviews page exists at https://www.capterra.com/p/129634/Synchroteam/reviews/Capterra___4402953/ but its contents were not read.
- Momentum: unverified. The listings are dated 2026, which suggests the product is still on sale.

## Pricing
- From $22 per user login per month, with a 14-day free trial (Capterra-derived snippet; list price only). Per-user pricing could be heavy for small AMC firms with many technicians. Plan minimums were not verified.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- No fire-safety templates for extinguisher, hydrant, sprinkler or alarm checklists were found.
- No UAE Civil Defence or local-authority report format was found.
- Arabic reporting and AMC contract renewal tracking were not confirmed.

## Opening
A fire-safety AMC reporting tool with local checklists, Arabic output and Civil Defence formats could beat a generic FSM tool aimed at small UAE contractors, though the complaint evidence is thin.
