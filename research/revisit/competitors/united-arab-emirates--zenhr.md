# ZenHR (United Arab Emirates) - competitor check for C1082 (Emiratisation tracker)

## Verdict: strong

ZenHR has a UAE-localized HRMS with Emiratisation tracking, Nafis enrolment and quota reporting built in. It also offers a free Emiratisation calculator. It is well reviewed and recently funded.

## Evidence
- ZenHR markets automated Emiratisation tracking, Nafis enrolment and quota reporting. It lets users track Emirati hires separately, set targets and generate compliance reports. Source: https://www.zenhr.com/en/hr-software-uae and https://blog.zenhr.com/en/the-best-hr-software-for-uae-companies-in-2026-key-features-why-zenhr-leads-the-market (via search summary; the pages were not opened).
- A free Emiratisation calculator shows the company's gap and fine exposure under MOHRE Tawteen rules: https://www.zenhr.com/en/emiratisation-calculator
- It is positioned for SME through enterprise, with a bilingual English/Arabic interface, WPS, gratuity and MOHRE support. Source: https://blog.zenhr.com/en/zenhr-supports-mohre-requirements-for-hr-and-payroll-in-the-uae-2025
- Ratings: G2 4.6/5 (55 reviews), Capterra 4.7/5 (161 reviews). Sources: https://www.g2.com/products/zenhr-solutions-zenhr/reviews and https://www.capterra.com/p/157129/zenHR/reviews/
- Complaints (G2/Capterra summaries; no UAE- or Emiratisation-specific complaints found):
  - mobile app lacks features;
  - integration problems with external tools;
  - limited customization of reports and workflows;
  - occasionally poor support;
  - a missing-punch attendance case is unoptimized;
  - errors when entering bank details.
- Momentum: a $6M bridge round led by Impact46 was reported in September 2026, with funds going to AI tools, automation, analytics and expansion. It operates in 11 MENA markets. Sources: https://menastartupdigest.com/?p=50372 and https://managers.tn/2026/09/03/zenhr-leve-6-millions-de-dollars-pour-accelerer-son-expansion-dans-la-region-mena/ (via search snippets). No acquisition or shutdown found.
- Arabic-language search returned no relevant reviews or complaints. Reddit and Facebook searches found nothing about ZenHR (unverified beyond that).

## Pricing
Not published; available on request via demo (unverified beyond search summary). It is a full HRMS, so a small buyer pays for the whole suite, with no standalone Emiratisation product price found.

## Fit gaps
- Emiratisation is a module inside a full HRMS, not a standalone lightweight tracker.
- Firms under 20 employees are exempt from Emiratisation (per search results), so the target segment is mid-size and larger employers.
- Reported weaknesses are generic (mobile app, integrations, customization), not specific to Emiratisation.
- Whether it covers deeper Tawteen items such as verification of Nafis/MOHRE filings is unverified.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: a cheap standalone Emiratisation and Nafis compliance tool for companies that do not want a full HRMS, or an add-on that works alongside other HR systems. Even that gap is thin.
