# Access Group (United Kingdom) - competitor check

Killed idea: C1096 Homecare council invoicing and ECM exports. 5 WebSearch calls used. No URL was known beforehand.

## Verdict: strong

Access covers both sides of council invoicing: provider-side homecare rostering/invoicing and council-side commissioning and payment. It is a large, acquisitive vendor.

## Evidence
- Access Care Rostering lists payroll and invoicing generation/management and exports to CSV, XLS and PDF (G-Cloud listing, applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/785679061423787 or /504378394870042; the search summary did not say which listing carries which claim).
- Access Care Planning is described as part of the Access Care Suite, used by 5,800+ registered providers (same G-Cloud listings).
- Council side: Access Adam Social Care Commissioning covers brokerage, package management, invoicing and payment (theaccessgroup.com commissioning-software pages). Access CM is described as electronic monitoring and finance for commissioned home care, interfacing with Mosaic so councils pay for services used rather than commissioned. Case study: Staffordshire County Council / MLCSU invoicing (theaccessgroup.com local-authority-payment-solution-case-study).
- Momentum: many acquisitions in care software (Webroster, Servelec, iCareHealth, Oysta, C360 per press results). No 2026-specific acquisition found; unverified.
- "ECM" in this idea may not be a UK term. Search hits for "ECM export" were mostly California Enhanced Care Management pages, which are irrelevant. Which UK format ECM refers to is unverified.

## Complaints
- One Trustpilot review (ie.trustpilot.com/users/5444c7790000640001912175) rates The Access Group 1/5 for poor after-sales support: slow account manager, urgent requests getting call dates 2 weeks out. It is a single review about the group generally, not specific to homecare invoicing.
- No Reddit, app-store, G2 or Capterra complaints found in the searches. No outage or failed-filing news found.

## Pricing
- Published price guide exists (theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/health-social-care/sectors/home-care/home-care-software-prices/ and blog hsc-home-care-software-price-guide). Search snippets gave no figures, so actual prices are unverified. Models mentioned: per back-office user, per carer, per care recipient.
- G2 says Access Domiciliary Care Software has no public pricing; quote only.
- Access states it serves agencies from new to national, so small buyers are not excluded on paper.

## Fit gaps
- Council-facing invoice formats and exports for individual councils: no evidence either way. Access sells to councils (Adam, CM), so its strength may be on the commissioner side, and a provider may still have to re-key or export for each council's portal (unverified).
- Support responsiveness (single review).
- Likely bundle/suite purchase, so heavy for a tiny agency (unverified).

## Opening
Only a thin one: a lightweight, council-portal-specific invoice and export tool for small agencies not on the Access suite, and only if per-council format gaps are confirmed.
