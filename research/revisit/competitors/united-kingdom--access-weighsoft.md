# Access Weighsoft (United Kingdom)

Killed idea: C1098, DWT (digital waste tracking) phase 1 for receiving sites.
Searches used: 4 of 6, all in English (UK product, no local-language sources needed).

## Verdict: strong

Access Weighsoft is a Defra-listed DWT provider with a built-in DWT app aimed at waste receivers, and it is backed by The Access Group.

## Evidence
- Access Weighsoft (formerly Isys Weighsoft) is a browser-based, modular waste, recycling, aggregates and ready-mix concrete suite. It includes weighbridge, skips, trade waste, hazardous and invoicing modules. It is sold by The Access Group, Colchester, founded 1996, and listed on G-Cloud. Sources: https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/waste-management/software/weighbridge/ ; https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/269393318025998
- The Access Group's own DWT pages say Weighsoft has a Digital Waste Tracking app. It has a submission dashboard, failed-submission reasons with resubmission, and filters by status. The same pages say it passed all 14 Defra Provider Approval Test scenarios with no exemptions and is a Defra-confirmed DWT provider as of June 2026. They also note that from October 2026 DWT starts with waste receivers. These are vendor claims and I did not check them against Defra. Sources: https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/waste-management/digital-waste-tracking-app/ ; https://help-weighsoft.theaccessgroup.com/en/articles/12505415-digital-waste-tracking
- Mobile apps work offline and sync every 30 seconds, with signature and photo capture (vendor claim). Source: the weighbridge page above.
- Complaints: none found. Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice show about 3.5/5 from only 4 reviews, with no specific complaints in the snippets I saw. There was nothing from G2, Trustpilot, Reddit or forums, and no news of outages or failed filings. Review volume is too small to say anything. Source: https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1021876/isys-weighsoft

## Pricing
- No public pricing. G2 says the vendor has not published prices, and the product is sold by quote with modules chosen per customer. Source: https://www.g2.com/products/access-weighsoft/pricing
- Whether it is overpriced for small receiving sites is unverified. No price or minimum was found.

## Fit gaps
- Only vendor-side claims were found, so fit gaps are unverified.
- It is a full ERP and weighbridge suite, so it is probably heavier than a small site that wants only DWT submission. This is inference, not evidence.
- It needs the Access suite (the DWT app is described as built into Weighsoft Evo). A standalone DWT tool for sites on other systems or on paper is not offered, as far as I could see (unverified).
- Weighbridge integration may require supported hardware (unverified).

## Momentum
Actively developed. It had a Defra DWT approval as of June 2026 and an active help centre, and The Access Group runs comparison and marketing pages against rivals such as Waste Logics and VWS PurGo.

## Opening
Little. A narrow opening may remain for a cheap, standalone DWT submission tool for small receiving sites that run no Access suite, but this incumbent has already shipped approved DWT, so the gap looks mostly closed.
