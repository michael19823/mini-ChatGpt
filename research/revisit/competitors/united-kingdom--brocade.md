# Brocade (united-kingdom)

Idea it killed: C1095 Residential PEEPs (April 2026). Searches used: 6 of 6 (English only; UK market). Note: only search-result snippets were available (no WebFetch), so product detail is thin.

## Verdict: beatable

A real, UK-specific, PEEP-aware product exists (brocade.app, not the unrelated Brocade/Broadcom networking firm), but it is a young, narrowly positioned compliance tool for higher-risk buildings. I found no independent evidence of customers, traction or product depth.

## Evidence
- brocade.app is a compliance platform for UK higher-risk buildings: fire risk assessments, safety cases, scheduled checks, resident notifications and an audit trail. It has a "PEEPs Management Software" page and a "Residential PEEPs ... before April 2026" guide, so it targets the dropped idea directly. Sources: https://brocade.app/ , https://brocade.app/peep-management-software , https://brocade.app/guides/peeps-guide
- Its content is SEO-style (guides on PEEPs, safety case reports, "best building safety software" listicle written by itself): https://brocade.app/best-building-safety-software . This suggests content-led marketing, not proof of a large customer base (inference).
- Complaints: none found. No Trustpilot, Capterra, G2, app-store or Reddit reviews surfaced for it; searches for reviews returned only the unrelated Brocade Communications networking company. Absence of reviews likely means small or new, not that users are happy.
- Momentum: guides dated 2026 imply active development and marketing around the April 2026 regulations. Funding, size, acquisition: unverified.
- Not mentioned by the housing-association compliance vendor lists that surfaced in search (BRIKHousing, Propeller Powered PROP-FS, Evident, Infinity Group, FireCert), so it appears less established than those.

## Pricing
Not found in search results. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Positioned for higher-risk buildings / building managers; the PEEP regime also reaches landlords with scattered or lower-rise stock and social housing providers (fit for those unverified).
- Appears to be a broad building-safety suite with PEEPs as one module, not a resident-level PEEP workflow tool (unverified; based on page titles only).
- Whether it supports the BEEP template, High-Rise Building Portal sharing, fire and rescue authority data sharing, or consent handling: unverified.

## Opening
A focused, resident-level Residential PEEP workflow (identification, person-centred assessment, consent, BEEP template, portal sharing) for small managing agents and housing providers could undercut a broad suite whose pricing and depth are unproven.
