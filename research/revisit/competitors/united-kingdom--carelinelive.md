# CareLineLive (United Kingdom)

Idea killed: C1096, homecare council invoicing and ECM exports.
Searches used: 4 of 6 (English only; no UK-specific local-language terms needed).

## Verdict: strong

It already covers rostering, ECM, eMAR, payroll and invoicing for UK homecare. It exports invoices to ContrOCC, CM2000 and Xero. It is well rated, funded and growing.

## Evidence
- All-in-one cloud homecare platform: rostering, care planning, ECM, eMAR, payroll, invoicing, family portal. Over 700 providers and 25,000+ carers (UK and Australia). Source: https://www.softwareadvice.com/product/509834-CareLineLive/ and https://carelinelive.com/our-solution/
- Invoice and payroll export "in a few clicks" to Xero, Sage, CM2000 and ContrOCC, per search summary of carelinelive.com. ContrOCC is a council-side system, so council invoicing is already addressed. Whether it produces specific council ECM-format files is unverified.
- Complaints: Trustpilot 4.5/5 from 168 reviews, with the company replying to all negative ones. The one complaint seen was about app login, sync and hanging, and unresolved tickets. Source: https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/carelinelive.com. Complaints are a small minority. No Google Play or App Store, Reddit or forum complaints were found in the searches.
- Momentum: strong. Majority investment from Accel-KKR in June 2025, part of MAS Technicae Group. Holds ISO 27001, ISO 9001, Cyber Essentials Plus and NHS DSPT. Listed on G-Cloud 14. Source: https://www.homecareassociation.org.uk/corporate/carelinelive.html

## Pricing
Source: G-Cloud pricing documents and https://carelinelive.com/pricing/ (figures as relayed by search summary, ex VAT).
- eRoster: £8.10 per carer per month.
- BYOD: £19.70 per carer per month.
- Managed phones: £45.00 per carer per month.
- Carer Companion app: £16.50 per user per month (under 100), falling to £11.00 (1000+).
- Minimum £120 per month. No extra charge for admin users or add-ons.
- Not overpriced for small agencies; the minimum is low.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed for a small UK agency. Possible gap: council-specific ECM or invoice file formats beyond ContrOCC and CM2000 (unverified).
- Offline and mobile use were not checked.

## Opening
Little. A standalone council invoicing or ECM export tool would only work as a niche add-on for agencies on other rostering systems, or for a council-specific format CareLineLive lacks (unverified).
