# Ecosurety (United Kingdom) - killed C1097: pEPR packaging data

## Verdict: strong

Established, well-resourced packaging EPR compliance scheme (founded 2003, Bristol, B Corp) with a data-reporting service built into membership. A standalone data-reporting tool for pEPR would be competing with the scheme itself.

## Evidence
- Positions itself as a leading packaging EPR compliance scheme; offers data-driven tools and webinars on "understanding your packaging EPR data requirements" (FDF webinar listing, fdf.org.uk, 2024).
- Active and growing: acquired City to Sea's Refill Return platform (letsrecycle.com; circularonline.co.uk). Earlier sold its WEEE and batteries schemes to Reconomy (resourcemedia.eco), so it now focuses on packaging.
- Members include some of the largest UK retail and consumer goods businesses (per PwC/Letsrecycle coverage of its policy director). Ecosurety has also worked with hundreds of producers.
- Complaints: none found. Searches on reviews and Trustpilot returned no Ecosurety review page (the Trustpilot hit was an unrelated company, Ecosure). This is not evidence of satisfaction, only that no public reviews surfaced in 6 or fewer searches. Local-language search is not meaningful here (English-only market).
- Searches used: 4 of 6.

## Pricing
- Ecosurety's own fees: not found (unverified). Schemes typically quote per member. Statutory pEPR base fees are set by Defra, not by the scheme (e.g. plastic GBP 485/t, glass GBP 240/t for 2024 packaging, per letsrecycle/PwC coverage).
- Reporting thresholds: over GBP 1m turnover and over 25 t packaging; small producers GBP 1m-2m, large over GBP 2m.

## Fit gaps
- Appears aimed at brand owners and larger producers (unverified for small-firm pricing or minimums).
- Data tooling is tied to scheme membership; no evidence of a standalone or multi-scheme data product (unverified).
- Not tested: product UX, supplier-data collection or ERP integrations.

## Opening
Only a scheme-agnostic, low-cost data-collection tool for small or mid-size producers and packers (or one that serves several schemes) leaves room; avoid competing with Ecosurety's bundled service.
