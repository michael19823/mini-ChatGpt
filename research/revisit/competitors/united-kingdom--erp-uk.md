# ERP UK (United Kingdom)

Idea killed: C1097 (pEPR packaging data). No URL was given. The searches found erp-recycling.org/uk.

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

ERP UK is a producer compliance scheme, a service business. It is approved by Defra as a packaging compliance scheme, not a data tool. Its offer is registration and reporting managed on the producer's behalf, plus PRN purchase. It does not sell packaging-data software to small producers. A product that helps producers prepare accurate pEPR data would be complementary to it, or aimed at producers who report themselves. Schemes such as ERP UK are possible channel partners.

## Evidence
- ERP UK was approved as a Packaging Compliance Scheme by Defra on 11 Sept 2012 and began offering packaging compliance in 2013, alongside WEEE and batteries. Sources: https://resourcemedia.eco/article/News/ERP_UK_operate_packaging_compliance_scheme-2280 and https://www.letsrecycle.com/news/erp-becomes-packaging-compliance-scheme/
- Its packaging service is described as complete management of registration and reporting, purchase of PRNs, and regulatory updates. Source: https://erp-recycling.org/uk/what-we-cover/streams/extended-producer-responsibility-for-packaging-waste/
- It publishes content on pEPR data accuracy. Source: https://erp-recycling.org/uk/news-and-events/2026/06/packaging-epr-data-accuracy-in-the-uk/
- Market context from search snippets: the Environment Agency has stepped up audits, with 240 site audits in 2025. Over 80% of audited producers reportedly had to resubmit data. This points to demand for data accuracy, but it is a general market signal and not a complaint about ERP UK.
- Momentum: it is active, with 2026 news posts on its site. It is part of the wider ERP group, which runs WEEE, battery and packaging schemes. No acquisition or shutdown was found.

## Complaints
No complaints about ERP UK were found. A Trustpilot-targeted search returned no reviews. A search for scheme complaints returned only general industry criticism of the pEPR policy and fees, and a Defra direct-debit double-collection issue. These are not specific to ERP UK. No Reddit, app-store or forum threads about ERP UK were surfaced.

## Pricing
No ERP UK scheme fees were found, so its pricing is unverified. Scheme fees are probably quoted individually. The government's illustrative base fees (not ERP UK's) are given in the search-result snippets, e.g. plastic GBP 485/t and paper/board GBP 215/t for 2025-26. Source: https://erp-recycling.org/news-and-events/2024/09/packaging-epr-defra-publishes-indicative-fees-for-uk/

## Fit gaps
- It is a managed service, not self-serve software.
- Its stated target is firms with turnover of GBP 1m or more and more than 25 t of packaging. Smaller businesses are outside the obligation and probably outside its focus (unverified).
- It does not appear to offer tooling for producers who handle their own RPD submissions or want to check data accuracy. The evidence is only that none was found, so this is unverified.

## Opening
A self-serve data-preparation or validation tool for producers who report themselves or who want to audit their scheme's work is not served by ERP UK. A tool could also be sold to schemes like ERP UK as a channel.
