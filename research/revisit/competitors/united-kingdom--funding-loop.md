# Funding Loop (United Kingdom, commercial) - fundingloop.co.uk

Killed idea: C1093 (nursery funded-hours headcount to 152 LA portals).

## Verdict
**strong**

## Evidence
- Product does what C1093 proposed: collects Parental Declaration forms, checks working-parent eligibility codes, and submits headcount data to local authority portals; says up to 90% of each form is pre-populated (search snippets of eyalliance.org.uk pages, saashub.com/fundingloop-co-uk, theoldstationnursery.co.uk journal, Apr 2023).
- Claims use by over 2000 nurseries, including over 80% of the top 25 nursery chains (vendor claim as quoted in search results, unverified independently).
- Won Best Supplier of the Year at the Your Nursery Business conference, May 2023 (as quoted in search results).
- Claims support for most UK council systems (search summary; exact count of the 152 LA portals covered is unverified).
- Sponsor of the Early Years Alliance Under 5 content (under5.eyalliance.org.uk "thank you to our sponsors"), so it has sector distribution.
- Complaints: Trustpilot pages exist (e.g. https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/fundingloop.co.uk, about 6 reviews; one regional page title shows 23) but search returned only titles, not review text. No complaints, outages or failed-filing news were found. Review content is unverified. G2 has a discussion page (https://www.g2.com/products/funding-loop/discuss), content not seen.
- Momentum: no acquisition, shutdown or stagnation news found. Searches for 2025-2026 news returned nothing relevant. Momentum is unverified; no sign of decline.

## Pricing
- £2.50 + VAT per child per term, no monthly subscription (search results). Cheap and usage-based, so it is not overpriced for small settings.

## Fit gaps
- Described as built for early years settings in England; Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland coverage is unverified.
- Searches did not show childminder-specific support (unverified, no evidence either way).
- No evidence of unsupported portals, missing languages or hardware needs. Gaps are unverified, not confirmed.

## Opening
Little opening for a generic headcount-to-portal tool in England: Funding Loop is entrenched, cheap and well-regarded. Any opening would have to be a niche it may not serve (childminders, devolved nations, or specific unsupported LA portals), and that needs confirming first.
