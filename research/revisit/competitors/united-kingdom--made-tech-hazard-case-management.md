# Made Tech Hazard Case Management (United Kingdom)

Idea killed: C1094, Awaab's Law phase 2 hazard case management.

## Verdict: beatable

A real, live, council-referenced product. It is priced and sold for mid-to-large landlords, so small landlords are left open. It also has serious rivals (BRIKHousing, DampTrack, Netcall Tenant Hub).

## Evidence
- Made Tech Housing launched a Hazard Case Management module for Awaab's Law: triage, tracking, tenant comms, audit trail. Camden and Barking & Dagenham are named partners. Source: https://www.madetech.com/?p=19904 and https://www.madetech.com/case-studies/camden-damp-and-mould-case-management
- It auto-assigns Awaab's Law categories (emergency / significant / out of scope) and calculates deadlines. It also has login-free damp and mould reporting with photos. Same sources.
- It is listed on G-Cloud 15, so it sells to the public sector. Source: https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/700063/798472290864771-service-definition-document-2026-01-26-0939.pdf
- Competing tools found: BRIKHousing damp and mould module, DampTrack, Netcall Tenant Hub. Source: https://info.infinitygroup.co.uk/-brikhousing-damp-and-mould-module
- Momentum: active. It is promoted with events and blogs and was newly added to G-Cloud 15 in Jan 2026.
- Complaints: none found. A G2 discussion page exists (https://www.g2.com/products/made-tech-housing/discuss), but I saw no review content. The Trustpilot results were for the unrelated furniture retailer "Home Made", so they don't count. No app-store, Reddit or forum complaints found. Absence of complaints is not evidence of satisfaction: the product is new and has few users.

## Pricing (G-Cloud 15 listing, ex VAT)
- Flat annual licence by property count, from £16,000 (up to 4,000 properties) to £40,000 (12,001 to 20,000 properties). Source: G-Cloud pricing document found via search; I did not open it, so treat the exact figures as search-snippet level.
- Implementation, integration and training are £819 per person per day.
- There is no published tier below 4,000 properties. Small landlords (a few hundred properties) would pay at least the £16k tier, which is likely overpriced for them. Unverified whether a smaller tier exists.

## Fit gaps
- Sold to councils and large housing associations on public-sector procurement. Needs implementation days.
- Private landlords, small housing associations, co-ops and managing agents are not clearly served. Unverified.
- Part of a wider Made Tech Housing suite (repairs, voids), so it may be a hard sell as a standalone module. Unverified.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve Awaab's Law hazard tracker for small social landlords (under 4,000 homes) and for the private rented sector as it comes into scope, priced well below £16k a year.
