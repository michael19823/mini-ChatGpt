# Netcall (united-kingdom, commercial) vs C1094: Awaab's Law phase 2 hazard case management

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Netcall plc is growing and actively developed. FY26 (year to 30 Jun 2026) revenue is reported as about 20% up to GBP 57.7m, adjusted EBITDA GBP 12.1m, net cash GBP 21.0m, AI sales nearly tripled. Source: https://kalkinemedia.com/uk/news/announcements/netcall-achieves-20-revenue-increase-to-ps577m-with-ai-product-sales-tripling-in-fy26 (also https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/netcall--net/fy26-trading-update/9678454).
- It acquired Jadu Holdings on 9 Dec 2025 (GBP 10.6m cash plus GBP 3.315m shares) and also lists Smart and Easy NV and Govtech Holdings. Jadu is a UK public-sector platform, so this likely deepens its council footprint (the council link is my inference, unverified).
- Reviews: Capterra shows Liberty Converse at 4.8/5 from 23 users, mostly positive. Source: https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/197657/liberty-converse
- Complaints: none found. The only criticism seen (G2 reviewer) is that the product is focused mainly on English-speaking markets, which does not matter in the UK. Source: https://g2.com/sellers/netcall
- No Awaab's Law-specific Netcall product or housing case study surfaced in 2 targeted searches. Its low-code platform (Liberty Create) could plausibly be configured for hazard cases, but that is unverified.

## Pricing
- Not published on G2 (contact vendor). G-Cloud 15 pricing documents for Liberty Converse (contact centre, not case management) list GBP 5,400 for 10 concurrent agents (Digital), GBP 6,600 (Voice) and GBP 7,800 (Digital and Voice), with extra agents at GBP 540, 660 and 780. Source: https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/700270/551197998236313-pricing-document-2026-01-29-1656.pdf
- Liberty Create pricing was not found (unverified). G2 lists a "per feature" model aimed at 201 to 10,000+ users, which suggests mid and large buyers.

## Fit gaps
- Generic low-code and customer-engagement platform, not a purpose-built Awaab's Law or housing hazard product (no evidence found of one).
- Appears to target larger organisations, with a quote-based model and implementation effort. A small landlord or small housing association is likely poorly served (inferred, unverified).
- No evidence found of offline or mobile inspector workflows for hazards.

## Opening
A narrow, cheap, off-the-shelf Awaab's Law hazard and timescale tracker for small landlords could undercut Netcall, but Netcall is well funded, growing and already sells to the public sector, so it can pivot.
