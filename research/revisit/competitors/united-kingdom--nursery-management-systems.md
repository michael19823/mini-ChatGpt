# United Kingdom: nursery management systems (Tapestry, Famly, Connect Childcare, Nursery in a Box, etc.)

Idea killed: C1093, nursery funded-hours headcount to 152 LA portals. Searches used: 4 of 6 (English only; no distinct local-language market, UK).

## Verdict: strong

Not because of the management systems themselves, but because the exact idea is already a shipped product (Funding Loop) that the management systems integrate with.

## Evidence
- Funding Loop describes itself as specialist nursery funding software for England. It collects parental declarations, checks eligibility codes and submits headcount data to local authority portals, with automated council portal entry. It says it integrates with all leading nursery management systems. Source: https://www.eyalliance.org.uk/?p=16564 and https://www.theoldstationnursery.co.uk/journal/funding-loop-the-quickest-way-to-claim-early-years-funding/ (search snippets).
- Funding Loop claims over 2000 nurseries, including over 80% of the top 25 chains (vendor claim, unverified).
- Tapestry added a Funding Loop-compatible export of funding and attendance data, next to Xero and QuickBooks exports. Source: https://tapestry.info/?p=23199
- Trustpilot has a Funding Loop page (https://at.trustpilot.com/review/fundingloop.co.uk). Search snippets did not show review content, so complaint content is unverified.
- Complaints: no complaints about headcount or LA submission were found. The only complaint-type result was a nursery charging "consumables" on funded hours, which is unrelated. Complaints about the systems themselves were not found in the snippets.

## Pricing
- Funding Loop: GBP 2.50 + VAT per child per term, no monthly subscription (search snippet; unverified on vendor site).
- Nursery in a Box: from GBP 149 per month flat (Capterra snippet).
- Connect Childcare: from GBP 50 per user per month (Capterra snippet).
- Famly, Blossom, Educare: not found.
- Funding Loop is cheap for small nurseries, so it is not overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps
- Funding Loop covers England only; Scotland, Wales and NI are not mentioned (unverified). The number of LA portals it supports was not found, so whether it covers all 152 is unverified.
- Management systems mostly export or integrate rather than submit to LA portals themselves.
- No evidence found of gaps for small nurseries.

## Momentum
Active: Tapestry shipped a new Funding Loop export and Funding Loop is widely adopted. Dates unverified.

## Opening
Little to none; only a niche such as a non-England nation or LA portals Funding Loop does not support, which would need checking.
