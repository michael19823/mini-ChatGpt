# OneAdvanced Care Invoicing (United Kingdom)

Dropped idea: C1096, homecare council invoicing and ECM exports.

## Verdict
strong

Established vendor (OneAdvanced) with a dedicated care-invoicing product aimed at larger providers. No product-specific complaints were found.

## Evidence
- Product page describes Care Invoicing as an invoicing solution for social care, "for larger care providers of all disciplines and all complexities" (https://www.oneadvanced.com/en-ie/products/care-invoicing/).
- Moray Council FOI response mentions Advanced supplying Staff Plan for Care at Home, so Advanced is embedded in council and care-at-home workflows (https://www.moray.gov.uk/information-governance-access-to-information-requests/foi-requests-responses/foi-requests-by-month-2024/foi-request-council-and-provider-care-services/). Not specific to Care Invoicing.
- Complaints: none found about Care Invoicing itself. Ombudsman cases on council homecare invoicing errors (e.g. Wandsworth, https://www.lgo.org.uk/decisions/adult-care-services/charging/22-014-710) concern council invoicing of residents. They do not name OneAdvanced, so they are not evidence against it.
- Company-wide Trustpilot (Birmingham office, 41 reviews, 4/5): mostly positive on support. One reviewer reported poor account-manager responsiveness after a takeover (https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/www.oneadvanced.com/location/the-mailbox-birmingham). Not specific to this product.
- No G2, Capterra, app-store or Reddit reviews found for this product (Capterra hit was for a different OneAdvanced product).
- Momentum: actively marketed on the vendor site. Details of recent releases are unverified.

## Pricing
Not published (unverified). Sales-led, positioned at larger providers.

## Fit gaps
- Targeted at larger providers, so small homecare agencies are likely underserved (inferred from positioning).
- ECM export and council-portal format coverage for Care Invoicing was not verified.
- Likely enterprise sales cycle and bundling.

## Opening
Small and mid-size homecare agencies priced out of an enterprise suite, but with no complaint evidence the opening is only a size and price gap.
