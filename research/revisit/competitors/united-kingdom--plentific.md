# Plentific (United Kingdom, commercial) - vs C1094 Awaab's Law phase 2 hazard case management

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Plentific already sells a dedicated "Awaab's Law Solution": case management (classify emergency / significant hazard / BAU), hazard and SLA tracking, dispatch to own workforce, subcontractors or Plentific's contractor network, resident comms. Source: https://www.plentific.com/awaabs-law-management/
- It has an Awaab's Law resource hub, readiness scorecards (contractors, workflows, resident comms, compliance reporting), and a blog on Phase 2 hazards and the 30 Nov 2026 deadline. Sources: https://www.plentific.com/awaabs-law-resource-hub/ ; https://www.plentific.com/resource-center/blog/awaabs-law-phase-2-explained-the-new-hhsrs-hazards-and-statutory-deadlines-for-2026/ ; https://labmonline.co.uk/?p=44711
- Listed on UK G-Cloud 15 (service definition document, Jan 2026), so purchasable by public-sector landlords. Source: assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk (g-cloud-15 doc 700063).
- Momentum: raised $100M Series C in Aug 2021; clients include Peabody; active product launches in 2025-26. No acquisition, shutdown or layoff news found in my searches (absence only; unverified).
- Complaints: Trustpilot page shows TrustScore 3.8 from about 204 reviews (nz.trustpilot.com/review/www.plentific.com), with search summary mentioning payment issues and poor communication. I did not read individual reviews, so those themes are unverified and are likely from contractor/tradesperson users, not Awaab's Law case handling. No G2 reviews found. Capterra listing exists with little review detail. No complaints specific to Awaab's Law tooling found.

## Pricing
Not published. No prices or minimums found (unverified). Enterprise/housing-provider sales model implied; G-Cloud listing exists but I did not retrieve a price.

## Fit gaps
- Aimed at housing providers with workforce/contractor operations; likely heavy for small landlords, small housing associations, councils with few stock units (inference, unverified).
- Bundled with repairs marketplace and wider platform; may not suit buyers wanting a standalone, lightweight hazard/case-log and evidence tool, or one that sits on top of existing housing management systems (inference, unverified).
- Whether Phase 2 hazards (excess cold/heat, falls, fire, electrical, structural, hygiene) are fully built into the product was not confirmed beyond blog/marketing content.

## Opening
Only a low-cost, standalone, lightweight Phase 2 case/evidence tool for small landlords priced below enterprise platforms; head-on competition with Plentific for large providers is not viable.
