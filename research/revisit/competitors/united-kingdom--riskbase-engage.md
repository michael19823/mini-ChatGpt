# RiskBase Engage (united-kingdom)

Idea it killed: C1095 Residential PEEPs (April 2026). Competitor type: commercial, no URL in the brief.

## Verdict: strong

It is a purpose-built product from an established vendor. It covers PEEPs, door checks and resident engagement for the same buyer the dropped idea targets.

## Evidence
- Engage is described as bringing resident communications, PEEPs, person-centred fire risk assessments (PCFRAs), flat entrance door checks and remedial works into one place. It collects occupancy, vulnerability and assistance information. Source: https://www.newsontheblock.com/news-opinion-prop-tech/bringing-peeps-and-door-checks-into-one-simple-process
- It reaches residents by email, SMS, WhatsApp, QR codes and post. Same source.
- RiskBase says it has about 20 years in risk assessment, inspection and compliance software. It claims more than 500,000 site visits a year. Its partners are housing associations, local authorities, fire safety consultancies and residents' management companies. These are the vendor's own claims, so treat them as unverified. Sources: the newsontheblock page above and https://www.newsontheblock.com/events-and-training/knowledge-bank-peeps-resident-engagement-and-door-checks-as-one-integrated-process
- RiskBase appears as a supplier on the UK G-Cloud framework (service 185642775794906, supplier 708467). That makes it easy for public-sector buyers to procure. Source: https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/supplier/708467
- It is also listed in the SHP Online directory: https://directory.shponline.co.uk/riskbase-comp312612.html

## Complaints
None found. The searches returned no app-store, Capterra, G2, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews for RiskBase Engage. Capterra results were for other products named "Engage". No UK-specific forum or news complaints surfaced. This is an absence of evidence, not evidence of satisfied users. Searching was limited: 4 searches ran successfully, and 2 more were blocked by a usage limit. No local-language search was needed because the market is English-speaking.

## Pricing
Not found. The G-Cloud listing exists, but I did not read its pricing document. Price and minimums are unverified. Housing-provider software like this is probably quote-based, but that is an assumption.

## Fit gaps
Nothing verified. The following are hypotheses only:
- It is likely aimed at social landlords and managing agents with large portfolios.
- It may not suit small residents' management companies or single-block landlords on price or onboarding.
- It is a bundled suite, so a cheap standalone PEEP tool might undercut it.

## Momentum
Active. It has recent marketing and a current G-Cloud listing. The news pieces explicitly tie it to the PEEP and door-check duties. I did not confirm exact launch or update dates.

## Opening
Any opening is limited to small blocks and small landlords, where a lightweight, transparently priced PEEP tool could work. That depends on pricing and fit gaps that are still unverified.
