# Shine Compliance (united-kingdom)

Idea it killed: C1095 Residential PEEPs (April 2026). Searches used: 6 of 6 (English only, since the UK market is English-language).

## Verdict: weak

It is a real, active multi-risk compliance suite, but I found no evidence that it has a residential PEEP product. It is a large-landlord asset-compliance platform, not a resident-level evacuation-plan tool.

## Evidence
- shineCompliance is made by ShineVision Ltd. It is described as a UK multi-risk compliance management platform covering asbestos, building condition, fire safety, gas, H&S, M&E and water. Features listed: real-time reporting, automated risk and priority warnings, iOS and Android surveying app with QR scanner, hierarchical accounts with audit trail, API links, CAD web visualiser, live property registers. Source: G-Cloud listing https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/341438098976418
- PEEP support: none found. Two searches (one targeting a PEEP/resident evacuation module) returned only generic PEEP guidance, nothing naming a Shine PEEP feature. This is "unverified", not proof of absence. The product is built around properties and assets, not individual residents.
- Complaints: none found. Searches for reviews returned only unrelated "Shine.com" (an Indian job portal) and other "Shine" entities, which I disregarded. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or forum reviews of shineCompliance surfaced.
- Momentum: unverified. It is listed on G-Cloud 14 (the pricing document is dated 2026-01-06, https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/documents/702867/773513541644131-pricing-document-2026-01-06-1426.pdf, though I could not confirm that this document is Shine's). No acquisition, shutdown or release news was found.

## Pricing
- G-Cloud listing, per the search summary: £750 to £62,900 per licence per year, with education pricing available. I did not open the pricing document, so treat the figures as unverified. The range suggests enterprise-scale tiers. The lower bound is within reach of a small landlord, but it buys a general compliance suite, not PEEP workflow.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of resident-level PEEP workflow: person-centred fire risk assessment, emergency evacuation statement, sharing with the Fire and Rescue Authority, review cycles. (Unverified.)
- It is aimed at duty holders running multi-risk compliance across large property portfolios, so it is heavy for a small landlord or managing agent who needs only the April 2026 PEEP duty.
- No complaint data, so no reported usability or reliability gaps either way.

## Opening
A lightweight, resident-focused PEEP tool for small and mid-size landlords and managing agents, with PEEP records that can be exported or integrated into suites like shineCompliance rather than competing with them.
