# Valpak (United Kingdom) - pEPR packaging data (C1097)

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Largest UK packaging compliance scheme (since 1997), per its own materials and trade press (https://www.letsrecycle.com/news/valpak-calls-for-new-standard-for-compliance-schemes).
- Sells pEPR data collection, reporting, fee calculation and submissions, plus a Product Data Hub (https://www.valpak.co.uk/valpak-passes-the-million-mark-milestone-through-new-product-data-hub-reporting/). Valpak says its packaging weight database holds over 50 million SKUs (vendor claim, unverified independently).
- Actively developed: 2025 service brochure on reducing pEPR costs (https://www.valpak.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/VAL00008_Service-Brochures-Reducing-packaging-EPR-costs.pdf).
- Complaints: none found for the current product. The one result was an old, unnamed member quoted in the Daily Telegraph on rising compliance costs, relayed via letsrecycle. Date not verified. Searches (English only; no UK-specific local language needed) did not surface Trustpilot, G2 or Reddit reviews of Valpak UK. Absence of complaints is not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing
- Valpak's own fee page not seen. Government-linked figures reported in search results: small-producer registration provisionally 631 (2025) to 696 (2026) GBP via a compliance scheme, versus 1,216 to 1,303 GBP direct (secondary source, provisional, unverified). Scheme service fees on top: unverified/not found.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. Possible gaps (unverified guesses): service-heavy and quote-based, so likely less attractive to very small producers wanting cheap self-serve data tooling.

## Opening
- Only a low-cost self-serve data/reporting tool for small producers, or a tool sold on top of any scheme; head-on competition with Valpak's incumbent scheme plus data hub is hard.
