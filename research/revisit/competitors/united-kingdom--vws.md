# VWS (VWS Software Solutions, UK) - competitor check

Idea killed: C1098 "DWT phase 1 for receiving sites" (about 25 Defra-listed vendors, including Access Weighsoft, Waste Logics, VWS, weighzIO and Wastebolt). No URL was given; the searches did not surface VWS's own site.

## Verdict: strong

An established, specialist UK incumbent with a weighbridge-integrated product (PurGo). I found no complaints, but that reflects a lack of public reviews, not customer satisfaction. Its DWT readiness is unverified.

## Evidence
- VWS Software Solutions makes PurGo, waste and recycling operations software with mobile sales, vehicle PDAs and tracking, customer service and transport management. It is used by local authorities, private waste operators, highways, haulage and quarrying firms (Capterra listing: https://www.capterra.com/p/205218/PurGo/).
- A competitor's comparison page (Access, so biased) describes PurGo as focused on skip hire and trade waste with native VWS weighbridge integration, and says Access Weighsoft covers more sectors: https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/waste-management/resources/weighsoft-comparisons/access-weighsoft-vs-vws-purgo/
- A trade article describes VWS as the UK market leader in on-board and bin weighing (hub-4.com, "Bin-weighing And Software Management Working As One"). This is a claim from a trade article and I could not verify it independently.
- Complaints: none found. Capterra shows 0 user reviews for PurGo. No Trustpilot, Reddit or forum complaints surfaced. Searches were in English only. The queries were UK-specific and the UK language is English, so no separate local-language search applied.
- DWT readiness: I found no source confirming that PurGo has implemented Defra's Receipt of Waste API. Other vendors (Access Weighsoft, WasteMatrix) are explicitly marketed as ready. VWS's status is unverified. Mandatory DWT starts October 2026 in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, and January 2027 in Scotland (per Avery Weigh-Tronix's guide: https://www.averyweigh-tronix.com/en-gb/weighing_guide/digital-waste-tracking-2026-what-the-new-defra-legislation-means-for-waste-operators/).
- Momentum: unverified. No acquisition or shutdown news was found.

## Pricing
Not published. Capterra says to contact the vendor and a free trial is available. Whether it is overpriced for small sites is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Per Access's comparison (biased source), it is narrower on sectors than Weighsoft. Skip hire and trade waste are its focus.
- Its strongest fit is VWS's own weighbridge hardware, which suggests hardware tie-in. This is inferred and unverified.
- DWT Receipt of Waste API support is not confirmed.

## Opening
Little visible for a small founder: a crowded, Defra-listed field with an entrenched hardware-linked incumbent. Any gap would be a cheap, hardware-agnostic DWT receipt tool for small sites, and this research found no evidence that VWS leaves it open.
