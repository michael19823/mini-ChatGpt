# Wastebolt (united-kingdom)

Idea killed: C1098 DWT phase 1 for receiving sites. Competitor URL was not known beforehand.

## Verdict: weak

Cheap, narrow and apparently small-scale. It is a real product, but it is a compliance-paperwork tool from a consultancy and not a weighbridge/site-operations system. Its fit with receiving sites (weighbridge integration, ticketing, accounts) is unverified.

## Evidence
- Wastebolt is AR Enviro's digital waste compliance platform for UK waste businesses replacing paper Waste Transfer Notes with signed, searchable records. Targets carriers, skip-hire firms, transfer stations, weighbridge operators and construction sites. Source: https://netzerocompare.com/software/wastebolt (search-result summary, not read in full).
- Features listed: WTNs, season tickets, hazardous and POPs records, e-signatures, plus mobile capture for drivers (skip collections, dockets, photos). Same source.
- Positioned narrowly on compliance records, not as broad waste-management software. Same source.
- AR Enviro is a waste permitting / environmental compliance / WAMITAB consultancy (farms, AD plants, skip-hire). Software looks like a side product. Same source.
- Momentum: no news, funding, release notes or acquisition found in 3 searches (a search for "Wastebolt reviews" returned only unrelated "GarbageBolt" US results, which I discarded). Momentum: unverified, no sign of heavy investment.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews for Wastebolt turned up. Absence of reviews suggests a small user base. UK-specific search terms were English only; the market is English-language.

## Pricing
- Starting price GBP 17.49 per month, free trial, monthly subscription, cloud browser app (per Net Zero Compare snippet; exact tiers and per-user or per-site limits unverified).
- That is already cheap enough for small buyers, so price is not an opening.

## Fit gaps (for a receiving-site DWT phase 1 product)
- Receiving-site features (weighbridge integration, gate/ticketing, site stock, invoicing, direct Defra/DWT service interface) are not mentioned in what I found; unverified whether it has them.
- Whether it is a Defra-listed DWT vendor is not confirmed by my searches.
- Focus on WTN capture suggests it is not an operations/back-office system.

## Opening
Little room on price or complaints; the opening is only if receiving sites need weighbridge-integrated operations plus DWT, which Wastebolt does not appear to cover.
