# Weightmans Awaab's Law Compliance Tool (united-kingdom)

Idea killed: C1094, Awaab's Law phase 2 hazard case management.

## Verdict: beatable

A real, current product from a law firm, aimed at social landlords. It is a consultancy-led offering, and the searches found no public price, user reviews or complaints. Its direct software rivals look stronger as software.

## Evidence
- Product page exists: https://www.weightmans.com/products/awaab-s-law-compliance-tool/. It describes a digital platform to log, track and resolve issues, manage repairs and claims, and analyse performance. It has an auditable compliance trail and safeguards for vulnerable tenants. It can integrate with existing property systems or run standalone. It is "developed and monitored by a team of more than 20 housing specialists".
- Launch news on weightmans.com (media-centre page "Weightmans unveils new compliance tool..."), tied to Awaab's Law phase one (27 October 2025, per search summary).
- Software rivals found in the same searches: Plentific "Awaab's Law Management" (https://www.plentific.com/awaabs-law-management/), Mobysoft RepairSense Damp and Mould Case Manager, and Made Tech damp and mould module (housing-technology.com). These are specialist housing-tech vendors and look more product-led than a law firm's tool.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews of the Weightmans tool surfaced in 2 searches. The tool is new and B2B, so little public review data is likely. Absence of complaints is not evidence of satisfaction.
- Momentum: recently launched, so active. Nothing found on acquisition or shutdown.

## Pricing
Not published in the results. Unverified. Law-firm tools are typically sold by enquiry to landlords, with no self-serve tier (this is an inference, not a source).

## Fit gaps (mostly inferred, unverified)
- Aimed at social housing providers in England. Small landlords, private-rented-sector landlords and small housing associations are not mentioned.
- Positioned as a legal/compliance service, so it may be heavy for small buyers.
- Phase 2 hazards (beyond damp, mould and emergency hazards) are not confirmed as covered. Check this.

## Opening
A self-serve, low-cost hazard-case tool for small social landlords or the private rented sector, plus phase 2 hazard coverage, could undercut a firm-led, enquiry-priced product. The more serious competition is Plentific, Mobysoft and Made Tech.
