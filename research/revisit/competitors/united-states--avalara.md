# Avalara (United States) - dropped idea C1110: PACT Act monthly reports and vape directories

## Verdict: beatable

Avalara is a generic tax-compliance platform. It has a PACT Act blog post and says it helps with PACT Act registration and filing, but no dedicated PACT Act monthly-report product for small vape retailers turned up. Its complaints and pricing leave room for a cheap, vertical tool.

## Evidence
- Avalara's blog post says the PACT Act now covers vaping and e-cigarettes, and its services are described as helping with PACT Act registration/filing, licenses, sales and excise tax setup and certificate management. No standalone PACT Act monthly-report product or vape directory was seen (searches were limited, so this is unverified). https://www.avalara.com/blog/en/north-america/2021/05/the-pact-act-now-includes-vaping-cannabis-and-other-e-cigarettes.html
- Complaints (summarised by a competitor's blog, taxcloud.com, and an aggregator, so partisan and secondhand): poor support, unexpected charges and complicated cancellation, annual price increases, and slow performance or integration issues. Ratings reported: G2 4/5 (770 reviews), Capterra 4/5 (349), Trustpilot 3.5/5 (473). https://taxcloud.com/blog/avalara-reviews/ and https://checkthat.ai/brands/avalara/reviews
- Specialist vendors target the vape PACT Act niche, e.g. Token of Trust (monthly reporting checklist for the June 10, 2026 deadline) and Flourish Software. This suggests the niche is served by others and not by Avalara's main product. https://tokenoftrust.com/blog/the-pact-act-monthly-reporting-checklist-what-every-vape-retailer-must-file-by-june-10-2026/ and https://www.flourishsoftware.com/blog/pact-act-the-vape-industry
- No Reddit, Facebook or app-store complaints were checked (search budget used on the above). Local-language search not applicable (US, English).

## Pricing
- Reported (third-party, partly unverified): Core Compliance at $799 per state per year for companies under $50M revenue, registrations about $403 per location, and a Returns plan from $19/month. https://zamp.com/article/avalara-pricing
- Avalara does not publish full pricing; quotes need a sales call and overages and renewal increases are reported (competitor-sourced). That is heavy for a small vape shipper filing in many states.

## Fit gaps
- Generic sales/excise tax tool; no evidence of vape-specific monthly delivery-report generation (recipient, product, brand, carrier per state) or a vape directory. Unverified.
- Per-state pricing and sales-led buying suit mid-size sellers, not small online vape shops.
- Support and billing complaints.

## Opening
A low-priced, vape-specific PACT Act monthly-report and state-directory tool for small shippers is open; the competition is mainly niche vendors (Token of Trust, Flourish), not Avalara.
