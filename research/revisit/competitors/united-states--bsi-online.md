# BSI Online (Backflow Solutions, Inc.) - united-states
Killed idea: C1104 Backflow tester multi-utility hub. Searches used: 5 of 6 (English only; no local-language variant needed for the US).

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Entrenched with utilities: claims 550+ water purveyors, 30+ years, a US patent on the concept (https://www.inh2o.org/associate-member-resource-directory-/bsi-online).
- Still gaining utilities: Aspen (notice 31 Jul 2025, https://aspen.gov/CivicAlerts.asp?AID=867), Blaine MN (planned switch early 2025), Friday Harbor WA (from 1 Dec 2025, https://fridayharbor.org/2160/Backflow-Testers). Actively expanding; no acquisition or shutdown found.
- Model: utility contracts BSI; testers file via CCN-based online reports, and BSI tracks tester licences and gauge calibration (https://www.roundrocktexas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/BSI-Online-Tester-Instructions-USA_9.6.22-3.pdf).
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, Trustpilot or BBB reviews of BSI surfaced. Not evidence of satisfaction; search coverage was thin.
- Not all utilities use BSI (e.g. Boulder runs its own portal, https://bouldercolorado.gov/backflow-portal-frequently-asked-questions), so testers still face multiple portals. Tester frustration with that is only inferred, not sourced.

## Pricing
Tester-paid filing fee per report, set per jurisdiction: Queen Creek AZ $14.95, Broomfield CO $14.95, Wilton Manors FL $28, Cedar Hill TX $30, Wichita Falls TX $30 (from the utility pages found by search). Utility-side contract pricing: unverified.

## Fit gaps
- The product is built for the utility, not the tester; testers are a fee-paying input. No tester-side business features were seen (scheduling, invoicing, customer CRM, one login across non-BSI utilities). Unverified: no product review was available.
- Does not cover utilities on their own or other portals.

## Opening
A tester-side tool (one workflow, multi-portal filing, invoicing) could sit beside BSI, but it would depend on utility portals and BSI's incumbency, so the opening is narrow.
