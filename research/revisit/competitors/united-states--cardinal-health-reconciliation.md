# Cardinal Health Reconciliation (united-states)

Dropped idea: C1106, Pharmacy MFP refund reconciliation.

## Verdict
not-a-real-competitor (for this idea, based on 3 searches that found no evidence of a Cardinal Health MFP reconciliation product)

## Evidence
- Searches on a Cardinal Health MFP/340B reconciliation tool, and on Cardinal Health and the Medicare Transaction Facilitator (MTF), returned nothing about such a product. Each search result summary said so explicitly. Absence in 3 searches is not proof that none exists (unverified).
- The MFP-specific reconciliation tools that did surface are other vendors:
  - Plenful "MFP Intelligence", which does claim-level reconciliation of expected vs received MFP refunds (https://www.plenful.com/use-cases/mfp-intelligence). Plenful says up to 20% of MFP refunds go missing, mainly in the 340B setting.
  - Beacon MFP, which manufacturers use to validate MTF payments (https://plenful.com/blog/four-key-challenges-to-prepare-for-as-medicares-maximum-fair-price-takes-effect).
  - Apexus 340B PVP Manufacturer Refund Service (https://340bpvp.com/manufacturers/manufacturer-refund-service). This is a manufacturer-side service.
- Pain points that exist in the market (the opening is real, but no Cardinal link):
  - NCPA survey: most member pharmacies wait 22 days or more for MFP refunds, and about a fifth wait more than 28 days (https://www.ncpa.org/newsroom/qam/2026/02/27/ncpa-shares-survey-results-cms-showing-mfp-refund-delays).
  - NCPA estimates a typical pharmacy floats about $27,000 a month in unreimbursed MFP cash flow (same cluster of NCPA/Becker's results).
- Complaints about Cardinal Health reconciliation: none found.
- Momentum: unverified. Nothing found on Cardinal-specific development in this area.

## Pricing
No published price found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Cardinal Health is a wholesaler, and no MFP refund reconciliation product from it was found.
- The MTF Payment Module (CMS) is free but optional for manufacturers, so refund timing and format vary by manufacturer. A pharmacy-side tool that matches refunds to claims across manufacturers is still needed.
- The real competition is Plenful and similar vendors, so those should be checked as the competitors for C1106.

## Opening
Cardinal Health is not a blocker for C1106. Independent pharmacies still lack a cheap claim-to-refund matcher, and Plenful is the one to check next.
