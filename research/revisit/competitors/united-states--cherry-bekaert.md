# Cherry Bekaert (United States) - competitor check

Idea killed: C1110, PACT Act monthly reports and vape directories.
Searches used: 5 of 6 (English only; the market is US, so no local-language search applies).

## Verdict: beatable

Cherry Bekaert does offer PACT Act monthly reporting, but as a high-touch professional-services engagement. It is not self-serve software, and it is not a directory product. A cheap, automated tool for small vape sellers is not blocked by it.

## Evidence
- Cherry Bekaert (cbh.com) markets "PACT Act: State Reporting Compliance Services". It says it prepares and submits monthly PACT Act reports for all applicable states. It also says it is one of the few providers of end-to-end, state-by-state PACT Act reporting, excise tax and sales tax filing, and contrasts itself with "automated-technology solutions" by offering direct access to consultants. Sources: https://www.cbh.com/?p=48282 , https://www.cbh.com/insights/articles/new-pact-act-reporting-and-excise-tax-requirements/ , https://www.cbh.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Brochure_SALT-PACT-Act-Tax-Compliance_767022846.pdf
- A software competitor is already in this space: Token of Trust sells PACT Act reporting software with tiered pricing. Source: https://published.tokenoftrust.com/pact-act-pricing/
- Momentum: Cherry Bekaert is active and growing. It has been backed by Parthenon Capital since June 2022, and the search summary lists 2026 acquisitions (Tarsus in January, RKE in February, Calvetti Ferguson in June, AcctZen in September). These are reported by search summaries and are unverified against the primary pages. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherry_Bekaert , https://accountingtoday.com/news/cherry-bekaert-acquires-pkf-mueller . PACT Act work is a small niche inside a large PE-backed firm, so it is probably not a core focus (inference).
- Complaints: none found about its PACT Act service. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews of it surfaced. The only criticism found was from employees (long hours, below-market benefits, billable-hour pressure on Glassdoor and Indeed), which is not relevant to buyers. I did not search for Reddit or vape-forum threads directly. Source: https://managementconsulted.com/cherry-bekaert/

## Pricing
- No published price for the PACT Act service. Quote-based (unverified: no price page found). A CPA-firm engagement for all-state reports plus excise and sales tax filing is probably priced well above software (inference, not verified).
- For comparison, Token of Trust lists $500/month for up to 3 states ($167 per report) up to $2,500/month for up to 50 states ($50 per report), per its pricing page. This was seen in a search summary only.

## Fit gaps
- Service-based, not a self-serve product. There is no instant onboarding, dashboard or API.
- Its site contains no directory or lookup tool for vape products or state requirements (unverified; I did not see the full pages).
- Likely aimed at mid-size or larger operators that want outsourced tax filing. Small shops that want only the monthly report may be underserved (inference).
- Per-state filing is handled by staff and not automated, so turnaround may be slower around the 10th-of-month deadline (inference).

## Opening
A low-cost, self-serve tool that generates and files state PACT reports for small online vape sellers, undercutting a CPA engagement. Its real rival is software such as Token of Trust, not Cherry Bekaert.
