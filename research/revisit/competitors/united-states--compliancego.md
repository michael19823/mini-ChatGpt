# ComplianceGO (united-states) - killed C1113: SWPPP inspections

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Cloud stormwater inspection and SWPPP document platform, founded 2007, Orem, Utah. Claims 1.47M+ inspections and ~13,000 users in 48 states (company site snippets: https://old.compliancego.com/construction-stormwater/ , https://compliancego.com/).
- Capterra: 4.5/5 from 122 reviews, 94% positive, 6% neutral, 0% negative (https://www.capterra.com/p/206627/complianceGO/). Praise includes support answering the phone quickly.
- Complaints: none found. Only 6 searches allowed (4 used); no Reddit, app-store or G2 complaints surfaced. Not exhaustive, and no local-language search applies (US, English).
- Momentum: acquired by SwiftComply on 2026-06-04, which also bought CloudCompli in 2025 and has 700+ municipal customers (https://businesswire.com/news/home/20260604586432/en/SwiftComply-Acquires-ComplianceGo-Further-Investing-in-Stormwater-Compliance-Software-for-Regulatory-Compliance-Teams , https://www.stormwater.com/stormwater-bmps/compliance/article/55386706/qa-swiftcomply-ceo-on-acquiring-compliancego-and-the-future-of-stormwater-compliance-software). Customers keep access. Active, better funded; some post-acquisition integration risk (unverified).

## Pricing
- Per search summary of Capterra/listing pages (exact figures unverified): $100-$200 per user per month, or $25-$40 per project per month; plans said to start around $25/month, no setup fee. (https://www.capterra.com/p/206627/complianceGO/)
- Not overpriced for small contractors; per-project pricing suits them.

## Fit gaps
- Focus is shifting to municipal (MS4) utilities after the acquisition, which may reduce attention to small construction contractors (inference, unverified).
- No evidence found of missing offline/mobile use, or of other gaps. Not verified either way.

## Opening
Little: well-reviewed and cheap for small buyers; only room is a very low-cost, contractor-first niche tool if SwiftComply drifts to municipal.
