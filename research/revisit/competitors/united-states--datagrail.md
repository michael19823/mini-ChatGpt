# DataGrail (united-states) - killed idea C1111: California DROP for data brokers

## Verdict: strong

DataGrail already sells a dedicated "Data Broker Compliance" product for the California DROP process. It is well funded and still shipping, but it targets mid-market and larger privacy teams, so small data brokers may be an opening.

## Evidence
- DataGrail markets "Data Broker Compliance" as end-to-end DROP automation for registered data brokers. It is described as built for the DROP batch process and works alongside existing DSR workflows. Sources: https://www.datagrail.io/solutions/drop-compliance/ , https://docs.datagrail.io/docs/request-manager/data-broker-compliance/overview
- Context: Delete Act obligations began 1 Jan 2026. From 1 Aug 2026 brokers must check DROP every 45 days and confirm deletion. Penalties are $200 per consumer per day. Source: https://www.datagrail.io/blog/regulations/the-delete-act-and-drop-what-you-need-to-know/ (vendor's own blog).
- Reviews: G2 shows about 4.7/5 from 206 reviews (search snippet). Reviewers say it appeals more to mid-market companies. Complaints found: consent banner metrics display, integration difficulties, limited customization, and a large engineering lift to reach full potential. Source: https://www.g2.com/products/datagrail/ . I did not verify the individual review pages. I found no complaints specific to the DROP product.
- Momentum: raised $45M Series C, $84.2M total, led by Third Point Ventures (https://www.securityweek.com/datagrail-raises-45-million-data-privacy-platform/amp/). Launched the Vera AI Agent in March 2026 (search summary, unverified). No acquisition found.

## Pricing
Not published. Custom pricing on G2 and Capterra. Minimums are unverified.

## Fit gaps
- It is a general enterprise privacy suite, and the DROP product sits alongside DSR workflows. Small data brokers wanting a lightweight, DROP-only tool may be underserved (inferred, unverified).
- Integration and engineering effort are reported as heavy.
- No self-serve published price.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, DROP-only tool for small registered brokers, whom DataGrail's custom-priced, engineering-heavy suite probably does not serve well.
