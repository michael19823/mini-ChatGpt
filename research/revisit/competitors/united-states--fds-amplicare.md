# FDS Amplicare (United States) - competitor check for C1106: Pharmacy MFP refund reconciliation

**Verdict: not-a-real-competitor** (for MFP refund reconciliation, based on public search; 5 WebSearch calls used, English only since the market is US)

## Evidence
- FDS Amplicare was acquired by Omnicell for a base price of $177.0M, closed 9 Sep 2021, and folded into the EnlivenHealth offering. It is described as SaaS financial management, analytics and population-health tools for pharmacies. FDS Amplicare had about $29M revenue in the 12 months to 30 Jun 2021. Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/926326/000092632621000028/exhibit991-closingfdsampli.htm ; https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210909006079/en/Omnicell-Completes-Acquisition-of-FDS-Amplicare
- Amplicare's own platform is described as plan comparison (Navigate enrollment portal), adherence and performance analytics, and in-workflow opportunity notifications. Nothing found about MFP refund reconciliation. Source: https://amplicare.com/platform?hsLang=en (search snippet only, not fetched).
- Searches for "FDS / Amplicare / Omnicell / EnlivenHealth" plus MFP reconciliation returned none of them. The MFP reconciliation vendors that did appear were Plenful, Liberty Software, PrimeRx, Outcomes and Cencora. Examples: https://plenful.com/blog/four-key-challenges-to-prepare-for-as-medicares-maximum-fair-price-takes-effect ; https://www.primerx.io/2026-medicare-mfp-changes-what-pharmacies-need-to-do-and-how-primerx-will-support-you
- Complaints: none found. Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice listings showed no reviews. No app-store, Reddit or outage items surfaced (the searches were not exhaustive).
- Momentum: no 2026 news found on Amplicare. Search results were only 2021 acquisition coverage. Product development status is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of any MFP refund tracking or reconciliation feature (absence in search, not proof it is not on the roadmap or sold privately).
- Positioned as an analytics and plan-enrollment tool, not a payment reconciliation tool.
- Owned by Omnicell, so it is likely sold as part of a larger pharmacy-services bundle (unverified).

## Opening
Amplicare does not appear to address MFP refund reconciliation, so the real competition for C1106 is Plenful, pharmacy-system vendors and the other names above, which should be checked separately. Omnicell could add the feature (unverified).
