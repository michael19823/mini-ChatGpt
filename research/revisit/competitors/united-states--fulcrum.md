# Fulcrum (united-states) - idea C1123: Lead service line inventory software

Competitor URL was not given; identified as Fulcrum (fulcrumapp.com), a field data collection platform. Identification is an assumption, but search results show it has a Lead Service Line Inventory app.

## Verdict: beatable

Fulcrum is a general-purpose field data collection tool with an LSL template, not a dedicated LSL inventory and compliance product. Its per-user pricing and 5-user minimum leave room for a cheap, small-system-focused product.

## Evidence
- Fulcrum offers a "Lead Service Line Inventory" app built around the EPA Revised Lead and Copper Rule spreadsheet. It captures photos and geospatial data in the field and integrates with GIS. Source: https://www.fulcrumapp.com/apps/lead-service-line-inventory-lsl/
- It has a city customer story (City of Wyoming) listed in results: https://www.fulcrumapp.com/customer-stories/city-of-wyoming/ (contents not read).
- G2 average 4.6/5 from 248 reviews; Capterra 4.7 from 238 reviews (as summarized by search). Sources: https://www.capterra.com/p/136604/Fulcrum/reviews/?page=2 , https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/fulcrum-review-4720904
- Complaints (search summary of G2/Capterra reviews, not verified against the pages): subscription model hard on startups; API minimum cost high for small businesses; one reviewer says Fulcrum kept raising prices and now requires at least 5 paid users; downloading photos is a hassle; no batch PDF printing; one user says almost every release ships with glitches. Complaints are not specific to LSL.
- Momentum: active. Acquired Wildnote in Feb 2026 (environmental compliance), released Audio FastFill (2025) and Insights (2026), and partnered with GeoMarvel (Mar 2025). Sources: https://www.privsource.com/acquisitions/deal/rJSDyX , https://www.fulcrumapp.com/newsroom/fulcrum-geomarvel-partnership/
- A search result (https://liveinthefuture.org/startups/lead-service-line-compliance-saas.html) claims enterprise solutions are overkill and overpriced for small rural systems. This is an opinion page and unverified. No Reddit, app-store or forum complaints about Fulcrum for LSL were found in the searches.

## Pricing
- Search snippets (toolradar, getapp) list Professional at about $43 per user per month, an Elite tier at about $39 per user per month, 5-user minimum on some plans, no permanent free plan. Unverified, as the pages were not read and the tier labels look inconsistent. Source: https://toolradar.com/tools/fulcrum/pricing
- At 5 users, about $215/month (about $2,600/year), before any GIS or consulting costs. This is likely too much for a tiny system with a single field worker.

## Fit gaps
- Generic data collection platform with an LSL template. It does not appear to handle the whole LCRI workflow: historical records review, EPA/state inventory-format submission, customer notification, replacement tracking. Unverified, based only on the app description.
- A 5-user minimum and per-user billing do not suit systems with 1-2 staff.
- Generic form builder means the utility must configure and operate it.
- Buyers often procure via consultants (per research/countries/united-states.md), which Fulcrum's approach does not change.

## Opening
A flat-price, state-format-ready LSL inventory and submission tool for small water systems (under 10,000 people), with no minimum seats, beats Fulcrum's per-user generic tool on price and fit.
