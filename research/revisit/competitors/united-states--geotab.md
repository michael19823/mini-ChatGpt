# Geotab (united-states) - killed idea C1114: Clean Truck Check

## Verdict: strong

Geotab is a real, certified, actively maintained CARB Clean Truck Check submission path. It does not cover fleets without telematics, which is the gap the idea would target.

## Evidence
- Geotab's GO9 device family is CARB certified for Clean Truck Check data collection and submission. Certification date is given as Nov 21, 2024. It runs through the MyGeotab "Emissions Diagnostics Report" add-in. Sources: https://support.geotab.com/mygeotab/mygeotab-add-ins/doc/carb-user-guide ; https://www.fleetequipmentmag.com/geotab-emissions-carb-certification/
- The GO device reads OBD data (fault codes, VIN, readiness status, NOx and other tracking data) and submits it to CARB continuously until a pass result. Same sources.
- Under CARB rules, telematics-based OBD submissions are the one route that needs no credentialed tester. Sources: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/clean-truck-check-credentialed-tester-overview ; https://hardworkingtrucks.com/heavy-duty/article/15637188/truck-reporting-system-opens-for-new-california-smog-check-program
- Complaints found were about Geotab in general, not Clean Truck Check. They come from search-result summaries of review sites, and I did not open the primary G2 or Capterra pages. Themes: customer support, billing disputes and cancellation friction; dated UI; promotional pricing cliffs and hidden fees; hardware reliability. Sources: https://tech.co/fleet-management/geotab-review-fleet-management ; https://g2.com/products/geotab/reviews?page=3 (unverified at review level).
- Reported ratings: 4.6 on G2, 4.7 on Capterra. Unverified, from a secondary summary.
- No Clean Truck Check-specific complaints, Reddit threads or forum posts found. Search returned nothing on this.
- Momentum: active. It is a major vendor with a new certified product as of late 2024. No sign of stagnation or shutdown.

## Pricing
- Not published; quote-only via resellers. Secondary sources say a software plus rented hardware bundle runs about $30-$40 per vehicle per month, and buying hardware outright about $80-$120 per unit. Unverified. Sources: https://costbench.com/software/fleet-management/geotab/ ; https://tech.co/fleet-management/geotab-review-fleet-management
- Clean Truck Check-specific add-on pricing: not found.
- For a small carrier that wants only 2 emissions tests a year, a full telematics subscription is likely more than the need justifies. This is an inference, not a verified price comparison.

## Fit gaps
- It requires Geotab GO9 hardware plus a telematics plan and a reseller relationship. There is no standalone, test-only option.
- It is built for fleets that want telematics. Owner-operators and small carriers with a few trucks, or non-California trucks that only occasionally pass through, may not want a recurring subscription.
- It likely works only on OBD-equipped vehicles of 14,001 lb GVWR and over, as the program defines them. This is a program scope limit, not a Geotab-specific gap.
- Complaints about support and billing suggest room for a simpler, transparent product. This is weakly supported.

## Opening
Small and occasional-California carriers who want pay-per-test compliance, reminders and status tracking without a telematics contract. Geotab and Zonar are telematics-bundled, and a lightweight service would still need a CARB-verified OBD test device or vendor partnership.
