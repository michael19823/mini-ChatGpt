# KinderSystems (united-states) - killed idea C1115: Child care subsidy attendance

## Verdict: strong

It is the state-contracted system of record for subsidy attendance, and it is not a product a small
provider chooses. Other vendors integrate with it rather than replace it.

## Evidence
- KinderSystems (formerly Controltec, renamed; merged with COPA, a Head Start data vendor, in 2021) sells
  subsidy management and time-and-attendance SaaS to state agencies, Head Start programs and providers, with
  20+ years in the field. Sources: https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/52890-22 ,
  https://cioinfluence.com/itechnology-series-news/controltec-announces-name-change-to-kindersystems/
- Products: KinderConnect (provider web portal), KinderSmart (parent mobile app), KinderBridge (integration
  with providers' child care management systems). Source: search summaries of the pages above (details unverified).
- Texas and Washington state agency documents show it deployed as the official attendance system (TWC and
  DCYF PDFs, for example https://dcyf.wa.gov/sites/default/files/pdf/25-1008_Notice.pdf). Tablet sign-in works
  offline and syncs later (Texas TWC attendance matrix).
- Third-party tools already integrate with it: Playground (AZ, IN, IA, LA, MO, NY, TX, VA), Famly and
  Lineleader. Sources: https://www.tryplayground.com/integrations/kindersystems ,
  https://help.famly.co/en-us/articles/10057494-kinderconnect-integration ,
  https://educate.lineleader.com/en/articles/9225874-kinderconnect
- Momentum: active and expanding; no sign of shutdown or stagnation (unverified beyond the above).
- Complaints: none found. The searches (4 searches run) surfaced no app-store, Reddit,
  Facebook or G2/Capterra reviews of the product. Only a Glassdoor employee rating (3.7/5, 55 reviews) came
  up, which is not product feedback. Absence of complaints is a search limitation, not proof of satisfaction.
- Documented friction (from official help docs, not complaints): attendance cannot be edited after a parent
  records it, parents can only record up to 6 days back, and generic matching/access errors occur.

## Pricing
Not published. Sold to states by contract; providers appear to use it free as the state's required system
(unverified). Integrators (Playground, etc.) charge for their own software; their prices were not found.

## Fit gaps
- Per-state mandated portal, so a provider has no choice and a competitor cannot replace it in covered states.
- Gaps are in provider workflow: double entry with a provider's own management software unless an integration
  exists, and limited editing after parent sign-in. Existing integrators already address double entry.
- Coverage is limited to the states that contract with it; other states use other vendors (not researched).

## Opening
Only a thin layer on top (sync/convenience for providers, or states KinderSystems does not serve), and
Playground, Famly and Lineleader already occupy it.
