# United States: many DSCSA vendors (commercial, no single URL)

Idea killed: C1124, DSCSA small-dispenser tracing (exemption extended).

## Verdict: strong

A crowded field of cheap, pharmacy-system-integrated DSCSA tools already prices at $50-125 per month per location. Small-dispenser demand is also deferred by the exemption.

## Evidence
- FDA extended the small-dispenser exemption from Nov 27, 2026 to Nov 27, 2027 (announced Aug 6). Small dispenser means the owning entity has 25 or fewer full-time pharmacists or technicians. FDA is running an assessment of the cost and practicality of technology for small dispensers. Note: the extension is to 2027, not "Nov 27" 2026. Sources: https://www.bipc.com/fda-extends-dscsa-exemption-for-small-pharmacies-through-november-27-2027-what-pharmacies-need-to-know ; https://www.afslaw.com/perspectives/alerts/refill-relief-fda-extends-dscsa-exemption-small-dispensers-assessment-begins
- Existing vendors named in search results: Advasur (NCPA-linked), InfiniTrak (BestRx integration), OneScan Pharmacy Pro (via Astrup Companies and RxBLU/Cypress), LSPedia, TraceLink, PioneerRx content. Sources: https://www.computertalk.com/advasur-a-solution-for-dscsa-compliance/ ; https://infinitrak.us/bestrx/ ; https://www.lspedia.com/blog/the-fda-just-extended-the-small-dispenser-dscsa-exemption-heres-what-you-still-need-to-do
- Industry-level pain (not complaints about a specific vendor): only 6.6% of products had fully compliant 2D barcodes, and trading partners send incomplete or inaccurate EPCIS data. Many independents are waiting before buying a solution. Sources: https://www.securingindustry.com/pharmaceuticals/just-6-6-per-cent-of-meds-have-dscsa-compliant-2d-barcodes/s40/a6955 ; https://www.lspedia.com/blog/dscsa-for-pharmacies-understanding-the-stabilization-period
- Complaints: none found about specific vendors (no app-store, G2, Capterra, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced). No Russian/Spanish-language results were relevant. The search budget was partly used; this is a "no complaints found" result, not proof there are none.
- Momentum: active. Vendors are marketing around the extension. Unverified: any acquisitions or shutdowns.

## Pricing (from vendor and search-snippet sources; verify before relying)
- InfiniTrak: integrated from $75 per month, non-integrated from $50 per month.
- OneScan Pharmacy Pro: $75 per month per location (Astrup), $125 per month per location (RxBLU/Cypress).
- Advasur: under $100 per month per location for the smallest clients; a 2022 document showed $83 (member) and $115 (non-member).
- This is cheap for the segment, so there is little room to undercut.

## Fit gaps
- Mostly tied to a specific pharmacy management system (e.g. BestRx integration); pharmacies on other systems may be less well served. Unverified.
- EPCIS data quality from trading partners is a market-wide problem that vendors cannot fully fix.
- No evidence found of gaps in language, offline use or multi-entity support.

## Opening
Little: a low-priced, crowded market with a deferral to Nov 2027 and a pending FDA assessment. The only angle would be a PMS-agnostic or free-tier tool, which is unverified as unmet demand.
