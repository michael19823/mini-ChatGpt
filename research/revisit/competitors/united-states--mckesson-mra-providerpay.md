# McKesson MRA/ProviderPay (united-states) - dropped idea C1106: Pharmacy MFP refund reconciliation

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Four searches found no evidence that McKesson MRA/ProviderPay offers a pharmacy-facing MFP refund reconciliation product. The MFP reconciliation pain is real and is currently addressed by others (Plenful, per prior files in this folder). Whether McKesson has an MFP-specific tool is unverified.

## Evidence
- Searches for "McKesson ProviderPay" / MRA with MFP returned no McKesson product page. The search tool itself noted that results did not mention ProviderPay or MRA in relation to MFP. Unverified: whether such a McKesson product exists.
- No McKesson-specific MFP tool (RxConnect, Health Mart) surfaced either. Unverified.
- No user complaints, reviews or outage reports about ProviderPay found (Reddit/review search returned only unrelated McKesson DIR-fee and employee-review pages). No complaints found.
- Market pain that remains open: NCPA survey reported most member pharmacies waiting 22+ days for manufacturer MFP refunds, about a fifth over 28 days (https://www.ncpa.org/newsroom/qam/2026/02/27/ncpa-shares-survey-results-cms-showing-mfp-refund-delays). Plenful writes that up to about 20% of expected refunds are missing (https://www.plenful.com/blog/mfp-refunds-missing-reconciliation); vendor claim, treat as indicative.
- CMS MTF Data Module sends X12 835 remittances to enrolled pharmacies and has a dispute/complaint intake (https://cms.gov/files/document/pharmacy-and-dispensing-entity-mtf-faq.pdf). This is a free government channel and a partial substitute for the idea.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of a reconciliation product aimed at small or independent pharmacies.
- Independent pharmacies lack direct manufacturer relationships (NCPA comments to CMS), so a wholesaler-based tool would not cover refunds from many manufacturers. Unverified for McKesson specifically.

## Momentum
Unknown for ProviderPay/MRA specifically; no recent news found.

## Opening
McKesson does not appear to occupy MFP refund reconciliation; the real competition is Plenful, PMS vendors and the free CMS MTF portal.
