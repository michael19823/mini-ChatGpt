# Net-Rx RecRx (united-states)

Idea it killed: C1106, Pharmacy MFP refund reconciliation.
Searches used: 5 of 6. Net-Rx URL (from search results): https://www.net-rx.com/

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

RecRx reconciles third-party claim reimbursements against payer contracts. It is aimed at long-term care (LTC) pharmacies. No source found says it reconciles Medicare MFP manufacturer refunds.

## Evidence
- Net-Rx is an "MHA Solution", with products Contract Assure, EditRx, Metric-Rx, Price-It Rx, RecRx, Reimbursement Suite and Script-IQ. It is sold mainly to LTC pharmacies and is listed as a PioneerRx connected vendor. Sources: https://www.net-rx.com/ , https://pioneerrx.com/connected-vendors/net-rx , https://blog.frameworkltc.com/blog/partner-highlight-net-rx
- RecRx is described as verifying claims against payer contracts to catch payment discrepancies. Net-Rx's page claims reconciliation of up to 100% of third-party claims. The search summaries did not mention MFP, manufacturer refunds or the Medicare Transaction Facilitator (MTF). This is absence of evidence from 5 searches, not proof, so the MFP point is unverified.
- Complaints: no customer reviews or complaints were found (no G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or app-store results). The only negative item was one employee review on Indeed (https://www.indeed.com/cmp/Net--rx). It said staff were overworked, training was poor and the system was "complicated". That is an employer review, not customer feedback.
- The MFP reconciliation need is real and is served by others. Plenful reports customers see 15-20% of MFP refunds short, late or never effectuated, and says reconciliation means manually cross-referencing the MTF, Beacon, the pharmacy system and TPAs (https://www.plenful.com/blog/mfp-refunds-missing-reconciliation). An NCPA survey found most independent pharmacies waited 22 days or more for refunds (https://www.ncpa.org/newsroom/qam/2026/02/27/ncpa-shares-survey-results-cms-showing-mfp-refund-delays). Other MFP products and vendors that appeared: Plenful MFP Intelligence / Command Center, Outcomes.com, PrimeRx, BestRx, CoverMyMeds guidance. These are the real competitors to check for C1106, not Net-Rx.
- Momentum: the Net-Rx site and posts look active (the "20+ years" claim appears in one summary, "15+ years" in others). No news on an acquisition or shutdown.

## Pricing
Not published. A testimonial says EditRx recoveries more than pay for the Net-Rx services each month. Sales contact only (1-866-336-3879). Unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Built for LTC pharmacy contract and claim reconciliation, not manufacturer MFP refund matching (MTF/Beacon remittances against dispensing records). The MFP gap is not confirmed either way.
- Vendor-sold with no public pricing, so likely unsuited to small independent retail pharmacies (inferred, unverified).

## Opening
MFP refund tracking for independent pharmacies (matching MTF/Beacon remittances to claims and chasing short or late refunds) is not covered by anything found from Net-Rx. The crowded part is Plenful and the pharmacy-system vendors, not RecRx.
