# Playground (United States) - competitor check

Idea killed: C1115 Child care subsidy attendance (Playground + KinderSystems in 7 states).

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Capterra shows 4.8/5 from about 193-194 reviews, 90% recommend, support 4.9, ease of use 4.7, value 4.7. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/220200/Playground/
- Reviewers praise the state subsidy attendance integration. One reports saving over 3 hours a week. Source: Capterra reviews, https://www.capterra.com/p/220200/Playground/reviews/ (paraphrased from search summary; exact wording unverified).
- Complaints found are minor: the mobile app is sometimes buggy on upload or crashes; the billing/statement portal was clunky at first; assigning charges is slow for large multi-age centers; staff who are not tech-savvy face a learning curve; data migration is laborious; one user wants monthly rather than quarterly payment. Sources: Capterra reviews pages (same URLs above). No Reddit, Trustpilot or app-store complaint threads were found.
- Subsidy integration: via KinderSystems, Playground submits attendance to state portals in AZ, IN, IA, LA, MO, NY, TX. OK and VA were listed as "coming soon" (the date of that page is unverified). Sources: https://www.tryplayground.com/integrations/kindersystems and https://tryplayground.com/changelog/texas-new-york-missouri-and-louisiana-child-care-subsidy-integration-dramatic-billing-improvements-and-further-enterprise-support
- Momentum: actively developed (changelog, help-center articles on submitting subsidy and CCA attendance, a food-program page). KinderSystems' parent Controltec was acquired by The Firmament Group in Oct 2021 (per search results; unverified beyond that). No sign of shutdown or stagnation.

## Pricing
Published price not found. Listings say a free version and free trial exist, with details on request. Capterra value-for-money is 4.7/5, so it is not seen as overpriced. Exact tiers: unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Subsidy auto-submission covers only about 7 states, so providers in the other 40+ states still key attendance by hand or use other tools.
- It is a general child care management suite, not a subsidy-only tool.
- Dependence on KinderSystems means coverage follows the states that use that vendor's portals.
- Minor app bugs and a billing learning curve. No evidence of serious dissatisfaction.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: subsidy attendance submission in states outside the 7 KinderSystems states (for example ones using other portals), or a lightweight add-on for providers who will not switch management suites. Where Playground already integrates, customers are happy.
