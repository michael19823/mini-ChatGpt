# Plenful MFP Intelligence (united-states)

Dropped idea: C1106, Pharmacy MFP refund reconciliation.

## Verdict
**strong** for health-system and hospital pharmacy buyers. Plenful is a designated Third Party Support Entity (TPSE) with named large deployments and an actively shipping product. It is unproven for small independent pharmacies, where an opening may remain.

## Evidence
- Plenful describes MFP Intelligence as accessing Medicare Transaction Facilitator (MTF) data and remittance information as a designated TPSE. It reconciles expected versus received MFP refunds at claim level and flags duplicate discounts where MFP and 340B both apply. Source: https://plenful.com/use-cases/mfp-intelligence
- Launched April 2026 with deployments at Henry Ford Health and St. Luke's Health System (per search-result summary of press coverage). Source: https://lifestyle.kbew98country.com/story/160846/plenful-unveils-mfp-command-center-to-help-health-systems-recover-up-to-20-in-missing-medicare-drug-price-refunds/
- Momentum is active. The "GFI Agent" (automated Good Faith Inquiry filing) was announced 8 July 2026: https://www.plenful.com/blog/announcing-gfi-agent-automatically-close-the-loop-on-missing-mfp-refunds. An MFP "Command Center" was also announced. Blog posts dated March and August 2026 continue the series.
- Plenful's own claim is that up to 20% of expected MFP refunds go missing, and GFIs must be filed within 120 days. This is vendor marketing, unverified independently.
- Complaints: none found. No reviews of this product were surfaced (no G2, Capterra, app-store or Reddit results). No local-language search applies (US, English). Only 4 of 6 searches ran; two earlier ones failed on a usage limit.
- Market pain is real. The NCPA survey reports 67% of independent pharmacy owners waiting 22 or more days for refunds. Source: https://www.ncpa.org/newsroom/qam/2026/02/27/ncpa-shares-survey-results-cms-showing-mfp-refund-delays

## Pricing
Not published in anything found. Unverified. The positioning is enterprise (health systems), so a small-pharmacy price is likely not targeted.

## Fit gaps
- Marketing and customers are health systems and high-volume pharmacy networks ("hundreds of GFIs a month"). Single-store independents are not evidently served (unverified).
- Whether it supports independents is unknown. I did not check the product page for segment limits.
- Other options in the space were not found beyond independent-pharmacy consultants (e.g. IPC) and the MTF itself, so competition from others is unverified.

## Opening
A low-cost, self-serve MFP refund tracker and GFI filer for independent pharmacies could fit under Plenful's enterprise focus, but only if Plenful does not already serve that segment (not verified).
