# PumpDocket (united-states)

Dropped idea: C1445 Florida FOG grease-waste compliance router. Searches used: 4 of 6.

## Verdict: strong

PumpDocket is an active, cheap, dispatch-plus-compliance product aimed squarely at grease trap haulers. It auto-generates state-specific manifests. Unverified whether Florida's FDEP grease waste service manifest is covered.

## Evidence
- Capterra and GetApp list PumpDocket, vendor Steady Grove, as dispatch and compliance software for septic, grease trap and portable restroom operators. (https://www.capterra.com/p/10037482/PumpDocket/, https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/pumpdocket/)
- Listings say it covers dispatch, recurring schedules, compliance-ready records, invoicing, QuickBooks integration, customer portal, e-signature, offline mobile, and iPhone and Android apps. They say it "auto-generates state-specific manifests". (Capterra regional pages, e.g. https://www.capterra.ca/software/1086703/PumpDocket)
- Support is by email, phone and chat (same listings).
- Florida context: since 1 July 2022 (s. 403.0741 F.S., SB 1110), haulers must give originators a signed manifest, or make it electronically available, and both retain it for one year. This is the need the dropped idea targeted. (https://floridadep.gov/node/33657)
- Complaints: none found. The search snippets showed no user reviews, and I could not open the review pages. Absence of complaints is not evidence of satisfaction.
- Momentum: the Capterra page is titled "Pricing, Alternatives & More 2026", so the listing is current. Release cadence and funding are unverified.

## Pricing
Starting price US$99.00, flat-rate model, free trial (Capterra/GetApp listings). The period (per month) and any per-user or per-truck minimums are unverified. At that level it is affordable for small haulers.

## Fit gaps
- Florida-specific FDEP manifest support is unconfirmed.
- It serves haulers. Whether it gives restaurants (originators) a compliance view, or router/manifest-sharing for them, is unverified. This may be a gap.
- No evidence of an originator-side portal beyond a generic customer portal.

## Opening
Little room on the hauler side. Any opening is on the restaurant (originator) side, such as manifest retention and inspection readiness, or in Florida-specific features if they are missing. Verify both before reviving.
