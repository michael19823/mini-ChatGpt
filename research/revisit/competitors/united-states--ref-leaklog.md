# Ref LeakLog (United States) - competitor to C1112 (AIM Act refrigerant records)

## Verdict: strong

It targets the same buyer (1-8 truck HVAC shops) with the same 15 lb AIM Act / 608 workflow at a low price. It looks new and has no reviews, so its quality is unproven.

## Evidence
- Cloud platform for HVAC/refrigeration contractors, by Elite Tech Global LLC. It auto-calculates leak rates at top-off, starts the 30-day repair clock, tracks chronic leakers, and produces audit-ready records. It covers the 15 lb threshold effective 2026. Sources: https://www.getapp.com/compliance-software/a/ref-leaklog/ , https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1109427/Ref-LeakLog
- Features reported in search snippets: 8 PDF report types (chronic leaker, service history, facility summary), QR asset tags, 3-year retention with append-only audit trail, mobile access for techs, no-signup demo at refleaklog.app/demo. Sources: Capterra and GetApp listings above.
- Complaints: none found. Capterra and GetApp (https://www.getapp.ca/software/2113903/ref-leaklog) show 0 user reviews. Searches for Reddit or forum complaints returned nothing relevant. This is not evidence that it is good, only that it is unreviewed.
- Momentum: appears to be a recent, bootstrapped launch (Indie Hackers listing: https://www.indiehackers.com/product/ref-leaklog). Development activity and customer count are unverified.
- Other tools in the same space (not the assigned competitor): OxMaint, iFactory, ServiceChannel refrigerant tracking, Facilio, LeakClock (appears in AlternativeTo).

## Pricing
- $29-$89/month, tiers differ by seats only, 14-day free trial with no card (per GetApp/Capterra listings). This is already priced for small shops, so there is little room to undercut it.

## Fit gaps
- No concrete gaps verified. Possible gaps, all unverified: enterprise or multi-site features, integrations with field-service software (ServiceTitan etc.), offline use, handling of non-HFC refrigerants, and the quality of its EPA report formats. The product is unproven with no reviews, and the seat-based pricing is the only structural limit seen.

## Opening
Little opening on price or segment. A new entrant would need a clear differentiator, such as integration with existing FSM/invoicing tools, offline field use, or a regulatory-filing format that this product lacks. Its lack of reviews and track record is the only visible weakness.
