# SepticCycle (united-states, commercial)

Killed idea: C1105 Septic county reporting router. Searches run: 4 of 6 (English only; the country is US, so no local-language search was needed).

## Verdict: weak

SepticCycle is a real product, but it is a general septic-business management tool. I found no evidence that it submits reports to county health departments, which is what C1105 would do.

## Evidence
- Capterra lists it as business management software for septic operations. It covers customers and properties, scheduling and dispatch, invoicing, contracts with e-signature, a technician mobile app, and Stripe Connect payments. Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/10038467/SepticCycle/
- It tracks tanks per property, pumping schedules, land application records, manifests, and license expirations. These are internal records and reminders. The search snippets did not mention submission to county or state agencies (unverified either way). Source: same Capterra page.
- Capterra regional pages show 0 user reviews. Sources: https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1088964/SepticCycle and https://www.capterra.ca/software/1088964/SepticCycle
- Complaints: none found. There were no reviews to find, and no Reddit, app-store or forum results mentioned it. This is not proof of satisfaction, only a lack of data.
- County reporting is fragmented. Examples include Loudoun County VA's online inspection-entry database, Snohomish Health District's contractor reporting guide, and Ventura County's monthly pumping report form. A generic tool is unlikely to cover every county's format. Sources: https://www.loudoun.gov/FAQ.aspx?QID=254, https://www.snohd.org/DocumentCenter/View/15281/Contractor-Reporting-Guide-91225, https://rmadocs.venturacounty.gov/environmental-health/programs/liquid-waste/forms/environmental-health-monthly-report-of-pumping-activities.pdf
- Momentum: unverified. I found no news, funding, acquisition, shutdown or release-history information. The only signals are a 2026 Capterra listing and zero reviews, which suggest a small or early-stage vendor.

## Pricing
- Capterra shows a starting price of $149. The billing period and plan limits are not stated in the results (unverified). Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/10038467/SepticCycle/
- A small hauler could likely afford this. It does not look overpriced for the segment, but it is also paying for features C1105 would not replace.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of per-county report formats or direct submission to health departments.
- No evidence of a multi-county router or a library of county forms.
- No evidence of a feed to regulators or of a homeowner-side view. All of this is unverified, based on search snippets only, because the vendor site was not fetched.

## Opening
A narrow county-reporting layer (per-county formats, one-click submission) could sit alongside or on top of tools like SepticCycle, since it does not appear to do this. It could also integrate with it rather than compete.
