# ServiceChannel (united-states) - idea C1112: AIM Act refrigerant records

## Verdict: weak

Real product with AIM Act and EPA refrigerant tracking, but it is an enterprise facilities-management platform sold to multi-site retail and restaurant chains. It is not a direct rival for a standalone tool aimed at small HVAC shops.

## Evidence
- ServiceChannel has a Refrigerant Tracking product. It claims automated capture, calculation and reporting for EPA, AIM Act, state and provincial rules, and support for the 15 lb leak threshold ahead of 1 Jan 2026. Sources: https://servicechannel.com/products/refrigerant-tracking/ ; https://servicechannel.com/blog/achieve-epa-compliance-refrigerant-tracking-software/
- Its buyers are enterprise facility managers: more than 500 enterprise customers, a network of more than 70,000 providers. Source: https://investors.fortive.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/76/fortive-announces-completion-of-servicechannel-acquisition (the exact figures came via the search summary, not read in full). Another listing says it targets mid-size to large multi-site retail enterprises (https://us.fitgap.com/products/servicechannel, via search summary).
- Momentum: Fortive bought it for about $1.2B in Aug 2021. It is still part of Fortive in 2026, with AI-enabled features mentioned in a Fortive Q2 2026 summary (https://statementdog.com/analysis/FTV/earnings_calls/315921). Actively developed, not stagnant.
- Complaints (from a search summary of Capterra/G2 reviews, not read directly): the field mobile app times out, fails to upload photos in low signal, and crashes; the system can be slow; implementation is lengthy and the issues list is confusing. Sources: https://www.capterra.com/p/38574/ServiceChannel/reviews/ ; https://facilio.com/blog/servicechannel-review/. Ratings: about 4.1/5 on G2 (127 reviews) and 4.4/5 on Capterra (125 reviews). Mostly positive overall. The complaints are generic, not about refrigerant records.
- No small-HVAC-shop-specific complaints, Reddit threads or outage news were found in these searches. Reddit and forum searches returned nothing relevant.

## Pricing
Not published. Listings say pricing is unavailable from the vendor. Unverified: it is likely a quote-based enterprise contract. The refrigerant module is bought by the facility owner (the customer). Providers (contractors) generally join the network through the customer's relationship. Provider fees were not verified.

## Fit gaps
- It is aimed at large multi-site chains, not independent HVAC contractors or a single-site owner.
- A small shop cannot buy it on its own; it works through a customer's ServiceChannel account (unverified in detail).
- Long implementation and a mobile app that struggles in low signal (offline weakness, per reviews).
- Refrigerant tracking is one module inside a broad work-order platform.

## Opening
A cheap, self-serve, offline-capable refrigerant and leak-record tool for small HVAC shops and single-site owners sits outside ServiceChannel's enterprise segment. The real competition is the other two (Trakref, Ref LeakLog), not this one.
