# ServiceCore (United States, commercial)

Ideas it killed: C1445 Florida FOG grease-waste compliance router; C1105 Septic county reporting router.

## Verdict: beatable

ServiceCore is a funded, active vertical field-service platform for septic, grease-trap and portable-toilet operators. It offers generic regulatory reports and export, but no evidence of county-specific or Florida FOG submission. A narrow filing router could sit beside it.

## Evidence
- Capterra: 4.0/5 from 51 reviews, value for money 3.8, features 3.6, 70% would recommend (search snippet of https://www.capterra.com/p/158918/ServiceCore/). Review text not read directly.
- Reporting is the most-cited complaint in one third-party review (https://makerstack.co/reviews/servicecore-review/, secondary, as summarized by search): users cannot pull custom reports or export financial data cleanly. Dumpster management and routing are also criticized. Unverified at source.
- Some users praise customer service (same source).
- ServiceCore's marketing says it has regulatory reporting: septic jobs by month, gallons pumped, gallons disposed by disposal site/zip/city, and emailed inspection reports (https://servicecore.com/lp-septic-software/). This covers internal reports and export. No evidence of Florida DEP grease manifest (Form 62-705.300(3)) or county-format submission.
- Momentum: $54M from Mainsail Partners in Feb 2022 (https://mainsailpartners.com/servicecore-announces-54m-investment-from-mainsail-partners-to-recapitalize-the-company-and-scale/). It bought Docket (roll-off software) in 2023. It claims 2,500+ businesses. It is not stagnant, though I found no 2026 news.
- No Reddit, Facebook or Trustpilot complaints were found. App Store reviews were not read.

## Pricing
- Not published; demo only. Per-truck pricing on an annual contract, office seats included. One aggregator says it starts around $200 per month (https://www.itqlick.com/servicecore/pricing, unverified). No free trial or plan.

## Fit gaps
- No evidence of Florida county or DEP manifest submission or FOG-specific filing formats. Searches returned none, which is weak evidence of absence.
- Targets operators who want a full platform (dispatch, billing, routing). A small hauler who only needs filing may not want the contract.
- Custom reporting and export are weak per reviewers.
- Local-language search was not applicable (US, English).

## Opening
A lightweight, per-filing compliance router for Florida FOG and county septic reports can sit beside or on top of ServiceCore exports, but it needs a check that ServiceCore has no county integrations.
