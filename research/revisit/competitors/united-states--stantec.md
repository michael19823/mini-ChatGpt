# Stantec (united-states) - competitor to C1123: Lead service line inventory software

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor

Stantec is an engineering consultancy that sells LSLI services (GIS cleanup, records review, ML prediction by consultants). It is not a self-serve software product for small utilities. The real software competitors are others (see below).

## Evidence
- Stantec's LSLI offer is a service: a team of data and GIS analysts that assess, clean and combine data into a central database, plus ML prediction via its "Stantec Altitude" platform. Source: https://www.stantec.com/en/ideas/content/blog/2023/how-to-create-lead-service-line-inventory-through-data
- Delivered through consulting engagements, e.g. Maryland Dept of Health LSLI technical services master contract (https://www.stantec.com/en/projects/united-states-projects/m/mdh-lead-service-line-inventory-technical-services-master-contract) and multi-city southeastern LCRR compliance support (https://www.stantec.com/en/news/2024/stantec-support-multiple-southeastern-cities-lead-copper-rule-revisions-compliance).
- No dedicated, separately listed LSLI software product found in search.
- Momentum: active (2023-2024 content and contracts). Altitude product details were not found in search (unverified).
- Complaints: none found (3 searches; no app-store, G2, Capterra or forum reviews surfaced). No local-language search applicable (US, English).
- Other software players appeared in results (not researched here): 120Water, BlueConduit, NSF, and a Duke/Nicholas Institute NC utility app. These are the more relevant competitors for C1123. Source: https://www.wateronline.com/doc/as-federal-war-on-lead-pipes-gains-traction-water-launches-additional-service-line-inventory-management-solutions-0001

## Pricing
No published pricing found (unverified). Consulting engagements are custom-quoted and likely out of reach for many small systems.

## Fit gaps
- Service-led, not self-serve software; a small utility must buy consulting.
- Context: ~97% of affected systems serve under 10,000 people and lack GIS skills/budget (per search summary of a Duke/Nicholas Institute source: https://nicholasinstitute.duke.edu/sites/default/files/publications/development-deployment-lead-service-line-inventory-application.pdf).

## Opening
Stantec does not block a cheap self-serve LSLI tool for small systems; judge the idea against 120Water, BlueConduit and NSF instead.
