# SWPPPTrack (united-states) - dropped idea C1113: SWPPP inspections

## Verdict: strong

Established, government-adopted, multi-app product line aimed at DOTs and MS4s; no complaints found. Caveat: its core is enterprise/agency workflow, so a small-contractor niche may be open (unverified).

## Evidence
- Described as stormwater pollution prevention management software with a mobile inspection app (inspections, action items, photos, plans) syncing to a custom database/workflow system. Targets MS4s, DOTs, local governments, builders, developers, contractors, consultants. Source: https://www.stormwater.com/stormwater-management/product/33043858/inspection-app
- Nebraska DOT uses it for statewide construction and maintenance stormwater compliance, replacing ECODatabase. Source: https://dot.nebraska.gov/projects/environment/swppptrack/
- Multiple iOS apps: PRO, DOT, DOT QA, BUILDTrack SWM. Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1099163624 (and sibling listings found in search). Vendor appears to be Web App Express Ltd (from search summary, unverified).
- Ratings: PRO 3.9/5 (7 ratings), DOT 4.0/5 (3 ratings) on the App Store, per search summary. Very few ratings.
- Complaints: none found in 3 searches (no Reddit, G2, Capterra or forum threads surfaced). Slashdot comparison pages showed 0 ratings. Not evidence of absence; coverage was thin.
- Momentum: multiple apps and a state DOT win suggest active; no dates verified, so unverified.

## Pricing
Not published in any result found. App is free to download; platform cost presumably by quote (unverified).

## Fit gaps (unverified inferences)
- "Custom tailored" database/workflow setup implies agency/enterprise onboarding, likely poor fit for a one-person inspector or small contractor.
- No evidence found of self-serve pricing, state-specific permit templates, or offline behavior either way.

## Opening
A self-serve, low-priced SWPPP inspection app for small contractors/inspectors could sit below SWPPPTrack's agency focus, but must also beat generic form apps such as GoCanvas and CloudCompli.
