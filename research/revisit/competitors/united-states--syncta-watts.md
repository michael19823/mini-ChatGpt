# Syncta (Watts), United States

Idea killed: C1104 Backflow tester multi-utility hub. Competitor type: commercial. Searches used: 4 of 6.

## Verdict: strong

Purpose-built cloud backflow test management product, owned by Watts (large water-products manufacturer). It already submits results on water-purveyor-required forms for testers and has a separate purveyor product. Complaints found are minor.

## Evidence
- Watts launched Syncta as cloud backflow test management software for purveyors, testers and property owners; it submits results on purveyor-required forms, with reminders, online scheduling and route optimization (trade press: https://www.pmmag.com/articles/101511-watts-introduces-syncta-backflow-test-management-software ; https://phcppros.com/articles/7926-watts-announces-syncta-backflow-test-management-software).
- Separate tester and purveyor solutions exist (https://www.syncta.com/backflow-testers , https://www.syncta.com/water-purveyors). Customer portal and iOS app exist (https://apps.apple.com/gr/app/syncta/id1173673034).
- Reviews: SoftwareFinder aggregate of 14 reviews, 4.2 overall, 86% positive, 0% negative (https://softwarefinder.com/field-service/syncta). Capterra has a reviews page (https://www.capterra.com/p/208106/Syncta/reviews/); TrustRadius also lists reviews (https://www.trustradius.com/products/syncta/reviews). I only saw search summaries of these, not the full text.
- Complaints (mild, paraphrased from search summaries): QuickBooks integration could be smoother; users want to filter by date and purveyor for scheduling large groups; still need separate accounting software for non-backflow work.
- A search summary said Syncta is "great but pricey" versus competitors; I could not tie it to a specific source. Unverified.
- No Reddit or forum complaint threads found. No outage or failed-filing news found.

## Pricing (from https://help.syncta.com/en/articles/2842141-syncta-s-plans, via search summary)
- Per Test: $25/month minimum, first 5 tests free, then $4.95 per test, unlimited testers.
- Per Tester: $99 per tester per month (billed annually; monthly available).
- Unlimited: $549/month, billed annually.
- A solo tester on the Per Test plan can pay little; heavy users or small firms with several testers pay more. Not clearly overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps
- Backflow-specific: weak on general multi-utility or non-backflow work (users keep separate accounting and other tools). Unverified whether it covers other cross-connection or utility compliance types.
- QuickBooks integration reported as imperfect.
- Scheduling and filtering for large batches reported as limited.
- Offline and mobile-use details not verified.

## Momentum
Actively marketed with an ongoing blog and a Watts-backed brand. Exact recent release cadence not verified.

## Opening
Little: only a thin niche in broader multi-utility or general field-service workflows, or tighter accounting integration, and a Watts-owned incumbent already holds the backflow core.
