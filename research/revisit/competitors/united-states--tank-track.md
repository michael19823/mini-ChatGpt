# Tank Track (united-states)

Killed ideas: C1445 (Florida FOG grease-waste compliance router), C1105 (Septic county reporting router).
Searches used: 5 of 6 (English only; the US market has no local-language variant).

## Verdict: strong (for septic reporting, C1105); beatable-to-weak for Florida FOG (C1445, unverified)

## Evidence
- Tank Track (TankTrack, LLC, Concord NH) is vertical software for septic, grease trap and portable toilet operators: scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, service reminders, pumping records. Source: https://tank-track.com/tank-track-vs-other-software-comparison-guide/ and https://www.pumper.com/editorial/2025/01/product-spotlight-efficiency-boosting-business-software-is-tailor-made-for-septic-service
- It has compliance and state reporting: automated forms for multiple states, e.g. an Ohio septage pumping report that auto-populates on job completion and e-sends to customers and local health districts in one click. This is directly what C1105 proposed. Source: https://tank-track.com/stay-compliant-and-organized-tank-tracks-state-reporting-and-pumping-records-features/ and https://tank-track.com/ohio-septage-pumping-report-form/
- Actively marketed and covered in 2025 trade press (Pumper magazine) and a press piece (claiborneprogress.net), so it is not stagnant. Capterra has a listing (https://www.capterra.ie/software/1085459/Tank-Track); I did not see review text or ratings.
- Florida FOG: searches found no page showing Tank Track supports the Florida DEP Grease Waste Service Manifest (62-705.900(2)) or the 30-day county/municipality delivery. Unverified either way; the site claims grease trap support generally.
- Complaints: none found. Searches for Reddit or independent complaints returned only Tank Track's own pages and a positive testimonial (vendor-hosted, so low weight). Finding none is not evidence of satisfaction.

## Pricing
$149/month first truck, $125/month each additional truck, 10% off annual billing, no free tier (per search summary of vendor material; exact vendor page not verified by me). Affordable for small pumpers, so not overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps
- Reporting forms are state-by-state; coverage of Florida county septic or FOG forms is unverified, and a gap there is plausible.
- It is a full operations suite; a pumper who just needs a standalone compliance filing router may not want to adopt it. Offline/mobile support and multi-county routing: unverified.
- Not aimed at the restaurant/originator side of FOG compliance.

## Opening
Narrow: a Florida-specific FOG manifest and county-delivery tool (or a lightweight add-on for pumpers on other software) is open only if Tank Track lacks Florida forms; a generic septic county reporting router is already covered.
