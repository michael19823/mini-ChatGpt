# Token of Trust (united-states)

Idea killed: C1110, PACT Act monthly reports and vape directories.

## Verdict: strong

It is a specialist product aimed at exactly this buyer. It is actively marketed and well reviewed, and it already covers PACT reports, excise tax and age verification. It is priced above what a very small retailer would pay.

## Evidence
- Token of Trust sells PACT Act report generation, real-time excise tax determination and age/ID verification for online vape retailers, and also helps with licensing and registration. Source: https://published.tokenoftrust.com/product/compliance/pact-act/
- It is actively publishing PACT content. It has a checklist titled "PACT Act Monthly Reporting Checklist: What Every Vape Retailer Must File by June 10, 2026". Source: https://tokenoftrust.com/blog/the-pact-act-monthly-reporting-checklist-what-every-vape-retailer-must-file-by-june-10-2026/
- Reviews are mostly positive. The search summary reports a 4.9 G2 rating from 59 reviews, with praise for easy setup, reliability and a responsive team. Sources: https://published.tokenoftrust.com/reviews/, https://www.g2.com/discussions/what-is-token-of-trust-used-for
- Complaints: the search summary mentions one 1.0/5 review. It cites poor customer service, nonstop issues, and being ghosted on a conference call. I did not see the review text myself, so treat it as unverified. No complaints specific to PACT reporting turned up, and no Reddit or forum threads about it either.
- Momentum: no acquisition, shutdown or funding news found. One search for it returned only unrelated crypto "token" results. The company looks active, based on its 2026 blog content.

## Pricing
Source: https://help.tokenoftrust.com/article/440-compliance-report-pricing (as summarized in search results; I did not read the page itself).
- Bundle of PACT and excise tax reports plus real-time excise tax: $4,500/month plus $1 per order. Setup fees may apply. Product Sync and PACT Submissions are not included.
- PACT report generation alone: from $600/month for up to 3 jurisdictions ($200 per report), up to $3,000/month for up to 50 jurisdictions ($60 per report).
- Excise tax determination alone: $600/month ($2.00 per order, up to 250 orders) to $3,100/month ($0.65 per order, up to 1,000,000 orders).
- Custom quotes are available through its Solutions Team.
- Cost for a small seller: the entry price is $600/month, which is about $7,200/year. That is heavy for a shop shipping to only a few states with low volume.

## Fit gaps
- Cost. The $600/month minimum and the $4,500/month bundle are aimed at mid-size and large online sellers. A micro-retailer is likely to find them expensive. Unverified: no review or forum thread says this directly.
- PACT Submissions and Product Sync are priced separately, so the full workflow costs more than the headline figure.
- Possible weak customer support, from the single negative review (unverified).
- No evidence found about the vape directory or product-listing side, such as manufacturer directory tracking, beyond its Product Sync add-on. I could not confirm what that covers.

## Opening
A low-cost, self-serve PACT monthly filing tool for retailers shipping to a handful of states could undercut the $600/month entry price. It would have to match the filing accuracy that Token of Trust's specialists already deliver.
