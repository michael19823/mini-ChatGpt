# Trakref / Fexa-Trakref (United States) - competitor check

Idea killed: C1112 (AIM Act refrigerant records)
Searches used: 5 of 6 (English only; US market, no local-language search needed).

## Verdict: strong

Trakref is an established, purpose-built refrigerant tracking and AIM Act compliance product, now backed by Fexa (a facilities-management SaaS company) and actively marketing AIM Act features. No product complaints were found.

## Evidence
- Product: cloud-based refrigerant compliance software for tracking refrigerant inventory, cylinders, recovery units and HVACR assets, plus compliance reports. Founded 2011 (Nashville). Sources: https://www.softwaresuggest.com/trakref, https://netzerocompare.com/software/fexa-trakref
- Momentum (active): Fexa acquired Trakref on 4 April 2023 to add refrigerant compliance and ESG reporting to its CMMS. Source: https://www.privsource.com/acquisitions/deal/X0SMYr and https://www.bassberry.com/experience/fexa-acquires-trakref/. Fexa publishes an AIM Act guide (https://fexa.io/guide/epa-aim-act-guide/) and a "Trakref Pro" page (https://trakref.fexa.io/trakref-pro/). Search summaries say a revamped version was released (date unverified).
- Complaints about the product: none found. G2 shows too few Fexa-Trakref reviews to rate (https://www.g2.com/it/products/fexa-trakref/reviews). This is an absence of data, not evidence of satisfaction.
- One loosely related remark: a user of the broader Fexa platform said its reporting is "pretty basic and fixed" (paraphrased from a search summary; it concerns Fexa, not Trakref; the source site was not confirmed).
- Employer-side only: Glassdoor has 5 reviews (3.3 rating) with negative management comments and high turnover (https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/TrakRef-Reviews-E950043.htm). This is not a product complaint; treat as weak signal of instability.

## Pricing
- No published pricing; custom quotes only (G2 pricing page, https://www.g2.com/products/fexa-trakref/pricing). Minimums unverified. Quote-only pricing and a multi-site facility focus suggest it is aimed above a small contractor, but this is unverified.

## Fit gaps
- Unverified (product not inspected, WebFetch blocked). Marketing centres on multi-site facility operators and CMMS integration, so a small HVAC contractor or single-site owner may not be the target. Whether it covers technician-side leak repair and technology-transitions records for tiny shops is unknown.
- Alternatives or other competitors were not surfaced in the search.

## Opening
Possible only for a low-cost, self-serve tool for small contractors and single-site owners, if Trakref/Fexa really targets large multi-site buyers; this needs product and pricing checks before relying on it.
