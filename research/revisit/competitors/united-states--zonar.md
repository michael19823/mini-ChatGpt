# Zonar (united-states)

Killed idea: C1114 Clean Truck Check. Type: commercial. URL: zonar.com (appears in search results).

## Verdict: strong (for the Freightliner/Thomas/Western Star segment); beatable elsewhere

Zonar is actively developed and holds a CARB executive order for Clean Truck Check. That is a real, certified product with hardware already on trucks. It covers only part of the market.

## Evidence
- Zonar says it was the first telematics provider certified by CARB for OEM-based Clean Truck Check compliance (announced February 2026). It also launched "Emissions Check" earlier. Sources: https://www.ccjdigital.com/alternative-power/article/15818544/zonar-first-oem-telematics-for-carb-compliance ; https://www.zonar.com/news-events/press-releases/telematics-provider-is-first-approved-for-oem-based-carb-compliance ; https://www.fleetmaintenance.com/equipment/emissions-and-efficiency/article/55236556/zonar-systems-zonar-launches-emissions-check-for-carb-clean-truck-check
- Coverage is about 400,000 factory-fitted V4 units on Freightliner Cascadia, Thomas Built Buses and Western Star. Data is collected and submitted to CARB automatically, with no extra hardware. Same sources.
- Capterra: 4.6/5 from 358 reviews (per search summary). The only complaints surfaced there were minor (GPS navigation context, some screens not intuitive). Source: https://capterra.com/p/126605/Ground-Traffic-Control/reviews/
- Momentum: the CB Insights result and the search summary say Zonar was acquired by GPSTrackIt in December 2024 (unverified beyond the search summary). Source: https://www.cbinsights.com/company/zonar-systems
- Complaints about cancellation fees, equipment-return charges of over $2,500, short battery life and unreachable support appeared in Trustpilot results whose URLs are for gpstrackit.com, not Zonar. I could not confirm they concern Zonar customers, so they are unverified for Zonar. Source: https://nl.trustpilot.com/review/gpstrackit.com
- No Reddit or forum complaints about Zonar's Clean Truck Check product were found.

## Pricing
Not published. Zonar quotes on request, typically per vehicle per month (unverified). A 2014 announcement cited bundles from under $20/month, which is too old to rely on. Source: https://www.schoolbusfleet.com/news/zonar-launches-new-telematics-bundle-packages. No Clean Truck Check price was found.

## Fit gaps
- The certification found covers OEM-installed Zonar hardware on those three truck makes. The search found no evidence of support for other makes, mixed fleets or owner-operators (not confirmed either way).
- It is a telematics subscription bundled with fleet software, not a standalone compliance tool.
- Nothing found on tracking test deadlines, fees or paperwork across non-Zonar vehicles.

## Opening
Small mixed-make fleets and owner-operators without Zonar hardware, who need a simple, cheap way to handle Clean Truck Check testing, deadlines and compliance records.
