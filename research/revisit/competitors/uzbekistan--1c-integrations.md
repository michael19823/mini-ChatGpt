# Uzbekistan: 1C integrations (E-invoicing ESF), idea C1133

## Verdict: weak (as a standalone competitor; limited evidence, search quota ran out after 2 of 6 searches)

## Evidence
- 1C integration is not a single product. It is a feature that each ESF operator ships as a module: Didox (didox.uz) says invoices can be exchanged without leaving 1C; Faktura.uz advertises an API and ready-made 1C modules (search summary of buxgalter.uz and operator pages). Source: https://buxgalter.uz/esf/kakogo_operatora_esf_vybrat
- The buxgalter.uz comparison tells users to ask each operator whether upload/download of incoming and outgoing invoices works for their specific 1C version. This hints that support varies by version (inference, unverified).
- Didox sending of ESF was reported as free at some point (https://buxgalter.uz/uz/publish/doc/text156636_otpravka_elektronnyh_schetov-faktur_v_didoks_stala_besplatnoy); current terms unverified.
- Complaints: none found. The one complaint search failed (usage limit), so absence of complaints is not evidence of satisfaction.
- Momentum: not assessed (unverified).

## Pricing
- Didox: no subscription, incoming free, outgoing 0 to 650 som per invoice by tier (one summary said Premium 300 som per sent ESF above 1,000/month).
- E-DOCS: base rate 550 som per invoice, volume tiers.
- Soliq-Servis (state): 3,500 to 1.8 million som per month, 8 tariffs.
- First month free at all operators. 1C module licence/setup fees from 1C partners: unverified.
- Per-invoice prices are low, so cost is not a real gap for small buyers.

## Fit gaps
- Per-version 1C compatibility is uncertain (see above); no evidence on small-business 1C editions, mobile use or multi-operator support. All unverified.

## Opening
Thin: operators already supply free or cheap 1C connectors, so only a niche (a multi-operator or version-agnostic ESF connector for small 1C users) might remain, and it is unproven.
