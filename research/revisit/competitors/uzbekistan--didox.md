# Didox (Uzbekistan) - e-invoicing (ESF), killed C1133

## Verdict: strong

## Evidence
- Didox is an accredited ESF operator (Didox Tech, part of Venkon Group), one of several operators alongside Faktura.uz, Soliqservis, E-docs and 1UZ Courier. Source: https://buxgalter.uz/publish/doc/text171838_kak_opredelitsya_s_operatorom_esf_sravnitelnaya_tablica
- Described by buxgalter.uz as one of the most popular services and, for most enterprises, free. Source: https://buxgalter.uz/ru/publish/doc/text161775_didoks_ustranil_peregruzki_na_svoem_servere1
- Complaints found: only server overloads and outages (head of Venkon reported fixing them, 21 August, year unverified) and issues with the "Act" document after the roaming operator changed its structure. Source: https://buxgalter.uz/ru/publish/doc/text161775_didoks_ustranil_peregruzki_na_svoem_servere1 ; https://buxgalter.uz/publish/doc/text214722_chto_delat_esli_cherez_didox_ne_otpravlyaetsya_akt
- No app-store, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum user reviews surfaced; no systematic complaints found.
- Momentum: active (mobile app, 1C/billing integration, tariff changes). Source: https://buxgalter.uz/oz/publish/doc/text157579_didox_mobile_-_hujjatlarni_smartfon_erdamida_almashing
- ESF is a government-mandated, state-regulated channel; the tax authority also offers its own free tool (Soliqservis). Source: https://gov.uz/ru/soliq/pages/elektron-hisobvaraq-fakturalar

## Pricing
- No subscription; incoming free; up to 10 outgoing docs/month free; 350-800 UZS per outgoing doc by tariff (buxgalter.uz comparison table).
- A separate buxgalter.uz item says sending ESF to Didox became free for most businesses (up to 1,000/month), with ~300 UZS/doc if support is needed. Conflicts with the table; dates unverified.
- Not overpriced for small buyers.

## Fit gaps
- None evidenced. Android mobile app (iOS unverified), 1C integration present.

## Opening
Essentially none for a core ESF operator (free, popular, regulated); only niche workflow layers on top of ESF (e.g. accounting/reconciliation) might remain.
