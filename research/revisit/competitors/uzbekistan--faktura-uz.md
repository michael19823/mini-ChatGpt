# Faktura.uz (Uzbekistan), competitor check

Idea killed: C1133 E-invoicing (ESF)

## Verdict: strong

Faktura.uz is one of a handful of licensed ESF operators (Didox, Faktura.uz, Soliq-Servis, e-invoice.uz). It is established, it has tariffs for small senders, and it ships an API and 1C modules. An e-invoicing entrant would face incumbents that work and are cheap per invoice. I found no complaints.

## Evidence
- Operated by OOO "Space Online Genesis"; listed as one of the ESF operators alongside Didox, Soliq-Servis and e-invoice.uz. Source: https://buxgalter.uz/esf/kakogo_operatora_esf_vybrat
- Offers an API for integration and ready modules for 1C. Source: https://www.spot.uz/ru/2019/12/30/factures/ (2019 article; current status unverified)
- Also pushes into contract signing (a podrobno.uz headline says businesses can sign contracts via faktura.uz), which suggests it is broadening its product. Source: https://podrobno.uz/cat/economic/teper-biznesmeni-smogut-podpisivat-dogovori-cherez-fakturauz/ (only the headline was seen; date unverified)
- Complaints: none found. Two searches (Russian and English) returned no app-store reviews, forum threads or review-site entries for Faktura.uz. A buxgalter.uz snippet mentions that systems, including Faktura.uz and Didox, blocked issuing ESFs for certain services. That is a regulatory restriction, not a product complaint. Source: https://buxgalter.uz/publish/doc/text207591_chto_proishodit_s_esf_i_kak_biznesu_rabotat_v_takih_usloviyah
- Momentum: the sources I found are mostly 2019-2020, and I found no recent product news, acquisition or shutdown. Current activity is unverified.

## Pricing
- 7 tariffs, first month free after connection. Included invoices range from about 5 (cheapest) to 2,500 (most expensive). Effective cost is roughly 600-900 UZS per invoice, lower on dearer tariffs. Overage is about 800-1,120 UZS per invoice, depending on tariff. Subscriptions range up to about 1.5 million UZS/month.
- Sources: https://www.spot.uz/ru/2019/12/30/factures/ and https://buxgalter.uz/esf/kakogo_operatora_esf_vybrat. The figures may be dated (2019-2020) and need re-checking.
- Judgment: a small sender can pay almost nothing on the entry tier, so it is not overpriced for the segment.

## Fit gaps
- None confirmed. Mobile app and offline use are unverified: I found no Faktura.uz app listing.
- ESF issuance requires an electronic signature (ERI) and registration with the tax authority. That is a state requirement, not a gap in Faktura.uz.
- The free government channel (Soliq) also exists. This weakens any plain ESF-issuing idea.

## Opening
Little room for generic ESF issuing. Any opening lies in a narrow vertical workflow or in a mobile or offline UX layered on top of the existing operators' APIs, not in replacing the operator.
