# Uzbekistan: local OFD vendors (competitor check for C1134, online cash registers / OFD)

## Verdict: not-a-real-competitor (low confidence, thin research)

The fiscal data operator (OFD) role in Uzbekistan appears to be state-run. The "vendors" are mostly POS or virtual-cash-register apps that plug into that state pipeline. They are not a commercial OFD market that a new entrant could take share from. Research was cut short: 2 of 6 searches hit usage limits, and 1 more returned a usage-limit error. Only 2 searches returned results, and no review sites were reached.

## Evidence
- Fiscal data from online cash registers is received, processed, stored and sent to the State Tax Committee by fiscal data operators. The State Scientific-Information Center "New Technologies" under the State Tax Committee is named as the operator. Source: search snippet from buxgalter.uz / lex.uz results (https://www.lex.uz/acts/-4603329, https://buxgalter.uz/oz/publish/doc/text156244_qadamma-qadam_yuriqnoma_onlayn-kassa_qanday_urnatiladi_va_u_bilan_qanday_ishlanadi). I did not open the pages, so the exact wording is unverified.
- Resolution 943 (23.11.2019) requires settlements with the public to be registered with an OFD, per the Paloma POS docs (https://dashboard.palomapos.com/docs/en/pos-v2/fiscalization-uz). It is a search-result summary, unverified in full.
- Third-party products connect to the state pipeline: Multikassa is a virtual cash register listed in the state register (https://joinposter.com/en/applications/multikassa), and Paloma POS works with the cloud kassa UzKassa (same Paloma docs link above).
- Ingenico discussed introducing online cash registers with the State Tax Committee (https://kun.uz/en/20225719). Details not read.
- Complaints: none found. No app-store, Reddit, forum or outage coverage was reached. This is a search limitation, not proof that none exist.

## Pricing
- One search snippet says the service for fiscal modules costs 412,000 soum (source: Uzbek-language search results, likely buxgalter.uz, https://buxgalter.uz/oz/publish/doc/text173458_elektron_raqamli_imzo_va_fiskal_modulga_tulovlar_uchun_yangi_rekvizitlar). The context is unverified. It could be a one-off module fee rather than a subscription.
- No published OFD subscription pricing was found. Unverified.

## Momentum
- The system is active and mandated by regulation (2019 resolution, with newer buxgalter.uz updates on payment requisites). It looks maintained by the state. Specific vendor momentum is unknown.

## Fit gaps
- Not established. I couldn't check small-business suitability, offline or mobile use, or language coverage.
- Possible gap (unverified): hardware is involved (fiscal modules), which is a homologation-type barrier. The method says to score such ideas lower.

## Opening
Little to none as an OFD: the operator role is state-held. Any opening would be a layer on top, such as POS or accounting integration for small sellers, and that would need separate validation.
