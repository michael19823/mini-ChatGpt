# EDICOM (Venezuela) - competitor check for C1138 (SENIAT digital invoicing, Prov. 0102/0121)

## Verdict: weak
EDICOM is a global enterprise EDI/e-invoicing SaaS. In Venezuela the searches found only marketing and educational content, with no evidence it is an authorized digital printer or homologated software vendor, and no evidence it serves small buyers. (6 searches budget; 5 used.)

## Evidence
- EDICOM publishes Venezuela landing pages, a blog and a webinar on SENIAT digital invoicing (Prov. SNAT/2024/000102), so it is positioned to sell there: https://edicomgroup.com/es/factura-electronica/venezuela , https://edicomgroup.com/es/blog/factura-digital-venezuela , https://edicomgroup.com/es/recursos/webinars/factura-digital-venezuela
- Those results are guidance and compliance explainers. The search snippets did not show whether EDICOM is on SENIAT's list of authorized imprentas digitales or homologated software. Status: unverified. SENIAT says it publishes the authorized providers list on its portal; I did not check it (no WebFetch).
- Local competition exists: e.g. Odoo Venezuela imprenta digital modules (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/l10n_ve_imprenta_digital). Their quality is unverified.
- Rules context (PwC, Mazars, KPMG, Crowe notes found in results): digital invoicing is mandatory since March 2025 only for certain taxpayers (exclusively online, mixed fiscal-machine plus online sales, or voluntary with SENIAT authorization). It needs SENIAT-homologated software plus an authorized digital printer.
- Complaints: none found. Searches in Spanish for reviews/quejas returned only EDICOM marketing and a directory entry with no ratings. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit results surfaced. This is not evidence of satisfaction, only of no data.
- Momentum: EDICOM is actively publishing Venezuela content in several languages (es, en, fr, nl, pt), so it is active. Whether it has live Venezuelan clients or a local entity is unverified.

## Pricing
No published price found for Venezuela (searches returned none). Unverified. EDICOM generally sells by quote to enterprises (general knowledge, not confirmed by these searches).

## Fit gaps
- Enterprise EDI/ERP-integration orientation; no sign of a self-serve small business or sole proprietor product (inferred from marketing material, unverified).
- No evidence of offline or mobile use, or of Bolivar/USD dual-currency workflow tuned to small Venezuelan merchants (unverified).
- Authorization status with SENIAT unverified; if it is not authorized it cannot issue fiscal control numbers.

## Opening
A low-cost, self-serve SENIAT-compliant tool for SMEs (via an authorized imprenta partner or homologation) is not contested by EDICOM, which targets enterprises. The binding constraint is the SENIAT homologation/authorization barrier, not EDICOM.
