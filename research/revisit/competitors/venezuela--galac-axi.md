# Venezuela: Galac AxI (Galac Software)

Idea it killed: C1140, inflation-adjustment and multi-currency accounting.
Searches used: 5 of 6 (Spanish and English-keyword mix).

## Verdict: strong

Galac AxI is a purpose-built, long-established Venezuelan product that covers exactly the dropped need. Fiscal inflation adjustment (ISLR), financial-statement restatement and multi-currency are all in the vendor's own materials. I found no public complaints.

## Evidence
- Galac describes AxI (Ajuste por Inflación) as an independent accounting auxiliary. It quantifies monetary gains and losses for tax purposes, determines net income under the ISLR regulation, and restates financial statements per the Federación de Contadores de Venezuela. It imports accounts and balances from the accounting module and produces comparative inflation-effect reports, monetary gain/loss and cash-flow statements. Sources: https://galac.com/PDF/Productos/Brochures/Brochure_AxI.pdf , https://galac.com/download/modulos-e-informes-ajuste-por-inflacion
- Galac's administrative system handles multi-currency (dollars, euros, petros) and records each entry at the exchange rate of the operation day. Source: https://galac.com/download/manejo-de-multimoneda
- Galac says it has over three decades of experience. Its accounting, IVA/renta, payroll and sales modules are integrated. Source: https://galac.com/download/modulos-e-informes-contabilidad (vendor claim, unverified independently).
- A Scribd document lists administrative system version 30.2 (https://es.scribd.com/document/1028537207/GALAC). The date is unverified.
- Complaints: none found. Searches for opiniones, quejas, problemas, soporte and foro returned only vendor material and CVs of professionals who list Galac skills. No app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit or forum reviews surfaced. This is absence of evidence, not evidence of satisfaction; the search tool is US-only and weak on Venezuelan forums.
- Momentum: no news found on acquisition, shutdown or outages. A store at store.galac.com hosts brochures. Recent 2025-2026 release activity was not verified.

## Pricing
Not found. No published prices or minimums surfaced (unverified). Contact-for-quote appears likely but is not confirmed.

## Fit gaps (all weakly evidenced)
- Installed desktop, module-based suite. A cloud or mobile offering was not confirmed (unverified).
- AxI is an auxiliary of a larger Galac accounting suite. A buyer who wants only a lightweight inflation or multi-currency tool, or who uses other accounting software, may be underserved. Import from non-Galac systems is unverified.
- No evidence either way on small-firm affordability or on handling of rapid changes in the BCV rate or fiscal rules.

## Opening
Only a narrow one: a cheap, cloud or mobile layer for small businesses and independent accountants that sits on top of non-Galac books. Galac covers the core need directly, so this is hard to win.
