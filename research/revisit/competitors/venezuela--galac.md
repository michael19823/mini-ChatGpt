# Galac (Venezuela), competitor check

Ideas it was blamed for: C1138 SENIAT digital invoicing (Prov. 0102/0121); C1139 multi-portal payroll filing (IVSS/TIUNA, BANAVIH FAOV, INCES).

## Verdict: beatable

Galac is a long-established Caracas desktop ERP vendor (accounting, admin/invoicing, payroll, IVA/ISLR). My searches found no evidence that it is homologated as an SENIAT digital invoicing solution, and none that its payroll files directly to the IVSS/BANAVIH/INCES portals. Both points are unverified because the searches were thin.

## Evidence
- Galac publishes brochures for accounting, administrative, payroll, and IVA/Renta modules. It is based in Caracas (Ciudad Center, Boleíta Norte). Sources: https://galac.com/download/modulos-e-informes-nomina ; https://store.galac.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/brochure_administrativo.pdf ; https://galac.com/galac/assets/download/manuales/Brochure-2024/Brochure-Nomina-2024.pdf
- The payroll brochure text describes multi-company payroll, vacations, benefits, interest, liquidations, meal tickets, and SAW integration. It is sold in editions for large companies, SMEs, entrepreneurs and students. The search snippets did not mention portal filing or export formats for IVSS, TIUNA, BANAVIH or INCES (unverified either way).
- Digital invoicing under Prov. SNAT/2024/000102 (Gaceta 43.032, 19 Dec 2024; mandatory from 19 Mar 2025) runs through approved software plus authorized digital printing houses (imprentas digitales). Source: https://edicomgroup.com/es/blog/factura-digital-venezuela . Nothing found tying Galac to that ecosystem. Other players visible: Edicom and an Odoo imprenta digital module.
- Complaints: none found. A Spanish-language search for complaints returned only unrelated results. Absence of reviews probably reflects thin web presence, not satisfaction.
- Momentum: the site has a 2024 brochure, so it is still maintained. No acquisition or shutdown news found.

## Pricing
Not found in search results. Unverified. The tiered editions (enterprise, SME, entrepreneur, student) suggest some small-buyer pricing exists.

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified)
- Desktop/licensed ERP model, so not a lightweight web or mobile tool.
- No found evidence of SENIAT digital invoicing homologation or imprenta integration.
- No found evidence of one-click multi-portal payroll filing.

## Opening
A web/mobile tool that automates the portal filings and SENIAT digital invoicing for small firms is not clearly blocked by Galac. Verify directly with Galac's current feature list before committing.
