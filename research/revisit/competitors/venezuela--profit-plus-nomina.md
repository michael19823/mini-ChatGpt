# Profit Plus Nómina (Venezuela)

Idea killed: C1139, multi-portal payroll filing (IVSS/TIUNA, BANAVIH FAOV, INCES).

## Verdict: strong

Profit Plus (Softech, now listed as AutesCorp Profit Plus) is the entrenched payroll tool in Venezuela. Job ads ask for "PROFIT" experience, which points to a large installed base. I found no evidence of direct portal filing, so it is a strong incumbent, but the filing-specific niche is not clearly covered.

## Evidence
- ComparaSoftware lists "AutesCorp Profit Plus" as an HR and payroll solution. It describes automated payroll calculation, bulk pay-receipt emails, labor-law compliance, benefits and loans control, and multi-company, multi-currency and multi-language support. Source: https://www.comparasoftware.com/autescorp-profit-plus
- Venezuelan job listings (cazvid.com) ask for payroll analysts with 2+ years of experience in the PROFIT system. Candidate CVs mention using Profit Plus for BANAVIH registrations and IVSS documents. This suggests the tool is used in these workflows, but I could not tell whether it files directly with the portals. Source: https://cazvid.com/empleo/analista-de-nominas-en-carrizal-miranda-company
- Complaints: none found. Searches in Spanish and English returned no user reviews or outage news for Profit Plus Nómina.
- Momentum: unverified. The vendor name appears as both Softech and AutesCorp, and I found no release or acquisition news.

## Pricing
Not found; unverified. The only source I saw (ComparaSoftware) gave no price.

## Fit gaps
- I found no documentation of direct submission to IVSS/TIUNA, BANAVIH or INCES. Export of files for those portals is unverified.
- It is a desktop-era product with a Shopify connector and no confirmed cloud or mobile version (unverified).
- Likely priced and sized for mid-size companies (unverified).

## Opening
A cheap, portal-focused filing layer (or a lightweight cloud payroll with IVSS/FAOV/INCES filing) could target small firms. It could also sit beside Profit as an add-on, but only if Profit really lacks portal file generation, which needs checking.
