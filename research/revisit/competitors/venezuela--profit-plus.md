# Profit Plus (Venezuela) - competitor check

Dropped idea: C1138, SENIAT digital invoicing (Prov. 0102/0121). Type: commercial. URL: none known (not verified).

## Verdict: weak (low confidence, mostly unverified)

Searches were thin. Profit Plus is a long-running Venezuelan accounting/administrative package, but nothing found shows it is SENIAT-homologated for digital invoicing. It may not be a real blocker for a digital-invoicing idea.

## Evidence
- Search snippets say Profit Plus versions 2k8 and 2k12 are used for accounting and administrative management in Venezuela. Evidence is job listings that ask for Profit Plus experience (cazvid.com listings, e.g. https://cazvid.com/empleo/analista-de-cuentas-en-valencia-carabobo-venezuela). Those versions are old, which suggests a legacy installed base.
- Regulatory context: Prov. SNAT/2024/000102 and SNAT/2024/000121 require taxpayers to issue digital invoices from SENIAT-homologated software, with an authorized imprenta digital, from March 2025 (https://edicomgroup.com/es/blog/factura-digital-venezuela, https://pwc.com/ve/es/assets/documentos/stl/NAC-Emisión-de-facturas-digitales-Ene2025-Rev.pdf).
- Homologation status: no source found says Profit Plus is homologated. This is unverified. It may have been homologated without being indexed.
- Complaints: none found (app stores, forums, review sites). No review-site listing for the Venezuelan product turned up. Name collisions with an unrelated Shopify app "ProfitPlus" and with a Slovak SOFTIP "Profit Plus" were ignored.
- Momentum: unverified. Only old version names (2k8, 2k12) surfaced. No sign of recent releases, acquisition or shutdown.

## Pricing
Not found. Unverified.

## Fit gaps (inferred, unverified)
- Likely a desktop/on-premise legacy product, probably weak on cloud or mobile use.
- Digital-invoicing compliance (homologation, imprenta digital integration) not confirmed.
- Small businesses and freelancers may be underserved.

## Opening
If Profit Plus is not homologated, or only sold as a legacy desktop ERP, a lightweight homologated invoicing tool for small Venezuelan taxpayers has room. Check SENIAT's homologated-software list directly before relying on this.
