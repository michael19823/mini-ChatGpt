# Sage payroll (Zambia) - competitor check for C1161 (payroll statutory returns: PAYE/SDL, NAPSA, NHIMA, 2026 PAYE)

## Verdict: strong

Sage already ships Zambia statutory support (PAYE, NAPSA, NHIMA, report templates, regular tax-table updates). It is a real, maintained product. The gap is mainly price, packaging and filing format, not features. Based on 3 searches only (budget up to 6); the local-language search was not run.

## Evidence
- Sage community hub carries Zambia-tagged threads on tax-table changes, NHIMA, WCF, and an announcement "zambia new nhima contribution schedule report available" (https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-vip-payroll-hr/f/announcements/153250/zambia-new-nhima-contribution-schedule-report-available). Also a Sage X3 thread on Zambia statutory changes, Jan 2022 (https://communityhub.sage.com/sage-global-solutions/sage_x3/f/ame-general-discussion/180030/x3-hr-payroll---zambia-statutory-changes---january-2022). Shows Sage keeps pace with Zambian changes.
- A search summary says Sage Business Cloud Payroll has built-in rules for ZRA PAYE, NAPSA, NHIMA and one-click statutory returns. Source text was a search snippet; I could not confirm it on a Sage page (unverified). Whether it covers Zambia specifically, versus South Africa, is also unverified.
- Sage Zambia support appears to run through the South African community hub and VIP/Pastel products, so it looks like a regional add-on rather than a Zambia-first product (inference).
- Complaints: none found specific to Zambia. Capterra South Africa listing shows 4.6/5 from 36 users, value for money 4.4, support 4.3 (https://www.capterra.co.za/software/215768/sage-business-cloud-payroll). Those are not Zambian reviews.
- Other competitors exist: an Odoo Zambia payroll module computing PAYE, NAPSA, NHIMA, SDL with statutory remittance summaries by agency (https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/l10n_zm_payroll); PayrollX (cloud, one-click PAYE/NAPSA/NHIMA reports); a bespoke Zambia payroll case study by Satva Solutions. Filing still goes through ZRA TaxOnline manually with the employer's credentials (per search summary, unverified).
- Momentum: active. Sage announced a Business Cloud Payroll price adjustment effective 1 Sept 2026 (https://communityhub.sage.com/za/sage-payroll/f/announcements/272302/pricing-update-continuing-to-invest-in-your-business-sage-business-cloud-payroll-pricing-guide-2026---2027).

## Pricing
- Search summary quoted a starting price of GBP 7.00, per-user model, free trial. Source and currency basis unclear (unverified). The 2026-27 guide is for the South African market and I did not read it. No verified Zambian kwacha price found.

## Fit gaps (mostly unverified, inferred)
- Likely desktop/licence-based and priced for SA-style SMEs and larger firms; not confirmed.
- No evidence of direct ZRA TaxOnline / NAPSA / NHIMA file upload or e-filing; reports appear to be printable schedules (unverified).
- Zambia treated as a secondary country; 2026 changes (PAYE bands 0% to ZMW 5,100 then 20/30/37%; NAPSA ceiling ZMW 37,236) not verified as shipped in Sage.

## Opening
A cheap, mobile-first tool that outputs files ready for TaxOnline/NAPSA/NHIMA upload for micro-SMEs could undercut Sage, but Sage plus Odoo and PayrollX cover the core calculation, so the idea needs a filing-automation or price edge.
