# Satva (Satva Solutions / PayrollX) - Zambia

Idea revisited: C1161, payroll statutory returns (PAYE/SDL, NAPSA, NHIMA, 2026 PAYE).

## Verdict: weak

Satva Solutions is a software development agency. Its Zambia payroll product, PayrollX, was built for a Zambian entrepreneur. I found no evidence of Zambian market traction. The product is a generic payroll tool that makes reports, not a filing-first tool.

## Evidence
- Satva's own case study says it built PayrollX, a cloud payroll platform for Zambia. The platform covers attendance, timesheets and shifts, salary processing, and PAYE, NAPSA, NHIMA and Skills Levy compliance. It offers "one-click PAYE, NAPSA and NHIMA reports", multi-branch support, multi-currency and bank integration. Source: https://satvasolutions.com/case-study/zambia-payroll-solution (self-published; the case study is marketing, not independent proof of use).
- A SoftwareSuggest listing exists for PayrollX: https://www.softwaresuggest.com/payrollx
- Complaints: none found. Searches for reviews returned only unrelated companies (Sattva, Satvat Infosol) and Glassdoor employee reviews of Satva Solutions, which say nothing about the product. I found no app-store, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot or Reddit reviews of PayrollX, and no Zambian forum posts or outage news.
- Searches in Zambian English and local terms turned up no mention of Satva by Zambian users. Zambian payroll results named other tools: Odoo l10n_zm_payroll, Fast Account, VIP and Micropay.
- Unverified: whether PayrollX files returns directly with ZRA TaxOnline, NAPSA or NHIMA. The sources only say "reports".
- Momentum: unverified. I found no release notes or dates.

## Pricing
- The SoftwareSuggest listing shows "starting at $20 per year", an "EasyX" plan priced per staff per year, and a 3-day free trial. The listing doesn't say whether $20 is per staff member or per company, so treat the figure as unverified. It is cheap in either case.

## Fit gaps
- Reports only: it produces "one-click reports", and I found no evidence of direct e-filing or portal submission (unverified).
- Marketing emphasises multi-branch, multi-currency and attendance, so it targets mid-size employers rather than micro-SMEs.
- No evidence of mobile or offline use, or of local-language support.
- No visible Zambian customer base or support presence.
- Whether it has been updated for 2026 PAYE bands is unverified.

## Opening
A filing-first tool for small Zambian employers is still open: one that prepares ZRA, NAPSA and NHIMA submissions and keeps up with PAYE changes. Satva is a low-visibility, report-level product with no public complaints, and it is not a barrier.
