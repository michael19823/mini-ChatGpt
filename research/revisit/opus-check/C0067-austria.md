# C0067 · Austria · NISG 2026 (NIS2) compliance

## Verdict and score
**Still closed. 4/10.** The mandate is real and recurring:
- NISG 2026 has been in force since 1 Oct 2026. It covers about 4,000 entities: medium and large firms (50+ staff or more than EUR 10M) in 18 sectors.
- Registration is due by 31 Dec 2026, self-declaration by 1 Oct 2027, and authority audits can be demanded from 1 Oct 2028.

But a solo founder faces a crowded and subsidised field:
- **Austria-specific tool already exists.** "NISG Plattform" (Capterra) runs from USD 2,500 a year flat and has a free version. Germany-built NIS2/ISMS platforms (SECJUR, Proliance, zettasecure and others) sell German-language NIS2 products into Austria.
- **Free and subsidised alternatives.** WKO gives away a free impact check and guide, plus webinars and workshops. WKO advertises **100%-funded NIS2 consulting** ("NIS2-Beratungsförderung"), and Cyber Security Schecks cover 40% of costs, up to EUR 10k. Subsidies push buyers toward consultants who bring their own tooling.
- **Auditors are central.** The self-declaration needs structured audit reports and action plans signed by management **and independent auditors**. The buying decision therefore sits with audit and consulting firms (KPMG, Forvis Mazars, IC Consult and others), not with an SME owner picking a SaaS.
- **Small market.** About 4,000 entities, many already in ISO 27001 programmes.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- Sonnet rated GRC tools "beatable" because they are priced for the mid-market. But NISG entities *are* the mid-market (50+ staff), and the Austria-specific NISG Plattform already sits at about USD 2,500 a year with a free tier. That undercuts the "cheap Austria-specific tool is open" claim.
- Sonnet missed WKO's free impact check and the 100%-funded NIS2 consulting subsidy. Its consultancy pricing (EUR 30–80k) came from a vendor blog and ignores the subsidy.
- The independent-auditor sign-off on self-declarations makes auditors the gatekeepers, which Sonnet did not note.

## Barriers
- The independent-auditor sign-off requirement favours audit firms.
- Subsidised consulting at 100%, plus 40% Schecks.
- The risk-management measures regulation (Verordnung) is still to be finalised and will be aligned to EU 2024/2690, so content may shift.

## Buyer and price
- **Buyer.** CIO or managing director of a 50–250 employee Austrian firm in an NISG sector (food production, waste, manufacturing, digital providers), or a small IT consultancy serving them.
- **Price anchors.** NISG Plattform at USD 2,500 a year. DACH SME platforms at EUR 5–40k a year (unverified summary).
