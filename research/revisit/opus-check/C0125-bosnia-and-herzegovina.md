# C0125 · Bosnia and Herzegovina · General FBiH software fiscalization / ESIR (ESET) POS for shops and restaurants

## Verdict and score
**Still closed. 3/10.** The original kill reason, a crowd of Serbian ESIR vendors, was poorly evidenced. Sonnet could not find YuTeam or BizniSoft at all. But a stronger non-competitor barrier closes the idea: the adopted FBiH Law on Fiscalization of Transactions (Sl. novine FBiH 9/2026, in force 12 Feb 2026) requires registered manufacturers of the fiscal system (ESET) to post a **guarantee of KM 1,000,000** (about EUR 511k). The amendment raised it from KM 500k. A small founder cannot realistically become an ESET manufacturer.

Two further problems:
- **Timing.** Bylaws and technical specs (XML schemas) were due within 180 days, by about mid-Aug 2026, but none were found published as of mid-2026. Mandatory application comes at the latest 18 months after entry into force, about Aug 2027. Phase dates are not fixed.
- **Incumbents.** Local POS vendors are already in the market, e.g. Logic(al), which claims 2,500+ points of sale in BiH. The Tax Administration is procuring the central system.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- Sonnet correctly found no trace of YuTeam or BizniSoft as BiH vendors. The named competitors were weak evidence, and the "95 Serbian accredited vendors" claim is about Serbia's 2022 regime and is unverified for FBiH.
- Sonnet did not check whether becoming an ESET manufacturer is open to small firms. It is not: the KM 1M guarantee sits in the law itself.
- Sonnet also missed that the bylaws and schemas have not been published (as of the latest coverage found), so no vendor can certify yet. That is a timing risk, not an opening.

## Barriers
- **Guarantee.** KM 1M guarantee for registered ESET manufacturers. This is decisive.
- **Certification and registration.** These depend on bylaws that are pending.
- **Local entity.** It is very likely needed for registration (unverified detail).
- **Timeline.** Application by about Aug 2027 at the latest; phase dates pending.
- **Price sensitivity.** Small traders already contest the cost: the earlier FBiH estimate was KM 80M for software fiscalization, and in Serbia small businesses complained that e-fiscalization raised costs.

## Buyer and price
- **Buyer.** Owner of a café or small shop in FBiH. There are more than 83,000 fiscal devices (2020 figure).
- **Price anchor.** Not found for FBiH (unverified). Serbian ESIR subscriptions are commonly a few EUR to about EUR 15 a month per device (unverified here). This is commodity pricing.
- **Possible wedge.** A partner/reseller layer on top of a registered ESET manufacturer, e.g. a niche vertical POS. That makes the founder a reseller, not an owner of the gap.
