# C0190 · Chile · Ley 21.719 data-protection compliance

## Verdict and score
**Narrow. 5/10.** The law applies from 1 Dec 2026 to all of Chile's 774,908 pymes, but pymes get a one-year grace period to Dec 2027 with written warnings only. The two obvious wedges are already taken.

**Clinics and health data (the source's suggested niche): closed.**
- Medilink, the leading Chilean clinic software, already markets Ley 21.719 compliance: ISO 27001, designation as an "Operador de Importancia Vital", access traceability and automatic consent capture.
- Confidata has a dedicated health-sector module covering Art. 16 bis sensitive data.
- Prey publishes a health-sector compliance guide.

**Generic pyme kit: crowded.**
- Confidata (GRC, 50+ modules, Chile-specific).
- AlayIAtrust (OneTrust plus counsel, with a "Ley 21.719 para pymes" page).
- Lawwwing (Spain-based privacy SaaS, active in Chile).
- Prey's free pyme guide.
- Buk's free HR data-protection kit, bundled in HR software that pymes already use.

**What is left: the voluntary Modelo de Prevención de Infracciones (MPI).** Its certification rules were published in **Decreto 662 (Diario Oficial, 9 Sep 2026)**.
- A certified MPI acts as a formal mitigating factor against fines of up to 20,000 UTM or 2–4% of revenue. Certificates last 3 years.
- It has 9 minimum elements: DPD, RAT (records of processing), risk matrix, protocols, reporting channels, internal sanctions, contract clauses and training.
- A cheap "MPI-ready" documentation-and-evidence workspace for 20–200 employee pymes, sold through accountants (contadores) and certifiers, is the narrow segment. It is mostly a templating product, and incumbents can copy it.
- One secondary claim, unverified: a simplified pyme MPI that is recognised as a seal for banks, Corfo and public tenders.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- Sonnet's two files were "unnamed consultants" and "unnamed privacy SaaS" (beatable, no prices). That was a triage failure: Confidata, AlayIAtrust, Lawwwing, Prey and Buk are named, active and targeting pymes.
- The clinic niche the source proposed is already covered by Medilink, the vertical incumbent.
- New: the pyme grace period (warnings only until Dec 2027) and the Decreto 662 MPI certification framework. The grace period weakens urgency; the MPI framework creates the only concrete, documentable deliverable.

## Barriers
- Grace period to Dec 2027 for pymes, with warnings only.
- MPI certification is voluntary, and the certifier market and prices are not yet visible (unverified).
- Vertical software (clinic, HR, accounting) bundles compliance features.

## Buyer and price
- **Buyer.** Owner or administration manager of a 20–200 employee pyme with customer databases (retail, education, services), reached through its accountant or an MPI certifier.
- **Price anchor.** None verified for pyme tools. Sanctions of up to 20,000 UTM (about USD 1.4M) are the fear anchor.
