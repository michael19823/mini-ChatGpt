# C0422 · India · PIBO plastic-EPR compliance software (self-serve for small PIBOs)

## Verdict and score
**Still closed. 3/10.** The target segment has been legislated away. The **Plastic Waste Management (Amendment) Rules 2024** (14 Mar 2024) exempt micro and small producers and brand owners from direct plastic-packaging EPR obligations. Their obligations shift to the manufacturer or importer of plastic raw material that supplies them. Micro and small firms keep only the recycled-content target. "Small PIBOs" are therefore no longer the buyers of EPR target-and-certificate tooling.

The remaining PIBOs are medium and large. As of 25 Jun 2026, 62,175 PIBOs were registered on the CPCB portal (26,564 by SPCBs/PCCs, 35,611 by CPCB). They are served by funded **EPR platforms that bundle software with credits**:
- **Recykal**: 650+ brands; real-time target tracking, digital documentation, filing alerts.
- **EcoEx**: an automated B2B portal and plastic-credit marketplace.
- **Banyan Nation**: EPR management.

They are also served by consultants and PROs (Saahas, ReCircle, Circulogy and others) charging ₹20k–1 lakh, with credits at about ₹1.05–1.50 per kg.

The original kill reason ("compliance is given away to sell certificates") holds. Margin sits in the credits, and the tracking dashboard is free or bundled.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- Sonnet assessed five consultancies and PROs and declared most of them "not-a-real-competitor" because they are services, not software. That misses the kill mechanism, which is software bundled free with credits, and it misses the real software-plus-marketplace players: **Recykal and EcoEx**.
- Sonnet did not check the **MSME exemption (PWM Amendment Rules 2024)**, which removes the "small PIBO" buyer the idea was built for.
- There is real pain in annual-return filing: MoEFCC extended FY2025-26 deadlines to 31 Dec 2026 for PIBOs and 31 Oct 2026 for PWPs because of portal difficulties, and environmental compensation applies for non-filing. But the platforms above already offer filing support.

## Barriers
- **MSME exemption**: micro and small PIBOs are out of scope for direct EPR.
- Credit-bundled "free" software from PROs and platforms.
- Frequent rule amendments (2024, 2025 and 2026 amendments) and CPCB portal changes.

## Buyer and price
- **Buyer.** Compliance or EHS manager at a medium or large brand owner or importer with multi-category plastic packaging.
- **Price anchors.** Consultants ₹20k–1 lakh; credits ₹1.05–1.50 per kg (Enviraj indicative live rates, unverified as of today); application fees ₹1k–50k.
