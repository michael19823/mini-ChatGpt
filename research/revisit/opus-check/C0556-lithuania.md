# C0556 — Lithuania: electronic construction work journal (ESDŽ)

## Verdict and score
**Still closed: 3.6/10.** Sonnet's verdicts were mostly "beatable, low confidence, nothing found",
and it missed the incumbent.
- **StatybosZurnalas.lt** (UAB Cotenders, operating since 2014) is the long-established ESDŽ
  platform. It was used on flagship projects such as the Vilnius University Medical Science
  Centre, and a Ministry of Environment note in the legislative record once described it as the
  *only* provider of ESDŽ subscription services on the market.
- **darbuzurnalas.lt** is run by Proptechas, an active Šiauliai start-up (exhibiting at RESTA,
  April 2026). It already pitches exactly the "simpler, saves small contractors time" angle Sonnet
  proposed, and is expanding into tenders and cost estimation.
- Freelancers on paslaugos.lt sell journal filling at ~€22/hour, or from €50 per construction
  notice.

The rules also require each provider to guarantee identification and authentication of every
signer, record traceability, reliable storage and state inspector access. Notifying
subcontractors requires a data interface with **Infostatyba**. That is a meaningful compliance
build for a solo founder in a market of ~2.8M people.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- Added the missing incumbent StatybosZurnalas.lt (Cotenders). Confirmed darbuzurnalas.lt/Proptechas
  as a real, active, funded-looking start-up rather than an unverified site.
- The "small contractors find incumbents pricey or clunky" hypothesis has no supporting
  evidence: no complaints were found by Sonnet or by me. darbuzurnalas.lt already targets that
  segment.
- Infostatyba is the state system, not a competitor (Sonnet was right). But integrating with it is
  a requirement for the full workflow, which raises the barrier.

## Barriers
- Regulatory requirements on the tool: signer authentication, traceability, storage and inspector
  access under STR 1.06.01:2016, plus an Infostatyba data interface.
- A small national market (Lithuanian-only), with two established local players.
- Switching costs: journals run for the life of a project.

## Buyer and price
The buyer is a small general contractor or works manager (statybos darbų vadovas), or a
self-builder. No published SaaS prices were found. The anchor is freelance filling at ~€22/hour or
€50+ per notice, and per-project pricing of roughly tens of euros a month is plausible (unverified).
