# C0679 — Netherlands: Wtta staffing compliance (agencies and inleners)

## Verdict and score
**Narrow: 5.7/10.** The agency side is closed: certifiers (Kiwa and other NEN 4400 / SNA
inspectors), ABU pre-audits, backoffice firms (Flexhub) and staffing suites will carry
agencies through the licence. Under the transition rules a valid SNA mark largely covers the Wtta
requirements. The open segment is the **inlener (hirer) control duty for mid-size hirers that
don't use an MSP**, mainly in construction, logistics, food processing, horticulture and
installation, which hire from many small agencies and through subcontracting chains. The law
(confirmed with no postponement, May and July 2026) requires them to:
- check the NAU public register before every hiring and **document when they checked**;
- **check further down the chain** (the actual employer of the worker in sub-lending);
- keep the evidence for **seven years**.

From 1 Jan 2028 the Labour Inspectorate fines hirers who use unlicensed suppliers. The register
itself (from 1 Jul 2027) gives a lookup, not alerts, a chain view or an audit file. Large hirers
get this from their MSP/VMS (Randstad Enterprise, Pro-Unity and others), which explicitly sells
"we check every party". Mid-size hirers have no comparable tool that I could find.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- Sonnet's verdicts hold: 4 of 8 named "competitors" (GNR, Wtta Kompas, HYTY, WTTA-Dossier) could
  not be found, and wttaregister.nl is the public register, not a product. The vendor rush in the
  original report is mostly on the agency side.
- Sonnet missed the **real inlener-side competitors**: MSPs and VMSs. They already police SNA
  status for large hirers and are now marketing Wtta checks. That removes the enterprise segment
  and narrows the gap to mid-size hirers that don't use an MSP.
- Confirmed timeline: transition registration Nov–Dec 2026; law in force 1 Jan 2027; applications
  May–June 2027; register 1 Jul 2027; enforcement 1 Jan 2028. The State Secretary (Vijlbrief)
  stood by it in May 2026 despite tight implementation, and SRA reported phased commencement in
  July 2026.
- Unknown: whether NAU will offer an API or bulk lookup. If not, monitoring means scraping or
  manual entry. If NAU adds free alerts, the gap shrinks.

## Barriers
- Timing: the register goes live only on 1 Jul 2027 and fines start 1 Jan 2028, so paying demand
  peaks in H2 2027.
- No confirmed NAU API.
- Incumbents can add it as a feature: VMSs, payroll/ERP vendors and KvK/credit monitoring
  (Creditsafe and others; not verified as Wtta-specific).
- The SNA ecosystem may extend its own register alerts (unverified).

## Buyer and price
The buyer is the HR, procurement or operations manager at a 100–2,000-employee hirer using 5–50
staffing and subcontracting suppliers, or the accountant/payroll adviser acting for SME hirers.
The fine exposure is up to EUR 90,000 per violation (country report). Inspection and ketenaansprakelijkheid (chain liability)
risks come on top. A plausible price is EUR 50–300/month by supplier count; no Wtta-specific
product price was found.
