# C0716 · Nigeria · MBS e-invoicing issuance (duplicate of C0713)

## Verdict and score
**Still closed. 3/10.** Issuing through the NRS Merchant-Buyer Solution (MBS) runs through NRS/NITDA-accredited System Integrators and Access Point Providers (APPs). That list is long and still growing:
- DigiTax (800+ clients), Redtech, eTranzact, Heirs Technologies, Pillarcraft, Doftwerks (SI plus APP), Pronalytics with Remita, Interswitch, Cryptware, Duplo, Nvono.
- Zoho Books via Remita.
- The source report counts 16+.

The SME segment Sonnet thought was "likely a gap" is already targeted explicitly. **Afri Invoice** launched for SMEs, freelancers and entrepreneurs and is built to the FIRS BIS 3.0 / MBS spec. Zoho brings a mass-market SME accounting product.

Emerging taxpayers (turnover under ₦1bn) have engagement from Jan 2027, go-live 1 Jul 2027 and enforcement in early 2028. By then the accredited providers will be competing on price for them. A new entrant needs NITDA/NRS accreditation under the National Regulatory Guideline for E-Invoicing, or has to white-label one of the accredited providers. Either way the result is a reseller with no structural edge.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- Sonnet found only Redtech and eTranzact and inferred that the SME tier was under-served. The source report and my searches show 16+ accredited providers, with new accreditations announced through 2026 (Heirs, Pillarcraft, Doftwerks, Pronalytics), and at least one SME-first product (Afri Invoice) plus Zoho.
- Sonnet treated certification as a barrier to work around by reselling. That confirms the gap is not open to an owner-founder.
- The pain for small firms is real: commentary warns the cost of formalisation could push micro firms into informality. That is a policy problem, not a software gap.

## Barriers
- NITDA/NRS accreditation as SI or APP, under the National Regulatory Guideline for Electronic Invoicing in Nigeria 2025 (updated 2026).
- Emerging-taxpayer go-live is 1 Jul 2027, with enforcement in 2028. Deadlines have already slipped once (the large-taxpayer deadline moved from 30 Jun to 31 Jul 2026).
- Micro-firm WTP is low, and the sector fears informality.

## Buyer and price
- **Buyer.** Owner or accountant of a sub-₦1bn B2B firm, e.g. a distributor or service company.
- **Price anchor.** Third-party estimates of ₦5k–15k a month for entry tiers and ₦15k–40k for active SMEs (unverified blog estimates). No provider price lists were verified.
