# C0868 / C0874 · Serbia · Waste DKO records and annual NRIZ reports

## Verdict and score
**Still closed (re-check after the 2026–27 law changes). 4/10.** None of the three named competitors could be confirmed: UPSEKO (upseko.org), eDepo (Extreme d.o.o.) and POTOS (PanMax). Sonnet failed to find them, and so did I, even searching the source's own domains. Extreme d.o.o. exists as an ERP/DMS vendor, but no eDepo page was surfaced.

The idea stays closed for non-competitor reasons:
- **Free government tool.** The forms are filed for free in the SEPA National Register of Pollution Sources (NRIZ) information system: GIO1–GIO5, DKO and DEO6 are all online forms at sepa.gov.rs. A paid product can only add daily-record keeping, reminders and multi-client handling on top of it.
- **Law in flux.** The current Waste Management Law has applied since 12 Dec 2025, with some provisions from 1 Jan 2027. A working group was formed on 25 Aug 2026 to draft further amendments, aligning with the EU Waste Framework Directive 2018/851 and Waste Shipment Regulation 2024/1157, for adoption in Q4 2026. Any workflow built now may change within months.
- **Weak enforcement.** Even municipalities mostly fail to report: 55 said reports simply weren't made. Small waste generators feel little pressure.
- **Low price anchor.** The source report's RSD 1,000 a month (about EUR 8.5) for UPSEKO is unverified, but it fits a market served by a free tool and cheap consultants.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- Sonnet's "not-a-real-competitor" verdicts stand as "not verified". Two independent search passes could not confirm any of the three products, so the triage list itself is suspect.
- Sonnet correctly flagged the SEPA/NRIZ portal as the real incumbent. I confirmed that DKO is one of the forms in that system.
- New fact: amendments are being drafted, with adoption planned for Q4 2026. That argues for waiting, not building.
- I also found UpOl, an academic or regional waste-records system for local communities (Niš University paper), though its commercial status is unverified.

## Barriers
- The free government portal is the system of record.
- Rules are pending (amendments in drafting, provisions from 2027).
- Enforcement is low.
- The waste catalogue (Q/Y/D/R codes) and hazardous-waste rules add domain complexity.

## Buyer and price
- **Buyer.** An environmental consultancy in Serbia handling waste records for 20–100 client firms, or a licensed waste collector or operator issuing many DKOs.
- **Price anchor.** About RSD 1,000 a month for the cheapest tool (unverified source figure). Consultant retainers are unverified.
