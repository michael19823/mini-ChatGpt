# C0928 · South Korea · Construction daily-worker labour reporting (퇴직공제, 근로내용확인신고, 4대보험)

## Verdict and score
**Still closed. 4/10.** The workflow is painful, monthly and fined. Late or missing 퇴직공제 reports carry a ₩1M fine on first violation, and the daily contribution rose from ₩6,500 to ₩8,700 for 2026. But dedicated software is a mature, cheap category.

**Dedicated programs.** Korean guides put construction 일용직 labour-cost programs at **₩30k–150k a month** ("월 3~5만 원이면 전용 프로그램이 다 해주며"). They cover:
- 4대보험 acquisition and loss reports
- 근로내용확인신고
- 국세청 지급명세서 (withholding statements)
- 퇴직공제 EDI to 건설근로자공제회

**Other suppliers.**
- Construction ERPs such as iQuest bundle 노무관리.
- Kmong freelancers and agencies sell outsourced 일용직 filing.
- New platforms like 일용이 (Connectin: worker app, site-manager app and HQ web; 250+ sites) cover identity checks, attendance, TBM and 공수 settlement.
- The electronic-card system (전자카드제) feeds attendance into 공제회 records. Large builders (e.g. Daewoo E&C, Mar 2026) are linking directly with 공제회. Terminal installation becomes a statutory duty from 8 Jan 2027.

A new entrant would compete on price against products already at ₩30k a month, and against free government EDI portals.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- **Sonnet mixed up two products.** It assessed a 2025 iOS app called 김반장 (Taewon Soft) as "weak". The source report meant **김반장 by 한국기업진흥원 (kbz.co.kr)**, a labour-cost reporting program that the source says has served about 10k construction firms over 20 years. My searches confirmed "김반장" as construction labour-cost reporting software covering 4대보험 and 퇴직공제 filings. They also surfaced only the iOS app for the kbz.co.kr name, so the 10k-firm claim and the link between the two products remain unverified.
- The other three competitor files were empty placeholders ("south-korea--.md"), so 공무링크, 일용이 and 진승정보기술 were never checked. I confirmed 일용이 (Connectin, 250+ sites). 공무링크's price and 진승정보기술 were not verified.
- New: a market price band of ₩30k–150k a month, and the 2027 electronic-card terminal mandate, which pushes reporting toward card-integrated systems that large builders and 공제회 control.

## Barriers
- Free government EDI, plus cheap incumbents at ₩30k–150k a month.
- The electronic-card mandate (terminals required from 8 Jan 2027) shifts data capture to card systems tied to 공제회.
- Domain complexity: 4대보험 eligibility rules for daily workers, 퇴직공제 thresholds (public works ≥ ₩100M, private ≥ ₩300M).
- A Korean entity and Korean-language support are needed.

## Buyer and price
- **Buyer.** Office manager (경리/공무) at a small specialty subcontractor (전문건설업체) with 20–200 daily workers a month.
- **Price anchor.** ₩30k–150k a month for dedicated programs (blog-reported, unverified per vendor).
