# C1002 — Taiwan: carbon / GHG inventory platforms (duplicate of C0999)

## Verdict and score
**Still closed: 3.1/10.** Sonnet's opening was "a cheap, self-serve Chinese-language GHG tool for
SME suppliers". The state is already giving that segment away.
- The MOEA SME and Startup Administration helped **over 13,000 firms** complete a free
  carbon-account health check in 2025. It targets **over 25,000 in 2026**, delivered through more
  than 2,000 trained accountants, bookkeepers and tax agents.
- It is adding **AI tools to help firms "keep carbon books"** alongside their accounts.
- MOEA also runs a free carbon-estimation digital tool on the 2050 Net Zero platform, plus the
  "碳顧問+" online advisory service.
- 綠學院 (Green Impact) offers a zero-cost trial inventory package for SMEs.

In the paid market, listed firms (精誠 Carbon Envision, 叡揚 NetZero 零碳雲 SaaS, 倍力 碳管家, 東捷
碳捷流, 伊雲谷), SME-leaning vendors (GSS Vital NetZero, Sustaihub, TPIsoftware GreenSwift) and SAP
with partners (ITIS CarbonX) cover every tier. The carbon fee (2026) applies only to emitters above
25,000 t, so SMEs face customer pressure, not a legal mandate.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- Sonnet called both the listed firms and SAP "beatable" on SME fit, but missed the **free
  government programme run through the accountants SMEs already use**. That channel is the very
  distribution route a newcomer would need, and it is now subsidised and adding AI tooling.
- It also missed Green Impact's zero-cost SME package and the MOEA carbon estimation tool.
- This idea duplicates C0999, and the same conclusion applies to both.

## Barriers
- A free, state-funded programme in the accountants' channel.
- No legal mandate for SMEs; the carbon fee covers only large emitters.
- Verification (ISO 14064-1) is often what buyers ask for, and software can't provide it.

## Buyer and price
The buyer is the admin or ESG person at an SME supplier to a listed Taiwanese or foreign brand.
The anchor is **free** (government health check and tool) or a zero-cost trial. Paid SaaS prices
are unpublished.
