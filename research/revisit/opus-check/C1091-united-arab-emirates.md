# C1091 · United Arab Emirates · Clinic insurance denial management (DHA/DOH)

## Verdict and score
**Still closed. 4/10.** The pain is real and well documented: 99 rejection codes, about 15% of claims rejected, and 60–113 day payment cycles. But funded, UAE-native players already target exactly this workflow, and small clinics too:
- **KLAIM**: Abu Dhabi, raised USD 26M, claims-management SaaS plus receivables funding, and names smaller clinics as a key focus.
- **SANTECHTURE**: UAE end-to-end RCM SaaS, CorroHealth investment June 2025.
- **Zavis AI**: a free claim-rejection analyzer for DHPO, Daman, Shafafiya and NEXtCARE, plus integrations with local clinic systems.
- **Manteq**: HIS with Alpha II claim scrubbing.
- **ClaimGuard AI**: a Dubai SME-backed student startup doing pre-submission rejection prediction.

The free Zavis analyzer sets the price of the "paste a rejection, get a fix" wedge at zero. A solo founder would compete on clinic access with KLAIM's funding offer, and that is a losing position.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- Sonnet treated "HIS vendors", "RCM vendors" and "outsourcers" as faceless categories with no complaints, and marked all three "beatable". That was a triage-list failure: it never identified the specific funded software competitors.
- I found KLAIM's clinic focus and funding, SANTECHTURE's RCM suite and investment, and the Manteq/Alpha II scrubbing partnership. I also confirmed what Zavis offers: a free analyzer and custom-priced clinic modules.
- Sonnet's "standalone low-cost analyzer is not served" claim is wrong. Zavis offers exactly that, for free.

## Barriers
- **Rails.** Access to the DHA eClaimLink/DHPO and DOH Shafafiya rails runs through the facility's credentials and integrations with its HIS. A third-party tool needs either HIS integration or manual remittance upload. I found no specific DHA registration rule for billing intermediaries (unverified).
- **Data protection.** Health-data rules apply: UAE ICT Health Law (Federal Law 2/2019) and data-localisation expectations. These are unverified in detail, but they are a known hurdle for overseas founders.
- **Local entity.** Selling to clinics and handling health data realistically needs a UAE presence.

## Buyer and price
- **Buyer.** Practice manager or owner of a 1–5 doctor polyclinic in Dubai or Abu Dhabi.
- **Price anchors.** Generic clinic SaaS runs USD 600–1,600 per month (vendor blog, unverified). Outsourced RCM typically takes 5–9% of collections (global benchmark, not UAE-verified). KLAIM and Zavis quote on request.
