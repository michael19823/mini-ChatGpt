# C1199 · Brazil · RENAVE for used-car lots

## Verdict and score
**Still closed. 4/10.** The trigger is real: RENAVE has been mandatory nationwide since 28 Sep 2026, with a "gravíssima" fine per vehicle. But three things close the gap for a small founder.
- **Entry is per state.** An "empresa integradora" is credentialed state by state through each Detran's Sistema de Credenciamento de Empresas (SCE). MG, AL (Portaria 739/2026), MT, PR, RJ and GO each have their own portarias. Credentials are valid for 5 years.
  - São Paulo, the largest market, uses a different model: since Oct 2024, entries are registered by Detran-SP-credentialed inspection (vistoria) companies.
  - A national product therefore means up to 27 credentialings and integrations.
- **Incumbents are established.** Dealer-management systems and integradoras have run RENAVE since 2021 for new-car dealers and since Oct 2024 in SP. The source names BNDV, Revenda Mais, Autoconf, RenaveFácil, RenaveAuto and Trillia B3, and SIG Veículos advertises full RenaveFácil integration. Small lots already buy a DMS (e.g. AutoSDR from R$297 a month), and those DMS bundle or partner for RENAVE.
- **Fees are fixed per transaction.** In RS, for example, a RENAVE transfer costs R$40.69 plus inspection. Integrator margin sits on top of state fees, which leaves little room for a cheaper entrant.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- Sonnet called the integrators "beatable" and "weak" without naming or pricing any. I could not find published integrator prices either (unverified), but I confirmed the credentialing regime.
  - It is not a single Senatran homologation, as the source said. It is per-state Detran SCE credentialing with ordinary corporate documents, valid 5 years.
  - That lowers the legal bar in any one state, but multiplies the work nationally.
- SP has been live since Oct 2024 through credentialed inspection firms. The incumbents have therefore been through 2 years of production use in the largest state, which weakens the "brand-new market" argument.
- Mandate-start complaints were not yet found (8 days in), so pain at small lots is unverified.

## Barriers
- Per-state Detran SCE credentialing (e-CNPJ, corporate documents, signed Termo de Credenciamento, 5-year portaria), plus Serpro/RENAVE integration.
- SP's distinct vistoria-based model.
- State fees per transaction, e.g. R$40.69 in RS.
- Must be a Brazilian company (CNPJ).

## Buyer and price
- **Buyer.** Owner of a small independent used-car lot (revenda de seminovos) with 10–50 cars in stock, outside SP.
- **Price anchors.** DMS from R$297 a month (AutoSDR). Detran RENAVE fee about R$40.69 per transfer (RS). No integrator per-vehicle prices found (unverified).
