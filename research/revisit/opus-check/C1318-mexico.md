# C1318 — Mexico: domestic-worker employers (IMSS)

## Verdict and score
**Still closed: 3.4/10.** The free IMSS service already does the core work. It registers the
worker in 5 steps, together with INFONAVIT. It generates payment lines and then emails a new one
every month before the 20th. A fee simulator is included. So the "monthly compliance pain" that a
product would sell against is already automated for free. Demand is also shrinking rather than
growing: IMSS counted 57,653 domestic-worker positions in August 2026, down from just over 60,000
in June 2025, against roughly 2.4–2.5M workers. Enforcement doesn't push households to pay, and
the one funded specialist (Symplifica) has gained little traction after several years.

## What changed versus the Sonnet evidence
- Sonnet rated Symplifica "beatable" because its traction is thin. That is right, but it reads the
  wrong way for a new entrant. A USD 3m-funded specialist that offers free trial months and still
  reports only ~5,000 affiliates across Colombia *and* Mexico (undated press figure) is evidence of weak willingness to
  pay, not an open gap.
- Sonnet treated the IMSS portal as covering "basic affiliation". In fact it also automates the
  recurring step: emailed monthly payment lines and flexible daily, monthly or annual payment.
- The ilovecfdi verdict (not a real competitor) stands, but it doesn't matter: the free IMSS tool
  is the real substitute.
- Registrations fell between 2025 and 2026, so the trigger is weakening.

## Competitors (corrected)
| Competitor | Type | Status |

| Symplifica | Funded specialist (USD 3m, Soros EDF); contracts, payroll, payment lines, worker app | Active; Mexico pricing not public (unverified); trial months offered |
| Accountants/gestores, free online calculators | Manual or informal | Common, unquantified |

## Barriers
- A free government tool covers the mandatory step from start to finish.
- Enforcement is weak: fines of up to ~MXN 41k exist but are rarely applied, so registrations stay
  at ~2%.
- B2C distribution means millions of individual households with low intent. Customer acquisition
  cost would exceed lifetime value at a likely price of ≤MXN 100/month.

## Buyer and price
The buyer is an urban middle-class household with one domestic worker. The price anchor is
Symplifica's Colombian plans at about COP 16.5–29.5k/month (~USD 4–7); the Mexico price is
unverified. IMSS contributions themselves run to a few hundred MXN a month, so there is little room
for a service fee on top.
