# Revisit pass: ranking after the Opus check

Date: 2026-10-06. This file covers the 30 revived ideas with need 7 or higher. Each one was
checked by Opus in `opus-check/` (two sessions of 15 ideas, up to 8 searches per idea). The 50 or so
revived ideas with need 6 have not been checked.

## Outcome

| Verdict | Count |
|---|---|
| Revived | 1 |
| Narrow (open in one segment only) | 7 |
| Still closed | 22 |

The Sonnet competitor pass was too optimistic. In most cases where Sonnet found every listed
competitor weak or "not a real competitor", the triage list was simply wrong. A direct
"<workflow> software <country>" search found the real incumbents, as with Lithuania
StatybosZurnalas.lt, Korea 김반장 and Hong Kong Info-Tech/Workstem. Other ideas are closed by
barriers that aren't competitors:
- accreditation or homologation: Nigeria, Rwanda, DR Congo, Bosnia, Brazil RENAVE;
- free government tools: Serbia NRIZ, Mexico IMSS, Taiwan MOEA, Paraguay e-Kuatia'i;
- mandates that exempt small firms: India PIBO, Bulgaria SAF-T, Turkey CBAM de minimis;
- unpublished specs: Senegal.

## Caveats

- Sonnet competitor verdicts are not reliable on their own. Of the 30 ideas Sonnet called revived,
  Opus kept 1, narrowed 7 and closed 22.
- The PPAT score assumes that no priced app exists. Check the Indonesian app stores and notary
  forums by hand before you build.
- The need-6 revived ideas (about 50) would need the same check. Given this hit rate, expect about
  2 more ideas that are revived or narrow.
